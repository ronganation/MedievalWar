## 3. Strong Designer Checklist Status Lines

- [CHARACTER-POPULATION-AUTHORITY-COMPLETE-PENDING-OWNER] The project now has a substantive static authority for population sources, latent people, persistence, promotion, and emergence, with execution and canonical activation still closed.
- [SYNTHETIC-EXECUTABLE-PROOF-PASS] V1.8.1 is accepted as an identity-clean synthetic executable proof.
- [PERSISTENT-PERSONHOOD-PROVEN] Opportunities, trials, mandates, recovery, repeat service, demotion, exit, and death operate on persistent person records rather than vacancy-generated replacements.
- [READINESS-DISCOVERY-SEPARATED] Formation and domain readiness remain distinct from audience recognition, discoverability, availability, and appointment.
- [REPEAT-CAREER-PROVEN] BASE demonstrates two separate successful mandates for one persistent person with retained prior history.
- [RECOVERY-PROVEN] Branch A demonstrates failure, remedial formation, recovery review, restored readiness, new contact and trial, and later successful service.
- [MIXED-REVIEW-PROVEN] Branch B demonstrates a structured mixed-service review with deterministic closure rather than automatic success or failure treatment.
- [LIFECYCLE-CLEANUP-PROVEN] Death, exit, and demotion close active mandate and obligation state without deleting historical identity records.
- [RELATIONSHIP-SURVIVAL-BOUNDED] The focused fixture preserves a relationship identifier and provenance reference through lifecycle transition; exhaustive relationship simulation is not claimed.
- [DETERMINISTIC-REPLAY-PROVEN] The supplied BASE fixture reproduces identically.
- [BRANCH-DIVERGENCE-PROVEN] BASE, A, and B share an equal turn-four checkpoint and later produce distinct replay hashes.
- [CHECKPOINT-RESTORATION-NOT-ROLLBACK] The package proves checkpoint serialization, reload equality, and continuation, not transactional failed-turn rollback.
- [INTERPRETATION-FORMALIZED] The Simulation Evidence, Interpretation, and Reuse Authority records what each artifact means and which claims may be reused.
- [NO-CANONICAL-SYNTHETIC-LEAKAGE] Synthetic people, schedules, overrides, and branch outcomes must not be copied into canonical Iberia merely because the executable proof passed.
- [NO-IMPLEMENTATION-PROMOTION] Registry, Opening State, production saves, migration, payloads, importers, runtime schemas, and Unreal work remain closed.
