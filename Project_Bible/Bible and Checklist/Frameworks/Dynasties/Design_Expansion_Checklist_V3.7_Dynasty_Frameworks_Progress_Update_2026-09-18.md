## 15. Advanced-Save and Migration Firewall

Advanced saves may not be rewritten toward the January 1062 opening. Migration and future implementation may not:

- restore dead or removed Dynasty members;
- reset Branch, Household, headship, succession, relationship, or Founder outcomes;
- recreate ended marriages, treaties, claims, offices, or obligations;
- remove reconciliation, breach, exile, partition, usurpation, restoration, or extinction history;
- restore opening Candidates, Recognition, Regency, or consolidation state;
- reset Dynasty AI strategy history or Campaign Objective state;
- reveal hidden or external-private information;
- rebuild runtime state from static definitions;
- remove Campaign divergence.

Ambiguous migration is quarantined rather than guessed. Repeat-application idempotence remains a future migration-owner validation requirement.
