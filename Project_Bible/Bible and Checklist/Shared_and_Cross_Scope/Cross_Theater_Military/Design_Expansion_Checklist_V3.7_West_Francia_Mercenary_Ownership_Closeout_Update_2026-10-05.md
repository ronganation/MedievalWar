## 1. Purpose

This standalone update records the bounded completion of the West Francia mercenary canonical-ownership, employer-access, shared-family collision, and successor-disposition tranche.

It supplements but does not replace or directly edit `Design_Expansion_Checklist_V3.7.md`. It exists to:

- preserve the completed West Francia mercenary relationship matrix;
- preserve the shared-family collision and successor dispositions;
- record the consistency-review result and current closeout boundary;
- prevent duplicate crossbow, slinger, regional, domestic, and mercenary cards or pools;
- preserve exact source, company, replacement, route, and employer ownership;
- distinguish a shared multi-theater core family from a theater expression;
- hold conditional profiles without deleting useful design work or promoting unsupported content;
- stop additional West Francia mercenary paperwork or unit creation merely to increase file count;
- route future work only through evidence-backed source admission, a separately justified shared master profile, or a new workstream gate.

This update creates no unit, company, recruitment pool, opening formation, access right, exact value, stable ID, Registry record, payload, runtime state, or Unreal asset.
