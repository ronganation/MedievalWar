Terms such as Demesne, Household, Sergeant, Militia, Knight, Hird, Royal, Guard, Heavy, Light, and Professional are not banned. Their exact meaning and gameplay expression must be adapted to the realm and unit. The shared naming standard is guidance for clarity and anti-duplication, not a terminology veto.

## 6. Practical Unit Retention Test

Retain or create a separate family when the unit supplies a meaningful difference in one or more connected areas:

- recruitment source or locality;
- obligation, institution, household, contract, or access route;
- availability, cost, upkeep, replenishment, or recovery;
- equipment, training, leadership, formation, or mobility;
- battlefield role, battle cycle, strengths, weaknesses, or counters;
- campaign utility, garrison value, strategic access, or AI behavior;
- visual and realm identity.

Merge, treat as a variant, or use an alias only when the difference is mostly naming, presentation, or minor equipment without a meaningful player or AI decision.

## 13. France Working Questions Per Unit

For each selected profile, answer only the practical questions needed for design:

1. Why does the player recruit this unit?
2. Why does the AI recruit this unit?
3. What source or institution supports it?
4. Where and under what conditions is it available?
5. What is its battlefield verb and role?
6. What are its strengths, weaknesses, and counters?
7. What nearby France unit is most similar, and why are both still useful?
8. Does the unit remain useful after later developments?
9. How does it replenish or recover at a readable level?
10. What is still uncertain or speculative?
