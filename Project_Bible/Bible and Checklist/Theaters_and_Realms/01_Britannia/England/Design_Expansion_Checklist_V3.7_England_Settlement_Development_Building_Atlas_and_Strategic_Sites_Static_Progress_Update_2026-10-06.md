## 4. What this tranche substantively added

### 4.1 England building identity

The Realm portfolio maps F01-F15, from agriculture and housing through routes, defense, muster, institutions, administration, workshops, maritime support, health, public identity, communication, estates, and frontier recovery. It preserves broad construction freedom with soft capacity and finite throughput instead of tiny permanent slots or a single universal building tree. One complex can express multiple functions; one family can use multiple assets. A definition, an instance, an operating facility, and output remain different things.

The Early England shared foundation precedes the scenario-defining Anglo-Saxon Continuity and Anglo-Norman English Development overlays. Neither overlay deletes inherited institutions, instantly replaces architecture, mandates conquest, or grants all regions the same capability. Hybrid and regionally plural outcomes remain conditional on owners, rights, labor, materials, acceptance, and investment. This two-direction structure is England-specific, not a universal cultural-fork template for other Realms.

### 4.2 Atlas and player-facing clarity

The atlas records fifteen family cards and 106 candidate branch cards, six candidate combined complexes, four development-overlay cards, and five regional-lens cards. The full atlas and active settlement planner are separate but connected information surfaces. Candidate card IDs are navigation aids, not canonical Registry IDs. The first England building-planner visual is retained as a synthetic reference; the denser combined planner/map graphic is an exploratory density warning, not final UI authority. No further visual prototype is required by the latest static handoff.

### 4.3 Town, castle, burh, and host eligibility

The England settlement-expression portfolio defines town/civic and castle/fortified-center progressions, burh treatment, town-castle relationships, and attached, distributed, and independent sites. The host reconciliation checks all fifteen families against town, burh, castle, estate, port, ecclesiastical, frontier, and independent-site contexts. Eligibility is not availability, construction, operation, or an opening fact. Conversion must account for what persists, what is disrupted, and what requires renewed owner validation; it is neither a free upgrade nor a visual reskin.

### 4.4 Strategic sites and raids

The outlying-site extension defines five representation modes: background capacity, attached site, independent node, distributed network, and temporary operational site. Separate nodes need distinct control, access, raid, defense, supply, occupation, or battle value; decorative resource dots and one node per resource type fail the map-readability test. The focused reconciliation adds force/mission feasibility, distinctions among entry, local security, military holding, occupation, administration, operation, and lawful ownership, and owner receipts for lasting damage, stock changes, displacement, access, knowledge, and recovery. A raid card does not create a force, target stock, battle map, successful seizure, or persistent outcome.

### 4.5 Britania and external-owner boundary

The integrated review passed England's functional portfolio but did not locate a dedicated current canonical Britania architecture-expression authority in its search. A search miss was explicitly **not** treated as proof of absence. England may use owner-neutral expression tokens for static atlas content, but may not prescribe Scotland, Ireland, Wales, island, or Norse expressions; final material palettes, meshes, icons, map landmarks, and exact 1062 architecture remain owner-gated. A new theater authority is not justified merely for symmetry.

## 5. Owner and noncreation boundaries

The building/atlas layer defines possible functions, candidate eligibility, planning cards, and readable decisions. Geography and province owners decide physical nodes and regional relations. Population controls people, workforce, specialists, migration, and return. Property, law, and government control rights, mandates, jurisdiction, and lawful transfer. Economy, production, treasury, trade, supply, and construction owners control inputs, projects, delivery, stock, operation, and recovery. Military and battle owners control forces, readiness, defense, raids, and combat results. Information owners control knowledge, confidence, delay, concealment, and reports. Opening State owns exact January 1062 assignments. Presentation/art owners control final visuals. Registry, importer, runtime, save, and migration remain separate technical owners.

A building, program, technology unlock, card, or site definition does **not** create people, equipment, horses, ships, money, goods, offices, institutions, armies, recruitment pools, rights, operating output, or historical facts. Population informs finite recruitment but is not directly spent as army manpower. Owner separation routes content; it must not strip strategic meaning from the England portfolio.
