#### 3.2 Early foundation completed

The shared Early portfolio contains ten mature unit families across four combat categories:

```yaml
early_infantry:
  - LocalMuster
  - Ceithern
  - TuathSpearmen
  - LuchtTighe
  - Ostmen
early_foot_archers_skirmishers:
  - DeisiJavelinmen
  - Saigteoiri
  - TuathSlingers
early_cavalry:
  - RoyalMountedRetainers
early_mounted_skirmishers:
  - MountedCeithern
```

The Early roster is treated as a persistent provider-bound foundation rather than a disposable opening tier. Community defense, indigenous light infantry, local ruler contingents, household troops, Norse-Gaelic urban-maritime forces, regional missile traditions, royal mounted retainers, and mounted scouts retain separate providers, battlefield purchases, campaign bargains, and replenishment constraints.

The refined Early README also established:

- A distinct battlefield purchase and campaign bargain for every family.
- Protected Early identities during High development.
- A prohibition on automatic whole-family promotion.
- An honest combined-arms model that describes potential cooperation rather than guaranteed simultaneous availability.
- A closed broad-expansion gate unless a named provider, battlefield, campaign, availability, and recovery gap survives comparison with all existing Early profiles.

#### 3.3 Traditional High portfolio completed

The Traditional High portfolio contains fifteen indexed unit profiles:

```yaml
traditional_high:
  infantry: 6
  foot_archers_skirmishers: 4
  cavalry: 4
  mounted_skirmishers: 1
  total: 15
```

This portfolio develops Gaelic and established Irish military capability through selective veteran concentration, improved household forces, formation archery, crossbow adoption, cavalry development, and mounted support. It does not erase the Early foundation or automatically transform every Early provider into a High card.

Traditional High admission and persistence require:

- Continued usefulness for the Early family.
- Separate provider depth or an explicitly limited contributor relationship.
- Real equipment, horse, ammunition, officer, training, and recovery requirements.
- A battlefield purchase readable beside both Early and Hiberno-Norman alternatives.
- No automatic promotion caused by experience, date, culture, building, or research alone.

#### 3.4 Hiberno-Norman High portfolio completed

The Hiberno-Norman High portfolio contains twelve indexed unit profiles:

```yaml

  infantry: 5
  foot_archers_skirmishers: 3
```

This is a bounded lordship, settlement, retinue, missile, and cavalry portfolio. It is not a universal replacement for Gaelic Ireland and does not imply automatic Normanization of every region or provider.

Access remains conditional on actual lordship, settlement, retinue, political, institutional, equipment, and horse capacity. Controlled battlefield overlap with Traditional forces is permitted where provider identity and campaign economics remain distinct.

The empty Hiberno-Norman mounted-skirmisher folder is retained honestly. Folder symmetry is not sufficient authority to create a unit.

The controlling model is additive and selective:

```text
Shared Early Foundation
        ↓
Selective contributor transfer, independent provider growth,
new equipment, training, institutions, political access, and recovery capacity
        ↓
Traditional High and Hiberno-Norman High portfolios coexist
without deleting the Early families or sharing one unlimited pool
```

Progression authorities unlock eligibility and capability. They do not create people, officers, weapons, armor, bows, crossbows, ammunition, horses, tack, remounts, workshops, training time, transport, or replacement capacity.
