The final evidence reconciliation fingerprints twelve source files and binds the family to the accepted twenty-four-test Historical Emergence replay.

### 3.6 Information and Intelligence

The Information and Intelligence tranche established eight review-only information domains, source references, report candidates, access review, summaries, contradiction and quarantine handling, and an AI-readable but command-ineligible boundary.

### 3.7 Realm AI and Personality

The Realm AI and Personality tranche established a source-backed review-only personality profile, eight strategic contexts, priority bands, risk and opportunity review, proposal explanations, uncertainty preservation, and absolute no-decision and no-command firewalls.

### 3.8 Cross-Realm and Theater Integration

The Cross-Realm and Theater tranche established a review-only Scotland to Britania association declaration; external Realm boundaries for England, Ireland, and Wales; cross-Realm information envelopes; diplomacy and recognition references; maritime and island interfaces; Historical Emergence external conditions; and AI external-context review.
