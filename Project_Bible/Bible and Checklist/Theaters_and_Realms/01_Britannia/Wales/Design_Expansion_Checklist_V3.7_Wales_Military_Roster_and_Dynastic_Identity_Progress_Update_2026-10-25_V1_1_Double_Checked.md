## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales has completed a bounded post-closeout design tranche focused on the military roster, Early-to-High development, faction replayability, dynastic military relationships, provider channels, and emergent formations. This work does not reopen broad Wales Realm design or the accepted Unreal Engine 5.8 implementation families. It adds review-only roster and identity authorities beneath the existing constitutional, political, historical, and implementation boundaries.

The current Wales Units and Roster workstream now records:

```yaml

```

The accepted design philosophy is now explicit:

- Early formations persist and may improve rather than disappearing at a date boundary.
- High capabilities emerge through historical windows, world state, player or AI commitment, real providers, officers, equipment, animals, training, missions, and recovery.
- Research can improve organization and reduce friction but cannot create people, horses, equipment, officers, consent, or recruitment pools.
- Dynastic identity can route or qualify providers but never substitutes for an actual provider.
- Characters and houses usually alter existing unit availability, command, banners, missions, obligations, replenishment, and political consequences rather than generating duplicate roster cards.
- Historical Momentum and Historical Emergence use authored conditions and pressures rather than fixed guaranteed outcomes.
- Wales remains expandable through named, evidence-backed gaps, but broad speculative unit or authority expansion is closed again after this tranche.

## 4. Wales Replayability and Faction Identity Closeout

Wales now has a realm-specific replayability model rather than a universal cultural fork.

```yaml
identity_question: CanDynasticRegionalHouseholdAndLocalMilitaryCapacityBecomeCoordinatedWelshPowerWithoutErasingTheRelationshipsThatProducedIt
replayability_dimensions:
  - DynasticRecognition
  - RegionalParticipation
  - PoliticalCoordination
  - ProviderCooperation
  - MilitaryOrganization
  - HouseholdConcentration
  - MissileAndLongbowDevelopment
  - RegionalSpecialization
  - CoalitionReliability
  - RecoveryAfterLoss
```

Different campaigns may prioritize coordinated infantry, regional defensive anchors, heavy assault, household protection, operational raiding, domestic longbows, Gwent archery, princely missile reserves, broad mounted screening, frontier security, Mathrafal intervention, or household cavalry in different orders.

These are not permanent binary locks. A successful realm may broaden into several capabilities, but the route, losses, political relationships, provider strain, equipment, officers, horses, and recovery remain consequential.

## 5. Historical Momentum and Emergence Interpretation

The Wales roster and dynastic authority adopt the existing Historical Momentum principle:

```yaml
Historical_Momentum: PressureNotPredestination
Historical_Emergence: AuthoredConditionalOpportunityNotFixedGuaranteedEvent
calendar_only_trigger: prohibited
single_event_only_trigger: prohibited
historical_window: required_where_applicable
world_state: required
qualified_people_and_providers: required
material_and_organizational_inputs: required
player_or_AI_response: consequential
non_emergence: valid_outcome
alternate_person_or_formation: valid_whenHistoricallyAndSystemicallyQualified
```

A historical figure, resistance leader, restoration force, household formation, or regional military capability may emerge only when its historical and campaign conditions overlap. The framework may support a historical person where biography and date permit, a different qualified person where history diverges, several local actors, institutional leadership, or no major emergence at all.

This design does not authorize arbitrary renamed copies of historical figures. Candidate identity, biography, age, location, relationships, supporters, institutions, political conditions, and military capacity remain separately validated.
