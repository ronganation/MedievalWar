## 3. Realm Identity and Design Foundation Closed

Bavaria now has a developed static foundation built around:

- an office-centered Bavarian duchy;
- the separate Nordheim patrimonial and household power base;
- regional acceptance, cooperation, competition, and negotiated authority;
- differentiated governance, title, law, institutional, property, and service ownership;
- conditional development without a compulsory binary cultural fork;
- separate historical, biological, and campaign-season progression tracks.

The Bavaria-Nordheim firewall remains controlling:

```text
Bavaria Realm != Nordheim Household
Ducal office != patrimonial ownership
Otto's office resources != Nordheim property
Shared objective != shared ownership
Coalition != absorption
Office loss != household erasure
Realm transformation != Nordheim transfer
```

## 14. Reopening Conditions

Bavaria should reopen only for:

- material historical evidence;
- a demonstrated gameplay gap;
- a corrective authority defect;
- explicit Opening State authorization;
- explicit implementation authorization.

The following are not valid reopening reasons:

- document-count momentum;
- symmetry with another Realm;
- a stale footer suggesting another review;
- desire for placeholder characters or sites;
- desire to demonstrate technical progress without authorization.
