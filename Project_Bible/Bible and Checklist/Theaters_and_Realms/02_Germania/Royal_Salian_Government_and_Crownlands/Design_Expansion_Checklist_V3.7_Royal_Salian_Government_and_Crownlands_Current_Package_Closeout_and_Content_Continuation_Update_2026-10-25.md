### 9.1 Native Unit Admission Standard

Each new Royal Salian unit or tightly bounded family must establish:

- historical and faction ownership;
- recruitment provider and finite pool;
- direct royal, household, ministerial, settlement, castle, regional, ecclesiastical, or campaign access as appropriate;
- equipment and battlefield role;
- strengths and weaknesses;
- cost, availability, replenishment, and progression;
- distinction from Theater-owned regional units, mercenaries, and external princely forces;
- player-facing name and roster purpose.

The normal workflow is one unit at a time: draft in chat, refine, then pass through repository-aware integration when needed.
