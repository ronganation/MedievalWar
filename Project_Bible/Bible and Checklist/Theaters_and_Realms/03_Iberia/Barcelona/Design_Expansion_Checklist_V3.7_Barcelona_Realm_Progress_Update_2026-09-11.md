## 3. Realm Identity Preserved

The current package preserves Barcelona as a developed but bounded comital polity rather than a generic centralized state. The design retains:

- Ramon Berenguer I as the principal comital ruler without turning every authority reference into unrestricted practical control;
- Almodis de la Marche as a major political, dynastic, household, documentary, patronage, and diplomatic actor without automatic co-rule;
- Guislabert I as an independent ecclesiastical principal while preserving person, office, institution, site, building, chapter, and jurisdiction separation;
- child profiles as ReviewOnly design authorities rather than automatically active opening actors;
- comital, dynastic, household, ecclesiastical, territorial, legal, economic, route, supply, and military ownership boundaries;
- finite recruitment pools and source-based capability rather than recruitment spam or direct population-as-army-manpower conversion;
- Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence as causal definitions and eligibility surfaces rather than scripted outcomes;
- external-owner firewalls for shared Registry, ecclesiastical, neighboring Realm, taifa, maritime, military, and private character state;
- new-campaign-only Opening State behavior with apply-once and advanced-save protection.
