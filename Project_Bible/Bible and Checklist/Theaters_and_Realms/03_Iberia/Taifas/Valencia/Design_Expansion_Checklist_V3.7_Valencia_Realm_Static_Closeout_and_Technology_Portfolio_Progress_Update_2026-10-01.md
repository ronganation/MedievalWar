## 8. Reopening Conditions

Reopen Valencia design only when at least one condition is evidenced:

- Stronger evidence changes an existing person, site, institution, office, relationship, force, quantity, chronology, or presentation disposition.
- Two current authorities conflict.
- Registry work exposes an identity, alias, ownership, or ExternalLock defect.
- Batch 0 discovers a required project-native interface absent from the authority model.
- Executed fixtures or runtime validation expose a deterministic, serialization, owner, rollback, migration, or presentation defect.
- An owner issues a controlling adjudication requiring reconciliation.

Do not reopen broad Valencia design because a folder appears incomplete or another planning file could be created.
