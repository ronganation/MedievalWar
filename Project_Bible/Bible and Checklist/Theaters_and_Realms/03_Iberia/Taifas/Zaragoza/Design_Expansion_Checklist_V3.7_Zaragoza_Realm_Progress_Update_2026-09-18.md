## 10. Reopening Conditions

Reopen Zaragoza design only when at least one condition is evidenced:

- stronger evidence changes an existing person, place, institution, office, relationship, force, quantity, chronology, or presentation disposition;
- two current Zaragoza authorities conflict;
- Registry work exposes an identity, alias, ownership, migration, or External Lock defect;
- direct project inspection exposes a required project-native interface absent from the implementation plan;
- executed fixtures or runtime validation expose a deterministic, serialization, owner-routing, rollback, migration, presentation, AI, or battle defect;
- an owner issues a controlling adjudication requiring reconciliation.

Do not reopen broad Zaragoza design merely because more documentation could be created.
