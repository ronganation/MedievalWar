## 10. Cross-Realm Foundation Strategy

The Drengot sequence should now be used as a reference for sequencing, not as a mandatory file-for-file template.

For another Realm:

1. inspect the Realm's current static authorities and physical Unreal source;
2. identify which general foundation capabilities already exist;
3. add only substantive missing native families;
4. preserve Realm-specific identity rather than forcing Drengot symmetry;
5. consolidate stable information behind a deterministic read-only provider or bundle when justified;
6. add at most one useful consumer that proves the bundle can support real downstream inspection;
7. compile, automate, preserve predecessor hashes, and close the first foundation group;
8. defer deeper specialist, runtime, exact-opening, map, and battle work until shared architecture or Realm gameplay requires it.

A Realm may need fewer or more implementation families than Drengot Capua. Numerical symmetry is not a design requirement.
