1. Translation record envelope and source validation.
2. Deterministic import-candidate accounting.
3. Owner-batch and handoff-envelope planning.
4. Runtime-proven Gameplay Tag verification.
5. Partial typed projections that populate only source-supported fields.
6. Explicit blockers for every unsupported or owner-controlled field.
7. Focused owner tests, combined projection tests, and complete faction regression.
8. Copy-first rollback, fingerprints, manifests, receipts, and evidence packaging.
