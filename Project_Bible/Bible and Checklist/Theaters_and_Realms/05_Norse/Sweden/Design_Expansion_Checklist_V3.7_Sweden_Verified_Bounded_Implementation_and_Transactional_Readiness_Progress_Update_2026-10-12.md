## 12. Current Stop and Reopen Rules

Stop the generalized Sweden implementation workstream here.

Do not create another Sweden infrastructure, audit, readiness, dry-run, or review layer merely because the previous layer is complete.

Reopen Sweden only when at least one of these conditions is met:

- a specific owner disposition changes one or more promotion candidates from blocked to eligible;
- an owner resolves the claims/succession collision or authorizes a bounded reconciliation;
- a verified implementation or validation failure exposes a genuine source defect;
- a separately authorized Registry or Opening State gate is issued;
- the project explicitly authorizes runtime or military implementation;
- the project enters Late Era or later-era design scope;
- an approved scope change requires new Sweden content.

Do not reopen Sweden to:

- create symmetrical documentation;
- add more Early or High cards without a real gap;
- repeat current owner boundaries;
- duplicate Norse Theater units;
- manufacture eligible promotion operations;
- infer owner approval from technical readiness;
- treat a green Editor test suite as active campaign implementation.
