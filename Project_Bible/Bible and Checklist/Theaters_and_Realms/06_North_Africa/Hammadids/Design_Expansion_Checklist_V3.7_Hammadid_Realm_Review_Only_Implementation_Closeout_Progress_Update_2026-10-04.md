## 7. Why A16 and A17 Are Deferred

### 7.1 A16 cross-domain faction AI

The current authority foundation contains substantial Realm-personality and AI-planning material, but the next lane approaches cross-domain prioritization, arbitration, fairness, knowledge boundaries, and explanation behavior. That boundary should be developed more deliberately before another implementation tranche is authorized.

Deferral is not a missing-authority claim and not a failure of A15. It is a scope decision intended to prevent review projection from drifting into premature command execution, hidden-state access, or runtime behavior.

### 7.2 A17 decisions and events

General decisions, dilemmas, crises, opportunities, consequences, and event ownership require mature cross-system ownership and consequence routing. A17 remains separate from technology emergence, military emergence, presentation, and faction AI.

No event is scheduled, instantiated, or activated by this closeout.

## 9. Reopen Conditions

Hammadid implementation may reopen only for a named, bounded gate supported by at least one of the following:

- a verified change to a controlling source authority;
- an accepted change to an A1-A15 predecessor contract;
- a concrete defect in the accepted Hammadid projections or tests;
- an explicit owner disposition that changes currentness, lineage, package admission, identity, entity-kind, or external-owner status;
- separately authorized A16 cross-domain faction-AI design and implementation after its ownership and behavior boundary is mature;
- separately authorized A17 event-family design and implementation after cross-system event ownership is mature;
- an authorized exact Registry, Opening State, payload, importer, persistence, migration, runtime, UI, asset, or Battle gate.

Do not reopen for symmetry, numbering continuity, owner silence, historical plausibility alone, an empty folder, an available filename, or the desire to keep a long-running agent occupied.
