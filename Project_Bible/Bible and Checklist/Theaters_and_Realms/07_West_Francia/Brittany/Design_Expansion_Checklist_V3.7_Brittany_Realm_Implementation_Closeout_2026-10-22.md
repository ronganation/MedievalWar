### 12.1 Authentication limitation

The current intake validates structure, provider identity, owner routes, Realm and scenario scope, exact subject, exact receipt type, evidence references, currentness, revocation, supersession, deterministic fingerprints, and upstream agreement.

Authentication evidence IDs are provenance references only. No project cryptographic signature verifier currently exists.

A future verifier requires a shared project trust authority defining canonical signed bytes, approved signature algorithm and format, trusted issuer key or certificate source, key identifiers, key rotation, revocation, supersession, verification provider, and failure behavior.

A Brittany-specific cryptographic trust system must not be invented.
