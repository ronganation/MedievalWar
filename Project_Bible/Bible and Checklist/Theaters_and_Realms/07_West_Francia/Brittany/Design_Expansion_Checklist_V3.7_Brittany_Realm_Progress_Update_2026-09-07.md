## 3. Realm Identity Preserved

Brittany remains a compact, politically dense Realm defined by restored ducal authority without completed territorial consolidation.

The current package preserves:

- recognized ducal dignity without universal direct control;
- Rennais, Penthièvre, Nantes-Cornouaille, and Dol-Combourg political distinction;
- regional, household, dynastic, ecclesiastical, and military agency;
- finite recruitment pools and source-based musters;
- route, castle, port, access, supply, and local-defense constraints;
- Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence without scripted outcomes;
- player choice without routine administrative click inflation;
- external-owner firewalls;
- owner-routed implementation and save-state boundaries.
