## 5. Major Authority Work Completed

### 5.1 Foundation and history

- Historical reconciliation is modernized to the current Flanders authority chain.
- Historical evidence, chronology, and uncertainty remain separately controlled.
- Historical Memory, Historical Momentum, and Historical Emergence are modernized to current V2 authorities.
- Later history remains conditional and world-state-driven rather than guaranteed by date.

### 5.2 Characters, dynasty, household, governance, and law

- Character Authorities are developed to current parity scope.
- Character Network and Dynasty and Household authorities are modernized.
- Governance, offices, law, delegation, titles, claims, lordships, succession interfaces, courts, residence, officers, and domestic politics have current owner authorities.
- Baldwin of Mons remains the expected heir without transferring Hainaut control or resources to Flanders.
- Richilde remains an essential Hainaut owner interface.
- Robert remains strategically meaningful without a predetermined hostile path.
- Matilda remains a cross-realm dynastic and diplomatic interface without becoming a Flemish-controlled agent.

### 5.3 Communities, population, economy, and institutions

- Communities, culture, population, and institutions are modernized.
- Population, settlement, society, labor, and migration have a specialist owner.
- Economy, property, revenue, treasury, and development have a specialist owner.
- Ecclesiastical institutions, jurisdiction, patronage, advocacy, reform, character admission, and officeholder reconciliation have current authorities.
- Exact population, treasury, price, stock, labor, and revenue values remain unvalidated rather than invented.

### 5.4 Territory, routes, supply, and logistics

- Territory, settlements, geography, and climate are modernized.
- Historical routes, rivers, crossings, ports, and seasonal access have a dedicated evidence authority.
- Supply, logistics, transport, and recovery have a specialist authority.
- Winter does not automatically create flood, freeze, storm, route closure, or quantified supply state.

### 5.5 Military

- Military roster, recruitment obligation, command, and strategic access have a current authority.
- Exact roster families, finite recruitment pools, and Opening Inventory have a dedicated authority.
- Population may inform recruitment-pool availability and recovery but is not directly spent as army manpower.
- Unit family remains separate from unit instance.
- Recruitment source remains separate from recruitment pool.
- Command eligibility remains separate from command assignment.
- External contribution remains separate from domestic recruitment.

### 5.6 Knowledge, communication, diplomacy, and Realm AI

- Knowledge, Communication, Diplomacy, and External Interfaces now have a specialist authority.
- Realm Personality and AI are modernized to current V2 authority.
- Message, agreement, performance, consent, owner command, and external execution remain distinct.
- Realm AI reads permitted observations, explains, and proposes. Owner systems validate and execute.
