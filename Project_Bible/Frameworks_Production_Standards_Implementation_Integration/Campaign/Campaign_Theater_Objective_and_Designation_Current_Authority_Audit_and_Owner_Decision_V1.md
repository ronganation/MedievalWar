## 1. Purpose

This audit determines whether Medieval War already possesses a current shared owner for Campaign Theater objectives, Theater standings, non-sovereign designations, cross-Theater objective links, victory-condition interpretation, and their Campaign Experience presentation. It distinguishes existing Realm campaign-experience coverage from the missing shared integration layer and selects the smallest justified next authority.

The target experience is a Modern Medieval II campaign: the campaign map remains central, objectives remain concise and strategically meaningful, deeper causality is available when relevant, and success changes available choices and world response without turning the game into a court-management interface or one universal progress meter.

## 4. Current Owner Inventory

### 4.1 Project Bible

The Project Bible owns the campaign spine, selective-inheritance rules, player workload gate, definitions versus runtime separation, AI proposal-only doctrine, and the rule that Victory is not Government.

### 4.2 Campaign Theater

The Campaign Theater owner supplies Theater identity, scope, association, external interfaces, presentation context, bounded AI relevance, and cross-Realm pressure summaries. Theater association creates no allegiance, ownership, route rights, commitment, or government.

### 4.3 Political Constitution

## 24. Final Decision

Medieval War already possesses a strong Realm Campaign Experience precedent and strong constitutional rules for readable campaign play. What is missing is one shared integration authority that turns those parts into a consistent Modern Medieval II objective system across Realm, Theater, and cross-Theater play while preserving each Theater's political character.

The next file is therefore the shared Campaign Theater Objectives, Standing, Designation, and Cross-Theater Integration Framework V1. It will be a bounded system authority, not an implementation payload and not another Realm-content campaign.
