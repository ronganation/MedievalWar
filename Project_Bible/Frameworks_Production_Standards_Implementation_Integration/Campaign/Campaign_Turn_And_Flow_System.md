# Overview

The Campaign Turn & Flow System governs the passage of time and establishes the order in which all campaign systems are processed.

This system serves as the master simulation framework for:

- Economy
- Population
- Resources
- Trade
- Technology
- Characters
- Dynasties
- Governments
- Religion
- Culture
- Recruitment
- Diplomacy
- Supply
- Warfare
- Sieges
- Captives
- AI

Every campaign system ultimately resolves according to this structure.

---

# Core Philosophy

The world evolves continuously.

The player is not the only actor.

Characters age.

Children mature.

Dynasties rise and fall.

Governments strengthen or weaken.

Technology spreads.

Cultures evolve.

Religions expand or decline.

Rebellions emerge.

History is generated through interaction between systems.

The systems generate history while players become attached to the people shaping it.

# Why 7 Months?

The campaign must balance:

- Character attachment
- Dynasty progression
- Government evolution
- Cultural development
- Technological development
- Historical progression

Advantages:

✅ Faster historical progression

✅ Multi-generational gameplay

✅ Technology progression feels meaningful

✅ Governments evolve naturally

✅ Campaign remains manageable

✅ Seasonal variation occurs naturally

# Seasonal Rotation System

The campaign still uses all seasons.

Seasons rotate naturally due to the 7‑month turn cycle.

This prevents repetitive seasonal patterns.

Examples:

Spring
↓
Autumn
↓
Summer
↓
Winter
↓
Autumn
↓
Spring

No two campaigns experience seasons in exactly the same rhythm.
