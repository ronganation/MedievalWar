## 24. Static Acceptance Fixtures

### 24.1 Positive fixtures

- A valid offer expires because the candidate never receives it.
- A candidate accepts in principle, but appointment waits for lawful authority and resources.
- An appointment exists, but service activation is delayed because arrival or mandate is missing.
- A capable officeholder receives additional support rather than automatic promotion.
- A review separates poor performance from an impossible mandate and inadequate resources.
- Responsibility narrows without disgrace.
- A person resigns, but release and handback remain pending.
- Dismissal ends one service without creating exile or universal Reputation loss.
- Retirement reduces strategic function while preserving identity, history, relationships, and property.
- A former officeholder returns through a new opportunity without automatic restoration.
- No candidate accepts, and the owner delays, redistributes, or closes the opportunity.

### 24.2 Negative fixtures

- Turn recommendation directly into appointment.
- Treat silence as consent.
- Grant office, command, authority, treasury, staff, forces, equipment, or property from an offer.
- Treat appointment as competence or loyalty.
- Create Development evidence from an inactive honorary appointment.
- Expand responsibility without authority, readiness, capacity, or resources.
- Dismiss a person and automatically confiscate property, imprison, exile, or disgrace the person.
- Treat retirement as deletion or incapacity.
- Restore a former office automatically when the person returns.
- Replay a famous historical career over divergent campaign state.
