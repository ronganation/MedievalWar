## 1. Executive Verdict

Medieval War requires a clear integration layer explaining how people gain or lose access to patrons, sponsors, mentors, households, courts, institutions, protection, recommendations, and specialist support. The game already has constitutional owners for identity, relationships, development, underage roles, ancillary attachments, offices, institutions, households, resources, and current strategic function. The missing layer is coordination, not another framework.

```yaml
integration_verdict:
  duplicate_patronage_or_mentorship_framework_required: false
  CharacterNetwork_retains_directed_relationship_and_access_ownership: true
  CharacterDevelopment_retains_learning_readiness_and_formation_outcomes: true
  AncillaryAndRetainer_retains_provider_attachment_ownership: true
  UnderageCharacter_retains_child_safe_roles_protection_and_formation_access: true
  HouseholdInstitutionOfficeGovernmentAndResourceOwners_retain_substantive_state: true
  OpportunityEnvironment_retains_opportunity_and_receiving_side_architecture: true
  integration_gap: AccessSupportRecommendationProtectionFormationAndOwnerHandoff
  implementation_promotion_authorized: false
```

The authority answers one bounded question:

> How do patrons, sponsors, mentors, households, institutions, protectors, recommenders, tutors, retainers, and supporting providers open or constrain opportunities without automatically creating competence, office, loyalty, authority, resources, or success?

## 5. Patronage Constitution

Patronage is a directed relationship and support channel. A patron may introduce, recommend, protect, host, sponsor, fund, advocate, supervise, or request consideration. A client or beneficiary may accept, refuse, negotiate, redirect, leave, or fail.

```yaml
PatronageRelationshipInterface:
  PatronRef: required
  BeneficiaryRef: required
  Scope: required
  RelationshipObservationRefs: required
  ContactAudienceAndAccessRefs: required
  ProposedSupportClasses: []
  ExpectedObligationsAndLimits: []
  EffectiveWindowAndReviewRule: required
  PublicPrivateAndActorVisibleDisposition: required
  ConfidenceContradictionAndSourceRefs: required
  CreatesLoyaltyOfficeAuthorityCompetenceOrResources: false
```

### 5.1 Valid patronage contributions

- introduction;
- audience access;
- recommendation;
- advocacy;
- protection proposal;
- household or institutional placement proposal;
- bounded funding or support proposal;
- information transmission;
- formation opportunity;
- supervised responsibility opportunity;
- service offer or nomination proposal;
- mediation or reconciliation route.

### 5.2 Patronage limits

- A patron cannot guarantee acceptance by another owner.
- A patron cannot create competence.
- A patron cannot spend treasury, assign property, create staff, or release equipment without the relevant owner transaction.
- A patron cannot create loyalty, trust, gratitude, or factional alignment by declaration.
- A patron cannot bind an institution, household, office, commander, or Realm outside valid authority.
- A patron may be sincere, strategic, conflicted, opportunistic, constrained, unsuccessful, removed, killed, disgraced, inaccessible, or opposed.

```yaml
  AncillaryTutorOrSpecialistAttachmentRefs: []
  HouseholdInstitutionOfficeOrCampaignContextRefs: []
  DurationReviewSuspensionAndEndRules: required
  CreatesLearningReadinessLoyaltyOrSimilarityAutomatically: false
```

### 7.1 Mentorship rules

- A mentor relationship may exist without effective teaching.
- Effective teaching requires access, time, communication, relevant competence, suitable tasks, support, and evidence.
- A tutor attachment may support mentorship but does not automatically create a mentor relationship.
- A mentor may recommend a student without the student being ready.
- A successful student need not adopt the mentor's loyalties, enemies, personality, Reputation, or Archetype.
- A failed mentorship does not prove universal incapacity in either participant.
- Mentor capacity is finite.
- Mentorship may create dependency, obligation, rivalry, imitation, conflict, factional pressure, or disappointment only through owner-recorded evidence.

Household access is a practical and political route into residence, protection, service, audience, formation, information, or placement. It is not household ownership or permanent membership.

```yaml
  EffectiveWindowReviewAndExitRules: required
  HouseholdOwnerDecisionRequired: true
  CreatesHouseholdMembershipPlacementCapacityOrLoyalty: false
```

### 8.1 Household-access forms

- temporary audience;
- guest reception;
- protected residence;
- wardship or foster placement proposal;
- household service consideration;
- court participation;
- formation or observation placement;
- temporary specialist support;
- messenger or envoy reception;
- claimant, exile, hostage, or protected-person reception;
- return, release, transfer, or removal.

### 8.2 Household-access limits

- Access may be partial, supervised, temporary, contested, conditional, revoked, or unknown.
- Admission does not create trust.
- Residence does not create membership.
- Membership does not create independent household capacity.
- Household protection does not create immunity from law, war, capture, finance, logistics, or political consequence.
- A household need cannot generate a person.

Institutional access routes a person toward formation, procedure, archives, service, membership, audience, teaching, legal or religious process, and bounded opportunity. An institution remains independent of the person providing an introduction.

```yaml
  EffectiveWindowExpiryAndExitRules: required
  InstitutionOwnerDecisionRequired: true
  CreatesMembershipOfficeAuthorityLearningOrResource: false
```

### 9.1 Institutional-access forms

- audience or consultation;
- study or observation;
- apprenticeship or supervised service;
- archive, text, record, or procedural access;
- religious, legal, scholarly, administrative, diplomatic, or craft formation;
- temporary specialist attachment;
- membership consideration;
- office or function consideration;
- institutional protection or hosting;
- interinstitutional or cross-Realm introduction.

### 9.2 Institutional-access limits

- Access is scoped and procedure-bound.
- Institutional membership is not office.
- Office is not personal ownership of the institution.
- Access to records does not create universal knowledge.
- Formation access does not prove learning.
- Recommendation by one member does not bind the institution.
- Institutional prestige does not transfer automatically to the person.

## 30. Final Verdict

Medieval War now has a clear cross-framework bridge for patronage, sponsorship, mentorship, household access, institutional access, protection, recommendation, and bounded provider support. The authority preserves the difference between a relationship and an attachment, between access and admission, between formation and learning, between sponsorship and resources, and between recommendation and appointment.

Households and institutions can admit, host, form, protect, or reject through their own capacity and procedure. Underage people retain child-safe access, protection, and supervision. Every consequential transaction remains with its substantive owner.
