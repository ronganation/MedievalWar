## 8. Recommendation Constitution

A recommendation is a directed, source-attributed communication proposed for a defined audience and purpose.

```yaml
  TransmissionReceiptAndVisibilityRefs: required
  EffectiveWindowStalenessWithdrawalAndReviewRule: required
  CreatesAudienceAccessSelectionAppointmentTrustReputationOrCompetence: false
```

### 8.1 Recommendation rules

- A recommender can speak only from available knowledge and valid authority.
- Recommendation quality depends on evidence, relevance, reliability, conflict, timing, audience, and purpose.
- A positive recommendation may be ignored, rejected, contradicted, or accepted only for further review.
- A negative recommendation does not prove incompetence or disloyalty.
- Repeated recommendations do not become truth through repetition alone.
- Famous identity does not increase recommendation reliability automatically.
- A recommendation may itself become evidence about the recommender's judgment or interests only through the appropriate owner.

## 9. Living Reputation Constitution

Living Reputation is an audience-specific, evidence-backed assessment owned by the current Reputation authority family. It is not a universal score and cannot substitute for the underlying evidence.

```yaml

  ProposedConsequenceOwnerRefs: []
  TraitReputationOwnerDecisionRequired: true
  CreatesCompetenceAuthorityRelationshipOfficeTitleClaimOrResources: false
```

### 9.1 Reputation rules

- Reputation must remain audience-specific unless evidence supports broader transmission and reception.
- Reputation may be positive, negative, mixed, contested, local, stale, recovering, or unknown.
- Reputation may influence access, support, caution, monitoring, pricing, negotiation, selection review, or opposition only through owner-approved consequences.
- Reputation does not change private intent or factual competence.
- Current conduct may reinforce, contradict, weaken, rehabilitate, or redirect Reputation.
- Registry definitions do not create Reputation instances.

## 22. Static Acceptance Fixtures

### 22.1 Positive fixtures

- A direct witness knows a military action, while a distant court remains unaware.
- A recommendation reaches an institution but leads only to evidence review.
- An official record confirms service without creating universal Reputation.
- Two audiences hold different confidence about the same person.
- A local positive Reputation remains unknown cross-Realm because no valid transmission exists.
- Contradictory reports preserve contestation until reviewed.
- A negative Reputation weakens after evidence-backed rehabilitation.
- Historical Memory preserves a dead figure's contested interpretation without creating living agency.
- Legend grows later but cannot backfill the opening campaign.
- No Reputation consequence is created because evidence or audience receipt is insufficient.

### 22.2 Negative fixtures

- Give every Realm knowledge of a famous person.
- Convert a witnessed action directly into universal Reputation.
- Convert recommendation into selection or appointment.
- Convert institutional recognition into competence or authority.
- Convert Reputation into Trait, loyalty, relationship, office, title, or resources.
- Let rumor overwrite verified contradictory evidence automatically.
- Let repetition create truth.
- Let Historical Memory act as current living knowledge.
- Let later Legend alter opening Reputation or access.
- Delete contested or stale state during migration.

```yaml
negative_fixture_result: RejectFatalWithoutMutation
```

## 28. Final Verdict

Medieval War now has a bounded bridge from evidence and communication to audience-specific knowledge, recognition, recommendation, living Reputation, Historical Memory, and Legend. The authority prevents omniscient fame, universal Reputation, recommendation shortcuts, institutional overreach, memory leakage, and later Legend from rewriting the opening campaign.

Different audiences may know, doubt, recognize, recommend, remember, or mythologize the same person differently. Every claim retains sources, transmission, confidence, contradictions, visibility, and effective windows. Character Network, Trait and Reputation, Historical Memory, Legend, institutions, communication, and opportunity owners retain their own state.
