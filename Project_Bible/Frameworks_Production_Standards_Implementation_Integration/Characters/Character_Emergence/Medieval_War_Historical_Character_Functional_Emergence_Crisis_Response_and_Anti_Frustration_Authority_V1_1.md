## 1. Corrected Executive Verdict

Medieval War needs a specialist companion beneath the existing Historical Emergence and Character Emergence authorities. This companion governs how owner-backed pressure becomes a functional need, how a movement or receiving environment develops, how valid people compete to answer that need, and how major crisis emergence remains legible and fair without becoming an arbitrary reinforcement system.

It does not replace the existing Historical Emergence framework, reopen the completed six-file career package, or create a superior general character framework. It supplies a distinct crisis, movement, occupation, resistance, restoration, parity, and anti-frustration constitution.

## 3. Hard Design Question

> When campaign conditions evoke the historical function associated with a significant person or movement, what need actually exists, which people may answer it, what movement and capacity support the response, and how can the result challenge either player or AI without forcing history or manufacturing advantages?

This companion defines functional-need recognition, sequence shapes, candidate sourcing, movement formation, capability thresholds, mobilization, player-visible telegraphing, counterplay, escalation, de-escalation, fragmentation, exhaustion, and no-candidate outcomes.

## 28. Strengthened Static Acceptance Fixtures

### Positive fixtures

- Pressure exists but neither movement nor candidate forms because networks, knowledge, or support are insufficient.
- A movement forms without a dominant leader and remains distributed or locally fragmented.
- An existing ordinary person becomes recognized without changing candidate band automatically.
- An independent earlier person fills a neutral resistance-leadership function without borrowing a later famous identity.
- A campaign-grown captain becomes visible through real defense, survivor organization, recognition, and patron support.
- A later named historical person competes only during a valid chronology and is not guaranteed selection.
- Passive support develops without becoming armed cells or a field force.
- Armed cells cannot become a field force without concentration, command, equipment, supply, assembly, and mobilization.
- Accommodation reduces pressure without deleting memory or forcing universal loyalty.
- Arrest can suppress, fragment, or increase martyrdom risk only after audience and Historical Memory review.
- A movement persists after leader loss only where support, organization, and capacity remain.
- A governor, scholar, reformer, merchant, chronicler, or diplomat can emerge without becoming a general.
- AI and human factions use the same emergence causality, capacity, information, and recovery requirements.
- Late-stage failure may leave no effective response when earlier mitigation windows were ignored or lost.

### Negative fixtures

- Spawn a famous person because the historical year was reached.
- Pull a future identity backward because a similar function became necessary.
- Spawn a renamed replacement celebrity without an independent identity.
- Give a losing faction a free army, population, equipment, subsidy, claim, or support.
- Create resistance or restoration from final-settlement capture alone.
- Treat grievance, sympathy, passive support, or an active network as a field force.
- Promote every movement through one mandatory linear ladder.
- Make arrest, execution, exile, or death automatically produce martyrdom.
- Grant the AI hidden candidate knowledge or exemption from capacity and recovery.
- Guarantee that every actor-visible late-stage crisis still has an effective solution.
- Rebuild a depleted movement after cooldown without new or recovered sources.
- Treat no candidate, collective leadership, dormancy, fragmentation, or failure as missing content.
