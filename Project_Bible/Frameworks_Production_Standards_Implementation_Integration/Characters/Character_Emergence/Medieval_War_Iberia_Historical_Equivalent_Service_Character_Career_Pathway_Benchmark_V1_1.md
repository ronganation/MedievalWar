## 1. Executive Verdict

This benchmark supplies the missing Band 2 proof case for Iberia. It defines how an original, independently sourced person may be considered for a historically plausible service career when a current role need persists but a named historical route is blocked, invalid, unavailable, incompatible with campaign history, or simply less suitable under present conditions.

The benchmark does not create a substitute celebrity. It does not rename a generated character after <Person>Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar</Person>, <Person>Yusuf al-Mutaman</Person>, or another historical figure. It does not copy a famous biography and remove the name. It requires an identity source that exists independently of the blocked named route, then subjects that person to ordinary lifecycle, evidence, access, readiness, authority, resource, comparison, failure, and nonoccurrence rules.

## 2. Benchmark Question and Player Promise

The benchmark asks:

Can Iberia produce a credible service character from an independently existing social, household, institutional, community, regional, or prior-service source when a comparable strategic need survives the failure of a famous route, without cloning the famous person, manufacturing competence, reserving the career, or guaranteeing the historical outcome?

The player-facing promise is not that history will supply a replacement <Person>Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar</Person> or replacement <Person>Yusuf al-Mutaman</Person>. The promise is that the campaign world may already contain another person whose own formation, access, obligations, limitations, and current opportunity make a meaningful career possible.

## 3. Band 2 Constitutional Boundary

```text
Comparable role need != replacement person
Blocked named route != permission to clone identity
Historical plausibility != fabricated biography
Equivalent function != equivalent life
Independent identity != generated from vacancy
Opportunity context != inherited relationships
Service similarity != inherited reputation
Possible career != reserved outcome
```

A Band 2 historical equivalent is an independently sourced person evaluated against a current need. The person is neither a disguised Band 1 figure nor a vacancy-generated talent. A generated person may enter Band 2 only when the generation route supplies an independently valid identity source and the no-leakage review passes; otherwise a campaign-grown person remains Band 3.

## 8. Career Route Modules

### 8.1 Household or court service route

An independently sourced person may become visible through an existing household or court context. The route requires known identity, contact, receiving capacity, access, current availability, and a bounded service need. Residence does not create membership; access does not create office; patronage does not create loyalty.

Valid outcomes include supervised service, temporary placement, another candidate, rejection, delayed access, return to prior context, or no route.

### 8.2 Regional and frontier intermediary route

A regional person may carry local familiarity, communication channels, prior service, or community recognition. These inputs remain evidence-bound. Regional origin does not create authority over the region, universal local acceptance, property, military following, language mastery, or safe passage.

The route may support reporting, mediation, logistics, settlement support, inspection, communication, or recovery. Governorship and command require separate transactions.

### 8.3 Administrative and records route

Prior exposure to records, petitions, accounts, correspondence, provisioning, or office procedure may justify review for bounded administrative work. Every assignment requires mandate, information access, supervision, support, review, and handback.

Competence does not create office. A successful trial does not guarantee promotion. Records access does not create universal knowledge.

### 8.4 Diplomatic communication route

A candidate may support contact, message delivery, audience preparation, interpretation, translation, safe-conduct coordination, or supervised negotiation only where actual communication capacity, route access, mandate, and receiving consent exist.

Language, travel, culture, or religion alone do not create trust, diplomatic authority, treaty power, or foreign relationships.

### 8.5 Garrison, logistics, and recovery-support route

A candidate with relevant existing experience may assist supply coordination, reporting, route preparation, quartering, repair, settlement, prisoner or message handling, or garrison administration. This route does not create a force, command, stockpile, transport capacity, property right, or territorial authority.

### 8.6 Institutional mediation route

An independently sourced institution-attached or legally, religiously, or procedurally experienced person may assist consultation, records, correspondence, mediation, teaching support, custodial work, or bounded representation.

Institutional attachment does not create office, doctrine, authorship, universal knowledge, court access, or political authority.

### 8.7 Cross-Realm service route

A valid candidate may serve outside the source context only after current obligations, release, consent, route, safe conduct, transport, arrival, reception, payment, support, reporting, review, and exit are resolved by their owners.

Cross-Realm service does not transfer identity ownership and does not erase prior obligations, household ties, reputation differences, or local knowledge limits.

### 8.8 Trial service and proof route

Where readiness is plausible but insufficiently established, the receiving owner may propose a bounded trial assignment. The trial must have a real task, scope, supervisor, resources, duration, reporting, failure treatment, and exit.

A trial does not suspend ordinary risk. It may confirm readiness, expose limitations, produce mixed evidence, redirect the candidate, or end without strategic activation.

### 9.1 Named candidate remains valid and best supported

The named person may receive the proposal. The equivalent remains a separate person and may be considered elsewhere. Band 2 existence does not displace Band 1 automatically.

### 9.2 Named candidate exists but equivalent is better positioned

The independently sourced equivalent may receive the proposal when current readiness, access, availability, acceptance, obligations, or support are stronger. The named person remains in the campaign and may pursue another route.

### 9.3 Named route is invalid

The equivalent may be reviewed only if the role need remains current and the independent identity source passes validation. Named-route invalidity creates no replacement person by itself.

### 9.4 Dynamic candidate is better supported

A campaign-grown Band 3 person may receive the function when lived evidence and current fit are stronger. Historical-equivalent status grants no middle-tier priority between famous and dynamic candidates.

### 9.5 No candidate qualifies

The owner delays, narrows, distributes, resolves institutionally, selects another solution, or accepts nonoccurrence. The campaign does not generate a free equivalent to complete the pattern.

## 11. Development, Setback, and Recovery

Equivalent candidates do not receive ready-made competence. Their readiness must arise from their own source and evidence.

```yaml
development_rules:
  - ExperienceIsDomainAndContextSpecific
  - PriorServiceEvidenceMustIdentifyScopeAndSupport
  - ExposureAloneCreatesNoCompetence
  - OneSuccessCreatesNoMastery
  - NamedRouteFailureCreatesNoDevelopment
  - EquivalentClassificationCreatesNoReadiness
  - SetbackIsSpecificNotUniversal
  - RecoveryRequiresCurrentEvidenceAndOwnerState
```

Valid setbacks include procedural error, poor judgment, route failure, communication failure, access loss, conflicting obligation, patron removal, resource failure, local resistance, mandate overreach, capture, injury, political exclusion, or function mismatch.

Valid recovery includes narrower responsibility, changed supervision, retraining, restitution, restored access, different employer, domain redirection, recovery time, return to prior context, retirement, or lightweight simulation.

## 14. Player Experience

The player should understand:

- why the role need still exists;
- why the named route is blocked, less suitable, or irrelevant to the current comparison;
- where the equivalent candidate independently came from;
- what is verified, uncertain, disputed, or hidden;
- how the candidate became known to the receiving actor;
- what the candidate has actually done and is ready to do;
- what access, mandate, resources, support, and obligations remain unresolved;
- why another named, equivalent, or dynamic candidate may be preferable;
- what can fail, redirect, narrow, end, or never occur;
- which owner controls the next decision.

The UI must not present the candidate as “the new” famous person, display copied biographical milestones, reveal private intentions, or promise the historical outcome.

### 18.1 Positive fixtures

- A role comparable to a named historical route persists after that named route becomes incompatible with campaign history.
- An existing lightweight person with an independent regional service background is reviewed as a Band 2 candidate.
- The candidate’s own service evidence supports a bounded trial assignment.
- The candidate becomes strategically useful without receiving the named figure’s identity, household, relationships, reputation, or destiny.
- A named candidate remains in the campaign while the equivalent receives a different current function.
- A dynamic Band 3 candidate surpasses the equivalent for one role because current evidence is stronger.
- A court rejects the equivalent because access or capacity is absent.
- A cross-Realm offer fails because release, safe conduct, transport, payment, or reception cannot be resolved.
- Trial service reveals limitations and redirects the candidate into a narrower function.
- The candidate returns to a prior household, institution, community, or lightweight context without deletion.
- No independent identity source exists, so no equivalent is created.
- No candidate qualifies, and the campaign resolves the need institutionally or accepts nonoccurrence.
