# 1. Concise Verdict

Character Network V1 has a valid and largely clear constitutional purpose: it owns **directed relationship state, relationship dimensions, relationship summaries, information-access relationships, and network-derived strategic interpretation**. It does not own the people, offices, titles, claims, dynasties, institutions, facts, memories, reputation, traits, archetypes, formal authority, or substantive typed arrangements that relationships connect.

The framework is constitutionally sound but requires bounded reconciliation in six areas before implementation ownership is fully clear:

1. **Relationship edge versus typed arrangement:** guardianship, wardship, fosterage, mentorship, patronage, fealty, delegation, marriage, office holding, and coalition contracts are not generic relationships. Their substantive records remain with their established owners; Character Network owns the directed relational consequences and indexed links.
2. **Information access versus historical knowledge:** Network owns current directed access and transmission pathways. Historical Memory owns evidence, testimony, narrative, memory, and preserved historical knowledge objects.
3. **Loyalty versus alignment and obligation:** loyalty cannot be a universal trait or one mutual score. Network may own directed loyalty-related state only when target, context, source, visibility, and strategic output are explicit.
4. **Dynastic and succession alignment versus dynasty or succession truth:** Network may own directed support and alignment state, while Dynasty and Succession owners retain membership, branch, claimant, candidacy, stage, and transfer truth.
5. **Network-node activation versus Character Foundation simulation tier:** Network may maintain network participation/index state, but Character Foundation owns character activation and simulation tier. Network node tier cannot become a second character lifecycle authority.
6. **Responsibility, influence, and delegated-authority records:** Network may expose relationship consequences and indexed links, but must not duplicate Development capacity, Foundation workload interfaces, office/institution state, or Delegated Authority records.

No new framework is required. The correct action is **Refactor boundary wording, Relocate duplicated runtime truth to existing owners, Validate save/migration behavior, and retain Character Network as the sole owner of directed relationship state.**

# 2. Constitutional Ownership Review

## Relationships

### Character Network owns

- reusable relationship-type definitions;
- directed relationship edges;
- source and target references;
- relationship dimensions relevant to strategic interaction;
- current relationship state summaries derived from underlying dimensions;
- relationship provenance and major-change references;
- relationship visibility and information-access boundaries where those are Network concerns;
- event-driven relationship reevaluation;
- bounded strategic outputs derived from relationship state;
- Network-side indexes to typed arrangements owned elsewhere.

### Character Network does not own

- person identity;
- kinship, marriage, guardianship, office, title, claim, delegation, coalition, service, promise, or oath truth when a typed owner exists;
- current Trait, Reputation, Archetype, Development, or Historical Memory state;
- facts or participation;
- institutional, legal, territorial, military, or economic outcomes;
- formal authority.

### Boundary

A relationship edge describes how one node relates to another in a bounded strategic context. It does not replace substantive records that create legal status, authority, custody, service, or institutional position.

## Directed Relationships

### Character Network owns

- source-to-target orientation;
- direction-specific dimensions;
- direction-specific information and visibility;
- direction-specific recent benefit, harm, obligation, grievance, dependency, and cooperation;
- asymmetrical derived summaries and outputs.

### Rule

Every strategically active relationship must identify a source and a target. If both directions matter, each direction must be represented or derivable only through an explicit, evidence-supported rule. One record must not be silently mirrored.

## Asymmetric Relationships

### Character Network owns

- different values and states for A toward B and B toward A;
- different access, trust, fear, obligation, grievance, and alignment in each direction;
- different visibility and information certainty for each observer.

### Prohibition

A flat mutual Friend, Rival, Enemy, Ally, Mentor, Student, Loyalist, or opinion score cannot remain authoritative when direction matters.

## Loyalty

### Character Network may own

- directed loyalty or commitment toward a defined person, office, dynasty, institution, realm, claimant, cause, or agreement when the target and context are explicit;
- loyalty-related confidence, willingness, service reliability, or alignment as Network dimensions;
- provenance for the current directed state;
- bounded strategic outputs such as voluntary service, muster reliability, faction support, or refusal risk.

### Character Network does not own

- a universal personal Loyalty trait;
- legal fealty or oath validity;
- office duty;
- dynasty membership;
- automatic loyalty from kinship, alliance, title, culture, religion, or proximity;
- authority to compel performance.

### Boundary

Loyalty is directed, contextual, mutable, and source-dependent. Formal obligations may influence loyalty but do not determine it.

## Trust

### Character Network owns

- directed trust between nodes;
- trust dimensions relevant to political, military, institutional, personal, or information contexts;
- changes caused by known conduct, fulfilled promises, betrayal, service, information, and relationship events;
- observer-bounded trust summaries.

### Character Network does not own

- fact truth;
- evidence authenticity;
- a universal Trust trait;
- institutional acceptance merely because one person trusts another.

### Boundary

Trust modifies decisions and cooperation. Trust cannot replace institutional procedure, evidence, law, or authority.

## Familiarity

### Character Network owns

- directed interpersonal familiarity and current contact familiarity;
- familiarity arising through repeated contact, shared service, court access, correspondence, mentorship, or other validated links;
- bounded effects on communication, uncertainty, and relationship evaluation.

### Character Network does not own

- regional, cultural, religious, institutional, maritime, or domain familiarity tracked by Character Development;
- knowledge of facts merely because two characters are familiar;
- universal social knowledge.

### Boundary

Interpersonal familiarity belongs to Network. Functional or regional familiarity belongs to Development. Familiarity does not automatically create trust, loyalty, or information.

## Friendship

### Character Network owns

- directed friendship-related trust, affection where modeled in general-audience-safe strategic terms, willingness, protection, access, and support;
- the summary label only as a derived relationship state.

### Character Network does not own

- a permanent mutual friendship fact without directed evidence;
- automatic political alliance, loyalty, authority, knowledge, or service;
- a Friendship trait.

### Boundary

“Friend” may remain a player-facing summary of directed relationship state. It must not replace underlying dimensions or be mirrored automatically.

## Rivalry

### Character Network owns

- directed rivalry, competition, grievance, claim conflict, reward dispute, succession conflict, jurisdiction conflict, or strategic opposition;
- asymmetrical intensity and visibility;
- bounded risk outputs.

### Character Network does not own

- the underlying claim, title, office, law, fact, or historical grievance record;
- a Rival trait;
- automatic hostility or rebellion.

### Boundary

Rivalry is a directed relational state built from valid sources. The source conflict remains with its owner.

## Patronage

### Character Network owns

- directed patron-client relationship state;
- access, sponsorship expectation, dependence, obligation, reward satisfaction, gratitude, grievance, and political alignment;
- patronage-network visibility and contact routes.

### Other owners retain

- office, money, land, title, privilege, command, marriage arrangement, institutional appointment, education assignment, and resource transfer;
- Development opportunity and growth;
- Historical Memory patronage of chronicles or commemoration;
- legal or institutional authority.

### Boundary

Patronage in Network records the relationship created by grants, access, service, and expectations. It does not execute the grant or own the resource.

## Fealty

### Character Network owns

- directed acceptance, confidence, willingness, grievance, and service relationship surrounding fealty;
- observer-specific knowledge of fealty behavior;
- Network consequences of fulfilled or broken obligations.

### Other owners retain

- formal oath or fealty record;
- vassalage status;
- title and jurisdiction;
- legal obligation;
- sanctions and authority.

### Boundary

Formal fealty is a typed legal/governance relationship. Network owns relational consequences, not legal validity.

## Obligations

### Character Network owns

- directed perceived or socially/politically recognized obligation;
- obligation satisfaction, dispute, expectation, and relational consequences;
- Network-side references to typed promises, service, grants, oaths, rewards, and contracts.

### Character Network does not own

- the underlying contract, law, office duty, promise record, service record, debt, title obligation, or delegated mandate where an owner exists;
- enforcement or legal outcome.

### Boundary

Network may summarize relational obligation. Substantive obligation records remain typed and owner-specific.

## Guardianship

### Character Network owns

- Guardian-to-Ward and Ward-to-Guardian directed relationship edges;
- trust, dependency, information access, cultural/religious influence references, grievance, protection confidence, and relational consequences;
- Network indexing of the arrangement.

### Underage Character owns

- guardianship requirements;
- guardian arrangement state;
- ward identity and age-specific restrictions;
- protection, residence, education authority limits, duration, review, and removal requirements;
- role separation from regent, custodian, protector, tutor, and household head.

### Boundary

The Underage arrangement creates and validates guardianship. Character Network represents the resulting directed relationship state. Network cannot appoint a guardian or merge guardian with regent, protector, tutor, or owner.

## Wardship

### Character Network owns

- directed relational effects of wardship;
- trust, dependency, obligation, resentment, access, and influence between ward, guardian, household, and relevant actors.

### Underage Character owns

- formal wardship arrangement;
- authority basis, residence, duration, education responsibility, visitation, removal, marriage-authority limits, and political guarantees.

### Boundary

Wardship is a typed Underage arrangement, not a generic relation label.

## Fosterage

### Character Network owns

- directed effects such as familiarity, obligation, dependency, resentment, contact, cultural influence references, and access;
- Network edges among child, foster household, birth household, guardians, and political actors.

### Underage Character or applicable Fosterage owner retains

- fosterage status, placement, duration, return conditions, household, safety, education, and legal/customary basis.

### Boundary

Fosterage does not transfer identity, claim, ownership, dynasty, loyalty, or authority. Network records consequences only.

## Mentorship

### Character Network owns

- Mentor-to-Student and Student-to-Mentor directed relationship edges;

### Character Development owns

- progress, experience, milestones, readiness gains, setbacks, and outcomes.

### Boundary

Network owns the directed relationship. Development owns the developmental effect. The existence of a mentor edge does not create readiness.

## Dynastic Alignment

## Information Access

### Character Network owns

- the truth or substance of historical evidence, testimony, narrative, memory, or fact;
- all information systems or intelligence operations;
- automatic knowledge from kinship, alliance, friendship, co-location, or membership.

### Boundary

Information access is a pathway, not knowledge itself. Historical Memory owns historical-information objects and observer-specific known references. Access may permit transmission, but transmission and successful knowledge change require validated events and owner processing.

### Source-information owner retains

- evidence, narrative, memory, fact, report, or intelligence content;
- whether the source is authentic or accurate.

### Boundary

Network may enable transfer. Network cannot create information or convert access into knowledge automatically.

# 3. Boundary Review

## Interaction with Character Foundation

Character Foundation owns canonical person identity, lifecycle, activation, simulation tier, location, core availability, and specialist references. Character Network references the person and maintains Network participation only.

### Required reconciliation

- Network node tier must not become a second Character Foundation simulation tier.
- Network `bActive` or equivalent node participation state must not mean living, character-active, or constitutionally agentic.
- Network may request Foundation activation review when relationship relevance increases.
- Deactivating a person from full Foundation simulation cannot erase required Network edges, obligations, claims, custody, evidence access, or relationships needed by active actors.
- Legacy/dead nodes cannot create new living relationships or perform new actions.

## Interaction with Historical Memory

Historical Memory owns evidence, testimony, narrative, reception, memory, historical records, preservation, and legacy. Network owns current directed relationship and information-access pathways.

### Required reconciliation

- Witness-to-event, audience-to-narrative, patron-to-chronicler, and similar Network edges index or contextualize Historical Memory objects; they do not replace them.
- Network information access may permit transmission. Historical Memory validates evidence/narrative availability and known references.
- Historical grievances and memories may affect Network grievance/alignment through bounded source references. Network does not copy memory state.
- A dead witness's prior testimony may persist in Historical Memory, but the Network node cannot generate new contact or testimony.

## Interaction with Reputation

Reputation owns current character-audience perception. Network owns directed person/node relationships and contact pathways.

### Required reconciliation

- Reputation may influence how one node responds to another, but Network must retain its own directed relationship state and provenance.
- Network trust or alignment must not be automatically converted into public Reputation.
- Reputation visibility and audience acceptance remain Reputation-owned.
- Network may provide audience/contact/information pathways without owning the Reputation state.
- Public labels such as Loyalist, Friend, Rival, or Enemy must not be treated as universal Reputation unless separately validated by Reputation Authority.

## Interaction with Character Development

Development owns experience, aptitude, capacity, readiness, mentorship progress, milestones, opportunity, setbacks, recovery, and domain familiarity. Network owns directed relationship conditions enabling or constraining Development.

### Required reconciliation

- MentorId/StudentId and contact edges may be indexed by Network, while Development owns the mentorship process and outcome.
- Trust, access, compatibility, dependency, and patronage expectation may be Network inputs.
- Teaching quality, progress, experience, readiness, and milestones remain Development-owned.
- Interpersonal familiarity remains Network-owned; regional/cultural/institutional familiarity remains Development-owned.
- Relationship existence does not create readiness.

## Interaction with Underage Character

Underage owns guardianship, wardship, fosterage, underage arrangements, age-specific restrictions, education direction, minor eligibility, protection requirements, and transition to independent agency.

### Required reconciliation

- Network owns directed relationship effects and contact/information links.
- Underage owns arrangement validity, role separation, duration, residence, authority basis, protection, education authority, review, and removal.
- One legacy `GuardianId` cannot populate guardian, protector, tutor, custodian, regent, military executor, and household head.
- Network edges do not transfer ownership of the child, claim, household, or authority.
- Fosterage and wardship do not create loyalty automatically.

## Interaction with Character Archetype

Archetype owns current strategic function. Network owns relationships that may support, constrain, expose, or respond to that function.

### Required reconciliation

- Archetype may influence expected interaction patterns or role fit, but cannot directly write Network relationships.
- Network support may contribute to Archetype evaluation, but cannot activate an Archetype.
- Relationship labels cannot substitute for current strategic function.
- Archetype does not create office, authority, knowledge, loyalty, or relationship state.

# 4. Migration Review

## Migration ownership

Migration Registries own:

- canonical node and person ID mapping;
- deprecated identifiers;
- direct aliases or redirects when authorized;
- migration versions;
- ambiguity handling;
- quarantine;
- source-to-destination mapping provenance.

Character Network validates accepted Network records and relationship state after mappings resolve. It does not invent mappings or bypass quarantine.

## Legacy migration sources

Potential legacy sources include:

- flat Friend, Rival, Enemy, Ally, Mentor, Student, Spouse, Sibling, and opinion fields;
- universal Loyalty, Trust, Familiarity, Influence, or Relationship values;
- mutual relationship labels;
- Character System relationship arrays;
- Character Trait Library entries such as Loyalist or Conspirator;
- one `GuardianId` and inverse ward lists;
- embedded office/title/claim/dynasty/household/institution references;
- office or title traits;
- memory and Reputation traits;
- saves, Data Tables, import schemas, Blueprints, runtime structures, and serializers;
- current source records referenced by Character System migration documents.

## Required migration dispositions

| Legacy source meaning | Destination handling |
|---|---|
| Flat mutual Friend | Directed Network state only where each direction is supported; otherwise quarantine or bounded unresolved provenance |
| Rival/Enemy | Directed grievance/opposition state with fact/source provenance; do not mirror automatically |
| Ally | Distinguish formal alliance contract from directed cooperation/confidence; alliance owner retains contract |
| Mentor/Student | Development mentorship record plus directed Network edges; do not create readiness |
| Spouse/Sibling | Typed marriage/kinship record plus optional directed Network state; do not infer loyalty/knowledge |
| Loyalist trait | Directed target/context-specific Network alignment; ambiguous target quarantines |
| Conspirator trait | Typed conspiracy/plot participation where available plus directed Network state; no permanent intrinsic label |
| GuardianId | Underage arrangement review plus directed edges; never populate all roles |
| Office/title trait | Relocate substantive office/title truth; Network may create holder/institution relationship effects after validation |
| Memory trait | Historical Memory; Network consumes bounded grievance/alignment source reference only |
| Reputation title | Reputation Authority; Network may consume audience-specific perception as input |
| Universal opinion score | Decompose into supported directed dimensions or quarantine; do not preserve as universal score |
| Global Influence score | Decompose to owner-specific influence or archive; no universal Network influence authority |
| IsHeir/IsRuler/IsGovernor | Succession/title/office owner; Network may respond to authoritative status |

## Migration rules

1. Preserve exact source provenance.
2. Resolve source and target through canonical mappings.
3. Require explicit direction.
4. Do not mirror an edge without source evidence.
5. Do not infer loyalty or knowledge from kinship, alliance, marriage, office, title, culture, religion, or location.
6. Separate typed arrangements from relational consequences.
7. Preserve asymmetry.
8. Preserve observer-specific visibility and information uncertainty.
9. Separate current relationship state from Historical Memory and Reputation.
10. Preserve chronology and last-major-change source where available.
11. Quarantine unresolved target, direction, semantics, source, audience, or arrangement role.
12. Use direct mappings; alias chains are prohibited.
13. Write only canonical destination references.
14. Make migration versioned, deterministic, dependency-aware, and idempotent.
15. Never overwrite advanced saves with Opening State defaults.
16. Do not invent IDs, nodes, relationships, evidence, audiences, or content.

## Migration blockers

- Exact Character System source schemas and serializers remain unavailable.
- Dedicated exact relationship/node migration mappings do not currently exist.
- Some legacy labels lack target, direction, context, or source provenance.
- One legacy value may conflate relationship, legal arrangement, office, memory, Reputation, and trait semantics.
- Save conversion is not implemented or runtime-validated.

These are implementation blockers, not constitutional ownership blockers.

# 5. Save Ownership Review

## Character Network save ownership

Character Network saves only its authoritative mutable state and required references:

- directed edge source and target;
- relationship type or context;
- active directed dimensions;
- derived relationship state only if constitutionally authoritative rather than a regenerable presentation summary;
- Network-side information-access and visibility state;
- provenance and last-major-change references;
- Network participation/index state distinct from Foundation activation;
- bounded strategic resource or stress state when Network-owned;
- references to typed arrangements and external records;
- deterministic continuation and reason data required by Network.

## Saved by other owners

- identity, lifecycle, activation, simulation tier, location: Character Foundation;
- Development, mentorship progress, readiness, milestones: Character Development;
- underage arrangements: Underage Character;
- archetypes: Character Archetype;
- evidence, narrative, memory, legacy: Historical Memory;
- current character-audience Reputation: Reputation Authority;
- traits: Trait Authority;
- office, title, claim, law, delegation, regency, dynasty, institution, coalition, and formal contract state: respective owners;
- migration mappings and quarantine: Migration Registries/migration runtime.

## Save invariants

- Direction and asymmetry survive save-load-save.
- Typed arrangement references remain external and valid.
- Network does not serialize a duplicate monolithic character object.
- Foundation activation and Network participation remain distinct.
- Dead/legacy nodes cannot resume living interactions after load.
- Unknown references fail safely or quarantine.
- Opening State relationship defaults initialize new campaigns only.
- Advanced save state outranks defaults.
- Derived summaries regenerate unless an approved deterministic cache exists.
- Deprecated IDs and flat mutual legacy fields are not written after conversion.
- Source and content versions are preserved.

# 6. Validation Review

## Fatal

- A strategically relevant relationship requiring direction is stored only as one mutual score.
- A directed edge lacks source or target.
- A source or target resolves to an invalid or ambiguous canonical node.
- A relationship is mirrored automatically without evidence.
- Kinship, marriage, alliance, office, title, culture, religion, proximity, or information access automatically creates loyalty.
- Information access automatically creates knowledge or relationship alignment.
- Network creates or owns person identity, lifecycle, activation, simulation tier, or living agency.
- Network node tier overrides Character Foundation simulation tier.
- Network owns or duplicates Trait, Reputation, Archetype, Development, Historical Memory, office, title, claim, dynasty, law, authority, institution, or fact truth.
- Network appoints a guardian or treats Guardian as owner, regent, protector, tutor, custodian, or military executor without separate valid records.
- Wardship or fosterage transfers identity, claim, dynasty, ownership, loyalty, or authority automatically.
- Mentorship relationship directly creates Development readiness or milestones.
- Succession alignment creates a claim, candidacy, heir status, recognition, installation, or transfer.
- Historical Memory evidence/narrative/memory is copied as Network truth.
- Reputation is stored as universal Network relationship truth.
- Static definitions contain mutable relationship values, current offices, titles, claims, guardians, or information state.
- Opening State contains later relationships, betrayal, loyalty, office, marriage, guardianship, or Reputation outcomes.
- Migration guesses target, direction, context, arrangement role, identity, source, or destination.
- Deprecated IDs enter modern content or saves.
- Alias chains are accepted.
- Equal state, inputs, versions, and seed produce unequal results.

## Warning

- An active edge has no strategic output or validation purpose.
- A relationship dimension has no source provenance.
- A derived summary is persisted without cache ownership and invalidation.
- Trust or loyalty lacks a target or context.
- Familiarity duplicates Development-owned domain familiarity.
- Witness or memory access duplicates Historical Memory truth.
- Patronage relationship lacks a typed grant, service, or expectation source where one is required.
- Fealty relationship lacks reference to formal status where formal status is claimed.
- A full Network node has no strategic function.
- Network processing evaluates irrelevant lightweight nodes every turn.
- Normal turns create excessive low-impact relationship decisions.

**Disposition:** Office/Institution owner retains office truth. Network represents directed holder/institution, delegator/delegate, office-holder/ruler, and office-holder/constituency relationships where strategically relevant.

## Titles conflated with relationships

**Disposition:** Title and Claim owners retain truth. Network represents holder-to-subject, claimant-to-supporter, ruler-to-region, and rival-claimant alignment only.

## Universal attributes and Authority

Legacy Martial, Administration, Diplomacy, Intrigue, Learning, Logistics, Authority, Influence, or Loyalty values may have directly driven relationships.

**Disposition:** Do not preserve universal scores as Network authority. Development, formal authority owners, Trait, Reputation, and Network each retain bounded state. AI reads these owners and proposes actions.

## Monolithic character object

The legacy system combined identity, traits, relationships, offices, memories, reputation, education, lifecycle, and AI behavior.

**Disposition:** Supersede monolithic ownership. Network receives only directed relationship and Network-owned contextual state. Foundation and specialists retain their respective records.
