## 1. Focused Purpose

Baldwin V begins the Tier B multi-attribute sequence and receives a full-strength focused treatment comparable in analytical depth to the refined Sweyn sheet. Tier B remains a calibration function, not a reduced document standard. Tier B is a review function, not a statement of lesser character quality. Baldwin tests whether the system can represent a ruler who is battle-capable and strategically experienced while deriving exceptional value from government, brokerage, dynastic reach, institutional patronage, and political connection rather than top-end direct battlefield command alone.

## 2A. Opening Strategic Thesis

Baldwin opens as a mature and unusually connected comital ruler. Baldwin's comparative strength is not merely duration of rule. Baldwin combines personal government, strategic military credibility, castellan and household coordination, market and route protection, dynastic brokerage, Church-facing patronage, and access to the French minority-government environment.

Baldwin's central tension is:

```text
Personal Presence and Senior Brokerage
versus
Distributed Capacity and Succession Continuity
```

Baldwin is strongest when personal authority is used at decisive points and bounded delegation carries routine work. Baldwin is most exposed when simultaneous French, Flemish, Hainaut, Norman, imperial-facing, military, and institutional demands concentrate too much work in one person.
