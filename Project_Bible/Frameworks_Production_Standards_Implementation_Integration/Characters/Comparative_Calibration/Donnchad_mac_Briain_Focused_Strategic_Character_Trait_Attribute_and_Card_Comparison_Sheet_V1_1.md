## 1. Focused Purpose

Donnchad supplies the third Tier B ruler anchor and the first elderly incumbent under active dynastic displacement pressure. Donnchad tests how Medieval War represents long experience, reduced current capacity, active title possession, founder-memory pressure, dynastic seniority, succession bargaining, honorable withdrawal, and the difference between career formation and present availability.

Tier B is a calibration role, not a lesser character tier. Donnchad may be below Harald, William, Malcolm, Sweyn, and Baldwin in current personal field-command availability while remaining one of Ireland's most consequential opening actors.

## 2. Opening Strategic Thesis

Donnchad begins alive, active, and holding the Munster kingship. Donnchad is not a former ruler waiting for a scripted pilgrimage. The opening problem is whether possession, long experience, surviving supporters, and dynastic seniority can still produce a defensible government or enforceable settlement before coalition decline, age, succession weakness, or military defeat removes choice.

```text
Long Possession, Royal Dignity, and Dynastic Memory
versus
Declining Coalition, Reduced Availability, and Succession Pressure
```

Donnchad succeeds if the campaign converts remaining leverage into durable Munster government, protected followers, branch continuity, or an honorable and enforceable transition. Donnchad fails if inherited prestige encourages resistance after the coalition and command structure needed to sustain resistance have collapsed.
