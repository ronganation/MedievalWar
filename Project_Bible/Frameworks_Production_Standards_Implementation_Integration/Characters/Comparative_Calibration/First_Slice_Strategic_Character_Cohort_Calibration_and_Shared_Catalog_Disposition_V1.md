## 6. Attribute Model

The classic strip is Command, paired audience-scoped Chivalry/Dread, typed Authority/Influence, applicable directed Loyalty or ruler-cohesion summary, and Piety/Religious Standing. Expanded summaries may include Governance, Intrigue/Information, Health/Availability, Personal Security, and Campaign Mobility. They are read models over owner-backed evidence, not independent universal truth scores.

## 7. Numeric and Command Bands

The prototype uses visible 0-10 icon families; Command's provisional mapping is 0-1 Limited, 2-3 Developing, 4-5 Capable, 6-7 Strong, 8-9 Leading, and 10 Exceptional. Values clamp without wraparound. Only Command has this named mapping in the current evidence; final numeric ranges and balance for other attributes remain deferred to UI and simulation tests. Do not average evidence conflicts or use title rank as a score.

## 8. Trait Stage Calibration

Positive capability: Practiced -> Proven -> Renowned -> Exceptional. Disposition: Emerging -> Established -> Defining. Negative conduct: Concern -> Pattern -> Serious -> Notorious. Conditions: Minor -> Significant -> Severe. One ordinary event advances at most one stage; evidence must be attributable, sufficiently distinct, current where relevant, and reviewed by the owning system. Contradictions remain visible. No opening Exceptional trait is required; no stage is awarded by fame, quota, date, title, or one routine victory.

## 9. Applicability by Character Class

| Class | Application rule |

| Cleric / institutional actor | Office, institution, personal practice, canonical standing, and secular mandate are separate. |

The shared catalog's strategic-character default applies. Underage applicability is developmental-only unless explicitly defined; a Register hypothesis is not an opening assignment.

## 10. Comparison Invariants

Keep base career formation separate from temporary health, appointment, forces, supply, staff, access, and effective availability. Command experience differs from Battlefield Commander skill; maritime familiarity differs from Sea Commander; negotiation differs from mediation; Provincial Administrator differs from Proven Governor only where distinct evidence and effects exist. Chivalry and Dread can coexist and require audience-specific conduct evidence.

## 11. Ownership and Lifecycle

Shared Trait/catalog definitions belong to the trait owner; qualified experience and readiness to Character Development; current function to Character Archetype; directed access and relationship observations to Character Network; office, mandate, authority, jurisdiction, and institution to their specialist owners; audience reception and memory to reputation/history owners; presentation to the card read model. Definitions do not create instances. Opening State alone assigns opening instances. Campaign progression requires evidence and owner review; retirement or reduced presentation does not erase identity or history.

## 12. Underage and Historical Boundaries

Philip remains a crowned minor with distributed minority government; Command is Not Rated, not zero. Formation does not grant adult agency or automatically convert to a Trait. Later reign, outcomes, titles, epithets, and reputations cannot be loaded backward into January 1062. Cohort sheets provide hypotheses, not external historical verification or live assignments.

## 13. Network and Relationship Boundary

Use directed, scoped Network observations for trust, access, loyalty, support, recommendation, knowledge, and service. Relationship state is not mutual by default and cannot create consent, appointment, mandate, office, recognition, or agreement. Cross-realm access is not resources. Cohort proposals for loyalty, partnership, claimant sponsorship, and connectors route to Network or the relevant authority when their substance is relational.
