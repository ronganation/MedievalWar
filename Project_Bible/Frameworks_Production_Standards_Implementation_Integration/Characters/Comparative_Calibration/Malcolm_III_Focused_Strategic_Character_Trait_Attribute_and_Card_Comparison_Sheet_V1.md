## 1. Focused Purpose

This sheet adds Malcolm III as the third Tier A command-ruler anchor without creating another full Harald- or William-sized benchmark package.

Malcolm tests a different high-level ruler profile:

- politically and militarily experienced without being a top-end conquest benchmark by default;
- strong in defensive, border, limited-expedition, and consolidation command;
- dependent on negotiated regional cooperation;
- focused on converting personal kingship into durable coordination;
- exposed to personal-kingship dependency, succession uncertainty, and overload.

## 1A. Calibration Intent and Non-Ranking Rule

Malcolm's `Strong` command hypothesis is a positive calibration anchor, not a statement that Malcolm is categorically inferior as a character or ruler. The comparison is limited to opening military-command evidence and specialization.
