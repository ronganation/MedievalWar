## 1. Focused Purpose

Matilda is the next character because the current sequence needs a mature dynastic and household principal whose strategic value does not depend on battlefield command. Matilda tests government during absence, household capacity, child formation, succession continuity, patronage, documentary practice, cross-realm access, and consort influence without converting marriage into co-sovereignty.

```text
Dynastic Continuity, Household Government, and Cross-Realm Access
versus
Derivative Authority, Role Overload, and Contested Regency Expectations
```

Matilda must feel as strategically important as the rulers and commanders already compared, but through a different set of systems.
