## 1. Focused Purpose

Odo is the next focused character because the cohort now needs a major cleric whose strategic power arises from office, institution, family access, learning, patronage, audience framing, records, and separately authorized secular service.

```text
Layered Ecclesiastical Service and Institutional Reach
versus
Office Accumulation, Dynastic Capture, and Overmighty Personal Power
```

Odo must feel powerful without collapsing person, bishopric, See, chapter, property, dynasty, court access, sanctions, patronage, and secular delegation into one character-owned statistic.
