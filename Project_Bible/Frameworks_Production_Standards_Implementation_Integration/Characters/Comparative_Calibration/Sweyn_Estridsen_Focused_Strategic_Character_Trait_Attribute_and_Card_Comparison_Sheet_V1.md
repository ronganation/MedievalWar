## 1. Focused Purpose

This sheet adds Sweyn Estridsen as the fourth Tier A command-ruler anchor and completes the first four-character command-scale comparison.

Sweyn tests a distinct high-level ruler profile:

- an established king shaped by contested kingship, defeat, exile or refuge, return, recognition, and coalition reconstruction;
- a maritime-strategy principal whose exact personal fleet command remains scenario-dependent;
- a ruler whose strength lies as much in survival, recovery, negotiation, and regional recognition as in direct battlefield dominance;
- a coalition sovereign attempting to turn personal durability into institutions, records, delegation, dynastic continuity, and government capable of surviving absence;
- an active opponent to Harald Hardrada without a scripted Battle of Niså, peace, survival, defeat, or future settlement.

## 1A. Calibration Intent and Non-Ranking Rule

Sweyn's profile is not a universal ranking against Harald, William, or Malcolm. The comparison distinguishes military command, maritime strategy, coalition rulership, recovery, negotiation, governance, and institutional development.
