## 1. Focused Purpose

William FitzOsbern is the first non-ruler specialist anchor. The comparison tests whether a subordinate can equal or exceed rulers in operational command, logistics, fortification, and delegated execution without acquiring sovereign authority, universal jurisdiction, independent grand strategy, or guaranteed loyalty from other magnates.

```text
Exceptional Operational Execution and Trusted Delegation
versus
Concentrated Dependency, Overload, Reward Pressure, and Overmighty Concern
```

FitzOsbern matters because FitzOsbern turns approved strategy into mobilization, command structure, supply, fortification, occupation, reporting, and transferable procedure. FitzOsbern is not simply a high-stat general.
