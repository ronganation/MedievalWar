## Concise Verdict

The legacy Character Trait Library is not a constitutionally coherent trait catalogue. Of its **86 entries**, **43** remain trait candidates, while **43** are reputations, memories, development or underage records, Network state, archetypes, an office-derived state, bounded quirks, or archive candidates. The library must remain a read-only legacy catalogue during extraction and must then be archived after destination records, migration rules, and validation are available.

No new framework, registry, ID, realm content, or constitutional owner is created by this audit. The absence of dedicated registries does not block ownership classification. It blocks only exact ID assignment and redirect implementation.

## Constitutional Boundaries Applied

- Character Foundation owns identity, lifecycle, simulation tiers, activation, bounded runtime core, health-state boundary, and specialist interfaces.
- Character Development owns domain-specific progression, experience, readiness, milestones, mentorship development, and transformation.
- Underage Character owns childhood education direction, age-specific restrictions, guardianship requirements, and underage arrangements.
- Character Network owns directed relationship state, alignment, obligation, grievance, information access, and target-specific social/political state.
- Character Archetype owns current strategic function and role fit.
- Historical Memory owns evidence, narrative, audience reception, memory, later reputation, epithets, and legacy.
- Offices, titles, claims, institutions, and authority remain with their existing constitutional owners.
- A trait may describe a persistent tendency, condition, habit, learned characteristic, or recognized quality, but it cannot represent office, title, claim, relationship, archetype, memory, reputation, later epithet, or guaranteed behavior.

- Any Reputation Title is active at opening solely because later history used the epithet.
- `Veteran of Hastings` is active before a qualifying Hastings event occurs.
- `Crusader Veteran`, `Witness to the Black Death`, or `Victor of Jerusalem` is preloaded as a guaranteed later outcome.

- A current archetype is stored as permanent identity or grants office, title, claim, command, or authority.
- Educational paths guarantee adult personality, role, office, or readiness.
- Health conditions create universal authority loss or deterministic incapacity without owner rules.
- A trait changes deterministic fact, creates knowledge, or bypasses an owning subsystem.
- The migration invents IDs, aliases, or registries.

## Warning

- A retained trait has only unconditional percentage bonuses and no owner-specific strategic converter.
- A trait duplicates an archetype, reputation, health state, or quirk.
- A quirk has no meaningful strategic output and exists only to increase generic event frequency.
- A reputation is treated as universal rather than audience-specific.
- A memory lacks a qualifying fact/event reference, evidence/access path, or chronology.
- A Development entry is copied into a universal character attribute.
- Trait acquisition is guaranteed from one education choice or one minor action.
- The normal player workload is increased by routine trait or quirk prompts.

# Legacy Progression and AI Rules

## Trait Progression

The legacy ladders `Brave -> Renowned Hero`, `Builder -> Master Builder`, and `Logistician -> Supply Master` are **not accepted as automatic trait evolution**.

- `Brave` may remain a tendency.
- `Renowned Hero` is likely audience reception or reputation and requires actual known performance.
- `Builder` may remain a learned administrative tendency only after separation from Builder archetypes and reputation.
- `Master Builder` requires evidence-based classification as Development, Archetype, or Reputation rather than automatic tier advancement.
- `Logistician` is Development-owned in this audit.
- `Supply Master` would require evidence of a current function, office, or archetype and cannot be produced by a generic trait ladder.

## AI Trait Usage

Legacy combinations such as `Brave + Ambitious = Expansionist` or `Builder + Organizer = Development Focused` may be used only as non-authoritative evaluation inputs. They cannot create fixed AI personalities, Realm Personality weights, offices, authority, or guaranteed actions. AI reads traits, archetypes, development, relationships, known information, law, authority, objectives, resources, and risk, then proposes commands to owning systems.

May contain stable concept definitions, categories, acquisition/evolution rules, conflicts, visibility rules, AI interpretation, validation, and UI labels.
