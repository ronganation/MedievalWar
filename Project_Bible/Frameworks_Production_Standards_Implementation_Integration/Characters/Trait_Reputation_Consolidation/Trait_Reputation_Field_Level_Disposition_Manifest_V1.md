## 4. Semantic and Ownership Rules

- A person is not an office, title, dynasty, household, institution, region, or realm.
- Trait is not identity and trait is not destiny.
- Reputation is audience-specific reception, not fact or universal knowledge.
- Memory, evidence, narrative, reception, and reputation remain separate.
- Archetype describes current strategic function and is not a permanent personality trait.
- Development, underage roles, network relationships, health states, offices, and titles remain with their current owners.
- Unknown is not zero.
- Names and display labels are not primary IDs.
- Canonical IDs are not assigned by this manifest.
- Alias chains are prohibited.
- Deprecated IDs cannot enter new content or saves.
- AI may read approved state and propose commands; owners validate and execute mutations.
