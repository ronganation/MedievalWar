## 1. Purpose

This review independently challenges the completed Dynasty framework modernization family before Checklist closeout. It does not repeat the manifest by assumption. It tests whether the five current authorities form one coherent family, whether predecessor content was preserved, whether ownership remains separated, whether time and persistence rules align with the current Campaign constitution, and whether the sequence can stop without another framework, wrapper, or implementation artifact.

The review covers:

- Dynasty Network V3;
- Dynasty Relationship V3;
- Dynastic Succession V3;
- Founder Dependency V2;
- Dynasty AI V3;
- the Dynasty current-authority index;
- the Dynasty source manifest and 64-check static receipt;
- predecessor and implementation-proposal dispositions;
- the next-gate and anti-churn decision.

This review creates no Dynasty, Branch, Household, Relationship, Succession, Regency, Founder Dependency, AI, Objective, Registry, Opening State, save, payload, importer, or Unreal runtime state.
