## 1. Purpose

This authority defines the reusable shared contracts through which Islamic religious identity, congregational worship, mosques, Friday worship, qadis, jurists, scholars, teachers, preachers, juristic opinions, endowments, charity, learning, protected communities, public recognition, ruler conduct, mediation, cross-faith relations, religiously framed mobilization, and institutional continuity interact with Medieval War campaign systems.

It does not create a Muslim Papacy, universal clergy, one legal hierarchy, one religious loyalty score, one piety currency, or one Jihad button.

It is a shared authority for Islamic religious-political systems. Faction authorities determine which portfolios are historically applicable, evidenced, active, unresolved, or absent in a particular date and place.

```text
Islamic Religious Life != Muslim Papacy
Shared Islamic Authority != Identical Muslim Faction
Common Contract != Universal Hierarchy
```

## 41. Validation Catalogue

### Fatal

- Islam is modeled as a Papal hierarchy;
- a qadi, jurist, scholar, preacher, mosque, or ruler receives universal authority;
- a religious site silently becomes an institution or hierarchy;
- an office or function creates a named person;
- khotba or Friday recognition creates universal loyalty, title, claim, or territorial control;
- endowment definition creates property or Treasury funds;
- protected-community status collapses into one tolerance score;
- shared religion creates loyalty, alliance, knowledge, or military commitment;
- a juristic opinion becomes a runtime command;
- a ruler directly owns judicial outcomes or private belief;
- conversion directly changes Population loyalty or culture;
- Jihad appeal creates armies, funding, Supply, command, Objectives, Victory, occupation, or Government;
- AI mutates owner state directly;
- Unknown is treated as absent or false;
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save;
- universal seven-month strategic-turn logic replaces the three-track model;
- compile or runtime validation is claimed without evidence.

### Warning

- every Islamic faction receives identical institutions;
- every jurist or scholar becomes a full Character;
- one piety or legitimacy score replaces typed inputs;
- legal tradition is assumed for every person or case;
- ruler patronage is treated as ownership;
- religious difference automatically creates hostility;
- player presentation hides Evidence, uncertainty, authority, or jurisdiction;
- faction content duplicates shared or external reverse state;
- implementation names are asserted without project-native verification.
