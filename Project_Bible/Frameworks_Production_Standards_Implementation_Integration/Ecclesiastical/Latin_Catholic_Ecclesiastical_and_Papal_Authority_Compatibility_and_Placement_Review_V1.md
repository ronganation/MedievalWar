## 1. Executive Verdict

The Latin Catholic authority family is substantively compatible with the new shared religious constitution and the modernized Ecclesiastical V2 foundation. The current Papal-relations V2 authority remains the controlling Latin Catholic specialist. It does not require a broad rewrite or a replacement Catholic framework.

The principal required treatment is bounded authority routing and placement reconciliation:

1. retain the Papal-relations V2 specialist unchanged in substance;
2. identify the shared constitution and Ecclesiastical V2 as its current parent authorities;
3. retain Papal Favor reconciliation as doctrine and derived-summary guidance rather than a competing state owner;
4. retain the Papacy conflict handoff as deferred-scope provenance and future implementation guidance;
