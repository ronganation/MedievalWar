## 1. Purpose

This audit establishes the source-control and ownership process for Medieval War's shared economy and material-flow family before any new universal economic owner is created.

The audit covers resources, extraction, cultivation, production, labor interfaces, property, possession, custody, use rights, revenue rights, markets, prices and availability observations, trade, transport, shipment, delivery, stocks, treasuries, accounts, taxation, rents, tolls, customs, fees, credit, debt, tribute, compensation, investment, construction interfaces, maintenance, damage, recovery, military provisioning interfaces, AI evaluation, player explanation, persistence, migration, replay, rollback, and implementation evidence.

The first objective is not to create a giant Economy Framework. The first objective is to identify what already exists, which owners are current, which portfolios are integration-only, which Realm authorities demonstrate reusable capability, which older systems contain unique mechanics, and which capabilities lack a clean shared owner.

```text
Audit != Framework Creation
Economic Integration != Economic Runtime Ownership

Resource Definition != Resource Production
Trade Agreement != Material Delivery
Settlement Wealth != Treasury
Currentness Gap != Capability Gap
```

## 3. Modern Medieval II Economic Design Promise

The shared economic family must preserve Medieval II's readable campaign economy while correcting the limitations of passive settlement income, resource icons, automatic trade, frictionless Treasury, instant conquest value, and AI decisions based on hidden universal scores.

### 3.1 Preserve

- settlements, ports, roads, markets, mines, farms, fisheries, forests, workshops, estates, warehouses, crossings, and trade routes as visible campaign concerns;
- readable income, expenditure, recruitment cost, upkeep, construction cost, taxation, trade, and Treasury feedback;
- economic pressure as a reason to defend, negotiate, raid, blockade, invest, recover, or wage war;
- strategic clarity and limited routine management;
- economic differences among Realms and regions.

### 3.2 Modernize selectively

Use later Total War and mod ideas only where they strengthen the Medieval II campaign spine:

```yaml
SelectiveInheritance:
  StainlessSteelAndMedievalIIEcosystem:
    - slower development
    - regional specialization
    - historically grounded resources
    - differentiated settlement roles
    - stronger financial constraints
  AttilaAnd1212AD:
    - devastation
    - displacement
    - damaged infrastructure
    - occupation cost
    - recovery
    - food and route pressure
  RomeIIAndDEI:
    - province coordination without erasing local nodes
    - population labor production and recruitment interdependence
    - AI sensitivity to economic consequences
  Shogun2:
    - concise economic presentation
    - clear income sources and blockers
    - low screen clutter
  EmpireAndNapoleon:
    - ports roads markets resources trade routes
    - chokepoints blockades raiding and interdiction
    - economic harm without annexation
  CrusaderKingsStyleCausality:
    - property rights
    - inheritance dower custody grants and confiscation through correct owners
    - contracts obligations institutions and political reception
```

Mods and later games are comparison sources, not constitutional owners. A mechanic is selected only when it improves historical causality, strategic readability, AI competence, Realm differentiation, material conservation, or mod extensibility.

### 3.3 Reject

- one universal Wealth, Prosperity, Settlement Value, Trade Value, Resource Value, Economic Strength, or Diplomatic Value score as complete truth;
- resource icons that create output, Units, equipment, horses, ships, money, or bonuses by presence alone;
- ports that create trade, fleets, customs revenue, or military lift automatically;
- markets that create goods, transaction volume, prices, or Treasury receipts automatically;
- population used as directly spendable money or army manpower;
- conquest that creates full taxation, property rights, production, or integration instantly;
- research, buildings, dates, Objectives, or AI that create people or material capacity;
- player micromanagement of every cargo, household, market transaction, or local ledger;
- AI exemptions from capacity, route, agreement, Treasury, property, labor, or delivery rules.

## 9. Resource and Map Interface

The economy audit must produce a map-facing Resource and Economic Geography handoff without making the Economy owner the physical map owner.

Every resource-bearing location requires separation among:

- physical place and geometry;
- recognized resource or productive potential;
- evidence and confidence;
- legal property, customary use, extraction, or access rights;
- current control and security;
- labor, knowledge, tools, animals, water, and infrastructure;
- seasonal and environmental context;
- operator and operating state;
- output, loss, stock, route, market, and delivery;
- player-visible discovery and uncertainty.

The eventual map implementation should know where a resource is physically relevant, but map placement alone must not create active production, ownership, market access, or military availability.

## 10. Economic AI and Strategic Causality

A strong Modern Medieval II AI must reason from typed economic causes rather than compare universal values.

```text
Actor-Visible Needs
+ Current Rights And Access
+ Production Stock And Treasury State
+ Routes Transport Security And Agreements
+ Political Legal Institutional And Military Constraints
+ Realm Personality And Current Objectives
+ Risk Uncertainty And Opportunity Cost
=
Explainable Economic Proposal Or Abstention
```

The AI should be able to explain proposals such as:

- protect a market, port, crossing, production site, warehouse, mint, estate, caravan route, or convoy;
- negotiate access, trade, credit, compensation, relief, tribute, toll, customs, storage, transport, or safe conduct;
- invest in repair, maintenance, storage, roads, bridges, ports, mills, workshops, defenses, or recovery;
- raid, blockade, interdict, occupy, or threaten without assuming annexation;
- prefer a modest strategic node over a rich but unsustainable target;
- delay war because Treasury, transport, labor, Supply, or political capacity is unavailable;
- pursue a resource or route because it removes a bottleneck rather than because it has a universal value score;
- abstain where knowledge, authority, capacity, ownership, or delivery is unresolved.

```text
Economic Desire != Claim
Need != Right
Right != Access
Access != Capacity
Capacity != Availability
Availability != Delivery
Profitable != Politically Acceptable
Strategically Valuable != Best Target
AI Proposal != Execution
```

## 11. Player Experience and Workload Gate

The default economic presentation should show:

- available and restricted Treasury;
- major expected and received revenue sources;
- critical material shortages and dependencies;
- major market and route conditions;
- production damage or recovery affecting strategic choices;
- important property or revenue disputes;
- major investments and commitments;
- two or three decisive reasons for an economic recommendation;
- the principal blocker and valid next action.

Routine production, local exchange, minor accounting, small shipments, ordinary maintenance, and low-impact price movement should process automatically. The player should not move individual cargo stacks, manage every household, or manually calculate every market price.

## 12. Modding and Extensibility Gate

The shared family must provide future modders with reusable contracts rather than hard-coded historical outcomes.

A mod should be able to add:

- new resource definitions and production chains;
- new property and tenure classes;
- new market, port, fair, mint, estate, workshop, warehouse, toll, or customs definitions;
- new rights, obligations, contracts, credit forms, currencies, and payment media;
- new trade and transport networks;
- different institutional, cultural, religious, legal, or political economic arrangements;
- new AI preferences and Realm-specific evaluation weights;
- new UI summaries and Codex material;
- new migrations and validators.

A mod should not need to bypass owners, fabricate stock from definitions, collapse every economy into faction gold, or rewrite Campaign core state for each setting.

## 16. Candidate Shared Family Decision Gate

Do not create a new shared owner unless all are true:

- the capability recurs across multiple developed Realms;
- no current shared owner controls the mutable state;
- the shared integration authority cannot safely own it without becoming a runtime owner;
- World, Campaign, Diplomacy, Governance, Military, Historical, Character, Dynasty, and Ecclesiastical owners cannot own it cleanly;
- one shared owner reduces duplication and migration risk;
- the capability supports AI and player explanation;
- definition, Opening State, runtime, evaluation, presentation, save, migration, and evidence can be separated;
- conservation, ownership, transactions, idempotence, rollback, and advanced-save protection can be stated;
- the capability remains moddable without hard-coded historical outcomes.
