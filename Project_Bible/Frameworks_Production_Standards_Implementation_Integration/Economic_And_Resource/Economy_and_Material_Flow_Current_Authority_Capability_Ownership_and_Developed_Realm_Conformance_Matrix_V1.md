## 1. Purpose

This matrix compares the shared Economy integration authority, current adjacent frameworks, directly located Realm Economy authorities, current cross-references, manifests, specialist audits, and package-level evidence across every currently developed Realm package in the five accepted Theater scopes.

The matrix does not include theoretical future Realms. A future Realm will enter the shared Economy conformance gate when the package is actually developed.

The matrix separates:

- directly opened Economy authorities;
- directly located but not fully opened Economy authorities;
- exact Economy authorities verified through a current benchmark, manifest, related-authority list, or package inventory;
- embedded Economy capability without a verified standalone Economy owner;
- unresolved exact owner routing;
- capabilities already owned outside Economy;
- genuine candidate gaps for shared ownership.

```text
Search Match != Current Authority
Cross Reference != Full Content Review
Embedded Capability != Standalone Owner
Developed Realm Coverage != Runtime Implementation
Future Realm != Current Baseline
```

## 9. Realm Conformance Questions

Every current or future Realm Economy authority should answer:

- What Economy owner or combined owner controls the Realm package?
- Which shared definitions are consumed?
- How are Property, possession, custody, administration, operation, and revenue rights separated?
- How are Population, labor, production, stock, trade, Treasury, and military Supply separated?
- How are markets, ports, routes, carriers, agreements, and delivery connected?
- How are tax, toll, rent, customs, contribution, tribute, ransom, compensation, gifts, loans, and credit separated?
- How are institutions, households, Dynasties, offices, communities, merchants, external owners, armies, fleets, and private capital protected?
- What is known, unknown, stale, estimated, disputed, hidden, or ReviewOnly at the opening?
- What Realm-specific strategic economy makes the faction distinct?
- What AI proposals and abstention rules apply?
- What player summary exposes the decisive causes without micromanagement?
- What saves, migrations, rollback, evidence, and external locks protect the state?
