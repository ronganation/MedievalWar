## 2. Why the Family Must Reopen

The August modernization workspace correctly concluded that Governance owns administration rather than law, legitimacy, delegation, personality, relationships, succession, or ecclesiastical authority. However, its README froze the family and routed work elsewhere before the subsequent Political Constitution, cross-Theater coverage, delegated-authority, shared-owner implementation, and player-control work existed.

The source <File>Realm_Governance_Framework.md</File> still contains concepts the modernization workspace itself superseded:

- Domain Capacity as a ruler limit;
- Regional Authority as one authority score;
- Crown Authority as a 0 to 100 central-control score;
- loyalty, prestige, influence, and dynasty membership as compressed governor evaluation;
- generic Royal Domain, Church Territory, and Dynastic Holding categories;
- AI evaluation built on the superseded universal scores.

Therefore the family cannot honestly remain frozen while its named root substantive framework conflicts with later ownership decisions.
