# 1. Concise Verdict

The modern Governance vocabulary can be reduced to **36 owner-only concepts** grouped into four constitutional categories:

- **Governance Structure:** what administrative organization exists and how responsibilities and dependencies are arranged;
- **Governance Reach:** where that organization can operate effectively;
- **Governance Capacity:** how much valid administrative work the organization can carry under current conditions;
- **Governance Performance:** what the organization actually accomplishes, delays, or fails to accomplish.

This requirement set does not reintroduce relocated ownership. Governance consumes external law, authority, offices, people, readiness, relationships, evidence, reputation, titles, claims, succession, dynasties, resources, military state, provinces, and public order only through bounded references or query results.

`Crown Authority`, `Regional Authority`, ruler-owned `Domain Capacity`, and automatic loyalty remain superseded. No substitute universal score is introduced.

# 3. Governance Ownership Statement

## Governance owns exactly

Governance owns the reusable administrative state and rules required to answer four questions:

1. **Structure:** What government administration exists, which administrative responsibilities are assigned, and how are offices and institutions connected operationally?
2. **Reach:** Where and through which validated structures can administration operate effectively?
3. **Capacity:** How much valid administrative responsibility can the structures process under current staffing, communication, resource, authority, and dependency conditions?
4. **Performance:** What administrative work is completed, delayed, blocked, degraded, or recovered, and why?

Within those limits, Governance owns:

- realm-administration topology;
- direct and regional administrative arrangements;
- frontier administrative arrangements;
- administrative office structures within Governance scope;
- administrative responsibility portfolios;
- administrative dependency and coordination structures;
- structural centralization/decentralization;
- administrative coverage and presence;
- communication-dependent administrative reach;
- reach constraints;
- administrative burden, operating capacity, strain, continuity, redundancy, and expansion pressure;
- state-side administrative support for policy implementation, legal enforcement, fiscal administration, military administration, and religious administration;
- bounded government performance, failure, recovery, and explanations.

## Governance does not own

Governance does not own:

- realm philosophy, values, fears, preferences, or AI objectives;
- law, policy, custom, decree, charter, privilege, exemption, judgment, jurisdiction, consent, compliance, appeal, or legal dispute;
- delegated authority, regency, emergency grants, review, revocation, transfer, or crown-capture state;
- religious institutions, religious offices, canonical recognition, church jurisdiction, sanctions, or ecclesiastical property;
- person identity, lifecycle, activation, simulation tier, or availability truth;
- personal capacity, readiness, experience, or Development milestones;
- relationships, loyalty, trust, patronage consequences, fealty consequences, obligations, alignment, visibility, contact, or information-access pathways;
- evidence, testimony, narrative, memory, historical records, preservation, legacy, or factual history;
- Trait or Reputation state;
- office identity outside Governance's administrative meaning;
- title, claim, succession, legitimacy, dynasty identity/membership/headship/strategy;
- money, production, prices, treasury, armies, command, battle outcomes, province identity/control, or public-order truth.

A Governance result may influence another owner only through a bounded output. The receiving owner validates and writes its own state.

# 5. Modernized Governance Principles

1. **Administration, not sovereignty:** Governance represents administrative organization and operation. It does not create ownership, title, claim, legitimacy, law, or authority.
2. **Structure before score:** Administrative structures, responsibilities, dependencies, and reach are authoritative. Universal authority or control scores are prohibited.
3. **Office separation:** Office, person, title, authority, delegation, responsibility, capacity, readiness, and performance remain distinct.
4. **Authority by reference:** Governance acts only after a valid authority source or delegated scope exists where required.
5. **Law by reference:** Governance supports implementation and enforcement but neither owns nor silently alters Law and Policy state.
6. **Church-state separation:** Governance owns state-side administration. Ecclesiastical Authority owns church institutions, offices, recognition, jurisdiction, and sanctions.
7. **People are inputs, not government:** Character Foundation supplies people; Development supplies capacity/readiness; Governance supplies administrative context and performance.
8. **Relationships stay directed:** Network cooperation, loyalty, trust, obligation, contact, and information access remain Character Network-owned.
9. **History remains external:** Evidence, records, memory, narrative, and factual history remain Historical Memory or fact-owner state.
10. **Perception is not performance:** Trait and Reputation may shape risk or response but cannot become administrative capacity, authority, or performance.
11. **Reach is not jurisdiction:** Administrative reach cannot create legal scope, title rights, military control, legitimacy, loyalty, or public order.
12. **Capacity is not readiness:** Governance operating capacity is structural. Character readiness and competence remain Development-owned.
13. **Performance is contextual:** Government performance must expose structure, burden, reach, dependencies, inputs, bottlenecks, and outcomes.
14. **External owners write consequences:** Governance sends bounded results; Economy, Military, Province, Public Order, Law, Network, Succession, and other owners decide their outcomes.
15. **Vacancy-safe structures:** Administrative structures persist through holder vacancy or death. Dead people do not retain living agency.
16. **Opening State discipline:** Opening States initialize valid starting administration only. Later outcomes are not preloaded, and advanced saves outrank defaults.
17. **Deterministic administration:** Equal state, inputs, versions, and seed must produce equal Governance results, reasons, and diagnostics.
18. **Selective exposure:** The player sees major administrative structures, constraints, risks, and choices, not routine bureaucracy.
19. **No ownership by metaphor:** Terms such as direct rule, frontier command, church territory, dynastic holding, and governor are descriptive until their external records validate them.
20. **AI proposes, owner executes:** Realm Personality and AI may evaluate and recommend. Governance validates and writes Governance-owned changes only.
