## 2. Purpose

The Law and Policy Framework governs how durable rules, recognized customs, negotiated privileges, temporary policies, legal procedures, disputes, judgments, remedies, enforcement requests, restoration obligations, and reforms become politically and strategically consequential.

The framework exists to reject the following simplifications:

```text
Law != Permanent Modifier
Policy != Law
Royal Intention != Valid Rule
Valid Rule != Actor Knowledge
Knowledge != Acceptance
Acceptance != Compliance
Compliance != Enforcement
Judgment != Remedy Performed
Punishment != Restoration
Case Closure != Restoration Complete
Harsh Enforcement != Effective Government
```

The framework must deepen campaign strategy without converting Medieval War into continuous legal administration.

## 3. Core Design Thesis

```yaml
CoreThesis: ARuleBecomesEffectiveOnlyThroughValidDefinitionApplicableBindingKnownProcedureOwnerCapacityAndObservedResponse
CorePromise:
  HistoricalDifference: PreservePluralCustomaryInstitutionalAndRegionalLegalOrders
  PlayerChoice: SurfaceOnlyMaterialLegalAndPolicyDecisions
  StrategicEffect: RouteConsequencesToCampaignMapArmiesSettlementsInstitutionsEconomyDiplomacySuccessionAndPopulation
  Explainability: ShowAuthorityJurisdictionProcedureAccessEvidenceRemedyAndRecovery
  NonDeterminism: PreserveDisputeResistanceFailureReformAndNonoccurrence
```

No single legitimacy, recognition, authority, compliance, enforcement, justice, or legal-strength score may replace separately owned state.

## 4. Medieval II and Total War Heritage Rule

Law and policy must remain legible through:

- succession and office continuity;
- taxes, obligations, exemptions, and privileges;
- recruitment, military service, requisition, occupation, and frontier defense;
- settlements, regions, markets, ports, routes, and institutions;
- public order and political settlements;
- property, migration, recovery, and development;
- diplomatic agreements and multi-Realm government;
- a small number of consequential player decisions.

Routine petitions, notices, records, minor exemptions, hearings, local judgments, and compliant execution are aggregated unless they create material strategic consequences.

## 52. Validation Rules

### Fatal

- missing stable identity or source provenance;
- active binding without valid definition and owner receipt;
- missing authority, jurisdiction, or subject scope where required;
- static definition stores mutable holder, compliance, case, enforcement, or emergency state;
- law directly mutates another owner's state;
- delegation, regency, ecclesiastical, political, or title action exceeds owner-confirmed scope;
- emergency measure lacks trigger, limits, review, sunset, and handback;
- judgment creates material enforcement or restoration without owner request and receipt;
- identical state and input produce nondeterministic results;
- migration discards unresolved cases, exceptions, emergencies, or restoration.

### Warning

- rule exists mainly as a passive modifier;
- one legal ladder is imposed on every Realm;
- publication assumes universal receipt;
- compliance collapses inability, objection, refusal, and resistance;
- enforcement has no capacity, proportionality, or backlash cost;
- reform lacks transition and restoration;
- every petition interrupts the player;
- harsh enforcement is always optimal;
- charters, customs, and precedents lack source and archive references;
- the Law system duplicates Economy, Population, Recruitment, Supply, Province, Succession, Ecclesiastical, Governance, or Diplomacy calculations.

## 54. Static Fixtures

### Positive

- A written rule is valid in one jurisdiction but remains unknown in another because publication and receipt differ.
- A custom remains legally material despite lacking a written statute because evidence, continuity, audience recognition, and successful invocation exist.
- A petition is received by Governance but becomes a legal case only after standing, jurisdiction, forum, venue, access, and procedure review.
- A judgment requests property return, compensation, and corrected records from separate owners and remains incomplete until receipts arrive.
- An emergency rule expires, authority is handed back, and restoration continues after expiry.
- A policy reduces levy calls without rewriting the underlying military-obligation rule.

### Negative

- A Rule Definition automatically becomes active law.
- A ruler's intention creates universal compliance.
- Publication at court proves every settlement knows the rule.
- A document proves validity without provenance or challenge.
- A fine is treated as completed restitution.
- Case closure automatically restores office, property, reputation, access, and relationships.
- Law directly creates money, units, people, office occupancy, legitimacy, recognition, or military force.

### Abstention and recovery

- A forum abstains because jurisdiction is credible but venue and access are unsafe.
- Enforcement is narrowed or deferred because no capable owner can execute proportionately.
- A failed reform is suspended and reviewed without erasing the old rule or historical consequences.
- An unresolved privilege dispute remains quarantined instead of being guessed into active state.
