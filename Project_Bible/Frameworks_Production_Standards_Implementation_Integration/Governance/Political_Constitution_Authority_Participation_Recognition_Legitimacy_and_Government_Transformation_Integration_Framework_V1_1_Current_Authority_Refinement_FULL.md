## 1. Strong Framework Verdict

Medieval War requires a political constitution that explains who may act, through which office, title, institution, council, custom, mandate, recognition audience, region, or settlement, with what limits, capacity, opposition, and possibility of review. The system must preserve the direct strategic readability of Medieval II while using selective CK3-style political causality to explain why rulership, succession, law, diplomacy, regional administration, war settlements, and government change succeed, stall, fragment, reverse, or fail.

This framework is an integration authority. It does not replace Realm Governance, Law and Policy, Dynastic Succession, Dynasty Network, Character Network, Ecclesiastical Authority, Province, Realm Personality, Trait and Reputation, Diplomacy, Historical Systems, Opening State, Registry, or specialist resource owners. It defines shared constitutional transactions and owner routing between them.

```yaml
framework_thesis:
  RealmIsAContextNotOneMind: true

  PoliticalAuthorityIsTypedAndScoped: true
  LegitimacyIsActorRelativeAndMultidimensional: true
```
