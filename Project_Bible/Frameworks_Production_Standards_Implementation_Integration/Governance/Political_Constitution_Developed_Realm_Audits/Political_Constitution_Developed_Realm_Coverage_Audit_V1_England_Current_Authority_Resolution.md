## 5. England Political Form Preserved

England's coverage is not a copy of Ireland, Wales, or Scotland. The package describes a functioning institutional kingdom under Edward the Confessor with lawful kingship, distributed executive power, regional consent, institutional capacity, material and demographic constraints, and unresolved succession.

The political structure includes:

- lawful royal government;
- separate Crown, person, dynasty, title, office, institution, household, region, and province identities;
- the Witan as an active political community rather than a permanently assembled parliament;
- great earldoms with legal authority, military execution, regional government, and uneven local acceptance;
- ecclesiastical offices and institutions with domestic and canonical recognition layers;
- multiple incomplete succession pathways rather than one predetermined claimant;
- configurable Mercian transition rather than a silent opening default;
- regional pressure, opposition, and foreign interest without scripted 1065 or 1066 outcomes.

No new political wrapper is required to make these distinctions visible.

## 6. Law, Delegation, and Participation Resolution

The current package index names <File>England_Law_and_Policy_Package.md</File> Version 1.0 as design authority complete, with Opening State law bindings and active policy selection pending. The Opening State and Registry rebuild repeat this owner and expose law references for royal succession custom, Witan recognition, earldom authority, military service, royal revenue, regional custom, church-state jurisdiction, sanctuary property, charter privilege, delegation, regency and minority, and emergency authority.

The Witan is represented as an institution and political community with counsel, recognition, office settlement, taxation and military consultation functions. Support must be represented through participants and blocs rather than one ownerless Boolean.

```text
Witan != PermanentlyAssembledParliament
Counsel != Consent
Recognition != Installation
Earldom != Province
Delegation != Sovereignty
ActiveLawDefinition != ActiveOpeningBinding
```

The shared Political Constitution Integration Framework should be referenced by England's package and final Foundation Review, but does not replace the England Law and Policy Package or the shared Regency and Delegated Authority Framework.

## 7. Succession, Recognition, and Legitimacy Resolution

The Opening State provides a nonvacant kingship, separate claim and candidate records, Witan and Church recognition dimensions, coronation readiness, government readiness, adult capacity, military protection, and observer-specific evidence.

The Registry rebuild separately inventories characters, dynasties, titles, offices, institutions, households, regions, claims, evidence, mandates, promises, services, decisions, events, and scenario flags. It explicitly prohibits conversion of claim existence into title possession, candidacy activation, Witan recognition, coronation, or government readiness.

```text
ClaimExistence != CandidacyActivation
Candidacy != WitanRecognition
WitanRecognition != ChurchRecognition
Recognition != Coronation
Coronation != GovernmentReadiness
MilitaryCapacity != CrownOwnership
DynastyStrength != UniversalLegitimacy
```

The Realm-specific legitimacy portfolio is distributed across historical evidence, dynasties, characters, offices, Witan recognition, ecclesiastical recognition, regional acceptance, performance, and foreign recognition. The shared framework supplies the common observation contract. A separate England legitimacy file would duplicate existing owners.
