## 2. Search and Evidence Method

The audit used two passes:

1. broad Realm-package and governance-family searches across all six Realms and the West Francia Theater;
2. exact-name and likely-authority-family retries for package indexes, governance, law, titles, claims, succession, Opening State, Registry, integration audits, and handoffs.

The first pass produced unrelated cross-Theater results for several Realms. Those results were excluded from Realm disposition. Exact package controls and Realm-scoped authorities were prioritized. No absence conclusion is based solely on a failed filename query.
