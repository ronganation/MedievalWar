## 4. Outcome Decision Challenge

### 4.1 Outcome A rejected

The existing shared stack does not contain one proven authority that already integrates the complete participation lifecycle, actor-relative legitimacy portfolio, political escalation routing, and Government Transformation transaction.

### 4.2 Outcome B rejected

No single existing authority controls the full scope. An additive successor to Realm Governance would violate its completed administrative ownership boundary. A successor to Character Network, Law and Policy, Dynastic Succession, Ecclesiastical Authority, Province, or Diplomacy would improperly absorb other owners.

### 4.3 Outcome C upheld

The project already distributes political truth among valid owners. One bounded integration authority is the least duplicative and most constitutionally coherent response.

### 4.4 Outcome D rejected

Substantial shared and Realm political authorities already exist. Differing filenames and distributed ownership do not establish absence.

Recognition is directional, proposition-specific, scoped, conditional, time-bounded, reviewable, and separately owned. The framework allows institutional, regional, dynastic, legal, elective, acclamatory, ecclesiastical, military, urban, communal, diplomatic, customary, and settlement-based recognition without requiring all classes in every Realm.


The lifecycle supports contest, suspension, removal, deposition, displacement, restoration, renunciation, settlement, and ending. This is sufficiently complete for later Realm mapping.

## 16. Realm AI and Player Experience Challenge

Realm AI may inspect actor-visible state, identify valid actors and owners, compare alternatives, request consultation or recognition, route proposals, and explain blockers. Realm AI cannot create participants, consent, legitimacy, claims, titles, offices, resources, political facts, or Government Transformation.

The player-facing contract preserves the Modern Medieval II objective: important political causality with bounded routine interaction. Routine turns target a concise political summary and a limited set of strategically meaningful choices. Crisis detail remains available without becoming mandatory court micromanagement.

## 17. Opening State, Save, Migration, Replay, and Rollback Challenge

The framework separates Definition, Opening State, runtime, transient evaluation, presentation, save, and migration. Exact January 1062 facts remain under Opening State.

The deterministic sequence requires version validation, owner and External Lock resolution, actor-visible state, authority and jurisdiction checks, participation and capacity checks, alternatives including no action, owner routing, receipts, staged effects, hashes, separately authorized commit, postconditions, replay inputs, and rollback journal.
