## 1. Concise Verdict

Medieval War already contains a substantial political and governmental architecture. Realm Governance is complete for its declared administrative scope. Law and Policy, Dynastic Succession, Dynasty Network, Character Network, Ecclesiastical Authority, Province, Realm Personality, Trait and Reputation, Diplomacy, Campaign Data Architecture, Opening State, and developed Realm political-order authorities already own major portions of political life.

The audit therefore selects **Outcome C**. Authority is distributed across several valid shared and Realm files. The genuine missing capability is not another universal government system. The gap is one bounded shared constitutional integration authority connecting political entities, authority sources, participation, recognition, legitimacy queries, political escalation, and Government Transformation while preserving every specialist owner.

## 6. Proven Realm Extraction Sources

### Wales

Wales provides a concise political-order constitution with Realm, ruler, title, office, recognition, installation, capacity, control, acceptance, property, command, dynasty, and precedent separated. It provides strong continuity gates and a consultative-body contract while keeping exact Title, Office, Claim, Law, Ecclesiastical, Military, Geography, Economy, and Opening State truth with their owners.

### Ireland

Ireland provides the broadest developed political topology and Government Transition portfolio. It models multiple governments inside one Realm context, councils and participation, offices and mandates, urban and ecclesiastical political communities, retained local government, clientage, continuity, vacancy, installation, consolidation, handback, and reversible Government Transformation.

### Sweden

Sweden provides mature Crown, title, office, claimant, recognition, installation, and succession separation, including directional regional and assembly recognition without assuming one national electorate.

### Almoravids

The Almoravid movement-state provides confederational constitution, participation, commitment, juristic consultation, contribution, renewal, breach, fracture, route access, and movement-centered government. It proves that the shared framework must not impose a territorial Western monarchy template.

### Badajoz and other developed Realms

Badajoz provides explicit delegated-authority, jurisdiction, oversight, reporting, conflict, law, claim, title, and office interfaces. England, Scotland, Norway, Wales, Ireland, Sweden, the developed Taifas, and other mature packages provide additional structural comparisons without transferring Realm facts.

```text

-> Proposal
-> Authority Basis
-> Participants
-> Consultation
-> Opposition
-> Conditional Consent
-> Compact Or Settlement
-> Reserved And Transferred Powers
-> Office Institution Resource And Personnel Requirements
-> Installation
-> Implementation
-> Recognition
-> Compliance
-> Performance
-> Review
-> Amendment
-> Failure
-> Reversal
-> Restoration
-> Consolidation
```

A transformation may support personal, household, office-centered, council-mediated, regional, retained, frontier, client, composite, divided, civic, confederative, imperial, transitional, restored, centralizing, decentralizing, integrating, or separating arrangements where Realm evidence authorizes them. These are capabilities, not universal government types or Opening State facts.

## 16. Realm AI and Player Experience

Realm AI may identify valid actors, offices, institutions, participants, claims, regions, risks, costs, capacity, alternatives, and owner routes. Realm AI may propose investigation, consultation, delay, delegation, negotiation, compromise, retention, refusal, withdrawal, restoration, or no action.

Realm AI may not create people, claims, offices, consent, legitimacy, recognition, money, forces, property, capacity, or historical facts, and may not execute Government Transformation.

The player should see the political issue, authority basis, required participants, supporters and opponents by issue, evidence and uncertainty, consultation and ratification needs, practical capacity, regional and institutional reception, risks, costs, reversible alternatives, implementation owners, and review or exit conditions.

## 17. Opening State, Save, Migration, and Determinism

Definitions, Opening State, runtime state, transient evaluation, presentation, save state, migration state, Historical Evidence, Historical Memory, Historical Momentum, and Historical Emergence remain separate.

Opening State alone owns exact January 1062 political facts. Advanced saves may not restore opening rulers, revive ended mandates, recreate removed offices, erase transformations, reset recognition, overwrite runtime claims, or collapse regional arrangements.

Ambiguous identity, title, office, institution, jurisdiction, claim, recognition, direction, ownership, or government state must be quarantined rather than guessed. Migration must be idempotent. Equal authoritative inputs, save state, actor-visible information, versions, processing order, and deterministic seed must produce equal results.
