## 1. Targeted Verdict

```yaml
Britania:
  realm_governance_coverage: AlreadyCovered
  Theater_foundation_and_association_material: Substantial
  current_objective_and_designation_layer: IncompleteOrNotRecoveredAsOneCurrentOwner
  disposition: ExistingTheaterAuthorityNeedsAdditiveModernization
  new_broad_Theater_framework_required: false
  one_bounded_Britania_objective_and_designation_addendum_justified: true
  addendum_creation_authorized_now: false
```

Britania does not need another Realm-governance authority or a large parallel Theater framework. The useful gap is narrower: an objective and designation layer that can interpret partial, majority, negotiated, recognized, or comprehensive Britania outcomes without treating them as one binary `Control Britania` flag.

The detailed requirements and rewards should remain with Campaign Objectives and presentation owners. The Theater layer should provide typed eligibility, scope, membership, control-summary inputs, political-settlement interfaces, and designation candidates. This keeps the experience fluid and readable in a Modern Medieval II campaign while retaining deeper political causality behind the result.

## 2. Verified Current Authority Position

<File>Campaign_Theater_Framework_V1.md</File> defines a Theater as an organizational, visual, strategic, and data-facing layer above Realm packages and below the campaign world. It supports regional presentation, AI attention, cross-Realm pressure, route and climate context, onboarding, content organization, and bounded simulation queries. It explicitly states that a Theater is not a Realm, faction, title, political order, culture, alliance, diplomatic bloc, guaranteed war zone, substitute for geography, or ownership claim over contained Realms.

<File>Design_Expansion_Checklist_V3.7.2_Theater_Progress_Block.md</File> records <File>Britania Theater Content V1</File> as static-checked and the initial Theater design, compatibility, and filesystem-organization phase as complete.

<File>Britania_Canonical_Association_Decision_Record_V1.md</File> is a static-checked decision record for Registry reconciliation and definition-fixture authorization. It permits movement toward a definition-only fixture using supported Realm identities and explicitly withholds compile, runtime, importer, save-load, migration, and replay validation.

<File>Campaign_Theater_Registry_and_Association_Owner_Reconciliation_V1.md</File> is a read-only Registry, scenario-binding, and association-owner reconciliation. It authorizes no Realm-package mutation.

## 4. Design Direction: Objectives Carry Detail, Theater Carries Interpretation

The recommended division follows the user's design direction.

```yaml
Campaign_Objective_owner:
  owns:
    - ObjectiveDefinition
    - PlayerFacingRequirements
    - ProgressPresentation
    - RewardsConsequencesAndFollowOnChoices
    - FailureRecoveryAndNonoccurrence

Britania_Theater_owner:
  owns:
    - TheaterScopeAndAssociationClasses
    - TypedEvaluationQueries
    - MajorityAndComprehensiveCoverageInterpretation
    - DesignationEligibilityEnvelope
    - CrossRealmPoliticalSettlementRouting
    - ConciseTheaterSummaryForAIAndPlayerPresentation

Specialist_owners:
  own:
    - RealmGovernmentAndAllegiance
    - TitlesClaimsSuccessionAndRecognition
    - TerritoryOccupationAndAdministration
    - DiplomacyTreatiesTributeHostagesAndSettlement
    - RoutesPortsSupplyInformationAndForces
```

The Theater should therefore avoid embedding a long CK-style decision tree. It should expose enough typed information for objectives and designations to produce a meaningful campaign moment.

## 5. Britania Outcome Bands

The following are proposed design categories, not currently verified active mechanics.

```yaml
proposed_Britania_outcome_bands:
  Presence:
    meaning: MeaningfulRealmTerritoryOrAssociationInsideTheTheater

  Predominance:
    meaning: MajorityStrategicWeightWithoutComprehensivePoliticalSettlement

  Paramountcy:
    meaning: BroadRecognizedPreeminenceAcrossMultipleRealmsOrInstitutionsWithoutUniformGovernment

  ConsolidatedDominance:
    meaning: SustainedMilitaryPoliticalAdministrativeAndRouteAdvantageWithMajorOppositionContained

  ComprehensiveSettlement:
    meaning: OwnerConfirmedPoliticalSettlementAcrossTheRequiredBritaniaScopes
```

These bands should not be forced into a universal numeric ladder. A campaign may achieve one form of predominance without another. Maritime reach, political recognition, controlled territory, protected claimants, diplomatic submission, retained government, and administrative capacity remain separate facts.

```text
Majority Territory != Paramountcy
Paramountcy != Annexation
Military Dominance != Comprehensive Settlement
Tribute Or Submission != Uniform Government
Recognition != Obedience
Alliance != Theater Control
```

## 7. Designations

Designations should be presentation and recognition outcomes backed by owner state, not magical titles that create control.

```yaml
proposed_designation_classes:
  TheaterPredominantPower:
    creates_title: false
    creates_claim: false
    creates_control: false

  RecognizedParamountPower:
    creates_domestic_acceptance: false
    requires_directional_recognition: true

  BritaniaPoliticalSettlementLeader:
    creates_treaty_or_settlement: false
    requires_owner_confirmed_settlement_refs: true

  GuardianOfBritaniaRoutes:
    creates_route_access_or_security: false
    requires_route_supply_and_protection_inputs: true
```

A designation may unlock presentation, prestige, AI interpretation, diplomatic options, or objective follow-ups only when those effects are separately owned and authorized. The designation itself cannot create the underlying facts.

## 8. Majority and Entirety Evaluation

The Theater needs typed evaluation rather than one fixed percentage.

```yaml
Britania_evaluation_dimensions:
  - AssociatedRealmContexts
  - ControlledStrategicRegions
  - AdministeredSettlements
  - MilitaryOccupationAndFieldSecurity
  - PortCrossingAndSeaLaneAccess
  - DiplomaticSubmissionProtectionTributeOrClientage
  - DirectionalRecognitionByRealmInstitutionAndRegion
  - ClaimantAndSuccessionPressure
  - EcclesiasticalAndInstitutionalSupport
  - AdministrativeCapacityAndCommunication
  - ActiveOppositionResistanceAndExternalSponsors
  - PoliticalSettlementAndRetainedGovernmentTerms
```

`Majority` should be interpreted by the objective definition. A territorial objective may use regions or strategic settlements. A political objective may require a set of recognizing audiences. A maritime objective may use relevant ports, crossings, and protected routes. A comprehensive settlement objective should require owner-confirmed political arrangements rather than raw map color.

`Entirety` should never mean every tile, every character, every minor holding, or universal loyalty. It should mean the defined strategic and political scopes required by that objective have been resolved at the owning-authority level.

## 9. Fluid Modern Medieval II Presentation

The default presentation should remain concise.

```yaml
player_view:
  normal_summary:
    - CurrentBritaniaStanding
    - OneOrTwoLeadingObjectives
    - PrincipalBlockers
    - MajorRecognizingOrOpposingRealms
    - MostImportantStrategicConsequences

  detailed_view_optional:
    - DimensionBreakdown
    - RecognitionAudiences
    - SettlementAndRetainedGovernmentTerms
    - ClaimantAndExternalSponsorPressure
    - RoutesPortsAndMaritimeAccess
    - EvidenceUnknownsAndReviewConditions

  routine_decision_count: 0To3StrategicallyMeaningfulChoices
```

The game should feel like a modernized Medieval II campaign, not a continuous constitutional worksheet. Deeper causality appears when a claim, installation, retained government, major political settlement, designation, or Theater objective reaches a meaningful decision point.

Realm AI remains responsible for actor-specific proposals. Theater AI provides a cross-Realm summary and objective interpretation.

## 11. Conditional and Emergent Paths

The Theater layer should support conditional outcomes rather than guaranteed historical scripts.

```yaml
conditional_paths:
  - EnglandConsolidatesBritania
  - WalesOrScotlandBecomesTheaterPredominant
  - IrelandRemainsFragmentedOrDevelopsATheaterRole
  - NormanOrNorseExternalPowerSustainsAClaimantOrExpeditionaryOrder
  - MultipleRealmsPersistUnderNegotiatedBalance
  - MaritimePredominanceExistsWithoutLandPredominance
  - ComprehensiveSettlementNeverOccurs
```

Later historical outcomes may inform possible objective families, but they do not enter the January 1062 Opening State unless an Opening State owner explicitly supports them.
