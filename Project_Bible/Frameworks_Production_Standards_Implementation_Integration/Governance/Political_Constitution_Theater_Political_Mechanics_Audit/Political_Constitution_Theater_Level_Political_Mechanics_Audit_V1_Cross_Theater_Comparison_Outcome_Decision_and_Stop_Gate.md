## 1. Purpose

This file closes the bounded Theater-level political-mechanics audit across Britania, West Francia, the Norse Theater, Iberia, and North Africa. It compares the recovered Theater authorities and Realm-owner findings, decides what capability is already covered, identifies the smallest remaining shared opportunity, fixes the next sequence, and stops the audit before it becomes another Realm or Theater documentation loop.

The review is not an authorization to create active objectives, designations, Registry records, Opening State records, payloads, importer artifacts, or Unreal assets.

## 11. Theater-Differentiated Objective Opportunities

### 11.1 Britania

Britania may support objectives involving Realm survival, predominance, paramountcy, maritime crossings, claimant threats, protected political order, and comprehensive settlement. A comprehensive outcome may preserve several Realms rather than require total annexation.

```yaml
Britania_character:
  - MultipleRealmSurvivalAndRecognition
  - CrossChannelAndMaritimePressure
  - ClaimantAndProtectedHeirMovement
  - PredominanceParamountcyAndSettlementWithoutUniformGovernment
```

### 11.2 West Francia

West Francia may support objectives around the Capetian Crown, royal access, princely predominance, service, negotiated compliance, corridor security, balance of power, and cross-Channel ascendancy.

```yaml
WestFrancia_character:
  - ConsecratedCrownWithUnevenReach
  - PowerfulTerritorialPrincipalities
  - DirectionalServiceRecognitionAndMediation
  - RoyalRestorationPrincelyPredominanceOrNegotiatedOrder
```

### 11.3 Norse

The Norse Theater may support kingship continuity, government during royal absence, North Sea and Baltic access, maritime predominance, assemblies, cross-Realm claims, hostages, clientage, and political settlement. Trade, raid, war, and tribute remain separate.

```yaml
Norse_character:
  - MaritimeKingshipAndExpeditionaryReach
  - GovernmentDuringRulerAbsence
  - AssemblyRecognitionWithoutUniversalElectorate
  - NorthSeaBalticAtlanticObjectiveScopes
```

### 11.4 Iberia

Iberia may support composite-Crown survival, frontier kingdoms, county and march consolidation, Taifa defense, paria and protection orders, cross-faith settlement, Pyrenean, Ebro, Strait, and western-approach objectives, and conditional Maghrebi intervention. No universal Reconquista meter is authorized.

```yaml
Iberia_character:
  - CompositeMonarchiesCountiesFrontierKingshipsAndTaifaCourts
  - PariasProtectionClientageHostagesAndCrossFaithSettlement
  - PyreneanEbroStraitAndWesternApproachScopes

```

### 11.5 North Africa

North Africa may support movement-state cohesion, confederational commitments, capital foundation, Saharan routes, juristic recognition, Western Maghrebi consolidation, Strait access, Iberian calls, limited projection, refusal, overextension recovery, and plural political orders.

## 21. Final Decision

The reviewed Theaters already possess substantial context, association, pressure, AI, presentation, and owner-routing foundations. No Theater authority requires immediate modernization. The only recurring capability gap is a shared Campaign Objective and designation integration contract that can interpret Theater standing without seizing political, territorial, diplomatic, military, route, institutional, or administrative ownership.

The next step is therefore one current-authority audit of Campaign Objective, Campaign Experience, designation, standing, victory-condition, and cross-Theater integration material. No new framework will be created until that audit proves which current owner exists and what substantive capability remains missing.
