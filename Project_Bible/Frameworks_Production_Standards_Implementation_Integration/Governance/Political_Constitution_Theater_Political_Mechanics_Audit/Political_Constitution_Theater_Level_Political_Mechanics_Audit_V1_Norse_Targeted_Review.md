## 1. Concise Verdict

The Norse Theater already has a shared Campaign Theater foundation, a recorded Norse Theater Content V1 authority, a single active implementation checklist, developed and politically differentiated Realm packages for Norway, Denmark, and Sweden, current Realm diplomacy authorities, and evidence for maritime, assembly, dynastic, route, external, and expeditionary interfaces.

The present search did not recover the complete physical text of Norse Theater Content V1. Therefore this audit does not authorize a copy-first successor, bounded addendum, or source patch.

The strongest current disposition is:


The likely useful experience layer is objective-driven and interpretive. The Theater should provide query scopes and summaries for maritime predominance, dynastic reach, assembly recognition, expeditionary pressure, route security, trade, raiding, political settlement, government during absence, and cross-Theater influence. Campaign Objective and specialist owners should retain the actual definitions, state, thresholds, rewards, consequences, and mutations.

## 4. Norse Theater Design Problem

The useful Theater problem is not one shared Norse government. It is the interaction among three political orders and several external theaters through sea routes, dynastic ambitions, expeditionary campaigns, assemblies, royal absence, trade, raiding, tribute, clientage, religious institutions, and political settlement.

```text
Shared Maritime Theater != Shared Government
Maritime Reach != Territorial Ownership
Raid != War
Trade != Allegiance
Tribute != Taxation
Clientage != Annexation
Assembly Recognition != One Universal Electorate
Government During Absence != Transfer Of Sovereignty
Dynastic Claim != Active Claimant War
Route Access != Route Security
```

## 9. Majority and Entirety Interpretation

The Norse Theater should not use one universal map percentage.

```yaml
NorseEvaluationDimensions:
  - AssociatedRealmContexts
  - ControlledStrategicRegionsAndSettlements
  - PortsCrossingsAndSeaLaneAccess
  - FleetAndExpeditionaryCapacity
  - RouteSecurityTransportSupplyAndStorage
  - TradeRaidWarAndProtectionState
  - DirectionalRecognitionByRealmsAssembliesInstitutionsAndRegions
  - ClaimsSuccessionHostagesAndProtectedHeirs
  - TributeClientageServiceAndPoliticalSettlement
  - EcclesiasticalInstitutionalAndMissionaryNetworks
  - InformationCommunicationAndWarningReach
  - ActiveOppositionExternalSponsorsAndRecoveryState
```

`Majority` and `entirety` must be objective-specific.

A maritime objective can evaluate ports, sea lanes, supply, fleet reach, crossing security, and political access.

A political objective can evaluate recognition, settlements, claims, assemblies, hostages, and Realm survival.

A cross-Theater objective can evaluate Britania, West Francia, Baltic, Rus, or North Atlantic interfaces without claiming those regions as Norse Theater property.

```text
Every Port != Every Region
Every Sea Lane != Every Realm
Every Realm Recognizes != Every Realm Obeys
Every Claim Resolved != Every Realm Annexed
```

## 11. Assemblies and Recognition

The Theater may summarize assembly or regional recognition only when owner-approved records exist.

```text
Assembly Participation != National Electorate
Attendance != Consent
Recognition != Installation
Regional Recognition != Universal Realm Recognition
Military Acclamation != Permanent Political Settlement
```

Sweden's directional regional recognition must not be imposed on Norway or Denmark. Denmark's assemblies and regional contributions must not become a universal Norse constitutional template. Norway's personal kingship and government-during-absence problem must remain Norway-specific.

## 12. Government During Absence

Government during ruler absence is a major Norse campaign opportunity because maritime and expeditionary action may remove a ruler or principal commander from the Realm's political center.

The Theater may identify that an expedition creates a cross-Realm or maritime pressure. It may not appoint a regent, delegate an office, move the treasury, create a council, transfer sovereignty, or guarantee continuity.

```yaml
TheaterQueryMayEvaluate:
  - RulerOrCommanderAbsence
  - ExpeditionDurationAndDistance
  - CommunicationDelay
  - HomeDefenseAndRegionalAcceptance
  - SuccessionMinorityAndHouseholdRisk
  - DelegatedAuthorityAndMandateValidity
  - ExternalClaimantAndSponsorPressure

SpecialistOwnersRetain:
  - Regency
  - Delegation
  - OfficeOccupancy
  - GovernmentState
  - MilitaryCommand
  - Succession
  - Relationships
  - Communication
```

Political depth should appear at meaningful moments:

- succession or recognition crisis;
- major expedition;

- cross-Realm claimant coalition;
