## 1. Concise Verdict

North Africa already has a substantial current Theater foundation and a developed Almoravid political portfolio. The reviewed authorities define a bounded western Maghreb and Saharan-interface Theater candidate, the Almoravid movement-confederation-state as its leading Primary Realm candidate, an inactive Iberian external interface at opening, a differentiated relationship grammar, owner-gated association and migration controls, and conditional later projection beyond the core.

The Theater does not require a replacement foundation or another broad political-mechanics framework. The worthwhile remaining design opportunity is a bounded Campaign Objective and designation layer that interprets movement-state consolidation, confederational cohesion, route-based reach, juristic authority, capital foundation, western Maghrebi settlement, Strait projection, and external intervention without creating one territorial-empire meter or assuming inevitable Iberian conquest.

## 9. Majority and Entirety

One universal percentage is not appropriate.

```yaml
NorthAfricaEvaluationDimensions:
  - ConfederationalParticipationAndCommitment
  - RealmPolityCommunityAndInstitutionRecognition
  - StrategicRegionsSettlementsStrongpointsAndMarkets
  - RouteCaravanPortCrossingAndSupplyAccess
  - MilitaryReadinessOccupationAndFieldSecurity
  - AdministrativeReachAndLocalAcceptance
  - ReligiousAndJuristicRecognition
  - ContributionTributeProtectionClientageAndService
  - LeadershipCoordinationAndGovernmentContinuity
  - CapitalFoundationAndUrbanConsolidation
  - InformationCommunicationWarningAndDeception
  - ExternalIberianMediterraneanAndTransSaharanPressure
  - OppositionBreachWithdrawalFractureAndRecovery
```

`Majority` must be defined by the objective. A route objective may care about access, caravans, crossings, markets, protection, and supply. A political objective may care about participant commitment, recognition, retained government, and settlement. A cross-Strait objective may care about transport, consent, logistics, diplomacy, military support requests, and sustainable return routes.

`Entirety` does not mean every tile, settlement, tribe, community, route, caravan, ruler, or institution. It means every scope specifically required by the objective has been resolved by its owners.

## 14. Movement, Confederation, and Fracture

The current Almoravid Movement authority already owns participation, commitment, routes, trade interfaces, contributions, breach, renewal, and fracture.

A Theater objective may evaluate those records, but it cannot collapse them into one cohesion meter that automatically grants or removes political obedience.

```text
Shared Interest != Permanent Alignment
Confederational Participation != Ownership
Contribution != Unlimited Recruitment
Breach != Automatic Rebellion
Withdrawal != Automatic Hostility
Fracture != Immediate Realm Deletion
Renewal != Permanent Loyalty
```

A strong campaign model should allow:

- renewed commitment;
- conditional participation;
- uneven contribution;
- temporary refusal;
- participant withdrawal;
- negotiated return;
- leadership dispute;
- regional fracture;
- political recovery;
- nonoccurrence of major collapse.

## 19. Save, Migration, Replay, and Determinism

Any future objective or designation must preserve:

```yaml
RequiredStateSeparation:
  - TheaterDefinition
  - RealmDefinition
  - RealmAssociationCandidate
  - ObjectiveDefinition
  - ObjectiveRuntimeState
  - DesignationEligibility
  - DesignationPresentation
  - OpeningState
  - SaveState
  - MigrationState
  - HistoricalMemory
  - HistoricalMomentum
  - HistoricalEmergence
```

Advanced saves must not:

- reactivate an opening-inactive Iberian association;
- recreate ended confederational commitments;
- restore withdrawn participants;
- complete a capital project;
- erase settlement or fracture;
- reapply opening recognition;
- replace runtime rulers or leadership arrangements;
- reset objective progress;
- remove campaign divergence.

Deterministic evaluation requires equal authoritative state, actor-visible information, content versions, processing order, and deterministic seed.
