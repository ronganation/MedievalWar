The current West Francia Theater authority already contains the core model the user recalled: a recognized but capacity-limited Capetian Crown provides a common political horizon, while power remains territorial, personal, negotiated, and unevenly enforceable. It explicitly rejects equating Crown recognition with direct administration or Theater association with vassalage.

The useful opportunity is therefore not another governance or Theater-content authority. It is a later Campaign Objective and designation layer that consumes the existing Theater and Realm interfaces. That layer can reward or acknowledge changing relationships to the Crown, princely predominance, royal access, negotiated service, corridor influence, political settlement, and cross-Theater power without forcing all Realms into one hierarchy.

## 4. Capetian Crown and Principality Relationship Model

The current files support a layered model rather than a binary vassal or independent fork.

```yaml
relationship_dimensions:
  - CrownRecognition
  - RoyalDignityAndConsecration
  - ServiceExpectation
  - ActualServicePerformance
  - CounselMediationAndAccessToTheKing
  - TitleSpecificOrHoldingSpecificObligation
  - DynasticKinshipAndMarriage
  - EcclesiasticalRecognitionAndMediation
  - MilitaryProtectionAndHostConvocation
  - PoliticalSettlementAndNegotiatedCompliance
  - RegionalAndUrbanInterests
  - ExternalImperialCrossChannelOrIberianInterfaces
```

Each relationship remains directional, scoped, and owner-routed. One prince may recognize the king while refusing a summons, negotiating service, controlling royal access, supporting an alternative policy, or pursuing an external interest. Another may cooperate through kinship, church mediation, a title-specific obligation, or common defense without yielding direct administration.

```text
Recognition != Obedience
Service Expectation != Service Performance
Counsel != Consent
Kinship != Ownership
Royal Access != Realm Government
Title-Specific Obligation != Global Subordination
Refusal != Automatic Rebellion
```

## 9. Allegiance and Authority Presentation

The player-facing model should avoid one loyalty percentage for every principality.

```yaml
recommended_summary_fields:
  - RecognizesCapetianCrown
  - CurrentServiceExpectation
  - RecentServicePerformance
  - CurrentRoyalAccessAndMediation
  - TitleOrHoldingSpecificObligations
  - ActivePoliticalSettlementOrDispute
  - PrincipalDynasticAndEcclesiasticalConnections
  - CurrentExternalInterfaces
  - KnownCapacityAndComplianceLimits
```

The normal Theater view should show a concise relationship summary and one or two leading objectives. Detailed views may expose the specific Recognition, service, title, claim, route, diplomatic, and institutional sources.

```text
One Summary Line != One Hidden Master Score
Allegiance Label != Permanent Loyalty
Capetian Horizon != France-Owned Hierarchy
```

## 11. Control West Francia Interpretation

The developed-Realm closure already states that a future `Control West Francia` or comparable objective must query Realm, Province, Military, Diplomacy, Political Constitution, Historical, and Campaign Objective owners. It must not become one Boolean that overwrites those systems.

```yaml
possible_control_interpretations:
  RoyalRestoration:
    emphasis: CrownRecognitionServicePoliticalSettlementAndLimitedAdministrativeReach

  PrincelyPredominance:
    emphasis: MilitaryDynasticCorridorDiplomaticAndRoyalAccessPreeminence

  TerritorialDominance:
    emphasis: StrategicRegionsSettlementsAndMilitarySecurityWithoutAutomaticPoliticalSettlement

  NegotiatedOrder:
    emphasis: StableRecognitionRetainedGovernmentObligationsAndPeaceAcrossSeveralRealms

  CrossChannelPower:
    emphasis: ChannelPortsExpeditionaryReachClaimsTradeAndSettlement
```

`Control` should be objective-specific and visible to the player. It should never mean France automatically owns Normandy, Flanders, Brittany, Aquitaine, or Burgundy.
