## 1. Purpose

This audit determines whether Theater-level political mechanics require new shared or Theater authorities after completion of the shared Political Constitution framework, developed-Realm coverage audit, and PCINT-03 no-change closure.

The audit does not assume that a phrase such as `Control Britania`, `Dominate West Francia`, `Unify Iberia`, or `Control the North` is one mechanic, one flag, one claim, one title, one government, or one victory condition. The audit first identifies the existing Theater owners, Realm owners, campaign-objective owners, diplomacy owners, military and Province owners, recognition owners, route and Supply owners, Historical Systems owners, Registry owners, and Opening State owners that would contribute to any later Theater evaluation.

```text
Theater Association != Realm Government
Theater Objective != Claim
Theater Dominance != Uniform Administration
Military Occupation != Political Settlement
Recognition Summary != Domestic Acceptance
Control Summary != New Territory Owner
Victory Condition != Opening State
```

## 6. Theater Objective Constitution

Any later Theater political objective must separate:

```yaml
theater_objective_stages:
  - DefinitionOnly
  - ReviewRequired
  - EligibleUnactivated
  - ActiveOwnerApproved
  - Blocked
  - Deferred
  - Contested
  - PartiallySatisfied
  - Failed
  - Recovering
  - CompletedOwnerConfirmed
  - Nonoccurrence
```

A Theater objective must define:

- eligible Realm or actor classes;
- evidence and world-state requirements;
- territorial, military, diplomatic, political, institutional, route, Supply, and information inputs;
- excluded private state;
- recognition and settlement requirements;
- failure, delay, recovery, reversal, and nonoccurrence;
- owner routing;
- player explanation;
- save, migration, replay, and rollback behavior.

No objective may create a title, claim, government, relationship, recognition, territorial control, army, access right, route capacity, or settlement merely because the objective exists.
