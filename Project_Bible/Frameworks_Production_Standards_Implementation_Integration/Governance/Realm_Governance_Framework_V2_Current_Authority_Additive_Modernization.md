## 2. Purpose

Realm Governance defines how a realm attempts to administer strategically meaningful territory, institutions, offices, responsibilities, people, and public functions through finite structures, communication, resources, authority receipts, and review.

Governance answers:

- What administrative arrangement exists?
- Which responsibilities are assigned, where, and through what structure?
- Which offices, institutions, regions, and routes connect the center to action?
- What capacity and information make execution possible?
- What burdens, dependencies, delays, failures, and recovery needs exist?
- How does administration scale, fragment, adapt, and survive leadership change?

Governance does not answer by itself:

- who lawfully owns a title;
- who holds an office;
- whether a claim is valid;
- whether a delegation exists;
- whether actors consent or recognize an action;
- whether law authorizes an act;
- whether people, money, forces, transport, or supplies exist.

## 3. Core Philosophy

Power may win territory. Government must still make decisions travel, records persist, responsibilities reach owners, resources arrive, orders remain lawful, and failures recover.

## 4. Medieval II and Total War Heritage Rule

Realm Governance must remain visible through:

- settlements and regions;
- roads, rivers, crossings, ports, and communication;
- offices, administrators, commanders, and institutions;
- fiscal, military, legal, and religious administration;
- regional and frontier arrangements;
- expansion, occupation, devastation, and recovery;
- succession and continuity;
- public decisions and explainable consequences.

The player manages important arrangements, appointments, jurisdictions, dependencies, reforms, crises, and recoveries. The player does not approve every clerk, message, witness, payment, or local routine.

No realm must use every arrangement. Realm-specific authorities select and adapt only historically justified arrangements.

## 9. Direct Administration

Direct administration means public responsibilities are routed through principal government structures without an intervening regional administrative arrangement. It does not mean the ruler personally owns, visits, or performs every function.

```yaml
DirectAdministrationRequires:
  - ValidPublicFunctionAndOwner
  - AdministrativeStructure
  - ResponsibleOfficeInstitutionOrMandate
  - StaffRecordsCommunicationAndReview
  - ResourceAndRouteReceipts
  - LawAuthorityAndJurisdictionReceipts
  - ContinuityAndReplacementPlan
BenefitsMayInclude:
  - FasterCoordinationWhereReachIsStrong
  - ClearerResponsibility
  - MoreDirectInformation
RisksMayInclude:
  - Overload
  - DependenceOnFewPeopleOrRoutes
  - DelayedRegionalResponse
  - WeakLocalKnowledge
```

Direct administration is not inherently superior to regional administration.

## 44. Static Fixtures

### Positive fixtures

- A small realm uses direct administration for selected public functions while household property remains separate.
- A regional administrative arrangement operates through an admitted office, mandate, records, routes, and review without making the administrator owner of the region.
- A frontier arrangement gains response speed but creates workload, resource, review, and continuity costs.
- A large realm distributes fiscal, military, legal, and ecclesiastical administration differently by function.
- Government survives a ruler's death because records, offices, delegates, routes, and handback procedures exist.
- An AI proposes retrenchment because burden, route disruption, and vacancy exceed supported capacity.

### Negative fixtures

- Territory count automatically exceeds Domain Capacity.
- Crown Authority 75 makes every region obedient.
- A dynasty member is automatically loyal and competent.
- Appointment creates authority, money, soldiers, staff, records, and performance.
- A bishopric is treated as one Church Territory owned by the realm.
- Conquest instantly creates integrated administration.
- Governance writes law, legitimacy, recognition, relationships, or succession.

### Recovery fixtures

- A government narrows commitments after overload, restores records and communication, revises delegation, and recovers gradually.
- A region remains part of the realm while administration temporarily fails and owner-specific recovery proceeds.
- Office loss produces review and replacement rather than instant collapse or historical autofill.
