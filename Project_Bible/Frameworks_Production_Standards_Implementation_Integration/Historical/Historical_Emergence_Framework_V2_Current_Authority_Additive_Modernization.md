## Historical Emergence Framework

Medieval War — Systems/Historical\_Emergence\_Framework.md

## Overview

The Historical Emergence Framework governs the appearance of significant historical, semi-historical, and dynamically generated individuals throughout a Medieval War campaign.  
The framework exists to create:
- Historical immersion
- Character variety
- Dynamic storytelling
- Long-term campaign evolution
- World responsiveness  
without relying upon rigid scripted spawns.  
Historical figures should emerge because the world creates opportunities for them.  
Not because a date has been reached.

## Core Philosophy

Historical Momentum creates pressure.  
Historical Emergence creates people.  
The framework attempts to answer:  
Why did extraordinary individuals appear?  
Why did specific movements emerge?  
Why did particular leaders rise to prominence?  
The answer is:  
Conditions.  
The world produces opportunities.  
Individuals emerge to exploit those opportunities.

## Historical Emergence vs Scripted Characters

Incorrect:  
Year = 1220  
Spawn Roger Bacon.

The world creates the possibility.  
The systems create the person.

## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

Historical Emergence provides rare, explainable opportunities for significant historical, semi-historical, and dynamically generated people to enter the campaign when owner-confirmed world conditions make such a person plausible.

The system must preserve all of the following:

- historical pressure without guaranteed history;
- campaign divergence;
- rarity and strategic significance;
- visible causes rather than unexplained spawning;
- biological and chronological plausibility;
- identity integrity and duplicate prevention;
- Character, Dynasty, Household, office, title, claim, command, and capacity ownership;
- valid nonoccurrence.

```text
Historical Emergence != Date Spawn
Eligibility Window != Activation
Historical Identity Match != Guaranteed Appearance
Dynamic Equivalent != Generic Free Character
Emergence Candidate != Character
Character Admission != Office Or Command
Nonoccurrence != System Failure
```

## 8. Historical Identity and Dynamic Equivalent Paths

### Historical identity path

A historical identity match requires:

- compatible chronology and biological age;
- compatible geographic and cultural context;
- no existing canonical or historical identity collision;
- continuing plausibility despite campaign divergence;
- sufficient evidence for identity-specific presentation;
- owner-approved Character Definition and Registry disposition.

### Dynamic equivalent path

A dynamic equivalent may be proposed when:

- the strategic role remains plausible;
- the historical individual is chronologically, geographically, biologically, or politically implausible;
- the campaign has diverged materially;
- no canonical historical identity should be reused;
- a generated identity can be created through approved Character and Registry owners.

```text
Dynamic Equivalent != Renamed Historical Person
Role Similarity != Shared Identity
Historical Absence != Permission To Duplicate
Divergence != Random Spawn
```

## 21. Opening State and Advanced-Save Firewall

Opening State may establish already-living January 1, 1062 people only through its own owner-controlled records. Historical Emergence cannot retroactively create them from definitions.

Advanced saves cannot:

- replay Opening State emergence;
- recreate a dead, retired, rejected, or already represented historical identity;
- remove admitted dynamic equivalents;
- replace a dynamic equivalent with a historical identity solely because a date was reached;
- reset eligibility, rarity, saturation, or nonoccurrence;
- pregrant later titles, offices, claims, command, fame, or Development;
- reveal hidden or external-private information;
- remove Campaign divergence.

Ambiguous migration enters quarantine rather than being guessed.

## 23. Determinism and Candidate Ordering

Where several candidates qualify, ordering must be deterministic.

```yaml

  - RarityClass

```

Randomness may vary bounded presentation only when a seeded rule and replay receipt exist. Randomness may not override identity collisions, biological plausibility, owner approval, or capacity requirements.

## 24. Player-Facing Presentation

The player-facing emergence explanation should show:

- why the person could emerge;
- which pressures and world conditions mattered;
- whether the person is historical, semi-historical, or a dynamic equivalent;
- what is known and uncertain;
- what support and limits exist;
- what the person does not automatically own;
- relevant failure, delay, and nonoccurrence conditions.

Routine candidate evaluation remains hidden. Immediate notification is reserved for owner-approved admission, major rejection or failure visible to the player, identity conflict, or critical migration quarantine.
