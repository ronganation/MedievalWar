## 4. Historical Memory Assessment

### 4.1 Strengths retained

Historical Memory already defines a strategically meaningful reception layer between past events, evidence, witnesses, narrative formation, audience adoption, commemoration, preservation, and political consequence. The framework explicitly separates Event, Fact Record, Evidence, Witness, Narrative, Memory Tradition, Reception, Commemoration, and Legacy.

The reconciliation package further locks the central owner boundaries:

- Campaign History or applicable strategic owners retain deterministic facts and participation truth.
- Character Foundation retains identity and lifecycle.
- Character Network retains directed relationships and information-access truth.
- Reputation retains current character-audience perception.
- Historical Memory retains evidence, testimony records, narratives, reception, memory traditions, historical records, commemoration, preservation, legacy, and bounded source provenance.

### 4.2 Readiness strength

The package distinguishes definitions, Opening State initialization, authoritative runtime state, derived query results, save metadata, migration data, validation data, trace data, and quarantine behavior. It also requires sole writers and deterministic ordering.

### 4.3 Current defects and compatibility risks

Historical Memory should not receive another wholesale expansion, but the following items require a narrow compatibility review against current authorities:

These are compatibility checks, not authorization to rewrite the 16-contract package or regenerate the 270-row inventory and catalogue.

## 5. Historical Momentum Assessment

### 5.1 Strengths retained

Historical Momentum has the correct foundational doctrine:

- history applies pressure but does not predetermine outcomes;
- the campaign should begin after major pressures emerge but before major outcomes occur;
- political, military, economic, religious, cultural, and dynastic pressures should produce direction, opportunity, context, and motivation;
- Momentum should feed Historical Emergence and Narrative Arcs without forcing historical outcomes.

### 5.2 Material gaps

The framework does not yet provide the current project with sufficient authority for:

```yaml
MissingOrThinAuthorityAreas:
  - StableDefinitionsVersusOpeningRecordsVersusRuntimeState
  - SoleWriterAndOwnerBoundaries
  - MomentumAdmissionAndRetirementRules
  - GeographicRealmTheaterDynastyInstitutionAndCharacterScopes
  - SourceOwnerReceiptsAndVersionedInputs
  - ActorVisibleKnowledgeAndUncertainty
  - PressureAccumulationDecayContradictionAndSaturation
  - CrossDomainConservationAndNoMaterialCreation
  - ObjectiveCandidateVersusObjectiveInstanceBoundary
  - RealmAIEvaluationVersusOwnerTransactionBoundary
  - HistoricalMemoryInputAndOutputBoundary
  - SaveOwnershipMigrationQuarantineAndAdvancedSaveProtection
  - DeterministicOrderingAndTrace
  - ThreeTrackTemporalProcessing
  - UnrealEngine5_8CandidateVersusVerifiedBoundary
  - PhasedImplementationDependenciesAcceptanceEvidenceRollbackAndOwnerGates
```

The statement that AI generally follows Momentum unless strong strategic reasons exist is too broad for current authority standards. Momentum must provide bounded observations and proposal weights, not command AI behavior or execute owner state.

The six original categories remain useful but should not be treated as a closed universal enum. Geography, institutions, population, supply, succession, legitimacy, diplomacy, and realm conditions may contribute through owner-backed inputs without transferring ownership to Momentum.

## 6. Historical Emergence Assessment

### 6.1 Strengths retained

Historical Emergence has the correct central doctrine:

- significant people should emerge from conditions rather than rigid date scripts;
- historical individuals are not guaranteed;
- dynamic equivalents may appear when the campaign has diverged;
- legendary figures remain rare;
- emergence should be understandable, causally grounded, and integrated into normal Character and Dynasty systems.

This matches the standing requirement that exceptional figures arise through conditional Historical Emergence driven by date windows and world-state thresholds rather than guaranteed scripts.

### 6.2 Material gaps

The framework currently risks implying that Emergence itself creates complete people. Current authority requires a stricter candidate and admission pipeline.

```yaml
MissingOrThinAuthorityAreas:
```

## 8. Three-Track Temporal Requirement

```yaml
HistoricalProgression:
  months_per_strategic_turn: 21

BiologicalProgression:
  months_per_strategic_turn: 7

CampaignSeasonProgression:
  steps_per_strategic_turn: 1
  order:
    - Winter
    - Spring
    - Summer
    - Autumn
```

Momentum may evaluate historical eligibility and long-running pressure through the Historical track, seasonal conditions through Campaign Season, and person age or biological plausibility only through owner-provided Biological state. Emergence must not equate a strategic turn with seven universal months.

## 11. Final Assessment

The bounded audit closes with a clear substantive sequence rather than administrative churn:

1. Preserve Historical Memory and its reconciliation package.
2. Modernize Historical Momentum first because it owns the pressure and opportunity interpretation consumed by Emergence.
3. Modernize Historical Emergence second with strict candidate, identity, lifecycle, rarity, and owner-transaction boundaries.
4. Create a Historical Memory compatibility addendum only if the detailed successor work exposes a real conflict that cannot be handled by precedence and interpretation.
5. Close the Historical folder with one index, one manifest, and one independent static review after the substantive work is complete.
