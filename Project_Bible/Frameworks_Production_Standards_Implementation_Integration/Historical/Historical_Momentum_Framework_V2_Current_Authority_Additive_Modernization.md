# Historical Momentum Framework
Medieval War — Systems/Historical_Momentum_Framework.md

---

# Overview

Historical Momentum represents the major political, military, economic, religious, cultural, and dynastic forces acting upon the world at campaign start and throughout campaign progression.

Historical Momentum is not scripted history.

Historical Momentum is not predetermined history.

Historical Momentum is pressure.

The systems create opportunities.

Players and AI create outcomes.

---

# Core Philosophy

History influences the campaign.

History does not control the campaign.

The purpose of Historical Momentum is to provide:

- Direction
- Context
- Opportunities
- Motivations
- Plausible Historical Development

without forcing predetermined outcomes.

---

# Historical Momentum vs Scripted History

Incorrect:

- William always invades England.
- Harold always becomes king.
- Wales always loses.
- A historical outcome always occurs.

---

Correct:

- Norman ambitions exist.
- Harold possesses influence.
- Wales faces pressure.
- Edgar possesses legitimacy.
- Foreign powers possess opportunities.

History nudges.

Players decide.

Systems react.

# Historical Momentum Categories

Historical Momentum typically manifests through six categories.

1. Political Momentum
2. Military Momentum
3. Economic Momentum
4. Religious Momentum
5. Cultural Momentum
6. Dynastic Momentum

# Political Momentum

Political Momentum represents governmental pressures and political developments already forming within a realm.

Examples:

England:

- Edward the Confessor's aging rule
- Succession uncertainty
- Growing Godwin influence

Normandy:

- Ducal ambition
- Cross-Channel interests

Scotland:

- Regional consolidation

Ireland:

- High Kingship rivalry

Political Momentum tends to influence:

- Government decisions
- Alliances
- Succession
- Court politics

AFTER major historical pressures have emerged.

BEFORE major historical outcomes have occurred.

## 10. Contradiction, Interaction, and Counter-Momentum

Examples include:

- expansion pressure versus overstretch and Supply constraint;
- succession pressure versus negotiated settlement;
- religious reform pressure versus institutional resistance;
- cultural continuity pressure versus political accommodation;
- military opportunity versus Population, Treasury, route, or seasonal constraint;
- dynastic ambition versus claimant weakness or external recognition limits.

```yaml

  - Redirecting
  - Constraining
  - Contradictory
  - MutuallyExclusiveForCurrentScope
```

The framework must preserve the source observations and reasons. It must not reduce every interaction to addition or subtraction of one hidden value.

## 23. Validation Rules

### Fatal

- Momentum is treated as deterministic fact or outcome.
- A Momentum observation has no historical basis or current owner-state basis where required.
- Momentum directly writes specialist state.
- A date alone activates, escalates, or resolves Momentum.
- Hidden or private state leaks through summary or explanation.
- Momentum creates an Objective instance, Character, Claim, Office, Unit, resource, or capacity.
- Opening State preloads later outcomes or overwrites an advanced save.
- Definition data contains mutable runtime state.
- Migration guesses ambiguous identity, meaning, audience, or destination.
- Equal state, inputs, versions, and seed produce unequal authoritative results.

### Warning

- Momentum has no bounded subject or scope.
- Momentum has no meaningful strategic influence channel.
- Momentum lacks retirement or contradiction rules.
- One universal score replaces category, direction, persistence, and uncertainty.
- Repeated low-impact pressure events create notification spam.
- AI explanation omits unknown, stale, or contested inputs.
- A source category is treated as a closed universal enum without demonstrated need.

## 27. Final Current-Authority Rule

```text
History Supplies Pressure
Players And AI Propose Responses
Specialist Owners Validate And Execute
Campaign Outcomes Remain Emergent
```

Historical Momentum succeeds when historically grounded pressure makes the campaign legible, differentiated, and urgent without scripting the result.

Historical Momentum fails when pressure becomes destiny, a date becomes a command, an AI weight becomes truth, or a historical example becomes mandatory future state.
