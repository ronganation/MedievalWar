This constitution defines the shared semantic and ownership contract for information, observation, reporting, communication, knowledge, belief, intelligence assessment, warning, secrecy, disclosure, deception, counterintelligence, and decision-facing explanation across Medieval War.

It does not create a universal spy system, a universal agent roster, a hidden omniscient state, a diplomatic outcome, a military outcome, a historical fact, a runtime network, an Opening State record, or an Unreal payload.

## 1. Constitutional Thesis

The campaign world must separate what exists from what any actor could observe, what was actually observed, what a source claimed, what was transmitted, what arrived, what was understood, what was believed, and what was later corrected.

```text
World State
!= Observable State
!= Observation
!= Claim
!= Report
!= Message
!= Delivery
!= Comprehension
!= Assessment
!= Belief
!= Truth
```

Information is strategically meaningful because it is partial, delayed, unevenly distributed, contestable, costly to acquire, vulnerable to loss, and dependent upon people, institutions, routes, languages, records, access, security, and time.

Information is not a spendable universal currency and must not become one opaque score.

## 2. Modern Medieval II Promise

Preserve the direct campaign readability of:

- visible armies and settlements;
- scouts, spies, diplomats, messengers, watchpoints, ports, roads, borders, and courts;
- map knowledge that improves through contact, travel, reports, and action;
- concise warnings and decision summaries;
- meaningful covert action and counteraction;
- strong player control over major strategic priorities.

Modernize that spine with:

- source-bound claims and provenance;
- actor-specific knowledge and belief;
- confidence, contradiction, staleness, and uncertainty;
- dispatch, transit, delivery, acknowledgment, and interpretation;
- secrecy, compartmentation, interception, compromise, and disclosure;
- warnings with thresholds, audiences, urgency, and review;
- deception that can fail, remain partial, or be exposed;
- deterministic traces and correction without retroactive omniscience.

Avoid:

- omniscient player or AI knowledge;
- agents that reveal perfect truth;
- mission spam;
- mandatory courier micromanagement;
- one universal intelligence score;
- one symmetric Realm knowledge pool;
- arbitrary misinformation without provenance or explanation;
- hidden dice replacing causal systems.

Immediate alerts are reserved for material events such as:

- critical warning;
- high-value message failure;
- confirmed compromise;
- major deception exposure;
- decisive contradiction;
- loss of the only valid source or channel;
- material correction that changes a major decision;
- owner-confirmed intelligence or counterintelligence crisis.

Routine deliveries, minor rumors, stale low-value records, and ordinary source updates should enter digests and summaries.

## 30. Advanced-Save Firewall

- restore lost, intercepted, destroyed, exposed, or superseded records;

## 32. Migration and Quarantine

Migration may:

- map stable old categories to current categories;
- preserve old provenance as legacy provenance;
- retain Unknown, IntentionalEmpty, ReviewOnly, and Quarantine states;
- mark records stale, superseded, or semantically ambiguous;
- preserve prior decisions and explanations.

Migration may not:

- invent holders, sources, dates, delivery, belief, disclosure, access, confidence, or truth;
- merge independent records without evidence;
- convert a report into a fact;
- convert a secret into public knowledge;
- activate an Agent, channel, network, warning, or deception operation.

## 33. Determinism and Ordering

Deterministic processing requires stable ordering by:

1. effective Historical date and Campaign Season;
2. event or transaction priority;
3. owner authority class;
4. source or channel identity;
5. record stable ID;
6. rule version;
7. source-state hash.

Randomness, where allowed by a specialist execution owner, must use stored seeds and produce replayable receipts. The shared constitution itself performs no random outcome execution.
