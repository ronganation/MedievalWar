## 1. Authority Verdict

Recruitment closure changes whether a source may create new formations or provide replacements. Recruitment closure does not delete existing formations, erase people, reset history, force conversion, or manufacture successor units. Veteran persistence preserves actual surviving formations and subjects while their sources, readiness, obligations, equipment, officers, animals, replacements, and political support remain sufficient for continued service.

## 3. Scope and Ownership

This constitution owns shared semantics for:

- closure of new formation, replacement, reinforcement, regional, institutional, contract, allied, mercenary, religious, household, and campaign-specific access;
- transitional overlap between earlier and later unit families;
- persistence of active veteran formations and cadres;
- restricted replacement, selective transfer, reorganization, demobilization, reserve use, local-only service, archival lineage, and actual extinction;
- source depletion, institution loss, political withdrawal, conquest, migration, demographic change, equipment change, horse loss, officer loss, contract expiry, and doctrinal development as closure causes;
- player and AI explanation of why a unit persists, narrows, closes, transfers, or disappears;
- realm, family, unit, progression, and era-index conformance.

This constitution does not execute closure, delete units, allocate IDs, set exact dates, create successor families, mutate recruitment pools, decide legal obligations, or activate runtime state. Those acts remain with their owners.

## 7. Closure Causes

```yaml
closure_causes:
  - source population or household depletion
  - institution loss dissolution capture abandonment or transformation
  - regional conquest devastation displacement assimilation or access loss
  - legal custom title office obligation or permission change
  - contract expiration breach withdrawal or market disappearance
  - allied affinity coalition religious or campaign service ending
  - equipment weapons armor ammunition or production failure
  - horse remount animal handler or fodder failure
  - officer cadre instructor formation or doctrine loss
  - route supply settlement stable workshop or recovery failure
  - repeated casualties and veteran extinction
  - political resistance legitimacy loss refusal or competing duty
  - deliberate military reform or source reallocation
  - new family emergence that attracts but does not automatically absorb qualified people
  - chronology that permits review but does not itself execute closure
```

A date, era label, technology, building, victory, conquest, or successor card is never sufficient by itself. The controlling owner must identify the actual changed causal inputs.

## 28. Positive Validation Fixtures

- New Norman Milites recruitment narrows while existing veteran formations persist during transitional overlap.
- A formation enters `ReplacementOnly` and cannot spawn a new formation.
- Returning wounded rejoin an existing veteran formation after recovery without creating new people.
- Qualified individuals transfer to a mature family only after source release and destination acceptance.
- A depleted veteran unit remains active but reduced because officers or mounts are missing.
- A regional militia remains available for local defense after field recruitment closes.
- A mercenary company ends one contract without becoming a permanent domestic pool.
- A destroyed institution closes training and replacement while surviving field veterans continue temporarily.
- Demobilization returns people, equipment, animals, obligations, and history to their owners.
- Extinction occurs only after actual formation, cadre, source, and reconstitution routes fail.

## 29. Negative Validation Fixtures

- Every Early unit disappears when High begins.
- A successor card automatically converts all parent formations.
- Closure deletes people, horses, equipment, wounds, experience, or history.
- `ReplacementOnly` permits raising a new unit.
- Closed recruitment is bypassed by research or a building.
- Veteran persistence provides infinite full-strength replenishment.
- A date alone proves source extinction.
- A disbanded foreign or mercenary formation becomes a generic domestic pool.
- Incompatible depleted formations merge solely to restore headcount.
- Higher-difficulty AI ignores closure or receives hidden replacements.

## 30. Fatal Validation Rules

```yaml
fatal_if:
  - closure dimension cause owner or affected source is missing
  - closure deletes active formations or people without separate valid outcome records
  - progression forces automatic conversion or identity reset
  - closed capacity remains available for prohibited recruitment
  - replacement draws from an invalid or duplicated source
  - veteran persistence exists without actual surviving formation or cadre
  - transfer duplicates people equipment animals officers or assignments
  - reorganization erases ownership lineage casualties obligations or history
  - extinction is declared while recoverable missing captured cadre or source paths remain unresolved
  - reopening occurs without restored causal inputs and owner approval
  - static authority executes Registry Opening State runtime or Unreal mutation
```

## 31. Anti-Exploit Constitution

- Closing and reopening cannot be toggled to duplicate pools, reservations, people, equipment, animals, or formations.
- Save and load cannot reset closure, replacement limits, contract expiry, source depletion, veteran casualties, or transfer history.
- A unit cannot disband and reform instantly to bypass replacement closure.
- Parent and successor families cannot share the same people simultaneously.
- Demobilization cannot erase arrears, grievances, wounds, captivity, equipment losses, or service memory.
- A garrison cannot become a field veteran and remain a full garrison simultaneously.
- Captured people or property cannot bypass closure.
- AI receives no hidden closure exemptions.
