## 13. Captured and Riderless Horses

Battle may leave horses riderless, dispersed, injured, exhausted, captured, or recoverable. Their fate is separate from the rider's fate.

```yaml
horse_post_battle_states:
  RecoveredWithOriginalRider:
    effect: Retain pairing subject to condition
  RecoveredWithoutRider:
    effect: Return to owner or remount review
  Dispersed:
    effect: Missing until recovered or lost
  InjuredOrLame:
    effect: Recovery treatment retirement or loss
  CapturedByEnemy:
    effect: Captor custody pending spoils and ownership resolution
  CapturedFromEnemy:
    effect: Potential remount transport property ransom return or sale
  Killed:
    effect: Removed from living horse capacity
  Unsuitable:
    effect: May serve another valid role but not intended combat use
```

A captured or recovered horse enters neither a formation nor the remount reserve automatically. It must pass:

```yaml
captured_mount_integration_gates:
  - secure custody
  - ownership spoils ransom or return decision
  - health soundness and maturity inspection
  - temperament assessment
  - intended-role suitability
  - tack armor and equipment compatibility
  - rider compatibility and pairing
  - quarantine where required
  - training and adaptation
  - handler farrier fodder water and quarters capacity
  - route to receiving formation
```

A cavalry formation can therefore lose riders while retaining some horses, lose horses while retaining riders, or lose both. Replacement readiness depends on the limiting layer, not total nominal headcount.

## 17. AI Recruitment Constitution

AI uses the same pools, reservations, costs, diplomacy, religious restrictions, routes, and recovery rules as the player.

```yaml
AI_must_evaluate:
  - role and mission need
  - current and expected threats
  - source availability
  - competing reservations
  - people equipment mounts officers and training
  - realm doctrine and roster strengths
  - retained weaknesses
  - household regional political and religious effects
  - mercenary allied and domestic alternatives
  - route supply duration and recovery
  - expected battlefield use
  - ordinary safe capacity versus emergency overdraw
  - service-distance duration and source-pressure band
  - demobilization return and receipt state
  - garrison assignment and site-support release state
AI_must_not_receive:
  - hidden people
  - hidden recruitment pools
  - hidden horses or equipment
  - automatic diplomatic contingents
  - ignored religious restrictions
  - free replenishment
  - automatic emergency overdraw
  - instant pool refund from departure disbanding or demobilization
  - immunity from recruitment closure
  - reduced costs unavailable to the player under equal rules
```

Higher difficulty should principally improve information use, planning horizon, role composition, source conservation, target selection, timing, and adaptation. It should not rely on invisible resource creation. Lower difficulty may use simpler planning or less efficient choices without violating ownership and conservation.

Realm-specific AI authorities determine how a realm uses the shared rules based on its roster, doctrine, political constraints, regional opportunities, and capability gaps.

## 19. Anti-Exploit Constitution

- One source cannot feed several active consumers simultaneously.
- Disbanding does not convert foreign, household, order, or regional people into a generic realm pool.
- Payment cannot create unavailable personnel, equipment, horses, officers, routes, or willingness.
- Papal favor cannot spawn a ready religious formation.
- Excommunication cannot be bypassed by renaming an order unit as a generic crusader unit.
- A holy-order foundation date cannot be bypassed by a building or technology.
- A generic crusader formation cannot automatically become a named order.
- Captured prisoners do not automatically replenish the victor.
- Captured horses do not instantly become compatible military remounts.
- Battle victory does not heal the wounded, recover the missing, replace officers, or restore cohesion.
- Allied units do not replenish from generic host population.
- Mercenary companies do not become permanent domestic pools when their contract ends.
- AI receives no hidden exemptions from these rules.
- Emergency conditions do not automatically expand the ordinary pool.
- Demobilization, disbanding, garrison departure, or return does not instantly refund all capacity.
- Wounded, captured, missing, deserted, and returned-pending-accounting people are not ordinary available capacity.

## 20. Validation Fixtures

### Positive

- A Breton mounted contingent request fails when Brittany cannot spare riders or horses despite a valid alliance.
- A valid affinity creates a request pathway without creating a Breton pool.
- A Crusader Sergeant formation is considered only when a valid expedition, people, sponsor, equipment, command, route, and religious standing exist.
- A named military order remains unavailable before its validated foundation and institutional presence.
- Excommunication blocks papally authorized service where the religious authority requires standing, while unrelated private troops remain separately evaluated.
- A rider is killed but the horse survives; the horse enters custody and remount review rather than disappearing or instantly joining another unit.
- Captured enemy horses produce potential property and remount capacity only after inspection, ownership, pairing, and training.
- Replacements restore headcount gradually while veteran cohesion, officers, equipment, and rider-mount pairing remain distinct.
- An AI realm hires mercenaries to fill a real capability gap when the contract, market, finance, route, and supply pass.
- Safe recruitment consumes ordinary validated capacity without automatically triggering a major economic penalty.
- Emergency overdraw requires extraordinary authority and produces pressure and recovery receipts.
- A demobilizing formation remains unavailable until people property obligation route and source receipts resolve.
- Garrison release ends the site claim but does not restore pool availability before full accounting.

### Negative

- An alliance unlocks unlimited Breton cavalry.
- A character association unlocks an entire foreign roster.
- A cathedral spawns Templar Sergeants before a validated order exists.
- Papal favor creates soldiers, horses, armor, or officers.
- A religious unit freely attacks prohibited targets without institutional consequences or review.
- Every rider death also deletes the horse regardless of battle outcome.
- Every surviving rider automatically retains a usable horse.
- Captured enemy cavalry converts directly into the victor's cavalry unit.
- A recruitment building replenishes a destroyed elite pool by itself.
- Higher-difficulty AI receives hidden manpower or horse pools.
- Emergency automatically expands ordinary recruitment capacity.
- Demobilization instantly refunds all people equipment officers mounts and support.
- Site departure creates immediate recruitment capacity before return and accounting.
- Wounded captured missing deserted and returned-pending-accounting people count as available.
