## 1. Review Verdict

The 21 declared developed Realms across six Theater groups are compatible with the current shared Military and Battles architecture at the static-design level. The shared portfolio is broad enough to support Realm-specific military identity, finite recruitment, assignment, progression, muster, readiness, campaign operations, Battle transition, Battle consequences, and player-facing Battle Preview explanation without requiring identical Realm package shapes or one Military file per Realm.

Compatibility does not prove that every Realm package currently contains complete current military, presentation, implementation, or Battle-integration files. Exact package-level coverage remains mixed. Several Realms have direct or strongly surfaced Military evidence; several require current package-index and capability mapping; Badajoz retains existing blockers; and Realm files must not be fabricated merely because enterprise search does not surface a dedicated Military authority.
