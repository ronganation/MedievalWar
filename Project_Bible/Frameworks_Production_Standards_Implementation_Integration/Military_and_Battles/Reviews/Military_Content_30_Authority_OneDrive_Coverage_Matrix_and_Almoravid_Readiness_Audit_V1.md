## 12. Final Principle

The 30-item OneDrive package now supplies the shared military-content spine from roster and recruitment through assignment, muster, readiness, maneuver, battle, occupation, siege, raids, information, command, logistics, infrastructure, maritime operations, theater force management, seasonal sustainment, and person-level campaign consequences.

Almoravid authorities should now define how the realm performs those verbs through its own historically grounded sources, institutions, geography, military organization, strategic interfaces, strengths, weaknesses, and development paths.

The package is sufficiently developed to resume Almoravid work. New shared frameworks should be exceptional, evidence-driven additions rather than automatic successors generated from footer chains.
