## 1. Executive Verdict

The Military and Battles family does not have a broad conceptual vacancy comparable to an empty foundation framework. A substantial shared authority portfolio already exists across Military Content, campaign operations, recruitment, readiness, Battle transition, Battle consequence, Standards and Production, strategic integration, implementation schemas, Realm packages, reviews, manifests, and legacy sources.

The primary current gap is one coherent family navigation and ownership layer that resolves current control, version lineage, specialist boundaries, cross-folder references, Realm consumption, Battle integration, player-facing explanation, and evidence status without physically flattening the repository.

One targeted shared interface gap is sufficiently plausible to warrant a dedicated review: Battle Preview, pre-Battle information, Battle commitment explanation, and Military UI. Existing sources provide inputs and presentation hooks, but the current discovery did not verify one complete controlling cross-owner interface authority.
