This benchmark tests whether the shared mercenary framework and regional variation standard can preserve useful Southern Italy and Sicily distinctions using the current, reviewed Project Bible evidence. It is an evidence and owner-routing assessment, not a historical roster, regional authority, content admission, or production approval.

## 1. Benchmark question

Can the shared lifecycle and regional identity rules describe Southern Italian and Sicilian service forms without collapsing contract forces, allied contingents, external service, household retinues, regional recruitment, and commercial circulation into one generic mercenary pool? The answer must be grounded in current owner sources and must preserve all unresolved evidence as unresolved.

## 2. Scope boundary

The test area is Southern Italy, including the Southern Norman package in Apulia and Calabria, and the Sicily Muslim package. The benchmark does not extend conclusions to all Italia, the Mediterranean, or every actor operating in either package.

## 3. Intended use

Use this document to evaluate the shared parent authorities, identify which content concepts are supportable as service-form definitions, record blocked factual portfolios, and recommend one bounded source/owner batch. Do not use it to make or import campaign content.

## 4. Explicit non-goals

This document does not create or promote a unit, company, captain, broker, market, route, contract, price, quantity, recruitment pool, opening force, alliance, or active offer. It does not conduct a source search outside the repository or implement the framework.

## 5. Governing authority hierarchy

The campaign constitution and current source/owner authorities govern. The shared Mercenary Service and Contract Integration Framework and Regional Ecosystem Standard are static owner-review candidates, not runtime authority. Realm and theater owners retain their state; this benchmark neither overrides nor promotes any of them.

## 19. Service-form taxonomy

Maintain separate classes for contracted force, allied contingent, external military service, household retinue, regional recruitment, and civilian commercial activity. These classes may interact, but their legal basis, authority, provider, payment, command, access, and opening-state evidence differ.

## 20. Contracted force

A contract is a relationship requiring parties, authority, scope, term, consideration, command, delivery, breach, and closeout. The [Southern Norman successor authority](../../../../Theaters/Italia/08_Realm_Packages_and_References/Apulia_and_Calabria/05_Military_Logistics_and_Maritime/Southern_Norman_Contract_Forces_Allied_Contingents_and_External_Military_Service_Authority_V1_FULL.md) supports a bounded contract-force service-form authority for design review. It does not prove a particular contract or provider was active on the opening date.

## 21. Allied contingent

An allied contingent remains owned by its source actor and enters another theater only through a supported directed relationship, authorized command arrangement, access, transport, and supply. The sources reviewed do not authorize reclassifying an external allied force as a mercenary unit.

## 22. External military service

External service is a distinct owner-routed relationship, not a regional market. No reviewed source establishes an exact Southern Italy/Sicily provider, direction of service, opening availability, terms, or delivered force.

## 23. Household retinue

Retinues remain attached to the household, character, or political authority that owns them. A follower, guard, or household dependent must not be relabeled a mercenary solely because service is military or compensation may exist.

## 24. Regional recruitment

Recruitment from a place or population is owned by its relevant Realm, population, and military roster authorities. A regional identity does not create a finite pool, consent, skill, equipment, muster, or current availability.

## 25. Commercial and civilian actors

Merchants, brokers, carriers, port officials, scribes, and transport providers may support military movement only through their proper owners and evidenced agreements. Commercial networks are not mercenary providers, and trade access is not military service.

## 29. Role versus identity

A battlefield role may be described at a generic level without asserting a regional identity. No reviewed evidence justifies a unique Southern Italy or Sicilian weapon, formation, combat performance, equipment package, or tactical bonus for a mercenary profile.

## 30. Named companies and captains

No named company, captain, broker, or provider is admitted by the reviewed sources for the opening state. Such names remain quarantined until an exact source supports identity, chronology, location, service relationship, and relevant owner review.

## 31. Markets and brokers

The [Sicily economy authority](../../../../Theaters/Italia/08_Realm_Packages_and_References/Sicily_Muslim/Sicily_Muslim_Economy_Property_Agriculture_Irrigation_Markets_Trade_and_Material_Flow_Authority_V1_FULL.md) recognizes markets, merchants, and networks as economic capability families but expressly says exact opening markets, routes, prices, goods, and stocks were not created. Neither that authority nor the reviewed military sources establishes a mercenary market or broker. No market availability may be inferred from Mediterranean commerce.

## 32. Routes and circulation

The [Sicily geography](../../../../Theaters/Italia/08_Realm_Packages_and_References/Sicily_Muslim/Sicily_Muslim_Strategic_Geography_Settlements_Fortifications_Ports_Routes_and_Regional_Development_Authority_V1_FULL.md) and [maritime](../../../../Theaters/Italia/08_Realm_Packages_and_References/Sicily_Muslim/Sicily_Muslim_Maritime_Forces_Transport_Port_Defense_Coastal_Operations_and_External_Lift_Authority_V1_FULL.md) authorities identify cross-strait and Mediterranean connections as research or operational interfaces while holding exact routes, access, vessels, crews, lift, and capacity. Circulation may direct research; it cannot create a safe route, a provider, transport, or a recruitable force.

## 33. Availability lifecycle

Keep these states distinct: historically documented; locally present; contactable; politically accessible; offerable; negotiated; contracted; mustered; equipped; transported; arrived; accepted; and operational. Evidence for an earlier state does not imply a later one.

## 34. Contract and payment lifecycle

Any future service record must distinguish proposed terms, accepted terms, payment obligation, payment delivery, muster, movement, performance, breach, and closeout. The reviewed sources establish no opening mercenary price, wage, duration, payment source, or delivered service.

## 35. Command and operational control

Origin, employer, contracting authority, tactical commander, political sponsor, and logistics owner may differ. No relationship among these roles is assumed by this benchmark. Command cannot be inferred from faction affiliation, religious identity, or physical presence.

## 36. Identity and naming

Regional naming requires source support for the actual identity claimed. A modern label, translated generic role, place association, language, religion, or later unit tradition is insufficient. Use neutral service-form descriptions until a current owner-admitted source supports a historical name.

## 37. Language, religion, and ethnicity safeguards

Language, religion, ancestry, and community identity create no automatic military skill, political allegiance, contract eligibility, hostility, loyalty, legal status, or combat behavior. The [Sicily population authority](../../../../Theaters/Italia/08_Realm_Packages_and_References/Sicily_Muslim/Sicily_Muslim_Population_Communities_Languages_Institutions_Labor_Mobility_and_Social_Order_Authority_V1_1_Corrected_Integrated_Successor_FULL.md) specifically rejects these deterministic inferences.

## 38. Cross-region duplication test

Do not treat the same generic infantry role under two place names as meaningful regional variation. A distinction must identify evidence for different service basis, provider institution, command, equipment, movement, or political relationship and must not duplicate an existing owner record.

## 39. Meaningful variation test

Regional variation is useful only when it changes a source-supported player decision or state relationship. Differences in prose, color, translated naming, or unverified numerical stats fail. On present evidence, political-center and owner-routing distinctions pass as design boundaries; combat-stat distinctions do not.
