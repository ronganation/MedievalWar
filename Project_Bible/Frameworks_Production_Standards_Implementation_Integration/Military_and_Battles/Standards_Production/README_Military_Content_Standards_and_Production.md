## Purpose

This folder contains the shared authoring, classification, roster-planning, naming, formation, maturity, review, implementation-handoff, and specialist-production standards used to develop Medieval War military content.

The files work together. No individual standard replaces the current shared military-content authority, Realm-specific military authorities, complete Master Unit Profiles, or separately owned implementation authorities. Folder presence, filename recency, a manifest, or a static check does not by itself confer adoption, canonical status, or runtime authority.

## Precedence and Nonduplication

- The current Military Content V1.2 authority remains the parent for shared roster, recruitment, development, and implementation-handoff doctrine.
- Realm-specific military authorities retain Realm identity, provider systems, roster identity, political and institutional context, and Realm-specific expression.
- Master Unit Profiles retain complete unit-family substance and Unit Specialization.
- Shared semantic classes provide reusable meaning but do not create universal loadouts, identical statistics, or cosmetic reskins.
- Faction Military Expressions preserve Realm-specific people, institutions, equipment access, service culture, battlefield rhythm, visual language, recovery, availability, and campaign tradeoffs.
- The roster-authoring standard owns substantive roster construction. The manual-review standard owns anti-churn owner decisions and readable mapping.
- Roster admission, progression vocabulary, recruitment pools, assignment, recovery, regional access, allied service, mercenary service, and religious-order service remain with their framework owners.
- Formation keys reference shared capability definitions; they do not replace the full formation standard.
- Unit Definition Candidates remain noncanonical. Game-Facing Unit Cards derive from Master Unit Profiles and do not replace them.
- Exact numerical balance, canonical admission, Registry, Opening State, importer, runtime, save, migration, and Unreal work require separately authorized gates.

## Normal Military Production Sequence

1. Unit ideation.
2. Substantive Master Unit Profiles.
3. Roster and branch reconciliation.
4. Faction Military Expression Consolidation.
5. Unit binding to shared semantic classes, faction expressions, and unit specializations.
6. Qualitative implementation handoff.
7. Cross-faction and cross-tier comparison.
8. Provisional numerical definition.
9. Battle testing and balancing.
10. Game-Facing Unit Card, art, animation, audio, UI, accessibility, and localization production.
11. Canonical Unreal implementation through separately authorized gates.

This sequence is dependency-aware rather than rigid. Early ideation does not require every later artifact. Existing profiles remain valid substantive sources and do not require immediate mass retrofitting. A roster-authoring checkpoint does not claim numerical, Game-Facing Unit Card, or canonical readiness.
