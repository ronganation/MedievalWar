## 1. Executive Verdict

A real shared integration gap exists, but the evidence does not support creating a new universal owner for every fact commonly described as public order.

Current authorities already own most causal inputs and executable outcomes:

- Province owns regional administration, control, integration, autonomy, devastation, recovery, and Province-level Public Order inputs;
- Population owns demographic and social state, migration, displacement, resettlement, and demographic recovery;
- Law and Policy owns rules, jurisdiction, promulgation, enforcement authority, exemptions, appeals, compliance with specific rules, resistance, reform, repeal, and sunset;
- Political Constitution owns participation, recognition, legitimacy, Government transformation, escalation, and political settlement routing;
- Government owns offices, delegation, administrative capacity, continuity, and lawful action requests;
- Military owners retain garrisons, occupation, suppression attempts, military control, Battle, siege, withdrawal, pursuit, and force outcomes;
- Historical owners retain facts, Evidence, Memory, Momentum, Emergence, grievance reception, and later historical interpretation;
- Information owners retain reports, knowledge, warning, communication, rumor, contradiction, secrecy, and deception;
- Economic and Property owners retain fiscal burden, Property loss, market disruption, reconstruction, material recovery, and owner receipts;
- Religion and institution owners retain religious standing, jurisdiction, community institutions, and tradition-specific conduct.

The missing shared capability is the typed interpretation and routing of public response across those owners. The project needs a common contract distinguishing compliance, accommodation, petition, evasion, noncooperation, passive resistance, organized resistance, disorder, revolt, rebellion candidature, suppression, negotiated settlement, restoration, reconciliation, and durable recovery without reducing all of them to one universal score.

```text

Public Order Is A Derived, Actor-Visible, Multi-Owner Strategic Condition
```

## 17. Player-Facing Requirements

The player should see:

- which communities, institutions, elites, or regions matter;
- what response is currently observed or credibly reported;
- the strongest grievances, demands, stabilizers, and unknowns;
- whether compliance appears voluntary, negotiated, coerced, partial, or unknown;
- whether resistance is passive, organized, violent, or only a candidate;
- why a proposed policy, concession, enforcement action, suppression attempt, or settlement is available;
- which owner will execute the consequence;
- what major risks remain after action.

The player should not manage one hidden attitude score for every person or settlement.
