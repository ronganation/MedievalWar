## 1. Strong Authority Verdict

Medieval War requires a shared integration authority that explains how owner-backed conditions become typed public responses without turning public order into one universal loyalty, unrest, obedience, or rebellion score.

This authority is not a new sole writer for Province state, Population, Law, Government, political legitimacy, military control, Historical Memory, Information, Religion, Economy, Property, or Realm AI. It connects those owners through a bounded public-response lifecycle, routes proposals and consequence requests, preserves uncertainty, and produces concise player-facing explanations.

```text
Owner-Backed Conditions
+
Affected Audiences And Communities
+
Actor-Visible Information
+
Law, Custom, Authority, Capacity, And Conduct
+
Material, Security, Institutional, And Historical Context
=
Typed Public Response Interpretation
```

```yaml
integration_thesis:
  PublicOrderIsDerivedNotSoleOwnedTruth: true
```

## 2. Modern Medieval II Design Promise

### 2.1 Preserve

- clear settlement and Province stability summaries;
- visible leading causes of cooperation, strain, unrest, and recovery;
- meaningful governor, garrison, taxation, cultural, religious, devastation, and occupation consequences;
- decisive strategic choices during revolt, rebellion, occupation, and restoration;
- readable risks without revealing hidden organization or perfect future knowledge;
- faction and regional difference;
- a small number of meaningful player decisions.

### 2.2 Modernize selectively

```yaml
SelectiveInheritance:
  MedievalIIAndMods:
    use:
      - ReadableSettlementAndProvinceStability
      - VisibleReasonsForDiscontent
      - GovernorAndGarrisonInteraction
      - RebellionAsCampaignMapConsequence
      - StrongRegionalDifference
  AttilaAnd1212AD:
    use:
      - OccupationDevastationDisplacementAndRecovery
      - ReligiousInstitutionalAndCulturalPressure
      - ProvincialCrisisAndResettlement
  RomeIIAndDEI:
    use:
      - PopulationCommunityAndMaterialConstraints
      - ProvinceCoordinationWithoutHomogeneousPopulationMood
      - AIRespectForCapacityAndRecovery
  Shogun2:
    use:
      - ConciseCauseSummaries
      - SmallDecisionBudget
      - StrongCrisisReadability
  CK3StyleCausality:
    use:
      - GrievanceAuthorityRecognitionParticipationAndSettlement
      - ActorAndAudienceSpecificLegitimacy
      - ConditionalEscalationAndDeescalation
```

### 2.3 Reject

- one universal Public Order, Loyalty, Obedience, Stability, Fear, or Rebellion score as complete truth;
- instant rebellion at a fixed threshold;
- automatic hostility from cultural or religious difference;
- garrisons as automatic loyalty;
- military control as political legitimacy;
- population loss as pacification;
- suppression as reconciliation;
- Agent missions directly creating public response outcomes;
- Events directly mutating cross-owner state without owner receipts;
- buildings, research, decisions, dates, or scripts creating people, leaders, armed groups, armies, or material capacity;
- identical public-response structures for every Realm and Theater;
- player micromanagement of every petition, rumor, grievance, local incident, or enforcement action.
