## Purpose

The Realm Development Framework defines the official method for creating, modernizing, completing, implementing, validating, and freezing every realm in Medieval War.

The objective is not to create factions.

The objective is to create living medieval political ecosystems that remain playable as Total War campaigns.

## Core Philosophy

A realm is not:

- a territory
- a ruler
- a faction bonus
- a roster
- a personality score
- a dynasty
- a collection of character biographies

A realm is:

```text
History
+
Identity

+

+

+

+

+

+
```

working through a campaign state.

## Core Promise

```text
Deep simulation internally.

Selective exposure externally.

Clear strategic consequences.
```

## Medieval II and Total War Heritage Rule

Every realm must remain legible through:

- map position
- settlements
- provinces
- armies
- recruitment
- diplomacy
- succession
- institutions
- strategic objectives

Character and political depth must strengthen those systems rather than replace them.

## Selective Inheritance Test

Every borrowed mechanic must answer:

1. Does it create meaningful strategic choice?
2. Does it affect the campaign map, armies, settlements, diplomacy, succession, institutions, or territory?
3. Can AI use it?
4. Can the player understand the result?
5. Is the workload justified?
6. Does it preserve historical emergence rather than scripting history?

If not, simplify, defer, or reject it.

- disputed claims
- uncertain ages
- unclear appointments
- contested recognition

A realm fails if the player spends more time maintaining internal systems than commanding:

- armies
- settlements
- provinces
- diplomacy
- succession
- institutions

Normal target:

- one primary realm objective
- zero to two supporting objectives
- limited major political prompts per turn
- summary-first UI

- decision inputs
- strategic output

- constraints

- deterministic processing

## 1. V3 Purpose

The Realm Development Framework defines the project-wide method for creating, researching, modernizing, completing, reviewing, validating, freezing, and handing off Realm packages without confusing documentation maturity with active Campaign state or implementation evidence.

The framework exists to ensure that every Realm package:

- remains a legible Total War campaign entity;
- represents a living medieval political ecosystem;
- preserves historical uncertainty and divergent outcomes;
- covers strategically necessary capabilities without symmetry-only files;
- routes every mutable state to its constitutional owner;
- connects people, Dynasties, institutions, Government, Geography, Population, military capacity, Supply, Objectives, and AI without creating one monolithic Realm object;
- protects Opening State and advanced saves;
- advances toward Unreal Engine only through explicit owner gates and evidence.

### 7.6 Read-Only Implementation Discovery

Maps approved semantic contracts against verified live-project architecture without source or asset mutation.

### 7.7 Implementation Pass

Occurs only after static owner closure, read-only discovery, synthetic fixture validation, rollback preparation, and explicit promotion authorization.
