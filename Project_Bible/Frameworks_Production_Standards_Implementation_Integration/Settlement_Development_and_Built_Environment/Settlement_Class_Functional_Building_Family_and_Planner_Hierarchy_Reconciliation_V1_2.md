## 1. Reconciliation Verdict

Medieval War retains two primary settlement progression bases as its shared strategic spine:

```yaml
PrimarySettlementClasses:
  - TownAndCivic
  - CastleAndFortifiedCenter
```

The fifteen shared functional building families operate inside, beside, or through those settlement classes. They do not replace the settlement classes and do not become fifteen peer settlement types.

```text
Settlement class answers what kind of strategic settlement this primarily is.
Settlement stage answers how far that class has developed.
Settlement expression answers the locally meaningful subtype or configuration.
Functional building family answers what capability area is being developed.
Building branch answers how that function is emphasized.
Realm expression answers how the function is historically and institutionally realized here.
Opening State answers what actually exists on 1 January 1062.
Runtime state answers what exists, operates, changes, or fails during play.
```

This file is the current cross-document hierarchy authority and reconciles the corrected authority family. It does not create a new settlement framework, universal building tree, exact Realm portfolio, opening settlement, building instance, recruitment pool, runtime payload, or Unreal implementation.

- broad construction choice;
- strong recruitment, economic, religious, defensive, and civic relationships;
- the need to convert or adapt captured settlement structures when strategically useful.

Modernize through:

- subtypes and hybrid forms;
- regional and Realm expressions;
- attached and outlying sites;
- finite labor, materials, rights, maintenance, and throughput;
- damage, mothballing, conversion, repair, and recovery;
- active planner versus full atlas separation;
- live state, uncertainty, accessibility, filters, and focused comparison.

Reject:

- one universal tree;
- pure faction reskins;
- tiny fixed slots as the universal constraint;
- instant free conversion;
- buildings that create people, pools, horses, ships, equipment, goods, or authority;
- fifteen building families presented as fifteen primary settlement types.

## 21. Conformance Rules for Norway and Future Realms

A Realm portfolio conforms when:

1. all primary settlements declare Town and Civic, Castle and Fortified Center, or an approved hybrid relationship;
2. local expressions map beneath or between those classes;
3. attached sites, independent nodes, networks, and temporary sites remain relationship classes rather than peer primary classes;
4. building definitions map to shared functional families or prove a substantive new gap;
5. local names and appearance do not create duplicate mechanics automatically;
6. recruitment support remains an interface rather than troop creation;
7. definitions remain separate from opening instances and runtime state;
8. the active planner and complete atlas use different information density.
