## 1. Purpose

This authority governs how siege engines and artillery enter Medieval War. It preserves the readable Medieval II progression from familiar mechanical engines toward stronger counterweight, gunpowder, rocket, and monumental weapons while replacing universal building unlocks with historically grounded access to engineers, artisans, timber, rope, metal, animals, powder, ammunition, transport, finance, and political or commercial connections.

A technology, date, building, culture, institution, reform, or decision may unlock eligibility. None of those creates crews, machines, foundries, powder, shot, animals, transport, or recruitment pools by itself.

This authority controls the common framework. Individual profiles still control the identity, operating principle, battlefield role, provider relationships, faction access, campaign availability, and implementation requirements of each engine family.

The most important refinement is terminological and structural: **Catapult is not automatically a separate third machine between Mangonel and Trebuchet.** Medieval terminology is inconsistent, and modern usage often treats catapult as a broad category. A separate Catapult card survives only if later research establishes a distinct mechanism, provider base, tactical purchase, art direction, and historical niche. This prevents Medieval War from inventing a duplicate stone-thrower merely to reproduce a vanilla progression ladder.

## 5. Development Families

### 5.1 Common Mechanical Foundation

```yaml
families:
  - BallistaOrDefensibleSiegeCrossbow
  - TractionTrebuchetOrMangonel
```

These are the first shared families to investigate and develop. They may appear across multiple factions through local construction, inherited engineering practice, urban specialists, military artisans, contracted engineers, technical exchange, or imported expertise. Shared access does not require identical names, visuals, crews, ammunition, handling, cost, or performance.

The Ballista family provides direct-fire bolt projection. The Traction Trebuchet or Mangonel family provides human-powered arcing stone or authorized payload projection. Their target sets, deployment needs, crew behavior, logistical burdens, and battlefield geometry must remain distinct.

### 5.2 Advanced Mechanical Siege

```yaml
families:
  - CounterweightTrebuchet
  - RegionalHeavyTrebuchet
  - SpecialistIncendiaryOrSiegePayloadEngine
  - DefensibleDistinctStoneThrower
```

Counterweight trebuchets require a stronger engineering, logistics, finance, timber, rope, labor, transport, siege-command, and ammunition base than lighter mechanical engines. Large examples may be constructed or assembled for a siege campaign rather than maintained as ordinary permanent field units.

A Defensible Distinct Stone Thrower is a reserved slot, not an approved unit. It exists only to accommodate future evidence for a regional mechanism or tactically distinct machine that is not adequately represented by the traction or counterweight families.

### 5.3 Early Gunpowder Artillery

```yaml
families:
  - EarlyBombard
  - LightOrFieldGun
  - DefensiveMultiBarrelGun
  - EarlyMortar
```

Gunpowder opens a new development channel rather than instantly replacing mechanical artillery. Access requires powder supply, suitable metalworking or foundry capability, trained gunners, appropriate shot, transport, safe storage, maintenance, and institutional willingness to make sustained investment.

### 5.4 Mature and Specialist Gunpowder Artillery

```yaml
families:
  - CulverinAndLongGun
  - SerpentineAndLightAntiPersonnelGun
  - MatureMortar
  - HeavyBombard
  - BasiliskOrComparableLongHeavyGun
```

These families require improving metallurgy, construction or casting practice, ammunition and powder preparation, trained crews, stronger carriages, transport animals, workshops, finance, and replacement capacity. They are not distributed merely because a faction survives into a later date.

### 5.5 Exceptional and Regional Artillery

```yaml
families:
  - GrandOrMonsterBombard
  - RocketLauncherOrRocketFrame
  - FactionSpecificSiegeEngine
  - CampaignBuiltMonumentalEngine
```

Exceptional weapons require a specific institutional, regional, dynastic, commercial, technical, military, or historical foundation. They are not universal capstone units. A monumental bombard or rocket system must justify production, provider access, ammunition, specialist crews, transport, cost, reliability, deployment, and battlefield limitations.

## 7. Operational Forms

Siege machinery does not always enter a campaign as a finished permanent battlefield card. Profiles may use one or more operational forms.

```yaml
operational_forms:
  field_train_engine:
    description: TransportableMachineOrPreparedComponentsAccompanyingAnArmy
  campaign_built_engine:
    description: EngineConstructedOrSubstantiallyAssembledNearTheTarget
  garrison_engine:
    description: DefensiveMachineMaintainedAtAQualifiedFortificationOrCity
  arsenal_reserve:
    description: StoredComponentsToolsAndSpecialistsRequiringMobilization
  contracted_engineering_train:
    description: TemporaryAccessThroughHiredOrAlliedSpecialists
  naval_or_port_engine:
    description: SeparateFutureUseRequiringSpecificMaritimeAuthority
```

Individual profiles must identify allowed operational forms. A garrison engine does not automatically become a mobile field unit, and access to engineers does not automatically produce a complete engine at the army's location.

## 9. Faction and Regional Access

```yaml
access_channels:
  - IndigenousEngineeringTradition
  - UrbanCraftAndCommercialNetwork
  - RoyalOrPrincelyArsenal
  - CastleAndFortificationWorks
  - MercenaryEngineerContract
  - AlliedSubjectOrVassalTechnicalTransfer
  - MaritimeArsenalAndPortAccess
  - CapturedOrImportedEquipment
  - HistoricalEmergenceOrConditionalInvestment
  - CampaignSpecificConstructionProgram
```

Many factions may eventually obtain common mechanical engines when real access exists. Not every faction receives every advanced engine. Shared engines may differ through crew quality, reliability, cost, construction speed, transport burden, ammunition, range bands, accuracy, deployment, recruitment depth, and replacement capacity.

Technically connected states may gain stronger or earlier channels, but no faction receives superiority merely from its label. Each advantage must be expressed through actual providers, institutions, contacts, investments, and campaign systems.

Captured equipment does not automatically confer reproduction capability. Continued access requires compatible ammunition, trained crews, maintenance knowledge, spare parts, transport, and political control.

```yaml
common_limitations:

  - RestrictedFiringArcsOrLineOfSightAsApplicable
```

Artillery must create a combined-arms obligation. Engines should require screening, suitable terrain and placement, command attention, protected ammunition, and withdrawal or abandonment decisions. They must not function as self-sufficient super-units.

Exact dates and regional availability belong in family and faction profiles after historical review. The framework rejects one universal discovery event that grants identical artillery to every faction.

## 13. Gunpowder Transition Boundary

Gunpowder work is now represented by completed Early, Mature, and Exceptional master profiles and frameworks. The following entry requirements continue to govern every later faction, regional, institutional, scenario, and named-asset binding.

```yaml
gunpowder_entry_requires:
  - HistoricallyDefensibleDateAndContactWindow
  - PowderKnowledgeAndSupply
  - QualifiedMetalworkingConstructionOrFoundryRoute
  - TrainedGunnersAndSafetyPractice
  - ShotAndAmmunitionPreparation
  - CarriageTransportAndDraftCapacity
  - StorageMaintenanceAndReplacement
  - FactionOrRegionalProviderAuthority
gunpowder_does_not_require:
  - RemovalOfAllMechanicalEngines
  - UniversalFactionAccess
  - IdenticalCannonProgression
  - AutomaticGrandBombardCapstone
```

Bombards, light guns, multi-barrel weapons, mortars, culverins, serpentines, heavy guns, and monumental bombards should become separate branches when their mechanisms, providers, target sets, and historical access justify them.

## 14. Admission Gate for Each Unit

A siege or artillery profile must establish:

- Historical and regional plausibility.
- A named engine or defensible player-facing family name.
- A controlled mechanical or gunpowder identity.
- Its battlefield and siege purchase.
- Its allowed operational forms.
- Crew composition and engineering supervision.
- Ammunition and supply requirements.
- Construction, procurement, contracting, capture, or import route.
- Transport, deployment, repair, abandonment, and recovery burden.
- Recruitment pool and provider constraints.
- Faction and regional access rules.
- Temporal and world-state access conditions.
- Relationship to predecessor, parallel, and successor engines.
- AI-readable strengths, weaknesses, targets, firing conditions, and deployment behavior.
- Art, audio, effects, animation, scale, crew-interaction, damage-state, and destruction needs.
- Clear nonduplication against every adjacent artillery family.
