## 3. Purpose and exact scope

This authority defines how Medieval War represents knowledge, inquiry, patronage, research attention, sponsored development, institutional adoption, reform, hybridization, rejection, progress over time, regional realization, and Realm-specific development portfolios.

It owns shared development vocabulary, lifecycle, authoring contracts, lens and subdomain classification, progress and condition semantics, cross-owner feasibility routing, opportunity-cost doctrine, conformance requirements, AI proposal boundaries, and player-facing explanation requirements.

It does not own people, population, money, property, goods, equipment, horses, ships, buildings, institutions, offices, relationships, agreements, routes, territorial control, training state, units, recruitment pools, Opening State facts, mutable runtime values, or save state.

## 8. Four lenses and their distinct roles

The four lenses are useful precisely because they answer different strategic questions. They may overlap on an individual development, but they should not be collapsed into one broad category.

```yaml
LensQuestions:
  Military: HowCanTheRealmRaiseOrganizeEquipCommandSustainAndUseForce
  Civic: HowCanCommunitiesSettlementsInstitutionsAndPublicServicesFunctionAndRecover
  Economic: HowCanLandLaborResourcesProductionMarketsCreditTradeAndTransportSupportCapacity
  Statecraft: HowCanAuthorityRelationshipsInformationPolicyLegitimacyAndCoordinationDirectTheRealm
```

### 8.2 Civic lens and subdomains

Civic remains distinct from Economic because Civic concerns collective function, public capacity, settlement services, institutional continuity, law-facing local administration, community organization, and recovery. Economic development concerns production, exchange, finance, material capacity, and movement of goods.

## 10. Research, progress, effort, and time

Research is the allocation of attention, patronage, investigation, coordination, experimentation, teaching, record keeping, negotiation, inspection, or reform effort. Progress should normally advance through a combination of time and satisfied conditions rather than through a single abstract currency alone.

```yaml
DevelopmentProgressInputs:
  required_or_supporting_as_defined:
    - Time
    - SponsorContinuity
    - InstitutionalActivity
    - QualifiedPeopleAndSpecialists
    - FundingAndMaterialSupport
    - KnowledgeQualityAndAccess
    - LocalSecurityAndPoliticalSupport
    - RelevantBuildingsSitesAndFacilities
    - RepeatedPracticeInspectionOrExperience
    - ExternalContactOrAgreementWhereRequired
PossibleProgressEffects:
  accelerate:
    - StrongSponsorAndStableMandate
    - MultipleQualifiedProviders
    - AppropriateFacilities
    - ReliableFundingMaterialsAndRoutes
    - RepeatedSuccessfulUse
    - SupportingAlliesInstitutionsOrNetworks
  delay:
    - WarDamageOrOccupation
    - MissingSpecialists
    - PoliticalOpposition
    - SupplyOrFundingFailure
    - SecrecyContradictionOrPoorKnowledge
    - CompetingProgramsUsingSameCapacity
  pause_or_block:
    - RequiredOwnerRefusal
    - LostAccess
    - InstitutionClosed
    - ExternalOwnerLock
    - CriticalUnknown
  degrade:
    - SpecialistLoss
    - InstitutionalCollapse
    - EquipmentOrFacilityDecay
    - LongDisuse

Rules:
  building_is_relevant_condition_not_progress_factory: true
  event_may_change_conditions_not_grant_capability_directly: true
  player_action_may_improve_inputs_not_bypass_owner: true
  exact_progress_formula_authorized: false

```

A player can improve progress by creating or protecting the conditions that make a program viable, such as sponsoring a workshop, securing an agreement, funding stores, maintaining roads, protecting specialists, supporting a school, retaining veterans, improving records, or stabilizing a region. Some actions may accelerate progress, reduce risk, unlock a missing condition, or improve regional realization. Other actions may impose costs elsewhere.

## 11. Small developments, bundles, and usefulness

Not every useful development warrants a full standalone node. Small improvements should matter without bloating the portfolio.

```yaml
DevelopmentScale:
  FoundationalProgram:
    meaning: BroadInstitutionalOrRealmDirectionSupportingMultipleCapabilities
  MajorDevelopment:
    meaning: DistinctStrategicChoiceWithVisibleCostsAndSeveralEffects
  SpecialistDevelopment:
    meaning: BoundedCapabilityOrRegionalInstitutionalImprovement
  PracticeOrProcedure:
    meaning: SmallerImprovementBestNestedWithinProgramOrLineage
  AssignmentOrStandard:
    meaning: OwnerAppliedConfigurationNotSeparateTechnology
BundlingRules:
  tiny_modifier_only_nodes: discouraged
  small_development_allowed_when:
    - PlayerCanUnderstandItsPurpose
    - ItChangesARealDecisionOrBlocker
    - ItSupportsAVisibleLineageOrRegionalDifference
    - ItHasARealCostRequirementOrTradeoff
  bundle_when:
    - SeparateChoiceWouldBeTrivial
    - EffectsShareTheSameSponsorInstitutionAndInputs
    - NoDistinctCounterplayOrPlayerDecisionExists
  retain_separate_when:
    - DifferentRegionsCanRealizeDifferently
    - DifferentOwnersOrInstitutionsAreRequired
    - MeaningfulCompetitionOrAlternativeExists
    - DistinctFactionIdentityOrGameplayRoleExists
```
