## 1. Purpose

This assessment determines whether Medieval War currently possesses a single controlling technology, research, adoption, reform, and faction-development authority; maps the visible ownership already held by current shared and Realm authorities; preserves useful preliminary design; identifies material gaps; and decides whether a bounded additive successor workstream is justified.

It does not design a final technology tree, assign exact technologies, create research costs or turn times, allocate stable IDs, define opening selections, activate effects, generate equipment or personnel, change Realm packages, or authorize Unreal implementation.

```text
Preliminary Note != Controlling Authority
Military Development Coverage != Complete Cross-Domain Technology System
Current-State Assessment != New Runtime Owner
Research Option != People Equipment Institution Or Unit
Knowledge != Adoption
Adoption != Regional Realization
Regional Realization != Fielded Capability
Framework Gap != Permission To Duplicate Existing Owners
Static Successor Justified != Exact Tree Authorized
```

## 17. Stop conditions

Stop successor work if:

- a newer current technology or Realm-development owner is discovered;
- complete training, economy, production, institution, governance, diplomacy, knowledge, event, AI, presentation, or save ownership cannot be routed;
- the draft duplicates current owner state;
- the four lenses are treated as new mutable-state owners;
- technology directly creates people, equipment, institutions, loyalty, agreements, or formations;
- Realm-specific evidence is generalized into mandatory symmetry;
- unavailable capabilities cannot explain decisive blockers;
- exact values would be fabricated;
- implementation is proposed without approved definitions, IDs, schemas, owner transactions, persistence, migration, rollback, and retained evidence.
