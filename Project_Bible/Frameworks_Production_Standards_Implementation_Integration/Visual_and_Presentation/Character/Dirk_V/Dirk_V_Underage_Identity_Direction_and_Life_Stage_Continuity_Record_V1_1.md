## 4. Persistent Identity Direction

Dirk requires an independent synthetic identity. Gertrude may inform broad dynastic continuity, but Dirk must not be produced through literal parent-face blending or by shrinking an adult face.

```yaml
persistent_identity_controls:
  - ChildFaceProportionFamily
  - EyeSpacingAndBrowFamily
  - NoseDevelopmentDirection
  - JawAndChinDevelopmentDirection
  - HairlineAndHairGrowthFamily
  - RecognizableSilhouette
  - PermanentMarksIfLaterAcquired
  - BaselineChildLifeStage
not_fixed_yet:
  - ExactAdultFace
  - ExactAdultHeight
  - ExactAdultBuild
  - AdultBeardPattern
  - AdultCommandAppearance
  - AdultVoice
```

### Identity anchors

- broad, readable child facial proportions rather than a miniature adult skull;
- stable eye spacing and brow relationship capable of maturing gradually;
- a nose and jaw direction that can develop forward without an abrupt face replacement;
- age-appropriate hairline and hair texture;
- a silhouette distinguishable from adult advisers and household officers;
- natural surface treatment without beauty-filter smoothing or heroic scarring.
