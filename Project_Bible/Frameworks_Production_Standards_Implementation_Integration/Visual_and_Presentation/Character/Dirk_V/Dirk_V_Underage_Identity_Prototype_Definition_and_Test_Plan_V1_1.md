## 2. Prototype Question

The prototype must answer one primary question:

> Can Medieval War establish one age-appropriate, repeatable opening identity for an underage hereditary count that remains clearly separate from Gertrude, reads correctly at compact and expanded sizes, and provides reliable anchors for a later life-stage continuity study?

```yaml
primary_question: PersistentOpeningChildIdentity
secondary_questions:
  - CompactReadability
  - ChildBodyScale
  - ClearIdentitySeparationFromGertrude
  - GuardianWardCompatibilityForLaterJointUse
  - StaticFallbackSuitability
not_required_for_static_prototype:
  - LivingPortrait
  - LaterLifeStageAsset
  - AdultRulerAsset
  - Voice
  - RuntimeIntegration
```

## 4. Opening Identity-Master Purpose

The opening master establishes:

- Dirk's synthetic persistent identity;
- child-appropriate facial and body proportions;
- a broad opening child read without an exact age claim;
- a separate identity from Gertrude;
- protected-minor and hereditary-count compatibility;
- restrained comital-household status;
- suitability for compact cards and an expanded panel;
- a source for future living motion or life-stage continuity only if separately authorized.

The opening master does not establish:

- adult command;
- personal government;
- a guaranteed adult personality;
- armor or battlefield presence;
- marriage status;
- a complete age-progression portfolio;
- exact household staff or residence.

## 11. Compact Crop Plan

### 128-pixel test

The 128-pixel crop should preserve:

- complete face;
- hair silhouette;
- upper shoulders and mantle;
- enough garment color to separate Dirk from adult household figures.

### 64-pixel test

The 64-pixel crop should preserve:

- child face mass;
- hair silhouette;
- key garment color block;
