## 2. Refined Executive Decision

Candidate V1 contains a strong synthetic identity foundation and a workable mature political-authority presentation. The complete portrait is not accepted because the combined palette, pose, lighting, framing, harbor-adjacent background, fastening, trim, and Christian pendant repeat too much of the established Harald portrait grammar.

Candidate V1's face and practical veil category should remain the identity source for Candidate V2. Candidate V2 must establish a distinct Holland-regency grammar without turning Gertrude into a crowned sovereign, battlefield commander, decorative consort, or modern photographic subject.

## 4. Harald Template Similarity Review

The issue is cumulative repetition, not the isolated use of blue, red, gold, a brooch, or a three-quarter portrait.

| Element | Harald prototype grammar | Gertrude Candidate V1 | Candidate V2 correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palette | Deep blue mantle, deep red garment, strong gold trim | Nearly the same primary arrangement | Shift toward slate, blue-gray, soft green-gray, natural linen, muted madder, brown-red, and dull ochre |
| Pose | Three-quarter view toward image-right | Closely repeated | Use near-frontal or modest image-left orientation |
| Lighting | Warm interior source and cool maritime opening | Similar split | Use diffuse winter window light with restrained warm bounce |
| Background | Dark hall and harbor or water opening | Similar hall and waterfront structure | Use a quiet household-government chamber with only a low-detail river, wetland, or winter opening |
| Fastening | Round gold-toned shoulder brooch | Similar prominent round brooch | Use a smaller aged-silver or dark-bronze penannular or plain radial fastener |
| Christian marker | Hanging cross pendant | Similar central pendant hierarchy | Remove the pendant or integrate a tiny cross-form detail |
| Trim | Strong gold decorative borders | Similar high-contrast borders | Use one narrow low-contrast woven edge |
| Framing | Close cinematic bust | Similar bust structure | Use a slightly wider head-and-upper-torso composition with one controlled hand and optional document |

Candidate V2 should differ through composition and hierarchy, not merely recolor the same template.

## 7. Veil and Headwear Review

The pale linen veil category works. The dark, gold-patterned forehead band reads too much like metal regalia and echoes Harald's circlet hierarchy.

Candidate V2 should use:

- pale or natural linen veil;
- narrow woven cloth band, stitched edge, or plain cloth fillet;
- no jeweled, engraved, or metallic circlet effect;
- no crown hierarchy;
- no tall fantasy or later-period structured headdress.

```yaml

forehead_band: REDESIGN

```

## 8. Mantle, Garment, Trim, and Fastening Review

Candidate V1's garments are clearly layered, but their combined navy, dark red, strong gold trim, and round gold-toned brooch reproduce the Harald template too closely.

Candidate V2 should use:

- muted slate-blue, blue-gray, weathered teal, or soft green-gray mantle;
- muted madder, warm brown-red, dull ochre, or dark natural-wool gown;
- pale linen neckline;
- one small aged-silver or dark-bronze penannular or plain radial fastener;
- one narrow low-contrast woven edge;
- no high-contrast vertical gold border.


## 9. Christian Marker Review

The hanging cross is not inherently invalid, but its central placement repeats Harald's portrait hierarchy and competes with the desired regency identity.

Preferred V2 treatment:

1. omit the hanging pendant; or
2. use a tiny cross-form detail integrated into the fastening or neckline.

```yaml
hanging_pendant_required: false
preferred_treatment: NoneOrTinyIntegratedCrossFormDetail
faith_marker_priority: Tertiary
```

## 10. Document and Regency Context

The sealed document is useful because it communicates correspondence and government activity. Candidate V1 includes too much competing foreground detail through the scroll, book stack, table fittings, jewelry, and background.

Candidate V2 should use:

- one optional folded or rolled sealed document;
- one visible hand resting on or holding the document;
- one plain writing surface;
- no large book stack;
- no ornate inkwell focal point;
- no readable writing;
- no large banner or tapestry device.

```yaml
document_context: RETAIN_AS_OPTIONAL_SECONDARY_ELEMENT
visible_hand: One
book_stack: Remove
foreground_clutter: Reduce
readable_text: prohibited
```

The face and veil silhouette remain the primary recognition anchors.

## 11. Background Review

Candidate V1 contains a banner-like dark field with a large gold animal form, candles, carved interior detail, and a waterfront settlement with a vessel. These elements introduce heraldic risk and repeat Harald's hall-and-harbor presentation.

Candidate V2 should use:

- quiet plaster, timber, or restrained textile surfaces;
- no animal-shaped motif;
- no heraldic field;
- diffuse winter light from a narrow opening;
- at most a low-detail river, wetland, or low settlement suggestion;
- no prominent ship, harbor skyline, castle silhouette, or exact residence claim.

```yaml
current_banner_like_motif: REMOVE
current_harbor_emphasis: REMOVE_OR_STRONGLY_REDUCE
v2_background: QuietHouseholdGovernmentChamber
exact_residence_claimed: false
```
