## 6. Christian Marker Review

The hanging cross is smaller than the earliest court candidate but remains too prominent for this restrained derivative. The chain and pendant occupy a central, high-contrast area and compete with the face, fastening, and garment color masses.

Preferred V2 treatment:

1. use a substantially smaller and simpler cross on a finer chain; or
2. remove the hanging pendant and use a small cross-form fastening or garment detail.

## 7. Brooch and Mantle Fastening Review

The round brooch is too large and contains a dense central motif that can be read as an emblem. No personal, household, dynastic, or realm device is authorized by this image.

Candidate V2 should use a smaller round or penannular fastening with radial geometry, restrained interlace, or a plain raised-boss pattern. The mantle should converge into one readable fastening point without secondary chains, pins, or clasps.

## 11. Character-Presentation Direction

This Harald workstream now supports three major static visual surfaces:

```yaml

  - CourtOrAuthority
  - CampaignOrCommand
  - CouncilTravelOrDiplomacy
```

This three-surface set is appropriate for Harald as the first major opening character and as an identity-system prototype. It should not automatically become a universal requirement for every character.

Provisional reusable direction:

- every implemented character needs a persistent identity source;
- major opening characters may justify one or more role derivatives;
- minor or later-emerging characters may use fewer static assets;
- role, office, and faction-leader changes should reuse the persistent identity;
- the eventual live UE5 character should carry most wardrobe, office, and state variation.

These points are implementation and framework inputs, not a final project-wide portrait-count authority.
