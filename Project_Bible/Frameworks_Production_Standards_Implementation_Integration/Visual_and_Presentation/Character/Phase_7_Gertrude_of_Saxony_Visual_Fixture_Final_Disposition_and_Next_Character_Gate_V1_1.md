- One identity master can be sufficient.
- A political-government character does not require campaign or travel derivatives by default.

- Office and role status belong mainly in UI and later live-model state.
- Scenic portrait iteration stops when the identity function is satisfied.
- Compact and expanded crops derive from the accepted master.

## 6. Why Underage Characters Are the Next Meaningful Comparison

The completed fixtures cover:

- a mature ruler and military commander;
- an adult political woman, regent, guardian, and household-government principal.

An underage ruler would test a different problem:

- age-appropriate identity;
- legal authority without adult capacity;
- guardian, regent, household, and protector separation;
- visual growth without an instant adult swap;
- compact presentation of a strategically important child;
- future living-portrait and age-progression continuity.

A third fixture is still not automatic. The comparison must determine whether an underage visual master is needed now or should wait for the later age-progression prototype.
