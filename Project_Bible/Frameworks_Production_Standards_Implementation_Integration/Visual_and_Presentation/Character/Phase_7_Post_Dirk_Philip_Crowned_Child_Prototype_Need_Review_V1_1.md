## 2. Executive Decision

**Philip I still adds distinct framework value, but only a bounded crowned-child prototype definition is authorized next.**

Dirk successfully answered the general underage identity question:

- a child can have a repeatable persistent identity;
- child neck, shoulder, and upper-torso proportions can remain legible;
- the identity can survive 128-pixel, 64-pixel, and expanded-panel presentation;
- protected status and legal importance can be presented without adult command;
- static fallback art can close without living animation or a full age portfolio.

> Can Medieval War layer recognized kingship, a child-scaled crown, ceremonial regalia, and royal UI treatment over an underage persistent identity without visually granting adult executive capacity?

- Philip remains Philip when traveling, ill, captive, resting, studying, or outside ceremony;
- the crown belongs to office and context;
- a permanent crown would make compact and household presentation inflexible;
- one persistent identity must survive future life-stage and role changes.
