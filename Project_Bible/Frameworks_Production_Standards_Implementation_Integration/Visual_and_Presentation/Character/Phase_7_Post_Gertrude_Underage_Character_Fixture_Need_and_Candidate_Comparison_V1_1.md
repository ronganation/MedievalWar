## 2. Executive Decision

**Do not create a third polished visual fixture now.**

Dirk V and Philip I both provide excellent underage-character design tests, but the shared framework has already established the required visual architecture:

- one persistent identity;
- age-appropriate composed presentation;
- no adult armor or command at opening;
- bounded life-stage progression;
- static compact cards;
- optional expanded living portrait later;
- no birthday switch into adult competence.

The next safe action is to create **identity-direction records**, not portraits, for Dirk and Philip. These records should define each child's visual thesis, opening age uncertainty, life-stage continuity, prohibited adult presentation, and future identity-generation rules. The actual identity masters should wait until the age-progression and living-portrait prototype gate opens or until a concrete first-slice UI implementation needs the asset.


## 3. Why an Underage Fixture Is Valuable but Premature

An underage ruler tests problems not covered by Harald or Gertrude:

- legal authority without adult capacity;
- age-appropriate scale, clothing, posture, and voice;
- guardian, regent, custodian, educator, and protector separation;
- child identity that remains recognizable through adolescence and adulthood;
- ceremonial presence without battlefield command;
- dynamic maturity by domain rather than a universal birthday switch.

However, generating a polished child portrait now would answer only the easiest part, visual appearance. It would not validate the harder system problems: age morph continuity, household-bound presentation, dynamic office state, living-portrait stability across life stages, or transition into adult rule.

```yaml
underage_system_value: HIGH
immediate_scenic_portrait_value: LOW_TO_MEDIUM
identity_direction_value: HIGH
prototype_value_after_age_system_definition: VERY_HIGH
```

## 4. Dirk V Authority Findings

Dirk is Holland's living underage hereditary count and principal claim bearer. Dirk is the legal center of Holland's minority government, while Gertrude and the household perform the practical burdens of government. Dirk has no opening army command, independent diplomacy, adult government, marriage agency, or independent household. citeturn58search9

The authority reports a birth around 1052 or 1054 while preserving the exact opening age as unresolved. At the January 1062 opening, that supports a broad child range of approximately seven to ten without authorizing a precise age. citeturn58search9

```yaml
candidate: DirkV
realm: Holland
opening_archetype: ProtectedMinorAndHereditaryClaimPrincipal
opening_role: UnderageHereditaryCountUnderGertrudesRegency
exact_opening_age: Unknown
approximate_visual_range: ChildApproximatelySevenToTenWithoutExactClaim
opening_command: None
identity_priority: High
visual_asset_urgency: Medium
```

### Dirk's strongest framework value

- Direct mother-and-ward continuity with the completed Gertrude identity.
- A child count who must remain visible and politically meaningful.
- Age-appropriate ceremony, education, petition observation, and guarded travel.
- Future growth into several possible ruler types rather than a predetermined military restorer.
- High value for a later child-to-adult identity-continuity prototype.

### Dirk's main risk

Dirk is closely connected to the just-completed Gertrude workflow. Creating Dirk immediately could continue Holland portrait production because the realm is already active rather than because the project needs a third visual asset now.

## 5. Philip I Authority Findings

Philip is France's crowned underage realm-anchor ruler. Philip is already king and a central Capetian continuity figure, but coronation and recognition do not create adult government, independent army command, unrestricted travel, marriage policy, or universal household control. citeturn58search10

Philip's authority emphasizes that Philip is neither an inert ward nor an adult monarch in a smaller body. The presentation problem is to show genuine kingship and sacred recognition while preserving child scale, protected status, supervised participation, and incomplete personal capacity. citeturn58search10

### Philip's strongest framework value

- Distributed minority government rather than a simple one-regent model.
- Strong test of crown, office, UI frame, and wardrobe as dynamic presentation layered over a child identity.

## 12. Age-Progression Prototype Gate

Age progression should not be prototyped by generating a random child picture and an unrelated adult picture.

```yaml
required_before_age_progression_prototype:
  - AcceptedIdentityDirection
  - DefinedPersistentFacialAnchors
  - DefinedLifeStageSequence
  - DefinedWardrobeAndOfficeStateSeparation
  - DefinedTemporaryConditionSeparation
  - LivingPortraitOrLiveModelTestPlan
  - StaticFallbackPlan
```

The first age-progression prototype should use one selected underage character across at least:

- opening child or protected-minor stage;
- participating minor or adolescent stage where needed;
- transitioning ruler or young adult stage;
- adult ruler stage.

Only the opening stage requires immediate campaign availability. Later stages are conditional outputs of biological progression and world state.
