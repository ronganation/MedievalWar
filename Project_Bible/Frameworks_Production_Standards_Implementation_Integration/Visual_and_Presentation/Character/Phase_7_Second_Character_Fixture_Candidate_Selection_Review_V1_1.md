## 2. Refined Selection Decision

**Gertrude of Saxony remains the recommended second Phase 7 character fixture, subject to full current-authority verification.**

Gertrude presents the strongest deliberate contrast with Harald Hardrada. Harald tested a mature ruler whose presentation included royal authority, military command, and maritime campaigning. Gertrude tests a political woman whose opening importance is expected to center on regency, guardianship, household government, crisis leadership, and dynastic diplomacy rather than routine battlefield command.

The available evidence indicates that Holland is admitted to first-slice scope, its opening government is organized around minority rule and regency, and Gertrude's mature authority classifies Gertrude as a realm-anchor regent and household-government principal. citeturn38search64turn38search66turn38search71
