## 2. Executive Decision

Select **Dirk V** as the preferred future underage identity-continuity prototype.

Dirk provides the most bounded and implementation-useful first test because Dirk directly connects to the completed Gertrude identity, tests guardian-and-ward continuity across two independently controlled identities, and can validate child-to-adult facial continuity without first requiring a royal regalia system, ecclesiastical ceremony layer, or France's broader distributed-minority government package.

Philip I remains the retained candidate for a later crowned-child and dynamic-office prototype. Philip is not automatically committed as the second prototype. After Dirk evidence is reviewed, the project must decide whether Philip still adds enough distinct value to justify a separate prototype.

## 3. What the Prototype Must Prove

The first underage prototype must answer a narrow technical and visual question:

> Can Medieval War preserve one recognizable child identity through age-appropriate static presentation, optional living motion, and a later life-stage continuity test without replacing the face or granting adult capacity prematurely?

```yaml
primary_test:
  - PersistentIdentityAcrossChildAndLaterStage
  - ChildAppropriateBodyScale
  - GuardianWardPresentationCompatibility
  - StaticCompactAndExpandedReadability
  - LivingPortraitIdentityStabilityIfPrototypeOpens
secondary_test_if_explicitly_enabled:
  - DynamicRoleAndConditionPresentation
  - TravelAndHouseholdStateCompatibility
not_in_first_test:
  - FullAdultPortfolio
  - BattlefieldCommand
  - VoiceLibrary
  - CompleteGovernmentSimulation
  - RuntimeOpeningStateIntegration
```

## 4. Dirk V Selection Rationale

### 3.1 Controlled relationship context

Gertrude's identity master and dynamic-presentation handoff are already complete. Dirk's record explicitly preserves separate identities while defining a lawful guardian-and-ward relationship. This creates a controlled two-character continuity test without requiring both people in one identity master.

### 3.2 Strong age-continuity test

Dirk's direction defines a bounded sequence:

- opening child;
- participating minor;
- transitioning ruler;
- personal ruler.

The prototype can test opening-child identity first, then one later stage only when needed. It does not require all stages at once.

### 3.3 Lower presentation dependency

Dirk's base identity does not require:

- a crown;
- sacred regalia;
- an ecclesiastical ceremony layer;
- a distributed royal-government presentation;
- adult armor;
- battlefield command.

This makes Dirk the cleaner first test of identity continuity itself.

### 3.4 Better stop boundary

The Dirk prototype can stop after:

- one opening identity master;
- one compact crop;
- one expanded crop;
- one optional restrained living loop;
- one later-stage continuity study only if the opening identity passes.

No scenic portfolio is required.

## 5. Philip I Deferral Rationale

Philip tests a different and important problem:

- the crown as an office and ceremonial layer;
- kingship without adult capacity;
- protected royal childhood;
- distributed minority government;
- sacred and ecclesiastical presentation;
- later transition to personal kingship.

Philip is deferred because Philip's test combines identity continuity with crown, office, ceremony, and distributed-government presentation. After Dirk's evidence is reviewed, Philip requires a fresh need decision rather than automatic execution.

## 9. Opening Identity-Master Purpose

The opening identity master should establish:

- age-appropriate child facial proportions;
- recognizable persistent identity anchors;
- separate identity from Gertrude;
- restrained comital household status;
- protected-minor presentation;
- compact-card readability;
- suitability as a later living-portrait source.

The opening master should not attempt to depict:

- adult command;
- later personal government;
- armor;
- marriage state;
- a guaranteed future personality;
- all possible household events.

## 10. Later-Stage Continuity Study Boundary

A later-stage continuity study is not automatically a production asset.

```yaml
later_stage_study_default: NonCanonicalPrototypeEvidence
preferred_stage: ParticipatingMinorOrTransitioningRuler
adult_stage_first: not_recommended
production_promotion_requires:
  - IdentityContinuityPass
  - AgeAppropriateBodyScalePass
  - NoAbruptFaceReplacement
  - GameplayNeed
```

The study should verify development direction while avoiding a complete adult portfolio.

## 11. Living-Portrait Prototype Boundary

If living motion is included, use:

```yaml
motion:
  - SubtleBreathing
  - OneSlowBlink
  - MinimalGazeShift
  - SmallPosturalAdjustment
  - RestrainedClothSettling
manual_play: true
replay: true
continuous_looping: false
static_fallback_required: true
reduce_motion_disables_autoplay: true
```

Reject:

- face morphing;
- adult gestures;
- newly generated objects;
- changing hands or clothing;
- unsupported speech;
- motion that implies adult command.
