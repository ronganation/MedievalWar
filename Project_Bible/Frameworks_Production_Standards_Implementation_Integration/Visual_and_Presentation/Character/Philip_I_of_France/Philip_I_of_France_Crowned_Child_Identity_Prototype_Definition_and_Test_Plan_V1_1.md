## 5. Bareheaded Persistent Identity Purpose

The bareheaded master controls:

- synthetic facial identity;
- child head, neck, shoulder, and upper-torso proportions;
- hairline, hair texture, and silhouette;
- broad underage read without a precise age claim;
- compatibility with compact and expanded UI;
- later crown-layer and life-stage continuity.

The bareheaded master does not establish:

- permanent crown use;
- adult government;
- adult command;
- throne presentation;
- a universal guardian or regent;
- complete royal ceremony;
- an adult king appearance.
