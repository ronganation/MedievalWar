## 2. Authority roles

- **Strategic Geography** owns physical locations, routes, nodes, crossings, geographic access, physical-control observations, and geographic battle-context admission.
- **Province** owns regional administration, control interpretation, governability, integration, autonomy, accommodation, public-order synthesis, and recovery synthesis.
- **Population** owns aggregate demographic state, workforce, composition, migration, displacement, population recovery, and recruitment burden.
- **Supply** owns logistical networks, sources, stockpiles, routes, transport, allocation, military supply state, and sustainment.
- **Peasantry** owns bounded lowest-pool, communal, local-garrison, and emergency-muster specialization.
- **Population Military Source Branch and Service Obligation** owns the bridge from population branches to accountable military sources, obligations, compliance, release, and recruitment-pool interpretation.
- **Garrison Support** owns site support, movable garrison assignments, local support capacity, bounded free or reduced upkeep, departure, transfer, and return.
- The ownership assessment is historical and currentness evidence, not the controlling substantive framework.
- The minor-territory handoff is the immediate substantive source and retained design lineage for the produced V1.1 integration-authority candidate.
- The minor-territory integration authority is a static candidate pending owner review; it does not take ownership from existing specialists.
- Recruitment Pool V1.2 is the current static review candidate, pending owner review. V1.1 is its immediate substantive source and historical/reviewed lineage only; neither folder placement nor a current-candidate label means owner adoption.
