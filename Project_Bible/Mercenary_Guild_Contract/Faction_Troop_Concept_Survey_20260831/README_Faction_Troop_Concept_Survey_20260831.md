## Status

- Document type: Read-only faction and basic troop-concept survey record
- Scope: Britannia, Norse, Iberia, and Western Francia
- Source boundary: Project Bible evidence only
- Authority status: ReviewOnly brainstorming and source recovery

## Survey Result

The Project Bible contains four theater roots: Britannia, Norse, Iberia, and West Francia. Theater placement is contextual only. A theater association does not establish realm ownership, playable status, route access, military access, recruitment access, alliance, war, or runtime activation.

## Full Survey Realms

The seven realms with full lightweight survey coverage and one example concept card each are:

- England: distributed royal, earl, household, shire, burh, and maritime capacity.
- Wales: terrain-shaped regional concentration under a Wales-level polity, without a permanent national army.
- Ireland: multi-realm, household, coalition, settlement, and Irish Sea military structure.
- Scotland: royal direction and regional contribution constrained by incomplete central reach and unresolved northern structures.
- Norway: royal, regional, household, and maritime power balanced against expeditionary overextension.
- Denmark: assembly and contribution-dependent maritime military structure across Jutland, islands, and eastern lands.
- Sweden: regional and community contributions with no standing national army or active opening force roster.

## Candidate Inventory

Britannia primary package candidates are England, Wales, Ireland, and Scotland. Normandy is a Western Francia primary package and a Britania expeditionary/maritime association. Norway and Denmark are Norse primary packages with Britania maritime/historical-legacy relevance. Orkney is an external-lock/review association; Isle of Man, Hebrides, and Shetland were not confirmed as separate faction packages.

Norse primary packages are Norway, Denmark, and Sweden.

Iberia packages found are Almoravids, Aragon, Barcelona, Leon_Castile, and Taifas. Theater-supported candidates additionally include Pamplona, Urgell, Zaragoza, Toledo, Badajoz, Seville, Valencia, Granada, and Almeria, with package and activation status varying.

Western Francia packages found are Aquitaine, Brittany, Flanders, France, and Normandy. Theater-supported primary candidates additionally include Anjou, Blois-Chartres and Champagne interests, Burgundy, and Toulouse.

## Source Families

Shared military authority and standards govern roster architecture, recruitment semantics, unit lineage, and development but do not create units. Realm military authorities exist for England, Wales, Ireland, Scotland, Norway, Denmark, Sweden, and Valencia. England also retains early cards, a High Era roster index, and progression concepts. Flanders has an explicit force/recruitment profile in its core package.

## Concept Card Rules

Each file in `Example_Concept_Cards` is an illustrative review-only concept card. Its working concept name is not a verified historical formal title. The classification is exactly one of the project survey classifications. The cards deliberately omit numeric tactical statistics, unit size, cost, stable identity, exact recruitment-pool values, and runtime data.

Cards for package-backed or military-authority-backed candidates may use `ExistingProjectConcept` or `PlausibleDesignComposite` as appropriate. ## Preservation Notes

Retain distinctions between emergency manpower, customary service, town defense, household retainers, regional troops, crews, mercenaries, siege labor, and later adoption. Avoid generic quality ladders, universal heavy cavalry, automatic longbows, automatic crossbows, permanent national fleets, and identical Norse or British rosters.
