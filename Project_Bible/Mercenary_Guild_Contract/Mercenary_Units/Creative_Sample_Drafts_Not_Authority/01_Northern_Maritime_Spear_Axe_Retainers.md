## 2. Creative pitch
A compact dismounted hired following for expeditions, landings, and hard defensive work. Its hook is a seasoned close formation, not raiding spectacle.

## 4. Service-form boundary
Potentially contracted service outside household obligation. It is not automatically a retinue, allied contingent, external service, regional recruitment, or institutional service.

## 5. Battlefield identity
Primary: close infantry. Secondary: escort and rearguard. Strong in chokepoints and landing perimeters; poor in pursuit, open missile exposure, and isolated flanks. Synergies: scouts and missile support. Counters: missiles, flank attacks, and heavy cavalry.

## 6. Equipment and appearance concept
Spears, hand axes, mixed shields, and varied textile, leather, or mail-like protection are working options. Avoid uniform Viking styling, invented emblems, and standardized kit.

## 7. Campaign employment concept
A ruler might seek them to anchor a landing, guard baggage, or stiffen a threatened line. Mission, duration, command, pay, supply, transport, withdrawal, and return are unresolved; no route, price, or availability is claimed.

## 8. Recruitment and finite-capacity questions
