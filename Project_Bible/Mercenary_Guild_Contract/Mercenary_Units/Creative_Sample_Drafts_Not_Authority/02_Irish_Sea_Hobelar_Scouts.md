A fast mounted service concept for seeing first, screening a column, carrying messages, and pursuing a broken enemy. Its niche is information and movement, not miniature knight shock.

## 5. Battlefield identity
Primary: reconnaissance, screening, pursuit, and communication. Secondary: harassment. Strong in rough ground and exposed flanks; poor in sustained melee and dense spear lines. Synergies: infantry columns and ambushes. Counters: spears and disciplined missiles.

## 6. Equipment and appearance concept
Light horses, short spears, javelins, knives, and modest protection; varied tack and shields. Reject later generalized hobelar assumptions and ethnic behavior claims.

Seek them for screening, courier work, pursuit, and flank watch. Horse supply, remounts, command, payment, access, and withdrawal remain questions; no route, market, price, or availability.
