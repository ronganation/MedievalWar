## 2. Creative pitch
Flexible mounted service for scouting, flanking, pursuit, and a selective charge without becoming elite household knights. Its fun comes from choosing when to commit.

## 4. Service-form boundary
Potential flexible contract service; not automatically household, allied, external, regional, or institutional.

## 5. Battlefield identity
Primary: mobile medium cavalry. Secondary: reconnaissance and pursuit. Strong on exposed flanks; poor against dense spears, prepared missiles, and prolonged melee. Synergies: infantry pins and scouts. Counters: spears and concentrated missiles.

## 6. Equipment and appearance concept
Lances or spears, sidearms, mixed shields, varied protection, and varied horses. Reject standardized knightly heraldry, later chivalric assumptions, and fixed bravery claims.

## 7. Campaign employment concept
Seek for flank security, pursuit, counter-raids, and mobile reserve. Horse supply, remounts, command, access, payment, and withdrawal remain unresolved.

## 8. Recruitment and finite-capacity questions
Possible captain-led riders; test collisions among horses, saddlers, officers, household, and allied duties. No pool count is proposed.

## 9. Development and chronology
High candidate; do not back-project later knightly or free-company forms. Opening-1062 disposition: ResearchRequired.

## 10. Player-facing identity
Card: Medium cavalry that scouts, flanks, and charges selectively. Strengths: flexible mobility; pursuit; selective shock. Weaknesses: horse burden; spear vulnerability; limited staying power. Any future availability depends on exact source, access, contract, and finite capacity.

## 11. AI employment
Use for flanks and pursuit, not disposable scouts or heavy knights. AI must abstain where source, access, command, supply, or replacement evidence is missing; it must not assume hidden hiring or unlimited reinforcement.

## 12. Art, audio, and presentation hooks
Varied coastal and inland materials, mixed shields and tack, clear calls without caricature; card shows riders turning an exposed flank.

## 13. Meaningful-difference and no-duplication test
Nearest overlap: Breton Light Cavalry, Norman knights, household cavalry, mounted contingents. Keep separate only if medium flexible contract role is distinct. If the distinction cannot be evidenced, this is a MergeOrRejectCandidate.
