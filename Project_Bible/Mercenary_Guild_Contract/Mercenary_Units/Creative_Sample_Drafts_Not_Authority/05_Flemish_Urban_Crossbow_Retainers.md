## 2. Creative pitch
Prepared missile support that rewards cover, ammunition discipline, and protection. It should not be a generic crossbow block that works equally well everywhere.

## 4. Service-form boundary
Potential hired missile support; not automatically guild, household, allied, external, regional, or institutional service.

## 5. Battlefield identity
Primary: prepared missile support. Secondary: defensive overwatch. Strong behind cover; poor in open melee, pursuit, and unsupported forward deployment. Synergies: spears and protected positions. Counters: fast cavalry and flankers.

## 6. Equipment and appearance concept
Crossbows, bolts, sidearms, varied protection, and optional cover are working concepts. Do not force pavises, guild marks, or standardized kit.

## 7. Campaign employment concept
Hire to defend an approach, support a siege line, or punish exposed troops while protected. Ammunition, transport, command, payment, access, and withdrawal are unresolved.

## 8. Recruitment and finite-capacity questions
Possible patron-supported specialists; test collisions with crossbows, bolts, armorers, carriers, civic roles, and officers. No pool count is proposed.

## 9. Development and chronology

## 10. Player-facing identity
Card: Prepared crossbow support that needs protection and supply. Strengths: prepared fire; defensive support; cover synergy. Weaknesses: ammunition burden; protection dependence; slow repositioning. Any future availability depends on exact source, access, contract, and finite capacity.

## 11. AI employment
Use behind protection; never assume endless bolts or urban access. AI must abstain where source, access, command, supply, or replacement evidence is missing; it must not assume hidden hiring or unlimited reinforcement.
