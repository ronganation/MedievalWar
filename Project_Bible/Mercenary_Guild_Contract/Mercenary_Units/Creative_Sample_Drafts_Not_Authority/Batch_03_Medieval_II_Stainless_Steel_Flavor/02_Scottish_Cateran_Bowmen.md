## 2. Total War Pitch
A rough-edged body of bowmen traveling with axe-bearing guards. They are not a massed longbow line; they harry a flank, defend a camp edge, and punish pursuit through uneven country. The silhouette is a compact missile block with visible sidearms and mixed shields. This is a creative working proposal, not a historical finding.

## 5. Battlefield Profile
Primary role: mobile short-range missile support. Secondary role: camp-edge defense. It fires while shifting and uses axes only against already weakened enemies. Bows, knives, round shields, and practical protection make a clear silhouette. It likes wooded edges and rolling ground, but cavalry and prolonged frontal combat are dangerous. Protect it with steady infantry. Spamming it produces too little staying power and too many vulnerable missile carriers.

## 6. Campaign and Recruitment Identity
A ruler hires these bowmen when a Scottish contact can supply people accustomed to moving with a war band. Access might follow diplomacy, a border expedition, or allied service. Replacement depends on local obligations and trusted intermediaries. A contract can end when a season or political bargain ends. Their bows and mixed protection are not an infinite reserve. No prices, pools, routes, or active availability are defined.

## 7. Medieval II and Stainless Steel Flavor
The Medieval II appeal is a regional missile unit with enough bite to survive a mistake but not enough armor to replace line infantry. The Stainless Steel feel comes from local war-band texture and a difficult choice between reach and fragility. The card shows bows first and axes second. Its memorable strength is mobile flank pressure; its limitation is that cavalry can erase the advantage quickly. The campaign story is a border ruler borrowing a seasonal company.

## 8. Visual and Audio Direction
Use wool tunics, muted cloaks, small shields, simple bows, and visibly mixed axes. Banners should be modest. Card art puts an archer in front of an axe guard. Animations emphasize quick nocking and an abrupt defensive brace. Voices are clipped field calls without caricature. Avoid standardized tartan, plate armor, oversized bows, and firearms.
