## 3. Portfolio key

Each Realm below receives all six required shared portfolio dispositions:

- `Mapped`: a Realm owner or deliberately distributed current owners are evidenced.
- `External Owner`: the economic portfolio is not owned by the Economy authority; the external owner and typed handoff are named.
- `Deferred With Reason`: the package has evidence or an embedded capability, but exact owner/currentness acceptance remains unresolved.
- `Genuinely Missing`: no current owner or adequate embedded lifecycle was evidenced. This was not established for any reviewed package.

The mapping describes authored authority scope, not runtime readiness. `Mapped` does not mean compiled, imported, active, or runtime-validated.

## 5. Cross-cutting currentness findings

The following defects recur across the reviewed Realm authorities and are bounded contract/interface issues, not justification for new parallel owners:

1. Formal transaction envelope, stable owner versions, input/output hashes, prepare/commit/abort/retry/idempotence, conservation summary, rollback journal, external locks, and typed receipts are incomplete in several older authorities.
2. Production authorities often combine productive potential, active production, accepted output, civilian stock, local retention, and tradable surplus without the shared family’s explicit lifecycle separation.
3. Civilian commercial consignment, carrier capacity, dispatch/transshipment, arrival inspection, acceptance, delivery loss, return, and recovery are frequently deferred or only interfaced.
4. Investment/construction authorities often stop at development or damage concepts and need explicit feasibility, accepted progress, inspection, owner acceptance, commissioning, maintenance, closure, salvage, and recovery handoffs.
5. Denmark is the only reviewed Realm with an explicit queue-level finding of universal seven-month language, an outdated Unreal Engine 5 target, and older dependency routing. Other reviewed current authorities generally declare the three-track model and Unreal Engine 5.8.
6. AI and presentation surfaces are required to remain proposal-only and owner-routed. No evidence in the reviewed currentness records authorizes direct cross-owner state mutation.
7. Opening quantities, inventories, routes, property, people, labor, money, goods, forces, and agreements remain evidence-bound. The report does not promote any shared definition into an opening instance.

## 6. Creation-gate decision

No new or corrected substantive Realm authority is justified in this run.

The gate fails for creation because:

- existing owners are present for all reviewed substantive Economy families except unresolved package surfaces;
- Norway and Hammadids are deliberately distributed/embedded and require owner resolution, not symmetry;
- Leon-Castile requires an exact package audit before absence can be established;
- Flanders, Seville, and several other packages need bounded mapping or currentness addenda, not replacement authorities;
- the strong current authorities already carry distinct owner boundaries, save/migration/rollback contracts, and validation surfaces;
- creating a new file would risk duplicate ownership or fabricated opening content.

### Prioritized action list

1. **Bounded correction — Denmark:** perform the queue-authorized copy-first currentness remedy for the existing Denmark owner, correcting the seven-month universal turn language, Unreal Engine 5 target, and dependency routing while preserving all substantive content.
2. **Resolve embedded owner — Norway:** reconcile the exact integrated Economy authority against route, military, governance, opening, and AI authorities; accept or reject embedded ownership with a six-portfolio map.
3. **Map interfaces — Hammadids:** use the distributed Agriculture, Production, Economy Material Flow, Fiscal, and Property authorities as the current owner set; do not create a generic Hammadid Economy duplicate.
4. **Open and map — Flanders and Leon-Castile:** complete exact source/package-owner review before accepting or declaring gaps.
5. **Bounded compatibility — Almoravids, Sweden, England, Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Normandy, Aquitaine, Barcelona, Aragon, Badajoz, Seville, Valencia, and Zaragoza:** add only portfolio, receipt, lifecycle, or owner-interface corrections supported by their existing evidence.
6. **Retain and verify — France, Brittany, Burgundy, and Zirids:** preserve current substantive owners and complete static acceptance/physical verification gates.
