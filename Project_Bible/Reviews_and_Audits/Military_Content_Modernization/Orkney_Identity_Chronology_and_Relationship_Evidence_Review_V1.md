## 1. Purpose

This review evaluates the current Project Bible interpretation of Orkney's identity, the succession from Thorfinn Sigurdsson, the opening treatment of Paul and Erlend Thorfinnsson, and Orkney's relationships with Norway and Scotland. It distinguishes current project decisions from external historical orientation and records which claims are safe for bounded design use.

It does not create a new Orkney Realm package, title, office, government, household, relationship record, troop definition, Registry entry, or Opening State binding.

## 5. External Historical Orientation

External orientation confirms that the chronology is genuinely disputed and should not be converted into false precision.

A general reference summary states that the <External>Orkneyinga Saga</External> was written long after many events it describes and that the historical character of its earlier sections is debated. The same source identifies recurring themes involving disputes among earls, relationships with the Norwegian crown, and activity in the Hebrides. citeturn74search239

A general summary of <Person>Paul Thorfinnsson</Person> and <Person>Erlend Thorfinnsson</Person> says that the saga provides few accurate dates and that the death date of their father is uncertain. It reports that a commonly stated date is 1065, but also notes a scholarly interpretation linking the succession to events around 1058. citeturn74search230

Another general reference summary gives approximately 1058 as a possible death date for <Person>Thorfinn Sigurdsson</Person>, based on circumstantial chronology involving a Norwegian expedition and Ingibjorg's later marriage, while explicitly acknowledging substantial chronological problems. citeturn74search234

These web sources are orientation sources, not the scholarly works themselves. The project should retrieve and evaluate the primary or scholarly works cited by them before promoting any historical claim.
