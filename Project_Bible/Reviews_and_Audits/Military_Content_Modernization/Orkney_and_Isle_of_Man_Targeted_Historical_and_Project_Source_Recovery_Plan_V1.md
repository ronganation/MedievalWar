## 1. Purpose

This plan defines the targeted internal and external source-recovery work required before Orkney or the Isle of Man can receive owner-approved political classifications, strategic sites, households, military sources, regional recruitment concepts, contingent contracts, or exact opening-state data.

The plan is intentionally evidence-first. It does not fill gaps with general medieval knowledge, modern geography, saga narrative, neighboring-realm assumptions, or game-design convenience. It separates project-source recovery, historical-source research, authority routing, evidence grading, contradiction handling, and admission gates.

## 3. Research Objectives

```yaml
research_objectives:
  common:
    - establish the best-supported political condition at the opening boundary
    - identify geographic anchors and strategically relevant sites
    - recover local household, community, institutional, and property sources
    - distinguish local defense, maritime support, household force, and negotiated contingent capacity
    - identify service limits, logistics, and opportunity costs
    - preserve uncertainty and contradictory chronology
    - route each output to its proper Project Bible owner

  Orkney_specific:
    - test the timing and nature of any co-rule or succession transition near 1062
    - distinguish local Orcadian authority from Scottish and Norwegian relationships
    - recover evidence for local maritime, household, and defense capacity without inventing quantities

  Isle_of_Man_specific:
    - determine the strongest supportable political classification near 1062
    - recover the island's relationship to Irish Sea powers and route systems
    - establish whether household, civic, community, or maritime sources can be modeled without premature faction creation
```

## 7. External Historical Research Tiers

External research begins only after internal source recovery identifies the unresolved questions.

```yaml
external_source_tiers:
  Tier_1_PrimaryOrNearPrimary:
    includes:
      - contemporary annals and chronicles
      - legal, ecclesiastical, diplomatic, numismatic, epigraphic, or archaeological evidence
      - medieval saga or narrative evidence with explicit date and transmission caution
    use: strongest evidence but still requires source criticism

  Tier_2_ModernScholarlySynthesis:
    includes:
      - peer-reviewed articles
      - academic monographs
      - edited scholarly volumes
      - university press reference works
      - national heritage research publications
    use: preferred interpretation and bibliography layer

  Tier_3_InstitutionalCollections:
    includes:
      - national archives
      - museums
      - heritage agencies
      - historic environment records
      - university library guides
    use: site, collection, archaeology, and source-discovery support

  Tier_4_ReputableOrientation:
    includes:
      - curated historical reference sites
      - educational summaries with citations
    use: search-term and bibliography discovery only

  Tier_5_UncontrolledOrCommercial:
    includes:
      - unsourced blogs
      - genealogy pages
      - marketplace descriptions
      - crowd-edited summaries without source verification
      - image captions and travel pages
    use: lead generation only
    cannot_establish_project_fact: true
```

chronology_rules:

## 12. Conflict and Contradiction Handling

```yaml
conflict_protocol:
  - preserve all materially relevant claims
  - identify whether conflict concerns date, identity, title, relationship, geography, or interpretation
  - prefer contemporary evidence where sufficiently clear, but record limits
  - use modern scholarship to evaluate transmission and context
  - do not force false precision from approximate chronology
  - do not combine incompatible claims into a synthetic certainty
  - route unresolved opening facts to Opening State and owner review
  - retain rejected or superseded claims in the evidence ledger
```

## 13. Source Rejection Criteria

```yaml
rejection_or_quarantine_criteria:
  - no identifiable source
  - circular citation
  - modern tourism or marketplace text used as historical authority
  - genealogy assertion without reliable documentation
  - map or image caption used as sole proof
  - later institution projected into 1062
  - later kingdom or dynasty projected backward
  - saga narrative treated as contemporary without caution
  - modern national possession treated as medieval ownership
  - coastline treated as proof of fleet or crew
  - famous military stereotype substituted for local evidence
  - exact force, population, vessel, or pool quantity without defensible evidence
```

## 17. Search and Capture Procedure

```yaml
research_procedure:
  - record pre-search question and owner
  - search current internal authorities first
  - record exact internal path and passage
  - identify contradiction and missing-owner issues
  - search scholarly and institutional external sources for unresolved questions
  - capture bibliographic details and relevant claim summaries without copying protected text
  - distinguish quotation, paraphrase, interpretation, and design suggestion
  - classify chronology and confidence
  - route each claim to a Project Bible owner
  - run admission, contradiction, and anachronism checks
  - create no active record or stable ID
```

## 18. Quality Checks

```yaml
quality_checks:
  completeness:
    - every research question has a source result or explicit NotFound disposition
    - every admitted claim has at least one citable source
    - major disputed claims include competing positions

  chronology:
    - every opening claim is evaluated specifically against 1 January 1062
    - later facts are clearly marked

  ownership:
    - every output has one accountable Project Bible owner or remains unresolved
    - theater association is never substituted for realm ownership

  military:
    - troop labels are working labels unless historically verified
    - vessels, crews, households, equipment, routes, and supply remain separate
    - no population-to-army conversion
    - no realm-wide unlock

  evidence:
    - weak sources are used only as leads
    - uncertainty and rejected claims remain visible
    - no unsupported exact numbers
```
