## 1. Purpose

This receipt preserves Saxony's completed Realm, character, dynasty, historical, military, and unit-development work while formally deferring Saxony from the current first-slice playable and fully simulated character-implementation scope.

The deferral is not a rejection of Saxony, a removal from the Project Bible, or a finding that the Realm lacks design value. The deferral prevents a partially integrated Saxony from forcing placeholder Germania and Holy Roman political-order systems into a bounded coastal first slice that can operate without continuous playable territory.

## 5. Why Saxony Is Deferred

Saxony's campaign identity is tied to systems extending beyond a standalone ducal household or military roster. A strong implementation may require owner-controlled treatment of:

- royal and imperial authority;
- ducal jurisdiction and delegated government;
- wider prince, count, bishop, and regional-power relationships;
- imperial Church and secular-investiture interfaces;
- crown, election, recognition, and succession structures;
- Germania-wide titles, claims, offices, obligations, immunities, and service;
- external military commitments and political constraints;
- relationships among Saxony, neighboring German powers, the Crown, and institutions;
- map ownership, political geography, and non-playable regional actors.

```yaml
completed_units_alone_establish_Realm_readiness: false
completed_Realm_foundation_alone_establishes_Opening_State_readiness: false
partial_character_portfolio_may_be_bulk_populated: false
placeholder_imperial_systems_allowed: false
isolated_Saxony_implementation_without_dependency_review: false
```

Admitting Saxony now could create a temporary political model that later requires structural replacement when the wider Germania and imperial systems are implemented. The deferral avoids that rework.
