Medieval War — Systems/AI_Master_Template.md

# Overview

The AI Master Template defines how different factions express unique personalities while using the same underlying AI Behavior System.

The AI Behavior System defines:

"HOW the AI thinks."

The AI Master Template defines:

"WHO the AI is."

Every faction receives:

- Strategic Personality
- Military Personality
- Economic Personality
- Diplomatic Personality
- Religious Personality
- Cultural Personality
- Government Personality

No two factions should feel identical.

---

# Core Principles

The AI does not cheat.

The AI differs through:

- Priorities
- Personality
- Decision Weighting
- Long-term Goals

Not through artificial advantages.
