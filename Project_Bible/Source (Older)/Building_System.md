# Overview

Buildings represent the physical development of settlements and provinces.

Unlike later Total War games, Medieval War does NOT use restrictive building slots.

If the settlement tier supports the structure,
and all requirements are met,
the building may be constructed.

Buildings are limited by:

- Settlement Tier
- Population
- Resources
- Economy
- Religion
- Culture
- Era
- Technology / Innovation
- Province Identity

NOT arbitrary slots.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every building should serve at least one meaningful purpose.

Buildings should influence:

- Recruitment
- Economy
- Culture
- Religion
- Loyalty
- Public Order
- Supply
- Trade
- Diplomacy
- Court Politics
- Guild Influence
- Dynasty Prestige
- Technology
- Espionage
- Military Logistics

No "filler" buildings should exist.
