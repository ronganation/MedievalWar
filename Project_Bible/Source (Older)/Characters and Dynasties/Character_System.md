# Overview

The Character System governs all individuals who influence the medieval world.

Characters are not merely trait containers.

Characters are:

- Political actors
- Military commanders
- Religious leaders
- Governors
- Nobles
- Guild leaders
- Dynastic heirs
- Rivals
- Heroes
- Villains

The Character System combines:

- Medieval II emergent traits
- Rome II offices and political roles
- Crusader Kings character growth
- Medieval War's province and court simulation

Characters serve as the personal layer beneath larger systems.
