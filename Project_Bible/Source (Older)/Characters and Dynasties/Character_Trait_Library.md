# Overview

The Character Trait Library defines the personality, skills, flaws, quirks, memories, and reputation characteristics used throughout Medieval War.

Traits are one of the primary drivers of emergent storytelling.

Traits may be gained through:

- Education
- Experience
- Age
- Events
- War
- Religion
- Court Politics
- Random Development
- Relationships
- Dynastic Circumstances

Not all traits are beneficial.

Not all traits are harmful.

Many exist simply to create memorable characters.

# Trait Progression

Characters may gain traits through:

- Education
- Battle
- Governance
- Religion
- Marriage
- Events
- Captivity
- Age

Traits may evolve over time.

Example:

Brave
→ Renowned Hero

Builder
→ Master Builder

Logistician
→ Supply Master

# AI Trait Usage

AI evaluates:

- Personality
- Ambition
- Loyalty
- Aggression
- Governance

Trait combinations help define AI behavior.

Examples:

Brave + Ambitious
= Expansionist

Builder + Organizer
= Development Focused

Zealous + Fanatic
= Religious Extremist

Diplomat + Statesman
= Coalition Builder

# Notes

Traits are one of the primary storytelling tools of Medieval War.

Not every trait exists for balance.

Many exist to generate:

- Stories
- Rivalries
- Friendships
- Legends
- Dynastic Histories

Characters should feel memorable, unique, and imperfect.
