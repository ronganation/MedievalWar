# Overview

The Dynasty System defines how noble houses, royal houses, cadet branches, and dynastic networks interact throughout Medieval War.

Dynasties are one of the primary engines of medieval history.

The system governs:

- Dynasty Identity
- Prestige
- Legitimacy
- Dynasty Networks
- Dynasty Relationships
- Succession
- Claims
- Marriages
- Cadet Branches
- Character Development
- Heirs
- Claimants
- Pretenders
- Dynasty Continuity
- Dynasty Extinction

The Dynasty System serves as the bridge between:

Characters

↓

Governance

↓

Politics

↓

History

---

# Core Philosophy

Rulers come and go.

Dynasties endure.

Most major medieval political events can be traced back to dynastic interests.

The purpose of the Dynasty System is to create:

- Long-term continuity
- Meaningful succession
- Intergenerational storytelling
- Political realism
- Emergent conflicts

without requiring excessive genealogy simulation.

# Dynasty Prestige

Prestige represents the reputation of a dynasty.

Range:

0 – 100

---

- Victories
- Titles
- Expansion
- Marriage Alliances
- Successful Governance
- Religious Recognition

---

Prestige Decreases Through:

# Notes

The Dynasty System serves as one of the foundational pillars of Medieval War.

Historical Momentum creates pressure.

Historical Emergence creates people.

Dynasties provide continuity.

Succession determines inheritance.

Characters shape outcomes.

Narrative Arcs tell the story.

The purpose is not to script history.

The purpose is to simulate the conditions through which medieval history emerges.

Kingdoms may survive rulers.

Dynasties may survive kingdoms.

The interaction between both helps create the living world of Medieval War.
