# Overview

The Combat Resolution System governs how battles are fought, influenced, resolved, and interpreted throughout Medieval War.

Combat is determined by far more than unit statistics.

Battle outcomes are influenced by:

- Unit Quality
- Unit Experience
- Terrain
- Weather
- Seasons
- Morale
- Fatigue
- Commanders
- Supply
- Religion
- Culture
- Technology
- Formation Usage
- Siege Conditions
- Army Composition

Combat is intended to reward preparation, positioning, logistics, and leadership rather than raw unit strength alone.

---

# Core Philosophy

The strongest army should not always win.

Preparation matters.

Leadership matters.

Terrain matters.

Supply matters.

Morale matters.

A poorly supplied elite army should be vulnerable.

A skilled commander should be capable of overcoming superior numbers.
