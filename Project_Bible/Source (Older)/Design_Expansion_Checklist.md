# Overview

This checklist defines all major campaign systems in Medieval War and the optimal order in which they should be developed.

It incorporates every system created so far, newly added systems, and systems inspired by:

- Medieval II
- Stainless Steel
- Broken Crescent
- Attila 1212 AD
- Crusader Kings
- Rome II

while maintaining Medieval War's own identity.

This document serves as the official design roadmap for Medieval War.

# Notes

This checklist is designed for incremental expansion.

Systems are ordered by dependency to ensure smooth development.

Medieval War is built around:

Historical Momentum

↓

Historical Emergence

↓

Dynasties

↓

Characters

↓

Character Networks

↓

Narrative Arcs

↓

History

The objective is not to script history.

The objective is to simulate the conditions that make history possible.

The systems generate the story.
