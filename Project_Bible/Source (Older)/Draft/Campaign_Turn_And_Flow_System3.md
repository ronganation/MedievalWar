# Overview

The Campaign Turn & Flow System governs how time advances, how world systems update, and how the simulation evolves.

This system acts as the master timeline for:

- Economy
- Population
- Resources
- Trade
- Governments
- Dynasties
- Characters
- Recruitment
- Technology
- Religion
- Culture
- AI
- Events
- Sieges
- Captives

Every campaign system ultimately resolves through this framework.

---

# Core Philosophy

The world should evolve continuously.

The player is not the only actor.

Kings age.

Children grow.

 Dynasties rise and fall.

Rebels emerge.

Technology spreads.

Religions expand.

Trade routes prosper or collapse.

History is generated through interaction between systems.

# Turn Length

Recommended Prototype Value:

# Why Monthly Turns?

Advantages:

- Character progression feels meaningful.
- Campaign events occur regularly.
- Recruitment pacing feels responsive.
- Sieges remain manageable.
- Technology feels alive.
- Dynasties can evolve.

Monthly turns provide enough granularity while allowing long-term progression.
