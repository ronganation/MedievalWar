# Design Expansion Checklist
Medieval War — Systems/Design_Expansion_Checklist.md

## Overview
This checklist outlines optional and recommended systems that expand the depth, realism, and strategic complexity of Medieval War.  
These systems integrate directly with the Unified Military System Template and can be added incrementally.

---

# 1. Supply System (Highly Recommended)
- Supply radius  
- Supply sources (settlements, castles, ports, camps)  
- Out-of-supply penalties  
- Winter attrition  
- Desert attrition  
- Naval supply  
- Siege starvation  
- Supply buildings (granaries, depots, caravansaries)

---

# 2. Faction Identity System (Recommended)
- Cultural bonuses  
- Recruitment differences  
- Terrain affinities  
- Religious tendencies  
- Economic strengths  
- Agent strengths  
- Guild preference weights  
- Unique faction traits

---

# 3. Era Progression System (Recommended)
- Era unlock conditions  
- Unit availability per era  
- Building upgrades  
- Guild expansions  
- Religious order unlocks  
- Economic changes  
- Era-based events

---

# 4. Agent System (Recommended)
- Agent types (Spy/Assassin hybrid, Diplomat, Priest, Merchant, Explorer)  
- Agent leveling  
- Mission success rates  
- Recruitment influence  
- Loyalty influence  
- Supply influence  
- Governor traits  
- General traits

---

# 5. Loyalty & Public Order System (Recommended)
- Causes of loyalty  
- Causes of unrest  
- Effects on recruitment  
- Effects on economy  
- Effects on rebellions  
- Effects on agent success  
- Effects on religious orders  
- Cultural loyalty vs faction loyalty

---

# 6. Population System (Optional)
- Population growth  
- Population caps  
- Recruitment population cost  
- War population penalties  
- Peace bonuses  
- Settlement overcrowding penalties

---

# 7. Diplomacy & Trade System (Optional)
- Trade route stability  
- Trade goods  
- Diplomatic bonuses  
- Mercenary availability  
- Guild influence  
- Religious order influence  
- Trade agreements and embargoes

---

# 8. Combat Resolution System (Optional)
- Attack vs defense formula  
- Morale breakpoints  
- Fatigue accumulation  
- Charge mechanics  
- Armor penetration  
- Shield values  
- Terrain modifiers  
- Formations and cohesion

---

# 9. Siege System (Optional)
- Siege phases  
- Wall strength  
- Gate strength  
- Starvation  
- Surrender thresholds  
- Siege equipment effects  
- Siege morale modifiers

---

# 10. Naval System (Optional)
- Naval units  
- Naval transport  
- Naval supply  
- Naval blockades  
- Naval mercenaries  
- Coastal recruitment bonuses

---

# 11. AI Behavior System (Optional)
- Aggressive factions  
- Defensive factions  
- Naval factions  
- Raider factions  
- Religious factions  
- Mercenary-heavy factions  
- Personality-driven behavior

---

# 12. Economy System (Optional)
- Taxes  
- Trade  
- Resource production  
- Upkeep  
- Recruitment cost scaling  
- Guild economic bonuses  
- Inflation / prosperity events

---

# Notes
This checklist is designed for incremental expansion.  
You may implement any system at any time without breaking existing templates.  
The Medieval War Design Agent will automatically use updated system rules for future unit generation.
