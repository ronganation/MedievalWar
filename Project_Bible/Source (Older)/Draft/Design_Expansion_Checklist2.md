# Design Expansion Checklist
Medieval War — Systems/Design_Expansion_Checklist.md

## Overview
This checklist outlines recommended and optional systems that expand the depth, realism, and strategic complexity of Medieval War.  
These systems integrate directly with the Unified Military System Template, Agent System, Faction Identity System, and Era Progression System.

This is the official roadmap for Medieval War’s full campaign design.

# ============================
- Supply radius  
- Supply sources (settlements, castles, ports, camps)  
- Out-of-supply penalties  
- Winter attrition  
- Desert attrition  
- Naval supply  
- Siege starvation  
- Supply buildings (granaries, depots, caravansaries)  
- Supply Officers (Agent System integration)
