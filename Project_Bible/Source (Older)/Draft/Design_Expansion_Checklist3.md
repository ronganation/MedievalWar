# Design Expansion Checklist
Medieval War — Systems/Design_Expansion_Checklist.md

## Overview
This checklist defines all major campaign systems in Medieval War and the optimal order in which they should be developed.  
It incorporates every system created so far, newly added systems, and systems that naturally emerged from the design of Agents, Diplomacy, Religion, Loyalty, and Era Progression.

This is the official roadmap for Medieval War’s full campaign design.
