# Purpose

The Prototype Campaign Framework defines the first playable campaign for Medieval War.

This document is not a gameplay system.

It is a development framework.

Its purpose is to define:

- First Playable Map
- Start Date
- Playable Factions
- AI Factions
- Victory Conditions
- Prototype Scope
- Vertical Slice Requirements

The goal is to test all major campaign and battle systems within a manageable playable area.

---

# Prototype Philosophy

The prototype campaign exists to validate:

- Recruitment
- Economy
- Buildings
- Population
- Dynasties
- Characters
- Governments
- Resources
- Trade
- Religion
- Culture
- Technology
- Supply
- Combat
- Sieges
- Naval Operations
- AI

The prototype is not the final campaign map.

The prototype is the foundation upon which future expansions are built.

---

# Start Date

Recommended Start Date:

1060 AD

---

# Why 1060?

The campaign begins several years before the Norman Conquest.

This allows:

- Dynamic historical outcomes
- Player agency
- System testing
- Alternate history

History becomes possible but not guaranteed.

---

# Historical Pressures

The world begins with emerging historical tensions.

Examples:

- Norman ambitions
- Norse influence
- Anglo-Saxon succession concerns
- Scottish border interests
- Welsh independence
- Irish clan rivalries

These pressures influence the campaign.

They do not force outcomes.
