# Purpose

The Prototype Campaign Framework defines the first fully playable campaign experience for Medieval War.

The prototype campaign exists to validate:

- Core gameplay systems
- Campaign progression
- AI behavior
- Character systems
- Dynastic systems
- Recruitment
- Supply
- Economy
- Trade
- Technology
- Siege warfare
- Naval logistics

The prototype is not intended to represent the full medieval world.

The prototype exists to prove that the simulation architecture functions properly before global expansion.

---

# Core Philosophy

History should influence the campaign.

History should not control the campaign.

The prototype campaign follows:

Historical Pressure

not

Historical Script

Historical outcomes remain possible.

Historical outcomes are not guaranteed.

---

# Campaign Start Date

Spring 1060 AD

---

# Why 1060?

By 1060:

- Normandy has largely consolidated.
- England remains intact.
- Norse influence remains significant.
- Scotland remains independent.
- Irish clan politics remain relevant.
- Welsh resistance remains active.
- Flanders and Brittany retain regional importance.

This date allows:

- Dynamic Norman expansion
- Dynamic English succession
- Dynamic Norse ambitions
- Dynamic Scottish opportunities

while maintaining strong historical authenticity.
