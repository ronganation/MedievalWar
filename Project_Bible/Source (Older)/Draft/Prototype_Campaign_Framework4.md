# Purpose

The Prototype Campaign Framework defines the first fully playable campaign experience for Medieval War.

This prototype serves as the foundation for:

- Campaign systems
- Character systems
- Dynastic systems
- Government systems
- Trade systems
- Recruitment systems
- AI systems
- Siege systems
- Naval systems

The prototype is not a limited scenario.

The prototype is a living historical sandbox.

History is encouraged.

History is not guaranteed.

The systems generate history.

---

# Core Design Pillar

The systems generate history while players become attached to the people shaping it.

The player should feel connected to:

- Characters
- Dynasties
- Kingdoms
- Cultures
- Governments

while still seeing the world evolve around them.

---

# Campaign Start Date

Spring 1060 AD

---

# Why 1060?

The campaign begins before the Norman Conquest.

Advantages:

- William can rise naturally.
- Harold can strengthen England.
- Norse ambitions remain active.
- Scotland has agency.
- Wales remains independent.
- Irish politics matter.
- Brittany and Flanders remain important.

The historical world exists.

The outcome remains open.
