# Campaign Sustainment System
Medieval War — Systems/Campaign_Sustainment_System.md

## Overview
The Campaign Sustainment System governs supply, logistics, movement, attrition, reinforcement, weather, seasons, terrain penalties, cultural bonuses, agent interactions, general traits, resource-based supply boosts, naval supply, siege starvation, and expedition rules.

This unified system merges:
- Supply System
- Logistics System
- Supply & Logistics Master File (core)
- Additional movement, terrain, faction, and seasonal details

All values are subject to balancing and may be adjusted as new systems are added.

---

# ============================
# SUPPLY SYSTEM
# ============================

## Supply Sources
Supply is generated by settlements, ports, trade routes, and faction traits.

### Towns
- Provide basic supply
- Higher tiers increase supply radius
- Loyalty increases supply efficiency

### Castles
- Provide strong supply
- Increase supply radius
- Reduce attrition
- Provide elite reinforcement

### Ports
- Provide naval supply
- Enable coastal reinforcement
- Vulnerable to blockades

### Trade Routes
- Provide long-distance supply
- Disrupted by rebels, raids, war, or agent sabotage

---

## Supply Radius (Settlement-Based)
- Village: Small
- Town: Medium
- Large Town: Large
- City: Very Large
- Huge City: Very Large
- Motte & Bailey: Medium
- Castle: Large
- Fortress: Very Large
- Citadel: Massive
- Port: Coastal radius

Armies outside supply radius begin suffering penalties.

# SUPPLY CONSUMPTION
# ============================

## Low Consumption Units
- Levies
- Light infantry
- Skirmishers
- Archers

Effects:
- Lower upkeep
- Lower attrition risk
- Longer endurance when unsupplied

## Medium Consumption Units
- Medium infantry
- Professional infantry
- Light cavalry

Effects:
- Standard upkeep
- Standard attrition
- Moderate endurance when unsupplied

## High Consumption Units
- Heavy infantry
- Elite infantry
- Heavy cavalry
- Noble units

Effects:
- High upkeep
- High attrition risk
- Poor endurance when unsupplied

# ============================
# ATTRITION SYSTEM
# ============================

Attrition applies each turn based on:
- Supply status
- Weather
- Terrain
- Season
- Army composition
- General traits
- Agent presence
- Siege conditions
- Naval conditions

### Base Attrition Values
- Low Risk: 1–2% per turn
- Medium Risk: 3–5% per turn
- High Risk: 6–10% per turn

### Extreme Conditions
- Desert: +3%
- Snow/Winter: +2%
- Mountains: +2%
- Swamp/Marsh: +2%
- Siege: +5%
- Naval (Deep Sea): +4%
