# TERRAIN DEFINITIONS & GAMEPLAY IMPACT

### Plains
Open terrain ideal for cavalry charges and ranged visibility.

### Hills
Elevation advantage; ranged units gain accuracy and range.

### Forest (Sparse)
Light cover; reduces cavalry effectiveness.

### Forest (Dense)
Heavy cover; excellent for ambushes; cavalry severely penalized.

### Marsh
Soft ground; slows movement; penalizes heavy units.

### Snow
Cold terrain; penalizes non-winter units; reduces movement.

### Mountains (Low)
Steep terrain; penalizes cavalry; boosts defensive infantry.

### Mountains (High)
Extreme elevation; very slow movement; excellent defensive terrain.

### Farmlands
Open fields; similar to plains but slightly softer ground.

### Floodplains
Soft wet ground; penalizes heavy infantry and cavalry.

### Taiga
Cold dense forest; boosts ambush units; penalizes cavalry.

### Desert
Heat penalties; ranged units suffer; cavalry slightly boosted.

### Desert Mountains
Extreme heat + elevation; harsh attrition; cavalry heavily penalized.

### Drylands
Arid plains; neutral terrain with slight heat penalties.

### Oasis
Small fertile area; boosts morale slightly; neutral combat.

### Swamp
Extreme movement penalties; heavy units suffer greatly.

### Steppe
Wide open grasslands; excellent for cavalry maneuvering.

### Jungle
Dense vegetation; boosts ambush units; penalizes armor.

### Ocean / Sea Deep
Impassable to land units.

### Sea Shallow
Allows amphibious landings; movement penalties.

### Beach / Coastal Sand
Landing zones; neutral terrain with slight movement penalty.

### River Crossing (Shallow)
Units crossing are vulnerable; defenders gain large bonuses.

### Bridge Crossing
Extreme choke point; defenders gain massive bonuses.

### Urban
Tight streets; boosts infantry; penalizes cavalry.

### Castle Walls
Boosts archers and defenders; penalizes attackers.
