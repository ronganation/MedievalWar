# Terrain System

## Overview
Terrain determines movement, combat effectiveness, visibility, morale, and fatigue. Terrain is visible on the campaign map and previewed before battles, allowing players to choose favorable positions or attack routes.

---

# Core Terrain Types

## Plains
Effects:
- Cavalry charge bonus
- Fast movement
- Low fatigue
- Archers have clear lines of sight

Best for:
- Norman cavalry
- Anglo-Saxon archers

---

## Hills
Effects:
- Major defensive bonus
- Archers gain range advantage
- Uphill attackers suffer fatigue
- Morale bonus for defenders

Best for:
- Shield-wall formations
- Defensive armies

---

## Forests
Effects:
- Reduced visibility
- Cavalry penalties
- Skirmishers gain bonuses
- Ambushes more effective

Best for:
- Irish skirmishers
- Norse raiders

---

## Marsh / Fens
Effects:
- Very slow movement
- High fatigue
- Heavy infantry penalties
- Irish units gain bonuses

Best for:
- Irish guerrilla tactics

---

## Rivers
Effects:
- Movement restricted
- Crossing penalties
- Defenders gain major bonuses

Best for:
- Defensive chokepoints

---

## Bridges
Effects:
- Extreme chokepoint
- Defenders gain massive bonuses
- Archers highly effective

Best for:
- Stamford Bridge-style battles

---

## Coastal Terrain
Effects:
- Naval supply
- Vulnerable to raids
- Wind affects archers

Best for:
- Norse coastal raids

---

# Weather Zones

## Clear
Normal combat.

## Rain
- Reduced archer effectiveness
- Increased fatigue

## Fog
- Reduced visibility
- Ambushes more effective

## Snow
- Reduced movement
- Lower morale for non-native factions

---

# Seasonal Effects

## Spring
- Normal movement
- High visibility

## Summer
- High fatigue
- Better cavalry performance

## Autumn
- Rain increases
- Reduced archer effectiveness

## Winter
- Severe movement penalties
- High attrition
- Reduced supply

---

# Pre-Battle Terrain Preview

Before battle, players see:
- Terrain type
- Elevation map
- Weather conditions
- Seasonal modifiers
- Possible attack routes
- Defender advantages

This allows strategic planning before deployment.

---

# Attacker Approach Choices

Attackers may choose:
- Frontal assault
- Flanking approach
- High-ground approach
- Forest approach
- River crossing
- Night attack

Each choice changes:
- Terrain modifiers
- Deployment zones
- Morale effects

---

# Notes
Terrain is a core tactical system. It interacts with combat, formations, supply, logistics, weather, and AI behavior. Players should always know what terrain they are fighting on before entering the battlefield.
