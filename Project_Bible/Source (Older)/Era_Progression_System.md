# Era Progression System
Medieval War — Systems/Era_Progression_System.md

## Overview
The Era Progression System defines how the campaign evolves over time.  
Eras unlock new units, buildings, guild tiers, religious orders, economic changes, cultural shifts, terrain usage changes, supply/logistics improvements, and historical events.

This expanded version integrates:
- Terrain & Climate System  
- Seasonal & Weather Systems  
- Cultural Geography System  
- Settlement Classification System  
- Province System  
- Global Map Systems  
- Campaign Sustainment & Logistics System  
- Faction Identity System  
- Agents  
- Guild Politics  
- Court Politics  
- AI Master Template  

Eras are modular and can be expanded or shifted depending on campaign scope.

---

# ============================
# ERA STRUCTURE
# ============================

Medieval War uses a **three‑era progression**:

### **Early Era (1000–1150)**
- Feudal fragmentation  
- Levy armies  
- Shieldwall warfare  
- Early knights  
- Limited crossbows  
- No gunpowder  
- Weak economies  
- Strong cultural identity  
- Strong terrain dependence  
- Primitive logistics  
- Low supply radius  
- Wooden castles  
- Basic guilds  
- Basic religious influence  
- High cultural resistance  
- High regional identity  
- Limited trade networks  
- Weather/seasonal effects severe  
- Ambush-heavy warfare  

### **High Era (1150–1300)**
- Feudal consolidation  
- Professional armies  
- Elite knights  
- Longbow development  
- Crossbow dominance  
- Greek fire (Byzantines)  
- Mamluk cavalry (Islamic factions)  
- Pike formations (Swiss)  
- Strong guilds  
- Religious orders  
- Stone castles  
- Advanced logistics  
- Expanded supply radius  
- Province edicts unlocked  
- Cultural pressure increases  
- Trade networks expand  
- Ports become economic hubs  
- Terrain specialization increases  
- Weather penalties reduced by infrastructure  
- AI becomes more aggressive and strategic  

### **Late Era (1300–1450+)**
- Gunpowder introduction  
- Plate armor  
- Pike‑and‑shot precursors  
- Halberdiers  
- Gendarmes  
- Landsknechts  
- Advanced siege engines  
- Strong economies  
- Centralized states  
- Citadels  
- Universities  
- Banking houses  
- Advanced guild tiers  
- Cultural blending increases  
- Religious orders decline  
- Logistics becomes highly efficient  
- Supply radius expands dramatically  
- Terrain penalties reduced by technology  
- Weather effects mitigated by infrastructure  
- AI becomes highly expansionist

# ============================
# ERA UNLOCK CONDITIONS
# ============================

Eras unlock through **global triggers**, **regional triggers**, **faction triggers**, and **province triggers**.

## **Global Triggers**
- Year threshold  
- Major historical events  
- Papal decrees  
- Crusades  
- Mongol invasions  
- Black Death (Late Era trigger)  
- Global trade expansion  
- Climate shifts (Medieval Warm Period → Little Ice Age)  

## **Regional Triggers**
- Cultural development  
- Trade expansion  
- Religious dominance  
- Technological diffusion  
- Province development  
- Settlement tier upgrades  
- Guild expansion  
- Terrain exploitation (e.g., longbow regions)  

## **Faction Triggers**
- Settlement tier upgrades (City, Castle, Fortress, Citadel)  
- Guild establishment  
- Religious unity  
- Economic strength  
- Diplomatic milestones  
- Military achievements  
- Cultural pressure thresholds  
- Logistics improvements  
- Agent network development  

## **Province Triggers**
- All minor settlements upgraded  
- Province edicts unlocked  
- Province cultural unity  
- Province economic specialization  
- Province supply network completed

# ============================
# ERA TRANSITION RULES
# ============================

### Transition from Early → High
Requires:
- Year ≥ 1150  
- At least one faction reaches Castle or City tier  
- At least one major event triggered (Crusade, Norman consolidation, etc.)  
- Trade routes established  
- Cultural pressure threshold reached  
- Province development milestone  
- Logistics improvement milestone  

### Transition from High → Late
Requires:
- Year ≥ 1300  
- Gunpowder discovered  
- Black Death event  
- Multiple factions reach Fortress or Citadel tier  
- Banking or university buildings constructed  
- Province edicts unlocked  
- Advanced guild tiers established  
- Cultural blending threshold reached
