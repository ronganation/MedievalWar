# Faction Identity System
Medieval War — Systems/Faction_Identity_System.md

## Overview
The Faction Identity System defines how each faction behaves, fights, grows, and interacts with the world.  
It establishes:
- Cultural identity  
- Military doctrine  
- Economic foundations  
- Terrain & climate affinities  
- Weather & seasonal affinities  
- Recruitment identity  
- Diplomatic tendencies  
- Special mechanics  
- Era progression identity  
- Province & settlement preferences  
- Cultural geography integration  
- Supply & logistics behavior  
- Agent & guild interactions  
- Court politics tendencies  
- AI personality templates  

This system is modular and expandable.  
Factions can be added, removed, renamed, or reworked as eras progress.

# ============================
# 1. Cultural Identity
# ============================

Cultural identity determines:
- Morale behavior  
- Fatigue tolerance  
- Cohesion  
- Recruitment style  
- Economic tendencies  
- Diplomatic compatibility  
- Terrain/weather affinity  
- Cultural pressure strength  
- Cultural resistance strength  
- Province homeland bonuses  
- Settlement preferences  
- Supply resistance  

### Anglo‑Saxon
- Shieldwall tradition  
- Strong cohesion  
- Levy‑based early armies  
- High discipline  
- Weak cavalry tradition  
- Strong farmland economy  
- Forest affinity  
- Rain/fog tolerant  
- Strong cultural pressure in Midlands/North  
- Strong town/village identity  

### Norman / Anglo‑Norman
- Feudal knights  
- Castle‑based recruitment  
- Strong heavy cavalry  
- Strong heavy infantry  
- High armor culture  
- Strong urban identity  
- Strong castle identity  
- Plains/farmland affinity  
- Strong road usage (logistics bonus)  
- Strong cultural pressure from cities  
- High guild presence  

### Gaelic (Scots, Irish)
- Highland infantry  
- Ambush tactics  
- High morale  
- Poor armor  
- Terrain specialists  
- Strong forest/marsh/hill affinity  
- Strong cultural resistance  
- Strong religious identity  
- Strong local supply  
- Weak long-distance supply  
- Strong village/fort identity  

### Norse (Future)
- Berserkers  
- Axe infantry  
- Strong raiding economy  
- High aggression  
- Weak cavalry  
- Snow/coastal affinity  
- Naval supply bonus  
- Strong cultural pressure along coasts  
- Strong winter identity  

### Byzantine (Future)
- Elite infantry  
- Greek fire  
- Cataphracts  
- Strong trade economy  
- High discipline  
- Urban identity  
- Heat tolerant  
- Strong cultural pressure in cities  
- Strong guild identity  

### Islamic (Future)
- Mamluk cavalry  
- Naphtha bombers  
- Desert affinity  
- Strong horse culture  
- High religious unity  
- Strong trade identity  
- Strong cultural pressure in desert regions  

### Swiss (Future)
- Pike supremacy  
- Mercenary economy  
- High cohesion  
- Strong urban identity  
- Mountain affinity  
- Strong guild identity  

### Italian (Venice, Genoa, Milan)
- Crossbow culture  
- Pavise units  
- Trade economy  
- Naval identity  
- Urban identity  
- Strong guild identity  

### Iberian (Castile, Aragon, Portugal)

- Strong cavalry

### French (Capetian France)
