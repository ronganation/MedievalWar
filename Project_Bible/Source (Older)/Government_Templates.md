Medieval War — Systems/Government_Templates.md

# Overview

Government Templates define how realms are organized politically.

Government determines:

- Succession
- Court Structure
- Nobility
- Vassal Relationships
- Religious Authority
- Recruitment
- Taxes
- Diplomacy
- Province Administration
- Law & Policy Options
- Court Politics
- Government Stability

Every faction uses a Government Template.

Government Templates evolve through Era Progression.

# Feudal Kingdom

Examples:

- England
- Scotland
- France
- Castile

---

## Characteristics

Strong nobles.

Weak central authority.

Large vassal networks.

---

## Advantages

- Strong levies
- Strong noble recruitment
- Strong regional identity

---

## Weaknesses

- Succession crises
- Noble rebellions
- Fragmentation risk

---

## Government Focus

Military and noble politics.

---

# Government Authority

Authority represents government strength.

Scale:

0-100

Higher authority provides:

- Loyalty
- Stability
- Tax efficiency

Lower authority increases:

- Rebels
- Succession crises
- Vassal disobedience

---

# Government Legitimacy

Legitimacy comes from:

- Dynasties
- Religion
- Culture
- Victories
- Stability

Low legitimacy increases:

- Pretenders
- Civil wars
- Court instability
