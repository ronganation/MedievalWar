# Guild Politics System
Medieval War — Systems/Guild_Politics_System.md

## Overview
The Guild Politics System governs the influence, competition, privileges, and political power of guilds within settlements and across factions.  
It blends Medieval II’s guild mechanics, CK3’s economic factions, Rome II’s provincial politics, and historical medieval guild structures.

This system interacts directly with:
- Diplomacy & Trade System  
- Economy System  
- Agent System  
- Loyalty & Public Order System  
- Court Politics System  
- Era Progression System  
- Faction Identity System  
- Event System  
- AI Master Template  

Guilds shape recruitment, trade, production, diplomacy, and internal politics.

# 13. Province-Level Guild Behavior

Guilds now operate at the **province level**, not just settlement level.

## 13.1 Province Guild Influence
Province Influence =  
Sum of all settlement guild influence + province trade value + province resource value.

High province influence grants:
- Province-wide economic bonuses  
- Province-wide recruitment bonuses  
- Province-wide trade bonuses  
- Province-wide loyalty bonuses  
- Province-wide public order bonuses  

## 13.2 Province Guild Dominance
If a guild dominates a province:
- It can demand province edicts  
- It can influence governor loyalty  
- It can influence province tax policy  
- It can influence cultural tolerance  
- It can influence religious tolerance  

## 13.3 Province Guild Conflicts
Province-level guild conflicts trigger:
- Province instability  
- Province economic collapse  
- Province trade wars  
- Province guild uprisings  

---

# 14. Settlement-Level Guild Behavior

Guilds now interact directly with settlement classification.

## 14.1 Cities
- Highest guild influence  
- Highest trade value  
- Highest economic bonuses  
- Highest political leverage  

## 14.2 Castles
- Military guild dominance  
- Armorer, Swordsmith, Bowyer, Horse Breeder, Siege Engineer guilds thrive  

## 14.3 Villages
- Artisan, Timber, Fishermen’s guilds dominate  
- Low political leverage  

## 14.4 Ports
- Navigator, Merchant, Banking, Caravansary guilds dominate  
- Naval trade bonuses  

## 14.5 Religious Centers
- Religious orders dominate  
- Religious diplomacy bonuses  

## 14.6 Resource Sites
- Mining, Timber, Horse Breeder guilds dominate  
- Resource monopolies

# 15. Law & Policy System Integration

Guilds respond directly to faction policies.

## 15.1 Merchant Privileges Policy
- Merchant Guild influence +20%  
- Banking House influence +20%  
- Trade hubs gain +15% income  
- Noble loyalty –10%  

## 15.2 Guild Autonomy Policy
- All guild influence +15%  
- Court politics instability +10%  
- Religious loyalty –10%  

## 15.3 Guild Suppression Policy
- Guild influence –25%  
- Public order +10%  
- Trade value –15%  
- Guild rebellion chance +20%

# 16. Vassalage & Subjugation Integration

Guilds influence vassal behavior.

## 16.1 Guild Pressure on Vassals
- Merchant Guild demands trade access  
- Banking House demands tribute  
- Religious Orders demand obedience  
- Military Guilds demand recruitment rights  

## 16.2 Guild-Sponsored Vassal Rebellions
If vassal loyalty < 40%:
- Guilds may fund rebellions  
- Guild agents may sabotage overlord supply  
- Guild agents may destabilize provinces  

## 16.3 Guild-Supported Vassal Integration
High guild influence increases integration chance.

---

# 17. War Weariness Integration

Guilds react strongly to prolonged war.

## 17.1 War Weariness Effects on Guilds
- Merchant Guild: –trade value  
- Banking House: +loan defaults  
- Artisan Guild: –production  
- Military Guilds: +recruitment demand  
- Religious Orders: +zealotry  

## 17.2 Guild Demands for Peace
At War Weariness ≥ 60:
- Merchant Guild demands peace  
- Banking House demands peace  
- Artisan Guild demands peace  

At War Weariness ≥ 80:
- Guilds may trigger uprisings  
- Guilds may destabilize court politics  
- Guilds may sabotage war effort
