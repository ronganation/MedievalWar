## Historical Individuals Registry
  
Medieval War — Systems/Historical_Individuals_Registry.md

## Overview
  
The Historical Individuals Registry maintains profiles for notable historical figures who may emerge during a Medieval War campaign.

The registry exists to support:

- Historical Emergence
- Character Development
- Historical Authenticity
- Emergent Storytelling
- Alternate History

The registry does not guarantee the appearance of any individual.

Historical figures emerge only when conditions support them.

## Core Philosophy
  
History does not create great individuals.

Conditions create opportunities.

Individuals seize opportunities.

The purpose of this registry is to:

- Define important historical figures
- Identify emergence conditions
- Support dynamic world simulation
- Preserve historical authenticity

without forcing predetermined outcomes.

### Macbeth
  
Category:

Political Figure

Military Figure

Culture:

Gaelic Scottish

Dynastic Association:

House Moray

Historical Role:

King of Alba

Military Leader

Regional Power Broker

#### Historical Status

Pre-Campaign Historical Figure

Macbeth does not appear as an active character during the Prototype Campaign.

His reign and downfall remain highly influential to contemporary Scottish politics.

#### Historical Importance

Macbeth represents one of the most significant alternative paths in Scottish political development.

His rise demonstrated the power of regional dynasties and the importance of Moray within the political structure of Scotland.

Although defeated before campaign start, his legacy continues to influence dynastic legitimacy, regional authority, and royal consolidation.

#### Historical Momentum Impact

Macbeth contributes to:

- Royal Consolidation Momentum
- House Moray Influence
- House Dunkeld Legitimacy
- Regional Authority Momentum
- Scottish Political Memory

The Scotland of 1061–1062 exists largely because of the political consequences of Macbeth's rise and fall.

#### Narrative Functions

- Historical Background
- Political Memory
- Moray Identity
- Alternative Scottish Kingship
- Legitimacy Debates

#### Dynamic Historical Equivalent

Future campaigns may generate:

"Macbeth-Type Regional Claimant"

when powerful regional rulers challenge centralized authority.

#### Related Entries

- Malcolm III
- House Dunkeld
- House Moray
- Scottish Consolidation Arc
- Historical Momentum

#### Notes

Macbeth serves as one of Scotland's most important pre-campaign historical figures.

He is not an active participant in the Prototype Campaign.

However, his legacy remains visible in Scottish dynastic politics, regional authority structures, and the continuing relationship between House Dunkeld and House Moray.
