# Law & Policy System
Medieval War — Systems/Law_and_Policy_System.md

## Overview
The Law & Policy System defines how rulers govern their realms through legal codes, administrative policies, religious mandates, cultural directives, military laws, and economic regulations.

It governs:
- Tax policy  
- Religious tolerance  
- Cultural tolerance  
- Military law  
- Merchant privileges  
- Guild privileges  
- Papal/Caliph/Patriarch compliance  
- Province edicts  
- Settlement policies  
- Court politics influence  
- Diplomacy influence  
- Loyalty & public order impact  
- Economic impact  

This system integrates with:
- Loyalty & Public Order System  
- Diplomacy & Trade System  
- Religious System  
- Court Politics System  
- Cultural Geography System  
- Settlement Classification System  
- Province System  
- Supply & Logistics System  
- Era Progression System  
- Agent System  
- Guild Politics System  
- War Weariness System  
- Event System  
- AI Master Template  

Law & Policy is the ruler’s primary tool for shaping internal stability and external diplomacy.

# ============================
# 1. Policy Categories
# ============================

Medieval War includes six major policy categories:

1. **Tax Policy**  
2. **Religious Policy**  
3. **Cultural Policy**  
4. **Military Law**  
5. **Merchant & Guild Privileges**  
6. **Foreign Compliance (Papal/Caliph/Patriarch)**  

Each category contains multiple policy options with unique bonuses and penalties.

---

# ============================
# 2. Tax Policy
# ============================

Tax Policy determines economic output and public order.

## 2.1 Tax Levels
### Low Taxes
- +Public Order  
- +Province Stability  
- –Income  
- +Cultural Loyalty  
- +Religious Loyalty  

### Normal Taxes
- Balanced  
- Standard income  
- Standard public order  

### High Taxes
- +Income  
- –Public Order  
- –Province Stability  
- –Cultural Loyalty  
- –Religious Loyalty  

### Oppressive Taxes
- ++Income  
- ––Public Order  
- ––Province Stability  
- +War Weariness  
- +Rebellion Chance  

## 2.2 Tax Policy Effects
- Settlement public order  
- Province stability  
- Cultural resistance  
- Religious resistance  
- Guild satisfaction  
- Noble satisfaction  
- Court politics alignment

# ============================
# 10. Court Politics Integration
# ============================

Policies are a major driver of court politics.

Effects:
- Nobles demand policy changes  
- Guilds demand privileges  
- Religious leaders demand compliance  
- Court Advisor manipulates policy outcomes  
- Policies trigger scandals  
- Policies trigger rebellions  
- Policies influence succession  

Policies are one of the top causes of political instability.

---

# ============================
# 11. Diplomacy Integration
# ============================

Policies heavily influence diplomacy.

Effects:
- Religious tolerance improves diplomacy  
- Cultural tolerance improves diplomacy  
- Merchant privileges improve trade diplomacy  
- Military oppression worsens diplomacy  
- Religious defiance worsens diplomacy  
- Papal/Caliph sanctions affect diplomacy  

Policies shape diplomatic blocs.

# ============================
# 13. War Weariness Integration
# ============================

Policies influence war weariness:

### Reduce War Weariness
- Cultural tolerance  
- Religious tolerance  
- Low taxes  
- Merchant privileges  

### Increase War Weariness
- Oppressive taxes  
- Cultural oppression  
- Religious oppression  
- Military oppression  

Policies can stabilize or destabilize wartime politics.

# 14. Era Integration
# ============================

- Weak policy enforcement  
- High instability  
- Frequent rebellions  

### High Era
- Stronger policy enforcement  
- Province edicts unlocked  
- Stronger guild influence  
- Stronger religious influence  

### Late Era
- Strong bureaucracy  
- Strong economic influence  
- Reduced rebellion success  
- Advanced policies unlocked  

Era progression affects:
- Policy effectiveness  
- Policy penalties  
- Policy events
