# Overview

The Mercenary Framework governs all hireable military forces that operate outside traditional recruitment structures.

Mercenaries represent:

- Professional soldiers
- Free companies
- Adventurer bands
- Exiled warriors
- Foreign specialists
- Religious companies
- Guild-sponsored companies

Mercenaries provide military flexibility at increased financial and political cost.

---

# Core Philosophy

Mercenaries solve problems.

Mercenaries are not permanent military foundations.

Advantages:

- Immediate availability
- No population cost
- Specialized troop types
- Regional military access

Disadvantages:

- Expensive
- Loyalty concerns
- Political consequences
- Limited availability
