# Mercenary System
Medieval War — Systems/Mercenary_System.md

## Overview
The Mercenary System governs the availability, recruitment, cost, loyalty, cultural identity, and political impact of mercenary forces across the Medieval War campaign.

It models:
- Region-based mercenaries  
- Culture-based mercenaries  
- Era-based mercenaries  
- Trade-route influenced availability  
- Guild-sponsored mercenaries  
- Religious-sponsored mercenaries  
- Event-driven mercenary uprisings  
- Mercenary loyalty  
- Mercenary betrayal  
- Mercenary price fluctuations  
- Mercenary diplomacy  

This system integrates with:
- Settlement Classification System  
- Province System  
- Supply System  
- Economy System  
- Cultural Geography System  
- Religious System  
- Diplomacy & Trade System  
- Guild Politics System  
- Court Politics System  
- Law & Policy System  
- Vassalage System  
- War Weariness System  
- Event System  
- Population System  
- Agent System  
- Era Progression System  
- AI Master Template  

Mercenaries are a dynamic, high-risk, high-reward component of medieval warfare.

# ============================
# 2. Mercenary Availability
# ============================

Mercenary availability depends on:

## 2.1 Region
Regions determine which mercenaries appear.

Examples:
- Highlands → Highland Raiders  
- England → Saxon Huscarls-for-Hire  
- Desert → Desert Skirmishers  
- Steppe → Horse Archers  

## 2.2 Culture
Cultural zones influence availability.

Examples:
- Norse culture → Viking mercenaries  
- Celtic culture → Raider mercenaries  
- Islamic culture → Ghazi mercenaries  

## 2.3 Era
Era progression unlocks new mercenaries.

### Early Era
- Raiders  
- Levy mercenaries  
- Light cavalry  
- Light infantry  

### High Era
- Professional swordsmen  
- Heavy cavalry  
- Crossbowmen  
- Religious order mercenaries  

### Late Era
- Gunpowder mercenaries  
- Condottieri  
- Elite heavy infantry  

## 2.4 Trade Routes
Trade routes increase availability of:
- Navigator’s Guild Marines  
- Merchant Guild mercenaries  
- Banking House Condottieri  

## 2.5 Guild Influence
High guild influence increases:
- Guild mercenary availability  
- Guild mercenary discounts  
- Guild mercenary loyalty  

## 2.6 Religious Influence
High religious influence increases:
- Religious mercenary availability  
- Religious zealotry  
- Religious loyalty

# ============================
# 5. Mercenary Loyalty
# ============================

Mercenaries track loyalty separately from faction troops.

## 5.1 Loyalty Factors
### Positive
- High pay  
- Successful battles  
- Guild sponsorship  
- Religious alignment  
- Cultural alignment  
- Low war weariness  

### Negative
- Low pay  
- High war weariness  
- Cultural mismatch  
- Religious mismatch  
- Supply collapse  
- Province instability  

## 5.2 Loyalty Effects
- High loyalty → stable mercenaries  
- Low loyalty → desertion  
- Very low loyalty → betrayal  

---

# ============================
# 6. Mercenary Betrayal
# ============================

Mercenaries may betray the faction.

## 6.1 Betrayal Triggers
- Unpaid wages  
- High war weariness  
- Oppressive policies  
- Cultural mismatch  
- Religious mismatch  
- Guild suppression  
- Province instability  

## 6.2 Betrayal Outcomes
- Mercenaries leave  
- Mercenaries join enemy  
- Mercenaries form rebellion  
- Mercenaries sack settlement
