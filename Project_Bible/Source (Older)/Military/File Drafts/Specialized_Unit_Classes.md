# Specialized Unit Classes
Medieval War — Systems/Specialized_Unit_Classes.md

## Overview
Specialized Units represent unique battlefield roles, mechanics, or historical capabilities that do not fit standard infantry, cavalry, ranged, or siege categories.  
They often require special recruitment conditions, appear only in certain regions or eras, or exist as mercenaries.

Specialized Units may:
- Use unique weapons or mechanics  
- Cause morale shock  
- Ignore armor  
- Provide support effects  
- Have extreme strengths or weaknesses  
- Require special terrain or weather conditions  
- Be faction‑exclusive or era‑exclusive  

These units enrich faction identity and allow the AI to generate historically grounded, flavorful rosters.

# ============================
# SPECIALIZED CAVALRY
# ============================

## Camel Riders (Future Era)
Camel units frighten horses and excel in desert climates.

### Subtypes
- Camel Spearmen (melee)
- Camel Archers (mounted archers)
- Camel Skirmishers (mounted javelins)

### Attributes
- Frighten horses  
- Excellent desert performance  
- Poor forest/mountain performance  
- Medium armor  
- High endurance  

---

## Elephant Units (Future Era)
Massive shock units with devastating charge power.

### Subtypes
- Elephant Archers  
- Elephant Spearmen  
- Elephant Tower Units (late era)

### Attributes
- Devastating charge  
- High morale impact  
- Extremely vulnerable in forests, marsh, mountains  
- High supply consumption  
- High attrition risk  

---

## War Hounds (Relevant to Britain)
Fast, morale‑shocking animal units used by Celtic factions.

### Attributes
- High speed  
- High morale impact  
- Low armor  
- Good in forests and hills  
- Poor in heavy terrain  
- Specialized cavalry class

# ============================
# SPECIALIZED RANGED / FIRE UNITS
# ============================

## Greek Fire Throwers (Future Era — Byzantines)
Elite incendiary infantry using siphon‑based flame projectors.

### Attributes
- Short‑range incendiary attack  
- Armor‑ignoring damage  
- Massive morale shock  
- Dangerous to use (risk of self‑burn)  
- Excellent in urban and siege terrain  
- Poor in rain and forests  
- Requires Greek Fire Workshop  

---

## Naphtha Bombers (Future Era — Middle East)
Throwable fire bombs used by Islamic factions.

### Attributes
- Short‑range explosive fire attack  
- Causes panic  
- Effective against armored units  
- Dangerous to use  
- Poor in rain  
- Good in urban terrain  

---

## Pavise Crossbowmen (Western Europe)
Crossbowmen equipped with large pavise shields.

### Subtypes
- Standard Pavise Crossbowmen  
- Elite Pavise (e.g., Genoese Pavise Crossbowmen)

### Attributes
- High missile defense  
- Slow reload  
- Excellent in sieges  
- Excellent in urban terrain  
- Poor in forests  
- Heavy armor variant of crossbowmen

# ============================
# SPECIALIZED INFANTRY
# ============================

## Berserkers (Future Era — Norse)
Frenzied shock infantry with uncontrollable behavior.

### Attributes
- High attack  
- High morale  
- Low defense  
- Uncontrollable charge behavior  
- Good in forests and hills  
- Poor in urban terrain  

---

## Shieldwall Specialists (Anglo‑Saxon / Norse)
Elite infantry trained specifically for shieldwall cohesion.

### Attributes
- High defense  
- High cohesion  
- Low fatigue  
- Poor charge  
- Bonuses in hills and urban terrain  
- Penalties in marsh/swamp  
- Can be a specialized variant of Medium or Heavy Infantry  

---

## Swiss Pikemen (Mercenary / Late Era)
Elite pike formations famous across Europe.

### Attributes
- Extremely high cohesion  
- Extremely high anti‑cavalry capability  
- Excellent in plains, steppe, urban  
- Poor in forests, marsh, mountains  
- Often available as mercenaries  
- Late era or high era unit  

---

## Varangian Guard (Future Era — Byzantines)
Elite shock infantry wielding two‑handed axes.

### Attributes
- High attack  
- High morale  
- Good in urban and siege terrain  
- Poor in forests and marsh  
- Heavy armor  
- Faction‑exclusive  

---

## Mamluk Elite (Future Era — Egypt)
Elite cavalry or infantry depending on era.

### Attributes
- High discipline  
- High morale  
- Excellent desert performance  
- Strong charge (cavalry variant)  
- Strong melee (infantry variant)  
- Faction‑exclusive

# ============================
# SPECIALIZED SUPPORT UNITS
# ============================

## Horn Blowers / War Drummers
Morale‑boosting support units.

### Attributes
- Increase nearby morale  
- Increase cohesion  
- Reduce fatigue  
- No direct combat role  
- Vulnerable if isolated  

---

## Sappers / Miners
Siege specialists used to undermine walls.

### Attributes
- Dig tunnels  
- Undermine fortifications  
- Weak in combat  
- Specialized siege infantry  
- Often available only during sieges  
- Dangerous work → high attrition

# ============================
# SPECIALIZED UNIT RULES
# ============================

### Recruitment
Specialized units may require:
- Unique buildings  
- Unique faction traits  
- Unique cultural conditions  
- Specific eras  
- Specific regions  
- Mercenary availability  

### Terrain Interaction
Specialized units have unique terrain modifiers defined in:
**Systems/Terrain_Combat_Modifiers.md**

### Weather Interaction
Fire‑based units suffer penalties in:
- Rain  
- Heavy rain  
- Thunderstorms  
- Snow  

### Morale Effects
Some specialized units cause:
- Fear  
- Panic  
- Shock  
- Cohesion disruption  

### AI Generation Rules
The AI Master Template will:
- Recognize specialized units  
- Apply correct terrain/weather modifiers  
- Apply correct recruitment conditions  
- Apply correct era restrictions  
- Apply correct faction exclusivity

# Notes
Specialized units enrich faction identity and provide unique tactical options.  
They are intentionally rare, powerful, and flavorful.  
This system integrates with Terrain, Weather, Seasonal Effects, Recruitment, and AI Generation Rules.
