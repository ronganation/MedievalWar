# Recruitment System

## Overview
Recruitment determines how factions raise armies.  
It depends on settlement level, buildings, resources, economy strength, supply lines, population, loyalty, and regional stability.

This system follows a **Medieval II–style dual settlement progression**:
- **Economic Settlements** (Village → Town → Large Town → City → Huge City)  
- **Military Castles** (Motte & Bailey → Castle → Fortress → Citadel)

Economic settlements generate wealth and population.  
Castles generate professional troops and elite units.

All settlements can build any building type as long as the settlement level allows it.  
There are **no restrictive building slots**.

# Settlement Level (Dual Progression System)

## Economic Path (Wealth & Population)
### Village (Tier I)
- Militia  
- Levy infantry  
- Basic skirmishers  
- No cavalry  

### Town (Tier II)
- Basic infantry  
- Basic archers  
- Light skirmishers  
- Limited regional units  

### Large Town (Tier III)

- Advanced archers  
- Basic cavalry

### City (Tier IV)
- Professional infantry  
- Heavy infantry  
- Crossbowmen  
- Heavy cavalry (limited)  
- Guild units (full tier)  

### Huge City (Tier V)
- Elite infantry (limited)  
- Advanced crossbowmen  
- Advanced guild units  
- Religious order recruitment (limited)

# Building Requirements (Medieval II Style)

## Military Buildings
**Barracks:** Infantry (militia → professional → elite)  
**Armory:** Heavy infantry, elite infantry  
**Stables:** Light cavalry, heavy cavalry  
**Knightly Hall:** Norman Knights, elite cavalry  
**Siege Workshop:** Rams, towers, catapults, trebuchets  

## Economic Buildings
**Farmstead:** Militia, levy units  
**Market:** Basic infantry, skirmishers  
**Guild Hall:** Guild units (Swordsmith, Archery, Merchant)  
**Blacksmith:** Units requiring iron  

## Religious Buildings
**Chapel / Church / Cathedral:**  
- Religious order units (depending on tier)  
- Recruitment bonuses  
- Loyalty bonuses  

## Strategic Buildings
**Castle / Stone Keep:** Elite infantry, Huscarls, Norman Foot Knights  
**Port:** Naval supply, coastal recruitment, mercenaries  
**Watchtower:** Faster reinforcement, regional units  
**Mason’s Guild:** Construction bonuses, siege defense bonuses

# Recruitment Time

### Base Times
- Militia: Fast  
- Basic Infantry: Medium  
- Advanced Infantry: Slow  
- Cavalry: Slow  
- Elite Units: Very Slow  
- Siege Units: Slow  

### Recruitment Time Modifiers (Expanded)
Recruitment slows when:
- Loyalty is low  
- Supply lines are disrupted  
- Region was recently raided  
- Population is low  
- Economy is weak  
- Settlement tier is low  
- Enemy agents sabotage recruitment  
- Religious unrest is high  
- Governor has negative traits  

Recruitment speeds up when:
- Loyalty is high  
- Supply lines are stable  
- Population is high  
- Economy is strong  
- Guild buildings are present  
- Governor has positive traits  
- Friendly agents assist recruitment  
- Religious unity is high

# Reinforcement System

## Reinforcement Conditions
Armies reinforce faster when:
- Supply lines are stable  
- Region loyalty is high  
- Economy is strong  
- Settlement tier is high  
- Roads and ports are unblocked  
- Religious unity is high  
- Governor traits are positive  

Reinforcement slows when:
- Rebels block roads  
- Ports are blockaded  
- Raids occur  
- Population is low  
- War exhaustion is high  
- Religious unrest is high  
- Enemy agents disrupt supply

# Guild Recruitment (Updated and Expanded)

Guilds represent professional organizations that influence recruitment, economy, religion, exploration, espionage, and military specialization.  
Guilds may provide units, recruitment bonuses, economic bonuses, or strategic advantages.

Guild units require:
- Guild Hall  
- City or Castle  
- Strong economy  

## Swordsmith Guild
- Improves swordsmen attack and morale  
- Faster recruitment for sword infantry  
- Reduced cost for sword units  
- City / Huge City required  
- Barracks + Blacksmith synergy  

## Archery Guild
- Improves archer accuracy and reload  
- Faster recruitment for archers  
- Reduced cost for archer units  
- City / Huge City required  
- Timber resource synergy  

## Merchant Guild
- Unlocks Merchant Cavalry Militia  
- Trade bonuses  
- Increased mercenary availability  
- Town / Large Town / City  
- Market + Port synergy  

## Horse Breeder’s Guild
- Faster cavalry recruitment  
- Reduced cavalry cost  
- Improved cavalry replenishment  
- Stables + Horses synergy  
- Town / Large Town / Castle  

## Explorer’s Guild
- Increased map visibility  
- Faster agent movement  
- Unlocks “Explorer Scouts” (light recon unit)  
- Port + Market synergy  
- Town / City / Huge City  

## Theologian’s Guild
- Religious influence bonuses  
- Reduced religious unrest  
- Unlocks low‑tier religious auxiliaries  
- Chapel / Church / Cathedral synergy  
- City / Huge City  

## Espionage Guild
- Enhances Shadow Agents (Spy/Assassin hybrid)  
- Faster recruitment for espionage units  
- Increased sabotage success  
- Increased infiltration success  
- Improved counter‑espionage  
- Reduced cost for espionage units  
- Watchtower + Market synergy  
- Town / Large Town / City  

## Mason’s Guild
- Construction bonuses  
- Siege defense bonuses  
- Reduced building time  
- No direct unit recruitment  
- Castle / Fortress / Citadel  

## Ironsmith Guild (Blacksmith Guild)
- Improves armor quality for infantry and cavalry  
- Reduced cost for armored units  
- Faster recruitment for heavy infantry  
- Synergy with Iron resource  
- Barracks + Armory synergy  
- Town / Large Town / City / Castle  

## Woodsmen Guild
- Improves siege equipment construction speed  
- Reduced cost for siege units  
- Faster recruitment for archers and skirmishers  
- Timber resource synergy  
- Siege Workshop + Archery Range synergy  
- Town / Large Town / City  

## Tanner / Saddler Guild
- Improves leather armor quality  
- Reduced cost for lightly armored units  
- Faster recruitment for light cavalry  
- Synergy with Leather resource (future)  
- Stables + Market synergy  
- Town / Large Town / Castle
