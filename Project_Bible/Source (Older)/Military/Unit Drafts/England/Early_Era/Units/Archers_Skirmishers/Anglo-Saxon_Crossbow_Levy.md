Summary Description:
Anglo-Saxon Crossbow Levy represents England's early and uneven adoption of crossbows. These militia crews can threaten armored targets, but they lack Norman training standards and show weaker accuracy, slower reload drills, and lower battlefield confidence.

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Early Crossbow Adoption - Access to armor-piercing bolts before full professional integration
- Low Training - Poor reload drill and weak formation discipline
- Low Accuracy - Inconsistent hit rate compared with true Norman crossbow units
- Slow Reload - Reduced sustained missile output

Strengths:
- Early access to armor-piercing missile fire
- Useful in static defense and narrow approaches
- Cheaper than professional crossbow formations

Weaknesses:
- Lower attack, armor, and morale than Norman Crossbowmen
- Slow reload and inconsistent accuracy
- Very weak in melee and vulnerable when flanked

Tactics (player):
- Place behind spear lines in defensive setups
- Focus dense or armored targets at short-to-medium range
- Avoid mobile skirmish exchanges and exposed flanks

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Holds prepared positions and fires in support of infantry lines
- Prioritizes armored infantry over fast skirmishers
- Retreats if pressured by cavalry or direct melee threats
