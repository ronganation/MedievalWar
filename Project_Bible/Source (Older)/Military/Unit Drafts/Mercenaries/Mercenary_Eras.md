# Mercenary Era Unlock System

## Early Era (1000–1150)
Available:
- Mercenary Spearmen
- Mercenary Swordsmen
- Mercenary Archers
- Mercenary Javelinmen
- Irish Kern Mercenaries
- Welsh Spearmen
- Norse Raiders
- Viking Axemen
- Saxon Huscarls
- Highland Rabble Javelinmen

Unavailable:
- Flemish Pikemen
- Flemish Crossbowmen
- Norman Mercenary Knights
- Breton Men-at-Arms

## High Era (1150–1300)
Unlocks:
- Breton Light Cavalry
- Breton Men-at-Arms
- Flemish Crossbowmen
- Flemish Pikemen
- Galloglaich
- Irish Hobelar Mercenaries

## Late Era (1300+)
Unlocks:
- Norman Mercenary Knights
- Elite crossbow mercenaries
- Heavy pike mercenaries

## Special Event Unlocks
### Crusades
- Breton Light Cavalry
- Breton Men-at-Arms
- Norman Mercenary Knights

### Mongol Arrival
- Heavy anti-cavalry mercenaries become more common
