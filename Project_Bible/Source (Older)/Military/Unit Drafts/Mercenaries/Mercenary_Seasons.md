# Seasonal Mercenary Availability

## Spring (Campaign Season Begins)
- High availability of light infantry
- High availability of javelin skirmishers
- Medium availability of archers
- Low availability of heavy infantry
- Low availability of cavalry

## Summer (War Season)
- High availability of all infantry types
- High availability of cavalry
- Medium availability of elite units
- Low availability of crossbowmen (harvest season)

## Autumn (Retreat Season)
- Medium availability of infantry
- Low availability of cavalry
- High availability of raiders (Norse, Highland, Irish)
- High availability of mercenary archers

## Winter (Scarcity Season)
- Very low availability of all mercenaries
- Only raiders and desperate sellswords appear
- Heavy infantry almost never available
