## Description
Highland Rabble Javelinmen are lightly equipped Scottish skirmishers drawn from poor Highland clans. Armed with javelins and small targe shields, they excel in hit-and-run tactics across rugged terrain. Though poorly armored, their speed and armor-piercing javelins make them effective light mercenaries.
