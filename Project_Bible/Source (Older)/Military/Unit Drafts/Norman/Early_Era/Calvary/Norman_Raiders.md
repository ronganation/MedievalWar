Summary Description:
Norman Raiders are light cavalry used for scouting, pursuit, and hit-and-run attacks rather than prolonged shock combat. They are faster and more flexible than heavier Norman cavalry, making them ideal for raiding enemy camps, harassing exposed troops, and screening larger formations.

Tactics (player):
- Use to screen your army and raid vulnerable supplies or flanks

- Support larger cavalry or infantry by forcing enemy movement and morale loss

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Circle around enemy flanks and strike exposed targets
- Target damaged units, stragglers, and supply carts
- Retreat once enemy heavy cavalry or disciplined infantry engages
