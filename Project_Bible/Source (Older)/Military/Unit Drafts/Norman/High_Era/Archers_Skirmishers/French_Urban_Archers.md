Summary Description:
French Urban Archers are the Norman faction's high-era urban missile branch. They are built around town recruitment, street fighting, and defensive support, combining practical archery with the more organized civic military culture of the French-influenced path.

Equipment & Appearance:
- Primary weapon: Bow with field arrows for sustained support fire
- Secondary weapon: Sword or knife
- Armor: Padded armor with some mail reinforcement
- Shield: Small shield for close defense
- Typical appearance: town-raised Norman archers with a more civic and urban look than field levies

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Urban Archers - Better performance in towns and fortified streets
- Volley Fire - Useful for repeated ranged support and suppression
- Defensive Missile Troops - Stronger when defending prepared positions
- French Influence - Represents urban and civic military organization
- Transitional Unit - Bridges the early levy archers into the hybrid path

Strengths:
- Strong in urban defense and settlement fighting
- Good suppression fire against exposed infantry
- Reliable ranged support in mixed terrain

Weaknesses:
- Weaker than specialist crossbowmen against armor
- Vulnerable if caught in prolonged melee
- Less effective in open cavalry-heavy battles

Tactics (player):
- Defend walls, gates, streets, and choke points
- Support infantry by softening exposed targets before contact
- Keep them behind defensive lines where possible

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Prefer defended firing positions and urban terrain
- Target lightly armored infantry and support troops
- Retreat toward allied infantry if cavalry closes quickly
