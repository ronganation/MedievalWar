Summary Description:
Norman Crossbowmen are the High Era expression of Norman ranged professionalism. Drawing on earlier crossbow traditions and the growing importance of disciplined missile fire in castle warfare and field battles, they provide strong ranged pressure against armored opponents and heavy infantry. Their crossbows are especially valuable in defensive battles and in support of Norman shock cavalry.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
By the High Era, Norman armies increasingly valued disciplined missile troops for siege work, fortified positions, and battlefield support. Crossbowmen offered a practical bridge between earlier levy archers and more specialized late-medieval infantry, giving Norman commanders a reliable way to pressure armored opponents and hold key ground.
