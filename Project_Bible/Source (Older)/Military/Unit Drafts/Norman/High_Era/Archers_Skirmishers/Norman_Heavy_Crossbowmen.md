Summary Description:
Norman Heavy Crossbowmen are the pure Norman answer to the High Era missile split. They are slower and more deliberate than ordinary crossbowmen, but their heavier weapons and disciplined fire make them a powerful answer to armored enemies and fortified positions.

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):

Strengths:

Tactics (player):

- Keep them protected from cavalry and skirmishers

Tactics (AI behavior):

- Support the main line from behind cover or fortifications
- Withdraw to a stable line if threatened in melee
