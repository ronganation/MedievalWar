Summary Description:
French Chivalric Knights are the continental prestige cavalry of the Norman High Era split. They emphasize formal knighthood, drilled shock action, and aristocratic battlefield authority while remaining part of the Norman military tradition rather than a separate faction identity.

Core Stats (game values):
- Melee Attack (primary): Lance - 10 (Armor-piercing on charge)
- Melee Attack (secondary): Sword - 7
- Charge Bonus: 12 (very high)
- Melee Defense / Defense Skill: 6
- Armor: 8 (Mail / Reinforced Harness)
- Shield Block: 4 (Kite Shield / Large Shield)
- Hit Points (per soldier): 2
- Morale: 9 (Very High)
- Speed: Fast
- Fatigue Resistance: High

Equipment & Appearance:
- Primary weapon: Heavy lance, supported by sword work in melee
- Secondary weapon: Sword
- Armor: High-quality mail and reinforced cavalry harness
- Shield: Kite shield or large shield
- Typical appearance: elite Norman mounted knights with continental feudal styling and confident battlefield bearing

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Devastating Charge - High-impact shock attack on enemy lines
- Chivalric Discipline - Better morale and cohesion than lighter cavalry
- Anti-Armor Specialist - Strong against armored infantry and heavy targets
- Shock Cavalry - Excels at breaking morale and formation
- French Influence - Reflects more formal continental knightly doctrine

Strengths:
- Excellent shock impact and morale breaking
- Strong anti-armor and anti-infantry performance
- Highly effective in open ground and decisive engagements

Weaknesses:
- Vulnerable to disciplined spear lines and pikes
- Suffers in dense woods and broken terrain
- Very expensive and demanding to maintain

Tactics (player):
- Use as the decisive striking force in open terrain
- Commit against routed, disordered, or weakened infantry
- Protect flanks and avoid terrain that destroys charge momentum

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Seek open ground for impact and charge exposed targets
- Prioritize breaking enemy infantry or armored targets
- Avoid sustained combat in dense woods or river crossings
