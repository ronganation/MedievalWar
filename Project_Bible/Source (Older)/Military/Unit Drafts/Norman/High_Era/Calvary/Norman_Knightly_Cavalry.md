Summary Description:
Norman Knightly Cavalry are the standard high-era mounted knights of the Norman martial tradition. They are less extravagant than the great southern aristocratic lances of the Sicilio-Norman elite, but they are still disciplined, well-armored, and highly capable in the open field. Their role is to exploit openings, pressure enemy lines, and support the broader Norman battle plan.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
