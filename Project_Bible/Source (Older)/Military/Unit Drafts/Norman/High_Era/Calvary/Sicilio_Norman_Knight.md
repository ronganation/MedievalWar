Summary Description:
Sicilio-Norman Knights represent the elite cavalry tradition forged by the Norman expansion into southern Italy. Their heavy armor, powerful lances, and disciplined shock tactics let them break infantry lines, disrupt formations, and win key engagements when committed at the right moment. They are the high-era expression of Norman knightly power.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
Sicilio-Norman Knights embody the Norman state’s capacity to turn conquest into military prestige. Descended from the same martial culture that produced Norman horsemen in the north, they became a symbol of the wealth, discipline, and ambition of the Norman rulers of southern Italy. Their charges were feared but their power depended on ground, timing, and the discipline of their own line.
