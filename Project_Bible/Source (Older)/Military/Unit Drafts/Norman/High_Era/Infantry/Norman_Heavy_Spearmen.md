Summary Description:
Norman Heavy Spearmen are the professional, battle-tested infantry of the High Era Norman tradition. Armed with heavy spears, sturdy shields, and stronger discipline than earlier Norman line troops, they serve as the backbone of Norman battlefield formations and are especially effective when defending ground, repelling cavalry, and advancing in close order.
