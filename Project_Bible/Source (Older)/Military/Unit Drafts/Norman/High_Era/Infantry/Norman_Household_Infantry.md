Summary Description:
Norman Household Infantry are the disciplined infantry of the Norman household tradition. They are not as glamorous as the great mounted knights, but they form the hard core of a lord’s personal retinue and the backbone of more martial Norman campaigns. They are dependable fighters with strong shield discipline and a vigorous commitment to holding ground.
