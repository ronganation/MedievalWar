Summary Description:
Norman Swordsmen are the High Era infantry of the French-leaning Norman path. They carry broad-bladed two-edged swords, often of substantial length and with crucifix guards and wheel-shaped pommels, giving them a strongly Norman martial identity. Their mail, shields, and disciplined line fighting make them reliable close-combat fighters in both field and town battle.
