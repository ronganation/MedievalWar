## Description
Town Militia form the backbone of early urban defense. Armed with basic spears and minimal training, they are more reliable than peasants but still prone to breaking under sustained pressure. Their strength lies in numbers and their familiarity with city fighting.

## Lineage (Era Evolution)
- High Era: City Spearmen  
- Late Era: Urban Guard / Armed Burghers
