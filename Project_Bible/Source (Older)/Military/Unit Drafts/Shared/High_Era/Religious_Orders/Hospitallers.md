Summary Description:
The Hospitallers are disciplined military brothers of the Order of Saint John, famed for their defensive role in the Holy Land and their steadfastness in both offense and defense. They are elite heavy cavalry suited to guarding strongholds, supporting crusading armies, and breaking enemy lines when needed.

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Heavy Cavalry — Strong charge impact and heavy armor penetration
- Knightly Charge — Gains major offensive bonus when charging infantry or cavalry
- Holy Order — High morale and cohesion, reinforced by religious unity and discipline
- Elite Cavalry — Superior training and stability compared to ordinary feudal knights
- Defensive Escort — Strong in garrison and escort missions, particularly around fortifications
- High Morale — Very resistant to panic and reliable in prolonged melee

Strengths:
- Strong shock and escort capability
- Excellent morale and cohesion
- Effective in defensive and offensive operations around fortifications

Weaknesses:
- Very expensive and slow to recruit
- Vulnerable in dense terrain or broken ground
- Requires strong logistics and religious infrastructure

Tactics (player):
- Use to guard important objectives, castles, or supply routes

- Provide a secure escort for important commanders or siege operations

Tactics (AI behavior):

- Seek open terrain for charges against infantry and weakened cavalry
