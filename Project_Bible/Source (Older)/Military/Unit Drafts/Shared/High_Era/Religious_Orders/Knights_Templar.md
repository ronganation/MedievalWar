Faction: Religious Orders / Crusader States

Era: High

Classification:
- Primary: Cavalry

- Tier: Elite (religious-order shock cavalry)

Unit Size (men per unit):
- Standard: 60
- Small: 30
- Large: 120

Role / Combat Role:
- Shock cavalry for decisive charges
- Anti-infantry and anti-armor breakthrough
- Elite escort and battlefield prestige unit

Summary Description:
The Knights Templar are elite mounted warriors of a holy order, famed for discipline, religious zeal, and devastating mounted charges. They are among the most formidable heavy cavalry available in the High Era, combining superior armor, strong morale, and the tactical cohesion expected of a professional military brotherhood.

Core Stats (game values):
- Melee Attack (primary): Lance — 11 (Armor-piercing on charge)
- Melee Attack (secondary): Sword — 7
- Charge Bonus: 14
- Melee Defense / Defense Skill: 7
- Armor: 8 (Mail + Partial Plate)
- Shield Block: 3 (Heater Shield / Knight Shield)
- Hit Points (per soldier): 1
- Morale: 10 (Very High)
- Speed: Medium
- Fatigue Resistance: High

Equipment & Appearance:
- Primary weapon: Lance for shock charge and mounted impact
- Secondary weapon: Sword for close-quarters combat
- Armor: Mail hauberk with partial plate and reinforced gauntlets
- Shield: Large heater shield bearing the order’s insignia
- Typical appearance: White mantles over armor, red cross emblems, disciplined formation, mounted with warhorses and banners

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Heavy Cavalry — High charge impact and strong armor penetration when attacking in formation
- Knightly Charge — Gains major offensive bonus when charging and striking enemy line infantry or cavalry
- Holy Order — High morale and strong cohesion under pressure; benefits from religious unity and order discipline
- Elite Cavalry — Superior training and stability compared to ordinary feudal knights
- Anti-Armor — Excellent at breaking through infantry and armored targets when used correctly
- High Morale — Better resistance to panic and more reliable in prolonged melee

Strengths:
- Devastating shock charge and anti-armor potential
- Excellent morale and cohesion under pressure
- Strong against infantry and armored targets when used in the right terrain

Weaknesses:
- Very expensive and slow to recruit
- Vulnerable if caught in dense terrain or forced to fight in unfavorable ground
- Requires strong economy, religious unity, and high-tier infrastructure to field effectively

Tactics (player):
- Use as a decisive shock reserve to break enemy lines or destroy weakened infantry blocks
- Charge enemy cavalry or entrenched infantry on open ground where their momentum can be maximized
- Protect flanks and support heavier infantry with disciplined mounted pressure

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Seek open terrain and favorable charge angles
- Target weak infantry blocks, exposed artillery, or routed enemy cavalry
- Hold formation under pressure and exploit morale advantage once the enemy begins to falter

Cultural Variants & Availability:
- Base variant: Knights Templar — elite Holy Order cavalry available to Crusader factions with strong religious unity
- Related variant: Hospitaller / St. John’s knights — similar role, slightly more defensive and defensive-campaign oriented
- Regional variant: Crusader states may field order cavalry with local heraldry and slightly different morale or upkeep depending on province and ideology

Lineage / Evolution (Early → High):
- Early Era: Order household knights / crusader horsemen — early mounted retainers and elite household cavalry
- High Era: Knights Templar — this unit; the full professional order cavalry of the High Era

Upgrades & Variants:
- Possible upgrade path: prestige order cavalry with improved armor, stronger charge, and higher morale
- Variants: Templar lancers, Templar sergeants, and crusading order cavalry with different heraldic banners

Animation / Sound / Model Notes:
- Suggested animation set: Charge, impact, disciplined wheeling, mounted sword strikes, banner waving
- Sound set: War horns, heavy hoofbeats, banner and shield impact, battle cries
- Model notes: Large heavy cavalry model with mounted knight, white mantle, red cross, and visibly reinforced armor

Event Integration:
- Appears in crusading campaigns, holy war events, and defensive actions around sacred strongholds
- Can be used during “Defend the Holy Site”, “Relief of the Siege”, and “Knightly Muster” events

Objective Integration:
- Used in objectives such as “Break the Enemy Line”, “Hold the Ridge”, and “Defend the Holy Stronghold”

Designer Notes / Balancing:
- Position as a premium elite shock cavalry unit rather than a general-purpose cavalry unit
- Keep recruitment expensive and restricted so the unit remains rare and impactful
- Ensure their power is strongest in open terrain and against infantry-heavy enemies

Historical Flavor / Lore:
The Knights Templar were one of the most famous military orders of the High Middle Ages, famed for their discipline, piety, and battlefield prowess. Their role in crusading warfare and their reputation for spiritual and martial discipline make them a natural fit as elite order cavalry in Medieval War.
