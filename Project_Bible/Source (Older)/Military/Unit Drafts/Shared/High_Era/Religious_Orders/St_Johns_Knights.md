Role / Combat Role:

Summary Description:
St. John’s Knights are elite mounted warriors of the Order of Saint John, known for blending military skill with a strong tradition of protection and service. They are powerful cavalry units who can both hold defensive positions and deliver devastating mounted blows when the moment demands it.

Core Stats (game values):
- Melee Attack (primary): Lance — 10 (Armor-piercing on charge)
- Melee Attack (secondary): Sword — 6
- Charge Bonus: 13
- Melee Defense / Defense Skill: 7
- Armor: 8 (Mail + Partial Plate)
- Shield Block: 3 (Heater Shield / Knight Shield)
- Hit Points (per soldier): 1
- Morale: 10 (Very High)
- Speed: Medium
- Fatigue Resistance: High

Equipment & Appearance:
- Primary weapon: Lance for mounted shock attack
- Secondary weapon: Sword for close-quarters combat
- Armor: Mail hauberk with plate reinforcement and strong protective gear
- Shield: Large heater shield with white cross emblem
- Typical appearance: White mantles or surcoats, red cross emblems, disciplined order formations, strong ceremonial and battlefield presence

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Heavy Cavalry — Strong charge impact and heavy armor penetration
- Knightly Charge — Gains major offensive bonus when charging infantry or cavalry
- Holy Order — High morale and cohesion, reinforced by religious unity and discipline
- Elite Cavalry — Superior training and stability compared to ordinary feudal knights
- Defensive Escort — Strong in garrison and escort missions near hospitals or fortifications
- High Morale — Very resistant to panic and reliable in prolonged melee

Strengths:
- Strong shock and escort capability
- Excellent morale and cohesion
- Effective in both offensive charges and defensive reinforcement

Weaknesses:
- Very expensive and slow to recruit
- Vulnerable in dense terrain or broken ground
- Requires strong logistics and religious infrastructure

Tactics (player):
- Use to protect key strongholds, supply lines, and important commanders
- Break enemy infantry lines on open ground with disciplined charges
- Support defensive actions while remaining ready for counterattack

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Prioritize protection of objectives and strongholds
- Seek open terrain for charges against infantry and weakened cavalry
- Hold formation and counterattack when the enemy begins to falter

Designer Notes / Balancing:
- Position as a premium elite cavalry unit with strong defensive and escort utility
- Keep recruitment expensive and restricted so the unit remains rare and impactful
- Ensure their power is strongest in open terrain and in the defense of key positions

Historical Flavor / Lore:
St. John’s Knights were renowned for combining military duty with protective service and charitable missions. Their presence in the Holy Land and their strong reputation for order and discipline make them compelling as a high-tier religious-order cavalry unit in Medieval War.
