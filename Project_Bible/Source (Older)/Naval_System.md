# Overview

The Naval System governs maritime power across the Medieval War campaign.

Naval power affects:

- Trade
- Supply
- Exploration
- Warfare
- Diplomacy
- Piracy
- Coastal Invasions
- Religious Expeditions
- Mercenary Transport

Navies are strategic assets rather than independent mini-games.

---

# Core Philosophy

Control of the sea creates:

- Wealth
- Mobility
- Supply
- Influence

A strong navy allows a faction to project power beyond its homeland.

---

# Naval Roles

Fleets serve several purposes:

1. Transport
2. Supply
3. Trade Protection
4. Blockade
5. Piracy Suppression
6. Exploration
7. Fleet Combat
8. Amphibious Warfare

# Ports

Ports are naval recruitment centers.

Port Progression:

Fishing Port
→ Trade Port
→ Naval Port
→ Grand Harbor

Ports influence:

- Fleet recruitment
- Trade
- Naval supply

---

# Naval Recruitment

Fleet recruitment depends on:

- Port level
- Resources
- Technology
- Economy
- Government

---

# Naval Supply

Fleets require supply.

Supply affected by:

- Ports
- Coastal control
- Naval infrastructure
- Weather
- Distance

Out of supply fleets suffer:

- Attrition
- Reduced movement
- Reduced combat effectiveness

The map is divided into naval zones.

- Irish Sea
- English Channel
- Baltic Sea
- Mediterranean

- Weather
