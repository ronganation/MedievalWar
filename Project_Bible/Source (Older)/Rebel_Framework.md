# Overview

The Rebel Framework governs all non-state resistance movements, insurgencies, uprisings, pretenders, outlaws, pirates, and adventurer hosts.

Rebels are not simply spawned armies.

Rebels represent:

- Political instability
- Cultural resistance
- Religious resistance
- Noble ambition
- Economic collapse
- Social unrest
- Historical opportunism

Rebels are a permanent part of the medieval world.

---

# Core Philosophy

Rebels are not a faction.

Rebels are an ecosystem.

Different rebel movements possess different goals, motivations, rosters, and AI behavior.

A peasant revolt should not behave like a pretender army.

A cultural resistance movement should not behave like pirates.

Most rebels do not seek conventional victory.

- Disruption
- Survival
- Independence
- Restoration

Pretender rebels are an exception.

---

# Notes

Rebels are one of the primary sources of unpredictability in Medieval War.

They ensure:

- Dynamic campaigns
- Internal conflict
- Alternate history
- Emergent storytelling

Rebels should feel like living political forces rather than randomly spawned armies.
