# Religious System
Medieval War — Systems/Religious_System.md

## Overview
The Religious System governs faith, conversion, unrest, religious blocs, religious diplomacy, religious orders, and global religious mechanics such as Crusades, Jihads, Holy Missions, and Holy Gatherings.

It blends Medieval II’s Papacy and Crusades, CK3’s religious authority and conversion mechanics, Attila’s cultural/religious pressure, and Rome II’s agent-driven religious influence.

This system interacts directly with:
- Agent System  
- Loyalty & Public Order System  
- Diplomacy & Trade System  
- Era Progression System  
- Faction Identity System  
- Court Politics System  
- Event System  
- AI Master Template  

Religion is one of the strongest forces shaping medieval geopolitics.

# ============================
# 2. Religious Loyalty
# ============================

Each settlement has a **Religious Loyalty %** toward the faction’s religion.

### Effects of Religious Loyalty
- Public Order  
- Conversion speed  
- Religious agent success  
- Religious order recruitment  
- Diplomatic relations  
- Rebellion chance  
- Cultural integration  
- Court politics stability  

### Religious Loyalty Levels
- 0–25%: Hostile (high unrest, rebellion risk)  
- 26–50%: Unstable (conversion needed)  
- 51–75%: Mixed (manageable)  
- 76–100%: Loyal (stable, bonuses)  

Religious Loyalty also interacts with:
- Global religious mechanics (Crusade, Jihad, Holy Mission, Holy Gathering)  
- Religious blocs (Catholic, Orthodox, Islamic, Pagan)  
- Faction identity (religious vs. secular tendencies)

## 3.1 Faction-Level Religious Conversion

Factions may convert to another religion if:
- Political pressure  
- Diplomatic incentives  
- Economic incentives  
- Religious agent influence  
- Events (CK3-style)  
- Ruler traits  
- Faction stability  

### Faction Conversion Effects
- Unlocks new religious agents  
- Unlocks new religious orders  
- Changes diplomatic relations  
- Changes unrest  
- Changes recruitment  
- Changes faction identity traits  
- Triggers conversion events  

### Historical Examples
- Lithuania → Catholic  
- Norse → Christian  
- Rus → Orthodox  
- Iberian factions → Catholic/Islamic influence  
- Balkan factions switching between Catholic and Orthodox  

Faction conversion is rare, high-impact, and often tied to major event chains and era progression.

# ============================
# 4. Religious Unrest
# ============================

Religious mismatch causes unrest.

### Causes of Religious Unrest
- Low religious loyalty  
- Religious agent pressure  
- Inquisitor investigations  
- Papal/Caliph/Patriarch sanctions  
- Cultural mismatch  
- High taxes  
- Oppressive policies  
- Recent conquest  
- Religious schisms  
- Era instability  

### Effects of Religious Unrest
- Lower public order  
- Lower loyalty  
- Higher rebellion chance  
- Lower recruitment  
- Lower agent success  
- Diplomatic penalties  

Religious unrest can escalate into:
- Religious uprisings  
- Religious rebellions  
- Religious civil wars (when combined with court politics and cultural unrest)

# ============================
# 6. Global Religious Mechanics
# ============================

## 6.1 Crusade (Catholic)
- Pope declares  
- Cardinals vote  
- Catholic factions may join  
- Religious order recruitment boosted  
- Crusade events triggered  
- Diplomatic shifts occur  

## 6.2 Jihad (Islam)
- Caliph declares  
- Council of Ulama votes  
- Islamic factions may join  
- Morale, movement, recruitment boosted  
- Diplomatic shifts occur  

## 6.3 Holy Mission (Orthodox)
- Patriarch declares  
- Synod votes  
- Defensive or regional  
- Orthodox unity boosted  
- Diplomatic shifts occur  

## 6.4 Holy Gathering (Pagan)
- Tribal Council / Great Council of Elders declares  
- Defensive only  
- Tribal unity boosted  
- Conversion resistance increased  

These global mechanics:
- Reshape alliances and rivalries  
- Create temporary religious blocs  
- Interact with Era Progression (Crusades/Jihads are primarily High Era phenomena)  
- Drive major event chains and AI behavior

# ============================
# 8. Religious Diplomacy
# ============================

Religion heavily influences diplomacy.

### Positive Factors
- Same religion  
- Religious alliances  
- Shared crusade/jihad participation  
- Papal/Caliph/Patriarch arbitration  
- Religious order treaties  

### Negative Factors
- Different religion  
- Religious wars  
- Excommunication  
- Jihad targets  
- Holy Mission targets  
- Religious sanctions  

Religious diplomacy interacts with:
- Faction Identity System (religious vs. pragmatic factions)  
- Court Politics (religious advisors, zealot factions)  
- Loyalty & Public Order (religious loyalty vs. cultural loyalty)

# ============================
# 10. Era Integration
# ============================

### Early Era
- High religious fragmentation  
- Frequent religious unrest  
- Strong conversion pressure  

### High Era
- Crusades/Jihads  
- Strong religious blocs  
- Strong religious institutions  

### Late Era
- Religious warfare declines  
- Economic diplomacy rises  
- Gunpowder reduces religious dominance  

Era progression also affects:
- Religious conversion speed  
- Religious institution power  
- Religious order availability  
- AI priorities (religion vs. economy vs. military)

# ============================
# 11. AI Integration
# ============================

AI uses religion to:
- Evaluate alliances  
- Evaluate enemies  
- Join crusades/jihads  
- Convert settlements  
- Deploy religious agents  
- Respond to religious pressure  
- Manage religious unrest  
- Avoid or trigger religious wars  
- Follow Papal/Caliph/Patriarch decrees  

AI adapts across eras:
- Early: religious fragmentation  
- High: religious blocs  
- Late: economic diplomacy  

AI also:
- Evaluates faction-level conversion  
- Uses religious institutions as diplomatic tools  
- Leverages religious orders for military advantage  
- Reacts to religious events and global religious mechanics
