Medieval War — Systems/Resource_System.md

# Overview

The Resource System governs the production, distribution, consumption, trade, and strategic value of resources throughout the Medieval War campaign.

Resources influence:

- Economy
- Recruitment
- Supply
- Buildings
- Trade
- Diplomacy
- Technology
- Guilds
- Population
- Naval Development

Resources are the foundation of long-term faction strength.

---

# Core Philosophy

Not all resources are equal.

Some resources support:

- Population
- Supply
- Recruitment

Others support:

- Trade
- Wealth
- Prestige

Others provide:

- Military advantages
- Strategic leverage
- Technological advancement

Control of resources drives conflict.
