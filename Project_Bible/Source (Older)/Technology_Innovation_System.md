# Overview

The Technology & Innovation System governs how knowledge develops, spreads, and transforms the medieval world.

Innovation is driven by:

- Culture
- Religion
- Guilds
- Universities
- Trade
- Characters
- Governments
- Dynasties
- Conquest
- Diplomacy
- Era Progression

Medieval War does not use a traditional research tree.

Instead, innovations emerge through historical development, investment, education, infrastructure, and interaction with the wider world.

---

# Core Philosophy

Innovation is not invented in isolation.

Knowledge spreads through:

- Trade
- Pilgrimage
- Scholarship
- Guild networks
- Diplomacy
- Religious institutions
- Migration
- Conquest

Technological development should feel organic.

# Innovation Progress

Instead of Research Points, factions generate:

Innovation Progress

Innovation Progress contributes toward:

- Discovery
- Adoption
- Diffusion

Each innovation has a threshold.

Example:

Advanced Mail Armor

Discovery:
100 Progress

Adoption:
150 Progress

Mastery:
250 Progress
