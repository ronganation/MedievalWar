# ============================
# 1. Noble Titles
# ============================

Noble titles define aristocratic hierarchy and political influence.

## 1.1 Noble Title Hierarchy
From lowest to highest:

1. **Knight / Thane / Huscarle / Retainer**  
2. **Baron / Lord / Chieftain**  
3. **Count / Earl / Emir / Voivode**  
4. **Duke / Prince / Bey / Khan**  
5. **King / Queen / Sultan / Caliph / Emperor**

## 1.2 Noble Title Effects

### Knight / Thane
- Minor loyalty bonus  
- Minor recruitment bonus  
- Minor court influence  

### Baron / Lord
- Province-level influence  
- Settlement loyalty bonus  
- Minor diplomatic weight  

### Count / Earl / Emir / Voivode
- Province governance eligibility  
- Strong loyalty bonus  
- Strong court influence  
- Moderate diplomatic weight  

### Duke / Prince / Bey / Khan
- Multi-province influence  
- Strong diplomatic weight  
- Strong noble loyalty  
- Succession influence  
- Court faction leadership  

### King / Queen / Sultan / Caliph / Emperor
- Faction-wide authority  
- Maximum diplomatic weight  
- Maximum court influence  
- Maximum succession control  
- Religious influence (Catholic/Orthodox/Islamic realms)

## 1.3 Noble Title Loyalty
Higher titles grant:
- Higher loyalty  
- Higher public order influence  
- Higher rebellion suppression  
- Higher court politics influence  

Lower titles are more prone to rebellion.

# ============================
# 2. Military Ranks
# ============================

Military ranks define command hierarchy and battlefield influence.

## 2.1 Military Rank Hierarchy

1. **Captain**  
2. **Commander**  
3. **Marshal / Strategos / Emir of Arms**  
4. **General**  
5. **Field Marshal / Grand Commander**  

## 2.2 Military Rank Effects

### Captain
- Minor morale bonus  
- Minor recruitment bonus  

### Commander
- Army loyalty bonus  
- Army morale bonus  
- Minor supply bonus  

### Marshal / Strategos
- Province military influence  
- Strong morale bonus  
- Strong recruitment bonus  
- Strong supply bonus  

### General
- Army-wide loyalty  
- Army-wide morale  
- Army-wide supply efficiency  
- Court politics influence  

### Field Marshal
- Multi-army influence  
- Maximum morale bonus  
- Maximum supply bonus  
- Strong diplomatic weight  
- Succession influence  

## 2.3 Military Rank Loyalty
Higher ranks:
- Increase army loyalty  
- Reduce desertion  
- Reduce war weariness penalties  
- Increase recruitment quality  

Lower ranks:
- More sensitive to war weariness  
- More prone to rebellion
