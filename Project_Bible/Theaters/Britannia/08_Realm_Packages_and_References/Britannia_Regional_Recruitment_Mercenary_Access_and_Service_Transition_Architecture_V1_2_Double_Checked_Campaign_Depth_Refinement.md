## 1. Purpose

This authority defines how Britannia's regional military sources, native Realm rosters, theater-owned mercenary profiles, regional-and-mercenary crossover profiles, displaced or released personnel, allied contingents, cross-water service networks, and external employers relate without duplicating people, equipment, horses, companies, or replacement pools.

It connects existing content across England, Wales, Scotland, Ireland, the Hebrides and western seaways. It does not create a single Britannia population, army, market, or unrestricted mercenary pool.

### 19. Player-Facing Regional Recruitment Loop

```text
Identify a real regional source and mission
  -> establish territorial presence or recognized access
     -> evaluate authority, cooperation, service obligation, privilege, or agreement
        -> protect local defense, households, economy, routes, and competing duties
           -> qualify people, leaders, equipment, animals, vessels, institutions, and supply
              -> reserve one finite formation and its support atomically
                 -> recruit for local, contingent, or expeditionary service actually permitted
                    -> return survivors, losses, equipment, obligations, and recovery to the real source
```

A player should see the opportunity, principal blocker, permitted service, source condition, local-defense effect, replacement rule, and return obligation. The loop must remain readable through familiar recruitment panels and relationship information rather than household-level micromanagement.

### 24. Player-Facing Mercenary Service Loop

```text
Identify an existing company, released group, displaced survivors, or qualified formation
  -> identify the captain, representative, current people, equipment, and location
     -> establish contact, route, and employer reception
        -> negotiate mission, area, duration, pay, command, conduct, supply, reinforcement, breach, recall, and return
           -> reserve the company to one live employer and contract
              -> travel and enter service through real routes
                 -> serve, renew, expire, breach, recall, return, settle, fragment, reform, or become extinct through actual state changes
```

A unit card defines a family. It does not prove that a company currently exists. Money can pay for a valid company and contract but cannot create people, leaders, equipment, vessels, horses, or cohesion.

### 33A. Access Grant, Suspension, Revocation, and Restoration

```yaml
AccessLifecycle:

    effect: RelationshipAndSourceEvaluationPermitted
  Negotiating:

    effect: OnlyExplicitRightsBecomeUsable
  Reserved:
    effect: ExactCapacityRemovedFromCompetingClaims
  Serving:
    effect: FormationBoundToCurrentAssignmentAndTerms
  Suspended:
    effect: NewUseBlockedWhileExistingFormationReceivesDisposition
  Revoked:
    effect: NewRecruitmentAndNormalReplacementClose
  Restoring:
    effect: TrustInstitutionsRoutesAndCapacityMustRecover
  Closed:
    effect: NoActiveRightSurvivesExceptExactPersistenceTerms
```

Access may change because obligations are fulfilled or breached, rulers or controllers change, the source is devastated, cooperation falls, routes close, contracts expire, local danger rises, or the source lacks recoverable capacity. Revocation does not erase a deployed formation. It triggers a disposition for continued service, restricted replenishment, recall, negotiated return, defection through campaign systems, capture, settlement, or dissolution.
