## 1. Strong Verdict

England is a mature, distributed Realm package with substantive authorities across history, law, government, delegation, characters, dynasties, political networks, ecclesiastical content, routes, military capability, economy, population, supply, AI, Opening State, Registry, and implementation boundaries. Those authorities contain important diplomacy-facing material, but none of the current owner surfaces reviewed establishes itself as England's complete Realm diplomacy and external-relations owner.

```yaml
EnglandPackageMaturity: HIGH_AT_STATIC_DESIGN_LEVEL
```

The correct response is not to rewrite the England package and not to add diplomacy sections to every existing authority. The bounded gap is one Realm-level diplomacy and external-relations authority that references existing owners, preserves their private state, and supplies the missing England-owned diplomatic lifecycle and cross-owner handoff.
