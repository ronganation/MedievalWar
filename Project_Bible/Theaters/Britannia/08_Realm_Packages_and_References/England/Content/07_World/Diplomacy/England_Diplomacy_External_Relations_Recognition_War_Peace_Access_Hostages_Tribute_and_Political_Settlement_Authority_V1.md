## 1. Executive Authority Verdict

England requires one integrated Realm diplomacy authority because its mature package already owns the historical, legal, governmental, dynastic, ecclesiastical, geographic, military, economic, population, supply, AI, Opening State, Registry, and handoff inputs to diplomacy, but those owners do not own the complete Realm diplomatic lifecycle.

This authority fills that exact gap. It does not replace or compress any current England authority. It owns England-specific diplomatic definitions, objectives, proposal and agreement lifecycles, cross-owner transaction contracts, Realm-facing war and peace interfaces, player explanation, AI request envelopes, and implementation boundaries. All exact opening state and material execution remain with their existing owners.
