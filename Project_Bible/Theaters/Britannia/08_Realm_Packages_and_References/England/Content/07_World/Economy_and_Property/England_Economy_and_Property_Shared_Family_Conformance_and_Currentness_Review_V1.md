## 1. Concise Verdict

England already has a substantial integrated Economy and Property authority. It should be retained as the immediate substantive source and current Realm owner candidate. It is not a symmetry wrapper and does not require replacement.

The authority strongly covers Property, estates, productive activities, Markets, price and scarcity observations, revenue rights, assessment, collection, arrears, dues, Treasuries, transactions, exemptions, disputes, inheritance, dower, grants, confiscation, restoration, compensation, economic damage, recovery, Province summaries, Settlement summaries, and owner handoffs.

The current defect is shared-family currentness rather than missing Realm capability. The authority predates the completed Economic and Material Flow family mapping and does not explicitly close several newer contracts, especially civilian commercial shipment and delivery, complete project lifecycle and commissioning, transaction idempotence, direct migration and advanced-state protection, typed receipts, cross-owner versioning, rollback conservation, and phased implementation gates.

## 13. Final Review Position

England is economically well developed at static design scope. Its authority is especially strong in Property decomposition, estates, Markets, pricing and scarcity, revenue, assessment, collection, Treasury, inheritance, dower, confiscation, restoration, institutional boundaries, and economic recovery. The authority should remain England-specific and integrated.

The required next step is not a new Economy design from scratch. It is a complete copy-first additive successor that preserves the full 1,115-line source and integrates the completed shared family's missing transaction, trade-delivery, project, receipt, owner-version, idempotence, migration, rollback, advanced-state, and formatting contracts. Only after that successor passes direct completeness and owner review may England be marked Conformant.
