# England High Era Roster Index (Finalized)

This document outlines the complete, finalized roster for England in the High Era, including cultural branching paths, core units, regional units, and evolution lines from the Early Era.

---

## Heavy Infantry

### Core (Both Paths)
- **City Guard** — Urban heavy infantry; available only in Large City and Huge City.
- **Feudal Men-at-Arms** — Light professional heavy infantry.

### Hybrid Anglo-Norman Path
- **English Foot Knights** — Two-handed elite heavy infantry; evolved from Huscarls.
- **Norman Heavy Infantry** — Evolved from Thegn Swordsmen.

### Anglo-Saxon Revival Path
- **Saxon Huscarl Guard** — Elite axe infantry; evolved from Huscarls.
- **Saxon Foot Guard** — Heavy shield infantry; evolved from Thegn Swordsmen.

---

## Medium Infantry

### Core
- **Guild Swordsmen** — Urban professional swordsmen.

### Hybrid Path
- **Norman Swordsmen** — Evolved from Seax Warriors; professional Anglo-Norman sword infantry with broad two-edged blades and kite shields.

### Anglo-Saxon Path
- **Mailed Seax Warriors** — Evolved from Seax Warriors.

### Axe Infantry (Both Paths)
- **Nottingham Axemen** — Hybrid path evolution of Saxon Axemen.
- **Improved Saxon Axemen** — Anglo-Saxon path evolution.

## Notes

This roster reflects the cultural branching system where players choose between a **Hybrid Anglo-Norman path** or an **Anglo-Saxon Revival path**. Core units remain available regardless of the chosen path, while specialized units evolve based on cultural alignment.
