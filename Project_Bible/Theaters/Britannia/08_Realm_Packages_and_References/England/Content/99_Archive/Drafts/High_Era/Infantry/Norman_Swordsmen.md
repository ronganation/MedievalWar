Summary Description:
Norman Swordsmen represent the Anglo‑Norman infantry traditions that shaped England’s High Era armies. They fight with broad-bladed two-edged swords, often of substantial length and with crucifix guards and wheel-shaped pommels, giving them a distinct Norman identity. Their mail armor, kite shields, and disciplined infantry training make them reliable close-combat fighters in line and town engagements.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
Norman swordsmen are defined by their broad, two-edged blades and their practical but disciplined use of the sword in close combat. The crucifix guard and wheel pommel became iconic in art and later in popular imagery, and they help distinguish these troops as a distinctly Norman fighting tradition.
