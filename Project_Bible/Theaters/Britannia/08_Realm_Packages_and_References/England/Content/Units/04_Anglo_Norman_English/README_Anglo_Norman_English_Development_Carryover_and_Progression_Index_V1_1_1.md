## 1. Purpose and V1.1 Update

This README preserves the complete V1 path structure and adds the recovered **Mounted Men-at-Arms** authority to the Anglo-Norman English Development view. It also locks the intended elite-foot distinction: **English Foot Knights are sword-and-shield knightly infantry, while Dismounted King's Men are the later two-handed and poleaxe royal foot elite.**

The path remains a fluid English development direction rather than a rigid replacement roster. Regional English personnel, surviving native institutions, Norman and continental practices, castles, courts, contracts, workshops, horses, equipment, and campaign experience combine at different rates in different regions.

```yaml
regional_outcomes:

  - SharedUnitIsAdoptedThroughPathSpecificConditions

  - NativeAndContinentalInstitutionsIntegrate
  - CrossAdoptionOccursWithoutPathReplacement
  - DevelopmentStallsRegressesOrRecoversWithInstitutions
```

```text
Path Selection != Instant Roster Replacement
Research != People Equipment Horses Officers Or Pool
Castle != Professional Company
Cultural Influence != Automatic Obedience
Calendar Date != Achieved Institution
```

One region may field Anglo-Norman Sergeants, Mounted Men-at-Arms, and Crossbow Sergeants while retaining Early English local service. Another may develop Feudal Knights without possessing enough professional mounted companies for a broad Mounted Men-at-Arms pool. King's Men may never emerge if crown, personnel, horse, officer, equipment, or political conditions fail.

Mounted Men-at-Arms are the cavalry parallel to foot Men-at-Arms in military function, but not a free mounted mode of the infantry card. They require riding competence, compatible military or selected service mounts, remounts, tack, mounted weapons, horse support, company officers, and explicit release from foot, garrison, household, or escort assignments.

## 12. Recommended Era Position

Mounted Men-at-Arms should not be presented as a higher tier that appears only after Feudal Knights. The two bodies can emerge in different order according to the institutions achieved.

```yaml
HighEra:
  MountedSergeants: ProfessionalSupportFoundation
  FeudalKnights: ConditionalKnightlyShockWhereHouseholdsAndHorsesMature
  MountedMenAtArms: EarlyPreparationOrNarrowIntroductionWhereProfessionalMountedSourcesPass

MatureHigh:
  MountedMenAtArms: BroaderArmoredProfessionalMountedCompanyGrowth
  FeudalKnights: MatureScarceKnightlyShock

HighToLate:
  MountedMenAtArms: StrongestNaturalExpansionWindow
  FeudalKnights: PersistAndModernize

LaterHighOrLate:
  KingsMen: ConditionalRoyalMountedCeiling
```

```text
Mounted Sergeants -> Mounted Men-at-Arms is not automatic
Mounted Men-at-Arms -> Feudal Knights is not automatic
Feudal Knights -> King's Men is not automatic
```

This produces overlapping institutions rather than a linear upgrade ladder.

## 13. Mounted Role Separation

```yaml
MountedSergeants:
  advantage: AvailabilityScreeningDisengagementAndEconomy
MountedMenAtArms:
  advantage: ArmorMissionBreadthCampaignEnduranceAndPostContactControl
FeudalKnights:
  advantage: KnightlyCohesionPrestigeWarhorsePriorityAndPeakShock
KingsMen:
  advantage: CrownTrustCommandProtectionAndPeakRoyalConcentration
```

Mounted Men-at-Arms fail as a separate card if they become merely mature Mounted Sergeants with larger statistics. They survive only through broader personnel sourcing, stronger mounted-equipment and horse requirements, company organization, sustained heavy service, different assignments, planned dismount, and distinct opportunity costs.
