## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Light Bolt Warship is England's Later-Early introductory mechanical-missile vessel: a standard English warship adapted to carry one small mechanical bolt weapon, a compact operating crew, a very limited bolt supply, and basic bracing for disrupting exposed boarding preparations and supporting narrow fleet, port, landing, or coastal approaches.**

Its signature is **Prepare, Align, Loose, Recover, Withdraw**.

- **Battle strengths:** Distinct heavy local shot, useful boarding-preparation disruption, narrow approach support, and meaningful target economy

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Align, Loose, Recover, Withdraw

### 8.1 Prepare

Secure a screened position, brace the device, move one bolt into the operating zone, and clear crew passage and firing arc.

### 8.2 Align

Wait for a useful exposed target, range, angle, relative motion, and safe line past friendly ships and rigging.

### 8.3 Loose

Release one bolt against a boarding preparation, dense exposed crew group, local missile station, or immediate landing threat.

### 8.4 Recover

Inspect the weapon, reset the mechanism, restore the deck, and decide whether the next scarce bolt justifies another firing cycle.

### 8.5 Withdraw

Secure the weapon and leave contact when the screen fails, the cycle is interrupted, bolts run low, weather worsens, or boarding risk becomes unacceptable.
