## 1. Path Identity

Anglo-Saxon Continuity develops England's existing maritime institutions rather than freezing the realm in the opening era.

```yaml
path_identity:
  - EnglishShipfyrdAndTerritorialSeaDuty
  - CoastalTownAndPortContribution
  - EnglishRoyalEarlAndHouseholdSupport
  - DevelopedOarAndSailFleetWork
  - PersistentEarlyEnglishWarships
  - SelectiveAdoptionOfUsefulCrossChannelEquipmentWithoutInstitutionalReplacement
not_a_role:
  - HistoricalStasis
  - AntiNormanIsolation
  - VikingReskin
  - GuaranteedExclusionOfCogsHolksCrossbowsOrMechanicalWeapons
```

The path preserves English institutional continuity while allowing equipment, port, craft, and tactical development when physical providers and resources exist.

## 16. Final Ruling

Anglo-Saxon Continuity is a developed English High naval pathway, not an unchanged Early roster. The path centers the Shipfyrd Great Warship and King's Great Ship, persists useful Early ships, and conditionally accesses Crossbow Warships, Springald Warships, Esneccas, Cinque Ports Warships, Balingers, Cogs, and Holks where actual English providers and physical capability support them. The path supports Total War-style line combat, coastal defense, amphibious response, fleet contact, and withdrawal without individual-crew micromanagement.
