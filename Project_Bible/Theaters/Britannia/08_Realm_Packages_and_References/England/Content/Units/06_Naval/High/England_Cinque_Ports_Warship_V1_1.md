## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Cinque Ports Warship is England's High-era port-service combat vessel: a Crown-called, port-backed warship used to guard Channel routes, escort convoys, intercept raiders, challenge suspicious shipping, fight short boarding actions, and secure prizes before its costly service term expires.**

Its signature is **Muster, Guard, Challenge, Seize, Return**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Cinque Ports Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Chartered port-service warship
- **Signature:** Muster, Guard, Challenge, Seize, Return
- **Best use:** Protect convoys and Channel routes, challenge raiders, reinforce royal expeditions, and capture suitable prizes
- **Strengths:** Strong institutional readiness, reliable escort discipline, useful boarding complement, good port coordination, and meaningful prize capability
- **Weaknesses:** Time-bounded service, high commercial opportunity cost, limited freedom away from assigned waters, moderate rather than elite close combat, and declining reliability when compensation or return terms fail
- **Player instruction:** Keep the warship connected to the assigned convoy, route, or port mission, challenge suitable targets, and return before service obligations fracture.

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Guard, Challenge, Seize, Return

### 6.1 Muster

A participating port or network supplies a complete ship, crew, fighting complement, equipment, and service relationship.

### 6.2 Guard

The vessel takes an escort, route-defense, port-approach, or expeditionary station.

### 6.3 Challenge

The warship identifies a suspicious, hostile, or threatening vessel and attempts to force compliance, diversion, disengagement, or battle.

### 6.4 Seize

The crew boards or compels surrender only when contact geometry, local strength, mission law, and return capacity permit.

### 6.5 Return

The ship returns to its port relationship, resolves prize and compensation questions, releases crews, repairs damage, and restores commercial capacity.

```yaml
success_condition: ProtectAssignedMovementOrRouteAndResolveSuitableThreatWithoutBreakingServiceNetwork
failure_condition: EscortSeparationOverpursuitCompensationFailureHeavyLossOrMissedReturn
```
