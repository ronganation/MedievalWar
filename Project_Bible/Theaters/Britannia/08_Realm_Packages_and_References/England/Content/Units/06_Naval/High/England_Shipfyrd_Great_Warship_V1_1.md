## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Shipfyrd Great Warship is England's High-era developed line and fleet-work warship: a larger oar-and-sail vessel concentrating territorial, coastal-town, port, royal, and lordly contributions for heavier screening, sustained peer-warship contact, mutual relief, and formation holding without becoming a royal command ship.**

Its signature is **Muster, Form, Press, Relieve, Return**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Shipfyrd Great Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Developed line fleet-work warship
- **Signature:** Muster, Form, Press, Relieve, Return
- **Best use:** Hold a heavier English screen, escort important movement, contest peer warships, reinforce an ordinary line, and relieve standard Shipfyrd vessels
- **Strengths:** Better formation endurance, larger crew and fighting complement, sustained peer contact, strong mutual support, useful oar-and-sail response, and greater staying power than the Shipfyrd Longship
- **Weaknesses:** High crew and oar burden, expensive timber and repairs, poor distributed patrol efficiency, lower availability, congestion risk, and vulnerability to fire, specialist missiles, or several-direction attack
- **Player instruction:** Use the vessel as a line anchor and mutual-support ship, not as a lone flagship or routine coast-patrol replacement.

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Form, Press, Relieve, Return

### 6.1 Muster

Assemble a complete developed hull, maritime crew, fighting complement, oars, sail, stores, and valid provider package.

### 6.2 Form

Take a line, heavy-screen, escort, or supported wing position while retaining mutual relief and withdrawal space.

### 6.3 Press

Use formation mass, oars, sail, supporting missiles, and boarding threat to contest a suitable peer vessel or threatened sector.

### 6.4 Relieve

Replace a damaged Shipfyrd vessel, reinforce a local screen, counter a breakthrough, or cover a friendly withdrawal.

### 6.5 Return

Break contact in formation, escort damaged ships, seek refuge, repair, and release or rotate provider contributions.

### Shipfyrd Great Warship

*Developed English shipfyrd line warship*

Shipfyrd Great Warships concentrate larger English hulls, crews, rowers, fighters, and port support into powerful line vessels. They hold heavier screens, contest peer warships, relieve ordinary Shipfyrd vessels, and escort major movement. Their value comes from formation endurance rather than royal command, and their immense crew and repair burden makes them inefficient for routine patrol.
