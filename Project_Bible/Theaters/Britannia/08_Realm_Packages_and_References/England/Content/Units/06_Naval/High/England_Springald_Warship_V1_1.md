## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Springald Warship is England's High-era low-volume mechanical-missile specialist: an English warship fitted with one compact springald or equivalent validated bolt weapon, a dedicated operating crew, heavy bolts, bracing, tools, and protected stowage for disrupting boarding preparations, threatening exposed concentrations, and supporting port, landing, and fleet approaches.**

Its signature is **Emplace, Range, Disrupt, Displace, Withdraw**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Springald Warship

- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Low-volume mechanical-missile specialist
- **Signature:** Emplace, Range, Disrupt, Displace, Withdraw
- **Best use:** Break up exposed boarding preparations, threaten dense deck concentrations, deny a narrow approach, and support landings or port actions from a screened position
- **Battle strengths:** Consequential local bolt effects, useful approach denial, strong boarding-preparation disruption, and a role distinct from bows and handheld crossbows
- **Battle weaknesses:** Very low rate of fire, narrow useful arc, heavy deck-space burden, finite expensive bolts, weak boarding margin, and serious vulnerability if the screen fails
- **Campaign strengths:** Converts developed English ports, craftsmen, weapon crews, and royal or town investment into a distinct High naval specialist
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Competes with workshops, siege equipment, fortification weapons, port craft, Crossbow Warships, and other specialist naval crews
- **Player instruction:** Fire only into valuable exposed concentrations, preserve the weapon crew and withdrawal route, and never treat the vessel as a general-purpose artillery ship.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Emplace, Range, Disrupt, Displace, Withdraw

### 8.1 Emplace

Move into a screened firing sector and establish a stable hull, weapon position, crew passage, and withdrawal route.

### 8.2 Range

Select a broad exposed target area and wait for a useful range, angle, and firing window.

### 8.3 Disrupt

Release a limited heavy bolt against boarding preparation, exposed missile crews, dense defenders, steering personnel, or another suitable local concentration.

### 8.4 Displace

Move before enemy close-action ships exploit the narrow arc, reload interval, or predictable position.

### 8.5 Withdraw

Secure the weapon and leave contact when bolts, crew order, equipment condition, weather, or screen integrity make further fire unsafe.
