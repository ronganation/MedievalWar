## 1. Controlling Decision

Keep <File>Halberdiers_V1_1_Master_Unit_Profile.md</File> as the single controlling England halberd-family candidate. Do not create separate Halberd Militia, Halberd Men at Arms, or City Halberdiers cards under current evidence. Preserve all three as profiles or access paths within Halberdiers: Experimental Civic Halberd Files, Professional or Contract Halberd Company, and Exceptional City Halberdiers. Domestic recruitment remains conditional on later English weapon, chronology, owner, and cross-realm evidence.

## 2. Historical Boundary

The external search describes the halberd as a two-handed combined-head polearm prominent in Europe from the thirteenth through sixteenth centuries and associates early documented use strongly with German-language and Swiss contexts. The search also surfaced broad material on medieval English urban military service, but did not establish a dedicated English civic Halberdier formation. These results support a later weapon and possible contact, contract, guard, or adaptation pathway, not automatic England-wide recruitment.

## 3. Internal Evidence Boundary

The current enterprise search surfaced no exact dedicated source files for Halberd Militia, Halberd Men at Arms, or City Halberdiers. It did surface the older England progression chart, which contains urban and men-at-arms development paths, and the current Men-at-Arms profile, which classifies that unit as professional armored melee infantry. These sources are useful comparators but do not prove the three halberd candidates.

## 5. Halberd Militia Disposition

Halberd Militia becomes the Experimental Civic Halberd Files profile. The profile represents a qualifying locality testing combined-head weapons with selected civic personnel, mixed equipment, shallow drill, restricted local duty, and significant overlap risk with Bill Militia and City Guard. The profile does not create a recruitable national militia card. If the base Halberdier historical gate fails, this profile becomes legacy evidence or integrates into City Guard or Billmen adaptation.

## 6. Halberd Men at Arms Disposition

Halberd Men at Arms becomes the Professional or Contract Halberd Company path. The current <File>Men_at_Arms_V2_Master_Unit_Profile.md</File> identifies Men-at-Arms as professional armored melee infantry, so the halberd path must not duplicate that unit through weapon substitution alone. The profile requires a distinct combined-head weapon discipline, owner or contract, selected personnel, guard or breach mission, sidearm training, workshop support, and bounded service. Contract access is preferred where domestic English adoption is not proven.

## 7. City Halberdiers Disposition

City Halberdiers becomes the Exceptional City Halberdiers owner profile. It requires a major civic owner with distinct personnel records, specialist weapons, officers, workshops, stores, local-security replacement, and a high-value guard or breach assignment. City size, wealth, a guild, or a building alone does not qualify the profile. The same people and equipment cannot also support City Guard, Bill Militia, or base Halberdiers.

## 8. England and Scotland Cross-Realm Rule

England and Scotland legacy halberd concepts cannot remain palette and statistic variants. A future direct comparison must resolve chronology, weapon form, owner, personnel source, adaptation route, tactical role, and campaign cost. If Scotland has the stronger domestic formation evidence, England may retain contract access, imported guards, or limited owner-specific adoption. If both have valid but different evidence, each realm must receive a distinct institutional pathway.

## 9. Bill and Pike Family Firewall

Billmen and Heavy Billmen own England's broader bill tradition and sustained heavy-bill pressure. Burgh Pikemen own deep frontal denial. Halberdiers survive only through shorter combined-head weapon behavior, guard frontage, breach stabilization, pike-flank support, controlled counterattack, and owner- or contract-specific service. If those distinctions cannot be implemented and recognized by AI, the halberd family must integrate into bills, pikes, or urban guards rather than retain an extra card.

## 10. Professional and Elite Infantry Firewall

Halberdiers do not inherit professional armored melee identity from Men-at-Arms, knightly identity from English Foot Knights, royal poleaxe identity from Dismounted King’s Men, or heavy reserve identity from Heavy Billmen. Any household or professional profile must subtract real personnel and equipment from the correct owner and preserve nonknightly combined-head weapon specialization.

## 11. Profile Qualification Matrix

| Profile | Required owner | Personnel | Distinct use | Primary failure route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Experimental Civic Halberd Files | Exceptional civic or local authority | Selected civic guards or militia | Trial guard frontage and limited breach support | Integrate into City Guard, Billmen, or legacy evidence |
| Professional or Contract Halberd Company | Paid company, magnate, Crown contract, or external owner | Selected professionals or contractors | Guard, breach, siege, and controlled counterattack | Integrate into Men-at-Arms, bills, or contract-only roster |
| Exceptional City Halberdiers | Major city or exceptional burgh | Distinct specialist civic contingent | High-value gate, arsenal, institution, and breach protection | Downgrade to base Halberdiers or City Guard profile |

## 12. Finite Pool Rule

No profile creates new people. Civic personnel must be removed from City Guard, Bill Militia, watch, or other local assignments. Professional personnel must be removed from company, household, Men-at-Arms, Billmen, or contract pools. Weapons, armor, sidearms, officers, workshops, stores, and transport remain physical and exclusive. Qualification is reversible when these layers are lost.

## 13. Profile Upgrade and Downgrade

Profiles qualify through actual owner, people, weapons, officers, training, workshops, service, and mission. Experimental files can mature into an owner-specific body only after repeated successful service and stronger support. Contract companies do not automatically create domestic adoption. Exceptional City Halberdiers downgrade when local security, specialist equipment, officers, finance, or civic cooperation fails. All downgrade paths preserve surviving people and equipment.

## 14. AI and Player Clarity Gate

Campaign AI must choose among bills, pikes, City Guard, Men-at-Arms, and halberd profiles based on mission and source. Battle AI must use Halberdiers for guard frontage, breach stabilization, pike-flank support, and controlled counterattack. If AI uses the formation as generic anti-armor infantry, the separate family has failed. Player UI must explain owner, profile, weapon, service, opportunity cost, and downgrade conditions without requiring statistical comparison.
