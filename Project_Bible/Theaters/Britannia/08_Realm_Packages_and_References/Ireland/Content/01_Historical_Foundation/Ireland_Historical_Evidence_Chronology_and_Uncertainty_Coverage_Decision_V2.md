## 1. Concise Verdict

The current enterprise search did not locate standalone Ireland files titled as a Historical Evidence Register, Historical Timeline and Chronology authority, or Historical Uncertainty Register. The search did locate the Ireland Historical Reconciliation generations, Opening State generations, typed-record inventory, readiness audit, source-to-owner compatibility manifest, import fixture manifest, Foundation Review, and Realm coordination authorities. It also located shared Historical Memory, Historical Momentum, and Historical Emergence frameworks and Realm-specific precedent files for England, Scotland, Wales, and Norway.

This result does not prove that the three Ireland files are physically absent. It establishes only that they were not verified by the current search. Because Ireland’s current Historical Reconciliation V3 already preserves substantial evidence, chronology, and uncertainty content, no standalone Ireland authority should be created merely to mirror another Realm. The correct next step is a bounded physical-package verification and source-coverage decision. If a substantive Ireland source is found, it must be copied first and modernized. If no source exists and the controlling architecture requires a separate owner surface, a new authority may be proposed only through an explicit owner-authorized creation gate.

