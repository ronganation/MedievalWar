## 1. Authority Thesis

Ireland Historical Momentum translates owner-approved facts, memories, claims, relationships, institutions, military conditions, routes, economy, supply, household arrangements, and regional recognition into directional pressure. It does not script historical outcomes.

```text
Momentum != Event
Pressure != Outcome
Historical Date != Trigger
Definition != Runtime State
```
