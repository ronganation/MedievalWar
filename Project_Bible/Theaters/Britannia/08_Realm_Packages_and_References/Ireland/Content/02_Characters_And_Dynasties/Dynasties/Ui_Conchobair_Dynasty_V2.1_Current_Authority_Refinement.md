## Core Dynasty Thesis

The Uí Conchobair begin in January 1062 as the restored Síl Muiredaig royal line holding Connacht through Áed in Gai Bernaig, but restoration has not become stability. Áed recovered the overkingship after the rupture created by Tadg in Eich Gil's death and the intervening Ua Ruairc provincial order. Fifteen years later, his authority remains stressed by western resistance, Bréifne's credible alternative claim, and a succession field containing adult, developing, and underage sons.

The dynasty's central problem is continuity after Áed. Tadc mac Áeda is an adult military and succession figure exposed to the opening western conflict. Ruaidrí na Saide Buide is a protected child or young adolescent and a reserve route, not a guaranteed heir. Other sons may become candidates, rivals, officers, or branch leaders only through actual development and recognition. The dynasty succeeds when personal restoration becomes regional government, protected households, distributed experience, and a succession that can survive Áed's removal.

## Historical Foundation

Tadg in Eich Gil ruled Connacht before his death in 1030. His removal opened a period in which Art Uallach Ua Ruairc and the Uí Briúin Bréifne political tradition held provincial supremacy. Áed in Gai Bernaig later pursued vengeance, defeated rivals, and recovered the Connacht overkingship in 1046.

The dynasty therefore begins from a sequence of kingship, violent rupture, rival provincial rule, restoration, and renewed resistance. Áed's restoration provides current possession and political credibility, but it does not erase Ua Ruairc precedent, western autonomy, family competition, or the need to transform military success into ordinary government.

- household, guardian, protector, fosterage, hostage, education, and regency state;
- directed relationship, grievance, recognition, and patron-obligation state;
- Clonmacnoise institutional interfaces;
- Historical Memory and audience Reputation references.

Opening State initializes a new campaign only. Loading a save must never overwrite advanced western, succession, household, title, claim, relationship, or patronage state with January 1062 defaults. Deprecated IDs resolve directly, log migration, and are replaced before the next save.

## Determinism Requirements

- Equal state, inputs, content version, and seed produce equal dynasty evaluations and command proposals.
- Iteration order for members, candidates, households, relationships, western actors, and AI options is stable and canonical.
- Dynasty AI proposes commands rather than directly changing state.
- Command validation order is explicit.
- Random selection uses approved seeded streams.
- Migration produces the same canonical result regardless of load order.
- Unknown or quarantined IDs fail safely and cannot silently select a historical person, branch, or claimant.

## Performance and Exposure Guardrail

Dynasty simulation should expose only decisions with strategic consequence. Routine family updates, minor relationship drift, and ordinary household activity remain internal or summarized. Player-facing choices should concentrate on:

- western war and settlement;
- Áed's government and sudden-removal preparation;
- Tadc's command exposure;
- Ruaidrí's protection, fosterage, and education;
- adult candidate development;
- Bréifne frontier and claim pressure;
- external patronage and client-capture risk;
- Clonmacnoise mediation or sanctuary;
- succession, exile, restoration, and divided authority;
- wider-kingship timing.

Do not expand the Uí Conchobair roster solely for historical completeness. Promote a person when independent decisions affect title, claim, succession, command, household, western government, diplomacy, institution, or Historical Momentum.

## Final Design Summary

The Uí Conchobair begin with a restored kingship, not a secure dynasty. Tadg supplies remembered rupture. Áed supplies present authority and the burden of converting restoration into government. Tadc supplies immediate adult continuity under dangerous military exposure. Ruaidrí supplies a protected, long-term possibility rather than an automatic succession. Western actors and Bréifne retain independent political agency.

The dynasty succeeds when Áed's personal restoration becomes enforceable western government, distributed command, protected households, several credible candidates, and transfer capacity that survives him. It fails when one death, one battlefield, one captured child, or one failed western settlement recreates the interruption from which the dynasty recovered.
