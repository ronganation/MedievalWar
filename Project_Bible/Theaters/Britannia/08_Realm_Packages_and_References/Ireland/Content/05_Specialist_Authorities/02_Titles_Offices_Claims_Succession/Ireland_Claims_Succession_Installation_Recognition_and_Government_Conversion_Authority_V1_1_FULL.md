## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland's claims and successions must operate as political processes rather than bloodline shortcuts, fixed heir lists, universal legitimacy scores, or scripted accessions. Blood, genealogy, dynasty, prior possession, office, custom, memory, regional support, institutions, military success, patronage, household security, and access may matter. No single factor creates title possession, candidate rank, recognition, installation, government capacity, or durable rule.

```text
Valid Claim or Succession Need
+ Eligible and Available Candidate
+ Evidence, Custom, Procedure, and Audience-Specific Recognition
+ Household, Military, Geographic, Economic, and Institutional Feasibility
+ Installation, Handover, and Government Conversion
+ Consolidation, Resistance, Review, and Replacement Capacity
= Bounded Succession Outcome
```

The January 1062 opening preserves occupied kingships and different claim conditions. Donnchad holds Munster while Toirdelbach is the principal challenger. Áed in Gai Bernaig holds Connacht while Áed Ua Ruairc has an inactive Connacht route. Diarmait is the strongest practical wider-leadership contender without uncontested all-Ireland kingship. Gofraid begins outside Dublin without title, office, or control. Protected minors retain future routes without adult authority or automatic precedence.
