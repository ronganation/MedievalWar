## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland on 1 January 1062 is one realm context but not one state, one government, one crown, one dynasty, or one administrative hierarchy. It is a connected political ecosystem of provincial kingships, regional governments, local authorities, dynastic branches, households, Dublin's distinct urban-maritime government, ecclesiastical institutions, claimants, clients, tributaries, hostages, protected minors, councils, and strategic communities.

Government must remain legible through the Medieval II campaign spine: rulers, settlements, armies, titles, offices, taxes, recruitment, diplomacy, succession, and territorial objectives. Character-driven causality must explain who can govern, delegate, resist, negotiate, succeed, or fail without replacing map strategy or turning every household participant into a separately managed bureaucracy.

```text
Valid Ruler, Title, Office, or Mandate
+ Governable Territory and Accessible Political Center
+ Household, Officers, Records, Revenue, and Communication
+ Military Security and Supply
+ Regional, Local, and Institutional Participation
+ Law, Custom, Recognition, and Enforceable Agreements
+ Succession and Replacement Capacity
= Functioning Government for a Defined Scope
```

No term in that equation substitutes for another.
