## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland in January 1062 does not possess one uniform island-wide legal code, court hierarchy, police system, or sovereign legislature. Law operates through plural and overlapping sources: custom, title and office authority, household and dynastic practice, local and regional participation, agreements, oaths, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, property and possession, military power, public memory, and practical enforcement.

```text
Valid Authority and Jurisdiction
+ Known Rule, Custom, Agreement, Privilege, or Policy
+ Proper Notice, Hearing, Evidence, Counsel, and Decision Process
+ Available Remedy and Lawful Enforcement
+ Regional, Local, Household, Institutional, and Political Reception
= Bounded Legal or Policy Effect for a Defined Scope
```

A ruler may announce a policy without being able to enforce it. A custom may be recognized without applying universally. A judgment may be valid but unenforced. A settlement may end one dispute without resolving every underlying claim or relationship.
