## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland's January 1062 church is a plural network of monasteries, ecclesiastical offices, communities, local churches, regional traditions, patrons, properties, archives, schools, burial networks, sacred places, communication routes, and political relationships. It is not one faction, one hierarchy, one opinion score, one ruler-owned resource, or a later reformed diocesan system projected backward.

The verified opening content supports <Place>Clonmacnoise</Place> as the best-developed active institution and <Person>Ailill Ua hAirechtaig</Person> as the living holder of its abbacy. The institution, office, occupancy, person, place, community, property, jurisdiction, archive, and <Person>Saint Ciarán</Person> founder legacy remain distinct. Other Irish institutions belong in the realm's wider ecclesiastical landscape only at the level supported by their sources. <Place>Clonard</Place> and <Place>Kells</Place> remain historically relevant but unresolved for exact opening office sequences and holders.

```text
Verified Institution and Place

+ Defined Jurisdiction and Procedure
+ Route, Security, Patronage, and Communication Access
+ Audience-Specific Recognition and Political Independence
```

Sacred standing does not substitute for material capacity, lawful process, local cooperation, or protection.
