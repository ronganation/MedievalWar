## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland's economy must be represented as a distributed network of properties, rights, obligations, producers, labor, goods, storage, markets, ports, routes, households, governments, institutions, and negotiated access, not one universal wealth score. Political authority, legal ownership, physical possession, operation, revenue entitlement, and available cash remain separate.

```text
Valid Property, Right, or Economic Activity
+ Owner, Possessor, Operator, Labor, Inputs, and Access
+ Production, Storage, Market, Route, and Exchange Capacity
+ Accounts, Revenue, Obligations, Spending Authority, and Payment
+ Security, Damage, Recovery, and Political or Institutional Cooperation
= Functioning Economic Value for a Defined Actor and Purpose
```

A rich territory may yield little usable revenue. A treasury balance does not prove liquidity at a distant court. A market does not guarantee trade. A port does not create ships or access. A tax claim does not guarantee collection.

## V1.1 Additive Validation Rules

### Fatal

- duplicate transaction reapplies mutation;
- one TransactionId is accepted with a changed payload;
- stale owner versions commit;
- rollback creates net value, Property, rights, stock, cargo, materials, progress, or recovery;
- port, route, Market, agreement, project, money, building, decision, or elapsed time creates capacity, delivery, people, materials, progress, or recovery;
- civilian cargo becomes military Supply without Supply acceptance;
- Opening State or migration overwrites advanced state;
- AI commits owner state;
- foreign private state is authored locally.

### Error

- missing owner version, receipt, reservation, acceptance, reconciliation, state hash, external lock, or rollback journal;
- output creates stock before acceptance and custody;
- remittance lacks arrival, acceptance, receipt, or reconciliation;
- shipment lacks cargo conservation, carrier capacity, route access, or destination acceptance;
- project progress exceeds accepted work;
- commissioning lacks physical-owner acceptance;
- economic recovery lacks owner-facet receipts.
