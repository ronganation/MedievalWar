## 1. Purpose and controlling disposition

This addendum is one narrow controlling disposition over the V1.1 general
Unreal-work prohibition for the exact review-only projection described
below. It authorizes creation, compilation, focused automation, preservation
automation, and evidence recording for that projection only.

The disposition does not promote the V1.1 economic authority, accept it on
behalf of the Economy owner, complete any economy implementation batch, or
authorize economic state. V1.1 remains controlling for economic semantics,
source boundaries, owner boundaries, uncertainty, unknown values,
anti-fabrication, and all prohibited state.

The V1.1 statements
`UnrealProjectMutationAuthorized: false` and
`payload_importer_runtime_or_Unreal_work_authorized: false` are superseded
only insofar as they would prohibit the exact three Unreal source/test files,
their compilation, the automation expressly listed here, and additive
evidence recording. Every other meaning of those V1.1 prohibitions remains
in force.

This disposition is additive to the Ireland package controls. Their general
prohibitions remain in force except for this precise, bounded authorization.
No general Unreal modification or implementation authority is granted.
