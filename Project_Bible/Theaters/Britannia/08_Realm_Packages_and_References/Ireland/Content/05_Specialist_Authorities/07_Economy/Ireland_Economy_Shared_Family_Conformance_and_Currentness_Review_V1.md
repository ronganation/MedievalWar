## 14. Final Review Position

Ireland is already one of the stronger developed Realm Economy packages. It has an integrated economic constitution, regional differentiation, explicit Dublin and Clonmacnoise boundaries, owner separation, anti-fabrication controls, extensive validation cases, save protection, and economy-specific transaction integrity.

The correct remedy is therefore narrow in authority count but deep in contract quality: preserve the full existing authority and add the completed shared family's transaction, receipt, owner-version, civilian-stock, shipment, commissioning, finance-instrument, remittance, migration, rollback, and handoff contracts. No additional regional authority, symmetry wrapper, opening payload, or Unreal implementation is justified.
