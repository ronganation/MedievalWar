## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland must read as a connected but politically plural island whose provincial heartlands, central routes, western approaches, river systems, coastlines, ports, settlements, sacred centers, borderlands, and maritime interfaces shape political and military possibility. Geography supports the Medieval II campaign spine through visible settlements, direct army movement, sieges, field battles, finite recruitment access, supply, diplomacy, government conversion, and recovery.

```text
Verified Physical Place or Area
+ Approved Representation Tier
+ Geography, Adjacency, Terrain, Hydrology, and Access
+ Distinct Strategic Function
+ Owner-Controlled Operation, Control, Capacity, Damage, and Recovery
= Readable Campaign-Map Geography
```

Ireland must not become one province, one settlement per political identity, or a carpet of famous-name icons. Major settlements remain anchors. Separate ports, crossings, monasteries, forts, markets, estates, resources, and route nodes require independent strategic justification.
