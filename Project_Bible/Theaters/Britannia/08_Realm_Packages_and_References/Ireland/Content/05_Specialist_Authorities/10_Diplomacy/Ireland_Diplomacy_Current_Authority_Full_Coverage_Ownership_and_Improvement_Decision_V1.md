## 7. Substantive V1.1 Improvement Gaps

### 7.1 Current shared diplomacy chain

V1 references older integration authorities and Wales V1 as a structural benchmark. V1.1 should add the completed shared diplomacy foundation, shared subordination framework, allied-contingent external-service framework, and the final England, Scotland, and Wales structural benchmarks without transferring their facts.

### 7.2 Diplomatic-principal constitution and collective participation

V1 has strong principal classes but needs a consolidated record for principal authority, jurisdiction, topic, counterparty, consultation, ratification, review, and succession. It also needs a participation contract separating invited, represented, heard, supported, witnessed, consented, ratified, implemented, and performed across households, regional rulers, dynasties, ecclesiastical institutions, Dublin actors, military followers, and local communities.

### 7.3 Agreement continuity and amendment lineage

V1 needs explicit continuity rules for death, incapacity, capture, succession, deposition, office loss, government conversion, client independence, amendment, partial replacement, suspension, withdrawal, renewal, expiry, termination, and archive lineage.

### 7.4 Obligation, reservation, delivery, inspection, and acceptance

The current commitment and performance model should be expanded to separate promise, capacity, reservation, instruction, mobilization, execution, delivery, receipt, inspection, partial acceptance, acceptance, rejection, cure, waiver, and unperformed remainder.

### 7.5 Breach, excuse, mediation, remedy, and appeal

The breach model should add responding principal, excuse, external interference, cure window, restitution, replacement, security, partial release, mediation, arbitration, appeal, retaliation proposal, and unresolved disposition. Allegation must not become proof.

### 7.6 Clientage, submission, tribute, and retained-government continuity

V1 is already strong here. V1.1 should align it directly with the current shared subordination framework and add continuity through succession, government conversion, resistance, separation, restoration, handback, integration, and per-term performance without reducing Irish relationships to one vassalage label.

### 7.7 External-owner lock semantics and atomic transactions

Every Dublin, Irish Sea, island, England, Scotland, Wales, Normandy, Norse, and external Church interface needs consistent External, ExternalOrUnresolved, and ExternalLocked semantics plus expected versions, owner receipts, all-or-nothing commits, and rollback state.

### 7.8 Financial diplomacy breadth

V1 covers tribute, restitution, compensation, and economic settlement. V1.1 should include subsidy, gift, loan, credit, guarantee, ransom, reparation, extraordinary aid, toll or customs agreement, provisioning purchase, arrears, security, and owner-routed acceptance.

### 7.9 Person-arrangement breadth and welfare

V1 strongly covers hostages and fosterage. V1.1 should add explicit refuge, hospitality, release, safe return, treatment, education, religion, contact, inspection, review, and harm-recovery records while preserving the distinct agency of minors and all persons.

### 7.10 Irish Sea and island external-owner routing

V1 rejects premature assignment of Man, Hebrides, Orkney, and other islands. V1.1 should formalize island, port, fleet, local-government, dynasty, and Realm owner locks without assigning any unsupported opening state.

### 7.11 Government-conversion and political-settlement interface

The current claims and government-conversion successor is now stronger than the version cited by V1. V1.1 should directly consume the V1.1 current authority and represent title, recognition, installation, retained government, regional acceptance, and practical consolidation as separate owner-routed dimensions.

### 7.12 Source-to-successor coverage and package blocker proof

V1.1 should contain a complete preservation ledger, source-to-authority coverage matrix, package blocker register, and literal completeness checks.

### 7.13 Phased implementation handoff

Replace the prior next-action pointer to household authority, which now exists, with a current phased diplomacy handoff containing dependencies, acceptance criteria, evidence, rollback, and owner gates for static closure, package routing, Registry and Opening State planning, shadow-mode transactions, and real-payload or Unreal promotion.

### 7.14 Formatting and submission hygiene

The V1 contains escaped underscores and hyphens, many unfenced structured records, HTML validation tables, and compressed transitions. V1.1 should preserve all substantive content while normalizing the additive layer to fenced YAML, fenced text inequalities, current stable footer wording, honest statuses, and no escaped underscores or hyphens.
