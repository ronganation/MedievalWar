## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland has a substantial frozen historical and strategic foundation, a refined authority layer, an implementation-readiness layer, and early Unreal-facing design material. The package does not require broad redesign. It does require a full copy-first additive current-authority pass because the discovered source generations overlap, refined filenames do not by themselves prove literal completeness, early Unreal guidance predates the current UE 5.8 shared architecture, and current readiness language is broader than the available execution evidence supports.

The first Irish gate is therefore source control, not implementation.
