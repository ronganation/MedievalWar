## 1. Concise Verdict

The evidence supports retaining the bounded January 1062 opening core already represented by the current Ireland Opening State while preserving unresolved specialist collections as definitions, qualitative references, intentional empty collections, owner decisions, external-owner decisions, Registry review, quarantine, rejection, or later-history exclusion. This table prepares evidence for a possible additive V2.2 specialist-binding addendum. It does not create that addendum and does not modify or activate the existing V2.1 authority.

Dublin remains split among effective control, office occupancy, power-center identity, and unresolved title forms. Gofraid remains externally owned and Ireland-referenceable without an Ireland-local definition. Exact military, population, recruitment, economy, treasury, Supply, reciprocal diplomacy, culture distributions, and actor knowledge remain unbound.

```text
Evidence Support + Correct Chronology + Correct Entity Kind + Canonical Reference + Specialist Owner Approval + Opening State Owner Approval = Eligible Specialist Binding
```

## 3. Evidence Classes and Binding Dispositions

```yaml
evidence_classes:
  Supported: sufficient current authority exists for bounded consideration
  SupportedWithUncertainty: material support exists but precision or interpretation remains unresolved
  QualitativeOnly: condition is supported without an exact quantity or instance
  DefinitionOnly: definition or schema exists without evidence for an opening instance
  OwnerDecisionRequired: evidence does not replace explicit owner selection
  ExternalOwnerRequired: reciprocal or externally private state requires another owner
  RegistryResolutionRequired: canonical form, alias, kind, or collision remains unresolved
  Quarantined: identity, chronology, ownership, or kind prevents use
  LaterHistoryExcluded: proposition belongs to later development
  Rejected: proposition is unsupported or constitutionally incompatible
binding_dispositions:
  RetainExistingOpeningBinding: preserve an already established bounded V2.1 fact or reference
  EligibleForV2_2ReferenceOnly: may be cited without creating specialist state
  EligibleWithExplicitUncertainty: may be included only with unresolved fields preserved
  PendingOwnerDecision: do not bind until the owner provides a receipt
  PendingExternalOwnerDecision: do not bind reciprocal or private state from Ireland alone
  PendingRegistryResolution: do not use unresolved canonical forms
  PreserveIntentionalEmpty: keep the collection empty
  Quarantine: exclude until resolved
  ExcludeLaterHistory: do not backdate into 1 January 1062
  Reject: do not include
```
