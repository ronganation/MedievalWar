## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland can pause the Historical and AI schema workstreams without loss. Both now terminate in pending owner-response ledgers, and further local paperwork would not create the missing approvals or implementation evidence.

The package has verified substantive sources for Realm Personality, Realm AI, Opening State, Character Network, Historical authorities, and at least one Ireland Dynasty authority. The current search did not verify complete Ireland-specific source authorities for Military, Supply, Economy, Strategic Geography, Population, Diplomacy, Governance, Law, Ecclesiastical content, or a consolidated Title, Office, Claim, and Succession package. Those domains must not be created merely by copying the Wales filenames.

The next substantive non-historical authority selected is a copy-first additive refinement of the verified Ua Briain Dynasty source. This advances an actual Ireland Realm system with a real immediate source while preserving the future military modernization sequence for the point at which an Ireland military source is physically verified.
