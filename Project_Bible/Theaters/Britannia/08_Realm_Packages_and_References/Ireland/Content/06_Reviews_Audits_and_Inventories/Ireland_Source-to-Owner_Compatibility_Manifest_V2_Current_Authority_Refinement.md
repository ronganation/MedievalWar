## 1. Concise Verdict

The complete V1 manifest remains the substantive field-level compatibility portfolio and is retained unmodified. V2 adds current authority routing for Ireland’s completed Opening State, typed-record, Registry, import-mapping, Government, Strategic Geography, Ecclesiastical, Claims and Succession, Character Network, Historical, Personality, AI, character, and dynasty work. V2 does not redesign Ireland, erase the V1 dispositions, invent new shared architecture, or treat static documentation as implementation evidence.

The five V1 actions remain controlling: Keep, Refactor, Relocate, Supersede, and Quarantine. V2 updates destinations and cross-owner boundaries where current Ireland authorities now exist. It also replaces the obsolete universal seven-month turn statement with the three-track model and corrects Gofraid and Dublin treatment through Registry V2.3.
