## 1. Concise Verdict

Ireland now has a mutually compatible specialist-authority layer for the domains directly evidenced in the current Realm package: Government and Political Order; Titles, Offices, Regional Administration; Claims, Succession, Recognition, Installation, and Government Conversion; Ecclesiastical Institutions, Offices, Property, Sanctuary, and Patronage; Strategic Geography, Regions, Settlements, Routes, Ports, and Control; Character Network; Historical Reconciliation, Memory, Momentum, and Emergence; Realm Personality; Realm AI; current character authorities; and current dynasty authorities.

At the design-contract level, those authorities preserve the January 1062 opening without collapsing person, title, office, institution, power center, claim, dynasty, relationship, geography, Historical state, AI state, or advanced-save state. The package consistently supports Diarmait as Leinster ruler and effective Dublin controller at separate owner layers, Murchad as holder of the Dublin ruler office, Donnchad as Munster title holder, Toirdelbach as claimant rather than opening king, Aed in Gai Bernaig as Connacht ruler, Aed Ua Ruairc as Breifne ruler with a separate Connacht claim, Conchobar as Mide ruler, and Ailill as Clonmacnoise office holder while institution and power center remain distinct.

This audit does not claim full specialist coverage for military forces, command payloads, population quantities, recruitment pools, economy, treasury, supply, culture distribution, diplomacy agreements, law implementation, or actor knowledge. Those domains remain deferred to existing shared or later Ireland-specific owners where exact current Ireland authorities were not verified in this audit surface. The reviewed package is coherent for documentation consolidation and importer handoff, but it is not authorized for Registry promotion, Opening State activation, migration execution, Unreal modification, or runtime import.

```text
Broad Verified Specialist Coverage + Consistent January 1062 Boundary + Correct Owner Separation + Current Registry and Import Handoff - Complete Physical Repository Scan - All Exact Bindings - Runtime Evidence = Static-Checked Integration Candidate, Not Active State
```
