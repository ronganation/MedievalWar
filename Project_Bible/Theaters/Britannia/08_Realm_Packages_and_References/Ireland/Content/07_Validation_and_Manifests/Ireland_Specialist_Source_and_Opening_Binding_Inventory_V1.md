## 1. Concise Verdict

The Ireland specialist package contains enough current authority to inventory bounded January 1062 opening bindings across Realm context, political order, claims, ecclesiastical content, strategic geography, characters, dynasties, Character Network, Historical systems, Realm Personality, AI, Registry, and save initialization. The evidence supports a stable opening core and a larger body of reusable definitions, explicit uncertainty, owner decisions, external-owner dependencies, historical-only references, later-history exclusions, intentional empty collections, quarantines, and rejected propositions.

This inventory does not populate every specialist schema and does not alter the current Opening State. Exact military, population, recruitment, economy, treasury, Supply, diplomacy, culture-distribution, law, and knowledge payloads remain empty, qualitative, deferred, or owner-pending where current evidence does not establish them. Supported source plus correct entity kind plus January 1062 compatibility plus specialist owner plus Registry resolution plus Opening State approval is the required binding chain.

MinimalReference: A bounded reference may exist without activated specialist state.
IntentionalEmpty: The collection remains empty until evidence and owners approve entries.

RegistryReviewRequired: ID, alias, kind, or collision review is required.
