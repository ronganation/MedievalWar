## 1. Executive Verdict

Ireland must not become **Wales with Irish names**, a unified Total War faction with universal recruitment, or a full Crusader Kings map in which every local polity is independently simulated at equal depth.

Ireland's preferred middle-ground identity is a **politically assembled military host**. A ruler seeks to convert household strength, provincial recognition, subordinate obligations, hostages or guarantees where supported, ecclesiastical relationships, coastal access, Norse-Gaelic towns, allied companies, and military success into a host capable of campaigning beyond one local ruler's immediate resources.

The decisive campaign question is:

> **Can temporary military superiority become durable political authority before the host, its providers, and its negotiated obligations disperse?**

```yaml
core_identity:
  political:
    - Provincial kingship
    - Contested overkingship
    - Competing regional sovereignty
    - Client and subordinate rulers
    - Ecclesiastical relationships
    - Norse Gaelic urban and maritime centers
    - Coastal trade and external alliances
    - Later Anglo Norman intervention adaptation and accommodation

  military:
    - Light adaptable infantry
    - Raiding ambush and route warfare
    - Household professional cores
    - Politically assembled campaign hosts
    - Regionally and socially distinct contingents
    - Mobile mounted reconnaissance
    - Coastal and external military exchange
    - Later Galloglaich heavy infantry

  universal_rule: IrishMilitaryPowerIsAssembledFromRelationshipsAndProvidersRatherThanProducedByOneNationalBarracksSystem
```

## 4. Goldilocks Political-Military Model

### 4.1 Player-facing realm interface

The player engages with one primary Ireland realm interface, but the realm does not imply uniform direct control over every provider.

### 4.2 Provincial and major regional power layer

Major provincial and regional powers remain meaningful providers, rivals, clients, coalition partners, or alternative centers of kingship.

```yaml
candidate_major_interfaces_pending_opening_review:
  - Ulster
  - Connacht
  - Munster
  - Leinster
  - Meath
  - Norse Gaelic coastal and urban centers
```

This list is an organizational hypothesis, not an opening ownership map.

### 4.3 Client and subordinate ruler layer

Minor rulers need not become full equal-depth factions. They may provide:

- local fighters;
- guides and route knowledge;
- food, shelter, and passage;
- household retainers;
- hostages or guarantees where historically supported;
- regional specialists;
- temporary campaign obligations;
- refusal, delay, divided service, or defection.

### 4.4 Household layer

Royal and noble households provide the comparatively dependable core around which broader hosts assemble.

### 4.5 External and urban layer

Norse-Gaelic towns, maritime rulers, allied Scottish or Isles-based houses, mercenary companies, ecclesiastical centers, merchants, and later Anglo-Norman or Hiberno-Norman relationships remain distinct providers.

## 24. Combined-Arms Identity

```text
Scouts and mounted riders locate the enemy
                    ↓
Ceithern and javelin troops harass routes and provoke disorder
                    ↓
Archers or additional missiles pressure movement where developed
                    ↓
Household troops preserve the campaign host and receive light troops
                    ↓
Heavier household infantry or later Galloglaich anchor or break contact
                    ↓
Light infantry and cavalry exploit the result
                    ↓
The host regroups before service and political cohesion collapse
```

The core contrast is:

> **Ceithern create instability around the enemy. Household warriors and later Galloglaich create stability inside the Irish host.**

## 25. AI Doctrine

```yaml
IrishMilitaryAI:
  strategic_priorities:
    - Assemble only the host required for the objective
    - Preserve household and provincial minima
    - Maintain provider-specific service terms
    - Use raids for military political or supply effect
    - Avoid destructive action against intended future possessions
    - Preserve return routes and reward distribution
    - Integrate external companies without duplicating their source
    - Pursue durable recognition only when administrative capacity exists

  battle_priorities:
    - Use light troops to scout harass ambush and disengage
    - Avoid prolonged frontal combat without a stable core
    - Preserve household troops for rally and crisis response
    - Use heavy external or household infantry as anchor not disposable attackers
    - Withdraw fragmented contingents before host collapse
```
