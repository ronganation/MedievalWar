## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Ceithern are Ireland's foundational indigenous light-infantry family: locally or regionally provided fighting men whose strength comes from mobility, short-range missile pressure, opportunistic close action, route use, ambush participation, raiding, and the ability to scatter and regroup rather than remain trapped in prolonged formation combat.**

Their signature is:

> **Harass, Close, Scatter, Regroup.**

They are not disposable peasants, universal professional skirmishers, generic swordsmen, or a homogeneous national regiment. They are the broad fighting-men layer from which Irish rulers, regional providers, and campaign hosts draw adaptable light infantry.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ceithern
- **Support name:** Gaelic Fighting Men
- **Class:** Flexible indigenous light infantry
- **Signature:** Harass, Close, Scatter, Regroup
- **Battle promise:** Use terrain, short-range missiles, loose movement, and local openings to disrupt a stronger enemy; close only when the target is exposed; scatter before heavy retaliation; regroup when a captain, route, and supporting host remain available.
- **Best use:** Ambushes, column harassment, raiding, broken-ground screening, attacking isolated missile troops, exploiting disorder, protecting a retreat, and forcing slower enemies to spend time and formation order.
- **Strong against:** Exposed crews, unsupported archers, dispersed levy, fatigued light infantry, baggage, isolated foragers, and enemies already disordered by terrain or another Irish contingent.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable light infantry, cavalry in restricted ground, and formed infantry whose flank or route is genuinely exposed.
- **Weak against:** Fresh armored infantry, disciplined spear formations, heavy cavalry in open ground, protected crossbows, sustained missile fire, and any battle that removes the Ceithern route to scatter and regroup.
- **Campaign bargain:** Ceithern provide broad, flexible fighting capacity by drawing real people from local and regional obligations. Calling them into the host reduces local defense, labor, household, raid, transport, and other provider capacity and creates service, reward, casualty, and return obligations.
- **Flavor:** **They do not need to hold every step of ground. They need the enemy to lose order crossing it.**

```yaml

  supported_by:

    - Familiar companions and provider ties
    - Clear target or route responsibility
    - Current ground knowledge
    - Open scatter and regroup routes
    - Remaining darts or javelins

  spent_by:
    - Loose movement

  broken_by:
```

## 10. Core Battle Loop

```text
Observe the enemy route or exposed body
                  ↓
Harass with darts, javelins, stones, or threat
                  ↓
Force movement, facing, delay, or disorder
                  ↓
Close only against a valuable exposed target
                  ↓
Scatter before heavy retaliation fixes the formation
                  ↓
Regroup at captain, terrain marker, or supporting host
                  ↓
Recommit, screen withdrawal, or return to provider duty
```

The Ceithern do not need to rout the enemy to succeed. Forcing a stronger formation to slow, turn, dress ranks, expend ammunition, protect baggage, or abandon a route may satisfy the mission.

## 23. Combined-Arms Relationships

### Household Core

Household warriors receive Ceithern after scatter, protect the ruler, and stabilize the host. Ceithern screen, harass, and expose threats that the household core should not chase.

### Provincial Spearmen Candidate

Provincial Spearmen, if admitted, own coherent defensive frontage. Ceithern own movement, harassment, and selective close action. Spear-equipped Ceithern must not erase that distinction.

### Ostmen Candidate

Ostmen may provide better protected urban or maritime infantry. Ceithern can operate around them but do not share urban manpower or equipment automatically.

### Royal Mounted Retainers

Mounted retainers relay information, pursue, and intervene. Ceithern should avoid blocking cavalry lanes and should not receive perfect scouting knowledge automatically.

### Later Gaelic Archers

Gaelic Archers may provide more sustained missile pressure after adoption. Ceithern remain short-range, mobile, and flexible.

### Later Galloglaich

```text
Ceithern create instability around the enemy.
Galloglaich create stability inside the Irish host.
```

No Galloglaich relationship applies at the 1062 opening.
