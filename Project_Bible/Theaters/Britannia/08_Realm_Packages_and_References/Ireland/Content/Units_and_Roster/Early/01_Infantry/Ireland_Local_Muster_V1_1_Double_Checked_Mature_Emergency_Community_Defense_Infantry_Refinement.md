This profile defines Ireland's proposed lowest-tier emergency community infantry. It creates no January 1062 population pool, settlement obligation, ruler, provider, local law, weapon stock, formation, garrison, opening placement, payload, importer, runtime behavior, or Unreal asset.

### 0A. Why This Unit Exists

Ceithern already cover the historical family represented in English by `kern`. Kerns are therefore **not** a missing lower tier beneath Ceithern. `Kern` is an anglicized continuation of the Ceithern name and belongs to the same family unless a later period profile requires different presentation.

The missing roster lane is lower and narrower: people who are **not currently qualified Ceithern, household troops, organized spear contingents, or Ostmen**, but who can be summoned locally during immediate danger to delay an attack, guard dependents and stores, reinforce a settlement perimeter, carry supplies, watch routes, and preserve time for real fighting formations to respond.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Local Muster are emergency community defenders assembled from eligible people who are not already committed to stronger military, household, labor, transport, maritime, or protected-dependency roles. Their task is to preserve time, access, people, stores, and escape routes during immediate danger, not to behave as a cheap campaign army.**

Their signature is:

> **Assemble, Delay, Protect, Disperse.**

Their battlefield purchase is emergency mass and local purpose. Their key weakness is that cohesion disappears rapidly when the immediate community-defense purpose becomes unclear, the formation is taken too far from home, stronger troops fail to arrive, or casualties exceed what an improvised body can absorb.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Local Muster
- **Support name:** Irish Community Levy
- **Class:** Emergency settlement-defense infantry
- **Signature:** Assemble, Delay, Protect, Disperse
- **Battle promise:** Assemble eligible local defenders during immediate danger, delay raiders or assaulting light troops at a settlement lane, camp edge, store, ford, or evacuation route, protect the people and resources behind the line, then disperse back into local responsibilities when relief arrives or the immediate purpose ends.
- **Best use:** Emergency settlement defense, camp protection, baggage guard, route watch, evacuation screens, fire response, delaying raiders, and adding short-lived depth behind better infantry.
- **Strong against:** Looters, isolated raiders, very light troops, disorganized pursuers, small breaches, and enemies reluctant to spend time on a defended local objective.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable emergency levies, tired Ceithern-sized raiding groups, and cavalry restricted by barriers or settlement geometry.
- **Weak against:** Formed Ceithern, Tuath Spearmen, Ostmen, household warriors, heavy cavalry, professional missiles, armored infantry, and any prolonged offensive field battle.
- **Campaign bargain:** The muster creates immediate defenders only by pulling real people away from food production, crafts, transport, repair, caregiving, sanitation, firefighting, and recovery. Even without battle deaths, long mobilization can damage the provider.
- **Player instruction:** Use the muster to buy time and protect something concrete. Do not march it as a substitute for the fighting host.
- **Flavor:** **The muster is strongest when everybody knows what must be saved and where safety lies.**

## 5. Collision Firewall

### 5.1 Ceithern

Ceithern are fighting men capable of mobile harassment, controlled scattering, selective close action, raiding, and useful regrouping. Local Muster lack that campaign-fighting identity. A person who becomes qualified and assigned as Ceithern must leave the available emergency muster pool.

### 5.2 Tuath Spearmen

Tuath Spearmen require spear-capable fighters, shield stocks, captains, a defined frontage, and enough organization to receive serious contact. Local Muster may form a rough barrier or short emergency line but cannot reproduce `Gather, Brace, Receive, Release` at full card quality.

### 5.3 Lucht Tighe

Lucht Tighe are retained household professionals. Local Muster protect local people and resources. Household troops may stiffen or command one sector, but their finite people cannot be duplicated inside the muster.

### 5.4 Ostmen

Ostmen are Norse-Gaelic urban and maritime provider specialists with compact spear-axe organization and trade-supported equipment. Local Muster are broader emergency defenders and cannot inherit Ostmen equipment, town identity, maritime skill, or political access.

## 8. Muster Purpose

**Muster Purpose** is the unit's decisive resource. It represents the shared understanding of why people have assembled, what is being protected, where the local line is, how long the danger may last, and where defenders should go when the emergency ends.

```yaml
MusterPurpose:
  supported_by:
    - Visible local threat
    - Concrete protected objective
    - Recognized local leader
    - Familiar ground
    - Dependents stores homes or escape routes behind the line
    - Credible relief
    - Short expected service
    - Clear dispersal signal

  spent_by:
    - Waiting under threat
    - Missile exposure
    - Casualties
    - Conflicting orders
    - Movement away from protected objective
    - Night confusion smoke and fire
    - Pursuit beyond familiar lanes

  broken_by:
    - Objective lost or abandoned without explanation
    - Local leader lost
    - Formation marched far from provider
    - Professional enemy assault
    - Escape route closed
    - Relief proven impossible
    - Casualties among protected people
    - Command attempting offensive field service

  restored_by:
    - Reconnection with local leader
    - New concrete defensive task
    - Relief arrival
    - Safe withdrawal
    - Physical rest and reassembly

  not_restored_by:
```

## 9. Core Battle Loop

```text
Threat becomes immediate and local
               ↓
Eligible defenders assemble at a known point
               ↓
Leaders assign one concrete objective and escape route
               ↓
The muster delays raiders or assaulting light troops
               ↓
Protected people, stores, animals, or wounded move to safety
               ↓
Relief arrives, the enemy withdraws, or the objective becomes untenable
               ↓
The muster disperses back into local responsibilities
               ↓
The provider recovers labor, food, tools, and local security
```

## 22. Combined-Arms Integration

### Ceithern

Ceithern intercept, harass, and counterraid beyond the immediate objective. Local Muster hold the protected lane, move dependents, and avoid following Ceithern into pursuit.

### Tuath Spearmen

Tuath Spearmen take over a serious defensive frontage when enough qualified fighters, shields, spears, and captaincy exist. Local Muster provide labor, depth, evacuation, and emergency replacement but do not gain the spear unit's formation promise.

### Lucht Tighe

Lucht Tighe respond to the decisive crisis and protect the ruler or command. Local Muster preserve the background functions that household troops cannot cover everywhere.

### Ostmen

Ostmen organize specialized town and port defense through proper providers. Local Muster may supplement fire response, evacuation, stores, and secondary lanes without entering the Ostmen manpower ledger automatically.
