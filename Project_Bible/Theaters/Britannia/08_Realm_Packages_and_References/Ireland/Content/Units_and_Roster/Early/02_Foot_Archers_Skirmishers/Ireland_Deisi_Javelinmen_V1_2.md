### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Deisi Javelinmen are trained southern regional light infantry who approach through a protected lane, cast dedicated javelins at short effective range, exploit a real disruption through a bounded charge or flank action, and withdraw before heavier enemies or reserves fix the formation.**

Their signature is:

**Approach, Cast, Exploit, Withdraw.**

The unit is stronger and narrower than ordinary Ceithern. The Deisi purchase is neither passive skirmishing nor a guaranteed javelin charge. The purchase is a disciplined assault decision:

1. reach a useful range and angle;
2. release a concentrated cast;
3. read what physically happened;
4. charge only if a real opening exists;
5. withdraw while a route still exists.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Deisi Javelinmen
- **Support name:** Javelinmen of Deisi Muman
- **Class:** Trained regional javelin assault infantry
- **Signature:** Approach, Cast, Exploit, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Reach a useful angle, strike with a concentrated close-range cast, exploit only the disorder actually created, and leave before prepared reserves or heavy troops convert the attack into an attritional melee.
- **Best use:** Flanks, road and ford approaches, counter-skirmishing, counter-raids, attacks on archers and crews, pursuit disruption, and supporting Ceithern or Tuath Spearmen.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exposed archers, crews, raiders, disrupted pursuers, and lightly protected cavalry constrained by terrain or another formation.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded troops changing facing, tired medium infantry, cavalry already slowed or fixed, and comparable regional missile infantry.
- **Weak against:** Fresh heavy infantry, prepared spear lines, compact Ostmen defense, ordered Lucht Tighe, heavy cavalry in open terrain, sustained archery, and extended melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** A valid southern provider spends practiced throwers, captains, dedicated javelin stocks, regional defense, transport, hunting capacity, and replacement time to field a more decisive but less flexible light-infantry card than Ceithern.
- **Flavor:** **The cast creates the opening. Discipline decides whether to take it.**
