## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Irish Bowmen are lightly protected provider-bound shooters who watch one approach or flank, loose measured arrows against exposed light targets, screen friendly movement, and return before cavalry, close infantry, or superior missiles fix them.**

Their signature is:

**Watch, Loose, Screen, Return.**

Their decisive resource is **Shooting Purpose**: the formation's ability to connect a real observation sector, usable bows, compatible arrows, a recognized leader, a protected firing lane, one bounded target relationship, and a physical route back to the provider or receiving host.

## 2. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Irish Bowmen
- **Support name:** Local Bowmen
- **Class:** Early light bow skirmishers
- **Signature:** Watch, Loose, Screen, Return
- **Battle promise:** Watch one approach, apply measured bow pressure against exposed light targets, screen a friendly movement or frontage, and return before cavalry or sustained counterfire closes.
- **Best use:** Behind Tuath Spearmen, on a protected flank, near a camp or settlement approach, covering Ceithern withdrawal, and discouraging exposed skirmishers, crews, or messengers.
- **Strong against:** Unshielded light infantry, exposed crews, dispersed skirmishers, messengers, and troops delayed by terrain or friendly frontage.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded light infantry, moving cavalry held at distance, and medium infantry exposed from a favorable angle.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry, armored infantry, crossbows, developed Gaelic Archers in a sustained exchange, concentrated missiles, poor visibility, blocked withdrawal, and close melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** The card requires actual shooters, bows, strings, arrows, carriers, local leaders, practice, repair, and a provider able to spare them from hunting, watch, escort, labor, and local defense.

## 6. Core Battle Loop

```text
Watch one approach, flank, route, or protected sector
        ↓
Mark a useful exposed target and confirm the return lane
        ↓
Loose measured arrows rather than emptying the stock
        ↓
Screen friendly movement or force a light enemy response
        ↓
Cease before cavalry, crossbows, or close infantry fix the body
        ↓
Return behind Tuath Spearmen, terrain, camp works, or another receiving formation
        ↓
Recount shooters, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, carriers, and provider obligations
```
