## 2. Historical and Admission Verdict

The sling remained a practical medieval missile weapon and could be used by troops preceding armies, harassing formations, attacking flanks, and covering withdrawal. ## 3. Direct Unit Verdict

**Tuath Slingers are locally supplied Early Era missile specialists who loosen into casting files, range exposed troops and support elements, sustain repeated projectile pressure, and withdraw behind spears, household infantry, or terrain before direct combat closes.**

Their signature is:

> **Range, Loose, Suppress, Withdraw.**

Their purchase is persistent low-cost harassment rather than close assault or reliable killing. Déisi Javelinmen create a violent short-range opening. Saigteoiri provide bow-based missile delivery. Ceithern combine mobile harassment with opportunistic close combat. Tuath Slingers maintain repeated disruptive pressure from farther away than thrown missiles while remaining extremely weak if fixed.

## 4. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Tuath Slingers
- **Support name:** Irish Slingers
- **Class:** Early regional foot skirmishers
- **Signature:** Range, Loose, Suppress, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Establish loose casting files, maintain repeated stone or prepared-projectile pressure against an exposed formation, disrupt pace and facing, then withdraw before cavalry or formed infantry reaches the unit.
- **Best use:** Infantry flanks, slopes, banks, field edges, broken approaches, counter-skirmishing, covering Ceithern withdrawal, and pressuring exposed missile troops or crews.
- **Strong against:** Unshielded light troops, enemy skirmishers, crews, carriers, exposed archers, loose formations, and pursuers forced through a restricted route.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded infantry from an exposed angle, cavalry constrained by terrain or spears, and medium infantry already disordered.
- **Weak against:** Armor, good shields, cavalry with an open approach, archers, crossbowmen, formed infantry, poor casting space, and any sustained melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Low weapon cost is balanced by specialist practice, selected ammunition, carriers, local providers, limited protection, and poor survival when withdrawal fails.
- **Flavor:** **The stones are cheap. The practiced rhythm, open ground, and safe withdrawal are not.**

## 7. Core Battle Loop

```text
Select a flank, slope, bank, field edge, or protected rear lane
                         ↓
Loose the formation and establish ammunition access
                         ↓
Range an exposed formation, missile body, crew, or approach
                         ↓
Maintain alternating sling pressure
                         ↓
Force shields, spacing, pace, or facing to change
                         ↓
Shift before the enemy fixes the casting body
                         ↓
Concentrate briefly or cover friendly movement
                         ↓
Withdraw behind spears, household infantry, or terrain
```
