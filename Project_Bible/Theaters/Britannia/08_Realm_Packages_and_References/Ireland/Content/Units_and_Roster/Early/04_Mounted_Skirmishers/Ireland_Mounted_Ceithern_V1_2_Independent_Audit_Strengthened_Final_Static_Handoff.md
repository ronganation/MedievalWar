### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Mounted Ceithern are a small, conditionally available body of Irish fighting men with suitable light horses, local route knowledge, short-range missiles, and enough mounted practice to scout, warn, harass exposed targets, carry a light infantry mission across distance, and dismount when terrain or combat makes mounted action unwise.**

Their signature is:

**Scout, Cast, Dismount, Return.**

They are not early Hobelars, Royal Mounted Retainers with cheaper equipment, or Ceithern magically placed on horses. Their purpose is to establish the **pre-Hobelar development foundation**:

- horses provide operational reach rather than knightly shock;
- javelins provide short-range disruption rather than sustained mounted skirmishing;
- scouting provides information with delay and uncertainty;
- dismounting allows the formation to complete a light-infantry mission in unsuitable ground;
- every mounted mission must preserve a route back to handlers, horses, provider, or receiving host.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Mounted Ceithern
- **Support name:** Irish Mounted Scouts
- **Class:** Early mounted scout and javelin retainers
- **Signature:** Scout, Cast, Dismount, Return
- **Battle promise:** Find and screen routes, threaten exposed troops with a limited mounted cast, dismount when the ground or mission favors foot action, and preserve horses and riders through an ordered return.
- **Best use:** Scouting, flank warning, counter-scouting, route security, ambush support, restrained javelin harassment, dismounted seizure of a ford or track, and covering a withdrawal.
- **Strong against:** Isolated scouts, messengers, exposed crews, routed troops, scattered light infantry, and unsupported skirmishers.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable light horse, lightly protected cavalry already slowed, and infantry whose flank is exposed while another formation fixes it.
- **Weak against:** Royal Mounted Retainers in direct contact, prepared spears, heavy cavalry, protected archers, dense infantry, prolonged missile exchanges, marshes, woods, and blocked horse-return routes.
- **Campaign bargain:** Fielding the unit spends scarce suitable horses, riders with both route and infantry experience, handlers, tack, javelins, scouts, guides, fodder, local messenger capacity, and provider recovery.
- **Flavor:** **Ride to know the road, cast to unsettle it, fight on foot when needed, and bring the horses home.**
