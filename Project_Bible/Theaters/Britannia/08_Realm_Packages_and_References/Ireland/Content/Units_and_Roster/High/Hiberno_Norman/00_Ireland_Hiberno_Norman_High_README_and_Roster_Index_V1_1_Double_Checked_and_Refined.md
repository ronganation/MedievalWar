## 1. Folder Identity

The Hiberno-Norman High portfolio is not a replacement roster for Gaelic Ireland and is not a universal Normanization path. It represents later Irish military development through:

- Lordship households, castles, fortified towns, garrisons, roads, workshops, tenure, and stored equipment.
- Military Accommodation between Gaelic and lordship providers.
- Gaelicised frontier relationships and mixed-host command.
- Selective adaptation of existing Irish military families.
- Sharper lordship heavy-cavalry and field-crossbow specialization.
- Finite people, horses, officers, equipment, workshops, transport, service obligations, and recovery capacity.

## 4. Roster-Wide Authority

### Hiberno-Norman Lordship, Military Accommodation, and Gaelic Integration Development Authority V1.1

Controls the second major Irish military grammar:

- Gaelic, Hiberno-Norman lordship, and Gaelicised frontier expressions may coexist.
- Military Accommodation is multidimensional rather than one culture meter.
- Castle Reach depends on garrisons, routes, supplies, workshops, relationships, and countryside access.
- Castle capture does not transfer providers, service obligations, workshops, or political legitimacy automatically.
- Mixed hosts require physical command, liaison, signals, passages, supply, withdrawal, and dispute procedures.
- Capability transfer does not equal cultural replacement.

## 5. Infantry Portfolio

### Dismounted Serjeants

```yaml
signature: FormAdvanceReceiveSecure
role: LordshipGarrisonFieldFrontageRoadConvoyAndOccupationInfantry
source: ContinuingLordshipServiceTenureGarrisonOrPaidInstitution
```

The dependable workhorse infantry of lordship power. They advance in order, receive one serious threat, secure roads, gates, convoys, camps, towns, and captured positions, and sustain continuing garrison-field service.

### Marcher Retainers

```yaml
signature: ScoutGuardRaidRally
role: GaelicisedFrontierReconnaissanceGuardCounterRaidAndRecoveryInfantry
source: MixedLordshipRetainersGuidesClientsAndLiaison
```

A coherent Gaelicised frontier company. Their value comes from route knowledge, guarded objectives, bounded counter-raiding, recovery burdens, and castle-backed rally rather than a generic Norman-Gaelic equipment mixture.

### Galloglaich of the Marches

```yaml
signature: HoldClearSecureReturn
role: LordshipIntegratedHeavyPassageClearingAndSecurityTransferInfantry
source: ExistingGalloglaichCompanyOrSettledKindredUnderDurableLordshipIntegration
```

An existing Galloglaich company adapted to marcher service. The company holds one critical passage, conducts a short heavy clearing action, transfers security to ordinary lordship or frontier troops, and returns before becoming permanent garrison infantry.

### Muire of the Marches

```yaml
signature: GuardRelieveContestRejoin
role: MixedHouseholdSectorReliefSwordContestAndPoliticalMilitaryReconnection
source: ExistingMuireOrLandedRetainerProviderUnderDurableMixedHouseholdCompact
```

A mixed-household relief formation. It guards one sector, answers one physically reachable crisis, assumes a bounded threat, contests the immediate enemy, and reconnects with the household compact.


An Irish archer tradition adapted to frontier and mixed-host service. The card observes one approach, suppresses a bounded threat, covers one named movement or asset, and withdraws through a receiving position.


The field-service expression of Ireland's crossbow capability. It trades prepared-position endurance and static bolt depth for transport, temporary shelter, serjeant support, deliberate field fire, and movement between strongpoints.

## 7. Cavalry Portfolio

### Mounted Serjeants

```yaml
signature: PatrolSupportChargeReturn
role: LordshipPatrolConvoyKnightSupportCounterRaidSettlementReliefAndBoundedMediumContact
source: ContinuingLordshipMountedServiceInstitution
```

The practical mounted-service layer of the lordship system. It handles roads, convoys, counter-raiding, settlement relief, knight support, bounded charges, and ordered return.

### Hiberno-Norman Knights

```yaml
signature: MusterChargePressReform
role: MainLordshipHeavyLanceWing
source: CastleTenureHouseholdHorseArmorLanceAndSupportInstitution
```

The normal scarce heavy-lance formation of the Hiberno-Norman path. Its battlefield ceiling depends on charge geometry, horse-rider pairs, squires, tools, roads, reform space, ransom, succession, and provider recovery.

### Lordship Banner Lancers

```yaml
signature: GatherFixBreakWithdraw
role: MagnateGradeSingleBreakthroughAndCounterHeavyReserve
source: SelectiveConcentrationFromMatureKnightlyCapacity
```

A tiny magnate-grade reserve above the normal knightly wing. It gathers selected rider-horse pairs, commits to one physically fixed high-value target, delivers the strongest single breakthrough of the path, and withdraws before entanglement.

### Cavalry Development and Progression Authority V1.1

Controls two persistent Early mounted foundations and the three Hiberno-Norman High expressions.

