## 9. Hold

Hold establishes bounded heavy frontage around a gate shoulder, road junction, ford, breach approach, convoy crisis, settlement lane, or frontier strongpoint.

```yaml
hold_states:
  Establishing
  OrderedHold
  PressuredHold
  CompressedHold
  BreachedHold
  FragmentedHold
```

Hold may check light and medium infantry, contain heavy troops briefly, and protect the clearing or handoff relationship. It does not grant permanent anchoring or several-direction immunity.

## 10. Clear

A valid clearing action requires:

- One obstruction whose removal matters
- A protected or acceptable approach
- Captain release
- Sufficient Company Integration
- A known halt marker
- A viable handoff and return relationship

```yaml
clear_commitment_bands:
  FileCorrection: RestoreOrOpenSmallLocalSpace
  ShortClear: MainHeavyActionAgainstValidObstruction
  CrisisClear: ExceptionalActionToSavePassageOrReceivingForce
  RecallBoundPressure: FollowOnlyInsideHandoffRelationship
  DeepPursuit: ProhibitedByDefault
```

Clearing may displace light or disordered medium infantry, break a localized obstruction, reopen a passage, or force a reserve response.

It cannot guarantee armor defeat, rout, breach clearance, cavalry stoppage, or victory against fresh supported heavy infantry.

## 11. Secure and Transfer

```yaml
security_states:
  ObstructionRemoved
  PassageUsable
  ImmediateThreatChecked
  ReceivingFormationEntering
  ResponsibilityTransferred
  TransferFailed
  PositionAbandoned
```

Transfer requires:

1. Identify the receiving formation or authority.
2. Establish signals and friendly identification.
3. Stabilize the passage.
4. Move wounded personnel, attendants, and obstructions.
5. Allow the receiving formation to occupy the security boundary.
6. Confirm responsibility.
7. Withdraw Galloglaich files by group.

Winning the clearing action without completing transfer is incomplete success.

## 17. Matchups

### Strong or favorable

- Light infantry in sustained contact
- Disordered medium infantry
- Raiders or missile troops trapped near a passage
- A localized obstruction already fixed by friendly troops
- Settlement and road fighting with controlled frontage

### Conditional

- Comparable heavy infantry
- Dismounted Serjeants or Muire in prepared order
- Galloglaich from another company
- Heavy cavalry in restricted ground with spear support

### Weak

- Fresh supported Galloglaich or elite heavy infantry in prolonged combat
- Heavy cavalry in open ground
- Crossbows and concentrated archery
- Encirclement
- Several separated objectives
- Long isolation from attendants and support

## 21. Trust, Succession, and Contract Failure

Provider trust considers:

- Whether the mission justified heavy-company risk
- Whether lordship support and handoff functioned
- Whether captain, standard, attendants, wounded personnel, equipment, and baggage returned
- Whether pay, land, hospitality, ransom, burial, treatment, and repair were honored
- Whether the company was used as disposable garrison infantry

Succession may result in continuation, renegotiation, divided service, neutrality, independent kindred service, departure, or defection.

Victory does not reset the compact.

## 24. AI Contract

The AI should:

- Commit the company only to a valuable passage or obstruction.
- Require Dismounted Serjeants, Marcher Retainers, a garrison, or another receiving force for transfer.
- Avoid prolonged heavy combat after the objective is cleared.
- Protect attendants, baggage, and return route.
- Preserve the company when lordship support or Military Accommodation fails.

The AI must not:

- Use the unit as disposable line infantry.
- Gain it from castle ownership alone.
- Duplicate Galloglaich across Gaelic, mercenary, and lordship pools.
- Treat the unit as armored Dismounted Serjeants.
