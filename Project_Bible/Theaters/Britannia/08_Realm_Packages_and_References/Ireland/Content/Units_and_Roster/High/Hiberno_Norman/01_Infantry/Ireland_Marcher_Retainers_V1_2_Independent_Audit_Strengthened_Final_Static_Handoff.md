## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Marcher Retainers are mature High Era frontier infantry maintained by Gaelicised or locally adapted Hiberno-Norman lordships. They scout the near routes with guides and selected files, guard a road, convoy, pass, settlement edge, or castle approach, conduct a bounded counter-raid against exposed enemies, and rally back to a recognized standard, garrison point, or mixed-host receiving line.**

> **Scout, Guard, Raid, Rally.**

The formation's defining purchase is **frontier continuity**. Marcher Retainers combine local route knowledge and counter-raiding practice with the persistent equipment, command, storage, and recovery institutions of a lordship.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Marcher Retainers
- **Support name:** Retainers of the Irish Marches
- **Class:** High Era Gaelicised frontier infantry
- **Signature:** Scout, Guard, Raid, Rally
- **Battle promise:** Read the near ground, secure one route or frontier objective, strike exposed raiders and support troops, and rally to castle-backed or mixed-host support before a larger formation fixes the company.
- **Best use:** Roads, fords, wooded edges, settlement outskirts, convoy routes, camp approaches, counter-raids, flank security, and limited attacks on exposed missile or light infantry.
- **Strong against:** Raiders, scouts, dispersed light infantry, exposed archers, crews, loot carriers, fragmented pursuers, and small hostile detachments.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable medium infantry, Ceithern caught away from favorable routes, and tired spear troops attacked from a useful angle.
- **Weak against:** Galloglaich, Armored Lucht Tighe, formed Dismounted Serjeants, heavy cavalry, protected crossbows, deep spear formations, and prolonged open-field melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Requires a functioning frontier compact, local guides and relationships, lordship retainers, serviceable equipment, castle or town support, recognized signals, reward and return agreements, and simultaneous protection of local and lordship minimums.
- **Flavor:** **The castle gives the company somewhere to return. The countryside teaches the company how to survive getting there.**
