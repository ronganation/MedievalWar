## 2. Controlling Player-Facing Sentence

> Guard one mixed-household sector, relieve a threatened gate, hall, chapel, district, or command relationship, contest the immediate enemy through disciplined sword-and-shield fighting, and rejoin the household compact before prolonged attrition or political fracture consumes the retainer body.

## 3. Direct Unit Verdict

Muire of the Marches are not Normanized Muire and not generic marcher infantry. They represent an existing Irish landed-retainer tradition entering a durable mixed-household relationship with a Gaelicised or accommodated lordship.

The unit remains Irish in source identity, captaincy, retainer practice, and sword-and-shield character. The marcher relationship adds mixed command, restricted-ground relief, castle and settlement support, household liaison, service negotiation, and the need to reconnect two political-military systems after battle.

## 4. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Muire of the Marches
- **Support name:** Marcher Household Men
- **Class:** High Era mixed-household sword-and-shield relief infantry
- **Signature:** Guard, Relieve, Contest, Rejoin
- **Battle promise:** Protect one politically important household or settlement sector, enter a bounded threatened passage, win or stabilize one close infantry contest, then reconnect with the household compact before the retainer core is isolated.
- **Best use:** Gates, streets, halls, camp districts, estate approaches, chapel or administrative sectors, relief passages, and mixed-host command areas.
- **Strong against:** Raiders, light infantry, exposed missiles, disrupted spearmen, and comparable medium infantry in restricted ground.
- **Conditional against:** Ordinary Muire, Dismounted Serjeants, household infantry, and tired heavy troops with support.
- **Weak against:** Fresh Galloglaich, Armored Lucht Tighe in crisis posture, heavy cavalry, protected crossbows, concentrated archery, encirclement, and long unsupported frontage.
- **Campaign bargain:** Requires an existing Muire or qualified landed-retainer provider, a real mixed-household compact, captain and liaison, equipment, protected local functions, service terms, and postbattle political reconnection.
- **Flavor:** **The march is held by households that have learned to fight beside one another without becoming the same household.**

## 28. AI Contract

The AI should:

- Guard one politically or militarily important mixed-household sector.
- Answer one real relief call through a known passage.
- Contest light or medium infantry in restricted ground.
- Reject prolonged heavy combat, open-ground cavalry, deep pursuit, and relief without a rejoin plan.
- Preserve captain, standard, liaison, and source-provider capacity.

The AI must not:

- Use the unit as generic medium infantry.
- Gain it from castle ownership or Gaelicisation alone.
- Duplicate ordinary Muire people and equipment.
- Substitute it for Dismounted Serjeants or Galloglaich of the Marches.

## 29. Autoresolve Contract

Autoresolve should account for:

- Household Compact
- Sector and relief mission
- Restricted-ground relationship
- Captain, standard, liaison, and passage
- Equipment, support, and rejoin route
- Enemy missiles, cavalry, heavy infantry, and several-direction pressure
- Provider and political recovery cost

Autoresolve must not convert mixed culture, better armor, or castle support into automatic combat power.
