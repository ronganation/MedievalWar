## 2. Category Purpose

The category represents two different ways that established Irish missile traditions can enter the Hiberno-Norman or Gaelicised-lordship military world.

```yaml
BowmenOfTheIrishMarches:
  source_chain: SaigteoiriToGaelicArchersToQualifiedMarcherExpression
  development_type: FrontierInstitutionalAdaptation
  battlefield_purchase: ObservationSuppressionNamedCoverAndSupportedWithdrawal

CrossbowmenOfTheIrishLordships:
  source_chain: IndependentIrishCrossbowmenEmergenceThenQualifiedFieldAdaptation
  development_type: LordshipFieldDoctrineAndProviderAllocation
  battlefield_purchase: TemporaryPositionHighValueDirectFireAndControlledDisplacement
```

These formations are not a light-to-heavy ladder. Both are High Era specialist expressions, but the bowmen carry an Irish archery tradition into frontier service while the crossbowmen carry an independently developed crossbow institution into recurring field operations.
