### Naming Verdict

**Crossbowmen of the Irish Lordships** is the recommended player-facing name.

It communicates that the formation belongs to Ireland's later lordship military system without claiming that every crossbow user is culturally identical or imported. **Lordship Crossbowmen** remains the readable support name. **Marcher Crossbowmen** remains an alternate presentation for providers whose identity is specifically frontier-oriented.

## 3. Direct Unit Verdict

Crossbowmen of the Irish Lordships are a selective field-service expression of the existing Irish Crossbowmen family. They do not replace ordinary Irish Crossbowmen, who remain superior in durable prepared defense around castles, towns, ports, gates, and established shelter.

The new company adapts crossbow specialists to recurring lordship field operations:

- movement between strongpoints;
- convoy protection;
- support of Dismounted Serjeant advances;
- temporary fieldworks;
- siege approaches governed by separate siege authority;
- captured positions not yet fully fortified;
- mixed-host field battles;
- deliberate displacement before the reload position is fixed.

The formation is not universally stronger. It exchanges prepared-position endurance, deep shelter, and large static bolt reserves for field coordination, temporary protection, movement discipline, and repeatable displacement.

## 4. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Crossbowmen of the Irish Lordships
- **Support name:** Lordship Crossbowmen
- **Class:** High Era field crossbow company
- **Signature:** Prepare, Advance, Punish, Displace
- **Battle promise:** Move behind a protected lordship advance, prepare a temporary direct-fire position, punish one exposed valuable target, and displace before the enemy reaches the reload cycle.
- **Best use:** Roads, convoys, fieldworks, siege approaches, settlement outskirts, captured positions, serjeant advances, mixed-host flanks, and castle-to-castle operations.
- **Strong against:** Exposed armored retainers, compact infantry crossing a clear lane, missile troops in direct exchange, crews, and cavalry constrained by terrain or friendly spears.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded infantry, moving cavalry, comparable crossbow companies, Gaelic Archers with protection, and targets at marginal angle.
- **Weak against:** Fast cavalry, Ceithern infiltration, dispersed troops, prolonged archery without protection, blocked roads, lost pack transport, close melee, and positions without a receiving formation.
- **Campaign bargain:** Stronger field access requires trained users, tools, portable shelter, pack and carrier capacity, roads, workshops, serjeant protection, and direct competition with castle and town crossbow defense.

```text

        ↓

        ↓
Prepare tools, bolts, files, shelter, and target register
        ↓
Mark one valuable exposed target
        ↓
Release by files, section, or one deliberate concentration
        ↓
Read the result and enemy response
        ↓

        ↓
Displace before cavalry, missiles, or infantry fix the company
        ↓
Rebuild the Field Cycle at the next receiving position
```

## 27. AI Contract

The AI should:

- Move behind a real receiving formation.
- Select valuable exposed targets.
- Avoid wasting bolts against dispersed low-value troops.
- Prefer file or section fire unless one decisive release is justified.
- Hold loaded deterrence against approaching cavalry or light infantry.
- Displace before the receiving frontage or road connection collapses.
- Preserve users, tools, transport, and workshop relationships.

The AI must not:

- Treat the card as mobile archers.
- Fire effectively while advancing.
- Use the unit as melee infantry.
- Receive free replenishment from castle proximity.
- Duplicate static Irish Crossbowmen capacity.
