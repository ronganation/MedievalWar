### 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lordship Banner Lancers are a rare Late High Era elite mounted reserve gathered beneath a great lordship banner from selected knights, veteran lance files, squires, and the best available battle horses. They wait behind the ordinary knightly wing, fix one decisive target through position and supporting pressure, deliver a concentrated breakthrough charge, and withdraw before their scarce horses, officers, and magnate household become trapped in prolonged combat.**

**Gather, Fix, Break, Withdraw.**

### 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Lordship Banner Lancers
- **Support name:** Destrier Lancers
- **Class:** Rare Late High Era elite Norman-Irish shock cavalry
- **Signature:** Gather, Fix, Break, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Hold a tiny magnate-grade reserve until infantry, crossbows, Mounted Serjeants, or ordinary Hiberno-Norman Knights create one real opening; then deliver the strongest concentrated Norman-side charge in the Irish roster and leave before the breakthrough turns into a melee trap.
- **Best use:** Decisive flank charge, countercharge against committed cavalry, attack on an exposed enemy commander or banner, relief of a failing sector, and breaking elite infantry already fixed and disordered.
- **Strong against:** Light and medium cavalry, exposed missile troops, disordered armored infantry, tired noble cavalry, fragmented reserves, and high-value targets without a prepared spear screen.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable heavy cavalry, shielded heavy infantry from the flank, and prepared noble cavalry whose formation has already been disrupted.
- **Weak against:** Braced spears, stakes, ditches, protected crossbows, fresh deep infantry, bog or deep mud, enclosed streets, layered reserves, horse exhaustion, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Requires a great lordship or coalition capable of concentrating selected riders, captains, banners, superior battle horses, armor, lances, squires, grooms, farriers, remounts, ransom credit, and replacement capacity beyond the protected needs of ordinary knights and Mounted Serjeants.
- **Flavor:** **The knights open the battle. The banner lancers are committed only when one charge can decide it.**
