## 5. Infantry Portfolio

### Armored Lucht Tighe

The developed household crisis expression of Lucht Tighe. The card concentrates household equipment, leadership, protection, and service depth without deleting the broader household family.

### Veteran Ceithern

The experienced Ceithern expression. The unit develops route knowledge, harassment, selective close action, regrouping, and veteran leadership while preserving ordinary Ceithern as the wider and more recoverable light-infantry family.

### Muire

A High Era landed-retainer sword formation rooted in household, estate, service, and political obligations rather than a generic sword-infantry tier.

### Galloglaich

A scarce Norse-Gaelic heavy-infantry retinue. Galloglaich are company- and provider-bound, expensive to lose, and cannot be produced by a generic heavy-infantry technology or building.

### Ulster Swordsmen

A Late High regional sword-assault formation. The card adds a distinct Ulster provider and assault identity rather than functioning as generic improved Muire, Lucht Tighe, or Galloglaich.

## 6. Foot Archers and Skirmishers Portfolio

### Veteran Deisi Javelinmen

A rare veteran concentration from the broader Deisi family. Its stronger assault sequence consumes experienced cadres, leaders, equipment, carriers, and recovery capacity rather than replacing ordinary Deisi Javelinmen.

### Gaelic Archers

The mature formation-archery family. Gaelic Archers require deeper shooter pools, leadership, bow-and-arrow craft, carriers, target allocation, concentration, and relocation practice while Saigteoiri remain the lower-burden persistent bow family.

### Irish Crossbowmen

An independent High Era specialist family requiring qualified users, crossbows, bolts, strings, spanning tools, workshops, storage, repair, shelter, and collective mechanical fire-cycle practice. Irish Crossbowmen are not a direct upgrade from bow, sling, or javelin families.

## 7. Cavalry Portfolio

### Gaelic Horsemen

The normal Traditional High Era regional mounted wing. Gaelic Horsemen own formation, flank threat, bounded medium charge, counterattack, and physical rally.

### Hobelars

A mature operational formation joining patrol, scouting, route security, credible dismounted combat, horse holding, remounting, and repeated relocation. Mounted Ceithern remain a historical foundation and separate persistent unit rather than an automatic Hobelar upgrade.

### Ridire

A scarce noble mounted reserve for stronger committed contact, a short controlled drive, high-value intervention, and difficult political and provider recovery. Ridire do not replace Gaelic Horsemen.

## 8. Mounted Skirmishers and Support Portfolio

### Horseboys

Horseboys own horse-line control, relay, spare-horse and tack movement, fragile defensive dart screening, and bounded recovery. The card does not create free remounts, healing, ammunition, or replenishment and does not promote into combat cavalry.
