### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Armored Household Warriors are the developed High Era expression of Ireland's ruler-bound household infantry: a compact, better-protected formation that guards the command sector, receives one serious local attack, launches a controlled counterstroke, and recovers before the household's trusted military core is consumed.**

Their signature is:

**Guard, Receive, Counter, Recover.**

The separate card survives only because its mission is narrower, heavier, and more expensive than Lucht Tighe. Lucht Tighe remain the flexible Early and persistent household front that can hold, rally, open passages, and return. Armored Household Warriors commit a mature equipment and retainer packet to one severe crisis. They can withstand heavier contact and deliver a stronger bounded counterattack, but they are slower, rarer, less flexible, and more damaging to lose.

### 2. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Armored Household Warriors
- **Support name:** Armored Lucht Tighe
- **Class:** High Era armored household infantry
- **Signature:** Guard, Receive, Counter, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Protect one critical command-sector frontage, receive a serious attack in compact armored files, counter when the enemy becomes committed or exposed, and recover before fatigue, missiles, flanking, or pursuit destroy the household core.
- **Best use:** Command-sector defense, local heavy reserve, relieving a failing frontage, defending a gate shoulder or camp center, protecting a ruler's withdrawal, and countering medium or heavy infantry already fixed by another Irish formation.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, medium infantry in direct contact, disordered spearmen, exposed retainers, breach attackers, and enemies committed against Tuath Spearmen or Lucht Tighe.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable household infantry, heavy infantry of similar scale, and cavalry constrained by terrain, spears, or a protected flank.
- **Weak against:** Galloglaich in a prolonged company fight, sustained crossbow fire, concentrated archery, heavy cavalry on an open flank, encirclement, repeated commitments, and deep pursuit.
- **Campaign bargain:** The formation requires trusted retainers, officers, armor, shields, weapons, attendants, maintenance, household income or land support, protected residence security, and long recovery after losses.
- **Flavor:** **The household can survive one terrible crisis. The ruler must decide which crisis deserves it.**

### 5. Core Battle Loop

```text
Assign one critical command-sector frontage
                    ↓
Guard the principal, standard, route, or crisis point
                    ↓
Receive serious local pressure in compact files
                    ↓
Read enemy commitment, reserves, flanks, and recovery lane
                    ↓
Counter the committed or exposed threat
                    ↓
Halt before pursuit separates the household
                    ↓
Recover wounded, officers, standard, armor, and formation
                    ↓
Resume guard duty, hand off the frontage, or withdraw
```

### 16. Combined Arms

- **Tuath Spearmen** hold the broader frontage and create relief space.
- **Lucht Tighe** maintain the flexible household sector, passage, rally, and continuity role.
- **Armored Household Warriors** answer the single severe command-sector crisis.
- **Muire** retain their landed-retainer swordsmen lane and must not be reduced to spare household swords.
- **Galloglaich** own the contracted heavy-company contest and superior prolonged endurance.
- **Gaelic Archers and Irish Crossbowmen** shape and punish enemy approach while requiring the household to manage real firing and receiving lanes.
- **Royal Mounted Retainers and Hobelars** guard flanks, routes, and recovery without granting automatic safety.
- **Ceithern and Veteran Ceithern** create disruption and return through physical passages.
