## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Galloglaich are compact Norse-Gaelic heavy-infantry retinues whose disciplined files anchor an important part of the Irish host, receive serious enemy pressure without immediately yielding, break exposed or committed troops through controlled heavy close action, and endure long enough for the ruler's wider plan to work.**

Their signature is:

> **Anchor, Receive, Break, Endure.**

Their purchase is not generic high armor. Galloglaich combine company cohesion, heavy equipment, captain-led close combat, contractual service, and confidence in the fighters beside them. They provide Ireland with a true heavy-infantry center while remaining scarce, politically bounded, costly, and vulnerable to isolation, missiles, cavalry maneuver, fatigue, and the loss of captains or attendants.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Galloglaich
- **Support name:** Gallowglass
- **Class:** Contract Norse-Gaelic heavy infantry retinue
- **Signature:** Anchor, Receive, Break, Endure
- **Battle promise:** Hold one important piece of the battle line, receive serious pressure in ordered files, drive into an enemy already committed or exposed, and remain coherent through the close fight longer than ordinary Irish infantry.
- **Best use:** Command-sector anchoring, defending a narrow critical frontage, meeting enemy heavy foot, controlled counterattack, fighting around gates and breaches, and serving as the receiving body for withdrawing Ceithern or Hobelars.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, medium infantry in direct close combat, exposed household or retinue troops of comparable scale, shield lines already fixed or disordered, and enemies forced into a narrow frontage.
- **Conditional against:** Heavy infantry of comparable experience, cavalry caught in restricted ground, and spear formations attacked after their order has been disrupted.
- **Weak against:** Sustained protected crossbow fire, strong archery concentration, heavy cavalry on an open flank, deep encirclement, exhaustion, poor command support, and prolonged pursuit away from the company standard.
- **Campaign bargain:** The ruler purchases dependable heavy infantry through contracts, rewards, land, hospitality, political relationships, imported or locally maintained equipment, and acceptance of an armed retinue with its own captain, kinship, service expectations, and bargaining power.
- **Flavor:** **Where the host must not bend, the Galloglaich stand. Where the enemy has already committed, the Galloglaich answer.**
