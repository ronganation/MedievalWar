## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Muire are High Era landed-retainer sword infantry who guard a recognized lordly or estate-centered military relationship, advance behind broader frontage, contest one important infantry sector through disciplined sword-and-shield fighting, and return before a finite provider body is consumed in indefinite attrition.**

Their signature is:

**Guard, Advance, Contest, Return.**

Their defining battlefield purchase is not elite fear or heavy plate. It is **repeatable medium-heavy sword infantry from a finite landed-retainer provider**, able to fight longer and more deliberately than Ceithern, but broader and less ruler-bound than Lucht Tighe and materially lighter and less contract-specialized than Galloglaich.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Muire
- **Support name:** Landed Retainer Swordsmen
- **Class:** High Era sword-and-shield retainer infantry
- **Signature:** Guard, Advance, Contest, Return
- **Battle promise:** Protect one provider-linked sector, advance under cover, defeat light infantry and contest comparable medium infantry, then return before prolonged heavy contact destroys the retainer body.
- **Best use:** Supporting Tuath frontage, exploiting a stabilized local fight, guarding a lordly standard or camp sector, settlement combat, and controlled pursuit after a genuine break.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exhausted spearmen, exposed archers, raiders fixed in contact, and medium infantry attacked with local support.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable swordsmen, household infantry, disrupted heavy infantry, and cavalry in constrained ground when ordered and shielded.
- **Weak against:** Fresh Galloglaich, heavy cavalry, protected crossbows, concentrated archery, deep spear frontage, encirclement, and long unsupported melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better retained infantry requires real landed or household relationships, experienced captains, swords, shields, helmets, selective mail, service terms, reward, repair, casualty settlement, and protected local functions.
- **Flavor:** **The lord's land is guarded by people who expect to return to it.**
