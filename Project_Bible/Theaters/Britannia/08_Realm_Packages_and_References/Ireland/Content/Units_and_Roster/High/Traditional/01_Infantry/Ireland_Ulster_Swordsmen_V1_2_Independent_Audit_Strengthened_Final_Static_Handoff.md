## 1. Classic Inheritance and Adaptation Verdict

The classic Britannia card presents Ulster Swordsmen as trained heavy infantry from Ulster using two-handed swords. The original card emphasizes high attack, armor piercing, a strong charge, limited armor, no shield, and weaker defense than comparable Scottish swordsmen.

That classic gameplay silhouette is worth preserving, but not its literal statistics, automatic armor piercing, standardized equipment, or unsupported assumption that a complete regional formation exists throughout the campaign.

```yaml

  - TwoHandedOrLongSwordAssaultSilhouette

  - LowShieldProtectionAndDefensiveRisk
  - ForestConcealmentAndRegionalRoutePotentialWherePhysicallyValid
classic_elements_reworked:
  ArmourPiercingStat: ReplacedByTargetEquipmentAngleSpaceOrderAndWeaponEffectCeiling
  Attack13Charge8: ReplacedByApproachOpenBreakWithdrawLoop
  HeavyInfantryLabel: ReinterpretedAsAssaultWeightNotUniversalHeavyArmor

  AutomaticUlsterAvailability: Rejected
  ForestHideAttribute: ReplacedByRealTerrainConcealmentAndRouteUse
historical_status: StrongClassicAdaptationCandidateExactRegionalFormationPendingNamedEvidence
```

The exact historical basis of the classic unit is uncertain. Even community discussion commonly identifies the card as difficult to connect to a specific Ulster formation and potentially overlapping later Galloglaich or much later two-handed sword traditions. That uncertainty requires a strict late chronology and separate-card gate rather than literal copying.

## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Ulster Swordsmen are conditional Late High Era regional assault infantry who approach behind friendly frontage or terrain, open a short close-combat lane, break into an exposed or disordered infantry sector with powerful two-handed sword work, and withdraw before missiles, cavalry, or prolonged heavy contact consume the company.**

Their signature is:

> **Approach, Open, Break, Withdraw.**

Their purchase is offensive regional sword pressure. They do not guard a landed provider sector like Muire, receive a ruler's crisis like Armored Lucht Tighe, or anchor prolonged heavy contact like Galloglaich. They depend on another formation to create or protect the approach, then deliver a short, dangerous assault against the right target.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ulster Swordsmen
- **Support name:** Northern Sword Companies
- **Class:** Late High Era regional assault infantry
- **Signature:** Approach, Open, Break, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Move through a protected approach, enter one opened or weakened infantry sector, deliver severe short-duration sword pressure, and leave before the unit becomes fixed against armor, spears, missiles, cavalry, or fresh reserves.
- **Best use:** Flank assaults, counterattacks, forest-edge ambushes, gate and camp-lane fighting, exploiting Déisi or missile disruption, and breaking medium infantry already engaged by Tuath Spearmen, Muire, or Armored Lucht Tighe.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exposed missile troops, disordered spearmen, tired medium infantry, shield-poor troops, and enemies compressed in a short lane.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable sword infantry, shielded medium infantry attacked from a useful angle, and heavy infantry already badly disordered.
- **Weak against:** Braced spears, protected crossbows, concentrated archery, heavy cavalry, fresh Galloglaich, deep shielded formations, encirclement, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** The company requires practiced sword users, suitable long or two-handed swords, officers, approach discipline, maintenance, regional service relationships, and recovery capacity for a formation that suffers sharply when an assault stalls.
- **Flavor:** **Open the lane. Break the local fight. Leave before the sword company becomes the battle line.**

## 7. Core Battle Loop

```text
Select one exposed, disordered, compressed, or turning infantry sector
                         ↓
Approach behind terrain, spears, shields, or friendly pressure
                         ↓
Open a physical passage and establish sword-working space
                         ↓
Break into the target with short concentrated close pressure
                         ↓
Read whether the enemy gives ground, stalls, or receives fresh support
                         ↓
Halt before local success becomes deep pursuit or fixed attrition
                         ↓
Withdraw through the marked passage or behind the receiving formation
                         ↓
Recount files, wounded, weapons, officers, and provider capacity
```
