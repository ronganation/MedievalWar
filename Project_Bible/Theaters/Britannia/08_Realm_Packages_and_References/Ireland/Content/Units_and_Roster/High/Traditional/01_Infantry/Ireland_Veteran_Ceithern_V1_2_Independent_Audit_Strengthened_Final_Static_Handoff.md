## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Veteran Ceithern are experienced indigenous light infantry led by proven captains who can enter a contested sector, disrupt the enemy through repeated controlled pressure, press a genuine weakness longer than ordinary light foot, and disengage before professional resistance turns experience into attritional loss.**

Their signature is:

> **Infiltrate, Harass, Press, Disengage.**

Their unique purchase is **controlled depth of mission**. Ordinary Ceithern excel at flexible harassment and survival. Veteran Ceithern can carry a more demanding operation farther, coordinate several subgroups, judge when disruption has become exploitable, and extract themselves under pressure.

## 2. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Veteran Ceithern
- **Support name:** Veteran Gaelic Fighting Men
- **Class:** Experienced indigenous light infantry
- **Signature:** Infiltrate, Harass, Press, Disengage
- **Battle promise:** Enter through a screened or difficult route, harass with better timing than ordinary Ceithern, press exposed missile troops, flanks, routes, or fragmented infantry after lesser troops would need to scatter, and disengage under captain control before heavier formations close.
- **Best use:** Deep flank missions, counter-skirmishing, ambush command, route seizure, attacks on protected missile troops after their screen is displaced, covering a difficult withdrawal, and coordinating ordinary Ceithern groups.
- **Strong against:** Archers, slingers, crews, ordinary skirmishers, raiders, tired light infantry, fragmented medium infantry, and isolated support elements.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable veteran light infantry, cavalry in restricted ground, medium infantry already disordered, and fixed flanks with an open disengagement route.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry in open ground, fresh heavy infantry, braced spears, compact Ostmen, ordered Lucht Tighe, protected crossbows, and prolonged melee without an exit.
- **Campaign bargain:** Veterans provide superior mission control but come from a narrow stock of surviving fighters, proven captains, guides, missile carriers, and trusted provider relationships. Losses destroy experience that cannot be purchased instantly.
- **Flavor:** **Ordinary fighting men know how to leave danger. Veterans know how long danger can be used before leaving.**

## 4. Veteran Control

**Veteran Control** is the unit's ability to preserve one shared mission across separated files and repeated contact without losing recall, timing, target discipline, or the route home.

```yaml
VeteranControl:
  supported_by:
    - Proven captain and subgroup leaders
    - Shared service history
    - Current route and target information
    - Recognized signals
    - Missile reserve
    - Open disengagement routes
    - Receiving formation or rally point
    - Credible provider mission

  spent_by:
    - Infiltration
    - Several-direction harassment
    - Repeated contact
    - Pressing a local weakness
    - Covering another unit
    - Carrying wounded prisoners or seized material
    - Disengaging under pursuit

  broken_by:
    - Captain and subgroup leader loss
    - Route closure
    - Several incompatible targets
    - Heavy cavalry fixation
    - Prolonged melee
    - Friendly collapse
    - Deep pursuit
    - Provider or commander order conflict

  restored_by:
    - Real separation
    - Recognized successor
    - Subgroup recount
    - Reconnection at rally point
    - Rest resupply and renewed information

  not_restored_by:
    - Cooldown
    - Veteran title
    - Entering difficult terrain
    - Winning one melee
```

## 5. Core Battle Loop

```text
Identify a valuable route, flank, screen, or support target
                         ↓
Infiltrate through a protected, screened, or difficult approach
                         ↓
Harass with missiles, movement, and several controlled groups
                         ↓
Read whether the enemy merely reacts or actually loses order
                         ↓
Press the real weakness with short close action or route seizure
                         ↓
Recall subgroups before cavalry, reserves, or heavy infantry close
                         ↓
Disengage through planned routes and rear-guard actions
                         ↓
Reform for another mission, screen the host, or return to provider
```

## 9. Press Contract

The ability to **Press** is the principal distinction from ordinary Ceithern.

```yaml
Press_window_open_when:
  - Enemy missile screen is displaced
  - Target flank is turning or fragmented
  - Route guard is isolated
  - Comparable light infantry is tired or low on missiles
  - Another Irish formation fixes the target
  - Enemy pursuit separates from support

Press_window_closed_when:
  - Heavy reserve arrives
  - Spears face the approach
  - Cavalry controls disengagement route
  - Veteran Control falls below shared mission threshold
  - Ammunition and subgroup leaders are spent
  - Target offers only low-value attritional melee

Press_outcomes:
  RouteSeized: EnemyMovementOrSupportIsInterrupted
  ScreenBroken: MissileOrScoutBodyLeavesUsefulGround
  FlankPressed: EnemyFacingAndReserveAreCommitted
  ShortMeleeWon: ExposedTargetIsDrivenOff
  FalseSuccess: LocalTargetFallsButVeteransCannotDisengage
  Repulsed: TargetHoldsAndVeteransMustWithdraw
```

Press is not a damage ability. It is permission to continue the mission when experienced judgment, target state, and a viable exit justify another level of commitment.
