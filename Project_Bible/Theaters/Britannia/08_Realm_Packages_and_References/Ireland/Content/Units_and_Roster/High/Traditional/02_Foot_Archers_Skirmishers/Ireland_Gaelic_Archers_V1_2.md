## 1. Historical and Chronological Verdict

Military archery has substantial archaeological and historical evidence in medieval Ireland, but that evidence does not justify a universal native archer formation at the 1062 opening. The roster card represents a later, developed Gaelic military response in which access to bows, arrows, practiced shooters, captains, and collective formation use becomes sufficient to support a recognizable field unit.

## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Gaelic Archers are High Era Irish formation archers developed through sustained access to bows, arrows, practiced shooters, captains, and collective drill. They screen an Irish frontage, loosen selected enemy bodies through controlled medium-range shooting, concentrate when a valuable target is fixed or exposed, and relocate before cavalry or heavier missile troops destroy their position.**

Their signature is:

> **Screen, Loosen, Concentrate, Relocate.**

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gaelic Archers
- **Support name:** Saighdeoiri, pending linguistic review
- **Class:** Trained medium-range formation archers
- **Signature:** Screen, Loosen, Concentrate, Relocate
- **Battle promise:** Maintain useful missile pressure behind or beside the Irish line, loosen enemy light and medium troops through controlled shooting, concentrate arrows when another formation fixes a valuable target, and relocate before cavalry, crossbows, or heavier infantry close.
- **Best use:** Supporting Tuath Spearmen, defending approaches, countering skirmishers, shooting into exposed flanks, protecting camps, and preparing attacks by Ceithern, Deisi Javelinmen, household troops, or mounted retainers.
- **Strong against:** Unshielded light infantry, skirmishers, exposed crews, dense troops without strong missile protection, cavalry held at distance, and targets fixed in unfavorable facing.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded medium infantry, protected archers, and cavalry whose approach is disrupted or blocked.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry with a clear lane, crossbows or superior archers with protection, compact shielded infantry, heavy armor, wet or damaging weather, and close melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Archers require years of shooter development, usable bows, bowstrings, large arrow stocks, fletching, repair, captains, practice space, and protected providers. They cannot be filled from every Ceithern body or settlement.
- **Flavor:** **The bow gives the Irish host reach. Discipline decides whether that reach becomes pressure or wasted arrows.**

## 6. Core Battle Loop

```text
Take a protected position with clear firing and relocation lanes
                         ↓
Screen enemy light troops and approaching threats
                         ↓
Loosen selected enemy bodies with controlled shooting
                         ↓
Read shield facing, density, movement, protection, and mission value
                         ↓
Concentrate arrows when a valuable target is fixed or exposed
                         ↓
Shift fire, conserve arrows, or relocate as the battlefield changes
                         ↓
Withdraw behind frontage before cavalry or melee contact
                         ↓
Reform, redistribute arrows, and resume support
```
