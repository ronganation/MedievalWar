## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Irish Crossbowmen are High Era urban, castle, port, household, or garrison specialists who prepare powerful direct-fire weapons behind protection, mark a valuable exposed target, release a deliberate bolt volley, and shelter or reposition during the slow spanning and loading cycle.**

Their signature is:

> **Prepare, Mark, Release, Shelter.**

Their battlefield purchase is deliberate protected direct fire against valuable targets. Gaelic Archers own sustained formation shooting and relocation. Irish Crossbowmen own a slower, heavier, more positional fire cycle whose dangerous release depends on preparation, protection, target visibility, bolt supply, and a secure reload position.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Irish Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Protected direct-fire missile specialists
- **Signature:** Prepare, Mark, Release, Shelter
- **Battle promise:** Establish a protected firing point, load and span deliberately, release bolts against an exposed high-value target, and shelter or shift while the formation prepares the next shot.
- **Best use:** Walls, gates, breaches, defended roads, town approaches, castle yards, camp works, prepared field positions, and protected flanks of a stable Irish line.
- **Strong against:** Exposed armored retainers, heavy infantry without adequate missile protection, compact troops crossing a clear lane, crews, archers caught in a direct-fire exchange, and cavalry forced through a narrow approach.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded infantry, moving cavalry, comparable crossbows, and targets at poor angle or marginal range.
- **Weak against:** Fast flank attacks, Ceithern infiltration, sustained archery without shelter, cavalry reaching the reload position, dispersed targets, bad visibility, wet or damaged mechanisms, and close melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Crossbowmen require expensive weapons, compatible bolts, strings, spanning devices, craftsmen, spare parts, protected drill space, specialist leadership, and a provider capable of maintaining the equipment between campaigns.
- **Flavor:** **The shot is dangerous because the position, target, and reload cycle have been prepared.**

                         ↓

                         ↓

                         ↓

                         ↓

                         ↓

                         ↓
