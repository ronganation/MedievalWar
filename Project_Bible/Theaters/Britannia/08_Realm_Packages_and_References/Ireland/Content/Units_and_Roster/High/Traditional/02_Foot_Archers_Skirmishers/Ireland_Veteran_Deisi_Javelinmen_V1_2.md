## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Veteran Deisi Javelinmen are High Era regional assault specialists who conceal several mutually supporting files near a decisive approach, concentrate selected javelins against one committed or exposed target, enter the immediate disruption with shields and sidearms, and extract before fresh reserves, cavalry, or prolonged melee close the route.**

Their signature is:

> **Conceal, Concentrate, Break, Extract.**

Their purchase is one coordinated local assault. Veteran Ceithern own the wider, longer, several-group operational mission. Ordinary Deisi Javelinmen own the more available regional assault. Veteran Deisi Javelinmen own the hardest short-range concentration and the disciplined extraction that follows it.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Veteran Deisi Javelinmen
- **Support name:** Deisi Assault Javelinmen
- **Class:** High Era veteran close-javelin assault infantry
- **Signature:** Conceal, Concentrate, Break, Extract
- **Battle promise:** Hide or mask several assault files near one decisive approach, unleash a tightly controlled javelin concentration, enter the disruption for a short local break, and extract before the enemy stabilizes.
- **Best use:** Ambushes, counterattacks, exposed shield angles, short passages, settlement approaches, missile-line attacks, and targets fixed by Tuath Spearmen, Muire, or Armored Lucht Tighe.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, missile troops, tired medium infantry, disordered spearmen, shielded troops attacked from a useful angle, and enemies compressed in a short lane.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable veteran light infantry, medium infantry with intact shields, and heavy infantry already badly disrupted.
- **Weak against:** Cavalry, braced spears, protected crossbows, deep heavy infantry, fresh reserves, long missile exchanges, open terrain without extraction, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** The card consumes experienced Deisi cadres, qualified file leaders, selected javelins, shields, sidearms, helmets, regional trust, and recovery time while reducing the provider's ordinary Deisi capacity.
- **Flavor:** **The first cast opens the fight. The veterans know exactly how long to stay.**
