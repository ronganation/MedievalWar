## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Gaelic Horsemen are High Era regional medium cavalry who form a coherent mounted wing, threaten an enemy flank, charge a fixed or exposed target with meaningful but non-knightly force, and rally before prolonged melee destroys their horses and formation.**

Their signature is:

> **Form, Threaten, Charge, Rally.**

Their unique purchase is a real non-royal mounted battle wing. Royal Mounted Retainers remain the scarce household escort and crisis-intervention force. Mounted Ceithern remain light scouts and javelin riders. Hobelars remain operational light cavalry and mounted infantry. Horseboys maintain the support system. Gaelic Horsemen deliver the stronger regional mounted attack.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gaelic Horsemen
- **Support name:** Provincial Horsemen
- **Class:** High Era regional medium cavalry
- **Signature:** Form, Threaten, Charge, Rally
- **Battle promise:** Form a coherent mounted wing, force the enemy to protect a flank, charge a target already fixed or exposed, break contact before fresh reserves close, and rally for another bounded commitment.
- **Best use:** Flank attacks, counterattacks, reinforcing a threatened wing, driving off light cavalry, breaking exposed missile troops, exploiting disruption by Gaelic Archers or Déisi Javelinmen, and pursuing a genuine rout.
- **Strong against:** Exposed archers and crossbowmen, light cavalry in a controlled contest, fragmented infantry, tired medium infantry from the flank, crews, and routed troops.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable regional horsemen, medium infantry with an exposed flank, and heavy infantry already compressed or wavering.
- **Weak against:** Braced spears, ordered Galloglaich, prepared knights, dense heavy infantry, stakes, ditches, deep mud, concentrated crossbow fire, and prolonged mounted melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gaelic Horsemen require actual regional riders, suitable combat horses, captains, standards, spears, shields, sidearms, tack, fodder, remounts, and a lordly provider able to sustain their absence and losses.
- **Flavor:** **The threat turns the enemy. The charge punishes the opening. The rally preserves the mounted wing.**
