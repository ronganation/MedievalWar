The recognizable Hobelar belongs to a later development horizon than the campaign opening. ## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Hobelars are mature High Era light horsemen who use small agile mounts to cover campaign distance, scout and patrol, threaten exposed troops, dismount for serious fighting or route defense, and remount before heavier enemies can fix the formation.**

Their signature is:

> **Ride, Scout, Dismount, Fight, Remount.**

Their unique purchase is operational mobility joined to credible dismounted combat. Mounted Ceithern perform bounded reconnaissance and limited mounted-javelin work. Royal Mounted Retainers provide household escort, mounted intervention, and pursuit. Hobelars carry a larger and more durable campaign mission, moving as horsemen and choosing whether the decisive action occurs mounted or on foot.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Hobelars
- **Class:** High Era light cavalry and mounted infantry
- **Signature:** Ride, Scout, Dismount, Fight, Remount
- **Battle promise:** Reach a useful sector faster than foot troops, screen and observe while mounted, dismount before terrain or enemy formation makes mounted combat wasteful, fight as disciplined light-to-medium foot around routes and objectives, then recover the horses and remount before the formation is trapped.
- **Best use:** Patrol, counter-raiding, route security, flanking movement, dismounted defense of crossings or camp approaches, rapid reinforcement, pursuit of broken light troops, and denial of enemy scouting.
- **Strong against:** Scouts, raiders, exposed archers, fragmented light infantry, unsupported crews, and enemies surprised by a rapid dismounted reinforcement.
- **Conditional against:** Comparable light cavalry, medium infantry attacked from an angle, and cavalry constrained by broken ground or defended crossings.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry in open ground, braced spears, armored infantry, protected crossbows, dense missile fire, attacks on the horse-holding point, and prolonged combat without remount access.
- **Campaign bargain:** Hobelars consume suitable horses, experienced riders, tack, weapons, grooms, guides, fodder, patrol time, and a mature recruitment institution. Losing the horses may preserve the fighters but destroys the unit's operational purchase.
- **Flavor:** **The horses win the distance. The soldiers decide where the real fight happens.**

## 6. Core Battle Loop

```text
Ride through a current and supportable route network
                         ↓
Scout, patrol, screen, or threaten while preserving the horses
                         ↓
Choose whether the useful action is mounted or dismounted
                         ↓
Dismount before terrain, spears, or objective geometry defeats mobility
                         ↓
Fight a bounded foot action around the selected route or objective
                         ↓
Break contact before heavy reserves or horse-holding threats close
                         ↓
Recover riders, horses, handlers, equipment, wounded, and reports
                         ↓
Remount, relocate, pursue, patrol again, or return to provider
```
