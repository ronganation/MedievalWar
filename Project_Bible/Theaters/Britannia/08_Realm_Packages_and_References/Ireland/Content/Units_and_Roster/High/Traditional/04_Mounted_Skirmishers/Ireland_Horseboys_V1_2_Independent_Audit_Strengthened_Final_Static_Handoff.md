## 1. Historical and Terminology Verdict

The classic Horseboy is first and foremost a horse-keeper and military servant attached to mounted warriors, not an independent conventional cavalryman. Later descriptions place horseboys below kerns, Galloglaich, and horsemen in the military hierarchy, identify horse care as their primary duty, and also describe them as useful against enemies with darts. Records further describe mounted warriors maintaining multiple horses and servants, with a servant riding a hackney while carrying harness and spears or leading the principal service horse.

## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Horseboys are a late High Era mounted-retinue support formation whose members lead spare horses, carry harness, spears, provisions, and messages, release darts against exposed threats when the service column is attacked, and recover riders, horses, equipment, and information before the cavalry system collapses.**

Their signature is:

> **Lead, Carry, Cast, Recover.**

Their purchase is support mobility under danger. Horseboys do not outfight real cavalry. They preserve cavalry endurance, remount potential, messenger capacity, and post-contact recovery while providing a weak but useful dart-skirmishing screen when threatened.

## 3. Thirty-Second Total War Card

- **Unit:** Horseboys
- **Support name:** Irish Horse-Keepers
- **Class:** Mounted support and dart skirmishers
- **Signature:** Lead, Carry, Cast, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Keep the spare horses, harness, missiles, and recovery route connected to an Irish mounted force; cast darts at exposed scouts or pursuers when threatened; and recover riders, animals, and equipment before the support train is overrun.
- **Best use:** Supporting Royal Mounted Retainers, Hobelars, Mounted Ceithern, messengers, remount points, camps, pursuit recovery, and withdrawal routes.
- **Strong against:** Isolated scouts, messengers, disordered light foot, wounded pursuers, unprotected carriers, and enemies already fixed by actual combat troops.
- **Conditional against:** Ordinary skirmishers, raiders, and light cavalry only when terrain, numbers, missiles, and a clear escape route favor withdrawal.
- **Weak against:** Formed infantry, archers, crossbowmen, cavalry, armored retainers, prolonged melee, loss of the horse line, several-direction pressure, and any mission that treats the formation as ordinary cavalry.
- **Campaign bargain:** Horseboys increase mounted endurance and recovery only by consuming real servants, hackneys, spare horses, tack, provisions, darts, guides, and provider obligations.
- **Flavor:** **The horseman wins the charge. The horseboy makes sure a horse, spear, and road still exist afterward.**

## 6. Core Battle Loop

```text
Receive assigned horses, riders, equipment, loads, and routes
                         ↓
Lead spare horses and carry the mounted retinue's support packet
                         ↓
Hold behind protection while the combat formation operates
                         ↓
Cast darts or shift only when scouts, pursuers, or raiders threaten
                         ↓
Recover unhorsed riders, wounded personnel, weapons, and remounts
                         ↓
Reconnect the correct rider, horse, tack, message, and route
                         ↓
Withdraw the support packet before serious combat reaches it
                         ↓
Return capacity to the cavalry provider or prepare the next mission
```
