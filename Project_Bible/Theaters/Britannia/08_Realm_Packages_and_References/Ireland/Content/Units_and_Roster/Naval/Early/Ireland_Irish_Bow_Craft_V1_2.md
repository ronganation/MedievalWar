The Irish Bow Craft is Ireland's shared Early mobile archery-support vessel. The profile combines suitable regional hulls, pilots, sailors, shipboard bowmen, archer leaders, ammunition handlers, bowyers, fletchers, small ports, and coastal providers into a low-burden missile-support craft. The unit remains distinct from general Coastal Craft, offensive Raiders, prepared Port-Town Warships, and later Gaelic, Hiberno-Norse, and Dublin missile ships.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Irish Bow Craft** is Ireland's affordable Early vessel for mobile bow pressure, landing cover, withdrawal support, convoy assistance, and suppression of exposed crews. It operates behind or beside a combat screen and shifts firing position rather than accepting sustained boarding contact.

The player recruits the Irish Bow Craft to **add inexpensive, mobile ranged pressure to an Early Irish fleet while preserving heavier ships for prepared combat and Raiders for aggressive contact selection**.
