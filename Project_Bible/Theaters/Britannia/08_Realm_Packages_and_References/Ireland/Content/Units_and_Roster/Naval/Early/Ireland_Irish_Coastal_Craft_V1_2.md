The Irish Coastal Craft is Ireland's broadly available Early naval foundation. It represents small but combat-capable regional vessels supplied through coastal settlements, river mouths, harbors, local rulers, pilots, fishers, shipowners, craftsmen, household fighters, and levies. The unit is neither a generic fishing boat with weapons nor an Irish copy of a Norwegian longship or Scottish Birlinn.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Irish Coastal Craft** is an affordable Early patrol, escort, dispatch, interception, and local landing-support vessel. It gives fragmented Irish rulers and coastal communities useful sea coverage before deeper port-town or High-era fleet institutions mature.

The player recruits the craft to **protect local routes, carry warning and dispatch, intercept light threats, screen more valuable ships, and support short coastal landings without paying for a prepared warship**.

  - LocalLandingAndReembarkationSupport

campaign_purchase:

  - LowProviderBurden

  - SpecialistBowPressure

  - HeavyBoardingCommandOrExpedition
