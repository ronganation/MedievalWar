This authority establishes Ireland's naval architecture for the First Slice. Ireland does not receive one free centralized national navy. Maritime capability arises from regional coasts, river mouths, ports, island communities, royal and provincial rulers, Hiberno-Norse towns, merchants, shipowners, pilots, craftsmen, retained fighters, and actual political control.

Dublin may support a stronger Hiberno-Norse and selectively Scandinavian-influenced naval channel, but Scandinavian influence never duplicates Norway's roster or creates ships, officers, crews, equipment, ports, and recruitment pools automatically.

## 1. Direct Realm Verdict

Ireland should begin with a **shared regional Early foundation** and develop into two interacting High portfolios:

1. **Gaelic and Regional Irish Maritime Development**
   - Coastal lordships
   - Provincial rulers
   - River, estuary, island, and western coastal communities
   - Landing, raiding, escort, warning, and regional fleet service

2. **Hiberno-Norse and Port-Town Maritime Development**
   - Dublin as the strongest center
   - Waterford, Wexford, Limerick, and Cork relationships where applicable
   - Urban shipowners, merchants, craftsmen, retained fighters, and overseas contacts
   - Selective Scandinavian-influenced hull, crew, and fighting practices

A later **Hiberno-Norman Maritime Development** channel may emerge from lordship, castle-port, merchant, crossbow, cavalry-transport, and sailing relationships. It should not be forced into the opening naval roster before its providers and distinct battle purchases are established.
