The Port-Town Warship is Ireland's shared Early prepared-combat vessel. The profile represents qualified Irish ports capable of combining a suitable hull, trained crew, fighters, stores, berths, repairs, and political authority into a warship that can hold an escort or port-defense station under pressure. Dublin may be the strongest provider, but the profile is not Dublin-exclusive and does not consume the future High-era Dublin Warship.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Port-Town Warship** is Ireland's Early prepared-combat, formal-escort, port-defense, and sustained-boarding vessel. The ship protects convoys and mission vessels, contests ordinary peer warships, resists Raider pressure, supports harbor defense, and remains in contact longer than Coastal Craft or Raiders.

The player recruits the Port-Town Warship to **hold one prepared formation or port approach, protect an important vessel, and win ordinary boarding contests without committing a later High-era fleet ship**.
