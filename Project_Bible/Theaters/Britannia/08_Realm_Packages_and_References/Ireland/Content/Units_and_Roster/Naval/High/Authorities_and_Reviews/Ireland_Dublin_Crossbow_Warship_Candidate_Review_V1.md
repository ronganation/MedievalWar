## 1. Candidate Question

Does Ireland need a dedicated High Dublin crossbow vessel for deliberate counter-missile pressure, disruption of exposed officers and signal crews, boarding preparation, and protected station work without duplicating the Hiberno-Norse Bow Warship, Dublin Warship, command lane, or later mechanical artillery?

## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Dublin Crossbow Warship** should be admitted as Ireland's developed High urban-port vessel for measured counter-missile pressure, deliberate attacks on exposed officers, signal crews, boarders, and other priority personnel, and preparation of one favorable boarding or interposition window from behind a reliable fleet screen.

The player recruits the vessel to **take one protected firing station, wait for a clear high-value exposure, deliver slower and more deliberate crossbow pressure than a Bow Warship, prepare one decisive fleet action, and recover before limited bolts or a close threat ends the mission**.
