## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Dublin Warship** should be admitted as Ireland's repeatable Hiberno-Norse and Port-Town High urban fleet screen, formal escort, prepared boarding, port-response, and fleet-integration warship.

The player recruits the vessel to **sortie from a principal urban port, establish an organized fleet station, interpose against a priority threat, coordinate boarding and convoy protection, and recover through a deeper but concentrated port network**.
