## 1. Candidate Question

Does Ireland need a High Hiberno-Norse bow-support warship that sustains mobile bow pressure, supports convoys and landings, suppresses exposed crews, and operates inside a developed urban fleet without duplicating the Early Irish Bow Craft, Dublin Crossbow Warship, Dublin Warship, or Gaelic Bow Warship?

## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Hiberno-Norse Bow Warship** should be admitted as Ireland's High urban-port mobile bow-support vessel for sustained crew pressure, convoy protection, landing and reembarkation cover, withdrawal support, and counter-Raider fire from behind a prepared screen.

The player recruits the vessel to **occupy one protected firing sector inside a worked-up port fleet, sustain useful bow pressure longer and more reliably than the Early Bow Craft, reserve ammunition for one decisive landing or withdrawal window, and leave when the screen or return route fails**.
