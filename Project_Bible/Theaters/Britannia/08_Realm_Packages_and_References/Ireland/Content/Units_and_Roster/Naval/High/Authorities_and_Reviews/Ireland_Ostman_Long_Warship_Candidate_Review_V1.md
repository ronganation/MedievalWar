## 1. Candidate Question

Does Ireland need a High Hiberno-Norse fast warship that intercepts exposed targets, conducts developed raids, screens rapid landings, boards aggressively, and disengages under control, without duplicating the Early Irish Sea Raider, Dublin Warship, Western Sea Raider, or Norway's longship families?

```yaml
  - NoNorwegianRosterTransfer
  - FiniteCrewRefugeLandingPrizeAndRepairBargain

```

## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Ostman Long Warship** should be admitted as Ireland's High Hiberno-Norse fast interception, developed raiding, aggressive boarding, rapid-landing, and controlled-breakoff warship.

The player recruits the vessel to **depart a qualified Hiberno-Norse port network, overtake or cut off an exposed target, commit fighters for a short decisive action or landing, and disengage before prepared fleet screens or heavy ships convert speed into entrapment**.

```yaml
one_sentence_purchase: HighIrishHibernoNorseFastInterceptionDevelopedRaidAggressiveBoardingRapidLandingAndControlledBreakoffWarship
```
