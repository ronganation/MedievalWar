Does Ireland need a repeatable Gaelic and Regional Irish High warship that assembles distributed provincial providers into a functioning fleet screen, protects regional missions, relieves pressured ships, and holds formation without becoming a Dublin Warship, Royal Muster Warship, Great Provincial Warship, or statistical upgrade to the Early Port-Town Warship?

## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Provincial Fleet Warship** should be admitted as Ireland's repeatable Gaelic and Regional Irish High fleet screen, escort, relief, formation-holding, counter-Raider, ordinary-boarding, and withdrawal-guard vessel.

The player recruits the vessel to **connect ships raised through several regional providers, preserve one compact fleet or mission under pressure, relieve damaged or isolated elements, and withdraw in order when no principal urban port can or should carry the whole operation**.

