## 1. Candidate Question

Does Ireland need a High Gaelic and Regional western-sea raider that develops coastal raiding, pursuit, rapid landing, prize recovery, dispersed refuge use, and controlled breakoff without replacing the Early Irish Sea Raider, Ostman Long Warship, Provincial Fleet Warship, or Scotland's Western Sea vessels?

## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Western Sea Raider** should be admitted as Ireland's High Gaelic western-coastal vessel for developed raiding, route pursuit, rapid coastal landing, bounded prize recovery, dispersed refuge use, and controlled withdrawal from exposed western waters.

The player recruits the vessel to **use regional pilotage and refuge knowledge to approach an exposed coastal target, strike or land quickly, seize only what the crew and escape route can support, and disappear through a western refuge network before organized pursuit or bad weather closes the operation**.
