## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Dublin Crossbow Warship** is Ireland's High urban-port specialist for deliberate counter-missile pressure, disruption of exposed officers and signal crews, and preparation of one favorable boarding or interposition window from behind a reliable screen.

The player recruits the ship to **take one protected firing station, observe until a clear priority exposure appears, loose one measured crossbow volley, create a short fleet-action window, and recover before slow reloads, limited bolts, or close contact ends the mission**.
