## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Dublin Warship** is a repeatable High-era urban fleet screen, formal escort, prepared boarding, port-response, and fleet-integration vessel. It sorties from a qualified principal port, forms an organized station, interposes against a mission-critical threat, commits prepared fighters under favorable conditions, and returns through a concentrated but finite urban repair network.

## 1C. Practical Urban Fleet Purchase

The Dublin Warship must feel like an organized principal-port response vessel rather than a richer Port-Town Warship. The player recruits it to **sortie on time, establish one prepared formation, interpose against one mission-critical threat, commit boarders only when the screen can survive, and return before channel congestion or repair overload traps the fleet**.
