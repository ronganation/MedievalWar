The Hiberno-Norse Bow Warship is Ireland's developed High urban-port bow-support vessel. It combines a qualified hull, formal fleet stations, worked-up naval archers, ammunition handlers, bowyers, fletchers, port workshops, shipowners, merchants, and a prepared combat screen. The unit is neither a larger Irish Bow Craft nor an undeclared Dublin Crossbow Warship.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Hiberno-Norse Bow Warship** is Ireland's High urban-port vessel for sustained mobile bow pressure, exposed-crew suppression, convoy protection, landing and reembarkation cover, withdrawal support, and counter-Raider fire from behind a prepared screen.

The player recruits the vessel to **occupy one protected firing sector, acquire a mission-relevant exposed target, sustain useful bow pressure while the fleet screen holds, reserve ammunition for one decisive cover window, and recover before close contact or port-return failure destroys the specialist force**.
