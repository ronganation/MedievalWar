The Hiberno-Norse Great Warship is Ireland's conditional High heavy fleet-center profile. The vessel concentrates a rare large hull, substantial crew, prepared fighters, stores, damage-control personnel, deep berths, major yards, merchants, shipowners, and concentrated urban repair capacity. The ship is powerful because Ireland accepts an exceptional provider burden, not because the unit ignores fleet geometry, damage, draft, or return constraints.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Hiberno-Norse Great Warship** is a scarce heavy fleet center, major-convoy escort, bounded expedition-support vessel, sustained boarding platform, and damage-endurance ship.

The player recruits the vessel to **muster one exceptional heavy package, anchor one compact mission, endure pressure that would displace medium warships, commit to one sustained multi-phase close action, recover enough fighting and damage-control capacity to preserve the mission, and withdraw before the repair burden becomes strategic collapse**.
