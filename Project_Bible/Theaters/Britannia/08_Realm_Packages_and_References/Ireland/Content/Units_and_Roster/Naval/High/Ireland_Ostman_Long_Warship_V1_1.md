## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Ostman Long Warship** is Ireland's High Hiberno-Norse vessel for fast interception, developed raiding, aggressive boarding, rapid landing, bounded prize-taking, and controlled breakoff.

The player recruits the ship to **locate an exposed target or landing opportunity, cut off the route, strike before prepared support arrives, land or seize only within the remaining crew and return margin, and break contact before speed becomes entrapment**.
