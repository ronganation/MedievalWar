The ship connects mixed-provider vessels, protects one compact mission, relieves pressured elements, and preserves an ordered withdrawal.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Provincial Fleet Warship** is a repeatable High-era regional screen, escort, relief, formation-holding, ordinary-boarding, and withdrawal-guard vessel. It is the practical naval center of a federated provincial fleet rather than a royal command ship or heavy expedition vessel.

The player recruits the ship to **connect vessels raised through several regional providers, protect one compact fleet or mission, relieve a pressured element without collapsing another sector, and withdraw in order when continued contact would destroy the fleet's provider capacity**.
