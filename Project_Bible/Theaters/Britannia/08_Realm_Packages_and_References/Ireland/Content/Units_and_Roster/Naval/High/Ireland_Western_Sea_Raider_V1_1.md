The Western Sea Raider is Ireland's developed High Gaelic western-coastal raiding vessel. It combines a suitable light-to-medium hull, regional pilots, shipowners, sailors, fighters, landing parties, prize crews, western coastal lordships, and a finite network of bays, coves, estuaries, islands, headlands, anchorages, and friendly refuges. The profile is not an Ostman Long Warship reskin, an upgraded Early Irish Sea Raider, or a transfer of Scotland's established Western Sea portfolio.

### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Western Sea Raider** is Ireland's High Gaelic western-coastal vessel for developed raiding, route pursuit, rapid coastal landing, bounded prize recovery, dispersed refuge use, and controlled withdrawal from exposed western waters.

The player recruits the vessel to **use regional pilotage and refuge knowledge to approach an exposed coastal target, strike or land quickly, seize only what the crew and escape route can support, disperse when organized pursuit develops, and recover through a finite western network before weather or enemy action closes it**.

#### Western Sea Raider

_High Irish Gaelic western-coastal raiding, landing, prize, and refuge-network warship_

Western Sea Raiders combine suitable regional hulls with local pilots, shipowners, sailors, fighters, landing parties, prize crews, and a finite network of western bays, coves, estuaries, islands, headlands, and friendly refuges. They approach exposed coastal targets, strike or land quickly, seize only supportable prizes, disperse against organized pursuit, and reassemble through regional recovery networks. They perform poorly in prepared fleet combat and become vulnerable when overloaded, cut off from pilots, or denied refuge.
