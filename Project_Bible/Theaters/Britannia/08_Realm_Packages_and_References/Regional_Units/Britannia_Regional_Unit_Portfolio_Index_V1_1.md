### 2. Modern Britannia Regional-Unit Rule
```yaml
canonical_source: RegionCommunityProviderNetworkOrCrossRealmMilitaryTradition
canonical_profile_owner: BritanniaTheaterRegionalUnits
realm_access: RelationshipBased
controller_access: ConditionalNotAutomatic
recruitment_pool: FiniteAndSourceBound
population_relationship: PopulationInformsAndConstrainsPoolButIsNotDirectArmyManpower
development_effect: UnlocksOrImprovesCapabilityButDoesNotCreatePeopleEquipmentHorsesLeadersOrPools
duplicate_realm_profiles: ProhibitedUnlessASeparateInstitutionAndMilitaryIdentityIsSubstantivelyJustified
```

A Britannia regional profile must identify its canonical source, possible controller relationships, finite physical and political recruitment inputs, distinct battlefield purchase, recovery behavior, controller-change consequences, and separation from neighboring realm-owned units.

### 3. Ownership, Access, and Nonduplication Constitution
```yaml
folder_ownership_rule: CanonicalProfileLivesOnceAtTheaterLevel
realm_folder_rule: RealmPackagesReferenceRegionalProfilesButDoNotDuplicateThem
control_rule: TerritorialControlAloneDoesNotCreateAccess
relationship_rule: AccessRequiresAValidCurrentPoliticalSocialMilitaryOrContractualRelationship
source_rule: TheSamePeopleEquipmentHorsesLeadersAndPoolCannotBeCountedForSeveralControllersAtOnce
research_rule: ResearchMayUnlockOrganizationTrainingOrEquipmentUseButCannotCreatePhysicalInputs
building_rule: BuildingsMaySupportAssemblyStorageRepairTrainingAndAdministrationButCannotCreateTheSourcePopulation
mercenary_rule: PaidExternalServiceMayBeOneAccessExpressionButDoesNotAutomaticallyConvertTheCanonicalUnitIntoAMercenaryFamily
opening_rule: IndexPresenceDoesNotAuthorizeOpeningAvailability
```

Regional and mercenary remain related but separate classifications. A regional unit may serve under contract without becoming canonically mercenary-owned. A mercenary company may draw from a regional source without owning the complete regional tradition or its domestic replacement pool.

### 8. Currentness and Supersession Rules
- The five active Markdown profiles named in V1.1 are the current portfolio records for their families.
- Every active profile should remain paired with its matching manifest and static check.
- The older Danelaw Spearmen V1, Hobilars V1 and V1.1, Wessex Axemen V1.1, and Woodsmen V1 profiles remain predecessor evidence and must not be mistaken for active current files.
- A new version becomes current only after direct comparison, owner review, filename verification, and folder-placement verification.
- Chat drafts, realm-folder variants, brainstorming records, and external references do not supersede a canonical regional profile merely by being newer.
- If ownership later moves a family into a realm package, this index must record the disposition rather than silently removing the family.
- Similar weapon classes, cultures, or regional labels are not sufficient reasons to merge legitimate distinct units.

### 11. Relationship to the Regional Framework
The current five profiles now express the portfolio architecture consistently:

- one theater-owned canonical profile per family;
- region-owned or source-owned people and providers;
- conditional realm and controller access;
- finite recruitment and recovery;
- explicit protected assignments and no double counting;
- no automatic access from conquest, cultural similarity, research, buildings, or folder placement;
- controller change preserves provenance and does not duplicate sources;
- regional and mercenary service may intersect without becoming the same ownership category;
- regional flavor is carried through source, equipment, service, presentation, availability, cost, recovery, and battlefield behavior rather than innate bonuses.

### 12. Index Maintenance Protocol
When a regional profile is added or revised:

1. verify the actual folder state and exact filename;
2. identify and read the immediate substantive predecessor;
3. compare source and successor section by section;
4. preserve substantive predecessor content or record justified removals;
5. determine canonical source ownership before granting realm access;
6. update the active profile, version, disposition, signature, and collision notes in this index;
7. verify matching manifest and static-check artifacts;
8. update relationship or access matrices separately;
9. preserve Opening State, Registry, payload, importer, runtime, and Unreal boundaries until separately authorized;
10. record superseded, moved, retained, rejected, or merged files honestly.
