**The Holk is Britannia's shared High-era developed sailing warship: a higher-sided, more defensible Cog-family vessel carrying a larger fighting and missile complement for convoy defense, protected missile pressure, port and passage control, and sustained defensive combat under sail.**

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Holk
- **Support name:** Britannian Developed High-Sided Sailing Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Developed defensive sailing warship
- **Signature:** Sail, Position, Ward, Pressure, Endure, Withdraw
- **Best use:** Defend convoys and ports, hold a supported sailing line, protect missile crews behind higher sides, resist boarding, and punish exposed attackers
- **Battle strengths:** High-sided defensive geometry, protected missile positions, strong prepared counterboarding, useful stores and endurance, and better survival than the Cog in fleet combat
- **Battle weaknesses:** Wind dependence, slow tactical reaction, poor pursuit, high port and craft burden, congestion risk, fire vulnerability, and limited offensive boarding against maneuverable warships
- **Campaign strengths:** Provides Britannia's developed sailing-combat family and the natural High progression from Cog access
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Competes with merchant construction, ports, workshops, crossbow equipment, crews, stores, and heavy warship programs
- **Player instruction:** Establish favorable wind and formation, keep the Holk's defensive side toward the threat, protect withdrawal space, and force oared attackers to approach through missiles and high-sided resistance.

## 7. Core Battle Loop: Sail, Position, Ward, Pressure, Endure, Withdraw

### 7.1 Sail

Use wind, route, ports, and formation to reach a defensible convoy, port, or fleet position.

### 7.2 Position

Present the most defensible side and protected missile stations while maintaining friendly arcs and a retreat route.

### 7.3 Ward

Use higher sides, shields, defenders, and prepared counterboarding to deny easy enemy access.

### 7.4 Pressure

Apply bows, crossbows, or other validated missiles from protected stations against approaching vessels and exposed personnel.

### 7.5 Endure

Absorb limited attack while preserving rigging, steering, crew order, damage control, and formation connection.

### 7.6 Withdraw

Break formation, seek refuge, or sail away before fire, rigging loss, several-direction attack, or boarding collapse makes survival impossible.

```yaml
success_condition: HoldDefensiveSailingPositionProtectConvoyOrSectorAndPunishApproachWithoutIsolation
failure_condition: FireRiggingLossCalmSeveralDirectionAttackCongestionBoardingAccessOrBlockedWithdrawal
```
