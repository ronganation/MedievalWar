## 1. Additive Rebuild Verdict

House Dunkeld remains the ruling royal dynasty associated with the current holder of the Scottish kingship at the selected opening. The source authority was already constitutionally strong: it separated dynasty from person, Crown, Realm, Government, household, title, claim, succession, Network, Church, and future outcome. This successor preserves that complete design and expands it to the current Scotland specialist stack, Project Bible 2.4, Design Expansion Checklist 3.7, Unreal Engine 5.8, and the three-track temporal model.

The strategic center remains continuity beyond one successful ruler. <Person>Malcolm III</Person> gives the dynasty current royal prominence, but this prominence is not a transferable resource and does not settle dynasty headship, succession, claims, custody, property, regional cooperation, military support, ecclesiastical recognition, economic capacity, or Supply. Adult kin, protected children, cross-Realm family interfaces, households, offices, institutions, properties, routes, and regional actors remain separately owned.
