## 1. Concise Verdict

House Moray is a historically significant Scottish dynasty associated with Moray's regional political environment and with audience-specific memories of prior rulers. The dynasty provides lineage context, historical continuity, regional association, strategic questions, and conditional future possibilities. It does not own Moray, regional government, a title, an office, a collective claim, alternative legitimacy, local forces, one political strategy, one relationship state, one market, one route system, or one body of Supply.
