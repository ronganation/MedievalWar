# 1. Review Method

## Evidence Test for Every Direction

A direction may become eligible only if all applicable tests pass:

1. **Source endpoint:** canonical person ID resolves.
2. **Target endpoint:** canonical person ID resolves.
3. **Entity kind:** both endpoints are persons for a person-to-person edge.
4. **Lifecycle:** both persons are alive on 1 January 1062 under the approved scenario interpretation.
5. **Participation:** the source may participate in Character Network.
6. **Opening basis:** at least one source-grounded fact makes a relationship record plausible at opening.
7. **Direction:** evidence supports this source's relationship toward this target, not merely the reverse.
8. **Context:** the relationship question is bounded to a meaningful issue.
9. **Provenance:** the factual basis can be traced to an authority.
10. **Visibility:** the source can plausibly perceive the target, arrangement, or relevant fact.
11. **Information boundary:** access is not treated as knowledge, and knowledge is not treated as belief or loyalty.
12. **Arrangement boundary:** a Network edge does not replace genealogy, marriage, custody, household, office, title, treaty, patronage, fealty, claim, or succession.
13. **Uncertainty:** disputed or reconstructed facts stay explicit.
14. **Underage safety:** child-facing directions use age-appropriate dimensions only.
15. **No outcome back-projection:** later kingship, rebellion, hostility, exile, death, or alliance is not imported into 1062.
16. **Reverse-direction independence:** the reverse direction receives its own review.

## Eligibility Does Not Mean Initialization

`Eligible for later bounded fixture review` means only that a valid evidence case exists for creating a record with bounded context. A separate fixture-authoring pass must still decide:

- whether the edge exists at opening;
- which contract-approved dimensions apply;
- whether each dimension is known, unknown, inapplicable, or explicitly initialized;
- visibility and audience;
- confidence and provenance;
- source chronology;
- saved-state and migration behavior.

## Unknown Is Not Zero

A deferred or omitted edge does not mean hostility, indifference, no kinship, no access, no trust, no importance, or a value of zero. It means the project lacks a valid directed opening record.
