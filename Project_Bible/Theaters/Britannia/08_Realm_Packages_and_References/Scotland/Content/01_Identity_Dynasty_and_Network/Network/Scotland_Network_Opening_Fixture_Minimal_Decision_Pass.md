## Constitutional Rule

`Edge Exists != Relationship Value Exists`

A basis-only edge requires:

- canonical person endpoints;
- explicit direction;
- external relationship-basis owner and reference;
- bounded context;
- provenance;
- uncertainty;
- strategic use;
- visibility policy;
- zero dimension records.

Current contact evidence remains necessary for contact, access, knowledge, and familiarity dimensions, but is not required merely to record a validated structural kinship basis.
