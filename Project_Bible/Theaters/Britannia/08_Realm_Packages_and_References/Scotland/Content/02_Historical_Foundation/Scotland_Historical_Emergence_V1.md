## 1. Concise Verdict

Scotland's historical environment can produce recognizable but non-scripted political, dynastic, regional, ecclesiastical, military, maritime, economic, household, and institutional developments. Historical Memory makes a pathway intelligible. Historical Momentum makes the pathway strategically relevant. Evidence, Chronology, Uncertainty, information access, actors, institutions, capacity, and owner decisions determine whether the pathway becomes eligible, forms, matures, consolidates, redirects, reverses, fails, or closes.

No pathway activates because a historical date is crossed, a famous later outcome is known, a pressure reaches a scalar threshold, or a designer expects Scotland to centralize. The Campaign may approach, transform, replace, delay, or permanently close recognizable possibilities.

```text
Memory != Emergence
Momentum != Emergence
Pressure != Pathway
Eligibility != Activation
Activation != Owner Selection
Selection != Resolution
Resolution != Historical Outcome
Formation != Maturation
Maturation != Consolidation
Consolidation != Permanence
Failure != Historical Reset
Historical Name != Guaranteed Character
```

## 10. Maturation and Consolidation

Maturation requires actual operational behavior, not paperwork alone. Tests may include:

- Mandates issued, received, and reviewed.
- Roles staffed without ownership collapse.
- Records, seals, archives, or custody working where relevant.
- Treasury or account authority separated from custody and spending.
- Sites, routes, messages, property, Supply, and security available.
- Underage protections functioning.
- Guarantees and obligations honored.
- Regional and institutional response recorded.
- Replacement, oversight, and failure procedures available.

Consolidation additionally requires repeated successful operation, survival through leadership or seasonal stress, owner-approved continuity, and no hidden dependence on a single person or unsupported historical assumption.
