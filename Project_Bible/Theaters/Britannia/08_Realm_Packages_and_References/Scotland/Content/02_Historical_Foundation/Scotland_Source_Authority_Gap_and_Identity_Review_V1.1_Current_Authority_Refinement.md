- Named Person

- Legacy Figure

A top-level file may have one primary classification while containing embedded references of other classes. Embedded references are reviewed separately and are not promoted into files or authorities.

# 1. Concise Verdict

The available Scotland package is sufficient for a controlled authoring intake, but not for realm-wide modernization or Opening State certification.

The source set contains:

- **6 top-level Named Person records** suitable for authoring intake with restrictions;
- **1 Office-Titled Record** that must remain identity-quarantined;
- **2 Dynasty Records** suitable as supporting sources but not as person profiles;
- **4 Collective Records** that provide realm, AI, personality, historical, and relationship intent but cannot own person identity;
- **1 completed pilot review artifact**, which governs intake findings but is not itself a Scotland content identity record;
- several embedded named-person references without dedicated Scotland character records;
- several embedded Legacy Figure references;
- no top-level Title-Titled, Institution-Titled, or Household Records.

The six identifiable person files are:

1. Malcolm III
2. Ingibjorg Finnsdottir
3. Donald III
4. Máel Muire mac Donnchada
5. Máel Snechtai mac Lulaich
6. Gille Coemgáin of Moray

All six are **Authoring Ready With Restrictions** rather than unqualified Authoring Ready. The absent Scotland Opening State, Title Package, Office Package, Institution Package, Succession and Claim authorities, and Migration Map prevent certification of opening roles, title possession, offices, succession order, claims, canonical identifiers, or current relationship state.

The record titled “Bishop of St Andrews” is not a sufficient person identity. It is an **Office-Titled Record** whose personal identity, office, and institution meanings are merged. It must remain **Quarantined** for canonical-person authoring while its valid strategic intent is preserved for later routing.

Scotland can begin a bounded modernization intake only after accepting these classifications. The source package does not require a new character framework or Scotland-specific profile format.
