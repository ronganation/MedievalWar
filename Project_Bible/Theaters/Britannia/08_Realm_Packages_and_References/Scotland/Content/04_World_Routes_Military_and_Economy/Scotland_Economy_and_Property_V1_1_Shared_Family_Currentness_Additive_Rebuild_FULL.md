## 1. Concise Verdict

Scotland's January 1062 economy must be represented as a distributed network of separately owned land, buildings, rights, custody, use, labor, production, obligations, accounts, storage, markets, routes, access, transport, delivery, consumption, damage, dispute, and recovery. Kingship does not make <Person>Malcolm III</Person> the owner of Scotland, all land, every revenue, every market, every ecclesiastical holding, or every household resource.

The current Scotland package supports strategic economic questions, not exact opening balances. <Place>St Andrews</Place> is a bounded site and institutional interface. <Place>Scone</Place> and <Place>Dunfermline</Place> remain ReviewOnly. Moray's exact property and economic structure remains unresolved. External island and cross-Realm interfaces remain owner-locked. This authority therefore creates strong schemas, owner routing, validation, player presentation, and Opening State handoffs without inventing property holders, treasury totals, prices, production quantities, stocks, markets, minting, trade flows, or revenue.
