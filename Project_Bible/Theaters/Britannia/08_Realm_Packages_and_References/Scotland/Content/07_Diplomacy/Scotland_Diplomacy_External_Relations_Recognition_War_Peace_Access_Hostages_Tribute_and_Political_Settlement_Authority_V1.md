## 1. Executive Authority Verdict

Scotland possesses a mature distribution of political, legal, historical, dynastic, ecclesiastical, route, military, economic, Supply, Opening State, Registry, and AI authorities. Those owners provide diplomatic inputs, constraints, and execution capability, but none owns Scotland's complete Realm diplomacy lifecycle.

This authority closes that bounded gap. It owns Scotland-specific diplomatic definitions, principal and representation portfolios, proposals, agreements, obligations, performance, breach, war and peace interfaces, access and island-facing terms, person arrangements, financial diplomacy, cooperation, subordination interfaces, external-owner transactions, player explanation, AI request envelopes, and implementation handoff. It creates no exact January 1062 diplomatic instance and takes no private state from another owner.
