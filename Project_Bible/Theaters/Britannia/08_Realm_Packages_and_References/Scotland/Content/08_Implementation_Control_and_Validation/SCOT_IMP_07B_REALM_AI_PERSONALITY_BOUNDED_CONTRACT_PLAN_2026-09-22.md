SCOT-IMP-07A inventoried 43 AI and Personality authority records and 353 active source seams across eight readiness families. The accepted assessment is `ReadyForBoundedRealmAIPersonalityContract`.

It establishes reusable architecture with a Scotland gap for strategic-priority interpretation and risk-and-opportunity posture.

## 3. Constitutional Separation

```text
Realm Personality owns identity, values, fears, preferred approaches, rejected approaches, risk posture, and explanation language.
Realm AI owns interpretation, priority weighting, proposal formation, and review diagnostics.
Information and Intelligence owns what information is available, uncertain, contradicted, quarantined, or excluded.
Historical Memory owns remembered interpretations, not current observation.
Historical Momentum owns pressures and readiness, not decisions.
Historical Emergence owns conditional pathways, not AI-selected outcomes.
Political and institutional owners own legitimacy, succession, law, Church, regional, diplomacy, military, economy, supply, and route facts.
Command owners own any future executable command admission.
```
