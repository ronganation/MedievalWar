# Character Role

Máel Snechtai serves as one of the most important members of House Moray.

He carries the legacy of previous Moray rulers and represents the continuation of one of Scotland's most influential alternative power centers.

Unlike Malcolm III, Máel Snechtai does not possess royal authority.

Unlike Donald III, he does not belong to House Dunkeld.

His importance comes from representing a different dynastic future.

House Dunkeld currently dominates Scottish kingship.
