## 2. Consolidated Scotland Faction Thesis

**Scotland is an infantry-centered many-host faction whose strength comes from assembling different forms of military service into one army without flattening them into a uniform national roster. Broad spear hosts create frontage and time. Regional troops exploit terrain, missile opportunities, and exposed sectors. Royal and magnate followings provide scarce reliability and decision power. Practical cavalry protects the host, selected magnate cavalry commits against a real opening, and later knightly development adds formed lance shock without replacing older Scottish arms.**

```yaml
faction_identity:
  campaign: PoliticallyAssembledLayeredMilitaryCapacity
  battle: BroadHostCreatesTimeNarrowArmsMakeDecisions
  development: AddInstitutionsWithoutErasingOlderFamilies
  visual: RegionalAndSourceVariationInsideReadableUnitFamilies
  recruitment: FiniteSourceInformedPoolsWithoutMiniFactionMicromanagement
```

## 4. Current Portfolio by Military Layer

### 4.1 Common and emergency field capacity

#### Hill and Glen Muster

- Low-tier regional mixed light foot.
- Owns broken-ground screening, obstruction, local defense, bounded counterpressure, dispersal, and fallback.
- Sits above a possible unformed local-alarm floor but below a practiced warband.
- Must not become Caterans with worse statistics or a generic Highland rabble card.

#### Scottish Spear Host

- Main Early common-field infantry backbone.
- Owns broad long-spear frontage, prepared frontal mounted denial, enemy binding, and protection of other arms.
- Must remain affordable and repeatable relative to royal, magnate, and professional formations.
- Is not yet a mature schiltron or pike unit.

### 4.2 Royal service

#### King's Spear Retainers

- Scarce royal-service spear-and-shield infantry.
- Owns reliable control of one critical short front, prepared reception, relief, and controlled counter-entry.
- Must not replace the Spear Host's width or become generic elite mixed infantry.
- Proposed later convergence toward a royal armored-foot or King's Foot Guard role remains conditional.

### 4.3 Magnate service

#### Mormaer's Swordsmen

- Scarce magnate-facing sword-and-shield decision infantry.
- Owns one fresh close-combat intervention, short ground-taking, securing, relief, and disciplined return to the standard.
- Must beat ordinary foot in its intended fresh short contact.
- Must not become royal guard, axe assault infantry, or a dismounted cavalry card.

#### Mormaer's Horsemen

- Selected magnate-mounted spear-and-shield following.
- Owns deliberate supported mounted contact, chosen-wing commitment, short coherent melee, separation, and reform.
- Must remain better in contact and narrower in availability than March Riders.
- Must remain below later formed knightly lance shock.

### 4.4 Scottish regional-source specialists

#### Cateran Axemen

- Practiced regional axe-led raiding and flank infantry.
- Owns route selection, concealed approach, vulnerable-target attack, short axe pressure, and breakaway.
- Historical name and 1062 admission remain under chronology hold.
- Any mercenary expression must share one underlying people-and-equipment reality with the Scotland family rather than duplicate it.

#### Forest Bowmen

- Dedicated common regional foot archers.
- Owns protected shooting-lane acquisition, finite arrow pressure, target economy, relocation, and withdrawal.
- Forest is a fieldcraft identity, not a required terrain tag or magical bonus.
- Canonical Scotland ownership is retained; exact forest, estate, household, and community source qualification remains under review.

#### Galwegian Javelinmen

- Scotland-owned Galwegian shielded javelin-assault foot with a distinct Galloway-linked source and service identity.
- Owns host-shoulder approach, finite close cast, assessment, bounded press or refusal, and rally.
- Separate-card survival remains conditional on historical equipment support and real battlefield separation from mercenary Caterans.

### 4.5 Practical mounted security

#### March Riders

- Practical mounted screen and light-contact cavalry.
- Owns outer-wing security, threat without mandatory contact, selective interception, recall, and controlled pursuit.
- Scotland ownership is retained; exact March terminology, chronology, and source qualification remain under review.
- Must not become cheap knights, automatic scouts, or later Border Reivers projected backward.

### 4.6 Conditional later mounted development

#### Scottish Feudal Knights

- Conditional later mail-armored knightly lance cavalry.
- Owns aligned formed impact, countercharge, short post-impact fighting, separation, and material recovery.
- Not admitted to the 1062 opening.
- Develops through actual Scottish land-service and knightly household networks without replacing March Riders or mormaer-facing cavalry.
