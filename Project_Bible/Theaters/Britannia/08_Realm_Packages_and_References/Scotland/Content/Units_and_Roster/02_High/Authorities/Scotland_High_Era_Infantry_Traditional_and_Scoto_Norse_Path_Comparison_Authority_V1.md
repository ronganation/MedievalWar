The removed `Scotland_Hebridean_Retainers_V1_2_Final_Chat_Refined_Full_Master_Unit_Profile.md` is not counted. The Scoto-Norse portfolio instead uses the later `Hebridean Sea Retainers` family as the active western retainer concept.

## 2. Executive Portfolio Verdict

Both paths pass as coherent High Era infantry portfolios.

**Traditional Scotland** emphasizes the maturing common host, coordinated spear warfare, civic military institutions, Crown intervention, maintained armored infantry, Highland regional assault, and the first specialist pike and great-weapon developments.

**Scoto-Norse Scotland** emphasizes western community frontage, ship-connected expeditionary infantry, Hebridean retained shields, island household axes, Kintyre regional assault, aggressive Gallovidian spear service, and Crown-western convergence.

## 5. Direct Cross-Path Comparator Pairs

### 5.1 Ordered Spear Host versus Isles Spear Levy

**Ordered Spear Host owns:** coordinated movement, halted frontage, formation restoration, mature common-host command, and the Wall of Ash identity.

**Isles Spear Levy owns:** affordable western frontage, local and expeditionary depth, simpler service requirements, and support for western retained and maritime infantry.

### 5.2 Highland Warband versus Kintyre Warband

This is a meaningful collision, but not a deletion case.

**Highland Warband owns:** broad Highland regional ground-taking, flexible rough-ground assault, and consolidation within Scotland's Traditional portfolio.

**Kintyre Warband owns:** Kintyre and western provider identity, Scoto-Norse equipment and formation flavor, western settlement and relationship access, and integration with the Western Sea portfolio.
