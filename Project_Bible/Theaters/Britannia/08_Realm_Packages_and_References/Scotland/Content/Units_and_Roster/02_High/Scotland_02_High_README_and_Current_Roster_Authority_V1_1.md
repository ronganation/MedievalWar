### 2.4 Traditional path identity

Traditional development builds stronger domestic military institutions through the maturing common host, civic service, Crown and magnate intervention, maintained armored infantry, developed field archery, knightly shock, elite mounted reserve control, and frontier organization. It normally provides the stronger complete cavalry institution and broader formal military architecture, but pays through political, equipment, horse, cadre, civic, castle, and specialist-recovery burdens.

### 3.4 Scoto-Norse path identity

Scoto-Norse development builds a western regional and relationship-driven combined-arms portfolio. The direction emphasizes affordable frontage, maritime-compatible infantry, retained shields, household axes, sling and javelin harassment, practical and selected western archery, mounted-javelin reaction shaping, conditional Irish Sea mobility, and one strong regional cavalry contact family. It gains variety and fluidity but remains dependent on fragmented providers, political relationships, routes, infantry protection, missiles, terrain, ships or transport where relevant, and finite recovery capacity.
