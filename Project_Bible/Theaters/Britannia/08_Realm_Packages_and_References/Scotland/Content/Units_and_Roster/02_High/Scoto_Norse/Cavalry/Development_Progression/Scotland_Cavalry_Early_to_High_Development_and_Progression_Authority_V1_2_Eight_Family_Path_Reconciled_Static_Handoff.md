## 1. Direct Portfolio Verdict

Scotland's cavalry progression is a coexistence portfolio, not a straight ladder from weak horse to strong horse.

- March Riders own practical outer screening and mounted response capacity.
- Mormaer's Horsemen own selected magnate contact held for a worthwhile local commitment.
- Horsemen of Mann own conditionally accessible Western Sea mobility, pursuit, and withdrawal protection.
- Isles Mounted Companions own scarce Scoto-Norse medium-heavy charge impact with bounded axe or sword follow-through.

## 56. Eight-Family Acceptance Fixtures

All fixtures remain proposed and unexecuted.

- High Era begins while March Riders and Mormaer's Horsemen remain active.
- Traditional development becomes favored but lacks sufficient heavy horses and cadre. Knight and Lancer recruitment remains blocked.
- Western development becomes favored but no mounted-javelin provider exists. Western Hobelars remain unavailable.
- Manx relationship is absent. Horsemen of Mann remain unavailable despite Western development.
- Western lordship packet is incomplete. Isles Mounted Companions remain eligible but not ready.
- March Riders and Western Hobelars operate in the same army without duplicating screening.
- Western Hobelars and Horsemen of Mann engage the same exposed missiles; Hobelars shape reaction while Horsemen win direct contact and pursuit.
- Horsemen of Mann protect withdrawal while Companions reform.
- Scottish Feudal Knights retain stronger complete knightly shock than Companions.
- Lowland Lancers retain two-decision reserve control rather than universal first-charge superiority.
- March Wardens solve a frontier objective that March Riders cannot, or trigger their merge gate if systems cannot express it.
- Recruitment closure preserves surviving companies in all eight families.
- One rider, horse, leader, or equipment packet cannot refill two families.
- AI recruits infantry or supply instead of another cavalry card when mounted need is already met.
