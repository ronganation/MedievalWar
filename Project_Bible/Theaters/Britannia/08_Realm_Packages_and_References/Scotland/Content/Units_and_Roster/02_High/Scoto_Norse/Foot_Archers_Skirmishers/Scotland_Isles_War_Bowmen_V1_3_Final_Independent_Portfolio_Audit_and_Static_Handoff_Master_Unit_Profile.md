This profile develops **Isles War Bowmen** as the selected, higher-impact bow formation of Scotland's Scoto-Norse High Era path. The unit does not replace Western Sea Bowmen. Western Sea Bowmen retain broader recruitment, better sustained field endurance, and more economical general archery. Isles War Bowmen concentrate selected shooters, stronger serviceable bows, more consistent arrows, better fire leadership, and improved protection into a scarcer formation built to exploit one important shooting window.

The unit is not an English or Welsh longbow clone, not a hidden crossbow substitute, and not Highland Bow Retainers under a western name. Its identity is concentrated selected bow fire with withdrawal before melee.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Isles War Bowmen
- **Class:** Selected concentrated war-bow infantry
- **Signature:** Position, Select, Concentrate, Close
- **Battle promise:** Concentrate selected regional bow fire against one exposed high-value target, create meaningful local casualties or disorder, and preserve the selected archers before the battle becomes a prolonged ammunition exchange or melee.
- **Best use:** Formation flanks, exposed medium infantry, enemy archers, cavalry before contact, disordered troops, and targets already fixed by western spears or retainers.
- **Strong against:** Exposed light and medium infantry, valuable missile troops, cavalry approaching without missile suppression, and formation sides lacking shield orientation.
- **Conditional against:** Better-protected infantry at useful range and targets already pressured by other western missiles.
- **Weak against:** Heavy frontal protection, dispersed light troops at poor range, crossbows in a prepared exchange, cavalry reaching the bow line, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Stronger bows, arrows, leaders, and selected shooters produce greater value per important release, but with lower numbers, higher cost, slower recovery, and less total shooting endurance than Western Sea Bowmen.
- **Player instruction:** Do not scatter the arrows across every target. Protect the line, wait for a valuable exposure, concentrate the release, and close the mission before the target or angle becomes wasteful.
- **Flavor:** **Western Sea Bowmen sustain the battle; Isles War Bowmen decide which target must suffer now.**

## 9. Core Battle Loop: Position, Select, Concentrate, Close

### Position

Occupy a protected supporting location behind western infantry or suitable terrain. Preserve clear shooting and withdrawal lanes.

### Select

Select an exposed, committed, or valuable target. Selection does not create a hidden accuracy bonus; it represents fire-leader target control, range judgment, section coordination, and refusal to waste selected arrows.

### Concentrate

Deliver controlled concentrated releases while range, angle, target value, and friendly safety remain acceptable.

### Close

Cease the mission when the angle disappears, friendly troops enter the arc, cavalry threatens, or the remaining arrows should be preserved.

### Withdraw or Reposition

Move behind the battle line or to a second prepared position if formation order, ammunition, and protection permit.

## 11. Formation and Order Portfolio

### War Bow Support Line

The normal protected shooting formation.

**Strengths**
- coherent concentrated release;
- better fire leadership;
- useful protection and equipment order;
- clear relationship with supporting infantry.

**Weaknesses**
- smaller frontage than general archers;
- poor performance when rushed;
- dangerous if cavalry reaches the formation;
- lower total arrow endurance than Western Sea Bowmen.

### Concentrated Release

A contextual fire order against a marked high-value target.

**Strengths**
- concentrates selected arrows;
- increases local casualty and disruption potential through fire density;
- rewards exposed flanks and committed targets.

**Weaknesses**
- consumes ammunition quickly;
- risks overkill;
- poor against dispersed targets;
- requires fire control and a clear arc.

### Close Mission

Ceases fire and preserves remaining arrows when the tactical bargain becomes poor.

### Withdraw Behind the Line

A physical movement order, not a free escape.

## 20. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- recruit only when the army already has protection and general archery;
- preserve Western Sea Bowmen as the broader arrow base;
- reserve selected shooters and stronger bows for high-value formations;
- account for arrow, string, bow, leader, and provider recovery;
- do not fill every missile slot with War Bowmen.

### Battle AI

- deploy behind reliable infantry protection;
- prefer exposed medium infantry, enemy archers, cavalry approaches, and formation flanks;
- avoid wasting arrows against dispersed or heavily shielded frontal targets;
- close the mission when cavalry threatens or friendly troops enter the arc;
- withdraw rather than stand in melee;
- attempt a second mission only when ammunition and formation order remain useful.
