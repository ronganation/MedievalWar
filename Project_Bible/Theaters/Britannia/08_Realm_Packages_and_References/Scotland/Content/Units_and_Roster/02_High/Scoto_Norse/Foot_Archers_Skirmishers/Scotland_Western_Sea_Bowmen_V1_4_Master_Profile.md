## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Western Sea Bowmen are practical western regional archers who establish a protected firing position behind the Isles' formed troops, deliver useful sustained bow pressure against exposed and moderately protected targets, displace before cavalry or heavy infantry closes, and support a maritime army whose ammunition and replacement chain remains finite.**

Their signature is **Establish, Loose, Displace, Support**.

Their defining purchase is **dependable regional missile support within a Western Sea campaign system**. They are broader and less socially restrictive than Highland Bow Retainers, but less expert and less capable of taking dangerous exposed shooting positions. They are more mobile and expedition-compatible than Forest Bowmen, but do not inherit forest ambush, concealment, or specialized woodland fire control.

Their finite battle resource is **Shooting Position**, meaning enough archers, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, frontage, visibility, protection, ammunition access, and withdrawal space remain connected to sustain useful fire without becoming trapped.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Practiced western regional bow infantry
- **Battle promise:** Establish behind a credible western front, sustain useful bow fire, displace before the protection collapses, and keep the Western Sea army supplied with ordinary regional missile pressure.
- **Best use:** Behind Isles Spear Levy, on protected slopes, settlement edges, harbor defenses, secured beachheads, and flanks where escape and resupply routes remain open.
- **Strong against:** Exposed light infantry, enemy skirmishers, lightly protected ordinary foot, compressed defenders, and units already fixed by spear or retainer formations.
- **Conditional against:** Moderately protected infantry through angle, volume, fatigue, and sustained exposure rather than hidden armor-piercing bonuses.
- **Weak against:** Heavy armor, cavalry, counter-battery archery from superior range or position, bad visibility, ammunition loss, wet strings, and a collapsed protective front.
- **Campaign bargain:** Scotland gains a practical western bow family by reserving actual archers, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, service time, transport space, stores, repair, and return capacity from qualified western sources.
- **Player instruction:** Establish behind formed protection, choose a useful target, preserve ammunition and the displacement lane, and move before the firing position becomes a trap.
- **Flavor:** **The western bow does not win by legend; it wins by arriving supplied and shooting from ground the army can still hold.**

## 4. Core Battle Loop: Establish, Loose, Displace, Support

### Establish

Take a firing position with visibility, frontage, protection, ammunition access, and a known displacement route. Establishing is a physical action, not a passive deployment bonus.

### Loose

Deliver measured bow fire against an exposed, fixed, compressed, or advancing target. Fire quality depends on range, angle, visibility, fatigue, ammunition, bow condition, and target protection.

### Displace

Move before cavalry, heavy infantry, superior missiles, smoke, weather, or friendly movement makes the position untenable. Displacement sacrifices current fire to preserve the bow company.

### Support

Reestablish behind Isles Spear Levy, Sea Retainers, Marines, terrain, settlement defenses, or another protected front. Support means shaping exposure and sustaining pressure, not independently winning the melee battle.

## 16. Terrain, Weather, Siege, and Settlement Use

Favorable ground includes protected slopes, coastal rises, harbor approaches, settlement edges, firm fields behind a spear line, and secured beachheads with dry equipment and visible lanes.

Poor ground includes open cavalry exposure, confined streets without displacement space, heavy rain, strong adverse wind, smoke, fog, deep woodland without relevant practice, and waterline fighting before reception security.

The unit is useful in settlement and harbor defense once assigned a real firing lane. It is not a ladder, breach, or gate-crush assault unit.
