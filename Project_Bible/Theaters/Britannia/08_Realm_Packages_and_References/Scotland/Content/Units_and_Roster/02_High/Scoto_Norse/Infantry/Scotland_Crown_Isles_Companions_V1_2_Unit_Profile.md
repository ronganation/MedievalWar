This profile develops **Crown-Isles Companions** as a possible mature convergence unit linking Scotland's royal-consolidation development with the lordships and military households of the western seaways. The card does not represent the Crown manufacturing western retainers or erasing their providers. It represents selected contingents whose local lordship service, Crown-recognized obligation, officers, standards, equipment claims, and campaign purpose align strongly enough to create a dependable composite reserve.

The concept is deliberately later and more conditional than the core western roster. It is not an opening unit, not a universal path reward, not a royal guard recruited directly from a building, and not an excuse to combine the best statistics of Hebridean Sea Retainers and Isles Household Axemen.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Crown-Isles Companions
- **Class:** Superior composite command-reserve infantry
- **Signature:** Rally, Reinforce, Press, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Enter a threatened sector in good order, restore physical continuity through disciplined reinforcement, push back an exposed enemy, and withdraw or yield passage before elite reserves or exhaustion trap the unit.
- **Best use:** Crisis reinforcement, line relief, defense of commanders and standards, stabilized counterpressure, protecting a controlled withdrawal, and connecting regional contingents to the royal battle plan.
- **Strong against:** Ordinary infantry already committed or disordered, enemy breakthroughs lacking fresh support, light and medium troops attacking a weakened Scottish sector, and wavering local formations that still possess a recoverable line.
- **Conditional against:** Superior armored infantry when the Companions enter with flank, missile, or reserve support.
- **Weak against:** Fresh elite heavy infantry, deep spear formations, concentrated missiles during approach, heavy cavalry on exposed ground, and prolonged fighting without relief.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain a dependable high-quality reserve only by aligning Crown authority, western lordship consent, qualified retainers, officers, equipment, political trust, and a bounded service mission.
- **Player instruction:** Keep the Companions out of routine attrition. Commit the unit where the line can still be saved, restore the sector, press only while the local advantage exists, and recover before the reserve becomes another exhausted front-line formation.
- **Flavor:** **Neither a royal regiment nor an island warband, but the difficult agreement that lets both fight as one reserve.**

## 4. Historical and Political Foundation

The High Medieval western seaboard was not a simple peripheral recruitment zone waiting for direct Crown administration. Somerled and the descendants associated with his lordship ruled significant western territories through dynastic, military, ecclesiastical, and maritime relationships. Those magnates could serve as effective agents of royal authority or resist it when interests diverged. ## 8. Core Battle Loop: Rally, Reinforce, Press, Recover

### 8.1 Rally

Receive an order, identify a threatened but recoverable sector, and align officers, standards, files, and approach lane. Rally does not restore another unit through an aura.

### 8.2 Reinforce

Enter through a real physical lane, extend or replace exhausted frontage, protect the threatened flank, and reconnect the local Scottish line.

### 8.3 Press

Once the sector stabilizes, apply bounded counterpressure against the exposed enemy using spear, shield, axe, sword, and succeeding files. Pressing is optional; stabilization may be the correct outcome.

### 8.4 Recover

Halt, reform, yield passage, or withdraw behind another line before the Companions are exhausted in prolonged contact.

## 10. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 10.1 Companion Reserve Order

The normal compact reserve formation balancing movement, protection, and rapid extension into a threatened sector.

**Strengths**
- good order during short repositioning;
- protects leaders and standards;
- can enter a narrow reinforcement lane;
- preserves mixed weapons and files for the crisis.

**Weaknesses**
- limited frontage;
- poor value if used as a routine static line;
- concentrated missile target;
- no dedicated anti-cavalry depth.

### 10.2 Reinforce the Line

The principal contextual order.

**Strengths**
- extends or replaces failing frontage;
- reconnects separated friendly formations physically;
- provides disciplined local pressure;
- creates time for other units to rally or withdraw.

**Weaknesses**
- requires a real entry lane;
- may inherit the original unit's bad position;
- vulnerable if the enemy breakthrough is already complete;
- can become trapped by friendly congestion.

### 10.3 Companion Counterpress

A bounded follow-through after stabilization.

**Strengths**
- useful against disordered ordinary infantry;
- balanced spear, shield, and sidearm pressure;
- exploits an enemy that overextended during the breakthrough.

**Weaknesses**
- below Household Axemen in decisive melee;
- increasing fatigue and loss of reserve value;
- dangerous against fresh armored foot;
- reduced flank security.

### 10.4 Yield and Reform

A contextual order for handing the restored sector to fresh line infantry and recovering reserve order.

### Recruit when

- western lordship service and Crown integration have created genuine convergence, whether through path investment or campaign world state;
- the army needs a crisis reserve rather than another front-line company;
- Crown and western providers can sustain compatible command;
- existing core units already provide frontage, missiles, cavalry protection, and decisive attack;

### Do not recruit when

- the army simply wants the strongest available infantry statistics;
- Sea Retainers or Household Axemen already own the required battlefield task;
- regional loyalty, local defense, or Crown trust is weak;
- no physical reserve lane or relief doctrine exists;
- the unit would be used as routine attritional line infantry;

## 20. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- recruit only after the convergence gate is genuinely satisfied;
- preserve western provider duty floors and Crown political capacity;
- prefer one meaningful reserve over multiple elite stacks;
- do not replace Sea Retainers, Household Axemen, or royal infantry;
- close recruitment honestly if trust or service relationships fail;
- preserve survivors and cadre rather than disbanding and recreating the formation.

### Battle AI

- deploy behind the main line with a physical entry lane;
- avoid routine opening contact;
- identify threatened but recoverable sectors;
- reinforce before total collapse but after the need becomes real;
- stabilize first and counterpress only against an exposed enemy;
- yield or withdraw after fresh line infantry arrives;
- avoid fresh elite heavy infantry and open-ground cavalry;
- protect commanders and standards when no crisis commitment is justified.

### Preferred battle sequence

1. Core line and missile units establish the battle.
2. Gallovidian War Spears or other line infantry commit the chosen sector.
3. Sea Retainers protect critical command and local contact.
4. Household Axemen deliver the planned decisive attack if conditions permit.
5. Crown-Isles Companions remain in reserve rather than joining routine frontage.
6. The Companions reinforce the sector where the Scottish plan is failing but still recoverable.

### 28.1 Separate card versus command package decision

The unit survives provisionally as a separate card because the player makes a distinct battlefield decision: preserve a superior infantry body out of contact, choose a threatened but recoverable sector, enter through a physical lane, stabilize the sector through actual bodies and weapons, then decide whether to counterpress or recover.

```yaml

  current_result: KeepAsConditionalRecruitableOrAttachableUnitCandidate
  reasons:
    - PhysicalFormationHasOwnCasualtiesEquipmentFatigueAndPosition
    - ReserveTimingCreatesDistinctPlayerDecision
    - LineReliefRequiresActualFrontageAndEntryLane
    - UnitRetainsUsefulCombatValueAfterEntry
    - CampaignProviderAndPoliticalCostsAttachToRealPeople
  convert_to_command_package_if:
    - BattlePrototypeCannotRepresentPhysicalRelief
    - RoyalPathAlreadyOwnsAnEquivalentReserveCard
    - UnitDifferenceCollapsesIntoMoraleOrCommandBonuses
    - AvailabilityIsRestrictedToOneNamedCharactersImmediateHousehold
```

This is not a morale aura wearing a unit card. If implementation cannot preserve the physical formation, the concept should become a command package rather than survive through artificial bonuses.

### 50. V1.2 Final Verdict

Crown-Isles Companions are a strong conditional convergence concept and now possess a complete physical reserve cycle. The design should not yet be admitted. A completed royal-consolidation reserve or heavy-foot comparator is still required, and prototype review must confirm that physical relief and front transfer are legible enough to justify a separate card rather than a command package. Those are real external gates, not reasons to keep inflating the profile in chat.
