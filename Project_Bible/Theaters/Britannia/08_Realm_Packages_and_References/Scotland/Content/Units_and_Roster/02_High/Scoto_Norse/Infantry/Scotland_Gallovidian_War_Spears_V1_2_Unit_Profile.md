This profile develops **Gallovidian War Spears** as an aggressive regional spear formation from Galloway whose mature High Era expression can connect Scotland's western military network to a royal-consolidation path. The unit is not another Isles Spear Levy tier, not a fantasy shock horde, and not a claim that the exact named regiment existed.

The concept uses the attested prominence of Galwegian spearmen in Scottish armies as a historical foundation while rejecting hostile chroniclers' caricature of uniformly naked, irrational, or tactically incapable warriors. Accounts of the Battle of the Standard are valuable evidence for a distinct and forceful Galwegian contingent, but they are partisan accounts of one battle and cannot define the complete unit family.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gallovidian War Spears
- **Class:** Good aggressive regional spear infantry
- **Signature:** Advance, Fix, Press, Endure
- **Battle promise:** Advance into ordinary infantry with enough order and determination to force a sustained spear contest, commit and occupy the enemy line, and create the decisive commitment lane for Scotland's retainers and heavy infantry.
- **Best use:** Forward battle line, aggressive spear advance, sustaining pressure against ordinary infantry, contested rolling ground, supporting regional war hosts, and creating a target for Household Axemen or royal reserves.
- **Strong against:** Ordinary spear or shield infantry of lower quality, militia, light infantry, fragmented formations, and cavalry attempting an unsupported frontal attack.
- **Conditional against:** Better armored infantry when supported by missiles, flank pressure, and fresh reserves.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated archery, crossbows, elite armored foot, heavy cavalry on exposed flanks, prolonged isolation, and any advance that loses formation before contact.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better and more forceful than mass levy spears, but regionally finite, politically mediated, leadership sensitive, and costly to keep on distant service.
- **Player instruction:** Do not treat the War Spears as a passive wall. Screen the approach, maintain flank protection, force the chosen enemy into contact, and commit the decisive reserve before the formation is exhausted or enveloped.
- **Flavor:** **The line moves forward so the rest of the army can choose where the battle will be decided.**

### Scottish common or ordered spear families

The general Scottish spear host owns national mass, broad recruitment, defensive anti-cavalry depth, and central army frontage. Gallovidian War Spears own a regionally specific aggressive advance and fixing role. They must not become the universal best Scottish answer to cavalry.

### Hebridean Sea Retainers

Sea Retainers own compact protection, formation recovery, command defense, and reliable short contact. War Spears own broader battle-line pressure and a more offensive advance. Retainers remain better when the task is protecting a commander, standard, confined sector, or landing recovery point.

### Isles Household Axemen

Household Axemen own the decisive elite counterattack and concentrated heavy melee. War Spears create and maintain the contest into which the axemen commit. War Spears must not equal their anti-infantry finishing power.

### Gall-Ghaidheil Skirmishers and Western Sea Bowmen

Missile troops shape the approach and punish exposed defenders. War Spears convert that shaping into sustained contact. They do not gain meaningful ranged attacks merely to operate independently.

### Royal or burgh infantry

Royal and burgh units may possess stronger standardized command, equipment maintenance, castle or settlement service, and central availability. Gallovidian War Spears retain regional provider identity, aggressive field character, and political mediation.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Advance, Fix, Press, Endure

### 8.1 Advance

Move in ordered spear files behind missile screening and with flanks protected. The advance should be purposeful but not a sprint or uncontrolled charge.

### 8.2 Fix

Enter contact against the chosen ordinary infantry body and force the enemy to face, brace, and spend reserves. The spear formation's value begins when the opposing line loses freedom to maneuver.

### 8.3 Press

Apply controlled forward pressure through depth, leadership, and succeeding files. Pressing improves local offensive output but increases fatigue, missile exposure, and flank risk.

### 8.4 Endure

Keep enough spear order to survive the enemy's first counterattack and hold the contest open for supporting retainers, axemen, cavalry, or royal reserves.

### 8.5 Relieve or Withdraw

Once the decisive formation arrives or the position becomes untenable, either yield passage in controlled files or withdraw before the unit becomes surrounded.

## 10. Formation Portfolio

### 10.1 War Spear Order

The default field formation balancing depth, frontage, and measured movement.

**Strengths**
- good ordinary infantry combat;
- credible resistance to unsupported frontal light cavalry;
- maintains useful formation during advance;
- supports relief and reserve passage.

**Weaknesses**
- vulnerable to sustained missiles;
- not as deep as a dedicated anti-cavalry spear hedge;
- average turning speed;
- exposed if unsupported on both flanks.

### 10.2 Pressing Spear Files

An offensive state for increasing local forward pressure once contact is established.

**Strengths**
- better momentum against ordinary infantry;
- concentrates succeeding files;
- increases the chance of fixing or pushing a wavering enemy;
- creates a clearer commitment opportunity for reserves.

**Weaknesses**
- greater fatigue;
- reduced ability to react to flank attack;
- weaker cavalry resistance during disorder;
- dangerous against elite armored infantry.

### 10.3 Receive Horse

A contextual defensive behavior rather than the unit's main identity.

**Strengths**
- credible resistance to frontal light and medium cavalry;
- may punish unsupported charges;
- buys time for deeper Scottish spear formations.

**Weaknesses**
- weaker than dedicated ordered spear hosts;
- minimal forward pressure;
- vulnerable to missiles and dismounted attack;
- flank or rear cavalry attacks remain dangerous.

### 10.4 Relief Passage

A contextual behavior allowing Sea Retainers, Household Axemen, royal infantry, or fresh spears to enter the committed sector.

## 19. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- recruit when good forward infantry and a valid Gallovidian provider are available;
- maintain cheap frontage and elite reserves around the unit;
- protect local Galloway duty floors;
- distinguish western and royal development investments without duplicating the card;
- avoid replacing every Scottish spear formation with War Spears;
- account for distant-service and recovery burdens.

### Battle AI

- screen the approach with bows or skirmishers;
- avoid advancing through concentrated missile fire without support;
- choose ordinary infantry rather than elite armored foot;
- maintain flank protection;
- apply Controlled Press only after contact is established;
- call or admit a decisive reserve before exhaustion;
- receive unsupported cavalry frontally when necessary;

## 20. Visual Direction

- organized but nonuniform regional spear files;
- long spears create the defining silhouette;
- small-to-medium shields in evidence-supported regional forms;
