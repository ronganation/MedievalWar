## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Hebridean Sea Retainers are a proposed selected western maritime contact-infantry family whose value comes from arriving through a valid island or western retained-service relationship, recovering enough order after supported deployment to secure one bounded coastal lodgment or difficult land objective, and then serving as useful shielded heavy foot in an ordinary field battle.**

Their signature is **Land, Recover, Commit, Return**.

Their defining purchase is **reliable selected foot after maritime movement**, not immunity to landing disorder. They are not ship crews promoted into elite infantry, not Scottish Vikings, and not later galloglaich imported backward. Their strength comes from a real provider relationship, selected retainers, robust shields, useful protection, officers accustomed to western service, and a campaign system able to assemble, transport, receive, supply, and return them.

Their finite battle resource is **landing order**, meaning enough people, leaders, shields, weapons, equipment, service cohesion, landing space, and receiving support remain connected for the formation to become a dependable land body after movement by sea or coastal route.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Hebridean Sea Retainers
- **Alternate working name:** Western Isles Retainers
- **Class:** Selected western maritime contact infantry
- **Signature:** Land, Recover, Commit, Return
- **Battle promise:** After genuine transport and landing support, recover order faster and more reliably than ordinary transported troops, secure one bounded coastal or settlement objective, and remain useful as quality shielded infantry after the landing is over.
- **Best use:** Supported beach or harbor reception, coastal counterattack, settlement approach, confined objective seizure, relief of a wavering western-service body, and ordinary short heavy-foot commitment beside a Scottish spear front.
- **Strong against:** Disordered light infantry, exposed missile troops after a secured approach, fragmented defenders, ordinary infantry in a short supported contact, and opponents unable to exploit the landing interval.
- **Conditional against:** Peer quality infantry when the retainers have recovered formation, retained shields and leaders, and operate within a bounded objective and relief plan.
- **Weak against:** Opposition during disembarkation, prepared missiles, cavalry on open ground, prolonged armored grinding, encirclement, disrupted transport, loss of officers or shields, and deep inland service without replacement or return support.
- **Campaign bargain:** Scotland receives quality western-service infantry only through actual island or western relationships, selected land-service retainers, compatible equipment, vessels, crews, embarkation capacity, provisions, reception, coastal supply, and return arrangements.
- **Player instruction:** Do not throw them directly from ship to melee. Secure the landing space, rebuild Landing Order, commit against one useful objective, and preserve the route needed to supply or return the formation.
- **Flavor:** **The sea delivers the household; discipline decides whether it arrives as an army.**

## 4. Scotland Roster Collision Firewall

### Highland Warband

Highland Warband owns connected regional assault, contested-ground fighting, and consolidation. Hebridean Sea Retainers own selected western-service reliability after maritime or coastal deployment and a shorter shielded contact commitment. They must not simply outperform Highland Warband in every land battle.

### Scottish Armored Foot

Scottish Armored Foot owns maintained protected-sector fighting, relief, and measured pressure through more sustained land-service support. Sea Retainers may contain meaningful mail among selected followers, but they must remain less capable of prolonged armored grinding and more dependent on western provider, transport, and return conditions.

### Galwegian Warband and Galwegian Javelinmen

Galwegian families own southwestern missile-to-contact and regional assault identities. Sea Retainers should not acquire their javelin rhythm by default, and southwestern maritime relationships do not automatically populate a Hebridean or Western Isles card.

### Cateran Axemen

Cateran Axemen own route-led attack, raiding, ambush, and breakaway. Sea Retainers are more selected and shielded, less available, less concealment-focused, and better suited to one defended objective or organized contact.

### King's Spear Retainers

King's Spear Retainers own scarce royal spear anchoring and critical-point control. Sea Retainers remain western relationship-bound and should not inherit royal command, general spear-anchor superiority, or whole-army stabilization.

### Norse mercenary families

Native or relationship-based Scottish access is not the same as mercenary contracting. People, payment, service, recovery, and ownership must remain separately tracked.

### Galloglaich

Later or external galloglaich identities must not be projected backward merely to provide a famous name, two-handed axe silhouette, or elite reputation. Any historical relationship is a specialist-review question, not a gameplay shortcut.

## 9. Battle Loop: Land, Recover, Commit, Return

### Land

Disembark through real physical space. Formation depth, shields, weapons, leaders, and support do not appear simultaneously. A contested landing can destroy the card before its ordinary battle promise becomes available.

### Recover

Rebuild files, facing, shield relation, officers, standards, equipment, and connection to the receiving Scottish force. Recovery is the candidate's central distinction, but it remains conditional rather than automatic.

### Commit

Take one bounded objective, displace a fragmented defender, reinforce a threatened sector, or deliver a short heavy-foot action. The unit should not enter indefinite armored grinding.

### Consolidate

Secure the lodgment, protect supply and withdrawal, accept relief, or establish an ordinary land-battle position beside other Scottish troops.

### Return

Complete the service relationship through supported withdrawal, re-embarkation where relevant, inland return, casualty accounting, equipment repair, and political settlement.

## 15. Combined-Arms Integration

- **Highland Warband:** takes or contests broader regional ground after the retainers secure a short lodgment or relieve a pressured sector.
- **Scottish Spear Host or Ordered Spear Host:** provides dependable frontage after the landing expands beyond one selected contact body.
- **Forest Bowmen and Highland Bow Retainers:** shape exposed defenders and protect the consolidation, but require ammunition, landing, and fire lanes of their own.
- **Galwegian Javelinmen:** create close disruption in southwestern contexts without becoming the same source or card.
- **March Riders and other horse:** gain no automatic maritime qualification; horses require separate vessels, feed, handlers, landing space, and recovery.
- **Scottish Armored Foot:** sustains prolonged protected-sector pressure after support and supply are established.

## 16. Terrain, Settlement, and Siege Use

Good uses include harbor approaches, coastal settlements, defended landing exits, short slopes above a reception point, compact objectives, gate interiors after entry is secured, and ordinary field flanks with a protected rear.

Poor uses include ladders, first-wave breach assault, deep street pursuit, open cavalry ground, unsupported waterline fighting, and extended inland operations detached from service and return support.

## 28. Revised Ordinary Battle Loop

### Recover

Establish or rebuild Retainer Order after march, transport, landing, relief, or local disorder.

### Protect

Secure a commander, standard, lighter western troop, compact objective, or exposed connection between formations. This is a physical assignment, not a morale aura.

### Commit

Deliver a short shielded contact action once the enemy is fixed, disordered, or unable to turn the retainer front into prolonged grinding.

### Return

Accept relief, shorten the frontage, return to the protected mission, or complete the service arrangement before casualties destroy the limited provider body.
