## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Isles Spear Levy are a proposed High Era western community and lordship spear family whose value comes from affordable regional frontage, practiced assembly around a recognized local authority, protection of landing or coastal operating space, and enough depth to receive cavalry or ordinary infantry while scarcer Western Sea troops prepare their decisive work.**

Their signature is **Muster, Receive, Deepen, Hold**.

Their purchase is **regional frontage with supported western mobility**, not elite spear combat. They provide the body that the Western Sea path presently lacks: broader and cheaper than Hebridean Sea Retainers, less violent than Isles Household Axemen, and more capable of sustained land frontage than Birlinn Marines should be.

Their finite battle resource is **levy depth**, meaning enough spear-bearing people, shields, local leaders, compatible sections, service time, equipment, and reserves remain connected to hold one useful frontage and replace local losses without pretending to be an ordered national host.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Isles Spear Levy
- **Class:** High Era western regional spear infantry
- **Signature:** Muster, Receive, Deepen, Hold
- **Battle promise:** Supply affordable spear frontage for a Western Sea army, deepen one threatened sector, protect compact elite units, and hold coastal or ordinary field ground without requiring royal or household-quality service.
- **Best use:** Width beside western specialists, prepared reception against ordinary cavalry, coastal settlement defense, beachhead expansion after another force secures reception, and containment of an infantry front while Household Axemen or Sea Retainers prepare a local decision.
- **Strong against:** Light and medium cavalry through prepared depth, ordinary infantry in a supported defensive contact, fragmented landing defenders, and threats forced to attack a known front.
- **Conditional against:** Better infantry when the levy has depth, stable flanks, local leaders, and relief.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, elite armored foot, heavy axe assault, outflanking, rapid formation changes, prolonged unsupported expeditionary service, and contested landing before order is rebuilt.

## 5. Western Sea Portfolio Firewall

### Hebridean Sea Retainers

Sea Retainers own compact shielded protection, recovery, commander or standard defense, and one controlled local commitment. Isles Spear Levy owns cheaper width, spear depth, and sustained ordinary frontage. The levy protects space; the retainers protect one important relationship.

### Isles Household Axemen

Household Axemen own the decisive local counterattack. Isles Spear Levy fixes the enemy, guards the flanks, receives cavalry, and gives the Axemen somewhere to recover. The levy does not inherit heavy axes or breakthrough power.

### Birlinn Marines

Birlinn Marines should own practical disembarkation, port security, shipboard flexibility, and rapid coastal response. Isles Spear Levy may be transported but recovers more slowly and requires more space. Once formed, it provides better spear frontage and cavalry resistance.

### Shared Scottish roster

The levy does not replace Scottish Spear Host in inland mass armies, Ordered Spear Host in coordinated High Era operations, Schiltron Spearmen in dense anti-cavalry commitment, or King's Spear Retainers at critical royal points.

## 9. Battle Loop

### Muster

Assemble a usable field body from an actual western source. The formation does not appear merely because the development path is selected.

### Receive

Prepare a known frontage against cavalry or infantry. The levy performs best when the threat is visible, the front is connected, and the flanks are supported.

### Deepen

Move reserve files into one threatened sector, replace losses, protect a gap, or build the shoulder needed by Sea Retainers or Household Axemen. Deepening one sector thins another.

### Hold

Maintain the position long enough for missiles, compact retainers, axemen, or broader Scottish forces to make the decision. Holding is successful even when the levy does not destroy the enemy.

### Shorten or return

Accept relief, contract the frontage, withdraw in order where possible, and return survivors to a provider that still needs local defense.

## 15. Combined-Arms Integration

- **Hebridean Sea Retainers:** protect the important short front while the levy supplies neighboring width and depth.
- **Isles Household Axemen:** counterattack from behind the levy after the enemy is fixed or disordered.
- **Birlinn Marines:** secure arrival space and port functions before the deeper levy formation assembles.
- **Western missile troops:** shoot from behind or beside a spear front with known withdrawal lanes.
- **Shared Scottish heavy foot:** relieves the hardest grinding sector after the levy has bought time.
- **Cavalry:** uses the levy as a safe shoulder but gains no automatic maritime transport.

## 16. Terrain, Siege, and Settlement Use

Favorable conditions include firm short slopes, open settlement approaches, coastal plains with secured flanks, harbor defenses, compact fields, and ground where depth can be built behind a clear front.

Poor conditions include deep mud, surf during contact, steep trackless slopes, dense woods, narrow streets that destroy spear depth, ladder assaults, breach crushes, and open fronts exposed to concentrated missile fire.
