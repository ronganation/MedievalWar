This file reserves the future naval identity of Scotland's Scoto-Norse development without prematurely building ship profiles. It records why naval development belongs to the path, what kinds of vessels and campaign systems should later be explored, which Scandinavian inheritances may be selectively adapted, and which design shortcuts must be avoided.

The campaign opportunity is to consolidate, coordinate, and expand real western maritime capacity into a stronger Scottish strategic arm.

## 1. Strategic Vision

Medieval War should allow a Scotland developed through Lords of the Western Sea to become a serious western maritime power even though Scotland should not begin as a centralized standing navy. The system should grow from regional seafaring capacity, western lordships, island communities, pilots, shipwrights, crews, ports, landing forces, transport obligations, and political relationships.

```yaml
strategic_promise:
  - GiveScotlandAPlayableSeaPowerRouteAbsentFromMedievalII
  - PreserveHistoricallyGroundedWesternMaritimeFoundations
  - AdaptNorseAndScandinavianNavalIdeasIntoADevelopedScotoNorseExpression
  - ConnectSeaPowerToLandCampaignsRatherThanCreateASelfContainedNavalMinigame
  - MakeFleetPowerPhysicalPoliticalRegionalAndExpensive
```

The player fantasy is not “Scotland suddenly receives a centralized standing fleet from faction identity or a technology node.” The player fantasy is:

> **What if a Scottish ruler successfully connected Crown ambition, western lordship power, island ship traditions, skilled crews, and the campaign geography of the Hebrides into a durable maritime arm?**

## 2. Historical Foundation and Creative Permission

The Hebrides and West Highlands depended heavily on sail-and-oar vessels for communication, warfare, transport, and political power. Birlinns and West Highland galleys were clinker-built, used oars and a single square sail, and operated effectively in islands, beaches, shallow channels, and western coastal waters. Galley imagery also carried strong lordship and status associations.

```yaml
historical_foundation:
  west_highland_galley_and_birlinn_use: StrongGeneralBasis
  Norse_and_NorthAtlantic_shipbuilding_relationship: StrongGeneralBasis
  fleets_as_western_lordship_power: StrongGeneralBasis
  transport_and_communication_role: StrongGeneralBasis
  exact_hull_dimensions_for_each_game_class: Unresolved
  exact_standardized_ship_classes: NotProven
  centralized_Scottish_navy_at_opening: Rejected
```

The majority of scholarship connects the birlinn to the wider Norse and North Atlantic clinker-built tradition, while the surviving evidence remains incomplete because no medieval West Highland galley hull has been recovered. Later reconstruction relies on carvings, documentary references, poetry, comparative shipbuilding, and experimental work. Future profiles must therefore distinguish historical evidence from gameplay classification.

The Scoto-Norse naval path may borrow heavily from Scandinavian naval design where geography, technology, and cultural transmission justify it. Borrowing does not mean copying a Scandinavian roster and repainting the sails.

## 6. Reserved Future Vessel Families


A fast, lightly burdened vessel for coastal reconnaissance, channel survey, message carrying, fleet screening, and warning. Its advantages should derive from hull burden, oar mobility, pilots, and route knowledge rather than invisibility.

```yaml
  - Grapple
```

The likely foundational fighting vessel. It should be capable without becoming elite, carrying crew and embarked fighters for patrol, interception, convoy escort, and ordinary boarding combat.

### 6.3 Isles Landing Galley

```yaml
concept: IslesLandingGalley
future_purchase: OrderedMarineLandingSupplyAndReembarkation
probable_tier: SpecializedAssaultTransport
core_verbs:
  - Approach
  - Land
  - Support
  - Recover
```

The vessel-side partner for Birlinn Marines. The ship owns transport capacity, approach handling, and recovery. Birlinn Marines own human landing discipline, beachhead security, port passage, and reembarkation organization.

### 6.4 Western Sea Transport

```yaml
concept: WesternSeaTransport
future_purchase: TroopSupplyToolAndCampaignMovement
probable_tier: Logistical
core_verbs:
  - Load
  - Carry
  - Land
  - Return
```

A transport and supply vessel rather than a weak warship. This class should make Scottish maritime strategy meaningful by physically moving soldiers, arrows, food, timber, tools, wounded personnel, and political passengers.

### 6.5 Lordship Galley

```yaml
concept: LordshipGalley
future_purchase: FleetCommandSuperiorRetainersAndConcentratedBoarding
probable_tier: Superior
core_verbs:
  - Lead
  - Concentrate
  - Close
  - Overwhelm
```

A superior command and boarding vessel expressing western lordship power. Its strength should come from crew, officers, maintenance, signals, retained fighters, and fleet coordination, not ahistorical size inflation or magical auras.

### 6.6 Galley Tender

```yaml
concept: GalleyTender
future_purchase: RepairResupplyAndFleetEndurance
preferred_container: ShipOrAbstractFleetSupportPendingSystemReview
```

This should become a selectable vessel only if repairs, water, food, spare oars, rigging, and casualty recovery are represented deeply enough to create a useful player decision.

### 6.7 Portage Crew Package

```yaml
concept: PortageCrewPackage
future_purchase: MoveEligibleLightVesselsAcrossAuthorizedNarrowLandRoutes
preferred_container: CampaignMaritimeServiceNotCombatShip
```

Portage should be rare, geographic, labor-intensive, and tied to eligible vessels and known routes. It must not become unrestricted land movement for fleets. Portage should also consume campaign time, labor availability, security, hull condition, and route preparation, and may be impossible in poor weather or against active opposition.

## 9. Mission Configuration and Capacity Tradeoffs

Future ship families should not be rigid fantasy hull classes if the same historical vessel could perform more than one mission. The game may use canonical hull families with mission loadouts, crew packets, or capacity allocations where that better fits evidence and implementation.

```yaml
mission_configuration_rule:
  hull_family_and_mission_are_related_but_not_identical: true
  possible_configurations:
    - ScoutAndDispatchFit
    - WarPatrolAndBoardingFit
    - TroopTransportFit
    - LandingSupportFit
    - LordshipCommandFit
    - SupplyAndRepairFit
  capacity_tradeoffs:
    MoreEmbarkedTroops:
      costs:
        - CargoAndProvisions
        - RowingEfficiencyOrEndurance
        - DeckWorkingSpace
        - MissileAndBoardingFlexibility
    MoreMissileTroops:
      costs:
        - PassengerOrCargoCapacity
        - DeckCongestion
        - ArrowAndEquipmentStorage
    MoreSupplies:
      costs:
        - FightingComplement
        - SpeedOrHandlingUnderBurden
    MoreCommandAndRetainers:
      costs:
        - GeneralTransportEfficiency
        - PoliticalAndUpkeepBurden
  one_vessel_performs_all_missions_at_full_value: false
```

This allows future research to determine whether Scout Birlinn, War Birlinn, Landing Galley, and Transport should be separate canonical hull cards, mission configurations of related hull families, or a mixture of both.

## 11. Player-Facing Strategic Identity

The future Scoto-Norse naval path should enable a Scottish player to:

1. Observe and contest western channels.
2. Reinforce islands and coastal settlements faster than land routes permit.
3. Escort transports and protect regional trade or tribute movement.
4. Raid, counterraid, land, and withdraw without every operation becoming a permanent conquest.
5. Concentrate local sea power through lordship relationships.
6. Move western infantry forces as a combined maritime system.
7. Threaten Ireland, Mann, the Hebrides, and western approaches when geography and diplomacy permit.
8. Build a maritime Scotland that is possible through campaign development, not guaranteed by faction selection.

```yaml

  not: ScotlandReceivesAFreeStandingNationalNavy
  instead: ScotlandCanDevelopARegionalMaritimePowerIntoADurableStrategicArm
```
