## 5. Early Realm-Owned Roster

### Scottish Coastal Warship

- **File:** `Scotland_Coastal_Warship_V1_2.md`
- **Purchase:** Connected local defense, firth and coastal coverage, escort, relief, interception, and flexible boarding
- **Signature:** Muster, Watch, Screen, Close, Return
- **Persistent High value:** Lower cost, broader coverage, escort efficiency, and mission-first fleet work

### Western Sea Raider

- **File:** `Scotland_Western_Sea_Raider_V1_2.md`
- **Purchase:** Route-driven raiding, pursuit, island response, landing screens, and timely withdrawal
- **Signature:** Range, Approach, Strike, Cover, Vanish
- **Persistent High value:** Lower-cost raiding, landing support, pursuit, breakoff, and refuge use

### Scottish Bow Warship

- **File:** `Scotland_Bow_Warship_V1_2.md`
- **Purchase:** Sustained mobile bow pressure, screening, boarding support, and landing or withdrawal cover
- **Signature:** Position, Harass, Cover, Displace, Return
- **Persistent High value:** Better cadence, mobility, availability, and lower equipment burden than crossbows

### Scottish Light Bolt Warship

- **File:** `Scotland_Light_Bolt_Warship_V1_1.md`
- **Purchase:** Rare compact mechanical disruption, short approach denial, and critical-moment support
- **Signature:** Prepare, Align, Loose, Displace, Recover
- **Persistent High value:** Cheaper secondary mechanical support and broader standard-hull compatibility

## 9. High Path Ownership

- **Traditional Scottish Development** uses Crown, magnate, burgh, port, firth, east-coast, North Sea, convoy, and formal-fleet institutions.
- **Scoto-Norse and Western Sea Development** uses Hebridean, island, western magnate, Galloway, Irish Sea, Norse-derived, Raider, landing, refuge, and sail-and-oar relationships.
- Conditional access to an individual ship does not transfer canonical ownership.
- Equal unit counts are not required.

## 10. Combined-Arms Fleet Contract

### Traditional Scottish fleet
- Firth Guards provide repeatable screening and convoy protection.
- Crown Firth Warships provide scarce command response and high-value escort.
- Great Warships provide the heavy center.
- Burgh Bow, Crossbow, and Springald Warships provide three distinct missile layers.

### Western fleet
- War Birlinns, Isles Warships, Raiders, and Lordship Galleys provide screen, pursuit, combat, and command.
- Western Sea Great Warships provide the heavy expedition center.
- Isles Bow, Crossbow, and Springald Warships provide distinct missile support.
- Armed Western Traders provide bounded auxiliary support.
- Isles Scout Birlinns provide reconnaissance and dispatch.

## 12. Total War Control Ceiling

Shared commands remain Center, Screen, Flank, Reserve, Missile Line, Landing Screen, Hold, Pressure, Intercept, Board, Support Target, and Break Contact. Unit-specific controls remain bounded to each profile's five-command ceiling. The player does not manage individual sailors, rowers, archers, signals, wages, or supply crates.

## 14. Asset-Family Strategy

Every path-owned High profile requires its own appropriate hull, crew layout, provider read, signals, stations, animation, audio, UI, damage states, and Unreal asset family. Broad counterpart roles do not authorize banner swaps. Cog and Holk assets remain Britannia-owned.

## 15. Review and Evidence Records

The root `Review` folder preserves realm-level Early and legacy decisions. Path-specific candidate and portfolio reviews may remain inside path `Authorities` folders as evidence after admission, but the active controlling pair in each path remains its README and progression authority.

## 16. Named Ships

Named vessels remain separate from recruitable unit classes. Named records own individual names, service history, capture, refit, loss, character links, and Historical Emergence. They create no free hull, crew, statistics, or immunity.
