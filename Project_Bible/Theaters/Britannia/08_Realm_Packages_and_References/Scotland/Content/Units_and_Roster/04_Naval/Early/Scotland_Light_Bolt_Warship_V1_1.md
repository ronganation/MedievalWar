## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Scottish Light Bolt Warship is Scotland's Early low-volume mechanical-missile specialist: a support vessel that prepares one deliberate firing lane, disrupts an exposed crew position or boarding preparation, then displaces before enemy contact or counterfire removes the weapon team.**

Its signature is **Prepare, Align, Loose, Displace, Recover**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Scottish Light Bolt Warship
- **Era:** Early
- **Class:** Compact mechanical-missile support warship
- **Signature:** Prepare, Align, Loose, Displace, Recover
- **Best use:** Disrupt boarding preparations, exposed missile crews, steering stations, landing approaches, and short chokepoint windows
- **Strengths:** High local disruption for an Early support ship, useful approach denial, strong combined-arms timing, and meaningful threat against exposed personnel
- **Weaknesses:** Very low shot volume, demanding alignment, vulnerable weapon crew, finite bolts, poor boarding strength, limited mobility while preparing, and low value against protected or dispersed targets
- **Player instruction:** Hold fire until a valuable exposed target enters a safe lane. Fire, then displace before melee contact or counter-pressure fixes the ship in place.

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Align, Loose, Displace, Recover

### 6.1 Prepare

Ready the weapon crew, bolt, tackle, stores, safety lane, and support relationship behind a Scottish screen.

### 6.2 Align

Bring the vessel, target, weapon mount, sea state, and friendly geometry into one workable firing solution.

### 6.3 Loose

Fire one deliberate bolt against a valuable exposed personnel group, working station, boarding preparation, landing transfer, or other physically suitable target.

### 6.4 Displace

Move before enemy boarders, missile crews, weather, congestion, or a collapsing screen destroys the firing solution or traps the vessel.

### 6.5 Recover

Reset the weapon, inspect equipment, prepare another bolt if available, or withdraw for resupply and repair.

failure_condition: WastedShotUnsafeArcWeaponFailureBoardingContactOrBlockedDisplacement
