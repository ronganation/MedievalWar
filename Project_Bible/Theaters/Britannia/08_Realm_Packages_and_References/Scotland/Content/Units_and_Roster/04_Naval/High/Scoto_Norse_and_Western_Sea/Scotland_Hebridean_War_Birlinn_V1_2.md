## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Hebridean War Birlinn** is Scotland's foundational western fighting vessel. It is the ordinary, dependable war-ready birlinn used for patrol, interception, convoy escort, local sea control, routine grappling and boarding, island reinforcement, and protection of movement through Hebridean and western coastal routes.

It is neither the cheapest improvised boat nor an elite lordship flagship. Its purchase is **usable general fighting capacity carried by a regionally grounded, shallow-draft, sail-and-oar hull**. It gives western Scotland a vessel that can show up repeatedly, escort lighter craft, contest ordinary enemies, and survive routine operations without consuming the concentrated providers, retainers, command staff, and prestige required by superior warships.

## 1A. Player-Facing Purchase and Battlefield Promise

The practical reason to recruit a Hebridean War Birlinn is straightforward: **the player needs a dependable western fighting ship that can escort, intercept, hold local contact, board ordinary targets, and return without paying for a specialist or heavy warship**. It is the fleet's working combat hull, not its fastest raider, strongest line ship, largest carrier, or best scout.
