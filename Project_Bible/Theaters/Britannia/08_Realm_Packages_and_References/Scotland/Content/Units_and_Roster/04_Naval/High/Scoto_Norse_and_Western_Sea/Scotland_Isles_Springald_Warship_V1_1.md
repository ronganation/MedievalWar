The Isles Springald Warship is a distinct Scoto-Norse and Western Sea mechanical-missile vessel. The unit mounts a compact springald or equivalent validated period mechanical engine on a western sail-and-oar combat hull. The unit is not a cosmetic conversion of the Traditional Scottish Springald Warship and does not share that profile's hull, station layout, providers, recruitment pools, visuals, audio, animation, UI, or Unreal assets.

## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Isles Springald Warship** is the Western path's scarce mechanical-disruption vessel. Protected inside a connected fleet, the ship uses a compact mounted engine to threaten exposed crews, oars, steering positions, boarding concentrations, light structures, and predictable approach lanes with low-volume but consequential shots.

The player recruits it to gain **mechanical shock, approach denial, and critical local disruption without receiving Isles Bow Warship cadence, Isles Crossbow Warship selective handheld pressure, Isles Warship general combat weight, or Lordship Galley boarding command**.

## 1B. Practical Battle Purchase

The Isles Springald Warship must feel like a rare mechanical specialist rather than a slower Crossbow Warship. The player recruits it to **hold one threatened lane, prepare one consequential shot, and force an enemy vessel to alter course, expose crew, delay boarding, or accept a credible risk of critical local damage**. Much of the unit's value comes from the threat of a prepared engine, but that threat remains physical, visible, narrow, and counterable.
