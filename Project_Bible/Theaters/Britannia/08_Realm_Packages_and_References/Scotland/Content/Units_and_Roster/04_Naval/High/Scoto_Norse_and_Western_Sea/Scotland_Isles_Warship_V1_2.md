## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Isles Warship is Scotland's High-era medium Western combat vessel: stronger and steadier than the Western Sea Raider, more affordable and more common than the Western Sea Great Warship, and optimized for island-route escort, local Raider-group coordination, medium boarding, landing cover, and local rally operations.**

Its signature is **Muster, Cross, Hold, Rally, Return**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Isles Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Medium Western combat warship
- **Signature:** Muster, Cross, Hold, Rally, Return
- **Best use:** Island-route escort, medium fleet line, Raider support, landing cover, and peer-warship contact
- **Strengths:** Strong medium boarding, useful counterboarding, good island-route handling, dependable escort, local rally, and lower burden than a Great Warship
- **Weaknesses:** Less pursuit and breakoff freedom than a Raider, less heavy endurance than a Great Warship, limited missile specialization, and vulnerable when its return route closes
- **Player instruction:** Hold the western line, support nearby Raiders and transports, rally one threatened group, and withdraw before prolonged heavy contact exhausts Return Reserve.

## 5. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Cross, Hold, Rally, Return

### 5.1 Muster

Assemble the medium hull, master, pilots, sailors, rowers, fighters, weapons, signals, supplies, and valid western provider relationship.

### 5.2 Cross

Use island channels, western routes, sail, oars, tide, pilotage, and a bounded weather window while preserving Return Reserve.

### 5.3 Hold

Maintain an escort shoulder or western line, contest a peer vessel, protect a landing lane, and absorb pressure that would force Raiders or transports to withdraw.

### 5.4 Rally

Reconnect one nearby Raider group, transport element, Coastal Warship, or damaged light vessel around a visible local rally point.

### 5.5 Return

Break contact while preserving the crew, route, refuge, and enough propulsion margin to reach safety.

```yaml
success_condition: HoldAssignedWesternSectorCompleteLocalRallyOrEscortAndReturn
failure_condition: BecomeIsolatedPinnedOvercommittedOrUnableToReachRefuge
```

## 19. Matchups and Counterplay

### Favorable

- Light or damaged vessels
- Disordered Raiders
- Peer medium vessels with weaker cohesion
- Landing and escort missions with connected support

### Conditional

- Other medium warships
- Crossbow or Bow Warships before extended missile exposure
- Holks when boarding access becomes available
- Great Warships when supported and locally advantaged

### Unfavorable

- Heavy Great Warship in sustained contact
- Concentrated Crossbow or Springald fire
- Several-direction attack
- Closed refuge or withdrawal route
- Open-water pursuit after Return Reserve is depleted
