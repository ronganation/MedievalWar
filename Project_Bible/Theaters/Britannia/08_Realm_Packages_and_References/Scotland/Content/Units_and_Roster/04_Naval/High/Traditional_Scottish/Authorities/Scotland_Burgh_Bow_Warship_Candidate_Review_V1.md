## 2. Proposed Direct Unit Verdict

The **Burgh Bow Warship** should be admitted as the Traditional Scottish path's developed mobile archery-support vessel. The ship combines a port-supported Traditional hull, organized archer stations, deeper ammunition handling, convoy and formation practice, and firth or coastal fleet integration to provide sustained mobile bow pressure, landing cover, withdrawal cover, and anti-Raider support.

```yaml
one_sentence_purchase: DevelopedTraditionalMobileBowSupportForFirthConvoyLandingWithdrawalAndCounterRaiderOperations
core_battle_loop:
  - Screen
  - Loose
  - Shift
  - Cover
  - Recover
not_owned:
  - CheapBroadEarlyAvailability
  - DeliberateCrossbowPressure
  - MechanicalDisruption
  - GeneralFleetScreenAndCounterboarding
  - WesternRaiderLandingAndIslandRouteDoctrine
```

## 3. Distinct Gameplay Need

The Traditional High roster now contains a medium screen, command responder, heavy center, Crossbow specialist, and Springald specialist. The remaining missile gap is sustained mobile bow pressure integrated into formal burgh, port, firth, convoy, landing, and withdrawal operations.
