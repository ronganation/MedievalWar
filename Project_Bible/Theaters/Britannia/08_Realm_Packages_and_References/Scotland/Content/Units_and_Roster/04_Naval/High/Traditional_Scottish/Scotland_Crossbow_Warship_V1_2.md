## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Scottish Crossbow Warship is Scotland's High-era deliberate missile specialist: a supported warship that positions several handheld crossbows, braces for a useful firing cycle, pressures exposed personnel and enemy missile crews, covers friendly approaches, and withdraws before melee contact collapses its specialist role.**

Its signature is **Position, Brace, Pierce, Cover, Withdraw**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Scottish Crossbow Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Deliberate handheld-crossbow support warship
- **Signature:** Position, Brace, Pierce, Cover, Withdraw
- **Best use:** Pressure exposed missile personnel, disrupt boarding preparations, support friendly heavy contact, and protect landing or withdrawal windows
- **Strengths:** Better conditional effect against protected exposed personnel than bows, distributed fire across several stations, useful counter-missile pressure, and strong support behind a connected screen
- **Weaknesses:** Slower cadence, high equipment and crew burden, fewer bolts than arrows, restricted maneuver while firing, weak boarding, weather-sensitive equipment, and severe vulnerability when the screen collapses
- **Player instruction:** Establish a protected firing lane, use deliberate fire against exposed high-value personnel, and withdraw before melee ships close.

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Position, Brace, Pierce, Cover, Withdraw

### 6.1 Position

Establish a connected support lane with useful arcs, stable deck conditions, and a protected withdrawal route.

### 6.2 Brace

Set crew spacing, ready bolts and spanning tools, and reduce unnecessary maneuver while preserving emergency response.

### 6.3 Pierce

Apply deliberate distributed fire against exposed personnel whose disruption supports the mission.

### 6.4 Cover

Support a Scottish Great Warship, Coastal Warship, Raider approach, landing, or withdrawal while friendly geometry remains safe.

### 6.5 Withdraw

Displace or break contact before boarding threats, weather, ammunition loss, or a collapsing screen remove the specialist advantage.

```yaml
success_condition: ValuablePersonnelPressureSupportsMissionWithoutLosingScreenEscapeOrSpecialistCrew
failure_condition: WastedBoltsMaskedArcInterruptedReloadBoardingContactOrBlockedWithdrawal
```
