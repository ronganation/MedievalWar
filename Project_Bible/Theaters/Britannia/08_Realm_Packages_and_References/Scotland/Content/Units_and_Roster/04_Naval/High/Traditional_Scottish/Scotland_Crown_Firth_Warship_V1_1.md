## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

The **Crown Firth Warship** is the Traditional Scottish path's scarce rapid-response command, high-value escort, and concentrated boarding vessel. The ship carries stronger officers, signals, standards, selected fighters, and a practiced crew into firth, convoy, relief, and realm-fleet operations. It responds faster than the Great Warship, leads one smaller connected group, and commits more concentrated close-action power than a routine Firth Guard.

Its player purchase is straightforward: **recruit the Crown Firth Warship when one compact Traditional Scottish fleet group needs fast command response, high-value protection, and decisive boarding power without committing a Great Warship**.
