**The Scottish Springald Warship is Scotland's High-era developed mechanical-missile specialist: a supported vessel that emplaces one compact mounted weapon, ranges a high-value exposed target, delivers heavy local disruption, displaces before hostile close action, and recovers through developed Scottish ports and workshops.**

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Scottish Springald Warship
- **Era:** High
- **Class:** Developed mechanical-missile support warship
- **Signature:** Emplace, Range, Disrupt, Displace, Recover
- **Best use:** Disrupt dense boarding preparations, exposed missile or weapon crews, landing activity, and short approach lanes
- **Strengths:** Strong local single-shot effect, better mechanical reliability than the Early Light Bolt Warship, useful priority-target pressure, and potent combined-arms timing
- **Weaknesses:** Very low firing volume, high structural and workshop burden, narrow arcs, vulnerable operators, weak boarding, poor value against dispersed or protected targets, and severe dependence on a connected screen
- **Player instruction:** Reserve the weapon for a High or Critical target, fire from a protected lane, then relocate before enemy contact or counterfire fixes the vessel in place.

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Emplace, Range, Disrupt, Displace, Recover

### 6.1 Emplace

Take a protected support position, secure the mount, ready the crew and bolt, and preserve a displacement route.

### 6.2 Range

Align the vessel and weapon against a physically exposed, high-value target while accounting for movement, weather, rigging, and friendly geometry.

### 6.3 Disrupt

Release one heavy bolt to create a local casualty, equipment loss, interrupted preparation, or temporary station failure.

### 6.4 Displace

Move before hostile boarders, counterfire, congestion, or a collapsing screen traps the vessel.

### 6.5 Recover

Reset, inspect, reload where possible, repair local failures, or return to a qualified port or workshop.
