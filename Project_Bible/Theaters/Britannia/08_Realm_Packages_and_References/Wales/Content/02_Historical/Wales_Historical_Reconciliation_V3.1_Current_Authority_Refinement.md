## Booyah Verdict

Wales is not a post-collapse restoration campaign on 1 January 1062. Wales opens at the unstable height of a living supremacy under Gruffudd ap Llywelyn. The campaign question is whether personal conquest can become durable order before external pressure, regional variation, battlefield reversals, and unresolved continuity transform that achievement into something else.

The design remains unmistakably Medieval II in play: provinces and strategic regions, settlements and strongpoints, armies moving through terrain and routes, diplomacy, tribute, raids, sieges, field battles, agents, local elites, and campaign-season decisions. The modernization adds deeper causality rather than replacing the campaign spine. Characters, dynasties, claims, memories, pressures, recognition, succession, and political emergence explain why territorial outcomes happen and why the same opening can produce radically different Welsh futures.
