## 15. Fatal Rules

- The Historical chain is described as runtime implemented.
- Owner packet creation is treated as owner approval.
- The two required opening selections are silently chosen.
- A candidate Memory record becomes active without audience and transmission bindings.
- A Momentum definition becomes active without owner inputs.
- An Emergence pathway becomes eligible or active from later history alone.
- <Person>Gruffudd ap Cynan</Person> gains adult agency at opening.
- St Davids typed entities collapse.
- A later ruler becomes an opening claimant or successor merely from chronology.
- Unknown specialist inventory becomes zero.
- Opening defaults overwrite an advanced save.
