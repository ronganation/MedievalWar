## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales at the 1 January 1062 opening is a living realm-level polity centered on Gruffudd ap Llywelyn. Broad personal rulership exists, but administrative reach is uneven, regional identities persist, local participation is not uniform, succession is unresolved, and future fragmentation is possible rather than active. Wales is not one political mind, Gruffudd ap Llywelyn does not personally own the Realm, and House Mathrafal, House Dinefwr, and House Aberffraw are dynastic continuity structures rather than opening regional governments.

This authority defines the political constitution that later Wales Title, Office, Claim, Succession, Law, Governance, Ecclesiastical, Military, Geography, Settlement, Route, Economy, Property, Population, Supply, Historical, Personality, AI, Registry, and Opening State authorities must obey. It preserves the Medieval II campaign spine by making rulership, regional accommodation, military protection, routes, communication, settlements, diplomacy, and succession politically consequential without reducing Wales to a single centralization meter or preloading later history.
