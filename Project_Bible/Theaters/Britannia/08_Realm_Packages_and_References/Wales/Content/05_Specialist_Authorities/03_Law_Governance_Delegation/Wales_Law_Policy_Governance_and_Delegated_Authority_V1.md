## 1. Concise Verdict

Implement Wales in January 1062 as a living but unevenly integrated realm whose law and government operate through the personal rulership of <Person>Gruffudd ap Llywelyn</Person>, recognized custom, negotiated regional participation, bounded household and institutional service, witnessed decisions, local enforcement, military capacity, communication, and revocable delegation.

Wales is not a centralized state with one codified legal system, one permanent bureaucracy, one universal regional office hierarchy, one treasury, one standing army, or one quantified authority score. Broad rulership is real, but execution remains dependent on people, routes, settlements, resources, recognition, cooperation, documentation, and the ability to make commands effective in particular places.

```text
Kingship != Universal Government Capacity
```

## 3. Benchmark Adoption and Wales-Specific Limit

The structural benchmark is the developed Realm law and governance pattern used for England and Scotland: typed legal sources, law and policy records, policy lifecycles, governance operating state, mandates, delegations, councils, office substitution, protected-person governance, guarantees, review, emergency powers, handback, save treatment, migration, AI, player presentation, and validation.

- request information;

## 31. Fatal Rules

Reject promotion if:

- Any Region becomes dynasty property by implication.
- Broad rulership becomes universal administration or obedience.
- A later historical ruler becomes an opening successor.
- <Person>Gruffudd ap Cynan</Person> receives adult authority, command, claim execution, or regency.
- A guardian, custodian, protector, educator, or dynasty representative is silently converted into a regent.
- <Person>Bleiddud</Person>, the Bishop office, the St Davids institution, the St Davids place, or the occupancy record collapse.
- Consultation is treated as consent.
- Witnessing is treated as a binding vote without authority.
- Military command becomes civil government automatically.
- Emergency power lacks trigger, scope, review, sunset, and handback.
- A mandate creates resources, routes, forces, property, or Supply by declaration.
- Wales creates reciprocal English, Mercian, Dublin, Irish Sea, or ecclesiastical external state unilaterally.
- Opening defaults replay over an advanced save.
- Any false implementation flag is promoted without retained evidence.
