## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales on 1 January 1062 possesses a real but incompletely documented economy distributed across land, households, regional communities, settlements, ecclesiastical institutions, productive activities, customary rights, obligations, storage, exchange, routes, and personal or institutional custody. Gruffudd ap Llywelyn's broad rulership does not make the ruler the owner of Wales, every estate, every market, every due, every productive activity, every ecclesiastical holding, or one national treasury.

The Wales economy must support a Modern Medieval II campaign through readable settlement and regional functions, visible production and revenue dependencies, meaningful damage and recovery, and clear strategic tradeoffs. It must not collapse those systems into one faction income number, one universal wealth score, a frictionless building economy, or exact unsupported January 1062 balances.

This authority creates the Wales-specific economic architecture, record contracts, regional portfolios, owner handoffs, player summaries, AI proposal boundaries, and validation gates. It creates no exact opening property holdings, treasury balances, tax rates, production quantities, market prices, revenue receipts, trade flows, stockpiles, debts, or transfers.

```text
Place and Resource Potential
+ Valid Property, Use, and Revenue Rights
+ Population, Labor, Tools, and Expertise
+ Infrastructure, Routes, Security, and Season
+ Collection, Custody, Accounts, and Delivery
- Local Consumption, Existing Commitments, Loss, Dispute, and Damage
= Conditional Economic Capacity
```

## 2. Modern Medieval II Vision

### 2.1 Campaign spine

The economic design preserves:

- settlements and strategically important sites as the visible anchors of economic play;
- farms, livestock, fisheries, woodland, craft, markets, routes, crossings, and coastal interfaces as explainable functions;
- taxation, customary dues, markets, investment, war damage, and recovery as legible campaign consequences;
- conquest, raid, blockade, protection, access, and development as materially different choices;
- regional recruitment and army support grounded in economy, population, property, and access;
- strong Wales identity without turning every historical association into an active asset;
- low routine micromanagement and high consequence for major decisions.

## 40. Validation Rules

### 40.1 Fatal

- A realm, ruler, dynasty, title, office, region, province, settlement, church, or military force is treated as automatic owner of property.
- Unsupported exact balances, prices, yields, rates, revenues, holdings, or stock quantities are activated.
- A child independently controls property, treasury, contracts, collection, or transfers.
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save.
- A later market, castle, Marcher lordship, conquest economy, property transfer, or institutional state is preloaded into January 1062.
- A wrong entity type, duplicate ID, broken owner, or unresolved collision is activated.

### 40.2 Error

- Property, possession, custody, administration, productive control, and beneficial use are conflated.
- Revenue right, assessment, collection, receipt, and spending are conflated.
- Resource presence creates production or stock.
- Population count creates revenue.
- Market identity creates goods, price, or income.
- Treasury authority creates delivered payment.
- Occupation creates property transfer.
- Production automatically becomes Supply.
- Unknown becomes zero.

### 40.3 Warning

- An economic record lacks evidence, chronology, confidence, uncertainty, owner, or location.
- Investment lacks labor, material, access, funding, security, opportunity-cost, or recovery consideration.
- Recruitment or military acquisition omits household, population, property, grievance, or recovery consequences.
- A UI summary hides decisive dependencies or presents derived value as authority.
- Economic action depends solely on the ruler without custody, records, administrator, or continuity review.

### 40.4 Informational

- A definition exists without an active scenario binding.
- An intentional empty collection is preserved.
- A qualitative band is used because precise evidence is absent.
- A regional portfolio remains ReviewOnly.
- A proposal is deferred pending a required owner.
