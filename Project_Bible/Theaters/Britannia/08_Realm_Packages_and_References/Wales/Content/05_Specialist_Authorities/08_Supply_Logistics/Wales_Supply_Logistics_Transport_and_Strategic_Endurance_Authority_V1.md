## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales on 1 January 1062 possesses functioning local provisioning, movement, storage, animal, labor, market, household, institutional, and coastal support, but the current evidence does not justify exact opening stockpiles, transport fleets, depots, route capacities, army consumption, siege reserves, or endurance values.

Supply must make geography, distance, weather, access, cooperation, force composition, and preparation matter while preserving direct Total War army movement. Wales should be difficult to invade, traverse, provision, and hold for reasons the player can understand. Wales should not become an impassable logistics puzzle, and Welsh forces should not receive magical homeland supply.

The opening design therefore establishes a network model and Wales-specific portfolios without activating values. Mountain approaches, crossings, regional courts, settlements, refuges, coastal interfaces, St Davids, and the Eastern Frontier may become logistical dependencies only after Geography, Economy, Population, Law, Military, and Opening State owners validate their identities and state.

```text
Authorized Source
+ Collected or Acquired Stock
+ Storage and Custody
+ Valid Route and Access
+ Transport and Handlers
+ Security and Local Cooperation
+ Delivery and Receipt
- Consumption, Loss, Delay, Spoilage, and Competing Demand
= Strategic Endurance
```

## 2. Modern Medieval II Vision

### 2.1 Campaign spine

Wales logistics must preserve:

- direct and readable army movement;
- settlements as primary support anchors;
- clear campaign reach and endurance;
- meaningful winter quarters, sieges, relief operations, and defensive depth;
- visible route, crossing, pass, coastal, and local-support dependencies;
- strategic raiding and denial without convoy spam;
- a small number of meaningful logistics choices;
- decisive real-time battles as expressions of campaign preparation.

- Supply is not one unexplained army bar.
- Production is not supply until collection or acquisition succeeds.
- Territory color is not access.
- A road is not transport capacity.
- A coast is not maritime supply.
- A port is not a fleet.
- Money is not delivered provisions.
- Alliance is not allied supply.
- Occupation is not full logistical control.
- Local population is not free transport labor.
- Winter is not one universal penalty.
- Attrition is not silent random unit deletion.

## 23. Land, River, Crossing, and Mountain Logistics

Land supply favors short distances, secure settlements, validated roads or tracks, local cooperation, transport animals, and suitable weather. It suffers from difficult terrain, damaged crossings, hostility, winter exposure, devastation, long distance, and animal-heavy forces.

River or crossing support requires navigability or usability, landing or transfer points, vessels or ferries where applicable, control, labor, and seasonal conditions. Geography owns physical truth. Supply owns throughput and transfer after validation.

Mountain and upland movement must be locally legible. Steepness, track condition, weather, load type, animal suitability, guides, dispersal, and fallback options may matter. One Wales mountain bonus or penalty is prohibited.

## 26. Garrisons, Sieges, and Blockades

Garrisons consume supply and compete with field forces and local populations. Larger garrisons may improve security while reducing reserves.

Besieging forces require provisions, fodder, military stores, route security, camp support, replacement access, and relief-force screening. Besieged settlements depend on stored supply, water, internal capacity, population burden, blockade state, and relief access. Siege owns progression; Supply owns sustainment pressure.

Blockade may reduce maritime transfer, trade, communication, garrison endurance, and recovery. Blockade requires Military and Geography state and does not itself capture a port or settlement.

## 32. Law, Governance, and Delegated Authority

Law and Policy own requisition, obligations, exemptions, emergency measures, passage, compensation, limits, appeals, promulgation, enforcement, and sunset. Governance owns administrative capacity, records, officers, communication, custody coordination, and continuity.

A supply officer, commander, ruler, governor, bishop, household head, or local elite may exercise only explicitly granted scope. Authority to request does not equal authority to seize. Authority to allocate does not equal authority to move or consume.

## 43. Validation Rules

### 43.1 Fatal

- Unsupported opening stock, source, route capacity, transport, demand, or endurance is activated.
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save.
- A wrong entity type, duplicate ID, unresolved owner, or ambiguous mapping is activated.
- Later ports, castles, Marcher networks, campaigns, depots, or conquests are backdated into January 1062.
- A child independently commands or authorizes supply.
- Supply mutates forces, property, population, law, geography, diplomacy, or historical truth outside its ownership.

### 43.2 Error

- Source, stockpile, storage, route, transport, access, consumer, and endurance are conflated.
- Production automatically creates stock.
- Territory control automatically creates access.
- Money automatically creates delivered supply.
- A road creates carts or handlers.
- A port creates ships or lift.
- Alliance creates supply commitment.
- Occupation unlocks all provincial sources.
- Unknown becomes zero.
- Attrition pressure directly deletes units without Military resolution.

### 43.3 Warning

- A record lacks owner, location, evidence, chronology, confidence, uncertainty, or state version.
- Endurance hides its limiting category or vulnerable path.
- Requisition omits lawful authority, local burden, compensation, damage, or review.
- Winter handling uses a geography-blind modifier.
- Maritime supply omits vessels, crews, loading, landing, weather, security, or receipt.
- The player is asked to manage routine transport units individually.

### 43.4 Informational

- A definition exists without an active opening binding.
- Intentional empty lists are preserved.
- Qualitative bands are used because precise evidence is absent.
- A candidate route, source, or site remains ReviewOnly.
- A proposal is deferred pending required owners.
