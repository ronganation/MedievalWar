## 6. Substantive Improvement Gaps

### 6.1 Current shared-diplomacy authority chain

The V1 cites useful integration authorities but does not identify the completed shared diplomacy foundation, shared subordination framework, and allied-contingent external-service framework as its controlling reusable lifecycle authorities. V1.1 should add them without deleting the older references.

### 6.2 Diplomatic principal constitution

V1 contains representatives and mandate records but lacks one consolidated Wales diplomatic-principal definition that distinguishes Realm, ruler, rulership institution, recognized government, regional authority, titleholder, officeholder, council, dynasty, household, ecclesiastical office, ecclesiastical institution, local community, protected person, military command, and intermediary.

### 6.3 Consultation and collective participation

V1 should be expanded with a Wales collective-participation contract separating invited, attended, heard, supported, witnessed, consented, ratified, and performed. Regional and institutional disagreement must remain separate from rebellion.

### 6.4 Agreement continuity and amendment

The agreement record needs stronger continuity rules for death, incapacity, capture, succession, deposition, office loss, government change, amendment, partial replacement, suspension, renewal, withdrawal, and archive lineage.

### 6.5 Obligation, inspection, and acceptance

V1 includes agreement performance but needs a complete obligation record separating promise, owner reservation, instruction, execution, delivery, receipt, inspection, partial acceptance, acceptance, rejection, cure, waiver, and unperformed remainder.

### 6.6 Breach and remedy depth

The breach record should add responding principal, excuse, external interference, cure window, restitution, replacement, security, partial release, mediation, arbitration, appeal, and unresolved disposition without making an allegation automatic proof.

### 6.7 Subordination and retained-government lifecycle

V1 distinguishes tribute from vassalage but lacks the full shared subordination family: homage, holding-specific service, tribute-only, protection, clientage, submission, marcher arrangement, retained government, regional compact, personal dependence, retained powers, autonomy, succession continuity, resistance, separation, restoration, handback, and integration.

### 6.8 External-owner transaction protocol

V1 has atomic processing language but needs an explicit transaction record containing proposed Wales changes, required external and material-owner changes, expected versions, receipts, unresolved locks, commit order, rollback state, and all-or-nothing dimensions.

### 6.9 External-owner lock semantics

The England, Ireland, Dublin, Scotland, Norman, Marcher, and external Church portfolios need consistent `External`, `ExternalOrUnresolved`, and `ExternalLocked` semantics. External lock must never mean refusal, absence, consent, or Wales-owned reverse state.

### 6.10 Financial diplomacy breadth

V1 emphasizes tribute and payment. V1.1 should cover subsidy, gift, loan, credit, guarantee, ransom, compensation, restitution, reparation, extraordinary aid, toll or customs agreement, and provisioning purchase while retaining material-owner locks.

### 6.11 Person-arrangement breadth

The current person arrangement should explicitly add fosterage, prisoner exchange, release, safe return, refuge, hospitality, and treatment, education, religion, contact, review, and return conditions. A person must never be treated as property.

### 6.12 Source-to-successor coverage proof

V1.1 should include a complete source-to-authority coverage matrix, direct V1-to-V1.1 preservation ledger, and literal completeness check.

### 6.13 Phased implementation handoff

V1 identifies a smallest safe inventory gate but does not provide the same complete, owner-gated phased implementation sequence now used by England and Scotland. V1.1 should define static definitions, Registry and Opening State planning, shadow-mode transactions, and real-payload and Unreal promotion with dependencies, acceptance criteria, evidence, rollback, and owner gates.

### 6.14 Formatting and submission hygiene

The retrieved V1 contains escaped underscores and hyphens, unfenced structured records, inline inequality blocks, and a compressed stable footer. V1.1 should preserve the substantive content while normalizing all structured records into fenced YAML, inequalities into fenced text, and the footer into the current stable form.
