## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales on 1 January 1062 is a living realm-level polity with external pressures, contacts, kinship interfaces, ecclesiastical channels, frontier concerns, trade and passage interests, and potential military relationships. The available authority does not establish an opening war, alliance, treaty, tribute arrangement, coalition commitment, passage grant, hostage exchange, maritime agreement, or mature Norman and Marcher diplomatic order.

Diplomacy must preserve the readable Medieval II loop of envoys, alliances, trade, access, war, truce, peace, threats, and territorial settlement while replacing opaque relationship scores and automatic compliance with typed agreements, directional interests, real authority, uncertain knowledge, enforceable commitments, and visible consequences.

England is a principal external pressure and negotiation context. Norman pressure is prospective rather than an accomplished opening system. Dublin, Ireland, the Irish Sea, Scotland, ecclesiastical actors, and other realms may create conditional channels or opportunities without automatic support, sanctuary, passage, recognition, trade, or military contribution.

```text
Known External Situation
+ Valid Diplomatic Objective
+ Authorized Representative
+ Counterparty Recognition and Authority
+ Feasible Terms, Access, Resources, and Enforcement
+ Explicit Acceptance and Ratification
= Bounded Agreement or Owner-Routed Action
```

## 2. Modern Medieval II Vision

## 35. Validation Rules

### 35.1 Fatal

- Unsupported opening war, treaty, alliance, truce, passage, tribute, hostage, coalition, or settlement is activated.
- An envoy or child binds Wales without valid authority.
- Wales mutates external realm-private state.
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save.
- Later Norman conquest, Marcher order, post-1063 fragmentation, or later alliance is backdated.
- A wrong entity type, duplicate ID, unresolved authority, or ambiguous counterparty is activated.

### 35.2 Error

- Kinship, marriage, friendship, recognition, alliance, commitment, contribution, and shared command are conflated.
- War, objective, victory, occupation, and settlement are conflated.
- Passage creates route capacity or Supply.
- Trade agreement creates goods or delivery.
- Tribute demand creates payment.
- Claim automatically creates war.
- Ceasefire, truce, peace, and reconciliation are conflated.
- Unknown becomes neutral.

### 35.3 Warning

- A proposal lacks objective, authority, counterparty, terms, expiry, feasibility, or knowledge provenance.
- An agreement lacks ratification, performance, breach, termination, or review rules.
- Coalition planning omits passage, Supply, transport, command, arrival, or withdrawal terms.
- Peace omits occupation, prisoners, hostages, payment, territory, implementation, or verification where relevant.
- AI uses hidden foreign intent or exact reverse-private state.

### 35.4 Informational

- A definition exists without an active opening binding.
- An intentional empty list is preserved.
- External pressure is represented without a fabricated agreement.
- A proposal is deferred pending required owners.
