## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales on 1 January 1062 possesses strong Welsh cultural and linguistic continuity, persistent regional identities, Christian institutions, household and community memory, and a living realm-level political identity under Gruffudd ap Llywelyn. These conditions do not create one homogeneous culture block, one national opinion, one universal language proficiency, automatic loyalty, automatic resistance, or permanent separation from neighboring peoples.

Culture must explain how communities interpret rulership, law, military service, taxation, institutions, diplomacy, settlement, language, memory, and change. It must not become an invisible bonus table. Welsh identity should remain strategically consequential because people, places, institutions, language practices, precedents, and regional histories carry continuity through conquest, accommodation, succession, migration, and recovery.

This authority defines Wales-specific culture, language, identity, community, custom, transmission, accommodation, and continuity contracts. It creates no exact opening population proportions, language map, dialect boundaries, loyalty values, identity assignments, cultural hostility, conversion state, assimilation schedule, named community roster, or runtime binding.

```text
People and Communities
+ Language Use
+ Place and Regional Memory
+ Custom and Institutions
+ Household and Intergenerational Transmission
+ Shared Experience and Political Interpretation
- Displacement, Suppression, Fragmentation, and Disrupted Transmission
= Social Continuity and Change
```

## 14. Welsh Language Boundary

Welsh language continuity is a core Wales identity interface, but this authority does not invent exact dialect maps, percentages, literacy rates, or universal proficiency. Welsh may support household life, regional society, political communication, customary practice, worship, oral tradition, naming, and identity where the relevant owners validate use.

Latin may have written, liturgical, scholarly, documentary, and diplomatic roles through institutions and trained people. English, Norse-related, Irish, Norman French, or other language contacts may arise through frontier, maritime, court, ecclesiastical, mercantile, military, migration, or diplomatic interaction only where evidence supports them.

```text
Welsh Continuity != Welsh Monolingualism
Latin Use != Latin-Speaking Population
English Contact != English Settlement
Norse Contact != Norse Community
Norman Contact != French-Language Administration
Interpreter Access != Personal Proficiency
```

## 25. Migration, Displacement, and Diaspora Interfaces

Population owns movement and quantities. Culture consumes origin, destination, community, household, language, institution, and memory state. Migrants and displaced people do not lose identity automatically, and host communities do not assimilate newcomers instantly.

```text
Migration != Assimilation
Displacement != Cultural Extinction
Return != Full Restoration
Resettlement != Integration
Diaspora Connection != Foreign Political Control
```

## 26. Law, Custom, and Governance Handoff

Customary practice may shape expectations, legitimacy, dispute, service, inheritance, assembly, patronage, and local participation. Law owns enactment, jurisdiction, promulgation, procedure, enforcement, exemption, and appeal. Governance owns administration and operating capacity.

Culture may explain why an action is accepted, negotiated, misunderstood, resisted, or reinterpreted. It cannot create legal authority or compliance.

## 27. Military, Recruitment, and Occupation Handoff

Culture may influence military identity, recruitment access, communication, cohesion interfaces, local guidance, reputation, occupation reception, and Memory. Military capability still requires forces, institutions, equipment, training, command, readiness, Supply, and lawful authority.

No Welsh cultural tag grants ambush, mountain, morale, loyalty, discipline, recruitment, or resistance bonuses from nothing. Occupation does not generate automatic resistance. Organized resistance requires actors, grievance, capacity, opportunity, leadership, and owner-routed emergence.

## 28. Economy, Property, and Social Continuity

Livelihood, property, labor, markets, migration, patronage, institutions, taxation, requisition, and recovery may strengthen or disrupt continuity. Economy and Property own those states. Culture records meaning, participation, expectation, transmission, and response.

A market, estate, craft, pastoral practice, or maritime livelihood may support identity without becoming a fixed ethnic occupation.

## 29. Diplomacy and External Relations Handoff

Shared language, religion, kinship, custom, or opposition may affect contact and interpretation but does not create alliance, recognition, passage, trade, support, or trust. Translators, clerics, merchants, dynasts, and frontier intermediaries may create channels when proper owners authorize them.

External actors retain reverse-private cultural and diplomatic state.

## 30. Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence

Historical Memory owns audience-specific recollection, precedent, transmission, reinterpretation, suppression, and erosion. Culture provides communities, languages, institutions, households, and practices through which memory may move.

Momentum may express strengthening continuity, regional revival, institutional recovery, accommodation, language pressure, displacement, or cultural politics. Emergence may develop new public identities, institutions, or political movements only when prerequisites are met. No later Welsh nationalism or predetermined decline is backdated.

## 39. Validation Rules

### 39.1 Fatal

- Unsupported opening culture, language, community, proportion, hostility, loyalty, or assimilation value is activated.
- Culture is used as race or a deterministic behavior rule.
- A child receives adult identity, education, language, marriage, or representation authority.
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save.
- Later Marcher society, Norman settlement, nationalism, language replacement, or cultural decline is backdated.
- A wrong entity type, duplicate ID, unsupported label, or ambiguous mapping is activated.

### 39.2 Error

- Culture, language, religion, region, realm, dynasty, and loyalty are conflated.
- Regional identity creates independent government.
- Shared language creates alliance or trust.
- Conquest creates assimilation.
- Conversion creates language replacement.
- Personal proficiency is inferred from community language.
- One observer label becomes authoritative self-identity.
- Unknown becomes homogeneous.

### 39.3 Warning

- A record lacks evidence, chronology, confidence, observer, owner, or uncertainty.
- Culture is reduced to modifiers without causal institutions or practices.
- Presentation uses stereotype, color alone, or unsupported symbols.
- A community is promoted into a formal faction or council without authority.
- Cultural change uses a universal per-turn percentage.
- Wales-level identity erases regional plurality.

### 39.4 Informational

- A definition exists without an active opening binding.
- Intentional empty state is preserved.
- Qualitative continuity is used because precise proportions are unavailable.
- A language contact remains ReviewOnly.
- An AI action is deferred pending an owner.

## 43. Prohibited Shortcuts

Do not:

- invent exact cultural, linguistic, literacy, bilingual, community, or identity proportions;
- treat Welsh identity as racial, biologically fixed, universally loyal, or uniformly hostile;
- treat Gwynedd, Powys, Deheubarth, or Arwystli as dynasty-owned cultures;
- assign automatic cultural authority to Gruffudd ap Llywelyn or later rulers;
- give Gruffudd ap Cynan adult cultural, educational, marriage, or diplomatic agency;
- merge Bleiddud, bishop office, Episcopal See, St Davids place, and Welsh cultural continuity;
- assume Welsh monolingualism, universal Latin literacy, or established foreign-language communities;
- backdate Norman settlement, mature Marcher society, later nationalism, or inevitable language decline;
- grant unit, morale, ambush, mountain, loyalty, or resistance bonuses from culture alone;
- reduce community response to one culture penalty;
- force every person into one culture and one language label;
- treat assimilation as passive per-turn progress;
- claim localization, accessibility, importer, compile, runtime, save, migration, replay, or performance validation without evidence.
