## 1. Concise Verdict

Wales on 1 January 1062 possesses functioning human communication, local knowledge, household and court reporting, ecclesiastical and diplomatic channels, route-based warning, oral transmission, and practical awareness of regional and external conditions. The evidence does not justify an omniscient Realm view, one national intelligence service, exact opening messenger routes, spy networks, beacon systems, archives, reporting chains, foreign informants, military scouts, or complete knowledge assignments.

Information must travel through people, places, routes, languages, institutions, records, observation, authority, and time. Knowledge must remain observer-specific, dated, sourced, confidence-bearing, and incomplete. Intelligence is an evaluated conclusion from evidence, not hidden access to authoritative truth.

The design preserves the readable Medieval II campaign map, fog of war, scouts, diplomats, armies, settlements, and visible threats while adding provenance, delay, contradiction, deception, communication failure, and owner-routed decisions without creating message-level micromanagement.

```text
Observation or Report
+ Identified Source and Provenance
+ Valid Communication Path
+ Delivery, Receipt, and Interpretation
+ Corroboration, Confidence, and Time
- Delay, Distortion, Bias, Secrecy, Interception, and Deception
= Actor-Specific Knowledge or Intelligence Assessment
```

- Wales-specific information, observation, report, message, communication, warning, intelligence, secrecy, and knowledge-state definitions.

## 25. Economy, Supply, Population, and Culture Handoffs

## 26. Historical Evidence and Runtime Knowledge

Historical Evidence supports authoring truth. Runtime knowledge belongs to campaign actors. A fact known to the project is not automatically known to Wales, the ruler, an army, an institution, AI, or the player.

Historical Memory is audience-specific recollection and interpretation, not automatically current intelligence. Momentum and Emergence may consume validated knowledge conditions but cannot create observations retroactively.

## 33. Validation Rules

### 33.1 Fatal

- Unsupported opening knowledge, spy network, message, source, warning network, archive, or secret is activated.
- AI or player reads hidden external or owner-private authoritative state.
- Historical truth is copied directly into runtime knowledge.
- A child independently directs intelligence or secrecy policy.
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save.
- Later outcomes are backdated as opening knowledge.

### 33.2 Error

- Fact, evidence, observation, report, message, belief, knowledge, and assessment are conflated.
- Sent means delivered or received.
- Relationship means information access.
- Route means successful communication.
- Confidence means accuracy.
- Rumor means false.
- Unknown means false or zero.

### 33.3 Warning

- Information lacks source, date, confidence, observer, subject, or provenance.
- Intelligence omits alternatives, gaps, or contradictions.
- Warning is presented as certainty.
- Secrecy uses magical invisibility.
- Player presentation hides information age or confidence.

### 33.4 Informational

- A definition exists without opening binding.
- Intentional empty state is preserved.
- A question remains unanswered pending collection.
- An assessment uses a qualitative confidence band.
