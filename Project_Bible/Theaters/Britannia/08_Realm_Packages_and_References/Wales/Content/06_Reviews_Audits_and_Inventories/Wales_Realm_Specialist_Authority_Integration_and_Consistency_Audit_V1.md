## 1. Concise Verdict

The Wales Realm package now has a broad, mutually compatible specialist-authority layer covering political order, titles and succession, law and governance, ecclesiastical content, military capability, population, economy, Supply, strategic geography, diplomacy, culture, and information. At the design-contract level, the authorities consistently preserve Wales as one living Realm-level polity on 1 January 1062 under Gruffudd ap Llywelyn, with broad personal rulership, uneven regional integration, persistent regional identities, English-facing pressure, prospective rather than mature Norman and Marcher pressure, and vulnerable continuity without scripted collapse.

The specialist layer is not ready for Opening State activation or Unreal implementation. The principal unresolved work is not another broad specialist authority. It is an integrated source-and-binding inventory, canonical ID audit, owner decisions, exact Opening State binding review, static package validation, manifest creation, and freeze-readiness review.

No reviewed authority authorizes exact opening armies, settlements, routes, properties, treasuries, population quantities, stocks, diplomatic agreements, culture distributions, knowledge assignments, or runtime payloads. Intentional empty collections and explicit unknowns remain valid safeguards and must not be treated as omissions to be filled automatically.

```text
Broad Specialist Coverage
+ Consistent January 1062 Boundary
+ Explicit Owner Separation
+ Shared Three-Track Temporal Contract
- Exact Binding Evidence
- Registry and Owner Receipts
- Implementation Validation
= Static-Checked Integration Candidate, Not Active State
```
