## Evidence Outcomes

### Eligible for Candidate Review

The direction has:

- canonical endpoints;
- a valid external basis or direct contact basis;
- chronology compatible with January 1062;
- meaningful directed strategic use;
- sufficient provenance for candidate-manifest review.

Eligibility does not authorize initialization.

### Deferred

The direction may become relevant, but source identity, location, participation, direction, contact, basis, context, visibility, or strategic use remains unresolved.

### Omitted

Current evidence does not support an opening edge candidate.

### Quarantined

The direction depends on an invalid entity, legacy conflation, duplicate identity, wrong chronology, or prohibited inference.

# 13. Validation Rules

## Fatal

- an unregistered or deprecated person ID enters the candidate set;
- a dynasty, region, office, institution, realm, or Historical Memory record becomes a person endpoint;
- later alliance or rivalry creates an opening candidate;
- an underage person receives adult political dimensions;
- a mutual relationship is created from one candidate;
- a candidate contains a relationship strength;
- unknown becomes neutral.

## Error

- candidate lacks an external basis;
- basis lacks provenance;
- source or target participation is invalid;
- direction has no strategic use;
- reverse direction is not reviewed independently;
- BasisOnly candidate contains dimensions;
- office occupancy creates a personal relationship automatically;
- dynasty membership creates political alignment.

## Warning

- participant location remains unresolved;
- candidate visibility policy is too broad;
- genealogy basis remains unversioned;
- restricted participant lacks final importer mode;
- a future-famous person receives deferred edges without opening evidence.

## Informational

- a participating node has no candidate edges;
- a candidate remains BasisOnly;
- a historically plausible relationship is omitted;
- no mutable dimensions initialize.

These may be correct results.
