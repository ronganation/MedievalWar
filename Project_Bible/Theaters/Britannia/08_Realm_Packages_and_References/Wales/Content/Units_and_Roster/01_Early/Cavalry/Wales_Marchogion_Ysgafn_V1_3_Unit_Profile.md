## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Marchogion Ysgafn are Wales's broad early light-cavalry field screen: locally sourced riders on serviceable light horses who move ahead of and around the host, discover immediate threats, keep enemy skirmishers away from vulnerable Welsh formations, contest open flank space, and convert a broken enemy into controlled pursuit without pretending to be noble shock cavalry.**

Their signature is **See, Screen, Scatter, Pursue**.

Their defining purchase is **mounted battlefield coverage at attainable cost**. They buy observation, warning, passage control, harassment, and pursuit, not elite combat superiority. Their finite battle resource is **screen integrity**, meaning enough connected riders, horses, leaders, signals, spacing, and withdrawal room remain to observe the flank, threaten light enemies, and disengage before heavy opposition fixes them.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Marchogion Ysgafn
- **Support name:** Welsh Light Horsemen
- **Class:** Early core light cavalry
- **Signature:** See, Screen, Scatter, Pursue
- **Battle promise:** Keep the army informed and its exposed movement protected, drive off vulnerable skirmishers, then pursue only after heavier Welsh arms have broken the enemy.
- **Best use:** Outer flanks, deployment screens, archer shoulders, counter-skirmisher lanes, withdrawal routes, pursuit corridors, and open spaces between the main army and dangerous enemy movement.
- **Strong against:** Isolated foot skirmishers, exposed light archers, messengers or scouts where represented, routing infantry, fragmented light horse, and unsupported baggage or artillery crews where scenario rules permit.
- **Conditional against:** Peer light cavalry when numbers, freshness, leaders, terrain, and withdrawal space favor the Welsh riders.
- **Weak against:** Formed spears, prepared bows or crossbows, medium and heavy cavalry, prolonged melee, confined ground, deep pursuit, and any target supported by a fresh reserve.
- **Campaign bargain:** Wales gains mounted coverage by drawing together actual riders, serviceable local horses, light weapons, shields, leaders, remount and recovery support, and limited service obligations that cannot be replaced by a stable building alone.
- **Player instruction:** Keep them moving around the battle, not through its center. Observe first, screen the vulnerable wing, scatter only exposed enemies, and preserve enough order to withdraw or pursue at the correct moment.
- **Flavor:** **The first riders to find the danger should not be the last riders trapped by it.**

## 5. Wales Cavalry Collision Firewall

### Mounted Guides

Mounted Guides should generate route knowledge, order transmission, deployment information, and pathfinding support with low combat power. Marchogion Ysgafn are the actual battlefield cavalry screen. They can fight vulnerable light enemies, but they do not inherit campaign-wide guide knowledge merely by existing.

### Mounted Raiders

Mounted Raiders own deliberate strategic raiding, supply disruption, cattle seizure, opportunistic attack, and escape. Marchogion Ysgafn own army-facing screening, immediate flank observation, counter-skirmishing, withdrawal protection, and battlefield pursuit. If future campaign mechanics cannot distinguish these purchases, Mounted Raiders should remain conditional rather than forcing duplication.

### Powys Border Horsemen

Powys Border Horsemen own frontier patrol, route security, counter-raid experience, interception, and delaying action in a regionally bounded service network. Marchogion Ysgafn are broader, less specialized, more replaceable, and less capable of delaying a serious mounted threat.

### Marchogion Uchelwyr

Marchogion Uchelwyr own noble reserve, controlled flank charge, command support, better equipment, and mounted-dismounted flexibility. Marchogion Ysgafn should not match their charge, protection, morale, political standing, or foot-combat value. The light riders create the information and open space that lets noble cavalry choose a favorable commitment.

### Teulu Marchogion

Teulu Marchogion own princely protection, elite mobile reserve, decisive counterstroke, command continuity, and household cohesion. Spending them on routine scouting is a campaign failure. Marchogion Ysgafn preserve the household by absorbing ordinary screen and pursuit labor.

### Scotland March Wardens comparator

March Wardens are a later frontier-service operational-cavalry concept. Marchogion Ysgafn must remain earlier, broader, lighter, and less institutionally specialized. Comparator design can sharpen coverage and counterplay, but no Scottish institution, source, value, or availability transfers into Wales.

## 11. Battle Loop

### Ride Out

The light horse move ahead of or outside the main host with a bounded sector, reporting route, and withdrawal plan. They should not be scattered across the entire map without cost.

### Observe

They discover what their riders can physically see, hear, track, or receive through reports. Observation quality depends on line of sight, terrain, weather, pace, leaders, local knowledge where actually supplied, and enemy concealment. It does not reveal hidden information automatically.

### Screen

They occupy movement space between danger and vulnerable Welsh troops. Screening provides warning, denies easy access to enemy scouts, and makes hostile light troops spend time or reveal direction. It is not a missile-immunity aura for the army.

### Scatter

Against exposed skirmishers or fragmented light horse, the unit makes a bounded mounted approach intended to drive the target away, disrupt firing, or force protection. Scatter ends when formed support appears.

### Refuse

When heavy cavalry, formed spears, or prepared missile troops threaten, the correct behavior is to turn away, use space, and preserve the screen. Refusal is part of skill, not cowardice.

### Pursue

Pursuit begins only after the enemy has genuinely lost order or route support. The purpose is to prevent rally, capture stragglers where systems support it, and protect friendly reorganization. It must end before fresh enemy troops cut off the return.

### Reassemble

Survivors rally, report, recover horses, restore equipment, and prepare to screen again at reduced or normal capability.

## 20. Horse Loss, Capture, and Recovery

Rider and horse casualties are separate losses. A surviving rider without a suitable horse cannot immediately restore mounted capability. A surviving horse without an available compatible rider, tack, care, and trust is not a complete replacement.

Recovery requires surviving people, treatment, sound horses, rider-horse pairing, tack repair, weapon and shield replacement, leaders, screen and recall practice, fodder, water, shelter, routes, service settlement, and release from competing transport or messenger needs.

Capture may produce ransom, local obligation, property, horse-recovery, and family consequences through proper owner systems. This profile creates none of those runtime systems.

## 21. Combined-Arms Integration

### Saethwyr and Gwyr y Bwa

Marchogion Ysgafn screen vulnerable archer shoulders, warn of cavalry or skirmisher approach, threaten exposed enemy missiles, and help cover withdrawal. They do not make archers immune to flanking or remove the need for spearmen.

### Helwyr

Helwyr conceal, harass, and use difficult ground. Light horse can watch open approaches, force enemy skirmishers to seek support, and pursue after an ambush causes disorder. Dense woods may favor Helwyr while denying the riders useful cohesion.

### Welsh Local and Northern Hill Spearmen

Spearmen provide safe rally shoulders and deny heavier cavalry direct access. Light horse extend awareness beyond the spear front and lure or channel threats toward prepared infantry without receiving the heavy charge themselves.

### Meirionnydd Spearmen

Meirionnydd Spearmen control the serious spear decision. Marchogion Ysgafn report and shape the approach, then leave the reception work to the regional specialists.

### Uchelwyr Retainers and Dismounted Teulu

The light horse identify which crisis is real, screen the movement of reserves, and pursue after elite foot restores the line. They do not inherit noble or household command authority.

### Uchelwyr and Teulu cavalry

Marchogion Ysgafn protect elite mounted strength from routine labor. They find targets, contest enemy screens, and preserve open lanes. Noble and household cavalry perform the higher-value intervention.

## 22. Terrain and Weather

Good conditions include open upland edges, rolling ground with visible routes, firm valleys, broad settlement approaches, dry tracks, and flank space connected to the Welsh main body.

Difficult conditions include dense woods, marsh, steep broken slopes without paths, stone walls, narrow streets, deep mud, ice, severe snow, smoke, darkness, and any terrain that prevents mutual visibility or safe turning.

Broken-ground mobility must be relative and physical. Locally known paths, guides, horse conditioning, pace, and formation width may reduce penalties where supported. Welsh identity alone grants no flat terrain bonus.

## 23. Siege and Settlement Use

Good uses include outer observation, road security, pursuit outside a breached position, messenger interception where represented, withdrawal cover, and threatening undefended rear approaches.

Poor uses include ladders, wall assault, dense gate melee, packed streets, breach fighting, stationary objective defense, and charging prepared troops through narrow entrances. Dismounting is not a baseline card promise and would require a separate validated assignment or implementation decision.

Marchogion Ysgafn are admitted only at the review-candidate level as Wales's broad early light-cavalry screen.
