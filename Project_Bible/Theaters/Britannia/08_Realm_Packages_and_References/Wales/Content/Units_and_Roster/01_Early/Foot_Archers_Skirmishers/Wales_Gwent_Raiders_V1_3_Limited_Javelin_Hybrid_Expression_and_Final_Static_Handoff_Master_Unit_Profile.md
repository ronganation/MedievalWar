## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Gwent Raiders are Wales's regional exploitation infantry: light, fast, impetuous raiding swordsmen who use concealment, secondary routes, and enemy disorder to overrun exposed missile troops, camps, baggage, livestock, and vulnerable settlement objectives before withdrawing from formed opposition.**

Their signature is **Infiltrate, Overrun, Seize, Withdraw**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gwent Raiders
- **Support name:** Gwent Raiding Swordsmen
- **Class:** Regional light raider infantry
- **Signature:** Infiltrate, Overrun, Seize, Withdraw
- **Promise:** Bypass the strongest enemy frontage, overwhelm exposed troops and vulnerable objectives, choose how to exploit access, and escape before disciplined opposition closes.
- **Best use:** Woods, border settlements, secondary streets, rear approaches, camps, baggage areas, livestock enclosures, missile flanks, and post-break pursuit.
- **Strong against:** Isolated archers, undeployed crossbowmen, crews, disordered levy infantry, routing troops, unprotected supply positions, and settlement defenders attacked from an exposed rear.
- **Conditional against:** Light infantry and ordinary troops when surprise, local superiority, route access, command control, and withdrawal space favor the raiders.
- **Weak against:** Formed spears, shielded swordsmen, heavy infantry, cavalry charges, disciplined reserves, concentrated missiles, prepared ambush defenses, and prolonged frontal combat.
- **Campaign bargain:** Regional access and raiding value consume finite Gwent manpower, captains, weapons, local knowledge, political cooperation, service time, and future mercenary availability.
- **Player instruction:** Do not ask them to win the line. Give them a route, an exposed target, an exploitation order, and a way home.
- **Flavor:** **The opening belongs to the army; what disappears through it belongs to Gwent.**
