## 1B. Closeout Meaning

“Complete” means the fixture answered the questions it was created to test:

- whether a mature unit profile could be distilled into a compact image;
- whether the single-figure portrait direction was stronger than a crowded scene;
- whether the retained source survived useful scale reduction;
- whether obvious mechanical and neighboring-identity drift required immediate regeneration;
- whether the workflow could stop honestly without claiming production or runtime completion.

It does not mean the art is final, implemented, historically certified, or permanently locked.

## 7. Historical-Material Review Still Required

Before production promotion, inspect the retained candidate against selected period- and region-relevant references for:

- helmet type and plausible prevalence;
- clothing, cloak, straps, and protection layers;
- visible bow form;
- arrowhead and fletching;
- plausible individual equipment combination;
- whether the comparatively equipped focal figure remains appropriate for a lower-readiness local bow family.

The review should correct material problems without forcing the unit into a caricature of poverty, disorder, or incompetence.
