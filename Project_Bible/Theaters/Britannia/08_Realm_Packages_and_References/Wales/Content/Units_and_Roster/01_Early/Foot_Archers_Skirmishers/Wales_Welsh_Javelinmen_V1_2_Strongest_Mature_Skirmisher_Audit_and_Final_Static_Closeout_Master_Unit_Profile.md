## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Welsh Javelinmen are Wales's close-range disruption infantry: lightly protected shield-bearing skirmishers who screen an approach, commit a small number of heavy throws into a vulnerable target, and collapse back through a prepared passage before enemy pressure converts their disruption mission into an unfavorable melee.**

Their signature is **Screen, Cast, Collapse**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Welsh Javelinmen
- **Class:** Close-range javelin skirmish infantry
- **Signature:** Screen, Cast, Collapse
- **Promise:** Disrupt one exposed or committed enemy body with a short, dangerous javelin cast, then clear the line before the target can answer effectively.
- **Best use:** In front of or beside Welsh spearmen, on broken approaches, against turning or compressed enemies, near settlement lanes, and on flanks with a prepared withdrawal route.
- **Strong against:** Exposed light infantry, unshielded or turned troops, compressed ordinary infantry, skirmishers caught at close range, and mounted troops moving through constrained approaches.
- **Conditional against:** Armored infantry, medium cavalry, and shielded troops when angle, range, density, support, and escape favor the cast.
- **Weak against:** Archers and crossbows at distance, cavalry catching them in open order, fresh formed infantry after ammunition is spent, broad open ground, blocked passages, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Low-ammunition tactical disruption from finite throwers, leaders, shields, javelin stocks, route support, and replacement capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Do not trade missiles at range or leave them fighting after the cast. Screen the approach, throw into a real opening, and collapse behind the formation that will exploit the disorder.
- **Flavor:** **Close enough to break the step, disciplined enough to leave before the answer.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Welsh Javelinmen provide the roster with a compact burst of close missile pressure. They are not miniature archers and not a cheap melee line. Their value comes from reaching a dangerous casting distance, concentrating scarce ammunition into the right target, and leaving a disrupted enemy for spearmen, archers, mobile infantry, or household troops to exploit.

A successful cast may kill, wound, damage shields, disrupt horses, force a turn, slow an advance, or open a short Answer window for Welsh Local Spearmen. The formation succeeds when the relationship changes in Wales's favor, even if the javelinmen do not remain to finish the fight.

## 6. Why the Card Exists

Welsh bows provide sustained ranged pressure. Welsh Local Spearmen protect ordinary frontage. Upland Spearmen own mobile spear screening and reform. Welsh Javelinmen own a different action: enter a dangerous short range, spend limited ammunition to distort a target's immediate combat condition, and clear the frontage for another Welsh body.

## 20. Failure Stories

**The early cast:** the player throws at long range into prepared shields. Ammunition disappears before the target becomes vulnerable.

**The blocked shoulder:** Welsh Local Spearmen never open the passage. Javelinmen collide with friendly files while the enemy closes.

**The heroic melee:** a successful volley tempts the player to leave the unit fighting armored infantry. The disruption tool is destroyed after fulfilling its real mission.

**The cavalry trap:** the cast succeeds, but mounted troops close the exit and catch the formation during collapse.

**The false second life:** ammunition is spent, yet the unit is treated as fresh spear infantry because some members carry melee weapons.
