## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gwyr y Cantref
- **Support name:** Cantref Fighting Men
- **Class:** Dependable territorial campaign infantry
- **Signature:** Muster, Hold, Advance
- **Promise:** Supply Wales with an ordinary but credible spear-and-shield line capable of protecting archers, holding useful ground, and campaigning more reliably than emergency musters.
- **Best use:** Main frontage beside other territorial bodies, settlement approaches, road and crossing defense, archer protection, moderate advances, and relief of worn common spearmen.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exposed skirmishers, light cavalry from a prepared front, disordered ordinary troops, and attacks that must cross a controlled spear approach.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry, peer territorial infantry, and better-protected enemies when supported by missiles, ground, leadership, and local numerical depth.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, open flanks, heavy cavalry after formation loss, fresh armored infantry in prolonged melee, deep encirclement, and repeated distant service without territorial recovery.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better reliability and endurance than levy infantry from finite territorial fighters, leaders, equipment, provisions, obligations, and return capacity.
- **Instruction:** Use them as the Welsh army's dependable connective tissue. Hold long enough for archers and specialists to work, advance when the enemy is disordered, and do not mistake reliability for elite grinding power.
- **Flavor:** **The cantref answers, the line holds, and Wales moves forward.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Gwyr y Cantref represent men with repeated territorial service assembled into a more dependable field body than a hurried local levy. They are not uniform professionals. Their strength comes from recognized local organization, familiar leadership, accumulated collective practice, and enough practical equipment to perform ordinary campaign work.

They provide the roster's center of gravity. Cheaper units can add depth, while regional veterans, noble retainers, archers, javelinmen, and household troops solve specialized problems. Gwyr y Cantref connect those elements by keeping frontage intact, receiving missile troops, and advancing once Welsh pressure creates a manageable opening.

## 21. Failure Stories

**The dependable line mistaken for an elite one:** Gwyr y Cantref hold ordinary pressure well, so the player leaves them grinding against armored infantry until territorial capacity is destroyed.

**The blocked archer passage:** an advance begins before Saethwyr or Gwyr y Bwa clear the shoulder. Spears and archers congest, the firing line collapses, and neither formation receives the enemy cleanly.

**The endless campaign:** the formation survives several battles but receives no return, equipment replacement, or territorial settlement. Headcount remains while readiness and future pool capacity deteriorate.

**The duplicate successor:** a nominally improved princely or Arfon unit reproduces the same battlefield and campaign purchase, creating roster bloat rather than progression.
