## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Meirionnydd Spearmen
- **Class:** Proven regional mobile spear infantry
- **Signature:** Hold, Climb, Counterthrust
- **Promise:** Control a difficult approach, keep useful spear order while crossing manageable broken ground, and answer a disrupted enemy with one disciplined forward thrust.
- **Best use:** Slopes, narrow ridges, lanes, wooded margins, crossings, archer flanks, and short threatened sectors.
- **Strong against:** Careless light or medium cavalry, disrupted ordinary infantry, lightly protected attackers in poor ground, and local breakthroughs that can still be contained.
- **Conditional against:** Heavy cavalry from the front, trained spear infantry, and armored melee troops when ground, facing, support, and exhaustion favor the Welsh formation.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, open flanks, deep encirclement, fresh heavy infantry in prolonged combat, repeated counterthrusts, and badly congested terrain.
- **Campaign bargain:** A powerful but finite regional spear resource dependent on eligible Meirionnydd-associated fighters, recognized leaders, long spears, shields, route knowledge, provisions, and political access.
- **Instruction:** Win the approach, not the whole battlefield. Counterthrust briefly, halt before pursuit destroys the spear front, and let Welsh missiles or mobile forces exploit the disorder.
- **Flavor:** **The hill gives the opening. Order gives the answer.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Meirionnydd Spearmen are not a fantasy mountain elite. They are a regional formation whose fighters, leaders, equipment, and collective experience make them more capable than a broad local muster at preserving spear order through difficult movement and turning an enemy's bad approach into a short Welsh counterattack.

They reward a player who reads ground and timing. Their strongest moment comes after the enemy has climbed, compressed, turned, or struck a prepared spear front without enough support. The spearmen may then move forward together, recover lost ground, or relieve another Welsh body. If they continue beyond leaders, working depth, and missile support, their long spears become awkward and the formation loses its advantage.
