## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Uchelwyr Retainers are Wales's flexible upper-status infantry: noble and lordly fighting men who support a threatened sector, commit against a valuable target, and adapt between spear-line reinforcement, flank reserve, command protection, and controlled dismounted assault.**

Their signature is **Support, Commit, Adapt**.

They are not the prince's teulu and not armored sergeants with Welsh names. Their value comes from broader noble access, personal fighting experience, better equipment than territorial infantry, and enough leadership to answer changing battlefield needs without matching the cohesion, loyalty, protection, or political centrality of a true household formation.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Uchelwyr Retainers
- **Support name:** Noble Retainers
- **Class:** Flexible upper-status Welsh infantry
- **Signature:** Support, Commit, Adapt
- **Promise:** Keep a capable noble reserve available, reinforce the point that matters, and change mission before prolonged combat wastes scarce retainers.
- **Best use:** Threatened flanks, command positions, archer shoulders, settlement lanes, broken-ground reserves, and short countercommitments.
- **Strong against:** Disordered ordinary infantry, exposed light troops, weakened spear bodies, enemy skirmishers caught near the line, and local breakthroughs that require a capable reserve.
- **Conditional against:** Peer medium infantry, cavalry from a prepared supported spear relationship, and armored targets when the retainers possess the right equipment, angle, ground, and allied pressure.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, fresh heavy infantry in prolonged melee, unsupported cavalry contact, deep encirclement, repeated crisis commitments, and destruction far from replacement relationships.
- **Campaign bargain:** Flexible quality from finite nobles, retainers, recognized lordly relationships, equipment, leaders, service obligations, and political trust.
- **Instruction:** Do not spend them as frontage. Support first, commit where the decision matters, then adapt or withdraw before quality becomes attrition.
- **Flavor:** **Where the line bends, the lord's men choose whether it breaks.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Uchelwyr Retainers represent the broader Welsh noble military layer beneath or beside the prince's immediate household. Their members may carry spears or lances adapted for foot service, shields, sidearms, and variable protection according to actual source capacity. Their status does not create uniform equipment or automatic battlefield excellence.

The player should treat them as a flexible reserve rather than a permanent main line. They may steady Local Spearmen, exploit disorder created by Saethwyr or javelinmen, guard a local lord, or occupy ground that cheaper troops cannot safely hold. Their flexibility has limits. Every mission change requires leaders, space, time, and a valid receiving relationship.

## 5. Why the Card Exists

Territorial infantry provide width, local legitimacy, and campaign endurance. The teulu provides the ruler's scarce personal household elite. Uchelwyr Retainers fill the middle: broader lordly and noble military capacity that can reinforce, protect, and countercommit without possessing the teulu's personal bond or the territorial body's mass.

## 22. Failure Stories

**The reserve spent as frontage:** the player places Uchelwyr Retainers in the main line at deployment. They survive the first contact but lose the flexibility needed to answer the actual crisis.

**The lord's isolated men:** retainers commit to protect a local lord but become separated from the Welsh line. Their equipment and status do not provide a safe exit.

**The endless adaptation:** repeated mission changes consume time, formation order, and leader control. The unit appears flexible on paper but reaches each task late and disordered.

**The political victory:** the battle is won, but casualties among a contested noble contingent damage future service and local trust.
