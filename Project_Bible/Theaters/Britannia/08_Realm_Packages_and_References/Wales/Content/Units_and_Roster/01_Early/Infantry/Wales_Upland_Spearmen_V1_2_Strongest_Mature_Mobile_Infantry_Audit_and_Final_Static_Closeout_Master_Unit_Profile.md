## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Upland Spearmen
- **Welsh-facing candidate:** Gwaywfilwyr yr Ucheldir, pending review
- **Class:** Mobile broken-ground spear screen
- **Signature:** Traverse, Screen, Reform
- **Promise:** Move through difficult approaches with less formation loss than ordinary spear infantry, protect mobile Welsh missile troops, and reform before the enemy converts contact into a prolonged fight.
- **Best use:** Slopes, woodland margins, ridges, narrow routes, flank screens, ambush shoulders, withdrawal lanes, and constrained cavalry approaches.
- **Strong against:** Exposed skirmishers, light cavalry in constrained ground, disordered light infantry, isolated scouts, and threats attempting to reach Welsh archers through broken terrain.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry, peer light spear infantry, and ordinary infantry when terrain, facing, missile support, and exit space favor the Welsh screen.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, fresh armored infantry, prolonged frontal combat, open flat ground, encirclement, congestion, and cavalry catching the unit while dispersed or traversing.
- **Campaign bargain:** Flexible movement and screening from finite upland fighters, leaders, compatible light equipment, route knowledge, provisions, and return support.
- **Instruction:** Do not ask them to win the main battle line. Use them to make the battlefield usable for Welsh archers, spears, ambushes, and withdrawals.
- **Flavor:** **Cross the rough ground, deny the approach, reform before the trap closes.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Upland Spearmen are not merely ordinary spearmen with a hill bonus. Their battlefield value comes from moving, compressing, expanding, screening, and reforming through terrain that disrupts heavier or less practiced formations. They protect the spaces between the Welsh army's stronger purchases.

They are most effective when the player gives them a route, a protected mission, and somewhere to reform. If ordered to stand indefinitely against armored infantry or to chase cavalry into open ground, their light equipment and moderate contact order become liabilities.
