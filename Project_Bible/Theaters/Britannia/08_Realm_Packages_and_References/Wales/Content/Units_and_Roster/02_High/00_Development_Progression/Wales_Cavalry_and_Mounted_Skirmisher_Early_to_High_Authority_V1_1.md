## 1. Controlling Portfolio Verdict

Wales has three Early cavalry foundations, two mounted formations stored in the High cavalry folder, and one High mounted-skirmisher specialist. They do not form a straight six-step power ladder.

```yaml
portfolio_model: PersistentEarlyMountedFoundationPlusConditionalMiddleAndHighSpecialization
calendar_only_unlocks: prohibited
automatic_upgrade: false
automatic_retirement: false
one_to_one_replacement_ladder: false
player_development_order_flexible: true
eventual_broadening: possible_not_guaranteed
rider_horse_and_provider_duplication: prohibited
```

Marchogion Ysgafn, Powys Border Horsemen, and Marchogion Uchelwyr retain permanent campaign value. Marchogion Mathrafal, Teulu Marchogion, and Teulu Skirmishers add narrower capabilities when the required dynastic, household, horse, equipment, leadership, and mission conditions exist.

Marchogion Ysgafn remain the economical army screen even after heavier or more specialized cavalry emerge. Better organization improves coverage, handoff, reporting, and recovery without turning the family into noble cavalry.
