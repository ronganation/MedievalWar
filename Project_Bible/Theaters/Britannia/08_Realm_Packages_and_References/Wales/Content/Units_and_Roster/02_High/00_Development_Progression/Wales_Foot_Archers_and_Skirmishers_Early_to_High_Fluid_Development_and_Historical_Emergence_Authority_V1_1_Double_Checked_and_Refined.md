## 1. Controlling Portfolio Verdict

Wales begins with four distinct missile and exploitation foundations and develops three mature High expressions. The progression is not a four-to-three replacement ladder. Early cards persist because inexpensive local shooting, ordinary field archery, close-javelin disruption, and sword-led raiding remain useful after longbow, regional-veteran, and princely-reserve capabilities emerge.

```yaml
portfolio_model: PersistentEarlyFoundationPlusConditionalHighSpecialization
```

The classic presentation makes Saethwyr the recognizable Welsh special unit. Medieval War should preserve that feel through battlefield importance, visual identity, longbow material culture, and a complete campaign bargain, not through universal superiority or instant availability.
