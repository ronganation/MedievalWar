## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Gwyr y Faner
- **Support name:** Men of the Banner
- **English conceptual label:** Ordered Welsh Spearmen
- **Class:** High Era coordinated spear-line infantry
- **Signature:** Dress, Receive, Advance, Restore
- **Battle promise:** Hold a more dependable Welsh front than local or ordinary territorial infantry, protect missile troops, receive cavalry under valid conditions, and advance through uneven ground while retaining enough order to halt and rebuild the line.
- **Best use:** Connected main line, archer screen, controlled slope advance, relief of worn local troops, settlement approaches, and battles where Wales must contest open ground without abandoning mobility.
- **Strong against:** Light and medium cavalry in prepared reception, ordinary infantry that has been disrupted by Welsh missiles, fragmented attacks, and enemies forced to fight across uneven approaches.
- **Conditional against:** Peer disciplined infantry and heavier cavalry when frontage, depth, support, footing, leadership, and relief are favorable.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, fresh armored assault infantry, deep heavy-cavalry pressure on poor footing, encirclement, broad unsupported frontage, exhausted advance, and prolonged grinding melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Dependability requires officers, standards, compatible spear-and-shield equipment, repeated collective practice, recognized cross-regional authority, service terms, supply, relief, and replacement cadres.
- **Player instruction:** Use them to make Wales's line coherent, not immobile. Dress the front, receive the attack, advance only through a real opening, and restore before temporary order becomes permanent exhaustion.
- **Flavor:** **Not the heaviest line in Britain, but the Welsh line that finally knows how to stay connected while it moves.**
