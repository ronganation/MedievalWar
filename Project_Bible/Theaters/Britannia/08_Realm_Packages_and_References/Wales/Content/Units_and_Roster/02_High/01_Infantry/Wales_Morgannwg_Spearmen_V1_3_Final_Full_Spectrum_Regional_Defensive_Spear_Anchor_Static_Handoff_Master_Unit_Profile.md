## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Morgannwg Spearmen are Wales's superior regional defensive spear anchor: experienced, better-protected spear-and-shield infantry who deliberately establish a critical point, contain cavalry or advancing infantry, and hold the enemy in a costly relationship long enough for Welsh missiles, axemen, raiders, and cavalry to decide the battle elsewhere.**

Their signature is **Plant, Contain, Enable, Withdraw**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Morgannwg Spearmen
- **Support name:** Armored Spearmen of Morgannwg
- **Class:** Regional armored defensive spear infantry
- **Signature:** Plant, Contain, Enable, Withdraw
- **Promise:** Establish a hard defensive point, deny cavalry passage, contain committed infantry, and preserve the fixed relationship until allied Welsh forces exploit it.
- **Best use:** Roads, bridges, shallow slopes, exposed archer flanks, settlement approaches, narrow objectives, retreat corridors, and defensive ambush anchors.
- **Strong against:** Cavalry on readable approaches, advancing infantry, ordinary spear pressure, and enemies forced through constrained ground.
- **Conditional against:** Heavy infantry when formation, protection, support, terrain, fatigue, and relief remain favorable.
- **Weak against:** Crossbows, sustained archery, artillery, several-direction pressure, prolonged isolation, forced movement, broken footing, and attacks after formation disruption.
- **Campaign bargain:** Experienced Morgannwg spearmen, officers, compatible long spears, shields, helmets, padding, mail, repair, supply, political cooperation, and regional recovery capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Choose the point before contact. Hold the enemy there. Let the Welsh army win around the formation. Leave before containment becomes annihilation.
- **Flavor:** **The enemy reaches the Morgannwg Spearmen, but the enemy does not pass them cheaply.**
