**Rhyfelwyr are Wales's dedicated heavy assault infantry: selected experienced warriors equipped for a short, destructive two-handed-axe commitment against shielded or armored infantry already fixed, disrupted, compressed, or exposed by Welsh spears, missiles, terrain, or breach geometry.**

Their signature is **Cover, Commit, Cleave, Break, Withdraw**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Rhyfelwyr
- **Support name:** Welsh Heavy Axemen
- **Class:** Heavy armor-piercing assault infantry
- **Signature:** Cover, Commit, Cleave, Break, Withdraw
- **Promise:** Deliver a powerful but time-limited two-handed-axe assault against shielded or armored infantry that Welsh combined arms have made reachable.
- **Best use:** Behind a spear line, beside a prepared passage, inside woods or settlement approaches, near a breach, and against formations already fixed or disordered.
- **Strong against:** Shielded medium infantry, armored infantry under pressure, exhausted heavy foot, defended gateways, barricade defenders, and compressed troops unable to exploit the Rhyfelwyr's open defensive profile.
- **Conditional against:** Fresh heavy infantry when approach, charge, support, frontage, fatigue, and withdrawal remain favorable.
- **Weak against:** Archers, crossbows, cavalry, prepared spears, open-ground envelopment, prolonged unsupported melee, repeated charges, and blocked withdrawal.
- **Campaign bargain:** Experienced infantry, specialist axe skill, substantial protection, suitable axes, leaders, collective assault practice, repair, treatment, replacement, and established military-center capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Protect the approach, spend them on a valuable infantry relationship, then extract or support them before success turns into exhaustion and encirclement.
- **Flavor:** **The Rhyfelwyr break the enemy line; the Welsh army must still bring them safely to it.**
