## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Saethwyr
- **Support name:** Welsh Longbowmen
- **Class:** Domestic longbow infantry
- **Signature:** Plant, Loose, Press, Displace
- **Promise:** Establish powerful Welsh longbow pressure from a useful lane, threaten exposed protection and mounted approaches, then move before the enemy closes or the arrow economy collapses.
- **Best use:** Protected slopes, long readable lanes, settlement approaches, flanks of spear formations, prepared defensive positions, and targets fixed by Welsh infantry.
- **Strong against:** Exposed infantry, missile troops, approaching light or medium cavalry, compressed formations, and shielded troops attacked from weak angles.
- **Conditional against:** Armored infantry and heavy cavalry when range, angle, sustained concentration, target exposure, arrow type, and support justify the expenditure.
- **Weak against:** Direct cavalry access, determined heavy infantry contact, superior crossbow positions, blocked relocation, poor visibility, depleted arrows, and repeated forced redeployment.
- **Campaign bargain:** Longbow-capable shooters, suitable bows, strings, arrows, officers, stake materials where used, carriage, repair, source relationships, and replacement cadres.
- **Player instruction:** Prepare only where the position matters, spend arrows on decisions rather than silhouettes, and displace before the proud firing line becomes a grave.
- **Flavor:** **The bow is Welsh; the victory still depends on ground, arrows, protection, and judgment.**
