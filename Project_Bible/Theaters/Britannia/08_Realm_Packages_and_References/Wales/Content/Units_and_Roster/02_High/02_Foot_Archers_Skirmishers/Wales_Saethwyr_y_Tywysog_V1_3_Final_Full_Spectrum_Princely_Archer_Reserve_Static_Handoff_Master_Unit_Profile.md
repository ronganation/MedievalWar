## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Saethwyr y Tywysog are Wales's protected princely archer reserve: selected archers assembled near the command position with stronger officers, ammunition discipline, protection, and supporting relationships, allowing them to preserve useful fire under pressure and relocate as a controlled reserve without becoming stationary English-style longbowmen.**

Their signature is **Guard, Loose, Endure, Relocate**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Saethwyr y Tywysog
- **Support name:** The Prince's Archers
- **Class:** Protected princely missile reserve
- **Signature:** Guard, Loose, Endure, Relocate
- **Promise:** Maintain controlled Welsh missile pressure from an important protected position longer than ordinary field archers, then relocate before the reserve becomes trapped or destroyed.
- **Best use:** Near the command position, behind Gwyr y Faner, on a protected slope or settlement approach, against valuable targets forced into readable movement, and as a reserve that reinforces a weakening missile sector.
- **Strong against:** Exposed medium infantry, mounted troops under constrained or repeated approach pressure, missile troops losing a duel, and high-value targets fixed by Welsh formations.
- **Conditional against:** Armored troops and heavy cavalry when range, angle, arrow supply, target state, protection, and sustained concentration are favorable.
- **Weak against:** Direct cavalry access, formed heavy infantry, concentrated crossbow fire, encirclement, obstructed lanes, depleted arrows, command displacement, and being forced into prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Selected archers, recognized officers, strong bows where available, arrows and carriage, helmets and padded protection, selective mail, sidearms, princely service relationships, command-position duties, and replacement capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Use the unit as a protected reserve, not as a range trophy. Put useful arrows on the most important target, preserve the lane, and move before protection becomes a trap.
- **Flavor:** **The prince's bow line does not shoot furthest by right; it remains useful when lesser formations have already lost control.**
