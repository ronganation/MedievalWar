### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Marchogion Mathrafal are a conditional Powys dynastic-regional cavalry formation: selected mounted retainers and qualified riders concentrated through a valid Mathrafal or owner-approved successor relationship, equipped for mobile axe-and-shield melee intervention, and committed against targets already exposed, delayed, disordered, or fixed by the wider Welsh army.**

Their signature is **Gather, Strike, Rally, Return**.

Their defining purchase is **a more combat-capable Powys mounted intervention without converting regional cavalry into generic knights**. They sit above Powys Border Horsemen in bounded close commitment, below Marchogion Uchelwyr in broad noble flexibility and mounted-dismounted options, and far below Teulu Marchogion in princely command priority and household cohesion.

Their finite battle resource is **dynastic cohesion**, meaning enough riders, horses, leaders, household trust, regional support, equipment, formation order, and an open rally route remain to gather around a recognizable provider, strike one valuable target, and return without destroying the political and military source that created the unit.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Marchogion Mathrafal
- **Support name:** Mathrafal Horsemen
- **Class:** Middle-to-High dynastic regional light-to-medium melee cavalry
- **Signature:** Gather, Strike, Rally, Return
- **Battle promise:** Concentrate a recognizable Powys mounted body, strike an exposed target with mobile axe-and-shield retainers, then reform and return before spears, heavier cavalry, or prolonged melee consume the dynastic source.
- **Best use:** Counter-raider intervention, attacking exposed archers, joining the flank of infantry already fixed by Welsh spears, exploiting delay created by Powys Border Horsemen, and short pursuit after a genuine enemy break.
- **Strong against:** Unsupported missile troops, fragmented light cavalry, disordered raiders, exhausted light infantry, routing troops, and engaged infantry whose flank is physically open.
- **Conditional against:** Peer light-to-medium cavalry when formation order, leadership, horse condition, support, and an exit route favor Mathrafal.
- **Weak against:** Prepared spears, dense medium cavalry, heavy knights, concentrated bows or crossbows, frontal infantry contact, deep pursuit, and any fight that traps the horsemen in sustained melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Wales gains a more forceful Powys mounted reserve by concentrating real riders, horses, tack, axes, shields, helmets, leaders, dynastic goodwill, service time, and regional support that cannot simultaneously remain in border patrol, local defense, messenger, noble, transport, or household assignments.
- **Player instruction:** Let the border riders find and delay the danger. Gather Mathrafal only when the target is worth direct contact. Strike from a useful angle, rally immediately after impact, and return before success becomes attrition.
- **Flavor:** **The riders of Mathrafal do not hold every road; they gather where one hard stroke will matter.**

### 5. Wales Cavalry Collision Firewall

#### Marchogion Ysgafn

Marchogion Ysgafn own affordable army screening, immediate flank observation, exposed-skirmisher displacement, withdrawal cover, and general pursuit. Marchogion Mathrafal do not replace that coverage. Using the dynastic formation to perform routine screen labor wastes scarce direct-contact capacity.

#### Powys Border Horsemen

Powys Border Horsemen own regional warning, patrol coverage, route interception, counter-raid response, delay, handoff, and restoration of frontier control. Marchogion Mathrafal receive the confirmed threat or opening and may execute the more dangerous close intervention. The border horsemen find and shape; Mathrafal gather and strike.

#### Marchogion Uchelwyr

Marchogion Uchelwyr are the main quality noble cavalry family. They own broader provider access, controlled spear or lance commitment, command support, and mounted-dismounted deployment choice. Marchogion Mathrafal are narrower, more Powys- and provider-dependent, more closely tied to axe-and-shield mounted melee, and normally remain mounted.

#### Teulu Marchogion

Teulu Marchogion own princely protection, household cohesion, command continuity, countercharge, and decisive elite intervention. Marchogion Mathrafal do not inherit princely proximity, bodyguard duty, household morale, or the same recovery priority.

#### Teulu Skirmishers candidate

Teulu Skirmishers, if retained, own a small household javelin screen that guards the Teulu intervention lane. Marchogion Mathrafal own regional melee commitment. One casts and draws; the other closes and strikes.

#### Uchelwyr Retainers and Dismounted Teulu

Mathrafal riders may overlap socially with some upper-status or retainer sources, but no person, horse, leader, weapon, or household capacity can populate several cards at once. The unit creates no free dismounted infantry card.

### 11. Operational and Battle Loop

#### Receive

Marchogion Mathrafal normally receive a confirmed threat or opportunity from Powys Border Horsemen, Marchogion Ysgafn, infantry, scouts, commanders, or visible battle conditions. They do not own broad reconnaissance.

#### Gather

Distributed riders and retainers form around a recognized leader and physical rally location. Gathering consumes time and may reduce the provider's other assignments.

#### Approach

The formation rides through protected or tolerable space toward a target already exposed, fixed, delayed, or disordered. An open path is not automatically a safe approach.

#### Strike

The horsemen deliver a bounded mounted intervention. Axe-and-shield identity becomes decisive after entry into close contact, not as magical ranged or impact lethality.

#### Break Contact

The formation must leave before prepared spears, heavy cavalry, or dense reinforcements trap it. Remaining in melee rapidly spends dynastic cohesion and horse condition.

#### Rally

Files reconnect, leaders recount riders, horses and equipment are assessed, and the player decides whether pursuit, emergency recommitment, or withdrawal is justified.

#### Return

Survivors return to the army's reserve, Powys regional duty, or their provider relationship. Return is part of success rather than an administrative afterthought.

### 25. Combined-Arms Integration

#### Powys Border Horsemen

The border horsemen watch, confirm, intercept, and delay. Marchogion Mathrafal gather against the confirmed target, strike if the window is valuable, and then return. The two units cannot share the same riders, horses, leaders, or route assignment.

#### Marchogion Ysgafn

Light horse maintain the broader screen, reveal support, and protect the Mathrafal approach from routine skirmishers. Mathrafal do not absorb general pursuit or observation duties.

#### Gwyr y Faner and Morgannwg Spearmen

Spears fix enemy infantry, protect rally shoulders, and receive heavier cavalry. Mathrafal attack only through a clear lane and should never charge across the spear frontage.

#### Saethwyr and Saethwyr Gwent

Archers slow, turn, compress, or disorder a target. Saethwyr Gwent may make one enemy movement fail, opening a short Mathrafal strike window. Fire and cavalry lanes must remain physically separate.

#### Rhyfelwyr

Rhyfelwyr break a fixed formation on foot. Mathrafal threaten evacuation, exposed missiles, or the flank. They should not collide into the same congested target without spacing.

#### Marchogion Uchelwyr

Uchelwyr provide the broader quality reserve and may accept a more consequential charge or dismounted deployment. Mathrafal are a narrower Powys dynastic intervention. Both should not be committed to trivial targets.

#### Teulu Marchogion

Mathrafal may remove a secondary threat so the Teulu remain available for command protection and decisive intervention. Mathrafal do not replace the household reserve.

### 26. Terrain and Weather

Good mounted conditions include firm rolling ground, broad valley edges, open settlement approaches, dry roads, accessible field boundaries, and enough room to strike and rally. Useful concealment may exist near woodland edges, but dense woods should break formation and visibility.

### 27. Siege and Settlement Use

Strong uses include relief outside walls, attacking exposed missile troops, controlling an outer road, counter-raiding, pursuit beyond a broken gate position, and reinforcing open settlement approaches.

Poor uses include ladders, wall assault, gate crush, breach fighting, dense streets, packed objective defense, and charging through barricades or prepared spear frontage.

Dismounting is not a baseline separate-card promise. An owner-authorized scenario may require riders to fight on foot after losing mounts or entering a constrained objective, but that emergency state does not create Uchelwyr Retainers or Dismounted Teulu.

### 32. Horse Loss, Wounds, Capture, and Recovery

Rider and horse losses remain separate. A surviving rider without a suitable horse cannot immediately restore mounted service. A surviving horse without a compatible rider, tack, care, and provider authority is not a complete replacement.

Captured riders, leaders, horses, equipment, or banners may create ransom, inheritance, intelligence, property, prestige, and regional-capacity consequences through their proper owners. This profile creates none of those runtime events.
