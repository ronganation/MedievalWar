## 1. Foundation Verdict

Germania is established at the current static Campaign Theater foundation scope for the central European interaction space relevant to January 1, 1062. The Theater provides organization, strategic context, presentation, bounded AI relevance, cross-Realm pressure categories, and research routing. It does not itself own the German kingship, the Roman imperial dignity, the Holy Roman political order, any Realm, title, office, person, territory, army, church, city, route, economy, relationship, or outcome.

The Theater's defining opening problem is not a monolithic empire at peace or a predetermined civil war. It is a royal minority embedded within distributed royal, ducal, comital, palatine, margravial, episcopal, abbatial, urban, dynastic, household, route, property, and frontier powers whose relationships must be represented separately.

Germania should present a dense constitutional and strategic landscape without reducing every attested lordship to a playable faction. Royal government, major regional powers, ecclesiastical lordship, frontier command, local urban and comital powers, dynastic households, property networks, river corridors, and cross-Theater interfaces all matter.
