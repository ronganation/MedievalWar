## 1. Purpose

This inventory creates the first controlled assertion and identity-collision surface for Germania. It records what is supported strongly enough for orientation, what is only a candidate, what is contradicted or chronologically unsafe, what must remain unresolved, which terms collide across Theater, political order, Realm, title, office, institution, person, dynasty, household, territory, settlement, and event layers, and which owner must resolve each issue.

This inventory is not a canonical Registry. It does not assign final IDs, adopt factions, create people, publish an Opening State, settle exact borders, instantiate local entities, execute Historical Emergence, or modify the Unreal project.


### 15.1 Merge criteria

Two labels may resolve to one entity only when source and owner review establish identity continuity at the same layer. Similar spelling, geographic overlap, later succession, title inheritance, office holding, dynastic marriage, or a mod's faction label is insufficient.

### 15.2 Split criteria

One familiar label must split into several records when the label conflates layers, contemporary entities, separate offices or institutions, distinct Upper and Lower structures, different current holders, external-owner state, or later historical forms.

### 15.3 Alias criteria

Localization, Latin, German, English, and conventional historiographical names may become aliases only after the owning identity is known. An alias must not change chronology, title status, or entity class.
