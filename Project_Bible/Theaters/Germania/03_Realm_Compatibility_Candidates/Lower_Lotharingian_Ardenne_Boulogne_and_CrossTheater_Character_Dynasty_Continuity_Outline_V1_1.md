## 1. Purpose

This outline preserves the cross-Realm and cross-Theater person, dynasty, household, marriage, succession, claim, service, exile, restoration, and historical-emergence questions connecting Lower Lotharingia, Upper Lotharingia, Boulogne, Flanders, the royal-imperial political order, and Italia.

It prevents later Realm development from:

- creating duplicate profiles for the same historical person;
- treating a person's future fame as opening Realm ownership;
- converting marriage or kinship directly into Realm transfer;
- guaranteeing historical succession, restoration, crusading participation, survival, or rule;
- stripping a person of prior obligations and consequences when political context changes;
- creating people later because an event requires a famous participant.

This is a routing and continuity outline. It does not create character authorities, exact Opening State instances, households, relationships, claims, titles, offices, contracts, military commands, or runtime transitions.
