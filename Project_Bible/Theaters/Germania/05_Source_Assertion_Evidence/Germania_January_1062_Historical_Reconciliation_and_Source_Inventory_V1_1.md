## 1. Purpose

This authority reconciles what can safely be said about Germania on January 1, 1062, what belongs to the near-term historical runway through the Kaiserswerth coup, what is known only at orientation scope, which claims remain disputed or incomplete, which later developments must not be projected backward, and which persons, institutions, titles, regions, routes, properties, and candidate powers require deeper source work before Realm selection or Opening State authoring.

The authority is historical and architectural. It does not publish the final Opening State. It does not create canonical persons, political orders, titles, offices, institutions, Realms, local entities, wars, events, relationships, forces, recruitment pools, sites, routes, or payloads.

```text
Historical Reconciliation != Opening State
```

## 11. Papal Schism and Italia Interface

The January 1062 setting includes a papal schism between <Person>Alexander II</Person> and <Person>Honorius II</Person>, born <Person>Peter Cadalus</Person>. <External>Encyclopaedia Britannica</External> states that <Person>Honorius II</Person> was chosen in October 1061 with support from Lombard and German bishops and <Person>Agnes of Poitou</Person>, entered Rome by force in April 1062, and that the loss of <Person>Agnes of Poitou</Person>'s regency changed the political context. citeturn88search172

### 11.1 Anti-back-projection rule

The 1061 to 1064 schism is not the later Investiture Controversy. It is a current ecclesiastical and political-order pressure involving papal claims, reform, royal and episcopal participation, Italia actors, and recognition. Later conflict may emerge through later world state.
