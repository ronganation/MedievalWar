## 1. Purpose

This file records six user-supplied Warfare pages as visual, equipment, costume, and roster-design research leads. It classifies each reference by stated date, subject, likely owner route, and evidentiary use. The illustrations and accompanying summaries are secondary comparative leads rather than direct opening evidence. They may inform questions about social status, equipment access, institutional service, frontier warfare, and later development, but they do not by themselves admit any unit, costume, weapon, organization, institution, or faction into the January 1062 opening.

```text
Illustration != Primary Evidence
Book Plate != Opening Unit
BroadDateRange != Exact1062Applicability
BohemianReference != RoyalSalianRoster
TeutonicKnight != 1062OpeningInstitution
PrussianOrWendishReference != GermanRoyalHousehold
VisualSimilarity != SharedRecruitmentPool
ResearchLead != Authority
```

## 3. Bohemian Knight Plate

The page identifies the figure as a thirteenth-century Bohemian knight, specifically <Person>Přemysl Ottokar II</Person> from a seal, and discusses later thirteenth-century equipment. It also states that Bohemia first became a kingdom in 1086. The plate is therefore chronologically unsuitable as a direct 1062 visual baseline. citeturn96view48

## 10. Implications for Future Roster Work

The strongest methodological idea is not a list of units. It is an ownership and recruitment distinction:

```yaml
FutureMilitaryLayersToTest:
  - RoyalAndDucalHouseholds
  - EcclesiasticalHouseholdAndInstitutionalService
  - RegionalNobilityAndMountedRetinues
  - LocalLevyAndCommunityDefense
  - FrontierAndTributaryContingents
  - ExternalAlliedOrClientForces
  - LaterMilitaryOrdersThroughHistoricalEmergence
```

These layers are questions for future source work. They do not authorize units or establish that every Realm uses all layers.
