## 1. Purpose and Verdict

This register is Bavaria's claim-level evidence control. It preserves the source hierarchy, chronology, admission decisions, research gaps, and later-history firewall already established by the source-deepening ledger and historical reconciliation, then adds normalized source, assertion, chronology, contradiction, confidence, protected-unknown, supersession, and consumer-routing records.

It does not rewrite narrative history, invent missing sources, or bind Opening State. Every consequential claim must remain traceable, scoped, precision-safe, reviewable, and properly owned.

## 2. Benchmark and Nontransfer Receipt

Developed Realm packages commonly maintain evidence, chronology, uncertainty, and reconciliation controls. Bavaria uses Normandy, Brittany, Aquitaine, Burgundy, and Flanders as structural benchmarks only. No foreign proposition, person, title, holding, institution, relationship, chronology, confidence judgment, contradiction, or opening value transfers.

Bavaria combines evidence, chronology, contradiction, confidence, and protected unknowns in one register because its reconciliation already owns historical admission and later-history exclusion. A separate uncertainty file would currently duplicate this authority.

## 4. Authority Boundary

This register owns evidence sources, atomic assertions, chronology, date precision, confidence, contradiction sets, alternative interpretations, protected unknowns, later-history exclusions, research targets, supersession history, owner routing, and consumer eligibility.

Historical reconciliation remains the opening-fact gatekeeper. Specialist authorities own substantive state. Events own memory, momentum, emergence, and nonoccurrence. Opening State and Registry remain downstream owner-gated consumers.

## 27. Gameplay Adaptation Firewall
