## 1. Realm Verdict

Bavaria is admitted as a distinct playable Germania Realm candidate because the opening contains a coherent strategic problem that cannot be represented cleanly through a single undivided Royal Salian faction: a powerful non-Bavarian magnate holds the Bavarian ducal office, but the office does not itself create a unified territorial state, property base, army, administration, or regional obedience.

The Realm is therefore not simply the Duchy of Bavaria as a colored map block. It is an office-centered political project operating through regional actors, institutions, service relationships, routes, material capabilities, and the independent Nordheim patrimonial base of its principal leader.

## 3. What the Realm Begins As and Does Not Begin As

### Bavaria begins as

- a playable office-centered regional project led by <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person>;
- a recently granted Bavarian ducal authority whose exact execution remains distributed;
- a dual-power-base faction connecting Nordheim patrimonial strength with Bavarian regional opportunity;
- a participant in the Royal Salian political order;
- a potential Royal-service, mediation, coalition, military, and regional-coordination actor;
- a Realm whose local actors retain their own property, forces, institutions, knowledge, and consent.

### Bavaria does not begin as

- uniformly owned or administratively integrated territory;
- a state personally owned by <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person>;
- a permanent capital polity centered automatically on <Place>Regensburg</Place>;
- a complete Bavarian treasury, army, population graph, route network, or institutional roster;
- a predetermined participant in Kaiserswerth, rebellion, or the Saxon conflict;
- the later Welf duchy;
- an exact Opening State or Unreal implementation.

## 38. Developed Realm Verdict

Bavaria is not merely an office with disclaimers attached. It is a playable attempt to construct durable regional action from a recent Royal appointment, the personal and patrimonial strength of <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person>, and cooperation from Bavarian actors who retain their own property, institutions, service sources, jurisdiction, knowledge, and interests.

The developed Realm layer must therefore support three simultaneous experiences:

1. a **Bavarian regional project**, in which the ducal office becomes more or less executable through performance and relationships;
2. a **Nordheim continuity project**, in which the leader's independent patrimonial base remains useful, finite, and separately owned;
3. a **Germania political-order project**, in which Royal service, regency politics, princely coalitions, mediation, and conflict can reshape the faction without predetermining rebellion.

## 41. First-Turn Realm Decision Portfolio

- **Clarify the office.** Identify functions that can presently be exercised, requested, negotiated, or only researched.
- **Establish Bavarian presence.** Choose direct travel, household-supported presence, a bounded representative, consultation, or a narrower regional priority.
- **Audit Nordheim continuity.** Review household, property, records, dependent protection, communication, and recovery capacity.
- **Map regional actors.** Prioritize only those counts, dynasties, churches, settlements, Royal interests, routes, and service owners that materially affect gameplay.
- **Choose a Royal posture.** Support, counsel, condition service, mediate, distance, or oppose a specific action when world state justifies it.
- **Review finite military capacity.** Determine contributors, pools, equipment, officers, routes, supply, home exposure, and recovery before making commitments.
- **Protect continuity.** Prepare delegation and handback for travel, campaign, captivity, illness, office loss, or death.
- **Prepare for government crisis.** Gather information and preserve several responses without scripting Kaiserswerth.
