## 1. Governance Verdict

Bavarian governance is an office-centered coordination system, not a centralized territorial administration. <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person> may hold the ducal office, but practical government depends on competence, presence, information, records, qualified people, resources, regional reception, and owner-approved cooperation.

The player experience is to make a recently granted office executable without pretending that appointment equals control. Governance succeeds when requests become lawful, receive the right actors, obtain actual capacity, produce documented actions, and survive the leader's absence or loss of office.

## 51. Developed Bavaria Governance Thesis

Bavaria's governing problem is not the absence of a title. It is the distance between holding the title and making the title useful. <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person> enters the campaign with personal standing, an independent northern patrimonial foundation, and a recent Royal appointment, but without a proven Bavarian property portfolio, centralized administration, automatic military service, uniform jurisdiction, or settled network of delegates.

The faction must therefore build government through repeated performance:

- appear or communicate where a problem exists;
- identify the actual owner and competent forum;
- listen before demanding;
- frame specific requests rather than universal commands;
- provide protection, mediation, coordination, or Royal access that makes cooperation worthwhile;
- preserve written and remembered continuity when <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person> leaves;
- accept that partial agreements and issue-specific refusal are normal;
- preserve Nordheim independence as recovery rather than draining it into nominal Bavarian authority.

## 54. First-Turn Governance Decision Packages

### Establish the Bavarian governing mode

The player chooses a bounded opening emphasis:

- **Personal circuit:** prioritize travel, audiences, and direct consultation at the cost of Nordheim absence and route burden.
- **Household-supported foothold:** establish records, hospitality, messengers, and petition reception before broader claims.
- **Regional consultation:** approach selected comital and ecclesiastical actors around one practical issue.
- **Royal-service posture:** preserve court access and readiness for Royal requests while limiting Bavarian commitments.
- **Patrimonial caution:** maintain a narrower ducal profile until people, resources, and acceptance are clearer.

No option creates a capital, bureaucracy, property, or obedience.

### Define the first public purpose

The opening government should pursue one or two visible purposes rather than claim universal control:

- protection of a route or gathering;
- mediation of a specific dispute;
- reception of petitions;
- preparation of counsel or assembly;
- coordination of a limited service request;
- communication of a Royal or ducal position;
- hospitality and safe access for regional actors.
