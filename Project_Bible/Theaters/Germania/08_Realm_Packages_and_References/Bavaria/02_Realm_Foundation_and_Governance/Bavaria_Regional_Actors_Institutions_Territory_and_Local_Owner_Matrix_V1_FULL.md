## 1. Purpose and Barrier Verdict

This matrix is the ownership barrier between the Bavaria Realm concept and every person, office, dynasty, institution, property, community, place, route, service source, force, and external polity the faction may interact with. It prevents the playable package from swallowing Bavaria merely because <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person> holds the ducal office.

The matrix also establishes the positive gameplay field. Regional actors are not disclaimers or passive modifiers. They may recognize, negotiate, serve, delay, counterpropose, appeal, resist, mediate, withdraw, reconcile, or remain outside a particular ducal project.
