## 2. Evidence Receipt and Current Ceiling

The current Bavaria evidence establishes:

- Otto of Nordheim as the principal playable leader and recent holder of the Bavarian ducal office;
- Richenza as Otto's spouse and a material Nordheim household and inheritance interface;
- Henry as a dependent-child candidate rather than an adult opening agent;
- Ethelinde as a conditional marriage and dynastic-interface candidate whose exact opening marriage state remains unresolved;
- other children as genealogy and household-ledger subjects first;
- Bavarian counts, dynasties, bishops, abbots, chapters, royal officials, settlement actors, and service contributors as admissible actor classes but not automatically admitted named characters;
- Agnes of Poitou and Henry IV as externally owned Royal Salian interfaces;
- later figures and relationships as Historical Emergence candidates rather than opening facts.

The current evidence does not establish a complete January 1062 character roster, exact Bavarian officeholders, exact household presence, exact locations, exact properties, universal loyalties, or opening political blocs.
