## 1. Adjusted Sequence Decision

The earlier Realm-personality and character-network concepts overlapped with Bavaria's existing foundation and domain AI doctrines. At V1 creation, the package lacked one primary owner for directed relationships and routing among admitted people, households, offices, institutions, regions, service sources, and external interfaces.

This authority was therefore the first file in the adjusted sequence. It consolidates the useful scope of the prior character-network proposal without duplicating diplomacy, governance, law, public order, military, events, or the regional-owner matrix.

The Bavaria Realm personality and integrated-decision authority and historical evidence register now exist in the supplied package sequence. They consume this network, integrate existing doctrine, and create no new factual state. Their existence does not bind an exact opening graph.

### Secular office and operating mandate distinction

Title and secular-office owners admit the office definition, holder, vacancy, challenge, loss, restoration, and competence. Governance owns how accepted competence is exercised through mandates, delegation, petitions, assemblies, reports, and administrative action. A governance mandate does not appoint a secular-office holder, widen title competence, or restore a lost office.

The network references the appropriate owner and version for each office node and each operating edge. ReportsToForMandate and DelegatesTo require governance receipts; an office-holder change requires the title owner. Ecclesiastical office identity and occupancy remain institution-owned. Diplomatic recognition remains a counterpart-scoped diplomatic receipt, not a second title-state writer.

The former combined TitleAndLaw routing row is separated to make these writes explicit. No title, holder, mandate, recognition or node is created by this clarification. Network promise, obligation, grievance and performance links remain references to their substantive owners, not duplicate agreements or legal duties.

## 14. Direction, Asymmetry, and Context

Each direction is stored separately. Otto may recognize an institution's competence while the institution recognizes only a narrow protective role. A regional actor may owe bounded service while disputing another demand. A household may depend on a route owner without trusting that owner's reports. An external government may recognize an office while withholding private intentions.

No relationship is universally positive or negative. Issue, place, function, audience, duration, and knowledge holder matter.

## 15. Knowledge and Visibility

Knowledge of an edge is holder-specific. The network records who knows, how, when, from what source, with what confidence, and whether information is public, private, restricted, rumored, disputed, stale, corrected, or externally locked.

```text
Edge Exists != Every Actor Knows
Observed Conduct != Known Motive
Public Claim != Private Commitment
Secret Promise != Public Relationship
Rumor Of Breach != Breach
```

## 16. Access and Communication Channels

Access may be personal, household, office, institutional, assembly, messenger, route, hospitality, legal, ecclesiastical, military, dynastic, or diplomatic. Each channel requires owner, participants, purpose, geography, security, authentication, delay, capacity, restrictions, and current availability.

Access to one actor or institution does not create access to every office, property, record, person, or decision.

## 17. Recognition Network

Recognition edges identify recognizer, recognized node, function, audience, geography, duration, conditions, evidence, and review. Royal appointment, Bavarian regional recognition, institutional recognition, household recognition, military command recognition, and external recognition remain separate.

The network routes recognition. Title supplies secular title/office recognition; diplomacy supplies counterpart-scoped diplomatic recognition; law supplies procedural standing and competence receipts; institutions supply their own recognition. Governance consumes those receipts and records operating acceptance within its mandate scope, but does not create or change secular-office identity, holder or title competence.

## 20. Patronage and Protection Network

Patronage and protection edges require provider, recipient, subject, resources or acts, conditions, audience, duration, owner state, and consequences. They may concern institutions, households, dependents, records, property interfaces, hospitality, care, burial, travel, or political support.

Patronage does not create ownership, appointment power, doctrinal control, obedience, or perpetual gratitude. Protection does not create loyalty.

## 21. Grievance, Dispute, and Repair Network

The network references grievances created by public-order, law, economy, military, household, property, institution, or event owners. It stores the directed affected relationship, issue, knowledge, communication, response route, mediation access, repair attempt, and relational consequence.

A grievance edge does not prove the grievance. Repair of an edge does not erase material harm, judgment, unpaid compensation, displacement, casualties, or memory.

## 22. Proposal, Petition, and Mediation Routing

A proposal or petition route identifies originator, recipient, intermediary, representative, authentication, forum, subject, access channel, delivery, acknowledgement, authority to answer, counterproposal path, delay, expiry, and outcome owner.

Mediation identifies mediator mandate, parties, subject, confidentiality, safe conduct, information access, proposed remedy, acceptance, performance, and closure. A mediator does not own the settlement or guarantee success.

## 23. Household and Dynasty Interfaces

The Nordheim dynasty and household authority owns kinship, marriage, succession, property continuity, dependents, widowhood, and membership. The network owns directed access, expectations, communications, support, protection, service, grievance, and knowledge edges among admitted nodes.

No child, spouse, dependent, or branch receives an edge solely from kinship. Marriage creates no identical opinions, shared treasury, automatic representation, or permanent alliance.

## 25. Regional Actor and Institutional Interfaces

The regional owner matrix supplies actor classes and institutions. The network may connect Otto, the ducal office, admitted regional actors, assemblies, settlements, ecclesiastical institutions, service sources, and community representatives through issue-specific edges.

One actor's recognition, service, grievance, or consent cannot propagate to all Bavaria without an explicit path and owner receipts.

## 26. Royal Salian and External Interfaces

Royal government, Royal household, Germania political-order, neighboring Realm, and other external nodes remain externally owned. Bavaria stores only its local directed edge and accepted counterpart receipts.

The counterpart owner controls reciprocal recognition, intent, promises, resources, agreements, performance, and private state. Missing counterpart data remains externally locked.

## 27. Ecclesiastical Network Interfaces

Ecclesiastical nodes may connect through jurisdiction, protection, patronage, hospitality, records, mediation, sanctuary, care, burial, service, property dispute, and external Church channels. Institution and office owners retain canonical, property, office, sacred, and community state.

No unified Church opinion or ecclesiastical bloc is permitted.

## 28. Military and Coalition Interfaces

The network can represent service requests, accepted command, contingent relationships, coalition cooperation, supply dependencies, escort, guarantees, casualty responsibility, demobilization, and withdrawal. Military owns formations and performance; diplomacy owns external commitments; network owns directed relationship and routing state.

Coalitions remain temporary groupings of separate nodes. Membership does not merge commands, resources, or intent.

## 30. Collision and Contradiction Handling

The network permits simultaneous conflicting edges: recognition and grievance, service and refusal on another issue, patronage and jurisdictional dispute, kinship and opposition, promise and incapacity, cooperation and mistrust.

Collisions trigger explanation and owner review. They are not averaged into one score.

## 31. Office Loss, Incapacity, Death, and Successor Transformation

When a person dies, becomes unavailable, loses office, changes household position, or leaves a context, edges are reviewed individually.

Dispositions include transfer by owner rule, persistence toward the person, persistence toward the office, suspension, dormancy, breach, closure, successor proposal, institutional fallback, disputed continuation, or extinction.

```text
Office Loss != Edge Deletion
Person Death != Promise Automatically Extinguished
Successor != Same Relationships
Former Duke != Former Person
```

## 32. Memory, Momentum, and Events Interface

Events own event instances, memory, momentum, and nonoccurrence. The network stores only directed edge effects and holder-specific knowledge created through accepted event receipts.

A remembered service may affect an expectation. A crisis may open a proposal route. A nonoccurrence may close an eligibility path. No change occurs without event-owner and network receipts.

## 33. Metrics Boundary

Permitted descriptive metrics include known path length, communication delay, access availability, unresolved obligations, issue-specific concentration, dependency bottlenecks, single-point-of-failure exposure, contradiction count, stale knowledge, and externally locked edges.

Universal loyalty, friendship, hostility, legitimacy, influence, trust, or power scores are prohibited as total truth. Derived indicators must expose inputs and limitations.

## 34. AI Consumption Doctrine

AI consumes only visible, owner-published, confidence-qualified network state. It must distinguish edge type, direction, issue, holder, access, capacity, performance, and external locks.

AI must verify mandate, choose a valid route, preserve Bavaria-Nordheim separation, check obligation against availability, distinguish promise from performance, protect household and local minima, prefer narrower requests or mediation when appropriate, avoid inferred reciprocity, explain decisions, and abstain when state is unknown.

## 41. Positive Validation Fixtures

- Otto recognizes an institution for one function while the institution recognizes only a bounded protective role.
- A regional actor owes limited service but holds a separate grievance.
- Nordheim support reaches Bavaria through a recorded and reversible relationship.
- A messenger delivers a proposal without creating acceptance.
- A private promise does not become public automatically.
- A Royal counterpart remains externally locked pending receipt.
- A breach strains one edge without producing universal hostility.
- Office loss suspends office edges while preserving supported personal and Nordheim edges.
- A successor establishes new recognition rather than inheriting every relationship.

## 42. Negative Validation Fixtures

- One opinion score replaces directed edges.
- Kinship creates trust, alliance, shared policy, or access.
- The ducal title creates loyalty or service.
- A promise creates agreement or performance.
- A grievance creates hostility or rebellion.
- One institution becomes the unified Church position.
- An external message reveals private intent.
- Bavaria and Nordheim merge because Otto connects them.
- A coalition becomes a permanent bloc.
- Office loss deletes or automatically transfers all edges.
- A historical date creates alliance or conflict.
- Network state directly mutates another owner's state.
