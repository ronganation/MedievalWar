Preserves household capacity, records, dependents, and stable succession preparation.

Uses admitted access to seek protection, mediation, remedy, safe conduct, or reconciliation.

## 33. Strategic Fantasy, Character Thesis, and Durable Tensions

**Strategic fantasy:** Build <Person>Richenza</Person> into the indispensable keeper of the Nordheim house, its protected dependents, its records, its disputed property, and its future branches, while deciding how much of that foundation may safely sustain <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person>'s Bavarian ambitions.

**Core question:** Can <Person>Richenza</Person> turn marriage, household standing, patrimonial knowledge, maternal responsibility, and crisis judgment into lasting personal agency without becoming a fabricated co-duchess, a passive spouse resource, or an abstract property screen?

### Strong development paths

- **Keeper of Nordheim:** builds dependable records, protected dependents, preserved claims, viable residences, communication, and household minimums.
- **Patrimonial Advocate:** turns uncertain inheritance into documented, enforceable, and sustainable property capacity without merging it into <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person>'s office.
- **Dynastic Broker:** negotiates marriages, household transfers, branch relationships, and settlements while preserving the agency of each participant.
- **Household Intercessor:** develops credible access to seek protection, safe conduct, restitution, recognition, or mediation.
- **Crisis Continuity Principal:** maintains the Nordheim center through absence, captivity, ban, deposition, incapacity, or death, then completes a valid handback or transition.
- **Independent Widow or Senior Household Principal:** preserves residence, property, records, relationships, and personal agency after the marital political structure changes.

### Failure transformations

- **Marginalized Spouse:** access and resources are restricted while political obligations continue.
- **Disputed Property Principal:** claims become the center of prolonged conflict, partial settlement, or loss.
- **Overprotective Dynast:** protection narrows the agency or opportunities of dependents and branches.
- **Partisan Household Center:** household access and information become captured by one succession or political interest.
- **Overextended Continuity Actor:** promises and responsibilities exceed staff, stores, information, or authority.
- **Displaced Principal:** office loss, conflict, or property seizure breaks residence and operating capacity without deleting the character.
