## 7. Modern Medieval II Economy Standard

Bavaria preserves a readable Medieval II economy of settlements, property, revenues, markets, roads, production, recruitment costs, maintenance, and supply. It selectively adds finite stocks, owner-specific property, delivery, seasonal labor and transport, damage, maintenance, recovery, and AI-visible bottlenecks.

The design rejects one abstract provincial wealth number as the only economic state. It also rejects inventory micromanagement for ordinary low-value goods when aggregate bands are sufficient.

## 10. Bavarian Office Resource Constitution

The Bavarian ducal office may use only resources attached to the office by valid grant, accepted regional support, lawful revenue, contract, gift, service, temporary use, or other owner receipt.

Office resources must identify purpose, controlling owner, custody, duration, accounting, permitted use, prohibited use, maintenance, loss, return, and end condition. Office loss triggers accounting and handback rather than silent absorption by the former holder.

## 11. Nordheim Patrimony Firewall

Nordheim property, household goods, revenues, animals, equipment, records, stores, labor, and credit remain privately or household-owned unless a specific transaction temporarily commits them to Bavarian activity.

```text
Nordheim Patrimony != Ducal Treasury
Nordheim Revenue != Bavarian Revenue
Nordheim Animals != Bavarian Transport Pool
Nordheim Equipment != Ducal Armory
Office Loss != Nordheim Confiscation
```

Every transfer requires owner consent, quantity or band, purpose, duration, accounting, risk, return, compensation, and memory.

## 12. Regional and Local Property Holders

Counts, dynasties, institutions, settlements, communities, households, markets, workshops, and local operators retain their own property and economic rights. The duke may request, negotiate, purchase, hire, receive, regulate within competence, or seek lawful remedy. Political authority does not create direct ownership.

## 13. Ecclesiastical Property Interface

Church institutions own or administer their admitted property, rights, goods, revenues, stores, animals, buildings, workshops, hospitality, care, and sacred assets through the ecclesiastical and property owners.

Patronage, protection, advocacy, or institutional cooperation does not create secular ownership. Emergency use, relief support, requisition claims, sanctuary, restitution, and temporalities require typed transactions and canonical or external receipts where applicable.

## 19. Accounting Entry Contract

Every entry requires account, date track, source or recipient, reason, right or agreement, amount or qualitative band, form, custody, place, authorization, supporting receipt, restriction, reversal or correction, and trace.

Money cannot be spent twice. Restricted or pledged funds cannot be silently treated as free treasury. Correction preserves the original entry and creates a reversing or superseding entry.

## 20. Budget Reservation and Competing Uses

A reservation identifies purpose, amount or band, account, goods where in kind, duration, cancellation, alternate source, and release. Shortage produces reduced scope, delay, substitution, negotiation, debt, arrears, or abstention rather than negative inventory without trace.
