## 13. Assignment and Reservation

Actual people, officers, standards, equipment, animals, garrison berths, cadres, muster space, route capacity, transport, supply, recovery capacity, and permission are reserved before activation. ### 37.2 Capture, identity and accepted custody

A capture report identifies incident, reporting formation, captor, subject or bounded group, identity confidence, actual or reported place, current condition, known property separately, and the source of authority. Repeated reports of the same person are reconciled. Unknown identity remains unknown; a generic named prisoner is not spawned.

Before accepting operational custody, identify a responsible custodian, admitted location, guards, food, shelter, records, communication, legal basis and health referral where required. A custody obligation may exist while support is inadequate; record the shortfall and seek relief or lawful disposition rather than declaring the people absent. A prisoner is not a recruit, asset, guaranteed bargaining value or source of private knowledge.

### 37.3 Transfer and failed handover

A transfer proposal states outgoing custodian, transit custodian, receiving custodian, subject scope, purpose, authority, receiving capacity, route, escort, support, care continuity, terms and review window. Preparation is not departure; departure is not receiving acceptance.

| Transition | Required evidence | Failure disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Held to prepared transfer | Accepted authority and receiving preflight; compatible inputs | Remain held; record missing dependency |
| Prepared to transit | Actual departure and transit-custodian acceptance | No departure receipt means no movement |
| Transit to handover pending | Arrival report and subject reconciliation | Keep transit custodian responsible pending acceptance |
| Pending to accepted handover | Matching outgoing and receiving receipts | Refusal/discrepancy triggers review; no duplicate custodian |
| Failed transit or refused reception | Actual location/status report and current capacity | Hold, reroute or return only through valid owners |

One responsible operational custodian is recorded at each accepted stage. A disputed or missing handover retains the last accepted responsibility and an explicit uncertainty record; it does not pretend to know the person's physical location. Canonical location updates require the person owner. The old custodian releases unused guard reservations only after a valid transition or approved replacement duty. Failed handover cannot teleport a captive home or delete the support obligation.

### 37.4 Ransom, exchange and release performance

Diplomacy owns proposal, agreement, reciprocal consent and performance terms; economy owns payment and delivery; law reviews applicable authority. Military verifies the subject, custodian, release order, escort and operational performance. A payment receipt cannot itself mark release. A release promise cannot mark a payment performed.

For staged exchanges, link each accepted person/group and material receipt to its own stage. Partial performance remains partial; unmatched obligations stay visible. Counterpart silence is not acceptance. A change of office or command reviews mandate continuity without automatically voiding or fulfilling the agreement.

### 37.5 Release, travel, reception and closure

Release ends the accepted restraint within its scope. Departure, route access, transport, support, receiving household/community and continued care remain separate. Safe conduct is not supply, and arrival is not reintegration or return to service.

Closure records receiving-owner acceptance, unresolved property or payment matters, residual care, delayed/missing subjects, and the end of military guard obligations. It may close the military phase while a health or household process continues. A missing person remains unresolved. Confirmed death routes to character/population and health disposition owners; custody closure cannot create death or burial.

## 42. Veteran Persistence and Lineage

Surviving formations may persist after ordinary recruitment closes when actual people, cadre, equipment, support, and authority remain. Persistence does not grant infinite replacement, frozen equipment, immunity from demobilization, automatic upgrade, or renamed successors without a transition.

## 43. Demobilization

```text
Mission end
→ orders and records
→ return movement
→ casualty and prisoner accounting
→ equipment and animal inspection
→ pay compensation and obligations
→ return to household and community
→ reservation release
→ household labor care and public-order effects
→ source recovery
→ veteran reserve or closure disposition
```
Demobilization does not instantly refill pools.

Demobilization also reconciles guards, custody transit, unresolved captives, siege works, camp obligations and receiving care. Ending the main mission does not silently cancel a prisoner transfer or release all escorts while people remain in transit. Unused reservations are released; unresolved duties receive a named responsible owner and review trigger.

## 44. Household and Community Return

Population and household owners receive survivors, wounded, disabled, missing, prisoners, dead, deserters, delayed returners, veterans, and new dependency burdens. Return may restore labor while creating care, debt, grievance, reputation, property claims, and migration pressure.

For custody return, record arrival and receiving-household/community acceptance separately. Population and household owners determine reintegration and dependency effects; health determines continuing care interpretation; military reviews any future service. Returned property, restored employment, command eligibility and public reception are separate receipts. Closure of one process cannot erase the others.

## 45. Formation Closure and Reorganization

A formation may become replacement-only, cadre-only, local-defense-only, suspended, transferred, reorganized, demobilized, or extinct as an active body while lineage remains. Era change never automatically deletes or upgrades formations.

## 46. Royal Service

Royal government may request counsel, escort, a contingent, a commander, route support, or campaign participation. Bavaria may accept, limit, condition, delay, substitute, mediate, or refuse according to competence, relationships, owners, and capacity.

## 50. Siege and Engineering

Siege capacity combines specialists, labor, tools, timber, rope, metal, stone, containers, transport, animals, guards, site access, time, supply, repair, and recovery. No generic permanent siege unit is created unless a later profile demonstrates a recurring unit identity.

### 50.1 Siege-operation identity and scope

A siege operation is a bounded campaign commitment involving an admitted objective, target, parties, accepted command, actual contributors, access, supplies, engineering capacity and an exit. It is not a building upgrade, a free siege unit or a date-triggered conquest. No opening target or engine type is assigned here.

Bavaria's coalition-and-service host must compare the objective with contributor service windows, Nordheim reserve exposure, local garrison duties, supply delivery and recovery. A prolonged commitment can lose contributor support even while the target remains unchanged. Service expiry is reviewed under its actual terms; it is not automatically desertion.

States may coexist as facets: negotiation can occur during sustained operations, and engineering work can be suspended without ending the whole siege. No elapsed-turn rule guarantees surrender, breach or progress. Operation closure records unresolved aftermath rather than pretending all downstream systems completed.

### 50.3 Preparation and investment

Review purpose, competence, target evidence, contributor acceptance, command, military capacity, terrain/access, target observations, camp support, engineering scope, supply, civilian/institutional safeguards, withdrawal and recovery. Unknown defensive condition narrows feasibility; the AI cannot inspect hidden defender stores or morale.

Investment records the actual scope of controlled approaches, observation and movement restrictions supported by admitted forces and territory context. Investment does not imply a complete blockade. Uncovered access remains possible or unknown. River presence does not create vessels or naval closure; water-facing control requires admitted capability and access.

### 50.4 Sustainment and engineering work

Military tracks operational commitment, formation readiness, command and supply requests. Economy supplies goods, tools, animals and delivery; population supplies finite workers; education supplies relevant learning evidence; built environment owns physical projects, condition, inspection and commissioning.

A proposed engineering task identifies purpose, site, access, responsible workers, materials, tools, protection, acceptance and recovery. Its progress is received from the execution owner, not advanced by a military countdown. Equipment operation requires actual compatible equipment and trained users. No machine blueprint, explosive method or real-world assault instruction is defined.

Camp support, transport, care, repair and contributor time compete with other commitments. An intact camp structure can be operationally unavailable through missing operators or supplies. Deteriorating support triggers resize, resupply, negotiation, suspension or withdrawal review; it does not automatically kill people or destroy structures.

### 50.5 Relief, interruption and contributor change

A relief force, route loss, command interruption, provider shortage, contributor withdrawal or changed political mandate can reopen feasibility. Record only observed/received enemy information. Compare sustaining the admitted scope, reducing it, responding through battle, seeking terms, or lifting the operation.

A departing contingent changes actual investment coverage and support. Do not leave its formations assigned to the siege or preserve their capacity as a ghost reserve. Replacement needs source acceptance, route, supplies and compatible command. A valid contributor withdrawal retains costs and obligations without becoming universal faction hostility.

### 50.6 Negotiation and capitulation performance

Diplomacy owns representatives, offers, agreement and reciprocal acceptance; law/title/property owners retain jurisdiction, rights and claims. Military supplies operational feasibility and performs only the accepted military terms.

A capitulation record references entry conditions, garrison disposition, arms/equipment treatment, prisoners, safe conduct, protected people and institutions, supplies, inspection, withdrawal or occupation scope, and unresolved rights where applicable. No one template applies to every target. Acceptance is not performance: departure, entry, handover and receipt are separately verified.

A surrendered garrison does not become a Bavarian formation. Disarmed equipment does not become ready inventory. Hostages are not automatically prisoners. Military entry does not transfer title, property or institutional control. Failed performance returns to agreement review and an operational decision; it does not silently grant victory.

### 50.7 Battle, entry and occupation handoff

If an admitted assault or relief battle occurs, section 35 supplies the battle contract. The result returns casualty, capture, equipment, access and observed damage receipts. Built environment inspects accepted physical damage; health interprets care needs; person/population confirms life and demographic effects.

Entry and temporary military control require actual formations, access, command, supply and accepted scope. Occupation duty is not ownership. A new garrison assignment changes source reservations and local security elsewhere. Institutions and civilians retain their own state and lawful interfaces; battle victory is not their consent.

### 50.8 Withdrawal and aftermath

Withdrawal records orders, routes, support, wounded and captives, equipment return or lawful loss, temporary works, guards, remaining obligations and receiving owners. It can be orderly, partial, delayed or unresolved. Abandoning a worksite does not erase property claims or create salvaged stock.

Aftermath sends bounded receipts to custody, health, built environment, economy, population, law, diplomacy, public order and events. Distinguish military mission completion from physical repair, civilian return, compensation, burial coordination and restored services. A failed siege can close with persistent costs; a successful siege can leave serious recovery needs.

### Additional custody and siege choices

| Decision | Material alternatives | Costs and completion |
|---|---|---|
| Custody exceeds support | Seek accepted transfer, negotiate release/exchange, request support, or narrow other commitments | Guards, food, care and transport compete with the host; closure requires actual disposition and reception |

## 53. Bavaria Military AI Doctrine

The AI identifies political purpose, authority, owners, people, households, season, safe capacity, equipment, animals, officers, routes, sites, supply, command modes, withdrawal, and recovery. It reserves actual inputs, selects sustainable force packages, accounts for casualties, and demobilizes. It cannot invent pools or chase historical campaigns without current causes.

Custody AI checks identity, responsibility, lawful basis, care, support, agreement stage and receiving acceptance before proposing transfer or closure. Siege AI checks contributor windows, observed investment coverage, camp support, engineering receipts, relief information, accepted terms and withdrawal capacity. Hard eligibility gates precede utility comparison. Neither AI reads private enemy condition, assumes ransom value, invents consent or treats victory as ownership. Missing evidence supports inquiry, a smaller valid option, or an explained hold.
