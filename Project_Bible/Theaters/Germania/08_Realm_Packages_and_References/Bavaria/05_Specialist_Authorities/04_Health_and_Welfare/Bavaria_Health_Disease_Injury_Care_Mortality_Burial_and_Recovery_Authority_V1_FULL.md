## 2. Developed Bavaria health thesis

Bavaria's care problem is the human cost of maintaining an unfinished ducal project across two separated power bases. A leader traveling to a Royal venue, a regional contributor remaining under arms, a household lending a caregiver, and an institution receiving travelers all create different care burdens. The ducal office can coordinate assistance, but cannot manufacture providers, commandeer every institution, or consume Nordheim reserves without a valid transfer.

The playable question is: which commitments can continue without abandoning the people who sustain them? Care can preserve continuity and make cooperation more reliable, but it uses the same food, shelter, labor, routes, escorts, money and institutional goodwill needed elsewhere. Success is measured in supported people, functioning households and credible recovery, not a universal health bonus.

Bavaria's defining pressures are dual-base distance, itinerant leadership, distributed regional ownership, conditional Royal service, finite military contributions, institutional autonomy, and possible post-ducal retrenchment. These are design applications of the established faction identity, not claims about actual opening illnesses.

## 3. Player promise and Total War scale

The player sets care priorities, protects recovery capacity, negotiates institutional help, and decides whether to delay, reduce or abandon a commitment. Ordinary care is resolved through bounded episodes and aggregate services. The player does not prescribe remedies, schedule every patient, or run a modern hospital administration.

Named-character episodes become visible when they materially affect leadership, travel, command, household continuity or a political decision. Cohort episodes become visible when they affect labor, recruitment inputs, displacement, settlement capacity or military readiness. Minor stable cases remain in summaries. Repeated prompts require a material change, not merely another evaluation tick.

A health warning should explain a concrete consequence: a muster cannot recover its wounded, a receiving household lacks support, an itinerary exceeds care access, or a relief promise has not reached its recipients. The system remains a Modern Medieval II campaign layer with richer causality, not a clinical simulation.

## 6. Named people and aggregate cohorts

A named episode links to an admitted character reference. Health may record a report, assessment, care attempt or recovery interpretation without copying the character's private health state. A character-owner receipt is required before changing availability, incapacity or life state.

An aggregate episode requires an admitted population scope, membership or sampling basis, and an uncertainty band. A cohort pressure does not diagnose every member. Aggregates may contain people who are unaffected, improving, deteriorating, missing, or awaiting assessment. The record must preserve that uneven distribution rather than converting it into one identical condition.

The same person cannot be counted in two overlapping aggregate death totals. Episode identity, casualty identity and demographic receipt identity must be reconciled before any population consequence is applied.

An episode is a bounded case, not a permanent character trait. It can be opened from a credible report, an incident receipt or an accepted aggregate pattern. Risk-only records remain distinct from condition episodes.

## 11. Assessment and diagnosis limits

Assessment compares observed condition, incident history, timing, available knowledge, communication, provider scope and competing explanations. The model can represent an uncertain illness or injury without assigning a modern disease label.

Assessment results are Unknown, Suspected, SupportedWithinModel, Contested or Superseded. SupportedWithinModel means the approved simulation model and available evidence support the interpretation; it is not modern clinical certainty. Historical practice does not prove effectiveness, and improvement after an intervention does not prove causation.

The document defines no dosage, invasive procedure, remedy recipe or treatment protocol. Such details are unnecessary for the campaign purpose. Treatment remains an abstract, evidence-qualified support attempt with an outcome model requiring separate calibration.

## 12. Exposure and outbreak admission

Exposure records identify a bounded contact or environmental context, duration or uncertainty, movement, crowding, food, water, care and relevant owner inputs. Contact alone does not create transmission. A traveler arriving does not create an outbreak.

An outbreak candidate requires related observations in a bounded place and window, a case-pattern definition, competing explanations, reporting coverage, population scope and an approved condition model. Repeated copies of one report do not constitute independent cases. Outbreak status may remain UnableToDetermine.

The event owner may present an assessment dilemma after this gate passes. It may not spawn an epidemic for dramatic pacing. No disease prevalence, transmission rate or regional susceptibility modifier is assigned. Identity, religion and language are not health-risk shortcuts.

## 13. Injury and functional consequences

An injury episode starts from an admitted accident, work, travel, conflict or battle receipt. Health interprets care need, monitoring and recovery; character or population owns resulting functional state, and military owns readiness.

Functional consequences are task-specific. A limitation may prevent mounted travel but permit counsel, correspondence or another useful role. It does not make a person universally incapable. Persistent effects require accommodation review rather than automatic removal from all campaign activity.

Battle casualty, injury episode, missing status, capture and death remain separate. Health cannot infer a fatality from a formation's casualty count, nor return an injured person to a recruitment pool simply because replacement capacity exists.

## 14. Care need, priority and finite capacity

Care allocation evaluates urgency, expected benefit within the approved model, time sensitivity, current capacity, continuity of existing care and opportunity cost. Social prominence is not the sole priority rule. Protecting a leader may have strategic consequences, but those consequences must be shown alongside displaced civilian or household support.

Care capacity is a vector: qualified provider time, caregiver time, suitable space, food and water access, materials, transport, security, communication and continuity. The limiting dependency constrains the assignment. A spare room cannot substitute for a provider, and a provider cannot substitute for missing shelter or supplies.

Priority bands are ImmediateReview, High, Scheduled, Monitoring and HeldUnknown. These are game-management bands, not clinical instructions. A lower-priority assignment can be delayed with a recorded review point; it cannot disappear silently from the backlog.

## 15. Providers, caregivers and learning

Provider definitions describe a function and scope, not an automatically created person. A care assignment needs an admitted person or bounded caregiver cohort, availability, access, relevant learning receipt and support inputs.

Education owns training and assessment; population owns anonymous workers and availability; character owns named private proficiency. Health records whether those inputs fit the episode and assigns only within accepted scope. Reading a text, holding a clerical office or belonging to an institution does not establish competence.

Caregiving consumes time that may otherwise support dependents, agriculture, transport, household records or military service. A provider can have several compatible tasks only if accepted time windows and capacity allow them. Scarcity can be addressed through bounded learning, substitutes, external help or reduced commitments, not instant expert generation.

## 16. Care settings and commissioning

Candidate settings include household space, institutional hospitality space, temporary shelter, a military care area, transit accommodation and a body-custody space. These are operating-use classes, not historically admitted hospitals.

Built environment supplies physical condition, capacity and commissioning. Property and institutional owners supply permission and limits. Economy supplies goods, fuel, containers and transport. Health verifies whether the setting can support the proposed assignment and records operating load.

A completed building may remain unusable for care because it lacks staff, access, supplies or security. A damaged institution may continue some care in an accepted alternative space without its original building being restored. Neither arrangement changes institutional identity or property ownership.

## 17. Care plan and support attempt

A plan states the recipient scope, goal, provider, setting, material inputs, route, authority, expected window, monitoring, interruption conditions and alternatives. The plan may be partial: shelter and continuing observation may be possible while a more specialized assessment remains unavailable.

An applied-care receipt records what actually happened, not what was promised. Outcome records separate observed change from attributed effect. A failed or harmful attempt remains in the trace and can trigger reassessment, withdrawal or remedy through the proper owners.

Care may be interrupted by loss of a provider, route closure, competing military use, lack of materials or withdrawal of permission. The system records the interruption, releases only unused reservations, and preserves consumed inputs and any resulting burden.

## 18. Food security and nutrition interface

Economy records stocks, ownership, allocation, transport and consumption. Health interprets whether received support is adequate for the bounded recipient and purpose within an approved model. Food held elsewhere in the Realm does not prove household access.

A relief package must pass sourcing, reservation, dispatch, delivery, acceptance and distribution before recipients receive a benefit. Shortage is not automatically famine, and high prices do not alone prove physical absence. The player can reduce a military commitment, negotiate supplies, redirect a delivery, protect household minima or accept slower development.

Nutrition-related condition models require separate calibration and evidence review. No opening diet, exact ration, mortality rate or regional nutritional modifier is supplied here.

## 19. Water, sanitation and shelter

Water access, delivery, observed condition and safety confidence are separate. Territory identifies the source and access; built environment identifies physical assets; economy and population supply containers, labor and delivery; health interprets reported risk and care consequences.

Sanitation is an operating service involving people, space, waste handling, upkeep and inspection. A rule or asset does not prove performance. Shelter similarly requires usable space, permission, supplies and operating support. Seasonal labels do not establish exact local exposure.

The player may prioritize inspection, repair requests, service labor, alternate access or temporary accommodation. These choices reserve real inputs and may compete with route work, harvest or garrison support. No modern municipal health department or universal sanitation network is invented.

## 20. Household care and dependency loop

The household loop begins with a supported need, identifies available carers and substitutes, tests food, shelter and time, reserves compatible support, monitors outcome and returns dependency effects to population or household owners.

Care can preserve household continuity while reducing immediate labor. Releasing every carer to military or construction service may improve one commitment and undermine another. The interface must show the affected household, the service being reduced, the alternative support and the review window.

Household membership does not create consent, skill or shared knowledge. Dependents do not become autonomous agents because an episode is opened. Care may remain partial or prolonged; closure requires an accepted handoff or a recorded unresolved disposition.

## 21. Institutional cooperation and ducal authority

The duke may request hospitality, care space, records, material support or mediation from an admitted institution. The institution may accept, qualify, delay, offer partial support or refuse. Patronage can support capacity only through actual people, property and resources.

Cooperation should be negotiated around a defined purpose and duration. The player may offer supplies, transport, protection, compensation or reciprocal support. Institutional autonomy, existing obligations and protected capacity remain visible. A refusal is not proof of hostility.

The health authority does not appoint an abbot, bishop or practitioner, alter canonical rights, convert an institution into a hospital or give Bavaria ownership of its stores. Successful care can generate a performance receipt for the network and public order, but cannot directly write loyalty.

## 22. Dual-base care geography

Bavaria and Nordheim maintain separate recipient scopes, carers, settings, supplies and reservations. Otto's travel connects them but does not merge their capacities. A provider accompanying the leader is unavailable elsewhere unless a valid substitute or compatible assignment exists.

Transfers require origin and destination receipts, route and travel feasibility, support during transit, acceptance and return or replacement planning. A recovery journey can be delayed or redirected; reaching Nordheim does not itself resolve the episode.

The player can preserve a small recovery reserve, support local Bavarian partnerships, use a limited traveling care arrangement or reduce itinerary ambition. The strongest approach depends on current evidence and capacity. No one option is a permanent faction bonus.

## 23. Leader absence and continuity

A reported concern affecting Otto is assessed without revealing private character state to every actor. Character owners decide availability; governance reviews mandates and pending matters; military reviews command; household owners review dependent and property support.

The player may delay travel, delegate a bounded task, seek local support, reduce service or return through an accepted route. Each option exposes a different field to absence. Richenza may support household continuity only through her admitted role and mandate; no automatic regency or medical competence is created.

## 24. Military care, evacuation and demobilization

Military submits a casualty or care request with formation, location, known status, command, transport and security context. Health opens linked episodes or bounded aggregate assessments without duplicating the casualty ledger.

Evacuation requires a route, people, conveyance, access, receiving capacity and a decision about the operational cost. A victorious force can still lack transport or a receiving setting. A garrison can compete with civilian households for care inputs; that competition must be visible.

Return-to-service is a military decision using accepted health and person inputs, not a health-issued unit upgrade. Demobilization routes survivors and continuing care to household or population owners. Wounded people do not instantly replenish pools; veteran formation persistence is distinct from bodily recovery.

## 25. Captivity, displacement and relief

Captives, displaced groups and travelers can receive care episodes only when admitted subject and location scopes exist. Custody and admission remain with law, military, population and diplomatic owners. Care eligibility does not settle a person's legal status.

Relief begins with verified need and destination capacity. It reserves people, goods, transport and access, tracks distribution and observes results. A promise of assistance is not delivered care. Refuge does not imply permanent residence, property or allegiance.

Release, movement, return and reintegration are separate. Continuing care may require an accepted destination and household support even after custody ends. Missing records remain unresolved rather than being converted into a convenient death or successful return.

## 27. Restrictions, consent and political burden

A restriction must identify its evidence, subject, place, purpose, duration, permitted alternatives, food and care support, communication and release conditions. ## 28. Mortality report and life-state handoff

## 30. Burial backlog, missing identity and aftermath

## 31. Recovery, recurrence and accommodation

Recovery reviews condition change, task-specific function, continuing care, household burden, access and residual effects. States include Stabilizing, Improving, Plateaued, Relapsed, PartiallyRecovered, RecoveredWithinScope and PersistentSupportRequired.

The goal is not to return every person to a former military or labor role. Accommodation can permit a different useful role, reduced duty, supported travel or continuing household participation. Character, population and military owners approve those changes within their scope.

Recovery can outlast the original event and persist through office loss, migration or institutional disruption. Money, elapsed turns, pardon and demobilization do not erase residual effects. An episode may close while a continuing-support record remains active.

## 32. Office loss and post-ducal care continuity

Losing the Bavarian office triggers a review of office-funded care commitments, mandates, access and institutional agreements. It does not cancel privately funded Nordheim support, delete recipients or transfer institutional property.

The player may arrange handback, seek a new sponsor, preserve a smaller household service, renegotiate local cooperation or accept a reduced operating footprint. Suspended care must retain its backlog and unmet need. An orderly transfer requires receiving-owner acceptance; silence is not handback.

A former duke can remain a patron, claimant, mediator or household principal where other owners allow. Care partnerships can persist as bounded relationships without restoring the ducal title. This is a concrete survival path, not an automatic restoration reward.

## 39. AI evaluation and hard gates

The AI first checks evidence, subject existence, authority, visibility, access and owner versions. It then tests provider scope, availability, setting, goods, transport and reservation collisions. Ineligible actions are not scored as if spending more money could make them valid.

Eligible alternatives are compared for time sensitivity, continuity, expected benefit within the approved model, burden on dependents, material and political cost, reversibility and recovery. Exact weights are deferred to calibration. The AI can choose a partial plan, delay, referral, negotiation or abstention.

The Realm strategy owner arbitrates competing care, military, household and development requests. It cannot use hidden diagnosis, future history or external private state. A trace explains the strongest blocker, the rejected alternatives, the residual risk and the next review trigger.

## 40. Player presentation and prompt budget

Views separate observed concern, assessment confidence, care need, available inputs, applied support, outcome and residual burden. A leader panel uses only authorized character information. Aggregate views show scoped bands and uncertainty, not unsupported exact rates.

Each consequential prompt shows affected recipients, decision scope, real alternatives, opportunity costs, external locks and likely dependencies. Color is not the sole signal; text, icons and patterns must remain readable with localization and accessibility support.

Routine monitoring stays in summaries. A new major prompt requires a changed condition, failed dependency, contested authority or material decision. No repetitive warning is generated merely because an unresolved episode persisted another tick.

## 45. Persistence, determinism and advanced saves

Persist observations, confidence, episode state, reservations, applied care, interruptions, outcomes, residual support, mortality confirmation, disposition and trace. Preserve source and rule versions so later review can distinguish a changed model from changed evidence.

Migration must not reset an advanced campaign to January 1062, restore ended episodes, resurrect people, refill consumed goods, erase impairment, invent providers or repeat demographic loss. Ambiguous mappings are quarantined with their unresolved dependencies.

Equal approved inputs, versions, ordering and random seed must produce equal outputs. Randomized outcomes require a declared model and stored seed; no probabilities are supplied here. A retry uses the same idempotency key and cannot reroll an unfavorable result.
