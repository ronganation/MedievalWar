Their architecture demonstrates that public order must connect participation, burdens, compliance, grievance, justice reception, resistance, coercion, occupation, reintegration, cohesion, AI, persistence, and validation without rewriting the source systems.

## 24. Accommodation, Settlement, Reintegration, and Restoration

Accommodation allows bounded cooperation without full agreement. Terms may include narrower burdens, substitution, exemption, qualified recognition, local autonomy within scope, sequenced procedure, neutral access, mediated service, temporary protection, monitoring, review, and exit.

A settlement requires parties, representatives, grievances, facts, obligations, concessions, guarantees, performance owners, review, breach, expiry, and restoration.

Reintegration may require safe return, household and community recovery, restored access, property and livelihood remedy, ending exceptional restrictions, renewed participation, record correction, guarantees, monitoring, and residual-harm tracking.

Restoration is owner-executed return or repair. Public order records whether it is delivered, usable, sufficient, partial, delayed, disputed, or accompanied by new harm.

```text
Settlement != Performance
Return != Restoration
Pardon != Reintegration
Demobilization != Reconciliation
Restoration != Renewed Trust
```


For a released or returned person, public-order reintegration begins only from accepted owner receipts for release, movement, arrival, household or community reception, and any continuing care or legal restrictions. Reintegration may remain partial after military custody closes. Public order cannot mark payment, release, arrival, household restoration, property restoration, command readiness, or health recovery complete.
