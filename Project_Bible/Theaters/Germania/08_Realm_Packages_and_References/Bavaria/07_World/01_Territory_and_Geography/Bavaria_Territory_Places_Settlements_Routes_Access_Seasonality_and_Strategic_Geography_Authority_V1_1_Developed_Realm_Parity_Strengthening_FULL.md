## 9. Dual-Base Spatial Constitution

The Bavaria campaign must preserve the practical distance between the Bavarian office and Nordheim patrimony.

The dual base creates choices involving:

- personal presence in Bavaria versus Nordheim continuity;
- household staff and records in one field or the other;
- messenger delay and route risk;
- escorts, hospitality, animals, and transport;
- competing crises;
- recovery if the Bavarian office fails;
- difficulty projecting one resource base into another.

```text
Nordheim Access != Bavarian Access
Nordheim Property != Bavarian Territory
Bavarian Office != Nordheim Control
Travel Between Fields != Instant Transfer
```

This authority writes location and spatial relationships. Population, economy, built, institution, law, and public-order owners write their own state.

## 14. Augsburg Spatial Portfolio

## 15. Passau Spatial Portfolio

## 20. Route Segment Definition

A route is composed of segments. Every segment requires endpoints, route class, surface or medium, distance or travel orientation, owner and access relationships, conveyance classes, ordinary and exceptional capacity, seasonality, security, accommodation, water and forage dependencies, toll or burden references, blockage, damage, repair, and evidence.

Route definitions must avoid false precision when only general connectivity is supported.

## 22. Access Constitution

Access is relationship-specific and purpose-specific. A traveler may possess access for counsel but not armed entry, hospitality but not storage, passage but not foraging, or landing but not market privileges.

An access record must identify requesting actor, granting or controlling owner, place or segment, purpose, people, goods, animals, arms, duration, conditions, tolls or burdens, escort, notice, revocation, breach, appeal, and memory.

## 23. Reach and Travel Model

Reach depends on actual location, route chain, movement mode, party size, baggage, season, weather, access, security, stops, animals, health, and urgency.

```text
Map Distance != Travel Time
Travel Time != Guaranteed Arrival
Messenger Reach != Army Reach
Army Reach != Wagon Reach
Known Route != Available Route
Fast Movement != Costless Movement
```

Travel estimates must be ranges or state-dependent outputs unless exact implementation data is later admitted.

## 24. Itinerant Governance and Presence

The territory owner makes itinerant governance spatially real. A duke has one primary location at a time. Presence improves direct access and information in one field while creating absence elsewhere.

A visit may improve relationships, receive petitions, convene counsel, inspect a problem, or coordinate service. Repeated visits do not automatically create a capital, permanent administration, property, or control.

## 25. Messenger Communication and Record Movement

Messages and records move through routes and couriers. Delivery depends on origin, destination, messenger, route, access, authentication, weather, security, replacement relays, and recipient reachability.

Records may be copied, lost, delayed, intercepted, damaged, disputed, or delivered after circumstances change. Territory owns physical movement; governance and information owners own message meaning and knowledge effects.

## 26. Muster Assembly and Service Geography

A valid muster or assembly site requires purpose, competent convener, access, space, water, food, forage, shelter, security, routes, participating owners, notice, season, duration, dispersal, and recovery.

```text
NamedMusterSite != Troops
AssemblyCapacity != Attendance
AccessibleSite != SuppliedSite
```

## 27. Trade Market Hospitality and Material Access Interface

Territory defines where markets, hospitality sites, stores, production sites, grazing, water, and transport interfaces may connect. Economy, population, property, institution, and built owners define the actual capacities, goods, rights, people, and structures.

A route reaching a market does not guarantee goods, credit, permission, safety, or affordable prices.

## 28. Crossings Bridges Fords Ferries Landings and Portages

Every crossing requires type, banks or approaches, owner, access, capacity, operating people and equipment, water or terrain condition, seasonality, queues, security, tolls, damage, alternatives, and recovery.

A bridge, ferry, ford, landing, or portage cannot be assumed from a place name or modern map. Loss of one crossing may reroute movement rather than make the region absolutely impassable.

## 32. Blockage Inspection Repair and Reopening

A blockage record identifies location, cause, knowledge, affected movement, responsible or controlling owners, alternatives, urgency, inspection, people, tools, materials, security, cost, work, verification, reopening limits, and later maintenance.

A ducal order does not repair a road or crossing. Research does not create workers or materials. Reopening may restore only partial capacity.

## 33. Occupation Control and Presence

Occupation and control require a present actor, forces or personnel, access, supply, communications, local responses, duration, and legal or political claims. Occupation does not automatically transfer property, jurisdiction, title, institutional allegiance, or population support.

Control may be direct, contested, intermittent, route-bound, site-specific, or nominal. One occupied site does not create control of the surrounding region.

## 34. Siege Raid Devastation and Displacement Interface

Territory records affected places, routes, access, damage, blockage, isolation, and spatial recovery. Military owners record forces and operations. Population owners record people, casualties, flight, displacement, and return. Economy and built owners record goods, production, buildings, and repair.

Raid damage cannot be reduced to a generic province penalty. Consequences should follow what was actually affected.

## 35. Strategic Geography Portfolio

Bavaria's strategic geography should produce understandable campaign choices:

- secure Danube-connected communication or rely on slower alternatives;
- maintain access among major ecclesiastical and political centers;
- preserve northern communication with Nordheim;
- invest in Alpine and southern access for service or missions;
- protect crossings and hospitality chains;
- choose personal presence between competing fields;
- accept that route closure may alter diplomacy, justice, logistics, and military response.

These are portfolios, not opening modifiers.

## 39. Decision Package Assembly or Muster Site

The player may choose a site based on political neutrality, actor access, institutional hospitality, routes, water, food, forage, shelter, defenses, or proximity to the problem.

## 40. Decision Package Danube or Alpine Orientation

Investment or political effort may improve information, relationships, maintenance, protection, or coordination. It cannot create vessels, roads, passes, labor, goods, or permission.

## 46. Opening State Consumption Contract

Opening State may consume only admitted places, regional fields, exact locations, settlements, routes, crossings, access, condition, and spatial relationships accepted by their owners.

It may not infer a capital from prominence, a route from two known endpoints, a crossing from a river, control from ducal title, a garrison from a fortress reference, an institution from a building, or population and goods from settlement status.

## 47. Positive Fixtures

- <Person>Otto of Nordheim</Person> can be present in Bavaria while Nordheim continuity incurs communication and response costs.
- <Place>Regensburg</Place> is strategically important without becoming automatic capital or ducal property.
- A route exists but armed access is denied.
- A messenger route is usable while wagon movement is restricted.
- Winter closes or degrades one segment while an alternative remains possible.
- A damaged crossing reopens at partial capacity after owner-executed repair.
- An assembly changes venue because hospitality, access, or political acceptance fails.
- Occupation of a site does not transfer surrounding property or jurisdiction.
- Danube movement requires real landing, vessel, access, season, and security receipts.

## 48. Negative Fixtures

- Bavaria becomes one uniformly owned and controlled map region.
- <Place>Regensburg</Place> is inserted as permanent capital without evidence.
- Modern roads, borders, or settlement scale are back-projected.
- A river creates automatic navigation.
- A place name creates a settlement, fortress, institution, market, or garrison.
- Ducal title creates route access.
- Map adjacency permits travel without a route chain.
- Research creates roads, workers, materials, vessels, or safe passage.
- Occupation transfers property and jurisdiction automatically.
- Historical travel creates one fixed opening travel time.
