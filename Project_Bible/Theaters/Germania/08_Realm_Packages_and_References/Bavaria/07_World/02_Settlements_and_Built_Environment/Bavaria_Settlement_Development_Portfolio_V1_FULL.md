## 9. Bavaria Settlement Identity

Bavaria's settlement identity is expressed through connected regional networks rather than one universal settlement model. Routes, river interfaces, ecclesiastical centers, ducal movement, local lordship, household production, markets, craft concentrations, agricultural support, woodland resources, animal transport, and defensible access can combine differently from place to place.

The design should support:

- politically important places without fabricated palace complexes;
- ecclesiastical centers whose institutions and physical assets remain distinct;
- markets whose activity depends on routes, operators, security, storage, and demand;
- route settlements whose strength may come from access and hospitality rather than scale;
- productive settlements constrained by labor, fuel, water, transport, or maintenance;
- defensive sites that control access without becoming self-sufficient cities;
- local and regional centers whose usefulness does not require ducal ownership;
- household-dense places with substantial economic life but limited formal monumental capacity.

## 11. Settlement Development State Model

Development is multidimensional. A settlement may improve in one dimension while stagnating or declining in another.

```yaml
DevelopmentDimensions:
  HouseholdCapacity: ShelterDomesticWorkStorageDependentsAndResilience
  ServiceCapacity: WaterWasteFireHospitalityCareBurialAndAccess
  ProductiveCapacity: WorkshopsProcessingToolsFuelWaterAndOperators
  ExchangeCapacity: MarketSpaceStorageInspectionSecurityAndRouteAccess
  AdministrativeCapacity: AudienceRecordsPetitionsCustodyDelegationAndContinuity
  InstitutionalCapacity: WorshipCommunityEducationHospitalityCareAndBurialSupport
  MilitarySupportCapacity: MusterStoresQuartersAnimalsWarningAndDefensiveAccess
  InfrastructureCapacity: RoadsApproachesCrossingsLandingsDrainageAndRetainingWorks
  RecoveryCapacity: InspectionMaintenanceRepairReliefSalvageAndRestoration
DevelopmentStates:
  Unknown: EvidenceInsufficient
  Fragile: EssentialDependenciesFrequentlyFail
  Constrained: DemandExceedsOneOrMoreCriticalCapacities
  Serviceable: CoreFunctionsOperateWithinAcceptedLimits
  Strengthening: SupportedImprovementsAreIncreasingReliableCapacity
  Specialized: DistinctCapabilitiesExceedLocalBaselineWithoutImplyingUniversalStrength
  Overburdened: UseExceedsMaintenanceServiceOrAccessCapacity
  Stagnating: ConstraintsPreventSustainedImprovement
  Regressing: CapabilityOrReliabilityIsBeingLost
  Recovering: AcceptedRepairReorganizationOrSupportIsRestoringCapability
```

No single score may silently collapse these dimensions.

## 12. Building-Family Constitution

Building families group compatible functions and development possibilities. They do not guarantee identical architecture, linear upgrades, or opening instances.

Each family definition must specify:

- purpose and player-facing identity;
- eligible settlement contexts;
- site, right, access, labor, material, operator, and maintenance dependencies;
- compatible and competing families;
- chain, branch, adaptation, and conversion possibilities;
- capacity dimensions affected;
- seasonal and environmental exposure;
- public-order burdens and benefits;
- military, economy, population, governance, institutional, territory, and presentation interfaces;
- failure, damage, closure, recovery, and reversibility logic;
- evidence and Opening-State restrictions.

## 26. Named-Place Research Interfaces

Potential research concerns include Danube and eastern-route transfer, crossings, landings, institutional sites, hospitality, storage, transport support, markets, records, water, defenses, and external movement interfaces. River importance creates no vessel, harbor, ferry, bridge, passage right, or military transport.

### 26.5 Nordheim

Nordheim requires a separate patrimonial expression covering household, residence, defense, storage, service, records, hospitality, animals, transport, routes, care, and recovery. Nordheim property does not become Bavarian ducal property through officeholding, shared methods, political service, or temporary use.

## 31. Construction and Conversion Decision Doctrine

New construction is only one response. The player and AI should compare:

- maintain existing capacity;
- repair defects;
- restore damaged capability;
- adapt an existing asset;
- convert underused space;
- reopen a closed service;
- improve a dependency or approach;
- distribute a function across several sites;
- provide a temporary service;
- abandon an unsafe or unsustainable asset;
- construct a new bounded asset.

The system should prefer the least wasteful owner-valid solution that meets the accepted need without concealing future maintenance and opportunity costs.

## 32. Interaction With Population and Households

Population determines households, labor, skills, migration, displacement, and settlement pressure. This portfolio may request labor or identify housing and service constraints, but it cannot convert population totals directly into workers or construction throughput.

Development must protect household survival, seasonal work, food production, care, fuel, shelter, and local defense minima. Requisition or compulsory burden can create grievance, avoidance, displacement, and development failure even when a project is physically feasible.

## 33. Interaction With Economy and Material Flow

Economy owns resources, production, property, revenue, animals, equipment, tools, stocks, transport, and delivery. Settlement development consumes confirmed reservations and does not duplicate goods across projects.

Markets, workshops, stores, stables, and service yards provide physical capacity only. They do not create trade, production, stock, animals, carts, money, tools, or supply.

## 34. Interaction With Governance, Law, and Institutions

Ducal competence, delegated action, local rights, assemblies, petitions, custom, privilege, jurisdiction, judgment, and institutional ownership determine what may be attempted and contested.

Development should permit negotiated, hosted, shared, temporary, local, ecclesiastical, and ducal arrangements. It must not assume that useful Realm capacity is centrally owned or that a ducal objective overrides property, institution, custom, procedure, remedy, or local acceptance.

## 35. Interaction With Military Systems

Settlement development may provide muster areas, secure storage, quarters, animal support, workshops, warning points, defensive access, repair capacity, and route control. Military authorities retain formations, recruitment, readiness, command, garrison assignment, equipment, supply, casualties, recovery, and demobilization.

Military use can overburden households, markets, routes, water, storage, animals, and maintenance. Such burdens must be visible rather than absorbed by a generic settlement modifier.

## 36. Interaction With Knowledge and Reform

Knowledge and development programs may unlock proposals, surveys, standards, methods, trials, organization, and adaptation. They may improve the quality of future decisions but cannot create the necessary people, property, money, materials, equipment, animals, access, political consent, construction, or operation.

```text
Knowledge Unlock != Physical Asset
Method Adoption != Available Skilled Labor
Design Improvement != Materials
Program Completion != Commissioned Capacity
Regional Diffusion != Universal Adoption
```

## 37. Public Order and Distributional Fairness

Every development choice has beneficiaries, burden bearers, excluded users, opportunity costs, and recovery obligations. The interface should show:

- who requested and authorized the work;
- who owns and operates the result;
- who supplies labor, materials, animals, land, access, and maintenance;
- which household, institution, route, market, or defense minimum is affected;
- who gains access and who may lose access;
- compensation, accommodation, petition, and remedy routes;
- reversibility, handback, and closure consequences.

Lawful construction can still be politically damaging. Resistance can reveal an unsustainable burden without erasing the underlying need.

## 43. Collision and Nonduplication Protection

The system must block:

- double reservation of labor, money, materials, animals, tools, transport, or route capacity;
- the same physical asset being represented as several free family instances;
- simultaneous incompatible uses without conversion or scheduling;
- ducal ownership inferred from Realm usefulness;
- ecclesiastical identity inferred from a physical structure;
- military formations inferred from defensive facilities;
- production inferred from workshops;
- stock inferred from stores;
- horses inferred from stables;
- bridge, ferry, landing, or harbor inferred from geography alone;
- population inferred from housing capacity;
- development rank inferred from political prominence;
- later architecture back-projected into 1062.

## 44. Persistence and Advanced Saves

Persist settlement development dimensions, archetype evidence, family expressions, specializations, dependencies, constraints, projects, maintenance, damage, closures, conversions, burdens, benefits, ownership references, confidence, and decision trace.

Migration must not reset an advanced save to 1062, reveal unknown assets, create missing buildings, normalize every settlement to one type, erase decline, repair damage, refill stocks, repopulate households, restore institutions, add garrisons, or convert Nordheim property into Bavarian property.

## 46. Positive Fixtures

- A politically important place remains architecturally unknown while receiving an administrative research portfolio.
- A market-route settlement strengthens storage and hospitality but remains constrained by route maintenance.
- An ecclesiastical center expands care-support space without transferring institutional ownership to the duke.
- A workshop expression remains inactive because skilled operators and inputs are unavailable.
- A bridge definition is eligible at a crossing, but no bridge instance is created.
- A defensive site improves warning and protected storage without generating a garrison.
- A household-dense settlement improves water and fire access before pursuing monumental construction.
- A damaged service asset is repaired because repair solves the need more safely than replacement.
- Nordheim supports a temporary Bavarian need through an explicit transfer and handback arrangement.
- An advanced save preserves specialization, maintenance debt, partial conversion, displacement, and recovery history.

## 47. Negative Fixtures

- Settlement rank generates walls, market, church, workshops, stores, garrison, and population.
- Regensburg's importance generates a capital palace complex.
- An ecclesiastical institution automatically generates a complete physical complex.
- A market building generates traders, goods, demand, revenue, and security.
- A workshop generates craftspeople, tools, inputs, and output.
- A stable generates horses or cavalry.
- A storehouse generates stock.
- A fortification generates a garrison.
- A development program instantly completes construction.
- A settlement specializes in every path without maintenance or opportunity cost.
- Ducal use confiscates local, institutional, or Nordheim property.
- Seasonal progression automatically completes projects.
- Later buildings are asserted as opening assets.
