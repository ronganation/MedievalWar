## 6. Bavaria Presentation Pillars
1. **Recent ducal appointment, incomplete authority.** Show recognized status and political opportunity without implying settled governance or uniform obedience.
2. **Dual power bases.** Bavaria and Nordheim must remain visually connected but unmistakably separate in ownership, distance, resources, people, and risk.
3. **Regional actors retain agency.** Counts, households, institutions, settlements, communities, contributors, and external powers must never collapse into one Bavarian voice.
4. **Itinerant reach.** Roads, Danube segments, Alpine approaches, messengers, hospitality, records, and travel should carry political meaning.
5. **Finite military and material power.** Service sources, pools, equipment, animals, supply, garrisons, casualties, demobilization, and recovery stay visible.
6. **Institutions are operational actors.** Ecclesiastical institutions, courts, archives, care, sanctuary, property, and jurisdiction are not decorative backdrops.
7. **Performance creates authority.** Protection, justice, consultation, service, communication, and follow-through should shape the player-facing read.
8. **Office loss is transformation.** Former-duke, restoration, retrenchment, service, coalition, or mediation paths stay legible.
9. **History remains conditional.** Kaiserswerth, Royal service, accusation, office loss, opposition, pardon, and restoration must never appear inevitable.

## 7. Anti-Flattening Rules
- Do not present Bavaria as one uniformly owned province.
- Do not present every Bavarian actor as loyal, hostile, culturally identical, or militarily interchangeable.
- Do not present Nordheim as a hidden Bavarian second treasury, manpower pool, or reserve army.
- Do not make Regensburg an automatic capital, complete court, archive, arsenal, garrison, or market complex.
- Do not reduce the faction to generic German cavalry, spearmen, Alpine imagery, or later Bavarian stereotypes.
- Do not use later Welf, Wittelsbach, royal, electoral, modern-state, or national imagery as opening identity.
- Do not turn Otto into a heroic super-unit, born rebel, or inevitable enemy of Royal government.
- Do not turn Richenza into a passive spouse modifier.
- Do not show every Church surface as ducal, Papal, or politically aligned.
- Do not use one relationship line, loyalty bar, public-order meter, or map tint as complete truth.
- Do not hide labor, households, routes, records, supply, maintenance, casualties, and recovery behind court imagery.

## 45. Positive Validation Fixtures
- Bavaria and Nordheim appear connected but separately owned and separately burdened.
- Otto reads as an established leader without royal regalia, hero aura, predetermined rebellion, or exact likeness claims.
- Regensburg reads as important without becoming an automatic capital or complete administrative complex.
- Unknown regional control uses qualified unknown rather than faction fill.
- A candidate unit family never appears as an opening recruitable card.
- A message line shows delivery and confidence without revealing foreign intent.
- An office-loss state changes campaign presentation without deleting the faction.
- A development trial appears locally realized while the rest of Bavaria remains unknown or blocked.
- Accessibility preserves meaning without color, motion, or audio.
- A version mismatch blocks or visibly degrades presentation rather than silently displaying stale truth.

## 46. Negative Validation Fixtures

- One map tint implies control of all Bavaria and Nordheim.
- Regensburg icon creates court, treasury, archive, market, garrison, and capital status.
- Generic German armor, music, architecture, or military silhouettes substitute for unknown evidence.
- Richenza is reduced to a passive spouse bonus.
- A child receives adult office, command, or heir presentation.
- A Church icon implies ducal property or obedience.
- Royal recognition appears as Bavarian loyalty.
- A route line implies permission, safety, speed, and supply.
- An event card presents Kaiserswerth or office loss as inevitable.

- Presentation writes owner state or creates an asset binding.
