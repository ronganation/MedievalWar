## 1. Executive verdict

Bavaria should behave as an ambitious but recoverable office-centered political project. Its purpose is to make the Bavarian ducal office useful through regional performance, selective Royal participation, material reliability, and relationships while preserving the separate Nordheim foundation. The faction must be capable of decisive action, not trapped in endless consultation. It must also be capable of refusing an attractive commitment when the necessary people, authority, routes, supply, or recovery do not exist.

The defining decision rhythm is **understand, negotiate, prepare, commit, perform, settle, recover, review**. Negotiation is not a mandatory delay before every action. Existing accepted authority and capacity can support immediate bounded execution. Conversely, high strategic value never turns missing authority or external consent into a mere scoring penalty.

This authority closes the integration gap between the many specialist AI doctrines. It selects and explains cross-system proposals; specialist owners still determine whether those proposals can execute and what actually changes.

## 3. Identity formula and personality statement

**Bavaria = executable appointment + independent patrimony + negotiated regional power + selective Royal opportunity + recoverable transformation.**

The Realm's strategic identity is neither a centralized Bavarian nation-state nor Otto's private household enlarged into a duchy. It is the attempt to connect a public office, a separately owned patrimonial foundation, and regional partners into durable action. It values the office because the office opens opportunities; it values Nordheim because independent continuity makes those opportunities survivable. Neither should automatically consume the other.

The core fear is **winning the title's appearance while losing the capacity to act or recover**. That fear generates concrete choices: limit a mission, retain a home reserve, negotiate a narrower service request, protect records and dependents, repair a bottleneck, or abandon an unrecoverable restoration attempt.

The central question is: **Can a recent appointee turn Bavaria into functioning regional power without exhausting Nordheim, erasing regional owners, or becoming dependent on one person's constant presence?**

## 4. Core values and decision effects

Five values define the design baseline. They are ranked for interpretation, not assigned numerical bonuses.

1. **Continuing agency.** Preserve a viable household, lawful acting principal, communication, and recoverable material base. This is not an absolute prohibition on risking Otto or troops; a survival emergency can justify exposure after alternatives are examined.
2. **Executable authority.** Prefer a smaller competence that can actually be exercised to a broader claim with no means of performance. Success is measured by delivered protection, settlement, service, or coordination, not the number of titles displayed.
3. **Reliable reciprocal cooperation.** Keep accepted promises, recognize contributor costs, and settle obligations. Reliability improves future options only through actual owner-recorded reception.
4. **Independent political flexibility.** Royal service, mediation, regional accommodation, and coalition participation remain available. No permanent loyalist or rebel label selects the answer.
5. **Durable capability.** Invest in people, records, routes, stores, delegation, and maintenance that remain useful beyond a single mission or leader's visit.

When values conflict, the decision trace names the conflict. Protecting Nordheim may delay Bavarian presence; honoring a service promise may constrain a new opportunity; preserving an institution's autonomy may reduce immediate ducal reach.

## 5. Realm, leader, dynasty, office, and player separation

Realm personality is the design interpretation layer. Otto's preferences and intentions belong to the character owner. Nordheim generational interests belong to dynasty and household owners. The ducal office's competence belongs to title and law. Player strategic focus is a choice of emphasis, not permission to rewrite those owners.

A character may recommend a riskier course than the Realm's default. A household may favor retrenchment while Bavarian partners favor continued presence. An office duty may remain important despite a player's preferred external campaign. These disagreements produce alternatives and costs, not automatic disloyalty.

The integrated AI records recommendations that have actually been communicated. It never invents Richenza's advice, a count's private intention, or a Royal response to fill a consultation matrix.

## 8. Opening interpretation and unknown-state policy

## 10. Internal contradictions and success modes

Six recurring tensions create Bavaria's dilemmas: office assertion versus regional acceptance; Bavaria versus Nordheim attention; Royal access versus independent flexibility; personal command versus government continuity; immediate extraction versus reliable partnership; restoration ambition versus post-ducal opportunity.

Success does not trigger universal hostility or a Realm Divide script. It creates owner-reviewed follow-up: partners may expect payment, increased office activity may require more clerks, a victory may expose supply debt, and a successful delegate may need a clearer mandate. The AI first consolidates delivered success before assuming capacity for another commitment.

Crisis mode is temporary. It can prioritize protection, safe access, records, and recovery, but cannot create emergency jurisdiction or a regent. Repeated owner-recorded changes may justify a slow personality reinterpretation. One defeat, one refusal, or one appointment does not overwrite the Realm's identity.

## 11. Strategic geography and operational fields

The Bavarian field, Nordheim field, Danube-connected field, western approaches, eastern contacts, Alpine interfaces, and Royal venues are planning portfolios, not owned polygons. The AI compares actual routes and access for each mission.

Regensburg may be useful for counsel or institutional access without becoming a capital. Freising may be useful for records or hospitality without supplying free clerks. Augsburg-facing travel may compete with eastern commitments. Passau-facing movement may depend on river-land transfers. None of these portfolios proves opening assets, permission, or travel time.

The strategic value of a node is its current contribution to an objective, alternatives, exposure, and recovery. A map label alone cannot outrank an essential route or a household's continuity.

## 12. Authority, expansion, and defense doctrine

Authority should expand through demonstrated functions: a received petition, a completed protection agreement, a negotiated service contribution, or a reliable delegated task. The AI may propose broader competence only when evidence and title/law review support it. It cannot manufacture jurisdiction from successful performance.

Expansion includes influence, access, recognized competence, and dependable cooperation, not only conquest. Territorial or military expansion requires an admitted objective, authority, force, supply, local response, and a settlement plan. There is no default Bavarian conquest target.

Defense protects people, essential assets, communication, local support, and a recoverable force. It may trade exposure or abandon a costly position through owners. Defense is not an order to keep every garrison immobile or refuse every field battle.

## 13. Household, succession, and leader-dependency doctrine

Before a major external commitment, compare Otto's availability, household protection, dependents, records, delegates, property exposure, and command replacement. Richenza may contribute only through accepted role and availability receipts. Children remain age- and evidence-controlled.

The AI distinguishes personal leadership dependency, administrative dependency, command dependency, and relationship dependency. Remedies include narrower commitments, trained successors, bounded delegates, institutional record continuity, reserve command, and explicit handback. These are development proposals, not spawned people.

Nordheim inheritance, household leadership, comital rights, military command, and Bavarian appointment remain separate succession surfaces. The AI cannot select a single universal heir or use later history to determine a successor.

## 14. Regional and ecclesiastical consultation doctrine

Consult the actors materially affected by a decision, not every actor in the graph. A military request needs contributor and burden assessments; an institutional property matter needs its institution and legal forum; a route project needs access and physical owners. Consultation should have a purpose and response window, not become recurring paperwork.

A refusal is examined for competence, burden, season, availability, terms, and alternative contribution. The AI can accept partial cooperation as strategically valuable. It may assert an accepted right through lawful procedure when negotiation fails, but coercion requires its own basis and consequences.

Church cooperation is institution-specific. Patronage, sanctuary, records, hospitality, care, and mediation have different owners and costs. The AI cannot treat one bishop's response as Bavaria's entire Church position or exchange patronage for automatic appointment power.

## 15. Military and material doctrine

A mission is bounded by political purpose, contributors, command, muster, equipment, animals, supply, route, home reserve, losses, return, and demobilization. Potential recruitment is not ready force. Local defense is not a free army. Theater regional units and mercenaries remain externally owned or independently contracted.

The AI prefers combined battlefield roles when admitted formations exist, but never creates unit cards or formations to fill a tactical gap. If the available army lacks reconnaissance or supply, it may resize, delay, hire through valid contracts, or decline the mission.

Economy proposals compare office resources and Nordheim resources separately. Money is not delivery. A workshop is not output. Repair competes with military equipment for labor and materials. Harvest, fodder, household minima, and maintenance are considered through population/economy receipts, not invented seasonal yields.

## 16. Diplomacy and Royal-service doctrine

Royal engagement is neither submission nor rebellion. An authenticated request produces alternatives: full service, limited service, substitution, delegation where allowed, clarification, delay, mediation, refusal, or abstention. Compare the specific terms, home exposure, contributors, routes, supply, reward uncertainty, and return.

Expected Royal standing is a forecast, not a reward receipt. The AI cannot assume a foreign actor will accept a proposal or reveal private intention. Coalition partners retain their own forces, objectives, command terms, and withdrawal options.

Hungarian, papal/Italia, Swabian, Bohemian, Alpine, and Saxon portfolios are issue-specific. Later campaigns show possible lanes in the supplied evidence model; they do not become opening assignments or scheduled objectives.

## 17. Law, public order, and restoration doctrine

The AI distinguishes a valid judgment, available enforcement, actual compliance, and accepted restoration. A favorable decision with no execution capacity may require mediation, sequencing, or a narrower remedy. Rank does not establish standing or proof.

Domestic response is issue-specific. Burden stacking can make a lawful request impractical. The AI compares inability, refusal, grievance, resistance, and armed conflict separately. Compensation promises remain outstanding until delivered and accepted.

After coercion, occupation, or office crisis, the plan includes restitution, care, repair, return, review, and residual harm. A pardon does not restore property; returned property does not restore trust; a reopened route does not erase its damage history.

## 18. Development and built-environment doctrine

Prefer investments that remove demonstrated bottlenecks: reliable records, a qualified operator, usable storage, safe access, equipment maintenance, or a route repair. Prestige construction can be considered only after its own inputs and competing essential needs are visible.

Knowledge passes through evaluation, trial, adaptation, adoption, realization, and maintenance. The AI cannot turn a development preference into a capability. A completed building still requires commissioning, people, goods, procedures, and maintenance.

A small reversible trial is often preferable under uncertainty, but not automatically best. A genuine immediate threat may require a larger lawful commitment. The trace explains why the chosen scope is proportionate.

## 19. Historical emergence and office-loss doctrine

Kaiserswerth, accusation, ban, office loss, pardon, opposition, and restoration remain conditional owner-controlled pathways. Historical dates can request an eligibility review; they never select an outcome or provide a hidden utility bonus.

On valid office loss, immediately review office-dependent mandates and resources. Preserve personal, household, patrimonial, and institutional links where their owners permit. Compare restoration, reconciliation, service elsewhere, mediation, retrenchment, and coalition options.

Restoration is not the only successful campaign future. A former duke may retain substantial agency through an accepted new role. Conversely, transformation cannot magically preserve resources that were actually lost or legally transferred.

## 20. Integrated decision pipeline

1. Consume resolved owner changes and the acting principal's visible information.
2. Identify a concrete threat, obligation, bottleneck, or opportunity.
3. Determine who may decide and who must execute.
4. Generate materially distinct options, including narrower action and no action where valid.
5. Request scoped specialist assessments and communicated actor recommendations.
6. Apply hard eligibility gates before comparison.
7. Compare eligible options across strategic benefit, burden, uncertainty, recovery, and displaced commitments.
8. Check portfolio collisions and dependency order.
9. Select a proposal or abstain with a review trigger.
10. Request owner preflight; reserve only through owners.
11. Revalidate versions and obtain execution receipts.
12. Record actual results separately from forecasts; review follow-up and recovery.

The pipeline is a future implementation design, not a procedure executed against project runtime in this delivery.

## 22. Priority and risk comparison

Priority combines importance, urgency, irreversibility, existing obligations, and current strategic focus. Urgency is not importance; importance is not feasibility. An expiring prestige opportunity cannot silently displace dependent care or a binding service return.

Risk domains include leader exposure, household continuity, office security, regional reception, Royal/external dependence, military losses, material flow, routes, institutional fragility, public-order harm, and irreversibility. Bands are Unknown, Limited, Manageable, Elevated, Severe, Critical, and Contested. Unknown is never Low.

For eligible alternatives, use qualitative comparison before numerical tuning. Favor strategic contribution, executable benefit, promise reliability, regional fit, and future options; penalize home exposure, burden, recovery debt, uncertain dependency, and irreversible loss. Weights, units, normalization, and balance thresholds remain deferred. This is not an executable formula with invented values.

## 23. Portfolio arbitration and attention

Maintain one primary objective and up to two supporting objectives as a design default. Existing recovery and maintenance duties remain tracked even when not displayed as strategic objectives. Emergency issues can interrupt the primary focus without deleting its commitments.

Conflict dispositions are coexist, sequence, narrow, delay, delegate, redirect, substitute, suspend, close, or hold. Arbitration records which action loses time or resources. No hidden cancellation is allowed.

Otto's attention is constrained by location, travel, communication, mandates, and the complexity of concurrent decisions. It is not a universal mana resource. The player receives a small set of consequential prompts plus a digest of routine owner-resolved work. Critical unresolved harm cannot be hidden in the digest.

## 24. Decision package: establish a working Bavarian foothold

**Question:** Which useful ducal function should be made reliable first?

Eligibility requires office orientation accepted by title, an authorized principal, and a material regional problem. Exact site, host, personnel, and costs are supplied by owners.

Options:
- Personal circuit: improves direct access and information but increases travel and Nordheim absence.
- Bounded receiving arrangement: supports petitions, records, and messages but requires a host, personnel, supplies, and lawful access.
- Issue-specific consultation: builds a limited coalition around protection or mediation but allows conditions and refusal.
- Narrow preparation: preserves resources while clarifying competence; risks continued nominal authority.

Success is an accepted functioning service, not a new capital or universal recognition. Failure can produce delay, a smaller scope, a different host, or withdrawal. Review after first performance, a refusal, changed access, or a household warning.

## 25. Decision package: Royal service versus regional consolidation

**Question:** Does this request justify exposing both power bases?

Compare full commitment, limited contribution, substitute support, diplomatic service, delayed participation, or refusal. Required assessments include authenticated terms, competence, contributor consent, home minima, routes, supply, command, return, and compensation.

Full commitment may produce greater opportunity but also greater absence and recovery burden. Limited service may satisfy part of the request without meeting all expectations. Refusal may preserve capacity while costing a visible opportunity; the external owner determines reception.

No option grants a reward or creates hostility directly. The AI records the request, offered terms, actual performance, and counterpart receipt. Review on changed mission terms, supply failure, home crisis, completion, or return.

## 26. Decision package: regional contributor cannot meet a burden

**Question:** Is the problem inability, disputed competence, or refusal, and what remains useful?

Options are inspect evidence, narrow the request, accept substitution, compensate through available resources, reschedule, mediate, refer to law, or enforce a valid obligation through competent owners. Enforcement is not an automatic response to nonperformance.

Population supplies labor and household effects; economy supplies goods and compensation; military supplies service availability; public order supplies reception; network routes the response. A partial contribution is recorded as partial, not full compliance or rebellion.

Review after a delivered explanation, seasonal change, compensation receipt, new capacity, or escalation. Repeated requests without changed state are suppressed.

## 27. Decision package: protect Nordheim without abandoning Bavaria

**Question:** How much can be committed south while retaining an independent recovery base?

Compare a bounded transfer, a smaller Bavarian objective, stronger communication, a delegate, delayed service, or patrimonial retrenchment. Each transfer has owner, purpose, duration, risk, accounting, and return.

The AI must show the opportunity cost: a messenger used for Bavarian coordination is unavailable elsewhere; a mounted household core sent away changes protection; money reserved for an expedition cannot also fund repair.

Success is compatible continuity in both fields, not a merged pool. Failure may require return, narrowed scope, compensation, or an accepted reduction of Bavarian reach.

## 28. Decision package: material bottleneck versus prestige opportunity

**Question:** Should the faction repair a limiting route, store, or equipment pipeline before pursuing an attractive mission?

Compare repair first, mission at reduced scale, an alternative route, external contracted support, or delay. Built environment determines the physical work; economy determines inputs and delivery; population determines workers; territory determines access and seasonal conditions.

The AI can select the mission when a real window and sufficient alternative capacity exist. It cannot justify skipping maintenance by assuming future loot or a Royal reward. Forecast income remains uncertain and cannot fund a present reservation.

Review when inspection, delivery, route condition, or opportunity window changes.

## 29. Decision package: judicial or office-security crisis

**Question:** How can Otto answer a challenge without confusing accusation, guilt, office, and private property?

Options include appearance with safeguards, representation where valid, jurisdictional contest, mediation, safe-conduct negotiation, conditional submission, delay for evidence, refusal with stated basis, or owner-reviewed resistance.

Law supplies standing, forum, evidence, procedure, and remedies. Title supplies office state. Diplomacy supplies Royal communication and reciprocal terms. Household and economy identify exposed people and property. Military assesses security but cannot determine guilt.

A selected response is not an office-loss event. Review after notice, evidence, safe-conduct receipt, forum change, judgment, appeal, or external decision.

## 30. Decision package: former-duke strategy

**Question:** Which future remains executable after valid loss of Bavaria?

Compare restoration, reconciliation without restoration, service elsewhere, mediation, coalition leadership, or patrimonial retrenchment. Each requires current actors, relationships, capacity, and external acceptance.

Restoration can be valuable but may be dominated by a less costly new role. The AI cannot maximize a historical restoration goal indefinitely while household continuity deteriorates. A failed restoration attempt preserves costs and memory rather than resetting the claim.

Office resources undergo handback or dispute through owners. Personal and Nordheim relationships are reviewed independently. Review when new recognition, settlement terms, office vacancy, capacity, or leader state changes.

## 31. Integrated consultation and disagreement

Request advice only from actors with accepted roles, access, availability, and relevant knowledge. Otto may provide principal direction; Richenza may provide household-continuity input where admitted; delegates report within mandate; contributors assess their own service; institutions assess their own jurisdiction and capacity; external actors respond through diplomacy.

Store the communicated recommendation, scope, confidence, date, source, and limits. Do not infer private emotion or motivation. Conflicting advice remains visible where authorized. The AI explains why one recommendation was outweighed without classifying its author as disloyal.

If a required advisor is absent, seek an accepted substitute, reduce scope, or hold. Never spawn a generic chancellor, marshal, bishop, or regent to complete the packet.

## 32. Objectives, success, and difficulty

Objective families include working ducal function, reliable regional partnership, secure dual-base communication, recoverable military service, maintained material capacity, credible mediation, and continuity beyond Otto. These are design definitions, not active opening objectives.

Completion requires an owner-confirmed outcome and follow-up disposition. A promise is not completion; an agreement is not performance; a battle win is not settlement. Partial success can be strategically meaningful.

The player experience should be demanding because authority and capacity must be coordinated, not because the UI hides costs or requires constant micromanagement. Difficulty tuning must not supply hidden knowledge, free troops, automatic recognition, or fabricated external consent. No difficulty rating or numerical handicap is committed here.

## 33. Failure, recovery, and adaptive review

Failures include nominal office, broken promises, material overextension, regional withdrawal, leader capture, lost communication, coalition collapse, military depletion, and failed restoration. A failure can alter posture without erasing the core identity.

Recovery plans identify remaining people, property, access, relationships, obligations, and maintenance. Retrenchment is an active strategy when it preserves future agency. Abstention requires a reason and review trigger; it is not permanent inactivity.

Review after material changes in life/availability, office state, regional response, route access, funds or stock, formation readiness, legal process, external terms, public-order pressure, or capability realization. Reevaluate affected candidates rather than the whole strategic universe every frame.

## 34. Player-facing explanation contract

A consequential recommendation displays: the question, acting authority, visible facts, protected unknowns, recommended option, two material alternatives where available, required inputs, displaced commitments, expected range, strongest blocker, exit, recovery, and review trigger.

Example design wording: 'Limited service preserves the household reserve and meets the contribution currently accepted by the military owner. Full service remains blocked by transport and return support. Royal reception is not yet known.' This is illustrative UI copy, not an actual opening report.

The explanation cannot expose a foreign private value, an undisclosed relationship, or an unreceived message. Accessibility requires plain-language summaries, expandable detail, non-color-only state, and no compulsory short timer for a major strategic choice.
