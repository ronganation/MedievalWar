## 5. Education: current static resolution

Education V1 now defines learning arrangements, practice, assessment, qualification recommendations, interruption, lapse, successor preparation, knowledge transmission, translation, cultural production, and technical preservation while preserving character, population, institution, military, governance, law, economy, built-environment, development and presentation ownership. No opening school, teacher, literacy rate, curriculum, archive inventory or personal proficiency is created.

### V1 finding retained as provenance

Population records occupation, skill availability, labor reservation, and training requests. It explicitly leaves education to another owner. Development governs adoption and program coordination while excluding literacy, skills, training, and qualifications from its writes. Ecclesiastical institutions provide possible hosts and function-specific learning capacity; household provides formation needs and coordination. Military owns formation training and readiness. These surfaces establish demand and consumers, not one general learning lifecycle.

The proposed education owner should govern learning definitions, teaching arrangements, knowledge transmission, assessment and qualification receipts. Character owners retain named-person private knowledge and exact proficiency. Population retains people and availability. Military retains unit training acceptance, formation drill, and field readiness. Institutional owners retain permission and host capacity. Development consumes qualified capability without manufacturing it.

A strong first file should cover:

- literacy, reading, writing, interpretation and correspondence as distinct capabilities;
- household formation, practical craft learning, clerical learning, and military specialist interfaces without one universal school model;
- teacher, learner, host, language, materials, time, practice, assessment and consent;
- interruption, departure, failed assessment, lapse, loss and successor preparation;
- manuscripts, copying, archives and technical preservation without taking ownership of legal or administrative truth;
- Bavaria-Nordheim separation and locally realized capability;
- player choices between immediate service and preserving teaching or successor capacity;
- record contracts, reason codes, persistence, migration and static fixtures.

No opening school, teacher, clerk, literacy rate, archive inventory or qualified practitioner should be fabricated. The next file should be a full substantive authority, not another entrance audit.

The provenance text above records why the capability was created or refined. Its prospective recommendation is no longer an active next-file instruction.

## 6. Archives: retain record ownership, add technical lifecycle

Governance sections 24-25 distinguish records, copies, authentication, seals, correction and custody. Household sections 25 and 47 coordinate correspondence and archives. Ecclesiastical institutions track archive capacity and restoration. Diplomacy records transmission and authentication. These are meaningful existing owners.

Recommended split:

```yaml
record_ownership:
  Governance: MandatesPetitionsAssembliesAdministrativeDecisionsAndAuthenticationAuthority
  Law: CaseEvidenceJudgmentAppealAndLegalTrace
  Economy: AccountsPropertyInventoryAndDeliveryRecords
  Diplomacy: MessagesCredentialsAgreementsAndExternalReceipts
  Household: MembershipCustodyAccessAndContinuityCoordination
  Ecclesiastical: InstitutionalArchiveRightsPermissionAndFunction
proposed_education_technical_scope:
  - ReadingWritingCopyingAndInterpretationCompetence
  - TeachingAndTransmission
  - MaterialPreservationAndReconstructionMethods
  - QualificationAndSuccessorPreparation
```

A preserved copy does not become an original. A reconstructed record does not resolve a contested right. An archive building does not create a trained custodian or accessible contents. Technical restoration must preserve uncertainty and prior trace.

Current disposition: the education authority owns technical learning, copying, transmission, and preservation methods; governance, law, economy, diplomacy, household, ecclesiastical and other record owners retain meaning, authority, custody, provenance, and access decisions. Built environment owns the physical storage asset and condition. No duplicate archive owner is required.

## 10. Environment: verify shared state before creating a Realm writer

Recommended approach: recover the shared environment authority, map its state to territory, economy, population, health and built consumers, and enrich the relevant Bavaria interfaces. Create a separate Bavaria environment authority only if local exposure, adaptation, maladaptation and residual-change portfolios cannot be adequately expressed through those owners.

## 13. Household and court: substantive coverage already exists

Sections 47-54 add practical outputs, capacity, burdens, officer admission and continuity. Governance sections 12-28 define mandates, assemblies, petitions, records, authentication and qualified personnel.
