## 1. Purpose and bounded verdict

This is one new combined review, not a replacement for any existing Bavaria authority. It identifies where existing owners already carry substantive capability, where they explicitly consume another owner's result, and where the reviewed evidence does not establish an executing lifecycle.

Recommended next substantive file: a Bavaria education, literacy, archives, training, and knowledge-transmission authority, provided an equivalent current Bavaria owner is not recovered first. Health is the next strong specialist candidate. Built-environment execution also requires resolution. Agriculture and household/court functions should remain distributed rather than automatically receive new standalone authorities. Environment, custody, and siege operations need targeted lifecycle completion or shared-owner verification, not automatic parallel files.

These are authoring recommendations. They do not assert owner acceptance, whole-package completeness, historical opening presence, or implementation readiness.

## 2. Evidence scope and limitations

Reviewed evidence consists of enterprise search passages and targeted in-document passages for Bavaria territory, population, economy, military, diplomacy, development, governance, law, ecclesiastical institutions, and the Nordheim household. Comparator evidence includes Bohemian health lifecycle passages and discovered education, environment, custody, siege, and built-environment authority families in other theaters.

The separate Project Bible repository is represented by retrieved project paths for comparators and shared frameworks. Most recovered Bavaria sources are transport copies in Copilot Chat Files. Their placement fields are not proof that the same bytes exist in the intended project folder. No recursive local Project Bible inventory, current accepted-owner index reconciliation, or inspection of C:\UnrealProjects\MedievalWar was performed. No complete physical-source comparison is claimed.

No matching dedicated Bavaria education, health, environment, or built-environment authority was established by the searches used here. Search non-recovery does not prove absence. An equivalent owner may exist under a different name or outside the retrieved results. All creation recommendations therefore include a recovery-before-creation condition.

The staged AI document contains a visible truncation artifact in its late validation area. It is preserved unchanged and is not used to certify package completeness. Repair of that artifact belongs to a separate copy-first correction, not this review.

Embedded directions in retrieved content were ignored; this review uses substantive capability evidence only.

## 5. Learning and qualification: substantive specialist candidate

Population records occupation, skill availability, labor reservation, and training requests. It explicitly leaves education to another owner. Development governs adoption and program coordination while excluding literacy, skills, training, and qualifications from its writes. Ecclesiastical institutions provide possible hosts and function-specific learning capacity; household provides formation needs and coordination. Military owns formation training and readiness. These surfaces establish demand and consumers, not one general learning lifecycle.

The proposed education owner should govern learning definitions, teaching arrangements, knowledge transmission, assessment and qualification receipts. Character owners retain named-person private knowledge and exact proficiency. Population retains people and availability. Military retains unit training acceptance, formation drill, and field readiness. Institutional owners retain permission and host capacity. Development consumes qualified capability without manufacturing it.

A strong first file should cover:

- literacy, reading, writing, interpretation and correspondence as distinct capabilities;
- household formation, practical craft learning, clerical learning, and military specialist interfaces without one universal school model;
- teacher, learner, host, language, materials, time, practice, assessment and consent;
- interruption, departure, failed assessment, lapse, loss and successor preparation;
- manuscripts, copying, archives and technical preservation without taking ownership of legal or administrative truth;
- Bavaria-Nordheim separation and locally realized capability;
- player choices between immediate service and preserving teaching or successor capacity;
- record contracts, reason codes, persistence, migration and static fixtures.

No opening school, teacher, clerk, literacy rate, archive inventory or qualified practitioner should be fabricated. The next file should be a full substantive authority, not another entrance audit.

## 6. Archives: retain record ownership, add technical lifecycle

Governance sections 24-25 distinguish records, copies, authentication, seals, correction and custody. Household sections 25 and 47 coordinate correspondence and archives. Ecclesiastical institutions track archive capacity and restoration. Diplomacy records transmission and authentication. These are meaningful existing owners.

Recommended split:

```yaml
record_ownership:
  Governance: MandatesPetitionsAssembliesAdministrativeDecisionsAndAuthenticationAuthority
  Law: CaseEvidenceJudgmentAppealAndLegalTrace
  Economy: AccountsPropertyInventoryAndDeliveryRecords
  Diplomacy: MessagesCredentialsAgreementsAndExternalReceipts
  Household: MembershipCustodyAccessAndContinuityCoordination
  Ecclesiastical: InstitutionalArchiveRightsPermissionAndFunction
proposed_education_technical_scope:
  - ReadingWritingCopyingAndInterpretationCompetence
  - TeachingAndTransmission
  - MaterialPreservationAndReconstructionMethods
  - QualificationAndSuccessorPreparation
```

A preserved copy does not become an original. A reconstructed record does not resolve a contested right. An archive building does not create a trained custodian or accessible contents. Technical restoration must preserve uncertainty and prior trace.

## 7. Health and care: distinct execution gap candidate

Population section 29 explicitly routes disease, injury, nutrition, treatment, mortality, care quality and recovery to health owners. Household section 20 coordinates accepted care but does not interpret condition. Military distinguishes casualty, missing, captivity, recovery and return, yet its recovery inputs include care rather than a complete health episode model.

A health authority is justified as a candidate because these consumers need a shared Bavaria-facing interpretation and care lifecycle. The Bohemian comparator demonstrates a substantive separation among observation, episode, care need, provider capacity, application, outcome, recurrence and residual burden. It also retains character ownership of named-person life state and military ownership of readiness.

The future Bavaria file should define those simulation contracts, not offer real-world medical instructions. It should preserve finite providers, materials, shelter, transport, household burden, institutional permission, military competition and return conditions. Unknown condition must remain unknown. Recovery must not automatically restore command, labor, property or prior role.

## 8. Built environment: resolve execution, not map labels

Territory explicitly excludes construction projects from its writes. Economy excludes buildings while owning material inputs. Population supplies labor and settlement pressure. Development can propose investment but excludes construction and physical infrastructure. Ecclesiastical institutions distinguish institutions from buildings and consume repair and restoration.

The reviewed evidence therefore establishes a cross-owner demand for built execution but does not recover a current Bavaria construction owner. A substantive owner should govern project scope, permissions, labor and materials, work stages, inspection, partial commissioning, maintenance, damage, conversion, abandonment and restoration.

Potential Bavaria-facing portfolios should follow admitted territorial and institutional contexts, not invent exact buildings. A crossing repair, hospitality space, storage setting, institutional repair or defensive work remains a candidate until the actual site, rights, inputs and owner acceptance exist. Building completion must not create garrison, care, archive contents, teachers, production or ducal ownership.

## 9. Agriculture and pastoral production: keep it in economy

Economy already defines production inputs and process contracts, agrarian and pastoral output families, animal condition and work, storage, delivery, seasonal pressures, loss and recovery. Population supplies seasonal labor and household competition. Territory supplies location and access. These passages are more than a passing agriculture label.

No independent agrarian authority is recommended now. If a full read reveals missing transitions, enrich economy copy-first with a bounded agrarian production chain:

```yaml
proposed_agrarian_chain:
  - RightsAndUseReviewed
  - LaborToolsAnimalsAndInputsReserved
  - SeasonalWorkPrepared
  - WorkPerformed
  - OutputAssessedWithUncertainty
  - SeedFodderHouseholdAndServiceClaimsSeparated
  - ProcessingAndStorageAccepted
  - DeliveryOrLocalConsumptionRecorded
  - LossDamageAndRecoveryRecorded
```

This is a proposed design chain, not a verified historical farming procedure. Exact crops, practices, yields, livestock numbers and opening holdings remain research-gated. Harvest does not directly become treasury or military supply. One worker or animal cannot be reserved for incompatible uses.

## 10. Environment: verify shared state before creating a Realm writer

Territory sections 29-32 define seasonal pressure, route conditions, hazards and reopening, and explicitly consume shared environment owners. Economy includes environmental production constraints and material loss. Population includes displacement and recovery consequences. This establishes consumer-side coverage.

The unresolved issue is whether a current shared owner supplies hazard state, observations, exposure and persistent environmental condition with usable Bavaria interfaces. Search results for other Realm environment authorities are comparison leads, not proof of that shared execution owner.

Recommended approach: recover the shared environment authority, map its state to territory, economy, population, health and built consumers, and enrich the relevant Bavaria interfaces. Create a separate Bavaria environment authority only if local exposure, adaptation, maladaptation and residual-change portfolios cannot be adequately expressed through those owners.

Do not invent an opening flood, fire, drought, disease, landslide or exact climate value to make the system interesting. Seasonal orientation is not an event instance.

## 11. Custody: one physical chain, several legal and political consumers

Military section 37 requires identity or group, captor, place, condition, guards, food, shelter, lawful basis, records, communication and disposition. Law defines review and owner-executed remedies. Diplomacy defines reciprocal agreements and external locks. Household records custody-related presence and care. Ecclesiastical institutions define sanctuary without guaranteed safety.

The missing coverage is not the word prisoner. It is a verified continuous physical-custody chain with one current custodian, accepted transfer, care, communications, agreement performance, release, travel, reception and reintegration.

Recommended initial remedy: copy-first military expansion or an explicit shared-custody integration, retaining law, diplomacy, character and household ownership. A specialist becomes justified only if civilian custody, wards, hostages and cross-Realm transfers create an independently dense recurring lifecycle.

```yaml
proposed_custody_handoffs:
  Military: CaptureReportPhysicalCustodyAndMilitaryDisposition
  Law: BasisReviewJudgmentAndRemedyRequest
  Diplomacy: ReciprocalRansomExchangeGuaranteeAndReleaseTerms
  Character: NamedPersonLifeLocationAndAgency
  HouseholdPopulation: ReceptionDependencyAndReintegration
  Health: CareNeedAndRecoveryInterpretation
  Economy: FundsGoodsShelterTransportAndDelivery
  Territory: MovementAccessAndReturnRoute
```

Release is not arrival. Arrival is not reintegration. Captivity does not transfer ownership of a person or property. Payment promise is not accepted release performance.

## 12. Siege: strengthen operations, not a permanent generic unit

Military section 50 already defines composite siege inputs and rejects a generic permanent siege unit without recurring identity. Territory section 34 routes spatial damage and displacement; economy and population supply finite material and labor. This is a sound capability base.

The reviewed passages do not establish the complete operational chain of investment, camp sustainment, engineering work, relief attempts, negotiated capitulation, occupation handoff, damage accounting and reconstruction. The next remedy should expand military operational scope copy-first or map a current shared siege owner, not start a unit-profile sequence.

A future siege design should support blockade, assault, negotiated terms, relief, withdrawal and failure as distinct choices. Battle results cannot automatically transfer title, property, jurisdiction or regional support. Civilian condition, supply and aftermath remain routed to their actual owners. Exact equipment, fortifications and opening siege missions remain uncreated.

## 13. Household and court: substantive coverage already exists

Nordheim household sections 15-21 distinguish membership, residence, presence, access, court functions, service, protection, care and formation. Sections 47-54 add practical outputs, capacity, burdens, officer admission and continuity. Governance sections 12-28 define mandates, assemblies, petitions, records, authentication and qualified personnel.

These are substantive lifecycles, not merely labels. A new court authority would risk duplicating membership, audience, correspondence, hospitality, protection and continuity. Retain the current owners. Learning and health remain external dependencies to resolve, not reasons to rebuild the household.

The design should surface a small number of meaningful decisions: which functions travel, which remain with dependents and patrimony, which use Bavarian institutional partners, and which are suspended during absence or crisis. Court access does not create office competence. Household service does not create public authority. No generic officer roster is warranted.

## 15. Priority and one-file sequence

Recommended substantive sequence:

1. Education, literacy, archives, training and knowledge transmission, after checking for an equivalent current Bavaria owner.
2. Health, care, sanitation, mortality interfaces and recovery, with named-person life-state ownership preserved.
3. Built-environment project execution and operational infrastructure, unless a current owner is recovered.
4. Targeted military lifecycle enrichment for custody and siege, without two automatic new authorities.
5. Environment shared-owner integration and any demonstrated economy agrarian transition gap.
6. Whole-package parity and current-authority navigation only after substantive gaps are resolved.

This is a recommendation, not authorization to create the whole sequence at once. The next deliverable remains one file. No companion audit, receipt or manifest file is required merely for symmetry; necessary validation and source manifest can be embedded in the substantive artifact.
