## 20. Handoff Acceptance Criteria

The definition-only handoff passes when:

- the physical package and current successor set are verified;
- every proposed field has a controlling authority;
- definition and instance records remain distinct;
- unknown, zero, absent, external-locked, and not-applicable remain distinct;
- Bavaria and Nordheim ownership remain separate;
- external owners retain their private state;
- the three temporal tracks remain separate;
- no later history is forced into January 1, 1062;
- no schema or candidate template creates live state;
- evidence, rollback, and promotion requirements are explicit.
