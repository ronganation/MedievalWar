### 12A.3 Sekerníci Early-to-High progression

The Early and High Sekerníci files are two substantively different canonical era profiles belonging to one developing Sekerníci lineage. The Early profile is the axe-and-shield assault foundation. The High profile develops the established axe-bearing tradition into a broader-provider, more organized bounded-assault expression. Both files remain, while the relationship is progression rather than two unrelated duplicate families.
