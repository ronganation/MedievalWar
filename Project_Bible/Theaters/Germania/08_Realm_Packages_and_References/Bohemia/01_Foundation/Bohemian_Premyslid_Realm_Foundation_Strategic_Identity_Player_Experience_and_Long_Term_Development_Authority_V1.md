## 10. Player Experience Thesis

The intended experience is a legible Total War-style campaign in which the player builds power through places, households, branches, institutions, service relationships, finite material capacity, and selective military action. Depth should appear through clear blockers, consequences, competing commitments, and recoverable failures rather than through opaque simulation or administrative micromanagement.
