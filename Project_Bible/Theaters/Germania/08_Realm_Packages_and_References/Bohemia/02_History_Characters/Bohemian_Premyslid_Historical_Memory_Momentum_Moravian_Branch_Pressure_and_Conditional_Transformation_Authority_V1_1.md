## 47. Memory and Momentum Player Experience

The player should understand:

- what is established historical fact;
- who remembers what and through which channel;
- which interpretation is contested;
- what repeated actions reinforce or relieve pressure;
- why a threshold is not yet met;
- which owners and resources are missing;
- which transformations are possible, blocked, reversible, or unnecessary;
- what alternative success looks like without reproducing later history.

The interface must not expose hidden private state or convert historical prominence into inevitable success.

## 53. Reversal, Relief, Collapse, Supersession, and Dissolution

    meaning: ANewValidSettlementOrTransformationReplacesThePriorCandidate
  Dissolution:

    meaning: OwnerReviewedEndStateWithResidualMemoryAndConsequencesPreserved
