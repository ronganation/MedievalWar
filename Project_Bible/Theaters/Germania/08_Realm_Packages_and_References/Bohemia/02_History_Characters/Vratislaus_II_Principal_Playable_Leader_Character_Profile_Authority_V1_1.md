## 1. Developed Profile Verdict

Vratislaus II is the opening human face of the Bohemian Premyslid campaign: a recently elevated duke whose personal survival, dynastic relationships, Moravian experience, Hungarian connection, ducal recognition, ecclesiastical choices, and political-order strategy create unusual opportunity without supplying automatic control.

```yaml
CentralCampaignQuestion:
  CanVratislausConvertRecentAccessionDynasticPluralityMoravianExperienceAndCentralEuropeanRelationshipsIntoAResilientBohemianPoliticalOrderWithoutOverreachingAgainstFamilyChurchPoliticalOrderOrFiniteMaterialCapacity
```

The profile must remain valid if Vratislaus never becomes king, receives another dignity, reconciles with Jaromir, never creates an Olomouc diocese, loses Moravian influence, cooperates with Poland or Hungary, opposes either, distances Bohemia from the Royal Salian government, supports it selectively, avoids the historical imperial campaigns, suffers defeat, becomes captive, loses office, dies before 1085, survives beyond 1092 through divergent play, or leaves succession unresolved.

## 7. Core Character Thesis

Vratislaus's defining strength is experience across the exact fault lines that can break the Realm: dynastic rivalry, Moravian regional power, exile, external assistance, ducal succession, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, and political-order recognition.

Vratislaus's defining weakness is that every useful connection belongs to another owner. Brothers possess their own agency. Moravian fields have contested assignments and independent interests. Church institutions own offices and jurisdictions. Hungary and Poland retain external consent. The political order controls recognition mechanisms that Vratislaus cannot self-award. Household, property, military, and government capacity remain finite and separately owned.

```yaml
CentralTension:
  label: PersonalDucalStandingVersusDurableDistributedPower
  risk_of_overreach: TreatingTitleDynastyHouseholdMoraviaChurchAndExternalRelationshipsAsOneCommandSystem
  risk_of_underreach: FailingToConvertRecentAccessionIntoRecognizedInstitutionsCapacityAndContinuity
  durable_path: BuildDirectedRelationshipsOwnerReceiptsDelegatedCapacityAndRecoverableInstitutions
```

### 45. Sharpened Opening Identity and Strategic Function

Vratislaus begins as:

- the living principal playable ruler candidate for Bohemia;
- a duke who has held the Bohemian position only since 1061 at orientation scope;
- a ruler formed by Moravian association, dynastic conflict, exile, Hungarian assistance, reconciliation, and recovery;
- the principal personal coordinator among a ducal center, several Přemyslid brothers, disputed Moravian allocations, a developing ecclesiastical field, and external political-order relationships;
- a credible conditional commander whose political value can exceed direct battlefield use;
- a father and dynasty member whose continuity problem cannot be reduced to one automatic heir;
- a candidate for later personal royal elevation, provided a valid grantor, recognition, service, standing, and political settlement exist.

- holder of a hereditary crown;
- owner of Bohemia, Moravia, Prague, Olomouc, the Přemyslid dynasty, the household, the army, the treasury, or the Church;

- beneficiary of automatic Hungarian or Polish support;
- commander of a free ducal host;

- destined to receive the crown in 1085;
