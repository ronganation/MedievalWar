## 4. Title and Office Thesis

The Bohemian ducal title should be politically important without becoming a container for Bohemia, Moravia, the Přemyslid dynasty, or every regional actor. The player must establish what the title means to different audiences, secure recognition for specific uses, distinguish public office from household service, defend or narrow claims, and preserve continuity when title, person, dynasty, household, regional power, and later personal kingship do not align.
