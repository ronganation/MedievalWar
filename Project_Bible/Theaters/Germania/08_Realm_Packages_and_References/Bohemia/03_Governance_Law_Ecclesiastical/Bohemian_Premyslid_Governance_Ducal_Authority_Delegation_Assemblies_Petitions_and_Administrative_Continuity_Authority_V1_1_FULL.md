## 5. Bohemian Governance Thesis

The gameplay problem is not to maximize a government meter. The problem is to determine who may do what, for which matter, in which place, for how long, through which records and routes, with whose recognition, supported by which household and material capacity, subject to what review, and followed by what handback.

<Person>Vratislaus II</Person> may be the principal initiator without personally administering every function. <Person>Conrad I</Person>, <Person>Otto I</Person>, <Person>Jaromír</Person>, regional actors, institutions, household participants, and external political-order actors remain independent participants rather than passive components of a ducal machine.

## 37. Player-Facing Explanation

- the initiating actor;
- authority and competence basis;
- affected matter, place, branch, institution, or audience;
- mandate or delegation;
- known and unknown facts;
- required participants and recognition;
- communication and capacity blockers;
- review and handback;
- alternatives including delay, referral, negotiation, and no action.

The UI must not expose hidden external state or show a universal authority, loyalty, administration, or legitimacy score.
