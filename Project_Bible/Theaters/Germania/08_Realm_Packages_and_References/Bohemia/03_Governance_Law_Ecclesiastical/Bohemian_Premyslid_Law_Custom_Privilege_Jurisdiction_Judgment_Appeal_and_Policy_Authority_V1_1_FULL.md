## 31. Ecclesiastical Legal Boundary

The future ecclesiastical authority will own institutions, offices, canonical jurisdiction, sanctuary, spiritual process, patronage, protection, archives, burial, and external Church interfaces. ## 32. Political-Order and External-Law Boundary
