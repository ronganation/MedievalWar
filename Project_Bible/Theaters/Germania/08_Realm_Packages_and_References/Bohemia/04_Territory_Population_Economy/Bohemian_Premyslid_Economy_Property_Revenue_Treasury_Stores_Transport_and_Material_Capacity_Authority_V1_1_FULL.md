## 4. Player and AI Promise

The economic game is not one wealth meter. It is the problem of identifying who holds which right, who possesses and administers an asset, what revenue may be assessed, what is actually collected, what goods physically exist, what is reserved or in transit, which obligations compete, and what can recover after loss without fabricating resources.
