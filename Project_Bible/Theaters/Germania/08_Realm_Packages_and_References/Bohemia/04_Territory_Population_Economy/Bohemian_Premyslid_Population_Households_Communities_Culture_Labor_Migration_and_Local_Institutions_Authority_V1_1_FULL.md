Bohemia requires a plural human-capacity model connecting people collectively to households, communities, settlements, institutions, culture, language, religion, labor, migration, service burdens, devastation, and recovery. Population is not faction property, one national identity, one loyalty score, one workforce, or an army-manpower currency.

The population game should make communities, labor, burden, mobility, identity, institutions, recruitment pressure, devastation, and recovery strategically meaningful without requiring the player to allocate individual workers or manage every family. Player-facing summaries must emphasize the leading causes, strongest constraints, current burden, and available owner-routed responses.
