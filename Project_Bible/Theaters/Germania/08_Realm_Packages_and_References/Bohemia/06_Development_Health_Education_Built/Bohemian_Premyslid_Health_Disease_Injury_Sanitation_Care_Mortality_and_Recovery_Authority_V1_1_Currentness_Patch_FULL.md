## 1. Developed Realm Health Verdict

Bohemian health must be modeled as subject, place, episode, evidence, access, and owner specific. It is not one Realm meter. Health pressure may affect people, households, communities, institutions, settlements, travelers, displaced groups, garrisons, and field forces differently. Care requires real capacity, access, materials, time, and authority. Recovery may be partial, delayed, recurrent, or leave residual burden.

## 5. Player and AI Promise

Health systems should create readable strategic tradeoffs among prevention, observation, care, relief, movement, military necessity, household continuity, and long recovery. The player directs priorities and accepts consequences without manually prescribing every treatment or managing every patient. AI must explain the strongest known pressure, missing dependency, uncertainty, opportunity cost, and residual risk.
