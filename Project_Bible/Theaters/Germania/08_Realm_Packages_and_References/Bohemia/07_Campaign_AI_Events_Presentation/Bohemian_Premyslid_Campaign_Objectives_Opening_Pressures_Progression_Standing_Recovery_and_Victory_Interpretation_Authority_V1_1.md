The Bohemian package now has sufficient owner-separated static authorities to define campaign-objective semantics without creating active objectives or opening values. This authority turns verified Realm pressures and development possibilities into readable player and AI directions while preserving multiple viable futures, partial outcomes, recovery, and nonoccurrence.
