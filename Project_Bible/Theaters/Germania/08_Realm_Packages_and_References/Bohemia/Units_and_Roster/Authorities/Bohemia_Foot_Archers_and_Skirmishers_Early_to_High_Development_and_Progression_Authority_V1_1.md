```yaml

  PersistentSameFamily:

      - LucistniciHotovosti

    source_environment: HradstiLucistniciAndOtherEligibleBowSources

  InstitutionalUrbanBranch:
    source_environment: KusniciHotovostiAndOtherQualifiedCrossbowCapacity
    destination: MeststiKusnici
    rule: UrbanInstitutionRequiresIndependentPeopleWeaponsBoltsMaintainersOfficersStoresTransportAndAdministration

    source_environment: MatureMeststiAndOtherQualifiedCrossbowPaviseCapacity
```

No unit automatically upgrades because the date, technology, building, settlement tier, or faction treasury changes.
