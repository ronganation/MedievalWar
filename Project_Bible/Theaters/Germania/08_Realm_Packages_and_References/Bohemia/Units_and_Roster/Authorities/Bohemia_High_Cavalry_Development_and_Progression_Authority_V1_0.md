The High mounted roster is not merely the four files stored under High cavalry and mounted skirmishers. It also includes the persistent development of Jízdní Průzkumníci and Knížecí Jízdníci. The player therefore receives six active core and specialist mounted lanes, plus conditional Moravian regional access where a separate relationship authority permits it.

No family automatically replaces another. High development expands the mounted military society around the Přemyslid host rather than converting all riders into knights.

```yaml
  - JizdniKusnici

```

The stages indicate capability relationships, not one mandatory universal unlock order. A qualified mounted-crossbow institution may develop through different providers than household or knightly cavalry. Mature knightly depth should normally appear later within High than persistent scouts and core retainers because its warhorse, armor, landholding, command, and provider burden is greater.

## 27. Final Authority Verdict

Bohemia's High mounted system now has a coherent six-family core and specialist structure, with Moravian regional mounted access preserved separately:

- Jízdní Průzkumníci provide the persistent information, screening, communication, and pursuit foundation.
- Knížecí Jízdníci provide the persistent core ducal mounted reserve and routine intervention.
- Knížecí Jízdní Družina provide household trust, command integration, and one exceptional crisis decision.
- Panošská Jízda provide the rally, remount, messenger, wounded, recovery, and controlled-exploitation architecture around expensive mounted power.
- Rytířská Družina provide the mature knightly institution, formed heavy charge, and peer-heavy contest.
- Jízdní Kušníci provide a separate mounted-crossbow lane built around position choice, prepared fire, and vulnerable transitions.

The authority does not force a universal promotion ladder. Scouts do not become retainers. Retainers do not automatically become household companions. Squires do not automatically become knights. Household companions do not automatically convert into the broader knightly institution. Mounted crossbowmen require a separate specialist packet.

The High portfolio is strong but not permanently closed. Additional High or Late mounted units remain welcome when research proves a distinct provider, mission, physical packet, campaign opportunity cost, and player experience. The next practical work is visual progression, provider and settlement testing, High README reconciliation, and a deliberate Late-era mounted research docket.
