## 1. Executive Portfolio Verdict

```yaml

```

Bohemia's first Late infantry tranche is intentionally compact. It adds three institutions that High does not already own:

1. **Sudličníci Zemské Hotovosti** add provincial polearm reach, conditional hooking, anti-mounted follow-through, and formation disruption.
2. **Pěší Zbrojnoši** add contract-maintained professional continuity and sequential assignment flexibility.
3. **Městští Pavézníci** add dedicated directional protection for another connected body, movement, or worksite.

The Late era does not replace the High roster. Knížecí Kopiníci, Sekerníci, Mečníci Knížecího Dvora, Hradní Stráž, Knížecí Pěší Družina, crossbow families, archers, and locality specialists persist when their institutions and missions remain useful.

## 22. Final Authority Verdict

Bohemia's first Late infantry tranche is coherent and intentionally limited:

- **Sudličníci Zemské Hotovosti** provide the new provincial polearm institution and its aggressive reach-and-disruption mission.
- **Pěší Zbrojnoši** provide the new contract professional infantry institution and its long-campaign flexibility.
- **Městští Pavézníci** provide the new urban pavise-protection institution and its directional cover mission.

These families do not form a universal promotion ladder and do not replace the High roster. Each requires a separate provider, physical packet, recruitment pool, battlefield mission, campaign cost, and recovery path. Existing families continue through Late-era maturation wherever their missions survive.

The authority therefore closes only automatic expansion. It does not permanently close Late development. Advanced crossbowmen, firearm infantry, later guards, regional families, and locality specialists remain research lanes, but none should be created until it survives direct comparison against the full persistent and new Late portfolio.
