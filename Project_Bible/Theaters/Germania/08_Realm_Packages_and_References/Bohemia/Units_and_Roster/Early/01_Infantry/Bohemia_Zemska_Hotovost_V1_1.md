## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Zemská Hotovost is Bohemia's broad early territorial spear host: locally mustered spear-and-shield infantry whose strength comes from assembled communities, defensible ground, known leaders, usable depth, and the determination to preserve the line long enough for Bohemia's rarer troops to decide the battle.**

Its signature is **Muster, Set, Endure, Preserve**.

Its battlefield purchase is not disposable bodies and not a universal anti-cavalry multiplier. The unit buys dependable low-cost frontage when given time to assemble, establish facing, protect its shoulders, and fight around a clear defensive purpose. Zemská Hotovost can deny easy passage, protect archers, check light cavalry from the front, occupy roads and settlement approaches, and preserve the broader host while ducal retainers, axemen, border troops, or cavalry act elsewhere.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Zemská Hotovost gathers the broad territorial strength of Přemyslid Bohemia into a usable field formation. The people who answer the call do not form a permanent regiment, and their equipment is not uniform. They arrive through communities, estates, districts, and recognized local relationships able to provide armed people, leaders, food, shields, spears, and enough accepted obligation to sustain a temporary host.

The unit becomes useful through preparation rather than individual superiority. Once neighboring groups settle around recognizable leaders and standards, establish a common facing, and place enough depth behind the spear front, the formation can survive pressure that would scatter an emergency gathering. Its cohesion remains conditional. Missile casualties create gaps. Broken shields and spears thin the receiving front. Flank pressure forces several groups to turn at once. Prolonged pursuit separates people from the leaders and neighbors who made the formation work.

The player's satisfaction comes from turning ordinary Bohemian military capacity into meaningful battlefield control. Zemská Hotovost holds the approach while archers shoot, prevents an enemy horse probe from rolling the line, occupies the ground beneath a hill, or preserves the center until ducal troops can strike. The unit does not need elite kill counts. It needs to make the rest of the army possible.

## 4. Why the Card Exists

Bohemia requires a credible military floor before ducal retainers, Moravian contingents, frontier specialists, castle-district troops, and developing professional formations can possess meaningful value. If the territorial foundation is worthless, every stronger unit becomes mandatory. If it is too strong, Bohemia's military and political development loses purpose.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Set, Endure, Preserve

### 8.1 Muster

A real territorial source supplies eligible people, local leaders, serviceable weapons, shields, food, assembly space, and a recognized purpose. Treasury and building access can support the process but cannot skip it.

### 8.2 Set

The formation establishes facing, frontage, depth, standards, neighboring groups, and protected shoulders. A moving or hurried company possesses spears but not the complete unit promise.

### 8.3 Endure

The unit receives ordinary infantry pressure or a readable mounted approach. It preserves the line long enough for missiles, axemen, cavalry, retainers, or another formation to act.

### 8.4 Preserve

When the mission is achieved or the line is becoming unsafe, the formation shortens, redresses, accepts relief, or withdraws through a real route. Preservation is active unit management, not passive cowardice.

### 8.5 Return

Surviving people, leaders, weapons, shields, standards, and obligations are accounted for. Battlefield survival does not automatically restore provider capacity.

### Recruit when

- the army needs broad affordable frontage;
- bowmen, huntsmen, or other light troops require protection;
- a road, ford, hill, settlement approach, or camp sector must be held;
- stronger ducal or household troops should be preserved for decisive contact;
- local providers retain enough defense, labor, leadership, and equipment;
- the campaign can support assembly, service, relief, return, and recovery.

### Do not recruit when

- the army needs pursuit, reconnaissance, or aggressive flanking;
- selected armored infantry must be defeated head-on;
- the relevant communities are already depleted or recall-pressured;
- concentrated missiles make a slow receiving line untenable;
- a town, district, Moravian, ducal, or household formation better fits the mission;
- the only reason is that the unit appears cheap in coin.

## 36. Failure Stories

**The hurried muster:** the enemy arrives before groups, leaders, shields, and frontage settle. The formation possesses weapons but never gains its complete battlefield promise.

**The glorious pursuit:** the line checks light cavalry or pushes back poor infantry. The player chases beyond standards and friendly support, turning a local success into territorial losses.

**The empty countryside:** repeated cheap recruitment drains leaders, labor, shields, transport, and local-defense capacity. Treasury remains healthy while the actual pool closes.

**The arrow-worked front:** the player leaves Set Spears active under sustained missiles. Shields reduce some exposure, but casualties, fatigue, broken equipment, and leader loss destroy Muster Integrity before contact.

**The false elite:** inflated morale and armor allow Zemská Hotovost to perform the work of ducal spearmen or household foot. Bohemia's military progression and social layering collapse.

**The erased Moravian distinction:** broad recruitment silently consumes Moravian providers and makes a separate regional roster meaningless. Regional ownership and pool integrity fail.

Affordable and widely available from healthy territorial providers, the formation can protect archers, hold roads and settlement approaches, and check light cavalry when its spears are properly set. Its order depends on preparation, local leaders, secure shoulders, and a clear defensive purpose. Missiles, heavy shock, flanking, prolonged campaigning, and reckless pursuit can quickly unravel the host.

## 52. Final Strong First-Draft Verdict

**Zemská Hotovost passes as Bohemia's first accepted static Early unit profile.**

The formation gives Bohemia a real military foundation rather than disposable levy filler. It is broad enough to anchor an early army, affordable enough to appear regularly when valid providers exist, and limited enough that town watchmen, Moravian contingents, castle-district troops, ducal spearmen, household foot, axemen, border troops, and cavalry all retain meaningful space.

Its identity is not “peasants with spears.” It is the field line created when Bohemian communities, territorial obligations, local leaders, practical equipment, routes, food, and Přemyslid coordination successfully converge. On the battlefield the unit **Musters, Sets, Endures, and Preserves**. In campaign it consumes and risks the same capacity that sustains local defense, households, agriculture, transport, and political cooperation.

The key strength is dependable ordinary usefulness. The player can trust Zemská Hotovost to solve a normal early defensive problem when the formation receives time, facing, support, and a clear mission. The player cannot trust it to defeat selected armored foot, survive concentrated missiles, absorb a clean heavy-cavalry strike, protect an open flank, or campaign indefinitely without damaging its source.
