## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Strong pass as Bohemia’s missing Early middle-tier bow family.**

The unit sits between Lučistníci Hotovosti and Knížecí Střelci:

- Lučistníci Hotovosti buy breadth, economy, and arrow volume.
- Hradští Lučištníci buy repeated service, better equipment consistency, simple target control, and more reliable formation handling.
- Knížecí Střelci later buy selected personnel, ducal officers, stronger inspection, concentrated fire control, and superior campaign continuity.

The card must not become an Early duplicate of Knížecí Střelci. Its advantage is dependable service, not elite accuracy, exceptional range, armor penetration, or a royal label.

## 3. Exact Player Purchase

The player buys a **more dependable Early shooting line**, not a stronger version of every archer function.

Hradští Lučištníci provide:

1. better equipment compatibility than broad territorial archers;
2. more reliable file leaders and simple signals;
3. improved target assignment and cease-fire discipline;
4. better formation redress and controlled displacement;
5. greater readiness for castle-district, road, ford, camp, and defended-approach service;
6. a middle recruitment tier whose provider pool remains finite.

The player does not buy:

- Lesní Lovci reconnaissance or concealment;
- Knížecí Střelci selected ducal fire control;
- crossbow pressure against armor;
- independent protection from cavalry;
- unlimited arrows;
- a universal castle-garrison unit;
- automatic promotion from Lučistníci Hotovosti.

## 4. Core Battle Loop: Assemble, Align, Shoot, Displace

### Assemble

A complete service packet gathers at a validated district or receiving host. Repeated service helps, but the company still requires people, leaders, bows, arrows, food, and a real military assignment.

### Align

The company establishes a clear shooting front, working depth, target sector, friendly boundaries, ammunition access, and withdrawal route.

### Shoot

Files deliver measured fire against one assigned target class. The formation is more reliable than territorial archers but cannot sustain complex target cycling or concentrated ducal fire indefinitely.

### Displace

The company ceases fire, passes through a real lane, reforms, recounts ammunition, and resumes only when the new position is usable. Displacement is controlled movement, not skirmish-mode immunity.

## 19. Player-Facing Card Draft

### Hradští Lučištníci

_Bohemian castle-district archers_

Archers assembled through castle districts, strongpoints, towns, and recognized service providers. Hradští Lučištníci possess better leaders, equipment consistency, and shooting order than broad territorial archers, but lack the selected personnel and ducal fire control of Knížecí Střelci. Protect the formation, assign worthwhile targets, and displace before enemy cavalry or infantry reaches the line.

**Strengths**

- reliable middle-tier bow fire;
- better formation and target discipline than territorial archers;
- useful defensive and field support;
- lower cost and broader access than ducal-selected archers.

**Weaknesses**

- poor armor penetration;
- dependent on spears and terrain;
- vulnerable to cavalry, crossbows, and melee;
- finite ammunition;
- less command depth than Knížecí Střelci.

## 21. Failure Stories

The design fails if:

1. Hradští Lučištníci are merely Lučistníci Hotovosti with higher accuracy.
2. The card equals Knížecí Střelci in command, equipment, or campaign continuity.
3. Castle ownership automatically creates recruitment.
4. The unit gains reconnaissance or concealment equal to Lesní Lovci.
5. Better shooting becomes armor penetration.
6. Early and High versions appear as duplicate cards.
7. Selected personnel branch upward without reducing the source pool.
8. The family becomes mandatory because it is simply superior per cost.
