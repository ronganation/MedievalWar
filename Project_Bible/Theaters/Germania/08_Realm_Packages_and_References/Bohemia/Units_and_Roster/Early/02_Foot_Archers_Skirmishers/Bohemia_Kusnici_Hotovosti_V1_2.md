## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Kušníci Hotovosti are Bohemia's restricted Early Era crossbowmen: a small missile formation assembled through validated muster, fortified-place, town, landholding, or specialist providers able to prepare a protected firing position, load comparatively slow weapons, threaten armored or shielded targets more credibly than ordinary bows, and withdraw before close combat destroys the company.**

Their signature is **Prepare, Load, Threaten, Withdraw**.

The unit is not a broad levy-bow replacement. Lučistníci Hotovosti retain cheap volume and rate. Lesní Lovci retain reconnaissance and harassment. Moravští Lučištníci retain regional field archery. Kušníci Hotovosti purchase restricted penetration threat, defensive firing, and high-value target pressure at lower rate, smaller pools, higher material cost, and greater dependence on maintainers and ammunition.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Kušníci Hotovosti
- **English support name:** Muster Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Restricted Early Bohemian crossbow infantry
- **Signature:** Prepare, Load, Threaten, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Deliver a small number of deliberate, dangerous bolts against worthwhile targets from a protected position.
- **Best use:** Behind spears, at gates and fords, on defended roads, against exposed armored files, shielded troops from favorable angles, enemy missiles, and cavalry already halted by infantry.
- **Strong against:** Exposed missile troops, unshielded or turned ordinary infantry, light cavalry halted by spears, and individual armored targets at suitable range.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded maintained infantry and medium cavalry when angle, range, protection, bolt supply, and target density favor the crossbowmen.
- **Weak against:** Cavalry contact, disciplined archers through greater rate, dense skirmish targets, flanking fire, blocked withdrawal, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Every company consumes qualified operators, crossbows, strings, prods, triggers, spanning tools, bolts, maintainers, officers, food, transport, and protected source capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Prepare before shooting, hold bolts against poor targets, and withdraw before the enemy closes.
- **Flavor:** **The Early crossbow is not a shower of arrows. It is a scarce machine, a slow loading rhythm, and a bolt reserved for a target worth the trouble.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Kušníci Hotovosti gather a narrow body of people able to operate and care for chronology-appropriate crossbows. The formation may draw from validated town, strongpoint, landholding, ducal-service, or specialist networks, but no provider is automatic. Muster identity describes service and campaign access, not an undifferentiated peasant pool.

The company works best behind spears or fortified access. Its bolts have greater threat against armor and shields than ordinary arrows, but penetration is never guaranteed. Crossbow type, spanning method, bolt condition, range, angle, target armor, shield facing, weather, and operator experience remain decisive.

Low rate of fire and finite ammunition prevent the card from replacing bowmen. Severe losses to weapons, strings, maintainers, experienced operators, or bolt production can close recruitment even when people remain.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Load, Threaten, Withdraw

### 8.1 Prepare

Establish a protected arc, loading depth, bolt access, weapon inspection, friendly safety, and withdrawal lane.

### 8.2 Load

Span, place, and ready the crossbow through a chronology-appropriate method. Loading consumes time, attention, muscular effort, and equipment condition.

### 8.3 Threaten

Fire deliberate bolts at one suitable target. The threat may force caution or cause serious casualties, but no target is guaranteed to be killed or penetrated.

### 8.4 Withdraw

Leave before cavalry, infantry, or superior missiles establish decisive contact; reform behind protection and account for weapons, strings, tools, and bolts.

## 12. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 12.1 Early Crossbow Line

The default formation balances firing front, loading depth, protection, maintainers, and withdrawal.

### 12.2 Hold Bolts

Preserve scarce ammunition and avoid revealing the company against poor targets.

### 12.3 Deliberate Target Fire

Several files engage one bounded target at working range. This is normal target control, not a damage ability.

### 12.4 Counter-Missile Position

Spacing and cover adjust against enemy missiles. Archers may still beat the unit through rate and volume.

### 12.5 Controlled Withdrawal

The company leaves through a real lane before close contact and reforms behind spears, barriers, or terrain.
