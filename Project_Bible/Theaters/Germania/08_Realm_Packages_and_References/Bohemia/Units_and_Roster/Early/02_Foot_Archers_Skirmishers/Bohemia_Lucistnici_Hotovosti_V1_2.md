## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lučistníci Hotovosti are Bohemia's broad territorial archers: community-sourced bowmen who muster behind the spear line, screen an approach with affordable missile fire, deliver useful volleys against exposed targets, and withdraw before cavalry or maintained infantry can destroy them.**

Their signature is **Muster, Screen, Volley, Withdraw**.

The unit is not an elite forest archer and not a long-range armor killer. Its battlefield value comes from affordable missile presence, broad but finite providers, useful volume against ordinary targets, and a clear relationship with Bohemia's spear formations. Accuracy, equipment, leadership, ammunition, and staying power remain uneven.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Lučistníci Hotovosti
- **English support name:** Bohemian Territorial Archers
- **Class:** Light Bohemian territorial bow infantry
- **Signature:** Muster, Screen, Volley, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Provide affordable missile volume against ordinary exposed targets while depending on terrain, ammunition, and spear protection for survival.
- **Best use:** Behind territorial spears, on gentle slopes, at settlement approaches, across roads and fords, against light infantry, and in defensive combined-arms lines.
- **Strong against:** Unarmored levies, exposed skirmishers, light infantry, stationary targets, and cavalry already halted or disordered by spears.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded infantry, light cavalry, and peer archers when range, cover, elevation, ammunition, and protection favor the Bohemian formation.
- **Weak against:** Armored infantry, shields from the front, crossbows, aggressive archers, cavalry, flanking, rain, smoke, and close melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Each company consumes finite bow-capable people, bows, strings, arrows, wood, iron, feathers, leaders, food, labor, and local hunting or defensive capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Protect the formation, select exposed targets, avoid wasting arrows on heavy armor, and begin withdrawal before cavalry reaches the shooting line.
- **Flavor:** **The Bohemian host does not need every arrow to pierce mail. It needs enough arrows, delivered at the right moment, to make the enemy approach in disorder.**

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Lučistníci Hotovosti gather bow-capable people from eligible Bohemian communities into a field formation. Some contributors may possess hunting experience, local defensive practice, or repeated service, but the card does not assume a uniform class of professional archers. The formation's equipment and skill vary across providers.

On the battlefield, the archers work best behind a credible spear line. Their volleys punish exposed light troops, disrupt an approach, and add attrition while better Bohemian units preserve the ground. Against armor and well-presented shields, the player should expect suppression and fatigue rather than dramatic killing power.

The campaign cost extends beyond people. Bows, bowstrings, arrows, shafts, heads, feathers, quivers, repair, food, and collective practice must exist. Heavy losses or careless ammunition expenditure weaken both the active company and the provider's ability to field another one.

### 8.2 Screen

The formation occupies protected shooting ground and contributes early missile pressure without separating from its spear protection.

### 8.3 Volley

Archers deliver controlled shooting against worthwhile targets while leaders preserve ammunition and order. The word volley describes coordinated battlefield fire, not perfect synchronized drill.

### 8.4 Withdraw

The unit leaves through a protected route before cavalry, infantry, or superior missiles establish decisive contact.

## 12. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 12.1 Territorial Shooting Line

The default formation balances broad missile volume, moderate spacing, and manageable control.

### 12.3 Concentrated Volley

A bounded target area receives denser fire at higher ammunition expenditure and greater fatigue.

### 12.4 Hold Arrows

### Recruit when

- an army needs affordable missile support;
- adequate spear protection and withdrawal routes exist;
- enemy light infantry, archers, or unshielded troops are expected;

### Do not recruit when

- armored targets dominate the expected battle;
- cavalry protection is missing;
- bows, strings, arrows, leaders, or qualified people are depleted;

## 34. Failure Stories

**The English longbow clone:** range, penetration, and visual silhouette drift upward until the unit becomes an elite longbow formation. Bohemia's missile progression collapses.

**The infinite quiver:** ammunition is treated as a cooldown rather than arrows, transport, and material. Target choice stops mattering.

**The forest overlap:** levy archers gain concealment, scouting, and loose harassment equal to Lesní Lovci. The specialist woodland card becomes redundant.

**The arrow shield:** massed archers are left without spear protection. Volume does not stop cavalry once the formation is reached.

**The armor fantasy:** arrows receive broad anti-armor power to remain relevant. Crossbows and specialist missile development lose purpose.

**The empty workshop:** repeated recruitment consumes bows, strings, arrows, wood, iron, feathers, and skilled repair faster than providers recover.

## 49. Final Strong First-Draft Verdict

**Lučistníci Hotovosti pass as Bohemia's first accepted static Early missile-infantry profile and broad territorial bow-support formation.**

The card gives the roster an affordable ranged foundation without copying elite archer traditions from other factions. Bohemia's spear formations create the protected ground. Border Wardens reveal and delay the approach. Lučistníci Hotovosti add the volume of arrows needed to punish exposed light troops, weaken an advance, and force the enemy to respect the combined line.

Their identity is not “peasants with bows.” It is the capability created when bow-capable people, community leaders, bows, strings, arrows, craft materials, food, transport, service, and protection converge into a usable company. On the battlefield the formation **Musters, Screens, Volleys, and Withdraws**. In campaign it consumes real people, equipment, ammunition, labor, and local defensive capacity.

The profile keeps the unit appropriately bounded. Range and accuracy are ordinary. Armor penetration is poor. Ammunition is finite. Weather, target selection, friendly firing arcs, cavalry, and withdrawal matter. Moravian Archers, Woodland Huntsmen, Ducal Archers, and Early Crossbowmen all retain clear reasons to exist.
