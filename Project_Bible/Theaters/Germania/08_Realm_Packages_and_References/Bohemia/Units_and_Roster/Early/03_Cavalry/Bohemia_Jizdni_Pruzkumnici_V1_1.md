## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Jízdní Průzkumníci are Bohemia's broad Early Era light horsemen: modestly equipped mounted scouts who range ahead of the host, screen exposed approaches, mark enemy movement, carry warning, pursue broken troops, and refuse combat with formed heavy opposition.**

Their signature is **Range, Screen, Report, Pursue**.

The card is intentionally broader than a narrow scout detachment but lighter than ducal mounted retainers. The formation buys the player mounted presence, information, flank security, pursuit, and rapid response without granting shock-cavalry power. Riders may carry light spears, small shields, and a restricted number of javelins where supported, but the card is not a mobile missile platform.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Jízdní Průzkumníci gather riders and suitable horses from eligible Bohemian service, estate, patrol, messenger, and regional horse-access networks. The company is not a uniform cavalry regiment. Its broad role depends on practical riding skill, reliable mounts, local leaders, working tack, routes, fodder, and the ability to report what the riders observe.

In battle, Mounted Scouts range beyond the main line, watch flanks, contest enemy skirmishers, and preserve communication. They can charge exposed missile troops or broken infantry, but lack the weight, armor, cohesion, and horse quality required for sustained shock action. A successful player avoids proving bravery against prepared spears.

Campaign use must remain finite. The riders and horses also support patrol, messenger, escort, transport, estate, and local-defense responsibilities. Severe horse losses, overwork, poor fodder, route disruption, or long distant service can close the pool even when eligible people remain.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Range, Screen, Report, Pursue

### 8.1 Range

Move beyond the main body through passable ground while preserving communication and return routes.

### 8.2 Screen

Watch an approach, cover a flank, contest light enemy elements, and prevent easy surprise without accepting decisive contact.

### 8.3 Report

Identify and report one meaningful threat, route, exposed target, or change in enemy movement. Observation matters only when the information reaches the receiving force.

### 8.4 Pursue

Once the main army creates a rout or clear disorder, exploit speed to capture, disperse, and prevent easy regrouping. Pursuit must end before the company outruns support.

## 12. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 12.1 Mounted Screen

The default loose but connected formation covers an approach and avoids unnecessary contact.

### 12.2 Close Screen

A tighter formation improves control against enemy light troops but increases missile density and reduces observation width.

### 12.3 Mark Threat

Leaders identify one meaningful enemy body or route and begin a real report relationship. Marking is not a magical target debuff.

### 12.4 Short Mounted Check

Light spears, shields, and restricted javelins oppose scouts, routers, or very light cavalry. The order is unsuitable against formed infantry or heavy cavalry.

### 12.5 Controlled Pursuit

The company pursues a broken target through a known lane, with explicit limit and rally point.

### 12.6 Break Contact

Riders disengage through real space before heavy contact. Late disengagement can fail.

## 38. Failure Stories

**The cheap knight:** armor, charge, and morale rise until Mounted Scouts replace ducal retainers. The mounted roster collapses.

**The free radar:** observation instantly reveals enemy numbers, identity, and movement across the map. Riders, messengers, routes, and reporting become meaningless.

**The steppe drift:** restricted javelins become endless mounted skirmishing or bows are added for convenience. The card becomes a different military tradition.

**The heroic screen:** the company remains against heavy cavalry or formed infantry after warning is complete. Light horse is destroyed performing a mission the card never promised.

**The endless pursuit:** routers lure the scouts beyond support into fresh enemy reserves or broken ground.

**The horseless refill:** riders return instantly while suitable mounts, tack, remounts, and horse recovery are ignored.

**The stable factory:** a building manufactures riders and horses from treasury alone.

*Bohemian mounted scouts and light horsemen*

## 53. Final Strong First-Draft Verdict

**Jízdní Průzkumníci pass as Bohemia's first accepted core Early cavalry profile and broad light-horse foundation.**

The card opens the mounted roster without immediately handing Bohemia armored retainers or household shock cavalry. Foot wardens still control restricted ground. Mounted Scouts provide open-ground range, flank warning, reports, raider response, anti-skirmisher pressure, and pursuit. Heavier mounted families retain the decisive charge and hard melee.

Their identity is not “cheap cavalry.” It is the capability created when qualified riders, suitable horses, leaders, messengers, tack, weapons, routes, fodder, remounts, treatment, and a clear mission converge into a complete mounted company. On the battlefield the unit **Ranges, Screens, Reports, and Pursues**. In campaign it consumes the same people and horses needed for patrols, messages, transport, estates, breeding, and higher mounted families.

The profile remains firmly Early Era. Armor is light, javelins are restricted, no bows are assumed, and shock power is modest. Information must be observed and delivered. Pursuit requires a broken target, passable ground, a limit, and a rally point. Horses recover independently from riders.
