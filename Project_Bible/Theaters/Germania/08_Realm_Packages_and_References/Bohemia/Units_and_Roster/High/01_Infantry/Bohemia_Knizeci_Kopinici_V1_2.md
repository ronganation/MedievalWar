## 3. Player-Facing Description

Knížecí Kopiníci are selected spear infantry maintained through the military relationships of the Přemyslid court and ducal administration. They are not one modern standing regiment, and they are not simply wealthier levies. Their battlefield reliability comes from stronger service continuity, recognized officers, inspected equipment, repeated collective practice, and more dependable integration into the ducal host.

The formation can receive a serious ordinary attack, resist cavalry from a prepared front, and provide a stable neighbor for less disciplined troops. Unlike broad territorial musters, the ducal spearmen can make a controlled forward movement and preserve useful order. This does not make them shock infantry. Their advance is deliberate, shielded, and dependent on intact officers, standards, depth, and supporting formations.

Campaign availability must remain narrow enough to matter. Every company competes for selected personnel, officers, equipment, food, administrative attention, and money or service support. Severe losses damage more than headcount because the unit's value depends on trained relationships that are slower to rebuild than a broad muster.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Form, Receive, Stabilize, Advance

### 8.1 Form

Officers, standards, files, weapons, shields, depth, reserve, and support establish a disciplined spear line.

### 8.2 Receive

The formation accepts an important frontal attack while preserving enough order to deny rapid penetration.

### 8.3 Stabilize

The unit closes gaps, supports an adjoining territorial formation, relieves a pressured sector, or restores the army's center. Stabilization requires physical position and does not operate as an aura.

### 8.4 Advance

If the enemy loses order and support remains intact, the formation moves forward in controlled steps to restore ground or maintain pressure. It does not launch an unrestricted shock charge.

## 34. Failure Stories

**The infinite ducal army:** buildings and treasury turn every available person into a ducal spearman. Selection, officers, equipment, territorial defense, and service relationships disappear.

**The passive morale aura:** nearby levies become stronger merely because the card is present. Stabilization no longer requires contact, frontage, relief, or physical risk.

**The spear charge:** controlled advance becomes a shock bonus. The unit steals the roles of axemen, swordsmen, cavalry, and household foot.

**The false elite:** mail and morale increase until Knížecí Kopiníci match household companions. Bohemia loses its upper military hierarchy.

**The officer graveyard:** repeated costly victories consume commanders, file leaders, veterans, standards, and instructors. Headcount refills while the unit's defining reliability does not.

**The unsupported center:** the line receives successfully but lacks relief, missiles, flank protection, or a decisive arm. Reliability delays defeat but cannot win the whole battle alone.

## 49. Final Corrected Mature Verdict

**Knížecí Kopiníci pass as Bohemia's canonical High-transition spear infantry and first dependable maintained ducal field-line formation.**

The profile advances the roster into a new military layer. Zemská Hotovost provides broad territorial frontage. Městská Stráž secures towns and immediate settlement responsibilities. Moravská Hotovost provides regional adaptability. Hradská Hotovost provides an upper-territorial anchor and one-crisis reserve. Knížecí Kopiníci provide the reliable spear core around which a serious Přemyslid field army can be organized.

Their identity is not “heavy levy spearmen.” It is the capability created when selected personnel, officers, veterans, standards, inspected equipment, food, support, administration, and repeated collective practice converge into a coherent ducal company. On the battlefield the unit **Forms, Receives, Stabilizes, and Advances**. In campaign it consumes the same cadre, equipment, political access, and administrative support needed by other ducal military responsibilities.

The unit preserves an earlier developmental lineage but is canonically placed and presented as the opening High-era transition anchor. It possesses meaningful helmets, better shields, limited mail, stronger officers, more consistent formation behavior, and better campaign continuity than territorial infantry. It does not possess universal mail, elite household morale, shock-infantry power, passive stabilization auras, or infinite ducal recruitment. Its controlled advance creates measured pressure, not a heroic charge. Its stabilization requires physical relief, not proximity magic.
