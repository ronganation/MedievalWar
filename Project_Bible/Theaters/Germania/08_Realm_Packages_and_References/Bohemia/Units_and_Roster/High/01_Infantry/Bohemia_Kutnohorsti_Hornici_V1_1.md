## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Kutnohorští Horníci are a High Era, campaign-conditioned mining and siege-works contingent drawn from validated Kutná Hora mining providers and related technical labor. The formation surveys ground, prepares approaches and earthworks, supports undermining and countermining, handles obstacles and siege labor, and defends its worksite in an emergency without becoming fantasy pick-armed shock infantry.**

Their signature is **Survey, Prepare, Undermine, Defend**.

The unit is deliberately narrower than Mečníci Knížecího Dvora, Hradní Stráž, Sekerníci, or ordinary spear infantry. Its battlefield value comes primarily from enabling siege and defensive works, shortening or contesting engineering tasks, and preserving prepared positions. Practical tools may be used in desperate close combat, but tools do not create universal armor penetration.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Kutnohorští Horníci gather experienced miners and associated technical labor from validated Kutná Hora institutions and providers. The card does not treat all miners as soldiers or all mining as siege expertise. A usable contingent requires foremen, survey skill, timbering knowledge, excavation practice, tools, lighting, hauling, materials, guards, food, transport, and a defined engineering mission.

During a siege, the contingent can inspect ground, prepare approaches, establish covered or reinforced work areas, support undermining, detect or contest enemy mining, move debris, prepare obstacles, and repair practical fieldworks. In battle, these functions must remain tied to real time, terrain, materials, routes, and protection.

The company can defend a worksite briefly with practical tools, spears, shields, bows or sidearms assigned by its provider and escort relationship. Emergency defense is a survival function, not an excuse to make the unit superior assault infantry.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Survey, Prepare, Undermine, Defend

### 8.1 Survey

Inspect one bounded site, approach, wall section, tunnel direction, earthwork line, obstacle, or suspected enemy mine through real access and time.

### 8.2 Prepare

Move tools, timber, rope, baskets, spoil, lighting, barriers, food, guards, and labor into a functional worksite.

### 8.3 Undermine

Excavate, timber, shore, listen, drain, remove spoil, contest an enemy work, or weaken one validated target. Undermining is a campaign or siege process, not an instant ability.

### 8.4 Defend

When attacked, preserve specialists, tools, access, timbering, and accumulated work until guards arrive or the contingent withdraws.

## 12. Work Portfolio

### 12.1 Survey Ground

Determine feasible approach routes, soil or rock conditions, drainage risk, obstacle requirements, and possible enemy mining signs.

### 12.2 Prepare Approach and Earthworks

Clear, level, reinforce, screen, drain, or protect one route or work area with available labor and materials.

### 12.3 Undermine

Excavate and timber a mine toward one validated structural target. Progress requires time, access, materials, spoil removal, and protection.

### 12.4 Countermine

Listen, probe, excavate, shore, contest, flood, smoke, block, or fight around one detected underground threat where historically and mechanically appropriate.

### 12.5 Obstacle and Gate Work

Remove, weaken, strengthen, block, repair, or prepare one practical obstacle, barrier, gate approach, or siege-work component.

### 12.6 Defend or Evacuate Works

Use assigned weapons, tools, barriers, and guards to preserve or abandon one worksite.

## 34. Failure Stories

**The pick fantasy:** practical mining tools become armor-destroying shock weapons and the contingent replaces assault infantry.

**The silver factory:** mine income automatically generates recruits, skills, tools, and siege capability.

**The instant breach:** undermining becomes a button that collapses any wall without ground, time, timber, counterwork, or risk.

**The engineer aura:** proximity passively improves every siege task without a defined worksite, materials, or labor.

**The compulsory anachronism:** the unit appears before validated Kutná Hora development and provider institutions exist.

**The all-purpose sapper:** miners operate engines, assault breaches, build fortresses, repair walls, scout, and fight as elites simultaneously.
