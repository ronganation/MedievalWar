## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Strong pass as Bohemia's eighth core High-era unit.**

The unit is justified because no accepted Bohemian High profile currently owns broad-provider offensive infantry pressure. Mečníci Knížecího Dvora solve connected infantry crises through maintained court discipline. Hradní Stráž owns defended-place security and relief. Knížecí Kopiníci own spear frontage and cavalry reception. Sekerníci instead accept greater exposure and casualties to force one short close-combat problem.

The concept fails if Sekerníci become:

- cheaper Mečníci;
- better armored than court infantry;
- equal to Knížecí Kopiníci against cavalry;
- castle guards with axes;
- mining or engineering specialists;
- dedicated war-flail troops;
- automatic armor destroyers;
- a universal assault solution.

## 3. Exact Player Purchase

The player purchases **one expendable but not disposable close-assault tool**.

Sekerníci add:

1. more offensive infantry pressure than spear formations;
2. broader availability than court-maintained swordsmen when complete providers exist;
3. stronger practical work against ordinary shields, doorways, light obstacles, and compressed formations;
4. meaningful value in broken, wooded, fortified, or confined approaches where cavalry cannot solve the problem cleanly;
5. a casualty and recovery bargain that makes repeated assault costly.

The player does not receive:

- heavy-armor immunity;
- magical shield bypass;
- universal anti-armor damage;
- main-line cavalry reception;
- court-quality formation restoration;
- household morale;
- siege-engine structural damage;
- free axes, officers, or replacement personnel from technology.

## 4. Core Identity: Approach, Brace, Press, Break Off, Recover

### Approach

The company reaches one usable assault lane behind terrain, shields, friendly missile pressure, or the protection of other infantry. An exposed advance under concentrated fire is a failure of employment, not a hidden faction bonus.

### Brace

Files align, shields and axes are prepared, officers identify the target and return route, and the company concentrates enough depth to survive first contact.

### Press

The company enters one bounded close assault. Axe work is most valuable against ordinary shielded files, compressed defenders, damaged formations, or opponents already fixed by allies.

### Break Off

Once the assigned frontage yields, the target is no longer vulnerable, fresh hostile troops arrive, or formation order falls, the company must disengage or accept steep attrition.

### Recover

Surviving files reform, replace broken shields and axe hafts, recover officers, treat casualties, and re-establish an actual reserve before recommitment.

## 8. Equipment Constitution

### Axes

The family should primarily use practical one-handed axes with shields, supported by bounded longer-axe or pole-axe expressions where historical and linguistic review supports them. The unit must not default to universal enormous two-handed axes.

### Shields

Shields remain important because the company needs to cross space and survive first contact. One-handed axe files should carry practical shields where providers can supply them. Longer-axe files sacrifice some protection and therefore require stronger terrain, formation, or friendly support.

### Armor

Protection should be mixed:

- practical helmets;
- padded protection;
- some mail among better equipped personnel;
- limited improved protection among officers and veteran front files;
- no universal coat-of-plates or heavy elite armor;
- no assumption that mining wealth equips every company.

### Equipment variation

Variation expresses providers and file function. Variation does not grant free loadout switching during battle.

## 11. Assault Target Contract

### Preferred targets

- ordinary shielded infantry already fixed or compressed;
- lighter spear or militia formations with vulnerable shoulders;
- disordered infantry;
- defenders at a breach, gate approach, street, woodland lane, or short slope;
- missile troops caught after protection has collapsed;
- one local intrusion requiring forceful counterpressure.

### Poor targets

- fresh heavy cavalry;
- long spear lines receiving from open ground;
- elite household infantry in unsupported even combat;
- deep disciplined polearm formations;
- fresh armored swordsmen with reserve support;
- dispersed skirmishers;
- enemies behind obstacles the company cannot physically reach.

### Armor-pressure ceiling

Axes may damage shields, helmets, limbs, weapons, and confidence in close contact. This does not mean guaranteed penetration of strong armor. Angle, weapon geometry, target movement, formation order, fatigue, personnel skill, and opportunity control the result.

## 12. Formation Portfolio

### Shielded Assault Body

Default approach and first-contact formation. Better protected, but not as wide or aggressive as a fully committed press.

### Concentrated Axe Press

Uses depth and selected longer-axe files to intensify one short frontage. Stronger pressure, greater exposure, and rapid Assault Cohesion expenditure.

### Confined-Lane Column

For streets, woodland approaches, gates, and narrow passages. Easier route control, but dangerous if the head stalls or the return lane closes.

### Counterpressure Reserve

Held behind connected infantry to strike one intrusion or exposed shoulder. Produces no value while inactive but preserves the company’s best readiness.

### Degraded Security Body

After the assault, the company can guard a camp route, store, short frontage, or withdrawal lane. It no longer receives fresh assault performance.

## 15. Relationship with Knížecí Kopiníci and Hradní Stráž

### Knížecí Kopiníci

Spears own sustained frontage, reach, and cavalry reception. Sekerníci may pass through an assigned lane to counterattack or exploit but cannot replace the spear line.

### Hradní Stráž

The guard owns watch, alarm, access control, interior routes, reserve allocation, and defensive relief. Sekerníci can perform one assault around a gate, breach, street, or courtyard but do not become permanent place-security personnel.

### Combined sequence

- spears fix and protect;
- missiles disturb or suppress;
- Sekerníci approach and press one exposed problem;
- Mečníci restore any connected friendly crisis;
- cavalry exploits only when a mounted opportunity exists.

## 16. Cavalry and Missile Contracts

### Against cavalry

Sekerníci should avoid receiving formed cavalry. Shields, terrain, obstacles, and connected spears may help the company survive, but axes do not create anti-cavalry doctrine.

### With friendly missiles

Arrows or bolts may suppress the approach target, but the firing lane must close before Sekerníci enter. Friendly fire and blocked lanes are real risks.

### Against enemy missiles

Shielded files can cross bounded exposure better than unshielded assault troops, but concentrated missile fire damages formation order before contact. Longer-axe files are particularly vulnerable.

## 17. Terrain and Settlement Use

### Favorable

- woodland edges with usable lanes;
- broken ground limiting hostile cavalry;
- gates, streets, courtyards, and breach shoulders;
- short slopes;
- river approaches and bridges after spear or missile preparation;
- camps, stores, and defended routes requiring counterpressure.

### Difficult

- open cavalry ground;
- deep mud during a concentrated assault;
- narrow routes with no breakoff passage;
- steep ground fragmenting files;
- dense woodland without command visibility;
- fortified obstacles requiring engineers rather than infantry pressure.

Terrain shapes access and protection. The unit receives no universal forest, siege, or urban bonus.

## 20. Degraded-Company Ceiling

A worked company may still:

A worked company may not:

The Sekerníci family should persist rather than automatically converting into a later elite.

- more reliable shields, helmets, and axe repair;
- selected longer-axe files;
- improved camp and assault logistics;
- better protected approach doctrine;

## 22. Political and Provider Opportunity Cost

Broad providers do not mean free manpower.

Recruitment competes with:

- local defense;
- agricultural, workshop, transport, and household labor;
- other spear, missile, cavalry, guard, and service pools;
- officers and veteran leadership;
- equipment production and repair;
- political trust that companies will be supported and returned.

Repeated misuse may slow recovery, restrict further recruitment, or increase compensation and political pressure. Territorial control alone does not restore provider trust.

## 28. Visual Direction

### Silhouette

- practical axes rather than fantasy oversized weapons;
- visible shields among one-handed files;
- limited longer-axe files used to create controlled visual distinction;
- helmets and mixed protection below court-infantry uniformity;

### Hierarchy

- officers and standards clearly readable;

## 30. Player-Facing Card Draft

### Sekerníci

Broadly recruited Bohemian axe infantry trained to force one short close-combat problem. Sekerníci pressure ordinary shielded or disordered infantry and perform well in confined approaches, but lack the reach, armor, and command order required for prolonged frontline combat.

**Strengths**

- strong bounded assault pressure;
- useful against ordinary shielded and disrupted infantry;
- effective in confined or broken approaches;
- broader provider base than court swordsmen;
- useful short defensive counterattack.

**Weaknesses**

- poor cavalry reception;
- vulnerable during exposed approach;
- high casualty and equipment burden;
- limited sustained melee performance;
- sharply reduced value after the first major assault.
