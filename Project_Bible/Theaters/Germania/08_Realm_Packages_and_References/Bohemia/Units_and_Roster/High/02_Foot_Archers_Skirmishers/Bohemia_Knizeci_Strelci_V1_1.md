## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Knížecí Střelci are Bohemia's High Era selected ducal archers: better-supported bowmen serving through validated Přemyslid military and administrative relationships, able to prepare a disciplined shooting position, maintain target and ammunition control, concentrate fire against one suitable threat, and relocate before the line is compromised.**

Their signature is **Prepare, Discipline, Concentrate, Relocate**.

This is not a direct upgrade that deletes the Early missile roster. Lučistníci Hotovosti retain affordable volume. Lesní Lovci retain reconnaissance, concealment, and harassment. Moravští Lučištníci retain regional ownership and cohesion. Knížecí Střelci buy selected personnel, more consistent equipment, stronger command, better target control, and greater campaign continuity at a narrower pool and higher cost.

## 2C. High-Era Faction-Identity Statement

Knížecí Střelci express the same institutional consolidation that moves Knížecí Kopiníci into High. Přemyslid authority has become better able to identify selected bowmen, preserve officers and instructors, inspect bows and strings, distribute arrows, assign protected shooting sectors, and sustain a company through a campaign. High-era development improves coordination and continuity; it does not manufacture archers, equipment, or ammunition.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Knížecí Střelci gather selected bowmen through validated Přemyslid service relationships. They are not a modern standing regiment and do not represent every archer attached to the ducal host. Their reliability comes from stronger selection, recognized officers, compatible equipment, repeated collective practice, administrative support, and more dependable ammunition distribution.

On the battlefield, the company prepares a protected shooting position and maintains better control over arcs, targets, fire tempo, and relocation than Early archers. It can concentrate enough fire to suppress or disorder suitable ordinary targets, but it does not gain crossbow-like armor penetration or fantastical longbow dominance.

Campaign availability must remain narrow. Severe losses remove experienced bowmen, officers, bows, strings, arrows, instructors, and administrative relationships that take longer to rebuild than broad territorial capacity.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Discipline, Concentrate, Relocate

Focus several files on one suitable target for a bounded period at increased ammunition and fatigue cost.

### 8.4 Relocate

Leave threatened ground through a prepared passage, reform, and resume shooting only if order and ammunition remain.

## 12. Formation Portfolio

The default formation balances controlled fire, officer reach, ammunition access, and protection.

Sustainable shooting at worthwhile targets with controlled ammunition expenditure.

A bounded target area receives denser fire from several files. Ammunition, fatigue, and vulnerability to other threats increase.

Spacing and target control shift against enemy archers. It does not guarantee superiority against crossbows or specialist missiles.

The company withdraws or shifts through a real passage, reforms around officers and standards, and resumes fire only after control returns.

## 33. Failure Stories

**The High-era delete button:** Knížecí Střelci replace every Early archer because their statistics are simply higher. The layered roster collapses.

**The longbow fantasy:** range and penetration rise until the card copies another faction's elite tradition.

**The crossbow without a crossbow:** concentrated fire is used to grant armor penetration. The later specialist missile lane loses purpose.

**The passive officer aura:** nearby archers gain accuracy or morale merely from proximity. Physical command and formation relationships disappear.

**The infinite ducal quiver:** selected service and treasury erase ammunition, craft, transport, and recovery costs.

**The exposed prestige unit:** the company is deployed without spear protection and destroyed by cavalry despite its higher cost.
