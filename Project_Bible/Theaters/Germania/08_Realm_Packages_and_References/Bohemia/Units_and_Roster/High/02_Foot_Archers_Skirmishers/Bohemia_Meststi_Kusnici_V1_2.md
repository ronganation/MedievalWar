## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Strong pass as Bohemia's sixth High-era unit.**

The unit purchases a distinct Total War function: deliberate, position-dependent crossbow fire that threatens protected targets more sharply than ordinary bow fire, but sacrifices firing rhythm, mobility, flexibility, and independence. The concept is strong only while the following limits remain firm:

- the crossbowmen do not replace Knížecí Střelci;
- town status does not create trained personnel, weapons, bolts, armor, or officers;
- pavises are conditional support equipment rather than a universal mobile wall;
- the company cannot fire, span, relocate, and defend itself at full value simultaneously;
- the unit is dangerous from a prepared position and fragile when caught between firing cycles;
- no steel-prod or Hussite-era identity is smuggled into the early High tier.

## 3. Exact Player Purchase

The player buys **a deliberate bolt-pressure company**, not a generic missile upgrade.

Městští Kušníci offer:

1. stronger pressure against armored or shielded targets than ordinary contemporary archery when bolts arrive from a prepared firing position;
2. useful fire through shorter urban, gate, bridge, street, wall, and broken-ground lanes where long mobile formations are less convenient;
3. a more stable defended-position presence than lighter missile troops;
4. a visible preparation and reloading vulnerability that must be protected by the rest of the army;
5. a town-linked recruitment bargain that competes for craftsmen, weapons, bolts, stores, trained files, and municipal readiness.

The player does not buy:

- superior battlefield range in every circumstance;
- rapid sustained shooting;
- a free pavise fortress;
- heavy infantry resilience;
- independent cavalry protection;
- automatic armor penetration regardless of distance, angle, target, ammunition, weather, or formation order.

## 4. High-Era Bohemian Identity Contribution

Bohemia's High roster should feel institutionally layered rather than uniformly heavy. Městští Kušníci add the urban and technical layer:

- ducal troops provide selected maintained capacity;
- regional and local troops provide depth;
- knights provide mounted decision;
- towns provide bounded concentrations of equipment, workers, stores, walls, and trained missile service;
- no institution creates unlimited manpower or equipment.

The unit supports a Bohemian identity of connected responsibilities:

> **Spears receive. Archers sustain. Crossbows prepare and penetrate. Swordsmen restore. Cavalry responds and decides.**

## 8. Equipment Constitution

### Primary weapon

The profile uses a period-appropriate High-era crossbow family rather than locking the whole era to one advanced mechanical type. The portfolio may mature from simpler wooden or composite constructions and basic spanning methods toward stronger, more expensive, and more demanding equipment where chronology and provider evidence support it.

### Protection

- helmets are common enough to make the company visually more prepared than emergency levy missile troops;
- body protection varies by town, wealth, service level, and individual access;
- padded protection and some mail are appropriate directions;
- universal heavy armor is rejected;
- armor cannot erase the vulnerability of stationary spanning and disrupted withdrawal.

### Sidearms

Short swords, knives, axes, or other practical sidearms may appear within a controlled visual band. Sidearms enable emergency self-defense, not competent prolonged melee.

### Pavises

Pavises are a **conditional equipment and formation-support layer**. They may appear in better supplied companies, siege service, fortified defense, or later maturation. They do not justify:

- universal full-company pavises at every date;
- movement at normal speed while fully screened;
- instant deployment;
- an interlocking anti-cavalry wall;
- immunity during relocation;
- a separate population of invisible shield bearers unless explicitly reserved and represented.

## 9. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Shoot, Shelter, Reposition

### Prepare

The company occupies a real firing lane, establishes depth, assigns targets, sets any available shields, and confirms a withdrawal or relief route.

### Shoot

The company releases controlled bolts against the assigned target or approach. The strongest effect comes from coherent volleys against exposed or compressed targets, not indiscriminate continuous fire.

### Shelter

The company survives the spanning interval through terrain, fortification, shields, friendly infantry, distance, or target suppression. Shelter is an army relationship, not a passive stat that follows the unit everywhere.

### Reposition

Once the lane is compromised, the target has moved, or ammunition and order decline, the company must lift equipment, recover files, and move to a genuine secondary position. Repositioning temporarily sacrifices fire output and protection.

## 12. Formation Portfolio

### Ordered Bolt Line

The default field formation. It preserves firing order and target control but depends on a clean lane and connected protection.

### Sheltered Crossbow Position

Uses available walls, stakes, terrain, friendly troops, or validated shields. Protection improves while mobility and lateral freedom decline.

### Narrow-Lane Detachment

A reduced frontage for gates, streets, bridges, wall walks, or confined approaches. This is not a free concentration bonus; fewer weapons can bear and congestion becomes dangerous.

### Withdrawn Reserve

The company remains behind the main line until a protected firing problem appears. This preserves readiness but produces no value while inactive.

### Emergency Close Defense

A degraded state after direct contact. Crossbows cease to function as a coherent missile system. Sidearms, loose files, and local survival replace deliberate fire.

## 13. Target Contract

### Preferred targets

- armored infantry exposed in a controlled lane;
- cavalry paused, compressed, approaching predictably, or engaged by friendly troops;
- officers, standards, dense reserves, and valuable formations where line of sight permits;
- attackers negotiating gates, streets, bridges, breaches, slopes, or obstacles;
- shielded infantry whose posture, angle, fatigue, or disruption creates useful openings.

### Poor targets

- fast dispersed skirmishers;
- targets behind hard cover;
- rapidly changing targets requiring constant turning;
- enemies already entering the crossbowmen's formation;
- distant low-value troops when bolts are scarce;
- cavalry with an open charge lane and no friendly protection.

Armor-threatening does not mean armor-deleting. Distance, angle, target motion, protection, bolt quality, weapon condition, weather, and formation order all matter.

## 15. Combined-Arms Contracts

### With Knížecí Kopiníci

Spears protect the crossbow position from mounted or frontal pressure. Crossbows punish opponents fixed, compressed, or exposed by the spear contest. Neither unit transfers its core responsibility to the other.

### With Mečníci Knížecího Dvora

Mečníci may restore a threatened firing sector or counterattack a short intrusion. Crossbowmen do not become safe merely because Mečníci exist, and Mečníci should not remain permanently tied to missile protection.

### With cavalry

Cavalry can threaten enemy skirmishers and exploit bolt-created disorder. Crossbowmen cannot safely trail an aggressive cavalry advance without time and lanes to reposition.

### With Knížecí Střelci

Archers sustain broader pressure while crossbowmen reserve bolts for selected targets. Simultaneous missile employment requires separate lanes, target priorities, ammunition logic, and command capacity.

## 16. Terrain, Weather, and Passage

### Favorable

- town approaches;
- walls and fortifications;
- bridges and controlled crossings;
- slopes with protected flanks;
- field obstacles;
- rear or shoulder positions behind reliable infantry;
- broken ground that limits hostile cavalry access without blocking the firing lane.

### Difficult

- dense woodland without clear lanes;
- deep mud during equipment movement;
- exposed open flanks;
- steep or narrow routes that separate bolts, shields, and personnel;
- rain, moisture, or conditions that degrade strings, storage, visibility, or footing;
- crowded army depth with no withdrawal passage.

Terrain affinity is positional. The unit receives no blanket urban or forest bonus for merely standing inside a map category.

Městští Kušníci should possess respectable service morale while the firing system remains intact. Morale deteriorates sharply when cavalry reaches the formation, officers are lost, the withdrawal route closes, or prepared protection is abandoned.

## 19. Siege and Settlement Use

The unit is naturally valuable in siege and settlement warfare because walls, gates, streets, towers, and prepared positions can protect the spanning cycle and shape firing lanes. That advantage does not turn every company into permanent garrison property.

- Field-recruited companies may serve in siege or defense and later return.
- A company assigned to a garrison consumes that company and its equipment.
- Wall deployment must respect actual access, frontage, ammunition supply, and withdrawal space.
- Crossbows do not inflict structural damage merely because they are powerful missile weapons.
- Siege value comes from personnel suppression, protected fire, approach denial, and confined-lane pressure.

## 20. Campaign Bargain

### Strengths

- useful urban-provider identity;
- dangerous prepared shooting against valuable targets;
- strong settlement defense and siege support;
- meaningful synergy with Bohemia's spear and sword layers;

### Costs

- finite trained crossbow pools;
- expensive weapons and maintenance;
- bolts, strings, components, and spanning equipment;
- officer and maintainer demand;
- weak independence in exposed field battles;
- competition between field army, town defense, castle service, and replacement needs;
- delayed recovery after weapon loss.

## 22. Progression and Persistence

Městští Kušníci should persist as a family rather than vanish through automatic replacement.

Possible maturation channels include:

- improved weapon consistency;
- stronger workshop inspection;
- better bolt manufacture and storage;
- more reliable spanning equipment;
- stronger helmets and variable body protection;
- improved officer, signal, and target discipline;
- conditional pavise detachments;
- better siege and settlement deployment.

These channels unlock capability. They do not create people, weapons, equipment, or pools. No progression channel should automatically transform the company into mounted crossbowmen, late pavise crossbowmen, handgunners, or Hussite wagon troops.

## 24. Campaign AI Contract

The campaign AI should recruit Městští Kušníci when qualified providers exist and the army composition can protect them. The AI should not treat them as a universal answer to armor, settlements, cavalry, and field skirmishing.

Preferred contexts:

- defensive armies near developed towns;
- siege forces with infantry protection;
- balanced field armies lacking deliberate bolt pressure;
- armies expected to fight armored opponents;
- garrisons where the actual company is committed.

Avoid over-recruitment when:

- spear and frontline depth are inadequate;
- no replacement weapons or bolts are available;
- multiple companies compete for the same provider packet;
- the army already has excessive slow missile depth;
- fast operational campaigning would repeatedly leave the companies exposed.

Town-supported crossbow companies trained to deliver deliberate bolt fire from protected positions. Their crossbows threaten armored targets and dominate confined approaches, but slow spanning, limited ammunition, and weak melee performance make them dependent on preparation and friendly infantry.

**Strengths**

- strong prepared pressure against protected targets;
- effective in towns, sieges, and controlled lanes;

**Weaknesses**

## 30. Failure Stories

The design has failed if any of the following becomes true:

1. Městští Kušníci are simply Knížecí Střelci with armor penetration.
2. Every developed town generates unlimited companies.
3. Pavises function as free permanent armor.
4. The company defeats cavalry without connected protection.
5. Crossbowmen remain fully effective while moving or after melee.
6. Steel-prod or Hussite-era capabilities appear throughout the early High period.
7. Silver income directly creates weapons, armor, or trained recruits.
8. The unit becomes the best missile choice against every target.
9. Settlement specialization becomes a blanket stat bonus on any urban map.
10. Casualties are replaced without accounting for trained personnel and weapons.

## 36. Final Strengthened Verdict

**Městští Kušníci pass as the next distinct High-era Bohemian unit.**

They add an urban crossbow layer without replacing the accepted ducal archers, spear line, sword reserve, or cavalry families. Their strongest expression is not “heavier missile infantry.” It is a bounded operational rhythm:

> **Occupy one protected lane, deliver deliberate bolt pressure against one valuable problem, shelter through the spanning cycle, and physically reset before the next mission.**

The card has passed final unit-level strengthening and should proceed to whole-High-roster integration and historical-linguistic-provider review. It should not yet be promoted into opening state, active recruitment, exact settlement assignment, battle statistics, or Unreal payload.
