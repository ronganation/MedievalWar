## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Strong pass as a distinct High-era Bohemian foot-missile family.**

The card owns a battlefield and campaign purchase not fully represented by either existing crossbow family:

- Kušníci Hotovosti own restricted, narrower Early technical crossbow service.
- Městští Kušníci own the broader developed urban crossbow institution and more flexible prepared bolt fire.
- Pavézoví Kušníci own a complete crossbow-and-pavise packet designed to establish and sustain one deliberately protected missile position.

The company is stronger in a prepared frontal missile exchange but materially worse in movement, deployment time, transport burden, congestion, emergency withdrawal, and provider scarcity.

## 3. Exact Player Purchase

The player buys:

1. one complete crossbow-and-pavise company;
2. a stronger protected spanning and loading cycle;
3. improved survival during frontal counterfire;
4. a more sustainable prepared-position mission than ordinary crossbow service;
5. controlled bolt pressure from one deliberately engineered infantry position;
6. severe transport, setup, crew, passage, and recovery obligations.

The player does not buy:

- a mobile fortress;
- self-moving shields;
- anti-cavalry spear-wall behavior;
- immunity from missiles;
- guaranteed armor penetration;
- rapid field relocation;
- engineering or fortification construction;
- a universal upgrade to every Městští Kušníci company;
- Hussite wagon, handgun, or fifteenth-century doctrine.

## 4. Core Loop: Deploy, Screen, Loose, Recover

### Deploy

The company reaches a valid site and physically establishes frontage, loading depth, pavise facing, bolt access, maintainers, carriers, friendly boundaries, and an exit route.

### Screen

The pavise packet protects the crossbow cycle only from the represented direction and only while its placement, spacing, supports, carriers, and formation order remain intact.

### Loose

Ready files release against one assigned priority relationship. Controlled staggering preserves useful fire while other files span and load under cover.

### Recover

The company ceases fire, gathers crossbows, bolts, tools, pavises, supports, wounded, carriers, and maintainers, then withdraws or establishes another position. Recovery is a physical military task, not an animation-free command.

## 28. Player-Facing Card Draft

### Pavézoví Kušníci

_Bohemian Pavise Crossbowmen_

Specialized crossbow companies trained to establish a protected missile position behind large pavises. Pavézoví Kušníci can sustain dangerous bolt fire and withstand frontal counterfire better than ordinary crossbowmen, but the complete shield-and-crossbow packet is slow, expensive, congested, and vulnerable to flank attacks, melee, and failed recovery.

**Strengths**

- strong prepared bolt pressure;
- improved frontal countermissile protection;
- valuable around walls, gates, bridges, streets, breaches, and siege approaches;
- sustained protected loading cycle;
- clear High-era specialization.

**Weaknesses**

- slow deployment and withdrawal;
- severe transport and personnel burden;
- poor flank, rear, melee, and cavalry defense;
- finite bolts and expensive equipment;
- narrow provider pool;
- much less flexible than Městští Kušníci.

## 30. Failure Stories

The design fails if:

1. Pavézoví Kušníci are merely Městští Kušníci with higher defense.
2. Pavises provide passive immunity without placement, facing, people, transport, or recovery.
3. The unit becomes anti-cavalry heavy infantry.
4. Every town, castle, or crossbow workshop creates the company.
5. The company redeploys rapidly.
6. Counterfire protection becomes guaranteed missile victory.
7. Universal steel crossbows, heavy armor, or Hussite systems define the High profile.
8. Městští Kušníci lose their flexibility and broader provider purpose.
9. Surviving headcount restores lost crossbows, pavises, bolts, carriers, maintainers, or practice.
10. Several companies share one invisible screen or support packet.
