## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Knížecí Jízdní Družina is Bohemia's High Era household mounted-companion formation: a very scarce body of trusted riders, superior mounts, recognized leaders, and concentrated equipment held near ducal authority to attend the host, preserve a decisive reserve, strike one suitable high-value target, and recover before elite capability is consumed in prolonged combat.**

Its signature is **Attend, Reserve, Strike, Recover**.

This is not Knížecí Jízdníci with higher statistics. Core ducal horsemen provide dependable mounted intervention. Household companions provide the narrower, more expensive answer when the field army requires exceptional mounted cohesion, command trust, harder impact, or protection of ducal authority. Mature knightly cavalry remains a later institutional lane rather than an automatic upgrade from this company.

## 2C. High-Era Household Identity Statement

Knížecí Jízdní Družina represent the point at which Přemyslid household power can concentrate a few trusted companions, superior horses, leaders, armor, standards, and support into a mounted decision tool. The formation is more politically intimate and materially demanding than Knížecí Jízdníci, but narrower than a mature knightly institution. Losing the company damages household security, command confidence, and dynastic military credibility as well as battlefield strength.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Knížecí Jízdní Družina gathers trusted riders serving through the Přemyslid household and its closest validated military relationships. It is not an automatic retinue attached to every character, and it is not a universal social class of knights. Its superior battlefield reliability comes from concentrated trust, leadership, equipment, horse quality, repeated service, and direct integration with ducal command.

The company should normally remain in reserve. It can defeat lesser cavalry, rescue a threatened mounted sector, strike disordered maintained infantry, or protect a critical command area. Its value collapses when prestige encourages repeated frontal charges, distant pursuit, or prolonged melee against fresh spears and elite opposition.

Severe losses damage much more than rider headcount. Trusted companions, senior leaders, superior mounts, armor, standards, remount relationships, household security, and political credibility recover slowly and independently.

Remain connected to ducal command, a protected reserve position, and the wider army's information network.

Break contact, rally, restore rider-horse pairings, inspect equipment, treat mounts, and return to reserve or leave the battle.

## 34. Failure Stories

**The automatic bodyguard:** every named character receives a household cavalry company. People, horses, equipment, providers, and opening authority disappear.

**The cheap knight:** statistics rise until the companions become mature knightly cavalry without the later institution, provider, or equipment development.

**The endless cycle charge:** rally is treated as a cooldown and horse condition is ignored. Elite cavalry becomes mechanical spam.

**The doubled household:** the same companions exist as foot, mounted, and named guards simultaneously.

**The prestige disaster:** the company charges fresh spears or crossbow-prepared ground because household status is mistaken for invulnerability.

**The horseless recovery:** riders refill while superior mounts, remounts, tack, and treatment are ignored.
