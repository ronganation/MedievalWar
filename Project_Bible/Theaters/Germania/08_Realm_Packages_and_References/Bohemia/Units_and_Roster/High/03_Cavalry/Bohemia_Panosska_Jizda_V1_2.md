## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Panošská Jízda is Bohemia's High Era mounted-squire formation: younger or subordinate mounted retainers, experienced attendants, and selected support riders organized to attend heavier cavalry, secure rally and remount space, protect standards and wounded, contest light horse, and exploit a broken enemy without pretending to be a second knightly charge unit.**

Its signature is **Attend, Support, Secure, Exploit**.

This is the right third High cavalry identity because it develops the military society surrounding household companions and mature knights. Knížecí Jízdní Družina own dynastic trust and one exceptional crisis decision. Rytířská Družina own the formed heavy charge and peer-heavy contest. Panošská Jízda own the support architecture that helps expensive mounted power assemble, rally, recover, and convert success without wasting elite riders on routine work.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Panošská Jízda
- **English support name:** Bohemian Mounted Squires
- **Class:** High Era knightly-support cavalry
- **Signature:** Attend, Support, Secure, Exploit
- **Battle promise:** Preserve the rally, remount, messenger, standard, and exploitation functions around expensive cavalry while retaining enough mounted combat power to defeat light threats and pursue broken enemies.
- **Best use:** Behind household or knightly cavalry, near rally routes, on command and baggage approaches, against light cavalry, during withdrawal, and in controlled pursuit after heavier troops create the decision.
- **Strong against:** Routers, exposed skirmishers, messengers, wounded or scattered enemies, very light cavalry, and raiders threatening the mounted reserve.
- **Conditional against:** Peer support cavalry and ordinary light horse when numbers, ground, leaders, and formation favor the squires.
- **Weak against:** Formed spears, medium and heavy cavalry, maintained infantry, disciplined missiles, frontal charges, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Every company consumes trained squires and attendants, riding horses, remounts, tack, shields, spears, sidearms, leaders, messengers, fodder, treatment, and knightly or household support capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Keep the formation near the cavalry system it supports. Secure the rally, escort remounts and standards, intercept light threats, and exploit only after the real charge has succeeded.
- **Flavor:** **Bohemia's knights do not ride alone. Behind the lance line are the riders who hold the spare horses, recover the scattered, guard the standards, and make victory survivable.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Attend, Support, Secure, Exploit

### 8.1 Attend

Remain physically connected to one household, knightly, core-cavalry, command, or withdrawal system.

### 8.2 Support

Escort spare horses, equipment, messengers, standards, wounded, and dismounted riders; maintain the protected route between the mounted reserve and its support area.

### 8.3 Secure

Defend one rally point, remount lane, baggage edge, command route, or withdrawal sector against light threats.

### 8.4 Exploit

Once heavier cavalry or infantry creates a real rout or local collapse, pursue and disperse suitable targets within a strict return limit.
