## 5. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Align, Charge, Reform

### 5.1 Muster

Qualified knightly riders, warhorses, leaders, armor, lances, tack, fodder, service, and support form a complete company.

### 5.2 Align

The company establishes charge frontage, depth, shoulders, target, distance, route, and rally relationship.

### 5.3 Charge

The formation delivers one disciplined heavy mounted commitment against a suitable target. Impact depends on real mass, speed, order, angle, ground, and enemy preparation.

### 5.4 Reform

Riders leave or conclude contact, restore files and rider-horse pairings, recover equipment and horse condition, and prepare for a different responsibility.
