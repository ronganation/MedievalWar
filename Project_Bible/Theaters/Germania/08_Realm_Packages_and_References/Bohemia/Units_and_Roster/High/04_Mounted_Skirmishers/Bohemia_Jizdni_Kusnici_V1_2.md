## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Jízdní Kušníci are Bohemia's High Era mounted crossbow-service formation: riders who use horses primarily to screen, raid, escort, and reposition a finite crossbow detachment, then halt or dismount to deliver deliberate armor-threatening fire before remounting and moving again.**

Their signature is **Ride, Position, Dismount, Shoot, Reposition**.

The unit is not a horse-archer substitute and not effortless circling fire. The horses provide operational reach and tactical repositioning. The crossbows provide slower, limited, high-value missile pressure. Sustained mounted melee, repeated close pursuit, and firing while continuously moving remain outside the normal promise.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Jízdní Kušníci combine riding skill with crossbow handling through a deliberately narrow service institution. The card does not assume that every crossbowman can ride, every rider can maintain a crossbow, or every horse tolerates missile equipment and repeated dismounting. A complete company requires compatible people, horses, weapons, loading tools, bolts, officers, maintainers, fodder, tack, and collective practice.

The formation rides to screen, escort, raid, or seize firing ground. Its normal firing doctrine is stationary and may require dismounting, depending on the final historical and implementation review. Once deployed, the company delivers limited deliberate fire, then relocates before hostile cavalry, infantry, or superior missiles establish contact.

The unit's campaign value comes from reach and flexibility rather than combat efficiency per body. Horses, crossbows, bolts, and trained personnel compete with mounted scouts, cavalry, foot crossbowmen, messengers, transport, and other specialist demands.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Ride, Position, Dismount, Shoot, Reposition

### 8.1 Ride

Move through passable routes toward one screening, raiding, escort, or firing-ground objective while preserving horse condition and communication.

### 8.2 Position

Choose a bounded firing site with a clear arc, protection, bolt access, horse holding area, and remount or withdrawal route.

### 8.3 Dismount

Where doctrine requires, riders hand off or secure horses, form crossbow files, establish loading space, and connect with horse holders and maintainers. Limited stationary mounted shooting remains a review-dependent exception, not the baseline.

### 8.4 Shoot

Deliver deliberate, limited bolts against a valuable target. Rate, penetration, accuracy, and safety depend on range, loading tools, crossbow type, target armor, protection, fatigue, and command.

### 8.5 Reposition

Break the firing position, remount through real space, recover rider-horse pairs, and move before hostile contact or counterfire becomes decisive.

## 12. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 12.1 Mounted Crossbow Screen

The company rides in a loose connected formation, providing route security and preparing to occupy useful firing ground.

### 12.2 Halt and Fire

Where historically and mechanically supported, riders stop and shoot from a stationary mounted posture with reduced rate, stability, and protection compared with a prepared dismounted line.

### 12.3 Dismounted Crossbow Line

The normal high-control firing posture. Horses are held behind or beside the line while crossbow files load and fire.

### 12.4 Mounted Reposition

The company ceases fire, reforms rider-horse pairs, and moves to a new angle, route, or withdrawal point.

### 12.5 Raid and Withdraw

The unit threatens exposed economic, support, messenger, or lightly defended targets, fires or dismounts conditionally, and leaves before a formed response arrives.

## 35. Failure Stories

**The royal anti-armor fantasy:** the company becomes an elite household unit whose only purpose is deleting armored troops.

**The horse-archer clone:** crossbows fire continuously while circling at speed, ignoring loading and horse control.

**The foot-crossbow eraser:** mounted crossbows gain equal ammunition, rate, protection, stability, and cost efficiency to foot formations.

**The cavalry disguise:** armor and melee rise until the company becomes heavy cavalry with bonus bolts.

**The instant remount:** horses teleport to riders after firing and enemy pressure cannot disrupt the transition.

**The infinite bolt case:** ammunition, maintenance, strings, tools, transport, and resupply never matter.
