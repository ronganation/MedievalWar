## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Městští Pavézníci are Bohemia's Late Era maintained urban pavise infantry: trained town contingents equipped to carry, deploy, overlap, advance, and reset large shields that protect missile troops, crossings, approaches, streets, siege works, and exposed infantry from frontal missile pressure without becoming a generic urban guard or an invulnerable portable wall.**

Their signature is **Deploy, Cover, Advance, Reset**.

This card completes the third Late infantry lane through a distinct battlefield purchase. Hradní Stráž retain place-bound access control, stores, signals, watch, and defensive relief. Pěší Zbrojnoši retain contract professionalism and assignment flexibility. Městští Pavézníci own physical missile protection, deliberate protected movement, and urban workshop capacity.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Městští Pavézníci
- **English support name:** Bohemian Urban Pavise Infantry
- **Class:** Maintained Late urban protection infantry
- **Signature:** Deploy, Cover, Advance, Reset
- **Battle promise:** Create one physical protected facing for missile troops or infantry, move that protection through a bounded approach, and reset before flanking, melee, fatigue, or equipment damage breaks the pavise line.
- **Best use:** Crossbow positions, siege approaches, streets, gates, bridges, fords, camp entrances, exposed missile sectors, and protected withdrawals.
- **Strong against:** Frontal archery, exposed enemy missiles reached under cover, light assault against a prepared facing, and ordinary troops forced to approach through a narrow protected sector.
- **Conditional against:** Crossbows and early firearms when pavise quality, angle, spacing, ground, target direction, and supporting troops favor the formation.
- **Weak against:** Flanking missiles, cavalry, heavy melee, polearms, fire, rough ground, several-direction pressure, broken pavises, and close combat inside the shield line.
- **Campaign bargain:** Every company consumes trained bearers, officers, large pavises, frames, leather, paint, iron fittings, carpenters, repair tools, transport, urban obligations, and collective deployment practice.
- **Player instruction:** Face the threat, protect one connected body, move carefully, and reset before the enemy turns the line or closes into melee.
- **Flavor:** **A pavise is not a wall. It is weight, wood, hide, iron, spacing, labor, and men disciplined enough to keep the protected face toward danger.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Deploy, Cover, Advance, Reset

### 8.1 Deploy

Place and overlap pavises against one identified threat direction with working bearers, officers, spacing, ground, and support.

### 8.2 Cover

Protect one connected missile body, infantry group, worksite, crossing, or withdrawal route from frontal missile pressure.

### 8.3 Advance

Lift, carry, roll, or reposition the pavise line through short bounded movement while accepting reduced protection, fatigue, and disorder.

### 8.4 Reset

Reestablish facing, overlap, footing, damaged equipment, spacing, and protected relationships before the enemy changes direction or closes.
