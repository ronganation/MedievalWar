## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Pěší Zbrojnoši are Bohemia's Late Era contract-maintained foot men-at-arms: armored professional infantry assembled through captains, wages, musters, equipment standards, and continuing administration, able to adapt between reinforced line combat, close assault support, escort, reserve, and fortified-duty assignments without replacing specialist polearms, household companions, court swordsmen, or castle guards.**

Their signature is **Contract, Muster, Adapt, Endure**.

The family is not simply Mečníci Knížecího Dvora with better armor. Court swordsmen remain a ducal-service formation specializing in connected reinforcement and frontage restoration. Pěší Zbrojnoši own later paid continuity, mixed professional composition, captain-led contracts, broader assignment flexibility, and the ability to remain in the field while treasury, supply, equipment markets, compensation, and political reliability survive.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Pěší Zbrojnoši
- **English support name:** Bohemian Foot Men-at-Arms
- **Class:** Late contract-maintained professional infantry
- **Signature:** Contract, Muster, Adapt, Endure
- **Battle promise:** Provide dependable armored foot for a longer Late campaign, able to reinforce the line, hold a difficult assignment, support an assault, or defend a camp or route without requiring household-level troops.
- **Best use:** Reinforced infantry sectors, flank support, siege approaches, camp and wagon defense, bridge and gate duty, counterattacks, and prolonged campaigns with strong finance and supply.
- **Strong against:** Levies, upper-territorial infantry, ordinary swordsmen, exposed missiles, and disordered maintained infantry.
- **Conditional against:** Peer men-at-arms, provincial polearms, heavy cavalry after proper preparation, and elite foot when terrain, equipment, fatigue, leadership, and support favor the company.
- **Weak against:** Dedicated polearms at working reach, formed heavy cavalry, crossbows and firearms, encirclement, unpaid service, broken supply, and prolonged unsupported assault.
- **Campaign bargain:** Every company consumes captains, contracted fighters, armor, swords, spears or pole weapons, shields where appropriate, wages, compensation, food, roads, transport, treatment, replacement equipment, and administrative enforcement.
- **Player instruction:** Use the company where professional continuity matters. Adapt to one assignment at a time, preserve the captain and contract, and do not pay elite prices merely to recreate an existing specialist card.
- **Flavor:** **The Late professional infantryman remains in the field because the captain, wage, armor, ration, road, replacement, and contract all continue to function.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Contract, Muster, Adapt, Endure

### 8.1 Contract

Define captain, personnel, duration, pay, compensation, command, equipment standard, assignment, discipline, relief, and return terms.

### 8.2 Muster

Inspect and assemble real fighters, officers, armor, weapons, standards, food, transport, medical support, and replacement arrangements.

### 8.3 Adapt

Adopt one bounded assignment: reinforced line, close support, escort, camp defense, siege support, reserve, or counterattack. Role change requires time, orders, space, and compatible equipment.

### 8.4 Endure

Maintain useful professional order through fatigue, loss, supply, and campaign duration, then withdraw, rotate, settle, or renegotiate before the company disintegrates.

*Bohemian contract foot men-at-arms*

Pěší Zbrojnoši provide the Late Bohemian army with paid, contract-maintained professional infantry.
