## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Sudličníci Zemské Hotovosti are Bohemia's Late Era provincial polearm infantry: developed field contingents equipped with chronology-valid sudlice and related practical pole weapons, capable of bracing against mounted pressure, hooking shields or riders, pressing engaged armored troops, and redressing before their longer weapons lose order in close congestion.**

Their signature is **Brace, Hook, Press, Redress**.

The family does not replace Knížecí Kopiníci. Ducal spears retain the cleaner defensive line, better cohesion under cavalry reception, and broader formation reliability. The Late polearm card trades some shield protection and static steadiness for aggressive reach, leverage, armor interaction, anti-mounted follow-through, and formation disruption.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Sudličníci Zemské Hotovosti
- **English support name:** Bohemian Provincial Polearms
- **Class:** Late aggressive polearm infantry
- **Signature:** Brace, Hook, Press, Redress
- **Battle promise:** Meet mounted or armored pressure with developed pole weapons, disrupt one engaged formation through reach and leverage, then redress before close congestion removes the weapon advantage.
- **Best use:** Supported anti-cavalry sectors, flanks of spear formations, attacks on engaged armored infantry, breach shoulders, confined approaches with working depth, and defensive counterpressure.
- **Strong against:** Light and medium cavalry from a prepared front, armored ordinary infantry already fixed, shielded troops under pressure, and lesser melee infantry unable to close cleanly.
- **Conditional against:** Heavy cavalry, peer polearms, maintained swordsmen, and household foot when terrain, depth, leadership, target fixation, and support favor the Bohemian formation.
- **Weak against:** Archers and crossbows, open flanks, several-direction pressure, dense woodland, severe congestion, sword-and-shield infantry inside the weapon head, and prolonged unsupported melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Every company consumes qualified provincial fighters, polearm heads, shafts, smiths, carpenters, officers, sidearms, helmets, armor, food, transport, and repeated collective practice.
- **Player instruction:** Brace against the charge, hook only after contact is controlled, press one fixed target, and redress before the formation becomes a crowded mass of long weapons.
- **Flavor:** **The Late Bohemian polearm is not merely a longer spear. It is a working weapon of point, hook, blade, haft, leverage, and disciplined files.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Brace, Hook, Press, Redress

### 8.1 Brace

Establish one prepared front with points, working depth, secure shoulders, officers, and space behind the files.

### 8.2 Hook

After contact or fixation, use hooks, blades, shafts, and leverage against exposed shields, weapons, limbs, tack, or riders. Outcomes remain conditional.

### 8.3 Press

Advance or counterpress through one bounded sector while the target is fixed and friendly support protects the formation's shoulders.

### 8.4 Redress

Stop, recover weapon spacing, replace broken shafts and exhausted files, reconnect standards, and shorten frontage before close congestion destroys Polearm Order.
