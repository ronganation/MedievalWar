## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority integrates Holland's mature non-roster Realm authorities into one coherent January 1, 1062, Winter opening-situation contract. It identifies the opening political center, regency, household, claims, territorial pressures, diplomatic relationships, geography, economy, public order, population, ecclesiastical interfaces, military-source posture, campaign objectives, AI orientation, and player-facing experience that must agree before an Opening State payload may be built.

This authority does not duplicate the controlling specialist authorities. It routes their accepted conclusions, preserves their ownership, records unresolved implementation gates, and defines the minimum cross-portfolio conditions for later Opening State population.

This file is not an Opening State payload, implementation register, stable-ID allocation, registry row set, importer input, runtime state, save/load contract, or Unreal asset package.

- Robert the Frisian remains externally owned and has no opening Holland office.
- Utrecht pressure exists without transferring every Utrecht institution or property to Holland.
- Holland's economy, population, public order, and settlement systems remain real but unbound where exact values are unresolved.
- Winter shapes routine and access without aging characters or advancing historical chronology.
- Military source posture is integrated without creating or reviewing unit profiles.
- The package is statically coherent while shared-register population remains blocked.
- A claimed settlement remains distinct from a controlled settlement, an accessible port, an owned property, and a revenue right.
- An unresolved exact location remains null rather than receiving a plausible default.
- A specialist conflict blocks promotion until an owner disposition or successor reconciliation exists.
- Regency transition checks biology, recognition, government capacity, and current crisis instead of using a birthday alone.

- Stable IDs or registry rows are allocated.
- Units and Roster content is created, revised, or audited.
- Unreal validation is claimed without editor evidence.
