## 3. Life-Stage Agency Matrix
| Life stage | Independent choice | Supervised choice | Prohibited or unavailable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protected Child | Age-appropriate preferences and trusted access | Education, residence, ceremony | Marriage, office, property settlement, diplomacy |
| Observing Dynast | Bounded participation and relationship formation | Patronage observation, dynastic instruction | Independent treaty or household government |
| Participating Dynast | Supervised intercession and household activity | Travel, representation, preliminary marriage discussion | Binding marriage without valid authority and process |
| Marriage-Eligible Principal | Consent-bearing participation where framework permits | Negotiated marriage, provision, household transfer | Ownership transfer, fabricated affection, automatic children |
| Adult Dynastic Woman | Household, patronage, intercession, diplomacy, property use where valid | Office-specific powers | Automatic co-rule, territorial command, battlefield command |

Agency expands by biological capacity, knowledge, recognition, household position, and institutional opportunity rather than rank alone.

## 29. Development Portfolio
### 29.1 Protected Comital Daughter
Prioritize safety, education, household standing, and stable sibling relationships.

### 29.2 Holland Dynastic Intermediary
Develop patronage, household negotiation, and links among Holland, Saxon, Flemish, and ecclesiastical networks.

### 29.3 Flemish-Connected Stepdaughter
If Gertrude marries Robert, Bertha enters a transformed household with increased opportunity and potential loss of autonomy.

### 29.4 French Royal Consort
If the Philip marriage occurs, Bertha gains royal rank and a new household while facing limited documentation, cross-Realm dependence, and marriage politics.

### 29.5 Dynastic Mother and Patron
If children and institutions emerge, Bertha may gain long-term political, patronage, and succession relevance.

### 29.6 Repudiated or Displaced Queen
If marriage breaks down, Bertha's residence, provision, children, court access, and Holland-Flanders relationships become crisis questions rather than a removal from play.

### 29.7 Alternate Marriage or Unmarried Adult
If the French path does not occur, Bertha may enter another dynastic settlement or retain household and patronage agency without marriage.
