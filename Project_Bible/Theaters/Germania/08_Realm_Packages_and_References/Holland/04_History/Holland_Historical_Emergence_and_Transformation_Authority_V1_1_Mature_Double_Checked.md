## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's later history should emerge through conditional pathways rather than fixed scripts. The historical record supplies recognizable possibilities: protection through Robert the Frisian, Utrecht recognition and enforcement, territorial loss and recovery, Dirk V's transition to personal rule, Bertha's dynastic marriage, the younger Floris's ecclesiastical formation, Investiture-era alignment, consolidation of comital Holland, and eventual urban, maritime, fiscal, military, and gunpowder development. None is guaranteed. Every pathway requires eligible actors, chronology, knowledge, relationships, institutions, routes, resources, capacity, current momentum, and owner-controlled outcomes.
