## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland requires historical memory because its 1062 identity depends on a deceased predecessor, a child count, an active regent, a vulnerable dynasty, inherited claims, contested territory, comital and monastic commemoration, disputed external recognition, and the continuing interpretation of loss, protection, autonomy, and recovery. Memory must be held by specific people, households, dynasties, institutions, communities, and governments. No memory is universal, omniscient, automatically true, or mechanically equivalent to loyalty, grievance, claim, trust, hostility, legitimacy, or momentum.
