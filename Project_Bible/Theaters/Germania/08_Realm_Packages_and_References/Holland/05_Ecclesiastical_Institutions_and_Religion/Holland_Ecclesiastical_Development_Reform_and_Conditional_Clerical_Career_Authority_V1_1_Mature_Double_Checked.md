## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's ecclesiastical development must emerge from institutions, people, resources, knowledge, relationships, political pressure, and current world state rather than from a universal technology ladder or a fixed historical script. Reform may strengthen, redirect, divide, constrain, or suppress existing religious capabilities, but it creates no church, cleric, office, building, book, estate, jurisdiction, or allegiance by itself. Clerical careers are conditional person-specific pathways. They require a living eligible character, age-appropriate formation, access to a real institution, provision, an available office or service role where relevant, and approval by every owning system.
