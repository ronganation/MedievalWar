## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's ecclesiastical life is a substantive faction system, not flavor attached to a generic church faction. It connects dynastic commemoration, burial, patronage, local worship, clerical literacy, archives, education, mediation, property, jurisdiction, advocacy, protection, reform, diocesan oversight, Imperial church politics, and conditional clerical careers. In January 1062, Egmond is the strongest bounded Holland-facing institutional surface, while Utrecht remains the dominant external diocesan and political-church interface. Exact local institutions, personnel, property, revenue, rights, and officeholders remain evidence-gated.
