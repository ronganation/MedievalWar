## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland requires a local religious portfolio below the level of Egmond Abbey and Utrecht's external diocesan institutions. Local churches, chapels, clerical services, worship communities, burial practices, literacy, records, mediation, education, hospitality, charity, and communication can materially shape government and society, but exact January 1062 institutions, clergy, offices, buildings, property, books, communities, and operational capacity remain evidence-gated. This authority establishes the complete framework without inventing a parish network or clerical roster.
