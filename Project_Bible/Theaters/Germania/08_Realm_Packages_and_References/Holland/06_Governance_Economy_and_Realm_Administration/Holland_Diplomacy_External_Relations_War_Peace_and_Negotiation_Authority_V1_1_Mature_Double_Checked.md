## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland’s diplomacy begins from a vulnerable minority government whose external relations are actor-specific, directed, unequal, and constrained by knowledge, communication, recognition, access, military reality, dynastic interests, property claims, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, and available resources. The realm does not possess a universal diplomatic stance toward another realm. Negotiation can produce proposals, understandings, promises, guarantees, truces, settlements, or agreements only when authorized actors communicate defined terms and the relevant owners recognize, ratify, execute, and maintain them.

## 4. Core Separations

Contact is not recognition.  
Recognition is not friendship.  
Kinship is not alliance.  
Protection is not ownership.  
Patronage is not submission.  
Negotiation is not agreement.  
Agreement is not ratification.  
Ratification is not communication.  
Communication is not implementation.  
Promise is not delivery.  
Guarantee is not performance.  
Truce is not peace.  
Peace is not reconciliation.  
War authorization is not an army.  
War aim is not possession or victory.  
Occupation is not title.  
Ceasefire does not settle every claim.  
Marriage proposal is not betrothal or marriage.  
Hostage, ward, guest, and prisoner are distinct states.  
Research and decisions create no envoy, treaty, actor, knowledge, route, army, hostage, marriage, payment, territory, property, guarantee, or compliance.
