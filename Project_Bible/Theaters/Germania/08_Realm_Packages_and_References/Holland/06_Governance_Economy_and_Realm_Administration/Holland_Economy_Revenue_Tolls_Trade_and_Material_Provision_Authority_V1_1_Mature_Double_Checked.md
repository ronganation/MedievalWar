## 4. Core Separations

Economic potential is not current production.  
Production is not available surplus.  
Surplus is not collected revenue.  
A right is not an assessed obligation.  
Assessment is not collection.  
Collection is not custody.  
Custody is not disposable treasury.  
Assignment is not payment.  
Payment is not delivery.  
A toll right is not a toll site.  
A toll site is not traffic.  
Traffic is not successful collection.  
A market is not a settlement economy.  
Trade access is not trade flow.  
Trade flow is not profit to the count.  
Goods are not transport.  
Transport is not delivery.  
Delivery is not usable provision.  
Research and decisions create no money, people, rights, markets, goods, ships, carts, animals, buildings, roads, ports, ferries, or routes.
