## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's landholding and property system must be represented through distinct estates, parcels, places, buildings, use rights, revenues, services, jurisdictions, custodianships, protections, inheritances, grants, disputes, and directed patronage relationships. The comital title does not make every asset comital property. Political control does not erase household, community, ecclesiastical, dynastic, customary, or external rights. Patronage can protect, support, recognize, negotiate, or transfer a defined interest, but it does not create unrestricted ownership, automatic loyalty, canonical appointment power, land, buildings, people, revenue, or institutional capacity.

## 4. Core Separations

Territory is not property.  
Political control is not ownership.  
Ownership is not possession.  
Possession is not beneficial use.  
Use is not revenue entitlement.  
Revenue entitlement is not collection.  
Jurisdiction is not ownership.  
Protection is not possession.  
Custody is not ownership.  
Administration is not beneficial ownership.  
A grant is not delivery.  
Delivery is not recognition.  
Recognition is not uncontested enjoyment.  
A gift is not unrestricted transfer of every associated right.  
Patronage is not purchase of loyalty or office.  
Inheritance eligibility is not succession.  
Succession is not possession or enforcement.  
Confiscation is not automatically lawful ownership.  
Occupation is not permanent title.  
Research and decisions create no land, property, building, holder, tenant, worker, right, due, service, revenue, document, boundary, or patronage relationship.
