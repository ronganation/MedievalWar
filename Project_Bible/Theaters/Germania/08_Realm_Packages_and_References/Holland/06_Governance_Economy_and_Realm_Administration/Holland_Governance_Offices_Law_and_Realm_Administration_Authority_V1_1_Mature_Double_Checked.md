## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland begins as a minority comital government centered on Dirk V's hereditary claim and Gertrude of Saxony's active regency. Government operates through the comital household, directed relationships, issue-specific consultation, temporary or continuing delegation, regional cooperation, records, routes, stores, accessible revenue, dispute handling, and external recognition. This authority develops that government into a full campaign system without inventing a modern cabinet, fixed bureaucracy, universal council, named officers, free administrative capacity, or an opening payload.

## 4. Core Separations

Government is not possession.  
Claim is not control.  
Title is not practical reach.  
Regency is not ownership of the ruler.  
Consultation is not a universal council vote.  
Advice is not consent.  
Consent is not execution.  
An order is not communication.  
Communication is not recognition.  
Recognition is not compliance.  
Compliance is not enforcement.  
Delegation is not creation of an officer.  
A function is not a canonical office.  
An office is not an officeholder.  
Appointment is not installation or operation.  
Judgment is not enforcement.  
Revenue right is not collected income.  
Research and decisions unlock no people, records, routes, buildings, stores, or administrative capacity.
