## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's settlements, waterways, crossings, landings, ports, ferries, roads, tracks, embankments, stores, halls, religious structures, defenses, and other built surfaces must exist as distinct, owned, finite, maintainable world-state records. A map label, strategic need, economic opportunity, military purpose, or development decision does not create a place, route, building, workforce, vessel, material stock, function, or capacity. Holland's environmental and riverine character should shape administration, trade, mobility, defense, and development without reducing the realm to a universal river bonus or projecting later urban and hydraulic systems backward into 1062.

## 4. Core Separations

A name is not a canonical place record.  
A settlement is not every building within it.  
A place is not its controller.  
Control is not property ownership.  
Property ownership is not jurisdiction.  
Jurisdiction is not practical operation.  
A waterway is not a usable route.  
A route is not access.  
Access is not transport capacity.  
A landing is not a port.  
A port is not traffic.  
A ferry right is not a ferry vessel, crew, service, or crossing.  
A building is not its function.  
Construction is not completion.  
Completion is not operation.  
Operation is not maintenance.  
Repair does not recreate lost people, rights, materials, records, or stored goods.  
Research and decisions create no place, route, building, boat, cart, animal, laborer, specialist, tool, material, right, function, or capacity.
