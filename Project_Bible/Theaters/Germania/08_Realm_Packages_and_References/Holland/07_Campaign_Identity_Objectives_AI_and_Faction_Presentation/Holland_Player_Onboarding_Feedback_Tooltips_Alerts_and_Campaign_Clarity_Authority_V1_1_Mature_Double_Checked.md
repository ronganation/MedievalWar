## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland's onboarding must teach the player how a vulnerable minority county actually works. The opening experience should clarify that <Person>Dirk V</Person> is the hereditary legal center while <Person>Gertrude of Saxony</Person> governs as regent and guardian; that claims, control, property, administrative reach, and recognition are separate; that river routes provide opportunity only when access, providers, transport, security, and capacity exist; and that objectives guide attention without creating the resources needed to complete them. Feedback should explain causality, uncertainty, ownership, and consequences while avoiding tutorial clutter, false precision, hidden trap choices, and military-roster intrusion.
