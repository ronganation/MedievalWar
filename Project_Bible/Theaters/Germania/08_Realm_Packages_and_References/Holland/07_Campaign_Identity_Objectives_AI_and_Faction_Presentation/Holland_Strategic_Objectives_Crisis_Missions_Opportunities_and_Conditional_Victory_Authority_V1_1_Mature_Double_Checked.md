## 1. Authority Verdict

Holland requires a strategic-objective portfolio that translates its minority government, dynastic vulnerability, fragmented reach, riverine geography, limited resources, institutional continuity, property disputes, and unequal diplomacy into readable player choices. Objectives must arise from current world state rather than fixed historical scripts. They may focus attention, expose alternatives, and route consequences, but may never manufacture the people, rights, income, routes, buildings, agreements, military forces, or institutional capacity needed to complete them.

## 4. Core Separations

An objective is not a command.  
An objective definition is not an active objective instance.  
Eligibility is not offering.  
Offering is not acceptance.  
Tracking is not completion.  
Success is not a free reward.  
Failure is not arbitrary punishment.  
A crisis is not guaranteed collapse.  
An opportunity is not guaranteed benefit.  
A historical precedent is not a scripted outcome.  
Victory is not permanent control of every domain.  
Defeat is not automatic campaign termination.  
Mission text is not canonical state.  
Objective urgency does not create authority, people, access, resources, or capacity.
