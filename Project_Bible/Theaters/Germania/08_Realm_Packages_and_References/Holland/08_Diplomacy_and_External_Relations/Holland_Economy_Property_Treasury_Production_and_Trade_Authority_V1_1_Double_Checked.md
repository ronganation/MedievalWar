## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines Holland's realm-level economy as a network of property, rights, obligations, production, labor, storage, markets, tolls, transport, credit, treasury decisions, and external trade. It establishes how a small and politically contested county can survive, recover, consolidate, and eventually develop strong commercial and maritime capacity without receiving wealth merely from its faction identity or from later Dutch history.

The authority separates gross production from collectable revenue, property from jurisdiction, treasury money from total social wealth, trade opportunity from realized exchange, and research from physical economic capacity. It creates no exact 1062 estate map, revenue total, treasury balance, commodity quantity, price, market flow, or runtime payload.

## 32. Acceptance Tests

### 32.1 Positive Fixtures

- A productive estate yields little treasury income because the right is disputed and collection access is weak.
- Holland lowers a toll, losing immediate revenue while increasing market activity and route use.
- Military requisition improves immediate defense but reduces civilian transport, trade, adherence, and later recovery.
- A port grows only after population, hinterland, storage, craft, security, routes, and market demand improve.
- Credit preserves the Realm during crisis while creating future revenue and political obligations.
- Imported military inputs remain vulnerable to route loss and supplier withdrawal.
- Holland remains prosperous as a compact Realm through connectivity, institutions, and specialization.
- A peace settlement reduces revenue but restores access and lowers military expenditure.
- A prosperous market district coexists with stressed rural households because benefits and burdens are unevenly distributed.
- Deferred maintenance preserves treasury in the short term while reducing route or facility capacity later.
- An in-kind grain obligation supports stores but cannot pay a cash creditor without transport, market access, and conversion.
- Loss of one supplier raises cost but does not collapse the economy when an alternative provider and route exist.

### 32.2 Negative Fixtures

- Every claimed property automatically pays full revenue.
- Gross production is deposited directly into the treasury.
- A market building creates buyers, sellers, goods, labor, and routes.
- Coastline generates trade income without craft, crews, landings, security, or demand.
- Research creates workers, ships, stores, tools, or commodities.
- A protector supplies unlimited free money.
- Exact 1062 treasury and population values are invented to complete a payload.
- Export income has no effect on domestic availability.
- Military recruitment ignores population, labor, equipment, and replacement.
- Later Dutch commercial wealth is granted as an opening faction trait.
- Credit is counted as treasury while the same future revenue remains fully available.
- A conquered estate automatically loses every prior customary and institutional right.
- Coin purchases goods that do not exist or cannot reach the buyer.
- Buildings, boats, roads, tools, and stores operate indefinitely without maintenance.
- One realm-wide price is asserted without a calibrated market framework.
