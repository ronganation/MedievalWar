V1.1 preserves that framework while improving the distinction between immediate control and durable order, between grievance and organized resistance, and between military cessation and genuine political, institutional, economic, and geographic recovery.

## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines how Holland's government earns, maintains, loses, and restores cooperation across households, settlements, landholders, institutions, providers, routes, and contested communities. It replaces a single abstract public-order number with traceable political, economic, institutional, geographic, and military causes.

Compliance is not affection. Resistance is not one universal rebellion state. Silence is not loyalty, taxation is not obedience, occupation is not legitimacy, and battlefield victory is not social recovery. Holland's opening minority government must build practical authority through recognition, protection, negotiation, performance, restraint, communication, and credible enforcement.

This file establishes doctrine and interfaces only. It does not assign exact opening values, named faction leaders, settlement bindings, resistance groups, event payloads, stable identifiers, or runtime state.

## 35. Acceptance Tests

### 35.1 Positive Fixtures

- A community recognizes Dirk V but evades a disputed toll.
- A settlement complies with military defense while opposing excessive requisition.
- Failure caused by route interruption is treated differently from deliberate refusal.
- Protector support improves security while increasing local suspicion and dependency.
- Defeating an armed revolt restores immediate control but leaves property and burden grievances unresolved.
- Remission and route restoration recover compliance more effectively than force where incapacity caused failure.
- Holland consolidates a conquered place gradually through protection, administration, accommodation, and repeated performance.
- Strong order in the comital core coexists with resistance in a disconnected contested area.
- Coercion produces immediate compliance but fails to create durable consolidation after the garrison withdraws.
- A performed settlement reduces grievance relevance, while a breached settlement strengthens later organization.
- Similar communities respond differently because routes, institutions, burdens, and alternatives differ.
- Post-conflict screening distinguishes coerced participants from organizers instead of assigning collective guilt.

### 35.2 Negative Fixtures

- Every dissenting actor becomes a rebel army.
- A single public-order number explains the whole Realm.
- Occupation grants immediate loyalty, revenue, recruitment, and institutional control.
- Poverty automatically creates revolt.
- Prosperity automatically guarantees compliance.
- Military repression permanently removes all grievances.
- Utrecht controls every local opponent.
- Gertrude or Dirk receives perfect knowledge of all local attitudes.
- Exact resistance values and named leaders are invented for an Opening State payload.
- Recovery occurs automatically when ownership changes.
- Grievance automatically creates a coordinated revolt without communication, resources, opportunity, or organization.
- Resistance spreads to every adjacent territory through proximity alone.
- Amnesty erases property damage, institutional conflict, and economic loss.
- Community membership is treated as proof of personal participation.
- A formal judgment restores order even when the ruling cannot be performed or enforced.
