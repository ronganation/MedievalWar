## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines how Holland's coastal, riverine, estuarine, wetland, island, settlement, port, crossing, ferry, road, and maritime geography must function as a connected campaign system. It establishes the distinction between claim, possession, practical control, access, influence, and route use while preserving exact opening polygons, settlement ownership, nodes, and payload bindings for later certified work.

Holland must not receive a generic water bonus. Geography becomes an advantage only when government, communities, pilots, boat crews, ferries, ports, stores, roads, strongpoints, finance, and military protection make movement and concentration possible.

## 27. Acceptance Tests

### 27.1 Positive Fixtures

- Holland holds a legal claim while Utrecht possesses or controls the disputed place.
- Holland negotiates ferry access without acquiring the ferry community or route.
- A working port improves trade and transport opportunity only when labor, craft, stores, security, and finance exist.
- A strongpoint delays an enemy but fails when isolated from supply and local support.
- Winter changes route reliability without imposing one universal map penalty.
- Holland remains viable as a compact connected Realm.
- Wider Frisian access requires specific relationships and local capacity.
- A damaged route returns through emergency, partial, restored, and improved states.
- Loss of a ferry does not isolate the Realm when a lower-capacity alternate route and provider remain available.
- A captured wetland district remains difficult to use because local guides and pilots have withdrawn.
- Military requisition increases immediate transport while reducing civilian trade, adherence, and later capacity.
- A settlement service area overlaps another center without transferring ownership of the shared communities.

### 27.2 Negative Fixtures

- Every territory inside a broad Holland outline is treated as opening property.
- A claim automatically supplies revenue and recruits.
- Coastline grants a naval base.
- A river grants free crossing or transport.
- Water terrain grants universal combat superiority.
- Later ports and cities appear fully developed in 1062.
- Occupation automatically transfers legal title.
- Regional units become Holland-owned because a region grants access.
- Exact boundaries are fabricated to complete the map.
- Research instantly creates roads, boats, ports, labor, or maintenance.
- Capturing a port instantly reveals all channels, tides, landings, and local routes.
- A single active crossing makes the entire network resilient.
- Military transport uses boats, crews, stores, and landings without competing with civilian demand.
- A campaign region automatically generates an exact battle map and deployment.
