## 1. Folder Purpose

This folder contains Holland's connected external-relations and realm-operating authorities. The portfolio begins with diplomacy but follows diplomatic decisions into geography, economy, compliance, recovery, settlements, buildings, and the built environment.

The folder therefore controls a coherent causal chain:

```text
Diplomatic relationship and recognition
-> access, claims, routes, and geographic control
-> property, production, treasury, and trade
-> compliance, resistance, reconciliation, and recovery
-> settlement development and physical built capacity
```

The folder does not replace Characters and Dynasty, Military, History, Ecclesiastical Institutions and Religion, Governance and Administration, Campaign Presentation, Units and Roster, Germania Theater authorities, or external Realm packages.

## 4. Authority Boundaries

### 4.1 Diplomacy

Diplomacy controls Holland's side of recognition, claims, protection, alliances, access agreements, war aims, truces, peace terms, tribute, guarantees, marriage negotiation, and external settlement.

Diplomacy does not own external characters, external forces, external institutions, exact routes, physical economic capacity, or settlement assets.

### 4.2 Strategic Geography

Strategic Geography controls geographic roles, claims, possession, practical control, routes, ports, crossings, service areas, chokepoints, connectivity, and access conditions.

Strategic Geography does not own production values, revenue, building payloads, unit profiles, or exact uncertified opening polygons.

### 4.3 Economy

Economy controls property relationships, production, consumption, surplus, collection, treasury, obligations, credit, markets, providers, transport capacity, and realized trade.

Economy does not create goods, workers, ships, routes, buildings, institutions, or recruitment pools through abstract research or income values.

### 4.4 Public Order

Public Order controls recognition, adherence, compliance, dissent, resistance, reconciliation, consolidation, and multidimensional recovery.

Public Order does not infer emotion, create named resistance leaders, replace law and governance, or convert economic stress into automatic revolt.

### 4.5 Settlement Development

Settlement Development controls construction, commissioning, operation, maintenance, damage, adaptive reuse, recovery, specialization, and visual built-state requirements.

Settlement Development does not create population, operators, materials, goods, institutions, troops, ships, or exact building payloads.
