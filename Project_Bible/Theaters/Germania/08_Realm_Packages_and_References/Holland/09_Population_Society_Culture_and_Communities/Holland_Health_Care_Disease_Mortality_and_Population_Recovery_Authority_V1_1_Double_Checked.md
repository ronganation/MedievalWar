## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines the population-level consequences of health stress, injury, disease, dependency, care, household support, institutional relief, mortality, provider loss, and long-term population recovery within Holland. It connects human wellbeing to households, food, shelter, water, routes, settlements, work, war, displacement, institutions, labor, recruitment pools, and recovery without turning health into one abstract Realm-wide meter.

The authority remains a historical campaign-design framework. It does not provide modern medical advice, diagnose individuals, create exact disease models, or claim precise opening mortality. It supplies causal boundaries for gameplay and future specialist implementation.

No exact opening health distribution, named healer, disease outbreak, mortality total, care institution, treatment payload, stable identifier, or runtime simulation is authorized.

## 32. Acceptance Tests

### 32.1 Positive Fixtures

- A household loses productive capacity because care demand increases, without deleting the dependent person's rights or agency.
- A skilled pilot survives but cannot work until shelter, tools, a craft, access, and recovery conditions return.
- A settlement restores housing and routes before provider skill and institutional care fully recover.
- Military losses reduce recruitment-pool recovery, household labor, and craft capacity through the actual people affected.
- An ecclesiastical institution supports limited hospitality while remaining externally institution-owned.
- Displaced people arrive with varied needs and capabilities rather than one uniform health state.
- Seasonal labor and shelter pressure affect local resilience without imposing one Realm-wide penalty.
- Population headcount recovers before full occupational, household, military, and cultural capacity.
- A recovering craft provider returns first to limited work instead of full production and simultaneous training duties.
- A household care shortage reduces other labor and forces explicit prioritization rather than generating free caregivers.
- Repairing housing does not mark a community recovered while livelihood, providers, and institutions remain disrupted.
- Permanent provider loss triggers household, property, service, and succession review without inventing a named replacement.
- An authorized outbreak response records uncertainty, finite enforcement, economic cost, and review conditions.

### 32.2 Negative Fixtures

- Holland receives an invented opening epidemic.
- One health meter determines mortality, labor, loyalty, and military performance.
- Missing people are automatically declared deceased.
- A care building creates healers, attendants, food, shelter, or treatment capacity.
- Disease is attributed to culture, identity, or political loyalty.
- Treasury instantly restores lost people, skill, care networks, or institutions.
- Every displaced person has identical health and dependency needs.
- Military casualties affect only army numbers and not households, providers, or recruitment recovery.
- Exact mortality, disease, injury, or recovery rates are fabricated.
- Modern hospitals, medicine, sanitation systems, or public-health administration are projected into 1062.
- Exposure automatically causes illness or death without an authorized framework.
- A recovering person returns immediately to full work because survival is confirmed.
- Care is allocated according to culture, identity, or political loyalty.
- Provider headcount replacement is treated as full skill recovery.
- A repaired building is treated as restored institutional care without people or resources.
- Unsupported personal medical details or diagnoses are invented for named characters.
