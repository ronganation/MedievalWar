## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines movement of people into, out of, within, and through Holland, including voluntary migration, seasonal mobility, contracted movement, household relocation, displacement, refuge, evacuation, exile, return, resettlement, and integration. It gives Holland a causal population-movement system grounded in people, households, routes, destinations, livelihoods, property, institutions, security, and political treatment.

Migration must not function as free population growth. Displacement must not become free labor, automatic recruitment, or a generic penalty. Return must not occur merely because combat ended or territory changed hands. Integration must mean workable participation in communities and institutions without requiring immediate assimilation or cultural erasure.

This authority creates no exact opening migrant population, refugee group, settlement allocation, route payload, returnee total, integration score, stable identifier, or Unreal object.

## 36. Acceptance Tests

### 36.1 Positive Fixtures

- A displaced ferry household arrives with boat-handling skills and dependents but requires shelter, tools, route access, and a relationship before restoring provider capacity.
- A merchant remains temporarily present and economically useful without becoming permanent population.
- Returnees recover shelter before property, livelihood, institutional, and political restoration are complete.
- Holland protects a critical craft provider while recording the labor and support cost to the host community.
- A household resettles permanently only after housing, livelihood, property treatment, routes, and local participation become viable.
- A community integrates into Holland's market and institutions while preserving distinct language or customary practice.
- Demobilized providers return through actual routes and household capacity rather than appearing instantly at origin.
- Recovery succeeds without requiring every displaced person to return to the original location.
- A safe-conduct agreement permits passage but still requires transport, supplies, escorts, and a usable route.
- A displaced craft household arrives without all tools, delaying restoration of the household provider role.
- A destination reaches shelter saturation and redirects later arrivals through a negotiated secondary movement plan.
- Political control is restored, but households delay return because property, livelihood, and institutional conditions remain unresolved.
- Household separation is recorded without treating missing members as deceased or dissolved.

### 36.2 Negative Fixtures

- A building creates migrants to fill its workforce.
- Displaced people become free labor, tax base, or recruitment.
- End of war causes immediate universal return.
- Return automatically removes current occupants and restores every former right.
- Temporary merchants and allied forces become permanent residents.
- Migration has no cost at origin or destination.
- Refuge capacity is unlimited.
- Movement occurs without route, transport, security, support, or destination capacity.
- Integration is represented as instant assimilation.
- Population is counted simultaneously at origin, in transit, and destination.
- A concept image is used to prove an exact displaced group or migration count.
- Departure is treated as successful arrival and integration.
- Safe conduct is treated as immunity from weather, attack, delay, or route failure.
- Every household carries all tools, animals, goods, and records without transport limits.
- Missing household members are automatically marked deceased.
- Host saturation is ignored while relief and housing remain unlimited.
- Restored political ownership forces immediate return and cancels nonreturning households claims.
