## 1. Folder Purpose

`09_Population_Society_Culture_and_Communities` contains Holland's population and social authorities. Together they define how people live within households and communities; carry language, culture, skills, knowledge, and memory; move or become displaced; provide labor and service; pass through the life course; sustain care and recovery; and express ordinary life through seasonal routines, consumption, hospitality, gathering, and recreation.

The folder converts population from an abstract number into finite people, households, communities, providers, institutions, relationships, and recovery processes. It does not create exact Opening State demographics, named characters, unit profiles, offices, settlement payloads, or runtime simulation.

## 5. Authority Ownership Map

### 5.1 Population and Society Umbrella

Owns population, household, community, provider, migration, integration, cultural, and social-capacity doctrine at the portfolio level. It does not replace its specialist successors.

### 5.2 Demography and Life Course

Owns anonymous and aggregate population accounting, life-course classes, dependency, biological progression, cohorts, population change, and generational replacement. Named births, deaths, marriages, ages, and succession remain Character and Dynasty-owned.

### 5.3 Households and Local Institutions

Owns household and community organization, representation, assemblies, local institutions, provider bodies, mutual aid, shared works, and collective capacity. Buildings do not create institutions, and representatives do not prove unanimous consent.

### 5.4 Social Status and Occupations

Owns multidimensional social position, occupations, skills, service, patronage, dependency, training interfaces, advancement, and downward mobility. It does not create offices, titles, named officeholders, tools, or provider headcount.

### 5.5 Education and Knowledge

Owns learning, apprenticeship, knowledge transmission, records, literacy context, message networks, information confidence, knowledge continuity, and the boundary between research and operational capability.

### 5.6 Health and Population Recovery

Owns population-level health stress, care, dependency, authorized disease and mortality boundaries, provider incapacity, convalescence, and multidimensional recovery. Named-character health and death remain specialist-owned.

### 5.7 Migration and Integration

Owns movement states, displacement, refuge, evacuation, return, resettlement, host capacity, integration, and population movement accounting. Movement does not transfer canonical ownership of people.

### 5.8 Language and Culture

Owns language-use contexts, cultural identities, customs, social memory, cultural contact, selective adoption, accommodation, naming principles, and cultural recovery. Culture does not create automatic loyalty, units, or military bonuses.

### 5.9 Daily Life and Social Rhythms

Owns daily-life doctrine for household and community routines, consumption access, hospitality, gatherings, seasonal preparation, recreation boundaries, ordinary disruption, material wear, and gradual normalization. It remains a full specialist authority, but every underlying good, route, health state, movement state, cultural fact, building state, or final visual asset remains controlled by its canonical specialist authority.
