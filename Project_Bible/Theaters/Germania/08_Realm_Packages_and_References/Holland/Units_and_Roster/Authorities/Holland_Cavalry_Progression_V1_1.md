## 1. Refined Executive Verdict

Holland now has a coherent four-family domestic mounted ladder:

1. **Roadward Riders** provide broad Early territorial screening, flank warning, counter-scouting, route control, reaction against small raiders, and controlled pursuit.
2. **Graaflijke Ruiters** provide scarce Early household escort, command security, crisis response, favorable-angle strikes, withdrawal cover, and extraction.
3. **Market Riders** provide conditional High-Era non-household escort, mounted threat, field-flank security, controlled medium charges, rapid reinforcement, and bounded pursuit.
4. **Banner Lancers** provide conditional High-Era domestic heavy shock, countercharge, decisive breakthrough, and banner-organized reserve.

## 5. Player-Facing Cavalry Ladder

```yaml
battlefield_ladder:
  outer_screen_and_warning: RoadwardRiders
  household_command_and_crisis_horse: GraaflijkeRuiters
  maintained_escort_field_flank_and_medium_charge: MarketRiders
  decisive_heavy_shock_and_countercharge: BannerLancers
```

### The ladder is not linear

- Roadward Riders remain useful after High-Era cavalry emerges because broad screening, warning, and pursuit are not household, medium, or heavy-cavalry jobs.
- Graaflijke Ruiters remain useful because command security and crisis response are political and household missions, not merely lower-stat versions of Market Riders.
- Market Riders remain useful because most mounted work does not justify spending heavy warhorses and lancers.
- Banner Lancers remain scarce because decisive heavy shock cannot replace ordinary escort, screening, and crisis coverage.

### Graaflijke Ruiters versus Market Riders

- Graaflijke Ruiters purchase household loyalty, command security, preserved political choice, and rapid response to one recoverable crisis.
- Market Riders purchase repeatable non-household escort, route protection, broader field-flank service, and controlled medium impact.

### Market Riders versus Banner Lancers

- Market Riders purchase flexibility, escort coverage, mounted threat, medium impact, and pursuit restraint.
- Banner Lancers purchase scarce heavy impact, countercharge, decisive breakthrough, and preserved shock reserve.

### Graaflijke Ruiters versus Banner Lancers

- Graaflijke Ruiters preserve the count's command mobility and answer a crisis.
- Banner Lancers assemble a wider, heavier domestic packet for decisive battlefield commitment.

## 17. Later Candidate: Mounted Arbalesters

### Provisional verdict

**Hold. Do not create the card yet.**

```yaml
working_name: MountedArbalesters
candidate_era: LateHighOrLaterConditional
candidate_role:
  - MountedMissileRepositioning
  - BoltThreatFromPreparedHalts
  - PursuitAndWithdrawalFireSupport
  - RapidTransferBetweenFiringPositions
candidate_is_not:
  - HorseArchers
  - FireWhileGallopingCrossbowCavalry
  - MarketRidersCarryingCrossbows
  - MountedHollandArbalestersWithoutSeparateProvider
  - MaasArbalestersTransferredToLand
```

### Required gate

- dedicated riders who are trained crossbow shooters;
- suitable horses and remount arrangements;
- crossbows, bolts, strings, spanning equipment, tack, and protected stores;
- a physical ride, halt, fire, reload, reposition, and escape doctrine;
- distinct captains and file leaders;
- clear separation from Market Riders, Holland Arbalesters, Maas Arbalesters, and mercenary mounted-crossbow families;
- finite rider, horse, equipment, ammunition, and recovery pools;
- meaningful firing, reload, mobility, armor, and contact constraints;
- historical, linguistic, equipment, and owner review.

### Reject if

- folder symmetry is the main justification;
- the company fires and reloads freely while galloping;
- existing riders, arbalesters, horses, or bolts are duplicated;
- the unit is simply better Market Riders;
- a regional or mercenary provider already owns the same purchase.

## 21. Decisions and Pending Questions

### Settled at static design level

- Holland has four domestic cavalry families.
- Roadward Riders and Graaflijke Ruiters occupy distinct Early lanes.
- Market Riders and Banner Lancers occupy distinct High lanes.

- no family automatically upgrades into another;
- whole-unit conversion is prohibited;

**Holland's cavalry progression now passes as a coherent four-family architecture: Roadward Riders control the outer screen and pursuit space; Graaflijke Ruiters preserve household command mobility and crisis response; Market Riders provide maintained medium escort and field-flank service; Banner Lancers deliver scarce domestic heavy shock.
