## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Low-tier local watch infantry
- **Signature:** **Ring the Alarm**
- **Battle promise:** Keep the alarm chain alive, challenge the uncertain approach, ring or dispatch the warning, secure one familiar post, and hold just long enough for proper troops to receive the threat.
- **Best use:** Gates, streets, store yards, settlement edges, ferry and landing approaches, road junctions, signal points, camps, and quiet garrison sectors.
- **Strong against:** Looters, isolated scouts, infiltrating raiders once detected, unformed crowds, and weak light troops entering a familiar constrained position.
- **Conditional against:** Huislieden, other low-tier watch formations, and disordered light infantry with local support.
- **Weak against:** Cavalry, professional infantry, organized spears, mass missiles, heavy assault, broad open terrain, prolonged field service, and several-direction attacks.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain persistent warning and low-demand security by committing local watchers, keys, signals, limited weapons, store access, route knowledge, and emergency-response capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Place the Bell Watch where a route and signal meet. Challenge first, ring only when warranted, keep the messenger road open, and hand the post to proper soldiers rather than chasing the raiders.
- **Flavor line:** **One bell wakes the street. The next carries the warning down the road.**

## 9. Core Battle Cycle

### Watch
Observe the assigned post, route, landing, street, gate, store, or settlement edge.

### Identify
Distinguish routine movement, suspicious activity, civilian disorder, fire, raid, military approach, and confirmed hostile action.

### Signal
Use actual bells, horns, runners, lights, markers, or local signals where present.

### Gather
Bring the eligible guard files together at the assigned post.

### Secure
Control keys, access, stores, records, exits, and the immediate lane.

### Delay
Use the familiar position, limited weapons, and local obstruction to slow weak attackers.

### Guide
Direct civilians away and proper troops toward the threat.

### Hand Off
Transfer the post to Holland Muster Spears, Toll Wardens, regional specialists, retainers, or later civic companies.

### Return
Restore the normal watch rotation, signals, keys, records, and local security.

## 21. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Bell Watch represent Holland's persistent local warning and security layer. They are not a uniform realm-wide police force and not a proper field regiment. A valid formation requires recognized watchers, combat-eligible guard files, signals, leaders, a familiar post, relief, and a clear route for passing the alarm and handing the position to stronger troops.

The Bell Watch are more reliable than Huislieden at observation, alarms, access control, and familiar-post defense, but remain substantially inferior to Holland Muster Spears. Their best victory is an early warning, an intact gate or store, a guided evacuation, and an orderly handoff.

### Recruitment text

**Low-tier alarm-post infantry for gates, routes, stores, landings, settlement warning, and brief familiar-post defense. More reliable than Huislieden in local duties, but lightly equipped and unsuited to open battle or prolonged field service.**

## 22. V1.1 Rename and Independent Double-Check Verdict

**Bell Watch** replaces **Local Watchmen** as the controlling card name. The predecessor name was accurate but generic. Bell Watch gives Holland an immediate visual and audio identity while representing a wider alarm network that may use bells, horns, runners, lights, markers, boats, or other locally supported signals. The name does not claim that every post owns a literal bell or that a uniform institution called the Bell Watch is historically attested.
