## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Huislieden
- **Class:** Tier 1 mixed-weapon common-folk task infantry
- **Signature:** **Put Them to the Task**
- **Battle promise:** Turn ordinary released contributors into one coherent low-demand task group, preserve carts, stores, people, routes, or rear positions, delay weak threats from a prepared anchor, and get the contributors home before trained troops close.
- **Best use:** Camps, carts, baggage, stores, animals, labor sites, prisoners, settlement approaches, quiet sectors, prepared obstructions, evacuation routes, and rear security.
- **Strong against:** Looters, isolated scouts, scattered raiders, unformed crowds, and light threats already obstructed by carts, fences, ditches, streets, or stronger troops.
- **Conditional against:** Other lowest-pool formations and disordered light infantry in a narrow supported position.
- **Weak against:** Cavalry, concentrated missiles, professional infantry, organized spears, prolonged melee, open ground, assault, pursuit, and complex movement.
- **Strategic bargain:** Preserve trained military pools by consuming broad civilian labor, household support, transport, tools, watch capacity, and recovery time.
- **Player instruction:** Assign the task before deployment, show the levy what must be protected and where to fall back, keep proper soldiers nearby, and leave before the anchor or escape route collapses.
- **Flavor line:** **Guard the carts, keep the road open, and bring the common folk home.**

## 26. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Huislieden represent the lowest pool of Holland's movable military labor: eligible and released contributors who can form a real task unit but lack the equipment, practice, or institutional source required by Holland Spearmen, regional formations, household troops, strongpoint guards, or civic companies. The name draws on later medieval Holland terminology for common people, but remains a gameplay abstraction rather than a claim of one uniform 1062 institution.

The levy protects carts, stores, animals, workers, camps, settlement approaches, evacuation lanes, and quiet sectors. Compatible weapons are grouped enough to keep the formation readable, but the company remains poorly drilled and fragile. Its value lies in completing necessary work and preserving better troops, not winning the main battle.

### Recruitment text

**Lowest-pool mixed-weapon peasant infantry for local guarding, obstruction, rear security, and emergency support. Cheap and useful around prepared tasks, but poorly ordered, weak in open battle, and extremely vulnerable to cavalry, missiles, and trained infantry.**

Huislieden are deliberately weak combat troops but must remain a useful military card. ## 46. Final Encyclopedia Addendum

Huislieden are not a weaker copy of Holland Spearmen. They are a different campaign purchase. The player spends broad, ordinary source capacity to cover tasks that would waste trained soldiers. The unit's best result is often a protected cart column, an open withdrawal lane, an intact store, or enough delay for spearmen, wardens, regional troops, or retainers to arrive.

A well-used Huislieden should feel economical and practical. A badly used one should expose the true cost of mistaking available people for replaceable soldiers.
