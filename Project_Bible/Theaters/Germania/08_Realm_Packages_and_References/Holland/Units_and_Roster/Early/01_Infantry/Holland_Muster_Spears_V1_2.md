## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Holland Muster Spears are the county's ordinary realm-core spear-and-shield infantry: community and regional files assembled under recognized comital authority to receive pressure, defend narrow approaches, hold a bounded frontage, and return before prolonged service strips the county's settlements, routes, and households bare.**

Their signature is **Hold the Narrow Ground**.

They are not disposable peasant filler. They are also not professional heavy spearmen. Their purchase is a dependable Early-Era receiving line formed from limited local sources whose battlefield usefulness rises sharply when the player chooses firm, bounded ground and protects the formation's shoulders.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Holland Muster Spears
- **Class:** Early realm-core defensive spear-and-shield infantry
- **Signature:** Hold the Narrow Ground
- **Battle promise:** Establish an affordable but credible receiving line, deny a narrow approach, protect more specialized troops, and preserve enough order to conduct relief or withdrawal after the immediate mission.
- **Best use:** Settlement approaches, roads, causeways, firm riverbanks, ferry approaches, camp edges, low ridges, field boundaries, and compact supported frontage.
- **Strong against:** Light cavalry in prepared frontal contact, raiders, scattered light foot, disordered pursuers, poor infantry, and ordinary attacks compressed into a short frontage.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry, comparable spear infantry, ordinary swords or axes, and broader infantry pressure. On firm ground with protected shoulders, they can actively contest ordinary foot rather than merely absorb it.
- **Weak against:** Sustained missiles, heavy shock infantry, concentrated axes, flank or rear attack, several-direction pressure, deep mud, open isolation, long unsupported melee, and forced pursuit.
- **Campaign bargain:** Useful local frontage purchased by drawing real fighters, leaders, spears, shields, food, and service time away from local defense and production.
- **Player instruction:** Shorten the frontage, protect the shoulders, receive one clear approach, relieve before exhaustion, and return the survivors to their providers.
- **Flavor line:** **The county is small. Every road, bank, and causeway must be made to count.**

## 11. Core Battle Loop: Assemble, Receive, Hold, Relieve, Return

### 11.1 Assemble

Bring eligible files to a real assembly point, inspect weapons and shields, establish leadership, assign frontage, and identify the protected mission.

### 11.2 Receive

Face a clear approach, set the spear line, and use shield coverage, depth, and bounded ground to disrupt or contain frontal contact.

### 11.3 Hold

Preserve the mission while trading Line Readiness for time, space, and protection. Holding does not mean remaining motionless until destroyed.

### 11.4 Relieve

Shorten the frontage, rotate tired files, pass the mission to another formation, or withdraw through a prepared route before the line becomes isolated.

### 11.5 Return

Demobilize survivors, reconcile casualties and equipment, restore local defense, and begin physical recovery.

## 21. Combined-Arms Position

### Comital Retainers
Retainers preserve command, household security, rally capacity, and crisis intervention. Holland Muster Spears hold broader ordinary frontage. The spearmen do not inherit household quality by proximity.

### Kennemerland Footmen
Kennemerland must own a distinct regional source and battlefield purchase if promoted. Holland Muster Spears remain the integrated realm-core family. Kennemerland flavor cannot be duplicated through a second Holland-owned pool.

### Rijnland Crossing Guard
Rijnland must own compact crossing-defense specialization after fighters are separated from ferry and transport personnel. Holland Muster Spears can defend approaches generally but do not automatically receive Rijnland's regional pool or specialist identity.

### Mounted Messenger and Scout Service
Mounted service identifies threats, carries orders, and protects communication. It does not create cavalry stats or donate horses to the spear formation.

### Island and Estuary Transport Service
Transport places the company near a landing or disconnected sector. The spearmen remain disordered and vulnerable until they physically disembark, assemble, and form.

### Future Town Crossbowmen
Crossbowmen provide missile pressure from behind or beside a protected frontage. Passage, line of fire, and withdrawal lanes must remain physical.

### Future Holland Serjeants or Men-at-Arms

## 36. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia text

Holland's counts cannot rely upon a large standing army. When danger threatens the county's settlements, roads, waterways, and narrow approaches, recognized communities and landholders may be required to provide spear-bearing files for local defense or bounded field service. These men are not professional heavy infantry, but under familiar leaders they can establish a useful line of shields and spear points.

Holland Muster Spears are strongest when the battlefield resembles the realm they protect: a short road, a firm bank, a causeway, a settlement approach, or a compact frontage whose shoulders can be secured. Here they can receive light cavalry, slow ordinary infantry, and give household troops or missile formations a physical line behind which to operate. On open ground, under concentrated missiles, or after their flanks are exposed, their limitations become apparent.

Calling the muster weakens the places it is meant to defend. Fighters, captains, weapons, food, and service time are finite. A successful commander therefore asks the spearmen to hold a necessary position, arranges relief, and returns survivors before one battle's victory becomes the county's next crisis.

### Short recruitment text

**Realm-core spear-and-shield infantry suited to narrow approaches and prepared frontal defense. Affordable and useful when supported, but vulnerable to missiles, flanking, heavy shock, mud, and prolonged service.**

### 40.1 Mature battlefield value

Holland Muster Spears should be dependable enough to win honest fights against poor or disordered infantry and to contest ordinary foot under favorable conditions. Their spear line is not merely a timer that delays defeat. When assembled, properly faced, and supported, they can preserve ground, punish a careless frontal approach, and make a short counterpress to recover lost frontage.

Their ceiling remains controlled. Heavy assault infantry, concentrated axes, strong crossbows, fresh heavy cavalry, exposed flanks, and prolonged unsupported melee remain serious counters. The refinement improves usefulness without promoting the card into household, professional, or heavy infantry territory.

### 40.2 Mature campaign value

The unit is a practical early backbone for a compact realm. It should appear often enough to hold ordinary Holland armies together, but each company still consumes real provider capacity, leaders, equipment, local defense, and recovery time. A player may build around several companies only by sustaining several valid sources rather than drawing unlimited cards from one abstract county pool.

### 40.3 Final naming verdict

Retain **Holland Muster Spears**. The name is clear, familiar, close enough to the scenario window for a strategy-game presentation, and already consistent with the faction-facing package. Do not burden the public card with **Holtland Spearmen** unless a later specialist localization review finds that the older form improves rather than harms readability. Use **West-Frisian Spearmen** as a contextual or localization alternative, not as an automatic replacement that broadens the unit into all Frisian populations.
