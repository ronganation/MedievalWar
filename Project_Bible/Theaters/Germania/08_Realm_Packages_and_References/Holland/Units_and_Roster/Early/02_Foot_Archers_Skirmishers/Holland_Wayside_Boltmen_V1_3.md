Their signature is **Loose and Move**.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Low-tier Early crossbow skirmishers
- **Signature:** **Loose and Move**
- **Battle promise:** Spread into small files behind or beside Holland's infantry, load slowly, release limited useful shots, and change position before cavalry, counterfire, or hard infantry catches the formation.
- **Best use:** Settlement approaches, camps, roads, banks, fences, gates, and protected shoulders behind Holland Spearmen.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exposed skirmishers, poorly protected ordinary foot, and disordered pursuers.
- **Conditional against:** Armored ordinary infantry at short range or favorable angle, but only as a limited threat.
- **Weak against:** Sustained archery, maintained crossbows, shields from the front, cavalry, fire, flanking, wet strings, bolt exhaustion, and melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain an accessible Early crossbow option with low volume, uneven equipment, modest training, and limited battlefield endurance.
- **Flavor line:** **They are not masters of the crossbow. They know how to load, loose, and move before the enemy reaches them.**

## 25. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Wayside Boltmen are ordinary military-eligible shooters assembled from Holland's small local crossbow stocks. Their equipment and practice are uneven, but a few controlled files can threaten exposed troops and add limited bolt pressure to a defensive line.

The company lacks the equipment, endurance, firing-post discipline, and provider depth of later Holland Arbalesters. Its best defense is to fire from protection and move before the enemy closes.

### Recruitment text

**Low-tier Early crossbow skirmishers with limited bolt stocks and modest armor pressure. Useful behind spears and around roads, banks, camps, and settlement approaches; vulnerable to cavalry, counterfire, equipment failure, and close combat.**
