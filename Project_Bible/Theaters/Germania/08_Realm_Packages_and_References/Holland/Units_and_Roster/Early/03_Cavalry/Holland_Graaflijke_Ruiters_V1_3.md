## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Scarce household light-to-medium cavalry
- **Signature:** **Ride to the Crisis**
- **Battle promise:** Preserve mounted choice until the decisive crisis, reach it before the enemy settles, break the exposed point through one coordinated strike, cover the army’s recovery, and return with enough horses and order to remain strategically useful.
- **Best use:** Open flanks, road networks, dry firm ground, command escort, pursuit of disordered enemies, protection of bowmen, and rapid reinforcement between separated sectors.
- **Strong against:** Skirmishers, exposed bowmen, light infantry, routing troops, isolated messengers, and disordered or fixed ordinary infantry struck from flank or rear.
- **Conditional against:** Light cavalry, ordinary cavalry, medium infantry from favorable angle, and weakened spear formations whose frontage is already fixed. A clean flank strike should be capable of breaking ordinary foot, but never a prepared spear front.
- **Weak against:** Prepared spears, dense shield formations, heavy cavalry, mud, marsh, narrow congested crossings, concentrated missiles, prolonged melee, and horse exhaustion.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain scarce mobility by committing trusted riders, suitable horses, tack, fodder, grooms, leaders, escort coverage, and remount recovery.
- **Player instruction:** Hold them as a real reserve. Screen only when useful, align beyond spear reach, strike the exposed side, disengage before the enemy closes, and rally before choosing pursuit or a second response.
- **Flavor line:** **The county is small, but its waters and roads divide every urgent mile.**

## 9. Core Battle Loop

### Observe

Use mobility to identify the real threat, approach, cover, spear frontage, and return lane.

### Escort

Stay near the protected person, message, bow formation, withdrawal route, or exposed flank until commitment is justified.

### Respond

Move quickly through an actual route to the threatened sector. Response speed depends upon ground, congestion, horse condition, and order.

### Strike

Attack exposed skirmishers, missile troops, disordered infantry, or an open flank through one controlled mounted action.

### Cover

Protect friendly withdrawal, rally, remount recovery, or reformation. Covering the crisis may matter more than pursuing the enemy.

### Return

Break contact, reform, recover separated riders and horses, and restore household duties.

## 29. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

The comital household requires riders for escort, messages, reconnaissance, and emergency movement, but those services do not automatically create a cavalry company. Graaflijke Ruiters appear only when eligible mounted fighters, suitable horses, leaders, weapons, tack, fodder, grooms, and remount capacity can be concentrated without stripping command and household security.

On the battlefield they are a scarce response force. They protect exposed missile troops, reach threatened flanks, punish skirmishers, attack disordered infantry, and cover a withdrawal. They are not knights and should not charge a prepared spear line. Their value lies in choosing the crisis, reaching it first, and leaving before the horses are spent.

### Recruitment text

**Scarce household light-to-medium cavalry suited to escort, crisis response, flank attack, anti-skirmisher action, and withdrawal cover. Strong when choosing favorable contact, but vulnerable to spears, heavy cavalry, missiles, poor ground, and prolonged melee.**

### 30.1 Corrected combat verdict

Graaflijke Ruiters are not merely messengers who can fight. They are selected mounted household fighters whose battlefield value comes from speed of decision, route selection, target exposure, coordinated short contact, and disciplined disengagement. A clean flank or rear strike can rout ordinary foot already fixed by Holland infantry. Exposed missiles and skirmishers should fear them.

Their ceiling remains controlled. They cannot break prepared spear frontage, trade evenly with heavy cavalry, or remain effective through prolonged melee. Their best result is a crisis resolved with enough horse readiness left to cover the army or return to household duty.

### 47.1 Final combat identity

Graaflijke Ruiters are Holland's scarce mounted answer to a battle that is beginning to turn. Their best position is not constant contact. It is a protected reserve lane from which they can observe several threats, shadow enemy movement, and commit only when an exposed target or endangered friendly sector justifies risking household mobility.

A clean commitment should feel decisive. Against exposed archers, skirmishers, broken cavalry, or ordinary infantry fixed from the front, the riders can rout or destroy the target. Against prepared spears, heavy cavalry, dense shielded frontage, or poor ground, commitment should feel reckless rather than heroic.
