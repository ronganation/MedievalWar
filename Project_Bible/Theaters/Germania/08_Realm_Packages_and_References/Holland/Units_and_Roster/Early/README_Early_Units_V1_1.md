## Holland Early Units

### 1. Purpose

This folder contains Holland's current Early-Era realm-owned unit candidates. It provides the working military base for later progression, provider certification, opening-state review, balance, and implementation planning.

The folder is a **design portfolio**, not an activated opening roster. No profile listed here independently creates an opening company, provider, recruitment pool, equipment stock, registry entry, payload, runtime binding, or Unreal Engine 5.8 asset.

### 3. Early Portfolio at a Glance

#### Infantry

- **Huislieden**  
  Lowest-pool mixed-weapon task infantry. Guard carts, stores, camps, workers, animals, rear approaches, obstructions, and withdrawal lanes. Their main value is practical task coverage rather than line combat.

- **Bell Watch**  
  Low-tier alarm-post and familiar-position infantry. Observe, classify, warn, secure one local post, delay weak threats, and hand the position to proper troops.

- **Holland Muster Spears**  
  Holland's ordinary realm-core spear-and-shield line. Assemble, receive frontal pressure, hold a bounded frontage, accept relief, and return to the source.

- **Graaflijke Getrouwen**  
  Scarce comital household foot. Protect the command center and resolve one compact crisis through breach closure, short close combat, extraction, or controlled counterattack.

- **Vlaardingen Toll Wardens**  
  Selected comital strongpoint infantry tied to Vlaardingen's toll, landing, stores, records, fortified approaches, and political authority. Hold and recover one prepared lane.

#### Foot Archers and Skirmishers

- **Reedbank Skirmishers**  
  Light cast-weapon approach screen. Observe, harass, disturb deployment, protect a flank or withdrawal, and pass behind the main line through a real escape lane before decisive contact.

- **Tideway Bowmen**  
  Holland-owned ordinary bow line. Deliver controlled file-based fire behind the spear frontage while managing arrow supply, section rotation, weather, and withdrawal.

- **Wayside Boltmen**  
  Low-tier Early crossbow files. Load, loose a limited useful bolt sequence, displace, and withdraw before cavalry, counterfire, or formed infantry catches the company.

#### Cavalry

- **Graaflijke Ruiters**  
  Scarce household crisis-response cavalry. Escort, screen, answer a recoverable crisis, strike an exposed flank or light target, disengage, cover recovery, and return.

- **Roadward Riders**  
  Territorial light cavalry for outer screening, flank warning, route control, counter-scout work, small-raider response, and bounded pursuit. Their mature loop is **Range, Screen, Turn, Pursue, Return**.

### 6. Core Combined-Arms Base

The Early portfolio supports a complete but deliberately restrained battlefield foundation:

- **Huislieden** cover rear tasks, property, obstruction, and emergency labor so proper troops can remain available.
- **Bell Watch** provides local warning, alarm transmission, familiar-post security, and physical handoff.
- **Roadward Riders** extend observation and screening beyond fixed watch posts.
- **Reedbank Skirmishers** shape the immediate approach and cover withdrawal.
- **Holland Muster Spears** provide the main defensive frontage and safe receiving line.
- **Tideway Bowmen** deliver sustained ordinary bow support from behind that frontage.
- **Wayside Boltmen** add low-volume bolt pressure against exposed targets and useful armor angles.
- **Graaflijke Getrouwen** remain near command as a compact foot crisis reserve.
- **Graaflijke Ruiters** answer an exposed outer crisis or exploit a favorable opening.
- **Vlaardingen Toll Wardens** secure one politically and economically meaningful strongpoint.

No single card replaces the others. The portfolio is strongest when warning, screening, frontage, missile support, reserve action, and recovery remain connected.

### 7. Collision Locks

#### Huislieden and Bell Watch

Huislieden own broad lowest-pool task service and emergency overdraw. Bell Watch owns persistent observation, alarm transmission, familiar-post control, and handoff.

#### Bell Watch and Roadward Riders

Bell Watch owns fixed local warning. Roadward Riders own mobile outer screening, route observation, and bounded pursuit. Neither creates the other.

#### Holland Muster Spears and Graaflijke Getrouwen

Holland Muster Spears own broad ordinary frontage. Graaflijke Getrouwen own scarce household command protection and one compact crisis intervention.

#### Holland Muster Spears and Vlaardingen Toll Wardens

Holland Muster Spears own general realm-core defense. Vlaardingen Toll Wardens own a selected comital strongpoint tied to Vlaardingen's toll, landing, stores, records, and authority.

#### Reedbank Skirmishers, Tideway Bowmen, and Wayside Boltmen

- Reedbank Skirmishers own short-ranged cast screening and fighting withdrawal.
- Tideway Bowmen own sustained ordinary bow-line support and arrow discipline.
- Wayside Boltmen own low-volume bolt harassment, modest conditional armor threat, and fighting displacement.

The Early missile cards must not collapse into cosmetic weapon swaps.

#### Graaflijke Ruiters and Roadward Riders

Graaflijke Ruiters own household escort, crisis response, stronger favorable-angle contact, extraction, and politically sensitive mounted duty. Roadward Riders own broader territorial screening, flank warning, counter-scouting, route control, and pursuit at a lower combat ceiling.

### 8. Regional Ownership Boundary

The Early folder contains Holland-owned profiles only. Germania-owned regional units such as **Kennemerland Footmen**, **Rijnland Crossing Guard**, and **West-Frisian Bowmen** remain canonically stored in the Theater regional portfolio. Holland may receive access through regional relationships, but those profiles and their people or equipment pools should not be copied into this folder.

### 9. Progression Boundary

These Early families provide the starting structure for later development, but this README does not define the full progression map.

```yaml

  - DevelopmentMayImproveEquipmentLeadershipSupplyAndDoctrine

  - WholeUnitAutomaticConversionIsProhibitedUnlessSeparatelyAuthorized
  - HighAndLateFamiliesRequireTheirOwnProviderRoleEquipmentAndCollisionReview
```

Examples of prohibited automatic conversion include:

- Huislieden into Holland Muster Spears or civic militia;
- Bell Watch into a professional town guard;
- Holland Muster Spears into pikes or men-at-arms;
- Reedbank Skirmishers into Tideway Bowmen;
- Tideway Bowmen or Wayside Boltmen into Holland Arbalesters;
- Graaflijke Ruiters into knights or later medium cavalry;
- Roadward Riders into Market Riders.
