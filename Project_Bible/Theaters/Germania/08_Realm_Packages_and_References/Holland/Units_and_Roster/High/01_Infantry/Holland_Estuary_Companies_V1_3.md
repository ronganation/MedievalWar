## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Estuary Companies are Holland's conditional High-Era river-landing and port-fighting infantry: organized combat companies embarked through real vessels and crews, landed under command, and used to seize or hold a quay, bank, ferry head, harbor approach, island landing, warehouse lane, or constrained waterfront position until heavier formations can arrive.**

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** High-Era river-landing and port-fighting infantry
- **Signature:** **Take the Landing**
- **Battle promise:** Cross by real transport, disembark under risk, secure one compact waterfront objective, establish a protected landing head, and either hand the position to regular troops or reembark before isolation destroys the company.
- **Best use:** Quays, ferries, banks, harbor gates, islands, warehouses, waterfront streets, landing heads, and embarkation protection.
- **Strong against:** Light defenders, disordered troops at a landing, missile crews caught before deployment, raiders, and ordinary infantry compressed in dockside lanes.
- **Conditional against:** Armored ordinary infantry with missile, surprise, obstacle, or numerical support.
- **Weak against:** Prepared pikes, heavy cavalry in open ground, concentrated crossbows, artillery, fire, blocked landing routes, deep mud, surf or current disruption, and prolonged isolation.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain a real maritime fighting arm by paying for fighters, officers, weapons, armor, vessels, crews, pilots, embarkation points, supplies, repair, replacement, and port opportunity cost.
- **Flavor line:** **The boat brings the company to the bank. Only order, weapons, and nerve can win the landing.**

## 10. Core Battle Cycle

### Muster
Assemble the fighters, officers, vessels, crews, equipment, stores, and mission packet.

### Embark
Load people and equipment without exceeding vessel capacity or stripping crews below safe minima.

### Cross
Move through an actual route affected by current, weather, visibility, enemy fire, and vessel condition.

### Approach
Choose the bank, quay, beach, island edge, ferry head, or harbor lane.

### Disembark
Land in an ordered sequence while the company is most vulnerable.

### Secure
Win the immediate compact position.

### Expand
Take only enough adjacent space to make the landing usable.

### Hold
Defend the landing head against counterattack.

### Hand Off
Transfer the position to Holland Muster Spears, County Men-at-Arms, Gateward Companies, Toll Wardens, or another valid formation.

### Reembark
Withdraw through actual vessels and loading points when the mission ends or becomes untenable.

### Return
Resolve people, vessels, crews, equipment, stores, claims, and damaged port capacity.

Estuary Companies represent Holland's conditional development of dedicated fighters for river, estuary, port, and landing operations. They are not ordinary crews pressed into combat. A valid company requires separate fighters, officers, equipment, vessels, safe crews, pilots, supplies, landing plans, and recovery.

Their battlefield purpose is compact and demanding. They secure the immediate place where waterborne movement becomes land combat, then hold, expand, hand off, or withdraw before the company is isolated.

### Recruitment text

**Organized High-Era infantry for river crossings, port defense, amphibious landings, quay fighting, and waterfront raids. Effective in compact landing objectives, but dependent upon real vessels, crews, routes, supply, and a recoverable exit.**
