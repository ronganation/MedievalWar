## 1. Why This Unit Comes Next

The portfolio now has mobile screening, ordinary spear frontage, strongpoint infantry, maintained armored foot, civic missile support, civic mixed polearms, household horse, and market-supported medium cavalry. The remaining major High-Era field gap is a **deep formation specialized in cavalry denial and controlled frontal reach**.

Gateward Companies own compact gateward contact control and mixed hooking or striking work. Holland Muster Spears own broad Early frontage. Pike Companies must instead purchase depth, drill, long-pike coordination, anti-cavalry frontage, and difficult formation movement.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** High-Era deep pike infantry
- **Signature:** **Set the Pike Field**
- **Battle promise:** Build a Low Countries pike field from disciplined files, deny frontal cavalry, hold Holland’s center under pressure, and advance the hedge only while frontage, shoulders, crossbow lanes, and Pike Depth remain coherent.
- **Best use:** Firm open ground, broad approaches, camp fronts, crossbow protection, anti-cavalry center, and combined-arms advances with protected flanks.
- **Strong against:** Cavalry approaching frontally, ordinary spears, militia, short-reach infantry pinned at the pike front, and exposed mounted pursuit.
- **Conditional against:** Armored ordinary foot, County Men-at-Arms, other pikes, and missile-supported infantry depending on angle, ground, depth, and support.
- **Weak against:** Flanking, rear attack, broken terrain, deep mud, concentrated crossbows, artillery, gate and street fighting, nimble skirmishers, and heavy infantry entering from a disrupted shoulder.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain powerful frontage control by sustaining trained files, officers, standardized long pikes, sidearms, limited protection, formation practice, repair, transport, and replacement depth.
- **Player instruction:** Treat the company as Holland’s adaptation of the Low Countries pike tradition. Measure the field, lock both shoulders, coordinate the crossbow lanes, advance in deliberate pulses, and stop before broken ground or a turning fight destroys the formation.
- **Flavor line:** **Holland's horsemen may own the road, but the pike companies decide where cavalry may cross the field.**

## 9. Core Battle Cycle

### Assemble
Gather the complete company, officers, pikes, sidearms, stores, and support.

### Measure
Set frontage, depth, spacing, shoulders, and the intended stop line.

### Dress
Align files, pikes, leaders, relief depth, and signals.

### Advance
Move slowly while preserving the formation.

### Set
Lower and stabilize the pikes for expected contact.

### Receive
Deny mounted entry or absorb ordinary frontal pressure.

### Hold
Preserve the field center without chasing.

### Wheel
Change direction only through deliberate controlled movement.

### Relieve
Rotate front files and replace broken or disordered sections.

### Recover
Create separation, shorten frontage, and restore Pike Order.

### Return
Resolve people, pikes, sidearms, protection, service, and provider debt.

## 24. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Pike Companies represent Holland's conditional High-Era ability to create a deep long-reach field formation. The unit requires more than long weapons. It depends upon compatible pikes, officers, repeated drill, formation depth, suitable ground, repair, transport, service terms, and replacement capacity.

The companies anchor Holland's field center, deny frontal mounted attack, protect crossbow positions, and hold enemies for County Men-at-Arms or cavalry to exploit. They remain vulnerable when their shoulders open, the ground breaks their files, missiles reduce their depth, or the battle forces rapid turning and pursuit.

### Recruitment text

**High-Era deep pike infantry for anti-cavalry frontage, field-center control, crossbow protection, and slow supported advance. Highly effective from the front while ordered, but vulnerable to flanking, missiles, broken terrain, congestion, and rapid maneuver.**
