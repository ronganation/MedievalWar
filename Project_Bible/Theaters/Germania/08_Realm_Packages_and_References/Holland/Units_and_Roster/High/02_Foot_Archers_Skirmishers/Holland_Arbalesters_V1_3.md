## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Holland Arbalesters are Holland's High-Era civic missile backbone: maintained town shooters able to hold walls, gates, streets, roads, banks, and prepared field posts while delivering deliberate armor-threatening bolt fire. They are slower and less mobile than West-Frisian Bowmen, but substantially stronger against protected targets and in defended firing positions.**

Their signature is **Set the Shooting Post**.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Maintained High-Era civic crossbow infantry
- **Signature:** **Set the Shooting Post**
- **Battle promise:** Build and preserve a civic fire base, rotate sections through the reload cycle, break armored or compressed assaults with disciplined bolt concentrations, and lift the post before the enemy closes or destroys it.
- **Best use:** Walls, gates, streets, banks, causeways, bridge approaches, camp edges, field obstacles, and positions protected by Holland Spearmen or Rijnland Crossing Guard.
- **Strong against:** Armored ordinary infantry, exposed cavalry, shielded troops from favorable angle, assault troops compressed on approaches, and missile troops caught in a superior protected exchange.
- **Conditional against:** Heavy infantry, medium cavalry, enemy crossbows, and moving targets when cover, range, bolt supply, and leadership favor Holland.
- **Weak against:** Fast flanking cavalry, dispersed bowmen, artillery, several-direction attacks, close combat, ammunition exhaustion, hurried relocation, and firing posts without infantry protection.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain serious civic missile power by committing trained townsmen, crossbows, strings, spanning devices, bolts, workshops, stores, officers, watch coverage, and town confidence.
- **Player instruction:** Do not chase the battle. Build the battle around the shooting post, keep a withdrawal lane, and move only before the enemy fixes the position.
- **Flavor line:** **The towns pay for every bow, bolt, and hour of practice. Their enemies pay when the lane is properly held.**

## 9. Core Battle Loop

### Muster

Assemble eligible shooters, officers, weapons, spanning devices, bolts, transport, and support from a certified civic source.

### Equip

Inspect crossbows, strings, mechanisms, quivers, bolt bundles, helmets, sidearms, and any approved portable protection.

### Position

Occupy a wall, gate, road, bank, causeway, field obstacle, camp edge, or infantry-protected lane with a usable firing arc and withdrawal route.

### Load

Span and load by sections. The whole company does not become simultaneously helpless if relief and section discipline remain intact.

### Shoot

Deliver deliberate aimed or controlled volley fire at exposed, armored, compressed, or high-value targets.

### Relocate

Move before cavalry, artillery, or infantry fixes the post. Relocation sacrifices fire and risks losing heavy equipment or ammunition.

### Resupply

Receive bolts, strings, repairs, and replacement devices through a real supply route.

### Return

Hand back people, weapons, stores, and civic duties, then resolve losses and recovery.

## 36. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Holland's towns may eventually maintain crossbow formations capable of defending walls, gates, streets, roads, banks, and field posts. Such a company requires more than possession of the weapon. Trained shooters, officers, workshops, strings, spanning devices, bolts, stores, transport, watch coverage, and accepted civic service must all exist together.

Holland Arbalesters are deliberate shooters. Protected by Holland Spearmen or fixed defenses, their bolts can threaten armored ordinary infantry and mounted troops. Their slow reload and heavy equipment make them vulnerable to rapid flanking, artillery, and close assault. A commander must protect the post, preserve bolts for the decisive target, and relocate before the enemy closes the lane.

### Recruitment text

**Maintained High-Era civic crossbow infantry with strong protected armor pressure and excellent wall, gate, passage, and field-post utility. Powerful when supplied and defended, but slow, vulnerable in melee, and costly to remove from town security.**
