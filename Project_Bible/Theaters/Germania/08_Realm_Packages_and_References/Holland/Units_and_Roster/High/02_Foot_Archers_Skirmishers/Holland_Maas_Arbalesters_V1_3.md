## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** High-Era shipboard crossbow specialists
- **Signature:** **Clear the Deck**
- **Battle promise:** Establish safe firing stations aboard a suitable vessel, rotate loading and shooting files despite cramped movement, suppress exposed crews or a landing point, defend against boarding, then shift or disembark before the vessel becomes a trap.
- **Best use:** Deck defense, vessel approach denial, quay suppression, embarkation cover, and landing support.
- **Strong against:** Exposed crews, light missile troops, boarding files before contact, compact defenders at an open landing, and lightly protected vessels.
- **Conditional against:** Armored ordinary infantry at favorable angles and medium cavalry exposed near a bank or quay.
- **Weak against:** Fire, artillery, close boarding combat, heavy shields, bad firing arcs, vessel motion, bolt depletion, damaged craft, and several-direction attack.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain maritime missile power by sustaining dedicated shooters, crossbows, bolts, spanning devices, vessel stations, crews, stores, repair, and safe return capacity.
- **Flavor line:** **A crossbow is dangerous on a deck only when the deck, crew, firing lane, and reload space remain under control.**

## 10. Core Battle Cycle

### Muster
Reserve shooters, leaders, weapons, bolts, vessel capacity, crew support, and recovery.

### Account
Confirm mission, vessel, crew minima, stores, firing stations, bolts, and return plan.

### Embark
Load shooters and equipment without blocking vessel operation.

### Post
Assign firing, reload, bolt, movement, and emergency-defense spaces.

### Range
Identify hostile craft, exposed crews, landing defenders, or boarding threats.

### Load
Operate spanning devices and bolts within cramped physical space.

### Shoot
Release controlled section fire through a valid arc.

### Shift
Move sections without colliding with crew, stores, rigging, or other shooters.

### Cover
Suppress a landing, embarkation, withdrawal, or threatened deck sector.

### Landing Support
Coordinate with Estuary Companies without firing through friendly landing files.

### Disembark
Leave the vessel only through a real landing sequence and assigned shore post.

### Recover
Account for shooters, crossbows, spanning devices, bolts, vessel damage, and crew loss.

### Return
Restore civic, port, vessel, and missile capacity.

## 27. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Maas Arbalesters are dedicated missile specialists trained and organized to fight from suitable vessels and waterfront positions. They are not ordinary crews handed crossbows. A usable company requires shooters, leaders, weapons, bolts, spanning devices, firing stations, vessel capacity, safe crews, dry stores, repair, and a recovery plan.

Their strength lies in controlled deck fire, boarding defense before contact, and support for landing operations. Cramped stations, vessel motion, fire, damaged craft, bolt exhaustion, or close melee can quickly destroy their advantage.
