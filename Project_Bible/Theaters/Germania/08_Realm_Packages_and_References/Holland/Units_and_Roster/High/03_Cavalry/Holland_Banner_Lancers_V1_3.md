## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Scarce High-Era domestic heavy shock cavalry
- **Signature:** **Follow the Banner**
- **Battle promise:** Preserve the heavy horse behind Holland's line, threaten the enemy reserve, align one decisive charge against a fixed or exposed target, break through only as far as the objective requires, and rally before entanglement destroys the formation.
- **Best use:** Decisive reserve, countercharge, flank strike, rear strike, rout creation, and exploitation after pikes, crossbows, men-at-arms, or medium cavalry have shaped the target.
- **Strong against:** Disordered armored infantry, exposed missile troops, medium cavalry, broken formations, and ordinary infantry struck from flank or rear.
- **Conditional against:** Other heavy cavalry, prepared ordinary infantry after substantial disruption, and elite foot when combined-arms support and geometry are strongly favorable.
- **Weak against:** Deep pikes, prepared spears, concentrated crossbows, artillery, mud, ditches, narrow streets, horse exhaustion, several-direction pressure, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain a decisive mounted weapon by sustaining qualified riders, warhorses, lances, armor, tack, officers, banners, remounts, fodder, water, handlers, terms, replacement, and political reliability.
- **Player instruction:** Do not spend the banner merely because a target is visible. Hold the threat, choose the line, confirm the exit, charge through a real opening, then gather the company before the enemy reserve arrives.
- **Flavor line:** **The banner does not make the charge heavy. The riders, horses, lances, armor, and discipline gathered beneath it do.**

## 10. Core Battle Cycle

### Convene
Gather the certified banner, riders, horses, officers, equipment, stores, and support.

### Inspect
Check horses, lances, armor, tack, files, signals, exit, and rally plan.

### Form
Build the charge files and reserve without losing flank awareness.

### Threaten
Hold the enemy reserve, missile troops, or open flank through credible heavy-mounted presence.

### Align
Select a real target, approach, impact frontage, exit lane, and rally point.

### Charge
Commit the heavy horse against a fixed, exposed, or disordered target.

### Break Through
Pass only far enough to achieve the assigned objective.

### Break Off
Leave before infantry depth, pikes, crossbows, or enemy reserves entangle the files.

### Rally
Reassemble riders, horses, banner, officers, lances, and remount detail.

### Reform
Assess Charge Reserve and Warhorse Strength before another bounded mission.

### Return
Resolve service, casualties, horses, equipment, terms, claims, and provider debt.

## 25. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Banner Lancers represent Holland's conditional High-Era ability to assemble a domestic heavy-cavalry reserve beneath recognized banners. The banner coordinates the formation; it does not create riders, horses, armor, lances, officers, or loyalty. A viable company depends upon complete mounted providers, suitable warhorses, heavy equipment, practice, support, terms, and replacement.

The lancers are held for decisive work. They threaten enemy reserves, counter medium horse, and strike formations already fixed or disordered by Holland's pikes, crossbows, men-at-arms, spearmen, or medium cavalry. Their value falls quickly when they charge prepared reach, become entangled, or cannot rally.

### Recruitment text

**Scarce High-Era domestic heavy cavalry for decisive supported charges, countercharges, flank strikes, and bounded breakthroughs. Powerful against exposed and disordered targets, but expensive, difficult to replace, and vulnerable to prepared pikes, spears, missiles, bad ground, and prolonged melee.**
