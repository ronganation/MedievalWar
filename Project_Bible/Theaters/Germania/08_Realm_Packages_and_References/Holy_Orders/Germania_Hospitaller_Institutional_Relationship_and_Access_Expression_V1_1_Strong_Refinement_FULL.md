## 1. Definitive campaign promise

**Sustain the house, protect the journey, preserve the people.** A Germania ruler supports an independent Hospitaller relationship because care, hospitality, continuity and transregional connection have strategic value before any military contingent becomes available. Later armed service can protect those commitments or serve an accepted wider mission; it does not replace the charitable institution with a cavalry shop.

The authored campaign rhythm is **Receive, Endow, Staff, Shelter, Protect, Refer, Recover, Settle**. The central choice is how much actual capacity to commit locally, send onward or preserve for the next crisis. A successful relationship can remain nonmilitary. A functioning hospital can be strategically valuable while the Order refuses a war.

The strongest distinction is not a healing bonus. It is a durable receiving and support institution whose staff, supplies, premises, routes and obligations can be overcommitted. Military prestige does not erase civilian care, and charitable prestige does not create soldiers. The player receives meaningful support choices through a compact Total War-style interface, not individual patient administration.

## 4. One shared institution and directed Germania relationships

The shared institution owns identity, rule, recognition, membership, hierarchy, institutional missions and canonical formations. Germania authors local patronage, property, care support, transit, referrals, service access and closure relationships. A Realm owns only its own rights and contributions, not the Order.

Directed relationships matter: a ruler protects a house; the house accepts specified care obligations; a donor endows a revenue stream; the institution assigns personnel; a receiving house accepts a referral. None implies the reverse or every other relationship. Funding care does not purchase military command. Accepting patients does not grant sovereignty. Hosting members does not transfer their replacement pool.

A house can have corporate property rights under a host's jurisdiction. Custody, property, revenue, court competence, political sovereignty and physical control remain distinct. No Hospitaller province or separate Germania-created Order is generated. A transregional headquarters or territorial base elsewhere does not make every German house its sovereign territory.

## 11. Finite care capacity and the receiving contract

Care capacity is a derived result of staff, usable premises, food, water, supplies, maintenance, transport and continuing obligations. Nominal space is not a complete capacity measure. Money supports procurement and staffing only where real sources and access exist.

The same staff and supplies cannot sustain incompatible full commitments. Reserving support does not remove ownership or guarantee performance. Fatal pre-commit validation failure releases candidate reservations; interruption after actual travel or spending preserves consumed resources and continuing custody.

No hospital aura restores all nearby units. Care can support recovery only through the existing recovery owner's admitted rules. The interface should explain what is supported and what remains unavailable, not promise an unsupported percentage improvement.

## 12. Staff, members, workers and military personnel

Professed members, clerical personnel, administrators, care workers, servants, craftspeople, tenants, hired guards, guests and recipients are not one manpower pool. Membership and employment are different. A recipient does not become a recruit on discharge; a worker does not become a knight by long service.

Use functional personnel categories until exact historical terminology is admitted. A person may be qualified for more than one task but cannot perform incompatible simultaneous primary duties. Reassignment needs authority, actual availability, compatible skills, equipment and any required preparation. Care workers are not silently stripped from protected duties to fill a field formation.

Candidate referral requires an actual eligible person and institutional acceptance. Exact vows, eligibility, household, inheritance, departure and discipline rules remain with shared membership and character owners pending date-specific evidence. This file creates no named person, biography, officeholder or character ID.

Ordinary staff and recipients remain aggregate. A full character is justified only when an actual strategic office, dispute, captivity or emergence decision requires one. Leadership death preserves the house's accepted obligations and resources rather than deleting the institution.

## 13. Property, endowment and recurring support

An endowment conveys only the rights the donor can actually grant. Separate ownership, income assignment, use, maintenance, jurisdiction, access and reversion. Existing tenants, communities, claimants and superior rights remain visible. A rent can support care without transferring the estate itself.

Recurring support should be legible: what pays for ordinary operations, which income is restricted, what is already pledged and what surplus can be transferred. An impressive estate with disputed receipts may provide less usable support than a smaller reliable grant. No exact income, price, yield or maintenance formula is assigned.

Ordinary safe recruitment is not automatically a major economic penalty. Pressure follows actual prolonged or extraordinary demands, disrupted production, losses and diverted transport. Likewise, a care house does not automatically drain the district simply by existing; costs follow its actual supported establishment.

Routine receipts and maintenance remain aggregated. The player intervenes when support materially fails, an obligation changes or a claim becomes consequential, not to approve every sack of grain.

## 14. Money, stores, remittance and restricted assets

Keep gross receipts, local operating costs, restricted gifts, deposits or other held assets where separately supported, arrears, reserves and transferable surplus distinct. A charitable gift cannot be counted once as unrestricted local wealth and again as a distant contribution.

Remittance can support the wider institution only under actual authority, available surplus, route and receiving arrangements. A German house's revenue is not automatically a Realm tax or a foreign sovereign's unrestricted treasury. The Order's name does not create universal credit, deposit or banking services.

Food, fodder, fuel, tools, bedding and other admitted supplies compete with other consumers through existing economy and supply owners. These categories are authored support questions, not an exact medieval inventory. A rich treasury cannot substitute for absent goods or blocked transport.

If support fails, preserve accepted obligations and show the decisive shortfall. Narrowing future commitments, arranging valid deliveries, seeking another patron or negotiating reduced scope can be preferable to an unworkable expansion.

## 15. Pilgrim journeys, referral and transport

A journey requires a sending location, intended receiver, valid route, transport where needed, supplies, access and credible reception. A chain of friendly houses is not automatic safe passage. A site that can receive a messenger may be unable to receive a large party or wounded contingent.

Referral is not transfer completion. Distinguish proposed destination, acceptance, departure, interruption, arrival and actual receiving handover through existing owners. Sending capacity does not become free before its obligations are resolved; receiving capacity cannot be reserved twice for incompatible arrivals.

Escort is a separate armed function, not a property of hospitality. An unescorted journey may remain possible under the map and information owners, but no guaranteed safety follows from institutional affiliation. Road, river and maritime options require real geography and means.

Chartered shipping or allied transport can support movement where available. No Hospitaller fleet, naval unit, port privilege or Germania maritime base is created. Later maritime development elsewhere requires its own chronology, vessels, crews and institutional authority.

## 16. Wounded returnees and military recovery boundary

The receiving agreement can support actual wounded or recovering personnel without changing their military source. Realm troops remain Realm-owned; mercenaries retain company and contract relationships; Order members retain institutional membership. Care does not merge recruitment pools.

Battle and health owners distinguish fit, wounded, missing, captured and dead personnel and return the actual outcomes. This file neither revives casualties nor converts treatment into a full replacement receipt. Equipment, horses, standards, pay and contract obligations remain separate from a person's care status.

Discharge or onward referral does not equal combat readiness. Rest, compatible replacements, equipment, mounts, officers, training, integration and veteran cohesion remain relevant through existing owners. A formation can retain survivors while remaining unfit for its previous role.

A ruler can choose to fund reception, arrange transport, reduce commitments or seek another valid receiver. The strategic payoff is preserving a real recovery path, not guaranteed recovery speed. No numerical medical benefit is promoted.

## 17. Later military service and finite force access

Military development requires recognized institutional authority, an accepted mission, eligible trained armed personnel, officers, equipment, suitable animals where needed, support and an actual formation contract. A hospital, donation, date or crusade alone supplies none of these.

Possible authored service requests include protecting an accepted movement, bounded site defense, relief or an attached field contribution where the institution's development supports it. No claim is made that every German house historically supplied such missions. The institution can accept, narrow, delay, negotiate or refuse.

Funding service does not transfer membership or replacement ownership. Joint command distinguishes strategic coordination, contingent command, tactical authority, mission restrictions and recall. A recall received during battle does not delete units or seize tactical control; physical closure follows actual battle and campaign resolution.

This file creates no Hospitaller Knights, Sergeants, Foot Brethren, Crossbowmen, Hospital Guards or generic garrison card. Future units remain possible when their battlefield identity, source, equipment, scarcity, service, recovery and AI use justify them. Shared-source cards are not universally prohibited, but a new label or posture alone is insufficient.

## 18. Care, security and field-service competition

The characteristic allocation dilemma is not charity versus warfare as an abstract morality meter. It is a collision between actual commitments: staff needed at a receiving house, transport needed for a relief movement, funds promised for maintenance, guards protecting stores and eligible armed personnel requested for a wider mission.

The institution may retain a critical receiving function while declining an offensive contribution. It may fund alternate support, accept a smaller military mission or request a valid relieving force. An immediate threat can change priorities, but cannot manufacture missing capacity or erase existing recipients.

The player should see the principal competing use before commitment. A larger escort may weaken site security; a distant remittance may reduce the local reserve; a new reception agreement may occupy support needed for returnees. Consequences follow actual reservations, not a permanent penalty for choosing the Hospitallers.

Protected minimums are site-, mission- and threat-specific. No universal bed ratio, garrison floor or percentage of members retained is invented. Ordinary allocation remains automated until a material strategic choice arises.

## 19. Local politics, privileges and institutional independence

A house operates among rulers, bishops, towns, landholders, communities and other institutions. Their rights and expectations may differ. Recognition by one actor does not settle every local title or access dispute.

Useful authored tensions include a donor demanding preferential use beyond the agreement, a town disputing burdens, a host redirecting restricted support, a house unable to meet promised reception, or competing patrons requesting the same resources. Distinguish inability, delay, partial performance, contested interpretation and deliberate breach.

Remedies can include narrower commitments, valid substitute support, restitution, amended terms, mediation, suspension, withdrawal or coercive action through competent owners. No automatic excommunication, revolt, confiscation or war follows every disagreement.

The institution's independence is meaningful but not immunity. Its sites can be threatened, occupied, restricted or lost under actual campaign conditions. A charitable purpose does not make buildings invulnerable or prevent ordinary warfare; conduct and political consequences remain contextual.

## 21. Failure, evacuation, closure and recovery

Distinguish lost recognition, leadership disruption, exhausted support, damaged premises, route interruption, occupation, arrears and disputed authority. One failure can limit a function without deleting the house or every foreign relationship.

Evacuation requires actual transport, receiving capacity, access and disposition of people, supplies, records, animals and property. Not every recipient or asset can necessarily be moved at once. The relevant owners resolve outcomes; this file specifies no clinical evacuation procedure or guaranteed safe conduct.

Closure ends future commitments only as validly resolved. Existing recipients, earned pay, restricted gifts, debts, custody and third-party claims do not disappear. A receiving institution accepts only what is actually conveyed and supported. Institutional suppression is not a clean refund or automatic asset transfer.

Recovery can involve reduced scope, renewed patronage, repair, restored routes, real staff, actual stores and valid receiving agreements. Reopening a building does not recreate lost personnel or resolve every claim. Surviving records and institutional memory remain useful without functioning as magical capacity.

## 23. AI priorities, information and counterplay

Institutional AI compares mission fit, accepted care obligations, real capacity, local security, route reliability, support terms and credible closure. It may accept a small sustainable house instead of a grand unfunded establishment, or refuse military service while continuing charitable support.

Realm AI compares this relationship with domestic, ecclesiastical, allied and hired alternatives. It should not fund a house solely for an unavailable knight card or assume every gift purchases exclusive access. It can support several institutions where real capacity and politics permit.

Player and AI use the same eligibility, reservation, information and recovery constraints. Reports distinguish confirmed, reported, contested, unknown and inapplicable facts. Unknown capacity is not automatically zero, and a favorable summary cannot override a known decisive blocker. No omniscient enemy-store, recipient or member totals are exposed.

Counterplay concerns actual routes, support, property, political access and armed protection. Threatening a movement does not automatically destroy it; occupying a site does not erase all transregional support. Exact utility weights, stochastic outcomes, tactical behavior and balance remain unimplemented and untested.

## 24. Worked qualitative campaign situations

All situations are authored design examples, not historical incidents or executed simulations.

**The empty new hospital:** a ruler supplies premises and expects immediate recovery support. The building is usable, but staff and recurring supplies are absent. Establish limited hospitality if valid, fund actual capacity or defer the wider promise. No hospital aura activates.

**The successful army that cannot recover locally:** the army wins but returns with wounded personnel while the nearest house already has accepted recipients. Arrange another receiver, real additional support or a reduced commitment. Victory does not override existing care obligations or restore the unit automatically.

**The estate that supports a distant mission:** a donor grants rent expecting a local cavalry contingent. The accepted terms support institutional care and remittance, not military service. Honor the bargain or negotiate a separate feasible mission; no automatic knight pool appears.

**The interrupted referral:** a receiver accepted a party, but the route fails after departure. Preserve actual travel, supplies and custody. Seek another valid route or receiver. Do not free both sites' commitments as though the movement never began.

**The inherited Templar burden:** an asset is assigned to the Hospitallers but possession, tenants and liabilities are disputed. Preserve the assignment as a claim and arrange actual handover. No fully staffed house or converted garrison appears.

**The protected house under pressure:** the host requests every available guard for an offensive while the site and its stores face a real threat. Offer a smaller contribution, valid relief, changed terms or refusal. Care obligations and military usefulness coexist without unlimited obedience.
