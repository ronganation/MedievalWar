## 1. Campaign promise and substantive scope

**Support the Temple without owning it.** A Germania ruler can offer property, protection, transit, material support or a bounded mission. The strategic return is a negotiated relationship with an independent institution, not a new branch of the ruler's ordinary recruitment menu.

The proposed play rhythm is **Offer, Agree, Establish, Support, Request, Serve, Settle**. A ruler may gain a useful contact and route relationship without ever receiving a field contingent. The Order may accept an estate while refusing a proposed war. Both outcomes are meaningful campaign results rather than failed attempts to unlock elite cavalry.

This is a new Germania relationship expression, not a replacement for a shared Templar institution authority, the West Francia authority, or the completed Teutonic work. Its mechanics are authored gameplay proposals. It does not certify that a particular German house performed every proposed function.

## 3. Institutional identity and chronology

The inventory and comparator treat the Templars as a later transregional religious-military institution. The January 1062 opening therefore contains no Templar house, membership, military formation or recruitment right under this expression. Earlier churches, estates, pilgrimage, armed households and religious patronage retain their own identities.

Later development requires the shared institution to exist and a local relationship to become feasible. Calendar advancement, a crusade call, a church building or a donation alone does not create the Order. This expression does not rewrite the shared emergence model or promise the same destination in every campaign.

Suppression is part of the comparator's historical lifecycle, not a free player-selected destruction command. The comparator identifies 1312 as the historical suppression anchor. Dynamic investigation, restriction, institutional closure and local disposition require their respective political and ecclesiastical owners. A calendar date alone cannot settle every property or person.

Relationships are directed. A ruler's protection of a house does not establish the house's obligation to fight for that ruler. An Order promise to escort one movement does not create a reciprocal right to command all its forces. A papal privilege, local title and practical possession may differ and must remain visible.

A cross-Theater link references one institution and the relevant local relationship. It does not duplicate the West Francia or Italia authority into Germania or turn an external house into a Germania province.

## 5. Offer and negotiate: the patronage bargain

The ruler's offer should name what is actually available: a property interest, rent, protection undertaking, access permission, material contribution or payment. The Order evaluates the offered support against its mission, existing commitments, local risks and capacity. Acceptance may be limited, conditional, delayed or refused.

Useful bargains include support for a house, safe transit for a particular movement, a funded escort, maintenance of an existing property, or a contribution to an accepted expedition. These are design choices, not assertions that every historic German Templar house supplied each service.

The player sees the offered benefit, the requested obligation, the strongest blocker and the continuing cost. Prestige may affect the relationship through admitted systems but cannot substitute for title, personnel, animals, equipment or stores. A gift already committed to another recipient cannot fund the same agreement twice.

## 6. Property, title and establishment

An accepted gift is not automatically an operating commandery. Resolve the transferor's competence, the asset conveyed, retained rights, existing occupants, obligations, disputes and receiving authority. A grant of income may create a revenue relationship without transferring the land itself.

Establishment requires an actual location, permitted use, suitable personnel and resources. A house can be small and nonmilitary. An estate can support another house without becoming a house. A commandery label cannot create a fortress, a full treasury or a mounted formation.

The proposal uses existing property and settlement systems. It creates no Templar building ladder. Local maintenance, labor, storage and accommodation remain real constraints, but ordinary safe recruitment does not automatically impose a major economic penalty. Extraordinary prolonged service or disrupted production is resolved by the relevant source owners.

## 7. Germania geography without invented coverage

Germania is the relationship-authoring scope, not proof of uniform Templar presence across every Realm. Local ruler, ecclesiastical, urban and property relationships must be attached only where supported. No matching house is created for each developed faction.

Lietzen is retained as a research anchor because E1 explicitly identifies a Templar commandery history. The 1229 timeline entry and 1232 establishment account are recorded separately. A future exact-site pass should distinguish foundation, first surviving attestation, physical construction and institutional occupation. Later surviving fabric is not automatically a reconstruction of the first Templar phase. Modern national borders and present-day place descriptions do not by themselves resolve project Theater or Realm ownership.

## 8. People, membership and candidate referrals

A donor, tenant, servant, guest, hired guard, pilgrim and professed brother are different relationships. Local employment does not convert workers into military brethren. A noble's gift does not automatically enroll the noble or transfer an entire household into the Order.

A referral can begin an admission process where the shared institution permits it. Assessment, acceptance, training, equipment, animals, officers and assignment remain separate requirements. The local Realm cannot refill an Order formation from its ordinary knight pool by changing the label.

Nonmember personnel can provide valid work or armed service under their own sources and terms. Such support does not need to become a new Templar unit card. Existing recruitment-pool and assignment owners retain finite availability and recovery responsibility.

## 9. Revenue, remittance and financial services

The relationship can support revenue collection and a contribution to the wider institution where the actual property and agreement permit. Gross income, maintenance cost, local obligations, arrears and transferable surplus are different quantities. The same revenue cannot simultaneously maintain the house, pay a local escort and fund a distant expedition at full value.

Custody, deposit, credit, payment and transfer functions remain conditional on exact evidence and admitted shared systems. The Templar name is not a universal banking bonus. A deposit remains a liability to its owner rather than free spendable Order wealth. A transfer instruction is not proof that money or goods arrived.

A wealthy house can lack available fighting personnel. A solvent agreement can still fail for absent transport or blocked access. Present these as distinct causes rather than an unexplained financial penalty. This file sets no interest rate, price, exchange rule, remittance percentage or income multiplier.

## 10. Routes, lodging and cross-Theater movement

A valid relationship may support correspondence, lodging, storage, assembly or movement where actual facilities and access exist. Each function has a separate capacity and obligation. A place that can lodge a messenger may not accommodate an army or its horses.

Cross-Theater movement requires a real origin, destination, route, transport and receiving arrangement. Political permission does not guarantee physical passage; a passable road does not guarantee legal entry. Chartered maritime support requires actual vessels, crews and terms. No Templar fleet is created here.

The local panel should distinguish permission granted, support arranged, departure, interruption and arrival using existing owners. No new route graph or movement executor is authorized. An institution-wide network cannot teleport members, equipment, funds or replacement horses.

## 11. Request service without purchasing the institution

The requester proposes a bounded purpose, location, command relationship, support and closure condition. The Order may accept a narrower mission, request different support, defer because of other commitments or refuse. Shared faith does not erase mission restrictions or competing obligations.

An accepted escort protects the specified movement within its terms. It does not become an unrestricted field army. A joint garrison retains separate sources and command responsibilities. A Realm-funded contingent remains Order-owned in membership and replacement even when the Realm pays agreed costs.

Service requires available forces. A paper agreement cannot strip an existing garrison silently or duplicate a distant contingent. If no suitable formation exists, the relationship may remain useful for contact, transit or material support rather than inventing soldiers to fulfill the player's request.

## 12. Command, recall and mission change

The agreement distinguishes institutional command, local coordination and tactical authority. These may be delegated differently. A host's operational request does not automatically appoint the host as the Order's superior.

If the mission changes, reassess consent, support and feasibility. Continuing beyond the accepted purpose can produce renegotiation, refusal, withdrawal or dispute through the responsible owners. Recall requires communication, a feasible route and a receiving arrangement; it is not instant removal from an ongoing battle.

The UI should show the practical restriction before commitment. The player should not discover after deployment that an essential limitation was hidden. Equally, the system must not guarantee safe return merely because the Order has a valid reason to recall its contingent.

## 13. Conservation, loss, return and recovery

Reserve people, mounts, equipment, stores and transport once. A member deployed abroad is not simultaneously available at a German house. A transfer changes location and assignment rather than cloning a pool. Temporary dismounting retains the same people and accountable mounts.

Mission settlement distinguishes fit survivors, wounded, missing, captured and dead personnel; surviving or lost animals; issued and recovered equipment; consumed stores; and outstanding payment or property obligations. These are conceptual disposition requirements for existing owners, not new save fields.

Return is not immediate recruitability. Care, repair, remounting, training and cohesion recovery require actual capacity. Releasing a reservation does not refund consumed goods or erase casualties. A receiving house may be unable to accept every returning burden at once, requiring another valid destination or reduced commitment.

## 14. Privilege, local resistance and disputes

A protection grant, exemption or institutional privilege must retain its issuer, scope and relevant counterparty. It cannot erase all local jurisdiction. Practical possession, legal title and a disputed claim remain distinct.

Proposed disputes include contested donation, unpaid support, denied transit, excessive service demands, damaged property, disputed custody and conflict over exemptions. These are gameplay categories, not newly certified incidents at a named site. Resolution can involve negotiation, bounded restitution, arbitration, restriction or escalation through existing systems.

Do not turn every disagreement into automatic revolt or war. Conversely, a favorable relationship score cannot override an unresolved title or access blocker. Local communities and existing occupants remain part of the property and governance state rather than disappearing when an Order receives a grant.

## 15. Investigation, suppression and local disposition

The comparator's suppression architecture is retained as a lifecycle requirement, not copied as a universal French outcome. A Germania house does not automatically experience every arrest, trial or seizure described for another jurisdiction.

Distinguish an allegation, restriction, investigation, custody action, institutional suppression, property decision and completed local transfer. Information about an external proceeding may affect decisions without resolving local competence or possession. The responsible owners must determine which action applies and what remains contested.

If a property is assigned to the Hospitallers, record the particular asset, obligations, custody, receiving institution and actual handover. E1's account of later Johanniter possession is a historical direction, not a complete medieval receipt or proof of an immediate universal transfer. Personnel do not change profession or become Hospitaller soldiers because a property changes hands. Debts, deposits, maintenance and claims survive until resolved.

## 16. Distinctiveness and cross-Order collision

This expression emphasizes a negotiated Germania support relationship with the Temple: patronage, property, referrals, remittance, transit, bounded service and eventual disposition. It does not import the Teutonic conditional territorial-state model as the default Templar path.

Hospitaller care and hospital capacity remain a distinct next institutional lane. Teutonic membership, fortress operations and recruitment sources remain separate. A Templar asset transferred to another institution does not merge histories, commands or military pools.

No generic Templar Knights, Templar Sergeants, Templar Crossbowmen or Templar Garrison card is created. This is not a permanent ban on justified future profiles. A later card should survive on meaningful battlefield role, source, mission, scarcity, equipment, recovery, AI use and player identity, not just a red cross or a requirement to fill every roster slot.

## 17. AI priorities and fair counterplay

The proposed institutional AI weighs mission suitability, support reliability, available capacity, local exposure, wider commitments and credible closure. It may accept a useful estate while refusing an unaffordable military promise. A Realm AI should compare institutional support with its other military and political options rather than always buying prestige.

Counterplay targets actual dependencies: deny a valid route through lawful political action, contest a title, threaten an exposed movement, force competing support demands or negotiate with the relevant party. None succeeds automatically. A raid does not destroy all regional wealth and an enemy does not learn hidden member totals merely by inspecting the relationship.

Player and AI use the same admitted information and finite assignments. Exact weights, tactical results, campaign balance and runtime behavior remain untested. The strongest visible bottleneck should govern even when the broader relationship appears favorable.

## 19. Worked qualitative situations

These are authored examples, not historical incidents or executed simulations.

**The generous estate:** a ruler offers a valuable rent but asks for immediate cavalry. The property agreement may proceed while the military request is refused because no eligible contingent is available. The player can retain the institutional benefit or change the bargain; no hidden recruitment pool appears.

**The escort beyond its terms:** a contingent agrees to protect a movement, then the host orders it into a separate offensive. The command and mission owners reassess consent and support. The contingent can negotiate or refuse without teleporting out of danger or erasing its obligations.

**The contribution that cannot travel:** a house has transferable surplus but the agreed route is interrupted. Retain it, arrange another valid route or renegotiate timing. The source does not lose the contribution and the destination receive it simultaneously.

**The disputed successor:** a competent decision assigns an asset to a receiving institution, but local possession and liabilities remain unsettled. The campaign preserves the dispute and actual custody. Neither the asset nor its staff is duplicated to make both parties' panels look complete.

## 21. Relationship lifecycle and state separation

The Germania expression should expose several linked but noninterchangeable conditions:

| Condition | Question | Typical blocker |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Does the shared institution exist and recognize this relationship channel? | Institution not emerged, recognition withheld or contact absent |
| Legal basis | Is an offer, grant, privilege or agreement valid for its stated scope? | Incompetent grantor, disputed title or missing consent |
| Local presence | Are actual authorized personnel and usable premises present? | No assigned staff, access denied or property unavailable |
| Operational capacity | Can the requested function be performed now? | People, animals, stores, funds, transport or accommodation committed elsewhere |
| Political tolerance | Will relevant rulers, lords, ecclesiastical authorities and communities permit the activity? | Jurisdiction dispute, restriction or local resistance |
| Wider connection | Can the local relationship communicate, remit, receive or return through a valid network? | Route interruption, receiving failure or uncertain information |
| Settlement integrity | Are payments, claims, returns, custody and liabilities current enough to continue? | Arrears, unreturned property, unresolved loss or breach |

These conditions remain separate. A legally valid house can be operationally weak. A wealthy estate can be politically contested. A recognized relationship can have no available contingent. Any compact summary must identify the decisive blocker and the owner responsible for resolving it rather than collapsing all conditions into "Templar influence."

Possible information states are **confirmed**, **reported**, **contested**, **unknown** and **inapplicable**, as supplied by the information owner. They are not percentages and do not expose hidden personnel or enemy plans.

## 22. Agreement formation, terms and amendment

A relationship agreement needs a bounded bargain. The following is a conceptual handoff contract, not a promoted schema:

```yaml
templar_relationship_agreement:
  parties: Identified institution and competent counterparties
  purpose: Property protection transit support remittance escort garrison or other admitted function
  granted_rights: Exact rights actually conveyed
  retained_rights: Rights not conveyed and continuing local claims
  promised_support: Money goods access protection transport lodging or other real contribution
  requested_service: Exact bounded obligation if any
  command_and_coordination: Institutional operational and tactical authority boundaries
  duration_or_review: Ending condition or review point without inventing a universal term
  closure_condition: Return settlement transfer expiry suppression or other valid end
  known_disputes: Contested facts claims and dependencies
  authoritative_resolution_required: true
```

Agreement formation follows **Offer, Verify authority, Negotiate, Accept, Reserve, Perform, Settle**. Until acceptance, an offer is not an obligation. Acceptance of one clause does not imply acceptance of every requested service. Conditional acceptance becomes effective only when its stated conditions are met.

Amendments require the same competence and consent appropriate to the changed term. A ruler cannot transform lodging into military command by unilateral reinterpretation. A mission cannot silently widen because the original objective became inconvenient. Emergency action may be resolved by existing battle or political owners, but it still produces a later accounting and dispute state.

## 23. Institutional capacity and opportunity cost

The authority should reason through separate capacity categories rather than a generic Order-power pool:

- professed and other admitted members by actual responsibility;
- officers, chaplains, administrators and specialist personnel;
- eligible armed personnel and formed contingents;
- horses, pack animals, equipment and accountable stores;
- property income, liquid funds, deposits and restricted assets;
- lodging, stabling, storage, care, repair and assembly capacity;
- correspondence, route, transport and receiving arrangements;
- political, ecclesiastical and local permission.

A contribution is available only when its owner confirms it and it is not committed incompatibly elsewhere. Wealth cannot replace absent members; members cannot replace horses; property cannot replace permission; permission cannot replace transport. The system may aggregate routine support, but aggregation must preserve the real decisive limitation.

Opportunity cost should be visible and causal. Sending personnel can reduce local administration, protection or service capacity. Remitting surplus can reduce the local reserve available for maintenance or emergencies. Hosting a contingent can consume accommodation and fodder. None of these outcomes is automatic merely because a relationship exists; they follow actual commitments returned by the responsible owners.

## 24. House functions and readiness contract

A house, estate, chapel, store, lodging place and commandery are not interchangeable labels. Each exact site may support only the functions established for it. Proposed function categories are contact, worship, administration, lodging, storage, property management, remittance, referral, care, equipment maintenance, assembly and bounded security.

For each claimed function, inspect:

```yaml
house_function_readiness:
  function: Exact supported purpose
  authority: Permission and institutional assignment
  personnel: Present eligible staff
  material_support: Premises equipment animals stores and money where required
  access: Legal and physical use
  network_connection: Sending receiving and correspondence relationship where required
  current_commitments: Existing reservations and obligations
  decisive_blocker: Strongest unresolved cause
```

A function can be available, limited, suspended, contested, unknown or inapplicable. One available function does not activate the rest. A lodging site does not become a recruitment center; an estate does not become a fortress; a chapel does not become a treasury; a commandery name does not create a full battlefield contingent.

## 25. Information provenance, freshness and fair warnings

The player and AI act on admitted knowledge rather than omniscience. Relevant statements should identify whether they come from an agreement, local report, institutional correspondence, legal claim, observed movement or another recognized source owner. The authority does not create intelligence mechanics, but it requires the relationship UI to preserve source and uncertainty.

Warnings should identify an actionable cause: **grant authority contested**, **assigned staff not confirmed**, **funds restricted**, **route permission absent**, **receiving house unavailable**, **contingent already reserved**, or **return arrangement incomplete**. Avoid unexplained relationship penalties and false numerical precision.

Changed information triggers reassessment rather than retroactive fabrication. If a route report proves wrong, resolve the actual interruption and resulting choices. Do not pretend the movement never departed or reveal all hostile forces because one messenger failed. Player and AI receive the same class of information through the same admitted owners.

## 26. Default, breach, remedy and proportional consequence

Failure and misconduct must be classified before consequences are applied:

- **nonperformance without breach:** a valid dependency failed despite reasonable compliance;
- **delay:** performance remains possible but the agreed timing or sequence changed;
- **partial performance:** some accepted obligations were completed and others remain;
- **material breach:** a party violated a term central to the bargain;
- **authority failure:** the supposed grantor or commander lacked power to bind the relevant asset or person;
- **external prevention:** war, access denial, seizure or another condition prevented performance;
- **contested allegation:** the facts or interpretation remain unresolved.

Possible remedies include cure, replacement performance from actual sources, amended support, restitution, compensation where a responsible owner can assess it, return of property, release from a term, suspension, arbitration, restriction, withdrawal or termination. Remedy must fit the breached obligation and preserve third-party claims. It cannot spawn replacement members, refund consumed supplies or erase casualties.

Repeated reliable performance may strengthen future willingness through the responsible relationship system. It must not unlock unconditional obedience or permanent access. Likewise, one local dispute does not automatically destroy the entire transregional institution.

## 27. Termination, suppression and final settlement

A relationship may end through expiry, completion, mutual release, lawful withdrawal, breach, loss of capacity, transfer, suppression or disappearance of a necessary party or asset. Ending the relationship stops future unperformed obligations only as resolved; it does not erase accrued debts, deposits, custody duties, casualties, claims or unresolved property.

Final settlement should account for:

1. people and their current status, destination and continuing care;
2. animals, equipment, stores and records still in custody;
3. local property title, possession, maintenance and encumbrances;
4. money owed, held, restricted, missing or disputed;
5. accepted missions still in progress or requiring safe closure;
6. third-party and community rights;
7. archives, correspondence and evidence needed for later claims;
8. the receiving institution where an actual transfer is authorized.

Suppression remains a multi-owner lifecycle, not one button. Assignment of property to a successor is not completed transfer. Seizure is not necessarily legal title. Closure of a house does not determine every member's fate. A successor institution receives only what is actually conveyed, accepted and delivered, together with any obligations that validly accompany the transfer.

## 28. Refined AI and player decision sequence

The relationship sequence is **Propose, Verify, Negotiate, Reserve, Perform, Reassess, Settle, Close**.

The AI should prefer agreements it can actually support and close. It may reject valuable property when accepting it would create obligations beyond its staff or mission. It may accept nonmilitary support while refusing service. Realm AI should not donate an asset it lacks authority to convey or request the same contingent already reserved elsewhere.

The player-facing view should distinguish:

- **what you are offering;**
- **what the Order accepts;**
- **what remains retained or disputed;**
- **what real contribution is reserved;**
- **what event changes or closes the bargain;**
- **what unresolved burden survives closure.**

This provides meaningful strategy without turning Templar relationships into manual accounting. Optional detail can reveal the causal record while the default view presents the bargain, strongest blocker, immediate opportunity cost and credible next action.
