- Title, property, jurisdiction, office, household, army, access, and practical government must not be inferred from one another.

- Specialist disagreement is a review question, not permission for this guide to choose a favorable result or declare supersession.
- Historical progression remains +21 months per strategic turn; biological progression remains +7 months; campaign season advances one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step. This guide advances none of them.
