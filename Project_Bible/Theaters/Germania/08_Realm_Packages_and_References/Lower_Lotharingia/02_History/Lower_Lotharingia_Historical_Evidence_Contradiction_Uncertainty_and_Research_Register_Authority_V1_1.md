## 1. Why a separate register is justified now

The reconciliation foundation already contains 20 proposition keys and 12 uncertainty keys. This companion preserves those keys and adds individual evidence dossiers, a new scholarly comparison, source-dependency mapping, and owner-specific research dispositions. That distinct working volume justifies a register rather than another summary wrapper. [I1]

The strongest new research result is not a convenient resolution. Herbert Zielinski's 1990 Matilda biography gives 1069 in its genealogy and tentatively places the wedding shortly before the elder Godfrey's death. Reindel's younger-Godfrey biography tentatively gives 1070; Lindner's older ADB account tentatively gives 1071. All are later than the opening. This deepens the chronology conflict while leaving the opening exclusion unchanged. [E2-NDB, E3-NDB, E3-ADB, E6]

The useful campaign consequence is precise: the existing younger Godfrey remains a person, not an opening duke or a completed Tuscan marriage package. Later marriage, succession, property, access, and political alignment remain separate propositions and owners. No army, unit card, or roster work follows from this register.
