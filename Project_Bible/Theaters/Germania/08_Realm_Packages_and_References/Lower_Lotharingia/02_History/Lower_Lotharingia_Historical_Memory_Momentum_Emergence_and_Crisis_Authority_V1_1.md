This is the substantive historical consumer layer after diplomacy in the recorded Realm sequence. It develops what the campaign remembers, how current pressures change opportunities, when existing people can gain prominence, and how crises can develop or fail to occur. It is not an opening evidence inventory, a historical fact certificate, an event script, or a character generator.

## 1. Campaign thesis: a remembered dignity must still be made useful

**The past can keep the ducal project alive; only present capacity can make it work.**

The general Realm authority frames Lower Lotharingia as a prestigious but fragmented ducal project: recognized dignity, bounded direct capacity, independent institutions, a restoration alternative, and western/Italia exposure. It rejects a guaranteed restoration and a title-generated army or treasury. [S1]

This file makes those distinctions playable across time. Frederick can build a record of protection and dependable government rather than rely on incumbency alone. A restoration principal can preserve an alternative political narrative without gaining automatic support. A successor can inherit surviving records and expectations without inheriting every relationship, promise, or office.

The player should experience consequences through settlements, routes, armies, diplomacy, institutions, and visible commitments. Memory adds reasons and persistence; momentum adds context and alternatives; emergence adds rare, grounded career opportunities. None replaces the Medieval II campaign spine.

## 4. Evidence to reception, not narrative to truth

A campaign act can produce a fact record. Surviving evidence may support several interpretations. Particular audiences can receive, accept, reject, preserve, or ignore those interpretations. Strategic effects require the relevant owner state rather than a narrative directly granting a bonus. [S4]

A proposed memory record identifies subject act, supporting evidence, provenance, custodian, audience, known access, disputed propositions, reception, preservation, and review. Authenticity and partisanship remain distinct: an authentic account can be selective, and a politically popular story can be inaccurate.

A commissioned chronicle does not create a valid claim. A captured archive does not transfer property. Missing records do not prove a right absent. Later historians' conclusions are not automatically known to opening actors. An audience is not one mind, and an outgoing account cannot manufacture foreign acceptance.

## 5. Memory portfolio: incumbency, protection, and restraint

Proposed campaign-grown subjects include dependable protection, performed service, honored settlement terms, corrected overreach, maintained routes, and supported household continuity. These become significant only when actual acts and evidence justify persistent memory.

Frederick's incumbency can provide a political frame, but this file assigns no opening reception or loyalty. Repeated performance may make continuity attractive to some actors while leaving other claims contested. A short-term military success can be remembered differently by protected travelers, requisitioned communities, and institutional owners.

The strategic choice is whether to invest in durable performance and accessible records, accept a narrower commitment, or pursue a gain whose costs will persist. There is no repeatable prestige purchase for issuing the same proclamation.

## 6. Memory portfolio: displacement and restoration

S1 preserves Godfrey the Bearded as a restoration principal without creating a fixed opening war or guaranteed later restoration. This design allows narratives of displacement, service, reconciliation, rightful return, or damaging overreach to compete where evidence and audiences support them.

Remembered standing can keep an alternative politically relevant. It cannot replace a living eligible actor, a material base, recognition, access, or negotiated support. A claimant can choose patience, service, compromise, withdrawal, or force. Dormancy is not failure, and a date does not reactivate a dormant project.

A successful restoration need not erase Frederick's government record or household interests. A failed bid need not extinguish every claim. The titles and law owners retain the actual effects.

## 7. Institutional, local, military, and household memory

Stavelot-Malmedy and St Truiden retain separate evidence, reception, protection experiences, and disputes. One abbey's narrative cannot bind the other; St Truiden's Liège interface remains unresolved. Institutional memory may preserve a privilege claim without proving its scope.

Local communities can remember burdens and protection differently from cooperating elites. Military participants can preserve a campaign account without creating a universal army morale effect. Household records can preserve service and continuity without creating a public mandate or regency.

Ida's agency remains within existing character/household authority. Unresolved dependent identities, ages, and placements cannot be filled to make a succession story convenient. No new kinship, marriage, title, office, or opening residence is admitted. External Boulogne, Flanders, Hainaut, royal, and Tuscan state stays external. [S1, S8]

## 9. Momentum portfolio: title and capacity

S1's central tension is dignity without assured working capacity. Proposed observations compare known responsibilities with actual administration, resources, access, and accepted cooperation. A nominally broad title can coexist with a dependable small core; expanding claims can increase exposure without improving reach.

Options include consolidating the core, negotiating fewer reliable duties, strengthening supported communication, seeking mediation, or accepting a reduced role. Momentum describes why those options matter; it does not choose one or create the capacity needed to perform it. [S5]

Unknown holdings or revenue cannot be interpreted as zero, abundant, or secretly available. Exact strength, thresholds, and opening observations remain unbound.

## 10. Momentum portfolio: recognition, western options, and divided attention

Royal service can improve access while exposing home obligations. Western cooperation can offer alternatives without forcing an anti-royal alignment. Cross-Theater household interests can create opportunities while consuming attention, travel, and resources. These are inherited interface directions, not active opening alliances. [S1, S7]

A new threat can make a former rival a useful negotiating partner. A successful agreement can reduce pressure in one lane while increasing obligation elsewhere. The AI must read actor-visible reports, not the hidden intent of royal, western, institutional, or Tuscan counterparts.

There is no universal restoration meter, east-west alignment score, or pressure value that automatically triggers war.

## 11. Momentum portfolio: routes, institutions, and material strain

Proposed observations concern actual route disruption, overlapping protection commitments, damaged property, service shortfall, contested dues, and unperformed compensation. They require owner-backed events or reports. This file creates no exact road, toll, stock, or army.

A blocked corridor can change the feasibility of a campaign without proving an enemy plot. A protection failure can create a specific institutional dispute without universal hostility. A peace can reduce military pressure while leaving economic recovery incomplete.

Military, economy, geography, and supply authorities retain the missing material definitions. This suite does not certify those earlier sequence lanes complete or authorize roster creation before their prerequisites.

## 13. Existing people before new admission

Frederick, the two Godfreys, existing household actors, and externally owned people retain one canonical identity. A known person becoming prominent is a career/role change, not a second emergence spawn. A child growing older remains the same person and is not generated again at a famous date. [S6, S8]

Before proposing any new candidate, check existing active, latent, external, and aggregate representations through the relevant character owner. A role need can be met by an existing person, supported delegation, institutional staff, an aggregate function, delay, or no action.

A dead or ineligible historical person is not resurrected. An unavailable person is not teleported. A dynamic equivalent cannot be the same historical identity under another spelling or a free replacement with copied traits, kinship, claims, and fame.

## 16. Restoration and succession as conditional campaign arcs

A proposed restoration opportunity needs a surviving political basis, relevant people, scoped recognition or negotiation, material capacity, communication, and a viable transition path. It may lead to service, compromise, coexistence, conflict, or withdrawal. It does not require every lane perfect for every option.

The younger Godfrey's future is conditional. A father's death or settlement does not clone a coalition, install an heir, or transfer all property. Frederick's title loss need not extinguish a separately supported household/core. A new holder can have recognition without working government. [S1, S8]

A historical date window can constrain a later researched opportunity but never execute it. No exact restoration date, death, marriage, or succession event is bound in this file. No future Bouillon fame or later crusading narrative is preloaded into opening knowledge or traits.

## 19. Twelve developed player decisions

### The Duke's Record of Service

**Trigger:** actual service is completed but its political interpretation is contested. **Choices:** preserve evidence, seek a scoped acknowledgment, let conduct stand, or prioritize another need. **Cost:** attention and access versus uncertain reception. No automatic title or universal prestige reward.

### A Return Remembered, Not Promised

**Trigger:** a restoration-facing audience receives a supported narrative. **Choices:** seek negotiation, build present support, wait, or narrow the ambition. **Cost:** maintaining an alternative versus material exposure. Memory cannot install a duke.

### Two Abbeys Remember Differently

**Trigger:** separate protection outcomes produce distinct institutional accounts. **Choices:** remedy a failure, clarify terms, preserve both records, or negotiate future scope. **Cost:** scarce capacity versus credible restraint. One institution's reception does not set the other's.

### The Archive Before the March

**Trigger:** movement or danger threatens actual records and custody. **Choices:** secure a supported copy, move a custodian, narrow what travels, or accept risk. **Cost:** transport/security versus immediate operations. No free archive or truth transfer.

### A Correction Before the Court

**Trigger:** a received report is materially contradicted. **Choices:** correct the known audience, investigate, seek a witness, or act with explicit uncertainty. **Cost:** delay and credibility versus compounding error. Correction does not erase the prior report.

### A Capable Person Already Serves

**Trigger:** a role need matches an existing eligible actor. **Choices:** bounded reassignment, supported delegation, retain the current duty, or use aggregate staff. **Cost:** one function's gain versus another's exposure. No duplicate named character.

### The Mediator Cannot Travel

**Trigger:** a plausible candidate lacks access or availability. **Choices:** another supported channel, remote message, delay, or no negotiation. **Cost:** lost opportunity versus transport/security. Eligibility does not teleport a person.

### The Son and the Old Promise

**Trigger:** an actual transition exposes inherited expectations. **Choices:** confirm valid terms, renegotiate, contest, or seek another role. **Cost:** continuity versus liabilities. The father’s coalition and private knowledge do not copy to the son.

### A Victory That Burdens the Core

**Trigger:** actual success leaves losses or obligations. **Choices:** consolidate, recover, negotiate, or continue at known risk. **Cost:** momentum now versus sustainability. Victory does not refill finite pools or create supplies.

### The Rival Offers Peace

**Trigger:** a genuine channel presents a bounded settlement. **Choices:** coexistence, narrowed terms, further negotiation, or conflict through diplomacy. **Cost:** immediate security versus conceded leverage. Rivalry need not become war.

### The Chronicler Needs Support

**Trigger:** a grounded preservation opportunity has actual institutional/material backing. **Choices:** limited patronage, existing records, another priority, or refusal. **Cost:** resources versus uncertain preservation and reception. No automatic famous author or accepted narrative.

### A Future Without the Famous Outcome

**Trigger:** campaign divergence makes a researched historical pathway ineligible. **Choices:** preserve current success, pursue a different supported role, or accept nonoccurrence. **Cost:** narrower historical resemblance versus genuine agency. No replacement event is forced.

These are proposed reusable decision families, not historical incident claims or active opening events.

## 20. Long-horizon divergence and recovery

A successful core-first campaign can reduce restoration opportunity without erasing the rival's biography. A negotiated order can preserve distinct households and claims. A failed campaign can leave service, refuge, mediation, or reduced lordship viable. None requires a predetermined territorial endpoint.

Memory can persist after pressure fades. Pressure can return because new conditions arise, not because a retired event needs replaying. A new actor can take up an old issue without inheriting every grievance or fact. Institutional continuity can outlast an officeholder while personal relationships change.

Recovery requires actual repair, resources, security, communication, and owner-executed remedies. Time, commemoration, or a peace announcement cannot restore population, treasury, forces, or trust. Reduced survival and nonoccurrence are valid campaign outcomes.

## 22. Persistence, independent time tracks, and implementation boundary

Historical +21 months, biological +7 months, and one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step remain independent. Chronology may affect researched eligibility; biological state affects actual people; season affects routes, supply, and availability through owners. No track alone creates a death, war, restoration, marriage, or appointment.
