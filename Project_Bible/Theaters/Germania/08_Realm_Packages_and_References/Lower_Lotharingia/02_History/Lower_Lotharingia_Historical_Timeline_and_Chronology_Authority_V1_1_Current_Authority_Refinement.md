## 1. Chronological thesis and player-facing meaning

**The opening is a point within several unfinished histories, not the first step of an inevitable restoration script.**

Frederick’s supported incumbency orientation, Godfrey the Bearded’s earlier Upper tenure, the Italian marriage network, uncertain direct-core property, and institutional relationships form overlapping histories. The later Lower ducal grant, younger Godfrey’s succession, and competing wedding chronologies belong after January 1, 1062. [I1, I2; E1–E4, E6]

The campaign can therefore support effective incumbency, negotiated return, coexistence, a different succession, or reduced but viable continuity. Those are proposed design paths, not claims about private opening intent. The chronology explains historical pressure without creating people, rights, money, armies, or an obligation to reproduce reference outcomes.
