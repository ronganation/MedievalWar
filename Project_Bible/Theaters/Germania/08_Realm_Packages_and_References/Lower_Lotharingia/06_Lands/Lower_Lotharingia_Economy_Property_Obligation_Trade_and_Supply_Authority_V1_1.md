## 1. Strong verdict and economic identity

**Make the dignity affordable. Make each promise deliverable. Keep enough capacity to act again.**

The general authority defines fiscal capability through direct property, market and route relationships, negotiated obligations, lawful contributions, and functioning administration. It explicitly rejects a treasury generated by ducal title or a tax base generated by the historic region. [S1]

The proposed Lower Lotharingian economy is a **bounded-core and performed-obligation economy**. Its distinctive challenge is converting fragmented rights, privately held resources, institutional cooperation, and corridor access into usable support without pretending the whole regional economy belongs to the duke. The central choice is not simply low tax versus high tax. It is which function to make reliable, who can support it, what must be given up, and whether the resulting commitment leaves a recoverable base.

Successful play can mean supplying one defensible seat, completing a limited protection duty, paying a smaller reliable force, or funding a corridor repair that makes future obligations cheaper to perform. Expansion can increase opportunities while worsening collection, transport, defense, and recovery costs. Political prestige and material solvency may diverge.

## 3. Readable Medieval II economy, deeper causes

Keep the familiar campaign spine: settlements, property, revenue, construction, trade, recruitment cost, upkeep, and supply. Add depth where it creates consequential choices: separated owners, finite reserves, competing labor and transport, delivery, maintenance, arrears, and recovery. The player should decide what to support, not approve every sack, nail, meal, or routine transaction.

A compact budget can aggregate valid accounts for planning, but it must distinguish spendable resources from restricted funds, expected receipts, disputed dues, and private resources not committed to public use. A summary is not a new universal treasury. Routine transactions can execute through supported standing arrangements; major shortages, private-public transfers, breaches, and irreversible losses require visible decisions.

## 4. Economic separations

```text
Ducal dignity != property or treasury
Property right != possession or unrestricted use
Revenue entitlement != assessment != collection != accepted receipt
Accepted receipt != unreserved balance != authorized expenditure
Production potential != work completed != suitable output != available stock
Money != goods, skilled labor, animals, equipment, or time
Market access != stock availability != purchase != delivery != acceptance
Service obligation != available people or transport != performed service
Protection or advocacy != ownership of institutional resources
Supply demand != reservation != issue != arrival != consumption
Title transfer != private property transfer
Repair != restoration of every lost function
```

These distinctions must explain practical outcomes. A duke can be owed money and unable to pay a garrison. A household can possess grain that is not public supply. A purchase can be valid while the cargo remains undelivered. A protection duty can consume more than its associated receipts provide.

Property is not a flag that supplies land, people, cash, grain, horses, jurisdiction, and inheritance simultaneously. A lease or use grant can permit a function without transferring ownership. ## 7. Baelen–Limbourg material-core portfolio

The proposed economic core has four competing uses: household continuity, seat maintenance and defense, administrative continuity, and wider ducal commitments. Each requires supported inputs. Investing in storage may improve resilience but consume labor and construction resources; concentrating troops may protect the seat while increasing food and fodder demand; moving records may preserve continuity while tying up transport.

The player should see **what the core can spare after existing commitments**, not an assumed surplus attached to the place name. A title-loss settlement can preserve some private property while reducing public receipts. A new duke cannot automatically spend the former holder's reserves. Later Limburg development supplies no opening buildings, productive rights, or wealth.

## 9. Revenue, assessment, collection, and remittance

Proposed revenue categories include supported property returns, rents, dues, tolls, market or court receipts where evidenced, negotiated contributions, grants, gifts, and service substitutions. Naming a category does not establish a specific entitlement or opening value.

```yaml
ProposedRevenueLifecycle:

  - NotifyOrRequestThroughValidProcess

  - CollectPartiallyOrFully
  - TransportAndRemitWhereRequired
  - AcceptIntoCorrectAccount
  - ReconcileCostsLossArrearsAndRemainingClaims
  - CloseWaiveRenegotiateOrCarryForward
```

Gross entitlement, assessed amount, collected amount, collection expense, remittance loss, accepted receipt, and available balance are distinct. Uncollected revenue is not spendable. A refusal, inability to pay, communication failure, disputed assessment, and intercepted remittance are different causes and should produce different responses.

No flat historic-duchy tax base is created. A stronger collection system can improve performance only within supported rights and resources; it cannot manufacture an entitlement. Forceful extraction remains possible through relevant action systems, but its proceeds and consequences must be recorded as such rather than relabeled lawful routine revenue.

## 10. Obligations, protection costs, and service substitution

Lower Lotharingia's proposed economic signature includes promises that cost resources before they yield useful standing. Protection, escort, hospitality, frontier service, communication, and mediation can require food, transport, staff, maintenance, or money. Political recognition is not payment.

An obligation should identify the obligated party, beneficiary, basis, scope, timing or trigger, permitted performance, limitations, and closure. An accepted duty is not proof of present capacity. Substitution between money, goods, transport, and service requires valid terms and actual acceptance; no universal conversion rate is invented.

Stavelot-Malmedy and St Truiden remain distinct institutional interfaces, not interchangeable income nodes. Antwerp responsibility does not create port revenue or a frontier treasury. Their specialist bodies were not fully reopened in this pass; no exact terms are assigned. Useful partial cooperation, such as hospitality without troops or passage without provisioning, remains valid.

## 11. Spending, reserves, and commitment collisions

A proposed expense identifies purpose, competent decision, source account, reservation, payee or consumer, delivery or performance condition, and closure. The same balance cannot fund a seat repair and a campaign simultaneously unless the actual unreserved amount supports both.

Expected future receipts may inform planning but cannot silently become cash. An emergency can justify revising priorities without erasing accepted promises. Options include staging work, narrowing a mission, seeking release, borrowing on supported terms, selling a permitted asset, requesting another contribution, or accepting explicit shortfall.

Routine expenditure should be aggregated within valid standing scope. Major use of private reserves, productive replacement stock, or resources already promised elsewhere must expose the displaced work and recovery burden.

## 14. Agrarian allocation and productive continuity

The proposed allocation review distinguishes assessed output from disposable surplus. Competing uses can include household subsistence, seed and replacement, fodder, processing losses, storage, supported dues, institutional commitments, sale, relief, and military provisioning. One claim must not erase the others without an explicit decision and consequence.

Protected minima are reviewable continuity requirements, not magical immunity. If emergency extraction uses seed, food, breeding stock, or essential tools, record the immediate gain and the productive or human burden. Do not assume a universal fixed priority order or exact reserve threshold.

Population owners retain households, labor availability, skill, migration, and demographic recovery. Economy consumes those inputs and reports burden; it does not create workers or directly subtract army recruits from settlement population. Finite recruitment pools remain population-informed through their owners, not a direct population-as-army-manpower model.

## 15. Animals, equipment, and maintenance

Animals are living capacity, not interchangeable currency. Draft, pack, riding, breeding, and livestock roles can compete. A horse exists separately from a trained mount, rider, handler, feed, water, shelter, tack, route, and accepted military assignment. A cart exists separately from draft power and crew.

Animal condition and fitness findings come from relevant owners. This file can describe ownership, reservation, feed and care inputs, economic use, loss, and replacement needs; it does not diagnose disease or certify fitness. Breeding orientation does not guarantee replacement.

Tools, arms, armor, vehicles, boats, and containers require suitable users, condition, maintenance, repair inputs, and return handling. An item in stock is not an equipped formation. Demobilization does not automatically return every animal and item in serviceable condition. Shared assets cannot work simultaneously in unrestricted agriculture, transport, construction, and war.

## 16. Markets, trade, tolls, and external exchange

The proposed trade loop connects owners, goods, terms, payment, carriage, delivery, and acceptance. Market access does not guarantee goods, acceptable prices, credit, transport, or security. A river corridor does not produce automatic trade income or shipping capacity.

Prices and availability should be place-, time-, condition-, and observer-specific where material. Exact prices and currencies are uncalibrated here. An interface can show qualified offers and known alternatives without exposing every foreign stock or private intention.

Toll rights, collection, crossing operation, and physical maintenance remain separate. Protection may improve conditions for exchange without creating a new toll entitlement. A market icon cannot produce ducal revenue unless the relevant right and collection process exist. Western, royal, ecclesiastical, and Italia-facing partners retain their own accounts, assets, consent, and performance.

## 17. Gifts, loans, debt, arrears, and guarantees

A gift is not a loan; a loan is not a grant; a guarantee is not payment; an arrear is not necessarily refusal; debt is not an automatic confiscation right. Proposed records preserve parties, amount or band, purpose, terms, restrictions, repayment, security where applicable, default, dispute, and closure.

Borrowing can bridge timing but cannot create goods or productive capacity. Pledging future revenue can reduce later flexibility. A guarantor may fail to perform, and a creditor may accept revised terms, partial payment, or another valid settlement. Enforcement belongs to the relevant law and political systems.

No modern banking structure, interest rate, opening creditor, loan balance, or historical credit arrangement is fabricated. These are supported design capabilities whose exact use requires evidence or later campaign transactions.

## 18. Storage, transport, and delivery

A storage site needs actual capacity, appropriate conditions, access, custody, containers or handling where relevant, and security. A building does not create goods or staff. Spoilage, damage, capture, and theft are possible material outcomes only when the relevant event or validated consequence supports them, not automatic penalties attached to a season label.

Transport needs a mission-specific combination of route, access or military circumstances, assets, handlers or crews, feed or fuel where relevant, time, and destination capacity. S3's alternative-route logic applies: a second route may share the same bottleneck or require different terms and resources. [S3]

A purchase is not arrival. Arrival is not acceptance. Acceptance at a depot is not issue to a household or army. A partially delivered cargo must leave a clear remainder, not report complete success or create the missing goods.

## 19. Civil, institutional, and military supply

Households, government, institutions, relief, construction, transport, garrisons, and field forces can compete for the same material base. A military request is important but not automatically first. Compare urgency, mission value, ownership, protected continuity, existing commitments, substitution, and recovery.

The military owner defines the force, operational requirement, authorized use, readiness meaning, and consequences of shortage. Economy supplies supported source availability and procurement evidence. The verified shared Supply contract must define the exact transfer and operational consumption boundary described in section 5.

A force can be funded but not supplied, supplied but not ready, or equipped but unable to concentrate. Mercenary hiring needs actual availability and accepted contract terms as well as funds and sustainment; it is not a troop-spawn purchase. Germania regional and mercenary profiles remain Theater-owned, without duplicated Realm cards.

## 20. Extraction, war damage, and recovery

Raiding, requisition, seizure, and emergency extraction can produce immediate material effects through the relevant systems. They must not be blocked merely because a target refuses consent, nor presented as consequence-free normal collection. Record what was actually taken, who possessed it, loss and transport, residual claims, and local effects.

A victory does not settle accounts, restore a market, replenish animals, repair a crossing, or erase debt. Material recovery may need security, labor, tools, inputs, transport, time, revised obligations, compensation, and a smaller operating scope. Partial recovery can establish a changed baseline rather than restore the opening.

Shortage does not itself diagnose illness or determine mortality. Economy reports food, water, shelter, or care-input failures to the relevant health and population owners. Environmental owners supply actual weather or hazard effects; a historical date or campaign season does not create a flood, harvest, fire, or economic loss.

## 21. Seasonal pressure and independent clocks

## 24. AI and player-facing economy

The proposed AI seeks deliverable commitments rather than the largest nominal budget. It compares source ownership, actual availability, delivery feasibility, accepted duties, productive continuity, home exposure, and recovery. It may act under uncertainty where valid, but cannot read hidden foreign inventories or invent consent.

A blocked large plan should prompt a smaller supported option when one exists. Abstention needs a specific reason and review condition, not permanent paralysis. Refusal of one contribution is not global hostility or disloyalty. Difficulty must not rely on free money, stocks, transport, or troops.

The main economic view should show spendable versus restricted resources, expected versus received revenue, major reservations, the decisive production or delivery bottleneck, obligations at risk, and recovery needs. Inspectable detail can show provenance and terms. One underlying failure should produce one main decision with linked effects, not repeated prompts for every consuming system. Material losses and breached promises cannot disappear into routine aggregation.

## 28H. Economic performance residue and strategic identity

Completed actions should leave persistent economic residue. A fulfilled protection duty may close an obligation but leave depleted stores. A repaired route may reduce one bottleneck while creating maintenance needs. A successful campaign may return equipment damaged, animals depleted, and debt outstanding.

The characteristic Lower Lotharingian success is not maximum accumulation. It is a smaller material network that repeatedly performs useful functions and preserves enough capacity for the next demand. The characteristic failure is converting prestige into commitments faster than property, collection, transport, maintenance, and recovery can support them.
