## 6. Directed relationship and uncertainty contract

A later accepted edge must distinguish source endpoint, target endpoint, relationship type, direction, historical context, evidence disposition, and owner. Title recognition, office competence, property entitlement, passage permission, local cooperation, and military command are different edges even when endpoints overlap.

A Frederick-to-office orientation does not establish office-to-Frederick active assignment. A Flanders interface does not imply hostility, alliance, passage, trade rights, or knowledge. An imperial context does not establish a particular investiture instrument or every local action's command chain.

Unresolved means evidence or owner disposition is insufficient. Deferred means deliberately outside this slice. Not applicable requires a scope reason. Known absent requires affirmative supporting evidence. Conflicting preserves disagreement. Null, false, zero, and empty collections must not erase these distinctions.

## 7. Transition and capacity safeguards

The source's later-holder context remains historical orientation, not a scheduled sequence. This packet creates no death, removal, restoration, investiture, or installation event. Any later transfer must separately review person eligibility, recognition, office powers, property, settlement compliance, military command, route access, revenue, record custody, and surviving agreements.

A title may remain recognized while routes, agents, supplies or local cooperation fail. That design possibility must remain representable without declaring such a failure active at opening. Likewise a functioning march requires causal capacity rather than a single uncaused control meter.

The source's operational portfolios remain useful context: observation, protected communication, route security, bounded defense coordination, customs and market interfaces, diplomacy, refuge and ransom. None is a preloaded asset. Finite recruitment, depletion, recovery, demobilization and Theater ownership of regional or mercenary units remain outside this packet's data-creation scope.
