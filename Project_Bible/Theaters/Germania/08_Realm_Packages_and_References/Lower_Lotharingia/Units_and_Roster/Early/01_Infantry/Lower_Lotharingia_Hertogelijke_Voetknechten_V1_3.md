## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Hertogelijke Voetknechten are Lower Lotharingia's scarce Early ducal foot retainers: command-connected infantry maintained for pressure points, damaged frontage, personal and institutional security, and one controlled local counterpressure mission.**

Their battle loop is:

> **Attend, Reinforce, Press, Stabilize, Return**

Their campaign loop is:

> **Maintain, Assign, Concentrate, Serve, Account, Recover**

They do not replace the broad spear line. They make the assembled host function where ordinary files are strained, separated, or losing command connection.

## 7A. Tactical Threat-Response Matrix

| Threat or problem | Proper response | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Cracking ordinary spear line | Connect to threatened files, restore standards, rotate worked troops | Entering without lane or command creates congestion |
| Disordered enemy infantry | Short bounded press behind intact leadership | Pursuit consumes reserve and return capacity |
| Heavy assault infantry | Stabilize, delay, and seek relief or support | Prolonged isolated close combat |
| Light or medium cavalry | Form around compatible spears and protected shoulders | Mixed weapons, hasty facing, or flank impact |
| Heavy knightly shock | Use terrain, spear support, reserve, missiles, or cavalry | Treating retainer cohesion as anti-cavalry immunity |
| Missile fire | Use shields, movement, terrain, and external missile answer | Prolonged exposure or enfilade |
| Command crisis | Protect standards, officers, messengers, and withdrawal | Company becomes static bodyguard and abandons field mission |

## 8. Governing Battle Loop

### 8.1 Attend

The company maintains proximity to the ducal command system, designated reserve, threatened sector, or assigned pressure point. Attend does not mean standing beside a character at all times.

### 8.2 Reinforce

The company moves through a real lane and connects to the receiving formation. Reinforcement requires threat, casualty, equipment, and command information.

### 8.3 Press

Fresh files conduct one short controlled local pressure mission against a fixed, disordered, or already engaged target.

### 8.4 Stabilize

The company halts pursuit, reforms files, restores standards, and re-establishes the surrounding line or command relationship.

### 8.5 Return

The company returns to reserve, command protection, relief, or provider recovery rather than remaining permanently committed.
