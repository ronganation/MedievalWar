## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Kasteelknechten are castellany-service infantry maintained around fortified nodes, gates, roads, stores, and relief relationships. They defend one connected strongpoint system, escort its critical movements, or form the infantry connection through which a field host reaches and relieves it.**

Their battle loop is:

> **Guard, Connect, Receive, Relieve, Restore**

Their campaign loop is:

> **Maintain, Watch, Release, Serve, Return, Reopen**

## 7A. Tactical Threat-Response Matrix

| Threat or mission | Proper response | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Gate or road approach attack | Hold one connected approach with depth, signals, and relief | Several entries or rear access fail |
| Field army reaches garrison | Open and protect one connection lane | Congestion, hostile shoulders, or broken signals |
| Convoy or wounded movement | Escort one movement with route control and reserve | Unit is drawn into broad battle away from movement |
| Ordinary open-field line | Hold a bounded sector with friendly shoulders | Extended frontage and isolation consume depth |
| Heavy assault infantry | Delay and connect to relief | Prolonged close assault without reserve |
| Cavalry pressure | Use spears, obstacles, terrain, and connected support | Open flank, hasty line, or pursuit |
| Siege missiles or archery | Use cover, shields, stores, rotation, and counterfire support | Prolonged exposed position or enfilade |

## 8. Governing Battle Loop

### 8.1 Guard

Maintain one defined node, gate, store, road, crossing, or command relationship. Guard is a named responsibility, not a universal defensive bonus.

### 8.2 Connect

Establish or preserve the physical lane between the company, the defended node, and a receiving field or relief body.

### 8.3 Receive

Accept one attack, returning body, convoy, wounded movement, or relief force through prepared access and signals.

### 8.4 Relieve

Transfer responsibility, rotate worked files, connect the field force to the garrison, or allow another body to pass safely.

### 8.5 Restore

Reopen access, recount stores and personnel, return files to duty, and recover the node relationship after disruption.
