This profile defines the **Lotharingse Boeren** as Lower Lotharingia's universal lowest-tier Early infantry family. The company represents rural households and agricultural communities called for emergency defense, desperate field support, labor-heavy camp or settlement duty, and the cheapest possible mass of locally armed foot. The unit is deliberately weak, disruptive to the rural economy, and unreliable outside bounded local purposes.

### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lotharingse Boeren are the cheapest, weakest, broadest, and least prepared infantry available to Lower Lotharingia: useful for emergency numbers, local defense, camp and rear support, and desperate battlefield presence, but poor against every properly formed military family.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Gather, Arm, Follow, Endure, Disperse**

Their campaign loop is:

**Call, Assemble, Divert Labor, Serve Briefly, Account, Return**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Gather

Rural files assemble through household, village, estate, or local authority relationships. Slow or incomplete assembly reduces the company before battle begins.

#### 10.2 Arm

People receive whatever simple compatible weapons, tools, limited shields, markers, and carrying equipment physically exist. Equipment diversity limits formation quality.

#### 10.3 Follow

The company follows a simple leader, friendly formation, road, barrier, camp line, or settlement boundary. Independent complex maneuver is not a defining capability.

#### 10.4 Endure

The files attempt to hold, support, carry, guard, or survive one bounded emergency mission. Morale depends heavily on visible support, local purpose, and a believable return route.

#### 10.5 Disperse

When leadership, support, mission legitimacy, or survival confidence fails, the company withdraws, scatters, returns home, or becomes a collection of local survivors rather than a coherent unit.
