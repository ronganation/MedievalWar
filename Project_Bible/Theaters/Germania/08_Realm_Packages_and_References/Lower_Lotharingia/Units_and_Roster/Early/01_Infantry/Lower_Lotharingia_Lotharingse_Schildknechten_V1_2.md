This profile defines the **Lotharingse Schildknechten** as Lower Lotharingia's ordinary Early Era shielded close-combat infantry. They provide a broadly recruitable field-infantry middle layer for compressed fighting, broken or restrictive ground, short controlled advances, spear-line support after contact, and local position clearing. They are not elite swordsmen, ducal retainers, castellany specialists, civic watch, spear-line infantry, or High Era axe-assault troops.

**Lotharingse Schildknechten are Lower Lotharingia's Early Era regional shielded close-combat infantry: ordinary field troops equipped to connect with the spear line, fight in compressed or restrictive ground, conduct one short controlled push, and hold or transfer a recently contested local position.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Approach, Connect, Press, Fight, Relieve**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Approach

The company moves through a real lane using shields, terrain, friendly cover, and controlled spacing. The unit is better suited than a broad spear formation to restrictive ground, but it cannot ignore missiles, obstacles, or congestion.

#### 10.2 Connect

Files establish contact with friendly infantry, terrain, or a bounded target. Connection prevents the company from becoming an isolated attacking crowd and preserves relief and return.

#### 10.3 Press

The Schildknechten conduct one short controlled advance or local melee push. The purpose is to gain or restore a bounded position, not to deliver elite assault shock.

#### 10.4 Fight

The company sustains shielded close combat using rotation, mutual protection, practical sidearms, and file leadership. Effectiveness declines with fatigue, shield damage, crowding, and cadre loss.

#### 10.5 Relieve

The company transfers the position, receives relief, withdraws worked files, or reconnects to the main line. It is not expected to hold every gained position indefinitely.
