## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lotharingse Speerlieden are Lower Lotharingia's Early main-line spear-and-shield infantry and the first battlefield expression of the faction's “Ducal Anvil.”**

They are broad but finite infantry gathered from compatible lordship, castellany, district, landholding, and bounded community-service channels. Once properly assembled, they provide the stable center around which missile troops, ducal retainers, crossing forces, scouts, and mounted contingents can act.

Their governing battle loop is:

> **Muster, Form, Receive, Hold, Relieve**

Their campaign loop is:

> **Release, Concentrate, Serve, Return, Recover**

| Threat | Prepared response | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Loose ordinary foot | Short controlled advance behind intact standards | Pursuit breaks shoulders and depth |
| Light cavalry probe | Present spear frontage and deny easy contact | Turning or pursuit disorders the line |
| Medium cavalry charge | Depth, shields, firm ground, leaders, and support | Hasty deployment, mud, flank contact, or leader loss |
| Heavy knightly charge | Terrain, depth, reserve, missiles, or friendly cavalry | Unsupported frontal confidence is treated as immunity |
| Archers or crossbows | Shields, movement limits, terrain, and friendly missile support | Prolonged exposure or enfilade |
| Axe or assault infantry | Hold initial contact and seek relief | Close pressure consumes depth and shield integrity |
| Flanking infantry | Shorten, refuse, connect to reserve, or withdraw | Several directions become active |

## 7. Battle Loop

### 7.1 Muster

The files must arrive with recognized leaders, compatible arms, standards, and a common assignment. Mixed providers that arrive separately do not instantly form one coherent company.

### 7.2 Form

The company establishes:

- one connected front;
- working depth;
- protected or supported shoulders;
- recognized standards and signals;
- one local reserve or relief relationship;
- one withdrawal lane.

### 7.3 Receive

The formation receives ordinary infantry pressure or mounted contact from a prepared relationship. The company does not chase cavalry or gain automatic anti-cavalry immunity.

### 7.4 Hold

The company maintains frontage while missiles, mounted forces, assault troops, or a reserve perform separate missions. Holding consumes cohesion, equipment condition, leadership, and service tolerance.

### 7.5 Relieve

Worked files transfer the sector or withdraw through a physical lane before formation integrity collapses. Relief preserves recoverable capacity but does not restore casualties, fatigue, shields, leadership, or provider trust.
