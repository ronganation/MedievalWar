| Threat or mission | Proper response | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Gate or street approach | Hold one prepared approach with barriers, depth, and signals | Rear access or alternate streets fail |
| Market theft or riot | Separate movement, protect stores, identify leaders, preserve exit | Company becomes scattered or uses force without authority |
| Fire during attack | Split bounded security and fire response | Too many files leave frontage or access traps smoke and people |
| Small raid or looting | Muster local files and deny access | Pursuit leaves the town system |
| Convoy, official, or wounded escort | Protect one named movement and route | Unit is drawn into broad combat |
| Open-field infantry line | Hold one restricted sector with support | Extended frontage consumes depth and cohesion |
| Heavy assault | Delay through barriers and seek relief | Prolonged close fighting without specialized support |
| Mounted pressure | Use spears, barriers, narrow access, and friendly support | Open ground, flank contact, or pursuit |

## 8. Governing Battle Loop

### 8.1 Watch

Observe one bounded town system, gate, market, store cluster, street approach, or movement. Watch creates information, not perfect warning.

### 8.2 Muster

Gather released files, leaders, tools, signals, and equipment at a named assembly point. A bell or order does not instantly form a company.

### 8.3 Contain

Limit one threat, movement, fire, disorder, or local breakthrough without spreading the company across the entire settlement.

### 8.4 Secure

Restore recognized control, protect people and stores, separate hostile and friendly movement, and connect relief or authority.

### 8.5 Reopen

Clear obstruction, restore records and access, release people and goods, recount losses, and return files to watch, work, or recovery.
