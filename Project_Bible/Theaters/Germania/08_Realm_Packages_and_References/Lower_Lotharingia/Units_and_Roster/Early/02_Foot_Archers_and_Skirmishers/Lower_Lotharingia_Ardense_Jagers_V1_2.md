### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Ardense Jagers are Lower Lotharingia's Early Era Ardennes woodland archer-skirmishers: limited regional files that use local terrain knowledge, dispersed movement, selective shooting, and controlled withdrawal to screen armies and punish exposed troops in wooded or broken country.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Observe, Disperse, Harass, Displace, Rejoin**

Their campaign loop is:

**Identify, Release, Inspect, Guide, Serve, Return**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Observe

The Jagers identify usable ground, visible threats, routes, obstacles, and withdrawal points. Observation is localized and can be wrong, delayed, or defeated by weather, concealment, and enemy deception.

#### 10.2 Disperse

Files spread enough to use cover, broken ground, and several limited firing positions without dissolving company command or losing reassembly routes.

#### 10.3 Harass

The Jagers deliver selective shots against exposed, moving, light, disordered, or high-value targets. They do not attempt to reproduce the sustained volley density of Lotharingse Boogschutters.

#### 10.4 Displace

Files leave a compromised position before cavalry, infantry, or superior missiles fix them. Displacement interrupts shooting and depends on known terrain, warnings, and open routes.

#### 10.5 Rejoin

The company reforms at a named point, accounts for missing files, redistributes arrows, and either resumes screening, protects a withdrawal, or returns behind the main force.
