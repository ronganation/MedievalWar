### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lotharingse Boogschutters are Lower Lotharingia's Early Era regional foot archers: ordinary but useful bowmen who screen deployment, harass exposed troops, support the main infantry fight, and punish slow or unprotected targets when given time, frontage, and protection.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Deploy, Screen, Loose, Reposition, Preserve**

Their campaign loop is:

**Call, Inspect, Equip, Muster, Serve, Return**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Deploy

The company occupies a position with a useful field of fire, manageable terrain, protected or observable flanks, and a withdrawal path. Deployment quality matters more than nominal range.

#### 10.2 Screen

The bowmen cover friendly deployment, discourage exposed movement, and contest enemy skirmishers without spending the full ammunition supply on low-value targets.

#### 10.3 Loose

The company delivers controlled individual or volley fire according to range, target exposure, ammunition state, and command quality. The unit should not fire at maximum rate indefinitely.

#### 10.4 Reposition

When the target, line, terrain, or threat changes, the company moves to another firing position. Repositioning interrupts fire and may disorder mixed-provider files.

#### 10.5 Preserve

The bowmen withdraw behind infantry, shelter from cavalry, conserve remaining arrows, rally files, and avoid a melee they are not constituted to win.
