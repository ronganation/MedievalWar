### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lotharingse Werpspiesknechten are Lower Lotharingia's Early Era shielded javelin-skirmishers: regionally raised light infantry who close under limited protection, throw a small number of heavy missiles at a chosen target, and withdraw before the enemy can turn missile disruption into sustained melee.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Screen, Approach, Cast, Disrupt, Withdraw**

Their campaign loop is:

**Call, Inspect, Bundle, Muster, Serve, Account, Return**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Screen

The company deploys ahead of or beside the main line with a named withdrawal lane and receiving body. The screen obscures friendly intentions and contests enemy light troops without surrendering the escape route.

#### 10.2 Approach

Files move to practical throwing distance using shields, spacing, terrain, friendly pressure, or enemy distraction. Approach is the unit's most dangerous phase because short range exposes it to arrows, cavalry, and sudden melee.

#### 10.3 Cast

Files throw by controlled signal, file sequence, or short concentrated volley. The aim is immediate local disruption, not prolonged missile exchange.

#### 10.4 Disrupt

The target checks movement, raises shields, loses formation, suffers casualties, or becomes easier for the main line to attack. Disruption is conditional and does not guarantee a rout.

#### 10.5 Withdraw

The company leaves before heavier troops close, passes through or around the receiving line, reforms, accounts for ammunition and casualties, and either accepts a restricted second mission or becomes spent.
