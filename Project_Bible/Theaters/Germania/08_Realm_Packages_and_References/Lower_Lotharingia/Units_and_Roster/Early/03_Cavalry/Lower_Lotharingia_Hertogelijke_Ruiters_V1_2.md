This profile defines the **Hertogelijke Ruiters** as Lower Lotharingia's limited Early Era ducal mounted reserve. They are trusted mounted retainers and selected service riders kept closer to ducal command, assembled more consistently, and used for command escort, rapid local intervention, threatened-sector reinforcement, disciplined counterattack, and controlled exploitation. They are not merely Lotharingse Ruiters with higher statistics, and they are not elite knightly shock cavalry.

### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Hertogelijke Ruiters are Lower Lotharingia's limited ducal mounted reserve: trusted, maintained riders positioned to remain connected to command, protect critical persons and standards, reinforce a threatened sector, deliver a disciplined counterattack, or exploit one opening without becoming a universal elite cavalry formation.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Attend, Receive, Redeploy, Intervene, Restore**

Their campaign loop is:

**Retain, Inspect, Mount, Attend, Serve, Account, Recover**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Attend

The company remains connected to the ducal command, protected person, standard, reserve position, or critical rear node. Attendance preserves readiness but consumes the unit's availability elsewhere.

#### 10.2 Receive

Orders, reports, and local warnings identify one threatened sector or opening. Information must reach the company physically and may already be delayed.

#### 10.3 Redeploy

The Ruiters move through a real route while maintaining enough order, horse condition, and command connection to intervene effectively.

#### 10.4 Intervene

The company conducts one controlled charge, counterattack, escort action, or sector restoration. The mission is bounded by the original command purpose.

#### 10.5 Restore

The company breaks off, reforms, reconnects to command, handles casualties, and returns either to escort, reserve, or recovery. It does not remain as general-purpose melee cavalry indefinitely.
