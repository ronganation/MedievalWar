**Lotharingse Ruiters are Lower Lotharingia's Early Era regional medium cavalry: mounted retainers and qualified riders used to protect flanks, reinforce threatened sectors, counterattack exposed troops, deliver controlled charges, and pursue enemies that are already breaking.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Observe, Position, Accelerate, Strike, Recover**

Their campaign loop is:

**Call, Inspect, Horse, Equip, Muster, Serve, Remount, Return**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Observe

The company identifies threats, ground, friendly positions, enemy reserves, and possible charge or withdrawal lanes. Battlefield observation is immediate and local, not strategic omniscience.

#### 10.2 Position

The Ruiters occupy a flank, reserve, covered approach, or counterattack position from which the company can accelerate without crossing friendly formations or unsuitable ground.

#### 10.3 Accelerate

Files build speed and alignment over real distance. A charge formed too close lacks impact; one begun too far spends horse stamina and invites missiles or evasive action.

#### 10.4 Strike

The company contacts one chosen target with controlled shock, then fights only long enough to achieve the assigned local purpose. Charge effect depends on formation, speed, angle, target order, terrain, horse condition, and support.

#### 10.5 Recover

The Ruiters break off, reform, check riders and horses, restore control, and either return to reserve, protect a flank, or conduct a bounded pursuit. Recovery is not instantaneous cycle charging.
