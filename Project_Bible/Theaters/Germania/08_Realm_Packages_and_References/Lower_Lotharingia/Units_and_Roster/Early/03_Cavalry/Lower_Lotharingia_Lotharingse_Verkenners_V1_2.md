This profile defines the **Lotharingse Verkenners** as Lower Lotharingia's Early Era light mounted scouts, route observers, battlefield screeners, and messengers. They extend local observation, investigate roads and crossings, maintain contact between separated bodies, warn against flank movement, and carry time-sensitive reports. They are not light shock cavalry, horse archers, strategic agents, or invisible reconnaissance units.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Range

The Verkenners move beyond the main body along assigned routes, flanks, ridgelines, crossings, or settlements while preserving a return path and communication chain.

#### 10.2 Observe

Riders identify visible movement, terrain conditions, obstacles, standards, mounted presence, camp signs, and route changes. Observation is partial, local, and time-bound.

#### 10.3 Report

Information is recorded or remembered, assigned a confidence level, and carried through a real messenger or relay path. Reports age while moving.

#### 10.4 Screen

The unit warns friendly troops, denies easy enemy observation where possible, contests light scouts, and avoids becoming fixed by stronger forces.

#### 10.5 Return

Files withdraw, relay final information, account for missing riders and horses, and recover before another extended mission.
