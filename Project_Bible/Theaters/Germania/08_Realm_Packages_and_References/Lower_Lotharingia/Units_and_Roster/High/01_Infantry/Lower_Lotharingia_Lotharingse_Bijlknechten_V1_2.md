### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Lotharingse Bijlknechten are Lower Lotharingia's High-Era axe-bearing assault retainers: limited, maintained infantry assembled to break one fixed shielded or armored formation, force one bounded passage, or deliver one controlled counterstroke after the main line has established the fight.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Fix, Approach, Strike, Open, Reform**

Their campaign loop is:

**Contract, Equip, Concentrate, Serve, Account, Recover**

They are not the broad line, a universal heavy-infantry tier, or a replacement for combined arms. Their value appears only when another formation, terrain feature, or tactical situation fixes the target and allows the axe company to reach contact without being destroyed or disordered.

### 8. Governing Battle Loop

#### 8.1 Fix

The target is held, compressed, distracted, or denied easy withdrawal by the main line, terrain, missiles, another assault body, or a prepared defensive situation. Bijlknechten do not create this condition by themselves without additional cost.

#### 8.2 Approach

The company moves through a real lane with protected shoulders and as little missile exposure as possible. Approach order must survive terrain, friendly congestion, enemy missiles, and changes in the target.

#### 8.3 Strike

The front files use controlled overhead and diagonal blows, shield hooks, and concentrated local pressure. Supporting files replace worked fighters and maintain enough depth to prevent the assault from becoming a loose crowd.

#### 8.4 Open

The company creates one local gap, forces one withdrawal, clears one bounded passage, or collapses one fixed enemy section. Opening is a limited tactical result, not permission for unlimited breakthrough.

#### 8.5 Reform

The company halts pursuit, restores files and standards, recovers wounded and damaged equipment where possible, and either holds briefly, transfers the sector, or returns behind the line.
