This profile defines the **Lotharingse Hellebaardiers** as a later High Era mixed-polearm infantry family. The unit combines thrusting reach, hooking and pulling capacity, concentrated chopping pressure, and disciplined close formation to contest armored infantry, control mounted enemies after their momentum is checked, and fight around gates, breaches, streets, bridges, and other bounded ground. It does not replace Langspiesknechten, Bijlknechten, Voetserjanten, Ridders te Voet, or ordinary spear formations.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Approach

The company moves to a bounded target using terrain, screens, shields where compatible, and friendly pressure. Long exposed approaches are a weakness.

#### 10.2 Set

Files establish weapon spacing, direction, target priority, supporting shoulders, depth, and a reset lane.

#### 10.3 Control

Thrusts, hooks, blade pressure, shaft leverage, and close formation deny easy movement and expose the target to concentrated attacks. Control is conditional and not a magical immobilization effect.

#### 10.4 Press

The company applies bounded close pressure against a fixed, compressed, armored, or checked target. It does not own a broad breakthrough mission.

#### 10.5 Reset

Files separate, restore spacing, replace damaged weapons, account for casualties, receive relief, and prepare either a restricted second action or withdrawal.
