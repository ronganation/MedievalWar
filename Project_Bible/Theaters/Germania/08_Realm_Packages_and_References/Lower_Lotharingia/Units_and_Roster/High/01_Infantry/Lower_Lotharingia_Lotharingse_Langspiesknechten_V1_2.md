This profile defines the **Lotharingse Langspiesknechten** as Lower Lotharingia's High Era maintained long-spear and early polearm infantry. The unit provides prepared cavalry denial, controlled reach against armored or aggressive foot, narrow-passage control, and defensive support for crossbows and other vulnerable formations. It does not replace ordinary Lotharingse Speerlieden, become universal pike infantry, absorb later halberd families, or function as superior heavy infantry in every melee.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Prepare

The company selects ground, assigns frontage and depth, inspects long weapons, protects its shoulders, and identifies the threat to be received. Preparation is part of the combat capability.

#### 10.2 Align

Files establish spacing, weapon angle, depth, standards, and local command. The formation must remain physically connected rather than receiving a hidden brace bonus.

#### 10.3 Receive

The company accepts one mounted or aggressive infantry approach. Reach, ground, order, depth, target speed, support, and morale determine whether reception succeeds.

#### 10.4 Fix

A checked enemy becomes temporarily held, slowed, disordered, or denied easy advance. Another formation must exploit the check because Langspiesknechten are poor pursuit and assault troops.

#### 10.5 Relieve

The company transfers the sector, shortens or reforms, replaces damaged weapons and worked files, or withdraws behind another body. It cannot remain fully prepared through continuous attacks without recovery.
