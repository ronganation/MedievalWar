This profile defines the **Lotharingse Ridders te Voet** as a limited High Era dismounted knightly infantry family. The company represents knightly and upper-retainer combatants deliberately fighting on foot when terrain, siege conditions, defensive necessity, command needs, or the tactical situation make mounted employment unsuitable. The unit provides elite armored staying power, command-linked morale, difficult-ground heavy infantry, and one controlled counterassault without replacing mounted knights, Hertogelijke Voetknechten, Schildknechten, Speerlieden, Kasteelknechten, or Bijlknechten.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Dismount

The formation deliberately gives up mounted mobility and shock to accept a foot mission. Horses, tack, and mounted equipment must be secured rather than disappearing from the campaign state.

#### 10.2 Form

Knightly and retainer files assemble under banners with compatible weapons, armor, shields, spacing, and a bounded objective. Status does not substitute for formation control.

#### 10.3 Anchor

The company occupies one critical position, protects one node, or reinforces one heavy infantry sector. Its narrow frontage and scarcity prevent broad line replacement.

#### 10.4 Counter

When the enemy becomes committed, disordered, or overextended, the company delivers one controlled armored counterassault. The mission is to restore or secure a local situation, not pursue a general breakthrough.

#### 10.5 Recover

The company halts pressure, receives relief, accounts for casualties and equipment, reconnects to horse custody, and either remains on restricted foot duty or prepares to remount later.
