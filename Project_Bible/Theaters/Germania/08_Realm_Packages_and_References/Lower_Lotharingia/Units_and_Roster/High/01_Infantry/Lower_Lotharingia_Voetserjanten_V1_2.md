This profile defines the **Voetserjanten** as Lower Lotharingia's High Era professional foot-sergeant family. The unit provides maintained, contracted, or repeated-service infantry for sustained field campaigning, disciplined line support, guarded advances, reserve relief, fortified-camp duty, and dependable close combat. It stands between broadly raised Lotharingse Schildknechten and extremely scarce Hertogelijke Voetknechten or Ridders te Voet without becoming a generic superior infantry unit.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Form

The company establishes files, standards, weapons, shoulders, depth, support, objective, and relief relationships. Professional readiness comes from practiced organization rather than a passive stat bonus.

#### 10.2 Advance

Files move through a bounded lane under shields, terrain, friendly cover, or missile support. The advance is more disciplined than broad regional infantry but remains vulnerable to missiles, cavalry, obstacles, and congestion.

#### 10.3 Engage

The company enters controlled close combat against a suitable target. It seeks local position, line support, relief, or limited counterpressure rather than elite breakthrough.

#### 10.4 Relieve

Worked files rotate, another formation receives the position, wounded and damaged equipment move rearward, and the company avoids becoming a permanently exhausted line.

#### 10.5 Reorganize

Files, cadre, equipment, contracts, casualties, camp support, and mission readiness are reassessed before accepting another bounded task.
