This profile defines the **Lotharingse Kruisboogschutters** as Lower Lotharingia's first dedicated High Era crossbow infantry family. They provide deliberate armor-pressuring missile fire, compact shooting frontage, prepared defensive value, and stronger performance against protected enemies than ordinary bowmen. They do not replace Lotharingse Boogschutters, become pavise-protected late professionals automatically, or provide rapid skirmishing fire.

**Position, Load, Aim, Loose, Cover**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Position

The company occupies ground with a clear arc, useful range, protected shoulders, reload space, and a withdrawal route. Position is part of the weapon system.

#### 10.2 Load

Files span, string where necessary, place bolts, inspect locks, and prepare under cover or mutual protection. Reload cannot be treated as an invisible cooldown.

#### 10.3 Aim

The company selects targets deliberately. Armor, shield angle, distance, movement, elevation, and friendly obstruction determine whether a bolt is worth spending.

#### 10.4 Loose

Files fire by controlled volley, file sequence, or command-selected shots. The company produces strong local missile effect rather than continuous arrow volume.

#### 10.5 Cover

After firing, files reload behind terrain, friendly infantry, prepared cover, alternating ranks, or another protected arrangement. If cover fails, the unit must withdraw or accept severe exposure.
