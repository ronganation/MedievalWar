This profile defines the **Pavese Kruisboogschutters** as a later High Era protected-crossbow infantry family. The unit combines maintained crossbows, deliberate fire, large portable shields, shield carriers or support files, prepared ammunition, and specialist deployment practice to dominate one deliberate missile position. The family is not a universal upgrade to ordinary Lotharingse Kruisboogschutters and is markedly less mobile, more expensive, more support-intensive, and more vulnerable when displaced from prepared cover.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Carry

The company moves pavises, crossbows, bolts, tools, and support through a real route. Movement is slower and more burdensome than ordinary crossbow deployment.

#### 10.2 Emplace

Carriers and files establish cover angle, spacing, firing arc, rear reload space, ammunition access, flank protection, and a withdrawal route.

#### 10.3 Load

Shooters span and load behind physical cover. Files must manage spacing, weapon compatibility, support traffic, and ready-shot timing.

#### 10.4 Loose

The formation conducts deliberate fire against targets worth the bolt, using protected reload to sustain a favorable exchange rather than match bow volume.

#### 10.5 Recover

The company either retrieves pavises and supplies in order or abandons selected equipment to avoid capture. Recovery is a real operation, not an instant undeploy command.
