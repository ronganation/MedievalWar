This profile defines the **Stedelijke Boogschutters** as Lower Lotharingia's High Era maintained urban bow infantry. The unit draws on developed civic, town-linked, contracted, armory, craft, storage, and repeated-service systems to provide more consistent bows, arrows, fire control, defensive preparation, and campaign reliability than ordinary Lotharingse Boogschutters. The company is not an elite longbow formation, a crossbow unit, or Stedelijke Wacht carrying bows.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Prepare

The company inspects bows, strings, arrows, positions, signals, screens, rear access, and withdrawal lanes. Prepared reliability is the defining High Era improvement.

#### 10.2 Deploy

Files occupy one maintained firing frontage or prepared defensive sector with enough space for shooting, arrow distribution, casualty movement, and withdrawal.

#### 10.3 Sustain

The company uses deliberate, normal, or accelerated cadence according to target value, position, fatigue, and arrow economy. Sustained fire is controlled rather than infinite.

#### 10.4 Shift

Files move between named sectors or adjust frontage before the position becomes obstructed, bypassed, or threatened. Shifting interrupts fire and requires command and rear space.

#### 10.5 Preserve

The company conserves arrows, withdraws behind formed troops or barriers, protects cadre, and preserves maintained equipment rather than remaining for low-value melee.
