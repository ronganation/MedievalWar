### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Screen

The company protects a flank, route, missile body, command element, or moving formation while preserving enough cohesion and horse condition for intervention.

#### 10.2 Position

Files occupy useful ground and establish target, support, exit, and reform relationships. Positioning should be deliberate but faster and less politically constrained than knightly shock preparation.

#### 10.3 Engage

The company charges or enters short mounted combat against a suitable target. Engagement seeks control, displacement, reinforcement, or limited counterattack rather than decisive elite shock.

#### 10.4 Disengage

Files break off before static melee, prepared reach, or fresh heavy opposition consumes the unit. Professional discipline should improve disengagement consistency, not make it automatic.

#### 10.5 Recommit

After rally, horse check, equipment check, casualty accounting, and restored formation, the company may accept another moderate mission if the battlefield and campaign budget permit.
