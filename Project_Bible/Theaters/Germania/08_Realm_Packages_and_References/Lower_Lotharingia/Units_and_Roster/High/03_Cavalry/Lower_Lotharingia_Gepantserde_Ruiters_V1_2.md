This profile defines **Gepantserde Ruiters** as Lower Lotharingia's later High armored melee-cavalry family. The company is maintained to survive ordinary missile pressure better than lighter cavalry, contest enemy cavalry after first contact, hold one mounted sector briefly, escort exposed high-value bodies, and apply controlled pressure when a knightly decisive charge is unavailable or unnecessary. The unit is not a cheaper knightly company, a universal upgrade to Bereden Serjanten, or a premature full-plate and fully barded gendarme formation.

**Gepantserde Ruiters are later High armored melee cavalry designed to receive or contest mounted pressure, survive ordinary return fire, deliver one moderate controlled charge, and remain effective in a longer but still bounded mounted engagement than lighter professional cavalry.**

Their governing battle loop is:

**Brace, Advance, Contact, Control, Withdraw**

Their campaign loop is:

**Select, Armor, Pair, Inspect, Serve, Repair, Recover**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Brace

The company identifies the mounted or protected mission, forms around standards, checks horse load and armor, and preserves enough order to receive or deliver controlled contact.

#### 10.2 Advance

Files move at a sustainable pace toward one sector. Heavy equipment and strong horses improve protection but reduce acceleration, turning, and route flexibility.

#### 10.3 Contact

The company delivers a moderate charge, countercharge, or protected interception. The goal is controlled contact and local mounted advantage rather than knightly decisive shock.

#### 10.4 Control

Armored files use protection, cohesion, shields, sidearms, and horse mass to remain effective briefly after first contact, fix an enemy mounted body, or protect a withdrawal.

#### 10.5 Withdraw

The company leaves before fatigue, polearms, fresh knightly shock, blocked ground, or cumulative horse loss turns staying power into attrition.
