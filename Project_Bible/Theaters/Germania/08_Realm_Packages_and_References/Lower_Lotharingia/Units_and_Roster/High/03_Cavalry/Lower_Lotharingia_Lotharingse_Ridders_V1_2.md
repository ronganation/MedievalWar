This profile defines the **Lotharingse Ridders** as Lower Lotharingia's dedicated High Era knightly shock cavalry. The unit concentrates knightly riders, trained warhorses, lances, armor, banners, squires, remount support, and lordly service relationships into a scarce mounted formation for one decisive charge, mounted counterattack, operational pursuit, or rapid high-value intervention. It does not replace Lotharingse Ruiters, Hertogelijke Ruiters, Ridders te Voet, Verkenners, or later heavier cavalry families.

**Observe, Form, Charge, Break, Reform**

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Observe

The company receives reports from Verkenners, commanders, banners, and visible battlefield conditions. Target choice must distinguish a real opening from a feint or prepared reception.

#### 10.2 Form

Files align behind banners with matched pace, lance readiness, sufficient frontage, clear ground, protected shoulders, and a reform route.

#### 10.3 Charge

The company accelerates through a real lane and commits at a useful angle. Ground, horse condition, formation order, target readiness, and missile exposure determine shock value.

#### 10.4 Break

The charge seeks one local displacement, rout, mounted defeat, or sector opening. The company may conduct short mounted melee but must not mistake contact for permission to remain indefinitely.

#### 10.5 Reform

Riders break off, gather behind banners, account for files and horses, replace weapons or mounts where physically possible, and decide whether to hold, pursue, counter again, withdraw, or recover.
