This profile defines the **Bereden Kruisboogschutters** as Lower Lotharingia's High Era mounted crossbow family. The unit uses horses for operational mobility, rapid repositioning, screening, pursuit harassment, and delivery of deliberate crossbow fire from useful angles. The company is not a horse-archer analogue, knightly cavalry, mounted shock formation, or automatic mounted version of every foot crossbow company.

### 10. Governing Battle Loop

#### 10.1 Ride

The company moves through a real route while preserving horse condition, formation, crossbows, bolts, and exit options.

#### 10.2 Position

Files occupy one useful angle outside immediate heavy contact. The company identifies target, range, cover, approach threats, displacement route, and rally point.

#### 10.3 Load

Shooters halt or use another validated method to span and load. Reload is slower, more exposed, and more formation-sensitive than bow fire.

#### 10.4 Loose

Files deliver deliberate bolts against targets worth the ammunition. The purpose is pressure, disruption, and positional effect rather than rapid continuous fire.

#### 10.5 Displace

The company moves before heavy cavalry, infantry, missiles, or terrain fix its position. Displacement preserves the unit's defining mobility.
