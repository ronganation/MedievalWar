## 1. Direct Roster Verdict

Lower Lotharingia should play as an **Imperial frontier ducal network that converts scattered obligations, defended nodes, working roads, crossings, towns, lordships, household service, and selective professional capacity into one concentrated combined-arms host**.

The roster identity is:

> **The Ducal Anvil and the Gathered Hammer**

The faction does not require an entirely unique weapon class in every category. Distinction comes from how familiar medieval troop types are raised, equipped, commanded, concentrated, committed, brought home, and rebuilt.

roster_archetype: ImperialFrontierDucalNetworkCombinedArmsHost
battlefield_identity: DependableFootCenterSelectiveMissileSupportValuableMountedStrengthAndControlledOperationalConcentration

## 4. Battlefield Responsibility Lanes

| Lane | Required Lower Lotharingian interpretation | Admission test |
|---|---|---|
| Main infantry center | Dependable spear-and-shield or equivalent formed foot from a real provider | Must hold frontage without becoming universal elite infantry |
| Ducal pressure-point foot | Maintained infantry tied to direct ducal or household service | Must retain scarcity and command relationship |
| Local and lordly infantry | Broader service that supplies depth and garrison capability | Must differ through duty, equipment, release, and recovery |
| Crossing and fortified approach troops | Defend or contest roads, gates, bridges, approaches, and relief lanes | Must not become passive terrain bonus infantry |
| Assault infantry | One bounded disruption or close-assault mission | Must have approach, support, breakoff, and recovery limits |
| Missile support | Selective bow or crossbow contribution from appropriate communities and workshops | Must not erase nonmissile providers or become free armor penetration |
| Reconnaissance and route control | Scout, screen, report, escort, and economically pursue | Must lose prolonged heavy combat |
| Ducal mounted core | Reliable mounted intervention around ducal capacity | Must remain below knightly or household extremes where applicable |
| Household mounted crisis force | Trusted scarce command protection and exceptional intervention | Must not become generic cavalry pool |
| Knightly decision force | Formed charge and peer-heavy mounted contest | Requires riders, warhorses, armor, lances, service, and coordination |
| Mounted support | Messengers, remounts, standards, wounded, rally, and pursuit control | Must not become discount combat cavalry |
| River and transport interface | Support movement, crossing, cargo, and campaign mobility where physically qualified | River presence alone creates no vessels, crews, rights, or troops |


## 4A. Operational Mission Packet Matrix

| Mission | Required roster packet | Principal failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Field concentration | Main line, missile support, mounted response, command, supply, return route | Good units arrive separately and cannot act as one host |
| Crossing seizure or defense | Scouts, formed foot, missile cover, engineers or labor where validated, reserve, protected exit | Formation becomes trapped against water or congestion |
| Fortress relief | Route intelligence, durable infantry, mobile intervention, supplies, garrison connection | Relief army fights before connecting to the defended node |
| Siege campaigning | Camp security, missile troops, assault or engineering support, transport, sustained supply | Expensive host remains concentrated without progress |
| Raid and counter-raid | Scouts, light or medium mounted troops, escorts, local intelligence, recall limits | Pursuit or raiding disconnects from return and home defense |
| Fighting withdrawal | Rearguard infantry, missile delay, mounted interception, wounded and baggage protection | Retreat turns into fragmentation and provider loss |
| Pursuit | Fresh mounted troops, scouts, command limits, remounts, return clock | Valuable cavalry is blown, scattered, ambushed, or recalled late |

No unit receives a hidden bonus simply because the mission occurs near a river, road, crossing, town, or fortress. The advantage comes from possessing and coordinating the appropriate physical packet.

## 9A. Provider-Specific Silhouette and Equipment Grammar

| Provider family | Visual and equipment read | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Ducal household | Better maintained kit, compact standards, stronger officer visibility, coherent but not uniform files | Universal elite plate or royal-guard fantasy |
| Lordship and castellany | Mixed serviceable equipment, local banners, visible mounted and garrison obligations | Random poverty or identical noble kit |
| Town and craft | Better repair, crossbows or shields where validated, carts, stores, practical civic marks | Modern municipal uniformity |
| Crossing and frontier | Lighter baggage, route gear, practical shields, scouts, messengers, weathered field equipment | Permanent skirmisher stereotype |
| Knightly and mounted | Visible horse-quality differences, tack, lances, shields, attendants, remount burden | Endless identical heavy cavalry |
| Regional or hired | Theater-owned identity retained, with Lower Lotharingian service context shown through contract, route, or employer markers | Repainting the unit as core faction property |

Equipment variation communicates source and readiness. Variation cannot secretly change the unit into several battlefield roles at once.
