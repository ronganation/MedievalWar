This resolution closes only the immediate planning question around EI-01: what the first Lower Lotharingian infantry family is, which providers may contribute to it, which name should be used during drafting, and what the first profile must own and must not own.

It does not admit the unit into the canonical roster or establish exact historical terminology, Opening State presence, provider locations, pool sizes, statistics, or implementation data.

## 1. Direct Resolution

EI-01 should proceed as the first unit profile concept.

The provisional drafting name should be:

> **Lotharingse Speerlieden**

English support name:

> **Lower Lotharingian Spearmen**

```yaml
primary_drafting_name: LotharingseSpeerlieden
English_support_name: LowerLotharingianSpearmen
name_status: ProvisionalPendingHistoricalAndLinguisticCertification
rejected_as_current_primary_name:
  - HeerbanSpeerlieden
  - LotharingischeSpeerlieden
reason:
  - HeerbanMayOverstateOneUniformInstitution
  - LotharingischeSpeerliedenIsAMixedLanguageConstruction
  - TheUnitDrawsFromSeveralBoundedObligationAndProviderChannels
retained_internal_descriptor: BroadObligationMainLineSpearFamily
```

**Lotharingse Speerlieden** retains the regional flavor of the original idea without mixing the German adjectival form `Lotharingische` with the Dutch-style noun `Speerlieden`. The name is intentionally more evocative than “Levy Spearmen” and less institutionally overconfident than “Heerban Speerlieden.” It gives the player a clear regional identity while leaving the detailed provider constitution inside the profile.

```yaml
roster_role: EarlyMainLineSpearAndShieldInfantry
```

The family represents the ordinary formed infantry around which Lower Lotharingian combined-arms hosts can organize. Its strength is not exceptional equipment. Its strength is that several local and lordly sources can, when properly released and connected, create a credible spear-and-shield line.

## 3. Provider Constitution

EI-01 is not raised by one universal provider. It is a shared family produced through several compatible but separately qualified service channels.

```yaml
eligible_provider_classes:
  LordshipService:
    contribution: LocalFightersFileLeadersShieldsSpearsAndServiceRelationships
    principal_cost: EstateAndLocalDefenseExposure
  CastellanyAndDistrictService:
    contribution: MusterOrganizationStandardsRoutesStoresAndSomeGarrisonExperience
    principal_cost: StrongpointRouteAndGarrisonExposure
  LandholdingService:
    contribution: EligibleHouseholdOrTenantServiceWhereHistoricallyAndLegallyValid
    principal_cost: ProductiveHouseholdAndEstateAbsence
  CommunityContribution:
    contribution: BoundedPeopleFoodTransportAndLocalSupportWhereObligationPasses
    principal_cost: LocalLaborSecurityAndEconomicPressure
ineligible_as_automatic_provider:
  - EveryRuralSettlement
  - EveryTownHousehold
  - EveryEcclesiasticalInstitution
  - EveryKnightlyLandholder
  - PopulationCountAlone
```

### Provider combination rule

One company may be assembled from several compatible files, but every contributing file remains traceable to its source. Mixed contribution does not erase differences in equipment, service duration, recall pressure, or replacement.

```yaml
company_assembly:
  minimum_requirements:
    - RecognizedMusterAuthority
    - CompatibleFilesAndCommand
    - SufficientSpearsShieldsHelmetsSidearmsAndSupport
    - OneDefinedMissionAndServiceDuration
    - ProtectedSourceMinimums
    - ReturnAndDemobilizationPlan
  mixed_provider_penalties_if_unresolved:
    - SlowerFormation
    - UnevenSignalsAndStandards
    - EquipmentMismatch
    - GreaterRecallRisk
    - LowerSecondMissionCapacity
```

## 5. Battlefield Purchase

The unit purchases a **credible ordinary formed line**, not elite performance.

```yaml
owns:
  - FormedSpearAndShieldFrontage
  - OrdinaryMountedThreatReceptionWhenPrepared
  - ConnectedInfantryHolding
  - ControlledShortAdvance
  - OneReliefOrLineTransfer
  - ProtectionForMissileTroopsOrOtherSupportBehindTheLine
wins_against:
  - DisorganizedOrdinaryFootInFrontageContest
  - LightOrMediumMountedPressureWhenPreparedAndSupported
  - ThreatsThatRequireAStableCenterRatherThanAssault
loses_against:
  - SustainedMissileExposure
  - FlankingAndSeveralDirectionThreats
  - EliteOrHeavyAssaultInfantryInProlongedContact
  - HighQualityHeavyCavalryWithoutDepthGroundAndSupport
  - CongestionBrokenCommandPoorGroundAndOverextension
```

### Battle loop

1. **Muster:** arrive as actual files with equipment and leaders.
2. **Form:** establish frontage, depth, shoulders, standards, and one relief lane.
3. **Receive:** meet ordinary infantry or mounted pressure from a prepared relation.
4. **Hold:** maintain the center while missiles and mounted forces perform their own tasks.
5. **Relieve:** transfer or withdraw before the broad service body disintegrates.
