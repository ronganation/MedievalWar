## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**Rijnland Crossing Guard are compact regional infantry organized to control the approaches, heads, exits, and immediate banks of important crossings. Shield-and-spear files close the usable lane, selected javelinmen punish troops compressed on the approach, and disciplined relief files prevent one narrow defense from becoming a trapped last stand.**

Their signature is **Seal the Crossing**.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Compact crossing-defense spear infantry with limited javelin support
- **Signature:** **Seal the Crossing**
- **Battle promise:** Seize or hold a short passage, break an ordinary assault through shield-spear depth and timed javelins, restore the near head when necessary, and hand the lane over without losing the company.
- **Best use:** Crossing heads, narrow roads, bridge exits, firm banks, causeways, settlement gates, camp approaches, and protected withdrawal routes.
- **Strong against:** Light cavalry forced into narrow frontal contact, light infantry, poor spearmen, disordered foot, ordinary shield infantry committed into one approach, and pursuers entering a prepared withdrawal lane.
- **Conditional against:** Ordinary shield infantry, medium cavalry without a clean charge, superior infantry while the frontage remains narrow, and enemy troops emerging disordered from water or a crossing lane. With intact Passage Control and relief depth, they should be capable of defeating ordinary shield infantry committed into the sealed approach.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated missiles, artillery, multiple approaches, flanking along the bank, heavy infantry with room to deploy, fresh heavy cavalry on open ground, and prolonged isolation without relief.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain a powerful passage-control and rearguard unit by consuming finite regional fighters, leaders, shields, spears, javelins, food, watch coverage, and crossing-security capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Control the approach, keep an exit, throw only when compression matters, seal the lane, and rotate before the unit is surrounded.
- **Flavor line:** **A crossing is only a road until armed men decide who may pass.**

## 10. Core Battle Loop

### Observe

Watch the approaches, identify water level, usable lanes, bank conditions, enemy route, and whether friendly traffic must still pass.

### Screen

Use selected light files and javelinmen to delay scouts and disrupt the first compression without surrendering the return lane.

### Receive

Form shields and spears across the shortest useful frontage. The line receives the first organized contact and protects the qualified throwers.

### Seal

Close the usable approach, throw into compressed targets, replace local gaps, and force the enemy to fight through a compact defended head.

### Recover the Head

If the near side or immediate exit is lost but the enemy remains compressed and disordered, relief files may conduct one short spear-and-shield counterattack supported by preserved javelins. This is a local recovery action, not a general pursuit across the crossing.

### Relieve

Rotate files, pass friendly troops or wounded through a real lane, shorten the frontage, or hand the position to another formation.

### Hand Off

Transfer the controlled lane to Holland Spearmen, a garrison, another regional formation, or a relieved guard company. A successful handoff preserves the crossing while releasing the specialist company from indefinite attrition.

### Return

Withdraw through the preserved exit, restore crossing security, account for personnel and equipment, and begin regional recovery.

## 23. Strong Battlefield Value

Rijnland Crossing Guard must be worth recruiting. They are not merely infrastructure with unit models. When correctly positioned, they can stop light cavalry, defeat light infantry, hold ordinary infantry at a short approach, and delay a superior force long enough for Holland or another qualified army to act.

Their strength is bounded rather than weak. A narrow defended head amplifies the unit's shields, spears, relief files, and finite javelins. Once removed from that operating logic, they remain respectable regional foot but lose the special value that justified a separate card.

## 27. Combined-Arms Doctrine

### Holland Spearmen

Holland Spearmen provide broader realm-core frontage. Rijnland Crossing Guard occupy the decisive short approach, manage passage, and protect a withdrawal or transfer point.

### Comital Retainers

Comital Retainers protect command and resolve the politically critical breach. Rijnland guards should handle ordinary crossing pressure so the household reserve is not consumed prematurely.

### Kennemerland Footmen

Kennemerland Footmen can attack or counterpress along the bank while Rijnland guards seal the head. Rijnland should not inherit Kennemerland's stronger ordinary close-fight identity.

### Missile troops

Archers or crossbowmen gain compressed targets behind the crossing guard's line. Their firing lane must remain physical and should not pass through friendly files.

### Cavalry

Cavalry protect open banks, pursue troops driven back from the head, and prevent the guard from being turned. The Crossing Guard itself is not a pursuit formation.

## 38. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

Rijnland's waterways, bridges, ferries, fords, banks, and causeways are valuable because they move people, messages, goods, and armies. Their operators do not automatically form fighting units. A Crossing Guard requires a separate body of eligible regional fighters, recognized leaders, shields, spears, a limited supply of javelins, and a political relationship that permits field service without abandoning local security.

On the battlefield, the guard controls a short approach. Spear and shield files receive the first contact while selected throwers punish troops compressed onto the crossing head. The formation can stop light cavalry, defeat light foot, and hold ordinary infantry where the enemy cannot easily deploy. Its strength collapses if the bank is turned, the exit is blocked, or concentrated missiles are allowed to work without interruption.

### Recruitment text

**Compact regional crossing-defense infantry with strong shields, useful spear frontage, and limited javelins. Excellent at controlling short approaches and friendly passage, but vulnerable to missiles, flanking, isolation, and open-ground heavy cavalry.**
