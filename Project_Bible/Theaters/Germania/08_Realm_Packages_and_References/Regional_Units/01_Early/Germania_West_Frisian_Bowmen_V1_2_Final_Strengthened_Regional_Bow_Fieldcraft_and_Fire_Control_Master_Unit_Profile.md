## 2. Direct Unit Verdict

**West-Frisian Bowmen are mobile regional missile infantry built around dispersed observation, controlled shooting, rapid displacement, and short concentrated volleys against exposed troops. They do not imitate Welsh longbowmen or later urban crossbow companies. Their strength is flexible fieldcraft, firing-lane selection, and survival in a water-shaped coastal theater.**

Their signature is **Loose and Reform**.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Class:** Mobile regional bow infantry
- **Signature:** **Loose and Reform**
- **Battle promise:** Win the missile battle through position and timing: screen the approach, range by sections, maintain pressure while moving, mass a decisive volley against the exposed point, and break contact before cavalry or crossbows can pin the company.
- **Best use:** Banks, roads, field boundaries, settlement approaches, dry coastal ground, flanks protected by Holland Spearmen, and positions overlooking a controlled passage.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, unshielded troops, exposed flanks, light cavalry under sustained fire, pursuing enemies, disordered ordinary foot, and troops compressed by terrain or friendly infantry.
- **Conditional against:** Ordinary shield infantry from favorable angle, medium cavalry before contact, and enemy archers when cover, ammunition, and leadership favor the bowmen. A well-timed concentrated volley should be capable of breaking ordinary infantry already fixed, flanked, compressed, or disordered.
- **Weak against:** Crossbows, armored infantry, frontal shield formations, fresh cavalry with a clean approach, heavy rain, deep mud, ammunition exhaustion, and close combat.
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain early mobile missile pressure by consuming a finite regional pool of trained bow users, leaders, bows, strings, arrows, transport, food, and local watch capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Fight in sections. Keep one route open, preserve arrows for the exposed point, alternate shooting and displacement, then reform behind Holland infantry before the enemy closes.
- **Flavor line:** **The coast offers little shelter, so the bowmen learn to move before the enemy can answer.**

## 9. Core Battle Loop

### Observe

Read routes, wind, rain, cover, friendly frontage, enemy shields, cavalry approach, and withdrawal lanes.

### Disperse

Open files to reduce missile losses and maintain several firing points. Dispersal lowers close-defense and cavalry safety.

### Shoot

Deliver controlled file or section fire against exposed troops. Early fire shapes movement rather than guaranteeing kills.

### Displace

Move before cavalry, crossbows, or infantry fixes the formation. Displacement requires space, signals, and a prepared lane.

### Concentrate

Close enough files and synchronize enough bows to deliver a short decisive volley against a flank, compressed formation, cavalry approach, or disordered pursuer.

### Withdraw

Pass through Holland Spearmen, Crossing Guard, terrain, or another protected lane before contact.

### Return

Account for casualties, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, and provider obligations before the pool recovers.

## 34. Encyclopedia and Recruitment Text

### Encyclopedia

West-Frisian Bowmen are a proposed regional formation rather than an assumed opening regiment. A valid company requires a distinct group of military-eligible bow users, recognized leaders, repeatable practice, serviceable bows, spare strings, finite arrows, and a political relationship that permits field service without stripping local watch and settlement defense.

On the battlefield they survive through movement. Dispersed files observe and harass, sections shift before the enemy fixes them, and a concentrated volley is reserved for exposed cavalry, an open flank, disordered pursuers, or troops compressed by Holland's infantry. They are useful regional bowmen, not longbow elites or disguised crossbowmen.

### Recruitment text

**Mobile regional bow infantry effective against light, exposed, and compressed targets. Flexible and useful when protected, but lightly equipped, ammunition-dependent, and highly vulnerable to cavalry and crossbows.**

### 35.1 Corrected battlefield verdict

West-Frisian Bowmen are not weak levy archers whose only purpose is to add arrows to the army. They are active regional missile infantry able to control the tempo of an approach, force light troops and cavalry to alter movement, punish exposed flanks, and deliver a dangerous concentrated volley against ordinary infantry already fixed or disordered by Holland's line.

Their ceiling remains controlled. They do not win static exchanges against maintained crossbowmen, penetrate heavy armor reliably, or survive fresh cavalry without protection. Their strength comes from timing, movement, several firing sections, target selection, and a preserved exit.

### 50.1 Final tactical identity

The company fights as several mutually supporting bow sections rather than one broad static line. One section observes or shoots, another displaces, and a third preserves a reform or withdrawal option. The player gains continuing missile pressure without receiving effortless skirmisher kiting.

The signature action, **Loose and Reform**, is not a damage boost. It is a controlled cycle that permits a short concentration of fire followed by disciplined separation and reformation. If the exit is blocked, leaders are lost, or sections spread beyond signal range, the cycle fails.
