## 6. Core Battle Loop: Assemble, Screen, Commit, Recover

### 6.1 Assemble

A validated Moravian provider supplies riders, suitable mounts, leaders, weapons, armor, tack, fodder, routes, support, and authorized service.

### 6.2 Screen

The formation protects the regional line's flank, contests light horse, and preserves a useful reserve position.

### 6.3 Commit

The company delivers one controlled charge or countercharge against a suitable target. Angle, distance, ground, horse condition, enemy formation, and support determine the result.

### 6.4 Recover

Riders rally, reform files, inspect horses and tack, recover equipment, and return to reserve before another commitment.

```yaml
  - Dense woodland barricades steep slopes mud or blocked rally space

```
