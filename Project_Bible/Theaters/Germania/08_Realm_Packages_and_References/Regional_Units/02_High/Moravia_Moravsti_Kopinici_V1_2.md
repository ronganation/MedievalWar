## 3. Player-Facing Description

Moravští Kopiníci gather selected and maintained spear infantry through validated Moravian landholding, strongpoint, district, dynastic, and regional-service relationships. The formation is more cohesive and better supported than Moravská Hotovost, but it remains regionally owned rather than absorbed into the permanent Přemyslid core.

On the battlefield, the contingent supplies defensive depth and line extension. It can hold one important sector, receive cavalry from a prepared front, reinforce Moravian archers, and connect Bohemian formations across mixed ground. It does not equal Knížecí Kopiníci in central command continuity, controlled advance, or dependable availability to every ducal host.

Campaign access is part of the card's identity. Moravian authorities, providers, local defense, political reliability, and recall pressure matter. Control of Moravia does not manufacture spearmen, officers, equipment, or loyalty.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Gather, Anchor, Extend, Return

### 8.1 Gather

A validated Moravian relationship assembles people, officers, spears, shields, armor, food, transport, standards, and service into one coherent contingent.

The formation establishes one prepared defensive sector with front, depth, shoulders, reserve, and relief.

The contingent connects to adjacent troops, lengthens or deepens one supported line, and receives cavalry or ordinary infantry pressure without opening an isolated gap.

After battle or campaign service, people, officers, equipment, obligations, and surviving capability return through real regional accounting. Continued external service increases recall and recovery pressure.

## 32. Failure Stories

**The ducal clone:** Moravští Kopiníci receive the same command, equipment, availability, and advance behavior as Knížecí Kopiníci with regional colors.

**The levy reskin:** the unit differs from Moravská Hotovost only through higher statistics.

**The annexation pool:** ownership of Moravia instantly transfers people, officers, equipment, and loyalty into the permanent Bohemian roster.

**The free regional morale:** proximity to Moravian units grants hidden combat bonuses instead of real command and geometry.

**The endless external service:** recall, local defense, transport, food, political cooperation, and regional recovery never matter.

**The duplicate manpower:** the same people simultaneously fill Moravská Hotovost, Moravští Kopiníci, archers, mounted retainers, and local defense.
