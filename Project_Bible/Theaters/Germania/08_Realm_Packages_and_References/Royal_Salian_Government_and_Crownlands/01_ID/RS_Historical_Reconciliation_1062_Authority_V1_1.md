- **RS-HIST-005:** Henry IV's later adult conflicts are not back-projected.
- **RS-HIST-006:** Kaiserswerth is neither opening-active nor guaranteed.
- **RS-HIST-007:** later Investiture Controversy structures are not predetermined opening mechanics.
- **RS-HIST-008:** German kingship does not equal Roman imperial dignity or direct political-order control.
- **RS-HIST-009:** palace, itinerary, claim, property, possession, administration, and revenue remain distinct.
- **RS-HIST-010:** service and military rights do not create opening forces.
- **RS-HIST-011:** shared faith does not create loyalty, policy, knowledge, or obedience.

- **RS-HIST-013:** every admitted opening fact has owner, date, scope, source, confidence, contradiction, and review.
