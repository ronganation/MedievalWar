## 8. Royal Government Spine

Royal government is the package spine because it is the layer through which the player attempts to act. Royal government is neither the king nor the political order. It is a network of mandates, people, offices, institutions, household functions, instruments, resources, places, routes, communication, recognition, and enforcement.

```yaml
RoyalGovernmentFunctions:
  - DecisionAndDelegation
  - CounselAndConsultation
  - AuthenticationAndDocumentation
  - JudgmentAndDisputeHandling
  - CourtAndItineraryCoordination
  - HouseholdProtectionAndAccess
  - PropertyRevenueAndProvisioningInterfaces
  - DiplomacyAndPoliticalOrderParticipation
  - RequestsForServiceAndEnforcement
  - CommunicationAndRecordContinuity
```

No function proves a named officeholder, permanent council, fixed bureaucracy, or direct territorial owner.
