## 5. Owner-interface matrix

| Surface | Primary owner | Royal Salian interface |
|---|---|---|
| Person and personal agency | Person authority | Store known role, communication, request, and owner-approved response only |
| Ecclesiastical office | Office owner | Reference competence, holder, vacancy, appointment, jurisdiction, and dispute |
| Institution and chapter | Institution owner | Request counsel, hospitality, records, care, mediation, service, or support |
| Spiritual jurisdiction | Shared and external church owners | Preserve doctrinal and jurisdictional boundaries |
| Temporal lordship and local government | Ecclesiastical, local-polity, property, and settlement owners | Receive owner-published control and capacity without absorption |
| Property, revenue, labor, and material service | Property, economy, population, and local owners | Model request-to-delivery without creating capacity |
| Royal office, law, and judgment | Government authority | Establish valid Royal mandate and procedure |
| Diplomacy, recognition, and political order | Diplomacy and Germania owners | Route communication, recognition, conflict, and external participation |
| Public order and restoration | Public-order owner | Send burden, exclusion, justice, protection, and remedy facts |
| Sites and infrastructure | Built-environment and local owners | Request access, hospitality, custody, care, or repair |
