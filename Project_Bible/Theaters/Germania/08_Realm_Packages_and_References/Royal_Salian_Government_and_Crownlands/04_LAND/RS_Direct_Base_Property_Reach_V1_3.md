```yaml
reach_layers:

    meaning: SupportedPropertyFiscalJurisdictionalOrUsageClaimWithTypedOwnerState

    meaning: PalaceCourtHospitalityProvisioningRouteEscortOrItineraryInterface

    meaning: SharedHolyRomanAndGermaniaConstitutionalParticipation
```

No layer automatically promotes into another. A waypoint does not become a route; a route does not become property; property does not prove collected revenue; recognition does not create obedience; negotiated service does not become delivered troops.

## 3. Direct-base definition

The Royal Salian direct base means the set of assets and capacities the government can actually use without first obtaining new external cooperation.

```yaml
direct_base_requires:
  - VerifiedOwnerOrAuthorizedUser
  - PracticalPossessionOrControl
  - IdentifiedAdministratorOrResponsibleActor
  - AvailableRevenueProvisioningSecurityOrServiceCapacity
  - RouteAndCommunicationFeasibilityWhenRelevant
  - DateCurrentnessAndEvidenceConfidence
  - ExternalOwnerInterfaces
  - OpeningBindingAuthorizationForOpeningUse
```

Possible components include supported direct property, fiscal rights, settlements, usable palace or court sites, treasury access, household resources, government instruments, routes, provisioning, escorts, and finite military capacity. Their existence and availability must come from their proper owners.

A direct base is not synonymous with:

- the total lands of Germania;
- every place visited by the court;
- every royal or dynastic claim;
- every institution recognizing the king;
- every service obligation;
- every site associated with Salian memory;
- every place shown beneath a broad historical map label.

## 3.1 Direct-base availability states

Ownership and present usability must be evaluated separately. Later records should identify one of the following availability meanings without converting them into runtime enums here.

```yaml
direct_base_availability_candidates:
  AvailableNow: VerifiedControlAndUsableCapacity
  AvailableConditionally: UsableOnlyWhenNamedConditionsAreMet
  ClaimedNotControlled: SupportedClaimWithoutPracticalControl
  ControlledButImpaired: PracticalControlWithMaterialOperationalRestriction
  TemporarilyUnavailable: CurrentLossOfAccessSupplyAdministrationOrSecurity
  Disputed: CompetingControlClaimOrJurisdictionPreventsReliableUse
  Unknown: AvailabilityNotEstablished
```

This distinction prevents a valid legal or historical claim from being treated as immediately spendable revenue, recruitable manpower, usable lodging, secure movement, or available military support.

## 7. Provisioning and hospitality

Provisioning must identify the requesting authority, owing actor, legal or customary basis, place, date, commodity or service, quantity or explicit unknown, available stock, delivery, cost, resistance, failure, and replenishment.

The existence of an obligation does not create food, horses, carts, vessels, lodging, escort, or revenue. The player and AI may plan around potential access, but operational systems must publish actual availability before execution.

## 8. Relationship-reach model

Relationship reach is the government's ability to influence or coordinate action through directed, scoped, and reversible relationships.

```yaml
relationship_types:
  - Recognition
  - Consultation
  - Mediation
  - Protection
  - Advocacy
  - ServiceRequest
  - NegotiatedService
  - DeliveredService
  - OfficeOrDelegation
  - CustodyAndAccess
  - HospitalityAndProvisioning
  - RouteAccess
  - EcclesiasticalParticipation
  - InformationExchange
  - ConditionalMilitarySupport
```

Each relationship record should identify source owner, target owner, direction, scope, conditions, date, confidence, obligations, practical delivery, breach or resistance, and expiry or currentness. Counterparty private state remains with the counterparty owner.

## 8.1 Relationship state and delivery discipline

Relationships require more than type labels. Later owner-approved records should distinguish whether a relationship is proposed, recognized, negotiated, available, delivered, resisted, breached, suspended, expired, or unknown.

```yaml
relationship_state_candidates:
  Proposed: RequestOrOfferExistsWithoutAgreement
  Recognized: ValidDirectedRecognitionExists
  Negotiated: ConditionalCommitmentExists
  Available: CommitmentCanCurrentlyBeCalledOrUsed
  Delivered: PeopleResourcesInformationOrAccessActuallyProvided
  Resisted: RelevantActorChallengesOrWithholdsPerformance
  Breached: PublishedFailureAgainstAnExistingCommitment
  Suspended: TemporarilyUnavailableWithoutAutomaticTermination
  Expired: NoLongerCurrentUnderItsTerms
  Unknown: CurrentStatusNotEstablished
```

These states are authoring meanings, not runtime enums. A relationship may contain several scoped obligations with different states.

## 8.2 Relationship overlap and double-counting rule

One counterparty may provide recognition, hospitality, information, route access, and military service under separate conditions. Those interfaces must not be counted as multiple independent resources when they depend on the same people, treasury, stock, route, or political commitment.

```yaml
double_counting_controls:
  same_underlying_force_counted_twice: Reject
  same_revenue_right_and_collected_revenue_counted_twice: Reject
  same_route_access_and_delivered_transport_counted_twice: Reject
  recognition_and_delivered_service_collapsed: Reject
  direct_and_relationship_capacity_collapsed: Reject
  shared_dependency_must_be_exposed: true
```

## 11.1 Player-facing capacity explanation

Every displayed reach item should answer four plain questions:

1. **What is this?** Direct control, claim, access, relationship, political-order participation, dispute, or unknown.
2. **What can it do now?** The specific available capacity, if any.
3. **Why is it available or blocked?** Owner, evidence, supply, access, administration, relationship, or security conditions.
4. **What could change it?** Valid acquisition, negotiation, delivery, breach, loss, restoration, or owner-published evidence.

A display should never promise a military, economic, governmental, or mobility benefit merely because a historically associated place or relationship exists.

## 11.2 Fog-of-war and counterparty privacy boundary

## 13. Acquisition, loss, and recovery

Direct-base changes must follow real campaign systems.

```yaml
change_channels:
  Acquisition:
    - ConquestFollowedByValidControlAdministrationAndIntegration
    - PurchaseExchangeGrantInheritanceOrSettlementWhenOwnerAuthorized
    - RecoveryOfSupportedRightOrProperty
  AccessChange:

  RelationshipChange:

  Loss:
    - ConquestGrantAlienationAdministrativeLossRouteFailureOrAccessWithdrawal
  Recovery:
    - RestorationSettlementReconquestReauthorizationOrRebuiltCapacity
```

No research item or policy decision creates the required land, people, officials, stock, transport, consent, or force.

## 13.1 Loss propagation and recovery boundaries

Loss must propagate only through supported dependencies.

- loss of route access may impair movement and delivery without transferring property ownership;
- loss of practical possession may stop revenue or use without erasing every legal claim;
- loss of an administrator may impair collection or government action without proving alienation;
- breach of military service may remove the delivered force without ending recognition or every other relationship;
- loss of a palace or waypoint does not automatically end the itinerant government;
- loss of one direct-base component does not automatically delete the entire constitutional faction.

Recovery must restore the affected dependency through a valid system. It may not reactivate all connected capacity automatically.

## 22. Six-Layer Reach Integration Contract

```yaml
      - exclusive_control
      - permanent_presence
  4_material_capacity:
    includes:
      - supported_people_goods_revenue_or_facilities
      - amount_or_scope_if_attested
      - availability_and_competing_obligations
    does_not_prove:
      - arrival
```

No downstream layer may be populated merely to complete a map, a territory outline, or a strategic score. The original V1.2 six-layer definitions remain controlling wherever this integration contract is silent.

## 27. Failure Propagation and Recovery
