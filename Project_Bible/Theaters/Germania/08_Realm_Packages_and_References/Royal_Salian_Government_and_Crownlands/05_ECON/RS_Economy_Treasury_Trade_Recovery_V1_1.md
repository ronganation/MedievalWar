## 21. Public-order interface

Economic actions send the following facts to the dedicated public-order owner:

- who bears or benefits from a demand;
- whether authority, notice, assessment, collection, delivery, and use are understood;
- whether capacity, exemptions, custom, timing, repetition, and agent conduct are disputed;
- whether payment, remission, remedy, compensation, restitution, relief, or repair is performed;
- whether losses, delays, diversion, corruption, coercion, or broken promises occur.

The public-order authority determines grievance, participation, compliance, resistance, justice, accommodation, restoration, and cohesion consequences. This authority does not convert material pressure directly into loyalty values.
