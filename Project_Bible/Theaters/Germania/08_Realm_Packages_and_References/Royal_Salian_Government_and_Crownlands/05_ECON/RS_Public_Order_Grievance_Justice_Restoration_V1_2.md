This authority defines the Royal Salian faction-specific public-order loop without inventing an opening situation. It governs how protection, burden, participation, information, justice, compliance, resistance, settlement, and restoration interact. ## 1. Core public-order thesis

**Royal Salian order is a maintained political relationship, not a passive obedience score.** It depends on the Crown and its agents making credible claims, providing or arranging protection, respecting or negotiating rights and customs, distributing burdens, hearing grievances, delivering judgment, supervising enforcement, and repairing failures.

## 2. Playable-faction promise

The player governs a mobile and negotiated kingship whose authority often travels through offices, households, property interfaces, institutions, local powers, assemblies, messengers, judgments, agreements, and reputation. The player can impose, bargain, mediate, protect, investigate, judge, pardon, compensate, restore, or defer. Every method creates a different balance of speed, cost, legitimacy, local participation, coercion, and future reliability.

```yaml
player_promises:

  GovernThroughRelationships: Work through verified local institutional household and political interfaces
  MakeBurdenVisible: Show who pays serves supplies hosts transports or loses access

  UseJusticeAsGovernment: Convert petitions inquiries hearings judgments and enforcement into political action
  RepairAfterFailure: Support relief compensation restitution remission reconciliation and institutional correction
  PreserveUncertainty: Distinguish known reported alleged disputed outdated and unknown conditions
```

## 8. Grievance lifecycle

```yaml
grievance_lifecycle:
  Latent: HarmOrConcernExistsButIsNotReliablyKnown
  Expressed: ComplaintPetitionRefusalRumorOrReportReachesAnInterface
  Registered: AuthorizedRecordAcknowledgesIssueWithoutPredeterminingTruth
  Assessed: JurisdictionEvidenceRiskAndAffectedOwnersAreIdentified
  HeardOrNegotiated: RelevantPartiesPresentClaimsEvidenceTermsOrMediation
  DecidedOrSettled: JudgmentAgreementRemissionPardonCompensationOrOtherDispositionIssued
  EnforcedOrPerformed: RequiredActionActuallyOccurs
  Monitored: ComplianceSafetyAndRenewedHarmAreObserved
  RestoredOrClosed: MaterialProceduralAndRelationalConditionsMeetClosureStandard
  Reopened: NewEvidenceNonperformanceRenewedHarmOrOwnerConflictReturnsIssue
```

A complaint does not become true merely because it is recorded. A judgment does not close the grievance until enforcement, remedy, and affected conditions are checked.


## 12. Justice architecture

Justice is a chain of access, jurisdiction, evidence, decision, enforcement, remedy, and review. The Royal Salian player does not automatically own every court, local custom, institutional jurisdiction, or dispute.

```yaml
justice_chain:
  Access: ActorCanReachARecognizedPetitionHearingMediationOrComplaintInterface
  Jurisdiction: ApplicableOwnerOfficeInstitutionCustomOrDelegationIsIdentified
  Notice: RelevantPartiesReceiveCurrentAuthenticatedInformation
  Evidence: ClaimsWitnessesRecordsMaterialFactsAndContradictionsAreAssessed
  HearingOrMediation: PartiesHaveApplicableOpportunityToPresentOrNegotiate
  Decision: JudgmentSettlementPardonRemissionOrReferralIsIssuedByValidAuthority
  Authentication: SealWitnessRecordMessengerOrOtherOwnerApprovedMethodSupportsValidity
  Enforcement: OrderPaymentReturnRestraintPunishmentOrProtectionActuallyOccurs
  Remedy: MaterialProceduralAndRelationalEffectsAreAddressed
  Review: AppealReconsiderationMonitoringOrReopeningOccursWhereApplicable
```


## 17. Restoration doctrine

Restoration is the reconstruction of an order relationship after violence, abuse, extraction, displacement, procedural failure, broken promises, or resistance. It is not an instant modifier reset.

```yaml
restoration_pillars:
  Safety: ImmediateThreatAbuseAndRetaliationAreReduced
  Subsistence: FoodWaterShelterFuelCareAndLivelihoodCanRecover
  Procedure: PetitionJurisdictionHearingJudgmentAndReviewAreCredible
  MaterialRemedy: ReturnRepairPaymentReliefOrCompensationActuallyOccurs
  Participation: AffectedActorsHaveAValidInterfaceForTermsAndMonitoring
  InstitutionalCorrection: ResponsibleOfficesAgentsForcesOrProcessesAreConstrainedOrImproved
  Communication: TermsResponsibilitiesTimelinesAndUnknownsArePublishedThroughValidChannels
  ReturnAndReintegration: DisplacedDemobilizedOrExcludedPeopleCanReenterUnderOwnerApprovedConditions
  Monitoring: RenewedHarmNonperformanceRumorAndRetaliationAreChecked
  Time: HouseholdCommunityInstitutionalAndTrustRecoveryCannotBeInstant
```
