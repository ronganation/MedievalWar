The purpose is to prevent the faction from being represented as a generic Holy Roman Empire, a contiguous territorial kingdom, Henry IV alone, or an interchangeable German royal roster.

## 2. Presentation thesis

**Present the Royal Salian Government as a vulnerable but prestigious distributed kingship whose authority travels through people, instruments, places, relationships, evidence, and delivered capacity.**

## 36. Validation catalogue

- **RS-PRES-001:** the faction is not presented as the entire Holy Roman Empire or Germania.
- **RS-PRES-002:** Henry IV is principal but not the whole faction identity.
- **RS-PRES-003:** kingship, regency, household, offices, dynasty, instruments, direct capacity, and political order remain distinct.
- **RS-PRES-004:** map association, claim, right, access, possession, administration, control, and ownership remain visually distinct.
- **RS-PRES-005:** unknown is not displayed as zero, absence, hostility, or false certainty.
- **RS-PRES-006:** final name, heraldry, palette, typography, layout, art, audio, and localization remain uncommitted.
- **RS-PRES-007:** later imperial or national symbolism is not back-projected without admission.
- **RS-PRES-008:** color is never the sole carrier of critical state.
- **RS-PRES-009:** portraits do not create mandate, office, location, knowledge, or consent.
- **RS-PRES-010:** property claims and potential revenue are not displayed as usable treasury.
- **RS-PRES-011:** public order is not reduced to a generic happiness percentage.
- **RS-PRES-012:** building icons do not prove ownership, readiness, capacity, staffing, or supply.
- **RS-PRES-013:** service rights do not appear as available armies.
- **RS-PRES-014:** regional and mercenary forces remain Germania-owned.
- **RS-PRES-015:** recognition and political participation do not appear as universal obedience or vassalage.
- **RS-PRES-016:** external private intent and hidden state are not revealed.
- **RS-PRES-017:** setback, repair, and recovery remain visible player-facing processes.
- **RS-PRES-018:** dramatic copy cannot override authority, owner, evidence, or system state.
- **RS-PRES-019:** invented quotations and emotions are rejected.
- **RS-PRES-020:** localization and accessibility requirements are present before implementation.
- **RS-PRES-021:** audio-only critical information is rejected.
- **RS-PRES-022:** comparative references do not copy another Realm's private presentation.
- **RS-PRES-023:** AI can request truthful presentation but cannot invent presentation truth.
- **RS-PRES-024:** three-track progression and conditional historical emergence remain visible.
- **RS-PRES-025:** no Registry, Opening State, payload, importer, runtime, map, save, asset, audio, code, or Unreal mutation occurs.
- **RS-PRES-026:** implementation validation is not claimed.
