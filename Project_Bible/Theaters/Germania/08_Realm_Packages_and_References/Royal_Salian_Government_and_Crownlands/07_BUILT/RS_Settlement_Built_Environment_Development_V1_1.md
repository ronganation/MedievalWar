It owns the Royal interpretation and decision interface, not settlement private state or universal construction semantics.

## 31. Player-facing information contract

- site and facility identity, place, owner, access, possession, administration, and control state;
- supported functions, readiness, capacity constraints, condition, maintenance, and damage;
- staff, labor, skill, materials, tools, animals, transport, funding, route, season, and security dependencies;

- differences among inspection, emergency stabilization, repair, restoration, adaptation, expansion, and new construction;

- which facts remain unknown rather than displayed as absent or zero.
