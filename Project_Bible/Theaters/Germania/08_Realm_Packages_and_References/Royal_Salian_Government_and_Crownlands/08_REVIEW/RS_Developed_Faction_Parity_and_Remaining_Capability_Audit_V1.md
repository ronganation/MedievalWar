## 2. Executive Verdict

**A. No new substantive authority is justified as the next task. Perform a bounded current-root-index and dependency/navigation reconciliation centered on the existing [00_CTL\RS_Package_Index_V1_2.md](<../00_CTL/RS_Package_Index_V1_2.md>). Do not execute that maintenance in this audit.**

The package has broad, substantive faction-private architecture: identity, objectives, multi-stage strategic AI, nine person authorities, dynasty/household, directed networks, event/memory routing, government/law, ecclesiastical interfaces, population, public order, economy, military, geography, built operations and presentation. It is not merely a collection of historical profiles. It is also **not yet a fully populated, accepted, implemented developed faction**.

The decisive P0 defect is current control: 15 of the root index's 40 Section 1.7 recorded paths are physically missing. All 25 existing entries still match their recorded hashes, so the result is not blanket hash corruption. Current government/diplomacy/Church/public-order successors, expanded character content and all eight current history authorities are not reliably routed. The older root Public Order hold conflicts with a physical V1.2 scoped-acceptance claim; that conflict is not silently settled here.

The strongest substantive parity deficits are populated native military profiles/era development and source-backed built/investment choices. They have existing architectural owners and require bounded research/population, not duplicate general faction, network, Church, public-order or history authorities. Exact opening state, owner acceptance, downstream integration and Unreal readiness remain false/incomplete.
