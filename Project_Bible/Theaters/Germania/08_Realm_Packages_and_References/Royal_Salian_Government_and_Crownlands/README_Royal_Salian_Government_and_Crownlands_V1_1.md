## 1. Package Purpose

This package defines the Royal Salian Government and Crownlands as a playable faction for the Grand Campaign opening on January 1, 1062.

Its central campaign identity is a recognized Salian kingship operating through the minority of Henry IV, the regency of Agnes of Poitou, the royal household, distributed crown resources, itinerant government, negotiated political relationships, and institutions whose cooperation cannot be assumed. The package separates the king, dynasty, regency, household, government, crownlands, political relationships, ecclesiastical interaction, population, economy, military capacity, built environment, and historical causality rather than compressing them into a unified imperial state.

This is a static design-authority package. It does not by itself activate Opening State, populate runtime records, authorize implementation, or claim Unreal Engine validation.

## 2. Current Root Structure

The live package is organized directly through its substantive folders. No separate control folder is required. This README is the current root entry point for the live package and supersedes deleted control-folder navigation without restoring that material.

### `03_GOV` — Government, Law, Diplomacy, and Ecclesiastical Interface

Contains Royal Salian government and political-order authorities covering regency, offices, delegation, continuity, law, judgment, diplomacy, recognition, information, Holy Roman participation, and faction-facing ecclesiastical or investiture interfaces.

These authorities define Royal Salian participation and policy. They do not privately own every prince, bishopric, abbey, papal institution, or external political actor.

### `04_LAND` — Crownlands, Political Reach, Places, and Routes

Contains the territorial and geographic layer: the direct royal base, relationship-based reach, crownland and property claims, provisioning relationships, chronology and itinerary evidence, custody questions involving regalia, seals, treasury, and archives, and the authorities for places, settlements, routes, and strategic geography.

Formal right, possession, revenue, physical access, relationship reach, and map ownership remain distinct.

### `05_ECON` — Population, Economy, Provisioning, and Public Order

Contains authorities for population, households, communities, labor, migration, recruitment base, property, revenue, treasury, trade, provisioning, extraction, expenditure, recovery, and the physically retained Public Order capability.

Public Order capability exists in the package, but any unresolved currentness or affected-owner acceptance recorded by the live authorities or the parity audit must remain unresolved until supported by explicit owner evidence.

### `06_MIL` — Military Architecture and Recruitment System

Contains the Royal Salian military architecture: recruitment, command, service, muster, supply, recovery, source comparison, opening military constraints, and roster-development structure.

The developed-faction parity audit found substantive military architecture but did not find developed native Royal Salian unit-profile content in the audited live military folder. Future military work should therefore create admitted native units under the existing architecture rather than create another general military authority.

### `07_BUILT` — Built Environment and Faction Presentation

Contains settlement and built-environment authorities together with faction presentation and visual-identity work physically retained in the folder.

The built-environment layer covers the faction-facing role of royal places, palace and court support, infrastructure, maintenance, repair, reuse, recovery, and development interfaces. The remaining content need is to populate usable, source-aware building, facility, adaptation, and investment choices under the existing authority rather than replace that authority.

### First: Royal Salian Native Military Units

- historical and faction ownership;
- recruitment provider and finite pool;
- settlement, castle, household, regional, ecclesiastical, or campaign access as appropriate;
- equipment and battlefield role;
- cost, availability, replenishment, and progression;
- distinction from Theater-owned regional units, mercenaries, and external princely forces;

This work should populate the architecture already present in `06_MIL`; it should not create another military framework.
