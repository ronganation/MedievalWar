## 36. Priority, Urgency, Capacity Burden, and Objective Overload

Objective priority and urgency remain separate.

```yaml
ObjectivePriorityDimensions:
  - StrategicImportance
  - TimeSensitivity
  - IrreversibilityRisk
  - HouseholdAndContinuityRisk
  - OwnerAndAudienceExposure
  - CapacityBurden
  - RecoveryCost
  - OpportunityValue
  - DependencyCount
  - ConfidenceAndVisibility
```

Objective overload exists when accepted or active objectives require more people, attention, rights, funding, materials, routes, institutional support, military capacity, or political cooperation than the authoritative owners can supply.

```text
High Priority != Immediate Urgency
Urgent != Strategically Important
Many Objectives != Progress
Accepted Objective != Reserved Capacity
Overload != Automatic Failure
```

The player or AI may narrow scope, delay, suspend, redirect, abandon, or replace objectives rather than receiving free capacity.
## 37. Compatibility, Sequencing, Suspension, Redirection, and Supersession

```yaml
ObjectiveCompatibilityClasses:
  - CompatibleInParallel
  - CompatibleIfSequenced
  - CompatibleWithCapacityReservation
  - CompetingButNegotiable
  - MutuallyExclusiveForCurrentWorldState
  - OwnerConflict
  - EvidenceInsufficient
  - Unknown
```

Suspension requires a reason, preserved state, review condition, expiry or resumption rule, and treatment of committed resources and expectations.

Redirection requires a changed world state or strategy, a successor objective candidate, preserved costs and consequences, and owner review.

Supersession closes the old objective as current guidance but does not erase its history, costs, standing effects, unresolved commitments, or lessons.

```text
Suspension != Failure
Redirection != Free Reset
Supersession != Historical Erasure
Abandonment != Automatic Defeat
Replacement Objective != Duplicate Reward
```
