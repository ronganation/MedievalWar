## 1. Purpose and Strong Presentation Verdict

This authority defines how the provisional Saxon Billung package should be presented coherently across faction selection, campaign map, settlements, households, institutions, diplomacy, military surfaces, campaign objectives, narrative, audio, localization, and accessibility.

It translates accepted owner state into a legible player experience. It does not create people, buildings, borders, forces, relationships, heraldry, art assets, audio assets, widgets, localization strings, or Opening State records.

> Present the faction as a Billung-centered ducal power whose prestige exceeds its direct reach, whose strength depends on household continuity and negotiated regional relationships, and whose future can become durable, plural, transformed, or strategically smaller without becoming a generic German kingdom.
