## 1. Purpose and Authority Boundary

This authority defines the provisional playable spine for a Billung-centered Saxon package without pretending that the Billung dynasty, the ducal title, all Saxon lands, counts, church institutions, local communities, Royal sites, and external frontier actors form one owner.

```text
Integrated Core != Canonical Realm
Coordination != Ownership
Ducal Title != Territory
Regional Power != Uniform Administration
Billung Dynasty != Saxony
Saxony Folder != All Saxony Controlled
Playable Package != Every Actor Absorbed
```

The file consolidates the package promise, player-control boundary, regional coordination model, pressure structure, historical emergence, AI limits, and future authority interfaces. It creates no exact opening records.

## 29. AI Boundary

The package AI may evaluate and propose:

- household and dynastic continuity;
- consultation and coalition;
- ducal requests and mediation;
- property and capacity clarification;
- ecclesiastical accommodation or competition;
- Royal and political-order positioning;
- route and frontier security;
- recovery from fragmentation or loss.

The AI may not invent owners, people, titles, property, service, forces, agreements, knowledge, or historical outcomes.

## 30. Player-Facing Explanation

Player-facing explanation should identify:

- which owner controls an action or resource;
- why a request may be refused or only partly fulfilled;
- whether a relationship is unknown, cooperative, qualified, contested, or hostile;
- which capacity is missing;
- which local, ecclesiastical, comital, Royal, or external owner is involved;
- whether an outcome is historical, conditional, altered, or prevented.

The interface must never imply that the player owns all Saxony.
