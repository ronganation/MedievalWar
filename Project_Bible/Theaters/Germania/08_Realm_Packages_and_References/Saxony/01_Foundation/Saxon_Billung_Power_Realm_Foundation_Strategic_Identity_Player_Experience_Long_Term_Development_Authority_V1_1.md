## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority consolidates the Saxon package into a developed faction proposition without replacing the detailed owners that already govern characters, dynasty, household, history, governance, law, ecclesiastical institutions, territory, economy, population, and military systems.

It owns the faction-level answers to five questions:

1. What is the playable Saxon Billung project?
2. Why is that project distinct from the Royal Salian package and other Germania candidates?
3. What should the player protect, build, negotiate, reform, and transform?
4. How should Early, High, and later development remain coherent without creating people or resources?
5. What forms of success, failure, recovery, fragmentation, and continuation remain valid?

## 4. Faction Thesis

> Build and preserve a durable northern regional power through Billung household continuity, ducal standing, negotiated comital and ecclesiastical relationships, defensible direct capacity, reliable routes, finite service, and conditional transformation without pretending to own all Saxony.

The faction is strongest when political standing, people, property, institutions, service, communication, and material capacity reinforce each other. The faction is weakest when title claims outrun practical reach, expeditions consume local defense, household continuity depends on one person, or regional actors are treated as automatic subordinates.

## 5. Player Fantasy

The player acts as the strategic steward of a Billung-centered network rather than the administrator of a centralized state.

The fantasy is to:

- convert respected ducal standing into usable but bounded coordination;
- preserve the Billung household and dynasty through crisis and succession;
- win cooperation without erasing independent counts, churches, communities, and local owners;
- turn routes, information, property, hospitality, and service into real operational reach;
- choose between broad coalition, smaller executable core, ecclesiastical accommodation, Royal cooperation, frontier engagement, and strategic retrenchment;
- survive defeat, captivity, succession stress, property fragmentation, and political reversal;
- build institutions that outlast a single principal where world state supports them.

## 6. Central Campaign Question

> Can a prestigious but materially limited ducal household become a resilient regional political order without exhausting its people, overclaiming jurisdiction, provoking every external owner, or collapsing when its principal disappears?

## 12. Success Is Not Territorial Completion

The package does not require conquest of all Saxony to count as successful.

Valid success can include:

- a smaller but dependable Billung core;
- a durable coalition of independent actors;
- recognized issue-specific ducal competence;
- safer routes and reliable communication;
- protected household continuity;
- stable property and revenue arrangements;
- negotiated ecclesiastical coexistence;
- credible service and defense without exhausting local pools;
- peaceful or bounded Royal participation;
- successful transformation after dynastic change.

## 13. Failure Is Not Automatic Campaign Loss

The faction can continue after:

- military defeat;
- failed service calls;
- refused recognition;
- property loss;
- ecclesiastical or comital opposition;
- household displacement;
- captivity or incapacity;
- disputed succession;
- regional fragmentation;
- strategic retrenchment.

Failure must preserve costs, grievances, losses, surviving capacities, memories, and recovery choices. A smaller executable project may be a valid recovery rather than a failed imitation of territorial unity.

## 28. Household and Dynasty Foundation

The household and dynasty are central carriers but do not own the whole faction.

The foundation expects the player to:

- protect the people who carry continuity;
- separate household service from political office;
- preserve correspondence, records, patronage, and residence;
- prepare succession without inventing heirship;
- support independent agency for Wulfhild where owner-backed;
- preserve alternative continuity if direct succession fails;
- prevent household exhaustion from becoming package collapse.

## 29. Character-Centered Experience

<Person>Ordulf Billung</Person> is the principal playable leader, but the faction must not become a one-person modifier set.

<Person>Wulfhild of Norway</Person> provides household, dynastic, patronage, communication, and continuity gameplay without becoming a passive spouse token or automatic regent.

<Person>Magnus Billung</Person> remains a protected continuity subject whose later role depends on age, capacity, household state, recognition, succession rules, and world state.

Other people are promoted only when evidence and capability justify full character treatment.
