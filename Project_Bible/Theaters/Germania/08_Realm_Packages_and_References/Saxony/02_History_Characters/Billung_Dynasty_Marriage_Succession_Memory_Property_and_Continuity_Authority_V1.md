## 1. Purpose and Authority Boundary

This authority defines the Billung dynastic layer needed by the Saxon package after the developed profiles for Ordulf Billung and Wulfhild of Norway. It turns genealogy, marriage, succession pressure, memory, burial, patronage, and continuity into player-facing systems without making the dynasty a territorial government or a substitute for person, household, title, property, institution, or Realm owners.

```text
Billung Dynasty != Saxony
Dynasty != Ducal Title
Dynasty != Household
Dynasty != Government
Dynasty != Every Member's Property
Marriage != Alliance
Succession Interest != Guaranteed Succession
Burial Memory != Territorial Ownership
Modern Scholarly Label != Contemporary Self-Designation
```

The authority creates static design contracts only. It creates no opening records.

## 39. Failure States

Possible dynasty failures include:

- membership confusion;
- false genealogy;
- overpromised marriage or patronage;
- disputed succession;
- property fragmentation;
- household split;
- loss of records;
- memorial decline;
- external-owner estrangement;
- premature Magnus elevation;
- continuity dependent on one person;
- dynasty used to justify unsupported control over Saxony.

Failure is not automatic extinction. It creates political consequences and recovery work.

## 40. Recovery and Transformation

Recovery can involve:

- verifying genealogy and claims;
- clarifying title versus property;
- renegotiating marriage or protection terms;
- rebuilding household continuity;
- restoring records and memory;
- recovering or settling property;
- accepting a narrower but credible dynastic scope;
- supporting female-line, collateral, affinal, or memorial continuity;
- separating the playable package from a failing dynasty claim.

The package can survive a dynasty transformation if owner-approved world state supports another political form.

## 41. AI Intent and Limits

- membership review;
- marriage and affinal negotiation;
- protection of minor or vulnerable members;
- succession preparation;
