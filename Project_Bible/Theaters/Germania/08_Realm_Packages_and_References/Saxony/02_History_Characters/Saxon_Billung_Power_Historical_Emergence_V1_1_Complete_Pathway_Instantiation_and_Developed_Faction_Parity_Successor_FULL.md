## 3. Authority Thesis

Historical Emergence converts current owner-approved Saxon state, accessible memories, active momentum, real participants, capacity, decisions, conflict, and external responses into major strategic situations. It does not replay a chronology.

A pathway can remain dormant, become eligible, be proposed, gain or lose consent, activate through owner events, compete, redirect, fragment, reverse, fail, dissolve, resolve differently from recorded history, or close without formation.
