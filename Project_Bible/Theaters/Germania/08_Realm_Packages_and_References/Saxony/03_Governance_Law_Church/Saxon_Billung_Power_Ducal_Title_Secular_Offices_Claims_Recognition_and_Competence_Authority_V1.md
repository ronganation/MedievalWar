## 4. Title and Office Thesis

The Saxon package should make the ducal title politically important without making the title a container for Saxony. A title gives historically and legally bounded standing. Standing can support precedence, petition access, service requests, mediation, representation, and issue-specific competence. Every practical effect still depends on evidence, recognition, mandate, communication, local cooperation, capacity, and owner-routed execution.

The player experience is therefore not "the duke commands Saxony." The player experience is "the duke must prove what the title means to different audiences, secure recognition for specific uses, maintain competent people and records, defend or narrow contested claims, and preserve continuity when title, office, household, dynasty, and regional power do not align perfectly."

## 37. Title, Office, and Claim Decision Loops

### Title Definition Loop

1. Identify the candidate title and date-specific terminology.
2. Separate title from person, territory, property, and government.
3. Establish competence and recognition by scope.
4. record conflicts, qualifications, and unknowns.
5. bind no holder until owner review.
6. review continuity, vacancy, and end conditions.

### Office Admission Loop

1. identify a supported function and terminology.
2. verify competence, scope, and appointment authority.
3. identify an eligible person rather than inventing one.
4. establish support, reporting, vacancy, and review.
5. create the office only after owner admission.

### Claim Loop

1. identify claimant, target, basis, and scope.
2. gather evidence and competing claims.
3. route through the proper governance and law owners.
4. record recognition audience and current status.
5. execute no consequence without the correct owner receipt.
6. review settlement, rejection, dormancy, expiry, or supersession.
