## 4. Saxon Ecclesiastical Player Promise

The Saxon ecclesiastical game is institution-specific and polycentric. Cooperation can strengthen records, mediation, hospitality, education, relief, memory, recognition, and settlement resilience. Abuse can damage trust, access, standing, communication, local cooperation, and historical memory.

The player should repeatedly ask:

- Which institution, office, holder, community, property interest, or jurisdiction is involved?
- What does each actor know, recognize, request, contest, or refuse?
- Which secular and ecclesiastical authorities overlap?
- What capacity, route, object, property, or person is actually available?
- Which owner must execute an accepted result?
- How will the action be remembered by institutions, communities, the dynasty, Royal owners, and external church authorities?
