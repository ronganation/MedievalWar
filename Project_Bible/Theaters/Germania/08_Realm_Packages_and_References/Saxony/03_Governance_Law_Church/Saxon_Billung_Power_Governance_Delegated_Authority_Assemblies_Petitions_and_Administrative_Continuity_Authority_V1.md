## 4. Governance Verdict

The Saxon Billung package uses a plural, personal, negotiated, and capacity-limited governance model. Ducal standing may authorize requests, consultation, mediation, and bounded action, but execution depends on a valid mandate, an identified actor, routes, communication, household support, local acceptance, institutional cooperation, available service, and a reviewable result.

```yaml
GovernanceModel:
  classification: PluralBillungCenteredDucalCoordination

  universal_authority_score: prohibited

```

## 5. Non-Negotiable Governance Rules

- Ducal standing is not direct administration.

- Governance reach is action-specific, audience-specific, route-specific, and capacity-specific.

- A mandate requires a grantor, recipient, basis, scope, duration, review, and end condition.

- Delegation does not transfer title, property, household, army, or sovereignty.

- Personal presence may improve access but does not create ownership or obedience.

- Household access is not public authority.

- Assembly attendance is not membership, consent, or permanent subordination.

- A petition creates a request for review, not a guaranteed audience or outcome.

- Administrative continuity may be partial and distributed.

- Unknown personnel or procedures remain unknown and are not fabricated.

- AI proposes actions. Authoritative owners validate and execute them.
