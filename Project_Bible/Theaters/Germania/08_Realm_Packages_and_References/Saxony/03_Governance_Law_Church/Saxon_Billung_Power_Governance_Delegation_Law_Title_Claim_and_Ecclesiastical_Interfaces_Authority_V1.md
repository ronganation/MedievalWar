## 31. Governance and Ecclesiastical Decision Loops

The authority defines six core loops:

1. **Petition and Judgment Loop**: receive, validate competence, hear or mediate, decide, communicate, enforce, review.
2. **Delegation Loop**: identify need, validate actor and scope, grant, report, review, narrow, revoke, or expire.
3. **Office Loop**: establish a supported office, identify a candidate, appoint or confirm, monitor, suspend, replace, or leave vacant.
4. **Title and Claim Loop**: identify basis, publish, receive recognition or dispute, negotiate or adjudicate, preserve consequences.
5. **Assembly Loop**: define purpose, invite real owners, gather petitions, consult, record, hand off decisions, adjourn.
6. **Ecclesiastical Interface Loop**: identify institution and issue, determine competent owners, negotiate patronage or procedure, receive outcome, preserve independence.
