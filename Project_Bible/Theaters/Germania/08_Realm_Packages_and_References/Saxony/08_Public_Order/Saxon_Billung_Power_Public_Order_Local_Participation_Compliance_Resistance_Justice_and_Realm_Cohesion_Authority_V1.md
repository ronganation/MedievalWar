## 3. Benchmark Use and Saxon Adaptation

The authority uses developed-faction public-order architecture as a structural benchmark only. No foreign grievance, revolt, office, institution, population value, faction state, or opening condition is transferred.

Saxon adaptation centers on:

- Billung household and ducal reach that remain materially limited;
- independent counts, lordships, ecclesiastical institutions, and local entities;
- issue-specific participation rather than universal obedience;
- finite service, labor, food, route, and recovery capacity;
- household, regional, ecclesiastical, Royal, and frontier pressures;

## 37. Faction AI Doctrine

The Public Order AI must:

- distinguish inability from refusal;
- identify the actual issue and owner;
- prefer verified local information;
- match burdens to material and demographic capacity;
- avoid turning every refusal into rebellion;
- prioritize mediation, substitution, review, and narrow settlement;
- preserve household, community, institutional, and recruitment recovery;
- use coercion only through competent owners and proportional scope;
- preserve exit and reintegration paths;
- abstain when people, owners, rights, or evidence are missing.

The AI may not fabricate hostile intent, collective guilt, rebellion, loyalty, unanimity, or external support.
