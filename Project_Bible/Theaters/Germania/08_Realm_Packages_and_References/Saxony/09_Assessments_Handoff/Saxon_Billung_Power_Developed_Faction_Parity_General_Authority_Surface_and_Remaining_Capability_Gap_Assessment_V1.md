## 2. Assessment Method

Every major surface is classified as one of:

```yaml
CoverageDisposition:
  - PresentAndSufficient
  - PresentButDistributed
  - PresentButNeedsSuccessor
  - MissingGeneralAuthority
  - MissingSpecialistAuthority
  - DeferredUntilDependency
  - NotApplicable
  - RejectedAsSymmetryOnly
```

A new file is justified only when it adds a capability that current owners cannot express cleanly.

Evaluation criteria:

- player-facing purpose;
- substantive owner clarity;
- interaction with existing authorities;
- Early, High, and later-era development;
- AI and campaign behavior;
- failure, recovery, and divergence;
- persistence and migration;
- avoidance of duplicated state;
- evidence and opening-boundary discipline;
- readiness for later implementation without activating it.

## 5. Current Package Weakness

The missing layer is not more constitutional detail. The missing layer is a coherent general faction-development surface connecting the existing constitutional and material systems into:

- a long campaign identity;
- recurring strategic choices;
- external relationships and knowledge;
- compliance, resistance, and cohesion;
- institutional learning and development;
- infrastructure and settlement investment;
- player progression and recovery;
- presentation and communication.

The current integrated core contains portions of these ideas, but it functions as a provisional package spine and owner boundary. It does not replace the broader general authorities needed for mature faction development.

## 16. Education and Training Boundary

The education authority may own learning pathways, teaching capacity, literacy, archives, apprenticeship, and transmission.

It may not create:

- people;
- schools or institutions;
- offices or clerical appointments;
- buildings;
- books or materials;
- equipment;
- trained units;
- technology adoption;
- automatic cultural conversion.

Every capability depends on person, population, household, institution, property, economy, route, and knowledge owners.

The player should control:

- major strategic direction;
- critical agreements and conflicts;
- exceptional reforms and investments;
- succession and crisis responses;
- major resistance, recovery, and campaign-objective choices.

Routine communication, maintenance, local administration, demographic adjustment, and low-impact execution should resolve through owner-valid AI where capacity exists.

The AI must preserve uncertainty, external ownership, finite capacity, and the option to abstain.
