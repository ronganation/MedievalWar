## 1. Purpose

This preflight tests whether a historically defensible and mechanically distinct January 1062 playable package can be built around Billung ducal, household, dynastic, property, service, and relationship capacities without converting all Saxony into uniform Billung ownership. It inventories source routes, separates owner classes, evaluates the physical `Saxony` folder against the provisional `Saxon Billung Power` working identity, decides which characters may advance beyond research status, and determines whether Saxon development should continue or return to a focused Saxony-versus-Swabia gate.
