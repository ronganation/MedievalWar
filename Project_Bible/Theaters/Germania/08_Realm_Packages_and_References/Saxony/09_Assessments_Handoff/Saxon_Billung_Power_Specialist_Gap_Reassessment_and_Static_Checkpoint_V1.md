## 5. Evidence Behind the Remaining Gaps

The current package repeatedly identifies exact direct property, revenue, stores, transport, fortresses, garrisons, equipment, horses, local entities, routes, forces, recruitment, supply, population, economy, and infrastructure as unresolved. These unknowns block material playability and Opening State, even though they do not invalidate the static political core.

The current authority suite also states that title, dynasty, household, patronage, obligation, route labels, and political standing do not create ownership, revenue, transport, units, recruitment pools, garrisons, or military capacity. Therefore those material systems require explicit owners rather than continued interface-only treatment.

## 7. Why the Territory and Access Authority Is Necessary

The package cannot explain residence, communication, service, jurisdiction, supply, military response, commerce, or institutional relationships without named places and routes. Current files identify <Place>Lüneburg</Place>, settlements, markets, mints, ports or landings, fortresses, crossings, river nodes, local communities, resource sites, frontier sites, Elbe routes, North Sea and Baltic interfaces, and overland routes as separate owner fields.

This authority must remain a geographic and local-entity contract. It must not claim all northeastern Saxony, establish fixed opening borders, create a capital, or turn route lines into transport capacity.

## 10. Why the Economy and Property Authority Is Necessary

The current Dynasty and Integrated Core authorities intentionally stop at property claims and material interfaces. They do not own an itemized property portfolio, collected revenue, treasury, stores, transport assets, provisioning, or recovery state. The Household authority also explicitly refuses to create a property portfolio or treasury.

A single material-capacity owner is therefore required to prevent title, dynasty, household, law, patronage, or settlement files from independently inventing resources.

## 13. Why the Military Authority Is Necessary

The current package contains military interfaces but no military owner. It identifies possible household, property or tenure, ducal, comital, ecclesiastical, local-defense, frontier, negotiated, and paid service sources while creating zero opening units, recruitment pools, troop numbers, wars, garrisons, equipment stocks, or force records.

That deliberate restraint now creates a real specialist gap. A military authority is needed to preserve the finite recruitment-pool philosophy and to keep obligation, political support, household protection, or marriage from becoming free units.

## 16. Why Population Is Conditional Rather Than Immediate

Population, communities, labor, and local institutions are plainly relevant, but the present package does not yet have a reconciled physical settlement field, property economy, or military recruitment owner. Creating population first would encourage abstract counts detached from local places, property, food, labor, service, and recruitment.

The correct order is to define the physical, economic, and military dependency contracts first, then admit a population authority only if those files expose capability that cannot be represented through owner interfaces.
