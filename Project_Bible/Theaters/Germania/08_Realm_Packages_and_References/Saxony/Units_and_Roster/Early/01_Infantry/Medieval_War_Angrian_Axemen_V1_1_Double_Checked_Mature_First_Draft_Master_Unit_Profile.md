### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Angrian Axemen are Saxony's early regional offensive infantry: shield-bearing axe fighters who follow the territorial line, identify one compressed or disordered enemy section, split that local resistance through short violent close combat, and secure the gained ground before pursuit destroys their order.**

Their signature is **Follow, Find, Split, Secure**.

They are not berserkers, Danish-axe housecarls, armored retainers, woodcutters handed weapons, or a universal answer to heavy infantry. Their axes give ordinary Angrian fighting men a more aggressive close-combat purchase than the spear line, but the unit reaches that purchase only when another part of the Saxon host creates the opening. Ordered spears fix the enemy, bowmen expose or disorder a section, terrain constrains movement, and the axemen commit at short distance. If they lead the assault across open ground, remain in prolonged frontal combat, or chase a broken target beyond support, the formation loses its decisive advantage.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Angrian Axemen
- **Class:** Early Saxon regional offensive infantry
- **Signature:** Follow, Find, Split, Secure
- **Battle promise:** Remain behind or beside the main line, commit against one disordered or compressed section, win a short close fight through axe pressure, and stop on the objective rather than pursuing.
- **Best use:** Behind Engern Spearmen, beside a threatened shoulder, against troops worked by Engern Woodland Bowmen, in villages and field boundaries, at road junctions, or in a controlled local counterattack.
- **Strong against:** Disordered ordinary infantry, weak shields, light troops caught in close combat, tired spear formations from the side, and enemies compressed by terrain or friendly frontage.
- **Conditional against:** Peer shielded infantry, militia spears, and medium infantry when angle, fatigue, support, formation depth, and commitment distance favor the axemen.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry, fresh elite infantry, concentrated missiles, long unsupported advances, disciplined spear fronts from the front, and extended pursuit.
- **Campaign bargain:** Finite Angrian fighters, practical axes, shields, leaders, food, service legitimacy, and local recovery capacity are concentrated into a short-range decision body.
- **Player instruction:** Do not begin with the axemen. Create the opening, send them through it, and halt once the local objective is secure.
- **Flavor:** **The spear line gives Saxony time. The Angrian Axemen decide what Saxony does with it.**

### 3. Player-Facing Description

Angrian Axemen represent the part of the territorial host selected or equipped for close offensive work. The unit does not assume that every contributor carries a specialized battle axe or that an attested Engern axe corps existed. It represents a regional formation in which enough men possess suitable axes, shields, physical confidence, leaders, and shared practice to operate as more than a random mixed levy.

The formation is deliberately dependent on the army around it. Engern Spearmen establish a front that the enemy must respect. Engern Woodland Bowmen force shields to turn, movement to slow, or light troops to yield. The axemen then follow protected ground, find one local weakness, and commit. Their success is measured by whether the hostile section gives way and the gained position becomes usable, not by how far fleeing enemies are chased.

#### 8.3 Emergency Axe Levy

An emergency axe levy, if admitted, should own rapid local assembly, weak field order, improvised equipment, and poor campaign endurance. Angrian Axemen own formed close-order commitment, recognized leadership, shields, and a credible field role.

#### 8.4 Saxon Sword or Spear Retainers

Retainers should own maintained service, superior equipment, command reliability, and longer crisis endurance. Angrian Axemen own broader regional sourcing, greater affordability, and narrower offensive responsibility.

#### 8.5 Household and Ducal Gefolge

Household and ducal troops own selected people, protected command duties, elite cohesion, and decisive crisis responsibility. Angrian Axemen cannot inherit elite morale or equipment merely by serving nearby.

#### 8.6 Later Armored Axemen or Ministerial Infantry

Any later armored axe family must own improved armor, maintained providers, stronger assault endurance, and different campaign cost. Angrian Axemen persist as a cheaper, more available regional decision arm.

### 9. Core Battle Loop: Follow, Find, Split, Secure

#### 9.1 Follow

Remain behind or beside the main line while spears, missiles, terrain, and command reveal the point of commitment.

#### 9.2 Find

Identify one local target whose disorder, exposure, fatigue, angle, compression, or weakened support makes a short assault plausible.

#### 9.3 Split

Commit across a short protected distance. Use shields, axes, depth, and leaders to force a local break, relief, or withdrawal.

#### 9.4 Secure

Stop on the objective, restore facing, close the gained frontage, protect the neighboring line, and prepare either relief or withdrawal.

#### 9.5 Rejoin or Release

If the formation remains coherent, it can return to reserve or reinforce another nearby sector. If not, it holds the secured ground or leaves active contact.

#### Recruit when

- the army needs a real early offensive infantry arm;
- Engern Spearmen or terrain can provide approach protection;
- Engern Woodland Bowmen or another source can create disorder;
- enemy ordinary infantry must be relieved, split, or pushed from a bounded objective;
- retainers should be preserved for a harder crisis;
- regional people, axes, shields, leaders, and service capacity are healthy.

#### Do not recruit when

- the army needs heavy cavalry defense;
- selected armored infantry must be defeated frontally;
- concentrated missiles dominate the approach;
- the regional source is already depleted or politically strained;
- professional retainers or later assault infantry are required for sustained close combat;
- the only plan is extended pursuit.

### 43. Failure Stories

**The open-field charge:** the player sees “Axemen” and sends the unit ahead of the spear line. Arrows and fresh spears strip Assault Order before contact.

**The false shield breaker:** the player attacks selected armored infantry frontally, expecting axes to ignore protection. The Angrian formation stalls, tires, and loses regional leaders.

**The victorious pursuit:** a disordered militia line gives way. The axemen chase beyond standards and support, where cavalry or fresh infantry destroys the elongated body.

**The crowded opening:** two axe units and a retainer formation compete for the same gap. Friendly congestion removes the short local pressure that justified the attack.

**The hollow provider:** population remains, but suitable axes, shields, captains, close-order practice, craft capacity, and political service have been exhausted.

### 60. Final Double-Checked First-Draft Verdict

**Angrian Axemen pass as Saxony's third double-checked mature unit candidate and first dedicated offensive infantry body.**

They give the early faction an actual decision arm without importing berserkers, housecarls, or inflated armor-piercing axe fantasy. Their strength lies in relationship and timing. Engern Spearmen establish the front. Engern Woodland Bowmen create movement and exposure. Angrian Axemen follow that line, identify one compromised section, commit across a short protected distance, and secure the result.

Their campaign identity is equally important. The formation consumes real close-combat fighters, recognized leaders, workable axes, shields, food, political service, craft capacity, and local defense. A region may possess population and tool axes while lacking the military people, equipment, leadership, and collective practice needed for a battleready unit.

The unit's hard ceiling remains clear. Fresh spears, elite armored infantry, cavalry, concentrated missiles, long approaches, stalled melee, and pursuit can all defeat the card. Later retainers, household troops, ministerials, and armored assault formations retain meaningful space.
