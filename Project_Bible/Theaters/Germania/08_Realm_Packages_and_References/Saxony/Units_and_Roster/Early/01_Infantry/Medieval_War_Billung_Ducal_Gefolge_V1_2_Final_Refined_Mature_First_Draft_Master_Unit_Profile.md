### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Billung Ducal Gefolge are the selected direct following of Saxony's ruling ducal house: trusted and exceptionally well-supported infantry who attend the duke or the ducal standard, anchor the command sector, decide one critical local contest, and preserve the political and military center of the army when ordinary formations can no longer bear the responsibility.**

Their signature is **Attend the Duke, Anchor, Decide, Preserve**.

They are not every Saxon household retainer gathered under a fashionable name. They are not an unlimited royal guard, a continental housecarl clone, or a late professional regiment. The family represents the narrow direct-ducal layer that becomes meaningful only after territorial, hall, and plural household forces have already been separated. Their strength is concentration of trust, officers, equipment, communication, and responsibility. Their weakness is scarcity: every death, captured standard, broken officer relationship, or lost equipment set damages the duke's ability to command and embody Saxon authority.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Billung Ducal Gefolge
- **Class:** Direct Saxon ducal-following infantry
- **Signature:** Attend the Duke, Anchor, Decide, Preserve
- **Battle promise:** Hold the ducal command sector, reverse one decisive local crisis, and preserve the army's political center without being spent as routine frontage.
- **Best use:** Ducal standard and command sector, army junction, critical counterattack, gate or camp reserve, high-value withdrawal, and the single fight whose loss would unravel the wider host.
- **Strong against:** Ordinary infantry, worked retainers, dangerous breakthroughs, peer household troops lacking fresh support, and light or medium cavalry caught against a prepared front.
- **Conditional against:** Elite household infantry, selected armored foot, and medium-to-heavy cavalry when preparation, depth, support, and terrain favor the Gefolge.
- **Weak against:** Concentrated crossbows, clean heavy-cavalry shock, envelopment, several simultaneous crises, prolonged unsupported elite combat, and catastrophic officer or standard loss.
- **Campaign bargain:** The duke concentrates a tiny direct cadre of trusted followers, officers, standards, best available opening equipment, maintenance, rewards, political capital, and household security into one signature formation.
- **Player instruction:** Keep the Gefolge near the command responsibility until the decisive crisis is clear. Commit once, restore the center, and preserve the cadre.
- **Flavor:** **Other Saxons answer the host. The Gefolge answer the duke, and the army watches what happens where the ducal standard stands.**

### 7. Core Battle Loop: Attend the Duke, Anchor, Decide, Preserve

#### 7.1 Attend the Duke

Remain near the ducal command, standard, designated command deputy, or the sector explicitly carrying the duke's strategic responsibility.

#### 7.2 Anchor

Set a compact, reliable position that gives surrounding Saxon formations a physical command reference without granting a passive aura.

#### 7.3 Decide

Commit against one crisis whose outcome determines the command sector, army junction, withdrawal, or political center.

#### 7.4 Preserve

Stop after the decision, restore the standard and command relationship, recover wounded and equipment where possible, and prevent success from becoming destructive pursuit.

#### Billung Ducal Gefolge

_Direct Saxon ducal-following infantry_

The Billung Ducal Gefolge are selected followers maintained close to Saxony's ruling house. They guard the ducal command sector, anchor the center of a mixed Saxon host, and intervene when a single local crisis threatens the army's political and military coherence. Better equipped and more tightly commanded than other opening infantry, they remain exceptionally scarce. Their destruction costs more than battlefield strength: it damages the duke's officers, standard, security, and authority.
