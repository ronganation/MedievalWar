### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Saxon Hall Spearmen are the dependable maintained infantry furnished by the halls, households, estates, and comital followings of Saxony's greater and lesser lords: selected spear-and-shield retainers able to attend a threatened sector, steady a failing territorial line, relieve exhausted troops, and restore a usable front without claiming the elite status of the duke's own following.**

Their signature is **Attend, Steady, Relieve, Restore**.

They are the roster's first true reliability upgrade. Engern Spearmen provide width and ordinary holding power. Hall Spearmen provide a smaller body of better-equipped, more consistently led fighters who can arrive at the important point and remain functional after ordinary territorial troops begin to fray. They are not invulnerable heavy infantry, a universal bodyguard, or a renamed Billung guard. Their provider is plural: several halls and lordly relationships can contribute companies, but each company remains tied to real people, equipment, obligations, loyalties, and political access.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Saxon Hall Spearmen
- **Class:** Maintained lordly retainer spear infantry
- **Signature:** Attend, Steady, Relieve, Restore
- **Battle promise:** Move to the sector that matters, absorb pressure more reliably than territorial troops, conduct a real relief, and leave behind a restored Saxon front.
- **Best use:** Behind Engern Spearmen, at army junctions, protecting bowmen or command positions, relieving a worked unit, holding roads and gates, and receiving a medium cavalry threat when properly set.
- **Strong against:** Ordinary infantry, light cavalry, tired territorial troops, disordered attackers, and pressure concentrated against a controlled frontage.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry, peer retainers, aggressive axe or sword infantry, and selected armored troops when support, facing, depth, equipment, and relief favor the Hall Spearmen.
- **Weak against:** Elite household infantry, heavy cavalry with a clean approach, prolonged concentrated missiles, envelopment, several simultaneous crises, and campaign losses that remove veteran files and leaders.
- **Campaign bargain:** Finite retainers, hall officers, mail and helmets, strong shields, suitable spears, food, pay or maintenance, political loyalty, and replacement relationships are concentrated into a reliable middle-tier company.
- **Player instruction:** Do not waste Hall Spearmen as expensive frontage. Keep them close enough to answer a crisis, relieve the failing line, and preserve the army's shape.
- **Flavor:** **The levy gives Saxony breadth. The halls provide the men expected to stand where breadth is no longer enough.**

### 3. Player-Facing Description

Saxon Hall Spearmen are drawn from the maintained fighting households and followings attached to Saxon lords, counts, estates, and halls. “Hall” names the social and military environment that sustains the formation. It does not claim one standardized hall institution across all Saxony, nor does it turn every lordly follower into a permanent professional soldier.

Compared with the territorial host, these men assemble more consistently, carry a greater concentration of serviceable helmets and mail, recognize a tighter command structure, and possess stronger expectations of extended military service. Their battlefield value lies in reliability. They attend the place where the host is bending, steady the contact, transfer responsibility from a worked formation, and restore a usable line. Their victory is often measured by what does not happen: the center does not split, the bowmen escape, the count's standard remains protected, or the axemen gain time to withdraw.

### 4. Why the Card Exists

The first four units create a strong territorial army, but none should become the answer to every hard contact. Saxony needs a middle infantry layer before household guards, the Billung ducal following, and later ministerial or castle institutions enter the roster.

#### 8.2 Angrian Axemen

Angrian Axemen own short local offensive decision. Hall Spearmen own reliable reinforcement, receiving, and relief. Hall Spearmen may counterpressure after stabilizing a sector, but they do not match the axemen's targeted assault purchase.

#### 8.3 Saxon Household Retainers

A future household-retainer family must own still narrower selection, superior cohesion, stronger close combat, protected responsibilities, and smaller pools. Hall Spearmen are not the highest household tier.

#### 8.4 Billung Ducal Gefolge

The Billung Ducal Gefolge must own direct ducal attachment, command-sector presence, political symbolism, best equipment concentration, and exceptional scarcity. Hall Spearmen can be coordinated by the duke without becoming the duke's personal following.

#### 8.5 Later Ministerial and Castle Infantry

Later ministerial or castle infantry must own institutional maintenance, fortified providers, stronger year-round continuity, and different social paths. Hall Spearmen do not automatically convert into them.

### 9. Core Battle Loop: Attend, Steady, Relieve, Restore

#### 9.1 Attend

Remain within supporting distance of the center, command position, missile shoulder, or threatened junction rather than occupying maximum frontage from deployment.

#### 9.2 Steady

Enter a bending but still recoverable sector, establish facing and depth, and prevent local disorder from becoming a general break.

#### 9.3 Relieve

Transfer pressure from a worked friendly formation through a real passage, overlap, controlled withdrawal, or replacement of frontage.

#### 9.4 Restore

Close the resulting gap, reconnect standards and shoulders, and return the sector to a useful defensive state.

#### 9.5 Hold or Re-form Reserve

Remain if the sector is still critical. If relief and replacements permit, return behind the line as a smaller reserve.

### 35. Failure Stories

**The expensive main line:** the player deploys every Hall Spearman in the front rank. When the Engern center bends, no reliable reserve remains to relieve it.

**The closed relief lane:** the Hall Spearmen arrive, but frightened friendly troops and wagons block the passage. Better soldiers cannot solve impossible geometry.

**The divided halls:** Saxony possesses enough retainers, mail, and officers, but rival lords refuse one ducal campaign. Physical capacity exists while the usable pool remains divided.

**The false ducal guard:** inflated equipment and morale allow Hall Spearmen to perform the Billung following's symbolic and elite crisis role. The social ladder collapses.

**The dead captain:** headcount remains high, but loss of the hall captain and file leaders destroys the timing required for relief and restoration.

### 50. Final Double-Checked First-Draft Verdict

**Saxon Hall Spearmen pass as Saxony's fifth double-checked mature unit candidate and first maintained middle-infantry formation.**

They are the correct next step because the first four units already establish territorial width, missile support, local offensive action, and mobility. The roster now gains a formation whose purchase is reliability: selected lordly retainers attend a crisis, steady a bending sector, conduct a real relief, and restore the line.

The name carries more flavor than “Saxon Spear Retainers” while preserving that original brainstorm role as the controlled fallback. It also expands Saxony's identity beyond Angria alone. The unit is sourced from plural Saxon halls, households, estates, and comital followings, making military access dependent on population, equipment, maintenance, loyalty, and political cooperation.

Its ceiling remains firm. Hall Spearmen do not replace Engern frontage, Angrian offense, ducal symbolism, household elites, or later ministerial institutions. Their equipment is better but not universally heavy; their morale is reliable but not unbreakable; their availability is wider than a ducal guard but politically narrower than the territorial host.
