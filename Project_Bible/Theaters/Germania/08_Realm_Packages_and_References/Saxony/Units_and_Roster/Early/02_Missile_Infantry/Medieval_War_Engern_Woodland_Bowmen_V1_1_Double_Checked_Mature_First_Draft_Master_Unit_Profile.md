### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Engern Woodland Bowmen are Saxony's early regional bow support: locally sourced archers who use workable ground, controlled shooting lanes, and neighboring spear frontage to screen an approach, mark a threat, work one exposed formation, and withdraw before the enemy turns missile pressure into close combat.**

Their signature is **Screen, Mark, Work, Withdraw**.

They are not English-style mass longbowmen, elite foresters, invisible woodland snipers, or a substitute for every later Saxon missile family. Their strength is useful, repeatable support from a finite regional source. They make an early Saxon line harder to approach cleanly, punish exposed light troops and disordered bodies, and force hostile cavalry to respect lanes and cover. They fail when ordered to win a shooting duel against superior missile troops, stand in melee, fire through friendly formations, or remain after the withdrawal route closes.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Engern Woodland Bowmen
- **Class:** Early Saxon regional bow infantry
- **Signature:** Screen, Mark, Work, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Occupy a useful shoulder or broken-ground lane, identify one exposed target, maintain disciplined shooting long enough to shape the approach, and leave before interception.
- **Best use:** Behind or beside Engern Spearmen, forest edges, field boundaries, village approaches, roads, low ridges, camp sectors, and lanes where a retreat path remains open.
- **Strong against:** Unshielded light infantry, exposed skirmishers, disordered formations, stalled cavalry, crews, and troops compressed by terrain or friendly infantry.

### 3. Player-Facing Description

Engern Woodland Bowmen come from regional communities in which bows, bow materials, hunting experience, local route knowledge, and enough collective practice can be assembled for war. The card does not claim that every hunter becomes a battlefield archer or that Engern maintains a standardized corps. It represents the smaller set of people, tools, leaders, and service relationships capable of producing a coherent missile formation.

The unit works through placement and judgment. Bowmen screen the Saxon host before contact, mark the most dangerous approach, and concentrate limited ammunition on a target whose exposure makes shooting worthwhile. Their arrows are most useful when terrain, spear frontage, congestion, or enemy disorder reduce the target's freedom to evade or close. The unit's last responsibility is withdrawal. If the player allows cavalry, heavy infantry, or a stronger missile body to close the route, the formation's value collapses quickly.

### 4. Why the Card Exists

### 9. Core Battle Loop: Screen, Mark, Work, Withdraw

#### 9.1 Screen

Move into a sector where the bowmen can observe and threaten an approach without sacrificing the withdrawal lane.

#### 9.2 Mark

Select one target whose angle, exposure, speed, armor, congestion, or tactical importance justifies ammunition.

#### 9.3 Work

Maintain controlled shooting long enough to inflict casualties, disorder movement, force shields to face, deny a comfortable approach, or create an opening for Saxon infantry and cavalry.

#### 9.4 Withdraw

Leave before the target closes, cavalry turns the shoulder, superior missiles establish dominance, or ammunition depletion converts the unit into weak infantry.

#### 9.5 Reposition or Release

The unit may occupy another lane if routes, ammunition, leadership, and support permit. Otherwise the bowmen screen the rear, guard equipment, or leave active combat.

### 45. Failure Stories

**The false foresters:** the player assumes woodland origin grants invisibility and sends the bowmen deep ahead. Cavalry closes the rear route and destroys a missile unit that was never designed as scouts.

**The proud duel:** the bowmen exchange arrows with maintained professionals on open ground. Engern ammunition, leaders, and bow users are consumed without shaping the battle.

**The empty quivers:** repeated low-value shooting leaves no arrows for the exposed cavalry and disordered infantry that the unit was meant to punish.

**The abandoned shoulder:** Engern Spearmen advance without coordinating passage or protection. The bowmen continue shooting until enemy light horse reaches the open side.

**The hollow provider:** the settlement still has population, but bowyers, strings, arrowheads, experienced leaders, and serviceable bows are depleted. Coin cannot produce a field-ready card.

### 61. Final Double-Checked First-Draft Verdict

**Engern Woodland Bowmen pass as Saxony's second double-checked mature unit candidate.**

They provide a real early missile arm without borrowing England's longbow identity or turning local bow use into fantasy forest warfare. Their strength is controlled support: screen the host, mark one exposed target, work it long enough to shape the approach, and withdraw before interception. Their campaign price is paid in finite bow users, leaders, bowyers, strings, arrows, materials, local defense, and service endurance.

The card strengthens Engern Spearmen without existing merely as an accessory. It can support roads, settlements, camps, field boundaries, and broken-ground lanes across a mixed Saxon army. It also retains hard ceilings: weak melee, vulnerable withdrawal, modest armor pressure, no magical concealment, no infinite ammunition, and no automatic evolution into professional archers or crossbowmen.
