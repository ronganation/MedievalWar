### 1. Executive Unit Identity

Saxon Levy Crossbowmen are an Early-Era, persistent-and-developable missile formation assembled from limited local musters, lesser strongholds, lordly stores, settlement defenses, and people capable of using early crossbows under basic officers. They provide meaningful bolt pressure before Saxony develops the professional fortified-provider system represented by Castle Crossbowmen.

Their identity is **limited local crossbow access**, not mass peasant weapon distribution. Crossbows are physical, costly, varied, and finite. Shooters require instruction; bolts and strings require compatibility; loading demands time and protection. The company is useful in settlement defense, crossings, roads, prepared field positions, and secondary armies, but less reliable, less durable, and less campaign-ready than Castle Crossbowmen.

Their signature is **Prepare, Loose, Reposition, Conserve**. The unit prepares a simple lane, releases a controlled volley or limited sequence, repositions before enemy pressure collapses its weak protection, and conserves specialist equipment and bolts for continued local service.

### 13. Core Battle Loop

#### Prepare

Choose a simple protected lane, sort compatible weapons and bolts, assign shooters, and ready spanning methods.

#### Loose

Release a controlled volley or limited sequence against a worthwhile target. Mixed weapons prevent perfect synchronization.

#### Reposition

Move before cavalry, missiles, or infantry collapse the position. Repositioning interrupts loading and can fragment the muster.

#### Conserve

Preserve bolts, strings, crossbows, tools, and shooters for later defense and future battles.

```yaml
success: Useful local bolt threat without losing the scarce packet
failure: Exposed loading, ammunition waste, melee interception, or abandoned equipment
```

### Appendix A. Provider Scenarios

#### Lesser Stronghold

A minor fortification owns several serviceable crossbows, compatible bolts, spare strings, tools, and a small group of trained shooters. The packet can support one local company but not a professional campaign formation.

#### Settlement Defense

A town or defended settlement retains crossbows for gates and walls. Distant field service risks weakening local defense and can trigger recall pressure.

#### Mixed Lordly Stores

Several small providers combine weapons and shooters. Compatibility problems reduce the effective files until bolts, strings, and tools are sorted.

#### Conquered Castle

Physical crossbows may survive conquest while loyal shooters, officers, store records, and local obligations do not transfer automatically.

#### Mature Provider

A levy packet develops workshops, officers, compatibility, and recurring service. Selected capacity may later support Castle Crossbowmen, while local levy crossbows remain for secondary defense.

### Appendix B. Battle Scenarios

#### Bridge Defense

Crossbowmen prepare behind Engern Spearmen and release bolts against compact attackers. Withdrawal becomes difficult if the spear line fails.

#### Open-Field Failure

The unit advances without cover, receives bow fire, and is caught while loading. Mixed equipment and weak officers prevent an orderly response.

#### Wall Defense

Physical cover protects loading and shallow ammunition provides meaningful local threat. The unit performs above its open-field value.

#### Cavalry Interception

Light cavalry reaches the flank during repositioning. The unit suffers heavily because it lacks shields, melee discipline, and loading space.

#### High-Era Secondary Army

Levy Crossbowmen support a lesser army while professional Castle Crossbowmen remain concentrated with the principal host.

Mixed crossbows can create asynchronous readiness. A Controlled Volley should assemble enough ready files for useful concentration without pretending that every weapon shares one spanning time. Opportunistic Fire lets files release as ready but reduces concentration and target discipline.

### Appendix W. V1.1 Siege, Settlement, and Open-Field Doctrine

#### Siege and Settlement Defense

Walls, gates, towers, bridges, and earthworks can protect the vulnerable loading cycle. Local bolt stores and short transport routes strengthen readiness. The unit performs above its open-field value because the physical position supports the weapon system, not because the roster card receives an abstract siege bonus.

#### Siege Attack

The unit can support approaches from protected positions but lacks the continuity, equipment compatibility, and officers of Castle Crossbowmen. It should not be the preferred assault-shooting family when professional packets are available.

#### Open Field

The unit needs spear frontage, cover, a safe rear route, and worthwhile targets. Movement during spanning or aiming disrupts the cycle. Open wings and rapid maneuver expose the formation to light cavalry, missile suppression, and infantry pressure.

```yaml
field_failure_signs:
  - Rear route closes
  - Friendly frontage collapses
  - Bolts are wasted on dispersed targets
  - Loading files are exposed
  - Mixed weapons prevent useful concentration
  - Officer control fails during repositioning
```
