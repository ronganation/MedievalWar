### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Billung Household Riders are the direct mounted followers of Saxony's ruling house: selected riders with better horses, equipment, officers, and trust than the Hall Riders, assigned to shield ducal movement, defeat a dangerous mobile threat, strike one exposed battlefield responsibility, and recover before the mounted cadre is spent.**

Their signature is **Attend, Shield, Strike, Recover**.

They are not Ritter, noble cavalry gathered indiscriminately, household knights under another label, or a mounted version of the infantry Gefolge. Their value lies in direct-ducal reliability under movement. They protect the duke, the standard, messengers, treasury, withdrawal, and open command routes. When committed offensively, they strike one vulnerable mounted, missile, or compromised infantry target and return before a harder cavalry body can trap them.

### 2B. Player-Facing Description

Billung Household Riders are drawn from the narrow mounted service surrounding Saxony's ruling house. The formation does not represent every mounted servant, every horse attached to a ducal estate, or a hidden body of early knights. It represents the smaller packet in which a trusted rider, a suitable and conditioned horse, compatible tack, maintained weapons and protection, a recognized officer structure, and direct ducal responsibility have all converged.

The company exists to make ducal movement credible. It can protect a standard or commander crossing dangerous open ground, contest enemy light horse that threatens messengers or withdrawal, strike an exposed missile body, or counterattack a mounted opponent already committed against the Saxon host. The company succeeds when the command relationship survives and the riders return in enough order to answer again.

### 6. Core Battle Loop: Attend, Shield, Strike, Recover

#### 6.1 Attend
Remain near the duke, recognized deputy, ducal standard route, or detached command responsibility.

#### 6.2 Shield
Control the open space needed for command movement, messenger passage, withdrawal, or reserve action. Shielding is physical position and threat, not an aura.

#### 6.3 Strike
Commit against one suitable mounted, missile, crew, or exposed infantry target whose removal protects the command relationship or decides a critical local movement.

#### 6.4 Recover
Break contact, rally, check horses and officers, restore the route, and preserve the company for another command responsibility.

### 20D. Failure Stories

**The expensive patrol:** the company performs ordinary reconnaissance and message work that Angrian horsemen could handle. When the ducal route is threatened, the selected mounts are tired and the reserve is absent.

**The cheap knights:** good horses, mail, and direct ducal colors tempt a frontal charge into prepared infantry or mature knights. The company loses the exact rider-horse-officer packet that made it exceptional.

**The duplicated household:** the Gefolge, personal guard, messenger cadre, and Household Riders all claim the same officers, trusted followers, mail, and maintenance. Saxony fields paper strength that the household cannot physically support.

**The closed recovery lane:** a successful countercharge becomes prolonged melee because friendly congestion and fresh enemy cavalry block the return. Direct-ducal confidence cannot substitute for physical escape.

**The paper remount:** treasury spending restores nominal horse numbers, but unsuitable, unconditioned, or mismatched animals do not restore mounted readiness.

**The succession shortcut:** riders and horses are assumed to transfer automatically to a successor. Direct trust, standards, officers, assignments, rewards, and command relationships remain unsettled, leaving physical capacity without a field-ready company.
