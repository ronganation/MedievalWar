### 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**Saxon Castle Crossbowmen are fortified-provider missile infantry maintained by validated castles, towns, ducal, comital, ecclesiastical, and lordly establishments: trained to prepare deliberate volleys, loose heavy bolts into selected armored or high-value targets, cover the long reset behind shields, terrain, walls, or friendly troops, and repeat without pretending to match the continuous shooting or woodland movement of Engern Bowmen.**

Their signature is **Prepare, Loose, Cover, Reset**.

The name identifies the strongest ordinary provider environment, not an automatic castle-building recruit. Some companies may emerge from towns, ecclesiastical establishments, ducal arsenals, comital households, or fortified lordly providers where the complete packet exists. The battlefield card remains one canonical Saxon crossbow family. Provider records carry local flavor and restrictions.

### 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Saxon Castle Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Developed fortified-provider crossbow infantry
- **Signature:** Prepare, Loose, Cover, Reset
- **Battle promise:** Establish a protected shooting position, loose deliberate bolts into selected armored or high-value targets, survive the exposed spanning and loading interval, and rebuild the firing sequence before enemy pressure reaches the line.
- **Best use:** Walls, gates, roads, breaches, settlement lanes, prepared field positions, behind Hall Spearmen or Household Companions, firing at armored cavalry, household infantry, officers, crews, and compact targets.
- **Strong against:** Armored or shielded targets lacking cover, cavalry and infantry moving through predictable lanes, exposed officers and crews, and enemies held in place by Saxon infantry.
- **Conditional against:** Peer missiles, moving cavalry, loose infantry, and targets at long range when visibility, bolt supply, weapon condition, elevation, and fire control favor the company.
- **Weak against:** Fast flanking cavalry, dispersed skirmishers, woodland fighting, sustained bow harassment, bad visibility, wet or damaged strings, depleted bolts, disrupted loading, and close combat.
- **Campaign bargain:** Finite trained shooters, officers, crossbows, spanning devices, strings, stocks, prods, bolts, quivers, shields or cover equipment where supported, craftsmen, workshops, stores, transport, maintenance, and provider access are concentrated into a specialist missile company.
- **Player instruction:** Do not advance Castle Crossbowmen as bow skirmishers. Prepare the lane, loose at the target worth the bolt, protect the reset, and move only when another position is genuinely better.
- **Flavor:** **Engern Bowmen shape the field through movement and volume. Castle Crossbowmen make a protected lane dangerous to armor.**

### 3. Player-Facing Description

Saxon Castle Crossbowmen represent the developed missile capacity of fortified and institutionally supported providers. Their crossbows, bolts, strings, spanning arrangements, tools, workshops, officers, and trained crews require more concentrated support than the regional bows carried by Engern Woodland Bowmen. The family therefore appears later and through narrower providers, even though individual crossbows or small local bodies may exist earlier where evidence permits.

In battle, the company creates deliberate danger rather than constant arrow volume. Crossbowmen choose a lane and target, prepare weapons and bolt stocks, loose under officer control, then become vulnerable while spanning and loading. Walls, earthworks, terrain, shields, friendly spear lines, and disciplined spacing turn that reset interval from a fatal weakness into a repeatable battlefield rhythm.

### 9. Core Battle Loop: Prepare, Loose, Cover, Reset

#### 9.1 Prepare
Select the shooting lane and target class, establish spacing, distribute bolts, inspect strings and spanning devices, and place available shields or terrain cover.

#### 9.2 Loose
Release one controlled shot or volley against a suitable high-value, armored, compact, or lane-bound target.

#### 9.3 Cover
Protect the exposed spanning and loading interval through walls, earthworks, shields, terrain, friendly infantry, alternating files, distance, or target suppression.

#### 9.4 Reset
Complete spanning and loading, replace damaged strings or weapons where the battle permits, restore target control, and prepare the next deliberate shot.

### 35. Failure Stories

**The castle-made company:** a building unlock creates shooters, weapons, bolts, strings, officers, and craftsmen without physical inputs. The roster gains paper strength rather than a functioning crossbow formation.

**The bowman imitation:** the player advances Castle Crossbowmen through open ground and expects continuous shooting. Bow harassment and cavalry reach the unit during loading.

**The empty bolt cart:** shooters and weapons survive, but compatible ammunition is gone. The company remains on the roster without its battlefield purchase.

**The universal pavise:** art and balance assume every provider has large shields, transport, setup space, and trained shield handling. Provider variation and mobility costs disappear.

**The automatic armor killer:** every hit penetrates every shield and armor class. Crossbows erase equipment, range, angle, target, and era distinctions.

**The workshop collapse:** field casualties seem manageable, but lost craftsmen, strings, tools, stocks, prods, and repair capacity prevent recovery.

**The exposed reset:** the first volley succeeds, but infantry protection moves away. Cavalry reaches the loading files before another shot is ready.

**The obsolete bowmen:** crossbows receive bow-like movement and rate while retaining higher impact. Engern Woodland Bowmen lose every reason to exist.
