## 2. Package Identity and Current Design Spine

Swabia is organized as a working political project rather than a uniformly owned territorial state. Its developed spine combines an appointed ducal office, Rheinfelden continuity, negotiated regional and institutional plurality, Royal and Burgundian interfaces, differentiated Rheinfelden, Zahringen, and Hohenstaufen development, finite military and material capability, and recoverable transformation after office or house change. Canonical Realm identity remains deferred.
