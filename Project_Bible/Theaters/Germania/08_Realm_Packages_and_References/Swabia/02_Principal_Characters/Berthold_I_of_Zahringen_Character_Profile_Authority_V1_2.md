## 9. Opening Character Identity

### Berthold begins as

- a supported living mature Swabian principal at orientation scope;
- the principal candidate for the newly admitted Zahringen house lane;
- an influential comital actor with remembered expectation of the Swabian duchy;
- the supported titleholder of Carinthia and Verona from 1061 at historical-orientation scope;
- a cross-regional political actor whose titles create obligations and opportunities rather than free owner state;
- a conditional commander, negotiator, house builder, patron, and succession anchor;
- a player-perspective candidate distinct from the holder of the Swabian ducal office.

### Berthold does not begin as

- duke of Swabia;
- permanent enemy of Rudolf of Rheinfelden;
- ruler of all Carinthia or Verona;
- owner of every later Zahringen, Baden, Breisgau, Thurgau, Ortenau, Baar, or Black Forest holding;
- founder of every later Zahringen institution, castle, monastery, settlement, or city;
- commander of a free external or Swabian army;
- holder of guaranteed family, succession, or branch outcomes;
- protected from loss, deposition, failure, imprisonment, incapacity, or early death.
