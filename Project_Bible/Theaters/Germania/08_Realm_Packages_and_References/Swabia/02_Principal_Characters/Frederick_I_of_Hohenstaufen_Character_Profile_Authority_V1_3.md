- protect Frederick from premature office, command, marriage, or independent service;
- preserve the unresolved parentage and adult-household-authority questions;
- involve Frederick in age-appropriate observation or attendance where supported;
- prepare for future adult house participation without naming Frederick the opening principal;
- preserve alternative futures where Frederick never becomes duke or major public actor.
