## 8. Character Identity in Play

<Person>Frederick of Buren</Person> should be experienced through evidence, memory, missing records, inherited obligations, disputed associations, and the consequences of an absent adult generation.

- whether a record belongs to the same Frederick;
- what parentage tradition the Hohenstaufen household accepts;
- whether a marriage carried relationships or property and on what terms;
- which obligations, rights, or disputes survived the character;
