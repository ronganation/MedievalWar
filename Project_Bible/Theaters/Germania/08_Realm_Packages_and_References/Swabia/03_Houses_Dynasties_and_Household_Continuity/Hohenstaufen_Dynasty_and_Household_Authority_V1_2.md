## 2. Authority Purpose

This authority owns the house-level political and continuity layer that the Frederick profile cannot own alone. It defines:

- Hohenstaufen identity, memory, membership confidence, branches, and continuity;
- household admission, access, protection, service, travel, records, workload, reserve, and replacement;
- patrimonial claims, possession, custody, administration, use, benefit, revenue, burden, damage, alienation, and inheritance;
- Royal, regional, ecclesiastical, military, and administrative service as owner-routed opportunities rather than automatic loyalty;
- marriage, dower, widowhood, children, dependency, guardianship, succession, and house survival;
- conditional development from non-ruling regional house to service house, institutional power, ducal candidate, office-holding house, or alternative regional future;
- long-horizon historical emergence, including possible later Hohenstaufen generations and the high-priority but non-guaranteed Frederick Barbarossa identity;
- player decisions, AI limits, visibility, anti-cheese protections, failure, recovery, removal, and nonoccurrence.

It does not own Swabia, the Swabian ducal office, Royal Salian government, the Holy Roman political order, Rheinfelden, Zahringen, regional actors, ecclesiastical institutions, exact Opening State, Registry data, runtime state, or Unreal implementation.

## 11. Opening Strategic Situation

The Hohenstaufen begin as an acknowledged design lane with an important underage dynast, but not with a resolved autonomous opening house principal and not as a ruling dynasty. Their greatest asset is political optionality. Their greatest weakness is that later fame can tempt the design to grant them property, relationships, institutions, public standing, or descendants that have not been established for 1062.

Opening pressures include:

- proving who belongs to the house and household;
- identifying what property or rights actually support house action;
- building service credibility without becoming an automatic Royal client;
- negotiating with Swabian regional and ecclesiastical owners;
- deciding whether to cooperate with, remain independent from, or eventually compete with the Rheinfelden;
- preserving resources while pursuing institution building or military service;
- building continuity before public-office competition;
- protecting valid alternative futures where the Hohenstaufen never gain the duchy.
