## 2. Authority Purpose

This authority owns the package-private continuity layer that neither Rudolf's person profile nor the Realm foundation may own alone. It defines:

- dynasty identity, kinship confidence, marriage memory, eligibility, and succession;
- household membership, access, protection, care, hospitality, travel, service, records, and finite resources;
- patrimonial claims, possession, custody, administration, use, benefit, revenue, burden, damage, and inheritance;
- widowhood, possible remarriage, dower, children, dependents, guardianship, and minority;
- Rudolf's absence, capture, incapacity, office loss, death, replacement, and recovery;
- player decisions, AI limits, explanation requirements, narrative hooks, and validation fixtures.

It does not own the Swabian ducal office, public government, regional actors, Burgundy, Royal Salian government, ecclesiastical institutions, exact Opening State, or implementation.

## 10. Opening Strategic Situation

The house enters 1062 with a supported principal and a recognizable High Rhine association, but without an authorized opening spouse, child, heir, household roster, property portfolio, officer set, or reserve. The scenario therefore begins with a strong political person resting on a materially and genealogically incomplete private base.

Opening pressures include:

- Rudolf's public workload across Swabia, Rheinfelden, Royal government, Burgundy, military, and ecclesiastical affairs;
- widowhood and disputed remarriage chronology;
- uncertain children, dependents, collateral kin, and succession;
- incomplete property, revenue, records, transport, hospitality, care, and protection capacity;
- risk that ducal obligations consume the private base;
- risk that private property is mistakenly treated as public office capacity;
- risk that Rudolf's removal exposes every unresolved layer simultaneously.

## 23. Household Decision Portfolio

- Admit, retain, release, relocate, protect, or refuse evidence-backed people.
- Reserve resources for dependents, officers, travel, hospitality, care, records, and emergencies.
- Choose Rudolf's travel party, home continuity, reporting chain, and household handback.
- Manage widowhood and possible remarriage without inventing an opening spouse.
- Appoint bounded administrators and messengers only after person and mandate admission.
- Balance political hospitality against security, scarcity, information risk, and disruption.
- Prepare for capture, incapacity, office loss, death, displacement, and succession.
- Keep private household capacity distinct from public ducal government.
