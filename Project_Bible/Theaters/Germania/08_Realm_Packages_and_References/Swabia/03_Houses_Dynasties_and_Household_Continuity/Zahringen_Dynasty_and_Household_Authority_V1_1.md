## 10. Opening Strategic Situation

The Zahringen enter the campaign with a mature principal and substantial political relevance, but their private and public capacities remain unresolved. Berthold's Swabian expectation is politically meaningful but does not create a current claim. Carinthian and Veronese dignities widen the house's opportunities while exposing the difference between title and executable rule.

Opening pressures include:

- verifying who belongs to the house and who is alive in 1062;
- determining which spouse, children, branches, dependents, and officers belong in the opening household;
- identifying what western Swabian property or rights can support action;
- preserving a home base while Berthold manages distant public dignities;
- deciding whether to cooperate with, compete with, or remain independent from Rheinfelden;
- defining the relationship with the younger Hohenstaufen lane;
- handling Royal compensation and service without automatic loyalty;
- building records, delegates, routes, reserve, care, and succession before Berthold's removal;
- preventing later Zahringen and Baden history from fabricating the opening.
