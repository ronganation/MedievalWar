## 1. Authority Verdict

The Swabian ducal title and office are admitted as a distinct public constitutional owner. The office is neither the Swabian faction nor the private property of the Rheinfelden, Hohenstaufen, Zahringen, Royal Salian government, or any regional lord. It can be held, contested, recognized, constrained, vacated, restored, or transformed without transferring all faction assets or erasing the private houses participating in Swabian politics.
