## 40. Player Decision Packages

### First Institutional Relationship

The player may prioritize Constance, Basel, Augsburg, Reichenau, Saint Blasien, Rheinau, Saint Gall as an external interface, or another supported institution. Choosing a portfolio does not activate an officeholder, property, jurisdiction, or guaranteed relationship.

Potential focuses include counsel, mediation, records, hospitality, education, care, safe conduct, route support, burial, memoria, or protection.

### Appointment Dispute

The player may support valid procedure, recommend or object where competent, protect participants, provide safe conduct, request external clarification, mediate, recognize temporal rights, abstain, or accept a contested result. The duke cannot appoint every bishop or abbot.

### Property and Protection Crisis

The player may protect, negotiate limited access, compensate, seek bounded emergency use, refer the dispute, suspend the request, contest a specific claim, or withdraw. Emergency use does not create ownership.

### Sanctuary and Political Refuge

The player may respect a claim, negotiate custody or safe conduct, seek mediation, contest applicability, secure a site without entry, or breach sanctuary and accept consequences.

### Records, Hospitality, and Administrative Burden

The player may fund capacity, narrow requests, compensate institutions, use multiple routes, accept delays, delegate, or withdraw. Institutions may refuse when capacity, rights, safety, or external obligations block performance.
