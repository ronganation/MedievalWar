## 2. Authority Purpose

Swabian governance answers how public work is received, routed, recorded, resourced, communicated, performed, reviewed, interrupted, recovered, and handed back. It provides the administrative bridge between the public ducal office and separately owned people, houses, institutions, regions, communities, law, forces, property, money, routes, information, and political recognition.

It defines:

- an expandable administrative topology rather than a presumed bureaucracy;
- responsibility portfolios and dependency chains;
- councils, assemblies, audiences, and consultation as evidence-gated participation forms;
- petition, request, grievance, and information intake;
- public offices, mandates, assignments, acting authority, and delegation interfaces;
- records, seals, authentication, messages, archives, and decision traces;
- reach, burden, operating capacity, strain, performance, continuity, failure, and recovery;
- household-facing, regional, legal, fiscal, military, ecclesiastical, and political-order interfaces;
- bounded player decisions and owner-routed AI behavior.

It does not decide who lawfully holds the duchy, generate officeholders, write law, create regional obedience, manufacture private loyalty, or activate January 1062 state.
