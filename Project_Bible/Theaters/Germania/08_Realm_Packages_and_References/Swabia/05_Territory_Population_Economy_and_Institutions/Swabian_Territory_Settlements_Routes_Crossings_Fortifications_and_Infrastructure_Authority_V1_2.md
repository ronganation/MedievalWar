This authority establishes the Swabian territory and infrastructure layer as a network of separately owned regions, settlements, communities, sites, routes, crossings, fortifications, services, and seasonal access conditions. ### 4.2 Source Portfolio Requirements

Before exact Opening State or canonical territorial promotion, Swabia requires a source-oriented inventory covering:

- geographic and regional terminology;
- settlements and communities;
- roads, tracks, river and lake movement, ferries, fords, bridges, and passes;
- markets, tolls, hospitality, storage, and service sites;
- fortifications, defensible sites, walls, gates, and strategic locations;
- ecclesiastical sites and immunities;
- property, jurisdiction, administration, control, protection, access, and service relationships;
- seasonal access, weather exposure, damage, maintenance, repair, and abandonment.

These inventories may be maintained inside later evidence packages. This authority does not require one wrapper file per category.

Territory owns the persistent physical and geographic definitions through which environmental state is interpreted. A separate shared or future Realm-specific Environment owner supplies dynamic environmental pressure and long-term environmental change.

```yaml

- TerrainHydrologyAndPhysicalPlaceDefinitions
- RegionSettlementRouteCrossingSiteAndInfrastructureDefinitions
- BaselineAccessConditionAndPhysicalCapacityContracts
- DamageMaintenanceRepairRecoveryAndAbandonmentStateAfterOwnerValidatedEvents
- SeasonalSensitivityAndRequiredEnvironmentalInputContracts

EnvironmentOwnerSupplies:
- WeatherEventsAndSeasonalConditions
- FloodFireDroughtStormSnowIceAndLandslidePressure
- SoilWaterForestPastureAndHabitatCondition
- ExposureAdaptationMaladaptationAndResidualEnvironmentalChange
- EnvironmentalHazardConfidenceDurationAndGeographicExtent

TerritoryConsumes:
- EnvironmentalReceiptsForAccessDamageCapacityMaintenanceMovementAndRecovery
```

```text
Campaign season != exact weather
Terrain definition != environmental event
River definition != flood event
Forest definition != available timber or environmental health
Environmental pressure != infrastructure damage until material effects are validated
Infrastructure damage != environmental recovery
```

If no dedicated Swabian Environment authority exists, the package must consume the current shared Environment owner and preserve the missing Realm-specific binding as unknown. Territory may not fill the gap by generating weather, hazard severity, ecological capacity, or recovery.
