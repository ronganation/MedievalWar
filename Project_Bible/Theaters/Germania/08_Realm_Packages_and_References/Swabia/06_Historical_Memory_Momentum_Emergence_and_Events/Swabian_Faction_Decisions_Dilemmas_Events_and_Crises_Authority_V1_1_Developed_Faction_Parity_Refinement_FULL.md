## 18. Opening Decision Family: Establish the Ducal Office

Candidate choices include prioritizing personal presence, a minimal record-and-message office, bounded mandates, regional consultation, issue-specific mediation, or a narrower executable competence. The family consumes title, governance, law, regional, route, population, economy, and political-constitution receipts. It creates no officeholder, clerk, revenue, recognition, mandate, or compliance.

## 19. Opening Decision Family: Separate Office and Rheinfelden Patrimony

Candidate choices include protecting household reserves, admitting bounded private contributions, creating accounting and handback requirements, seeking regional or institutional support, or accepting slower public development. The dilemma is not public versus private as a binary fork. It is how much private capacity can support public work without exhaustion, capture, or silent asset transfer.

## 20. Opening Decision Family: Consult Swabian Owners

The ducal office may prioritize evidence-backed regional lords, ecclesiastical institutions, communities, settlement interfaces, route holders, and service contributors. Options include consultation, mediation, narrower requests, qualified recognition, delayed action, abstention, or referral. One response never becomes Swabia-wide consent.

## 21. Opening Decision Family: Choose the First Public Priority

Potential priorities include office continuity, records and communication, route security, regional consultation, legal-petition capacity, institutional partnership, material accounting, household continuity, or finite military readiness. Choosing a priority creates no active program, building, officer, pool, or capability.

## 22. Royal Request and Service Suite

A Royal request may involve counsel, attendance, escort, mediation, military contribution, route support, administration, or another evidence-backed duty. Options may include acceptance, bounded acceptance, substitution, delay, clarification, mediation, refusal, or abstention. Results depend on mandate, contributors, routes, supply, household burden, performance, external acceptance, and return.

## 23. Burgundy Mandate Suite

Candidate events may involve renewal, narrowing, delegation, travel, communication, records, dispute, external refusal, overextension, withdrawal, or handback. ## 24. Regional Recognition, Service, and Assembly Suite

Options include recognize, consult, negotiate, narrow, substitute, defer, mediate, lawfully enforce through owners, or abstain. ## 25. Ecclesiastical Settlement and Protection Suite

Candidate events include patronage, advocacy, protection, immunity, sanctuary, property, record access, hospitality, burial, relief, appointment-interface, jurisdiction, and restoration matters. Options route through Law, Political Constitution, Ecclesiastical, Property, Governance, and external Church owners. Protection never implies ownership, and patronage never implies appointment.

## 26. Rheinfelden Household and Berthold Continuity Suite

Candidate events include widowhood memory, household reserve, dependent protection, guardianship, education, property custody, travel, absence, captivity, succession uncertainty, release, return, or principal death. Berthold receives no adult role, independent consent, command, governorship, marriage authority, or automatic heirship without the required owners.

## 27. Hohenstaufen Service-House Development Suite

Candidate events include bounded Royal or ducal service, property administration, institution-building proposals, patronage, marriage opportunities, recognition, reward expectations, office eligibility, failure, and retrenchment. The suite creates no castle, abbey, ducal claim, Agnes marriage, children, or famous future.

## 28. Ducal Vacancy, Claim, and House Competition Suite

A vacancy or contest requires a valid office lifecycle state, eligible candidates, claims or appointment routes, participation, recognition, installation feasibility, administration, and material capacity. Choices may support, balance, mediate, delay, recognize conditionally, preserve acting government, or accept continuing vacancy. House competition does not automatically create civil war.

## 29. Rival Kingship and Higher Political Ambition Suite

The later rival-kingship orientation is eligible only if the political-order crisis, participants, claims, procedure, recognition, military feasibility, routes, material capacity, and household continuity exist. Choices may include mediation, candidacy review, refusal, withdrawal, another candidate, bounded support, negotiated settlement, or nonoccurrence. The year 1077 triggers nothing.

## 30. Route, Crossing, Communication, and Access Incidents

Candidate incidents include damaged crossings, seasonal blockage, unsafe hospitality, message loss, contested tolls, guide withdrawal, convoy delay, route insecurity, or command-and-supply separation. A named route does not prove access, safety, transport, or delivery.

## 31. Material Capacity, Burden, and Recovery Events

Candidate incidents include disputed assessments, stock discrepancy, failed delivery, animal exhaustion, equipment damage, storage loss, productive interruption, debt pressure, household minima conflict, compensation delay, restitution, and changed recovery baseline. ## 33. Historical Marriage, Descendant, and Famous-Identity Opportunities

## 34. Fragmentation, Retrenchment, and Alternative-Swabia Crises
