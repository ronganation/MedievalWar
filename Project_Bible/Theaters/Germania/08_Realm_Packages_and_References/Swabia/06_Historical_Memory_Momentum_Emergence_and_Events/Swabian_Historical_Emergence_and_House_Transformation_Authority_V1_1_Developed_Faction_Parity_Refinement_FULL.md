Swabian Historical Emergence converts verified people, houses, offices, relationships, memories, momentum, resources, and political circumstances into conditional pathways. ## 2. Core Design Thesis

Swabian history must emerge from the campaign rather than sit inside the campaign as a schedule.

```text
History supplies possibilities.
Current people and owners supply participants.
Memory supplies interpretation.
Momentum supplies directional pressure.
Emergence evaluates pathway eligibility.
Decisions and events present material choices.
Specialist owners execute results.
```

The same opening may lead toward durable Rheinfelden rule, negotiated regional pluralism, Hohenstaufen development, another house, a transformed ducal office, qualified Royal service, mediation, rival kingship, fragmentation, or no dramatic transformation at all.
