## 2. Swabian Momentum Thesis

Swabia gathers momentum where memory, repeated performance, current people, valid authority, regional reception, routes, material capacity, and recovery reinforce one another. It loses or redirects momentum when those conditions separate.

```text
History supplies pressure.
Current owners supply state.
Memory explains precedent.
Momentum evaluates direction.
Emergence proposes pathways.
Players and AI choose actions.
Specialist owners execute outcomes.
```

The central campaign question is whether an appointed and politically plural duchy can turn service, negotiation, records, household continuity, and recoverable material capacity into durable Swabian power without confusing pressure with destiny.
