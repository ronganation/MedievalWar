## 2. Swabian Identity Formula and Central Question

**Swabia = appointed ducal office + Rheinfelden continuity + negotiated regional plurality + selective Royal and Burgundian engagement + conditional three-house development and transformation + recoverable capability.**

The central question is:

> Can an appointed duke convert standing, patrimony, relationships, routes, institutions, and finite service into durable public capacity without exhausting Rheinfelden, erasing other owners, scripting Zahringen or Hohenstaufen destiny, or depending on Rudolf's constant personal presence?

The core fear is winning the appearance of ducal power while losing the household, records, cooperation, material base, and replacement capacity required to act again.

## 10. Core Values and Decision Effects

Five interpretation values guide comparison without granting bonuses:

1. **Continuing agency.** Preserve a lawful decision process, viable household base, records, communication, essential people, and recoverable resources.
2. **Executable authority.** Prefer performed protection, mediation, administration, or service over unsupported title display.
3. **Reliable reciprocal cooperation.** Honor accepted commitments, recognize contributor costs, and close obligations through receipts.
4. **Political flexibility.** Keep service, mediation, accommodation, restraint, withdrawal, and transformation available.
5. **Durable capability.** Invest in people, routes, records, stores, institutions, officers, maintenance, and recovery that survive one mission or leader.

When values conflict, the decision trace must show the conflict rather than silently selecting one.

## 20. Doctrine: Ducal Authority and Regional Plurality

The AI expands authority through demonstrated function, not map color. Useful evidence includes received petitions, completed protection, recognized mediation, accepted service, functioning records, and successful handback.

Regional consultation is issue-specific. One count, bishop, abbey, settlement, route owner, or community cannot speak for all Swabia. Partial cooperation can be strategically valuable. Refusal is reviewed for authority, procedure, burden, timing, access, and capacity before any resistance conclusion.

## 21. Doctrine: Rheinfelden Continuity

Before major commitments, compare household protection, dependents, property exposure, records, reserves, travel, replacement leadership, and return. Rheinfelden resources support public work only through bounded transfers or service receipts.

The AI cannot spend the same people, stores, horses, equipment, or attention as both private reserve and public capacity. Success never converts Rheinfelden patrimony into permanent ducal ownership.

## 23. Doctrine: Royal Service and Burgundy

Royal engagement is neither submission nor rebellion. Authenticated requests generate options such as full service, limited contribution, substitution, counsel, mediation, delay, clarification, refusal, or abstention.

Burgundy is an external owner. The AI may assess Swabian mandate claims, travel, cost, access, delegation, compensation, performance, narrowing, withdrawal, handback, and recovery. It cannot invent Burgundian people, institutions, resources, permission, recognition, or acceptance.

## 24. Doctrine: Ecclesiastical and Institutional Relations

Institutional relationships are specific. Protection, patronage, advocacy, sanctuary, record access, hospitality, care, mediation, property, jurisdiction, and appointment interfaces remain distinct.

One institutional response cannot become "the Church position." The AI cannot trade patronage for appointment power, treat protection as ownership, or infer shared political policy from shared faith.

## 25. Doctrine: Diplomacy, War, Peace, and Settlement

The AI uses holder-specific knowledge and received messages. It cannot infer foreign private intent or reciprocal relationships.

War entry requires an authorized principal, objective, basis, notice, participants, command, routes, supply, finance, civilian safeguards, withdrawal, and settlement horizon. Victory does not create peace. Truce, peace, recognition, reconciliation, restoration, and integration remain separate.

Diplomacy supplies offers and received terms; specialist owners execute commitments.

## 26. Doctrine: Public Order and Restoration

The AI distinguishes inability, objection, scoped refusal, noncooperation, resistance, violence, and rebellion. Public order is actor-specific, not one unrest score.

Prefer clarification, accommodation, remedy, protection, restitution, compensation, review, and proportional enforcement where supported. Crisis closure is not restoration. Returned property is not restored trust. Reopened routes do not erase prior damage.

## 27. Doctrine: Military and Material Capacity

A mission is bounded by purpose, contributors, command, muster, equipment, mounts, supply, routes, home reserve, casualties, return, demobilization, and recovery.

Potential recruitment is not ready force. Local defense is not free field manpower. Military demand does not delete economic stock. If reconnaissance, supply, command, or return is missing, the AI may resize, delay, substitute, seek valid contracts, defend locally, or decline.

No unit card, formation, garrison, commander, or opening inventory is created here.

## 31. Integrated Consultation and Disagreement

Request advice only from admitted actors with relevant roles, access, availability, and knowledge. Store communicated recommendations with scope, confidence, date, source, and limits.

Conflicting recommendations remain visible where authorized. The AI explains why one option outweighed another without classifying the losing advisor as disloyal. If a needed advisor or representative is unavailable, seek a valid substitute, narrow scope, proceed without that input only where lawful, or hold. Never spawn generic courtiers to complete a packet.

## 33. AI Abstention and No-Action Doctrine

No action is valid when:

- required facts remain unknown and cannot be bypassed safely;
- no authorized principal or process exists;
- authority or jurisdiction is missing;
- participants refuse or are unavailable;
- resources, routes, officers, or recovery are insufficient;
- the proposal duplicates or conflicts with an accepted commitment;
- the likely result is less durable than a clear recovery or retrenchment alternative.

Abstention requires a reason and review trigger. It is not permanent inactivity.

## 34. Difficulty and Fairness

Difficulty comes from scarcity, uncertainty, divided ownership, political tradeoffs, recovery debt, and opportunity cost. It must not come from hidden AI knowledge, free resources, fabricated relationships, artificial rebellion thresholds, or historical railroading.

No numerical handicap or exact difficulty rating is committed here. AI and player use the same authority, consent, capacity, and external-lock rules. Presentation may summarize complexity but may not change truth.

## 37. Proposal and Execution Semantics

The AI proposes. Owners validate, reserve, commit, execute, reject, or return partial acceptance.

```yaml
ProposalDispositions:
- Accepted
- PartiallyAccepted
- Rejected
- HoldUnknown
- ExternalPending
- Stale
- NotApplicable
```
A partial acceptance triggers resizing or review. It cannot be reported as full success. Cross-owner work requires atomic or ordered commits with compensation. Compensation cannot erase travel, consumed goods, casualties, public knowledge, property change, or damaged relationships.

## 38. Determinism, Performance, and Observability

Identical visible inputs, owner versions, content versions, approved tuning, stable ordering, and authorized seed produce the same selection and trace.

Exclude wall-clock time, frame timing, memory addresses, localized strings, upload order, and arbitrary container iteration from decision ordering. Cache owner read models by version. Invalidate only affected decisions. Collapse duplicate issues. Record forecasts separately from outcomes to detect optimism, passivity, and repeated failed assumptions.

## 39. Persistence and Advanced-Save Protection

Persist accepted strategy state, selected postures, commitments, proposal status, player focus, deferrals, review triggers, owner versions, reasons, and traces when implementation is authorized.

Migration must never:

- reset an advanced save to January 1, 1062;
- revive Rudolf or another dead or unavailable person;
- restore a lost office, house asset, route, force, or agreement;
- activate missed historical events;
- force Rheinfelden, Hohenstaufen, Zahringen, rival-kingship, or Barbarossa outcomes;
- refill pools or erase recovery debt;
- convert unknown into friendly, hostile, passive, or aggressive.

Ambiguous mappings are quarantined.
