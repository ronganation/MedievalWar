## 1. Benchmark Purpose and Strong Verdict

The benchmark tests a problem not fully exercised by a single living named character. Frederick I is a probable living opening actor whose career may develop through house leadership, patrimony, service, relationships, institutions, military credibility, and public-office competition. Frederick Barbarossa is a later possible descendant whose identity may receive historical preference only after valid parentage, birth, house continuity, chronology, and world-state conditions exist.
