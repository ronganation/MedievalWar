## 6. Why One Integrated Governance Authority Is Correct

The missing subjects form one administrative lifecycle rather than several independent specialist systems:

```text
Public responsibility
-> consultation or assembly
-> petition or request intake
-> mandate or office routing
-> records and authentication
-> communication and execution attempt
-> reporting and review
-> failure, recovery, or handback
```

Splitting councils, petitions, delegation, records, and continuity into separate files now would create duplicated owner routing and weak interfaces before the package has any exact regional or institutional bindings.

The combined authority remains bounded because specialist owners continue to own law, recognition, temporary authority, people, houses, property, forces, economy, institutions, routes, and Opening State.

## 10. Councils and Assemblies

The governance authority should define administrative support for issue-specific councils and assemblies without creating a universal parliament.

```yaml
AssemblyAdministrationRequires:
  - CallingAuthorityReceipt
  - PurposeAndIssueScope
  - InvitedOrRequiredParticipants
  - RepresentationAndAccess
  - NoticeRouteSeasonSecurityAndVenue
  - RecordsWitnessesAuthenticationAndPublication
  - AdviceSupportOppositionConsentAndAbstentionRouting
  - FollowUpResponsibilitiesAndReview
```

```text
Invitation != attendance
Attendance != representation
Representation != being heard
Advice != consent
Consent != implementation
Assembly != legislature
```

Political Constitution owns participation and recognition; Law owns applicable procedure; Governance owns the administrative attempt to convene, record, route, and follow up.
