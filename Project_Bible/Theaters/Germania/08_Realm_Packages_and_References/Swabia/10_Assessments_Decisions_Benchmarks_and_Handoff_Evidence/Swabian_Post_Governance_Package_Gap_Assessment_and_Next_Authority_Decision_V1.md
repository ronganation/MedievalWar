## 1. Assessment Purpose

This assessment reviews the Swabian package after completion of the mature faction foundation, principal and underage character work, the Rheinfelden and Hohenstaufen house owners, the house-neutral ducal office, and the designed Swabian public-governance authority.

It determines:

- which capability gap now blocks reliable specialist expansion;
- whether regional actors and institutions must be mapped before territory, population, economy, military, diplomacy, and public order;
- whether Zahringen or another character should be admitted first;
- whether package navigation should intervene;
- which workstreams remain held.

This assessment creates no actor, institution, region, settlement, population, property, resource, force, relationship, Opening State record, or implementation state.

## 6. Why Regional Owners Must Come Next

Swabia is deliberately designed as a plural political field rather than one centrally owned territorial block. The package already depends on relationships among:

- the ducal office;
- the Rheinfelden and Hohenstaufen houses;
- counts and other dynasts;
- bishoprics and ecclesiastical offices;
- abbeys and monastic communities;
- settlements, markets, crossings, fortifications, and routes;
- local communities and service populations;
- Royal and political-order owners;
- Burgundy, Italia, Bavaria, Carinthia, and other external interfaces.

Until those actor and institution classes have one Swabian owner-routing matrix, later specialist authorities would risk converting broad geographic association into political ownership.

```text
Regional association != political ownership
Ducal recognition != regional obedience
Ecclesiastical presence != ducal property
Settlement location != faction control
Service possibility != available force
Route connection != guaranteed access
```

## 21. Why Territory Is Not Next

Territory before regional owners would encourage:

- map labels becoming political ownership;
- duchy boundaries becoming direct control;
- places becoming faction assets;
- settlements receiving invented rulers;
- routes receiving automatic access;
- institutions being absorbed into geography.

The regional matrix is the necessary ownership layer before a mature territory authority.

## 22. Why Military Is Not Next

The package still lacks verified:

- recruitment-source owners;
- population capacity;
- property and economy;
- equipment and horses;
- supply and routes;
- officer and command sources;
- regional service relationships.

Military identity remains preserved, but detailed military work is held.

## 23. Why Zahringen Is Not Next

Zahringen is strategically important, but immediate expansion would create a third major internal-house workstream before the package has mapped:

- the house's actual opening base;
- regional relationships;
- property and institutional position;
- distinct candidate lane;
- relationship to the ducal office;
- overlap with other regional actors.

The regional matrix must establish whether Zahringen deserves a full house authority, a major regional-principal profile, a conditional candidate lane, or another treatment.
