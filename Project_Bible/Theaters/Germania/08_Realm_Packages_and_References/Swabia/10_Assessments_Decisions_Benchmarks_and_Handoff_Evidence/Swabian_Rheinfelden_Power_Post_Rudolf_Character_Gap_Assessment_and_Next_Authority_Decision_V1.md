## 6. Why the Next Owner Must Be Combined

A dynasty-only file would leave household capacity, widowhood, access, service, care, travel, officers, resources, and continuity unowned. A household-only file would leave lineage, marriage memory, collateral kin, succession, inheritance, and dynastic property unowned. A patrimony-only file would risk treating property as a detached ledger without beneficiaries, household use, succession, or office separation.

## 10. Why Adelaide Is Not the Next File

Adelaide may become a major character, but the current sources do not establish that Adelaide was Rudolf's spouse on January 1, 1062. A dedicated Adelaide authority now would force the package to write around an unresolved opening relationship and could create a false opening household.

The combined dynasty-household authority can preserve all chronologies, establish a conditional admission gate, define what evidence would justify an Adelaide profile, and prevent later marriage history from becoming an opening binding.

## 15. Why Diplomacy and Political Order Are Not Next

Rudolf and the foundation already establish the Royal Salian, political-order, Burgundy, Italia, Bavaria, Carinthia, Alsace, and ecclesiastical interface questions. A detailed diplomacy authority becomes useful after the package has stable person, household, patrimony, office, government, and regional-owner writers.

Creating diplomacy now would risk turning research relationships into active agreements, recognition, access, hostility, or alliance.
