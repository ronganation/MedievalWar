## 3. Benchmark Question and Player Promise

The benchmark asks:

> Can one historically grounded person already plausible within the opening period gain, lose, redirect, or refuse strategic importance through court formation, service, office, command, patronage, political rupture, cross-Realm employment, independent action, and later memory without calendar spawning, duplicated identity, free resources, guaranteed biography, or legendary predestination?

The player-facing promise is not that Rodrigo will become El Cid. The promise is that an owner-accepted living person with a historically grounded identity may develop into a major actor when current world conditions, opportunity, capacity, relationships, authority, access, and decisions support that development.

## 7. Route-by-Route Reconciliation

### 7.1 Opening court or household context

- Rodrigo must already possess an owner-accepted identity and lifecycle state.
- Court or household placement requires the relevant household and relationship owners.
- Formation context may create opportunity for development but does not grant office or command.
- Failure disposition includes unknown placement, lightweight existence, another valid household context, delayed activation, or nonoccurrence.

### 7.2 Office or subordinate command

- A need for service does not create an office.
- Office, appointment, mandate, jurisdiction, command assignment, force assignment, rules of engagement, reporting, supply, and removal remain separate owner records.
- The 1065 standard-bearer reference is a historical pathway input, not an opening appointment.
- Alternatives include another bounded function, delayed readiness, refusal, another candidate, or no appointment.

### 7.3 Political transition and continued service

- A ruler transition may alter patronage, obligation, access, trust, office, command, and risk.
- Existing service does not guarantee continuity under a successor.
- Dismissal, continued service, renegotiation, reduced role, alternative patronage, or no strategic prominence remain valid.

### 7.4 Dismissal, exile, or voluntary departure

- Dismissal, exile, departure, and defection remain distinct.
- None of these states makes Rodrigo universally available.
- Prior obligations, property, arrears, household, following, route, safe conduct, receiving knowledge, and consent must be resolved.
- The historical 1081 exile reference does not force exile.

### 7.5 Zaragoza negotiation and service

- Zaragoza service is inactive at the opening and requires an existing Rodrigo, an existing authorized Zaragoza principal, mutual knowledge, access, consent, terms, office or command if applicable, pay, supply, following and force interfaces, and exit rules.
- Valid branches include service, bounded mission, negotiation without service, service elsewhere, reconciliation, declined offer, failed negotiation, early exit, and no Zaragoza service.
- No history reference creates Zaragoza forces, money, followers, horses, routes, or victory.

### 7.6 Reconciliation and return

- Reconciliation does not erase earlier dismissal, exile, arrears, grievances, service, memory, or foreign obligations.
- Return requires access, terms, current authority, relationship and contract review, and a valid new or restored function.
- Reconciliation may remain incomplete or may occur without renewed office or command.

### 7.7 Independent action

- Independent action requires existing agency, release or autonomy, household or following, force, resources, access, supply, finance, objectives, relationships, and opposition.
- Independence does not create an army, territory, legitimacy, or government.
- Valid outcomes include bounded independent action, negotiated service, failed enterprise, capture, reconciliation, another settlement, or withdrawal.

### 7.8 Valencia political rule

- Valencia opening rule remains prohibited.
- Later rule requires current political and military state, title, claim, conquest, agreement, recognition, local institutions, communities, government, property, forces, supply, finance, opposition, and succession interfaces.
- Military control does not automatically create legitimate rule.
- Valid alternatives include another ruler, service elsewhere, negotiated influence, failed campaign, partial control, settlement, or no Rodrigo rule.
