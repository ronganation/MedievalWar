## 1. Executive Verdict

Yusuf al-Mutaman is the strongest current project-backed nonmilitary benchmark because the repository already provides an individually developed character profile with administrative, diplomatic, institutional, scholarly, court, regional, succession, and dignified noninheritance paths. The profile is deliberately ReviewOnly at the opening and prohibits hindsight from creating adulthood, office, education, competence, heirship, rivalry, or later rule.

This reconciliation tests whether a historically referenced dynastic person can become strategically important through learning, records, consultation, administration, diplomacy, patronage, institutional work, regional coordination, or succession preparation without being reduced to a military commander or predetermined ruler.

## 2. Why Yusuf Is the Correct Benchmark

Yusuf tests questions Rodrigo does not:

- Can learning remain a process rather than an automatic genius trait?
- Can court access remain distinct from household, office, influence, and authority?
- Can supervised records, petitions, consultation, correspondence, and inspection produce dated evidence of readiness?
- Can institutional or scholarly importance exist without creating a free institution, scholarly office, work, patronage budget, or universal reputation?
- Can a dynastic person maintain a valuable future without inheriting?
- Can a better qualified jurist, secretary, scholar, administrator, or dynamic campaign character receive a function instead?
- Can succession relevance remain separate from nonmilitary competence and current appointment?

## 6. Nonmilitary Entrance Routes

### 6.1 Household and court formation

A confirmed household or court channel may provide access to observation, instruction, ceremony, records, or supervised tasks. Kinship does not create access, education, trust, preference, or office.

### 6.2 Records, petitions, and supervised administration

Administrative development may arise through bounded records, petition review, reporting, provisioning observation, or supervised inspection. Every task requires a supervisor, mandate, information rights, resources, reporting, review, and handback.

### 6.3 Diplomatic correspondence and envoy support

Yusuf may support correspondence, audiences, message delivery, or supervised negotiation after mandate, knowledge, communication, interpretation, route, and external-consent checks. Support does not create treaty authority.

### 6.4 Juristic, institutional, and scholarly formation

Study, juristic consultation, teaching observation, patronage review, manuscript or knowledge-project support, and institutional contact require existing people, institutions, texts or objects, access, lawful scope, resources, and evidence. None creates genius, doctrine, office, authorship, or institutional ownership.

### 6.5 Regional reporting and coordination

A bounded regional assignment requires location, route, local authority, acceptance, records, resources, reporting, review, and return of authority. Assignment does not create property, territorial share, or permanent governorship.

### 6.6 Continuity preparation

Continuity preparation may expose Yusuf to government, court, institutional, fiscal, diplomatic, and regional functions. It creates neither designation nor recognition. Another eligible person, shared transition, delayed transition, or no succession remains valid.

### 6.7 Viable noninheritance career

Where Yusuf does not inherit, valid paths include administration, diplomacy, institutional responsibility, scholarship where supported, regional coordination, household leadership, mediation, external service with consent, or another campaign-produced function. Noninheritance is not failure.

## 8. Candidate Collision Tests

### 8.1 Named Yusuf versus qualified officeholder

A current secretary, jurist, administrator, teacher, or officeholder may be better qualified for a function. Yusuf's dynasty does not override current competence, lawful appointment, access, or institutional standing.

### 8.2 Named Yusuf versus dynamic campaign-grown candidate

A campaign-grown character may receive an administrative, diplomatic, institutional, or scholarly function when current readiness, trust, local acceptance, expertise, access, or availability is stronger. Yusuf may develop elsewhere.

### 8.3 Yusuf versus historical-equivalent continuity candidate

If Yusuf is absent, invalid, dead, incapable, or unsupported but a continuity need persists, an independently sourced candidate may be reviewed. The equivalent inherits no Banu Hud identity, sonship, later rulership, relationships, or scholarly reputation.

### 8.4 No valid candidate

The valid outcome is delay, distributed institutional handling, a reduced mandate, another solution, or nonoccurrence. The game must not generate a free genius or administrator merely because a pathway exists.
