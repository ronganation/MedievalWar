## 1. Immediate Preflight Questions

For every candidate actor, answer the original questions:

- What exact polity existed on 1 January 1062?
- What did contemporaries call the polity and ruler?
- Who could make external decisions?
- Which titles did the ruler hold separately?
- Which regions had separate recognition, law, succession, or administration?
- Could the actor refuse an overlord or neighbor?
- Did the actor possess independent diplomacy, tribute, or war policy?
- What territory and sites were controlled, disputed, tributary, or merely claimed?
- What dates invalidate later map assumptions?
- Is the actor Tier I, II, III, or IV?
- Which Registry IDs exist?
- Which External Locks are required?
- What must remain ReviewOnly?
- What changes strategy enough to justify simulation cost?

### 1.1 Current additional questions

- Does the actor duplicate a strategic problem already represented by a developed Realm?
- Does the actor require a full opening Realm shell, or can a deployable-polity package express its meaningful agency?
- Is battle deployment required even if primary campaign selection is not?
- Which decisions remain independent, delegated, dependent, or external?
- Which forces, recruitment pools, equipment sources, horses, officers, ships, ports, or routes are actually evidenced?
- Which units are region-owned rather than faction-owned?
- Can shared Theater or framework contracts express the actor without creating a local duplicate system?
- What world-state and date-window conditions govern emergence, absorption, restoration, or dissolution?
- What exact Registry, owner, importer, save, migration, rollback, AI, UI, naval, and battle cost would full activation create?
- Would deferral until shared systems mature improve fidelity and reduce duplicated work?

## 6. Stop Conditions

Preserve the original stop conditions. Do not activate a faction if:

- the ruler is ambiguous;
- the actor exists only as a later name;
- territories are inferred from a later map;
- religion is the only grouping rule;
- independent consent cannot be identified;
- the actor adds no strategic choice beyond a region or title;
- external ownership is missing;
- migration cannot preserve advanced saves.

### 6.1 Current additional stop conditions

Stop the affected actor or package when:

- a current OneDrive package may exist but has not been opened;
- full-package status is justified only by map presence, fame, later importance, religion, or symmetry;
- the actor duplicates the strategic problem of an existing developed Taifa;
- an opening ruler, polity status, title, region, force, fleet, port, route, office, agreement, or numeric value must be invented;
- a deployable polity would be inflated into a full Realm merely for folder completeness;
- an organized local actor would be reduced to generic rebellion merely to avoid bounded design work;
- immutable V1, V1.1, or V1.2 compatibility evidence would need in-place editing;
- a Realm would be duplicated across Theaters;
- an external Realm's private state would be authored locally;
- static documentation would be presented as importer, Unreal, runtime, save-load, migration, replay, rollback, performance, AI, naval, or battle validation;
- more than one new full actor package would be opened simultaneously.

Continue unaffected read-only discovery, comparison, and shared-system work.
