## 1. Origin

<External>Encyclopaedia Britannica</External> states that the <External>Order of Calatrava</External> originated in 1158 when <Person>Sancho III of Castile</Person> ceded the fortress of Calatrava to <Person>Raymond</Person>, abbot of Fitero, for its defense. The source states that the knights and monks defending the fortress received papal recognition in 1164 and that the order later became closely affiliated with Morimond in 1187. citeturn111search59
