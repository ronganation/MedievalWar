# PART I. PURPOSE AND SOURCE DISCIPLINE

## 1. Purpose

This file decides which Iberian military-order institutions deserve later institutional packages, which chronology claims are safe enough to control opening and emergence boundaries, which candidates remain held, and which candidate should receive the first full gameplay comparison. It does not create an order, house, commandery, unit, pool, event, or opening-state record.

## 3. User-Supplied Book Excerpt Receipt

The supplied pages explicitly present a section titled “The Spanish Military Orders” and list Calatrava, Santiago, San Julián del Pereiro or Alcántara, Avis, Montegaudio, Saint George of Alfama, Mercedarian Knights, and Santa María. The pages also describe commanderies, frontier fortresses, senior offices, field-army concentration, knight and foot brethren, confrères, estate vassals, mercenary infantry and cavalry, auxiliaries, Muslim archers, and Jewish soldiers.

The material is accepted as a strong research lead. Until the book is identified and the relevant pages are transcribed in full, exact numbers, office distributions, order-specific service examples, and dates remain provisional rather than promoted authority.
