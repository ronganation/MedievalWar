## 1. Developed-Faction Verdict

Aragon's route system must turn a compact mountain Realm into a network-management campaign. The player should not experience Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza as adjacent colored areas that automatically share movement, knowledge, supply, recruitment, and government.

**Route fantasy:** hold the kingdom together by keeping a small number of difficult connections usable, trusted, supplied, observed, and recoverable while choosing which external approaches to negotiate, fortify, exploit, or deny.

A road or pass may physically exist while access, capacity, security, knowledge, transport, or seasonal usability remains limited.
