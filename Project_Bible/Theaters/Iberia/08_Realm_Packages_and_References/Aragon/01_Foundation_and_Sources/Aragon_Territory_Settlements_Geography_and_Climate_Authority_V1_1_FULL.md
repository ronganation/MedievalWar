## 67. Risks, Mitigations, and Acceptance Criteria

### Principal risks

- **The map becomes empty.** Preserve Jaca, the three-land distinction, the strongest castle and institutional candidates, route families, and frontier approaches.
- **The map becomes cluttered.** Enforce representation tiers and the independent-site admission gate.
- **Mountain geography becomes a universal bonus.** Require local routes, guides, supply, weather, formation, and actual access.
- **Jaca becomes a modern exclusive capital.** Preserve court, settlement, site, institution, market, castle, and household distinctions.
- **Later history overwrites 1062.** Apply evidence classes and later-outcome exclusions.
- **Conquest becomes consequence-free.** Require garrison, access, supply, repair, population, cooperation, occupation, and handback.
- **Three lands become one province.** Preserve separate regional operation, recognition, routes, burdens, and vulnerabilities.

### Acceptance criteria

The authority passes developed-faction static review when Aragon is geographically playable without false centralization; Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza remain connected but distinct; Jaca is meaningful without owning or operating everything; major sites and institutions remain individually typed; routes, passes, valleys, castles, property, control, access, population, economy, supply, institutions, and military state remain separately owned; representation tiers prevent both emptiness and clutter; terrain and Campaign Season produce local pressures without universal modifiers; occupation and recovery preserve consequences; map presentation is readable; AI receives bounded explainable summaries; saves preserve divergence; migration is atomic and idempotent; and no unsupported activation or validation is claimed.
