## 1. Strong Aragon Household Verdict

Aragon's royal household must be treated as the operating machinery that converts Ramiro's personal rulership into access, records, counsel, protection, hospitality, provisioning, communication, travel, and continuity. It is not a family-tree container and it is not the realm. The opening domestic game is **The Founder's Mountain Household**: one compact but mobile royal establishment must hold together the needs of a ruler, two politically distinct adult sons, younger dynasts, regional audiences, monasteries, castle-facing officers, messengers, guests, and a shallow material base without inventing a later permanent palace bureaucracy.

The player-facing problem is not court decoration. It is who may reach Ramiro, who controls records and messages, how legitimate Sancho prepares without becoming co-ruler, how elder Sancho receives lawful standing without forced rebellion, how Garcia and Sancha gain meaningful protected roles, how the court moves across difficult routes, and what survives if Ramiro is absent, captured, incapacitated, or dead.

## 11. Household Capacity, Workload, and Domestic Politics

Household capability is function-specific. Records may be stable while travel security is weak. Provisioning may be adequate at Jaca but poor during a winter movement. Access may be concentrated in one officer while protection is distributed. No universal HouseholdPower or CourtInfluence score replaces these conditions.

Domestic politics arise from observable and owner-routed issues: access to Ramiro, protection of younger dynasts, legitimate Sancho's preparation, elder Sancho's position, custody of seals and records, distribution of hospitality, route and residence choices, absence during campaigning, institutional liaison, promised rewards, property disputes, and succession continuity. These issues do not preload affection, jealousy, ambition, fear, resentment, or loyalty.

## 12. Protected People and Underage Safeguards

Garcia and Sancha retain age-appropriate agency, access, education, patronage, communication, household participation, and future eligibility through their owners. A protected person is never household property. Every guardianship, custody, education, fosterage, refuge, or residence arrangement must distinguish decision authority, daily care, contact, property protection, movement, review, termination, and restoration.

Women are not reduced to marriage inventory. Sancha may participate in household diplomacy, patronage, records, institutional relationships, property discussions, family mediation, and succession continuity where age and owner state permit. No routine battlefield command is created by this authority.

## 13. Residence, Mobility, and Jaca

Jaca is the principal royal center and supports ruler and household presence, reception, records, assembly, military concentration, and institutional coordination where owners validate capacity. It is not an immutable court location or proof of one permanent palace staff. The royal court may travel while `court.aragon.royal` continues. `court.jaca.ramiro` is a location-facing opening reference, not the entire household identity.

Movement requires route, season, lodging, transport, security, stores, staff, protected-person, communications, and destination receipts. A destination choice creates no magical government, recognition, local supply, or audience access.

## 14. Office, Access, and Information Rules

Domestic offices remain role definitions until a named holder or explicit vacancy state is owner-approved. Unknown officers remain unknown. No historically plausible steward, chamber officer, chaplain, clerk, guard captain, messenger, tutor, host, or custodian may be invented merely to complete a roster.

Access is typed by person, subject, place, duration, purpose, and information scope. Household membership does not reveal private correspondence, strategic plans, testamentary intent, external state, or every conversation. Message sent, delivered, received, authenticated, understood, and acted upon remain separate.

## 15. Continuity, Transition, and Restoration

Ramiro's death or incapacity does not automatically transfer the household to legitimate Sancho. Owners must process title succession, branch headship, household principalship, offices, property, records, seals, protected people, guards, residences, debts, obligations, relationships, and regional reception separately.

A continuity case may preserve officers temporarily, divide the household, relocate protected people, secure archives, transfer only limited access, establish an interim custodian, or fail partially. Restoration after capture, displacement, disputed succession, or emergency must return or reconstitute actual people, records, property references, access, residence, and functions rather than merely change a status label.
