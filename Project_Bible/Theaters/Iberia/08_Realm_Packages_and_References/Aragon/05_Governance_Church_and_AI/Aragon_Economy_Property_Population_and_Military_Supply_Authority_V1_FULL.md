## 1. Developed-Faction Verdict

Aragon must play as a materially constrained mountain kingdom whose strategic strength comes from converting dispersed households, valleys, castles, pastoral resources, institutions, route access, seasonal stores, service obligations, and limited exchange into reliable government and military endurance.

**Economic fantasy:** build durable capacity from a narrow base without stripping the three lands of people, animals, food, local trust, or institutional cooperation.

The player should distinguish wealth from treasury, population from recruitable people, property from jurisdiction, recognized dues from collected receipts, stores from available supply, production from transport, and battlefield success from the ability to provision and retain conquest.
