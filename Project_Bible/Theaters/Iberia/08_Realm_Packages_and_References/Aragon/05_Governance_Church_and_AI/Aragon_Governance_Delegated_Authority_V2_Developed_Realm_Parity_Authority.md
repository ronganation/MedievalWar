# 1. Concise Verdict

Implement Aragon as a **founder-dependent mountain monarchy** configured through existing Realm Governance and Delegated Authority systems.

The opening government should contain:

- one realm governance configuration: `governance.aragon.founder_dependent_mountain_monarchy`;
- Jaca as the leading royal center, not a later chartered city, settled cathedral government, or complete bureaucracy;
- separate governance surfaces for old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza;
- royal authority mediated through presence, household, castles, regional actors, monasteries, and bounded delegations;
- royal itinerary and regional-priority commands rather than repetitive travel clicks;
- no automatic equation between title, direct domain, practical control, regional recognition, property, jurisdiction, and taxation;
- no active Aibar, Javierrelatre, or Sos tenencia until holder, legal form, dates, jurisdiction, powers, review, revocation, vacancy, and succession are validated;
- typed founder-dependency functions rather than one Authority or Integration score;
- no automatic transfer of delegations when Ramiro dies or the title changes holder.

The authority rejects:

- universal feudal hierarchy;
- one kingdom-integration score;
- one direct-domain map color;
- tenencia as hereditary property by default;
- delegation as loyalty;
- castle custody as full valley government;
- title as administration;
- automatic delegation succession;
- routine court-travel micromanagement;
- later Fuero de Jaca, developed bishopric, cathedral chapter, or municipal institutions at opening.

Primary classification: **Implement bounded realm content under existing Governance authorities. Keep uncertain tenencias ReviewOnly. Make geographic and institutional limits strategically consequential.**

The player should govern through priorities, appointments, reviews, and exceptional interventions, not repetitive castle visits or routine maintenance clicks.

# 8. Authority Disposition Model

Every site or jurisdiction uses one opening governance disposition:

- `DirectRoyalDomain`
- `DelegatedRoyalCommand`
- `RegionallyMediatedRoyalAuthority`
- `CustomaryOrClaimedSuperiority`
- `DisputedOrSituational`
- `External`
- `ReviewOnly`
- `Quarantined`

## 8.1 Direct Royal Domain

Requires evidence of usable royal property, administration, revenue, garrison authority, or household presence.

## 8.2 Delegated Royal Command

Requires a canonical holder, office or delegation, jurisdiction, powers, dates, reporting, review, revocation, vacancy, and succession behavior.

## 8.3 Regionally Mediated Royal Authority

The ruler is recognized, but implementation depends on regional actors, routes, institutions, and presence.

## 8.4 Customary or Claimed Superiority

Rank, precedent, or claim exists without routine administration.

## 8.5 Disputed or Situational

Control, access, tribute, or protection is contested, temporary, or context-specific.

## 8.6 External

The site or authority remains externally owned even when Aragon raids, claims, besieges, influences, receives tribute from, or seeks it.

# 12. Granted and Prohibited Powers

## 12.1 Granted-power categories

- hold and defend a specified site;
- muster specified local obligations;
- receive defined tolls or revenues;
- maintain specified storage;
- conduct bounded justice;
- report frontier conditions;
- escort or protect a route;
- negotiate only within delegated limits;
- represent the ruler at a named assembly.

## 12.2 Prohibited-power categories

Unless explicitly granted, a delegate may not:

- alienate the castle;
- inherit the delegation automatically;
- create a title;
- declare war;
- form external alliance;
- appoint a successor;
- control church office;
- tax outside jurisdiction;
- transfer property;
- expand justice unilaterally;
- bind the ruler to tribute or peace;
- convert custody into personal sovereignty.

## 12.3 Explanation requirement

The player and AI should be able to see why a proposed act is permitted, prohibited, or requires higher authority.

# 21. Delegation Review, Revocation, and Vacancy

## 21.1 Review triggers

A delegation may be reviewed after:

- scheduled review date;
- ruler change;
- holder death or incapacity;
- site loss;
- command failure;
- report failure;
- abuse allegation;
- property dispute;
- regional complaint;
- external threat;
- explicit command.

## 21.2 Revocation

Revocation requires:

- valid revoking authority;
- valid reason or legal basis where required;
- communication;
- disposition of garrison, revenue, records, and property interfaces;
- owner-side response;
- possible relationship consequences through Character Network.

Revocation does not automatically succeed peacefully.

## 21.3 Vacancy

A vacancy may arise from:

- holder death;
- incapacity;
- resignation;
- revocation;
- capture;
- site loss;
- term expiry;
- title succession conflict.

## 21.4 Succession rule

Delegations do not pass automatically to the holder's heir or to the new ruler's heir.

Every continuation requires the delegation's own vacancy and succession rule.
