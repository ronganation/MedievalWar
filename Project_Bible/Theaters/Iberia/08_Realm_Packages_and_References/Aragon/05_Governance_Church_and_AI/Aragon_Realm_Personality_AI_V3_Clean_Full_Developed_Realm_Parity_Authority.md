## 1. Developed Verdict

Aragon is a small, founder-dependent, mountain polity with limited material depth, separate regional recognition surfaces, valuable routes and institutions, a strategically exposed southern frontier, and a succession that must be prepared without being scripted.

Its governing personality remains:

**Resolute, opportunistic, austere, dynastic, and frontier-facing.**

Its strategic identity remains:

**Survival + Recognition + Southward Opportunity.**

The AI should preserve the practical union of Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza; protect Jaca, home garrisons, recruitment recovery, reporting, routes, treasury, and household continuity; prepare the legitimate Sancho without guaranteeing succession; treat the elder Sancho as a capable person with separate property and delegated-authority interfaces rather than a default rebel; use institutions for specific records, mediation, hospitality, patronage, and route functions; and pursue frontier gains only when political purpose, military capacity, coalition terms, external response, supply, occupation, and recovery are credible.

The AI should be capable of bold action, conditional action, delay, negotiation, delegation, withdrawal, no action, or abstention. It must never use the calendar to force Graus, Barbastro, Ramiro's death, papal vassalage, Roman-rite reform, Jaca's later institutional development, the 1076 Pamplona union, later conquests, or Crown of Aragon identity.

Primary classification: **Implement one qualitative realm personality, one sovereign profile, bounded stakeholder-support profiles, typed owner observations, explainable proposal families, hard constraints, alternatives, no-action and abstention, deterministic traces, and strict external agency. Defer tuning and activation. Reject direct mutation, historical destiny, opaque universal scores, generic character stereotypes, and infinite recruitment.**

## 12. Character, Dynasty, and Network Inputs

Character owners supply identity certainty, lifecycle, availability, location, simulation tier, development bounds, agency eligibility, command eligibility, absence, captivity, injury, and assignment. Adult age does not itself create command, office, marriage, household leadership, or strategic readiness.

Dynasty and Title owners supply shared dynasty and branch references, branch headship, title-specific eligibility, expected-successor summaries, regional recognition, household continuity, founder-dependency gaps, public claims, and uncertainty. Aragon rulership, Sobrarbe recognition, Ribagorza recognition, branch headship, household leadership, active delegations, military command, and church patronage must remain separate surfaces.

## 13. Governance, Institution, Geography, Economy, Population, and Military Inputs

Governance supplies the recognized ruler, court identity and location, separate regional surfaces, regional attention, reporting quality, active validated delegations, vacancies, review and revocation conditions, route-dependent communication, founder-dependent functions, and owner-approved direct-domain summaries.

Ecclesiastical and Institution owners supply active institutions, office status, residence, jurisdiction, records, patronage, property and immunity disputes, hospitality, mediation, route support, Roda and papal locks, and reform readiness. Institution-specific support may never be collapsed into universal church approval.

Geography, Logistics, Economy, Property, Population, Recruitment, and Military owners supply routes, passes, seasonality, travel, settlement and castle value, supply corridors, storage, treasury, committed funds, revenue confidence, transport, occupation cost, population-supported pool depth, social or regional recruitment sources, available contingents, garrisons, commanders, mobilization delay, siege capacity, replacement depth, active wars, reserve requirements, and uncertainty.
