- `CommandEligible`
- `Blocked`

## 5.1 Meaning

- **ResearchEligible** permits information-gathering proposals.
- **ProposalEligible** permits local proposals.
- **NegotiationEligible** permits requests to external actors.
- **CommandEligible** means all necessary owners may validate a specified command.
- **Resolved** means the current pathway instance has ended, not that every issue is permanently settled.

## 5.2 No automatic escalation

Movement between states requires current owner summaries, valid commands, external responses, or events. Passage of time alone is insufficient.
