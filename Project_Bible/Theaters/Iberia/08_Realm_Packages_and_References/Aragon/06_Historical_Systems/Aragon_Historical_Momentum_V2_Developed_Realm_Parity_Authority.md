10. `pressure.aragon.material_depth`
11. `pressure.aragon.foreign_coalition_opportunity`

Momentum represents **current directional pressure**, not memory, event probability, destiny, or a hidden schedule.

The opening pressure set should make Aragon feel:

- held together but not administratively uniform;
- dependent on Ramiro's still-unreplaced functions;
- aware of a plausible successor whose readiness remains incomplete;
- constrained by same-name royal kin whose legal and delegated positions require clarity;
- dependent on sparse castles, routes, institutions, and officeholders;
- attracted toward Graus but exposed to Zaragoza's stronger diplomatic network;
- connected to trans-Pyrenean opportunities without controlling them;
- institutionally mobile and not yet settled at Jaca;
- materially shallow relative to strategic ambition;
- capable of coalition-building only through negotiated external agency.

The authority rejects:

- one universal Momentum score;
- one frontier-progress bar;
- one integration, legitimacy, church, or overextension score;
- automatic pressure-to-event conversion;
- date-triggered Graus, Barbastro, Pamplona, papal, or Jaca outcomes;
- actorless external pressure;
- religious identity as automatic hostility;
- Christian Iberia as one team;
- Muslim Iberia as one faction;
- later Spain or Portugal projected into 1062;
- Aragon owning pressures, intentions, or responses belonging to other Iberian packages.

Primary classification: **Implement typed, holder-specific pressures under Historical Momentum. Preserve current-state causality and cross-Iberian ownership. Defer numeric tuning. Reject event scripting and civilizational blocs.**

# 2. Iberian Expansion Compatibility Note

## 2.1 Future package scope

The project is expected eventually to include the rest of Iberia, including:

- Castilian and Leonese polities;
- Pamplona and other Basque-region actors;
- Galician and Portuguese-region polities;
- Catalan counties;
- taifa and other Muslim-ruled polities;
- ecclesiastical, dynastic, regional, and frontier actors crossing those packages.

## 2.2 Naming and identity rule

Future packages must use historically bounded polity, realm, dynasty, region, institution, or actor identities appropriate to the effective date.

The 1062 simulation must not use later nation-state identity as opening political ownership. In particular:

- “Spanish” may be a modern organizational or language label, not one opening realm;
- “Portuguese” may describe later cultural or package scope, but must not assume a fully formed later kingdom in every earlier scenario state;
- “Muslim” describes religious affiliation and cannot replace taifa, dynasty, ruler, city, army, community, or diplomatic identity;
- “Christian” describes religious affiliation and cannot create alliance, loyalty, knowledge, military access, or common strategy.

## 2.3 Cross-package pressure ownership

Aragon owns only Aragon-facing pressure states.

A future external package owns:

- its own pressure definitions;
- its own holder state;
- its own interpretation of Aragon;
- its own reinforcement and resistance;
- its own AI-readable summaries;
- its own emergence interfaces.

Reciprocal pressures are not automatically symmetric.

## 2.4 Shared Iberian interface requirement

Future Iberian packages should share a neutral pressure-interface contract supporting:

- canonical actor IDs;
- directional source and target;
- known versus hidden state;
- diplomatic agreement references;
- military and economic owner summaries;
- religious and institutional context without bloc behavior;
- versioned external locks;
- separate reverse ownership;
- deterministic compatibility validation.

## 2.5 Language and localization

Canonical IDs remain stable ASCII identifiers. Display names, UI descriptions, source notes, and localization may support Spanish, Portuguese, Catalan, Galician, Basque, Arabic, Latin, Aragonese, and other appropriate languages without changing identity or ownership.

# 7. Three Mountain Lands Pressure

## 7.1 Identity

- **Pressure ID:** `pressure.aragon.three_mountain_lands`
- **Holder:** `realm.aragon`
- **Opening class:** High
- **Direction:** Toward separate attention, communication, recognition, and continuity across old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza

## 7.2 Meaning

The realm is politically united under Ramiro but administratively uneven. Geography, routes, institutions, regional actors, and external connections prevent one uniform province model.

## 7.3 Contributing inputs

- regional recognition differences;
- reporting delay;
- route access;
- court location;
- ruler itinerary;
- delegation coverage;
- institutional mediation;
- succession preparedness by region;
- external pressure on western and eastern approaches;
- memory of the union under Ramiro.

## 7.4 Countervailing inputs

- effective royal presence;
- validated delegations;
- reliable reporting;
- institutional cooperation;
- regional assemblies;
- successor recognition;
- secure routes;
- successful dispute resolution.

## 7.5 Reinforcement

Pressure may rise when:

- one region is neglected;
- reports fail;
- routes close;
- ruler absence lengthens;
- succession uncertainty grows;
- delegations become vacant;
- external actors gain leverage;
- regional institutions contest authority.

## 7.6 Resistance and decay

Pressure may reduce when:

- regional recognition is renewed;
- communication improves;
- delegations are clarified;
- succession planning reaches each surface;
- institutions mediate effectively;
- court itinerary addresses neglected regions.

## 7.7 Prohibited effects

The pressure does not create separatism, loyalty loss, rebellion, or a universal integration modifier.

# 19. Resistance and Counterpressure

## 19.1 Resistance

Resistance is owner-state that reduces, redirects, or contains pressure without necessarily removing its causes.

Examples:

- successor preparation resists founder and heir pressure;
- regional assemblies resist three-land fragmentation pressure;
- a truce resists Graus pressure;
- a reliable external agreement resists intervention uncertainty;
- reserves resist material-depth pressure;
- validated officeholder and residence resist ecclesiastical-settlement pressure.

## 19.2 Counterpressure

A separate pressure may pull in another direction. For example:

- Graus frontier opportunity pushes toward action;
- material depth pushes toward restraint;
- founder continuity pushes toward keeping Ramiro available;
- external coalition opportunity may make action more feasible.

The system preserves all components rather than collapsing them into one result.

---

# 20. Contradiction and Reversal

## 20.1 Contradiction

A pressure may be contradicted when its assumed contributing state is disproved or replaced.

Examples:

- reported Castilian intervention capability proves unavailable;
- an office thought vacant has a validated holder;
- a route thought blocked is open;
- a delegation thought active is invalid;
- an external actor refuses coalition interest;
- occupation capacity improves or collapses.

## 20.2 Reversal

Pressure direction may reverse when current conditions change.

For example, foreign coalition opportunity can shift from encouraging inquiry to encouraging withdrawal after terms become unacceptable.

## 20.3 History preservation

Reversal changes current pressure state. It does not erase prior facts, memories, proposals, or decisions.

---

# 21. Decay and Persistence

## 21.1 Persistence

A pressure remains active while contributing current conditions remain active.

## 21.2 Decay

Pressure decays when:

- its current-state causes resolve;
- information becomes obsolete;
- target disappears;
- agreement changes;
- holder ceases to exist;
- another owner state invalidates the interface.

## 21.3 No calendar destiny

Pressure does not rise simply because the calendar approaches a historical date.

## 21.4 No maintenance clicks

The player does not click periodically to maintain or reduce pressure. Pressures respond to meaningful owner-state changes.
