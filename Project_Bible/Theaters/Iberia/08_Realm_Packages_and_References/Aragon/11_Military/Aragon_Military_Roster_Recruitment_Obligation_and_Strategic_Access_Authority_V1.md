## 1. Strong Aragon Military Verdict

Aragon is **The Three-Land Mountain Host**. It does not begin with a standing national army, a universal levy, large knightly masses, free castle garrisons, an inexhaustible mountain-warrior pool, automatic Pamplonese support, a scheduled Graus victory, or a preassembled Barbastro coalition. Military capacity must be assembled from Ramiro's household, legitimate Sancho's developing service, elder Sancho's conditional contribution, distinct regional communities, castles and strongpoints, local obligations, scarce horses and equipment, route knowledge, supply capacity, institutional assistance, and negotiated external components.

The campaign military question is: **How much force can Ramiro lawfully assemble, move, feed, command, preserve, and bring home without stripping the three lands of castle defense, household protection, labor, animals, stores, regional cooperation, and succession continuity?**

Aragon's intended battlefield identity is compact and source-driven: dependable spear and shield lines where a real muster succeeds; scarce household and regional mounted retainers; selected heavier foot; useful local missile and skirmishing support where evidence permits; strong defensive and relief behavior in broken terrain; route intelligence; garrison tradeoffs; and bounded siege capacity. Mountain geography shapes access, warning, fatigue, formation, supply, retreat, and local knowledge. It does not grant universal combat bonuses.

## 11. Recruitment, Obligation, and Pool Rules

Population informs and constrains finite recruitment pools through social class, region, household, labor, health, displacement, and recovery. It never functions as one-for-one army currency. A valid recruitment source requires eligible and uncommitted people, lawful access, functioning households or communities, equipment, training or cadre, officers, assembly access, route, supply, season, political cooperation, and recovery capacity.

Pool recovery depends on survivors, demobilization, returned equipment, restored households and communities, training, horses, labor, food, routes, institutions, and time through owners.

## 12. Command, Force Packaging, and Tactical Authority

A character becomes commander only through a valid appointment or mandate. Ramiro, legitimate Sancho, and elder Sancho do not receive free forces from identity, title, kinship, property, or profile quality. Every force package names contributors, unit instances, command hierarchy, objective, geography, route, supply, season, garrisons released, reserve, duration, reporting, extension, withdrawal, demobilization, and handback.

Tactical discretion must remain bounded by strategic authority and actual battlefield command. Battlefield roles and formations require training, equipment, leadership, cohesion, frontage, depth, terrain, fatigue, and morale. No character aura or faction stereotype substitutes for these inputs.

## 13. Garrisons, Sieges, Relief, and Home Defense

Castles and strongpoints require actual garrison sources, custody, stores, leadership, watch, maintenance, relief plans, and replacement. Releasing a garrison may create a field contingent but reduces local defense until owner-approved replacement arrives. Capturing a castle does not create a loyal garrison.

Siege capacity requires labor, tools, specialists, protection, timber and material access, transport, food, intelligence, routes, command, and time. Assault, blockade, negotiation, raid, bypass, relief, withdrawal, or abstention remain valid choices.

## 14. Battle Aftermath, Occupation, and Recovery

Battle aftermath records survivors, wounded, missing, captured, dead, deserters, equipment, horses, standards, prisoners, loot under law, route access, pursuit, relief needs, burial, ransom, demobilization, and owner notifications. Victory cannot refill pools or erase losses.

Occupation requires a continuing force, supplies, custody, law, property, church, local actors, communication, relief, administration, and exit or incorporation plan. Military authority may hold a site temporarily. It does not create title, property, revenue, legal jurisdiction, or durable government.
