## 1. Research Verdict

The current Aragon package strongly supports a compact, source-driven military architecture, but it does not yet support a fully named January 1062 roster or exact opening inventory. The three attached military files correctly keep candidate families, unit admission, stable IDs, pool values, command assignments, and opening forces separate.

This register advances the work by converting the audit's research queue into precise evidence findings, unresolved questions, source-quality rules, candidate effects, and owner routes. It does not pretend that a general military function proves a named unit.

### 2.1 Internal authority precedence

The attached general military authority controls military architecture, finite pools, obligations, musters, garrisons, strategic access, casualties, occupation, demobilization, and recovery. The attached exact-roster authority controls the 22 candidate definitions while creating no stable IDs or active inventory. The attached admission audit controls current candidate dispositions and states that zero candidates are admitted.

### 2.2 External-source discipline

External web results are discovery leads, not automatic authority. Search results used in this register provide context for Ramiro's small frontier realm, expansion pressures, campaigns against Graus, and uncertainty in later accounts. They do not provide a reliable January 1062 order of battle or candidate-by-candidate equipment inventory.
