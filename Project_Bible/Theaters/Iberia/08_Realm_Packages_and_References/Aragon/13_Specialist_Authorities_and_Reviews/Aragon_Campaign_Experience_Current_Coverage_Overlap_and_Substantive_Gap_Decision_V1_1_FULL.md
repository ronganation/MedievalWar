### 1. Executive Decision

Aragon has a real Campaign Experience owner gap. The current Aragon stack contains excellent strategic ingredients, but no current Realm authority owns the complete player-facing campaign portfolio required by the shared Campaign Experience framework.

Realm Personality and AI owns qualitative preference, visible-input evaluation, bounded alternatives, proposals, abstention, and explanation. Opening State owns exact January 1062 initialization and already exposes eight first-Winter opportunity families. Historical Emergence owns eleven conditional pathways, eligibility, stages, alternatives, blockers, expiry, and owner-command routing. Governance owns government surfaces, mandates, regional priorities, and administrative action. Law owns legal process, policy, custom, jurisdiction, remedy, enforcement, and restoration. Historical owners retain fact, memory, momentum, and emergence interpretation. None of those owners may be expanded into the missing Campaign Experience owner merely because they contain adjacent content.

### 4. Shared Campaign Experience Requirement

The shared framework requires Realm-level ownership for objective, pressure, progress, milestone, outcome, reward, recovery, standing, designation, and bounded victory interpretation definitions. It separates definitions from active instances; eligibility from visibility, offer, selection, and activation; progress from resources; milestones from rewards; completion from historical outcome; standing from authority; designation from title or office; and victory from government.

```text
Pressure Definition != Active Pressure
Pressure != Objective
Pressure != Crisis
Objective Definition != Objective Instance
Eligibility != Visibility
Visibility != Offer
Offer != Selection
Selection != Activation
Recommendation != Command
Progress Contributor != Progress
Progress != Resource
Milestone != Reward
Reward Eligibility != Reward Execution
Completion != Historical Outcome
Failure != Campaign Loss
Abandonment != Failure
Replacement != Reset
Recovery != Erasure
Nonoccurrence != System Failure
Standing != Authority
Designation != Title Or Office
Victory != Government
```

The intended player workload is one primary objective, zero to two supporting objectives, relevant optional opportunities, and recovery objectives when viability requires them. Campaign Experience focuses strategy rather than creating notification debt or a permanent checklist.

### 6. Overlap Findings by Source

#### 6.1 Realm Personality and AI

The AI authority already defines Aragon as resolute, opportunistic, austere, dynastic, and frontier-facing, with Survival, Recognition, and Southward Opportunity as its strategic identity. It contains proposal families, hard constraints, alternatives, no-action and abstention, clear operating records for the three lands, Jaca, succession, elder Sancho, Graus, Barbastro, ecclesiastical continuity, and recovery.

That coverage is intentionally proposal-only. Personality is not objective, objective is not command, and AI does not own execution. Campaign Experience may consume proposal and explanation outputs, but it must not absorb AI profiles, weights, observations, hard constraints, or decision authority.


#### 6.2 Opening State

Opening State V2.1 provides a strong January 1062 strategic condition, three land packets, five character packets, eleven candidate Momentum states, eleven candidate Emergence states, and eight first-Winter opportunity families. These are excellent candidate inputs for Campaign Experience, but Opening State explicitly owns selections and initialization rather than reusable objective definitions or runtime progression.

The opening opportunity deck must therefore be treated as a candidate source portfolio. It must not be silently reclassified as eight active objectives. Opening activation remains separately owner-gated.

```yaml
OpeningStateDisposition:
  overlap_strength: HIGH_AT_SCENARIO_SEED_LEVEL

```

#### 6.3 Historical Emergence

Historical Emergence V2 contains eleven alternatives-rich pathways and already handles eligibility, stage, branches, blockers, partial success, failure, withdrawal, expiry, contradiction, external consent, and owner-command routing. This is the highest-overlap domain.

A Campaign Experience authority remains justified because a pathway answers what may become eligible and how a multi-owner process is routed, while an objective answers what the player is trying to achieve, why it matters now, what contributes to progress, what blocks it, which milestones matter, what outcome class occurred, what recovery or replacement follows, and how standing or campaign victory should be interpreted.

Campaign Experience must reference Emergence pathways rather than restating their stage machines. One objective may reference several pathways. One pathway may support several alternative objectives. Objective completion cannot force a pathway outcome.

```text
Historical Pressure != Campaign Objective
Emergence Eligibility != Objective Eligibility
Emergence Pathway != Objective Definition
Pathway Stage != Objective Lifecycle
Accepted Proposal != Objective Completion
Owner Outcome != Campaign Reward
```

#### 6.4 Governance

Governance provides the three-land operating model, founder dependency, regional attention, royal itinerary, mandates, delegation review, Jaca, home-government capacity during campaigns, and occupation governability. These are Campaign Experience contributors and blockers, not Campaign Experience state.

A campaign objective may ask the player to strengthen continuity or stabilize a regional surface, but Governance determines whether any itinerary, delegation, reporting, appointment, or administrative action is valid and what it changes.

#### 6.5 Law, Policy, Custom, and Jurisdiction

Law provides deep definitions for regional legal plurality, royal protection, petitions, evidence, mediation, judgment, enforcement, restoration, service burden, tenencia disputes, Jaca development policy, safe conduct, resistance and reconciliation, emergency measures, and occupation law. These can support objective alternatives, costs, blockers, failure consequences, and recovery routes.

Campaign Experience cannot decide jurisdiction, treat refusal as failure, convert judgment to enforcement, grant a fuero, activate a policy, confiscate property, or classify a grievance as rebellion. Legal success may contribute to an objective only through an owner receipt.

### 7A. Dilemma, Opportunity, Event, Crisis, and Decision-Package Closure

The parent gap audit identified opening pressures, objectives, decisions, dilemmas, opportunities, events, crises, rewards, partial success, failure, recovery, and nonoccurrence as the possible Campaign Experience surface if the gap survived. The V1 decision covered objectives, pressures, outcomes, rewards, recovery, and nonoccurrence, but did not explicitly close the ownership of dilemmas, opportunities, events, crises, and campaign decision packages. This V1.1 correction closes that omission.

```yaml
CampaignFacingPackageDecision:
  CampaignExperienceOwns:
    - CampaignDilemmaDefinitionAndLifecycle
    - OptionalOpportunityDefinitionVisibilityOfferExpiryAndNonoccurrence
    - CampaignEventPresentationAndObjectiveInterpretation
    - CampaignCrisisDefinitionVisibilityEscalationInterpretationRecoveryAndReplacement
    - PlayerFacingDecisionPackagePurposeAlternativesCostsRisksBlockersAndOwnerRoutes
  CampaignExperienceDoesNotOwn:
    - HistoricalFactOrEventOccurrence
    - HistoricalEmergenceEligibilityOrPathwayStage
    - RealmAIEvaluationOrProposalSelection
    - SpecialistTriggerState
    - CharacterRelationshipGovernanceLawMilitaryEconomyDiplomacyOrExternalState
    - OwnerCommandExecutionOrOutcome
  Rules:
    - PressureDoesNotAutomaticallyCreateCrisis
    - EmergencePathwayDoesNotAutomaticallyCreateEvent
    - AIProposalDoesNotAutomaticallyCreateDilemmaOffer
    - OpportunityMayNeverOccur
    - CrisisMayReplaceNormalPortfolioWithoutCreatingUnderlyingEmergencyState
    - CampaignEventPresentationCannotCreateHistoricalFact
```

A campaign dilemma packages an owner-grounded strategic choice. An opportunity packages a conditional, potentially nonoccurring option. A campaign event presents an owner-confirmed occurrence and its campaign meaning without becoming the fact owner. A crisis interprets owner-confirmed conditions that temporarily require concentrated campaign attention; it does not create the emergency, rebellion, war, death, succession, or collapse state it presents.

### 19. AI, Player Choice, and Presentation Boundary

Realm AI may evaluate visible eligible objectives and propose selection, delay, replacement, recovery, abandonment, no action, or abstention. Campaign Experience owns objective meaning and lifecycle. Specialist owners own execution. The player may ignore recommendations.

The presentation should show one primary objective, limited supporting objectives, meaningful pressures and opportunities, known progress, principal blockers, costs, risks, alternatives, recovery, and required owners. It must not expose external private intent, hidden utility, future history, unsupported precision, or low-level maintenance noise.
