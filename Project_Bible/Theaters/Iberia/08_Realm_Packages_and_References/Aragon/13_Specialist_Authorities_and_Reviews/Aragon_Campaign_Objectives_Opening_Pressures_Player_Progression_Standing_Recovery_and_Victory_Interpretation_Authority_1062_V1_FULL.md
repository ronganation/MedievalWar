### 1. Strong Aragon Campaign Verdict

Aragon's campaign experience is the struggle to preserve a founder-dependent union of Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza while building enough household, regional, institutional, military, material, legal, informational, and diplomatic depth for the polity to survive Ramiro's absence or death. Its identity is **Survival, Recognition, and Southward Opportunity**. Expansion is one possible expression of that identity, not its mandatory conclusion.

The player should normally face one primary objective, zero to two supporting objectives, and only the optional opportunities that current owner state makes visible and relevant. The campaign must favor clear map-facing strategy over permanent checklist play. It must support preservation, negotiation, limited preparation, institutional investment, regional settlement, external inquiry, frontier action, delay, refusal, recovery, replacement, and nonoccurrence.
