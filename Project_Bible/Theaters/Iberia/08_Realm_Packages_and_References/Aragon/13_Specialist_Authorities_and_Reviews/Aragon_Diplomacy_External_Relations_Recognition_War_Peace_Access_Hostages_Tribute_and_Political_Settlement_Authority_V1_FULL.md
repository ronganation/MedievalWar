### 1. Strong Aragon Diplomacy Verdict

Aragon shall model diplomacy as directional, counterparty-specific, evidence-bound, reciprocal political action rather than as one attitude score. Contact, audience, representation, recognition, proposal, negotiation, consent, agreement, ratification, entry into effect, obligation, performance, breach, remedy, suspension, withdrawal, termination, and political settlement remain distinct states.

Aragon may author only its own decisions, proposals, recognition, commitments, and receipts. Every counterparty retains ownership of its own consent, recognition, private intent, agreement state, performance, and refusal. Kinship, faith, proximity, a common enemy, tribute, marriage, hostages, or Papal contact may shape proposals and interpretation but never create diplomatic state by themselves.
