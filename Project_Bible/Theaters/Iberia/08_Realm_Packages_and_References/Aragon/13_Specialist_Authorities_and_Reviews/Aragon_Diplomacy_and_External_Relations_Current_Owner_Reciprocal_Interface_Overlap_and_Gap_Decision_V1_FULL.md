### 1. Executive Decision

A genuine Aragon Realm-content gap survives the current-owner and reciprocal-interface review. Existing Character Network, Governance, Law, Routes, Information, Custody, Ecclesiastical, Military, Economy, Historical, Campaign Experience, and external-owner systems control essential diplomatic inputs and consequences, but none owns Aragon's complete diplomatic relationship and agreement lifecycle.

A dedicated Aragon Diplomacy and External Relations authority is therefore justified. It must coordinate contact, representation, recognition, proposals, counterproposals, negotiations, agreements, ratification, entry into effect, obligations, performance, breach, review, suspension, withdrawal, expiry, termination, settlement, and reciprocal external-owner receipts without authoring another Realm's consent or private state.
