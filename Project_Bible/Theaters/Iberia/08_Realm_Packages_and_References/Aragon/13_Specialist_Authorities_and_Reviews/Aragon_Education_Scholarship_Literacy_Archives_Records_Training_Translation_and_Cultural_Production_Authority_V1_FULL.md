## 1. Purpose and Strong Aragon Verdict

This authority defines how education, learning, literacy, numeracy, scribal and documentary practice, technical training, apprenticeship, texts, records, archives, translation, scholarship, patronage, historical transmission, and cultural production may operate in Aragon. It fills the owner gap between Aragon's strong Character, Governance, Law, Ecclesiastical, Economic, Military, Historical, Route, and Realm AI authorities without absorbing their state.

```yaml
AragonEducationVerdict:
  model: DistributedHouseholdEcclesiasticalRoyalRegionalCastleAndPracticalKnowledgeNetwork
  opening_problem: >-
    Represent the competence needed to govern, witness, record, correspond, account,
    teach, worship, administer castles and tenencias, sustain routes and supply,
    preserve memory, and transmit regional practice without inventing universal
    literacy, a mature chancery, fixed schools, libraries, archives, famous scholars,
    later municipal institutions, or automatic technology progress.
  player_promise: >-
    Build durable but uneven capacity through identified people, relationships,
    practice, texts, records, materials, travel, patronage, access, protection,
    and preservation while accepting loss, distortion, scarcity, regional
    difference, interrupted transmission, and incomplete recovery.
  opening_state_readiness: HOLD
  exact_opening_education_instances_created: 0
```

Aragon's educational system is not one building, one court, one church, one literacy percentage, or one technology meter. Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza may contain different holders, routes, practices, institutions, languages, registers, access conditions, and transmission risks. Regional labels create no competence by themselves.
