### 1. Executive Decision

Aragon has a real Faction Presentation owner gap. The current package contains substantial player-facing material, but that material is distributed across Realm identity, Realm Personality and AI, Opening State, Campaign Experience, Governance, Law, Education, Historical systems, geography, military, economy, routes, and ecclesiastical content. No current Aragon authority owns the complete presentation grammar that determines how accepted owner state is selected, distinguished, explained, mapped, narrated, heard, localized, made accessible, sourced, versioned, and prepared for later implementation.

The gap survives the overlap test because presentation direction is not merely a summary of existing authorities. It requires one bounded owner for faction-selection identity, visual hierarchy, anti-flattening rules, map-state grammar, uncertainty and owner traceability, narrative voice, terminology, Codex routing, audio direction, localization, accessibility, evidence and rights controls, asset metadata, persistence, determinism, performance requirements, and phased Unreal Engine 5.8 handoff.

### 6. Overlap Finding: Realm Identity, Personality, and AI

The current Realm and AI material already defines the strategic identity that presentation must express: a founder-dependent polity of Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza shaped by survival, recognition, and southward opportunity. It also supplies player explanations, proposals, alternatives, abstention, and uncertainty.

Presentation must consume that identity without absorbing AI observations, weights, proposal logic, or decision authority. A strong visual or narrative theme cannot become a personality input, selected objective, command, or result.

### 13. Developed-Realm Comparator Result

Developed presentation authorities for Genoa, Sicily Muslim, and the Hammadids demonstrate that a Realm presentation authority is a substantive owner rather than a cosmetic wrapper. Their recurring scope includes faction selection, visual thesis, map and system grammar, uncertainty, owner traceability, narrative, audio, localization, accessibility, evidence, rights, asset metadata, persistence, determinism, performance, and phased implementation gates.

Aragon has equally mature substantive inputs but lacks that owner. The comparator therefore strengthens the gap finding. It does not authorize copying another Realm's visual identity, battlefield silhouettes, palette, symbol, screen structure, narrative archetype, or audio language.
