### 11. Opening Briefing Direction

#### Briefing title

**The Three Mountain Lands**

#### Directional long briefing

**Aragon enters the winter of 1062 as a compact monarchy held together by Ramiro and by relationships that are real but not yet durable enough to be taken for granted. Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza share one Realm context, yet each retains its own routes, institutions, customs, government questions, military needs, and patterns of recognition. Jaca is the principal royal center, not proof that every later urban, legal, ecclesiastical, or royal development has already occurred.**

**The first task is continuity. The household, the two distinct Sanchos, the court, command responsibilities, castle records, regional reporting, and home defense must become more resilient without turning preparation into guaranteed succession or review into invented rivalry. The Realm's limited people, recruitment pools, money, equipment, routes, supplies, messengers, and institutional competence must support one another rather than disappear into one strength number.**

**Beyond the core, passes and external contacts offer information, access, protection, negotiation, and risk. To the south, Graus and other frontier possibilities may invite inquiry, preparation, action, restraint, or nonoccurrence. A battlefield victory does not supply an occupation plan, and a historical date does not command the player to repeat history.**

**Hold the three lands together. Build what can outlast the founder. Choose which opportunities are worth their cost, and leave Aragon capable of recovery when an answer fails.**
