### 1. Strong Aragon Health Verdict

Aragon's health model must treat health as an owner-routed lifecycle rather than one Realm meter. Risk, exposure, observed symptoms, assessment, diagnosis, care eligibility, provider and material availability, treatment, monitoring, recovery, long-term effects, death, burial, and public consequences remain separate states.

The three lands, Jaca, households, institutions, routes, garrisons, field forces, camps, displaced people, and frontier operations may face different constraints. A store of grain does not prove household food access. A water source does not prove safe water. An institution does not create a practitioner. A building does not create care. Money does not create medicine, competence, transport, or recovery.
