### 1. Strong Aragon Information and Intelligence Verdict

Aragon requires a bounded Realm-level owner for information requirements, source classes, collection requests, provenance, authentication, confidence, contradiction, freshness, assessment, warnings, dissemination, source handling, compromise, counterintelligence, deception interfaces, and agent-operation eligibility. It must not duplicate personal relationships, messages, routes, military reconnaissance execution, diplomatic consent, Realm AI, or historical truth.

The model is sparse, directed, evidence-ranked, actor-visible, and place-specific. Information is never universal truth. Intelligence is an assessment made from accessible evidence. A source is not a relationship, a message is not delivery, reconnaissance eligibility is not a mission, and an agent definition is not a person.
