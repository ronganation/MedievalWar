### 1. Strong Aragon Custody Verdict

Aragon shall model every prisoner, captive, hostage, detained person, released person, and returning person as a person-centered custody case rather than as inventory, currency, a battlefield reward, or diplomatic leverage detached from human state. Capture, identity, legal status, custody, guard, location, access, care, communication, personal effects, ransom, exchange, release, return, reintegration, and recovery remain distinct owner-routed states.

The authority coordinates the complete case lifecycle while leaving personal identity with Character owners, capture and guard execution with Military, law and release authority with Law and Governance, money and property with economic owners, health and burial with Health, claims and messages with Information and Routes, external consent with Diplomacy and external owners, and player-facing meaning with Campaign Experience and Presentation.
