### 1. Executive Decision

A genuine Aragon Realm-content gap survives the overlap review. Current Character, Law, Governance, Military, Economy, Health, Information, Public Order, Campaign Experience, and external-owner authorities each own essential inputs or consequences, but none owns the complete custody lifecycle from capture or surrender through identity, custody, status, care, communication, representation, transfer, ransom or exchange terms, release, return, reintegration, recovery, and unresolved nonreturn.

A dedicated authority is therefore justified. It must treat every prisoner, captive, or hostage as a person under owner-routed custody and legal, material, health, diplomatic, and relationship constraints. A person is never a resource, inventory item, ransom value, diplomatic token, or automatic military reward.
