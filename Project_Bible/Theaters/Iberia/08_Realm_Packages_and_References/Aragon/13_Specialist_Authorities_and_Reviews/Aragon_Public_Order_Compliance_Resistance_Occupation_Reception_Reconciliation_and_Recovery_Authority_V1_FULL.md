### 1. Strong Aragon Public Order Verdict

Aragon's public order is not one happiness value. It is the changing, actor-specific response of people, households, communities, institutions, officeholders, service constituencies, military constituencies, settlements, and regional political surfaces to authority, adjudication, burden, protection, communication, conduct, material conditions, historical experience, and available remedies.

Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza may respond differently to the same royal proposal because authority, custom, routes, evidence, burdens, property questions, command arrangements, and institutional mediation differ. Difference does not imply disloyalty. Delay does not imply refusal. Refusal does not imply resistance. Resistance eligibility does not create resistance. Resistance does not automatically become rebellion. Suppression does not establish acceptance or recovery.
