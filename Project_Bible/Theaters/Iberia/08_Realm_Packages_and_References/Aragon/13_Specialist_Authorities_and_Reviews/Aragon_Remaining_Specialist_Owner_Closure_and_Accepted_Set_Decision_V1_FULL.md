### 1. Executive Closure Decision

The remaining Aragon specialist-owner set is closed at static authority scope. The targeted modernization sequence has established or reconciled the necessary Realm ownership surfaces for Education, Campaign Experience, Faction Presentation, Public Order, Health, Settlement Development, Information and Intelligence, Custody, and Diplomacy without reopening closed Military or Historical domains and without entering Opening State or implementation work.

The accepted set is sufficient to authorize copy-first additive successors for the Aragon README and Current Authority Index. This closure does not authorize the Knowledge, Technology, Adoption, Reform, and Realm Development Portfolio yet. Package navigation and authority indexing must be reconciled first, including the bounded settlement-construction conformance remedy and exact accepted-path verification.
