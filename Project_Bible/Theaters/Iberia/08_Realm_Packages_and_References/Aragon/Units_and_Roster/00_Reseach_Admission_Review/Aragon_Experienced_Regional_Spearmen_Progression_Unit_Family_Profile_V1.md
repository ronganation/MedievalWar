```yaml

```

Experienced Regional Spearmen are a conditional progression family drawn from selected regional sources that preserve survivors, cadres, leaders, equipment, and repeated formation practice.

The family must coexist with Regional Spear and Shield Service. Aragon’s ordinary regional muster remains essential, while only a narrow subset of sources can support this more reliable formation.
