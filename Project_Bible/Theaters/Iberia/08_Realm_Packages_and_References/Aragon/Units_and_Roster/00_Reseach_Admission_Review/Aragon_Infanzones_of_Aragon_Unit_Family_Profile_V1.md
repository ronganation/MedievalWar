The term infanzón is historically associated with lower nobility in Aragon. Comparative material also indicates that northern Aragon’s legal distinction between infanzones and villanos emerged in the eleventh century. Those points support researching and using the term, but do not prove that a standardized battlefield unit named “Infanzones of Aragon” existed in January 1062. citeturn59search4turn59search5turn59search8

The card therefore remains a strong player-facing candidate whose exact opening sources, military obligations, horses, equipment, and terminology require later owner admission.
