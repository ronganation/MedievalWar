## 1. Profile Verdict

```yaml

profile_disposition: RetainConditionalAsLimitedLocalMissileFamilyCandidate
record_kind: CommunitySettlementCastleAndHuntingMissileFamily
opening_admission: NotAuthorized

not_longbow_or_crossbow_family: true
not_unlimited_missile_support: true
```

Local Archers provide Aragon with limited missile pressure, screening, harassment, siege defense, and strongpoint support. The family is assembled from exact local sources that possess usable bows, arrows, practice, leadership, and valid service access.

The family must remain modest, uneven, source-bound, and ammunition constrained. It should broaden Aragon’s battlefield options without creating a mature professional missile institution at the opening.
