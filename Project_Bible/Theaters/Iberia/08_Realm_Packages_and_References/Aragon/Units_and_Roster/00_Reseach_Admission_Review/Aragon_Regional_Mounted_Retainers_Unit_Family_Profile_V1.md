## 1. Profile Verdict

```yaml
working_family_name: Regional Mounted Retainers
profile_disposition: RetainConditionalAsScarceRegionalMountedFamilyCandidate

not_generic_jinetes_or_later_cavalry: true
```

Regional Mounted Retainers represent scarce mounted service contributed by separate regional households, lordships, castles, and related owners across Aragon’s Three-Land military environment.

The family survives only as a distinct card if regional ownership, negotiated service, uneven quality, home-defense burdens, command friction, and a flank-support role produce a play pattern meaningfully different from Royal Household Mounted Retainers.
