## 1. Profile Verdict

```yaml
working_family_name: Regional Spear and Shield Service
profile_disposition: RetainAsEssentialNativeCoreFamilyCandidate
record_kind: SharedUnitFamilyWithRegionalExpressions
opening_admission: NotAuthorized
historical_classification: DesignInterpretationBoundedByExistingAragonAuthority
core_fantasy: FiniteRegionalMusterThatFormsAragonsDefensiveBattleLine
not_a_professional_standing_corps: true
not_a_universal_peasant_mass: true
not_three_separate_units_by_default: true
```

This family is Aragon’s broad native infantry foundation. It represents spear-and-shield service assembled from distinct communities, settlements, valleys, castles, households, and local obligations across Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, and Ribagorza.

The family must feel useful enough to form a normal campaign army while remaining finite, regionally sourced, unevenly equipped, politically assembled, and costly to keep away from home.


The family’s purpose is not to win battles through raw quality. Its purpose is to give Aragon a dependable defensive line when the Realm successfully assembles people, shields, spears, local leaders, routes, and supplies.
