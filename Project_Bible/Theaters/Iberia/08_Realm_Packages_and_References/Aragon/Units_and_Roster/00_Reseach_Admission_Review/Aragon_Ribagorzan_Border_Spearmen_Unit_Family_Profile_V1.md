Ribagorzan Border Spearmen are a flexible frontier spear family assembled from validated eastern-valley, castle, route, settlement, and regional defense sources.

The card survives only if limited screening, raid response, formation transition, and frontier service create a meaningful battlefield and campaign distinction from Aragon's conventional, mountain, and dedicated javelin families.
