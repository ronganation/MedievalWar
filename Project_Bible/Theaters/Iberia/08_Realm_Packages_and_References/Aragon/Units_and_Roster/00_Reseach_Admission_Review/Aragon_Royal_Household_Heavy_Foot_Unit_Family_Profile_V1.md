
Royal Household Heavy Foot are Aragon’s scarce household foot reserve. The family concentrates a small number of eligible household retainers, leaders, shields, weapons, and better protection where actually available.

The unit’s value lies in dependable close combat, command protection, line stabilization, relief, and controlled counterattack. Its field use must reduce household and royal protection elsewhere.
