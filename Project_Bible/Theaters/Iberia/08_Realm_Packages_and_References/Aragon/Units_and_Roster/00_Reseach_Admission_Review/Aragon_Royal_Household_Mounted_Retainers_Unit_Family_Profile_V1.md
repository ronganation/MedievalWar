## 1. Profile Verdict

```yaml
working_family_name: Royal Household Mounted Retainers
profile_disposition: RetainAsScarceSignatureMountedFamilyCandidate

opening_admission: NotAuthorized

```

Royal Household Mounted Retainers are Aragon’s scarce signature mounted reserve. The family represents riders, trained mounts, equipment, attendants, and command relationships released from the royal household for a bounded military purpose.

The profile must deliver a satisfying mounted decision without giving Aragon mass cavalry, free character escorts, or later knightly institutions at the opening.
