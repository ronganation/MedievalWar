Jaca Muster Spearmen are Aragon's benchmark Early line infantry. The family represents eligible contributors assembled through Jaca-facing communities, settlements, routes, castles, local leaders, stores, and royal concentration interfaces.

The unit must be dependable enough to form the ordinary center of an Aragonese field army while remaining finite, uneven, locally burdened, and less capable than selected regional or campaign-developed spear families.

## 24A. Early-to-High Carryover: The Ordinary Spear Line

This is a proposed design integration, not certification of a uniform historical corps. Jaca Muster remains the first proper spear-and-shield line above Community Levy. It does not expire when the High Era begins. Its muster identity can mature through actual stores, compatible equipment, leaders, practice, service arrangements, and recovery while retaining broad ordinary field service.

```yaml
EarlyBaseline:
  equipment: ServiceableSpearsShieldsAndUnevenSourceValidProtection
  organization: LocalContingentsAssembledThroughJacaFacingRoutesAndLeaders
  battle_role: DressLineBraceHoldCoverAnchorRelieveAndWithdraw
HighMatureMuster:
  equipment: BetterMaintainedSpearsShieldsAndProtectionWhereActuallyAvailable
  organization: BetterInspectionCadresSignalsStoresRotationAndReturnAccounting
  battle_role: SameBaselineLineWithMoreReliablePreparationAndHandoffsWhereAchieved
HighDepletedMuster:
  equipment: OlderMixedOrIncompleteIssueMayPersist
  organization: ReducedCadresPracticeAndAssemblyAfterLossOrDisruption
  battle_role: ShorterFrontageReducedMissionOrLocalDefenseAccordingToReadiness
calendar_creates_improvement: false
research_creates_equipment_or_people: false
experience_alone_creates_new_card: false
automatic_obsolescence: false
```

A practiced muster may hold and withdraw better without becoming elite. Better protection does not grant heavy assault, universal charge reflection, or professional endurance. Selected Old Aragon depth, Sobrarbe approach control, and Ribagorzan frontier response remain separate decisions.

## 24B. Civic Development: A Real Possibility, Not a Promised Upgrade

A civic connection is useful when recurring local institutions begin to organize equipment, service, leaders, and defense more consistently. That can initially be an achieved source expression within Jaca Muster. The same card can draw from a better-organized settlement without a second roster entry.


If the proposed civic spearmen merely have better armor or a different label, keep the improvement inside Jaca Muster. A genuinely distinct civic institution may coexist with the muster rather than replace it everywhere. Independence and union are not universal spear-versus-sword equipment choices.
