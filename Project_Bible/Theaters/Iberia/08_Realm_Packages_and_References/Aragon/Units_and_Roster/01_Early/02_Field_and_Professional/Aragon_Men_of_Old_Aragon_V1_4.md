## 23A. Early-to-High Selected Spear Carryover

The following is a project design proposal, not historical certification of a named corps. Men of Old Aragon remain the selected conventional spear-depth formation as the campaign develops. High Era status alone neither upgrades the card nor makes it obsolete.

```yaml
EarlySelectedCore:
  equipment: SelectedServiceableSpearsSubstantialShieldsAndMixedSourceValidProtection
  organization: RecognizedCadresWorkingFrontImmediateDepthAndActualReliefBody

```

The High Era advantage should be a more dependable relief cycle when actual capacity supports it, not an unexplained armor tier. Rotation still redistributes fatigue rather than resetting it. A depleted formation can remain physically present while losing its defining depth and relief capability.

## 23B. Civic and Institutional Development Without Roster Collapse

A civic connection may improve equipment custody, recurring service, or cadre support in either family without creating another card. It does not automatically make Men of Old Aragon the civic successor to every Jaca formation.

## 31I. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Select sources that can provide more than ordinary muster readiness.
2. Assemble and inspect people, leaders, cadres, equipment, supply, and relief depth.
3. Divide the formation into working frontage, immediate depth, relief body, and reserve only where actual capacity passes.
4. Hold the critical conventional section while Jaca Muster covers broader ordinary frontage.
5. Use immediate depth to close local gaps without wasting the full relief body.
6. Prepare and signal a relief handoff before the engaged section collapses.
7. Replace exhausted files while preserving missile withdrawal and mounted counterattack lanes.
8. Reconstitute the withdrawn body rather than treating rotation as instant recovery.
9. Apply short controlled pressure only when depth, flanks, leaders, and fatigue permit.
10. Use remaining depth to preserve an orderly withdrawal if the wider battle turns.
11. After action, determine whether the source remains selected, falls back to Jaca Muster capacity, or closes for recovery.
