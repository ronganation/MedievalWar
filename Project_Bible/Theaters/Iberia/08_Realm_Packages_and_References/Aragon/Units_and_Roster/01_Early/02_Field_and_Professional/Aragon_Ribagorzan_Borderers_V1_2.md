Ribagorzan Borderers are light mixed frontier infantry assembled from exact valley, settlement, household, route, crossing, castle-related, and repeat-service sources. Their limited javelins, light spears, shields, and conditional close weapons support raid response, escort, route control, and brief defensive contact. They do not become full skirmishers and full spear infantry simultaneously.

Their distinct decision is whether limited screening and a vulnerable gathering phase can protect a route, convoy, flank, or withdrawal before local duty requires recall.

## 26A. Early-to-High Mixed Frontier Carryover

This is a proposed game-design integration, not historical certification of a named corps. The family persists because frontier response, escorts, route security, and withdrawal protection remain useful. High Era development improves actual capability rather than automatically replacing Borderers with professional spearmen or Almughavars.

```yaml
EarlyBorderers:
  equipment: LimitedJavelinsLightSpearsPracticalShieldsAndSourceValidCloseWeapons
  organization: LocalFilesLeadersWarningFallbackAndVulnerableGathering
  role: ScreenCastRetireGatherHedgeEscortAndReturn
HighDevelopedBorderers:
  equipment: BetterMaintainedIssueStoresReplacementAndProtectionWhereAvailable
  organization: MoreReliableWarningSignalsAmmunitionControlGatheringPracticeAndRecallAdministration
  role: SameMixedResponseRoleWithBetterSupportedTransitionsWhereAchieved
HighDepletedBorderers:
  equipment: OlderIncompleteOrAmmunitionPoorIssueCanPersist
  organization: LostCadresRoutesOrSupportRestrictCommonControl
  role: ReducedEscortObservationLocalDefenseOrRecoveryAccordingToReadiness
calendar_creates_improvement: false
technology_creates_people_equipment_ammunition_or_warning: false
experience_alone_creates_new_card: false
automatic_obsolescence: false
```

Better practice reduces avoidable disorder but does not make gathering safe under fast contact. More dependable ammunition supply does not grant unlimited volleys. Better equipment does not turn a rough hedge into Jaca's broad spear line or Old Aragon's layered relief formation.

## 34H. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Receive warning and verify that the mission belongs to frontier response, escort, route defense, raid interception, relief, or withdrawal cover.
2. Assemble exact local files and allocate limited javelins, light spears, shields, close weapons, leaders, and signals.
3. Move by a validated route without scattering the formation across unrelated approaches.
4. Deploy a loose screen only where observation, spacing, fallback, and ammunition permit it.
5. Cast one or more limited volleys according to actual ammunition and command.
6. Retire before fast contact closes the withdrawal lane.
7. Gather at the known point, recover facing, and reorganize shield and spear files.
8. Form a rough hedge or route block for brief contact while stronger troops, mounted response, or withdrawal completes its mission.
9. Apply short local counterpressure only to disordered raiders or exposed light infantry.
10. Avoid broad sustained melee and deep pursuit.
11. Cover friendly withdrawal, escort, or relief movement if required.
12. Return when recalled and restore frontier duty, equipment, ammunition, routes, and households.
