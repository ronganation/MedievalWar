## 1. Refined Definitive Unit Verdict

**Aragonese Levy Crossbowmen are Early Aragon's basic protected crossbow mass: locally sourced operators with mixed but serviceable weapons who can establish a defended firing position, hold or release deliberate bolts against worthwhile targets, reload under protection, and withdraw before enemy contact destroys the formation.**

The unit is intentionally ordinary. It does not need professional file rotation, a perfect synchronized volley, pavises, advanced mechanisms, or a rigid one-shot gimmick to matter. Some files may be loaded while others span, aim, recover, fail, or move. The formation can deliver a concentrated first release when prepared, but its everyday battlefield work is slower, uneven pressure from behind spears, walls, gates, crossings, camp defenses, and terrain.

The player recruits Levy Crossbowmen because several low-threshold detachments can create affordable bolt volume and force armored or committed troops to respect a defended lane. The player preserves later Aragonese Crossbowmen for missions requiring better target control, reload organization, field movement, reform, and endurance. If the levy formation is repeatedly moved, suppressed, flanked, or exposed during reload, its mixed readiness deteriorates rapidly and the correct order becomes withdrawal rather than another shot.

## 2A. Refined Power Contract

Under comparable range, angle, protection, visibility, and target exposure, Aragonese Levy Crossbowmen should:

- provide stronger immediate pressure against suitable protected targets than the basic Aragonese bow family;
- make a dense infantry or cavalry approach through a spear-held lane genuinely dangerous;
- create useful aggregate volume when several separately sourced detachments are concentrated;
- remain effective after the first release when protection, ammunition, local leadership, and enough ready files survive;
- perform especially well in bounded defense where movement and flank exposure are limited;
- lose efficiency sharply under suppression, repeated movement, poor passage, bolt mismatch, or frontage collapse.

They should not:

- maintain bow-like firing tempo;
- match Jaca Bowmen in organized bow control and movement recovery;
- match Aragonese Crossbowmen in reload organization, target economy, reform, and campaign endurance;
- match Crown Crossbow Sergeants in controlled salvoes, counter-missile discipline, collective command, or withdrawal order;
- match Pavise Crossbowmen in directional protection and sustained static position endurance;
- guarantee penetration, horse kills, morale shock, or cavalry stopping power.

## 2B. Refined One-Line Player Instruction

**Put them behind spears, hold for armor or a committed approach, let the mixed files shoot as they become ready, and leave before the reload space becomes the front line.**

## 3. Player Experience and Combat Fantasy

The player places Levy Crossbowmen behind a spear line or prepared obstacle and waits. Arrows may be spent more freely, but these bolts are held for the moment when armored infantry compresses against the frontage or cavalry commits to a predictable approach. The crossbow files step forward, loose, and fall back to reload. If the protecting line holds, another uneven but dangerous release follows. If cavalry breaks through, the reload space becomes a killing ground.

**Combat fantasy:** Several modest crossbow companies do not look impressive individually, but concentrated behind spears they can make an armored advance pay heavily before being swept aside or withdrawn.

**Flavor line:** **Hold the bolts. Let them come close.**

## 4C. Cross-Faction Benchmark Adaptation

The mature English levy benchmark demonstrates that a weak domestic crossbow option remains finite, equipment-led, workshop-dependent, and distinct from professional or mercenary troops. The Norman militia benchmark demonstrates a readiness spectrum from emergency mixed detachments through recurring local crews and shows that local crossbow duty is strongest around walls, gates, crossings, camps, and protected firing positions. The French trained crossbow benchmark reserves coordinated movement, reliable reform, steadier equipment issue, and moderate campaign endurance for the next institutional rung. Crown Crossbow Sergeants reserve selected section control, joint command, security, sustained position management, and professional displacement for the composite High Era card.

Aragon therefore keeps the lowest threshold and most brittle battle cycle. It gains useful mass, not mature organization.

## 11. Signature Battle Cycle

```text
Inspect operators, crossbows, strings, mechanisms, spanning methods, bolts, leaders, lanes, protection, and withdrawal.
Take position behind spears, works, terrain, walls, or another credible frontage.
Load before contact where time and safety permit.
Hold the shot against scattered, distant, obscured, or low-value targets.
Present limited files when a dense, armored, mounted, committed, or otherwise valuable target enters the lane.
Loose without assuming perfect accuracy, penetration, or morale shock.
Recover behind protection.
Span and reload through the actual method and available space.
Repeat only while bolts, weapons, operators, leaders, protection, time, and withdrawal still pass.
Withdraw before cavalry or formed infantry enters the reload position.
Account for people, weapons, bolts, tools, property, and source losses.
```

## 27. Tactical Scenarios

### Armored infantry approaching spears

The crossbowmen hold loaded files until the armored body commits to the spear frontage. Several levy formations release into the compressed target. The spears, not the crossbowmen, receive contact. If the frontage breaks, the crossbowmen withdraw immediately.

### Cavalry approach

The formation can punish cavalry riding through a predictable lane, particularly when spears deny direct access. The bolts may kill riders or horses, cause local hesitation, or do little. If cavalry reaches the reload line, the crossbowmen are in severe danger.

### Settlement defense

Crossbow files use a gate, barricade, broad street, courtyard edge, or wall assignment with nearby bolt stores and fallback space. This is one of the unit's strongest environments.

### Failed deployment

The player exposes the formation in open ground against faster enemies. The first release is incomplete, the files cannot recover safely, and the equipment pool suffers heavily. The card is affordable, but replacing crossbows and experienced operators remains slower than replacing ordinary bodies.
