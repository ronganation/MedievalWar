High Valley Javelinmen provide Early Aragon with a distinct foot-skirmishing card. The family represents selected contributors from validated high-valley, frontier, route-defense, and related sources that can assemble people, javelins, limited protection, local leaders, and practiced withdrawal behavior.

The unit is designed for screening, disruption, ambush, and broken-ground support. It does not claim that all Aragonese valley communities field formal javelin units in January 1062.

## 23A. Early-to-High Dedicated Skirmisher Carryover

This is proposed project design, not certification of a named historical corps. High Valley Javelinmen remain useful as dedicated short-range foot skirmishers when the High Era begins. Better stores, equipment care, leaders, and practice can improve the same family without giving it sustained melee, unlimited ammunition, or automatic obsolescence.

```yaml
EarlyScreen:
  equipment: FiniteJavelinsOptionalSmallShieldsLightProtectionAndSourceValidSidearms
  organization: LocalLooseFilesWithPracticedCastAndWithdrawal
  mission: ScreenApproachCastDisruptEvadeAndReform
HighPracticedScreen:
  equipment: BetterInspectedIssueReplacementAndProtectionWhereAvailable
  organization: MoreReliableTargetChoiceVolleyTimingSpacingSignalsAndReceptionCoordination
  mission: SameDedicatedScreenWithBetterSupportedWithdrawalWhereAchieved
HighDepletedScreen:
  equipment: OlderIncompleteOrAmmunitionPoorIssueCanPersist
  organization: LostLeadersPracticeOrSupportRestrictSafeScreening
  mission: ReducedLocalScreenObservationWithdrawalOrRecoveryAccordingToReadiness
calendar_creates_improvement: false
research_creates_people_javelins_or_practice: false
experience_creates_equipment: false
automatic_obsolescence: false
```

The practical reward is a better chance of using scarce missiles on a worthwhile target and escaping intact. That improvement is conditional, not guaranteed accuracy, evasion, or surprise. Heavy protection that obstructs movement or increases fatigue is not a free upgrade for a unit whose value depends on approach and withdrawal.

- **Unit:** High Valley Javelinmen
- **Class:** Regional light foot skirmishers

## 31I. Battlefield Signature Cycle and Contact Discipline

1. Move ahead or to the flank of the main Aragonese line only when a withdrawal route exists.
2. Use warning, terrain, concealment, or a protected approach without gaining magical stealth.
3. Hold javelins until the target enters a worthwhile short-range window.
4. Cast against exposed infantry, vulnerable flanks, disrupted troops, or a mounted approach where timing and angle pass.
5. Use the disruption window to force hesitation, disorder, shield response, or premature commitment.
6. Evade before fresh cavalry or formed infantry reaches sustained contact.
7. Withdraw through Jaca, Sobrarbe, or Old Aragon spear frontage where lanes are prepared. Borderers may provide brief local cover only when their actual hedge and gathering state permit; they are not equivalent main-line reception.
8. Reform if leaders, spacing, ammunition, and protection remain.
9. Cover withdrawal or hold a route only if the remaining javelins, terrain, and escape plan justify it.
10. Account for survivors, equipment, expended missiles, local-defense damage, and cadre continuity.
