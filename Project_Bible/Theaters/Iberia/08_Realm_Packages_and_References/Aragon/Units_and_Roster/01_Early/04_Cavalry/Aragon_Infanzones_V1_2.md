- **Unit:** Infanzones of Aragon
- **Class:** Upper-Early lesser-noble countercharge cavalry
- **Source:** Exact infanzón or otherwise admitted lesser-noble households, eligible riders, better regional mounts, lances or mounted spears, shields, protection, leaders, retinue support, service relationships, and political release
- **Availability:** Very scarce, politically conditional, household-bound, horse- and equipment-limited, release-controlled, recall-sensitive, and slow to recover
- **Battle promise:** Remain in reserve behind a stable infantry or cavalry screen, meet a committed enemy mounted attack, win or stabilize an ordinary cavalry melee, and deliver one controlled shock action against an exposed target
- **Best use:** Countercharge, protected reserve, cavalry melee, support for regional command, exploiting an enemy fixed by infantry, and controlled pursuit
- **Worst use:** Routine screening, messenger work, broad escort duty, frontal attack against braced spears, repeated unsupported charges, rough ground, and prolonged combat with superior heavy cavalry
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Releases politically important people, horses, armor, lances, retainers, household protection, local mounted response, bargaining capital, and recovery capacity
- **Counterplay:** Prepared spears, missiles, difficult ground, baited charges, stronger heavy cavalry, attacks that force household retention, horse-support interdiction, and political recall
- **Roster progression:** Aragonese Riders handle routine light-cavalry utility; Infanzones handle serious countercharge and ordinary cavalry melee; Royal Mesnada remains the exceptional decisive royal reserve

## 24A. Early-to-High Countercharge Carryover

The following is a proposed campaign integration, not a new historical finding. Infanzones retain the lesser-noble countercharge and cavalry-melee role wherever their sources survive. The High Era does not automatically make them obsolete or transform their service into the developed Aragonese Nobles system.

```yaml
EarlyCountercharge:
  equipment: SourceValidLancesSpearsShieldsMixedProtectionAndSuitableMounts
  organization: HouseholdServiceLeadersMountedPracticeReserveAndRallyControl
  role: ObserveCommitmentCounterchargeFightNecessaryMeleeBreakOffAndRally
HighPracticedInfanzones:
  equipment: BetterMaintainedMountsTackWeaponsAndProtectionWhereActuallyAvailable
  organization: BetterCadresCommitmentJudgmentRallyServiceCoordinationAndRecovery
  role: SameBoundedCounterchargeFamilyAlongsideDevelopedNobleCavalry
HighDepletedInfanzones:
  equipment: OlderPartialOrDamagedIssueCanPersist
  organization: HorseCadreRetinueOrRelationshipLossRestrictsCommitment
  role: ReducedReserveSupportOrRecoveryAccordingToActualReadiness
calendar_creates_improvement: false
research_creates_people_horses_armor_or_service: false
experience_creates_noble_status: false
automatic_obsolescence: false
```

The reward is a more reliable commitment and rally when actual resources support it, not guaranteed impact or unlimited cycle charging. An experienced formation with exhausted mounts or broken political terms may remain less usable than a newly assembled but well-supported one.

## 24B. Connection to Aragonese Nobles

The strongest carryover connection is a conditional development of eligible noble mounted sources into the existing High Era Nobles family. It is not a universal upgrade button. Sources can retain bounded Infanzon service, develop the fuller noble system, or become unavailable through losses and political change.

```yaml
RetainInfanzonService:
  result: PersistentCounterchargeFamilyWithAchievedReadiness
DevelopIntoAragoneseNobles:
  requires:
    - ReceivingNobleSourceAndServiceEligibility
    - ActualSuitableMountsProtectionLancesSidearmsAndSupport
    - DevelopedMountedContestShockPressAndBattleWingReformPractice
    - LeadersRetinueServiceDurationAndRecoveryCapacity
    - OriginalDutyReleaseAndReservationReconciliation
  result: ActualQualifiedPeopleAndPropertyEnterExistingDevelopedNobleFamily
  automatic: false
Coexistence:
  basis: DifferentEligibleSourcesServiceTermsEquipmentAndCommitmentDepth
  same_people_in_both: prohibited
SourceSpecificReplacement:
  condition: ActualInstitutionAbsorbsSourcePeoplePropertyRoleAndRecovery
  treatment: CloseOldRecruitmentForThatSourceAndResolveSurvivingService
  realm_wide_deletion: false
```

Infanzones answer a bounded mounted crisis; developed Nobles can sustain a more demanding mounted wing, controlled press, and reform where their own support passes. Better armor alone is insufficient to claim the latter institution. A veteran Infanzon formation may outperform a depleted Noble formation in a particular engagement; family labels are not absolute performance ceilings.
