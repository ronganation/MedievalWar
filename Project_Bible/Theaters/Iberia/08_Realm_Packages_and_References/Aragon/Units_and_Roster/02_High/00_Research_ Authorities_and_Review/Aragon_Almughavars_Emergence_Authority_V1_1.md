## 19. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Approach through an authorized raid, campaign, frontier, or expeditionary context.
2. Identify a route, camp, position, flank, rear, missile body, or isolated target worth disrupting.
3. Use fieldcraft, limited thrown weapons, speed, terrain, or allied pressure to create local disorder.
4. Close on an exposed, constrained, isolated, fatigued, or disordered target.
5. Sustain aggressive small-group pressure beyond the screening window owned by lighter skirmishers.
6. Exploit the local dislocation without becoming trapped in a broad heavy-infantry grind.
7. Break off, regroup, continue the mission, or withdraw according to command, supply, and objective.
8. Rally, account, recover, and return or continue campaign service.
