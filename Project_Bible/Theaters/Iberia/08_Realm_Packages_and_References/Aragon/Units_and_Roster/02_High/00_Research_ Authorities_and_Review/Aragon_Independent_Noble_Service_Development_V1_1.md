## 1. Controlling Decision

Independent Aragon can mature native noble mounted service around the canonical Shared High Era Aragonese Nobles card. This authority improves political release, retinue organization, horse and equipment support, developed mounted-wing practice, return, and recovery. It creates no duplicate card and does not automatically convert every Infanzon or Royal Mesnada contributor.

## 3A. Roster Carryover and Noble-Service Relationship Register

The Independent path does not replace Aragon's Early roster. Persistent families continue through their own sources and may improve within their existing cards. Only exact qualified contributors may transfer into developed noble service, and every successful transfer reduces the originating source's available capacity.
