## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Armored Jaca Spearmen** are Independent Aragon's developed High Era spear-and-shield center. The formation arises where Jaca-facing town, city, route, store, craft, leadership, and muster institutions can repeatedly concentrate selected contributors, sturdier shields, maintained spears, helmets, padding, and meaningful mail without inventing people or stripping every local duty.

The unit is not merely Jaca Muster Spearmen with higher armor statistics. Jaca Muster Spearmen remain the broad, affordable conventional field line. Armored Jaca Spearmen trade breadth, speed of replacement, and low cost for protected frontage, stronger missile endurance, more dependable mounted denial, deliberate line movement, and better reform after sustained contact.

## 9. Signature Battle Cycle: Cover, Brace, Endure, Advance, Reform

1. Assemble only after people, equipment, cadres, service, settlement support, and protected source minimums pass.
2. Deploy as the protected center or as the frontage guarding Crossbowmen and a mounted counterattack lane.
3. **Cover:** maintain shield and spear order while friendly missiles deploy, reload, or withdraw.
4. **Brace:** prepare against a mounted or infantry approach only when facing, footing, cohesion, and warning pass.
5. **Endure:** absorb manageable missile and melee pressure without treating armor as immunity.
6. **Advance:** move deliberately after the enemy is fixed, disordered, depleted, or forced to yield ground.
7. Halt before the line outruns flanks, missile support, or mounted protection.
8. **Reform:** restore spear spacing, shield contact, leaders, and working frontage before another commitment.
9. Withdraw in order when fatigue, flanking, heavy missiles, armor-piercing infantry, or support failure removes the formation's advantage.

## 17P. Final Player Fantasy, Description, and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Build the armored civic spear line that lets Independent Aragon stand in front of its crossbows, receive the enemy's mounted blow, and then walk the surviving formation forward instead of merely waiting behind a shield.

**Short description:** Better-protected Jaca spear infantry organized through developed town and city muster institutions to hold the center, shelter missile troops, and advance in controlled order.

**Encyclopedia description:** Independent Aragon can develop selected Jaca-facing sources beyond the broad ordinary muster. Where towns, cities, stores, workshops, records, cadres, routes, and service relationships converge, a narrower body of spearmen can be equipped and practiced for sustained protected frontage. Their stronger shields, improved helmets, layered protection, meaningful mail presence, and better file control allow them to endure pressure that would disorder an ordinary muster. They remain citizens, household contributors, and regional servicemen rather than a standing professional regiment.

Armored Jaca Spearmen should be placed where Aragon cannot permit its center to fold. They cover Crossbowmen, meet cavalry from a prepared front, hold against ordinary infantry pressure, and advance slowly when missile fire or mounted action has fixed the enemy. Their armor, however, increases fatigue, repair needs, replacement difficulty, and the cost of every casualty. Axemen, crossbows, flank attacks, broken ground, blocked withdrawal, and prolonged compressed melee remain decisive counters.

**Flavor line:** **Jaca musters the line. Independent Aragon teaches it to stand armored and move as one.**
