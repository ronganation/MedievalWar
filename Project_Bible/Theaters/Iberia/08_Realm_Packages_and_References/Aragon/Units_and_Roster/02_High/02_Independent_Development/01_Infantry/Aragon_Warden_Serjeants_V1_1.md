## 1. Decisive Unit Verdict

**Warden Serjeants of Aragon** are Independent Aragon's developed High Era strongpoint infantry. They emerge only where an exact castle or fortress can sustain a more reliable recurring body of appointed personnel, section leaders, maintained defensive equipment, post relief, internal movement, breach response, and bounded local service beyond the ordinary Warden duty roster.

They do not replace Castle Wardens. Wardens remain the broad custody, watch, warning, key, store, and prepared-position family. Warden Serjeants are the narrower formed response body used when ordinary post defense is no longer sufficient: the gate reserve, breach-containment line, courtyard counterattack, wall relief, internal-route security, and short controlled sortie formation.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Player Card

- **Unit:** Warden Serjeants of Aragon
- **Class:** High Era professional strongpoint infantry
- **Source:** Qualified Warden cadres, separately appointed strongpoint personnel, released castle-linked fighters, section leaders, maintained weapons, shields, protection, stores, routes, and recovery capacity from one exact developed strongpoint
- **Battle promise:** Hold the inner defense together when a gate, breach, wall access, courtyard, store route, or local sortie becomes the decisive fight
- **Best use:** Gate reserve, breach containment, courtyard counterattack, internal choke defense, relieving ordinary Wardens, protecting Crossbow positions and stores, securing a captured entrance, and short sorties with a clear recall line
- **Worst use:** Broad open-field frontage, long foreign campaigns, deep pursuit, first-wave siege assault, cheap occupation duty, or deployment that strips the parent strongpoint below safe custody and relief floors
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Converts a limited portion of strongpoint personnel, equipment, cadres, relief, stores, quarters, and readiness into a harder local response formation while increasing the cost of casualties and absence
- **Counterplay:** Simultaneous breaches, fires, crossfire, blocked internal routes, cadre loss, fatigue, deception, drawing the reserve to the wrong sector, assault before formation, or forcing prolonged open-field combat
- **Opening State:** Unavailable on January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 3. Player Fantasy and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Develop the castle's dependable fighting core, hold it behind the ordinary watch, and commit it at the moment an entrance, breach, courtyard, or inner route must not be lost.

**Short description:** Developed Aragonese strongpoint infantry trained and equipped to relieve ordinary Wardens, contain breaches, guard inner routes, and launch short controlled counterattacks.

**Flavor line:** **The Wardens keep the castle. The Warden Serjeants decide whether a breach becomes its fall.**

## 11. Signature Battle Cycle: Alarm, Contain, Counter-Entry, Restore

1. Maintain ordinary Wardens across the strongpoint and preserve a real Warden Serjeant response body.
2. Receive an alarm with uncertainty rather than perfect information.
3. Identify the threatened gate, breach, wall access, store route, courtyard, or internal choke.
4. Move through an actual internal route without stripping every alternate post.
5. **Contain:** establish a shielded spear stop line and prevent attackers from spreading.
6. **Relieve:** replace exhausted or collapsing Wardens where access and timing permit.
7. **Counter-entry:** advance a short distance against stalled, divided, or disordered attackers.
8. Halt at a planned stop line rather than pursuing into an exposed outer approach.
9. **Restore:** secure keys, routes, stores, posts, signals, casualties, and relief depth.
10. Withdraw, surrender, or fall back inward when fire, flanking, missiles, exhaustion, broken routes, or overwhelming assault destroys the local advantage.
