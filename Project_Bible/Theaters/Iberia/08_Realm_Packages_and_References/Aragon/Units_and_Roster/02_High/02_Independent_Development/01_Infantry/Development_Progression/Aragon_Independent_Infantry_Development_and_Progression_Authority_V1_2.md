## 1. Decisive Independent Infantry Verdict

Independent Aragon currently has four High Era infantry design candidates that emerge through different Early parents, providers, settlement environments, equipment systems, and military problems:

- **Armored Jaca Spearmen** develop a narrower protected spear center from qualifying Jaca-facing muster capacity.
- **Aragonese Serjeants** emerge as mobile institutional sword-and-shield service infantry from separately validated providers.
- **Pyrenean Axemen** emerge as a selected regional heavy-cleaving assault specialist.
- **Warden Serjeants of Aragon** develop as formed strongpoint response infantry from particular qualified castles or fortresses.

These are not four statistical upgrades. They are four different development choices. Early parents persist unless a specific source lane is actually absorbed, and surviving formations persist when recruitment closes.

## 10. Integrated Independent Battlefield Sequence

1. Jaca Muster, Men of Old Aragon, Sobrarbe, Men of the High Valleys, or Armored Jaca establish the appropriate holding or maneuver frontage.
2. Bows, javelins, and crossbows shape targets and protect approaches.
3. Aragonese Serjeants relieve, counterpress, or secure a local objective.
4. Pyrenean Axemen attack one fixed, disordered, flanked, or breached hard target.
5. Aragonese Serjeants, spear families, or Royal Mesnada Foot receive the Axemen's handoff.
6. Warden Serjeants remain at their exact strongpoint unless a bounded sortie or nearby relief mission passes.
7. Early parent families remain available where their people and equipment were not transferred.
