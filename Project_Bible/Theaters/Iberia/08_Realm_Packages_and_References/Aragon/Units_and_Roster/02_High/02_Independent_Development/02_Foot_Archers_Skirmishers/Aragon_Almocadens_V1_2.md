Almocadens are selected Aragonese frontier fighters and small-unit leaders organized to operate beyond the ordinary skirmishing screen. They approach through favorable ground, deliver a controlled short-range javelin cast, close against an exposed or disordered target, and disengage before heavier infantry, cavalry, or reserves can fix them in prolonged combat.

Their defining quality is not superior armor or unlimited missiles. It is the combination of fieldcraft, target judgment, short-range missile discipline, immediate weapon transition, local leadership, and the ability to end a successful action before it becomes a losing melee.

- **Unit:** Almocadens
- **Class:** High Era frontier javelin assault and vanguard infantry
- **Source:** Selected eligible frontier personnel, surviving experienced javelinmen, proven local leaders, repeat-service fighters, compatible equipment, and recognized service relationships that pass every emergence and muster gate
- **Availability:** Limited, conditional, source-bound, non-opening, and dependent on a compatible world state rather than a single building or fixed-date unlock
- **Battle promise:** Approach from advantageous ground, deliver a concentrated close-range cast, close on an exposed target, and disengage before fresh opposition fixes the unit
- **Best use:** Vanguard deployment, prepared ambushes, rough-ground flanking, attacks on exposed missile troops, local exploitation beside a fixed enemy, route pressure, and pursuit disruption
- **Worst use:** Frontal assault into fresh heavy infantry, receiving cavalry in open ground, prolonged melee, sustained missile exchange, deep unsupported pursuit, and static line duty
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Consumes scarce experienced people, local leaders, javelins, shields, sidearms, equipment, training time, route knowledge, and frontier-service capacity
- **Counterplay:** Long-range missiles, disciplined shields, early detection, fast cavalry, fresh reserves, blocked withdrawal, leader loss, open terrain, and forcing prolonged contact

- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 14. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Scout or receive reliable information about the target, ground, friendly support, and enemy reserves.
2. Select an approach that protects the formation without granting magical concealment.
3. Hold finite javelins until the range, angle, target, and friendly clearance justify the cast.
4. Deliver a concentrated short-range cast intended to create a local opening rather than guaranteed destruction.
5. Read whether the target is exposed, disordered, isolated, fixed, or still stable.
6. Close only when the local advantage and exit plan remain valid.
7. Fight briefly with shields and sidearms against the chosen target.
8. Break off before fresh heavy infantry, cavalry, missiles, or reserves establish overmatch.
9. Rally and reform around recognized leaders or a prepared point.
10. Recommit, cover withdrawal, or leave the fight according to ammunition, fatigue, casualties, and mission.
