## 4. Player-Facing Description and Combat Fantasy

Almughavars are experienced Aragonese campaign infantry trained and organized for aggressive movement beyond the ordinary skirmishing window. They use fieldcraft, limited thrown weapons, close-combat practice, and strong small-group control to disrupt movement or formation, close against an exposed target, exploit locally, and then regroup before their light protection and finite reserves become fatal.

**Combat fantasy:** Work around the main fight, force a valuable local reaction, cast at decisive range, close before the enemy recovers, exploit the flank, route, camp, or breach, then escape before fresh cavalry or heavy reserves arrive.

## 18. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Evaluate the mission, target, ground, friendly support, enemy reserves, exit, and recovery cost.
2. Approach through cover, screening, a known route, or operational pressure.
3. Raid, threaten, delay, or disrupt movement, supply, position, or cohesion.
4. Preserve finite missiles until the target and range justify commitment.
5. Cast, threaten, or close according to formation and target state.
6. Read disorder, support, cavalry, missiles, reserves, and withdrawal immediately.
7. Close only on an exposed, disordered, isolated, fixed, or constrained target.
8. Exploit a flank, rear, camp, route, support element, gate, street, breach, or local collapse.
9. Break off, regroup, continue the mission, or withdraw before the formation is fixed and destroyed.
10. Rally, account, recover, and return or continue campaigning.
