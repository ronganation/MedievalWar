## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Jaca Field Archers** are Independent Aragon's dependable mid-tier field-bow formation: selected shooters organized into known sections, equipped from inspected bow and arrow lots, supported by cadres and reserve carriers, and practiced to hold fire, sustain useful pressure, change lanes, and leave the frontage in order. They trade the wider availability and lower recovery burden of Aragonese Bowmen and Jaca Bowmen for repeatable army service, stronger target discipline, managed volleys, and better survival during a moving battle. They remain ordinary human archers with mixed equipment, finite arrows, weak melee, and no guarantee of superiority in raw range or lethality.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Player Card

- **Unit:** Jaca Field Archers
- **Class:** High Era trained field bow infantry
- **Source:** Selected qualified bow users, leaders, bows, strings, arrows, stores, carriers, repair, and service capacity assembled through a validated Jaca-facing field institution
- **Battle promise:** Establish a dependable protected bow line, manage finite arrows by section, sustain useful pressure longer than basic Bowmen, shift locally when lanes change, and withdraw in order before contact
- **Best use:** Behind Armored Jaca Spearmen or another credible frontage, supporting a balanced field army, firing controlled volleys into exposed or committed targets, defending prepared approaches, and covering disciplined withdrawal
- **Worst use:** Unsupported forward skirmishing, cavalry exposure, close assault, professional long-range duel, armor destruction as a primary mission, rain-soaked unsupported service, or prolonged campaigning after stores and cadence collapse
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Concentrates selected shooters, experienced section leaders, serviceable bows, compatible strings and arrows, dry stores, carriers, repair, practice, routes, protection, and recovery into a narrower and slower-recovering formation
- **Counterplay:** Cavalry, fast infantry, shields, armor, counterfire, broken sightlines, wet or damaged equipment, forced movement, disrupted stores, ammunition exhaustion, leader loss, and collapse of the protecting frontage

## 10. Signature Battle Cycle: Position, Hold, Volley, Rotate, Shift, Withdraw

1. Assemble only after selected shooters, leaders, bows, strings, arrows, stores, carriers, service, protection, and return pass.
2. Establish sections behind a credible frontage or prepared position.
3. Inspect equipment, identify targets, mark firing lanes, place reserve arrows, and preserve a withdrawal route.
4. **Hold** against distant, obscured, shielded, or low-value targets.
5. **Volley** or use controlled section fire when the target justifies finite arrows.
6. **Rotate** firing, ready, resupply, and recovering sections where space, leaders, and practice permit.
7. **Shift** local sections when lanes close or friendly frontage moves.
8. Continue only while ammunition, protection, visibility, command, and mission remain valid.
9. **Withdraw in order** before cavalry or formed infantry reaches the line.
10. Rally, account, resupply from actual stores, recover, and return surviving people and property.

## 24A. Final Player Fantasy, Description, and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Build the bow formation that lets Independent Aragon maintain disciplined pressure across an actual field battle. Jaca Field Archers do not wait for one perfect bolt window and do not scatter forward as skirmishers. They dress several controlled sections behind the army, hold against poor targets, release when movement or exposure makes the arrows worthwhile, relieve tired files, shift with the frontage, and preserve enough order to withdraw before enemy cavalry or infantry reaches them.

**Short description:** Selected Jaca-facing archers organized for controlled volleys, section relief, reserve-arrow handling, and dependable field-army support.

**Encyclopedia description:** Independent Aragon can develop its Jaca-facing bow sources beyond local musters and strongpoint duty. Where selected shooters, experienced leaders, inspected bows, compatible arrows, dry stores, reserve carriers, field practice, and reliable return arrangements converge, a narrower body of archers can serve repeatedly with the main army. Their strength lies less in a miraculous bow than in collective discipline: holding fire, choosing targets, maintaining safe lanes, relieving sections, accounting for arrows, and withdrawing before the enemy closes. Every improvement is costly. Concentrating these archers reduces broader local bow capacity, and losses among section leaders, veteran shooters, stores, carriers, and compatible equipment can lower the formation back toward ordinary Jaca or Aragonese bow service.

**Flavor line:** Jaca furnishes the bow. Independent Aragon teaches the line when to loose and when to leave.
