## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Veteran Archers of Jaca** are Independent Aragon's scarce upper bow formation: proven shooters and section leaders concentrated from surviving Jaca-facing service, selected for target judgment, counter-missile discipline, difficult firing opportunities, and the ability to preserve effectiveness when the battlefield shifts. The card does not represent a new bow technology, universal longbows, or a regiment of superhuman marksmen. Its value comes from accumulated service, trusted cadres, practiced observation, careful arrow expenditure, and the willingness to refuse low-value fire.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Player Card

- **Unit:** Veteran Archers of Jaca
- **Class:** High Era veteran precision bow infantry
- **Source:** Proven Jaca-facing shooters, section leaders, veterans, compatible bows, selected arrows, observation practice, field records, stores, carriers, service release, and recovery capacity
- **Battle promise:** Read the battle, refuse poor shots, pressure exposed high-value targets and opposing missile troops, relocate before being fixed, and preserve scarce veteran capability for another decisive window
- **Best use:** Counter-missile exchanges from protection, firing into exposed flanks or crossings, threatening visible leaders, standards, crews, and specialists where identification is credible, supporting spear and cavalry timing, and covering controlled withdrawal
- **Worst use:** Mass arrow dumping, unsupported missile duels against superior specialists, firing blindly at nominal officers, prolonged melee, cavalry exposure, frontal shooting into deep armor and shields, and routine garrison duty that wastes veteran concentration
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Concentrates scarce veteran shooters, cadres, selected equipment, ammunition, observation, trust, service history, and replacement potential into a small high-consequence card
- **Counterplay:** Conceal valuable targets, use shields and armor, suppress with mass missiles, force movement, threaten flanks, use cavalry, break observation, present several simultaneous threats, exhaust selected arrows, and kill or scatter cadres

## 3. Final Player Fantasy, Description, and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Field the experienced archers who do not need to fill the sky to influence the battle. Veteran Archers of Jaca watch the enemy's movement, preserve selected arrows, contest dangerous missile formations, punish exposed files and specialists, and shift before retaliation fixes them. The player wins by choosing the right target and the right moment, not by leaving fire-at-will active until the quivers are empty.

**Short description:** Scarce veteran Jaca archers organized for superior target discipline, selective volleys, counter-missile pressure, and preservation through changing battlefield conditions.

**Encyclopedia description:** Jaca's continuing regional service can produce more than a dependable field line. Some archers survive repeated musters, learn the behavior of shields, horses, missile crews, standards, and formations, and become trusted by leaders to refuse a poor shot as readily as to take a difficult one. Independent Aragon may concentrate such veterans into a narrow formation when actual people, equipment, selected arrows, cadres, observation, service release, and recovery permit. These archers remain vulnerable human soldiers. They cannot see through terrain, identify every officer, pierce any armor, or defeat massed enemies without protection. Their campaign value is high precisely because replacing their judgment and shared practice takes longer than replacing headcount.

**Flavor line:** The first arrow tests the wind. The next is loosed because it matters.

## 10. Signature Battle Cycle: Observe, Select, Hold, Loose, Reassess, Shift, Preserve

1. Assemble only when exact veteran people, cadres, equipment, selected arrows, protection, mission, return, and recovery pass.
2. Establish observation, firing lanes, section spacing, target priorities, cease-fire control, and withdrawal.
3. **Observe** the enemy without receiving perfect information.
4. **Select** a target whose exposure, importance, range, angle, movement, and protection justify veteran attention.
5. **Hold** against low-confidence, low-value, obscured, heavily protected, or politically restricted targets.
6. **Loose** independently or by concentrated file when command, lane, and shot quality pass.
7. **Reassess** target condition, enemy response, ammunition, counterfire, cavalry, and friendly movement.
8. **Shift** before the enemy fixes the position or the lane becomes unsafe.
9. **Preserve** selected arrows, leaders, veterans, equipment, and the formation for the next decisive window.
10. Withdraw and rally before contact collapses the veteran bow identity.
