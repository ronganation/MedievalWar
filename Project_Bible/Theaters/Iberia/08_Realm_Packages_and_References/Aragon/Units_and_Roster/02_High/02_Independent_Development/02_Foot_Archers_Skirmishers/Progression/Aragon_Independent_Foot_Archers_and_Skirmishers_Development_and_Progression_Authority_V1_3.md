## 1. Decisive Portfolio Verdict

Independent Aragon currently has three readable foot-missile and skirmisher development families:

- **Bow development** progresses from broad basic support through narrower organization, developed field service, and conditional veteran concentration.
- **Crossbow development** introduces a separate adopted weapon institution and may later branch into a protected pavise formation.
- **Foot-skirmisher development** preserves the Early javelin screen, may produce a selected Aragonese vanguard, and may gain conditional access to the wider Theater-owned Almughavar family.

The portfolio is not one vertical tier ladder. Each card owns a different source constitution, equipment system, battlefield cycle, campaign cost, recovery burden, and player decision.
