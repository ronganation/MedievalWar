## 1. Reconciled Completion Verdict

Independent Aragon's current foot-archer, crossbow, and foot-skirmisher portfolio is complete enough for a bounded static pause. It contains nine profile expressions across three readable families. The portfolio has persistent foundations, selective later branches, source-specific development, and one Theater-owned operational family expression. No additional card is justified merely to equalize family sizes or fill a perceived tier gap.

### Bow-Family Decision

Aragonese Bowmen remain the broad affordable bow floor. Jaca Bowmen preserve organized regional service. Jaca Field Archers own the dependable main-army field role. Veteran Archers of Jaca own the narrow veteran decision built around target economy, counter-missile pressure, and preservation. The veteran card must not collapse into flat accuracy, perfect information, automatic officer killing, or a universal longbow identity.

### Crossbow-Family Decision

Aragonese Crossbowmen are the more flexible prepared-position formation and remain dependent on real operators, weapons, bolts, spanning methods, maintenance, protection, and recovery. Pavise Crossbowmen trade mobility and flexibility for directional protection, protected reload, position endurance, setup, packing, handlers, transport, property risk, and flank exposure. If those pavise systems cannot create a distinct playable decision, accepted content returns to ordinary Crossbowmen and the separate card closes.

### Skirmisher-Family Decision

High Valley Javelinmen own the cheap short-range screening decision. Almocadens own one selected local vanguard action: cast, read the opening, close briefly if justified, then break off and reform. Almughavars own the deeper operational question of whether local success can be extended against routes, camps, flanks, rears, support elements, or campaign position without losing the experienced formation. The Aragon Almughavar file remains an expression and service interface, not a duplicate canonical family or population pool.

## 6. Negotiated Union Routing Constitution

Negotiated Union preserves the ownership of native Aragonese and Barcelona cards and preserves Theater ownership of the Almughavar family. Cooperation may alter assignments, support, command relationships, information, protection, mission scope, duration, return, and recovery. It does not merge people, weapons, ammunition, institutions, recruitment pools, or canonical profiles.
