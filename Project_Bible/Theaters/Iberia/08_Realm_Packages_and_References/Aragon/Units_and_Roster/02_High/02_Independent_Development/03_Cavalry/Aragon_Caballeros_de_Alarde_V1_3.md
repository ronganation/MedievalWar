Caballeros de Alarde are selected municipal horsemen capable of doing more than patrolling ahead of the army. Their value is the controlled escalation of a flank action. They screen and contest the outer field, close against light cavalry or exposed missile troops, use a small javelin supply to disturb the target or protect the approach, then commit with spear, shield, horse, and formation weight. They should win short fights against lighter mounted troops and exposed light formations, create a local opening, break contact, and reform before noble heavy cavalry or prepared infantry can fix them.

They are not a generic medium-cavalry line and not a disguised Jinete card. Their limited cast is subordinate to contact. Their municipal cohesion, equipment, formation practice, and willingness to enter a short direct fight separate them from broad Riders. Their lighter horses, mixed armor, restricted endurance, and town-service obligations keep them decisively below Aragonese Nobles and Royal Mesnada.

## 2A. Strengthened One-Screen Modern Medieval II Contract

### Battlefield

- Begin on the army's outer flank behind or beside Aragonese Riders and Jinetes.
- Contest light cavalry, raiders, and missile troops rather than wandering as a general scout card.
- Use limited javelins to disturb a target, force movement, or protect the final approach.
- Commit with spear and shield against light cavalry, exposed missiles, disordered light infantry, or an occupied flank.
- Win the short local action, sweep only while cohesion remains, then break contact and reform.
- Refuse fresh heavy cavalry, deep prepared spears, dense missiles, and prolonged unsupported melee.

### Campaign

- Develop only from exact municipal mounted households with sufficient horses, equipment, leaders, muster organization, and field release.
- Compete directly with town watch, escort, messenger, route-security, household economy, horse care, and future municipal mounted recovery.
- Remain unevenly distributed between towns rather than becoming one abstract realm-wide pool.
- Preserve casualties, lost horses, equipment wear, municipal grievance, reduced home coverage, and recovery time.

### Player experience

- Give Independent Aragon a strong municipal cavalry reward without copying noble, royal, household, confraternal, or Jinete roles.
- Reward target judgment, formation control, short commitment, timely breakoff, and reuse.
- Present one readable card rather than several thin town or equipment variants.

## 3. Player-Facing Encyclopedia

Caballeros de Alarde represent qualified mounted households summoned through the obligations and privileges of a developed Aragonese municipal community. They are not nobles merely because they own and maintain a horse, and they are not royal household cavalry. Their battlefield usefulness comes from combining local mounted readiness, practical protection, spears, limited throwing weapons where supported, and enough cohesion to screen a flank, disrupt raiders, sweep exposed missile troops, make a short charge, and disengage.

The formation is strongest when the player treats it as a municipal field detachment rather than a cheaper knight unit. Its members leave town watch, escort, route security, messenger, herd, market, convoy, and emergency-response functions behind when they join a field army. Losses therefore weaken the exact community that supplied the riders, horses, equipment, leaders, and replacements.
