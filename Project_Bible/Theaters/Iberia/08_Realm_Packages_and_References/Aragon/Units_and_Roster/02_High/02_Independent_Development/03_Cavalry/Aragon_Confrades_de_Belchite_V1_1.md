Confrades de Belchite are a specific Aragonese frontier confraternity whose military identity joins mounted response, local shock action, strongpoint relief, route protection, and continuing frontier obligation. They are not generic religious knights and are not a renamed pool of nobles. Their value comes from a recognized Belchite institution able to concentrate permanent brothers, term-service participants, leaders, horses, arms, supplies, and frontier support for a bounded campaign purpose.

The unit should feel committed and dependable without becoming the heaviest cavalry in the roster. It can intercept raiders, reinforce a threatened flank, strike a locally exposed enemy, relieve a frontier position, and pursue briefly. It should fail when used as disposable shock cavalry, when separated from its frontier support and return system, or when expected to equal mature transregional military orders.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Player Card

- **Unit:** Confrades de Belchite
- **Subtitle:** Brothers of Belchite
- **Class:** Developed Early-to-High Era frontier confraternity cavalry
- **Source:** A surviving or emergent Belchite confraternity with permanent brothers, qualified term-service members, recognized leadership, horses, arms, equipment, supplies, and frontier support
- **Battle promise:** Patrol a threatened frontier, gather around recognized leaders, intercept a vulnerable enemy, deliver a controlled mounted commitment, pursue only while the route remains safe, and return to the confraternity’s continuing defense mission
- **Best use:** Raider interception, frontier counterattack, flank pressure, strongpoint relief, route security, escort, short pursuit, and a timed charge against exposed or disordered troops
- **Worst use:** Repeated frontal charges into formed spears, prolonged melee against elite heavy cavalry, unsupported distant campaigning, deep pursuit, and treating temporary members or donated equipment as permanent replacements
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Draws permanent cadre, term-service participants, horses, arms, supplies, merchants, strongpoint capacity, settlement support, and institutional legitimacy into one mission
- **Counterplay:** Prepared spears, disciplined heavy infantry, missile pressure, difficult ground, horse exhaustion, blocked return routes, leader loss, extended service, and attacks on Belchite’s institutional support
- **Opening State:** Unavailable on January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 18. Signature Battle Rhythm

1. Patrol, scout, or receive credible warning through actual frontier relationships.
2. Gather permanent and term-service files under recognized command.
3. Select a target whose exposure, disorder, isolation, or engagement makes mounted commitment worthwhile.
4. Approach without exhausting the horses or losing file control.
5. Intercept, relieve, flank, or charge according to the real mission.
6. Maintain enough cohesion to halt, separate, and avoid being fixed in prolonged melee.
7. Pursue only while the enemy remains vulnerable and the return route remains protected.
8. Break off before formed spears, fresh cavalry, missile concentration, or terrain removes the formation’s advantage.
9. Rally, account for people and horses, and return to the frontier mission or supporting army.
10. Reconcile casualties, service terms, booty, property, grievances, strongpoint needs, and recovery before recommitment.
