The refined unit is Aragon's practical cavalry reserve for the moment when a flank bends, raiders reach vulnerable troops, light cavalry breaks through, a withdrawal needs protection, or an exposed formation must be rescued. It should normally begin behind or beside the main line rather than roaming as the army's widest scout screen. The player holds it back, identifies the crisis, commits it rapidly, wins a limited local action, and reforms before heavy cavalry or prepared infantry can pin it.

Escort, patrol, messenger, route-security, and frontier-response duties remain essential to its campaign source and opportunity cost. They no longer carry equal weight in the player-facing battle fantasy because Aragonese Riders, Breton Mounted Sergeants and Messengers, French Castle Mounted Sergeants, and Norman mounted-service families already cover significant portions of general screening, connection, escort, and exploitation.

# PART I. PLAYER-FACING CARD

## 1. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Royal Frontier Sergeants
- **Class:** Institutional light-to-medium mounted service cavalry
- **Era:** High Era conditional development; unavailable at the 1062 opening
- **Source:** Exact royal, lordly, castle, strongpoint, route, or frontier mounted-service providers admitted under Aragonese authority
- **Battle promise:** Hold as a mobile reserve, ride to the crisis, countercharge or rescue, and reform before heavier opposition fixes the unit
- **Best use:** rapid local relief, timed countercharges against light or committed medium threats, rescue of exposed troops, withdrawal cover, and reformation behind the line
- **Worst use:** prepared spears, fresh heavy cavalry, mounted-javelin exchanges, concentrated missiles, severe broken ground, unsupported prolonged melee, and deep pursuit
- **Strategic value:** supplies dependable mounted intervention without consuming the full political and equipment burden of noble heavy cavalry
- **Strategic cost:** field service removes riders, horses, leaders, tack, escort coverage, route security, strongpoint response, and recovery capacity from their home duties
- **Signature decision:** hold the unit out of routine fighting, commit it to the most important developing crisis, create separation, and reform for possible reuse

## 2. Player-Facing Description

Royal Frontier Sergeants are selected mounted servicemen released from exact royal, strongpoint, lordly, route, or frontier obligations for bounded campaign duty. Their place is not at the head of a grand knightly charge. They ride where the army needs speed, discipline, and direct intervention: joining a threatened flank, protecting messengers or command movement, driving away hostile scouts and raiders, covering a withdrawal, reaching an endangered strongpoint, or exploiting a brief opening against exposed light troops.

Their lances or spears give them a credible initial mounted commitment, and their shields, sidearms, practical protection, and service discipline allow a short controlled melee. Their lighter horses, lower armor ceiling, and broader service responsibilities keep them below Infanzones, developed Aragonese Nobles, and the Royal Mesnada in shock and staying power. Unlike Aragonese Jinetes, they do not build their role around mounted javelin harassment. Unlike mercenary light lancers, they come from persistent Aragonese service relationships rather than an independent company contract.

A valid formation requires actual riders, leaders, horses, remounts, tack, handlers, farriers, weapons, protection, supply, release authority, and a mission worth weakening royal escort, route, strongpoint, messenger, or local-response coverage. A royal order, castle, road, technology, or later date cannot create those inputs.
