**Decisive verdict:** Aragon presently retains seven mounted families in the reviewed Independent cavalry portfolio and its required earlier interfaces. The portfolio is broad but not redundant. Aragonese Riders, Infanzones of Aragon, and Royal Mesnada persist as earlier families. Caballeros de Alarde, Royal Frontier Sergeants, Confrades de Belchite, and Ricohombres of Aragon each survive as a separate Independent-development card because each owns a different provider system, commitment depth, battlefield decision, campaign opportunity cost, and recovery burden. No additional cavalry card is currently justified from the attached evidence.

## 6. Direct Player Choice Matrix

## 8. Why All Seven Families Survive

Aragonese Riders economically perform the many mounted tasks that would waste scarcer cards. Infanzones provide an earlier middle rung able to meet a serious mounted crisis. Royal Mesnada protects the ruler and converts finite household capacity into one exceptional intervention. Caballeros de Alarde give Independent Aragon a municipal cavalry reward centered on outer-flank combat. Royal Frontier Sergeants provide army-wide crisis response and recovery rather than general scouting or heavy shock. Confrades de Belchite turn one specific frontier institution into interception, strongpoint relief, local shock, and return. Ricohombres supply the developed nonroyal battle wing that can decide a mounted contest and briefly press an earned advantage.

A card fails if its provider system, battlefield cycle, opportunity cost, and recovery become cosmetic. Higher armor, prestige, later chronology, or a different owner label cannot preserve a redundant card.

## 13. Atomic Mounted Formation Transaction

```text
Select mission and candidate family
  -> verify canonical source, owner, service, political and institutional eligibility
  -> release people from incompatible duties
  -> inspect and reserve actual horses and remount depth
  -> establish rider-horse pairings
  -> reserve weapons, shields, armor, tack, standards and support
  -> preserve home-defense, household, route, strongpoint, municipal and protection floors
  -> establish leaders, precedence, formation practice, signals and mission limits
  -> verify terrain, approach, target, support, exit, rally, return and recovery
  -> reserve all inputs atomically
  -> field the formation.

Fatal failure restores the last valid state where possible and creates no duplicate reservation.
```
