## 5. Player-Facing Description and Combat Fantasy

Aragonese Jinetes are light mounted specialists trained to scout ahead of the host, threaten flanks and routes, cast finite javelins from useful range, withdraw before heavy contact, and reposition for another opportunity. Their speed is a managed capability rather than immunity: horses tire, javelins run out, formation control can break, and a blocked withdrawal can turn harassment into disaster.

**Combat fantasy:** Sweep across the open side of the battle, unsettle the enemy with controlled mounted casts, draw a response, turn away before being fixed, then return against a newly exposed flank, exhausted pursuer, broken formation, or retreat route.

## 18. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Scout and evaluate ground, target, missile cover, cavalry reserves, friendly support, exit, and horse condition.
2. Approach in open files or a mounted screen through usable ground.
3. Threaten the flank, route, missiles, support elements, or exposed formation.
4. Preserve finite javelins until range, angle, target, and withdrawal justify the cast.
5. Cast under leader control without allowing files to collapse into uncontrolled melee.
6. Turn away, withdraw, or reposition before heavy contact.
7. Repeat only while horse energy, rider control, ammunition, cohesion, mission value, and exit remain valid.
8. Close or pursue only against an exposed, disordered, isolated, fatigued, or broken target.
9. Refuse prepared spears, fresh heavy cavalry, strong missile zones, and blocked terrain.
10. Rally, account, recover, and continue screening, withdraw, or return.
