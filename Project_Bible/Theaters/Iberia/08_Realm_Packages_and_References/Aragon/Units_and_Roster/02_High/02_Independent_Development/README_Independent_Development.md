# Aragon High Era Independent Development

## Purpose

This folder contains Aragon's High Era Independent Development roster portfolio. It records the units and faction expressions Aragon can develop through its own people, institutions, equipment, service relationships, regional capacity, and campaign choices without requiring Negotiated Union or Domination.

Independent Development is a complete competitive path. It is not a fallback roster and it does not exist merely to provide source material for later Union units.

## Controlling rules

- Early Era units persist unless a specific source transfer, recruitment closure, or successor relationship says otherwise.
- High Era development does not create people, weapons, horses, equipment, leaders, recruitment pools, or readiness by date alone.
- Independent Development units remain Aragon-owned unless a profile explicitly identifies a Theater-owned regional family or another canonical owner.
- Political paths may change eligibility, assignment, reliability, command, recall, and recovery. They do not automatically duplicate or transfer canonical unit ownership.
- Surviving earlier formations are not deleted merely because a later family becomes available.
- No file in this folder establishes January 1, 1062 opening availability unless a controlling Opening State authority separately does so.
- Stable IDs, exact pools, exact statistics, payloads, runtime bindings, and Unreal activation remain outside this README.

## Independent Development roster identity

Independent Aragon develops a combined-arms High Era roster around:

- protected and institutionally developed spear frontage;
- reliable sword-and-shield service infantry;
- strongpoint and fortified-relief specialists;
- frontier vanguard and aggressive light infantry;
- native bow development;
- crossbow adoption and protected pavise shooting;
- selected veteran missile cadres;
- practical mounted service and mobile relief;
- developed noble cavalry;
- and mounted-javelin skirmishing.

The roster is not a straight upgrade ladder. Each card must retain a distinct source, battlefield decision, campaign cost, counterplay, and recovery burden.

# Independent Development battlefield model

A developed Independent Aragon army can be organized around:

1. **Protected center:** Armored Jaca Spearmen.
2. **Close relief and objective work:** Aragonese Serjeants.
3. **Fortified specialist duty:** Warden Serjeants.
4. **Frontier disruption:** Almocadens and the Aragon Almughavar expression in distinct roles.
5. **Bow progression:** Jaca Field Archers and selected Veteran Archers of Jaca.
6. **Crossbow progression:** Aragonese Crossbowmen and later protected Pavise Crossbowmen.
7. **Mobile relief:** Royal Frontier Sergeants.
8. **Decisive noble reserve:** Ricohombres.
9. **Mounted harassment:** Aragonese Jinetes.

This portfolio should remain fully playable without Barcelona. Negotiated Union may coordinate, assign, or create a few genuinely composite institutions, but it must not retroactively make Independent Development incomplete.

# Reference rules for Negotiated Union work

When designing a Negotiated Union unit:

- first identify the roster gap that neither native portfolio already owns;
- compare the candidate directly against every relevant Independent Development profile in this README;
- do not copy native units into a Union folder under new colors;
- do not merge native recruitment pools;
- do not remove native cards merely because a composite card exists;
- require a real shared institution, common practice, distinct battle decision, contributor cost, recovery, and dissolution behavior;
- preserve canonical ownership for Theater regional and mercenary families;
- favor a few high-value composite cards over a parallel duplicate roster.

For Catalano-Aragonese Spearmen, the Independent Development comparison set is:

- Armored Jaca Spearmen for protected native spear frontage and deliberate advance;
- Aragonese Serjeants for close press and objective security;
- Warden Serjeants for strongpoint fighting;
- Jaca Field Archers, Veteran Archers, Crossbowmen, and Pavise Crossbowmen for missile-line relationships;
- Royal Frontier Sergeants and Ricohombres for mounted relief and decisive counterattack support;
- Jinetes for disruption and enemy-shaping support.

The Union spear card must own a composite mixed-army decision that remains distinct from all of those native functions.
