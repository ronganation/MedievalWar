## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Breachmen of the Royal Host** are Aragon-led Domination's dedicated protected assault infantry. They are assembled from exact released close-infantry contributors, leaders, shields, weapons, protection, and support drawn from Aragonese and controlled, compliant, bargained, or compelled Barcelona sources. Their purpose is to cross one prepared final approach, defeat the immediate defenders, widen the opening enough for the Royal Host to pass, and hand sustained control to Spearmen, Sergeants, Wardens, or another proper receiving formation.

They are not Pioneers with better melee weapons. Pioneers alter the physical access problem. Breachmen fight through the defended opening after that work succeeds. They are not a domination copy of Companyia de la Santa Creu, Pyrenean Axemen, Crown Serjeants, or native Aragonese Serjeants. Their identity is a Royal Host assault body whose cohesion depends on imposed common command, functional contributor files, a protected approach, a finite entry commitment, and a credible receiver.

## 2. Thirty-Second Player Read

> **Let the Pioneers open the way. Send the Breachmen through the defended entry, break the immediate resistance, widen one usable foothold, and hand it to the line before the assault body is exhausted or isolated.**

- Best use: one qualified gate, breach, barricade, crossing exit, fieldwork opening, camp entrance, compressed flank, or fixed local sector.
- Worst use: broad main line, open cavalry ground, prolonged unsupported melee, deep pursuit, several objectives, or an approach still dominated by missiles and obstacles.
- Strategic value: supplies the missing offensive infantry transaction in the Royal Host.
- Strategic cost: concentrates scarce close fighters, shields, protection, leaders, medical support, transport, contributor compliance, and recovery capacity.
- Counterplay: expose the approach, preserve fresh depth, strike the flanks, close the receiver lane, counterattack during widening, or force the Breachmen to remain after culmination.

## 5. Direct Collision Firewall

### Companyia de la Santa Creu

Companyia is a negotiated, elite, chartered assault company with stronger shared legitimacy, prestige, selected assault concentration, and contributor agreement. Breachmen are an Aragon-led Royal Host formation whose cohesion is more command-imposed, source-variable, politically coercive, and dependent on the wider Host. They should be less elite, less reliable after command fracture, and more available only where domination can actually compel and sustain compatible contributions.

### Pyrenean Axemen

Pyrenean Axemen own fragile heavy-cleaving assault, armor and shield pressure, regional mountain sources, and forceful short commitment. Breachmen use protected shielded entry, mixed source-valid close weapons, controlled replacement files, and Royal Host passage integration. They do not gain the Axemen's heavy-weapon ceiling.

### Crown and Aragonese Serjeants

Serjeants own recurring close-infantry service, relief, sustained pressure, objective security, and reform. Breachmen own the dangerous first entry and short foothold before those formations take over. If Breachmen become the best sustained sword-and-shield infantry, the card fails.

### Sergeants of the Royal Host

Royal Host Sergeants own crisis selection, passage control, temporary sector assumption, handback, extraction, and reorganization. Breachmen own assault commitment through a prepared opening. Sergeants may rescue, relieve, or extract them, but should not be consumed as their automatic second wave.

### Pioneers of the Royal Host

Pioneers solve physical access. Breachmen solve immediate defended entry. Breachmen cannot clear major obstacles, build crossings, prepare fieldworks, or replace skilled work parties merely because some files carry tools.

## 12. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **Select** one bounded objective worth the political and military cost.
2. **Qualify** the approach, entry, defender depth, flanks, receiver, and exit.
3. **Prepare** through Pioneers, missiles, spears, cavalry threat, terrain, or another real support plan.
4. **Form** a shielded entry body while preserving finite replacement depth.
5. **Cover** the final approach and absorb directional damage without claiming immunity.
6. **Enter** the opening under common command.
7. **Break** the immediate resistance through compact close pressure.
8. **Widen** only enough for the receiver and casualty route.
9. **Hold briefly** against the first local counterattack.
10. **Hand off** the objective to Spearmen, Sergeants, Wardens, or another proper body.
11. **Recover** people, standards, wounded, weapons, shields, records, and political accountability.
12. **Withdraw or reform** before fresh depth, cavalry, crossfire, or fatigue destroys the formation.

## 29. Representative Battle Scenarios

### Pioneer-prepared breach

Pioneers reduce the obstruction and brace the immediate edges while Spearmen protect the approach. Breachmen pass through, defeat the first compressed defenders, widen one usable foothold, and hand the ground to Sergeants or Spearmen. Pioneers and Breachmen then recover separately.

### Fieldwork assault

Crossbows fix defenders behind a bounded fieldwork. Pioneers clear one physical lane. Breachmen enter under shields and force the local position. They halt when fresh enemy depth appears rather than rolling the entire line.

### Failed receiver

The entry succeeds, but the receiving Spearmen cannot reach the foothold because the passage is congested. Breachmen hold briefly, then must choose a costly withdrawal or risk destruction. Entry success does not erase handoff failure.

### Open-field misuse

The unit advances across exposed ground without suppression or a qualified corridor. Crossfire damages the shield body and cavalry reaches a flank before entry. The failure demonstrates that the card is not general heavy infantry.

### Compelled contributor fracture

Barcelona files have borne repeated entry losses and their leaders dispute another commitment. The assault body may still form, but replacement and handoff reliability decline. Political control does not manufacture trust.

## 29A. Mature Battlefield Power Floor

Breachmen must feel like a formation worth organizing an assault around. Their restrictions define where their strength applies; they do not excuse a weak payoff. A fresh, supported Breachmen body attacking through a qualified opening should overpower ordinary defenders on that limited frontage, rapidly defeat exposed missile troops, and place serious pressure on medium-heavy service infantry whose depth or facing has already been disrupted.
