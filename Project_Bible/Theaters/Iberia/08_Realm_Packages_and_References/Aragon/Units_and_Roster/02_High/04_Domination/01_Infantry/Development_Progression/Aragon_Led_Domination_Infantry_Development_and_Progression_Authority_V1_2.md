## 8. Integrated Infantry Sequence

SPEARMEN establish, sustain, narrow, deepen, relieve, or yield the ordinary Royal Host frontage.
SPEARMEN preserve missile, relief, casualty, worksite, and fallback passages.
PIONEERS inspect and mark one qualified physical access problem behind or beside protected frontage.
PIONEERS move finite workers, guards, tools, materials, and transport through a controlled approach.
PIONEERS open, reduce, fill, clear, brace, or stabilize the bounded task while protection holds.
SERGEANTS remain a scarce crisis reserve and passage-control body rather than extending the ordinary line.
WHEN a sector, worksite, missile lane, handoff, or withdrawal becomes threatened, SERGEANTS select the highest-value reachable crisis.
SERGEANTS control the passage, assume brief contact where necessary, and organize handoff or extraction.
PIONEERS brace and hand over the physical result, then recover workers, wounded, tools, records, and transport.
SPEARMEN or another proper receiving formation occupy and sustain the opened ground.
ALL THREE formations account separately for people, leaders, equipment, tools, materials, political burden, source debit, casualties, and recovery.

THREE PARALLEL INSTITUTIONS

ADJACENT, NOT INFANTRY
