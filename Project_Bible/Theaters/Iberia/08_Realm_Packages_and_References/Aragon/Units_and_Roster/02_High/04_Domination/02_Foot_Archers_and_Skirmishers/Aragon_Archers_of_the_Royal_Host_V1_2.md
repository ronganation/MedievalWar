## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Archers of the Royal Host** are the Aragon-led Domination field-bow line. They assemble exact released bow users, file leaders, compatible bows, arrows, strings, repair support, carts, and replacement capacity from Aragonese and controlled, compliant, bargained, or compelled Barcelona-facing sources. Their purpose is to maintain broad, economical arrow pressure across the Royal Host frontage, rotate sections through firing and recovery, punish exposed light and ordinary targets, support infantry movement, and yield before protection, arrows, cohesion, or contributor compliance collapses.

Their strength is **breadth with responsiveness**. They can cover a wider ordinary frontage and react to dispersed or moving targets more economically than Royal Host Crossbowmen. Their weakness is lower effect against armor, shields, and hard prepared positions, together with uneven bows, arrows, practice, leaders, and source consent.

## 4. Direct Collision Firewall

### Jaca Field Archers

Jaca Field Archers remain an Independent Aragonese developed field-bow institution with organized volley pressure, section relief, target discipline, and orderly displacement. Royal Host Archers draw from wider controlled sources, accept greater variation, and serve Aragon-directed operational frontage needs. They gain breadth of political access, not superior native practice.

### Veteran Archers of Jaca

Veteran Archers retain scarce veteran judgment, counter-missile pressure, selective volley control, and formation preservation. Royal Host Archers should lose an equal open contest of skill, cohesion, and selected target economy unless numbers, protection, terrain, or circumstance compensate.

### Crown Archer Companies

Crown Archer Companies remain the Negotiated Union's reciprocal mixed-realm field-bow line, built around sustained broad-front pressure, company relief, and negotiated battle-line coverage. Royal Host Archers use Aragonese mission priority, unequal section obligations, restricted replacement, and politically brittle cohesion.

### Crossbowmen of the Royal Host

Crossbowmen own prepared bolt sectors, loaded reserve, protected advance, hard-lane threat, and occupation-node endurance. Archers own wider ordinary arrow coverage, faster section response, and more economical pressure against dispersed, moving, or lower-value targets.

### Vanguard of the Royal Host

Vanguard files operate ahead of the Host to observe, warn, counter-screen, hand off, and contract. Archers operate nearer the main frontage with deeper ammunition, more stable sections, less reconnaissance, and stronger sustained missile output.

## 12. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **Assemble** exact people, bows, arrows, leaders, repair, transport, and protection.
2. **Sort** files by bow, arrow, practice, readiness, and source constraint.
3. **Assign** main, supporting, relief, reserve, and fallback sections.
4. **Mark** priority targets, no-fire lanes, friendly passages, and yield routes.
5. **Loose** by file or section against worthwhile exposed targets.
6. **Sustain** ordinary broad-front pressure without exhausting every section together.
7. **Relieve** tired, low-arrow, disrupted, or overexposed sections.
8. **Shift** pressure when the Royal frontage advances, contracts, or changes priority.
9. **Yield** the position before cavalry, melee, counterfire, or route failure traps the formation.
10. **Reform and account** for people, leaders, bows, strings, arrows, equipment, compliance, and source capacity.

## 29. Representative Scenarios

### Broad field frontage

The Vanguard reports several approaching light formations. Two Archer sections pressure the frontage while a relief section remains behind Spearmen. The player uses arrows against ordinary targets and preserves Crossbow bolts for armored files.

### Cavalry approach

Archers harass cavalry moving through a predictable lane but yield behind Spearmen before charge distance. Remaining arrows and bows survive because the formation does not mistake harassment for stopping power.

### Counter-missile pressure

A selected enemy missile formation begins to dominate the open exchange. Royal Host Archers may use terrain and supporting pressure, but should yield rather than compete as Veteran Archers or Crossbow Sergeants.

### Compelled-section fracture

One contributor has taken disproportionate losses and withholds replacement arrows. The affected section degrades to limited fire or withdraws by source. Other sections do not receive free ammunition or personnel.

### Protected shift

Spearmen transfer the frontage. One Archer section covers movement while another passes through a validated lane, reforms, and resumes pressure. A blocked passage turns the shift into congestion and output loss.

## 29A. Mature Battlefield Power Floor and Ceiling

A functioning Royal Host Archer formation must exert consequential pressure. Against exposed light troops, ordinary infantry in movement, and cavalry on predictable approaches, sustained arrows should force changes in speed, facing, spacing, route, or commitment. The formation is not limited to cosmetic damage, but neither political breadth nor mass release guarantees casualties or morale collapse.
