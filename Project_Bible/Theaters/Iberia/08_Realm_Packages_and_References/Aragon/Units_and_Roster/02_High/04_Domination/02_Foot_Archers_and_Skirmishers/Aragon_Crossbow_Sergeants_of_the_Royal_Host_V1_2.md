## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Crossbow Sergeants of the Royal Host** are a narrow, selected Domination crossbow core formed from exact reliable operators, file leaders, compatible crossbows, spanning systems, bolts, protection, maintainers, and recovery support released from Aragonese and controlled Barcelona-facing sources. They do not provide the broad firing-sector coverage of ordinary Royal Host Crossbowmen. They concentrate scarce reliable capability around one critical lane, counter-missile contest, command route, breach approach, gate interior, bridgehead, or fighting withdrawal.

Their player value is **control under pressure**. They prepare fewer shots, choose harder target windows, preserve a loaded reserve, endure a contested position longer than ordinary requisitioned sectors, and withdraw under sergeant control before the selected core is captured or destroyed.

## 4. Collision Firewall

### Crown Crossbow Sergeants

The Negotiated Union card remains a selected composite Crown crossbow core legitimized through negotiated partnership. The Domination formation is selected through Aragonese control, inspection, reliability screening, source bargaining, and compulsory access where authority passes. Its cohesion is more politically brittle, its replacement more unequal, and its command more overtly Aragon-directed.

### Crossbowmen of the Royal Host

Ordinary Royal Host Crossbowmen own breadth, several adequate sectors, protected advance, route and occupation coverage. Crossbow Sergeants own one critical lane, selected target timing, greater counter-missile discipline, command-route security, and controlled withdrawal.

### Aragonese Crossbowmen

Aragonese Crossbowmen remain a native adopted crossbow institution. Royal Host Crossbow Sergeants are a Domination composite selected core and cannot freely absorb native capacity or claim superior native continuity.

### Aragonese Pavise Crossbowmen

Pavise Crossbowmen retain prepared positional protection, equipment setup, protected reload, pack-up, and abandonment decisions. Royal Host Crossbow Sergeants may use source-valid protection but do not receive universal pavises or pavise-line endurance.

### Veteran Archers of Jaca

Veteran Archers retain their bow-specific veteran target judgment and formation preservation. Crossbow Sergeants use slower prepared bolt threat, spanning systems, selected lane control, and sergeant-led withdrawal.

## 11. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **Select** exact operators, leaders, weapons, bolts, spanning systems, and support.
2. **Inspect** compatibility, condition, protection, firing lane, reload depth, and fallback.
3. **Prepare** a main firing edge, loaded reserve, relief passage, and withdrawal route.
4. **Mark** the critical target and hold fire against poor opportunities.
5. **Release** one controlled salvo or file sequence.
6. **Cover** the reload and relief cycle with protection and remaining loaded files.
7. **Contest** enemy missiles only while exposure and expected value pass.
8. **Relieve or displace** before counterfire, cavalry, melee, or passage failure traps the core.
9. **Withdraw under sergeant control** through the prepared route.
10. **Recover and account** for people, weapons, bolts, tools, leaders, compliance, and source losses.

## 27. Representative Scenarios

### Critical gate lane

The selected core prepares behind protection inside a gate approach. Ordinary Crossbowmen cover adjacent sectors. Crossbow Sergeants hold loaded files for armored assault troops and withdraw through a Sergeant-controlled passage before melee enters the reload depth.

### Counter-missile contest

Enemy missiles dominate an exposed lane. The core uses cover, target timing, and relief to force displacement. If the enemy's range and protection prove superior, the formation yields instead of spending selected operators in an open duel.

### Cavalry commitment

Vanguard reports a cavalry approach. Crossbow Sergeants hold for a qualified lane while Spearmen prepare reception. A premature release wastes the critical threat; a late withdrawal risks the whole core.

### Source fracture

One contributor refuses to replace losses after repeated exposure. The formation reduces to a narrower lane rather than receiving fictional replacement people, weapons, or bolts.

## 27A. Mature Battlefield Power Floor and Ceiling

The selected core must be materially stronger than ordinary Royal Host Crossbowmen at its chosen task without becoming a universal elite upgrade. Its advantage is not free damage. It is the concentration of compatible weapons, reliable files, surviving sergeants, prepared reload depth, target patience, and a controlled exit around one mission-critical lane.
