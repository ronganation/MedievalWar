**Crossbowmen of the Royal Host** are an Aragon-led Domination formation assembled from exact compliant, compelled, castle, civic, regional, workshop-supported, or otherwise politically controlled crossbow sources. Aragon supplies command priorities, field assignments, protection demands, occupation missions, route obligations, and the authority to concentrate service. Operators, weapons, bolts, leaders, maintainers, carts, workshops, and replacement capacity remain tied to their real sources.

The unit's strength is **breadth under command**. It can establish more than one adequate firing sector, accompany an Aragonese spear-centered advance, reinforce a bridgehead or occupied settlement, and move from one controlled position to another. Its weakness is unequal quality and brittle consent. Weapon compatibility, bolt supply, leader acceptance, replacement restrictions, desertion, passive resistance, source withholding, and overloaded occupation duty can reduce the card long before nominal headcount disappears.

### 1A. What the player buys

- a domination-path crossbow formation that turns controlled partner capacity into usable field and occupation firepower;
- multiple adequate firing sectors rather than one selected professional salvo core;
- the ability to advance by protected stages behind Aragonese infantry;
- strong use in roads, crossings, camps, gates, broad streets, courtyards, and occupied strongpoints;
- visible coercion and compliance costs;
- no free Barcelona roster unlock and no best-of-both-realms technology package.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

**Short description:** Crossbow operators and equipment requisitioned from controlled towns, castles, regions, workshops, and compliant service sources, organized for Aragonese field, route, garrison, and occupation missions. Broadly useful and capable of operating in several firing sectors, but uneven in quality and vulnerable to command friction, restricted replacement, incompatible equipment, and collapsing local compliance.

**Combat fantasy:** Aragonese infantry takes the road, gate, ridge, bridgehead, or market approach. Royal officers assign the firing sectors. Requisitioned crossbow files establish behind the protection line, loose into approaching threats, then advance or displace by stages as the army imposes control. The formation wins by creating enough reliable bolt pressure over the ground Aragon needs to hold, not by possessing the Union's most disciplined salvo core.

**Flavor line:** **Take the road. Mark the sector. Serve the Crown.**

## 14. Signature Battle Cycle

```text
MUSTER exact compliant or compelled capability.
SORT operators, weapons, bolts, leaders, and support by compatibility.
ASSIGN one primary sector and only as many supporting sectors as leadership and protection can sustain.
PREPARE firing lanes, reload depth, bolt issue, passages, protection, and fallback.
ADVANCE under the Royal Host by protected stages when the mission requires movement.
LOOSE by sector against qualified targets.
HOLD or DISPLACE according to protection, bolts, compliance, and the royal mission.
ACCOUNT by source for people, weapons, bolts, desertion, damage, local-defense loss, and return.
```

### 14A. The Royal Host difference

The unit does not become better because Aragon dominates politically. Domination creates the ability to gather and direct more exact crossbow sources toward royal operations. Every additional sector also creates more command, compatibility, protection, transport, and compliance burden.
