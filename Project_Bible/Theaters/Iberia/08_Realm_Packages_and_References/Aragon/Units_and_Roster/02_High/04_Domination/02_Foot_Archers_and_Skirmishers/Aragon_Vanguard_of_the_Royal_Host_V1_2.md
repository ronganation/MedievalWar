## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Vanguard of the Royal Host** is the Aragon-led Domination advance screen. It is assembled from exact released scouts, light infantry, local guides, javelin or bow files, messengers, and accepted or imposed leaders drawn from Aragonese and controlled, compliant, bargained, or compelled Barcelona-facing sources. Its purpose is to extend the Royal Host's sight and warning, contest hostile screens, identify routes and threats, mark usable approaches, delay light enemies, and contract toward the Host before the screen is isolated.

The unit is not a generic skirmisher mob. Its value is the disciplined conversion of scattered light capability into an assigned observation, warning, counter-screen, and handoff system. It does not gain perfect reconnaissance from political control. False reports, delayed messages, local concealment, source friction, pursuit, leader loss, and broken rally routes can make the Royal Host advance on bad information.

## 4. Direct Collision Firewall

### Crown Vanguard Bands

Crown Vanguard Bands remain the Negotiated Union's reciprocal mixed-realm screen, built around wide screening, counter-skirmishing, mutual support, contraction, and phased disengagement. Vanguard of the Royal Host uses politically unequal service, Aragonese mission priority, assigned report chains, controlled rally points, and stronger route and occupation-column duties. It is more vulnerable to source mistrust, false or delayed reporting, unequal exposure, and withdrawal by contributor.

### Almocadens

Almocadens remain trained frontier-vanguard infantry built around concentrated casting, short closing action, and controlled disengagement. Royal Host Vanguard is broader and less assault-focused. It finds, warns, screens, delays, and hands off rather than specializing in a concentrated cast-and-close transaction.

### Almughavars

Almughavars remain experienced aggressive light infantry with raid, disruption, closing, exploitation, breakoff, and regrouping functions. Royal Host Vanguard does not inherit their historical emergence, aggressive operational autonomy, close-combat ceiling, or raiding identity.

### Crossbowmen of the Royal Host

Crossbowmen establish controlled firing sectors and occupation-node bolt pressure. Vanguard files work ahead and around the Host, gather information, contest hostile screens, and contract toward those firing sectors. Vanguard does not become a light crossbow detachment unless exact source files and a future reviewed profile establish that capability.

## 11. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **Assign** screen lanes, observation points, rally points, and return routes.
2. **Advance** ahead of the Host without exceeding messenger and contraction reach.
3. **Search** routes, flanks, crossings, broken ground, and settlement edges.
4. **Observe and mark** threats, usable ground, uncertain contacts, and blocked approaches.
5. **Screen** friendly movement and deny easy hostile observation.
6. **Harass or counter-skirmish** when a limited engagement improves the Host's position.
7. **Delay** hostile light troops without becoming fixed against heavier forces.
8. **Report and hand off** targets, routes, and warnings to the proper Royal Host formation.
9. **Contract** by phases through marked rally points.
10. **Disengage or withdraw by source** before isolation, cavalry, or political fracture destroys the screen.

## 28. Representative Scenarios

### Royal road advance

Vanguard files examine bends, side tracks, heights, and crossing approaches while messengers keep contact with the Host. An enemy screen appears. Selected files delay it while another lane remains under observation. Reports reach the Crossbowmen and Spearmen before the Vanguard contracts.

### False contact

A distant movement is reported as probable rather than confirmed. The player may investigate, shift the Host cautiously, or ignore it. The unit does not automatically reveal whether the contact is a decoy.

### Cavalry pressure

Enemy cavalry threatens an extended screen. Files nearest the threat use terrain and missiles to delay while outer files contract through rally points. A failed route or delayed order may leave one source isolated.

### Occupation perimeter

The Vanguard patrols roads and settlement edges beyond controlled gates. Wide coverage provides warning but weak mutual support. Resistance may feed false reports or target messenger routes.

### Counter-skirmish success

Enemy light troops are driven from observation ground. The Vanguard marks the exposed lane for Royal Host Crossbowmen rather than pursuing into formed infantry.

## 28A. Mature Battlefield Power Floor

The Royal Host Vanguard must contribute real battlefield force, not merely information management. A fresh working screen should defeat poor scouts, pressure ordinary skirmishers, deny easy observation, delay exposed light infantry, and create valuable target windows. Its restrictions prevent it from replacing aggressive light assault infantry or formed missile lines; they do not make it harmless.
