## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Cavallers of the Crown Standard** are the Aragon-led Domination path's selected noble heavy battle wing. The formation gathers exact politically released noble households, household retainers, experienced mounted leaders, warhorses, armor, lances or mounted spears, shields, sidearms, standards, handlers, farriers, remount support, and recovery obligations beneath one common battle standard and an enforceable precedence order.

The Cavallers are committed when ordinary maneuver cavalry, relief cavalry, and Royal Host Lancers cannot safely own the serious mounted contest. They meet a peer cavalry charge, deliver controlled heavy shock, remain in purposeful short mounted melee, press locally while the contest is still winnable, and break away before the noble wing is consumed.

Their battlefield strength is not unlimited shock. It is the ability to **hold together a politically difficult noble wing long enough to win one serious mounted fight and preserve a reformable remainder**.

## 5. Collision Firewall

### Ricohombres of Aragon

Ricohombres remain the Independent path's native developed noble battle cavalry, rooted in Aragonese magnate autonomy, household depth, political influence, and native command. Crown Standard Cavallers are a Domination-specific multi-house battle institution that requires exact release, precedence settlement, common battle command, and controlled partner participation.

### Royal Mesnada

Royal Mesnada retains ruler protection, Royal command continuity, exceptional household intervention, and protected Royal withdrawal. Crown Standard Cavallers are committed to the battle wing, not held primarily around the ruler's person.

### Infanzones of Aragon

Infanzones retain earlier lesser-noble mounted service, ordinary countercharge, regional command support, and lower political and material threshold. Crown Standard Cavallers use scarcer warhorses, heavier protection, deeper household retinues, and a higher contest ceiling.

### Cavallers de Montcada

Montcada retains named-house ownership, Montcada standards, Montcada political release, and the Negotiated Union's house-anchored heavy battle wing. Crown Standard Cavallers deliberately combine several exact households beneath a Domination standard and therefore face precedence and unequal-loss problems Montcada does not share in the same way.

### Cavallers de la Senyera

Senyera retains the highest-gate selected joint shock institution, one qualified decisive rupture, and rally around the Senyera. Crown Standard Cavallers have a lower single-impact ceiling but greater authority for peer mounted contest, purposeful short melee, local press, and heavy-wing reform.

### Lancers of the Royal Host

Lancers remain superior at common Royal Host passage, target preparation, one controlled impact, disengagement, and combined-arms reform. Crown Standard Cavallers are superior in the serious mounted fight after contact, but less efficient and less integrated for ordinary Royal Host impact missions.

## 14. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **ASSEMBLE** exact noble households, retainers, warhorses, standards, equipment, and support.
2. **RANK** households beneath the Crown Standard according to accepted or imposed precedence.
3. **HOLD** the heavy wing until a serious mounted contest or high-value sector justifies commitment.
4. **COUNTERCHARGE** a qualified committed cavalry threat or enter the selected heavy contest.
5. **CONTEST** through controlled heavy shock and purposeful mounted melee.
6. **PRESS BRIEFLY** only while the local decision remains attainable and the exit remains usable.
7. **BREAK OFF** before enemy reserves, Spears, Infantry depth, fatigue, or political fracture consume the wing.
8. **REFORM** households around the Crown Standard with actual surviving riders, warhorses, retainers, leaders, and equipment.
9. **ACCOUNT** honor, blame, ransom, succession, compensation, unequal losses, captured standards, and household recovery.

## 39. Representative Scenarios

### Peer noble cavalry attacks

Enemy heavy cavalry commits against a valuable Royal Host sector. The Crown Standard wing holds until the enemy's direction and support are known, countercharges with settled ranks, contests long enough to stop the mounted breakthrough, and breaks off before fresh enemy reserves create an attritional fight.

### Favorable local press

The initial countercharge disorders the opposing noble wing and exposes its unsupported files. The player commits part of the supporting rank to a brief press. The decision may win the local mounted contest, but it reduces breakoff insurance and increases unequal household loss.

### Montcada or Ricohombre comparison

The Crown Standard wing can contest a comparable heavy formation, but victory is not guaranteed by Domination authority. Household cohesion, warhorse quality, precedence, leaders, ground, support, and reform determine whether the wing survives as a common institution.

### Prepared Spears receive the wing

Enemy cavalry withdraws behind prepared Spears. Correct play stops the contest and reforms. Continuing turns a noble heavy-cavalry advantage into an avoidable political and material catastrophe.

### Standard lost

The Crown Standard falls while several household standards survive. The common wing may reduce to household-specific breakoff or fracture entirely. Surviving nobles and warhorses do not disappear, but common orders and reform cease to be reliable.
