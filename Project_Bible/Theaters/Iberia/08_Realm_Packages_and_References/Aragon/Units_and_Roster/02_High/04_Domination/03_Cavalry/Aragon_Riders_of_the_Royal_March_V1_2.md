## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Riders of the Royal March** are the Aragon-led Domination army's proactive outer-lane contact cavalry. They are assembled from exact released medium cavalry riders, suitable horses, file leaders, lances or mounted spears, shields, sidearms, practical protection, standards, messengers, handlers, remount capacity, and source-specific support.

Their purpose is to take useful mounted space before the decisive cavalry engagement, contest enemy screens and ordinary horse, drive exposed resistance from the outer lane, exploit a genuine break only to a declared limit, recall before heavier reserves close, and wheel toward another flank while enough order and horse energy remain.

They are not scouts, mounted skirmishers, relief cavalry, or another shock line. Their battlefield power is **repeated mounted initiative under disciplined limits**.

## 5. Collision Firewall

### Lancers of the Royal Host

Lancers own a prepared Royal Host passage, one qualified controlled impact, disengagement, and reform. Royal March Riders own proactive outer-lane initiative, interception, bounded pursuit, recall, and second-lane reappearance.

### Mounted Sergeants of the Royal Host

Mounted Sergeants remain held until one recoverable crisis requires relief contact. Royal March Riders operate before the crisis, denying enemy mounted freedom and creating threats on the outside lane.

### Aragonese Riders

Aragonese Riders retain broad native screening, escort, reconnaissance, pursuit, raid response, and withdrawal utility. Royal March Riders are narrower, more combat-focused, more expensive, and more dependent on common lane, contact, pursuit, and recall doctrine.

### Caballeros de Alarde

Caballeros de Alarde retain municipal source identity, limited casting where supported, short municipal attack, sweep, return, and home-duty debit. Royal March Riders are a composite Domination field institution, not a municipal cavalry rewrite.

### Cavallers d'Urgell

Urgell retains its county-owned Negotiated Union line-cavalry institution, county release, standards, home-duty cost, and perspective-specific identity. Royal March Riders use Aragon-led composite command, controlled partner files, Royal Host lane assignments, and politically unequal recovery.

### Royal Frontier and Crown Mounted Sergeants

Both Sergeant families retain crisis relief, mission transfer, withdrawal protection, and second-action logic. Royal March Riders do not wait behind the line to rescue endangered formations.

### Infanzones, Ricohombres, Montcada, Senyera, and Royal Mesnada

These cards retain serious countercharge, heavy mounted contest, noble shock, decisive rupture, or ruler-protection roles. Royal March Riders must refuse these contact ceilings.

## 11. Signature Battle Cycle

1. **OBSERVE** the outer lane, enemy screens, support, ground, pursuit landmarks, and heavy reserves.
2. **CLAIM** useful mounted space without outrunning Royal Host support.
3. **CONTEST** light or ordinary medium cavalry through threat, interception, or short contact.
4. **DRIVE OFF** exposed resistance where the matchup and exit pass.
5. **PURSUE** only after a real break and only toward a declared limit.
6. **LIMIT** the exploitation before enemy reserves, terrain, or fatigue closes the return.
7. **RECALL** files under working leaders and visible signals.
8. **WHEEL** through a known route while sorting riders, horses, weapons, and formation order.
9. **REAPPEAR** on another usable lane or adopt a reduced withdrawal-cover mission.
10. **ACCOUNT AND RECOVER** actual riders, horses, equipment, leaders, compliance, and home-duty capacity.

## 36. Representative Scenarios

### Enemy screen on the outer lane

The Royal March Riders claim the lane and contest an enemy screen. The objective is not total destruction. Once the opposing screen leaves useful ground, the player chooses whether to hold, make a short pursuit, or recall before heavier enemy cavalry arrives.

### Exposed missile flank

Enemy missile troops become exposed beyond Infantry support. The Riders drive toward the flank, but only while the recall route remains open. If prepared Spears turn to receive, the Riders break away rather than becoming weaker Lancers.

### Decoy retreat

Ordinary enemy cavalry withdraws toward a hidden heavy reserve. A pursuit landmark and reserve-sighting condition have already been assigned. Correct play recalls the Riders before the trap closes, preserving the formation for another lane.

### Reappearance

After driving off enemy light cavalry, the Riders recall and wheel behind the Royal Host. The second flank presents a useful opening. The formation reappears in Reduced or Ready state according to actual horse stress and order, not as a fresh teleported unit.

### Outer-lane loss

Fresh heavy cavalry and missile support dominate the lane. The Riders refuse the contest, observe, and protect withdrawal. Preserving the formation is preferable to proving possession through an unwinnable fight.
