## 1. Decisive Current Verdict

A visual and voice authority is justified now because presentation decisions affect profile writing, animation planning, UI, accessibility, localization, casting scope, and future implementation. The authority must remain static. It defines what later work must prove; it does not approve final scripts, voices, generated performances, or Unreal audio integration.

```yaml
current_action: EstablishStaticPresentationAndVoiceProductionStandard
not_current_action:
  - CastFinalActors
  - RecordFinalDialogue
  - ReconstructMedievalLanguages
  - CloneOrTransformAnyVoice
  - GenerateFinalVoiceAssets
  - CreateUnrealAudioBanks
  - BindVoicesToCharactersOrUnits
why_now:
  - PreventWrongFactionVoiceReuse
  - PreserveAragoneseBarcelonaAndNegotiatedUnionDifferences
  - GuideFutureWritingArtAnimationLocalizationAndCasting
  - CreateReusableStandardForOtherFactions
```
