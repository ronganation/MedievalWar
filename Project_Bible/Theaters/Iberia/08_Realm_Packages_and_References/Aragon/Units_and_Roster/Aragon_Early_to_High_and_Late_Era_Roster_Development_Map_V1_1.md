## 1. Purpose and Sequence Decision

This map preserves the resolved fourteen-card Early combat roster while defining the order in which later capabilities may emerge. It prevents two opposite errors: deleting useful Early cards merely because later institutions appear, and creating High or Late cards before their people, equipment, training, institutions, service relationships, and campaign roles exist.

```yaml
Early_roster_membership: Resolved
Early_profiles_to_reopen_for_existential_review: []
High_and_Late_units_created_by_this_map: []
new_exact_values_created: false
core_sequence:
  - PreserveAndClassifyEarlyPersistence
  - EstablishHighEraMilitarySourcesAndInstitutions
  - DevelopFirstCrossbowFamily
  - DevelopFirstHighEraInfantryInstitution
  - EstablishAlmughavarEmergence
  - EstablishMountedSkirmisherOrJineteLane
  - EstablishDevelopedNobleCavalry
  - IntegrateOrdersMercenariesAndExternalRelationships
  - ApplyBarcelonaAragonThreePathEffects
  - ReconcileCurrentHighEraPortfolios
  - DeferLateEraContinuationConvergenceConsolidationAndSuccessorsToDedicatedFutureReview
```

This file authorizes planning sequence only. It does not admit Opening State units, fabricate historical emergence dates, create pools, or activate implementation.
