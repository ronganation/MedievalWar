## 14. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Validate provider, obligation, service term, equipment, leadership, mission, and return.
2. Assemble known sections and inspect weapons and protection.
3. Deploy as sustained field infantry, escort, relief column, or prepared assault body.
4. Hold or advance under leader control.
5. Relieve Jaca Muster, Men of Old Aragon, Warden, or other frontage only where source and mission allow.
6. Conduct a bounded prepared assault against a suitable target.
7. Avoid uncontrolled pursuit or prolonged unsupported melee.
8. Withdraw, reform, and secure command, camp, route, or supply as assigned.
9. Account for personnel, equipment, pay or obligation, casualties, and return.
10. Restore institutional, political, and home-duty capacity.

## 22A. Current Implementation Disposition

The Aragonese Serjeants selection has served its intended purpose: it established institutional service infantry as a valid High Era development basis while rejecting a generic Sword Militia and refusing to manufacture a generic Castle or Town Infantry card. Current High Era work has diversified that basis through political-path-specific institutions rather than preserving one universal Aragonese Serjeants pool or forcing one name across every path.
