Barcelona has a strong older foundation but an uneven modern portfolio. Character, dynasty, Character Network, governance, ecclesiastical, Realm AI, Historical Memory, Historical Momentum, Historical Emergence, Registry, Opening State, schema, fixture, and handoff material already exist. ### 4.6 Realm Personality and AI

Retain consolidating, multi-surface, commercially alert, diplomatically transactional, ecclesiastically engaged, succession-aware, frontier-conscious, and outward-facing behavior without assuming later Crown of Aragon structures or scripted expansion.

### 4.7 Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence

The three historical-system authorities are preservation-heavy.
