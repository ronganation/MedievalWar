## 4. Pathway Status Contract

- **DefinitionOnly:** canonical pathway definition with no instance.
- **Eligible:** required observations pass, but no activation command is accepted.
- **Proposed:** an AI or player proposal exists and awaits owner validation.
- **AwaitingConsent:** at least one required participant has not consented.
- **Preparing:** valid owners are assembling information, resources, access, or agreements.
- **Active:** owners accepted the relevant commands and the pathway is being processed.
- **Suspended:** temporary blocker prevents progression without cancelling the instance.
- **Completed:** owner-defined completion conditions were met.
- **Failed:** owner-defined failure conditions were met.
- **Cancelled:** the authorized actor or rule ended the instance.
- **Expired:** the pathway or proposal exceeded its valid date or turn window.

No status may be inferred from narrative wording.

---

## 5. Common Stage Pattern

Local pathways use this general sequence where appropriate:

1. **Observed:** relevant owner state and pressure observations exist.
2. **Eligible:** hard conditions pass.
3. **Proposed:** a typed proposal is emitted.
4. **Validated:** required owners validate the proposal.
5. **Consent:** required participants decide independently.
6. **Preparation:** resources, access, information, and commands are assembled.
7. **Execution:** owning systems execute accepted commands and events.
8. **Resolution:** owner results determine completion, failure, suspension, or continuation.
9. **Aftermath:** owners publish consequences to Memory, Momentum, Network, AI, and save systems.

A pathway may omit irrelevant stages, but cannot bypass required owner validation or consent.
