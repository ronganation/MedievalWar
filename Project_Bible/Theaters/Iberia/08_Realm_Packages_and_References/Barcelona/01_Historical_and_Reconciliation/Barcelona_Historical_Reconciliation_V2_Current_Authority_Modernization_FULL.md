# 1. Concise Verdict

The January 1062 County of Barcelona should enter content production as an **independent Tier I faction under Ramon Berenguer I**, with Almodis de la Marche as an active, politically consequential spouse and household actor. The campaign shell may coordinate Barcelona, Girona, and Osona externally, but each internal surface must retain separate title, ecclesiastical, regional, property, castle-command, and recognition records.

The strongest opening facts are:

- Ramon Berenguer I is alive and ruling Barcelona in a reign extending from 1035 to 1076;
- Ramon also controls Girona and, after the 1054 family transition, Osona in broad political terms;
- Almodis de la Marche is alive, married to Ramon, and associated with political, legal, diplomatic, patrimonial, and cathedral activity;
- the House of Barcelona is the ruling dynasty, but is not the faction or realm;
- the Romanesque Barcelona Cathedral is active, having begun in 1046 and been consecrated on 18 November 1058;
- Bishop Guislabert is alive and remains Bishop of Barcelona on 1 January 1062, with a reported death on 5 December 1062;
- Mir Geribert is dead before the opening and cannot be an active rebel; the consequences of the conflict and 1059 submission survive through property, oaths, heirs, memories, and castle arrangements;
- Barcelona's growing preeminence is materially connected to paria relationships involving Zaragoza, Lleida, and Tortosa, but exact January 1062 amounts, payment status, protection terms, and routes remain unresolved;
- Peace and Truce institutions are historically available, but exact current rules and enforcement need local acts;
- the Usatges are a layered legal tradition, not a completed opening code;
- Carcassonne and Razès acquisitions, Barbastro's 1064 outcome, Almodis's 1071 death, Ramon's 1076 death, the twins' later joint rule, the Crown of Aragon, and the Gothic cathedral are excluded from Opening State.

Primary classification: **Promote the ruler, spouse, bishop, cathedral, core county surfaces, dynasty, and faction identities into the proposed Barcelona registry. Keep children, exact internal offices, paria contracts, legal provisions, castle holders, and peripheral jurisdictions source-gated. Reject all later constitutional and narrative leakage.**
