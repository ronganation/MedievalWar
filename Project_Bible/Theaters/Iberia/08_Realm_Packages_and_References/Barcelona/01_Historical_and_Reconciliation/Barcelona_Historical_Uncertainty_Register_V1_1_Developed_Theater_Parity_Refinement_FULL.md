## 1. Purpose and Verdict

This register owns the explicit representation of unresolved Barcelona historical questions at the January 1, 1062 opening. It prevents uncertainty from being converted into false precision, absent content, arbitrary defaults, or scripted later history.

The register is modeled on the developed-realm pattern of separating evidence, uncertainty, owner routing, opening effects, permitted fallbacks, prohibited inferences, resolution requirements, save treatment, and promotion gates. Barcelona-specific concerns include comital titulature and itinerary, Almodis's authority, underage and succession candidates, episcopal chronology, Mir Geribert's surviving consequences, parias, Peace and Truce, early Usatges, internal governance surfaces, routes, maritime capability, military obligations, and later polity identities.

## 3. Uncertainty Classes

```yaml
Confirmed:
  meaning: Supported strongly enough for bounded opening use
StronglySupportedBroadly:
  meaning: General proposition supported, exact details remain unresolved
Plausible:
  meaning: Historically coherent but insufficiently evidenced for precise activation
Contested:
  meaning: Sources or interpretations materially conflict
ReviewOnly:
  meaning: Retain for research and owner decision, do not instantiate
Unknown:
  meaning: Necessary value or fact is not established
IntentionalEmpty:
  meaning: Record or instance deliberately absent at opening
ExternalLock:
  meaning: State belongs to another realm, Theater, institution, or framework owner
Conditional:
  meaning: Future pathway eligibility only, subject to world state and owner gates
Quarantined:
  meaning: Identity or proposition cannot safely enter current content
Rejected:
  meaning: Disallowed by evidence or authority
AnachronisticAtOpening:
  meaning: Valid only for a later period, not January 1062
ExcludedLaterOutcome:
  meaning: Later fact that may inform a future pathway but cannot be preloaded
```

## 6. Cross-Authority Uncertainty Rules

### 6.1 Characters and households

- A missing birthday is not zero age.
- A child named in later narrative is not automatically present, recognized, healthy, located, or prioritized at opening.
- Marriage establishes a relationship proposition, not affection, trust, co-rule, alliance, property transfer, or command.
- Historical-only people may generate facts, memories, property questions, obligations, institutional legacies, and relationships without active character instances.

### 6.2 Titles, offices, and governance

- A title style in one act does not prove one universal display title or a distinct gameplay title.
- Control of a campaign shell does not prove every local holder, office, mandate, castle custody, jurisdiction, or recognition.
- Office eligibility does not create an office assignment.
- A bishop person, bishop office, diocese, cathedral institution, site, building, chapter, and property portfolio remain separate.

### 6.3 Agreements, economy, and property

- A paria relationship family does not prove a January 1062 agreement instance.
- An agreement reference does not prove payment, performance, access, military aid, or vassalage.
- A property association does not prove comital ownership, title ownership, institutional ownership, or household control.
- Qualitative wealth does not prove treasury, income, prices, stocks, labor, or production numbers.

### 6.4 Territory, routes, climate, and supply

- A known route does not prove safe, legal, open, toll-free, high-capacity, or seasonally passable access.
- A coastal settlement does not create a port capacity, convoy, fleet, or naval force.
- Winter does not create a specific storm, closure, flood, freeze, attrition value, or supply penalty.

### 6.5 Military

- Military tradition does not create an opening army.
- Castle networks do not establish exact garrisons or ownership.
- Population informs finite recruitment pools but is not directly spent as army manpower.
- External forces require external consent, logistics, command, and owner execution.

### 6.6 Historical systems and AI

- A fact is not automatically a Memory.
- A Memory is not automatically Momentum.
- Momentum is not automatically Emergence.
- Emergence eligibility is not an event outcome.
- AI may observe permitted uncertainty and propose responses. AI cannot resolve uncertainty or mutate owner state.
