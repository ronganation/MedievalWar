## 1. Developed Profile Verdict

Agnes is a reserved Barcelona child identity whose exact opening existence, age, parentage, household role, recognition, property, and future remain unresolved. She nevertheless merits a complete design because a daughter of a major comital household can become strategically meaningful through education, patronage, documentation, property, household government, diplomacy, institutional relationships, marriage negotiation, crisis continuity, and independent adult service.

**Character fantasy:** Grow from a protected dynastic child into an independently recognizable political woman whose value comes from judgment, preparation, relationships, property competence, patronage, and diplomacy rather than from being traded as a marriage token.

**Central question:** If Agnes is validated, can Barcelona develop her into a capable and respected adult with several viable futures while preserving consent, evidence, institutional boundaries, and her independence from any later marriage or lineage story?
