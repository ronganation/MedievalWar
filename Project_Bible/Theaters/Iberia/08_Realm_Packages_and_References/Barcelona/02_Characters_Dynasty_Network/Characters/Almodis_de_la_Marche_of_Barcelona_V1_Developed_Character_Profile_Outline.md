## 3. Developed Character Verdict

Almodis de la Marche begins as a living adult, Ramon's historically supported spouse, a Tier A political household actor, and one of Barcelona's three principal opening character candidates. She is not a passive consort bonus. Her strategic importance lies in the overlap among household continuity, succession preparation, documentary participation, patrimonial questions, patronage, information, diplomacy, external kinship, and Ramon's concentration of power.

Her campaign role is to give the Barcelona faction a second major political center whose interests may align with Ramon without being identical to his. She can improve continuity, widen information, strengthen documentary and dynastic action, mediate disputes, protect children, support patronage, and preserve government during pressure. She can also expose conflicts over access, succession, property, ecclesiastical standing, regional reception, outside kin, and the limits of informal influence.

**Character fantasy:** Shape the survival and direction of Barcelona through household authority, documentary competence, dynastic strategy, patronage, information, and negotiation without becoming a decorative spouse or an ahistorical battlefield ruler.

**Central character question:** Can Almodis convert proximity to the ruler and dynastic importance into durable, recognized, and accountable political capacity without having her agency erased by marriage or her influence mistaken for unlimited co-rule?

Almodis de la Marche is Barcelona's principal consort, Tier A political household actor, succession principal, documentary and patrimonial participant, patronage interface, information broker, and conditional continuity actor.
