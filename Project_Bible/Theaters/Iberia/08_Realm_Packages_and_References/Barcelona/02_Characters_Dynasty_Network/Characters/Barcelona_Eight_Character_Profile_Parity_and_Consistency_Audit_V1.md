## 3. Audit Method

The audit applied six layers:

1. Profile-internal completeness.
2. Cross-profile parity.
3. Person-specific distinctiveness.
4. Owner and authority separation.
5. Later-history and unsupported-inference control.
6. Portfolio-level implementation and migration safety.

The audit compares design depth, not raw byte equality. Different roles require different emphases.

## 6. Adult Trio Parity

### Ramon Berenguer I

Ramon has the strongest direct faction-leadership and battlefield-command profile. His depth covers government, delegation, regional coordination, diplomacy, succession preparation, fiscal and paria strategy, military command, defeat, recovery, and player attachment.

### Almodis de la Marche

Almodis has equivalent strategic seriousness without copying Ramon's ruler or general functions. Her depth centers on household continuity, documentary participation, patrimonial negotiation, patronage, diplomacy, succession preparation, information, and bounded authority.

### Guislabert I

Guislabert has equivalent institutional depth without becoming a secular officer or battlefield commander. His profile covers person-office separation, cathedral and chapter interfaces, witnessing, evidence, jurisdiction, patronage, Peace and Truce, mediation, institutional independence, and crisis continuity.

Adult-trio verdict: Pass. No member is a modifier attached to Ramon, and no profile absorbs another owner's state.

## 26. Cross-Profile Content Collision Audit

No fatal person-function collision was found.

Potentially adjacent surfaces were reviewed:

- Ramon and Almodis both address household and succession, but from ruler and independent household-political perspectives.
- Almodis, Agnes, and Sancha all address patronage and diplomacy, but their life stages, authority, and profile emphases differ.
- Guislabert, Agnes, and Sancha all address institutions, but person-office, patronage, and mediation boundaries prevent duplication.
- Ramon II and Berenguer Ramon II both permit future command and government, but their identity, comparison, reputation, diplomacy, and development emphases differ.

Result: Pass.
