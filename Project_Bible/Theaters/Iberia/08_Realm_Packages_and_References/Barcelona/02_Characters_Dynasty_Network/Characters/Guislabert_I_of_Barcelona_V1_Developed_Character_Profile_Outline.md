## 3. Developed Character Verdict

Guislabert I should be Barcelona's principal opening ecclesiastical character, a major non-battlefield strategic actor, and the living person through whom the player experiences the difference between the bishop, the episcopal office, the cathedral, the chapter, church property, clerical networks, canonical jurisdiction, public mediation, and comital patronage.

His value to the faction lies in connecting religious institution, legal memory, written evidence, public ceremony, dispute settlement, Peace and Truce practice, ecclesiastical property, social legitimacy, and external church channels. His usefulness can strengthen Ramon and Almodis, but he is not their officer, household possession, passive legitimacy bonus, or guaranteed supporter.

**Character fantasy:** Hold together sacred office, institutional independence, documentary credibility, public peace, and political usefulness while powerful secular actors seek cooperation that may become pressure or overreach.

**Central character question:** Can Guislabert make the Barcelona church an effective mediator, documentary authority, and source of public order without allowing comital patronage to absorb ecclesiastical jurisdiction or institutional independence to become political isolation?
