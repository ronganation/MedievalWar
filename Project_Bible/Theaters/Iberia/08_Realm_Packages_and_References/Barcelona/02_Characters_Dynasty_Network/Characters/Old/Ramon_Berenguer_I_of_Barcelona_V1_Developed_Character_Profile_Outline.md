## 3. Developed Character Verdict

Ramon Berenguer I begins as Barcelona's principal comital ruler, Tier A realm anchor, and primary human or AI decision actor. He is already capable, experienced, and politically central, but his authority does not equal a completed centralized state. His campaign is the struggle to turn dynastic position, comital leadership, negotiated regional cooperation, documentary government, ecclesiastical patronage, paria-linked opportunity, frontier pressure, maritime access, and personal relationships into a realm that can act coherently and survive his absence or death.

**Player fantasy:** Build a durable comital power from negotiated authority, regional coordination, wealth, law, patronage, military preparation, and strategic relationships, then prove that Barcelona can survive the ruler who made it powerful.

**Central campaign question:** Can Ramon turn personal comital leadership into durable institutions and succession capacity while balancing household pressure, regional elites, church authority, frontier opportunity, taifa relationships, external negotiation, and finite military resources?
