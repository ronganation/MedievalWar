## 1. Strong Barcelona Household and Court Verdict

Barcelona's 1062 domestic political system should center on a mobile and politically consequential comital household associated with Ramon Berenguer I and Almodis de la Marche, interacting with `court.barcelona.comital`, the Barcelona cathedral complex, distinct Girona and Osona surfaces, frontier Penedès and Olèrdola questions, external kin, clergy, messengers, petitioners, and regional intermediaries.

The household is not a static palace, universal government, dynasty roster, title, court building, or shared information pool. Ramon and Almodis remain separate adults with separately bounded authority, knowledge, property, access, and political goals. Guislabert I remains an independent ecclesiastical principal rather than a household officer. Peter Raymundi, Ramon Berenguer II, Berenguer Ramon II, Agnes, and Sancha remain independent ReviewOnly identities. No child receives opening residence, parentage, household membership, custody, guardianship, education, relationship edges, succession position, property, office, or runtime agency from profile existence alone.

The faction's domestic gameplay should derive from access, residence, documentary capacity, information, counsel, patronage, childcare, succession preparation, regional travel, hospitality, and crisis continuity. It must not invent a complete officer roster or preload later deaths, twin rule, violence against Almodis, or Crown of Aragon outcomes.
