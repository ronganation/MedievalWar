## 1. Strong Barcelona Legal Verdict

Barcelona opens as a comital multi-surface polity whose legal order depends on ruler presence, negotiated recognition, regional usage, documentary acts, oaths, settlement, church jurisdiction, Peace and Truce practice, castle and office interfaces, and practical enforcement. Barcelona is not one province and does not possess one uniform codified law, permanent bureaucratic court, completed Usatges code, universal Peace and Truce score, or unlimited comital jurisdiction.

The faction's legal identity is the struggle to turn Ramon Berenguer I's consolidated but mediated authority into durable order without erasing Girona, Osona, Penedès, Olèrdola, ecclesiastical competence, property distinctions, local reception, external consent, or uncertainty. Almodis de la Marche must remain a serious documentary, patrimonial, household, diplomatic, patronage, and succession actor without becoming an automatic co-ruler. Guislabert I must remain an independent person and bishop candidate whose office, cathedral, chapter, diocese, property, and jurisdiction do not collapse into secular government.

Later Crown of Aragon institutions, unified Catalonia, complete Usatges compilation, predetermined expansion, and later constitutional outcomes are excluded.

## 12. Barcelona-Specific Player and AI Identity

The Barcelona player should experience law as a tool of consolidation that is powerful only when records, witnesses, local recognition, church cooperation, routes, officers, money, and force make it usable. The strongest choice is not always maximal assertion. Partial settlement, confirmation of local rights, negotiated submission, delay, research, mediation, or abstention may preserve long-term authority better than unenforceable judgment.

AI must read owner-valid state, expose reasons, and abstain. AI cannot infer guilt from reputation, rebellion from refusal, consent from marriage, authority from office names, or title from military control.

## 13. Evidence, Knowledge, and Uncertainty

Documentary, oral, oath, customary, physical, possession, account, institutional, and reputation evidence remain distinct. Authentication, custody, availability, interpretation, confidence, visibility, contradiction, and date must be tracked. Unknown is not zero, guilt, innocence, invalidity, or universal knowledge.
