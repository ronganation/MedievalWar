## 1. Audit Verdict

The eleven-file Barcelona specialist chain is structurally coherent and preserves the same January 1, 1062 Winter opening, Project Bible 2.4, Design Expansion Checklist 3.7, Unreal Engine 5.8 target, three-track time model, owner separation, no-activation boundary, later-history firewall, external-owner locks, and advanced-save protection.

The specialist chain is suitable for owner reconciliation and controlled Registry and Opening State planning. It is not authorized for automatic promotion, source rewriting, exact opening population or economic values, exact route or map geometry, exact military pools or units, battles, runtime import, or Unreal implementation.

This audit does not replace the historical-suite audit, the older realm-stack review, character integration control, Registry, Opening State, or machine-readable package contract. It routes the new specialist layer into those owners.
