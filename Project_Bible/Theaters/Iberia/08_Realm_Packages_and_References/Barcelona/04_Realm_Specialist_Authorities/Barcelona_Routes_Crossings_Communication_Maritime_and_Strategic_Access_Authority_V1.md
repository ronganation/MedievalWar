## 1. Strong Barcelona Routes Verdict

Barcelona's route identity is the problem of connecting a Mediterranean comital core to Girona and northern trans-Pyrenean interfaces, Osona and Vic's inland basin, and the Penedès and Olèrdola frontier while preserving river-crossing uncertainty, seasonal friction, local access rights, messenger delay, and externally owned passage.

The authority does not flatten corridors into roads, rivers into navigable lanes, coast into port infrastructure, dispatch into receipt, or movement possibility into permission. Exact routes, crossings, passes, maritime lanes, schedules, coordinates, and travel times remain absent until owner validated.
