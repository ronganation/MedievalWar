## 1. Strong Barcelona Strategic Geography Verdict

Barcelona should play as a compact but internally varied Mediterranean and trans-Pyrenean comital realm whose power depends on controlling access among the Barcelona coastal core, Girona and northern approaches, Osona and Vic's inland basin and uplands, and the Penedès and Olèrdola frontier. Its strategic geography is not a single province, one city with hinterland, one road network, or one uniform climate.

The map must distinguish political association from legal title, property, settlement ownership, route access, garrison control, community acceptance, and military presence. It must preserve uncertainty rather than invent precise modern borders, exact settlement footprints, crossings, wall circuits, ports, travel times, or deployment coordinates.

The geography authority provides implementation-ready definitions, uncertainty, owner routing, visual hierarchy, and battle-context handoffs without creating map assets or mutating Unreal Engine 5.8.
