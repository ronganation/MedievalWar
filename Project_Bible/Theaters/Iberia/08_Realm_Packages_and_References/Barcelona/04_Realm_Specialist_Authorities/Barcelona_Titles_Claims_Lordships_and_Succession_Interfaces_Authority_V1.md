## 1. Strong Barcelona Title and Succession Verdict

Barcelona begins as an independent comital campaign faction under Ramon Berenguer I, with Barcelona as the primary title candidate and Girona and Osona as separately mediated political and title surfaces. The campaign shell must not flatten Barcelona, Girona, and Osona into one title or one uniform domain. Penedès and Olèrdola remain frontier and prior-conflict interfaces whose lordship, property, castle command, jurisdiction, and title questions are not solved by map color or historical association.

The faction's title and succession identity is a powerful but incomplete comital consolidation. Ramon has long-standing authority and broad association with Barcelona, Girona, and Osona, yet succession, title form, regional recognition, custody of offices and seals, household continuity, child status, and practical command remain separate. Almodis is a consequential political and succession-preparation actor without automatic co-rule. Peter Raymundi and the reviewed twin generation create contested future possibilities but no predetermined heir, disinheritance, partition, co-rule, conflict, or death. Agnes and Sancha remain independent people rather than marriage assets.

Later Carcassonne and Razès acquisitions, later twin joint rule, later deaths, and the Crown of Aragon are excluded from the opening state.
