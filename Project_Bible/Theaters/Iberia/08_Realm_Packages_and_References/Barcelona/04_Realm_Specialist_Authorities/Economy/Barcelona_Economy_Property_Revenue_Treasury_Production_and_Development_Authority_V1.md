## 1. Strong Barcelona Economic Verdict

Barcelona's opening economy should be a multi-channel comital economy made usable through local property rights, agrarian production, vineyards and livestock, mills and productive installations, castle and estate networks, urban markets and crafts, cathedral and church institutions, coastal and Pyrenean access, negotiated dues, in-kind obligations, documentary administration, and candidate paria income.

The realm does not possess one unified treasury, one uniform tax system, one fully monetized economy, one direct comital estate, one free labor pool, one total market, or one known opening balance. Barcelona, Girona, Osona and Vic, Penedès, and Olèrdola retain distinct property, production, collection, route, labor, institutional, and recovery questions.

Development gameplay should require authority, property access, labor, materials, transport, funding, legitimacy, maintenance, and protection. Wealth on paper must remain separate from assessed revenue, collected revenue, delivered value, liquid treasury, earmarked resources, and usable campaign capacity.

Roads, bridges, mills, presses, storage, markets, churches, fortifications, workshops, and settlement support require staged completion, inspection, operation, maintenance, damage, and restoration.
