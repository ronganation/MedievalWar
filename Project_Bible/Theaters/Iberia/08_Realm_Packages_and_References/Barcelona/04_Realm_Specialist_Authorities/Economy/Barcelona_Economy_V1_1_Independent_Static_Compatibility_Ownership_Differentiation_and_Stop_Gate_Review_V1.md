## 17. Final Position

Barcelona Economy is closed at static-authority scope. The V1 authority remains the complete substantive Economy owner, and the V1.1 addendum supplies the accepted bounded shared-family compatibility layer. The closure preserves all 40 portfolios, Barcelona, Girona, Osona and Vic, Penedes, and Olerdola differentiation, evidence-bounded Catalan tenure, Treasury multiplicity, conditional paria income, Household and ecclesiastical ownership, finite Population and recruitment boundaries, coastal and Pyrenean opportunity, and layered development and recovery.

The owner-created Barcelona Economy folder is accepted for the authority family. Keep the base authority, conformance review, compatibility addendum, and this closure review together there. No further folder move, rename, or renumbering is required.

Static design closure does not claim repository freeze. The creation hash remains a creation receipt until the current OneDrive byte identity and placement are independently verified. Registry, Opening State, payload, importer, runtime, save, migration, replay, rollback, performance, Economy simulation, balance, and Unreal validation remain outside this closure.
