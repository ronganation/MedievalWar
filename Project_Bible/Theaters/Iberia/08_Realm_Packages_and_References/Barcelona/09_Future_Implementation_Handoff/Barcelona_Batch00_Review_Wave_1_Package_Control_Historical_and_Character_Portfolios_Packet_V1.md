## 2. Wave 1 Purpose

Review Wave 1 packages three related portfolios for bounded owner review:

1. package navigation, current-state routing, and Batch 00 evidence organization;
2. historical authority definitions, evidence, uncertainty, and causality boundaries;
3. Character, Dynasty, Household, and directed Network definitions, including the eight-profile portfolio.

Wave 1 is not a promotion batch. It seeks a recorded disposition for the reviewed definition and routing scope while preserving every unresolved physical identity, full-open, activation, Registry, Opening State, runtime, and Unreal gate.

## 3. Review Outcome Vocabulary

```yaml
AcceptWithConditions:
  meaning: The bounded definition or navigation scope is accepted while listed evidence and downstream uses remain held.
Hold:
  meaning: No acceptance is recorded until specified evidence or correction exists.
RejectSpecificItem:
  meaning: A named record classification or claim is rejected without discarding the remainder of the portfolio.
ReturnForCorrection:
  meaning: A named defect must be corrected and re-reviewed.
NotApplicable:
  meaning: The owner does not control the proposed decision scope.
```

No outcome other than an explicitly completed and recorded owner decision changes status.
