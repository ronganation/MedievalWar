## 5. Maritime Legal and Institutional Context

The <External>Library of Congress Maritime Regulations</External> description states that the *Libre appellat Consolat de mar* is a collection of maritime and commercial ordinances and decrees of medieval origin. The description says the text derives from Barcelona's *Costumes de la Mar*, written between 1260 and 1270, and that definitive writing occurred in fourteenth-century Barcelona with additional legal texts. citeturn23view94
