## 0B. Controlling Effect

This authority controls Barcelona roster sequencing and candidate disposition at the static design level. It does not supersede the detailed source, equipment, battle, recovery, AI, UI, art, or implementation content of the unit authorities. It routes those authorities into one coherent roster.

Where this authority conflicts with the former exactly-20 proposal, this authority controls current design sequencing. The former proposal remains useful as a brainstorm, comparison, and collision record. It does not require twenty cards, numbered-slot survival, symmetrical tiers, automatic upgrades, or simultaneous availability.

Barcelona begins as a county whose military strength depends on communities, regions, castles, and a narrow comital household. The player should feel the difference between assembling broad defensive frontage, releasing a regional formation, drawing troops from custody, risking household retainers, and negotiating a wider mounted concentration.

## 12. Player-Facing Roster Summary

### 12A. Early Core Design Roster

1. **Catalan Spearmen**: broad community defensive frontage and missile protection.
2. **Catalan Bowmen**: conditional community bow pressure behind protection.
3. **Penedès Skirmishers**: screening, escort, delay, contact handoff, and disengagement.
4. **Osona Spearmen**: stronger regional medium field line and formation recovery.
5. **Castle Sergeants**: custody, access denial, relief, and bounded counter-entry.
6. **Comital Swordsmen**: scarce household close combat and command protection.
7. **Comital Horsemen**: scarce household mounted reserve, counterstroke, and extraction.

### 12B. Conditional Early or Transitional Cards

- **Barcelona Crossbowmen** only if direct source, operator, weapon, ammunition, maintenance, and chronology gates pass.
- **High-Status Mounted Contingents** only if mixed-source mounted weight creates a distinct player decision from Comital Horsemen.

### 12C. Capabilities and External Access

- **Barcelona Urban Defense** is a settlement and emergency capability, not a routine Early field card by default.
- **Almughavars** remain Iberian Theater-owned and conditionally accessible through relationships.
- **Catalan Mercenaries** remain separately owned contract-company access.
- **Military-order detachments** remain order-owned external relationships.

### 12D. Leading High Era Research Candidates

- Barcelona Civic Crossbow Militia
- Pavise crossbow package or, if fully justified, separate specialist card
- Evidence-gated later household or men-at-arms foot expression
- Evidence-gated armored mounted family
- Distinct maritime crossbow expression only if a complete maritime source and cycle pass
