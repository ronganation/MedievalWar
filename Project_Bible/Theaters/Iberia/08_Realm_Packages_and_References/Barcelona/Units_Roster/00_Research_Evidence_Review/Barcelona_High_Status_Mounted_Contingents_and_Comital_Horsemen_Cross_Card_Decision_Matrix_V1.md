## 5. Controlling Disposition

```yaml
HighStatusMountedContingents:
  default_expression: CampaignMountedConcentration
  battle_expression: ContextualUseOfComitalHorsemenOrAnotherOwnerValidMountedExpressionWithoutCreatingDuplicatePeopleHorsesOrEquipment
  contributor_records_remain_separate: true
  common_command_terms_remain_active: true
  donor_capacity_reduction_remains_active: true
  political_and_recall_risk_remains_active: true
  multiowner_aftermath_remains_active: true
  separate_recruitable_card: falseAtCurrentGate
ComitalHorsemen:
  status: PrimaryEarlyMountedBattleCard
  source_and_pool_not_expanded_by_concentration: true
```

The campaign concentration must not silently convert non-household contributors into household retainers. “Contextual use of Comital Horsemen” means the battle presentation can consume the established mounted mechanics and card language while the campaign records preserve actual source contributors, readiness, trust, command, and recovery. If that representation proves misleading, the concentration should remain an army-level capability or sub-contingent modifier rather than a field card.
