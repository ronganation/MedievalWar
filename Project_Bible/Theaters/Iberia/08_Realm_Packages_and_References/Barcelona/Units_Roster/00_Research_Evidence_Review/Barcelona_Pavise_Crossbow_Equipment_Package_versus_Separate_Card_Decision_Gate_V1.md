## 3. External Chronology and Use Boundary

A general pavise reference describes the pavise as an oblong shield used from the mid-fourteenth to early sixteenth centuries, often large enough to cover the body and used as stationary cover by archers, crossbowmen, and other infantry. The same source states that crossbow users could shelter behind it during reload and that larger shields could be carried by a specialist bearer or planted using a spike. citeturn25search158

```yaml
located_general_pavise_period: MidFourteenthToEarlySixteenthCentury
located_general_functions:
  - StationaryCover
  - ProtectedReload
  - SiegeUse
located_general_support_roles:
  - CrossbowmanOrArcher
  - SpecialistPaviseBearerForLargerShield
  - GroundDeploymentWithSpikeInSomeCases
Barcelona_specific_evidence_from_general_source: none
```

A separate historical-illustration reference says large pavises were much used by crossbowmen from the fourteenth to early sixteenth centuries, generally in siege work, and states that examples too cumbersome for the crossbow user could be held by another person. It also says the period term could refer more broadly to large, near-rectangular shields. citeturn25search161


These references are general and later. They do not establish Barcelona ownership, field use, municipal service, or a High Era card.
