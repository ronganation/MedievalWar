The **Catalan Community Levy** is Barcelona's cheapest, broadest, and least militarily qualified recruitable infantry family. Eligible local contributors assemble with mixed simple weapons, scattered shields, practical clothing, minimal leadership, and one limited task. The unit can move, join an army, enter a settlement or fort, guard a camp or route, and return where service and logistical gates pass. It is not a settlement-battle crowd and not an automatic free garrison.

The card exists beneath Catalan Spearmen. If a source can consistently provide a spear-defined majority, adequate shields, recognized formation leadership, basic facing and withdrawal discipline, and supported field service, eligible contributors must be evaluated for Catalan Spearmen rather than allowing this card to absorb a second spear roster. The Community Levy remains mixed, fragile, poorly ordered, and unsuited to dependable anti-cavalry frontage.

## 5. Player-Facing Description

Catalan Community Levy are Barcelona's lowest-pool infantry: eligible local contributors gathered with simple spears, axes, clubs, staves, knives, adapted implements, occasional bows only where the source genuinely supports them, scattered shields, and little military protection. Compatible weapons are grouped around a recognizable leader and a simple task, but the unit remains poorly drilled and fragile.

Their value is practical. Community Levy can guard baggage and stores, hold a barricade, fill a quiet sector, protect laborers, slow a raid, support a withdrawal, or occupy secondary ground while Catalan Spearmen, Osona Spearmen, Castle Sergeants, Comital Swordsmen, bowmen, skirmishers, and horsemen perform harder missions.

Ordinary safe recruitment does not require an enemy at the gates. A credible crisis may justify emergency overdraw from the same sources, raising numbers while worsening equipment consistency, leadership coverage, cohesion, morale, civilian pressure, and later recovery.
