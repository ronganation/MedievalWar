## 5. Player-Facing Description

Catalan Community Spearmen are locally raised spear-and-shield infantry drawn from qualified communities when recognized authority, obligation, consent, threat, equipment, leadership, and service terms permit. They form the broadest plausible domestic spear foundation of Barcelona's roster, but a population total or settlement label never creates a ready unit.

On the battlefield they provide affordable frontage rather than decisive assault power. Community Spearmen can protect bowmen, hold a road or approach, reinforce a threatened line, guard a camp edge, cover a withdrawal, or occupy ground while Urban Militia, Castle Sergeants, household troops, mounted forces, or conditional specialists perform harder tasks. The formation rewards clear objectives, supported flanks, stable facing, reasonable depth, and timely release.

Raising the unit carries a local cost. The same people and equipment may be needed for agriculture, crafts, carts, watch, transport, repairs, care, route security, or another military service. Heavy casualties weaken future recruitment, production, transport, confidence, and community defense. An inexpensive recruitment price must not make the formation strategically disposable.

## 15. Battlefield Rhythm

1. Reach a simple supported frontage.
2. Anchor a flank on terrain, friendly infantry, a protected objective, or reserve where possible.
3. Dress the spear line and face the principal threat.
4. Protect Community Bowmen or other missile troops when present.
5. Hold or delay while Urban Militia, Castle Sergeants, household troops, mounted forces, frontier troops, or conditional specialists execute more demanding actions.
6. Avoid pursuit and repeated unsupported turning.
7. Withdraw toward a known rally and return route before complete cohesion collapse.
8. Preserve survivors, leaders, and equipment for community recovery.
