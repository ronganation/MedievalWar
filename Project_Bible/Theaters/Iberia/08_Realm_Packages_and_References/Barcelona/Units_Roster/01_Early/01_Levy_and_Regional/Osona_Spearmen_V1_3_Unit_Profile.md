## 7. Battlefield Cycle

1. Deploy on a difficult wing, slope, irregular field edge, or uneven inland approach.
2. Advance in a practical medium-depth spear-and-shield order.
3. Set the formation before contact when possible.
4. Receive ordinary infantry or deter opportunistic cavalry.
5. Maintain shielded pressure while the spear line remains workable.
6. Transition gradually into close fighting as space compresses and order deteriorates.
7. Use axes and sidearms to sustain the infantry struggle rather than create shock.
