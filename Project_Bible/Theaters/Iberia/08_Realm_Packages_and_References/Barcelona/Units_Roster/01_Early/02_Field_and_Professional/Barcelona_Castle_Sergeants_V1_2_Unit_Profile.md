## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Barcelona Castle Sergeants
- **Class:** Restricted castle-linked custodial spear infantry
- **Source:** A validated castle, castellany, strongpoint, or connected service structure with eligible people, leadership, equipment, and spare defensive capacity
- **Availability:** Narrow, finite, source-specific, duty-reserved, command-dependent, and not created by construction alone
- **Battle promise:** Reliable defensive infantry for gates, passages, courtyards, camp entrances, crossings, relief, and bounded strongpoints
- **Best use:** Holding a critical entrance, reinforcing a pressured spear line, guarding a camp, securing a captured gate, protecting stores or command space, and supporting a relief operation
- **Worst use:** Long pursuit, unsupported assault, broad army frontage, prolonged missile exposure, rapid distant campaigning, and any mission that leaves the source castle dangerously underprotected
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Field release weakens the exact source site's custody, watches, reserves, relief organization, equipment depth, and recovery
- **Counterplay:** Missiles, isolation, fatigue, flanking, simultaneous threats, elite assault infantry, officer loss, blocked retreat, and attacks on the weakened source site

## 23I. Strengthened Battlefield Rhythm

1. Deploy at a gate, passage, courtyard, camp entrance, crossing, protected missile position, or reserve lane.
2. Establish facing, fallback, relief access, and protected duties.
3. Deny entry without breaking formation to chase light targets.
4. Rotate or reinforce files when space, leaders, and reserve depth permit.
5. Commit a reserve only after the dangerous point is identified.
6. Counter-enter against attackers who have crossed a threshold in poor order.
7. Stop when access control is restored rather than turning the intervention into an assault.
8. Withdraw toward a prepared point if the position is bypassed or no longer defensible.
9. Rally, account for people and equipment, and restore the source duty network.
