## 6. Player-Facing Description

Escutats del Comtat are sword-and-shield field infantry drawn from qualified sources across the county’s comital military network. The formation advances behind meaningful shield coverage, closes with swords as its principal weapon, and uses disciplined files to support a threatened frontage, control ordinary infantry contact, or occupy a dangerous gap.

Their strength is adaptability within a disciplined ceiling. Escutats can support a measured advance, reinforce uneven frontage, reface against a changing infantry threat, and sustain a conventional close fight. They cannot stop cavalry like an ordered spear line, control a gate like Castle Sergeants, or match the trust, equipment ceiling, command responsibility, and counterattack quality of Comital Swordsmen.

## 14. Battlefield Strengths and Weaknesses

### Strengths

- Flexible medium infantry rather than another fixed spear tier.
- Shielded approach and useful protection against ordinary missile pressure.
- Sword-and-shield equipment gives meaningful conventional close-combat value without household inflation.
- Better gap support and refacing than broad spear formations.
- Can support either spear-heavy or missile-heavy Barcelona armies.
- Broader and less politically restricted than Comital Swordsmen.

### Weaknesses

- Does not possess dependable anti-cavalry bracing.
- Sword supply and replacement are materially more restrictive than spear or tool-armed infantry.
- Less sustained line endurance than Osona Spearmen in their preferred fight.
- Less bounded defensive reliability than Castle Sergeants.
- Less close-combat quality, trust, and intervention power than Comital Swordsmen.
- Vulnerable to elite infantry, heavy cavalry, concentrated crossbows, and prolonged melee.

- **Player instruction:** Do not spend them as the opening arrow target or pretend that swords defeat reach. Commit through support, press the fight that Barcelona can actually win, then reform.

## 26C. Five-Part Battle Loop

### Shelter

Escutats begin behind, beside, or between the broader spear and missile elements. Shelter is not passive hiding. The player preserves shield condition, stamina, file order, and a usable passage while identifying the sector where swords will matter more than reach.

### Take Over

The company replaces a tiring formation, reinforces a pinned but coherent section, occupies an opened sector, or receives enemies after a deliberate fallback. A takeover needs physical space, leader coordination, missile control, and enough time to establish facing.

### Press

Once the frontage is coherent, Escutats use short controlled steps, active shields, and repeated sword work to force ordinary infantry backward. Press is sustained protected melee, not a shock charge or a hidden attack multiplier.

### Secure

The objective is usable ground: a gap, lane, camp edge, firing position, captured entrance, retreat route, or bounded frontage. The unit halts, restores alignment, protects the position, and refuses pointless pursuit.

### Hand Back

Broad spear frontage may resume the sector while Escutats reform for another bounded use. If handback is impossible, the company may remain in supported main-line duty, but fatigue and short reach steadily reduce the original bargain.
