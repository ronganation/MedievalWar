**Arquers de Girona** are selected regional bow companies, raised only when qualifying Girona-facing communities, settlements, estates, castle districts, households, or recurring-service sources can concentrate enough real shooters, bows, strings, compatible arrows, leaders, practice, and support to exceed the broad community-bow floor.

The unit's advantage is not magical range or regional marksmanship. Its advantage is company organization: clearer target sectors, more reliable cease-fire control, better section fire, better ammunition judgment, and more orderly shifting between protected lanes. This gives Barcelona a stronger counter-skirmishing and field-support bow choice without creating crossbows, elite archers, or a universal Girona archer institution.

## 5. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Arquers de Girona
- **Class:** Selected regional bow infantry
- **Source:** Exact Girona-facing sources able to concentrate qualified shooters, compatible bows and arrows, company leaders, section practice, service authority, protection, supply, and recovery
- **Availability:** Narrow, regional, ammunition-intensive, cadre-dependent, and less replaceable than Catalan Bowmen
- **Battle promise:** Win favorable exchanges against ordinary skirmishers, pressure exposed or turning troops, maintain sectioned fire, and shift between protected lanes before contact
- **Best use:** Behind steady infantry, on a protected flank, at a clear crossing or approach, beside a settlement or camp edge, countering ordinary enemy missiles, and covering bounded relief or withdrawal
- **Worst use:** Cavalry exposure, isolated open flanks, melee, heavy armor at poor angle, protected crossbows, blocked lanes, ammunition waste, and distant service without resupply
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Concentrates Barcelona's better regional shooters, leaders, bows, strings, arrows, containers, repair, transport, and formation practice into a narrower company while reducing local defense and the broad community bow pool where sources overlap
- **Player instruction:** Mark a target sector, spend arrows only where pressure matters, shift one section before the lane collapses, and preserve the company from melee
- **Flavor line:** Girona sends not more arrows, but better-ordered fire.

## 21. Strengths, Weaknesses, and Counterplay

### Strengths

- Better company organization than broad Catalan Bowmen.
- Stronger counter-skirmishing and target-sector discipline.
- Section fire can preserve bounded pressure during movement.
- Better cease-fire, lane handoff, reform, and withdrawal control.
- Useful behind Barcelona’s spear-heavy infantry structure.
- Strong regional flavor without relying on terrain magic.

### Weaknesses

- Narrower source and slower replacement than Catalan Bowmen.
- Still poor in melee and vulnerable to cavalry.
- Does not defeat armor through label or superior range.
- Cadre loss heavily degrades the defining company behaviors.
- Arrow, bow, string, and lane failures can collapse the role before headcount collapses.
- Cannot match crossbow shot weight or specialist archer consistency.

## 34F. Concentrate, Mark, Loose, Displace, Preserve

### Concentrate

Create a company from compatible selected sources without erasing ownership or draining every bow-capable community. Concentration is a campaign and deployment achievement, not a passive stat tier.

### Mark

Assign sectors, priority targets, cease-fire conditions, and displacement triggers. Mark improves judgment and reduces duplication. Mark never increases projectile damage.

### Loose

Apply controlled volleys, section fire, aimed loose fire, rapid pressure, or withdrawal cover according to target and ammunition value.

### Displace

Move one section or the whole company through a real lane, accepting temporary loss of fire, fatigue, disorder risk, and new range uncertainty.

### Preserve

Stop the exchange while the company still has shooters, cadres, bows, strings, arrows, and a route. Preserve may mean sheltering behind spears, withdrawing from cavalry, holding arrows for a later approach, or refusing a specialist duel.
