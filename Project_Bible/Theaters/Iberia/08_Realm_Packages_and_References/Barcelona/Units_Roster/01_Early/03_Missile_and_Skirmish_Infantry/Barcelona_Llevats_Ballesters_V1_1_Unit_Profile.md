**Llevats Ballesters** are Barcelona's lower-threshold called crossbow detachments. They assemble only where an exact settlement, castle district, lordship, household, community, or defensive site can release actual operators, serviceable crossbows, compatible bolts, spanning arrangements, leaders, repair support, and a protected mission.

Their battlefield value is ordinary but real: occupy a defended lane, hold loaded threat, release against a worthwhile commitment, reload behind protection, and clear the working space before infantry or cavalry reaches it. They are cheaper and broader in potential source than developed Barcelona Crossbowmen, but markedly worse at movement, reform, target coordination, repeated cycles, and sustained campaigning.

## 4. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Llevats Ballesters
- **Class:** Basic protected levy crossbow infantry
- **Source:** Exact local capacities with released operators, serviceable crossbows, compatible bolts, spanning methods, basic leaders, maintenance, protection, and return
- **Availability:** Conditional and potentially broader than developed Barcelona Crossbowmen, but finite, locally fragmented, equipment-led, and defensive-duty sensitive
- **Battle promise:** Create affordable bolt threat in a defended lane, especially against dense, armored, mounted, or committed targets
- **Best use:** Behind Catalan or Osona Spearmen, at gates, crossings, barricades, camp edges, broad streets, walls, and prepared slopes with reload depth and escape
- **Worst use:** Open unsupported ground, mobile skirmishing, repeated redeployment, cavalry contact, prolonged counterfire, melee, bolt depletion, and distant service without repair
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Removes operators, crossbows, bolts, spanning gear, leaders, maintainers, transport, stores, and local missile defense from exact sites
- **Player instruction:** Hold the loaded threat, spend bolts on the commitment that matters, reload only while the lane remains protected, and leave before the reload space becomes the front
- **Flavor line:** Load behind the shields. Loose into the commitment. Clear the lane.

## 5. The Five-Part Battle Cycle

### Gather

Assemble exact local detachments without pretending that levy status creates crossbow skill. Inspect operators, weapons, bolts, spanning methods, leaders, protection, reload depth, and withdrawal.

### Shelter

Deploy behind spears, walls, barricades, gates, terrain, or another credible frontage. Shelter is the essential precondition for useful repeated fire.

### Load

Individual files move through real readiness states. The company is rarely wholly loaded or wholly empty. Mixed weapons and methods produce uneven timing.

### Loose

Release deliberately against a valuable target or allow basic independent shots from ready files. No shot guarantees penetration, a horse kill, or morale collapse.

### Clear the Lane

Fired files recover to reload or the detachment withdraws behind protection. If enemy contact enters the reload zone, the missile role has failed.

## 23. Battlefield Strengths and Weaknesses

### Strengths

- Earlier and broader potential crossbow access than the developed family.
- Meaningful pressure against dense, armored, mounted, or committed targets.
- Strong value in defended gates, crossings, streets, walls, camps, and constrained lanes.
- Several real detachments can create useful aggregate bolt volume.
- Loaded threat can shape an approach even before firing.
- Clear combined-arms role behind Barcelona's spear-heavy infantry.

### Weaknesses

- Slow, uneven reload and weak movement recovery.
- Brittle leadership, passage, and equipment compatibility.
- Poor melee and severe danger from cavalry entering reload depth.
- Limited bolts and slow weapon or operator replacement.
- Vulnerable to suppression, counterfire, flanking, and repeated redeployment.
- Less target control, reform, endurance, and campaign flexibility than developed Barcelona Crossbowmen.
