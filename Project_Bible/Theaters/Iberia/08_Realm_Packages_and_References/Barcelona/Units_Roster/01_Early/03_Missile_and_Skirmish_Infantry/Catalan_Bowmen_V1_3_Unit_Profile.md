## 6. Player-Facing Description

Catalan Community Bowmen are locally supplied bow users raised only where a community can provide both a valid service contribution and an actual missile capability. The unit is not every hunter, every person able to draw a bow, or every settlement resident. It requires available people, practical familiarity, serviceable bows, strings, arrows, leaders, assembly, supply, protected firing lanes, and an orderly return plan.

In battle, the formation supports rather than replaces Barcelona's line. Community Bowmen fire most effectively from behind Catalan Community Spearmen or another credible frontage, from prepared settlement approaches, or where terrain creates clear shooting lanes and a safe withdrawal. They can pressure exposed light infantry, compressed attackers, enemy missiles, cavalry before contact, or disordered targets. They cannot rely on superior range, armor penetration, close combat, or magical terrain skill.

Recruitment creates a real campaign cost. Every formation removes scarce shooters and equipment from local defense while consuming arrows, strings, repair capacity, transport, stores, and community confidence. Heavy casualties destroy people with actual skill, while ammunition and craft losses can close recruitment even when population remains.

```yaml
invalid_transitions:
  - AssemblyDirectlyToElitePrecisionFire
  - DegradedToFullFireControlWithoutRecovery
  - MeleeContactToSafeShootingWithoutDisengagementAndReform
  - VictoryToInstantAmmunitionOrPoolRefill
```

Signature cycle:

1. Take position behind Community Spearmen, terrain, a wall, or another real protection source.
2. Inspect remaining bows, strings, arrows, and firing lanes.
3. Hold fire against poor or obscured targets.
4. Harass exposed, compressed, disordered, or lightly protected targets.
5. Concentrate only when command and target value justify the ammunition.
6. Shift locally if lanes close.
7. Withdraw before cavalry or formed infantry reaches the unit.
8. Rally near a leader, protection, and remaining ammunition.
9. Record losses and return surviving capacity to the owner.
