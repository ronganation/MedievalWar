**Cavallers del Comtat** are Barcelona's scarcer non-household mounted combat tier. They assemble from exact eligible mounted households, lordships, better regional riders, leaders, horses, shields, spears or lances, mail or other protection where actually supported, attendants, tack, and recovery capacity. Their purpose is not routine scouting or escort. They wait behind the lighter county riders and infantry line until an enemy cavalry commitment, exposed flank, local collapse, or relief crisis justifies a stronger mounted intervention.

They are capable enough to contest ordinary cavalry, countercharge a committed mounted threat, deliver controlled shock into exposed or fixed troops, continue briefly in close mounted combat, and reform for a second bounded mission. They remain below true household or later elite heavy cavalry in trust, cohesion, equipment consistency, charge ceiling, and recovery depth.

## 15. Signature Cycle: Hold, Answer, Press, Reform

### Hold

Remain behind or beside the main battle, preserving horse condition and formation order while Genets del Comtat or foot screens provide information.

### Answer

Move against a real mounted commitment, exposed flank, or local line crisis. Alignment, support, angle, and exit matter more than animation spectacle.

### Countercharge or Strike

Deliver a controlled mounted impact against cavalry already committed, ordinary cavalry with local advantage, or exposed infantry fixed by Barcelona foot and missiles.

### Press

Continue briefly with sidearms or shortened weapons only while leaders, standards, horse condition, friendly support, and breakoff space remain.

### Break Off

End contact before the formation is enveloped, pinned by spears, exhausted, or drawn into pursuit beyond support.

### Reform

Reassemble by leader, standard, source section, and surviving rider-horse pair. A second mission is earned through preserved order, not granted automatically.

### Return

Account for people, horses, equipment, ransom, property, household cost, political grievances, and recovery.

## 23. Battlefield Strengths

- Credible upper Early Era response to ordinary enemy cavalry.
- Strong countercharge when held in reserve and committed on time.
- Better mounted-melee tolerance than Genets del Comtat.
- Controlled limited shock against fixed or exposed targets.
- Can relieve a threatened line and reform for a second bounded action.
- Gives Barcelona serious native mounted combat without spending Comital Horsemen on every cavalry crisis.

## 24. Battlefield Weaknesses

- Scarce and politically harder to release than Genets del Comtat.
- Still vulnerable to prepared spears, missile concentration, bad ground, and superior heavy cavalry.
- Less trusted, responsive, and command-focused than Comital Horsemen.
- Mixed sources and equipment reduce perfect uniformity.
- Prolonged mounted melee rapidly damages horses, leaders, order, and recovery.
- Pursuit and overpress can convert victory into loss of the county mounted reserve.
