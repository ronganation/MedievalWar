- **Unit:** Genets del Comtat
- **Class:** Early light service cavalry
- **Source:** Exact released riders, combat-capable mounts, spears or light lances, shields where available, sidearms, tack, leaders, handlers, routes, feed, water, shelter, and service relationships
- **Availability:** Barcelona's broadest native mounted combat family, but still scarce, regional, horse-limited, recall-sensitive, and slow to recover

- **Best use:** Screening, flank threat, anti-skirmisher work, escort, raid response, route security, bounded pursuit, relief movement, and withdrawal cover
- **Worst use:** Prepared spears, fresh medium or heavy cavalry, sustained melee, repeated shock, steep or broken ground, crossfire, and deep pursuit

- **Flavor line:** The county's riders win space and time, not battles by themselves.

## 15. Signature Cycle: Range, Threaten, Strike, Return

### Range

Move ahead, beside, or behind the army through usable routes. Observe through physical presence, messengers, and line of sight. No perfect scouting is granted.

### Threaten

Force enemy missiles, skirmishers, riders, reserves, or exposed flanks to acknowledge mounted position. Threat is useful even without charging.

### Strike

Attack only exposed missile troops, loose skirmishers, isolated support, disordered infantry, or light cavalry under local advantage.

### Break Off

Leave before prepared infantry or stronger cavalry fixes the formation in melee.

### Pursue or Cover

Pursue broken troops within an explicit limit, or remain between pursuers and a withdrawing friendly force.

### Return

End service, account for people, horses, property, routes, fatigue, and local-defense consequences.

## 21. Battlefield Strengths

- Barcelona's broadest and most flexible mounted combat access.
- Useful screening, escort, interception, pursuit, and withdrawal cover.
- Faster reaction than foot formations where routes permit.
- Can punish exposed missiles and disordered infantry.
- Preserves Comital Horsemen and the future upper mounted tier for more serious combat.
- Regional expressions add flavor without creating redundant cavalry cards.

## 22. Battlefield Weaknesses

- Weak against prepared spears and coherent infantry frontage.
- Limited sustained melee and countercharge power.
- Outclassed by better equipped or disciplined cavalry.
- Vulnerable to missiles, crossfire, blocked exits, and difficult ground.
- Horse fatigue and rider-horse separation quickly reduce usefulness.
- Pursuit success can become strategic failure if recall and return are ignored.
- Broadest Barcelona cavalry does not mean abundant cavalry.
