The enjoyment of the card comes from **timing and obligation**. Comital Swordsmen should remain close to command, the household standard, a gate, a camp approach, a withdrawal lane, or another protected object. The player commits the unit when an ordinary line is failing or an enemy has entered a compact, high-value position. ## 6. Player-Facing Description

Barcelona Comital Household Foot Retainers are trusted members of the comital following assigned to protection, escort, camp, gate, command, and demanding field service. Their source is narrower than ordinary community or regional infantry. Household connection alone is insufficient: the formation requires eligible people, current trust and duty, weapons, shields and protection where available, leaders, collective practice, supplies, a protected-object plan, and a lawful release from competing assignments.

In battle, the retainers normally remain near the count, command group, household standard, camp, baggage approach, gate, crossing, or threatened reserve sector. They can stabilize a broken frontage, counterattack an enemy already fixed by other infantry, secure movement through a gate, cover the withdrawal of a protected person, or hold a compact position long enough for the host to reform. They should not fill the entire line or lead every assault.

Their strength is reliability for a specific purpose, not superhuman combat power. Heavy casualties can reduce personal protection, court and residence security, command continuity, experienced leadership, household trust, and future crisis response. Replacement people do not immediately restore the formation's memory.

## 26. Signature Battlefield Cycle

2. Deploy near enough to respond without filling routine frontage.
3. Let Community Spearmen, castle troops, urban defense, or regional contingents own their ordinary positions.
4. Track threats to the protected object, command lane, camp, gate, flank, and withdrawal route.
5. Reinforce, relieve, or counterattack only when the crisis justifies household risk.
6. Maintain a compact frontage, flank support, signal contact, and a route back to the protected object.
7. Hand prolonged broad frontage back to ordinary infantry where possible.
