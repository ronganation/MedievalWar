## 1. Decisive Battlefield Thesis

**County Shield Companies are Barcelona's recurring nonhousehold sword-and-shield infantry developed into a more dependable field tool: preserve a fresh company, take over a manageable infantry fight, press for limited useful ground, secure it, and return the frontage before short reach and fatigue reverse the gain.**

They should feel like proper fighting infantry. A healthy company can defeat weak foot, seriously contest ordinary trained infantry, occupy a supported sector directly, and win an ordinary close fight without waiting for a perfect relief puzzle. Their signature is choosing the right commitment, not gaining a magical ability when another unit becomes tired.

The High expression strengthens the same bargain already present in the Early family. More compatible swords and shields, retained leaders, practiced passages, better supply, and reliable handback make the company easier to use repeatedly. They do not become household guards, professional heavy line infantry, or shock troops.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit expression:** County Shield Companies
- **Canonical family:** Escutats del Comtat
- **Class:** Recurring-service medium sword-and-shield infantry
- **Weapons:** One-handed swords and substantial shields; limited source-valid secondary weapons do not redefine the card.

- **Battle promise:** Replace or reinforce tiring frontage, win the ordinary supported close fight, and leave usable ground behind.

## 11. Shelter

Keep the company behind, beside, or between friendly frontage while preserving stamina, shields, and a viable approach. Identify where short-range fighting will matter more than spear reach. Shelter is not permanent hiding: a favorable ordinary fight should trigger commitment before the army loses the opportunity.

Friendly missiles need safe firing and withdrawal relationships. The company does not inherit Host Spearmen's broad missile-shelter system or grant nearby bowmen a bonus.

## 12. Take Over

Use a real lane or yielding files. Confirm the target sector, outgoing unit's state, principal threat, flank support, and exit. A clean transfer is ideal, but reinforcing in place or occupying an empty supported sector are valid uses.

```yaml
takeover_outcomes:
  Clean: OutgoingFilesYieldAndSwordShieldFrontForms
  Pressured: TemporaryMixedFilesDisorderAndLoss
  ReinforceInPlace: AddActualDepthWithoutPassingThroughPinnedAllies
  OpenSector: EstablishFrontageDirectly
  FallbackReception: ReceiveOrdinaryEnemyAfterDeliberateFriendlyWithdrawal
  Congested: MixedFilesAndWeaponInterferenceRestrictPress
  Repulsed: EnemyOrMissilesPreventUsefulEntry
  Failed: FacingLaneOrCommandBreaks
```

A blocked lane is a physical problem. It cannot be solved by an animation that walks through occupied bodies or an ability that instantly restores the outgoing unit.

## 13. Press

Once facing is established, use active shields, repeated sword work, and short controlled movement to win an ordinary supported melee. Pressure is not a charge multiplier, morale aura, or guaranteed displacement. Enemy reach, armor, freshness, support, and ground determine whether progress is possible.

```yaml
press_outcomes:
  CleanPress: EnemyYieldsLimitedGroundWhileCompanyRemainsCoherent
  PressuredPress: GroundGainedWithDisorderFatigueAndLoss
  StalledPress: EnemyReachArmorOrReservePreventsProgress
  CongestedPress: FriendlyGeometryPreventsUsefulForwardWork
  Abort: HaltRefaceSeekReliefOrWithdraw
stop_when:
  - ObjectiveReached
  - FlankOrExitThreatened
  - FreshEliteOrMountedReserveArrives
  - ShieldsLeadersOrStaminaCannotSustainContact
  - FurtherGroundWorthLessThanCompanyRecovery
```

Do not repeat a stalled press immediately without changed conditions. A unit that can only succeed by cycling an ability has failed the intended Total War design.

## 14. Secure

Halt on useful ground: a gap, firing position, camp edge, lane, or bounded frontage. Restore alignment, identify the next threat, preserve casualty movement, and reconnect neighbors. Enemy retreat is not permission for deep pursuit.

Consolidating a captured entrance is possible after another force creates access. It does not grant castle custody, keys, jurisdiction, engineering, or automatic garrison status. Castellany troops remain the better access-control specialists.

## 15. Hand Back and Reform

A real receiving formation must exist, know the threat, and be able to hold. If the receiver is weak or absent, the company may remain on supported frontage. That is a legitimate fallback, but short reach and fatigue erode the original bargain.

## 16. Ordinary Utility Beyond the Cycle

A serviceable company can deploy directly into a supported main-line sector, receive ordinary infantry after a planned fallback, reinforce a flank, protect missiles during withdrawal, or consolidate ground opened by another arm. It is not useless without a perfect handoff.

The limitation is mission efficiency. Spears supply cheaper width and mounted deterrence. Regional spears endure the prolonged hard sector. Castle-service troops restore bounded access. Household swords protect command and intervene in exceptional crises. County companies win manageable field close fights without spending those scarce specialists.

## 17. Performance Envelope and Counterplay

These are proposed testing targets, not measured balance results.

- **Weak or disordered foot:** should win a supported ordinary fight.
- **Ordinary trained infantry:** should contest strongly and often win when committed fresh with support.
- **Peer sword-and-shield infantry:** equipment, leaders, fatigue, flanks, and reserves decide the outcome.
- **Tired spears at close range:** a useful target after their reach advantage is compromised, not proof that swords automatically defeat spears.
- **Fresh deep long weapons:** unfavorable frontal commitment; disrupt or flank with support rather than feed files into reach.
- **Fresh heavy or household elites:** normally need support or withdrawal; no routine unsupported victory.
- **Cavalry:** no dependable brace. Friendly spears, obstacles, warning, and mounted support remain necessary.
- **Bows and crossbows:** directional shields help, but prolonged fire and crossfire remain dangerous.

Enemy counterplay is to shoot before contact, preserve reach, threaten the flank, congest the passage, commit fresh reserves after fatigue, and block handback. The company should be dangerous when well used, not protected from every answer.

## 18. Shield Direction, Cavalry, and Threat Changes

Shield protection is strongest against the faced threat while ordered. Turning, crowding, damaged boards, leader loss, and multiple firing directions weaken mutual coverage. No all-round resistance is implied.

A cavalry threat can invalidate an otherwise favorable infantry press. The correct response may be halt, reface toward support, yield ground, or withdraw behind spears. Forming deeper does not turn swords into an anti-cavalry weapon. Heavy cavalry must remain a clear reason not to replace the spear line with shield companies.

## 19. Terrain, Weather, and Visibility

Open or semi-open ground with room for transitions is the default. Field edges, gentle slopes, broad approaches, and camp lanes can suit the role. Sparse woodland may require reduced frontage. Dense woods, deep mud, marsh, steep ground, tight stairs, and rubble can prevent useful passage and refacing.

Weather affects grip, footing, visibility, fatigue, signals, supply, clothing, and recovery through shared systems. Fire and smoke may close an exit or make an objective untenable. No Catalan, county, urban, or shield label grants a flat terrain or weather bonus.

## 20. Command, Morale, and Fatigue

Recognized leaders, familiar files, one clear objective, protected flanks, and a visible exit support cohesion. Repeated turns, broken handoffs, shield loss, heavy missiles, elite reserves, and cavalry pressure undermine it.

## 22. Cost, Availability, and Army Composition

Qualitative proposal: moderate-to-high equipment investment compared with ordinary spears, recurring but limited availability, and moderate-to-slow replacement when blades or leaders are lost. Exact statistics, prices, upkeep, unit size, duration, and pool values remain unset.

A qualified field army may support one or more companies when real sources pass. This is not a guaranteed entitlement or mandatory quota. Multiple companies should create a supported close-combat layer, not replace the entire line. If the mission needs cheap width, mounted reception, or quiet local defense, do not pay the sword-company cost.

## 23. Provider Cost, Service, and Return

Selection commits people, swords, shields, helmets, leaders, practice, transport, repair, and support that have other uses. Protected community, regional, castle, labor, household, and local-defense requirements remain real. One routine deployment should not automatically devastate every provider; consequences scale with duration, repeated losses, distance, and source threat.

Accepted service may be extended, reduced, recalled, or refused. Recall uses actual command and routes, not teleportation or unit deletion during combat. Handback and immediate safety must be resolved before a return can occur. The main UI should show the dominant blocker and cost, with detailed provenance available separately.

## 25. Siege, Settlement, and Maritime Use

In siege defense, use the company as a supported reserve, broad-street counterpressure body, or withdrawal line. In siege offense, consolidate a gained position or support camp and missile operations. Do not make it the default ladder or breach-storming specialist.

Temporary security does not confer civic office, prisoner jurisdiction, castle custody, or permanent occupation authority. Embarkation requires ships, crews, lift, supply, landing support, and return. No marine, boarding, mounted, or engineer identity follows from the sword-and-shield role.

## 30. Visual, Animation, Audio, and Accessibility

First read: swords and substantial shields, medium field infantry. Second: more coherent equipment grouping and practiced sections than an uneven formation of the same family. Third: visible leaders, repairs, and campaign wear. Maturity is visible through execution and maintenance, not identical mail or ceremonial polish.

Avoid Roman or Byzantine heavy-infantry coding, Almughavar back-projection, javelin silhouettes, household livery, castle-watch markers, and later uniform heraldry. Source variation remains restrained and readable.

Animate shielded approach, individual sword readiness, clean and pressured takeover, reinforcement in place, repeated sword work, stalled pressure, halt, refacing, secure handback, and fatigued withdrawal.
