## 3. Independent Barcelona Development Thesis

Independent Barcelona builds this family through its own surviving county relationships rather than through political union or domination. Development may strengthen:

- recurring host assembly and recognized command;
- retention of experienced file leaders;
- compatible spear and shield grouping;
- helmets, padding, footwear, belts, scabbards, tool kits, and repair;
- missile passages and protected firing relationships;
- relief lanes, reserve depth, and file exchange;
- food, water, shelter, transport, medical support, and return planning;
- casualty, property, obligation, grievance, and recovery accounting.

Independent development does not create people, weapons, armor, officers, carts, food, institutional trust, or recruitment capacity. It creates the opportunity to concentrate actual surviving inputs into a more dependable host formation.

## 10. Signature System: Host Line Rotation

Host Line Rotation is the unit's defining Total War behavior. It is not an instant fatigue reset or numerical bonus.

### Establish

The unit dresses a supported frontage, confirms neighbors, marks missile passages, preserves depth, and fixes its rally and withdrawal routes.

### Shelter

The spear line protects friendly bowmen or crossbowmen from ordinary frontal contact while allowing planned firing and withdrawal lanes.

### Receive

Files meet ordinary infantry pressure or a mounted probe while facing, depth, ground, flanks, and cohesion remain adequate.

### Rotate

When pressure and space permit, damaged or exhausted front files yield locally to prepared relief files. Rotation is slower and riskier under heavy contact, missile suppression, leader loss, congestion, or uneven equipment.

### Reform

After compression or partial disorder, surviving leaders restore enough facing and spear depth to hold, hand over, or withdraw.

## 11. Battlefield Role and Combat Cycle

1. March as source sections under a recognized host command.
2. Establish a broad but controlled spear-and-shield frontage.
3. Shelter friendly missiles while preserving movement lanes.
4. Receive ordinary infantry and deter frontal mounted probes.
5. Rotate pressured files where real depth and space permit.
6. Refuse pursuit that would break the line.
7. Reform after localized disorder.
8. Hand the sector to fresher specialists or withdraw before elite pressure destroys the formation.
9. Return surviving sources, leaders, and equipment for recovery.

## 12. Strengths

- Dependable affordable High Era frontage.
- Better section and file control than Catalan Spearmen.
- Strong integration with bowmen and crossbowmen.
- Conditional mounted-threat deterrence from the front.
- Useful relief depth and local file rotation.
- Better reform and withdrawal behavior than broad community spear formations.
- Broad enough to support major armies without becoming an unlimited pool.
- Frees Osona, castle, shield-company, and household troops for their specialized work.

## 13. Weaknesses

- Limited offensive killing power.
- Vulnerable to elite or heavily armored infantry.
- Vulnerable to concentrated crossbows and sustained archery.
- Repeated turning damages formation quality.
- Flank and rear contact can collapse the spear bargain quickly.
- Relief fails under congestion, leader loss, heavy pressure, or insufficient depth.
- Poor pursuit and breach-assault performance.
- More expensive in equipment and support than Catalan Spearmen.
- Loss of experienced host cadres significantly slows recovery.

## 17. Morale, Cohesion, Fatigue, and Rally

Morale is strongest when the formation retains recognized leaders, familiar source sections, clear service terms, supported flanks, visible missiles or reserves, and a viable return route. It weakens under broken promises, prolonged distant service, abandonment of source communities, heavy missile losses, leader death, neighboring collapse, flank threats, and closed withdrawal routes.

Rally requires surviving leaders or section cadres, physical separation from immediate pressure, enough coherent files to restore facing, and a recognizable rally point. Independent identity grants no hidden morale bonus.

## 18. Combined-Arms Contract

- **Catalan Community Levy:** absorbs low-demand rear tasks and emergency local defense.
- **Catalan Spearmen:** provide broader and cheaper community frontage where a full host concentration is unnecessary or impossible.
- **Host Spearmen of Barcelona:** supply the dependable Independent High Era main line.
- **Shieldmen of Osona:** hold difficult wings and sustain compressed infantry fighting.
- **Sergeants of the Castellanies:** secure gates, passages, camps, captured access points, and relief interventions.
- **County Shield Companies:** pass through or beside the host line to win manageable close fights and secure recovered ground.
- **Foot of the Comital Mesnada:** remain the scarce command-protection and crisis-counterattack reserve.
- **Bowmen and crossbowmen:** exploit the shelter, fixed targets, and controlled passages created by the host line.
- **Cavalry:** protects flanks, counters enemy mounted pressure, and exploits enemies fixed against the spears.

No proximity bonus is automatic. Combined-arms value requires actual positioning, passage, timing, support, and command.

## 20. Siege, Settlement, Route, and Maritime Boundaries

Host Spearmen are useful in siege defense as secondary frontage, missile protection, road and camp security, breach reserve depth, and organized withdrawal cover. They are poor first-wave breach or ladder troops.

They may defend settlements and broad approaches but do not acquire urban-watch, castle-custody, fire-response, crowd-control, or civic-office functions merely by being present.

They may embark only when real ships, crews, lift, supply, landing command, and return capacity exist. They receive no marine or boarding identity.
