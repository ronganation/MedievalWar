Independent development strengthens administration, task preparation, leadership identification, equipment custody, local support, withdrawal discipline, and return accounting. It does not turn Catalan Community Levy into militia, spearmen, professional infantry, an expeditionary force, or a disposable mass unit.

## 3. Development Thesis

> Independent Barcelona improves the Community Levy by making weak troops more usable, governable, and recoverable, not by pretending the lowest pool has become a trained High Era battle line.

```yaml
IndependentHighLoop:
  - Record
  - ProtectMinimums
  - Assemble
  - Group
  - AssignOneTask
  - Prepare
  - GuardOrDelay
  - RelieveOrWithdraw
  - Account
  - Recover
```

The High Era expression should feel visibly better organized than a hasty Early muster while remaining fragile, mixed, locally dependent, poorly suited to open battle, and expensive in community disruption when overused.
