## 1. Correction of Method

The earlier infantry maps were produced before the four current High profiles were fully built and strengthened. They remain useful for source separation, finite recruitment, no double counting, and Early-family persistence. They do not erase later completed design work merely because their earlier portfolio forecast expected fewer separate High expressions.

This authority therefore does not force the current roster backward into the earlier forecast. It updates the connective tissue around the current profiles and leaves final card admission to later portfolio and validation work.

```yaml
rules:
  - LaterCompletedProfileEvidenceMustBeReviewedBeforeOlderForecastLanguageIsApplied
  - EarlyFamilyPersistenceDoesNotProhibitLaterTransformationOrParallelHighExpression
  - SameAncestryDoesNotRequireSamePlayerFacingName

  - CurrentProfilesRemainIntactUnlessAProfileSpecificReviewFindsARealCollision
  - NoProfileIsDemotedSolelyToRestoreAnOlderZeroNewCardPrediction
```

```yaml
  source: ReleasedCastleAndCastellanyServiceSections
  mission: BoundedAccessReliefContainmentCounterEntryAndRestoration
  cost: CustodyWatchReserveEquipmentAndSourceExposure
collision_review: PreliminaryStaticRoleSeparationPassedFinalPortfolioBattleCampaignAndBalanceValidationPending
```

The spear count is not itself a problem. The current profiles pass only a preliminary static role-separation review. Final coexistence remains conditional on portfolio, battle, campaign, AI, and balance validation showing that no formation performs the others’ jobs at equal or lower source and campaign cost.

## 5. Current Battlefield Ladder

1. **Catalan Community Levy** supplies emergency bodies, rear work, local defense, and last-resort depth.
2. **Catalan Spearmen** may persist as broad cheaper community spear frontage where the developed host threshold is not met.
3. **Host Spearmen of Barcelona** organize the ordinary High field battle and missile relationship.
4. **Osona Spearmen** may persist as broader regional service below the selected High threshold.
5. **Shieldmen of Osona** own the narrower hard sector and recoverable sustained fight.
6. **Castle Sergeants** retain custody-first source service and bounded local release.
7. **Sergeants of the Castellanies** own coordinated cross-site relief and restoration.
8. **Escutats del Comtat / County Shield Companies** own nonhousehold sword-and-shield takeover and supported pressure.
9. **Comital Swordsmen** retain household command protection and crisis stabilization.

This ladder describes coexistence and development thresholds, not a compulsory upgrade chain.
