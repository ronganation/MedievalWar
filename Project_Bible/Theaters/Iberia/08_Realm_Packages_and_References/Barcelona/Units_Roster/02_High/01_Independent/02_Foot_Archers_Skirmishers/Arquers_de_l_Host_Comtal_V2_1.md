## 6. Player-Facing Description

Arquers de l’Host Comtal are developed archery detachments of independent Barcelona's comital host. The card forms only when qualified community-bow sources can release compatible shooters, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, carriers, repair relationships, and service time into a receiving field organization capable of section command, ammunition control, protected movement, relief, return, and recovery.

In battle, the unit supports the host across changing lanes. Sections establish protected fire, conserve arrows against poor targets, reinforce an important approach, hand a sector to another section, relocate behind the frontage, and restore useful fire after movement. The unit is more dependable than Catalan Bowmen in sustained field service, but remains ordinary bow infantry rather than elite marksmen. Companyies d’Arquers de Girona retain the narrower priority-fire and counter-skirmishing role.

Recruiting the successor creates concentration cost. Communities that release their better shooters, leaders, compatible equipment, arrows, carriers, and repair capacity may field fewer or weaker Catalan Bowmen and may lose local missile defense. Heavy casualties damage the host detachment and the underlying source network separately.


**Signature cycle: Assemble → Assign → Support → Hand off → Relocate → Recover**

- Assemble compatible source groups into recognized sections without erasing ownership.
- Assign lanes, target bands, ammunition policy, protecting formations, passage, and relief.
- Support the host with controlled sustained fire rather than maximum release rate.
- Hand a sector between sections when the frontage, target, ammunition, or threat changes.
- Relocate through a real protected lane before enemies enter contact distance.
- Recover people, bows, arrows, leaders, service trust, and source capacity through accountable receipts.
