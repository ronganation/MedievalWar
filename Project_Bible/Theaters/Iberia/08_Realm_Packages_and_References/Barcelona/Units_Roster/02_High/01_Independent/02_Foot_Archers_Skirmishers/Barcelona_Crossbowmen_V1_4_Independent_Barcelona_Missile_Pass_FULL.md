Barcelona Crossbowmen provide deliberate missile weight rather than sustained flexible archery. They are most useful when terrain, infantry protection, command, ammunition, and timing allow a prepared shot against valuable targets. Their weakness is the vulnerable period between volleys and their dependence on actual weapons, bolts, maintenance, and trained operation.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Barcelona Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Basic trained crossbow missile infantry
- **Battle promise:** Slow, hard-hitting missile pressure from prepared or protected positions
- **Best use:** Firing from behind spear infantry, slopes, field edges, walls or protected approaches where available; targeting armored, dense, exposed, or high-value enemies
- **Worst use:** Unsupported open frontage, rapid maneuver, running missile duels, cavalry exposure, close melee, interrupted reload, and long battles without bolts or maintenance
- **Primary tradeoff:** Better shot weight and target pressure than Community Bowmen, but lower mobility, lower sustained responsiveness, and more severe reload exposure
- **Source tradeoff:** Requires real crossbows, bolts, strings, spanning method, maintenance, operators, loaders where applicable, leaders, practice, and supply
- **Opening State:** Candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 13. Battlefield Rhythm

1. Secure a prepared, elevated, bounded, or infantry-protected firing position.
2. Establish ammunition, loading depth, leaders, signals, and withdrawal.
3. Load before the enemy enters the desired shot window where possible.
4. Hold fire against low-value or badly presented targets.
5. Present against dense, armored, exposed, or otherwise valuable targets.
6. Loose by file or controlled volley according to training and command.
7. lower weapons and begin the vulnerable reload cycle.
8. Use spear infantry, terrain, walls, field edges, or reserves to survive enemy response.
9. Reposition if the firing lane, flank, ammunition access, or withdrawal route fails.
10. Preserve weapons and trained operators rather than accepting futile melee.
