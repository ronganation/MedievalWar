The High card improves warning, reporting, contact classification, section leadership, consolidation, handoff, withdrawal, cadre retention, and regional recovery. Conditional javelin or thrown-spear use remains equipment-, practice-, and mission-dependent rather than universal.

### 4.4 Llevats Ballesters to Barcelona Crossbowmen

```yaml
canonical_family: BarcelonaCrossbowService
relationship: QualifiedEarlyToHighOrganizationalAndMaterialDevelopment
Early_purchase: LowerThresholdCalledCrossbowDetachmentsWithSiteDefenseDebit
High_purchase: PreparedPositionBoltPressureTargetEconomyReloadControlRepositioningAndFieldEndurance
free_parallel_pool: false
automatic_upgrade_of_every_parent_formation: false
automatic_parent_replacement: false
merge_if_distinction_cannot_be_represented: true
```

Llevats Ballesters are the Early lower-threshold expression: called, mixed, often site-derived detachments with brittle reload, limited movement, a real debit to defended places, and a strong return obligation. Barcelona Crossbowmen are the more complete expression, requiring better operators, compatible weapons, spanning, bolts, maintenance, command, shot-window discipline, protection, repositioning, and recovery.

The two cards may coexist only if those differences create visible campaign and battlefield decisions. They must merge if implementation reduces their difference to statistics or names alone.
