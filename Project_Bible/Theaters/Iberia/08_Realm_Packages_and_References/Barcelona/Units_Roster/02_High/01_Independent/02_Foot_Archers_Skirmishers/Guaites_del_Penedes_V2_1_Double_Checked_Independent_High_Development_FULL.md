## 7. Player-Facing Description

Guaites del Penedès are a developed independent Barcelona family of locally sourced frontier light foot suited to watch, patrol, escort, screening, route protection, and the defense of settlements, cultivation, castles, and broken approaches. They are not one permanent frontier regiment. Each formation requires a real source, current authority, eligible people, suitable equipment, leadership, mission, route, supply, and return plan.

On the battlefield, Guaites del Penedès operate ahead of, beside, or between more stable elements. They observe and report from assigned positions, screen movement, guard carts and messengers, delay raiders at constrained approaches, support flanks, and contest objectives where mobility and local information matter. They are less reliable than Community Spearmen in dense sustained frontage and less capable than mounted troops in operational reach, interception, and pursuit.

Recruiting them creates local risk. The same people may protect vineyards, fields, herds, property, route access, settlements, markets, castle districts, and warning chains. Losses damage not only future recruitment but the region's ability to detect, delay, escort, and recover from subsequent pressure.

## 23. Signature Battle Cycle

1. Receive a bounded route, objective, warning, escort, or screen mission.
2. Move in a controlled column and establish dispersed observation or protection.
3. Identify contact, obstruction, local threat, and withdrawal route.
4. Report and shape the approach rather than seeking decisive combat.
5. Delay, harass, escort, or screen according to equipment and mission.
6. Draw pressure toward Community Spearmen, Castle Sergeants, Bowmen, mounted forces, or terrain-supported positions.
7. Disengage before heavy infantry or cavalry fixes the unit.
8. Rally, account for people and equipment, and update route and threat information.
9. Return to owner or accept only an authorized follow-on assignment.
