## 6. Player-Facing Description

Barcelona's Cavalleria de l’Host Comtal gather capable riders from several political and military relationships into one temporary field concentration. Some contributors may arrive through comital ties, others through lordship obligations or castle-linked service. Their status and resources can raise the formation's horse, protection, weapon, retainer, and command ceiling, but no title guarantees combat quality.

Used well, the contingent can create the mounted weight needed to pressure a flank, answer enemy cavalry, reinforce a crisis, or deliver a supported charge against an exposed target. It is less immediately trusted and internally cohesive than the count's direct mounted retainers, but potentially broader. It must spend time assembling compatible riders, horses, equipment, leaders, signals, and service terms.

Losses propagate through several owners. Dead or captured contributors may remove household heads or heirs; horse and equipment losses reduce local capability; failed return or unfair burden can damage future service. The unit is powerful because Barcelona successfully concentrates real relationships and resources, not because a roster label creates knights.

## 22. Signature Battlefield Cycle

1. Assemble distinct contributions and establish accepted command, signals, precedence, and rally.
2. Deploy behind or beside the main infantry line rather than replacing scouts or household protection.
3. Preserve horse condition and cohesion while identifying a flank, cavalry threat, or disrupted target.
4. Apply mounted presence and pressure without committing to poor ground or prepared spears.
5. Countercharge, reinforce, or deliver a supported charge when the target and exit route justify the risk.
6. Break contact before mixed pace, fatigue, missiles, spears, or enemy reserves trap the contingent.
7. Rally by sub-contingent and common standard.
8. Pursue only under bounded recall.
9. Account and return each owner's people, horses, equipment, captives, obligations, and claims.
