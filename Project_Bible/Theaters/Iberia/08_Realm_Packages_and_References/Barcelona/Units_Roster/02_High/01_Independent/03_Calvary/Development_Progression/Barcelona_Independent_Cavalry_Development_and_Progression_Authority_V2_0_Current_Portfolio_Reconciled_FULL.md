### Genets del Comtat

The Early card owns broad source-bound road and flank utility:

- screening and warning;
- route and escort protection;
- anti-skirmisher interception;
- flank threat;
- bounded pursuit;
- withdrawal cover;
- the signature **Open the Road** mission at a lower organization threshold.

### Companyies de Cavall Lleuger

The High successor owns the developed light-cavalry network:

- recurring company and section organization;
- assigned route and flank sectors;
- relay reporting and contact classification;
- threat handoff to the correct infantry, missile, or mounted arm;
- controlled interception;
- explicit pursuit limits;
- rear-guard and withdrawal sequencing;
- recall, reassembly, remount administration, and recoverable company memory.
