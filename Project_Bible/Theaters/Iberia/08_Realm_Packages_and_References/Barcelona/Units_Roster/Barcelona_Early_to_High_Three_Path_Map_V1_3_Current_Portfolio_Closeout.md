## 1. Authority Verdict

Barcelona has a complete Early working roster of thirteen established unit families. High Era development will proceed through three distinct portfolios:

1. **Independent Development**
2. **Negotiated Union**
3. **Barcelona-led Domination**

The portfolios are not three replacement rosters. They are three political and institutional development environments applied to the same Barcelona-owned sources. An Early family can persist in all three paths while receiving different access, assignment, support, reliability, donor-cost, and campaign-pressure expressions.

A new High Era card is admitted only when the path creates a materially different formation with a distinct source, institution, battlefield loop, player decision, and recovery burden. Cosmetic path variants, title-only successors, and symmetry-driven additions are prohibited.

## 4. Path Definitions

### 4.1 Independent Development

Barcelona develops its native county, regional, castle, household, civic, and mounted institutions without political merger or subordination to another Realm roster.

```yaml
independent_path_priorities:
  - ImproveNativeSourceAdministrationWithoutCreatingResources
  - StrengthenCountyRegionalCastleCivicAndHouseholdCoordination
  - PreserveFiniteLocalRecruitmentAndDonorCosts
  - AdmitNewCardsOnlyWhereInstitutionalMaturationChangesTheBattleDecision
  - KeepNamedHouseAndCivicEmergenceConditional
```

### 4.2 Negotiated Union

Barcelona enters a cooperative political arrangement while retaining ownership of its units, pools, contributors, equipment obligations, and return receipts.

```yaml
negotiated_union_priorities:
  - PreserveBarcelonaOwnership
  - UseBoundedJointAssignmentsRatherThanMergedPools
  - DefineReleaseRecallCommandAndReturnTerms
  - PreserveCastleHouseholdRegionalAndCivicMinimums
  - AdmitCompositeInstitutionsOnlyWhenJointServiceCreatesARealNewFormation
not_granted:
  - AutomaticRosterMerger
  - SharedHouseholdPool
  - FreeTransferOfRegionalContributors
  - FreeReplacementAcrossRealmBoundaries
```

### 4.3 Barcelona-led Domination

Barcelona leads the political arrangement and can impose broader field, garrison, occupation, route, and compliance demands, but those demands still consume real sources and create political and recovery costs.

```yaml
barcelona_led_domination_priorities:
  - BarcelonaLedCommandAndCampaignAssignment
  - WiderButNotUnlimitedDemandOnBarcelonaSources
  - ExplicitOccupationRouteGarrisonAndComplianceCosts
  - CompliantContributionsRemainDistinctFromBarcelonaOwnedFamilies
  - DominationMustCreateTradeoffsRatherThanFreeRosterExpansion
not_granted:
  - RelabelingAragoneseUnitsAsBarcelonaUnits
  - InfiniteContributorCompliance
  - FreeOccupationForces
  - AutomaticEliteUpgrades
```
