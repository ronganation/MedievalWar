## 1. Strong Verdict and Player Promise

**Formation must be an informed political choice, not a disguised upgrade button or a loss-of-control trap.**

The player chooses whether to continue native independence or accept a supported Barcelona-Aragon settlement. The Union can offer common direction and combined actual capabilities, but also inherits real wars, obligations, institutional constraints and recovery needs. Independence remains a valid campaign outcome, not a failed quest.

Inside the Union, balanced or senior-led government describes the accepted political settlement. It does not create three factions, automatically choose the human controller, or erase either foundation. The player should understand the consequential differences without reading the full inheritance ledger.

## 2. Specialist Ownership and Nonduplication

The existing preformation campaign interface remains the native campaign's ongoing relationship and objective surface. This specialist adds the bounded decision at an actual emergence proposal. ## 3. Decision Journey Without a New Runtime State Machine

| Existing process context | Proposed player experience | Action boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity identified | Nonintrusive notice with reason and participating authorities | Inspect or ignore; no transfer |
| Negotiating | Terms and unresolved material issues | Request supported changes, defer or decline |
| Settlement accepted | Agreed terms with outstanding readiness checks | Not yet proof of completed formation |
| Formation ready | Final current summary and explicit confirmation | Submit to the existing Formation owner |
| Formed | Completion receipt and normal faction interface | No second acceptance or transfer |

These are presentation mappings to existing categories, not new allocated runtime enums. Closing a panel is not acceptance. Opening a notice does not start negotiation. A successful notification does not establish political truth.

## 6. Independence Versus Union Comparison

## 11. Institutional Continuity and Reform Expectations

The offer states that native laws, offices, religious institutions and local rights continue within their actual scope unless the accepted settlement validly changes them. Common government is not instant homogenization.

Separate formation terms from later reform opportunities. An available common-accounting reform is not an already implemented administration bonus. A proposed institutional repair is not a repaired building. Religious patronage is not ownership or universal obedience.

Only material institutional changes need first-view attention. Routine continuity belongs in expandable detail, not a mandatory sequence of charter confirmations after every formation.

## 12. Choices and Their Meaning

| Proposed action | Meaning | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Review terms | Inspect current proposal | Accept or spend resources |
| Request revised terms | Submit a supported negotiation request | Unilaterally rewrite the counterpart's agreement |

The exact button labels are proposed content, not final localized strings. No action is preselected to exploit accidental input. Decline and defer must be discoverable, not hidden behind a misleading affirmative-only flow.

## 15. AI Decision: Compare Real Alternatives

AI evaluates formation against continuing independently, deferring and requesting supported changes. It does not accept because a date arrived, a historical union occurred, a relation score crossed one threshold or the faction label sounds more advanced.

| Evaluation family | Relevant question | Shortcut rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Survival and security | Does common government improve feasible protection? | Stronger neighbor automatically deserves control |
| Authority and recognition | Can participating authorities sustain these terms? | Marriage alone establishes common rule |
| Resources and liabilities | What actual capacity and burden are included? | Sum all assets while ignoring debts |
| Institutional viability | Can common direction operate with protected rights? | Seniority cancels every local constraint |
| Diplomacy | Which wars, treaties and disputes continue? | Formation resets reputation and enemies |
| Succession and continuity | Are the actual character and government arrangements workable? | Invent an heir or guarantee future succession |
| Opportunity cost | What is lost by accepting, delaying or refusing? | Union always maximizes campaign success |

Weights, thresholds and numeric utility are deferred. The document defines required comparisons, not a tested AI scoring model.
