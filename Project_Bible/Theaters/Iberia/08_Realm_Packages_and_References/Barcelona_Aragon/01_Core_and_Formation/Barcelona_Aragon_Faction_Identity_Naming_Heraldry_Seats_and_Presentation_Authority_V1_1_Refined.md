## 1. Identity Verdict and Player Promise

**The player must recognize the combined faction without losing sight of its two foundations.**

Barcelona-Aragon is one emergent faction. Balanced, Barcelona-dominant and Aragon-dominant government change precedence and political emphasis, not the faction's fundamental identity. The main campaign name stays readable and stable while secondary presentation explains who directs common government and which institutions remain distinct.

The player should be able to answer three questions immediately: which faction controls this asset, what its native or institutional source is when relevant, and which government currently directs the realm. Those questions must not be collapsed into a single banner or color.

## 7. Heraldic Asset Categories

Separate the following visual uses before selecting artwork:

| Category | Function | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Faction emblem | Recognize the campaign actor | Every institution has identical arms |
| Ruler or dynastic arms | Identify a supported person or house | Personal arms automatically define all state ownership |
| Territorial emblem | Identify a region or settlement | Region is an independent controller |
| Institutional insignia | Identify a civic, household or other source | Institution owns the whole faction |
| Command or field standard | Read battlefield or campaign affiliation | A new unit profile or recruitment source |
| UI relationship badge | Explain status or provenance | A legally recognized title or treaty |

These are functional categories, not approved historical designs. Exact devices, tinctures, stripe counts, quartering, crowns, seals and chronology remain evidence-dependent. Do not construct a purported historical composite by mechanically joining native symbols.
