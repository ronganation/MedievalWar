## 1. Decisive Formation Verdict

**Form one larger playable realm, not a permanent vassal arrangement. Inherit its real condition, not an idealized replacement.**

Barcelona and Aragon remain separate native factions unless a valid Union settlement forms Barcelona-Aragon. Formation gives one human or AI controller direct control of the participating realm territories and transferable assets. The resulting faction carries forward damage, debts, commitments, rival claims, local institutions, and military recovery needs.

```yaml
independent_Barcelona_remains_Barcelona: true
independent_Aragon_remains_Aragon: true
resulting_union_faction_count: 1
union_expressions: [BalancedUnion, BarcelonaDominantUnion, AragonDominantUnion]
separate_domination_path: false
permanent_partner_vassal_required: false
human_controller_retains_campaign_control: true
source_ownership_erased: false
formation_creates_free_units_or_recruitment_capacity: false
```

The reward must be immediately playable: settlements can be managed, inherited eligible armies can receive orders, and the player can pursue a wider strategy. Political friction changes reliability, obligations, support, and development. It must not reduce the newly acquired half of the realm to a permanently inaccessible administrative exercise.

## 2. Authority Ownership and Interfaces

| Subject | This authority owns | Other authority retains |
|---|---|---|
| Faction formation | Eligibility categories, settlement scope, controller continuity, inheritance disposition | General faction identity and constitutional limits |
| Territory | Actual control transfer, exclusions, disputed and occupied land | Exact map geography and scenario bindings |
| Armies and characters | Continuity and reassignment without duplication | Unit profiles, character biographies, succession detail |

The older political framework remains useful evidence of preformation relationships. Its separate domination-path architecture must not be imported into this design. No existing framework file is silently rewritten or physically superseded by this artifact.

## 3. Formation Is a Campaign Opportunity, Not an Era Reward

A Union opportunity may arise through dynastic settlement, negotiated political agreement, mutual protection, or a settlement following conflict. None creates Barcelona-Aragon merely by existing. The settlement must establish a workable combined authority.

```yaml
required_categories:
  - ValidUnionSettlement
  - RecognizedBarcelonaAuthorityOrSuccessor
  - RecognizedAragonAuthorityOrSuccessor
  - WorkingCommonRuleAndContinuityBasis
  - DefinedTerritorialAndAssetScope
  - ResolvedInitialLeadershipExpression
  - ValidControllerContinuity
not_sufficient_alone:
  - Marriage
  - Alliance
  - JointVictory
  - OccupationOfBothCapitals
  - HighEraDate
  - TechnologyOrBuilding
  - SharedHeraldry
```

Recognition means sufficient accepted authority to operate the combined realm, not unanimous approval from every noble or settlement. Dissent may be inherited as a real integration problem. Conquest can contribute to a later Union settlement; conquest alone remains ordinary territorial acquisition and does not create a fourth political path.

Political agreement and military maturity are distinct. A relationship can develop before mature High Era institutions. This authority does not award the fourteen composite profiles, mature equipment, or trained cadres at the instant a political compact is signed. Exact chronology and any pre-High political transition remain controlled by the transition and scenario authorities.

Later expression changes are governance events within the same faction, not another formation transaction. Detailed thresholds, persistence, and renegotiation belong to the governance authority.

### 6.2 Direct Command After Formation

Ordinary inherited forces covered by the accepted settlement become directly orderable under Barcelona-Aragon without a separate consent prompt for every unit. Exceptional refusal applies only where a controlling household, claimant, contract, order, or institutional authority creates a real contrary disposition. Their source identity remains relevant to recruitment, casualties, recall conditions, and recovery, but is not a second AI controller. A genuinely refusing household or claimant force receives an explicit disposition instead of being silently granted or erased.

## 9. Occupied Land, Enclaves, and Third Parties

Formation does not move armies across borders, end sieges, open blocked roads, or grant military access through a third party. Included enclaves remain enclaves. Disconnected settlements retain their actual supply and access problems.

An enemy-held Barcelona or Aragon province may remain a Union claim and a later recovery objective. Its inhabitants, garrison, revenue, and recruitment capacity are not counted as immediately available Union assets. A surviving recognized successor can participate without every historical territory being recovered first.

If both native political authorities have collapsed without a recognized successor or settlement basis, the game must not invent them merely to trigger the faction. Restoration or claimant rules must first provide a valid political basis.

## 12. Refusal, Captivity, and Exceptional Military Dispositions

A Union can form despite limited refusal, but the exception must be real and bounded.

| Existing asset condition | Formation disposition |
|---|---|
| Eligible native army or garrison accepts settlement | Reassign direct command, preserve condition and source |
| Household allegiance unresolved | Explicit hold or settlement decision; no free replacement |
| Recognized claimant force refuses | Retain an identified claimant or hostile disposition under the relevant rules |
| External mercenary company | Preserve company, pay, contract and employer-change terms |
| Military-order contingent | Reevaluate actual order authority and assignment |
| Captured soldiers or leaders | Remain captive under existing captor and ransom state |
| Missing or dispersed people | Remain missing or dispersed until actual recovery |

Refusal is not a universal tax on every native unit, and ordinary included service does not require unit-by-unit negotiation. Most validly included forces should remain usable. Exceptional dispositions must not become a backdoor permanent partner-AI system.

## 21. Campaign-Facing Formation Sequence

This is a proposed design sequence, not executable runtime instructions.

1. **Offer:** present a valid Union opportunity with material terms and risks.
2. **Settle:** establish included territories, leadership expression, continuity, and major diplomatic conflicts.
3. **Check:** confirm that the campaign state still matches the accepted terms.
4. **Form:** transfer the agreed state as one complete or recoverable transition.
5. **Present:** show the new faction, direct control, inherited forces, liabilities, and immediate priorities.
6. **Continue:** resume ordinary campaign play with the real inherited conditions.

A changed siege, dead ruler, captured settlement, broken agreement, or invalid controller can require reevaluation before commitment. The player must see why the offer changed. No formation should occur twice because a prompt was reopened.

## 24. Worked Campaign Cases

### A. Balanced Union of two pressured realms

Both authorities survive, both control useful territory, and neither has settled precedence. They accept a balanced settlement. The combined faction inherits their actual armies, depleted pools, and external threats. The player gains direct control of the included territory without free replenishment. A weak-plus-weak Union is valid if its political basis works; it is not automatically a powerful empire.

### B. Weaker human Barcelona, stronger AI Aragon

An accepted settlement establishes Aragon-dominant Union. The human continues as Barcelona-Aragon, including the agreed Aragon territories and eligible forces. Aragonese seniority shapes government and obligations, not the identity of the controller. Existing Barcelona losses and Aragon commitments remain.

### C. Stronger Barcelona, damaged Aragon institutions

Barcelona-dominant Union forms with a recognized Aragon authority. Included settlements transfer, but damaged recruitment institutions remain damaged. Existing eligible Aragon forces continue; unavailable royal or regional capacity does not regenerate because Barcelona now commands the faction.

### D. Partner territory occupied by a third party

The settlement includes a valid claim to recovery, not control of the occupied province. The Union does not collect its revenue or recruit from it. A later liberation uses the actual war and restoration rules.

### E. Conquest without Union

Barcelona conquers Aragonese territory but no valid Union settlement follows. Barcelona remains Barcelona with ordinary conquest consequences. This does not create Barcelona-Aragon or require another separate domination faction.

### F. Existing forces at sea and under siege
