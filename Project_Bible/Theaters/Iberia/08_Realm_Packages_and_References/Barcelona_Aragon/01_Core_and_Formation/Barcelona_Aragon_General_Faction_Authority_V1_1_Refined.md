## 1. Faction Thesis

Barcelona-Aragon joins Barcelona's comital, castle, regional, civic, crossbow, urban, Mediterranean-facing, and trans-Pyrenean strengths with Aragon's royal, Three Land, mountain, valley, frontier, municipal-mounted, noble, and baronial strengths. The faction's purpose is not to flatten those traditions into one generic kingdom. Its purpose is to make their negotiated combination playable as a stronger but internally demanding realm.
