## 1. Decisive Character Verdict

**The Union inherits people and claims, not a fresh cast. A succession changes who rules, not who owns the player's campaign.**

Barcelona-Aragon is one faction with Balanced, Barcelona-dominant or Aragon-dominant government. Its characters carry the two native foundations' histories into common rule. A change of ruler can expose conflicting rights and obligations without automatically dividing the realm or replacing its controller.

```yaml
single_union_faction: true
union_expressions: [BalancedUnion, BarcelonaDominantUnion, AragonDominantUnion]
separate_domination_path: false
human_and_AI_direct_control: Preserved
controller_ruler_dynasty_and_senior_foundation_are_distinct: true
formation_duplicates_people_or_households: false
succession_automatically_changes_governance_expression: false
ruler_death_automatically_ends_union: false
```

The player promise is political continuity with meaningful stakes: secure a recognized successor, preserve useful households, handle rival claims, and keep armies and settlements functioning. It is not an exhaustive court simulator or a compulsory dynastic puzzle.

## 2. Subject Ownership and Interfaces

| Subject | This authority defines | Other authority retains |
|---|---|---|
| Character continuity | One person, preserved history, valid role reassignment | Native biography, verified kinship, individual character facts |
| Dynastic connection | How supported claims and settlement terms enter Union rule | Exact historical genealogy and native succession custom |
| Succession | Recognition, disputed outcomes, continuity and regency categories | Exact event values, scenario opening and individual admissions |
| Governance | Character-side inputs and recognition outcomes | Institutional reactions, burdens and seniority changes |
| Armies | Commander and household continuity | Unit definitions, battle abilities and recruitment pools |
| Dissolution | Claim and person provenance needed for a later split | Final territory, army and controller partition |

Formation transfers the actual included assets. Governance manages institutional responses. This authority must not duplicate those systems under a character label.

## 5. Common Rule and the Initial Ruler

The accepted Union settlement must identify a workable common-rule arrangement. For ordinary campaign presentation, the design preference is one recognized ruling authority and one direct controller. A consort, regent, native titleholder or senior adviser is not automatically another faction controller.

```yaml

joint_rule_is_automatic_default: false
```

If a particular campaign or historical scenario supports a more complex common-rule arrangement, its specific authority must define offices and recognition clearly. This general file does not invent universal co-rulership to solve every conflict.

## 6. Existing Rulers and Displaced Authority

| Preformation role | Valid design disposition |
|---|---|
| Ruler recognized for common rule | Continue as the same person with reconciled Union offices |
| Other native ruler accepts settlement | Retain valid title, household or agreed office without a separate campaign controller |
| Ruler remains captive | Preserve captivity and define valid interim government |
| Ruler rejects the settlement | Explicit claimant, opposition, exile or other supported disposition |
| Ruler dies before commitment | Reevaluate settlement and recognition before formation |
| Native authority survives through a successor | Use the actual supported successor, not a recreated former ruler |

A displaced ruler must not disappear simply because the faction name changes. Equally, retaining a person does not guarantee an army, court office, territorial grant or permanent political immunity.

## 13. Absence, Captivity, and Death

| Condition | Continuity treatment |
|---|---|
| Ruler absent on a valid mission | Use supported delegated government without teleporting the ruler |
| Ruler captured | Preserve captor, location and ransom state; evaluate interim authority |
| Ruler wounded or otherwise unable to act under existing rules | Use an explicit supported continuity arrangement |
| Ruler dies | Evaluate actual successor and recognition basis; preserve person history |
| Regent dies or is captured | Reevaluate the regency, not automatically the entire Union |
| Missing status unresolved | Preserve uncertainty; do not declare death merely to simplify succession |

A character's life-state transition is controlled by the character and campaign event systems. This authority consumes that outcome and must not invent it to force a desired political result.

## 14. Disputed Succession

A dispute is meaningful when supported candidates or institutions disagree over a real succession basis. It is not generated solely to keep the Union unstable.

```yaml
dispute_requires:
  - IdentifiedCandidateOrRecognizedClaimingAuthority

  - RelevantRecognitionOrInstitutionalConnection
  - ActualPoliticalConsequence
possible_responses:

  - SettleOfficePropertyOrObligationDispute
  - SeekRecognitionFromRelevantAuthorities
  - MaintainContestedRuleAndAcceptExplainedConsequences
  - ResolveActualArmedOppositionThroughCampaignPlay
```

The player chooses among valid political responses, not an unrestricted menu for appointing any person. A settlement can resolve one title while leaving another disputed. Detailed recognition effects belong to Governance; exact costs and legal customs require their owning authorities.

```yaml

```

An existing force can change allegiance only through the relevant character, institutional, army and campaign authorities. Such a change must preserve identity, condition and accounting. It must not duplicate the force or erase its losses.

A claimant's supporters are not automatically every institution from the same foundation. A Barcelona institution can recognize an Aragon-associated ruler, and an Aragon institution can oppose an Aragon-associated claimant where the actual relationships support that outcome.


## 22. Succession Event and Continuity Sequence

This is a proposed campaign-design sequence, not executable implementation instructions.

1. Establish the actual triggering character state without inventing death, absence or captivity.
2. Evaluate supported successor claims and the current Union settlement.
3. Identify whether routine succession, interim government or a real dispute applies.
4. Present only material choices and consequences to the player.
5. Resolve the recognized role and associated offices as one consistent transition.
6. Carry unresolved disputes into Governance and relevant campaign systems.
7. Resume direct faction play without recreating armies or territorial inheritance.

```yaml
event_safety:
  unique_campaign_event_identity: required
  changed_candidate_or_life_state_before_commit: Revalidate
  same_resolution_applies_successfully_twice: prohibited
  partial_role_transition_as_success: prohibited
  save_load_recovery_design: RequiredBeforeImplementation
  controller_continuity: PreservedUnlessSeparateCampaignDefeatRuleApplies
```

The death of the original player-associated ruler does not itself end the player's Barcelona-Aragon campaign. General defeat conditions remain owned by the campaign rules.

## 25. Worked Campaign Cases

### A. Routine recognized succession

A supported successor is available and recognized under the Union arrangement. The same faction continues. Existing armies, debts, recruitment capacity and governance expression persist. Relevant offices are reconciled without creating a new household army.

### B. Human Barcelona, Aragon-dominant Union

The recognized ruling arrangement follows the accepted settlement rather than the human's original ruler. The human continues controlling Barcelona-Aragon. Aragonese seniority affects government, not ownership of the player's campaign.

### C. Different recognition in the two foundations

A candidate has a supported native title claim but contested recognition for common rule. The player can pursue a valid settlement or maintain contested rule. The game does not grant every title automatically or immediately divide the faction.

### D. Recognized successor requires regency

An actual eligible successor cannot yet exercise ordinary rule under existing character rules. A supported interim arrangement preserves direct play. A regent's appointment does not create a new sovereign or erase the successor's claim.

### E. Ruler captured during a campaign

Captivity and ransom remain real. The faction uses a valid continuity arrangement while armies and settlements remain directly controllable. No ruler is teleported home to make the succession panel convenient.

### F. Claimant without an army

A claimant has a supported claim and some recognition but no released military source. The issue can affect diplomacy and institutional support. No rebel army appears merely because a grievance exists.

### G. Externally supported claimant

A sponsor provides actual funds or forces under valid terms. Those assets are accounted for once, with their real owner and conditions. Recognition alone does not transfer a province or create a full native roster.

### H. Displaced native ruler accepts an office

The settlement preserves the person's identity and a supported office or household role. That does not create another AI faction, guarantee permanent loyalty, or grant a duplicate army.

### I. Dynasty changes, Union survives

A valid succession settlement recognizes a different dynasty. Barcelona-Aragon continues with existing assets and liabilities. Native foundations remain, and governance evaluates any actual precedence change separately.

### J. No supported successor immediately available

The game uses an explicit valid interim or contested arrangement where the controlling rules permit. It does not invent a convenient relative. If common rule truly breaks down, the matter routes to the dissolution authority rather than silently deleting the Union.

These are proposed design cases, not historical examples or executed tests.
