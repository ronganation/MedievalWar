## 1. Strong Verdict and Distinctive Campaign Identity

**Barcelona-Aragon combines political direction without turning its foundations into one interchangeable society.**

Its distinctive challenge is making common rule useful while preserving or deliberately renegotiating the laws, offices, rights, religious relationships and local institutions through which that rule operates. Formation is not cultural conversion, universal legal standardization or ownership of every institution. A dominant expression is senior direction inside the Union, not permission to erase the junior foundation.

The player should gain coherent control of one campaign faction while facing a manageable set of consequential decisions: confirm inherited rights, settle a jurisdiction conflict, fund common administration, support a religious institution, or negotiate a bounded reform. Routine worship, every local lawsuit and every minor charter confirmation remain below the normal decision budget.

## 10. Patronage, Mediation, Care and Records

Patronage offers strategic choices: fund repair, support teaching or record work, request mediation, protect access, or sustain care where the actual institution has that function. Each needs real funds, personnel, materials, routes and acceptance as applicable.

| Choice | Potential benefit | Cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fund institutional repair | Restore supported services and continuity | Money, labor, materials and time; no instant restoration |
| Support records and interpreters | Improve accessible administration and evidence | Skilled people and access; records are not automatically true |
| Request mediation | Create an alternative to escalation | Parties may refuse; settlement still needs performance |
| Support care or hospitality | Sustain actual community services | Capacity-limited; no free healing or population creation |
| Confirm a supported privilege | Clarify expectations and reduce uncertainty | May constrain future fiscal or administrative choices |

Religious ceremony can communicate commitments; it does not produce universal legitimacy, money or obedience. A successful donation does not purchase unrestricted control.

## 20. Newly Incorporated Territories and External Institutions

Future expansion is conditional, not a historical entitlement. Newly acquired territory retains its actual population, law, institutions, property, damage and negotiated terms. Incorporation is not automatic extension of either foundation's law.

External religious superiors and foreign institutions retain their own authority and knowledge. A shared religious relationship does not grant the Union control over their private state or guarantee their support.

AI and human players face the same eligibility and capacity rules. A dominant government cannot bypass constraints simply because AI controls it. The explanation should name the issue and relevant limits without exposing hidden private state.

## 24. Formation, Reform and Lifecycle Handoffs

Formation consumes current institutional dispositions once. Later reform uses its own bounded authorized change; it does not rerun inheritance. A material change before commitment requires revalidation of affected terms.

On dissolution, current rights, offices, property, obligations and pending cases need explicit successor disposition. Do not duplicate institutions, revive expired privileges or reset later reforms to their original native form. An institution whose scope crosses successor borders requires an actual arrangement, not automatic duplication.

A later-start scenario must author its own dated institutional state. No 1062 Union opening is created, and no historical later institution is backfilled into an ongoing campaign because a date has arrived.
