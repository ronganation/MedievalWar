## 1. Decisive Lifecycle Verdict

**A Union can bend without breaking. If it breaks, its people, armies and territory must still have a real destination.**

Barcelona-Aragon should remain a rewarding faction to preserve, not a temporary bonus waiting for a compulsory breakup. A succession dispute, military defeat, local grievance or change of seniority does not by itself dissolve common rule. Valid separation is a consequential campaign outcome, not a routine governance notification.

```yaml
single_union_faction: true
union_expressions: [BalancedUnion, BarcelonaDominantUnion, AragonDominantUnion]
separate_domination_path: false
expression_change_is_dissolution: false

minor_objective_failure_is_automatic_dissolution: false
permanent_partner_vassal_required: false
assets_duplicated_or_deleted_for_convenience: false
```

The player promise is continuity with stakes. The player can repair a settlement, maintain contested rule, negotiate separation or face actual breakaway forces. Those outcomes must be explained through the existing campaign, governance and character systems rather than a new parallel political game.

## 6. Proposed Outcome Families

| Outcome | Campaign meaning | Conservation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Union repaired | Existing common rule continues | No new faction, army or treasury copy |
| Union renegotiated | Seniority or obligations change within the faction | Governance transition, not partition |
| Limited breakaway | Actual territory or institutions leave while a Union remnant persists | Only real assets and control change |
| Negotiated native separation | Supported Barcelona and Aragon successors resume separate control | One complete disposition per asset and liability |
| Contested separation | Rival authorities hold actual territory and unresolved claims | No automatic historical borders or peace |
| Union government displaced | Common-rule authority loses control but may retain valid claims | Exile and restoration require actual support |

These are resolution categories, not six additional permanent faction packages. A Union remnant is Barcelona-Aragon under contested conditions, not a newly invented fourth expression. If it no longer has a valid common-rule basis, the relevant settlement must explicitly resolve its identity rather than preserve the name by inertia.

## 19. Restoration: Recover Authority, Not an Idealized Past

A restored Barcelona or Aragon may be smaller, poorer or politically different from its pre-Union state. That is a legitimate campaign outcome. A Union government can also be restored if its recognized basis and actual assets support it. The label alone does not decide whether native independence or common rule returns.

## 27. Worked Campaign Cases

### A. Succession dispute, Union survives

A supported claimant contests common rule, but the realm retains workable government and actual forces. The player uses valid character and governance responses. No automatic partition or native faction reactivation occurs.

### B. Seniority changes after repeated failures

A recognized renegotiation shifts Aragon-dominant government to Balanced Union. The faction and controller continue. This is a governance transition, not dissolution, and assets are not redistributed.

### C. Negotiated native separation

A valid settlement restores separate Barcelona and Aragon authorities. The human chooses an eligible actual continuation, the other receives its own AI controller, and every included asset and liability receives one disposition. No permanent partner-vassal arrangement is imposed.

### D. Limited breakaway during war

A region and actual forces establish supported breakaway control while the Union persists elsewhere. The map reflects real control, not a full historical reset. Existing external wars and local source access are reevaluated.

### E. Mixed composite formation remains in the field

Future joint recruitment closes, but the actual formation survives until its valid service and contributor disposition is resolved. It does not become two full native units or vanish because its institution stopped recruiting.

### F. Enemy occupies a claimed native province

A separation settlement preserves a supported claim. The enemy retains actual control, revenue and occupation state under the campaign rules. The successor receives no free garrison or recruiting pool there.

### G. Army is across the new border

Its assignment and allegiance are resolved once. Movement, access, withdrawal or conflict follow supported rules. No teleport to a native capital occurs.

### H. Restored ruler remains captive

Recognition and actual personal availability differ. Captivity persists, and a supported interim government may be needed. Restoration does not release the person or cancel ransom.

### I. Re-formation after a costly separation

A new valid settlement reunites actual surviving authorities. Current debt, losses, claims and recovery carry forward. Prior objective rewards do not pay again, and no original full-strength snapshot is restored.

### J. Later-start formed Union

A separate scenario authority supplies the actual date, government, territory, assets and institutional eligibility. It may open with a mature Union, but does not automatically grant every composite profile or overwrite the 1062 campaign.

### K. No valid human continuation

The resolution has no supported playable successor or remnant. The game uses its general defeat or continuity rules. This file does not invent an exile faction or silently transfer the player to an unrelated realm.

### L. Healthy Union across generations

Recognized successions and working obligations continue. No compulsory dissolution timer exists. The faction can pursue ordinary expansion, diplomacy and development without artificial breakup events.

These are illustrative proposed design cases, not executed tests or historical examples.
