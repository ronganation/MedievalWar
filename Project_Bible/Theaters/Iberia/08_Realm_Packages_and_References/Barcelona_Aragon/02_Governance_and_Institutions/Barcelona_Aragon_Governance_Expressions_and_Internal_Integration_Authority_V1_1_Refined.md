## 1. Decisive Governance Verdict

**One realm to command. Two native foundations to hold together. Three ways to distribute political seniority.**

Barcelona-Aragon should feel like a substantial faction whose wider territory and complementary military institutions create new opportunities. Integration is the strategic work of making that strength dependable. It is not a substitute for direct control, a permanent vassal system, or a requirement to negotiate every ordinary order.

```yaml
faction_count: 1
governance_expressions: [BalancedUnion, BarcelonaDominantUnion, AragonDominantUnion]
separate_domination_path: false
independent_Barcelona_and_Aragon: RemainSeparateNativeFactionOutcomesOutsideUnion
direct_human_and_AI_control: Preserved
native_source_ownership: Preserved
expression_change_reforms_faction_from_scratch: false
expression_change_duplicates_roster_or_assets: false
```

The player should decide where to invest, whose support to secure, how to distribute burdens, and when to renegotiate a damaging settlement. The game should handle routine administration and source accounting underneath those decisions.

## 2. What This Authority Owns

| Area | Governance authority responsibility | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Union expression | Seniority, recognized obligations, expression changes | No new faction or top-level domination branch |
| Internal integration | Support, grievances, institutional cooperation and responses | No exact economic formula or runtime schema |
| Court and command | Political precedence and appointment consequences | No invented historical ruler or biography |
| Military obligations | Burden distribution, source cooperation and exceptional refusal | Unit roles, equipment and pool quantities stay with unit authorities |
| Capital and administration | Political consequences and continuity | Formation owns actual transfer; map authorities own locations |
| Crisis escalation | Proportionate warnings, remedies and institutional consequences | Succession and dissolution authorities own final resolutions |
| AI | Shared choices, priorities and abstention categories | Numerical scoring and executed behavior remain unvalidated |

The Formation Authority resolves initial controller, ruler and senior-foundation fields separately. This authority consumes that result. It does not rerun territorial inheritance when government changes.

## 3. Four Different Questions Must Stay Separate

```yaml
campaign_controller:
  asks: WhoIssuesPlayerOrAIOrdersForTheFaction
recognized_ruler:
  asks: WhoHasTheRecognizedPoliticalTitleAndSuccessionBasis
union_expression:
  asks: WhichFoundationHasSettledSeniorDirectionOrWhetherNeitherDoes
institutional_support:
  asks: WhichActualSourcesAcceptCurrentObligationsAndHowReliablyTheyCooperate
fields_are_not_interchangeable: true
```

A human who began as Barcelona can control an Aragon-dominant Union. A ruler associated with one foundation does not automatically make every institution from that foundation loyal. A balanced government can contain an influential ruler without becoming a dominant expression merely because of personal prestige.

This separation is essential to the player's comeback opportunity: accepting seniority for the other foundation need not end the campaign or hand control to the AI.

## 4. Shared Political Foundation

All three expressions preserve a recognized Union settlement, one campaign controller, direct territorial and army control, and surviving Barcelona and Aragon institutional identities. None promises unanimous support or identical rights in every region.

The combined government may govern firmly. Senior direction can create pressure and contested decisions. Rejecting a separate domination path does not mean prohibiting coercive actions, rebellion, or unpopular government; those are actions and consequences within the same faction, not another faction architecture.

## 5. Balanced Union: Bargain for Breadth

### 5.1 Identity and Player Promise

Balanced Union makes neither foundation the settled senior partner. Its appeal is broad cooperation: the government can build a coalition around shared defense, routes, command, and investment without treating one native foundation as merely subordinate.

**Player question:** Can you secure enough agreement to use both foundations' strength without letting every major decision become a stalled bargain?

```yaml
BalancedUnion:
  political_identity: SharedRuleWithoutSettledFoundationSeniority
  command: OneDirectFactionCommandWithNegotiatedMajorPrecedence
  investment_emphasis: SharedRoutesMutualDefenseAndComplementaryCapacity
  opportunity: BroadCooperationWhenBothFoundationsSeeReciprocalBenefit
  friction: MajorUnequalBurdensRequireCredibleJustificationOrCompensation
  exact_equal_spending_required: false
  routine_orders_require_two_sided_approval: false
```

### 5.2 Distinct Choices and Failure

A player can concentrate spending in one threatened frontier while supporting the other foundation through protected obligations, later investment, appointments, or compensation. Balance is reciprocal legitimacy over time, not a rule that every coin and regiment must be split evenly.

Its characteristic failure is paralysis or perceived favoritism: repeated unfulfilled bargains make sources less willing to accept exceptional demands. The remedy is a clearer settlement, a narrower immediate objective, or an explicit shift of senior direction. There is no permanent faction-wide morale bonus for selecting balance.

## 6. Barcelona-Dominant Union: Coordinate Through Senior Direction

### 6.1 Identity and Player Promise

Barcelona-dominant Union gives Barcelona-centered government settled senior direction while retaining Aragon as a native foundation inside the same realm. Its design emphasis follows the General Authority's comital, castle, civic, urban, crossbow-support, route and Mediterranean-facing interests. These are strategic priorities, not automatically granted historical possessions or naval forces.

**Player question:** Can senior administration turn wider resources into coordinated campaigns without treating Aragonese service as an inexhaustible reserve?

```yaml
BarcelonaDominantUnion:
  political_identity: BarcelonaSeniorDirectionWithinOneRecognizedUnion
  opportunity: MoreCoherentBarcelonaCenteredAppointmentsAndInvestmentPriorities
  friction: AragoneseRightsLocalDefenseAndServiceBurdenNeedCredibleProtection
  available_policy_emphases:
    - CastleUrbanAndRouteCoordination
    - CivicWorkshopAndMissileSupportWhereSourcesExist
    - MediterraneanOrTransPyreneanReachWhereActualCapacityExists
    - IntegrationOfAragoneseLandWarDepth
  creates_free_crossbows_ships_or_revenue: false
  erases_Aragonese_royal_or_regional_institutions: false
```

### 6.2 Distinct Choices and Failure

The government may favor a route, workshop, court appointment or expeditionary priority, but must still fund and sustain the people and infrastructure involved. Aragonese regional defense, noble obligations and household interests remain meaningful. Heavy repeated demands on those sources can produce a specific grievance rather than a universal anti-Barcelona penalty.

Its characteristic failure is overextension through unequal obligation: the senior foundation receives strategic benefit while the junior foundation bears losses or loses local security. Remedies include burden relief, a defensible joint command arrangement, investment in the affected region, or renegotiated precedence. The outcome remains Barcelona-Aragon throughout.

## 7. Aragon-Dominant Union: Concentrate Royal and Land-War Direction

### 7.1 Identity and Player Promise

Aragon-dominant Union gives Aragon-centered royal and military priorities settled senior direction. The design emphasis is Three Land continuity, mountain and valley defense, frontier obligations, and land-war coordination, while Barcelona's comital, civic, castle, household and wider-route capacities remain distinct contributors.

**Player question:** Can royal direction build a durable combined host without hollowing out Barcelona's local and civic foundations?

```yaml
AragonDominantUnion:
  political_identity: AragonSeniorDirectionWithinOneRecognizedUnion
  opportunity: MoreCoherentRoyalFrontierAndLandWarPriorities
  friction: BarcelonaComitalCivicCastleAndHouseholdInterestsNeedCredibleProtection
  available_policy_emphases:
    - RoyalCommandAndFrontierRelief
    - MountainValleyAndRouteSecurity
    - SustainableMountedAndFieldService
    - IntegrationOfBarcelonaMissileCastleAndCivicCapacity
  creates_free_horses_nobles_or_frontier_units: false
  erases_Barcelona_comital_or_civic_institutions: false
```

### 7.2 Distinct Choices and Failure

A land campaign can receive priority without automatically closing Barcelona's trade, civic development or household role. The player chooses what to sustain and what to postpone. Repeatedly consuming Barcelona's operators, stores or local defenders while denying representation can make those exact institutions unreliable.

Its characteristic failure is treating the combined realm as a larger royal manpower base rather than a reciprocal political settlement. Remedies include restoring service limits, securing civic and castle cooperation, sharing appointments, or changing strategic priorities. Aragonese seniority does not automatically determine the ruler's personal identity or the human controller.

## 8. Expression Comparison and Non-Optimality

| Expression | Main opportunity | Main pressure | Characteristic response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced | Broader reciprocal cooperation | Bargaining can delay exceptional concentration | Settle a shared priority and make compensation credible |
| Barcelona-dominant | Clearer Barcelona-centered administrative and route priorities | Junior Aragonese service can become overburdened | Protect regional obligations and demonstrate shared benefit |
| Aragon-dominant | Clearer royal, frontier and land-war direction | Barcelona institutions can feel consumed or sidelined | Preserve civic, castle and household capacity while sharing burden |

These are proposed political tradeoffs, not fixed numerical buffs. No expression is always best, and no native foundation is reduced to a stereotype or barred from developing capabilities outside its emphasis. A good inherited settlement may function well immediately; a poor one may need attention under any expression.

These categories route existing institutions and sources; they do not invent a full cast of political actors. Shared interests, losses, security and obligations matter more than a universal native-side loyalty switch.

Detailed individual characters and claims remain with the character authority. The game should not create a separate permanent meter for every category.

Local strain should remain local unless its cause or institutional connections justify wider effects.

## 11. Obligations, Burden, and Reciprocity

The governing settlement should identify the kinds of service expected, who supplies them, and the major limits. Ordinary accepted service proceeds without repeated consent prompts. Exceptional demands can change the bargain.

```yaml
ordinary_service: GovernedByExistingAcceptedObligationsAndActualCapacity
exceptional_demands_may_include:
  - ExtendedServiceBeyondAcceptedTerms
  - RepeatedDistantCampaigns
  - UnusualFiscalOrSupplyDemands
  - DiversionOfLocalDefenders
  - RepeatedLossesWithoutRecoveryOrCompensation
  - DisputedCommandOrCustodyAppointments
burden_evaluation:
  considers: CapacityExposureLossesServiceDurationAndPriorCommitments
  identical_quota_for_both_foundations: NotRequired
```

A smaller foundation should not owe the same absolute service as a larger one merely to look balanced. Equally, a stronger foundation should not gain unlimited claims on the weaker one's surviving people. Burden follows actual capacity, accepted terms and strategic need.

Compensation is not a button that restores casualties. It can settle a grievance or support recovery only when actual money, equipment, time, personnel and institutional action are available.

## 12. Player Decisions and Integration Levers

| Decision | Intended gain | Real tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Protect local service limits | Preserve trust and local defense | Fewer immediately available expeditionary forces |
| Seek exceptional campaign service | Concentrate strength for an urgent objective | Greater burden, cost and potential grievance |
| Fund damaged source recovery | Restore future service and address unequal losses | Competes with current campaigning and construction |
| Share a meaningful appointment | Broaden acceptance of common rule | Limits unilateral senior-side patronage |
| Prioritize a joint route or strongpoint | Connect both foundations' strategic interests | Requires actual investment and may postpone another project |
| Renegotiate precedence | Resolve an obsolete or failing bargain | Creates political uncertainty and may provoke affected interests |

These are policy and event-choice categories, not six mandatory new buildings or technologies. Use existing campaign systems where they can express the decision. Add a specialist mechanic only where the player-facing consequence cannot be represented adequately.

## 14. Army Command and Native Military Cooperation

One faction means one direct campaign command structure. A player can combine eligible native and composite units in an army without seeking a second realm AI's approval. Native origin still matters to recruitment and recovery, not to ordinary tactical order ownership.


Exceptional refusal requires an actual institutional, household, claimant, contract or obligation basis. It must have a visible scope and a valid disposition. Do not use political friction as arbitrary mid-battle disobedience unless a separate battle or character authority explicitly supports that behavior.

## 16. Fiscal and Administrative Integration

The formed faction uses the campaign-facing financial continuity defined by Formation. Governance decides how exceptional demands and investment priorities affect support; the economy authority owns exact revenue, upkeep, treasury, debt and administrative calculations.

```yaml
governance_finance_boundary:
  direct_settlement_management: Preserved
  one_campaign_facing_treasury: CompatibleWithSourceBoundProperty
  every_region_requires_separate_treasury_minigame: false
```

Integration can make coordination more dependable when actual administrative and route capacity improves. It must not be a universal tax on all revenue simply because the realm formed, nor an automatic efficiency bonus from a new faction name.

## 17. Regional Rights and New Conquests

Barcelona and Aragon foundations retain their actual regional identities. A newly conquered province is not automatically assigned Barcelona or Aragon identity because one expression currently dominates.

```yaml
regional_handling:
  existing_native_regions: PreserveActualInstitutionsAndSourceIdentity
  third_party_conquests: UseExistingOccupationAndRegionalAdministrationRules
  local_grievance: ApplyToAffectedRegionOrInstitutionBeforeWiderEscalation
  government_change_renames_or_converts_all_regions: false
  dominant_foundation_receives_every_new_province_as_native_source: false
```

The government can choose administrative priorities and appointments in new territory, but that does not fabricate native populations, workshops, municipal cavalry or recruitment pools. Regional and mercenary sources retain their canonical ownership outside the faction package.

## 18. Strain, Contest, and Proportionate Escalation

```yaml
escalation_categories:
  Warning:
    meaning: ARealObligationOrSupportProblemIsVisible
  Strain:
    meaning: CooperationOrCapacityIsReducedInAnIdentifiedArea
  Contest:
    meaning: ARecognizedInstitutionDisputesAnExceptionalDemandOrArrangement
  Crisis:
    meaning: MultipleMaterialFailuresOrASevereSuccessionSecurityOrSettlementBreachRequireAction
  Recovery:
    meaning: RemedyIsUnderwayButActualLossesAndCommitmentsPersist
fixed_mandatory_escalation_ladder: false
all_strain_becomes_rebellion: false
```

Use proportionate consequences. A delayed payment can affect the institution owed; a succession crisis can spread more widely where claims and recognition connect it to the realm. A severe actual event may begin at crisis level, but ordinary friction should not instantly trigger dissolution.

Responses can include fulfilling an obligation, reducing a demand, changing a mission, replacing an appointment under valid rules, compensating actual losses, renegotiating terms, or maintaining a contested policy and accepting its consequences. Firm action is a possible choice, not a separate domination path. Its consequences must be explained and grounded in the affected institutions.

## 19. Expression Changes: Deliberate Political Reorientation

An expression changes when a durable shift in precedence is recognized through settlement, institutional alignment, succession resolution or another valid political event. A single battle, temporary army count, capital click or payment must not flip government repeatedly.

```yaml
  - ClearAffectedObligationsAppointmentsAndSupport

expression_change_preserves:
  - FactionIdentityAndController
  - ActualTerritoryArmiesCharactersAndTreasury
  - SourceOwnershipAndSpentCapacity

expression_change_is:
  new_formation_transaction: false

```

A transition can resolve one dispute while creating another. Preserve existing useful cooperation rather than resetting every institution to an arbitrary starting score. Detailed persistence windows and thresholds remain deferred; the design requirement is resistance to oscillation and cheap bonus switching.

## 20. Succession, Regency, and Claimant Interface

A succession event can alter support, appointments and precedence, but does not automatically dissolve the Union or force the ruler's native foundation into seniority. Governance consumes the recognized character and succession outcome.

```yaml
succession_interface:
  character_authority_owns: PersonsClaimsHeirsRegencyAndRecognizedSuccessionBasis
  governance_owns: InstitutionalResponseAndObligationConsequences
  ruler_death_always_changes_expression: false
  claimant_exists_means_immediate_rebellion: false
  new_ruler_erases_prior_obligations: false
```

A minority or disputed succession may require continuity arrangements. Those arrangements should protect playable faction control while leaving political consequences real. No named dynasty, heir, claimant or guaranteed historical event is created here.

## 21. Dissolution and Restoration Boundary

This authority can identify a severe Union crisis and route it to the dissolution authority. It cannot invent the exact partition, duplicate successor armies, erase sources, or silently reactivate both native faction controllers.

```yaml
crisis_referral_requires:
  - ActualBreakdownOfRecognizedCommonRuleOrAValidSeparationSettlement
  - IdentifiedTerritoryCharacterMilitaryAndInstitutionalConsequences
  - ExplicitDissolutionAuthorityResolution
ordinary_local_strain_automatically_dissolves_union: false
governance_expression_change_reactivates_native_factions: false
surviving_people_assets_and_claims_deleted: false
```

A contested Barcelona-Aragon can persist while a settlement is sought. Repair, continued firm rule, rebalanced seniority, or valid separation remain possible according to the relevant authorities. Dissolution is not a mandatory punishment for failing a minor objective.

A functioning Union is a successful campaign state, not missing content. Governance depth remains available when circumstances change.

## 23. AI Governance and Fairness

The AI should choose priorities that fit actual threats, institutions, obligations and sustainable capacity. It can maintain any of the three expressions and should not treat the balanced option as automatically virtuous or dominant government as automatically unstable.

The AI can make an unpopular but strategically defensible decision. Fairness means shared rules and consequences, not perfect play or identical choices in every campaign.

## 24. Worked Design Cases

### A. Balanced Union, one urgent frontier

Both foundations accept concentrating forces on an urgent frontier. Balance does not require equal armies on both borders. The government protects the other foundation's minimum local defense and makes the service burden explicit. If promised relief never arrives, the affected sources develop a real grievance.

### B. Barcelona seniority, repeated Aragonese losses

A Barcelona-centered campaign repeatedly uses Aragonese contributors. Early losses do not automatically invalidate the settlement. Continued demands without recovery or compensation can reduce exceptional service cooperation. The player can fund recovery, alter the campaign, share command, renegotiate obligations, or persist with explained risks.

### C. Aragon seniority, Barcelona civic support

A royal land campaign needs missile and supply support. Barcelona institutions cooperate under accepted terms. Diverting operators or stores beyond those terms can impair local capacity. The remedy is a real allocation choice, not a free loyalty payment or a renamed crossbow card.

### D. Weak human foundation, strong senior partner

The human continues controlling Barcelona-Aragon while the other foundation has senior direction. Ordinary settlement and army control remain intact. Governance choices concern obligations and support, not whether the player may command the acquired territory at all.

### E. One battlefield defeat

A senior-side army is defeated. Actual losses affect capacity and confidence, but government does not instantly flip. If repeated failures produce a durable recognized shift in precedence, a later political event may open renegotiation.

### F. Healthy Union

Obligations are met, threats are manageable, and institutions cooperate. No artificial crisis is generated to keep the panel busy. The player can expand, build, recruit and fight normally.

### G. Contested appointment

An appointment disputes custody or command recognized in the settlement. The consequence attaches to that office and connected institutions. A replacement or renegotiated arrangement can resolve it without rebuilding the faction or granting universal support.

### H. Shift from dominant to balanced government

A recognized renegotiation establishes shared precedence. The faction, treasury, armies and source records continue. Some institutions welcome the change; others may contest lost privileges. Existing casualties and obligations remain, so the player cannot use constitutional reform to reset costs.

These cases illustrate proposed design behavior. They are not executed tests or certified historical events.

## 26. Downstream Handoff

The next substantive faction capability is **Character, Dynasty, Succession, and Claimants**. That authority should establish how surviving native characters, recognized common rule, heirs, regencies, claims and disputed succession connect to this governance model. It should not invent a fixed historical ruler for every counterfactual campaign.

Economy and administration should later quantify sustainable burdens and investment; campaign objectives and AI should translate accepted rules into readable choices; dissolution should own final separation and restoration. These dependencies do not authorize runtime implementation or require reopening unit profiles for cosmetic political differences.

The older framework's separate domination architecture still requires a bounded reconciliation when its own file is handled. This new authority does not claim that those physical files have already been corrected.
