## 1. Distinctive Purpose

**A durable Union is an adaptable common government, not a faction that must eventually erase its foundations.**

This specialist proposes long-term development choices and the conditions under which an existing Union can renegotiate its governmental expression. It does not replace the Governance Authority, repeat formation, or create an automatic ladder from balance to domination. Balanced, Barcelona-led and Aragon-led government can each remain durable outcomes.

The distinctive strategic question is which responsibilities should be coordinated, which should remain local, and whether the current distribution of common direction still fits accepted authority and actual capacity. Institutional cooperation can deepen without changing seniority; seniority can change without replacing every local institution.

## 12. Ruler, Dynasty, Regency and Office Continuity

Succession and expression are related but distinct. A dynasty change does not automatically switch seniority. A regent does not become sovereign through a display label. An office can continue while its holder changes through valid procedure.

A proposed settlement must use actual canonical people and current authority. Unknown office occupancy is not a vacancy and cannot justify inventing a replacement. Character and Governance resolve competence during regency or contested succession.

Existing appointments, personal obligations and claimant disputes retain their current dispositions unless a supported specific change affects them. No named historical ruler, heir or marriage is created here.

## 13. Rights, Institutions and New Territories

Local law, religious institutions, municipal privileges and regional rights remain scoped records. Seniority does not automatically override all junior-foundation competence. A common administrative improvement does not merge cultures or convert religion.

Newly incorporated territory joins under actual control and supported arrangements. ## 14. Fiscal and External Continuity

Expression evolution does not clear debt, reset treaty timers, remove breach history or turn restricted property into common cash. Preserve actual beneficiaries, obligors and scope. Diplomacy reviews only relationships materially affected by the new settlement.

A foreign counterpart can dispute the new ruler or precedence while maintaining trade or another compatible agreement. External refusal is not a universal veto on internally valid governance. A supported binding condition still matters within its actual scope.
