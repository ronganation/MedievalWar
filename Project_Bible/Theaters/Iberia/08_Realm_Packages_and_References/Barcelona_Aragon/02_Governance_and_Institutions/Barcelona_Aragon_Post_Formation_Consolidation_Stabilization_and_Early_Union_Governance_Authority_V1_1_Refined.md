## 1. Purpose and Distinctive Verdict

**Formation establishes one faction; consolidation makes its accepted government work in practice.**

Barcelona-Aragon must coordinate its actual resources, offices and obligations while preserving the two foundations' supported rights and institutional identities. This specialist proposes a condition-driven early-governance experience, not a second formation event, mandatory crisis, universal centralization ladder or temporary mini-faction system.

The player should make a small number of consequential choices: clarify responsibility, fund an urgent obligation, remedy an uneven burden, preserve a functioning local arrangement, or invest in common coordination. A well-prepared Union can begin quietly and govern successfully without artificial penalties.
