## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Hold a joined realm through workable government, credible protection, and proportionate responses, not by treating every disagreement as rebellion.**

Barcelona-Aragon is a higher-era Union outcome available through the campaign development of Barcelona and Aragon. Its public-order layer combines compatible inherited mechanics, preserves local conditions, and adds the coordination needed by common government. It is not a third society created from nothing, and it does not require a fresh loyalty system beside the project's existing campaign systems.

The distinctive choice is how to use the enlarged realm without making one foundation carry unsustainable burdens for the other's priorities. A player may concentrate forces for a frontier emergency, protect a threatened port, negotiate a disputed contribution, repair an occupied settlement, or preserve a functioning local arrangement. Each choice has a real opportunity cost. The aim is ordinary Total War campaign play with consequential local response, not a permanent court or petition simulator.

A functioning Union is a valid result. Formation does not require unrest, a compulsory consolidation penalty, or a scripted civil war. Conversely, a formed faction does not receive universal acceptance, repaired settlements, or erased occupation history.

## 5. Six Distinct Questions

| Question | Meaning | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Who actually governs or occupies the place? | Universal consent or title recognition |
| Security | Can ordinary movement, protection, and supported services function? | Agreement with government |
| Compliance | Is a specific obligation being performed? | Enthusiasm or permanent allegiance |
| Cooperation | Will relevant institutions support a defined action within their terms? | Unlimited material capacity |
| Constitutional acceptance | Is the common-government arrangement recognized in the relevant scope? | Every tax or appointment is accepted |
| Recovery | Which damaged functions or relationships are actually improving? | All grievances are resolved |

These are explanatory dimensions, not six compulsory meters. Where the shared campaign uses an aggregate public-order value, preserve that supported abstraction while retaining enough cause information to explain material changes. This file does not allocate a replacement numerical model.

## 7A. Public-Order Operating Loop

The Union public-order loop is deliberately bounded and player-readable:

1. **Observe** a material local condition through supported knowledge.
2. **Classify** the issue as capacity, access, obligation, protection, jurisdiction, occupation, or organized resistance.
3. **Scope** the affected place, audience, consequence, and producing owner.
4. **Choose** a feasible response or consciously retain the current policy.
5. **Commit once** through the system that owns the force, funds, authority, or access.
6. **Measure delivery**, not merely intent or payment.
7. **Reassess** only when material inputs change.
8. **Close, retain, or escalate** the issue without erasing its history.

```yaml
issue_lifecycle_states:
  - Observed
  - UnderAssessment
  - Actionable
  - ResponseCommitted
  - PartiallyDelivered
  - Stabilized
  - Resolved
  - Persisting
  - EscalatedToOwningAuthority
state_transition_requires_material_change: true
panel_opening_changes_state: false
same_issue_may_exist_in_multiple_states: false
resolved_issue_history_is_deleted: false
```

This is a design contract, not a newly allocated runtime state machine. The shared public-order implementation may use fewer internal states if it preserves the same player-facing distinctions and conservation rules.

## 8. Routine Government and Healthy-State Play

Ordinary accepted taxation, service, construction, recruitment, and army orders proceed through existing interfaces. They do not require a local consent prompt each turn. Material exceptional demands, changed terms, new threats, or real failures can justify attention.

A stable region can remain stable. A healthy Union may have no special public-order decision. The player can preserve an arrangement that works rather than pay recurring loyalty fees or complete ceremonial chores. Background administration continues without creating a special mission.

Routine display should emphasize the most consequential issue, not a catalogue of every minor complaint. Unchanged warnings are grouped or suppressed without hiding a worsening condition or due obligation.

## 10. Dual-Foundation Burden and Common Strategy

Barcelona-Aragon's distinctive pressure is cross-foundation opportunity cost. A maritime commitment can consume funds and defenders needed inland. A frontier campaign can divert civic support and recovery capacity from another region. The consequences follow actual sources, not fixed stereotypes.

| Government expression | Public-order emphasis | Protected boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Balanced Union | Credible reciprocity and explanation of unequal common burdens | No equal-spending or equal-casualty requirement |
| Barcelona-led Union | Protect affected Aragonese obligations when common priorities concentrate elsewhere | No blanket Aragonese opposition or automatic Barcelona compliance |
| Aragon-led Union | Protect affected Barcelona institutions when common priorities concentrate elsewhere | No blanket Barcelona opposition or automatic Aragon compliance |

A dangerous mission accepted within valid terms does not automatically create a grievance because losses were unequal. Repeated excessive demands, broken relief commitments, or actual protection failures can create a scoped issue. Expression changes preserve those histories and do not reset local response.

## 16. Response and Remedy Portfolio

| Response | Useful purpose | Tradeoff or constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve working arrangement | Avoid unnecessary disruption | A known inefficiency may remain |
| Clarify a demand | Resolve misunderstanding or conflicting scope | Does not settle a genuinely disputed right by assertion |
| Narrow or sequence a burden | Make performance feasible | Delays another objective or reduces immediate receipts |
| Deliver promised relief | Restore protection or service capacity | Uses real forces, funds, access, and time |
| Fund targeted repair | Recover an actual function | Competes with campaigning and recruitment |
| Hear a material petition | Establish a supported remedy route | Requires competent authority and usable evidence |
| Mediate a dispute | Avoid unnecessary escalation | Parties may refuse; agreement still needs performance |
| Maintain a supported policy | Continue a defensible strategic commitment | Accept actual scoped consequences |
| Refer a constitutional issue | Resolve a mandate or settlement problem | Governance owns valid change |
| Refer actual separation | Resolve real breakaway or dissolution | Lifecycle owns partition, not this panel |

A remedy identifies its beneficiary, scope, producing owner, actual inputs, delivered stages, and remaining obligation. One payment cannot discharge unrelated debts. Compensation does not restore dead people, refill pools, or guarantee cooperation.

## 17. Recovery: Restore Functions Without Erasing History

Recovery can involve security, access, services, fiscal performance, institutional cooperation, and settlement acceptance. These can improve at different rates. The design uses existing owner rules rather than assigning exact durations or a universal recovery meter.

```yaml
recovery_dimensions:
  Security: ActualThreatAndProtectionConditions
  Access: SupportedMovementAndCommunication
  Services: ActualPeopleBuildingsRecordsMaterialsAndFunding
  Obligations: DeliveredAndOutstandingPerformance
  Cooperation: CurrentIssueSpecificInstitutionalResponse
  PoliticalSettlement: RecognitionUnderGovernanceOwners
recovery_does_not:
  - ResetDamageOrCasualties
  - EraseBreachOrOccupationHistory
  - RefillRecruitmentPoolsByDeclaration
  - RestoreExpiredRightsWithoutAuthority
  - GuaranteePermanentAcceptance
```

Repairing a service can remove a material cause while a dispute remains unresolved. A negotiated settlement can end a dispute while repair is still pending. A restored ruler can receive recognition while the settlement remains poor or damaged. Show these distinctions rather than one falsely reassuring recovered label.

## 18. Crisis, Succession, and Lifecycle Interfaces

An institutional disagreement becomes a constitutional issue only when its actual subject concerns common mandates, protected settlement terms, or recognized authority. Union Crisis supplies dispute and remedy coordination; Governance owns valid constitutional change. Public-order consequences remain scoped to actual conduct and affected audiences.

Ruler death, regency, or a claimant does not automatically create unrest or dissolve the Union. Character and Governance resolve the changed authority. Revalidate only the affected obligations and remedies.

Actual separation routes to Lifecycle with current control, damage, service condition, issues, remedies, and obligations. Do not restore an ideal pre-Union snapshot. Re-formation consumes current surviving state and does not grant a second pacification bonus.
