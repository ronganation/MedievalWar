## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**A dispute over common rule is a problem to understand and govern, not proof that the Union must break apart.**

Barcelona-Aragon needs meaningful ways to challenge an unsupported demand, repair a breach, negotiate a burden, or restore working cooperation while remaining one faction. The player should distinguish an institutional disagreement from a constitutional challenge, and both from organized violence or actual separation.

This specialist proposes a bounded dispute-and-remedy portfolio. It does not create a universal crisis meter, compulsory civil war, permanent junior-partner AI, or a second dissolution system. Healthy government can continue without generating any crisis.

### 5A. Linked Issues, Contradictory Evidence and Observer Knowledge

A constitutional dispute can contain distinct fiscal, office and service questions. Link those questions without merging their parties, rights or remedies. Conversely, reports of the same underlying breach share one canonical issue. Record contradictory evidence and its provenance rather than silently choosing the favorable account.

## 6. Recognizing a Material Crisis Without Manufacturing One

A dispute becomes a material governance problem when its actual consequences affect common responsibilities, due obligations, access, institutional services or the recognized ability to direct government. Severity follows supported conduct and capacity, not cultural identity or a universal score.

```yaml
assessment_questions:
  - WhatActuallyHappenedAndWhatIsOnlyAlleged
  - WhichRightsMandatesOrObligationsAreAffected
  - WhichServicesOrDecisionsCannotProceed
  - WhoHasStandingAuthorityAndMeans
  - WhatChangesIfActionIsDelayed
mandatory_crisis_after_formation: false
single_petition_automatically_creates_rebellion: false
one_local_issue_makes_whole_union_unstable: false
```

A technically defective transfer routes back to Formation or the producing owner. Do not impose political unrest to conceal duplicate treasury records or missing transferred assets. A genuine breach is not dismissed as technical cleanup.

## 7. Three Expressions and Protected Continuity

| Expression | Relevant dispute question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| BalancedUnion | Does common action respect the accepted consultation and mandate arrangement? | Balance is not unanimity on every routine action |
| BarcelonaDominantUnion | Is Barcelona senior direction acting within its actual common mandate? | Seniority does not erase Aragonese rights |
| AragonDominantUnion | Is Aragon senior direction acting within its actual common mandate? | Seniority does not erase Barcelona rights |

The same standard of scope, evidence and performance applies to all three. No expression is inherently illegitimate, immune to challenge or doomed to deadlock. Political seniority does not decide the human controller. The junior foundation is not recreated as an autonomous AI actor whenever a dispute arises.

## 8. Standing, Competence and Representation

Use actual offices, institutions, people and current mandates. Governance and institutional owners determine who may petition, represent an affected interest, mediate, decide or accept a change. Do not invent a universal parliament, bilateral council, religious veto or unanimous noble vote.

A representative's acceptance binds only the scope actually authorized. A ruler, regent, officeholder, institution and player controller are not interchangeable. A local client cannot consent for another human participant. Unknown competence calls for investigation or a bounded hold, not a fabricated delegate.

Consultation, acknowledgment, binding acceptance and private support remain distinct. A nonbinding objection is not automatically a constitutional veto.

### 8A. Conflicted Decision-Makers and Concurrent Forums

If the proposed decision-maker is a party to the disputed conduct, identify that conflict. Use a supported review, appeal or accepted mediation route where available; do not invent an independent supreme court. If no competent route is currently established, preserve uncertainty and seek a supported arrangement rather than declaring the stronger party correct.

Concurrent forums require an explicit competence disposition. Filing twice cannot produce two compensations. An interim protection is not final judgment, and an appeal does not erase completed conduct or automatically suspend unrelated obligations.

## 9. Response Portfolio and Tradeoffs

| Proposed response | Useful outcome | Cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Investigate records and conduct | Clarify a material uncertainty | Access, staff, time and incomplete evidence |
| Explain or narrow a demand | Resolve misunderstanding or overreach | May reduce expected revenue or scope |
| Preserve current valid terms | Avoid unnecessary constitutional disruption | Underlying service problem may remain |
| Hear a petition or appeal | Provide an appropriate review route | Requires actual competent forum |
| Mediate | Seek acceptable performance terms | Parties may refuse; mediator needs standing |
| Negotiate timing or contribution | Make an obligation feasible | Another commitment may be delayed |
| Restore access or service | Remedy a concrete failure | Actual money, people, materials and security |
| Correct an unsupported action | Restore defined rights or responsibility | Does not erase incurred costs or other claims |
| Propose constitutional amendment | Address a genuine mandate problem | Governance acceptance and transition rules apply |

No response guarantees loyalty, obedience or universal reconciliation. Do not reward repression or repeated manufactured grievances with free resources. Preserve the option of no special intervention when the report is immaterial or ordinary administration is already handling it.

## 10. Interim Arrangements and Continuing Government

Where competent owners support them, temporary arrangements can preserve access, essential services, records or uncontested obligations while the disputed scope is reviewed. Define who authorizes the arrangement, what it covers, how it is funded, and when it ends or is reviewed.


Emergency action must have a supported mandate and actual means. Preserve conduct and costs after the measure ends. An interim arrangement cannot silently confiscate property, change expression or partition the faction.

## 11. Mediation, Review and Settlement

Proposed handling is deliberately bounded: identify the issue and standing; inspect material evidence; determine the competent route; compare feasible remedies; secure required acceptance; perform the agreed remedy through its owners; review actual delivery. This is a design proposal, not a historical universal court procedure.

Religious, civic, household or external mediation is available only where actual authority, relationships and communication support it. Patronage does not purchase a favorable judgment. A mediator does not become the Union's sovereign.

A settlement identifies changed and preserved rights, obligations, responsible performers, costs, effective scope, unresolved matters and review conditions. Required human consent is explicit. Closing a panel, silence or a timeout does not accept new terms.

## 12. Remedy Delivery and Reconciliation

Distinguish four outcomes: agreement reached, remedy delivered, services restored, and continuing cooperation. They can occur at different times. A payment is not universal forgiveness; a repaired archive is not proof that every actor accepts its contents.

```yaml
remedy_contract:
  scope: SpecificSupportedBreachOrProblem
  performer: CompetentOwnerAndActualCapacity
  delivery: ObservableOwnerResolvedOutcome
  history: PreserveConductCostsAndUnresolvedClaims
agreement_equals_delivery: false
enforcement_equals_consent: false
reconciliation_erases_all_history: false
```

A partially delivered lawful stage remains delivered if later performance fails. Adjust or renegotiate the remainder through the owner. No instant trust reset, universal amnesty, population creation or duplicated compensation is authorized.

### 12A. Remedy Scope, Resource Conflicts and Partial Performance

A remedy must identify the actual beneficiary, object, amount or service scope, performer and obligation being discharged. Do not compensate an entire foundation for one institution's loss without a supported settlement. A payment against one obligation does not discharge unrelated debts.

```yaml
remedy_delivery_requires:
  - CanonicalRemedyAndUnderlyingObligationReference
  - BeneficiaryAndAuthorizedScope
  - ActualResourcesAndCompetingCommitments
  - StageDeliveryAndRemainingBalanceWhereApplicable

same_loss_compensated_twice: false
proposal_reserves_resources_before_owner_commit: false
partial_performance_is_total_completion: false
```

When capacity changes, offer narrowing, sequencing or renewed negotiation. Preserve valid delivered stages and remaining obligations. Reconciliation is evidenced by actual restored cooperation or service, not a forced emotional label or universal trust reset.

## 13. Escalation and Organized Resistance Boundary

Petition, delay, nonperformance, refusal, obstruction and organized violence are distinct. Determine actual actors, organization, means, objectives and access before public-order systems resolve escalation. Culture, faith, language or native provenance does not establish hostility.

This file does not spawn rebel armies, assign every institution to a hostile bloc or transfer settlements because a crisis label appears. Security actions use existing campaign and public-order owners. Military rosters, unit profiles and recruitment systems remain unchanged.

A serious crisis can remain a dispute inside one faction. Actual rival government, war or separation requires the applicable owning rules and current evidence, not a scripted inevitability.

## 14. Constitutional Amendment Versus Dissolution

If remedy requires changed common mandates or senior precedence, refer the accepted proposal to Governance and the long-term expression-evolution interface. Preserve the same faction, current controller and unaffected rights under those owners.

If actual separation is proposed or occurs, Lifecycle determines eligibility, successor control, property, debts, projects, institutions and cases. This file supplies the current disputed and agreed dispositions; it does not execute partition.

Failure to amend is not automatic dissolution. Failure of mediation is not automatic civil war. Re-formation after separation uses a new supported settlement in the current world, not an old formation receipt.

### 14A. Referral to Governance or Lifecycle

A referral states the current issue, accepted and disputed terms, required authority, affected rights and pending performance. It is not a second constitutional acceptance or partition command. Governance revalidates expression changes; Lifecycle revalidates actual separation.

A rejected referral leaves the current valid settlement in force unless the controlling owner has established a different current state. No hidden transfer of territory, institutions, player control or treasury is permitted through a dispute-status update.

## 15. Succession, External Actors and Cross-Border Institutions

A ruler's death or contested regency can change who has competence to settle a dispute. Character and Governance revalidate affected authority without inventing heirs or deleting institutions. A personal claim is not automatically a claim to every Union asset.

External support, recognition or mediation follows actual diplomacy and knowledge. A foreign objection does not universally veto domestic settlement; a supported binding condition retains its actual scope. No automatic anti-Union coalition is created.

Cross-border institutions retain canonical identity. Border change, dispute or dissolution does not clone offices, archives, property or obligations. Shared custody, access and jurisdiction need explicit current arrangements through their owners.

## 16. AI Decisions and Knowledge

AI compares investigation, preservation, narrowing, mediation, remedy, deferral and valid amendment. Evaluate actual urgency, rights, authority, costs, service consequences and feasible alternatives. Do not treat seniority as proof of correctness or every junior objection as disloyalty.

An explanation identifies the principal known issue and available alternative without exposing private intentions or fabricated utility scores. A recommendation is not a committed settlement, and an accepted settlement is not completed performance.

## 19. Closure, Recurrence and Memory

Close the special dispute presentation when the issue has an explicit disposition and remaining work is handled through ordinary owners. Closure need not mean universal agreement or the end of every related claim.

A materially new breach can reopen a linked issue; an unchanged report should not create duplicate compensation or repeated notifications. Preserve prior settlements and actual performance history. No exact cooldown or mandatory recurrence date is assigned.

Chronicles retain what was disputed and resolved at the time. Current faction identity remains Barcelona-Aragon. A hostile label or contested title is shown as a perspective, not substituted for canonical ownership.

### 19A. Closure Is a Disposition, Not Erasure

Distinguish settled and performed, settled with delivery pending, referred, withdrawn, unresolved under ordinary administration, and superseded by a later valid settlement. These are explanatory categories, not newly registered states.

A withdrawn allegation is not automatically false; a closed interface is not a fulfilled obligation. Reopen only for a material new fact, actual new breach or supported review condition. Link recurrence to prior history without paying the same remedy again. Unrelated services and campaign orders remain available.
