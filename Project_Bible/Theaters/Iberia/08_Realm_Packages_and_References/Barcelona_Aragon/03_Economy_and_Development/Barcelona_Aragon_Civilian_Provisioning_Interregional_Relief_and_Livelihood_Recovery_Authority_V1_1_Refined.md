## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Relieve one part of the joined realm without exhausting another or consuming the resources needed for recovery.**

The player chooses between preserving local provision, releasing an accessible reserve, procuring supplies, restoring a connection, reducing an exceptional demand, repairing productive capacity, or postponing a military commitment. The strategic reward of Union is a wider set of options, not guaranteed abundance.

This specialist proposes cross-region civilian allocation decisions. It creates no second economy, food simulation, population model, relief treasury or public-order engine. Ordinary settlement management continues through existing interfaces. Healthy regions require no recurring relief prompt or compulsory shortage.

## 5. Five Distinct Questions

| Question | Meaning | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Need | Which civilian function lacks support? | Political refusal |
| Availability | What goods or services actually exist? | Uncommitted surplus |
| Access | Can the affected place receive support? | Completed delivery |
| Allocation | Which use has priority under valid authority? | New resources |
| Recovery | Can local provision become sustainable again? | Instant prosperity or recruitment |

These are explanatory distinctions, not compulsory meters. Unknown need is neither zero need nor confirmed crisis. A place can have goods nearby but lack access, or have access but no available supply.

## 6. Player Choice Portfolio

| Choice | Intended result | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve local provision | Avoid disrupting a functioning arrangement | Other demands may remain unmet |
| Release an accessible reserve | Meet an immediate supported need | Less resilience or supply elsewhere |
| Procure through admitted trade | Obtain actual additional goods | Funds, availability, terms and delivery |
| Restore a connection | Reconnect existing provision | Repair or protection competes with campaigning |
| Narrow an exceptional demand | Leave capacity with current users | Lower immediate receipts or military support |
| Restore productive function | Reduce repeated dependence on aid | Slower benefit and actual work inputs |
| Reprioritize an operation | Release supported military resources | Another objective is delayed or exposed |
| Defer with stated consequences | Preserve another essential commitment | Need and risk persist |

A reserve release can help without repairing production. Productive investment can help without meeting immediate need. No choice is universally best; no new menu is required for every village.

## 10. Civilian and Military Competition

A field army, fortress and civilian recipient can compete for the same supported supply or transport. Show the displaced commitment instead of assigning the same capacity three times.

Reducing an operation releases only what its owner actually releases. A withdrawn army can still need upkeep, movement and recovery. Relieving a fortress is not automatically civilian provisioning; civilian delivery does not automatically replenish its garrison.

A player may maintain a military priority while accepting disclosed civilian consequences. This file adds no manufactured-deprivation reward, identity-based allocation penalty or forced harmful policy. Exact available actions remain with shared campaign owners.

## 11. Livelihood and Productive Recovery

Target the actual bottleneck: damaged productive facilities, missing tools or inputs, inaccessible markets, disrupted labor availability or an unfulfilled valid obligation where modeled. Do not assume every shortfall has the same cause.

Separate immediate support, restored operating function and sustainable local provision. Repair does not create operators or goods. Delivery does not restore every livelihood. Funding does not guarantee a completed production cycle.

Season-sensitive work follows the campaign-season owner. Historical and biological tracks do not substitute for operational progress. No exact crop, yield, growth rate or agricultural calendar is authored here.

### 11A. Immediate Relief, Sustained Provision, and Productive Recovery

| Outcome | Meaning | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate relief | A bounded urgent need receives actual support | The cause is removed |
| Sustained provision | Continued supported access meets the relevant function | Local production is restored |
| Operating recovery | A damaged service or productive function works again | Full prior capacity or resilience |
| Productive recovery | Local capability can contribute under owner rules | Unlimited output or instant prosperity |
| Resilience | Supported alternatives or reserves reduce vulnerability | Immunity from future disruption |

## 13. Institutions, Rights and External Assistance

Existing providers contribute only through actual authority, capacity and terms. No charitable institution, officeholder or service network is fabricated. Direct faction control remains intact; this is not a universal consent minigame.

Promised external aid is not receipt. Agreement creates no ships, goods, funds or access. Preserve actual restrictions and delivered stages; restricted property cannot silently become spendable cash.

Governance resolves disputed mandates. Public Order evaluates conduct and affected scope. Incapacity is not automatically breach. Relief can address a material cause without settling unrelated rights or guaranteeing permanent acceptance.

## 14. Expressions, AI and Player Presentation

Balanced, Barcelona-led and Aragon-led government can prioritize any supported civilian need. Neither foundation is an unlimited donor; unequal spending is not itself constitutional failure.

AI compares immediate relief, access restoration, demand reduction, productive repair, military reprioritization and deferral using actor-visible evidence and actual capacity. No hidden enemy state, free goods, population refill or Union-specific exemption is granted.

Show the affected function, known need, main blocker, donor impact, displaced commitment and feasible alternatives. Group unchanged warnings, use text equivalents for color and icons, and expose material uncertainty. Healthy conditions remain quiet without ceremonial chores.

## 15. Completion and Campaign Lifecycle

Close an action against its declared scope. Delivery can finish while need remains; repair can restore one service while another stays blocked. Do not collapse these into one universal recovered label.

Repeated events create no second debit, refund or benefit. Preserve earlier evidence and delivered stages when conditions change. Panel navigation and reload do not refresh supplies or reroll outcomes.

Formation, succession and expression change preserve current conditions. Dissolution routes stocks, obligations, projects and population state once through Lifecycle. Reformation consumes surviving state. Later starts author actual conditions; opening defaults never overwrite advanced saves.
