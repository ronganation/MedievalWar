## 1. Decisive Material Verdict

**One faction budget and one practical recruitment interface. Real regional capacity, finite recruitment pools, and losses that must actually be recovered.**

The Union's material reward is a wider directly controlled realm with complementary institutions and more strategic choices. Its challenge is connecting and sustaining those resources, not obtaining permission from a second AI government. The player should recruit, build, tax and campaign through familiar Total War-style decisions while source accounting prevents free duplication beneath the interface.

```yaml
single_union_faction: true
union_expressions: [BalancedUnion, BarcelonaDominantUnion, AragonDominantUnion]
separate_domination_path: false
direct_human_and_AI_control: Preserved
campaign_facing_treasury: OneCombinedFactionBudget
recruitment_interface: OneFactionInterfaceWithSourceAndAccessCategories
recruitment_model: FiniteSourceBoundPools
population_is_direct_army_manpower_currency: false
one_generic_union_population_horse_equipment_or_recovery_pool: false
```

The game must not replace the satisfying territorial reward with a permanent integration tax, a treasury for every town, or a consent prompt for every ordinary unit. Material constraints should create choices about armies, investment, timing and geography.

## 2. Authority Ownership

| Area | This authority develops | Other authority retains |
|---|---|---|
| Treasury and budget | Combined presentation, inherited liabilities and spending priorities | Exact economic values and scenario balances |
| Administration | Practical connections, local continuity and investment consequences | Map topology, building definitions and institutional facts |
| Recruitment | Pool eligibility, capacity commitments, queues and competition | Unit roles, equipment envelopes and exact balance |
| Recovery | Casualty, equipment, horse and source-capacity continuity | Individual character outcomes and battle casualty generation |
| Governance | Material costs and capacity inputs | Recognition, obligations, grievances and seniority changes |
| Formation | Postformation material continuity | Actual faction and territorial transfer |
| Dissolution | Provenance needed for later partition | Final successor asset and controller disposition |

No ownership boundary justifies stripping useful strategic consequences from this authority. Interfaces identify who resolves detail; they do not remove the player's choices or costs.

## 3. Economic Identity and Strategic Promise

The General Authority's complementary foundations guide the design: Barcelona-facing civic, castle, urban, workshop, missile-support and route interests can combine with Aragon-facing royal, regional, mountain, valley, frontier and mounted-service interests. These are inherited design emphases, not universal historical production bonuses.

The formed faction can choose to sustain a broader army, repair depleted institutions, connect routes, improve local administration, or concentrate resources on a decisive campaign. It cannot maximize all of those at once without sufficient revenue, material supply and political cooperation.

## 7. Spending Priorities and Opportunity Cost

| Investment | Intended benefit | Opportunity cost or constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Maintain field forces | Keep existing armies usable | Upkeep competes with new recruitment and construction |
| Restore a damaged source | Recover future service capacity | Requires real resources and time; does not replace current losses instantly |
| Improve a route or strongpoint | Connect support and protect movement | Requires controlled access, actual work and investment |
| Develop workshop or training capacity | Support a valid capability | Needs operators, equipment supply and institutional eligibility |
| Sustain local defense | Preserve security and source continuity | Limits forces available for distant campaigns |
| Seek exceptional service | Concentrate strength for urgent need | Greater burden and possible recovery or governance costs |

No new building catalog is created here. Reuse existing buildings, decisions and campaign systems where they can express these investments. Add a specialist mechanism only when it supplies a distinct capability rather than another administrative wrapper.

Administrative integration and political support are separate. A cooperative region can be physically isolated; a connected region can dispute an exceptional demand. Governance owns the dispute, while this authority owns the material consequences.

## 12. Eligibility, Capacity, and Readiness Are Different

| Question | Meaning | Example design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Is the family eligible? | Institution, era, location and political basis permit the card | A locked institution cannot be bypassed with cash |
| Is capacity available? | The actual source has uncommitted recruitment capacity | A valid card can be temporarily exhausted |
| Is material support ready? | Required equipment, horses, operators and leadership exist | Available people do not automatically provide mounted service |
| Can the assignment be sustained? | Upkeep, service terms and support remain viable | Recruitment can be possible but strategically unaffordable |

```yaml
technology_or_decision:
  may_enable: CapabilityEligibilityOrDevelopment
  does_not_create: PeopleEquipmentHorsesOfficersOrRecruitmentPools
building:
  may_support: ValidTrainingProductionAdministrationOrMusterCapacity
  does_not_prove: InstantStaffedAndEquippedFormation
```

A clear recruitment panel distinguishes an exhausted pool from a political restriction or missing horse support. It should not label every problem as generic low loyalty.

## 26. Worked Campaign Cases

### A. Larger realm, depleted treasury

The Union forms with more settlements and eligible forces but also inherited upkeep and debt. The player gains strategic reach, not a cash bonus. Maintaining the existing army may delay new recruitment or construction.

### B. Cash available, horses missing

A mounted family is institutionally eligible and its source has capacity, but suitable horses and support are unavailable. Money can fund valid procurement or recovery, not instantly complete the unit. The panel shows the horse-support blocker rather than generic political disloyalty.

### C. Native and composite queues share a source

The player commits eligible capacity to a native formation. A composite formation using that same actual source cannot reserve it again. A genuinely distinct source remains available. The interface explains the competing commitment.

### D. Supportive but exhausted institution

A source accepts the Union and its obligations but has suffered losses. Governance recognition remains positive while recruitment capacity is reduced. The player can fund actual recovery or use another eligible source without manufacturing a political crisis.

### E. Queue canceled before completion

The cancellation resolves actual reserved funds, progress and capacity under the controlling rules. It neither refunds already consumed resources without basis nor permanently consumes people never assigned. Reopening the queue cannot create a second refund.

### F. Lost recruiting region, surviving field unit

An enemy takes the unit's source region. Future recruitment or recovery access is reevaluated. The surviving unit remains in its actual field state and requires a valid support or disposition outcome; it is not deleted or instantly replenished elsewhere.

### G. Aragon-dominant land campaign

The government prioritizes land-war support and draws on eligible Barcelona missile institutions. Actual costs, capacity and service terms apply. Repeated excessive demands can produce a governance problem, but no extra universal Union surcharge is invented.

### H. Barcelona-dominant route investment

The player funds a valid connection that supports both foundations' movement or administration. The benefit follows actual completed work and supported campaign rules. The decision does not spawn ships, crossbows or a new native population.

### I. Returning composite formation

Actual surviving contributors and recoverable equipment return through valid terms. Their source capacity is restored only as the relevant rules permit. The same people cannot remain assigned to the field formation and simultaneously refill a native pool.

### J. Ransom resolves captivity

An actual payment and agreement releases eligible captives under the character and campaign rules. Their wounds, location, service and recovery still need valid disposition. Payment does not return dead soldiers or refill the entire source.

### K. Constitutional reform

A valid expression change alters priorities and obligations. Treasury, debt, queues, losses and recovery continue. The player cannot switch government to obtain another formation bonus or fresh recruitment pools.

### L. Healthy combined economy

Income, upkeep, routes and source capacity support the current strategy. No artificial deficit or crisis is generated merely because the Union exists. The player can campaign normally and choose when to expand or invest.

These are illustrative proposed game-design cases, not executed tests or historical economic examples.
