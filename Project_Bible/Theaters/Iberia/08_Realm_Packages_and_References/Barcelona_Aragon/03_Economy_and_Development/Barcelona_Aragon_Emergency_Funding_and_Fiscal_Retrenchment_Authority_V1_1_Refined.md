## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Keep the joined realm capable of acting when it cannot afford every commitment, without treating either foundation as an unlimited emergency reserve.**

This proposed portfolio develops a specific strategic decision: when available funds cannot cover the chosen commitments, which purpose should be reduced, deferred, renegotiated or funded by an actually supported exceptional measure? The answer should change the campaign the player can sustain, not merely add a warning panel.

The Union keeps one practical faction budget and direct control. No second treasury, credit currency, banking minigame, permanent integration surcharge or compulsory fiscal council is created. A solvent realm continues ordinary play without invented emergencies. This is a candidate specialist doctrine, not an activated financial system.

## 4. Shortfall Is Not One Generic State

| Condition | Strategic meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Current cash shortfall | Spendable funds cannot cover an immediate supported use | Every future plan is unaffordable |
| Sustained negative position | Current commitments outpace supported receipts over the relevant horizon | A single cash grant fixes the structure |
| Restricted wealth | Assets exist but their use is limited | They are freely spendable money |
| Material shortage | Required goods, horses, personnel or access are absent | Cash alone resolves the blocker |
| Uncertain forecast | Expected receipts or costs are unresolved | The optimistic outcome is guaranteed |

Use the game's existing economic abstraction. This file assigns no new accounting period, liquidity ratio, insolvency threshold or forecast algorithm. Distinguish an actual deficit from a merely unattractive optional purchase.

## 5. Admission and Attention Gate

Open a bounded review only when a material payment, upkeep commitment, emergency operation or essential service faces a real funding choice. Routine construction selection and every tax change do not require a separate review.

Identify the purpose, actual spendable balance, existing commitments, affected obligation, known alternatives and consequence of delay. Separate committed expenditure from free cash and restricted property from usable receipts. A projected receipt remains projected until its owner records availability.

An unknown minor cost does not freeze the realm. A material unresolved value cannot justify unsupported spending. Healthy finances are a valid outcome, not a reason to manufacture a new crisis.

## 6. Response Portfolio

| Choice | Immediate purpose | Main cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow the operation | Fund a smaller useful objective | Reduced reach or payoff |
| Defer optional spending | Preserve funds for a current need | Delayed capability and actual cancellation rules |
| Cancel eligible uncompleted work | Recover only supported unused commitments | Sunk costs and delivered work persist |
| Reduce or redeploy military commitments | Lower a supported future burden | Movement, service terms and security exposure |
| Adjust ordinary taxation | Use the existing tax system | Actual local economic and public-order effects |
| Seek a bounded exceptional contribution | Meet a specific supported need | Authority, capacity, acceptance where required and burden |
| Renegotiate a due obligation | Change timing or scope by valid agreement | Counterpart may refuse; debt is not erased |
| Borrow or obtain an advance if supported | Bring actual funds forward under valid terms | Future obligations and provider availability |
| Decline the new commitment | Preserve the existing strategy | Opportunity forgone |

These are decision families, not a declaration that every option is implemented. Do not expose borrowing, advances, asset disposal or exceptional collection as executable actions without controlling support.

## 7. Union-Specific Burden and Priorities

Compare what each actual source can contribute and what the contribution displaces. The foundation with greater accessible cash is not automatically responsible for every emergency; the foundation facing the latest war is not automatically entitled to unlimited support.

Balanced government does not require equal payments. Barcelona-led and Aragon-led expressions can each favor land defense, maritime protection, source recovery or civilian continuity when current circumstances justify it. These are priorities inside one faction, not rival treasury controllers.

Ordinary orders remain direct. Consultation or consent is required only where an actual right or agreement makes it relevant. Neither native origin nor cultural identity supplies an automatic loyalty response, fiscal bonus or surcharge.

## 8. Exceptional Contributions

A proposed exceptional demand identifies its purpose, affected source or institution, authority, applicable limits, actual capacity and consequence for existing services. Where acceptance is required, the request is not a receipt until the relevant owner confirms performance.

A demand can be narrowed, staged, renegotiated or abandoned through existing rules. No universal emergency power overrides every charter or restricted asset. Conversely, an unrelated minor dispute does not block all ordinary collection.

Preserve the distinction between authorized demand, collection in progress, partial receipt and completed receipt. No fixed contribution percentage, universal duration, automatic unrest or permanent emergency tax is assigned. Expiry and renewal follow the bound measure's terms, not a new timer invented here.

## 9. Borrowing, Advances and Deferred Payment

These are conditional interfaces, not a newly created medieval banking system. Offer them only when shared project rules and actual counterpart capacity support them.

A proposal identifies the provider, amount or supported abstraction, beneficiary, receipt conditions, repayment or delivery obligations and any valid restriction. Approval alone does not place funds in the treasury. Actual receipt creates only the funds and liability confirmed by the producing owner.

An advance against a future receipt cannot also leave that same future receipt wholly uncommitted. Renegotiating a payment requires the applicable agreement; unilateral delay is not automatic forgiveness. No modern credit score, compound-interest formula, collateral seizure rule or named historical lender is introduced.

## 10. Retrenchment Without Free Refunds

Reducing a plan changes future choices; it does not reverse completed history. Canceling recruitment or construction releases only the recoverable portion under its owner. Completed work, consumed inputs and actual losses remain.

A reduced army may lower supported future upkeep only after actual service disposition. Disbandment does not instantly refill recruitment pools, return all equipment, create cash or remove obligations already incurred. A fleet abroad remains abroad until movement and custody resolve.

Show the capability lost or delayed: fewer forces available, a slower repair, reduced expedition scope or a postponed investment. Do not hide a severe security exposure behind the phrase budget savings.

### 10A. Retrenchment, Cancellation, Release, Savings, and Capability-Loss Disposition

| Action | Confirmed result | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow scope | Smaller supported commitment | Proportional refund or proportional success |
| Defer | Later decision or execution | Cancellation, savings, or preserved eligibility forever |
| Cancel | Owner-confirmed termination | Full refund or reversal of delivered work |
| Release assignment | End one supported commitment | Discharge, disbandment, or immediate savings |
| Disband or terminate service | Owner-resolved force disposition | Full pool refill, equipment recovery, or cash |
| Reduce service funding | Lower supported expenditure | Instant institutional destruction or restoration on repayment |

## 13. Exit and Fiscal Recovery

Close the emergency review when its bounded decision and owner handoffs are resolved, not only when every liability is paid or every unit is restored. Ordinary debt service and recovery can continue after the review closes.

Separate temporary cash relief from a sustainable future position. A one-time receipt may cover a payment while the recurring deficit persists. Reduced commitments may improve the outlook without producing an immediate cash credit.

End or renew exceptional measures through their actual terms. A healthy budget should return to quiet ordinary presentation. Closing the review creates no peace dividend, restored recruitment pool, victory reward or immunity from later shocks.

## 14. AI and Player Presentation

AI compares reducing scope, deferring spending, changing supported receipts, renegotiating and abstaining using actual knowledge, obligations and capacity. It receives no Union-specific free money or exemption from restrictions.

Show the current purpose, spendable funds, material shortfall, major committed costs, principal alternatives, displaced capability and known residual risk. Separate confirmed funds from expected receipts and future savings. Do not invent precise forecasts or disclose hidden counterpart resources.

Use compact summaries and expandable detail, text equivalents for icons and color, and suppression of unchanged notices. The normal campaign remains about armies, settlements and strategy, not approving every coin.

## 15. Formation, Lifecycle and Time

Formation inherits actual funds, liabilities, arrears, restricted assets and outstanding measures through its single transaction. No formation bonus clears a deficit. Expression changes do not reset debt or renew a contribution.

Dissolution requires current responsibility for each actual obligation and restricted asset. Do not clone a loan to both successors or silently forgive it. Reformation uses current surviving state; later starts author their own balances and commitments.

Historical +21 months, biological +7 months and one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step per strategic turn remain independent. Every future deadline names its clock. Date alone creates no receipt, repayment, recovery or default. Save reload does not reroll a decision or duplicate its financial result.
