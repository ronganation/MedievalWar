## 1. Purpose and Distinctive Player Promise

**Keep what both foundations have learned. Choose where to extend it, where to adapt it, and where specialization is worth preserving.**

Barcelona-Aragon is a higher-era Union outcome of campaign-developed Barcelona and Aragon. It inherits surviving practices and institutions, not a blank development tree. Its new opportunity is to connect complementary expertise and resources under common direction. Its challenge is establishing a useful practice in another place without exhausting the people and institutions that already sustain it.

This authority proposes the cross-foundation adoption layer. It is not a new technology engine, a second construction queue, a replacement native portfolio, or another constitutional-integration score. Routine building and recruitment remain ordinary campaign actions. Special attention belongs to meaningful transfers, adaptations, and competing development priorities.

## 4. Four Separate Layers

| Layer | Meaning | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Supported understanding or retained practice | Permission, trained people or operational capacity |
| Program | A funded or authorized development purpose | Completed introduction or local acceptance |
| Local realization | The practice works at an identified host | Realm-wide capability |
| Usable capability | Producing owner admits a campaign option | Unlimited output or recruitment |

```text
Knowledge != staffed institution
Shared government != universal adoption
Completed research != completed construction or training
Local realization != realm-wide unlock
Recorded learning != current operating capacity
```

The player may retain knowledge while losing the means to use it. A working institution may continue without requiring the player to research its practice again after formation.

## 6. Player Choice Portfolio

| Choice | Useful result | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve local specialization | Keep a reliable existing capability | Other regions remain dependent on its access and capacity |
| Extend an established practice | Establish a second viable host | Training, facilities, operating inputs and time compete with current needs |
| Adapt for a different host | Fit a practice to actual local conditions | Partial benefit or different requirements may result |
| Coordinate complementary institutions | Combine distinct contributions toward one objective | Shared access and scheduling become consequential |
| Recover a disrupted practice | Restore a previously usable service | Retained knowledge does not replace lost staff or facilities |
| Learn through valid external contact | Open a supported adoption opportunity | Contact, comprehension, access and actual assistance remain distinct |
| Defer or decline | Preserve resources and a working arrangement | Forego the proposed additional capability |

These are independent choices, not compulsory steps in a centralization ladder. Neither foundation must abandon its useful local practices for the Union to mature.

### 7A. Adoption Lifecycle and Decision Gate

1. **Identify** the actual problem and supported source practice.
2. **Compare** preserving specialization, extending, adapting, coordinating, recovering, or deferring.
3. **Validate** authority, evidence, host feasibility, donor exposure, inputs, and maintenance.
4. **Commit once** through the producing owners.
5. **Introduce and practice** at the receiving host.
6. **Confirm local realization** before presenting a usable capability.
7. **Maintain, narrow, suspend, reverse, recover, or retire** according to actual state.

```yaml
  - CanonicalPracticeAndProgramIdentity

  - MaintenanceAndRecoveryPath
  - NoDuplicateProjectCostOrBenefit
proposal_or_funding_equals_realization: false
realization_equals_universal_unlock: false
```

This is a design contract, not a new mandatory runtime pipeline or modal for ordinary development actions.

## 8. Transfer Without Exhausting the Donor

Distinguish copying a usable record, teaching a practice, temporarily seconding specialists, permanently transferring personnel, and moving equipment. These are not interchangeable costs.

A record can be copied without removing the original where supported, but a copy is not another trained workforce. Teaching consumes actual time and capacity. Seconded personnel cannot simultaneously provide full service at both hosts. Equipment moved to a receiving site is no longer available at its previous location unless actual quantities support both.

```yaml
donor_protection:
  preserve_actual_assignments: true
  disclose_service_loss_if_people_or_equipment_move: true
  same_specialist_full_time_at_two_hosts: false
  copied_record_duplicates_operating_capacity: false
  teaching_automatically_destroys_donor_knowledge: false
  temporary_secondment_equals_permanent_transfer: false
```

Exact personnel simulation is not required. Use the shared system's supported abstraction, while preserving meaningful capacity competition.

### 8A. Donor, Host, and Shared-Contributor Conservation

An adoption may involve one donor, several contributors, or a practice already shared by both foundations. Each person, facility, record, tool, material stock, transport service, and operating commitment is counted according to its actual use.

```yaml
conservation_rules:
  donor_service_and_host_introduction_use_same_full_capacity_twice: false
  one_shared_contributor_claimed_as_two_independent_native_benefits: false
  moved_equipment_remains_at_donor_without_actual_duplicate_stock: false
```

A working donor should not collapse merely because a record was copied. Conversely, a politically unified realm does not make scarce specialists simultaneously present everywhere.

## 9. Host Adaptation and Partial Realization

The receiving host needs relevant facilities, competence, materials, access, and lawful scope. A practice useful at one site may need adaptation elsewhere. Geography, available inputs, institutional purpose, and current conditions can matter; foundation identity alone is not a technical blocker.

Partial realization is valid when the producing owner can describe a useful bounded result. Do not present an incomplete service as fully delivered, and do not discard a useful limited result merely because the maximum proposal is not feasible.

## 11. Delivery, Maintenance and Recovery

Use the existing development-project lifecycle. Identify the need, authorize the supported scope, commit actual inputs once, introduce the practice, confirm local performance, and maintain the resulting service. These are design expectations, not an additional executable pipeline.

Separate a completed capital investment from an operating service. Maintenance failure affects the service actually missing inputs; it does not erase completed learning or impose a universal Union decay penalty.

```yaml
continuity_rules:
  completed_learning_survives_political_rename: true
  delivered_stages_survive_later_proposal_failure: true
  service_loss_erases_all_knowledge: false
  knowledge_survival_proves_service_operates: false
  recovery_requires_actual_missing_inputs: true
  cancellation_refunds_consumed_inputs_automatically: false
  retirement_rewrites_campaign_history: false
```

Recovery may restore records, access, facilities, training or operating resources as required. If evidence of a practice is lost, retain uncertainty rather than inventing perfect memory or assuming universal forgetting.

### 11A. Failure, Suspension, Reversal, Loss, and Recovery

| Disposition | Meaning | Preserved state |
|---|---|---|
| Blocked | A required input or authority is missing | Knowledge, evidence, and completed valid stages |
| Suspended | Operation or introduction pauses | Delivered work and current maintenance condition |
| Reversed | A prior change is deliberately withdrawn | Historical learning and actual rollback costs |
| Lost | Host capability no longer operates | Surviving records, people, and evidence where actual |
| Recovered | Missing operating requirements are restored | Prior history and intervening costs remain |
| Retired | Service is intentionally ended | Records and campaign history remain queryable |

```yaml
failure_does_not:
  - EraseAllLearningAutomatically
  - RefundConsumedInputsAutomatically
  - RestoreDonorCapacityWithoutActualRelease
  - DeleteAValidHostOrProgramRecord
recovery_does_not:
  - ReplayCompletionReward
  - CreateMissingPeopleFacilitiesOrMaterials
  - RestoreEveryRegionAtOnce
  - RewritePriorFailureOrLoss
```

A proposal failure leaves the current valid arrangement in place unless actual committed stages changed it. A technical error is not institutional rejection, and a political rejection is not proof that the practice is technically impossible.

## 12. Economy and Recruitment Handoffs

Economy remains the single owner of spending, reservations and material accounting. Existing project costs are not charged again by this panel. Distinct costs may exist for learning, facilities, introduction and operation only where the producing systems actually define them.

Military adoption can change eligibility or support a practice; it cannot create soldiers, horses, officers, equipment, crews or finite recruitment pools. Population informs sustainable capacity but is not directly spent as a universal army-manpower currency. A unit upgrade, retraining or conversion requires its own valid unit and recruitment rules.

## 13. Expressions, Rights and Healthy Continuity

Balanced, Barcelona-led and Aragon-led government may each preserve specialization, sponsor transfer, or coordinate complementary institutions. Seniority does not determine which foundation has useful knowledge. An Aragon-led government can extend a Barcelona-origin practice, and the reverse is equally eligible when actual conditions support it.

No expression change is required to adopt a practice. No adoption automatically changes seniority, law, culture, religion, controller or dynasty. A working local arrangement can remain unchanged without recurring loyalty payments, mandatory reform or artificial stagnation penalties.

## 14. Information and External Learning

Reports about another institution are evidence, not complete knowledge. Diplomatic contact does not guarantee teaching, access, comprehension or permission to use restricted material. Capturing a building does not automatically transfer all specialist competence.

Use current supported observer knowledge. Unknown foreign capabilities remain unknown. External assistance requires actual agreement and delivery through its owners. This file adds no agent roster, intelligence engine, or external private-state copy.

## 15. Player Presentation

The default view should show the purpose, source practice, receiving host, expected local result, main blocker, donor impact and major opportunity cost. Expandable detail can show evidence, delivered stages, operating needs and linked owners.

Use clear distinctions: known practice, proposed introduction, introduction underway, locally operating, limited, disrupted, or retained knowledge only. These are presentation concepts, not mandatory new meters.

Provide text equivalents for color and icons. Avoid repeated unchanged prompts. A healthy Union may have no special adoption decision. The panel must not imply that every native program is available everywhere or that all listed candidates already exist.

## 17. Persistence and Lifecycle

Preserve program identity, native provenance, actual hosts, completed stages, commitments, knowledge state and current operating capability. A repeated completion event grants no second benefit or debit. An ambiguous result requires authoritative-state reconciliation before retry, not speculative duplication.

Dissolution follows actual institutions, people, records, assets and valid rights through Lifecycle. It neither clones the same specialists into both successors nor automatically deletes knowledge already learned by both. Access to a former shared host can change without erasing a successor's retained understanding.

Later starts author their actual development state. Reformation consumes current surviving state, not the first Union's idealized snapshot. Historical, biological and ordered campaign-season tracks remain independent. Date alone completes no program or capability.
