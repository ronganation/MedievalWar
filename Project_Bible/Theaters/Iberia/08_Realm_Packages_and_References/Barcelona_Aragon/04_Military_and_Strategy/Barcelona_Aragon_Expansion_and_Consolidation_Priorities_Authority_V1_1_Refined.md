## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Win territory that improves the joined realm, then decide whether the next advance is worth the strength it takes away from holding the last one.**

This proposed portfolio compares continued expansion with consolidating gains, securing a connection, negotiating a supported settlement, reducing the objective or withdrawing. The reward for successful expansion remains real territorial control and useful surviving capability. Consolidation is not a compulsory tax paid after every conquest.

The Union remains one directly controlled faction. This authority introduces no overextension currency, province integration meter, new occupation menu, scripted conquest itinerary or third constitutional foundation. A land-focused or maritime strategy can succeed in every Union expression.

## 2. Evidence and Gap Qualification

The canonical Project Bible result for Public Order, Compliance, Resistance, Occupation, and Recovery V1.1 was inspected through search and find passages, especially sections 10–15 and the event-safety and acceptance material. The complete document was not reviewed in full in this pass.

That authority already distinguishes capitulation, occupation, settlement and annexation, preserves local conditions and defines occupation-policy questions. Those are not missing systems. The proposed extension is a campaign-level comparison across holdings and fronts: whether another acquisition strengthens the current strategy more than securing or relinquishing existing commitments.

This is a candidate design gap inferred from reviewed coverage, not a certified repository-wide absence. Shared strategic AI, native Barcelona and Aragon expansion doctrine, and all adjacent authorities remain incompletely reconciled. Project Bible 2.4 and Checklist 3.7 are named project baselines, not freshly audited compliance claims. ## 4. What Makes a Gain Useful

A gain can protect an actual approach, connect supported holdings, deny a consequential enemy position, provide a viable base, recover an existing source or improve a supported economic function. These are potential purposes, not guaranteed benefits of every settlement.

Compare actual access, surviving infrastructure, defensive position, current obligations and the opponent's actor-visible options. A rich-looking holding can be strategically expensive; a modest position can be useful if it protects a vital connection. Do not invent revenue, recruitment, route closure or naval basing from a map label.

No exact settlement ranking, fixed value formula or mandatory target list is assigned. The same holding can have different value after a route is lost, an agreement changes or another front becomes urgent.

## 5. Advance, Consolidate or Stop

| Choice | Intended benefit | Principal opportunity cost |
|---|---|---|
| Continue the advance | Exploit a supported opportunity | Less reserve, recovery and home protection |
| Secure the latest gain | Preserve useful control | Slower pursuit elsewhere |
| Restore a critical connection | Make existing holdings more usable | Force and investment tied to access |
| Relieve a threatened position | Preserve a consequential commitment | Another operation may be delayed |
| Negotiate a bounded settlement | Convert a feasible outcome into supported terms | Counterparty agency and performance |
| Reduce the objective | Retain a smaller useful position | Forgone reach or reward |
| Withdraw from an unsustainable commitment | Preserve surviving strength | Actual territorial and political consequences |
| Maintain the current arrangement | Avoid unnecessary churn | Existing costs and risks persist |

Offer only actions admitted by controlling systems. No new settlement sale, protectorate, client faction, colonization or population-transfer mechanic is created. Consolidation need not mean peace, and further advance need not mean neglect.

## 6. Admission and Decision Budget

A review is useful when another operation would materially compete with holding current gains, a connection changes, a major relief demand appears, or actual costs invalidate the existing plan. Routine movement and every settlement capture do not require a special council.

The compact question is: what useful purpose does the next commitment serve, what current purpose loses support, and what feasible alternative exists? The player may keep a working plan without reopening every previous acquisition.

Unknown minor details do not block unrelated action. Material uncertainty should qualify the comparison rather than silently becoming favorable certainty. No universal conquest cooldown or forced pause is assigned.

## 8. Holding Gains Without Permanent Occupation Chores

A healthy acquired holding can function under existing campaign rules. Do not invent damage, resistance or a mandatory integration purchase merely to activate this portfolio.

Where a material problem exists, distinguish security, applicable rights, service capacity, damage and recruitment eligibility. A garrison can help security without repairing a workshop or resolving a disputed obligation. An accepted settlement can coexist with incomplete recovery.

Consolidation is complete enough for the chosen purpose when the relevant owners confirm the needed current conditions. It does not require perfect public order, every building repaired, all claims extinguished or every recruitment pool full. Ordinary local management continues after the strategic review closes.

## 9. Cross-Foundation Priorities

A new land commitment can draw resources from coastal protection; a maritime opportunity can delay inland relief. Consequences follow actual assignments and resources, not a rule that Barcelona always chooses commerce or Aragon always chooses warfare.

Balanced Union does not require equal conquests, equal spending or equal losses. Either dominant expression may choose land or sea operations. Native territory, a new holding and an external agreement retain their different legal and material contexts.

New conquests do not automatically become a third foundation with a constitutional veto. Their supported rights and obligations still matter. Territorial expansion never creates a new native culture or unrestricted access to all Union unit cards.

## 10. Connections, Enclaves and Exposed Positions

Shared faction color does not prove a usable connection. Compare actual movement, permissions, support and alternate routes. A disconnected holding can remain useful under supported access; a connected one can still be difficult to defend.

Holding a fortress does not automatically close every nearby approach. Holding a port does not provide ships, transport or basing permission elsewhere. A fleet or army already committed abroad cannot simultaneously be a full home reserve.

A newly lost connection requires affected-plan reassessment, not automatic deletion of field survivors or instant loss of every holding. Existing movement, military and lifecycle owners resolve actual consequences.

## 11. Negotiated Alternatives and Withdrawal

A supported agreement may narrow an objective or change a commitment. A proposal is not acceptance; acceptance is not completed transfer or safe passage. Diplomacy owns counterpart agency and valid terms.

Withdrawal preserves actual force location, condition, embarked state, cargo and outstanding obligations. No teleportation, automatic refund, erased occupation history or restored recruitment capacity follows the decision. A position is not transferred merely because the player stops prioritizing it.

If the campaign does not support negotiated disposal or transfer, do not display it as an executable option. The player can still compare supported movement, defense and future investment choices without inventing a diplomatic mechanism.

## 12. Recruitment, Revenue and Recovery

Only actual controlled and eligible sources contribute under their owners. Claims, recognition and occupation labels create no people, equipment, horses, officers, crews or recruitment pools.

Regional recruitment remains canonically Theater-owned and accessed through valid relationships. Do not copy regional profiles into the Union or assume a new province supports native Barcelona or Aragon formations.

Revenue and source recovery follow current condition and access. Paying for recovery is not completed capacity. Losing a source can impair future recruitment while surviving field units retain actual identity and condition. Expansion does not refill depleted pools or erase inherited debt.

## 13. Execution and Closeout

Record the chosen purpose and affected existing plan references, then execute through producing owners. The review itself moves no army, transfers no territory and debits no funds.

Revalidate material changes before affected execution. A battle result, failed relief, changed agreement or new threat may justify narrowing or replacing a plan. Preserve completed stages, consumed resources and intervening events.

Close the strategic review when its decision and handoffs are resolved. Construction, recovery, movement or settlement performance may continue normally. Repeated review, notification or result events create no duplicate reward, reservation, debit or control transfer. An ambiguous execution result is reconciled with the producing owner before retry.

## 14. AI and Player Presentation

AI compares feasible strategic benefit, supported threats, current capacity, home exposure and alternatives. It need not always expand, always consolidate or always favor its original foundation. It receives no Union-specific free resources or hidden information.

Present the current purpose, gain at stake, main blocker, displaced commitment and feasible alternative. Separate actual condition from potential benefit and known threat from uncertainty. Expandable detail explains local causes without turning the main view into a province spreadsheet.

Use text equivalents for icons and color, group unchanged notices and preserve quiet healthy-state play. Exact AI weights, numerical thresholds and decision cooldowns remain deferred.

## 15. Victory, History, Lifecycle and Time

Territorial success can supply evidence for a bound objective or victory route only under its controlling eligibility rules. One capture is not automatic campaign victory. Historical achievement can persist while present control changes.

Formation, succession and expression changes preserve current control, damage, assignments, obligations and plans unless affected owners validly change them. Dissolution dispositions current holdings and commitments once; reformation uses surviving state. Later starts author their actual territorial situation rather than replaying earlier conquest.

Historical +21 months, biological +7 months and one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step per strategic turn remain independent. Date alone creates no acquisition, settlement, recovery or withdrawal. Reloading does not reroll the same controlled assessment or duplicate results.
