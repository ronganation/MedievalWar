## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Turn Barcelona and Aragon's surviving forces into one usable higher-era military without flattening every contingent into a generic Union army.**

Barcelona-Aragon is a formed political and military entity, not an alliance interface and not two armies controlled through a permanent partner AI. Once the Union forms, the player controls the eligible transferred forces directly. Those forces retain their actual units, commanders, locations, casualties, experience, equipment, obligations, recruitment sources, and recovery states.

The military identity of the Union comes from coordination:

- Barcelona-derived civic, household, regional, commercial, coastal, and other supported military contributions;
- Aragon-derived household, frontier, regional, mounted, castle, and other supported military contributions;
- genuine shared Union institutions and newly justified higher-era developments;
- theater-owned regional, mercenary, and military-order forces that remain externally owned systems.

The Union does not receive two full military systems added together without cost. It gains broader options and harder allocation choices.

### 9A. Assembly, Concentration, Deployment, and Release Loop

1. **Select purpose** from an actual campaign need.
2. **Identify eligible sources and existing forces** without duplicating capacity.
3. **Reserve** only the required contribution.
4. **Assemble** through actual locations, routes, officers, and access.
5. **Validate readiness** before deployment.
6. **Deploy and sustain** the force under one primary assignment.
7. **Reassess** after battle, access loss, objective change, or material depletion.
8. **Release, recover, reassign, or disband** through the owning systems.

## 12. Field Armies, Garrisons, and Reserves

Field army, garrison, local defense contribution, and recoverable reserve are distinct.

| Force status | Primary purpose | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Field army | Operational movement and battle | Available as full garrison elsewhere |
| Garrison | Supported settlement or strategic-point defense | A free expeditionary army |
| Local defense contribution | Bounded defense under admitted rules | Permanent standing force |
| Reserve or pool capacity | Potential future formation | Existing deployed unit |
| Recovering force | Restoration under actual rules | Immediately available reserve |

Garrison reassignment must preserve actual local exposure, movement, service, and public-order consequences. The same strength is not counted in both field and settlement defense totals.

## 18. Sieges, Relief, Isolation, and Withdrawal

A relief operation requires an actual force, route, timing, and commitment. Withdrawal is a valid strategic choice when it preserves capability or prevents unsustainable losses. It does not erase casualties, consumed movement, abandoned objectives, or political consequences.

## 22. Saves, Later Starts, Dissolution, and Reformation

Advanced saves preserve current forces, commanders, source conditions, contracts, locations, access, assignments, casualties, and recovery. Opening defaults never overwrite a live campaign.

Later starts author their own actual military state. The presence of a mature Union does not prove that every earlier force survived or that every roster option is currently available.

Dissolution routes each actual force once through Lifecycle, Character, Economy, roster, contract, and regional owners. Reformation consumes current surviving state and grants no second army, restored casualty total, or renewed contract.
