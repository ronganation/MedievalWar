## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Finish the war without losing the peace, exhausting both foundations, or pretending the army has already returned home.**

This Union portfolio compares retaining a deterrent, redeploying to another live threat, recovering depleted formations, releasing eligible service, and redirecting resources toward damaged civilian and productive functions. The end of one conflict is a strategic opportunity, not a free reset or compulsory demobilization ceremony.

A functioning peace requires no recurring review. Peace with one counterpart is not universal peace. This authority creates no second diplomacy engine, army registry, peace currency, recruitment pool, or recovery simulation.

## 2. Evidence and Genuine-Gap Qualification

The refined External Recognition, Diplomacy, Claims, and Treaty Succession authority already preserves active wars, truces, installments, return obligations, access, debt, custody, and claim-settlement boundaries. This authority does not replace those rules.

The bounded addition is the joined realm's postwar allocation decision: retain, redeploy, recover, or release actual commitments while completing accepted terms. Military Command, Economy, Maritime Defense, Fortress Network, Civilian Provisioning, Public Order, Campaign AI, and Lifecycle retain execution.

Repository-wide uniqueness, native Barcelona and Aragon reconciliation, historical validation, and runtime validation are not certified. No historical treaty, army, office, payment, duration, discharge rate, recovery rate, or numerical modifier is assigned.

## 4. Entry and Scope

A review is justified when an accepted peace, truce, completed limited operation, or materially changed threat makes current assignments worth reconsidering. No realm-wide review follows every battle.

Identify the affected conflict, parties, effective agreement, remaining wars, known threats, deployed forces, current access, and outstanding terms. A proposed peace is not accepted peace. Accepted terms are not completed performance. A ceasefire, truce, and permanent settlement retain their actual supported meanings.

Formation during a transition preserves current state. It does not replay peace, refresh a truce, forgive arrears, or release forces automatically.

## 5. Five Distinct Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Hostilities end in scope | One conflict changes under owner rules | Every war ends |
| Settlement is performed | Specified terms are fulfilled | Forces returned or recovered |
| Force is released | Assignment or service ends validly | Instant disbandment or free manpower |
| Capacity recovers | A producing owner restores a function | Full formation and provider pools simultaneously |
| Strategy changes | A new feasible priority is selected | Costs and losses are refunded |

These are distinctions, not new compulsory meters.

## 6. Postwar Choice Portfolio

| Choice | Strategic purpose | Principal tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Retain a field force | Deter a supported threat or meet another war | Upkeep and slower release |
| Redeploy | Address another active front | Movement, access, and home exposure |
| Recover in place or return | Preserve surviving experienced capability | Reduced immediate reach |
| Release eligible temporary service | End a supported burden | Lower immediate strength |
| End or renew contracted service | Match service to need and terms | Cost and availability |
| Reassign to garrison or reserve | Protect a holding or reaction capacity | Not also a full field assignment |
| Reprioritize civilian recovery | Restore a consequential function | Less immediate military investment |
| Maintain current arrangements | Avoid unnecessary churn | Existing cost and exposure continue |

No force must be disbanded merely because a treaty was signed.

## 7. Settlement Performance and Residual Obligations

Preserve the canonical terms version, parties, covered objects, completion state, and producing owners. Payments, transfers, returns, releases, and access changes occur once through existing systems.

Completed installments are not charged again. Pending obligations are not erased by expression changes or new strategic plans. A technical failure is not an in-world breach. Ambiguous results are reconciled with the producing owner before retry.

Partial performance remains partial. One completed term does not complete the entire settlement, and one unrelated dispute does not erase valid completed performance.

## 8. Return, Access, and Deployed Forces

Forces abroad retain location, casualties, equipment, movement, and service terms. Peace can change access without creating a safe route home. Use actual withdrawal, negotiation, rerouting, or violation rules. Never teleport or destroy a force by assumption.

An army in transit is not already available at its destination. Embarked forces retain carrier and custody relationships until actual disembarkation. Ending a blockade does not deliver cargo or repair a port. Ending a siege does not restore its garrison.

## 9. Demobilization Is Not a Recruitment Refund

Separate assignment release, discharge, disbandment, merge, retraining, and provider recovery. Each remains with its controlling owner.

Returning or disbanding a formation does not automatically refill a source pool, restore horses, refund recruitment cost, or convert all equipment into usable stock. Surviving people and assets cannot appear both in a retained formation and a released-capacity summary.

Population informs sustainable recruitment capacity but is not spent or refunded as direct army manpower.

### 9A. Retain, Redeploy, Release, Disband, Merge, and Recovery Disposition

| Disposition | Immediate meaning | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Retain | Preserve the current eligible force and assignment | Permanent readiness or free upkeep |
| Redeploy | Change assignment through actual movement | Arrival or instant home defense |
| Release assignment | End one supported commitment | Discharge, disbandment, or provider recovery |
| Discharge service | End eligible service under owner rules | Full population or equipment refund |
| Disband | Dispose of one formation through its owner | Erasure of survivors or full source refill |
| Merge | Combine supported surviving formations | Restoration of losses, equipment, or provider capacity |
| Recover | Restore an admitted capability over time | New people, horses, equipment, or funds |

## 10. Retained Deterrence and Other Wars

Compare known threats, continuing wars, treaty scope, actual garrisons, force condition, response access, and budget. Retaining an army can be correct. Reducing an overextended commitment can also be correct.

Balanced government does not require equal demobilization. Barcelona-led and Aragon-led expressions may each prioritize any supported threat. Native origin determines neither reliability nor entitlement to release.

A force committed to another war cannot also count as fully recovered reserve. A deterrent label grants no guaranteed peace or invisible combat bonus.

## 11. Source Recovery and Civilian Reinvestment

Distinguish replenishing a surviving formation from restoring the provider's future capacity. One may improve while the other remains depleted.

A reduced military commitment makes resources available only through producing-owner disposition. It does not instantly repair settlements, workshops, roads, ports, livelihoods, or recruitment sources.

No generic peace dividend, automatic prosperity reward, compulsory recovery tax, or fabricated damage is introduced.

## 12. Prisoners, Captures, and Custody

Settlement terms involving prisoners, hostages, captured vessels, equipment, or property use canonical records and existing owners. No named person or new captive policy is created.

A promise of release is not completed transfer. A captured vessel is not automatically a repaired and crewed Union asset. A return obligation cannot duplicate a person or item at both ends.

No universal ransom, exchange rate, prize entitlement, or imported modern legal doctrine is established.

## 13. Operating Loop and Closeout

1. Identify the conflict scope that changed.
2. Identify remaining wars, threats, forces abroad, and pending terms.
3. Compare retention, redeployment, recovery, release, and reinvestment.
4. Execute selected actions through existing owners.
5. Reassess after committed results.
6. Close each declared purpose independently.

Settlement performance, return movement, force recovery, and civilian restoration may finish at different times. Repeated closeout creates no second refund, reward, or release.

### 13A. Strategic Reset, Budget Release, and Residual Commitment Closeout

A strategic reset chooses the next bounded priority after current owners commit their results. It is not a refund event or a clean-slate campaign restart.

```yaml
reset_closeout_requires:

  - ActualForceLocationsConditionsAndAssignments
  - ValidlyReleasedFundsCapacityAndReservations

  - ChosenPriorityOrExplicitMaintainCurrentPlan
  - ReassessmentAndExitCondition
reset_does_not:
  - RefundConsumedCostsOrLosses
  - RefreshMovementReadinessOrRecruitmentPools
  - CancelUnrelatedValidObligations
  - CompleteRecoveryOrCivilianProjects
  - CreateNewObjectiveRewardOrVictory
partial_closeout_may_be_valid: true
```

Residual obligations continue through their owners. The postwar review can close once its decisions and handoffs are recorded, even while ordinary movement, payment, repair, or recovery continues.

## 14. Information, AI, and Player Presentation

AI compares continued campaigning, settlement, deterrence, redeployment, recovery, and release using actor-visible information and actual capacity. Counterparty acceptance is not guaranteed. No hidden information or free replacement force is granted.

Show what ended, what remains at war, pending material terms, forces still abroad, the principal budget or recovery blocker, and feasible alternatives. Group unchanged notices and expose material uncertainty.

A quiet mature peace is valid. No compulsory betrayal, rebellion, claimant, or new war is generated for activity.

## 15. Victory, Lifecycle, and Time

Peace or withdrawal may satisfy a bounded objective purpose but is not automatic short or long victory. Scenario-owned victory rules remain controlling.

Succession revalidates only affected terms. Dissolution routes obligations, forces, assets, and projects once through Lifecycle. Reformation consumes surviving current state. Later starts author their actual postwar condition.

Historical +21 months, biological +7 months, and one ordered campaign-season step per strategic turn remain independent. Date alone completes no return, discharge, payment, or recovery.
