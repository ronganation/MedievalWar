## 1. Purpose and Distinctive Player Promise

**Barcelona-Aragon converts two complementary strategic foundations into reach only when the player can keep the connection between them usable, fund the commitment, and protect what remains behind.**

This authority turns strategic geography into campaign decisions rather than passive bonuses. Barcelona contributes the stronger orientation toward civic concentration, commercial access, ports, and wider maritime opportunity. Aragon contributes the stronger orientation toward inland approaches, frontier defense, land concentration, and protected movement through difficult strategic space. The Union does not receive both portfolios for free. It receives the political opportunity to coordinate them.

The player-facing loop is:

1. identify a consequential connection, port function, access problem, or external opportunity;
2. determine whether the required route, permission, assets, and source capacity actually exist;
3. choose a bounded commitment;
4. accept the home-defense and competing-plan cost;
5. sustain, redirect, settle, withdraw, or recover without duplicating capacity.

A strong Union can become a connected regional power with selective expeditionary reach. It is not required to become an overseas empire, follow a scripted conquest sequence, or treat every port as a fleet base.

## 4. Strategic Connection Doctrine

A connection is a supported relationship among actual endpoints for a defined military, economic, political, or administrative purpose. Shared faction color does not prove a usable connection.


Several plans may reference the same geography, but they may not duplicate its capacity. Commercial, military, and administrative uses can share a corridor while retaining different permissions, costs, and disruption states.

## 5. Dual-Foundation Strategic Portfolio

### 5.1 Barcelona-oriented portfolio

Barcelona-oriented development may weight:

- concentration around supported civic and commercial infrastructure;
- protection of usable port and market access;
- negotiated trade and maritime permissions;
- embarkation, repair, or basing opportunities where actual systems support them;
- selective external operations whose return justifies the home commitment.

This weighting does not create ships, crews, cargo, port capacity, or military basing rights.

### 5.2 Aragon-oriented portfolio

Aragon-oriented development may weight:

- inland concentration and frontier relief;
- protection of supported passes, crossings, strongpoints, and valley approaches;
- sustainable land campaigning under finite recruitment and recovery;
- alternate access when a direct approach is politically or physically impaired;
- prevention of overextension while field forces are committed elsewhere.

This weighting does not prove that every pass is blocked, every strongpoint controls a region, or every road has the same capacity.

### 5.3 Union synergy and friction

The Union's distinctive advantage is coordination, not duplication. A viable inland concentration can protect a portward strategy. A usable maritime connection can reduce pressure on a land approach. Conversely, one foundation can consume resources needed by the other, and an expedition can expose the connection that made it possible.

```yaml
  - OneConservedSetOfAssetsAndSources
  - CompatiblePermissionsAndTiming
  - VisibleOpportunityCost
```

## 6. Land Connection Portfolio

| Purpose | Player decision | Required evidence | Principal failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connect complementary sources | Secure or restore a usable cross-foundation approach | Actual endpoints, movement support, access, source condition | Treating political union as physical delivery |
| Protect a threatened center | Hold or relieve a consequential approach | Map relationship, garrison, relief path, force availability | Assuming nearby ownership closes an entire region |
| Support field concentration | Sequence forces and support through a viable corridor | Current movement, supply abstractions, commitments | Assigning the same force to incompatible plans |
| Restore an impaired link | Repair, relieve, negotiate, or reroute | Known cause, owning-system repair or access action | Treating an order as completed recovery |
| Avoid a costly chokepoint | Use a real alternative or accept delay | Alternative topology and permission | Inventing an escape route to preserve an objective |

A castle, crossing, road, river, or pass matters only when controlling systems establish the relationship. No universal zone closure or terrain multiplier is created here.

## 9. Maritime Movement and Admission States

Maritime opportunity becomes actionable only through admitted campaign and naval systems.

| State | Meaning | Player presentation |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported | No controlling system or destination binding supports the operation | Context only, no executable order |
| Supported but inaccessible | System exists, but permission or connection is absent | Show the specific access blocker |
| Accessible but unprepared | Route exists, but ships, crews, force, budget, or support are missing | Show missing inputs and alternatives |
| Prepared but committed elsewhere | Required capacity exists but is reserved for another plan | Require reprioritization, not duplication |
| Ready | Target, access, assets, support, home-defense tolerance, and exit condition are credible | Offer bounded operation |
| Impaired during operation | A material condition changed after commitment | Reassess, reinforce, settle, reroute, or withdraw |

Every external commitment must show what remains unavailable at home. The same army, treasury commitment, transport capacity, repair slot, recruitment source, or diplomatic permission cannot support incompatible plans simultaneously.

## 12. Operation Portfolio

| Operation family | Strategic payoff | Core requirement | Opportunity cost | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Secure the Union connection | Preserve mutual use of actual centers and sources | Supported route and force | Assets unavailable elsewhere | Connection secure or strategy changes |
| Restore port function | Recover a real commercial or operational option | Owning-system repair and inputs | Competes with recruitment and defense | Function restored or project abandoned |
| Obtain limited access | Open one specific viable use | Counterpart acceptance and scoped agreement | Terms and obligations | Expiry, fulfillment, revocation, or renegotiation |
| Concentrate for limited land objective | Use complementary forces decisively | Viable approach and home-defense tolerance | Frontier or local exposure | Objective, peace, withdrawal, or reassessment |
| Prepare maritime expedition | Reach supported external opportunity | Admitted transport, access, support, exit | High asset and home-defense commitment | Objective, settlement, relief, or withdrawal |
| Relieve exposed force | Preserve deployed capability | Reachable force and route | May abandon another objective | Relief, merger, evacuation, or negotiated exit |
| Reroute a disrupted plan | Preserve purpose without fictional access | Actual alternative | Delay, cost, political obligation | Alternative stabilized or plan canceled |

These are strategic families, not additional political paths.

## 13. Access, Recognition, and Third Parties

Movement, trade, basing, repair, construction, and recruitment permissions must remain distinct. Recognition of the Union does not establish military access. Refusal to recognize a title does not automatically cancel every compatible commercial arrangement.

If access changes after commitment, the owning systems must expose supported alternatives. No army teleports home, receives indefinite passage, or disappears because an agreement expired.

## 14. Disruption, Isolation, and Recovery

A disruption affects only the functions that actually depend on the impaired connection.

```yaml
disruption_assessment:
  - AffectedFunctionAndScope
  - KnownCauseAndEvidence
  - RemainingAlternativeAccess
  - DeployedAndReservedCommitments

repair_order_is_completed_recovery: false
```

Recovery preserves actual time, cost, damage, losses, and delivered work. It does not reset the affected system.

## 15. Investment and Capacity Conservation

Connection and port projects use Economy and the relevant producing owner. They cannot create population, trained crews, horses, officers, ships, or recruitment pools by declaration.

A project benefiting both foundations receives one conserved effect unless controlling owners establish distinct services. Canceling a project releases only what remains recoverable. Completed work, consumed materials, damage, and deployed assets persist.

## 16. Union Expressions and Strategic Emphasis

| Expression | Primary weighting | Required restraint |
|---|---|---|
| Balanced Union | Reciprocal defense, connection resilience, negotiated allocation | No forced equal quotas or duplicated capacity |
| Barcelona-dominant Union | Civic concentration, port use, commercial access, selective wider reach | No automatic fleet, income, or military basing |
| Aragon-dominant Union | Inland defense, frontier concentration, protected land approaches | No prohibition on viable maritime action |

Expressions guide priorities. They do not grant exclusive actions, reset commitments, open blocked routes, or create assets.

## 17. Gains, Occupation, Return, and Dissolution

Capturing a port does not instantly provide loyal crews, repaired ships, native recruitment, functioning commerce, or recognized basing. Claims do not count as control or revenue. New territory remains under occupation, settlement, regional recruitment, diplomacy, and lifecycle owners.

Returning forces retain actual losses, condition, service obligations, and recovery needs. Manual battle and autoresolve must preserve the same campaign conservation rules.

On dissolution, lifecycle disposition must address current control, facilities, ships, transport, deployed forces, projects, agreements, and obligations without duplication. This authority supplies the questions but does not execute inheritance.

## 19. Player Interface and Attention Contract

The strategic panel or equivalent presentation should show:

- selected purpose and actual target or connection;
- present readiness state;
- dominant blocker;
- assets and sources committed;
- major home-defense exposure;
- competing plan using the same capacity;
- supported alternatives;
- reassessment or stop condition.

Use distinct language for **unsupported**, **inaccessible**, **unprepared**, **committed elsewhere**, **impaired**, and **recovering**. Do not label every disruption as disloyalty or every controlled port as ready.

No rendered UI, accessibility, localization, or command-center implementation is certified.

## 20. Time, Lifecycle, and Advanced Saves

The historical, biological, and campaign-season tracks remain independent. Date or season alone creates no fleet, holding, route closure, or expedition.

Later starts author their actual connections, permissions, assets, damage, projects, and commitments. Opening defaults do not overwrite current save state. Reloading does not reroll a strategic plan when controlled inputs are unchanged.

## 21. Worked Static Fixtures

| Case | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Union forms while no supported cross-foundation route exists | Political outcome is preserved; no fictional delivery or movement capacity appears |
| Port is controlled but ships are absent | No automatic fleet or expedition |
| Trade permission exists without military access | Commercial scope remains distinct |
| Military access exists without repair permission | Movement may be possible; repair is not granted |
| A functioning route has no current problem | No mandatory repair chore |
| One army is assigned to incompatible plans | Second assignment is blocked or first commitment is released through owner rules |
| Barcelona-dominant Union faces inland emergency | Inland defense may become the rational priority |
| Aragon-dominant Union has admitted maritime capacity | Maritime operation remains eligible |
| Claimed port is enemy-controlled | No revenue, basing, repair, or recruitment is granted |
| Access expires during deployment | Reassessment occurs without teleportation or indefinite passage |
| One service is disrupted | No automatic total regional shutdown |
| Maritime movement is unsupported | No executable maritime order appears |
| Expedition is reachable but lacks a credible exit | Readiness remains incomplete |
| Project is canceled after partial delivery | Actual completed work and spent cost persist |
| Expression changes during deployment | Assets, losses, permissions, and commitments persist |
| Later start contains usable connections | No replayed formation or invented crisis |
| Union dissolves while a fleet or army is deployed | Lifecycle owner resolves disposition once without duplication |

Fixtures are static design expectations, not executed tests.
