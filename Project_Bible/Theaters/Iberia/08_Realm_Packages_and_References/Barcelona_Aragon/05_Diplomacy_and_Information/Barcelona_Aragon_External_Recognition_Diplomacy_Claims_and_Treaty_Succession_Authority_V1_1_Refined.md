## 1. Strategic Verdict

**Barcelona-Aragon inherits a diplomatic position, not a clean slate and not automatic international approval.**

Recognized internal common rule forms one campaign faction. Each foreign counterpart can accept the new government, dispute a ruler, preserve an existing treaty, contest a claim, or oppose the Union for supported strategic reasons. These responses are separate questions. A hostile neighbor does not erase the faction's existence; a friendly neighbor does not surrender disputed territory by recognizing it.

The player should see meaningful choices: preserve a valuable agreement, renegotiate conflicting obligations, seek limited recognition, settle a claim, or accept the consequences of a deliberate breach. The design must not turn every formation into a mandatory tour of foreign courts.

## 2. Ownership and Specialist Boundary

| Subject | This authority | Existing owner |
|---|---|---|
| External recognition | Counterpart-specific scope and consequences | Emergence owns internal readiness |
| Treaty succession | Continuity, conflict and disposition categories | Formation owns the single inheritance transaction |
| Claims | Diplomatic acknowledgment and settlement effects | Character authority owns claim provenance and claimant validity |
| Wars | Conflict reconciliation and external obligations | Campaign systems own actual war execution |
| Payments and guarantees | Identify obligation and beneficiary | Economy owns debit, debt and payment accounting |
| Dissolution | Required diplomatic partition inputs | Lifecycle owns successor creation and partition |

This authority elaborates existing inheritance rules. It cannot cancel a controlling treaty rule merely because a new faction name appears. A conflict with an existing owner must be surfaced for bounded reconciliation, not silently resolved in favor of this file.

## 3. Six Recognition Questions

```yaml
recognition_dimensions:
  PoliticalExistence: CounterpartAcknowledgesACombinedGovernmentAsAnActor
  Government: CounterpartAcceptsTheCurrentCommonRuleArrangement
  RulerOrRegency: CounterpartAcceptsTheCurrentPersonOrRegencyExercisingAuthority
  TitlesAndClaims: CounterpartAcknowledgesSpecifiedRightsOrDisputes
  TreatySuccession: CounterpartAcceptsSpecifiedInheritedObligationsAndBenefits
  TerritorialSettlement: CounterpartAcceptsSpecifiedBordersOrControl
recognition_of_one_dimension_implies_all_others: false
```

These are design dimensions, not six compulsory meters. Present only distinctions relevant to the current relationship. A counterpart can trade with Barcelona-Aragon while reserving judgment on a disputed title. Recognition of a ruler does not require endorsement of every claim that ruler holds.

## 4. Counterpart Responses

| Response | Meaning | Gameplay boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledged | Specified aspect is accepted | No automatic alliance or land transfer |
| Conditional | Acceptance depends on explicit terms | Show condition, scope and responsible party |
| Reserved | Counterpart withholds judgment on a specified aspect | Not automatically hostility or treaty cancellation |
| Disputed | A specific claim, ruler or obligation is contested | Preserve valid existing arrangements outside the dispute |
| Hostile | Counterpart opposes the government or its conduct | War still follows the controlling war rules |
| Unknown | No supported response is available to the observer | Do not expose hidden AI intentions |

The response must name its subject. A generic 'unrecognized' label must not silently close all trade, invalidate all marriages or start a war.

## 5. Recognition Is Not a Universal Formation Veto

Internal recognition and formation readiness remain with the Emergence and Formation authorities. Foreign approval is not universally required.

```yaml
foreign_refusal_alone_blocks_formation: false
specific_binding_obligation_can_condition_settlement: true
binding_condition_requires:
  - ActualAgreementOrSupportedOverlordRelationship
  - ApplicablePartiesScopeAndTerms
  - CurrentValidityUnderControllingDiplomacyRules
  - ExplicitFormationConsequence
universal_papal_imperial_or_neighbor_approval_requirement: false
```

A supported overlord or guarantee relationship may matter to a particular settlement. It is not permission to invent a permanent veto for every foreign ruler or institution. If an obligation is knowingly breached, the player must see the applicable consequences; a design may permit breach where controlling systems allow it rather than pretending the obligation never existed.

## 6. Diplomatic Continuity Inventory

Before commitment, identify actual active agreements and outstanding obligations for both foundations. Keep original counterpart, parties, scope, beneficiaries, duration, arrears, conditions and breach history. Do not create treaties to fill a table.


The record is an interface to the Formation ledger, not another ledger that transfers assets again. Completed, expired and broken agreements retain history where relevant but must not become fresh active commitments.

Default to continuity where the controlling agreement supports it. Do not make every treaty require re-signing. Conversely, do not extend a limited agreement to the entire combined faction merely because the faction now has more territory.

## 8. Alliances, Guarantees and Mutual Defense

A continuing alliance preserves its actual defensive or offensive scope. Formation does not retroactively make every inherited war a mandatory allied war. Defensive and offensive commitments retain their actual trigger, party, scope, and validity; an offensive alliance does not automatically bind the new faction to a war already begun outside its terms. A guarantee preserves its named beneficiary and triggering conditions; it does not guarantee every new possession or future claimant.

If Barcelona guaranteed a realm that Aragon was fighting, flag the conflict before formation. Resolve through an accepted amendment, settlement, valid termination, or deliberate breach under the owning systems. No hidden priority rule may silently select the most advantageous obligation.

```yaml
conflicting_obligations:
  automatic_best_of_both_benefits: prohibited
  silent_cancellation_of_unfavorable_side: prohibited
  precommit_disclosure: required
  affected_scope_resolution: required
  unrelated_valid_agreements: Preserve
```

## 9. Inherited Wars and Internal Conflict

External wars do not vanish because the two foundations combine. Preserve participants, actual control, negotiated terms and relevant war history. If both foundations fight the same enemy, reconcile the relationship without duplicating war participation or rewards.

If Barcelona and Aragon are at war with one another, the accepted common-rule settlement must explicitly resolve that internal war before commitment. It must not leave the formed faction at war with itself or create a second peace reward for the same settlement.

Where one foundation is allied with the other's enemy, determine the actual scope of alliance obligations. If the proposed formation creates a contradiction, show the conflict and resolve the affected relationship. Do not manufacture a global war merely because a relation is complicated.

## 10. Truces, Nonaggression and Existing Peace Terms

A continuing truce retains its remaining duration and territorial or party scope. Formation must not reset a truce to full duration, erase its restrictions, or permit a prohibited attack through a renamed faction.

Peace installments, return obligations, hostages and guarantees retain their actual completion state. Completed payments are not charged again. Unpaid installments are not forgiven. A material amendment needs the agreement required by the controlling diplomacy rules.

```yaml
formation_resets_treaty_timers: false
formation_cleans_breach_history: false
formation_duplicates_peace_rewards: false
renaming_bypasses_nonaggression: false
```

## 12. Tribute, Debt and Financial Undertakings

Identify who owes what, to whom, and under which terms. The Economy Authority owns accounting. The diplomatic disposition preserves enforceability and beneficiary relationships without collecting a second debit.

A debt attached to a ruler, institution, territory or faction must not be casually reassigned across those categories. Where scope is unclear, preserve the disputed record and block unsupported benefits or forgiveness until the relevant owner resolves it.


## 13. Dynastic Agreements, Wards and Hostages

Canonical people remain with the Character Authority. Diplomatic agreements may depend on their identity, life state, custody, office or relationship, but cannot duplicate people or invent marriages and heirs.

Formation does not automatically release hostages, end wardship, change custody or complete an outstanding dynastic promise. Preserve actual terms and lawful or negotiated disposition under the project systems. If a participant dies or loses authority, revalidate the affected agreement rather than invalidating every diplomatic relationship.

No real historical person is assigned to these roles by this design document. Exact participants require their character and scenario evidence.

Barcelona-derived and Aragon-derived claims retain provenance. Common government can pursue or settle supported claims without erasing their origin. Two records referring to the same right are reconciled by identity and scope, not counted as two claims granting double leverage.

A third-party claimant is not automatically eliminated when Barcelona-Aragon forms. Conversely, foreign support does not create a valid claimant where the Character Authority has no supported basis.

Any transfer of actual territory remains with the controlling campaign transaction. A diplomatic promise of future delivery remains a promise until its conditions are met.

## 17. Foreign Response and AI Decision Logic

AI responses should use known interests: existing commitments, disputed borders, protection needs, credible threats, government stability, past conduct and the actual settlement. No counterpart is permanently scripted to approve or oppose the Union.

```yaml
AI_response_options:
  - PreserveExistingRelations
  - AcknowledgeSpecifiedAspects
  - ReserveJudgment
  - RequestSupportedClarificationOrRenegotiation
  - DisputeSpecifiedRights

  - OpposeThroughExistingDiplomaticAndWarRules

historical_future_knowledge: prohibited
exact_weights_and_cooldowns: Deferred
```

A refusal should identify a strategic or obligation-based reason where visible to the player. The AI need not disclose hidden plans. No automatic anti-Union coalition is created by this authority.

## 18. Player-Facing Diplomacy Summary

The formation offer should show only material changes: wars that continue, incompatible commitments, important access limits, significant debts and known recognition disputes. After formation, ordinary diplomacy should resume without compulsory repeated confirmation of compatible treaties.


The player can inspect detailed provenance when needed. Do not force a large legal ledger into the normal campaign turn. A healthy diplomatic transition is a valid outcome.

## 20. Unknown, Conflicting and Missing Evidence

If a required treaty or claim reference is missing, do not fabricate its contents. Distinguish a genuinely inactive relationship from an unresolved record.

```yaml
uncertainty_handling:
  immaterial_display_gap: PreserveKnownStateAndMarkUnknown
  material_obligation_or_conflict_gap: ResolveBeforeAffectedCommitOrBenefit
  contradictory_sources: SurfaceConflictAndReferToOwningAuthority
  absent_historical_binding: NoNamedScenarioAssignment
unknown_means_free_cancellation: false
unknown_means_permanent_global_block: false
```

The resolution must be proportional to the affected scope. An uncertain port privilege should not freeze every unrelated treaty, but it cannot grant access without support.

## 21. Dissolution, Restoration and Re-Formation Interface

On dissolution, the Lifecycle Authority must receive the current diplomatic inventory and proposed successor responsibility. Agreements do not automatically clone to both successors or revert to their original pre-Union terms.

Territorial agreements may follow actual territory; personal undertakings may follow the relevant person; faction-wide commitments require explicit partition disposition. These are scope principles, not automatic assignments overriding actual terms.

Re-formation uses the current diplomatic world, including later wars, debts and settlements. Restoration of a native faction does not revive expired alliances or erase valid intervening agreements.

## 22. Later Starts and Historical Evidence Boundary

A later-start scenario must supply its own dated diplomatic state. Do not replay emergence succession over a scenario already authored as formed, and do not import the Crown's later historical possessions as present claims or controlled land.

The user-provided Crown of Aragon overview remains background for composite rule only. This authority adds no new historical treaty claims, fixed foreign enemies, compulsory recognition ceremonies or named obligations. All detailed rules here are proposed project mechanics.

## 23. Worked Design Fixtures

| Case | Required design outcome |
|---|---|
| Both foundations have the same compatible trade agreement | Reconcile identity and preserve actual scope without duplicate benefit |
| Barcelona has a truce with Aragon's enemy | Expose affected conflict and resolve terms before inconsistent commitment |
| A neighbor recognizes the ruler but disputes a county | Preserve recognition and dispute as separate aspects |
| A port privilege covers one harbor | Do not extend it to the entire combined coastline |
| Both foundations owe money to one creditor | Preserve distinct valid obligations without double debit or unsupported netting |
| Barcelona and Aragon are at war with each other | Common-rule settlement explicitly ends the internal conflict once |
| A foreign ruler rejects the Union | No automatic deletion of the internally formed faction |
| A binding external obligation affects formation | Apply the actual supported condition, not a universal veto |
| An accepted proposal is reopened after commitment | Show current diplomacy; do not repeat transfer or rewards |
| A hostage remains abroad | Preserve actual custody and agreement terms |
| Government changes from balanced to Aragon seniority | Same faction and treaties; only supported material consequences change |
| Dissolution produces two successors | Explicit responsibility, no automatic treaty duplication |
| A claimant dies before commitment | Character owner resolves continuity; revalidate affected claim only |
| No material foreign dispute exists | Quiet continuity without invented crisis |

These fixtures are static design expectations. No simulation, battle, AI or runtime tests have been executed.
