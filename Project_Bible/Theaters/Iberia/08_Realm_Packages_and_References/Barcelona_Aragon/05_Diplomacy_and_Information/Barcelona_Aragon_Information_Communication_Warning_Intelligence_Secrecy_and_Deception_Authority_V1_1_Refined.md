## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**Combine two foundations' information without mistaking a larger realm for perfect knowledge.**

The formed Union should turn inherited coastal, inland, diplomatic, military and local reports into useful decisions. Its advantage is the opportunity to compare perspectives and coordinate responses. Its constraint is that reports can share one origin, arrive late, disagree, or never reach the decision-maker.

The player chooses where to seek clarification, which warnings justify a limited precaution, when to commit scarce forces, and what information must remain restricted. Routine campaign orders remain direct and familiar. This authority adds no permanent partner AI, compulsory messenger management, or permission prompt for every movement.

## 5. Distinct Information Layers

| Layer | Meaning | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | An admitted observer detected something | Complete understanding |
| Report | A communicated account | Truth or independent evidence |
| Assessment | Interpretation of available evidence | Confirmed world state |
| Warning | A consequential possibility requiring attention | An event already occurring |
| Confirmed condition | Owner-supported knowledge within a scope and time | Eternal or universal certainty |
| Decision | Chosen response to known risk | Execution or successful outcome |

```text
Message sent != message received
Authenticated sender != accurate content
Repeated report != independent corroboration
No report != no threat
Warning != confirmed event
Decision != completed military response
```

These distinctions are explanatory, not six compulsory meters.

## 6. Union Information Cycle

1. Identify the decision that needs information.
2. Use existing admitted observations and sources.
3. Deliver through supported communication paths.
4. Check origin, age, scope, contradictions and independence.
5. Assess plausible alternatives without inventing certainty.
6. Present a warning or summary proportionate to consequence.
7. Choose investigation, precaution, commitment, deferral or dismissal.
8. Reassess when material evidence or conditions change.

Use existing campaign and agent actions where adequate. This loop is not a new mandatory approval pipeline. A routine order need not wait for a perfect report; uncertainty is a strategic consideration, not a universal movement lock.

## 8. Confidence, Contradiction and Staleness

Assess source reliability, claim plausibility, directness, independence, age and relevant conditions separately where supported. A generally reliable source can be mistaken about one claim. An uncertain source can provide useful evidence that merits checking.

Contradictions may reflect different observation times, locations, interpretations, incomplete coverage or deception. Preserve alternatives until supported evidence resolves them. Do not average incompatible accounts into an invented exact enemy strength.

Age reduces usefulness according to the subject and shared rules. A report about a mobile force and a report about a fixed structure need not age identically. No exact expiry rate is assigned here. Refresh requires new evidence, not opening a panel or renaming the faction.

## 9. Communication and Message Authentication

A message has an actual sender, intended recipient, subject and delivery state where modeled. Existing systems determine whether a route, messenger, relay, port or other channel is available. This authority creates none of those assets.

Distinguish sent, received, delayed, missing, suspected alteration and confirmed compromise. Silence alone does not establish interception or betrayal. Authentication concerns the message's origin and integrity; it does not prove that the sender's account is correct.

A blocked route can delay new information without deleting previously received knowledge. Rerouting requires actual access and capacity. Translation and interpretation use supported competence rather than automatic perfect comprehension.

## 10. Dual-Foundation Coverage and Attention

The Union can compare reports from different actual operating areas. Coastal or port information may affect inland commitments; frontier warnings may change the forces available for maritime plans. These are dependencies, not fixed cultural assignments.

Balanced, Barcelona-led and Aragon-led government may each prioritize any supported threat. Seniority does not make one foundation's reports more truthful. Local evidence remains locally scoped until validly communicated or already available through shared campaign abstraction.

A single player controller retains the project's normal visibility and command conventions. This file does not impose hidden internal fog over all owned settlements or simulate every dispatch. Communication friction applies only where supported and consequential.

## 11. Warning and Response Portfolio

| Choice | Purpose | Opportunity cost or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Seek clarification | Resolve a consequential ambiguity | Uses actual observation or communication capacity |
| Cross-check another source | Test independence or contradiction | Another copy of the same origin is insufficient |
| Take a limited precaution | Protect against a plausible serious risk | Ties up real forces or resources |
| Commit to a response | Act when delay is more costly than uncertainty | Military and economic owners execute actual commitments |
| Maintain current plan | Avoid overreacting to weak evidence | Accept the disclosed residual risk |
| Defer and monitor | Preserve options pending material change | May lose response time |
| Correct or withdraw warning | Update recipients after new evidence | Preserve the earlier decision history |

A low-confidence warning can merit a reversible precaution when consequences are severe. High confidence does not create the force needed to respond. No universal numerical warning threshold is defined.

### 11A. Assessment Revision and Warning Lifecycle

## 13. Deception and Counterintelligence

Deception is a proposed game-level attempt to influence another actor's assessment through an admitted action. It does not rewrite authoritative world state or guarantee an enemy decision. A false report remains a claim in the recipient's knowledge state, not a real army, treaty or battle result.

Counterintelligence can seek corroboration, protect an existing channel, suspend reliance on a compromised source or reassess affected reports through existing systems. Suspicion is not proof. No automatic traitor, arrest, punishment or source removal follows from a conflicting message.

## 14. Sources, Capacity and Costs

Existing scouts, envoys, agents, institutions or reporting relationships remain with their canonical owners. No new named participant is allocated. A source cannot supply unlimited incompatible assignments, and an observation assignment may compete with another actual duty.

Economy owns real costs once. A report used by several authorities is not charged as several separate collections unless distinct work occurred. Source origin alone grants no bonus or penalty. Losing a source restricts future access but does not erase reports already received or prove that all prior reports were false.

## 16. AI Fairness and Player Explanation

AI decisions use their actor's supported knowledge, not the omniscient simulation state. Planning can account for uncertainty without receiving exact concealed forces or future events. Any difficulty exception belongs to an explicit shared authority; none is introduced here.

The default player view shows the consequential claim, relevant place, evidence age where known, confidence basis, contradiction, and feasible choices. Expanded detail can show origin relationships and authorized source information. Do not expose hidden truth through tooltips, reason codes, logs or confidence labels.

Use text equivalents for color and icons. Group repeated copies and unchanged warnings. Material corrections must reach affected decisions without requiring every old message to be reread. A quiet, functioning information system is a valid outcome; no artificial rumor or leak is required each turn.

## 17. Failure, Recovery and Campaign Lifecycle

Distinguish a lost message, inaccessible channel, compromised source, stale assessment and destroyed archive. Recovery targets the actual missing function. Repairing a route restores potential communication, not all missed observations. Restoring records does not automatically restore living sources or current knowledge.

Formation, expression change and ruler succession preserve received knowledge and current restrictions unless an affected owner validly changes them. Dissolution dispositions actual sources, records, access and messages once. Previously learned information is not arbitrarily forgotten, but future access is not guaranteed to both successors.

Later starts author actual observer knowledge. Reformation consumes current surviving state, not an ideal original network. The historical, biological and campaign-season tracks remain independent; no turn or date alone refreshes reports.
