## 1. Campaign Thesis: Turn a Union into a Power

**Win the wider realm. Make its combined strength work. Choose where that strength will matter.**

Barcelona-Aragon should feel like a substantial new campaign opportunity, not a political menu attached to the old factions. Formation can rescue a struggling player by bringing actual partner territory, forces and resources under direct control. The new realm then has to defend its enlarged commitments, sustain complementary military institutions and choose a credible direction of expansion or consolidation.

Its identity is coordinated breadth: native land-war, frontier, mounted, castle, civic, missile, household and route capabilities can support one strategy. Its weakness is concentration under competing demands. A player who commits every scarce source to one expedition may win a battle and leave the realm unable to replace losses or defend another frontier.

## 2. Strategic Identity and Player Freedom

The faction can become a durable regional power, a land-war coalition, a route-centered commercial and military realm, or a wider-reaching state when actual access and capacity permit. These are strategic directions, not additional political paths or exclusive technology trees.

The campaign should reward complementary use of institutions without forcing one approved army composition. Native forces can carry ordinary campaigns. Composite formations provide distinct capabilities where their own requirements are met; they are not compulsory purchases to prove that the Union exists.

## 5. Campaign Phases Are Situational, Not a Forced Ladder

| Planning phase | Typical purpose | Exit or reassessment basis |
|---|---|---|
| Establish common command | Make the inherited realm immediately usable | Actual control and material continuity are working |
| Secure the enlarged realm | Protect exposed centers, routes and sources | Threats become manageable or another need takes priority |
| Restore and connect | Repair consequential bottlenecks | Actual source and administrative capacity improves |
| Concentrate power | Prepare a defensible strategic operation | Forces, funds, access and support are adequate |
| Extend influence | Pursue a chosen territorial or diplomatic opportunity | Settlement, victory, changed risk or deliberate withdrawal |

These phases can overlap or be skipped where the inherited situation warrants it. A healthy mature Union need not replay formation chores. A threatened Union can defend first and postpone administrative improvement.

## 7. Immediate Security: Hold What the Union Actually Has

**Design title: Hold the Joined Realm.** Protect a materially threatened controlled center, muster source or route whose loss would impair the combined realm. Targets come from actual map and campaign state.

```yaml
security_objective:
  eligible_when: ActualThreatToIncludedTerritoryOrCriticalSupportedConnection
  success: ThreatResolvedOrReducedToSupportedManageableState
  valid_approaches: [Defense, Relief, NegotiatedSettlement, ThreatRemovalWhereValid]
  automatic_enemy_declaration: false
  requires_uncontrolled_historical_province: false
```

A defensive victory may satisfy the purpose without territorial conquest. A valid peace can also resolve a threat. The objective must distinguish temporary absence of an enemy from an actual durable resolution under the controlling campaign rules.

## 8. Consolidation: Make the Settlement Work

**Design title: Make Common Rule Endure.** Address a material inherited dispute or establish a functioning obligation arrangement where one is genuinely unresolved. Governance supplies the actual institutions, issue and response options.

A healthy Union can record this category as already satisfied or omit it. It must not invent a grievance to make the player earn basic settlement control.

```yaml
consolidation_objective:
  success_basis: ActualWorkingSettlementOrResolvedMaterialObligation

  ordinary_direct_control_locked_until_completion: false
```

The objective supports the faction's political identity while preserving Total War play. It does not require daily court management or universal agreement.

## 9. Recovery and Readiness: Rebuild a Useful Capability

**Design title: Restore the Host's Depth.** Repair an actual recruitment or recovery bottleneck that limits a chosen strategy. The target can be a valid mounted source, missile institution, household cadre, regional muster or another supported provider.


Completion must reflect actual recovery or readiness, not merely paying a fee. The material authority retains the distinction between restoring an existing formation and restoring future provider capacity. Exact useful-state thresholds remain deferred.

**Design title: Join the Realm's Strength.** Improve or secure an actual supported connection between important sources, settlements or operational areas. The route may be land or maritime only where the existing systems and actual assets support that form.

## 11. Diplomacy: Secure Room to Act

**Design title: Secure the Union's Position.** Resolve a real diplomatic vulnerability or obtain a valid agreement that supports the chosen strategy. Existing wars, access, recognition and external relationships remain actual campaign facts.

Possible supported outcomes include a peace, an access agreement, recognition, a defensive relationship or resolution of a conflicting inherited commitment. None is universally mandatory.

## 12. Optional Expansion: Choose the Next Horizon

**Design title: Choose the Next Horizon.** Pursue an attainable territorial, diplomatic or strategic opportunity once the realm can sustain it. The player can instead consolidate, defend or seek a different opportunity.

| Direction | Eligibility | Strategic choice |
|---|---|---|
| Land frontier | Actual threat, claim, opportunity or valid campaign basis | Concentrate field strength without stripping every local defense |
| Mountain and cross-regional connection | Supported geography and access | Secure movement and strongpoints rather than pursue empty map painting |
| Maritime reach | Actual ports, ships, crews, access and supported naval systems | Commit resources to a viable expedition rather than receive a free fleet |
| Diplomatic influence | Actual relationship and counterpart interest | Obtain useful position without compulsory annexation |

These are optional opportunity families, not four new faction paths. No named overseas holding, guaranteed fleet, compulsory religious war or fixed enemy list is created here. Specific historical opportunities require their own evidence and scenario binding.

## 18. AI Planning Loop and Commitment

This is a proposed design sequence, not an engine implementation recipe.

1. Assess known threats, actual controlled assets, finances, source capacity and valid political constraints.
2. Identify the most consequential problem or opportunity.
3. Compare feasible responses, including defense, recovery, diplomacy, limited war and postponement.
4. Choose a bounded plan with an actual target, force requirement, support basis and stopping condition.
5. Reserve or commit resources through the existing material rules.
6. Continue while the plan remains useful; reassess when a material change invalidates its assumptions.
7. Resolve gains, losses, commitments and recovery before treating capacity as available again.

```yaml
planning_rules:
  plan_has_target_and_exit_condition: true
  material_reassessment_triggers:
    - MajorTerritorialLossOrNewThreat
    - ChangedWarAccessOrTreatyState
    - SevereBudgetOrSourceFailure
    - SuccessionOrRecognitionCrisis
    - ObjectiveCompletionOrTargetInvalidation
  trivial_change_resets_entire_strategy: false
  repeated_plan_selection_duplicates_reservations: false
```

Persistence is not stubbornness. The AI should withdraw from an untenable operation rather than feed replacements into it indefinitely. Replanning must release or preserve actual commitments correctly, not create a fresh budget.

## 21. Operational Identity and Battle Handoff

The faction's design favors complementary forces: a dependable center, missile support, protected maneuver and a mounted role suited to the actual battle. Its shared roster can support relief, lane control, heavy mounted contest, decisive shock or harassment relay where those canonical profiles permit.

Operational planning should decide what the army needs to accomplish: hold, relieve, intercept, cover a withdrawal, conduct a supported assault or exploit a real opening. Tactical AI and unit authorities decide exact battle execution.

## 26. Worked Campaign Cases

### A. A struggling human forms a stronger Union

The player gains actual included partner territory and eligible forces. The first priority may be defending an inherited war or sustaining upkeep. No free composite army appears, and the human retains control even if the other foundation has seniority.

### B. Both foundations are weak but the settlement works

Balanced Union is valid. Objectives favor attainable security and recovery rather than an immediate distant conquest. The faction can improve its position without being treated as a failed empire.

### C. A healthy mature Union

No integration crisis is manufactured. The player can choose a supported expansion, diplomatic or development objective. The AI can concentrate on external strategy rather than endlessly repeating consolidation tasks.

### D. Barcelona-dominant government faces a land invasion

Immediate defense outranks optional wider reach. The government can use eligible native and composite land forces without changing expression. Seniority is not a prohibition on another strategic direction.

### E. Aragon-dominant government has a viable maritime opportunity

If actual ships, ports, access and support exist under admitted systems, the opportunity can be considered. Aragonese seniority does not prohibit it. Without those assets, the AI postpones it instead of receiving a fleet.

### F. Peace resolves the objective

A valid settlement removes the actual threat. The security objective can complete or be superseded according to its defined purpose. The player is not forced to invade simply because the original offer anticipated a battle.

### G. Target becomes invalid

A third party destroys or changes the target's relevant state. The objective is explicitly revised, withdrawn or failed according to its actual conditions. It is not silently redirected to an unrelated region.

### H. Scarce source competition

The AI needs both a native replacement and a composite formation using the same actual capacity. It chooses based on the current role gap and reserves the source once. It does not queue both at full strength.

### I. A succession changes recognition, not the faction

The AI reassesses affected political dependencies and risk. Existing armies, treasury and objective history continue. It does not rerun formation or collect completed rewards again.

### J. An offensive becomes unsustainable

Losses and access changes invalidate the plan. The AI can withdraw, negotiate or defend. It resolves existing commitments and recovery rather than repeatedly spawning replacements into the same failed operation.

### K. Hidden enemy force

The AI plans using supported intelligence and visibility. It does not receive exact hidden army or recruitment information from this authority. Discovery can trigger legitimate replanning.

### L. Player ignores an optional objective

Ordinary campaign control remains intact. The world responds through actual strategic consequences, not an invented punishment for declining guidance. Scenario victory rules remain separate.

These are proposed design cases, not executed fixtures or historical examples.
