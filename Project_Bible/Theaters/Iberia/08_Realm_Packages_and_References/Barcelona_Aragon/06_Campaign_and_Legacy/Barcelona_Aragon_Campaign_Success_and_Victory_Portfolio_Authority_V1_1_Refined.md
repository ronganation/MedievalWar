## 1. Purpose and Distinctive Player Promise

**Form the Union, make its strength endure, and decide what kind of power it becomes.**

Formation is a major campaign achievement, not automatic victory. This proposed portfolio turns the Union's broader territory, institutions and strategic choices into readable short and long campaign outcomes. The player should win through actual results, not an endless integration checklist or a compulsory recreation of a historical empire.

The short campaign asks whether common rule has become a viable strategic position. The long campaign asks whether that position supports a durable achievement beyond initial formation. Territorial strength, connected development and diplomatic position are alternative achievement routes, not new political outcomes or exclusive technology trees.

This is a static faction-specific design proposal. It creates no executable victory conditions, score currency, new resources or scenario activation.

## 4. Success, Milestone, Objective and Victory

| Term | Meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic success | A useful actual campaign result | A formal award |
| Objective completion | A mission's defined purpose is met | Campaign victory |
| Milestone | A recorded achievement at an eligible time | Current possession forever |
| Victory eligibility | A bound scenario permits evaluation | Conditions are satisfied |
| Victory confirmation | The scenario owner confirms the complete valid bundle | Free assets or automatic end of play |

Formation, a defensive victory, a repaired source and a signed treaty may each matter without winning the campaign. No generic Union-success meter is allocated. Existing campaign benefits remain valuable without an extra reward.

## 5. Short Campaign: Establish the Joined Realm

**Proposed player-facing title: A Realm That Can Stand.**

The proposed short-victory bundle combines a valid formed Union, a defensible current strategic position, and workable material support. Both foundations must remain substantively represented through the valid formation settlement and its current lifecycle; a renamed remnant does not pass merely because its label persists. Exact representation and viability predicates require scenario binding, not invented territory quotas.

| Component | Intended evidence | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Common rule exists | Actual continuing Union under a valid expression | Formation notification alone after dissolution |
| Joined position is defensible | Supported security outcome for the scenario's relevant holdings or connections | Universal peace or elimination of every enemy |
| Capacity is workable | Existing forces, budget and source support meet a bound useful-state test | Full treasury, full pools or every unit card |
| Material inherited obstacles are manageable | A defined consequential blocker is resolved, contained or validly settled where relevant | Manufacturing a grievance or forcing perfect local acceptance |

A healthy inherited situation can satisfy a component without replaying chores, under an explicit scenario recognition rule. An ongoing war or local problem need not invalidate success if the bound security and viability tests are met. Formation alone never grants the whole bundle.

## 6. Long Campaign: Make the Union a Durable Power

**Proposed player-facing title: The Joined Realm Endures.**

The long-victory proposal retains a current viable Union and requires a substantial supported achievement through at least one admitted route. It should demonstrate strategic depth beyond a momentary formation or one isolated mission. Exact scale and persistence remain scenario decisions.

The proposed logical relationship is a common viability core plus one fully satisfied route bundle. Do not silently require all routes, or combine fragments from several incomplete routes into an invented complete result. If the scenario supports hybrid achievement, its equivalence must be explicit and reviewed.

A recorded short victory can remain in history while current long-victory requirements are unmet. A later-start scenario may evaluate long success without replaying a short-victory ceremony when its binding permits that. The portfolio does not require a particular dynasty, capital, expression or later historical state name.

A hybrid route is not a loophole for adding several easy fragments. It must be intentionally defined and comparable to a complete admitted route. The common core cannot be satisfied twice or treated as part of several independent awards.

## 7. Alternative Achievement Routes

| Route | Distinctive result | Required reality check |
|---|---|---|
| Territorial and defensive power | Establish a meaningful controlled position and sustain its defense | Claims, temporary raids and ally holdings are not direct possession |
| Connected development | Make actual supported centers, sources or connections work together at a consequential scale | Construction spending, research labels and inaccessible stocks are not operating capability |
| Diplomatic position | Secure durable, useful agreements or recognition that materially support Union strategy | Unilateral declarations and unperformed promises are insufficient |

These routes describe proposed achievement categories, not calibrated victory predicates. Each needs a bound scale, evidence source and any persistence requirement before offering formal progress.

Maritime success can contribute to an admitted route where real ports, ships, access and operations exist. It is not compulsory, and no fleet or overseas holding is granted. A land-focused campaign remains valid. Native and composite forces can both contribute without an all-composite roster requirement.

## 8. Player Choice and Route Changes

Display available routes and their actual requirements before the player invests in them. A route choice is strategic guidance, not permission to issue ordinary orders or a new constitutional branch.

Where the scenario permits changing direction, preserve actual achievements and costs, then reevaluate the new route honestly. Do not refund spent resources, clone milestones or reset adverse world state. An unchosen optional route is not a failed objective.

A route becomes unavailable when its supported conditions genuinely cannot be bound or pursued. Explain that limitation; do not silently replace its target with an unrelated one. No compulsory historical enemy list or guaranteed diplomatic acceptance is introduced.

## 9. Evidence, Current Conditions and Persistence

Classify each bound requirement as a current-state condition, a historical milestone, or a sustained condition. This prevents one brief possession or expired agreement from being treated as permanent current strength.

| Requirement type | Evaluation principle | Example design category |
|---|---|---|
| Current state | Must be true at the admitted evaluation point | Current valid Union or actual control |
| Historical milestone | Preserve a valid eligible achievement once | A completed supported strategic undertaking |
| Sustained condition | Must satisfy an explicitly bound clock and interruption rule | Durable access or maintained strategic position |

These are categories, not assignments of exact targets. A scenario must specify when prior achievements count. Preformation history can be retained without automatically satisfying a Union-only achievement. Inherited mature capability can count where the scenario explicitly recognizes current state.

Unknown evidence remains unresolved, not false or complete. Actual evaluator truth and actor-visible explanation must remain separate so progress panels do not leak hidden enemy assets or intentions.

## 10. Timing and Evaluation Order

Every duration must name its clock. Historical progression is +21 months, biological progression is +7 months, and campaign season advances one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step per strategic turn. These tracks are independent; none substitutes for another.

A future scenario binding must define its evaluation point and event ordering. Victory cannot be confirmed from an inconsistent mixture of pre-battle control, post-battle forces and uncommitted treaty changes. Revalidate after relevant producing owners commit their results.

No exact hold duration, deadline or automatic timeout is assigned here. A date alone does not form the Union, repair capacity or fulfill a treaty. Any deadline must distinguish failure, expiry and target invalidation explicitly.

## 12. Succession, Expressions and Controller Continuity

Balanced, Barcelona-dominant and Aragon-dominant government share the same proposed victory architecture. Weighting or presentation may differ under existing owners, but changing expression neither completes a route nor erases achievement history.

Ruler death, regency or dynasty change is not automatic defeat. Revalidate only requirements actually dependent on changed authority or relationships. Direct player control remains intact; no permanent partner AI or junior-foundation approval loop is added.

Victory must attach to a defined campaign participant and continuing faction history, not a display name alone. The scenario and Lifecycle owners resolve any player-controller transition. No achievement is copied to a second controller without an explicit rule.

## 13. Dissolution, Restoration and Defeat

Distinguish loss of a current Union condition, formal faction dissolution, temporary occupation, player elimination and scenario defeat. None is inferred solely from one lost battle or an expression change.

Dissolution prevents confirmation of a bundle that currently requires a formed Union. It does not erase a previously confirmed victory or all historical milestones. Independent Barcelona and Aragon remain valid political outcomes; whether play continues under either belongs to Lifecycle and scenario rules, not an automatic punishment here.

Restoration or reformation consumes current surviving state. It cannot grant the same award twice or restore lost territory, units and agreements. A bound sustained condition resumes or restarts only according to its explicit interruption policy.

Exact defeat conditions remain with the scenario owner. This file adds no universal capital-loss defeat, dynasty-extinction defeat or automatic game-over on separation.

## 14. Rewards and Post-Victory Play

Natural strategic benefit is sufficient reward for many achievements. Any additional award requires an explicit controlling owner and actual material basis. This portfolio grants no money, army, fleet, recruitment refill or permanent faction-wide buff.

Confirm short and long results once per eligible definition and campaign identity. A repeated event, reload, expression switch or reformation must not replay an award. Distinct awards can coexist only when their definitions genuinely differ.

The proposed default is to offer continued campaign play after victory where the shared scenario supports it. A confirmed result remains historical even if the realm later weakens; current-condition panels can show the new state separately. Ending play, scoreboards and epilogues remain scenario and presentation decisions.

The scenario may end play or permit continuation, but the choice must be explicit. Continuing play does not freeze the world at the winning snapshot or immunize the Union from later decline.

## 15. AI, Difficulty and Player Explanation

AI can pursue the same supported outcome categories while retaining its broader survival and strategy rules. Victory pursuit must not override real source capacity, access, treaty agency or actor-visible information. No Union-specific free resources or omniscience is granted.

Show the selected bundle, common requirements, alternative routes, satisfied and unmet components, and the principal known blocker. Distinguish unconfigured, ineligible, in progress and confirmed states. Do not present an unbound numerical progress bar as measured fact.

Use concise player-facing purpose and optional detail. Provide text equivalents for icons and color. Suppress repeated unchanged notices without hiding invalidation. Never require perfect loyalty, every composite card or equal spending merely to make the checklist look complete.
