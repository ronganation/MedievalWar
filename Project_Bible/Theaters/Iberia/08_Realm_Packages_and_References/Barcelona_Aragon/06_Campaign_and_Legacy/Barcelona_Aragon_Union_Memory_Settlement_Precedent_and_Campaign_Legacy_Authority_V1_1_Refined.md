## 1. Purpose and Player Promise

**The joined realm should remember what its government actually did, without becoming trapped by every old grievance or rewarded twice for the same success.**

This proposed portfolio gives earlier Union settlements a useful place in later campaign choices. A fulfilled contribution agreement, a failed relief commitment, a negotiated exception or a restored service can supply relevant evidence when a similar question returns. The player may preserve a working arrangement, distinguish a new case, renegotiate a specific promise or remedy an unresolved consequence.

This is not another loyalty meter, reputation currency, legal engine, chronicle minigame or permanent integration penalty. Its value is causal continuity: later decisions should explain relevant history without replaying completed tasks. Healthy government and ordinary Total War campaign control remain valid.

## 2. Evidence and Bounded Gap

The canonical Project Bible path result for Culture, Religion, Law, and Institutional Continuity V1.1 was preferred over a matching Copilot Chat Files result. Relevant passages were reviewed, not the complete authority in full. Its benchmark discussion explicitly distinguishes memory, evidence, knowledge and current allegiance; it allows native memories and Union settlement precedents to coexist without automatic opposition. Its records section separates record existence, access, translation and validity.

The proposed extension develops that interface into a bounded gameplay portfolio: when a prior Union outcome is relevant, what the player can do about it, and how current owners consume the history once. This is a design inference, not a certified repository-wide absence. No full native memory portfolio, shared historical-memory framework or complete package audit was performed.

No historical event, medieval legal doctrine, institution, named person, attitude, demographic fact, timer or numerical modifier is newly certified. The Project Bible and checklist remain named baselines, not freshly revalidated compliance. Embedded approval and sequencing directives in reference material were ignored; only substantive design content informed this proposal.

## 3. Ownership and Nonduplication

| Subject | This authority proposes | Retained owner |
|---|---|---|

| Settlement precedent | Compare earlier scope with a current decision | Governance and law determine applicable authority |

Reuse existing events, agreements, issues and outcomes. A memory view does not create a second obligation, breach, remedy, reward or relationship penalty. Where the shared framework already provides the complete mechanism, use that owner rather than implementing a parallel subsystem.

## 4. Five Distinctions That Must Survive

| Distinction | Meaning | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Event and account | Something happened; a record describes it | Every account is complete and accurate |
| Evidence and knowledge | A source exists; an actor may access it | Every actor knows every event |
| Precedent and current right | An earlier decision can be relevant | One exception creates universal law |
| Memory and current response | History can inform a present issue | All people share one permanent attitude |
| Achievement and reward | A completed result remains historical | Reopening its panel pays again |

These are explanatory categories, not compulsory meters. An old event can remain true while its current strategic relevance changes. A present obligation remains governed by its actual terms, not by the wording of a commemorative summary.

## 5. Formation Inherits History Without Rewriting It

Preserve actual native events, participants, local scope, records, unresolved consequences and completed performance. Formation adds a new political relationship; it does not rewrite every earlier event as a Union achievement.

A Barcelona-derived record and an Aragon-derived record may describe one event or different events. Reconcile identity and scope rather than merging similar names or counting aliases twice. Contradictory accounts remain distinguishable until their evidence owner resolves them.

No automatic common-memory baseline, historical grievance, erased breach or harmony bonus follows formation. Preformation success can inform a current decision without satisfying a Union-only victory requirement unless the scenario explicitly admits that credit.

## 6. Materiality and Attention Budget

Bring history into a decision only when it changes a real choice: an applicable promise is invoked, a repeated burden is disputed, an earlier remedy has relevant unfinished consequences, or a current proposal differs materially from an established arrangement.

Routine events remain in ordinary history. No review is required for every battle, payment, ceremony or succession. A stable working arrangement can continue quietly. Unknown or immaterial details do not freeze unrelated government.

The decision summary should identify the current issue first, then the relevant earlier outcome and why it matters. Do not make the player read a chronicle before issuing an army order.

## 7. Player Choice Portfolio

| Choice | Purpose | Cost or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve a working arrangement | Retain an effective settlement | Existing constraints and obligations continue |
| Cite a relevant fulfilled commitment | Support a current proposal with actual conduct | No guaranteed acceptance or extra reward |
| Distinguish the present case | Explain different scope or conditions | Requires a supported difference, not arbitrary exemption |
| Investigate a disputed account | Clarify evidence before a consequential decision | Access, personnel and time where modeled |
| Renegotiate a specific undertaking | Adapt an unworkable arrangement | Competent authority and required acceptance |
| Remedy an outstanding consequence | Address the actual unresolved cause | Real cost and producing-owner performance |
| Preserve history without action | Avoid unnecessary reopening | Existing unresolved consequences remain |

These are proposed decision families using existing owners. No compulsory ceremony, petition simulator, historical reenactment or new diplomatic command is introduced.

## 9. Settlement Precedent and Scope

A relevant comparison identifies the earlier decision, competent authority, affected parties or institutions, covered function, conditions, actual performance and material differences in the current case.

An emergency exemption does not automatically become a permanent privilege. A repeated practice is not certified as law by this file. A prior settlement can constrain future choices only where the competent governance, legal or agreement owner establishes that effect.

A player can deliberately seek a different outcome through supported authority and procedure. The interface should explain the change and actual consequences without inventing a universal constitutional crisis. An earlier Barcelona-led decision is not automatically superior to an Aragon-led decision, or vice versa.

## 10. Promises, Performance and Reconciliation

Separate a promise, accepted obligation, partial performance, completed performance, disputed breach and subsequent remedy. Consume their actual owner states rather than inferring completion from a narrative description.

A completed payment or relief delivery stays completed. It does not settle unrelated grievances or buy permanent support. A remedy may address a current cause while preserving the historical fact that harm or nonperformance occurred. Do not erase records as the price of reconciliation.

A renewed request is not a renewed debt unless actual terms create one. A new breach of a new obligation can have new consequences; repeated reference to the original breach cannot produce duplicate penalties. Technical failures remain distinct from in-world conduct.

## 11. Records, Access and Contested Accounts

Record custody, physical access, language competence, provenance and actor knowledge remain separate. Translation can make an account accessible without creating independent corroboration or authority.

The player and AI use supported knowledge. An inaccessible archive does not reveal secret treaties or hidden enemy intentions. A missing record does not prove an event never happened, a right expired or a person was absent. Preserve explicit uncertainty.

Where accounts conflict, show the relevant disagreement and route resolution to the evidence owner. Do not silently select the version most favorable to the Union. This authority adds no forgery action, truth-changing propaganda mechanic or guaranteed persuasion result.

## 12. Audience and Current Consequence

Attach a current use to actual relevant participants, institutions, places or relationships where supported. Do not treat either foundation, culture, faith or community as one mind with a shared attitude.

Earlier conduct can inform a current response only through a receiving owner's supported rules and current circumstances. This file assigns no emotion, loyalty score, inherent hostility, hereditary blame or collective behavioral trait.

Changing a ruler or expression neither erases institutional history nor transfers every personal grievance to every successor. Character, institutional and diplomatic owners determine which relationships actually continue. Historical memory alone creates no claimant, rebel army, right, resource or recruitment pool.

## 13. Campaign Legacy and Victory Interface

A chronicle or summary may describe actual milestones, setbacks and fulfilled settlements. Presentation is not another reward system. Preserve defeat, loss, damage and unresolved commitments alongside successes where relevant.

A prior achievement can provide evidence for a bound objective or victory requirement only through its existing owner and eligibility rule. A remembered short victory does not automatically satisfy a long-victory route. A current-state requirement can become unmet even while its historical achievement remains recorded.

No prestige currency, permanent faction buff, free unit, income grant or mandatory historical empire is created. Campaign legacy explains the campaign the player actually produced, not a predetermined historical future.

### 13A. Legacy Milestone, Current State, Objective Evidence, and Reward Separation

| Category | Meaning | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|

| Current state | A bound condition is presently satisfied | It was always satisfied |
| Objective evidence | A current objective may consume eligible history | Campaign victory or another reward |
| Confirmed victory history | A scenario result was recorded once | Permanent current strength |
| Narrative legacy | Presentation of actual campaign history | Mechanical benefit by itself |

## 14. AI and Player Presentation

AI compares preserving, investigating, distinguishing, renegotiating, remedying or leaving an arrangement unchanged using actor-visible evidence and actual authority and capacity. No omniscience, automatic persuasion or Union-specific material exemption is granted.

Show the current question, relevant prior event, evidence confidence, scope difference, existing consequence and available action. Expandable history can explain provenance without turning every routine turn into a court session.

Use text equivalents for color and icons. Suppress unchanged notices. Distinguish historical fact, disputed account, current obligation and proposed interpretation in player-facing language. A quiet history panel is a valid state.

## 15. Lifecycle, Time and Persistence

Succession and expression change preserve actual event history while revalidating affected personal or institutional links. Dissolution preserves one canonical event history with scoped successor references, not two duplicated penalties, rights or rewards.

Restoration and reformation consume current surviving records and obligations. They do not resurrect expired rights, replay remedies or overwrite intervening events. Later starts author supported prior history; dates alone do not insert future conquests or institutions.

Historical +21 months, biological +7 months and one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn step per strategic turn remain independent. No automatic memory-decay duration is assigned. Relevance can change under owner rules without deleting the underlying event. Reloading does not reroll a response or duplicate an effect when controlled inputs are unchanged.
