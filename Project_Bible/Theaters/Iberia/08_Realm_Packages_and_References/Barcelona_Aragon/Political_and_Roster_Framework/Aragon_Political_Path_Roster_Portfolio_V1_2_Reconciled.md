## 1. Aragon Roster Thesis

Aragon begins as a Three Land mountain-frontier kingdom whose military strength comes from assembling royal, community, regional, valley, castle, household, noble, mounted-service, and frontier sources into one usable host. High Era development must preserve that identity while allowing political outcomes to change what institutions mature, who commands them, how reliably they serve, and which new formations emerge.
