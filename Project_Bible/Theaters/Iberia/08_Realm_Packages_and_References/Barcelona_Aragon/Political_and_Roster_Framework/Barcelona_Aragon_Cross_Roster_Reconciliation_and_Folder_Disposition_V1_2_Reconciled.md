## 2. Final Meaning of Shared

The existing `Shared High Era Core` concept should be narrowed. Shared inside a Realm package means common across that Realm's political outcomes, not jointly owned by Barcelona and Aragon.

```yaml
RealmCommon:
  meaning: SameRealmSourceAndCardRemainUsableAcrossSeveralPoliticalOutcomes
  ownership: OriginalRealm
DualRealmShared:
  meaning: ReservedForActualCompositeInstitutionOrTheaterOwnedRelationshipFamily
  ownership: CompositeInstitutionOrTheaterSource
PartnerAccessible:
  meaning: OtherRealmRetainsOwnershipAndReleasesABoundedAssignment
  ownership: OriginalRealm
```

A unit available under independence, union, and domination does not automatically belong in Realm Common when each path changes its institution, command, recruitment, recovery, and battlefield identity materially.
