## 0A. Purpose and Controlling Decision

This doctrine controls how Barcelona and Aragon move from inherited Early Era families into High Era continuations, context expressions, branches, and campaign emergence. It prevents an overextended shared core, a separate formed-faction Domination path, and cosmetic duplication.

```yaml
controlling_decision:
  - PreserveTheCommonEarlyMilitaryBase
  - ClassifyEveryFamilyByItsTrueDevelopmentMode
  - PreservePeopleInstitutionsEquipmentHorsesAndExperienceAcrossTransitions
  - LetIndependentUnionAndExternalControlContextsCreateVisibleMilitaryDifferences
  - UseSharedCardsOnlyWhenSourceBattleLoopAndPlayerDecisionRemainCommon
  - ReuseTheSuccessfulAragonDevelopmentProcedureWithoutCopyingAragonContent
  - RouteRecognizedUnionIntoTheBarcelonaAragonFactionAuthorityStack
  - SeparateDominantUnionExpressionFromOccupationCoercionAndSubordination
  - DeriveAchievementsFromFinishedFactionAndContextIdentity
```

## 1. Player-Experience Thesis

Military development must do more than repaint the same army. Independent development, recognized Barcelona-Aragon government, dominant Union expression, and external control should create recognizable campaign and military consequences while preserving the physical and institutional history that produced them.

```yaml
development_contexts:
  IndependentBarcelonaOrAragon: NativeFactionDevelopment
  RecognizedBarcelonaAragonUnion: OneFactionWithPreservedNativeSourcesAndCompositeEligibility
  BarcelonaDominantUnion: BarcelonaSeniorDirectionInsideRecognizedUnion
  AragonDominantUnion: AragonSeniorDirectionInsideRecognizedUnion
  ExternalDominationOrSubordination: OccupationComplianceResistanceClaimantAndRestorationOutsideRecognizedUnion
separate_formed_faction_domination_path: false
context_difference_must_be_visible_in:
  - RosterComposition
  - InstitutionalFlavor
  - SourceOwnership
  - BattlefieldRoles
  - RecruitmentAndRecovery
  - CommandAndReliability
  - CampaignOpportunityCost
context_difference_must_not_rely_only_on:
  - ColorSwap
  - SmallStatisticsChanges
  - HiddenBonuses
  - DifferentFolderPlacement
  - AvailabilityPercentage
```

## 2. Four Development Dispositions

Every Early or candidate High Era family must receive one primary disposition.

### 2.1 Common Continuation

The same family naturally persists and matures across political or control contexts.

```yaml
use_when:
  - PopulationAndSourceRemainPresentAcrossPaths
  - BattlefieldRoleRemainsFundamentallyStable
  - DifferencesCanBeHandledThroughEquipmentLeadershipAvailabilityAndPresentation
examples:
  - CommunityLevy
  - BroadBowFamilies
result: OneCanonicalFamilyWithLimitedPathInfluence
```

### 2.2 Shared Family with Context Expression

The same source family survives, but each context meaningfully changes service, equipment, command, reliability, combined-arms relationships, or presentation.

```yaml
use_when:
  - SamePeopleAndInstitutionPersist
  - CoreBattleLoopRemainsRecognizable
  - PoliticalOrControlContextChangesHowTheFamilyIsFielded
result:
  - OneCanonicalCard
  - ContextExpressionAuthoritiesWhereUseful
  - NoAutomaticPoolMerger
```

### 2.3 Context Branch

An Early source produces materially different High Era cards under different political or control contexts.

```yaml
use_when:
  - NewInstitutionOrCommandRelationshipChangesBattlefieldIdentity
  - SourceAssemblyAndRecruitmentBecomeSubstantivelyDifferent
  - SeparatePlayerFacingNameAndBattleLoopImproveTheRoster
result:
  - PathOwnedSuccessorOrBranch
  - EarlierFamilyMayPersist
  - ContributorsTransferOnlyThroughActualReleaseAndReservation
```

### 2.4 Campaign Emergence

The unit develops primarily through world state, service history, survival, experience, equipment, leadership, and campaign need.

```yaml
use_when:
  - NoSingleEraDateOrPoliticalPathIsSufficient
  - FormationDependsOnCampaignCausality
  - MultipleEarlierFamiliesMayContributeSelectively
result:
  - ConditionalEmergentCard
  - EarlierFamiliesPersistUnlessTheirExactSourcesChange
  - ContextInfluencesEmergenceWithoutAutomaticallyCreatingIt
```

## 3. The Narrow Shared Rule

A card belongs in Shared only when sharing makes the design easier and more truthful.

```yaml
shared_admission_requires:
  - SameCanonicalSourceFamily
  - SameCoreBattlefieldRole
  - SamePlayerFacingDecision
  - PoliticalPathChangesExpressionMoreThanIdentity
  - NoNeedForRepeatedExceptionalRules
  - NoMaterialLossOfFactionFlavor
shared_rejection_triggers:
  - OnePathCreatesTheInstitution
  - CommandOwnershipChangesTheBattleLoop
  - RecruitmentAndRecoveryUseDifferentSourceSystems
  - PathSpecificNameAndPresentationAreSubstantive
  - SharedProfileRequiresLargePoliticalExceptionBlocks
  - SeparateCardsWouldCreateBetterReplayValueWithoutFakeDuplication
```

Shared is not synonymous with available to every context. A path can gain conditional access to another path's card without making that card shared.

## 4. Carryover Constitution

Era change, political change, technology, buildings, decisions, research, money, or victory do not manufacture people, horses, equipment, leaders, experience, institutions, or recruitment pools.

```yaml
carryover_preserves:
  - ExactPeopleAndPopulationSources
  - LeadersCadresAndFormationMemory
  - HorsesRemountsAndPairings
  - WeaponsArmorShieldsAmmunitionAndTools
  - HouseholdsCommunitiesMunicipalitiesCastlesAndInstitutions
  - InjuriesCasualtiesCaptivityAndRecovery
  - ServiceTermsTrustGrievanceAndPoliticalRelationships
  - ExistingFormationHistory
carryover_allows:
  - ContinuedEarlierCard
  - InternalMaturation
  - SelectiveContributionToSuccessor
  - SourceSpecificReplacement
  - PathSpecificBranch
  - CampaignEmergence
carryover_prohibits:
  - WholeCardAutomaticUpgradeByDefault
  - SamePeopleInTwoFullCards
  - FreeEquipmentTransformation
  - InstantCrossRealmPoolMerger
  - DeletingSurvivingEarlierFormationsWhenRecruitmentCloses
```

## 5. Copy, Reference, and Successor Rule

Path-first design may require a card to appear outside its origin context. That access must use the simplest truthful method.

```yaml
CanonicalReference:
  use_when: SameCardSameSourceAndSameBattleIdentity
ConditionalCopy:
  use_when: InterfaceOrFolderNeedsAPathLocalCardButSubstanceRemainsMostlyCommon
  requirement: PreserveCanonicalSourceAndReplacementOwnership
ContextSuccessor:
  use_when: NewNameInstitutionBattleLoopRecruitmentOrRecoveryAreMateriallyDifferent
no_duplication_rule:
  - CopyingAProfileDoesNotDuplicatePeopleOrPools
  - ReceivingContextMustHaveActualAccess
  - DonorCapacityMustFallWhenContributionsMove
  - ReversalMayCloseFutureAccessWithoutDeletingExistingFormation
```

## 6. Category Doctrine

### 6.1 Levy and Regional

Default disposition: Common Continuation or Shared Family with Context Expression.

```yaml
CommunityLevy:
  disposition: CommonContinuation
JacaMusterSpearmen:
  disposition: SharedFamilyContextExpression
SpearmenOfSobrarbe:
  disposition: SharedFamilyContextExpression
high_development_may_change:
  - Protection
  - FormationControl
  - Leadership
  - ServiceDuration
  - RegionalRelease
  - CombinedArmsIntegration
  - VisualExpression
must_preserve:
  - RegionalSource
  - HomeDefenseAndLaborFloors
  - DistinctJacaAndSobrarbeBattlefieldIdentity
```

Regional successors should not all become identical Armored X Spearmen. Jaca should retain broader muster frontage and field-army integration. Sobrarbe should retain pass, constrained-approach, and difficult-ground defense.

## 8. Unit Admission Test

Before creating any new unit profile, answer in order:

1. What exact source owns the people and equipment?
2. Does an Early family already own the role?
3. Is this maturation, expression, branch, or emergence?
4. What development or control context naturally creates the institution?
5. Does the battlefield loop differ from adjacent cards?
6. Does campaign recruitment and recovery differ materially?
7. Can the idea be handled inside an existing card without losing fun or identity?
8. If copied elsewhere, what survives and who replaces losses?
9. Does the card preserve source capacity and avoid double counting?
10. Is the player-facing name readable, specific, and useful?

```yaml
reject_separate_card_when:
  - DifferenceIsOnlyBetterStatistics
  - DifferenceIsOnlyAppearance
  - DifferenceIsOnlyContextLabel
  - ExistingCardCanExpressTheIdeaCleanly
admit_separate_card_when:
  - SourceInstitutionIsDistinct
  - BattleLoopIsDistinct
  - CampaignCostAndRecoveryAreDistinct
  - PlayerChoiceImproves
  - AdjacentCardsRemainUseful
```
