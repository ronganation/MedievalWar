## 1. Barcelona Roster Thesis

Barcelona begins as a county whose military power is distributed among community and regional sources, castle custody, frontier service, comital household forces, mounted contributors, and developing missile capability. High Era development should strengthen this comital, castle, regional, civic, trans-Pyrenean, and Mediterranean-facing identity without turning Barcelona into a copy of Aragon's royal mountain-frontier army.

```yaml
Barcelona_player_promise:
  - BindComitalHouseCastleRegionalAndUrbanSources
  - DevelopACompactFlexibleArmyAroundReliableInstitutions
  - BalanceHouseholdCastleCommunityAndCivicPower
  - ExpandCrossbowCommandRouteAndExpeditionaryPotentialWhereSupported
  - PreserveUsefulEarlyFamiliesAcrossPoliticalChange
development_and_control_context_difference_must_appear_in:
  - ComitalHouseholdAndHighStatusMountedService
  - CastleAndRegionalCommand
  - CivicAndCrossbowDevelopment
  - JointCommandAndPartnerAccess

  - ComplianceResistanceAndRecovery
```
