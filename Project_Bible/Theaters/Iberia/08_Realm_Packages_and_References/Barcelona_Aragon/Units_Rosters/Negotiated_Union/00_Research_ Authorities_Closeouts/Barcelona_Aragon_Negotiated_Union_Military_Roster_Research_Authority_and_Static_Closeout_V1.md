## 1. Executive Verdict

The Negotiated Union infantry portfolio now contains five differentiated design candidates with a complete player-facing battlefield logic:

- **Catalano-Aragonese Spearmen** establish the practiced Crown center.
- **Crown Serjeants** conduct the recurring sustained close fight.
- **Companyia de la Santa Creu** breaks one hard prepared objective.
- **Royal-Comital Household Guard** preserves shared command through one serious local crisis.
- **Union Wardens** maintain connected shared-custody positions at qualified Crown sites.

The original portfolio map correctly rejected additional negotiated levy, regional spear, borderer, duplicate assault, duplicate household, and premature pike cards. The attached profiles now provide enough static design evidence to close the present infantry unit-creation sequence while preserving all five cards as candidates pending their stated reviews and unexecuted calibration.

## 2. Thirty-Second Portfolio Read

1. **Crown Spearmen:** receive, protect, relieve, and counterpush.
2. **Crown Serjeants:** enter, press, rotate, secure, and reform.
3. **Santa Creu Company:** approach, break, widen, hand off, and withdraw.

## 8. Integrated Battlefield Sequence

A mature negotiated army can use the portfolio in a coherent sequence without requiring all five cards in every army:

1. Crown Spearmen establish the center and protected missile frontage.
2. Native and Crown missiles shape the enemy approach and target state.
3. Crown Serjeants relieve a compressed section or win the recurring close fight.
4. Santa Creu attacks the one prepared objective that ordinary infantry should not be asked to break.
5. Crown Serjeants or appropriate native infantry receive the foothold from Santa Creu.
6. The Household Guard remains fresh until command, standards, or one critical sector faces a recoverable crisis.
7. Union Wardens appear only where a qualified shared Crown site requires enduring custody before, during, or after the field army's action.
