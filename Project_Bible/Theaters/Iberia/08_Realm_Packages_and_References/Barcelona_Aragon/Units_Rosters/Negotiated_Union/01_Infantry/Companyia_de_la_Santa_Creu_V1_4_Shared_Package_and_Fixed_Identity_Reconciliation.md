## 1. Mature Definitive Unit Verdict

**Companyia de la Santa Creu** are a scarce composite assault company created for one bounded task: cross a prepared final approach, break into one defended local objective, widen enough room for the Union host to follow, and surrender the sustained fight before the company's assault cadre is destroyed.

The company combines selected Aragonese and Barcelona close-infantry contributors under one charter, captain, objective, support plan, and relief agreement. Its battlefield quality comes from a protected approach, compact entry geometry, disciplined weapon placement, replacement depth, and the ability to halt and hand over the captured space. It does not come from religious immunity, universal armor-breaking weapons, or better statistics in every melee.

The unit's decisive player decision is not simply whether to attack. It is whether the objective, approach, enemy depth, supporting fire, available relief, and withdrawal route justify spending the company's finite assault momentum.

## 1A. Mature Strengthening Verdict

The failed V1.1 direction overemphasized procedure and made the company read like an assault-management system. V1.3 restores the unit as a feared, high-status battlefield formation. **Companyia de la Santa Creu are not merely troops who walk through a corridor and wait for relief. They are the Union army's chosen foot for breaking the place where the battle is hardest.**

Their assault is bounded, but it is not timid. When correctly prepared, the company can overpower ordinary heavy infantry on a constrained frontage, drive armored defenders out of a breach or gate interior, retake a contested objective through violent close action, and create the foothold from which the rest of the host wins the position. Their shields, armor concentration, veteran file leadership, mixed striking weapons, and replacement depth make them one of the most dangerous short-duration infantry formations available to the Negotiated Union.

The limits protect the roster rather than diminishing the fantasy. The company is not a broad line holder, cavalry counter, or endlessly replenishing super-unit. It is an elite assault weapon with enough tactical authority to justify its scarcity and enough counterplay to remain credible.

## 1B. Final Elite Card Identity

The mature Companyia is not merely a specialist that becomes acceptable under ideal circumstances. It is an **elite offensive infantry card** that gives the negotiated Crown something neither native roster supplies in the same form: a politically shared, heavily protected, veteran assault company able to cross danger, win brutal close contact, and convert a contested opening into a Crown foothold.

On the battlefield, the Companyia should be feared. Ordinary line infantry should not want to meet it inside a gate, breach, street, barricade, camp entrance, bridgehead, wooded edge, or compressed flank. Armored serjeants and household troops can resist it, but must expect a costly fight when the Companyia arrives fresh and supported. Missile troops caught after the protected approach should be rapidly overwhelmed. Defenders that lose their first rank or local leader should face an immediate danger of collapse as fresh Santa Creu files press through the opening.

The company also retains credible open-field value. It can serve as a compact **assault reserve** behind the Crown spear line, attack an enemy formation already fixed or disordered, retake a threatened section, or deliver a short, powerful infantry counterstroke. The card is not confined to sieges. The difference is that open-field use requires a local frontage and supporting arms rather than a wall breach.

### Correct abort conditions

- defending depth proves fresh and substantially greater than expected;
- prepared spears or polearms occupy the entry;
- supporting missiles cannot suppress the approach;
- friendly congestion blocks casualty or relief passage;
- one flank becomes exposed;
- the captain, standards, or native file leadership fails;
- relief is no longer available;
- another objective becomes strategically more important.

Aborting during the final approach costs fatigue, cohesion, shields, prestige, and possibly casualties. It is still better than sacrificing the company into an entry that cannot be widened or handed over.

## 9. Refined Battle Cycle and Culmination Point

1. **Select** one bounded objective and identify why it matters.
2. **Observe** frontage, depth, missiles, obstacles, flanks, casualty passage, relief, and withdrawal.
3. **Suppress or fix** defenders with crossbows, bows, spears, cavalry threat, terrain, or engineering support.
4. **Form** the shield front and preserve replacement depth.
5. **Approach** through the final danger under real fatigue and equipment damage.
6. **Enter** only when the opening remains qualified.
7. **Replace** degraded files without pretending casualties are healed.
8. **Strike** defenders whose formation, space, or attention is already constrained.
9. **Widen** only enough to admit relief and prevent immediate closure.
10. **Recognize culmination.** Stop when fresh depth, shields, leaders, corridor space, or relief confidence reaches failure threshold.
11. **Secure and hand off** the position to appropriate supporting troops.
12. **Withdraw and recover** people, leaders, standards, weapons, shields, and campaign records.

  - FlankBoundaryFailing
  - FatiguePreventingOrderedWideningOrWithdrawal
  - CasualtyRouteCongested

## 10A. Strengthened Assault Authority

The three abilities are not minor utility toggles. Together they create a powerful assault sequence whose rewards justify recruiting the card.

### Shields to the Front, strengthened identity

While the frontal shield body remains intact, the company should be meaningfully harder to disrupt with ordinary frontal arrows, bolts, thrown weapons, and loose infantry contact than routine close infantry. This protection is directional and destructible. Crossfire, elevated fire, heavy missiles, flank contact, obstacles, fatigue, and shield loss remain effective counters.

### Break the Entry, strengthened identity

Against a qualified target, Break the Entry should produce genuinely elite initial close pressure. Entry files hold the opening while striking files attack shields, limbs, weapons, and armored opponents at compressed range. The company receives no abstract universal armor piercing, but its selected weapons, experienced files, limited frontage, and prepared momentum make it dangerous to protected defenders who cannot use reach, depth, or maneuver effectively.

### Secure and Hand Off, strengthened identity

This order is not simply administrative withdrawal. The company violently stabilizes the captured foothold, turns outward against immediate counterattack, clears friendly passage, recovers standards and wounded, and remains a dangerous compact defense until relief arrives. If relief reaches the position correctly, supporting troops enter with less disruption and the company withdraws without surrendering the objective.

This power schema gives the card teeth. The player should notice immediately when the Companyia reaches the kind of fight it was built to win.


The mature card must feel worth its campaign cost. Its favorable engagements are therefore pronounced when the player earns them.

- Against ordinary swordsmen, axemen, or shield infantry fixed on a narrow frontage, the company should usually win the initial local assault if support and replacement depth remain intact.
- Against armored serjeants or household foot already disrupted by missiles or compressed by terrain, the company should generate strong casualties and cohesion damage during the first assault interval.
- Against breach and gate defenders without fresh depth, the company should be among the Union roster's best available choices.
- Against equal elite infantry in open, unconstrained combat, the result should become expensive and uncertain rather than automatically favorable.
- Against prepared spears, pikes, cavalry, crossfire, or layered reserves, the company should need combined-arms preparation and may still fail.

This is a specialist with a **high ceiling**, not a specialist whose restrictions erase its battlefield authority.

## 21. Representative Refined Scenarios

### Breach with functioning handoff

Crown Crossbow Sergeants suppress defenders and reinforcements while Catalano-Aragonese Spearmen protect the outer approach. Santa Creu advances under shields, breaks the compressed entry, spends part of its replacement depth, and widens a usable passage. Barcelona Castle Sergeants and Aragonese Serjeants pass through and assume sustained custody. The company withdraws with losses but retains its cadre.

### Correctly aborted assault

A gate opening appears weak, but observers identify fresh spears behind the first defenders and no safe relief route. Santa Creu halts during the covered approach and withdraws. The company suffers fatigue and prestige cost but avoids a catastrophic entry.

### Field assault against fixed armor

Crown Spearmen fix armored infantry while bolts disorder one side. Santa Creu attacks that limited sector, forces several paces, and opens a turning opportunity. The company then hands the frontage to native close infantry rather than trying to roll the entire enemy line alone.

### Culmination ignored

The first defenders fall, but replacement depth is nearly spent and relief is delayed. Continued attack reaches fresh enemy depth. The company loses formation, routes clog, and a successful entry becomes a destructive grind. The failure is command, not insufficient elite statistics.

### Religious identity tested

The Santa Creu standard remains visible, but the captain is lost, one contributor has suffered disproportionate casualties, and the relief route closes. The unit's symbol does not prevent fragmentation. Native leaders may withdraw files separately or attempt a costly temporary hold.
