## 1. Decisive Connection Verdict

Aragon's Early infantry does not disappear when High Era and Negotiated Union infantry develop. The correct structure is a **persistent-parent and selective-emergence portfolio**, not a universal upgrade ladder.

The Early families continue to own their established jobs:

- Community Levy remains the lowest-pool mixed task and emergency-support infantry.
- Jaca Muster Spearmen remain the broad ordinary spear-and-shield line.
- Spearmen of Sobrarbe remain the pass and broken-ground spear family.
- Castle Wardens of Aragon remain native strongpoint custody infantry.
- Men of Old Aragon remain the selected deep-line spear and relief family.
- Ribagorzan Borderers remain mixed frontier response infantry.

The Negotiated Union cards emerge only when repeated joint service or shared custody creates a distinct institution and battle decision. They do not replace their Early Aragon contributors, absorb their native recruitment pools, or become automatic High Era upgrades.

## 12. Battlefield Connection Examples

### Native center with negotiated reinforcement

Jaca Muster Spearmen hold broad frontage. Crown Serjeants relieve one compressed section and take the sword-and-shield fight. Men of Old Aragon remain available as native spear depth rather than being consumed into the Serjeant card.

### Composite Crown center with native specialists

Catalano-Aragonese Spearmen form the joint center. Spearmen of Sobrarbe protect a constrained flank; Ribagorzan Borderers screen a route; neither regional family is replaced by the composite center.

### Elite assault and handoff

Santa Creu breaks a qualified entry. Crown Serjeants or another appropriate field formation receive the foothold. Castle Wardens remain native strongpoint troops; Union Wardens assume enduring custody only if the site qualifies and a real shared institution exists.

### Command crisis

Royal-Comital Household Guard protects joint command. Native Royal Mesnada Foot does not disappear and may remain assigned to an Aragonese command or household mission where actual capacity exists.
