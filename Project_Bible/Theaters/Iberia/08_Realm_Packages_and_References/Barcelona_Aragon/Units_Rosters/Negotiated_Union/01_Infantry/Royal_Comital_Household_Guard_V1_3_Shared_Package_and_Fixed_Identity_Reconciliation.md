## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Royal-Comital Household Guard** are selected foot retainers temporarily drawn from the Aragonese royal and Barcelona comital households into a practiced joint guard formation. They serve beside the Union commander and standards as a compact crisis reserve capable of sealing a breach, protecting a threatened command position, clearing a narrow objective, and conducting a short disciplined counterentry.

They are not the main line, mass heavy infantry, permanent bodyguard of a unified national monarchy, or a replacement for either household's native retainers. Their value is preserving **dual-household command continuity** during one decisive crisis: holding the shared commander and standards together, sealing the local collapse, recovering the command group or critical formation, and handing the sustained fight back to native troops.

## 2. What the Player Buys

The player buys a small, expensive, dependable reserve positioned near the Union commander or critical objective. The formation should remain uncommitted while the main battle develops, then intervene where ordinary line troops are about to fail.

The card provides:

- strong local cohesion;
- quick response over short distances;
- better performance in confined or disordered crisis fighting;
- commander and standard protection;
- controlled counterentry into a breached frontage;
- and the discipline to stop, extract, and reform after a short intervention.

The player pays through scarce household personnel, better equipment, political visibility, command risk, and extremely slow replacement.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Player Card

- **Unit:** Royal-Comital Household Guard
- **Class:** Scarce joint household crisis infantry
- **Battle promise:** Remain near the command position, intervene against a local breakthrough, seal the threatened sector, protect the standards, and reform before the guard is trapped in a long melee
- **Best use:** Commander protection, breach stopping, counterentry, gate interiors, camps, courtyards, broad streets, threatened center relief, and recovering a collapsing local objective
- **Worst use:** Long main-line grinding, missile exposure, cavalry in open ground, pursuit, broad frontage, unsupported assault, and attritional siege fighting
- **Strategic value:** Gives the Negotiated Union one politically visible crisis formation that can protect command continuity and recover a local failure
- **Strategic cost:** Concentrates scarce retainers, household officers, armor, shields, sidearms, transport, status, and replacement obligations from both courts
- **Counterplay:** Draw the guard out early, pin it in a prolonged melee, shoot it while held in reserve, threaten several locations, isolate it from the commander, or surround it during counterentry
- **Opening State:** Unavailable on January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 4. Player Fantasy and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Keep the Crown Household Guard behind the center with the commander and standards. Watch the battle rather than chase kills. When a spear sector breaks, enemy assault infantry enters a gate, or hostile cavalry threatens the command position, commit the guard in one compact response. The formation absorbs the immediate crisis, clears enough space for ordinary troops to reform, then withdraws before prestige and veteran household service are squandered in a general melee.

**Short description:** Selected royal and comital household foot formed into a compact joint guard for commander protection, breach stopping, crisis counterentry, and extraction.

**Flavor line:** **Where the Crown is threatened, both households stand.**

## 8. Signature Battle Cycle: Observe, Intervene, Seal, Extract

1. Remain near the commander, standards, or designated critical reserve point.
2. Observe the center, gates, vulnerable missile position, and enemy reserve.
3. Identify a genuine crisis rather than an ordinary unfavorable fight.
4. Move by the shortest protected route.
5. Enter compactly against the breakthrough or threatened command sector.
6. Seal the local gap and restore a rally reference.
7. Conduct a short counterentry only while the hostile body is disordered, compressed, or unsupported.
8. Allow line infantry, castle troops, or other reserves to retake the sustained mission.
9. Extract the commander, standards, wounded, or threatened formation if required.
10. Withdraw and reform before the household guard becomes trapped in attrition.

## 23B. Finite Crisis Commitment

The guard should normally possess **one full crisis commitment** in a battle. This is not an arbitrary ability charge. It represents fresh compact order, unspent household leaders, intact standards, preserved supporting depth, and confidence that both contributor bodies remain under accepted command.

```yaml
commitment_states:
  Uncommitted:
    condition: FreshCompactLeadersStandardsDepthAndCommandAgreementWorking
    capability: FullGuardSealRecoverAndCounterentryPackage
  Committed:
    condition: GuardHasEnteredSeriousCrisisCombat
    capability: CanHoldWithdrawOrCompleteCurrentMissionButCannotRepeatFullFreshIntervention
  Reformed:
    condition: GuardEscapedContactAndRestoredSomeOrder
    capability: UsefulCommanderGuardAndDefensiveReserveReducedCounterentry
  Spent:
    condition: HeavyFatigueCasualtiesLeaderLossStandardLossOrFormationDisorder
    capability: ProtectSurvivorsStandardsAndWithdrawalOnly
  Fragmented:
    condition: ContributorOrCommandSplit
    capability: NativeHouseholdFilesActSeparatelyNoJointCrisisAbility
commitment_is_not:
  - MagicalChargeCounter
  - AutomaticAfterCooldownReset
  - GuaranteedOneUseRegardlessOfCondition
  - FatigueImmunity
```

A second serious intervention is possible only if the formation genuinely retains the people, leaders, order, and stamina to do it. The design direction should strongly discourage repeated fresh-strength activations.

## 23S. Final Ability Timing

### Hold the Joint Standards

- Activate while protecting a recognized command or rally point.
- Strongest before command disruption begins.
- Ends or weakens if standards, command connection, or household leadership are lost.
- Provides local stability, not a wide army aura.

### Commit the Household Reserve

- Use only while the formation retains compact order and reserve depth.
- Best against a narrow, recent, and recoverable crisis.
- Entry bonus should decay quickly after initial contact.
- Commitment materially reduces later response, even if casualties are light.

### Recover the Crown

- Define the recovery target before or during commitment: commander, standards, key formation, gate, camp entrance, or local frontage.
- Success occurs when the target is protected or extracted and relief or withdrawal becomes possible.
- The order should encourage halting and reforming rather than pursuit.
