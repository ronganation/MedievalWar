## 1. Final Static Unit Verdict

**Crown Archer Companies are the Negotiated Union’s steady broad-front bow formation: Aragonese and Barcelona archer companies that keep several lanes under ordinary arrow pressure, shift ready files toward a threatened sector, and relieve one another when a contributor moves, tires, resupplies, or suffers counterfire.**

The renamed card does not represent a new unit. “Companies” makes the existing organization visible to the player. The formation’s strength remains pressure continuity across a changing battle line. Contributor companies may cover separate sectors, overlap fire, concentrate briefly, relieve a weakening company, or displace by company toward another protected position.

“Crown Archer Companies” is preferred over “Crown Field Archers” because the latter states only weapon and environment, while the former communicates the card’s actual mixed-realm institution. The name avoids assigning the entire formation to Jaca, the Pyrenees, the Ebro, Barcelona’s coast, or another geography owned by only part of the contributor base. Crown Field Archers remains an accepted legacy and search name.

## 4. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Crown Archer Companies
- **Legacy name:** Crown Field Archers
- **Class:** Ordinary composite field-bow infantry
- **Source:** Exact released Aragonese and Barcelona bow users, file leaders, serviceable bows, strings, arrows, containers, maintainers, stores, transport, and common Crown field practice
- **Battle promise:** Maintain broad responsive arrow pressure across a mixed Union front and relieve weakening sectors without consuming selected native veteran archers
- **Best use:** Behind spears, on gentle slopes, across broad field fronts, against light or exposed targets, to shape approaches, cover deployment, and support a controlled withdrawal
- **Worst use:** Armor-heavy targets requiring bolt pressure, cavalry contact, prolonged melee, severe rain or supply failure, narrow ground that wastes the second company, or repeated movement without reform time
- **Strategic value:** Gives the Union a durable ordinary bow line whose contributor companies cover, overlap, relieve, and displace without erasing native ownership
- **Strategic cost:** Reduces native bow capacity and consumes trained archers, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, carriers, stores, transport, practice, and joint recovery
- **Counterplay:** Armor and shields, cavalry, counterfire, forced movement, several simultaneous threats, arrow depletion, wet or damaged equipment, and contributor separation
- **Opening State:** Unavailable on January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 13. Signature Battle Cycle: Form, Cover, Sustain, Concentrate, Relieve, Displace

```text
FORM the Aragonese and Barcelona companies into workable bow, arrow, and command groups.
COVER separate sectors behind a valid frontage or terrain position.
SUSTAIN ordinary company fire against light, exposed, moving, or approach-shaping targets.
CONCENTRATE both companies for a short arrow storm only when target value justifies deeper ammunition use and reduced coverage elsewhere.
RELIEVE a depleted, suppressed, or moving company with the contributor that still has useful ready files.
REFORM sectors after movement, casualties, weather, equipment failure, or command disruption.
DISPLACE by company toward another protected position before cavalry or formed infantry reaches the archer line.
```

## 28. Tactical Scenarios

### Broad Union frontage

Aragonese and Barcelona companies cover different approaches behind the spear line. Each uses ordinary fire in its own sector. The player preserves the whole-line concentration until one enemy formation exposes itself.

### Crown arrow storm

Enemy light infantry bunches around a crossing. Both companies concentrate briefly. The pressure may disrupt or drive away the target, but arrow use rises sharply and the opposite lane is temporarily less covered.

### Sector relief

Barcelona files are suppressed and low on arrows. The Aragonese company expands its sector while Barcelona files move back to resupply and reform. The covering company cannot indefinitely maintain both sectors.

### Hard target

Armored infantry approaches behind shields. Crown Field Archers harass exposed moments but conserve ammunition and allow Crown Crossbowmen to own the hard-target shot.

### Company-bound withdrawal

One company moves to the second line while the other keeps limited pressure. The first company redeploys, then covers the second company's retirement. Cavalry or broken passage can still defeat the maneuver.

### Arrow-budget decision

Enemy screens remain active, but armored infantry is approaching behind them. The player keeps one company on measured fire against the screen and conserves the second company's arrows rather than spending a Crown Arrow Storm on a target that crossbows will soon handle more efficiently.

### Unequal contributor quality

The Aragonese company contains more practiced files while the Barcelona company includes more replacements. The stronger contributor covers the pressured sector, but absorbs greater fatigue and ammunition cost. The weaker company remains useful for broad pressure and later relief rather than being treated as an identical statistical half.
