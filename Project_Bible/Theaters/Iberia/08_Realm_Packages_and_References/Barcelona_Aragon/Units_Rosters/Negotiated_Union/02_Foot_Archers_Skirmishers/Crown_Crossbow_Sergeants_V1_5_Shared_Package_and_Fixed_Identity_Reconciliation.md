## 1. Final Static Unit Verdict

**Crown Crossbow Sergeants are the Negotiated Union’s selected professional crossbow core. They concentrate experienced Aragonese and Barcelona operators, native file leaders, sergeant leadership and security, compatible crossbow-and-bolt groupings, and practiced target and withdrawal discipline into one limited formation.**

The unit’s value is not free crossbow power, universal armor, pavises, or rapid fire. It is the ability to hold a protected position, choose a consequential target window, deliver a controlled salvo, contest enemy missiles through selective exposure, retain order under limited pressure, and withdraw expensive operators and equipment under sergeant direction before the position collapses.

Individual files still present, shoot, recover, span, load, and suffer exposure. That physical cycle remains part of the simulation, but it is not the player-facing identity or a rotation minigame. Security sergeants improve cohesion, passage control, reform, and extraction; they do not convert the card into elite melee infantry.

## 4. Player Fantasy, Description, and Flavor

**Player fantasy:** Keep the Crown's selected crossbow files concealed behind the spear center while bows and skirmishers provoke movement. When armored infantry crowds a lane or cavalry commits to an approach, order the target marked. One section steps forward, releases, and disappears behind the shields to span and load while another prepared section covers the same ground. The unit wins through the quality of the chosen moment and its ability to preserve expensive crews, not through endless fire.

**Short description:** Selected Aragonese and Catalan crossbow files under joint command, trained to hold loaded sections, release controlled volleys against important targets, cycle behind protection, and withdraw under sergeant security.

**Encyclopedia description:** A durable union can produce more than temporary allied contingents. Where qualified operators, crossbows, bolts, sergeants, workshops, stores, native leaders, and common signals are repeatedly brought together, a selected joint crossbow formation may emerge. The Crown Crossbow Sergeants do not erase native crossbow institutions. They concentrate a smaller body for mixed-army target control, section exposure, position security, and orderly displacement. Their strength disappears quickly if the spear frontage breaks, commands conflict, bolts are incompatible or depleted, security files are spent, or the enemy reaches the reload space.

**Flavor line:** **Mark the target. Loose for the Crown. Recover the files.**

## 10. Signature Battle Cycle: Prepare, Select, Salvo, Contest, Hold, Withdraw

**PREPARE** a protected position with useful lanes, reload depth, flank security, and an open fallback route.

**SELECT** targets worth scarce bolts and collective exposure. Dense, armored, committed, or dangerous missile formations normally justify attention better than dispersed cheap troops.

**SALVO** when loaded strength, command, lane, and target quality align. The release creates exposure and a reload lull; it guarantees neither penetration nor rout.

**CONTEST** hostile missile troops through selective exposure, cover, cease-fire discipline, and position choice rather than endless open exchange.

**HOLD** only while protection, security sergeants, bolts, command, and exit remain sufficient. Sergeants control gaps and limited incursions but do not replace close infantry.

**WITHDRAW** expensive operators, portable crossbows, bolts, and records before the firing position becomes a trap. Establish a second position only when the new lane and protection pass.

## 30. Tactical Scenarios

### Crown center versus armored advance

Catalano-Aragonese Spearmen receive the approach while native bows maintain broad pressure. Crown files hold loaded sections until armored infantry compresses against the spear center. The marked section presents, releases, and recovers. The crossbows do not continue firing through the engaged spear line once the lane closes.

### Counter-missile exchange

Only limited sections expose at one time. The captain alternates observation, release, and recovery to avoid presenting the whole formation to enemy crossbows. Success is suppression and preservation, not guaranteed destruction.

### Cavalry approach

The crossbows punish a predictable frontal or oblique mounted approach while Crown Spearmen or another valid frontage prevents immediate contact. If cavalry bypasses the frontage, the unit must secure and displace. Security sergeants cannot hold a fresh cavalry attack.

### Siege lane

The formation controls a known gate, wall opening, or approach with stable bolt supply and reload depth. If the position becomes a melee, native castle, urban, or household infantry must take over.

### Failed position

Return fire suppresses the edge, one recovery passage blocks, and a security file is depleted. The player abandons damaged equipment where necessary and saves operators rather than demanding one more volley.
