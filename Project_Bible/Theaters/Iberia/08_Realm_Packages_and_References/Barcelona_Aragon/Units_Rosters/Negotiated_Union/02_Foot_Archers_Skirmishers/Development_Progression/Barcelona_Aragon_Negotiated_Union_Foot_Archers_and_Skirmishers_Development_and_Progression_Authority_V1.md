## 1. Decisive Portfolio Verdict

The Negotiated Union foot missile portfolio has progressed beyond the V1.1 planning state. It no longer consists only of Crown Crossbow Sergeants with Crown Crossbow Companies awaiting creation. The current static portfolio contains one composite advance screen, one ordinary composite bow line, one ordinary composite crossbow line, and one selected composite crossbow core.

1. Crown Vanguard Bands shape and survive first contact.
2. Crown Archer Companies maintain broad ordinary arrow pressure.
3. Crown Crossbow Companies deliver ordinary protected bolt pressure across contributor sectors.
