## 3. Player-Facing Encyclopedia

Alcántara Knight Brethren are professed mounted members of the Leonese military-order tradition that developed from San Julián del Pereiro and later assumed the Alcántara name and seat. The game represents that history through one evolving card. Before the Alcántara transition, the formation appears as Pereiro Knight Brethren, a small regional order contingent tied to a fragile house, frontier obligations, uncertain heraldry, and limited mounted capacity. After a valid institutional transition, the same people-and-source family may become Alcántara Knight Brethren, with a larger castle, crossing, commandery, and western-frontier mission, but never through an automatic date upgrade or duplicated pool.

The unit exists only when a real order institution, eligible brethren, leaders, horses, equipment, standards, support, route, mission, and return plan exist. A settlement order house may expose the player to the unit, but the building cannot manufacture it. Order access remains distinct from domestic knights, mercenary companies, allied contingents, and temporary Crusade forces.

On the battlefield, the brethren are elite heavy cavalry built around controlled commitment. Their strongest contribution is not a mystical bonus near a bridge or fortress. It is the ability to hold a heavy reserve, countercharge a committed enemy, break through toward a threatened castle or crossing, secure a convoy or handoff, fight briefly in mounted melee, rescue leaders and standards, and return to order. The player pays for every loss through the order’s small knightly population, horse stock, equipment, leadership, seat security, claims, and recovery capacity.
