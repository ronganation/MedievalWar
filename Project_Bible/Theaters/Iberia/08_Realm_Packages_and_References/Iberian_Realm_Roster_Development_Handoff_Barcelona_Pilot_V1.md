## 1. Message to the Receiving Chat

Continue Iberian Realm-roster development from the current OneDrive authorities, using Barcelona as the recommended first pilot Realm.

Do not begin from a blank roster and do not treat this handoff as authority to invent a January 1062 army. First perform full due diligence across the current Barcelona package, the Iberian theater authorities, the regional and mercenary unit layers, and the strongest developed Realm military packages available for comparison. Establish which files currently control Barcelona’s military work, which are historical predecessors, which are ReviewOnly candidate authorities, and which areas remain genuinely unowned before drafting or updating anything.

The goal is to develop Barcelona’s complete military and roster architecture in a strong, playable, historically bounded Total War-style form while integrating regional recruitment, mercenary access, service obligations, source ownership, finite recruitment pools, replacement, recovery, and campaign-battle roles. Barcelona must become a coherent Realm military, not merely a list of Catalan-flavored units and not a wrapper around the Iberian mercenary system.

## 4. Recommended Barcelona Pilot Sequence

### Step 1: Due-diligence and authority map

Produce an internal working map of:

```yaml
Barcelona_authority_map:
  - CurrentControllingMilitaryAuthority
  - ExactRosterAndOpeningInventoryGate
  - PopulationCommunityAndInstitutionOwners
  - TerritorySettlementRouteAndClimateOwners
  - SupplyTransportHorseEquipmentAndRecoveryOwners
  - CommandObligationAndStrategicAccessOwners
  - ExistingUnitFamilyDefinitions
  - ExistingRegionalMercenaryAlliedAndInstitutionalInterfaces
  - CurrentGapsCollisionsAndStalePredecessors
```

Do not create a separate map file unless the result needs durable project ownership. The first preference is to use the map to strengthen the substantive Barcelona authority.

### Step 2: Full roster-family gap audit

Assess whether Barcelona has sufficient coverage for:

```yaml
roster_coverage_questions:
  - HouseholdAndComitalMountedService
  - HouseholdAndComitalFootService
  - LordlyCastleAndVassalContingents
  - UrbanMunicipalMilitiaAndWatch
  - RuralLevyAndCommunityService
  - SpearAndShieldInfantry
  - MissileInfantry
  - LightInfantryAndScreening
  - MountedScoutingAndLightCavalry
  - MediumAndHighStatusMountedService
  - SiegeGarrisonCampEscortAndConvoyFunctions
  - MaritimePortAndRiverInterfacesWhereHistoricallySupported
  - RegionalCatalanAragoneseAndPyreneanInterfaces
  - MercenaryCompanyAccess
  - ReligiousInstitutionalAndLaterHolyOrderInterfaces
  - EraDevelopmentWithoutBackProjectingLaterCrownOfAragonPower
```

A gap does not automatically justify a new unit card. First determine whether the gap belongs to a domestic family, regional family, mercenary company, contingent, institution, campaign assignment, equipment development, or supporting service.

### Step 3: Resolve the Barcelona roster architecture

Define a coherent roster rather than a pile of unit names.

```yaml
roster_architecture_must_define:
  - CoreRealmMilitaryIdentity
  - EarlyEraFoundation
  - MidAndLaterEraDevelopmentDirections
  - BattlefieldRoleCoverage
  - RecruitmentSourceAndObligation
  - QualityAndProfessionalizationBands
  - EquipmentAndHorseConstraints
  - RegionalAndMercenaryInterfaces
  - GarrisonFieldEscortSiegeAndMaritimeAssignments
  - AIRecruitmentAndUse
  - PlayerFacingProgressionAndCounterplay
  - RecoveryReplacementAndReturn
```

Preserve the Medieval II-style finite recruitment-pool philosophy. Population may inform eligibility and capacity, but population is not directly converted into Rome: Total War-style army manpower.

### Step 4: Integrate regional recruitment

Barcelona’s recommended current regional relationship is:

```yaml
Barcelona:
  Regional:
    CatalanAlmughavars:
      state: ConditionalNativeAccess
      source_owner: ExactCatalanQualifiedRegionalSource
      canonical_profile_owner: IberiaTheater
      opening_1062_access: UnsupportedAndNotInitialized
```

Do not back-project mature Almughavar formations or later Mediterranean company structures into January 1062. The family requires historical emergence, a living source, leadership, equipment, organization, political access, and compatible world state.

Barcelona may gain Realm campaign service or an external contract formation only when the exact source, release, assignment, company, route, replacement, and return requirements pass.

### Step 5: Integrate mercenary access

Use conditional route language, not active-company language:

```yaml
Barcelona:
  Mercenary:
    CatalanMercenaries:
      state: ConditionalMercenaryAccessIfValidatedCompanyExists

    MercenaryJinetes:
      state: ConditionalContractAccessIfContactCompanyRouteAndTermsPass

    IberianMercenaryMacemen:
      state: ConditionalContractAccessIfContactCompanyRouteAndTermsPass

    IberianMercenaryLightLancers:
      state: ConditionalContractAccessIfContactCompanyRouteAndTermsPass
```

These are possible service relationships. They are not opening units, native recruitment pools, guaranteed companies, or permanent Barcelona roster ownership.

Catalan Almughavars and Catalan Mercenaries must remain separate:

```yaml
CatalanAlmughavars:
  identity: TheaterOwnedRegionalFamily
  default_service: Regional

CatalanMercenaries:
  identity: ExactContractCompanyFamily
  default_service: Mercenary

shared_population_pool: false
shared_company: false
automatic_conversion: false
```

### Step 6: Reconcile all source ownership and opportunity costs

For every roster family or access relationship, identify:

```yaml
required_relationship_fields:
  - CanonicalUnitFamilyOwner
  - ExactSourceOwner
  - EligiblePopulationOrCompanySource
  - ServiceObligationOrContractBasis
  - ProtectedMinimums
  - LeadershipAndCadre
  - WeaponsArmorShieldsAmmunitionHorsesAndTack
  - TrainingAssemblyMusterAndRoute
  - CurrentAssignmentAndCompetingClaims
  - ReplacementRecoveryAndReturn
  - RealmAccessAndStrongestBlocker
```

One person, leader, company, formation, horse, equipment lot, ammunition lot, or support cadre may not serve two primary assignments simultaneously.

### Step 7: Create or refine substantive Barcelona authorities

Prefer strengthening the current controlling Barcelona military authority over generating multiple wrappers.

Create a new specialist authority only when:

```yaml
new_file_gate:
  - TheTopicHasRealUnownedStrategicContent

```

Possible legitimate outputs may include a current unified Barcelona military-and-roster successor, justified unit-family authorities, or a Realm recruitment consumer section integrated into the substantive military authority. The receiving chat should decide this only after reading the full package.

## 7. Player-Facing and Total War Design Standard

The finished Barcelona military system should be recognizable and enjoyable at player level.

```yaml
player_facing_requirements:
  - ClearCoreRosterIdentity
  - DistinctBattlefieldRoles
  - FiniteRecruitmentAndOpportunityCost
  - MeaningfulEraProgression
  - RegionalAndMercenaryVarietyWithoutRosterDilution
  - VisibleSourceAccessAndReplacementConstraints
  - StrongAIRecruitmentAndBattleUseDirections
  - CounterplayAndFailureStates
  - NoUnitSpamOrUniversalPools
```

The roster should feel like a coherent historical Realm army that can evolve through campaign conditions, not a museum list and not a generic collection of every available Iberian unit.
