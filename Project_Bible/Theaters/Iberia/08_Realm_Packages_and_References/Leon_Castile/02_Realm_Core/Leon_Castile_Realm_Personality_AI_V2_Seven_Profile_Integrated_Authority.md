## 17. Ferdinand Decision Loop

1. Receive visible regional, military, institutional, household, agreement, route, resource, and succession observations.
2. Identify the highest consequential strategic pressure.
3. Generate bounded alternatives including action, conditional action, delay, and no action.
4. Apply hard authority, owner, route, resource, external-consent, and lifecycle constraints.
5. Compare capacity, recognition, readiness, continuity, external reaction, and succession risk.
6. Propose one or a bounded small set of actions.
7. Explain recommendation, uncertainty, expected map consequences, and rejected alternatives.
8. Reevaluate only on owner event, explicit expiry, Campaign Season, or Historical horizon.
