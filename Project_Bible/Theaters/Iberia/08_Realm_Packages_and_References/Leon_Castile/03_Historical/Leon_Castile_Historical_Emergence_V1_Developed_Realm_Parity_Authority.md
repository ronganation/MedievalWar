## 33. Compatibility, Counter-Emergence, and Arbitration

Compatibility may permit parallel operation, sequencing, fusion, split, successor pathways, or negotiated coexistence. Arbitration records shared participants, resources, claims, owners, positions, consent, blockers, priority or coexistence, residual costs, losing-pathway disposition, and deterministic trace. It cannot convert tactical priority into historical truth.
