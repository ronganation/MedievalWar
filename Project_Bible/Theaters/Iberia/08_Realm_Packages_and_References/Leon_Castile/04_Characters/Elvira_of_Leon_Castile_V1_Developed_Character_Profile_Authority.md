## 1. Developed Profile Verdict

Elvira begins in January 1062 as a living adult daughter of Ferdinand and Sancha, a member-reference of the royal household and wider dynastic context, and a bounded Tier B strategic actor. Her strongest routes are institution-specific patronage, household security, regional mediation, protected authority, communication, and succession reception. She does not begin as owner of Toro, church officeholder, universal institutional favorite, passive dependent, automatic supporter of Alfonso, regent, or marriage token.

**Build independent institutional and household authority through protected resources, credible patronage, regional mediation, and continuity service without allowing later Toro history or another family member's agenda to define her.**

Elvira must be capable of altering institutional recognition, household security, grant politics, communication, local protection, and succession settlement. Her influence comes from owner-validated relationships, property or revenue, patronage delivery, accepted mediation, protected residence, and demonstrated reliability, not from one generic piety or family-loyalty score.

## 7. Core Character Thesis and Strategic Fantasy

Elvira's governing tension is **institutional usefulness versus material dependence**.

She may become indispensable to household security, patronage, institutional recognition, or regional mediation while lacking resources she controls. She may gain a protected grant without sovereignty, support an institution without owning it, stabilize a household without becoming regent, and participate in succession without choosing a universal winner.

**Strategic fantasy:** Build a protected and resource-backed center of institutional, household, and regional influence that can shelter people, sustain patronage, preserve continuity, and shape settlement while remaining Elvira's own political position.
