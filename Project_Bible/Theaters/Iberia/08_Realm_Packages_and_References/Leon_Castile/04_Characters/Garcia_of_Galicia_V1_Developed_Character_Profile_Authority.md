## 1. Developed Profile Verdict

Garcia begins in January 1062 as a living adult royal son and one of three selectable Dynastic Stakeholder perspectives beneath Ferdinand I's sovereignty. His route is Galician regional coordination, plural ecclesiastical and secular recognition, western communication, route improvement, and conditional frontier responsibility. He does not begin as King of Galicia, administrator of Galicia, ruler of Portugal, controller of Coimbra, autonomous frontier commander, or universal recipient of Galician or church support.

**Turn distance, regional plurality, institutions, routes, and frontier opportunity into coherent authority without inventing a kingdom or preloading conquest.**

Garcia must become a viable faction-leading perspective by building real access, information, delegated authority, regional cooperation, finite military capacity, and continuity. His challenge is not a generic distance penalty. It is the need to connect multiple local and institutional actors through functioning routes, credible mandates, resources, and repeated performance while maintaining influence at Ferdinand's court.

## 7. Core Character Thesis and Main-Player Fantasy

Garcia's defining tension is **regional opportunity versus fragmented execution**.

He may possess a compelling western route without holding authority over any western jurisdiction. He may receive institutional recognition without gaining secular obedience, obtain a mandate without enough roads, messengers, officers, revenue, or force to execute it, and build Galician visibility while losing influence at the royal center.

**Main-player fantasy:** Assemble a coherent western power base from plural regions, institutions, routes, and service relationships, then preserve it through succession, noninheritance, or political rupture without relying on a scripted Galicia or Coimbra conquest.
