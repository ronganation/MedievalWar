## 1. Developed Profile Verdict

Sancha of Leon is a living senior dynastic principal whose Leonese royal origin, marriage to Ferdinand, household position, patronage, ceremonial relevance, institutional access, maternal connections, and continuity role make Sancha independently important to the composite monarchy. Sancha is not an attribute of Ferdinand, a passive spouse, or a guaranteed regent. Sancha's influence must operate through household, patronage, mediation, documentary, institutional, property, recognition, communication, and succession-preparation systems.

**Preserve Leonese continuity without turning continuity into automatic rule.**

Sancha's distinct campaign function is to connect old Leonese legitimacy and memory to the living Ferdinand-Sancha household and its uncertain future. Sancha can strengthen the composite monarchy, help one or several children become credible, mediate a settlement, protect household and institutional continuity, preserve viable alternatives during crisis, or become politically narrowed by loss of access, property, health, recognition, or family trust.

## 6. Core Character Thesis

Sancha's power comes from being both inside Ferdinand's ruling household and independently connected to Leonese royal continuity. That position creates access and relevance, but not ownership. Sancha's effectiveness depends on whether household, institutions, property, communication, records, patronage, and directed relationships remain functional.

Sancha should be able to influence which children receive opportunities, how service is interpreted, whether promises become recognized expectations, how institutions respond to succession preparation, and whether a crisis produces continuity or fracture. Sancha should not simply select a winner.

## 21. Failure, Recovery, Captivity, Incapacity, and Removal

Failure in mediation, patronage, or succession preparation creates consequences through owners. Failure does not remove Sancha's identity or erase Leonese origin.

Recovery may involve repaired communication, restored access, clarified property, successful patronage, household reconciliation, revised promises, institutional mediation, or a bounded governance mandate.

Captivity or incapacity may alter access, household coordination, communication, patronage, and succession preparation. Neither condition automatically transfers Sancha's property, relationships, knowledge, or role.

Death is Lifecycle-owned. Sancha's death triggers separate reviews of household continuity, patronage, property, institutional relationships, documentary custody, succession mediation, personal Networks, and Historical Memory. No child automatically inherits Sancha's relationships, knowledge, patronage, or property.
