Urraca begins in January 1062 as a living adult daughter of Ferdinand and Sancha, a member-reference of the royal household and wider dynastic context, and a bounded Tier B strategic actor. Her value lies in household continuity, communication, mediation, urban and institutional patronage, protected grants, recognition, and crisis continuity. **Build independent standing through household access, communication, patronage, mediation, and protected authority without allowing later Zamora history or sibling politics to erase her own agency.**

## 7. Core Character Thesis and Strategic Fantasy

Urraca's governing tension is **household centrality versus independent standing**.

She may become essential to communication and continuity while lacking an independent material base. She may receive a protected grant without acquiring sovereignty, mediate among siblings without being neutral, patronize an institution without controlling it, and gain urban recognition without inheriting jurisdiction. Her strongest development creates a durable center of access, property, protection, memory, and political exchange that belongs to Urraca rather than merely extending a brother's faction.

**Strategic fantasy:** Become the person through whom a fractured royal family can still communicate, bargain, remember promises, protect dependents, and construct settlements, while retaining the ability to choose, refuse, patronize, withdraw, and defend an independent future.
