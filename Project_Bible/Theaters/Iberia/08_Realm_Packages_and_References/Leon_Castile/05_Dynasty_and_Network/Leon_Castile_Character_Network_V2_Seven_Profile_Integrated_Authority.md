## 1. Developed Network Verdict

Proceed with a directed, asymmetric, sparse, evidence-bearing Network in which relationships, knowledge, communication, promises, obligations, recognition, mediation, patronage expectations, and succession-facing statements are separate records. Genealogy initializes genealogy only. The seven-person family does not automatically generate loyalty, trust, affection, hostility, support, knowledge, access, or reciprocal state.

The developed Network turns the character profiles into map-facing causality without reducing them to a universal opinion matrix. Ferdinand communicates sovereign proposals and receives reports. Sancha provides independent continuity and mediation traffic. Sancho, Alfonso, and Garcia build distinct stakeholder records. Urraca and Elvira become consequential through communication, patronage, protection, mediation, and institutional traffic. None begins with an active political edge.
