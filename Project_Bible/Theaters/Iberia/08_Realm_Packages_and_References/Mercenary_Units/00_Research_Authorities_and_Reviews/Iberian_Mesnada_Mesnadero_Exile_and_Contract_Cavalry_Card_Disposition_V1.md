```yaml

next_profile_gate: RealmConsumerProviderLeaderChronologyAndServiceRouteSelection
```

The proposed card combined three different things that must remain separate:

1. **Mesnada** is an organization or company of armed people serving under a ruler or principal lord.
2. **Mesnadero** is a person serving in a mesnada, not automatically an exiled mercenary knight.
3. **Caballeros villanos** belong to municipal or chartered mounted service and are not mercenaries merely because they are non-noble horsemen.

The viable mercenary concept is therefore not “Mesnada Knights.” It is an exact displaced or voluntarily contracted Iberian knightly company whose leader and followers have a valid reason, legal status, employer, contract, horses, equipment, route, and replacement system.
