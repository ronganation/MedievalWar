Catalan Mercenaries are high-tier aggressive foot soldiers who combine finite close-range javelin volleys with an unusually credible transition into hard close combat. Their purpose is not to hover indefinitely as light skirmishers. They approach through suitable ground or behind friendly pressure, cast at decisive range, exploit the disruption with spear and sidearm, and either press the local advantage or disengage before armor, cavalry, missile superiority, or exhaustion reverses the fight.

They should be among the strongest javelin infantry available where an actual source company, chronology, contract, route, and support package exist. Their strength comes from fieldcraft, cohesion, commitment timing, violent local pressure, and the ability to continue fighting after missiles are expended. It does not come from automatic armor penetration, supernatural ferocity, immunity to cavalry, or universal elite status.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Catalan Mercenaries
- **Class:** High-tier javelin assault infantry
- **Era:** Later conditional emergence; never an opening 1062 unit
- **Source:** Exact Catalan, Aragonese, Valencian, related Iberian, displaced, veteran, or company network validated for the specific expression
- **Battle promise:** Deliver a hard short-range javelin cast and immediately convert local disruption into dangerous close infantry pressure
- **Best use:** Rough ground, flanks, constrained approaches, exposed valuable infantry, disrupted cavalry, raids, ambushes, siege sorties, and aggressive support beside a stable main line
- **Worst use:** Prolonged missile duels, open ground under cavalry or archery dominance, frontal grinding against fresh heavy infantry, sustained spear-wall duty, and battles after javelins and cohesion are exhausted
- **Strategic value:** Purchases a mature aggressive infantry package without creating domestic Catalan population, traditions, companies, or replacement rights
- **Strategic cost:** Expensive contract, finite veterans, short-range exposure, ammunition replacement, command autonomy, route dependence, casualty sensitivity, and possible departure
- **Signature decision:** Cast and press, cast and disengage, or withhold the volley until a better local commitment appears

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Catalan Mercenaries are aggressive contract infantry whose javelins prepare rather than replace close combat. The company closes to dangerous range, delivers a disciplined cast against a chosen target, and follows with spears, heavy knives, swords, or other source-supported weapons before the enemy recovers. Unlike ordinary skirmishers, they can remain dangerous after their ammunition is gone. Unlike heavy infantry, they cannot win by simply standing in prolonged frontal combat under missiles or cavalry pressure.

Their exact appearance and equipment depend on the admitted source. The company may draw upon Catalan, Aragonese, Valencian, frontier, veteran, displaced, or Mediterranean service networks where historically and politically supported. A specific source name must not be used until the people, chronology, military tradition, company, equipment, and service route are validated.

Use Catalan Mercenaries to create a local crisis. Conceal or screen their approach, preserve the javelins for a worthwhile target, strike before the enemy can reorganize, and avoid becoming fixed in an attritional fight. Their casualties are difficult to replace because the company’s veterans, leaders, shared signals, fieldcraft, contract reputation, and source network remain finite.

## 30. Signature Battle Cycle

```text
Identify a valuable local target and approach route
-> move through cover, rough ground, friendly pressure, or a screened lane
-> preserve javelins until range, target density, and commitment pass
-> deliver a concentrated cast or controlled band volley
-> read the result immediately
-> press with spear and sidearm if disruption, support, flank, and cohesion pass
-> disengage if the target remains ordered or cavalry, missiles, or reserves threaten
-> rally behind terrain, friendly frontage, or a company standard
-> recover survivors and reusable equipment where possible
```

## 38. Counterplay

- Detect and expose the approach.
- Use archers, crossbows, slingers, or other missiles before the company reaches casting range.
- Present layered shields, stable frontage, reserves, and supporting cavalry.
- Refuse the company’s preferred rough ground or constrained local fight.
- Absorb or avoid the initial cast, then counterattack during transition.
- Force prolonged frontal melee.
- Threaten the withdrawal route.
- Use cavalry after the javelins are committed and the company is fixed.
- Stretch the company across several threats.
- Attack transport, food, routes, pay, leadership, and recovery.
