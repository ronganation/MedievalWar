Iberian Mercenary Macemen are medium melee companies hired to provide close-combat weight where an employer lacks a suitable domestic formation. The company advances behind a screen, through cover, or beside steady infantry; enters a bounded contact; applies controlled blunt-weapon pressure; holds or supports a critical local frontage; and rotates, reforms, or breaks contact before losses destroy the company. The family remains useful in later eras because breaches, streets, camps, courtyards, local reserves, and restricted infantry fights persist even when stronger polearms, missiles, pikes, bills, axes, and armored formations become available.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Iberian Mercenary Macemen are contract foot companies armed around practical blunt weapons and organized for close fighting. Their battlefield value lies between light assault troops and true heavy infantry. They can carry a local melee, support a breach after access exists, contest a gate or street, protect siege works, reinforce a camp perimeter, or counterattack through a narrow lane. They are not built to cross exposed ground under concentrated missiles, receive heavy cavalry without support, or grind indefinitely against deeper and better-protected infantry.

The company is a mercenary formation, not a universal Iberian population type. A valid unit requires actual people, an accepted captain and subleaders, compatible mace-armed practice, serviceable weapons, any shields and protection the company truly owns, supply, mission terms, a route, and replacement or return arrangements. Castilian-Leonese, western Pyrenean, Navarrese, Aragonese, Basque, Gascon, Catalan, Valencian, Andalusi, displaced, and mixed company expressions may exist only where exact source and service evidence supports them. A regional name never creates the company.

Maces can deliver punishing blunt contact, especially where armor, shields, crowding, and short frontage make edge-based cutting weapons less reliable. That does not mean the unit ignores armor, stuns enemies, strips an armor statistic, or smashes every shield. Results depend on weapon construction, swing space, timing, fatigue, formation, hit location, and the target's complete protection.
