# PART I. FINAL PLAYER CARD

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Mercenary Andalusian Infantry
- **Class:** contract spear-and-shield defensive field infantry
- **Quality:** organized to practiced, company-specific, not automatically elite
- **Source:** an exact independent, released, displaced, veteran, or reconstituted Andalusi infantry company with valid provider, captain, files, officers, equipment, service history, route, contract, and replacement source
- **Primary weapon:** spear or source-compatible regional pole weapon
- **Shield:** expected for the core profile
- **Sidearm:** sword, dagger, or approved regional sidearm
- **Protection:** light to moderate and variable; mail only where actually owned, fitted, serviceable, and chronologically supported
- **Primary role:** establish dependable defensive frontage, receive pressure, protect supporting troops, and relieve a threatened line
- **Best use:** support a field army, protect missiles and camps, hold crossings and settlement approaches, receive cavalry or infantry pressure with prepared formation, and hand off or withdraw before flank collapse
- **Major weakness:** limited pursuit and shock, missile exposure, flank pressure, fatigue, broken ground, officer loss, equipment inconsistency, and uncertain external replacement
- **Opening State:** rejected for January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. Player-Facing Description

Mercenary Andalusian Infantry are independent or owner-released companies hired to provide the disciplined defensive foot that an employer may not possess in sufficient numbers. Spear-and-shield files form beside missile troops, camps, crossings, or threatened ordinary frontage, receive mounted or infantry pressure, rotate or relieve worn files where space and leadership permit, and preserve an ordered route for withdrawal or handoff.

The company is neither every Andalusi spearman nor a generic Muslim mercenary unit. A valid company requires actual personnel, captain and file leaders, compatible formation practice, weapons and shields, campaign equipment, provider ownership, service terms, supply, route, return, and replacement. The employer purchases bounded service, not the Andalusi population, regional field institution, urban garrison, or future recruitment base.

Mail and defensive quality are company outcomes rather than magical traits. Some files may possess mail or better protection where evidence, ownership, fit, repair, heat, fatigue, and chronology support it. Spears can deter cavalry through reach, cohesion, ground, depth, flank protection, and timing. They do not confer a hidden anti-knight multiplier or guarantee that a charge fails.

## 3. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Move behind a screen or beside the main body.
- Select a defensible ordinary frontage, missile wing, camp edge, crossing, settlement approach, or withdrawal lane.
- Dress spear-and-shield files and preserve flank connection.
- Receive mounted or infantry pressure without wasteful advance.
- Reinforce or relieve a threatened friendly formation when route, timing, leadership, and cohesion permit.
- Countermove only against exposed or disordered light-to-medium enemies.
- Shorten frontage, hand off, reform, or withdraw before encirclement and exhaustion.
- Avoid elite assault missions, extended unsupported frontage, missile concentration, and pursuit.

### Campaign

- Hire an existing company without creating domestic Andalusi service.
- Preserve provider, source, captain, personnel, equipment, contract, casualties, claims, and replacement.
- Compete with the source's home defense, garrison, field army, officers, equipment, labor, transport, and other contracts.
- Provide pay, food, water, quarters, medicine, transport, stores, equipment repair, casualty terms, and return.
- Accept that recruitment may close while surviving veterans remain under contract or recovery.
