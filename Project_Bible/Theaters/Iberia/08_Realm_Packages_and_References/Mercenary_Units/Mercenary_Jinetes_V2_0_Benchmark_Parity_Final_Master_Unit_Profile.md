# PART I. FINAL PLAYER CARD

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Mercenary Jinetes
- **Class:** contract mounted-javelin light cavalry
- **Quality:** company-specific, normally practiced or established rather than automatically elite
- **Source:** an exact independent, released, displaced, veteran, Berber, Andalusi, native Iberian, frontier, or genuinely mixed company with valid riders, horses, leaders, javelins, shields, sidearms, routes, and replacements
- **Primary weapon:** cavalry javelins
- **Secondary weapon:** retained javelin, light spear, or source-approved thrusting weapon
- **Sidearm:** sword, long knife, or approved company sidearm
- **Shield:** adarga or another source-supported light shield expression
- **Protection:** light to moderate and company-variable
- **Primary role:** harass, screen, counter enemy light cavalry, and control pursuit
- **Best use:** probe a flank, pressure exposed missiles or light horse, cast at useful range, turn before contact, protect friendly withdrawal, and pursue only after a genuine break
- **Major weakness:** finite ammunition, prepared spears, concentrated missiles, fresh heavy cavalry, poor ground, horse fatigue, command failure, and closed withdrawal space
- **Opening State:** rejected for January 1, 1062
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. Player-Facing Description

Mercenary Jinetes are independent or released mounted companies hired for the mobile work that a slower army cannot perform well. The riders screen an advance, challenge hostile scouts and light cavalry, threaten exposed archers, cast javelins from a controlled approach, and turn away before heavier troops establish contact. When an enemy formation actually breaks, they can convert disorder into pursuit. When the enemy remains formed, supported, and alert, the company must preserve its distance and withdrawal route.

The card represents a mercenary service family rather than a single people or religion. A company may be Berber or Zanata-associated, Andalusi, native Iberian, frontier-derived, displaced, veteran, or genuinely mixed. Mixed composition is never a random cosmetic blend. It requires an accepted captain, workable subleaders, shared signals, compatible mounted-javelin practice, suitable horses and tack, understood property arrangements, and replacement rights. Christian and Muslim rulers may employ qualifying companies where exact politics, routes, law, reputation, and contract permit, but coin alone does not erase faith, kinship, prior service, conduct, or political consequences.

Mercenary Jinetes do not replace Granadine Jinetes or other domestic faction expressions. Domestic jinetes draw from regional households, frontier institutions, municipal or realm obligations, and faction development. The mercenary card instead purchases an existing company for a bounded mission while leaving its people, horses, equipment, memory, and future replacement capacity attached to the source.

## 3. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Screen the army and challenge enemy scouts, raiders, skirmishers, and light cavalry.
- Approach through usable ground and release finite javelins against exposed targets.
- Retain a javelin for thrusting when close contact is unavoidable or tactically justified.
- Pressure heavy cavalry from distance only when support and withdrawal space remain secure.
- Use feigned withdrawal only when leadership, signals, ground, cohesion, support, and enemy willingness permit.
- Protect friendly withdrawal and deny reckless enemy pursuit.
- Pursue only after a real break and terminate pursuit before isolation or horse exhaustion.
- Avoid prepared spears, dense missiles, close heavy cavalry, confined ground, and prolonged melee.

### Campaign

- Hire an existing company without creating a domestic jinete population.
- Preserve company source, captain, subleaders, riders, horses, remounts, equipment, language, reputation, and replacement route.
- Compete with other employers and the source's domestic defense, escort, messenger, breeding, and economic claims.
- Pay for food, water, fodder, quarters, medicine, veterinary care, ransom, equipment repair, travel, and return.
- Accept that losses, arrears, grievances, route closure, political change, or replacement failure may narrow or close future access.
