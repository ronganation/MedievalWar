The Bidets survive the card gate because the evidence supports a recognizable regional foot-javelin and light-spear family with a distinct equipment package, combat cycle, source-access decision, and campaign relationship. The family does not survive as one duplicate card per named realm. Navarre receives the primary card candidate; Aragonese and Basque-qualified sources receive conditional access or source variants when their actual gates pass.

## 2. Why a Separate Card Survives

```yaml
card_survival_factors:
  RepeatableSourceCandidate: true
  DistinctEquipmentPackage: true
  DistinctBattlefieldRole: true
  DistinctCampaignAccessDecision: true
  DistinctRecoveryAndReturnRelationship: true
  MovableCampaignUse: true
  PlayerReadability: true
  NotWeaponVariationAlone: true
  NotCosmeticRegionalVariantAlone: true
```

The family is more than generic javelin infantry because it combines:

- a Navarrese and Spanish-frontier source lane;
- conditional Aragonese and Basque regional access;
- limited foot-javelin release;
- light spear or lance follow-up;
- coutel or comparable sidearm;
- light or absent protection;
- harassment, threat, withdrawal, and repositioning;
- vulnerability to fresh shock and prolonged heavy combat;
- external-employment and return questions that do not exist for ordinary local levy cards.

## 6. One-Screen Candidate Card Contract

- **Working unit:** Navarrese Bidets
- **Class:** Regional foot javelin and light-spear infantry
- **Primary source:** Validated Navarrese regional source, with conditional Aragonese and Basque regional access
- **Battle promise:** Limited foot-javelin harassment followed by light spear threat, repositioning, or withdrawal
- **Best use:** Screening, broken or prepared ground, flank pressure, approach disruption, support against exposed light troops, and harassment before heavier formations engage
- **Worst use:** Receiving fresh cavalry, standing in prolonged heavy melee, direct assault, deep line holding, and extended missile exchange
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Useful regional capability with constrained source, ammunition, access, replacement, and return requirements
- **Counterplay:** Shock, armor, sustained missile pressure, forced melee, pursuit, isolation, and denial of withdrawal routes
