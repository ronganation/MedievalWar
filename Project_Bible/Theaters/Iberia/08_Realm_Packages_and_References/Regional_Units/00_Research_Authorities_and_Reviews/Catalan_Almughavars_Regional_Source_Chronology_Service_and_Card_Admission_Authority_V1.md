Almughavars survive as a distinct later regional family because their intended player decision is not the Bidet decision. Bidets preserve a short harassment window and withdraw before heavy contact. Almughavars accept a deeper operational commitment: raid or disrupt, close on a suitable target, sustain aggressive local pressure, exploit dislocation, and then regroup, continue the mission, or transition into later company service.

The family begins as a theater-owned regional source. A later mercenary company is a service state created by actual veterans, captains, contracts, routes, pay, transport, and political access. Mercenary employment does not create a second population pool or a duplicate unit family.
