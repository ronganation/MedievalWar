## 3. Player-Facing Description

Catalan Almughavars are regional campaign infantry whose light equipment, fieldcraft, leadership, and aggressive small-group practice support raiding, disruption, difficult approaches, decisive short-range missile use, and dangerous close exploitation. They are not ordinary skirmishers. When the target, ground, support, and withdrawal relationship pass, Almughavars can press beyond the initial cast and fight hard enough to turn local disorder into a captured position, broken flank, disrupted camp, severed route, or wider operational opportunity.

The family remains fragile when fixed in the wrong fight. Heavy infantry, stable spear or pike frontage, cavalry in open ground, long-range missiles, blocked exits, poor supply, lost captains, and repeated uncompensated casualties can hollow the formation quickly. Their strong campaign identity does not remove food, water, shelter, footwear, ammunition, medicine, command, transport, or recovery.

An Almughavar formation may later accept external contract service when its source, leadership, contract, route, support, and return terms pass. That does not transform the formation into the separate Catalan Mercenaries card. Catalan Mercenaries remain exact high-tier contract javelin-assault companies with their own company sources, pools, employment relationships, battlefield emphasis, recovery, and availability.
