## 5. Player-Facing Description

Navarrese Bidets are light regional infantry carrying darts or javelins, a lance or thrusting spear, and a coutel or comparable sidearm. They harass approaching or exposed enemies, threaten light troops after missile release, and withdraw or reposition before heavier opponents can pin them. Their equipment is light, their ammunition limited, and their survival depends on terrain, spacing, support, and a clear route away from fresh shock.

The card uses Navarre as the primary source lane while permitting conditional Aragonese and Basque source variants under the same family. Foreign employers such as France do not gain the regional population or replacement pool merely by receiving a contingent.

## 19. Battlefield Rhythm

- Approach through suitable open, broken, prepared, or screening ground.
- Maintain spacing and identify an exposed, light, disordered, or approaching target.
- Release limited darts or javelins under leader control.
- Reposition, threaten with the lance or spear, or support heavier friendly troops.
- Avoid becoming fixed in prolonged combat.
- Withdraw before fresh shock closes the route.
- Rally behind terrain or friendly protection.
- Preserve ammunition, equipment, and source personnel where the tactical objective no longer justifies exposure.

## 20. Strengths

- Distinct foot-javelin capability.
- Mobile screening and approach disruption.
- Light-spear threat after missile release.
- Useful against exposed light or already disordered targets.
- Valuable in broken, prepared, or flank positions.
- Conditional access across Navarrese, Aragonese, and Basque-qualified sources without duplicate cards.
- Can support heavier infantry and cavalry without becoming the line itself.
- Regional source identity creates meaningful campaign access and replacement decisions.

## 21. Weaknesses

- Limited ammunition.
- Light or absent armor.
- Weak against fresh cavalry shock.
- Poor for prolonged heavy melee.
- Vulnerable to concentrated missile pressure.
- Requires spacing and a withdrawal route.
- Vulnerable when pinned, isolated, or pursued.
- Source and replacement constraints increase during external service.
- Does not provide heavy line holding or assault capability.
