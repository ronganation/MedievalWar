## 9. Source Plan

### Priority source classes

1. Modern academic histories of taifa-period al-Andalus and the Aftasids.
2. Primary chronicles or reliable translations addressing al-Muzaffar, the Aftasids, and Ferdinand I.
3. Numismatic catalogues for ruler names and chronology.
4. Archaeological and urban studies for Badajoz, Mérida, Lisbon, Évora, Santarém, Alcántara, Coimbra, Viseu, and Lamego.
5. Historical geography and route studies for the Guadiana, Tagus, Mondego, Douro, and Atlantic corridors.
6. Specialist work on parias, political suzerainty, and taifa diplomacy.
7. Visual and material-culture sources only after identity and chronology stabilize.
