## 3. Developed Character Verdict

Al-Muzaffar is the principal human and AI reference through which the Badajoz taifa becomes legible at the January 1062 opening. His realm is broad, strategically important, and culturally productive, but its practical authority is uneven. It is pressured by recent northern losses, continuing exposure around Coimbra, paria coercion, rivalry with Seville, long communications, incomplete local administration, and unresolved dynastic continuity.

**Player fantasy:** Hold together a wide western taifa through informed personal rule, selective delegation, fiscal endurance, regional bargaining, frontier preparation, literary prestige, and dynastic continuity, while ensuring that the realm can survive the ruler around whom it is organized.

**Central campaign question:** Can al-Muzaffar convert established Aftasid sovereignty and court authority into durable regional government before distance, tribute pressure, external rivalry, military overextension, or an unresolved succession makes his personal strength a structural weakness?

The profile must remain valid if al-Muzaffar survives far beyond the disputed chronology, dies earlier through valid gameplay, loses Coimbra, preserves Coimbra, reconciles with Seville, escalates against Seville, pays tribute, resists tribute, delegates effectively, overcentralizes, produces a peaceful transition, or leaves competing continuity pathways. Later history is possibility and pressure, not destiny.

## 10. Opening Strategic Function

### 10.1 Stabilize the center without pretending the periphery is settled

Al-Muzaffar may prioritize records, petitions, audiences, regional reports, revenue verification, appointment requests, route inspection, and differentiated treatment of Badajoz, Mérida, Lisbon, Santarém, Évora, and Coimbra. Each site requires owner-valid control and capacity. A settlement colored as Badajoz does not automatically provide money, soldiers, ships, obedience, or accurate information.

### 10.2 Decide how to answer northern pressure

The ruler may investigate tribute demands, frontier intelligence, Coimbra readiness, route capacity, garrison needs, force availability, and the cost of resistance, negotiation, delay, partial compliance, or strategic concession. No option creates a payment, war, peace, or protection agreement without its owner.

### 10.3 Contain or redirect rivalry with Seville

The player may seek intelligence, negotiate, posture, reinforce, mediate, bargain over disputed interests, prepare a contingency, or abstain from escalation. Historical rivalry is a pressure and memory candidate, not an automatic hostile relationship or active war.

### 10.4 Turn court prestige into practical capacity

Literary and scholarly patronage may support legitimacy narratives, documentation, consultation, education, diplomacy, urban prestige, and institutional confidence where owner records exist. Prestige does not substitute for money, offices, routes, enforcement, or community consent.

### 10.5 Prepare continuity without scripting conflict

Al-Muzaffar may observe, educate, consult, test, provision, delegate to, or withhold responsibility from Yahya and Umar through valid systems. Both remain independent actors. Neither is automatically heir, co-ruler, governor, commander, rival, favorite, or guaranteed successor.

## 11. Opening Decision Portfolio

Each decision must expose authority, evidence, resources, opportunity cost, reversibility, and failure conditions.

1. **Verify the northern demand.** Determine whether a current tribute demand exists, who issued it, what evidence supports it, and what military threat backs it.
2. **Review Coimbra readiness.** Request current control, garrison, supply, route, command, and local acceptance evidence without inventing missing records.
3. **Choose a regional attention plan.** Prioritize the Guadiana center, Tagus corridor, Atlantic interface, Mondego frontier, or southern contingency.
4. **Create a bounded delegation request.** Select a real recipient only if Registry and Governance owners validate identity and mandate.
5. **Audit long-distance reporting.** Compare delays, contradictions, missing reports, and confidence across regional channels.
6. **Set Seville posture.** Choose observation, negotiation, defensive preparation, limited pressure, or de-escalation.
7. **Set court patronage priorities.** Choose documentary capacity, scholarship, legal consultation, public prestige, or diplomatic correspondence, subject to resources and recipients.
8. **Review Aftasid continuity.** Evaluate Yahya and Umar separately without ranking them through hidden preference or later history.
9. **Prepare government during absence.** Define handback, emergency authority, reporting, treasury access, military bounds, and succession contingencies before travel or command.
10. **Choose frontier risk tolerance.** Preserve scarce forces, reinforce a threatened surface, seek an agreement, or accept a bounded loss when capacity is inadequate.
11. **Review treasury exposure.** Compare defense, tribute, administration, patronage, household continuity, and reserve requirements without fabricating balances.
12. **Establish a failure recovery reserve.** Preserve money, recruits, equipment, transport, officers, and political flexibility through their owners.

## 22. Failure, Recovery, Captivity, Incapacity, and Removal

Failure is not deletion. A failed tribute negotiation, lost fortress, collapsed mandate, empty treasury, defeated army, disputed decision, or weakened succession produces evidence and consequences through owners.

### 22.1 Recovery portfolio

- Verify what failed and why.
- Preserve surviving people and finite recruitment pools.
- Rebuild supply, equipment, horses, officer capacity, and reserves.
- Restore routes and reports.
- Reconcile or replace failed mandates through Governance.
- Repair treasury and commitments through Economy.
- Negotiate ransom or release if captured.
- Protect household and continuity during incapacity.
- Review objectives and accept strategic contraction where necessary.
- Rebuild institutional confidence through delivered action, not free reputation.

### 22.2 Captivity and incapacity

Captivity does not automatically transfer sovereignty. Incapacity does not automatically select a successor. Both require lifecycle state, location, custody, communication, government, emergency authority, military, household, dynasty, succession, and Opening State owners.

### 22.3 Death and succession

A valid death ends the living person's agency but does not directly transfer titles, realm control, treasury, forces, offices, households, agreements, or relationships. Each owner evaluates continuity. The disputed historical endpoints never override campaign state.
