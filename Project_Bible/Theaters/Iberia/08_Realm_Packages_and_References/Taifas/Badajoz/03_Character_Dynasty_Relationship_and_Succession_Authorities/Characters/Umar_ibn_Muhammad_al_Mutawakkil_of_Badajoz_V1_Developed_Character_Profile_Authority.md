Umar is an independent Aftasid continuity candidate whose strategic value lies in becoming a credible alternative route for service, regional coordination, military preparation, diplomacy, and dynastic survival without being reduced to the eventual rival or replacement of Yahya. **Central campaign question:** Can Umar gain real responsibility and recognition without allowing later Évora association, sibling comparison, or eventual sovereignty to manufacture an opening power base?

The profile remains valid if Umar becomes ruler, receives bounded regional service, develops as a commander or diplomat, supports Yahya, competes on specific issues, forms an independent household, becomes a noninheriting principal, is marginalized, fails and recovers, or never activates at opening.
