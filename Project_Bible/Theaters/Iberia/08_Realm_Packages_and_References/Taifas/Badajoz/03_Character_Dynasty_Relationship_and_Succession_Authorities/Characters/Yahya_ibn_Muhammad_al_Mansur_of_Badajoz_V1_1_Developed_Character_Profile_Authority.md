## 3. Developed Character Verdict

Yahya is an independent Aftasid continuity candidate whose opening value lies in possibility rather than predetermined rank. He gives Badajoz a named person through whom administrative, regional, military, diplomatic, institutional, and dynastic preparation may become real campaign history. His profile is strong in playable potential but strict about evidence because exact age and opening agency remain unresolved.

**Character fantasy:** Earn a place in the future of the Aftasid realm through demonstrated service, judgment, learning, cooperation, and public recognition, while retaining a meaningful future if another person succeeds.

**Central question:** Can Yahya become a credible continuity principal without allowing kinship, expectation, or hindsight to substitute for preparation, authority, capacity, and consent?

The profile remains valid if Yahya becomes ruler, serves another ruler, governs temporarily, commands conditionally, becomes an institutional or diplomatic principal, is marginalized, fails and recovers, survives outside inheritance, or never receives active opening agency.
