```text

Hard constraints pass

```

## 79. Disengage State

Disengagement may be strategically correct where cost, exposure, or owner feasibility changes. The AI must explain sunk cost, recoverable consequences, affected theaters, and alternatives.

No relationship, agreement, force, or office is terminated directly by AI.

---

## 80. Abstention State

Abstention is valid when:

```text
No lawful command exists
No owner can execute
Information is catastrophically inadequate
All options violate hard constraints
Expected cost is disproportionate
Waiting preserves more strategic value than acting
```

Abstention records the next useful observation or event trigger.
