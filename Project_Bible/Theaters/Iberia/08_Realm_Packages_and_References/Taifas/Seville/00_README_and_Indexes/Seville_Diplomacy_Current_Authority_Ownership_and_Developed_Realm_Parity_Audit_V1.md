## 9. Ownership and Nonownership Finding

### 9.1 Diplomacy owns

- Seville-facing diplomatic identity and policy definitions.
- Realm-authorized principals, representatives, audiences, proposals, counterproposals, agreements, obligations, performance, breach, remedy, withdrawal, and termination interfaces.
- Seville-facing recognition, paria, tribute, pressure, protection, truce, access, service, alliance, coalition, war, peace, and settlement coordination records.
- External-pressure evaluation and player-facing explanation.
- External cross-Realm locks and received owner receipts.

### 9.2 Diplomacy does not own

- Person identity, age, health, agency, location, consent, dynasty, household, succession, or kinship.
- Government offices, jurisdiction, administrative control, title, claim, law, or institutional action.
- Treasury, property, revenue, goods, settlements, routes, ports, transport, supply, recruitment pools, active forces, command, or readiness.
- External Realm identities, private state, intent, consent, obligations, or performance.
- Registry publication, Opening State activation, machine-readable promotion, importer execution, runtime mutation, save format, migration, or Unreal assets.
