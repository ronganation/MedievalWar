The following content is strong and should survive copy-first modernization unless direct evidence requires correction:

- al-Mu'tadid is the January 1062 sovereign.
- al-Mu'tamid is succession-relevant but not automatically a formal heir.
- Ismail remains ReviewOnly unless identity, chronology, and opening life state close.
- Seville is an Abbadid urban-dynastic expansion polity.
- Integration before expansion is the primary strategic thesis.
- Later conquest, 1063 tribute pressure, 1069 succession, and Almoravid involvement remain non-scripted.

### Identity spine

- realm Seville and settlement Seville are distinct.
- Abbadid and Abbasid must not collide.
- Huelva and Saltés remain separate settlements.
- Saltés has one settlement identity and no duplicate site identity.
- Santa María do Algarve and Faro remain one settlement identity.
- Algeciras settlement, former polity, and operations remain separate.
- Former polities remain definition-only unless separately reactivated by authority.

### Map and economy spine

- seven settlement nodes remain distinct.
- Seville is the capital core.
- Mértola is Guadiana depth.
- Niebla, Huelva, and Saltés form a non-uniform western integration surface.
- Santa María do Algarve provides Algarve reach.
- Algeciras provides Strait projection without automatic Strait control.
- prosperity is not treasury cash.
- political authority is not fiscal realization.
- port, route, market, mint, property, production, stockpile, and shipment operations remain separately owned.

### Military spine

- Seville uses a flexible urban taifa combined-arms direction.
- military potential is not an active force.
- settlement authority does not create recruitment pools.
- strategic importance does not create garrisons.
- household context does not create guards.
- port potential does not create fleets.
- recruitment requires finite sources, authority, people, cooperation, equipment, funds, command, route, supply, and season.
- losses and replacement must matter.

### Diplomacy and AI spine

- pressure is not an agreement.
- payment is not submission.
- protection is not alliance.
- access is not knowledge.
- invitation is not intervention.
- intervention is not annexation.
- shared religion does not create alliance.
- religious difference does not create war.
- Realm AI reads approved observations, explains, proposes, and never mutates owner state directly.
