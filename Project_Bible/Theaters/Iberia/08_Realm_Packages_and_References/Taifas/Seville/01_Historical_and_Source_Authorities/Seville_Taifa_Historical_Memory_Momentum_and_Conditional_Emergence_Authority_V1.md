Seville begins in 1062 with a powerful but unstable historical inheritance. The Abbadid realm is remembered through urban sovereignty, dynastic rule, expansion, coercion, absorbed local polities, contested legitimacy, cultural prestige, external pressure, and the unresolved future of succession. Historical Memory records how actors interpret already owned facts. Historical Momentum records pressures and capacities produced by the current world state. Conditional Emergence allows historically recognizable people, institutions, relationships, conflicts, and outcomes to become possible only when date windows, evidence, identities, and world-state conditions align. ## 16. Contested Memory

Two memories may interpret the same owned fact differently. The system preserves source, audience, confidence, visibility, and contest rather than selecting a universal narrative automatically.

Victorious Narrative != Uncontested Memory
