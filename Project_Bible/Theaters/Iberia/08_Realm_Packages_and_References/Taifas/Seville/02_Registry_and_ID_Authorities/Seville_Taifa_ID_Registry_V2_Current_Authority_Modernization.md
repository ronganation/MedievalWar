## 3. Registry Vocabulary

### Proposed ID

A draft persistence identifier awaiting owner adjudication. It is not canonical and cannot be consumed.

### Preferred display name

The default presentation candidate. It is not a persistence key.

### Search/display variant

A normalized historical, linguistic, or modern form that resolves to the same proposed identity for discovery. It is not an alias or migration.

### Definition-only

An identity may exist for provenance and history but is prohibited from opening activation.

### ReviewOnly

A possible identity whose uniqueness or chronology is not sufficiently closed. It receives no proposed ID.

### Pending external lock

A request to an external owner. It creates no lock, identity clone, relationship, or scenario fact.

### Quarantine

A claim is preserved as unresolved validation metadata and cannot enter active content.

## 6. Identity-by-Identity Guardrails

### 6.1 Seville realm

`realm.seville` identifies the Abbadid polity. It does not identify the city, dynasty, government, household, sovereign office, title, army, treasury, or territorial polygon.

The proposed ID contains no opening controller, ruler, capital, tributary status, or later Almoravid status.

### 6.2 Abbadid dynasty

`dynasty.abbadid` is distinct from Abbasid identities. The validator must perform normalized collision checks against:

- Abbasid;
- al-Abbadi;
- Banu Abbad;
- display forms using or omitting the initial ayn;
- dynasty and person-name variants.

The dynasty identity creates no government, realm, household roster, succession rule, or property.

### 6.3 al-Mu'tadid

The person ID does not encode sovereign status. Reign and ruler role remain provenance and future Opening State concerns.

The Registry does not establish exact age, date of birth, location, office, title, command, household, relationships, traits, or reputation.

### 6.4 al-Mu'tamid

The person ID remains stable before and after any possible accession. No second person ID may be created for the later sovereign.

The Registry does not establish:

- formal heir office;
- exact designation date;
- governorship;
- command;
- location;
- age anchor;
- delegated authority;
- guaranteed accession.

### 6.5 Seville settlement

`settlement.seville` is distinct from `realm.seville`. It does not encode capital status, controller, palace, walls, port, mint, market, treasury, garrison, or property.

### 6.6 Mértola

The settlement and first-taifa IDs are distinct. Political absorption before opening does not enter either ID.

No Guadiana route, fortress, fiscal administration, garrison, recruitment, supply, market, property, or local acceptance is created.

### 6.7 Niebla

The settlement and first-taifa IDs are distinct. The settlement identity does not encode direct administration, walls, fortress, route, tax, supply, garrison, or market operation.

### 6.8 Huelva and Saltés

`settlement.huelva` and `settlement.saltes` are separate identities. Neither may redirect to the other.

`historical_polity.taifa_saltes_huelva` preserves the paired polity without converting either place into a realm.

### 6.9 Saltés type decision

Saltés is proposed as one Settlement identity because the current authority accepts settlement-level archaeological evidence.

The following duplicate is prohibited:

```text

```

Strategic characteristics belong to Geography and specialist operations, not a second identity.

The settlement identity creates no port, harbor, fortress, customs, metallurgy, fisheries, route, fleet, garrison, supply, population, or scripted abandonment state.

### 6.10 Santa María do Algarve and Faro

`settlement.santa_maria_algarve` is a single identity review for the historical settlement associated with Faro.

It is distinct from:

- the Algarve region;
- the former taifa;
- a port;
- a qadi office;
- walls;
- a tax jurisdiction;
- a trade route.

The proposed variants remain search/display forms. They are not an alias chain.

### 6.11 Algeciras

The settlement and former-polity IDs are distinct. The 1055/1058 chronology variance remains evidence metadata and does not alter the stable identity.

The settlement ID creates no port, fortress, customs, fleet, Strait route, garrison, supply corridor, or Almoravid interface.
