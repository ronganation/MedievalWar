### 0.2 Evidence and benchmark boundary

The developed Seville package supports a strong court and cultural direction through the literary identities of <Person>al-Mu'tadid</Person> and <Person>al-Mu'tamid</Person>, the dynasty's urban political setting, and the realm's prestige-conscious personality. External scholarship describes the taifa of Seville as an important cultural center and identifies both rulers as poets and patrons, while also stating that the court reached its zenith later under <Person>al-Mu'tamid</Person>. Therefore, the opening authority may establish cultural direction and patronage systems, but it must not back-project the later zenith, later court members, later family, or later patronage network into 1 January 1062.

Developed-realm benchmarks maintain separate household, court, character-network, communities, culture, and historical-memory authorities. This file uses those coverage patterns without copying another realm's household or court constitution.
