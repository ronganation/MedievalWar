## 1. Concise Verdict

Seville's opening domestic problem is not whether seven settlements are simply loyal or disloyal. It is whether Abbadid superior authority can become durable rule across distinct local communities, institutions, elites, property interests, memories, obligations, and settlement conditions without exhausting coercive, fiscal, administrative, or military capacity. Domestic order is a layered, audience-specific condition produced by legitimacy, compliance, service delivery, lawful process, security, communication, accommodation, and credible remedy. Grievance may exist without unrest; unrest may exist without rebellion; coercion may restore immediate compliance while damaging longer-term legitimacy.

## 30. Capital and Periphery Balance

Capital centralization may improve coordination while increasing distance, exclusion, or delayed response elsewhere. Local accommodation may improve compliance while reducing direct uniformity. Neither centralization nor local autonomy is inherently legitimate or illegitimate. Outcomes depend on authority, process, delivery, audience, and current conditions.

## 31. Coercive Legacy Boundary

Historical coercion may become a memory or grievance basis only for identified audiences with evidence and owner state. It does not assign fear, hatred, obedience, or resistance universally. New coercion may produce immediate security, delayed grievance, elite fragmentation, fiscal strain, or external consequences through owners.

## 32. Fiscal Order Boundary

Assessment, collection, arrears, exemption, remittance, corruption, and relief are Economy and Governance states. Fiscal burden may contribute to grievance only when an affected audience, obligation, event, harm, knowledge, and remedy pathway are represented.

## 33. Property and Livelihood Boundary

Property loss, disrupted access, damaged production, requisition, or disputed possession may create grievance through Property, Economy, Population, and Law owners. Political control does not permit automatic confiscation or compensation.

## 34. Religion and Institutional Boundary

Religious and institutional recognition may support legitimacy, remedy, mediation, records, education, charity, or public confidence when actual institutions and actors exist. Shared religion does not create uniform legitimacy. Religious difference does not create disorder.

## 35. Military Security Boundary

Military forces may deter violence, protect routes, support relief, enforce owner-approved decisions, or intensify grievance. Military presence does not automatically produce order. No opening garrison, commander, coercive operation, or rebellion exists.

## 36. Historical Memory Interface

Memory categories such as expansion, absorption, coercion, capital prestige, former-polity continuity, succession uncertainty, and external pressure may inform domestic observations only when audience and evidence are defined. Memory never substitutes for an active attitude record.
