## 1. Concise Verdict

Seville's territorial power should not be treated as ownership of every field, residence, workshop, market space, warehouse, fortification, quay, institution, or productive site within the seven politically bound settlements. Property is a typed bundle of claims, possession, use, income, access, maintenance, obligation, and remedy. Buildings are physical assets with condition and capacity, not abstract unlock icons. Productive infrastructure connects land, labor, knowledge, materials, water, transport, security, and investment, but it creates no output until the relevant owners validate operation.
