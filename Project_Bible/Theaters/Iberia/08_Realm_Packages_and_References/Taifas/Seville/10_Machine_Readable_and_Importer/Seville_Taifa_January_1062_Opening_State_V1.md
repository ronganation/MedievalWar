## 1. Concise Verdict

Initialize one active Seville realm on 1 January 1062 under al-Mu'tadid, with Seville as the capital settlement and principal political center. Activate al-Mu'tadid and al-Mu'tamid as living character records. Treat al-Mu'tamid as succession-relevant without creating a formal heir office. Bind Seville superior political authority to the seven supported settlements while leaving local administration, direct control, fortresses, ports, routes, markets, taxes, garrisons, and supply unresolved unless a specialist owner later validates them.

The opening is intentionally sparse. It creates no army, command, treasury, property, mint, port operation, tax record, agreement, relationship, officeholder, active former polity, or later conquest.
