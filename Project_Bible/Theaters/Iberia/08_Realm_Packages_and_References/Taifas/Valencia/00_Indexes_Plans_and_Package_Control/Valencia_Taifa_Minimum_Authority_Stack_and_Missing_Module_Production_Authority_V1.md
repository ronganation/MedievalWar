## 1. Corrected Verdict

The Valencia package is not ready to move directly from README creation to package freeze. The completed Character, Dynasty, Government, Religious and Juristic, Communities, Territory, Military, Visual, Historical, Core, and Opening State authorities form a strong developed design spine, but developed Realm precedents show that Valencia still lacks essential identity, economy, diplomacy, AI, registry, readiness, machine-readable, validation, importer, and implementation-handoff authorities.

The minimum target is not file-count parity for its own sake. The target is owner and capability coverage sufficient to prevent the Opening State, importer, or Unreal layer from inventing identity, money, property, agreements, AI behavior, runtime bindings, or validation claims.

## 5. Why the Four Substantive Authorities Are Required

### 5.1 ID Registry, aliases, quarantine, migration, and external locks

The current authorities use Realm, person, site, route, force-family, relationship, office, institution, and Opening State identifiers, but Valencia has no dedicated current Registry authority controlling stable IDs, aliases, ownership, quarantine, external locks, collision rejection, and migration. The Opening State must not become the de facto Registry.

### 5.2 Economy, treasury, property, production, markets, trade, stocks, and provisioning

Economic material is currently distributed across Core, Government, Communities, Territory, Religious and Juristic, Military, and Opening State. Valencia needs one specialist owner for treasury bands, custody, bullion and mint inputs, property classes, endowments interfaces, production capacities, markets, contracts, credit, prices, stocks, provisioning, transport inputs, emergency finance, receipts, and rollback. The authority must not invent exact balances or inventories.

### 5.3 Diplomacy, external relations, agreements, pressure, and cross-Realm locks

Toledo, Denia, Alpuente, Tortosa, Zaragoza, Christian powers, mercenary markets, transit, guarantees, advisers, contingents, trade, safe conduct, tribute, prisoner exchange, and intervention risk are strategically central. They currently remain dispersed and ExternalLock. Valencia needs one owner authority for relationship surfaces, knowledge limits, proposal and agreement separation, reciprocal-owner receipts, expiration, breach, withdrawal, and sovereignty consequences.

### 5.4 Realm personality, AI, information discipline, and decision explanation

AI priorities exist inside Core and specialist documents, but Valencia lacks a dedicated AI authority defining strategic personality, uncertainty use, owner-routed proposals, objective selection, resource checks, reversal thresholds, explanation requirements, anti-cheating rules, and campaign-battle continuity. AI must not become an execution owner or use future historical knowledge.
