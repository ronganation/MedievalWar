### 0.1 Concise Verdict

Valencia has enough current project authority to establish a controlled evidence and chronology register for the January 1062 scenario opening. The strongest project-supported propositions concern Abd al-Malik's recognized succession after Abd al-Aziz's death, Amirid dynastic continuity, Balansiya as capital, the capital's fortification and monetary functions, the huerta as a water-dependent productive core, the marriage connection to al-Mamun of Toledo, and a functioning inherited administration.

The project sources do not resolve Abd al-Malik's exact age, a formal regency, a documented heir, the identity and exact title of the principal senior administrator, the personal name of the Toledan consort, exact outer territorial control, exact quantities, or an opening alliance, protection agreement, military-access right, or Toledan overlordship. Those gaps remain explicit and do not justify invention.

This register records what the reviewed project authorities assert and how those assertions may be used. It does not claim fresh primary-source or specialist-source revalidation. External historical research remains a separate required deepening pass.
