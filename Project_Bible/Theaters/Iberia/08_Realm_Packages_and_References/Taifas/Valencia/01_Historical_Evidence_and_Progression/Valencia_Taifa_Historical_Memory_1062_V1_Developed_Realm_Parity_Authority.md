### 0.1 Developed-Realm Verdict

Valencia begins with several usable and conflicting pasts, not one realm-wide memory. The Amirid household can remember inherited dignity and burdens. Officials can remember continuity and professional competence. Jurists can remember lawful order and limits on coercion. Merchants can remember security, contracts, and seizure. Huerta communities can remember cooperation, repair, and extraction. Military clients can remember protection, pay, and service. Protected communities can remember predictable rule and vulnerability. External courts can remember Valencia as a serious polity, a useful partner, a dependent, or an opportunity.

Campaign History owns what happened. Historical Memory owns who received an account of it, what they accepted or disputed, how they interpreted it, how salient and accessible it remains, and how it may be transmitted, commemorated, suppressed, contradicted, reinterpreted, or forgotten.

No memory in this file is automatically active. Every opening instance requires a valid holder, subject, acquisition path, evidence access, interpretation, visibility, and Opening State owner receipt.

## 8. Memory Lifecycle

```text
Eligible fact, experience, object, institution, or narrative exists
then a valid acquisition or transmission path reaches a holder
then holder-specific reception and interpretation occur
then confidence, salience, availability, and visibility are recorded
then reinforcement, contradiction, contest, commemoration, suppression, reinterpretation, or erosion may occur
then transmission to another holder may become possible
then owner-routed implications may affect explanation, evaluation, or eligibility
then the memory may persist, transform, become dormant, be archived, be suppressed, or lose access
```

Memory age alone does not determine decay. Records, ritual, visible landscapes, political usefulness, repeated experience, institutional custody, household teaching, contradiction, displacement, and generational distance may affect persistence through owner-defined causes.

## 12. Collision, Contest, and Coexistence Portfolio

### 12.1 Predecessor Continuity Collision

The memory of Abd al-Aziz may be used to support Abd al-Malik, protect inherited offices, resist reform, justify selective confirmation, or criticize unsuccessful change. These interpretations may coexist among different holders. No global resolution overwrites them.

### 12.2 Protection Collision

The Toledan connection may be remembered as prudent kin diplomacy, deterrence, dependency, humiliation, rescue, opportunity, or intrusion. Valencian and Toledan audiences remain separately authored. Toledo's interpretation is externally owned.

### 12.3 Water and Extraction Collision

Court records may describe repair as sovereign public work while local communities remember burden allocation, delayed compensation, or negotiated custom. Documentary possession does not make one account universally accepted.

### 12.4 Walls and Countryside Collision

Urban audiences may celebrate refuge while huerta or suburban audiences remember strategic sacrifice. Successful capital defense does not erase losses outside the walls.

### 12.5 Law and Emergency Collision

The palace may invoke necessity while jurists, merchants, endowment custodians, or communities remember procedure, promises, compensation, and jurisdiction. A lawful-memory claim must reference actual process, not merely sovereign assertion.

### 12.6 Public Narrative and Private Doubt

A holder may repeat Amirid continuity publicly while privately doubting the new ruler, or invoke predecessor memory instrumentally without personal acceptance. Historical Memory records the reception state without inferring private emotion.

## 13. Transmission, Custody, and Availability Rules

- Direct participants, witnesses, narrators, custodians, and holders must remain separate.
- A scribe may preserve a record without sharing its interpretation.
- A qadi or institution may hold documentary access without every jurist knowing the contents.
- A household may transmit a narrative selectively.
- Public recitation does not prove acceptance.
- A monument, wall, canal, coin, or building can prompt memory but does not explain itself.
- Rumor retains source-chain weakness.
- Foreign courts receive only through valid diplomatic, merchant, traveler, captive, or documentary channels.
- Suppression changes availability and transmission, not the underlying historical fact.

## 14. Reinforcement, Erosion, Reinterpretation, and Replacement

Reinforcement requires a cause such as repeated relevant experience, independent testimony, documentary access, commemoration, visible maintenance, fulfilled promise, renewed conflict, or current decision relevance. Erosion may follow lack of access, generational distance, institutional loss, contradiction, changed material conditions, displacement, or declining political usefulness.

Reinterpretation preserves continuity of the remembered subject while changing its meaning. Replacement requires a newer memory with an actual subject and acquisition path. The new ruler can acquire personal credit for a performed success, but memory of Abd al-Aziz is not automatically transferred or deleted.

## 18. Character, Institution, Community, and Cross-Realm Interfaces

Character profiles may consume only holder-specific memory summaries with evidence, acquisition, and reception boundaries. Historical Memory does not infer feelings, personality, intelligence, loyalty, or private intent.

Institutional and community memories require actual owner-supported collective holders. One merchant does not stand for all merchants. One jurist does not stand for every legal actor. One community intermediary does not establish a universal communal view.

Cross-realm memories require external-owner routing. Valencia may define its memory of Toledo, but not Toledo's memory of Valencia. Reciprocal memory is never automatic.

## 19. AI Consumption and Explanation

AI may use a validated memory to explain why an option appears safer, riskier, more legitimate, or more politically costly to a specific holder. It may compare interpretations, cite uncertainty, seek evidence, and abstain. It may not convert memory into fact, relationship truth, universal opinion, exact pressure, command, or future knowledge.

Required explanation fields are holder, remembered subject, acquisition path, interpretation, confidence, salience, visibility, relevant current choice, and owner-routed implication.

## 20. Player Exposure

Expose memory only when it changes a strategic choice or explains actor behavior. Player-facing presentation should answer:

- What is remembered?
- Who holds or repeats it?
- How did they acquire it?
- What interpretation do they use?
- How confident and salient is it?
- Who can see it?
- What competing memory exists?
- Which current choices does it affect?

Avoid encyclopedic dumps, realm-wide lore, hidden destiny meters, and universal grievance or prestige bars.

## 22. Save, Migration, Determinism, and Rollback

Save data must preserve definition version, holder, interpretation, acquisition path, evidence known to holder, confidence, salience, reception, availability, visibility, contradiction, reinforcement, suppression, transmission history, and last review trigger.

Migration may map only explicitly equivalent definitions and holders. Unresolved or missing references are quarantined, never guessed. A renamed label cannot silently merge distinct memories. Rollback restores the previous definitions and state without deleting Campaign History.

Equal state, inputs, authority versions, and deterministic ordering must produce equal memory review results. No random selection among historical interpretations is permitted.
