### 0.1 Developed-Realm Verdict

Valencia begins with inherited capacity moving in several directions at once. Administrative continuity, dynastic recognition, urban confidence, walls, markets, and the huerta provide usable strength. Personal rule, succession preparation, outer obedience, route security, supply resilience, and the limits of Toledan involvement remain unsettled. Momentum expresses these carried capacities and pressures. It does not predict or command the future.

The central momentum problem is whether Abd al-Malik converts inherited competence into personal and institutional resilience before ministerial concentration, exposed corridors, supply weakness, or foreign penetration converts advantage into dependency.

## 31. Contribution, Resistance, Conversion, and Reversal Rules

A contribution requires a real owner state or performed event. An intended payment is not payment. A planned repair is not repair. A memory can change interpretation and proposal weighting only through a validated projection. It cannot supply resources or become an intensity value.

Momentum conversion always requires an action path. Personal Rule may be converted through performed judgment or command. Hydraulic Confidence may be converted through completed repair and accepted burden sharing. Merchant Confidence may support credit only if the economy and agreement owners produce an actual credit arrangement. Military Readiness may support an operation only if forces, commanders, equipment, routes, and supply exist.

Reversal is not failure by definition. High Ministerial Concentration may reverse through bounded office division. Falling Toledan Penetration may result from mutually honored terms rather than hostility. External Pressure may be redirected rather than eliminated.
