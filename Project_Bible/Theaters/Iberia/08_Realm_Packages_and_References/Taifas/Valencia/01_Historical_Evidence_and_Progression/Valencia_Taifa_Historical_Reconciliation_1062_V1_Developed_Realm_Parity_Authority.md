### 0.1 Reconciliation Verdict

Valencia should enter the January 1062 campaign as an independent Amirid taifa centered on Balansiya. It is not an empty crisis state, a passive victim, a generic rich Muslim faction, or a waiting room for later conquerors. It is a compact palace-centered city-region with a recognized ruler, a durable predecessor legacy, functioning government, major fortification, active monetary expression, strong markets, a productive irrigated core, and access to a stronger Toledan court through marriage.

Its defining weakness is not lack of capacity. It is the incomplete conversion of inherited capacity into secure personal authority. Abd al-Malik inherits the throne, but much of the information, payment, law, command, water management, market coordination, and regional compliance needed to exercise that throne remains distributed among offices, households, jurists, commanders, merchants, communities, and external relationships.

The developed campaign thesis is:

**Turn inherited water, walls, markets, law, coin, and government into durable sovereignty before concentrated delegation, exposed supply, uncertain outer authority, or Toledan protection narrows Valencia's freedom of action.**

## 4. January 1062 Realm Baseline

### 4.1 Political Form

Valencia is a sovereign taifa whose political life is concentrated in Balansiya but not reducible to the ruler alone. Government is personal, dynastic, literate, fiscal, judicial, military, urban, and locally mediated. Its institutions and social groups possess functions and interests that support the state without becoming separate sovereigns.

The campaign shell may coordinate Valencia's external diplomacy and war. It does not erase distinct offices, households, communities, sites, routes, properties, jurisdictions, and local compliance.

### 4.2 What Valencia Securely Possesses

- A recognized ruling dynasty and sovereign.
- Balansiya as capital and court center.
- Major fortification associated with the predecessor reign.
- A functioning inherited administrative apparatus.
- Monetary expression associated with Abd al-Malik's reign.
- A productive huerta and lower Turia system at broad regional level.
- Active market, judicial, religious, fiscal, defensive, and communicative functions at the capital.
- Diplomatic access to Toledo through marriage, bounded by external ownership.

### 4.3 What Valencia Does Not Securely Possess

- A smooth or modern outer border.
- Uniform direct control over every strategically associated settlement.
- A named and validated senior administrator.
- A formal opening regency.
- A named heir or documented collateral settlement.
- Exact troop, garrison, population, treasury, stock, revenue, or production values.
- An automatically operational military port or standing fleet.
- An automatic alliance, protection agreement, military access, or intelligence exchange with Toledo.

### 4.4 Strategic Opening Character

Valencia's position combines concentrated material strength with institutional and territorial narrowness. The capital is difficult to ignore and dangerous to lose. The irrigated core supports resilience but lies outside complete wall protection. Money can buy time, service, repair, or diplomacy, but cannot create recruits, equipment, horses, commanders, loyalty, or supply by declaration.

## 14. Historical Pressure Without Script

## 22. AI, Player-Facing, and Design Guidance

AI may observe validated state, explain tradeoffs, propose actions, compare options, and abstain where information is inadequate. It may not invent facts, infer unknown quantities, execute owner actions, convert later history into intent, or force historical outcomes.

Player-facing presentation should communicate that Valencia is materially capable but strategically concentrated. Unknown and ReviewOnly states should be legible rather than hidden as zero. Historical pressure should appear as choices and risks, not countdowns.

The campaign should preserve Medieval II as its strategic spine, finite recruitment pools, meaningful settlement and route control, and clear faction identity. Later Total War and mod precedents may inform usability and system presentation without copying recruitment spam or converting population directly into disposable manpower.
