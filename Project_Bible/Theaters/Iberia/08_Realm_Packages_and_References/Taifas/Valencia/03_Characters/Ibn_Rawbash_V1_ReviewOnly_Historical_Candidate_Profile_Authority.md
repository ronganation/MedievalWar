### 0.1 Candidate Verdict

Ibn Rawbash is historically relevant enough to preserve as a research-gated candidate, but not sufficiently resolved to create as an active January 1062 official or complete character. Current project authorities deliberately keep his identity, name forms, exact office, opening location, competence, influence, network, loyalty, and political alignment ReviewOnly or Unknown.

Accessible general references repeat a later narrative in which a figure rendered Ibn Rawbax participated in the 1065 removal of Abd al-Malik and then served under Toledo. A derived multilingual aggregation also presents an Arabic name rendering and describes him as an inherited minister. These references are useful for source discovery and name-form comparison. They are not strong enough to bind his January 1062 identity, office, intentions, or later conduct as uncontested fact.

The correct profile is therefore a quarantine authority. It preserves the candidate, identifies exactly what must be researched, and defines what could become available if evidence clears the gates. It creates no opening person, minister, governor, betrayal, loyalty, trait, office, relationship value, or scheduled event.

## 1. Authority, Ownership, and Quarantine Boundary

### 1.1 This Profile Owns

- The ReviewOnly candidate record for Ibn Rawbash.
- A transparent register of observed name variants and source limitations.
- Candidate functional roles that may be investigated without being activated.
- Research gates for identity, chronology, office, relationships, knowledge, conduct, and later-history treatment.

### 1.2 This Profile Does Not Own

- A canonical person identity.
- A January 1062 lifecycle, location, office, title, household, property, income, command, or participation record.
- Competence, loyalty, influence, network, alignment, ambition, personality, emotion, or intention.
- Relationship values with Abd al-Aziz, Abd al-Malik, the consort, al-Mamun, Toledo, officials, commanders, or communities.
- The 1065 crisis sequence, deposition, governorship, annexation, or historical outcome.
- Memory, Momentum, Emergence, Registry, Opening State, runtime, or Unreal mutation.
