The Toledan consort is a real bounded political person in Valencia's opening design, not a generic marriage modifier and not a license to invent a named princess. It does not establish her personal name, birth, age, marriage date, household roster, children, property, office, personality, loyalties, or exact political activity.

Her correct campaign role is historically grounded and potentially consequential: spouse, household principal, dynastic bridge, patronage actor, private-access participant, and possible diplomatic intermediary. None of those functions automatically makes her a spy, hostage, regent, Toledo agent, intelligence source, treaty maker, military commander, or guaranteed supporter of either court.

## 5. Historical and Political Position

### 5.1 Valencian Position

As the ruler's spouse, she may have access to private audiences, household information, gifts, attendants, charitable activity, family correspondence, and ceremonial participation where separately bound. She does not automatically possess public office, sovereign command, treasury custody, access to every state secret, or authority over officials.

### 5.2 Toledan Position

As a daughter of al-Mamun, she may possess family standing and possible access to Toledo. Toledo owns al-Mamun's character, policy, consent, communications, resources, and actions. Her parentage does not create instructions from Toledo or guarantee that Toledo trusts, protects, informs, or follows her.

### 5.3 Marriage as a Directed Relationship

The marriage creates a relationship basis. Each direction remains separate. Abd al-Malik's trust in her is not her trust in him. Al-Mamun's policy toward Valencia is not her political preference. Household access does not equal treaty authority.
