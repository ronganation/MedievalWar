## 19. Cross-Authority Interfaces

### 19.1 Government

Government owns offices, jurisdiction, delegation, oversight, and lawful authorization. Economy owns treasury, revenue, property, market, contract, credit, stock, and provisioning definitions. Neither authority may synthesize unknown holders.

### 19.2 Religious and Juristic

Religious and Juristic authority owns institution, juristic, protected-community, and endowment-governance definitions. Economy owns economic interfaces, valuation, receipts, contracting, and fiscal consequences. Endowment purpose and protected property cannot be overridden merely as a treasury convenience.

### 19.3 Communities

Communities authority owns people, households, labor capacities, confidence, cooperation, resistance, migration, and social institutions. Economy consumes no people directly. It requests and observes labor, credit, production, transport, market, and recovery capacities through owner-routed interfaces.

### 19.4 Territory

Territory owns sites, regions, control components, routes, crossings, physical conditions, and map bindings. Economy owns production, market, stock, trade, property, and transport relationships but cannot turn a site definition into economic possession or operational access.

### 19.5 Military

Military owns force definitions, runtime formations, finite recruitment pools, readiness, command, equipment, horses, and battle carryover. Economy owns pay, purchase, stock, transport, provisioning, debt, and restoration interfaces. An army cannot self-authorize finance or supplies.

### 19.6 Diplomacy

The future Diplomacy authority owns external relationship, proposal, agreement, safe conduct, tribute, guarantee, access, and external-response definitions. Economy owns local economic costs, benefits, contracts, credit, trade, stocks, and receipts. External commerce requires both authorities where foreign ownership or consent is involved.

### 19.7 Realm Personality and AI

The future AI authority may select objectives, assess risks, and propose actions based on permitted information. It cannot create funds, stock, contracts, access, consent, prices, quantities, or owner receipts. Economic actions remain owner-executed.

### 19.8 Opening State
