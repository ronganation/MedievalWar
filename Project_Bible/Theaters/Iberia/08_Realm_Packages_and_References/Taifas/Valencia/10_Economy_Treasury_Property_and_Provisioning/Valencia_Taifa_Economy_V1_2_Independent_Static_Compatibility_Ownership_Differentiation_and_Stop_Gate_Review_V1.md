## 16. Final Position

Valencia Economy is closed at static-authority scope. The V1.1 authority remains the complete substantive owner, and the V1.2 addendum supplies the bounded shared-family compatibility layer.

The design remains a modern Medieval II campaign system. Deep records support causality, AI, validation, and persistence, while ordinary economic life remains event-driven and owner-routed. The player manages major strategic choices and visible consequences rather than every market sale, rent, cart, mill, workshop, stock ledger, or routine payment.

Keep the four-file authority family together in `Valencia/10_Economy_Treasury_Property_and_Provisioning`.
