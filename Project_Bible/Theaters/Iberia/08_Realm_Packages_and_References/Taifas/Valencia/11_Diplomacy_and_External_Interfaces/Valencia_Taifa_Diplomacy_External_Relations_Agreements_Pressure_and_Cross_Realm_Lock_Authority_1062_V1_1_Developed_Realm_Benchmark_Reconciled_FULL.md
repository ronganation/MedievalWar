This authority creates Valencia's missing specialist diplomatic owner for January 1062. It consolidates external relationship, pressure, knowledge, proposal, agreement, access, guarantee, tribute, commercial, military-support, mediation, breach, withdrawal, and sovereignty interfaces that were previously distributed across the Core, Character, Dynasty, Government, Religious and Juristic, Communities, Territory, Military, Historical, Economy, and Opening State authorities.
