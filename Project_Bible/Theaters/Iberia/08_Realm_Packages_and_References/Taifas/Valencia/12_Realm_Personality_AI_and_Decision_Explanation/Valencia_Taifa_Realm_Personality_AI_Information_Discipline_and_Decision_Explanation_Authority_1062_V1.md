## 3. Concise Realm Verdict

Valencia is **The Court of Water, Walls, Markets, and Inherited Government**.

Its power rests on Balansiya's palace government, defenses, markets, mint function, workshops, juristic and communal institutions, and the irrigated Huerta and Lower Turia system. Its weakness is not poverty or institutional absence. Its weakness is the gap between inherited capacity and proven personal control, the concentration of value around a shallow strategic core, dependence on water, labor, cooperation, credit, routes, and confidence, and exposure to Toledo's protection, influence, and possible penetration.
