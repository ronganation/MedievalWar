## 2. Playable Realm

Valencia begins as an independent taifa centered on Balansiya, its fortified capital, palace, markets, monetary functions, judicial and religious institutions, and irrigated huerta. The realm has a compact core and a wider set of provisional or uncertain relationships whose practical value depends on roads, crossings, local mediation, command, payment, and communication.

### 2.1 Why Valencia Is Immediately Playable

- Recognized ruler and functioning court
- Firm capital and meaningful defenses
- Productive water-dependent rural core
- Working fiscal, judicial, religious, and market functions
- Medium military capability with limited high-readiness response
- Regional strongpoint and movement-corridor concepts
- Bounded marriage connection to Toledo without automatic alliance or access
- Road, river-corridor, and coastal exchange potential
- Defined opening problems, pressures, opportunities, and choices

### 7.1 Institutions

- Christian and Jewish communities remain internally diverse. Identity creates no automatic occupation, loyalty, espionage, finance role, military role, or foreign alignment.
