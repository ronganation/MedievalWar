## 5. Valencia Development Thesis for the Portfolio

The portfolio should organize development around a compact, wealthy, defensible, but politically exposed Andalusi taifa centered on Balansiya. Development should depend on court and Amirid continuity, urban government, irrigated huerta production, property and treasury, markets and crafts, coastal access, finite military capability, diplomacy and external pressure, Islamic religious and juristic institutions, records and learning, and conditional political survival or transformation.
