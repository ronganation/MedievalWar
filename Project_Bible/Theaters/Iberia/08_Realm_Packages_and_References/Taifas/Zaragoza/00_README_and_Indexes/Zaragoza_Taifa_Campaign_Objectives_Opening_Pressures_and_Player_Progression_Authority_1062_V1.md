## 0.1 Strong Campaign Experience Verdict

Zaragoza's campaign experience must express the Realm identity **Frontier Court at the Ebro Crossroads** through understandable pressures, competing objectives, staged progression, recovery, alternatives, and player explanation. Objectives must connect the capital, Banu Hud continuity, uncertain Upper March, separate Lleida actor, tribute and protection pressures, finite money and military capacity, communities and property, route and market reliability, fortifications and siege recovery, information quality, Aljaferia opportunity cost, and conditional cross-Realm developments.

This authority defines campaign-experience content and lifecycle semantics. It creates no active objective, pressure, reward, victory condition, deadline, historical outcome, resource, character, force, building, relationship, Opening State, or runtime instance.

## 0.2 Developed-Realm Benchmark Discipline

The developed Seville authority explicitly blocks objective, reward, victory-condition, historical-outcome, importer, and Unreal activation and is classified as a developed-Realm campaign-experience authority. Its static check reports ten pressure families, seven primary objective families, eight optional opportunity families, six progression domains, eight recovery-path families, and zero opening active objectives, rewards, victory conditions, scheduled historical outcomes, or campaign mutations. The Almoravid authority is classified as a new substantive campaign-experience authority built from current integrated and specialist authorities. Zaragoza adopts those capability and activation principles without copying Seville or Almoravid objectives, pressures, weights, rewards, or private state.
