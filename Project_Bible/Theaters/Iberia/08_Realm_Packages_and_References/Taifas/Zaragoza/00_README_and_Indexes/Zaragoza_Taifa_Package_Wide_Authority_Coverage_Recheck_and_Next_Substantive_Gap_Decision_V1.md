## 4. Why Campaign Objectives Is a Real Gap

The current Zaragoza package defines what the Realm is, what it owns, what it knows, what it can propose, how it recruits, how it fights, how it pays, how it communicates, how it develops, and how conditional history may emerge. It does not yet own the complete player-facing campaign structure that turns those systems into:

- opening pressures the player can understand;
- short-, medium-, and long-horizon objectives;
- competing objectives and opportunity costs;
- optional, conditional, and failed objectives;
- recovery objectives after setbacks;
- objective visibility and information rules;
- cross-domain progression without research creating resources;
- historical pathways without scripting future history;
- player explanation of success, partial success, failure, delay, abandonment, and nonoccurrence;
- AI-facing objective evaluation that remains proposal-only;
- campaign-level continuity through succession, external service, frontier change, and Almoravid pressure.

```yaml
missing_owner_subjects:
  - CampaignObjectiveDefinitions
  - OpeningPressurePortfolio
  - ObjectiveEligibilityAndVisibility
  - MultiHorizonProgression
  - CompetingAndMutuallyConstrainingObjectives
  - PartialSuccessFailureRecoveryAndAbandonment
  - PlayerFacingCampaignArcExplanation
  - ObjectiveSaveMigrationReplayAndAdvancedSaveProtection
```
