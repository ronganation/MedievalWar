## 1. Purpose

This gate reconciles the seven current Zaragoza substantive and Registry V2 authorities as one package-level design family. It verifies physical file identity, declared authority identity, version, revision date, exact byte count, exact line count, SHA-256, cross-domain opening boundaries, ownership separation, three-track time consistency, source preservation, and downstream promotion readiness.

It does not publish Registry identifiers, activate people, titles, offices, institutions, communities, sites, routes, relationships, properties, populations, buildings, forces, pools, resources, historical records, or Opening State entries. It creates no machine-readable gameplay payload and performs no Unreal Engine mutation.
