## 1. Principal Character Verdict

Ahmad al-Muqtadir is the supported opening ruler candidate and the strongest principal playable leader for Zaragoza on 1 January 1062. His campaign identity is not a generic taifa prince and not a fixed bundle of ruler statistics. He is the personal center of a Hudid frontier court whose authority must be converted into durable capital continuity, reliable information, bounded delegation, regional recognition, fiscal credibility, defensible military capacity, and survivable dynastic continuity under pressure from multiple neighboring powers.

The central character problem is straightforward but not simple: Ahmad may possess recognized rulership while lacking complete knowledge, uniform control, a populated documented court, a verified heir, secure external terms, and unlimited resources. His authority gives him standing to initiate policy. It does not manufacture implementation capacity.
