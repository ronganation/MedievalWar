## 1. Developed Character Verdict

Mundhir ibn al-Muqtadir is a real Banu Hud dynastic and later regional-rule and partition subject whose January 1062 presence, age, location, household, office, readiness, and heir status remain unverified. That uncertainty is not a reason to leave him as a one-line placeholder. It is the foundation of his design.

Mundhir's character fantasy is to become a credible Zaragoza continuity principal through evidence-supported existence, formation, service, judgment, recognition, and owner-valid responsibility rather than through hindsight. His central question is whether he can develop an independent and useful political identity without being reduced to “the future regional ruler,” forced into rivalry with Mundhir, or granted adult authority before his lifecycle and opening position are established.

Captivity creates custody, communication, ransom, household, military, and succession interfaces. Incapacity creates lifecycle and care interfaces. Death ends agency but does not select Mundhir, transfer a title, divide Zaragoza, or repair a save.
