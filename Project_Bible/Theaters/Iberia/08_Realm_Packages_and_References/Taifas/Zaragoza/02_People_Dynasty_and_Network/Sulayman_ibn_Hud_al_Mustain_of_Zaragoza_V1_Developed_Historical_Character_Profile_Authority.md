## 1. Developed Historical Character Verdict

Sulayman ibn Hud al-Mustain is the historical person through whom the formation of Banu Hud rule in Zaragoza and Ahmad al-Muqtadir's predecessor relationship become legible. He is not present to act in January 1062. His strategic importance survives through genealogy, dynastic identity, political formation, inherited claims and expectations, documentary questions, present-day invocations, and competing memories among living actors.

**Historical-character promise:** Make the founder-predecessor consequential through evidence, lineage, political inheritance, contested memory, and current choices without turning him into a ghost, permanent modifier, or source of automatic legitimacy.

**Central design question:** Can living Zaragoza actors use, dispute, reform, or supersede Sulayman's legacy while preserving the difference between the deceased ruler's historical causality and the authority, knowledge, resources, and decisions of the living?
