## 0.3 Framework Alignment

The Dynastic Succession Framework defines succession as a political transition rather than a single holder-ID replacement. It separates dynasty headship, Realm title, regional title, office, household leadership, institutional leadership, property, and military command. It also distinguishes eligibility, recognition, readiness, designation, transfer, consolidation, partition, displacement, and restoration. citeturn18search121

This authority applies that framework at Zaragoza's evidence depth. It does not import exhaustive genealogy, universal inheritance law, constant claimant calculation, genetics-driven gameplay, or automatic civil war.

## 38. Player-Facing Dynasty Brief

Sulayman al-Mustain supplies predecessor and formation context without posthumous control. Yusuf and Mundhir matter because later history identifies them as sons and future rulers, yet neither is verified as present, adult, prepared, designated, or recognized in January 1062. The player's task is not to reproduce the later partition. It is to build enough genealogical certainty, candidate preparation, household and court continuity, administrative and military replacement capacity, regional recognition, and external resilience for Banu Hud to survive whatever transition the campaign actually produces.
