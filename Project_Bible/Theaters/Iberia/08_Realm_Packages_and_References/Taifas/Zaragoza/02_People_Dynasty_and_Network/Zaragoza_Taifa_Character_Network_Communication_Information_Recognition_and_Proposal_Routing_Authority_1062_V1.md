## 0.1 Strong Network Verdict

Zaragoza requires a sparse, directed, evidence-ranked Character Network that routes communication, information, recognition, proposals, delegation, office review, command review, diplomacy, economic requests, and Historical System inputs without treating named association as an active relationship or collapsing every relationship into one opinion score.

At opening, Ahmad al-Muqtadir is the supported Realm-anchor principal. Yusuf al-Mutaman and Mundhir ibn al-Muqtadir remain ReviewOnly opening candidates. Sulayman ibn Hud al-Mustain is a deceased historical subject and cannot participate as a living node. Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar remains an externally owned Iberia Theater character with no opening Zaragoza service or relationship edge.
