## 0.1 Strong Diplomatic Verdict

Zaragoza is a capital-centered frontier court whose diplomacy must manage multiple independent actors, incomplete information, finite money and military capacity, contested routes, tribute and protection pressures, dynastic and personal channels, and the possibility of cross-confessional cooperation without reducing Iberia to permanent religious blocs. The opening diplomatic landscape is strategically rich but exact January 1062 relationships, agreements, tribute continuity, war states, access, guarantees, and private external intentions remain unknown unless separately owner-bound.

This authority defines Zaragoza's directed diplomatic capacities and transaction rules. It does not write another Realm's relationship, consent, knowledge, force, treasury, promise, or response.
