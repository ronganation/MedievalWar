## 0.1 Strong Economy Verdict

Zaragoza has a supported urban, monetary, market, craft, agricultural, corridor, tribute, and provisioning design domain, but the exact January 1062 treasury, revenue, property, production, price, wage, debt, stock, transport, and trade values remain unknown. This authority therefore establishes the specialist economic ownership model, transaction contracts, qualitative review surfaces, constraints, competing demands, failure modes, AI inputs, player explanations, save rules, and implementation gates without inventing quantities.

The Realm's strategic economic identity is a capital-centered Ebro and overland crossroads economy whose reach depends on usable routes, crossings, political relationships, local cooperation, custody, delivery, and finite transport. Regional wealth does not become sovereign liquidity. Coin evidence does not become treasury income. A tribute or paria context does not become a payment. A route label does not become trade flow. Productive capacity does not become output, stock, or delivery.

## 0.2 Source and Benchmark Disposition

The Zaragoza geography-economy authority explicitly separates regional prosperity from Realm treasury, property right from collectable revenue, production capacity from output, output from delivered stock, market activity from tax income, credit capacity from free money, tribute claim from receipt, coinage from treasury quantity, and stored goods from usable supply. This specialist authority preserves those separations and expands their economic operating contracts.

The developed Valencia economy authority provides methodology for specialist ownership, treasury custody, revenue stages, property bundles, production instances, markets, contracts, credit, stocks, provisioning, transport, emergency finance, economic knowledge, AI constraints, transaction receipts, and rollback. Zaragoza adopts that methodology but not Valencia's opening bands, Huerta system, maritime profile, properties, markets, stock assumptions, or private state.
