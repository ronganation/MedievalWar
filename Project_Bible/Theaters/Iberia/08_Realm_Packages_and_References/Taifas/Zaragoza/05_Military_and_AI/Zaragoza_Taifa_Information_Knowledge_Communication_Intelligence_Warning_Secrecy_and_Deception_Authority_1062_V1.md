## 0.1 Strong Gap and Capability Verdict

Zaragoza has communication routing in the Character Network, reconnaissance and reporting interfaces in Military, diplomatic messaging in Diplomacy, and information validation in Realm AI. Those authorities do not by themselves fully own source handling, collection plans, analytical judgments, warning thresholds, secrecy compartments, disclosure, counterintelligence, misinformation, deception proposals, compromise, source protection, or intelligence failure review. This dedicated specialist authority fills that real capability gap without replacing the existing owners.

The opening model remains sparse. No spy, scout, informant, courier, analyst, cryptographic practice, intelligence office, secret network, warning, hidden knowledge seed, intercepted message, misinformation campaign, or deception operation is created by this file.
