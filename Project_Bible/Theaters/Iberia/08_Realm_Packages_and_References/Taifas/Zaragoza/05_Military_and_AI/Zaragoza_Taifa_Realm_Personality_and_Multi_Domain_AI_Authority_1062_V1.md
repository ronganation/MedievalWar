## 0.1 Strong Realm Personality Verdict

Zaragoza's Realm personality is a frontier court at the Ebro crossroads: capital-centered, diplomatically adaptive, fiscally conscious, information-sensitive, historically legitimate through the Banu Hud, capable of patronage and institutional development, and repeatedly forced to balance northern pressure, Taifa politics, negotiated protection, finite military capacity, route security, and court continuity.

This is not a deterministic temperament, a ruler personality, or a script for historical outcomes. Realm personality supplies stable strategic emphases, tie-break preferences, risk lenses, and explanation vocabulary. Current characters, government, economy, diplomacy, geography, military state, Character Network knowledge, Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence remain separate owner-controlled inputs.
