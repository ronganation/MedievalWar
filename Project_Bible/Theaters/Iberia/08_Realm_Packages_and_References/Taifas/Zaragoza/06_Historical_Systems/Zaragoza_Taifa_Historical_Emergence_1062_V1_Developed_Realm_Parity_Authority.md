## 0.1 Developed-Realm Verdict

Zaragoza receives a full conditional Historical Emergence portfolio, not a scripted event chain. Every pathway requires a date window plus actual world state, existing entities, valid owners, evidence, visibility, capacity, alternatives, nonoccurrence, directed consequences, persistence, and rollback. Later recorded history is preserved as a supported possibility and comparative target, never as destiny.

The portfolio covers Hudid continuity, Yusuf, Mundhir, succession and partition, Zaragoza-Lleida relations, Graus, Barbastro, tribute and coalition pathways, the Aljaferia, frontier political development, military-service relationships, later Almoravid pressure, and an owner-correct interface for Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar. Rodrigo is not emitted as Zaragoza property or opening personnel. His future service to Zaragoza is treated as a cross-Realm, Theater-level character pathway requiring an existing character, current lifecycle and political state, separation or release from prior obligations, a valid negotiation channel, mutual consent, command and service terms, funding, forces, access, objectives, and exit conditions.

## 0.2 Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar Boundary

External reference research describes Rodrigo Diaz de Vivar as born about 1043, raised in the household of the future Sancho II, made royal standard-bearer when Sancho became king in 1065, later exiled by Alfonso VI, and subsequently serving the Muslim rulers of Zaragoza for almost a decade. These facts support a later conditional Zaragoza service pathway and an Iberia Theater character review, not an opening Zaragoza character assignment. The opening age remains evidence-bounded rather than fixed by this authority.
