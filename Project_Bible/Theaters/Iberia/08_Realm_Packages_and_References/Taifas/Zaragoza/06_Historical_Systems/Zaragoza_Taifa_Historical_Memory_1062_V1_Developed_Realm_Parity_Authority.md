## 0.1 Developed-Realm Verdict

Zaragoza begins with multiple usable and conflicting pasts, not one Realm-wide memory. The Banu Hud household may hold establishment, predecessor, succession, and court-continuity narratives. Government actors may remember central direction, record custody, legal procedure, negotiated service, and fiscal burden differently. Urban, market, juristic, religious, protected-community, route, military, and external audiences may each receive different evidence and interpret the same past through different interests.

Campaign History owns what happened. Historical Memory owns who received an account or trace, what they accepted or disputed, how they interpreted it, how salient and accessible it remains, and how it may be transmitted, commemorated, suppressed, contradicted, reinterpreted, instrumentalized, or forgotten.

No memory in this file is automatically active. Every opening instance requires a valid holder, subject, acquisition path, evidence access, interpretation, confidence, salience, visibility, effective date, and Opening State owner receipt.

## 8. Memory Lifecycle

1. An eligible fact, experience, object, institution, landscape, or narrative exists.
2. A valid acquisition or transmission path reaches a holder.
3. Holder-specific reception and interpretation occur.
4. Confidence, salience, availability, and visibility are recorded.
5. Reinforcement, contradiction, contest, commemoration, suppression, reinterpretation, instrumentalization, or erosion may occur.
6. Transmission to another holder may become possible.
7. Owner-routed implications may affect explanation, evaluation, or eligibility.
8. The memory may persist, transform, become dormant, be archived, be suppressed, lose access, or be forgotten by a holder.

Memory age alone does not determine decay. Records, ritual, visible landscapes, political usefulness, repeated experience, institutional custody, household teaching, contradiction, displacement, and generational distance may affect persistence through owner-defined causes.
