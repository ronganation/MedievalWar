## 0.1 Developed-Realm Verdict

Zaragoza begins with interacting pressures, not fixed historical momentum values. Hudid continuity, capital security, frontier exposure, Zaragoza-Lleida uncertainty, tribute and protection costs, finite military recovery, institutional confidence, market and route reliability, protected-community experience, patronage, Aljaferia investment, and succession preparation can support, oppose, relieve, redirect, or suspend one another.

Historical Momentum owns typed directional pressure derived from visible owner-approved current state. It does not own facts, memories, people, relationships, offices, sites, property, population, treasury, forces, AI preferences, pathways, or outcomes. No pressure is active merely because its definition exists or because later history makes it seem plausible.

Every active momentum instance requires an owner, current input signals, observation provenance, direction, qualitative band, confidence, visibility, persistence or decay rule, reevaluation triggers, projection targets, and Opening State or runtime owner receipt.

## 7. Pressure Lifecycle

1. A momentum definition exists.
2. Owner-approved current state emits one or more typed observations.
3. Visibility, confidence, freshness, and compatibility are checked.
4. Required signals and hard gates are evaluated.
5. Supporting, opposing, and uncertainty-amplifying signals are compared.
6. Direction and qualitative band are assigned only by the Momentum owner.
7. Interactions with other momentum instances are evaluated.
8. Typed projections may be sent to AI, Emergence, or another owner for review.
9. Changes, expiry, contradiction, or loss of inputs trigger reevaluation.
10. Momentum may rise, fall, redirect, suspend, decay, expire, or remain Unknown.
