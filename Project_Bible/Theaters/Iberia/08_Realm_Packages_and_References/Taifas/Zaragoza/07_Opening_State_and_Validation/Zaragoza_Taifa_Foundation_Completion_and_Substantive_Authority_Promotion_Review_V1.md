## 15. Stop Conditions

Stop the affected lane if:

- a source is claimed fully reviewed when only search metadata, a snippet, contents page, or abstract is available;
- a candidate identity is treated as canonical, reserved, or published;
- a later person, border, agreement, office, army, or palace feature is backdated;
- Lleida or another external actor is emitted as Zaragoza-owned private state;
- regional or institution-owned content is duplicated into Zaragoza ownership;
- population becomes direct army manpower;
- research or decisions create people, equipment, horses, officers, ships, buildings, resources, or recruitment pools;
- the compact phase expands through status-only wrappers or unapproved extra authorities;
- a physical folder, Opening State, payload, importer target, Unreal asset, or runtime record is created.

Continue unaffected substantive authority work.
