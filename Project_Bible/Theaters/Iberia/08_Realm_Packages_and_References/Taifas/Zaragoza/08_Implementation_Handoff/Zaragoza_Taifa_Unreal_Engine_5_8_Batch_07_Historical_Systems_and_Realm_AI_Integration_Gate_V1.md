## 29. Stop Conditions

Stop Batch 07 execution if:

- direct project inspection does not confirm current historical, AI, Registry, and persistence owners;
- the integrated Historical Systems V3 appears in a current dependency;
- a Memory instance lacks a holder, source, interpretation, confidence, owner, or date;
- a Momentum signal directly mutates world state or acts as a decision;
- Emergence uses a date window without world-state thresholds and owner routes;
- nonoccurrence, delay, failure, alternative, or recovery branches are absent;
- Realm AI reads hidden future knowledge or external private state;
- Realm AI executes an owner transaction;
- Realm Personality is used to infer ruler psychology or guarantee behavior;
- save, migration, replay, rollback, or deterministic hash handling is undefined;
