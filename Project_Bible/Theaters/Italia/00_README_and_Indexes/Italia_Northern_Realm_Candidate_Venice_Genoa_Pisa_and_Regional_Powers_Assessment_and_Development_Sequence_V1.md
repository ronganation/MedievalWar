## 15. Final Position

Northern Italia is the correct next Theater-assessment lane. Venice, Genoa, and Pisa are the strongest initial comparative candidates, but each must be researched independently and must not inherit a mature generic maritime-republic template. The immediate deliverable should be a comparative Realm-admission and source preflight, followed by three candidate dossiers and a parallel inland authority-field assessment. Only then should one first Northern Realm package be selected and developed strongly.
