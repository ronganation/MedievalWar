## 1. Start Here

Italia is a distinct Campaign Theater candidate for the Italian peninsula, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica, and the directly relevant maritime approaches. The Theater is organized around political fragmentation, competing territorial and institutional authorities, maritime power, island warfare, Norman expansion, Muslim Sicilian political plurality, surviving Byzantine control in Southern Italia, Lombard principalities, Papal politics, local cities and duchies, and imperial association without uniform direct rule.

The January 1, 1062 opening does not need to freeze the campaign into the historically persistent later order. The opening must accurately represent existing people, authorities, territories, claims, wars, capacities, and uncertainties. Later transformation can occur through Historical Momentum, Historical Emergence, decisions, diplomacy, conquest, succession, collapse, alliance, surrender, and world-state thresholds.

## 2. Scenario Flexibility and Historical Emergence Doctrine

Opening accuracy and campaign flexibility are complementary rather than opposed.

```text
Opening State != Predetermined Ending
Historical Emergence != Guaranteed Historical Script
Likely Historical Pressure != Forced Historical Victor
Later Persistent Realm != Required Opening Realm
Realm Transformation != Creation Of People Equipment Or Resources
Date Window != Automatic Activation
```

The player-facing Norman or Christian Sicily associated with later Medieval II traditions may emerge through play even if no such unified Realm exists at the opening. A future Norman Sicily or later Kingdom-level polity can arise only when its world-state prerequisites are satisfied.

```yaml
NormanSicilyEmergenceCandidate:
  opening_status: NotActiveAsUnifiedRealm
  emergence_class: ConditionalHistoricalEmergenceAndRealmTransformation
  possible_drivers:
    - NormanFootholdOrSustainedTerritorialControlInSicily
    - RecognizedLeadershipAndTitleDevelopment
    - SufficientMilitaryAndNavalTransportCapacity
    - SustainableSupplyAndFinance
    - LocalAllianceSubmissionOrAdministrativeIntegration
    - MuslimSicilianPoliticalFragmentationOrMilitaryDefeat
    - PapalDiplomaticOrEcclesiasticalRecognitionWhereSupported
    - ByzantineAndRegionalPowerResponse
    - ControlOfRequiredStrategicSettlementsAndRoutes
    - OwnerApprovedRealmTransformationThresholds
  date_alone_activates: false
  historical_result_guaranteed: false
  historical_winner_privileged_without_state_support: false
  player_or_ai_alternative_outcomes_allowed: true
```

Possible outcomes must include more than the historical Norman victory:

- a durable Muslim Sicilian polity or coalition;
- rival Muslim Sicilian successor powers;
- a Norman foothold that fails or remains limited;
- Norman conquest under a different internal settlement;
- local alliance, tribute, submission, or negotiated coexistence;
- Byzantine or other external intervention where supported by the world state;
- prolonged fragmentation;
- a later Norman Sicily, County-level polity, or Kingdom-level transformation only after its conditions exist.

The titles, names, thresholds, territorial requirements, and legal recognition for each transformation remain owner-controlled and evidence-gated.

## 9. Sicily Campaign Experience Doctrine

Sicily should offer a major campaign arc regardless of which actor the player controls. The campaign must not be designed solely as a waiting room for the historical Norman result.

```yaml
SicilyCampaignPillars:
  - FragmentedPoliticalAuthority
  - IslandAndStraitOperations
  - LocalAllianceAndSubmissionChoices
  - MultiConfessionalAdministration
  - ExternalIfriqiyanNormanPapalByzantineAndMaritimeInterfaces
  - SupplyTransportAndFootholdRequirements
  - CompetingClaimsAndRecognition
  - ConditionalRealmTransformation
  - AlternativePersistentOutcomes
```

For a Norman player, the arc may involve securing Calabria, transport, reconnaissance, landing sites, local allies, footholds, title recognition, sustainable administration, and rivalry or cooperation among Norman leaders.

For a Muslim Sicilian actor, the arc may involve political reunification, coalition, rivalry, external support, fortification, maritime defense, negotiated settlement, legitimacy, administration, and preservation or transformation of the island order.

For external powers, Sicily may be a diplomatic, commercial, military, religious, or strategic intervention space without automatic territorial control.

```text
Christian Player Expectation != Opening Norman Sicily
Muslim Opening Authority != Guaranteed Defeat
Historical Norman Success != Forced Campaign Result
Alternative Success != Unhistorical Freeform Without Constraints
```

- a successor to a Southern Norman Realm;

## 15. Prohibited Shortcuts

Reject any proposal that:

- makes a later Christian Norman Sicily the opening island Realm without January 1062 evidence;
- prevents Norman Sicily from emerging because the opening is Muslim-controlled;
- forces the historical Norman result regardless of world state;
- treats Historical Emergence as free creation of forces, people, ships, equipment, or recruitment pools;
- collapses competing Muslim Sicilian actors without research;
- gives Byzantine political control wherever Greek communities or rites exist;
- assigns later Venetian, Genoese, Pisan, Papal, imperial, or Norman holdings backward;
- treats a date window as automatic activation;
- creates Opening State war, territory, army, fleet, alliance, or tribute instances;
- claims runtime or Unreal validation from static documents.
