## 1. Strong Foundational Verdict

Create Italia as a distinct Campaign Theater candidate covering the Italian peninsula, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica, and the directly relevant maritime approaches as a research and design envelope. The Theater is defined by political fragmentation, layered political and ecclesiastical authority, maritime competition, Norman expansion, Lombard and autonomous southern powers, Byzantine territorial interfaces, Muslim Sicilian political plurality, island geography, and imperial association without uniform control.

The Theater does not begin as a synonym for modern Italy, the later Kingdom of Sicily, the Holy Roman Empire, the Papal States at their later extent, or a mature later map of maritime republics and communes.

## 4. January 1062 Authority Principles

The Theater must distinguish title, claim, recognition, effective administration, settlement control, military occupation, tribute, alliance, religious jurisdiction, and cultural presence.

```yaml
AuthorityDimensions:
  - FormalTitle
  - InvestitureOrRecognition
  - EffectiveTerritorialControl
  - SettlementAdministration
  - GarrisonOrMilitaryPresence
  - FiscalExtraction
  - JudicialAuthority
  - EcclesiasticalJurisdiction
  - DynasticClaim
  - VassalageHomageOrProtection
  - MaritimeAccessAndPrivilege
  - LocalConsentOrOpposition
  - KnowledgeAndClaimConfidence
```

No dimension automatically implies another.

### 4.1 Southern Italia

The historical reconciliation must separately determine January 1, 1062 conditions for:

- Robert Guiscard and the Hauteville-centered power;
- Roger de Hauteville and the timing of any 1062 Calabria division;
- Richard of Capua and Drengot authority;
- Benevento, Salerno, Capua, Naples, Amalfi, Gaeta, and other relevant southern actors;
- Byzantine-controlled and contested Apulian and Calabrian positions;
- Papal recognition, protection, alliance, conflict, claims, and territorial interests;
- war, truce, homage, tribute, and local submission relationships.

The Metropolitan Museum chronology identifies Byzantine rule in Southern Italy through 1071, Lombard principalities, autonomous Byzantine cities, Norman rule beginning in the eleventh century, and Papal Benevento. Those broad chronologies support a multi-owner Southern Italia model but do not determine exact January 1062 settlement ownership.

### 4.3 Northern and central Italia

Northern and central Italia require a layered association model. The Theater must not assign every city, bishopric, march, county, or duchy to a single Holy Roman Empire Realm. Nor may later communes and signorie be promoted backward into January 1062.

```text
Imperial Claim != Direct Administration
Royal Itinerary != Permanent Local Control
Bishopric != Secular Province
City Privilege != Full Sovereignty
Later Commune != Opening Commune
Later Signoria != Opening Government
```

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The foundation passes its design scope when:

- Italia exists as a bounded Theater candidate rather than a modern-national container;
- Sicily, Sardinia, and Corsica are included in the research envelope without assumed ownership;
- Southern Italia and Sicily are the first historical-reconciliation priority;
- Byzantine Southern Italia is separated from Sicily's political condition;
- Muslim Sicily remains open to multiple political representations;
- Papal institution and territorial government remain separated;
- imperial association remains distinct from direct control;
- maritime cities are not forced into symmetric mature-republic templates;
- Adriatic and Ionian interfaces are explicit without absorbing Illyria or Greece;
- the Holy Roman Empire is not required before Italia can proceed;
- the first Realm remains a conditional hypothesis;
- no Registry, Opening State, payload, runtime, or Unreal mutation occurs.

## 15. Prohibited Shortcuts

Reject any proposal that:

- treats a modern Italian map as the Theater authority;
- creates the later Kingdom of Sicily at opening;
- labels all Muslim Sicily as one faction without evidence;
- treats Greek-speaking or Greek-rite communities as Byzantine provinces;
- assigns all Northern Italia directly to one Imperial Realm;
- creates later communes or signorie at opening;
- assigns later Venetian, Genoese, or Pisan holdings to 1062;
- turns Sardinia and Corsica into empty or externally owned islands without local research;
- treats Papal religious recognition as automatic secular control;
- creates a new shared framework without a documented existing-owner failure;
- creates Realm packages for folder symmetry;
- creates Opening State armies, fleets, wars, claims, treasuries, or route instances;
- claims runtime or Unreal validation from this static authority.
