## 1. Purpose

This matrix classifies the principal Italia Theater candidates by opening political identity, likely representation class, strategic distinctiveness, player-facing value, historical and Registry blockers, cross-Theater dependencies, and development readiness. It is a design and owner-decision preflight, not a canonical faction list.

A candidate can matter greatly without requiring a full playable Realm package. Cities, duchies, bishoprics, island polities, external imperial territories, regional recruitment communities, claimant networks, institutional actors, temporary expeditions, and emergent successors must be represented through the owner class that best fits their actual 1062 condition.

```text
Important Actor != Full Realm
Playable Candidate != Adopted Playable Realm
City Name != Realm Identity
Later Famous State != Opening Faction
Historical Winner != Guaranteed Player Outcome
Strategic Difference != Cosmetic Roster Difference
Realm Package != Requirement For Representation
```

  military_or_security_context: required
  credible_ai_goals_and_constraints: required
  evidence_sufficient_for_bounded_package: required

## 3. Readiness Vocabulary

```yaml
ReadinessClasses:
  A_FirstWave:
    meaning: StrongCandidateForEarlyFullRealmDevelopmentAfterHistoricalReconciliation
  B_EarlyFollowthrough:
    meaning: StrongCandidateButDependsOnOneOrMoreOwnerOrHistoricalInterfaces
  C_TheaterEssentialNotYetPackageReady:
    meaning: MustBeRepresentedEarlyButFullRealmFormUnresolved
  D_ExternalOrInternalRepresentationFirst:
    meaning: BeginAsExternalOwnerInternalActorOrRegionalStructure
  E_HistoricalEmergenceOrSuccessor:
    meaning: NotAnOpeningRealmInThisFormButValidConditionalLaterIdentity
  F_ResearchOnly:
    meaning: InsufficientEvidenceForRepresentationDecision
```

Readiness is not a quality ranking. It is a dependency and evidence disposition.

## 6. Southern Norman Hauteville Power

```yaml
Candidate: SouthernNormanHautevillePower
OpeningClass: CompositeLordshipOrRealmCandidate
Readiness: A_FirstWave
RecommendedDisposition: FirstRealmDevelopmentCandidateAfterHistoricalReconciliation
CanonicalNameCommitted: false
CanonicalRegistryIdCommitted: false
OpeningTerritoryCommitted: false
OpeningWarsCommitted: false
```

### Strategic identity

The candidate's player experience should center on building durable political control from a fast-expanding but internally layered conquest power. Military success must be converted into recognized title, local administration, secure routes, sustainable finance, regional recruitment, Papal and ecclesiastical legitimacy, and workable relations with Norman followers, Lombard communities, Greek communities, cities, monasteries, and external powers.

```yaml
PlayerPromise:
  - ConsolidateACompositeSouthernItalianPower
  - BalanceRobertRogerAndOtherNormanLeadershipInterests
  - DecideWhenAndHowToProjectAcrossTheStrait
  - ReplaceRaidingAndMercenarySuccessWithDurableAdministration
  - NavigatePapalByzantineLombardAndLocalRelationships
DistinctiveConstraints:
  - UnevenTerritorialControl
  - NobleAndFollowerAutonomy
  - LimitedVerifiedNavalCapacity
  - ByzantineStrongholdsAndClaims
  - LocalResistanceAndNegotiatedSubmission
  - SupplyAndTransportRequirements
  - TitleAndRecognitionDependencies
FailureModes:
  - NormanInternalFragmentation
  - Overextension
  - FailedSicilianExpedition
  - LocalRevoltOrDefection
  - PapalOrRegionalCoalition
  - SupplyAndFinanceCollapse
```

### Package blockers

- exact January 1, 1062 titles and territorial control;
- Robert and Roger relationship and any date-sensitive 1062 division;
- relationship to Drengot Capua and other Norman leaders;
- Byzantine, Lombard, Papal, city, and monastic interfaces;
- exact war and truce status;
- whether the package is best named by title, dynasty, region, or composite authority.

## 7. Muslim Sicilian Political Centers

```yaml
Candidate: MuslimSicilianPoliticalCenters
OpeningClass: OneOrMultipleRealmCandidatesPlusInternalPoliticalActors
Readiness: A_FirstWaveResearchPriority
RecommendedDisposition: HistoricalReconciliationBeforeRealmCountDecision
UnifiedOpeningRealmAssumed: false
HistoricalDefeatGuaranteed: false
```

### Strategic identity

The Sicilian Muslim experience should not be a placeholder destined for removal. It should offer meaningful choices among coalition, rivalry, reunification, negotiated accommodation, external support, fortification, maritime defense, local governance, multi-confessional administration, and strategic use of island geography.

```yaml
PlayerPromise:
  - PreserveReformOrReunifyAFragmentedIslandOrder
  - BalanceLocalRivalsAndExternalSupport
  - DefendStraitsPortsAndInteriorStrongholds
  - ChooseCoalitionSubmissionAccommodationOrIndependentResistance
  - GovernMultilingualAndMultiConfessionalCommunities
DistinctiveConstraints:
  - PoliticalFragmentation
  - CompetingCentersAndClaims
  - NormanFootholdAndInvasionPressure
  - ExternalIfriqiyanSupportUncertainty
  - PortAndRouteVulnerability
  - UnevenLocalLoyalty
PossibleSuccessStates:
  - DurableIndependentPolity
  - UnifiedOrCoalitionSicily
  - MultipleStableSuccessorPolities
  - NegotiatedRelationshipWithNormanOrOtherPower
  - MaritimeAndCommercialResilience
```

### Representation alternatives

The historical reconciliation may support:

- several playable Muslim Sicilian Realms;
- one principal playable Realm with powerful internal centers;
- one or more full Realms plus dependent or rival regional actors;
- a coalition system with changing leadership;
- non-playable local actors within one broader Realm.

No form is preselected.

## 28. Stop Conditions

Stop the affected candidate if:

- a candidate receives a full Realm solely because it is famous later;
- a city becomes a Realm without independent decision-capacity evidence;
- two maritime powers receive cloned campaign identities;
- Muslim Sicily is collapsed for convenience;
- Norman Sicily is inserted at opening or guaranteed to emerge;
- Byzantine territory is detached from its external Realm owner without authority;
- Papal institution and territorial government are merged into one mutable owner;
- Northern Italia is assigned wholesale to a generic imperial Realm;
- Sardinia or Corsica is assigned from external claims alone;
- Registry, Opening State, payload, runtime, or Unreal work begins.
