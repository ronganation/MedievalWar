## 16. Stop Conditions

Stop the affected batch if:

- <Place>Venice</Place> is described through a later mature republican constitution;
- the doge is treated as personal owner of the Realm, fleet, Treasury, or all Property;
- lagoon influence becomes unsupported mainland sovereignty;
- Dalmatian relevance becomes automatic territory;
- Byzantine association becomes direct administration or free support;
- maritime fame generates fleets, crews, transport, or revenue;
- package files are created only to mirror another Realm;
- Registry, Opening State, payload, runtime, or Unreal mutation begins.
