## 1. Purpose

This ledger converts the completed Southern Italy and Sicily mercenary benchmark into one bounded content-development entrance. It does not repeat the shared framework, Southern Norman gap audit, Southern Norman contract-force authority, or regional benchmark. It decides what must be checked before the first Italia mercenary unit profile can be drafted and prevents allied contingents, household retinues, external service, regional recruitment, maritime support, or commercial circulation from being relabeled as mercenary content.

## 6. First Unit-Profile Admission Test

A proposed first Italia mercenary unit may proceed only if every mandatory test passes.

```yaml
mandatory_tests:
  SourceIdentity:
    pass_when: ExactNamedSourceOrBoundedHistoricallySupportedSourceFamilyExists
  Chronology:
    pass_when: OpeningEarlyHighOrLaterEraApplicabilityIsExplicitAndNotBackProjected
  ServiceBasis:
    pass_when: ContractServiceIsDistinguishedFromObligationAlliedRetinueRegionalAndExternalService
  BattlefieldIdentity:
    pass_when: UnitAddsClearTotalWarDecisionAndBattleLoop
  EquipmentFormation:
    pass_when: WeaponProtectionMountFormationAndSupportDirectionAreSourceSupportedOrExplicitlyHeld
  Capacity:
    pass_when: FinitePeopleLeadershipEquipmentAnimalsAndSupportOwnersAreIdentified
  Collision:
    pass_when: DomesticRegionalAlliedHouseholdAndExistingMercenaryFamiliesRemainDistinct
  Replacement:
    pass_when: OriginalOrApprovedCompatibleSourceRecoveryRouteIsDefined
  Naming:
    pass_when: HistoricalNameOrHonestGenericWorkingNameDoesNotInventSpecificity
  ActivationBoundary:
    pass_when: NoCompanyCaptainPoolOfferQuantityPriceRouteOpeningOrRuntimeStateIsCreated
admission_outcomes:
  - AdmitForFullUnitProfile
  - AdmitAsGenericWorkingRoleProfilePendingBetterName
  - RetainAsResearchCandidate
  - RouteToAlliedContingent
  - RouteToExternalMilitaryService
  - RouteToHouseholdOrRetinue
  - RouteToRegionalRecruitment
  - RejectAsGenericOrDuplicative
```
