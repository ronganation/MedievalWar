## 1. Purpose

This authority connects Italia to the existing shared Mercenary Crossbowmen family without copying, renaming, replacing, or absorbing the canonical unit profile. It defines the source, chronology, service, equipment, company, route, political-access, regional-expression, recovery, and no-double-counting tests that any Italia company must pass.

It also separates baseline Mercenary Crossbowmen from the later Mercenary Pavise Crossbowmen specialist. It creates no company, source, market, route, contract, opening pool, employer access, exact equipment pattern, or recruitment right.
