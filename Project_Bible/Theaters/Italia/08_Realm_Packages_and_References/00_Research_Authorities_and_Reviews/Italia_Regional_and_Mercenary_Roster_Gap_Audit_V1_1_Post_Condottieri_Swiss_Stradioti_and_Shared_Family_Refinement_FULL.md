## 1. Purpose

This audit determines the current state of Italia-connected regional and mercenary content after completion of the Condottieri, Swiss Halberdier, Swiss Pikeman, and Stradioti sequence. It distinguishes canonical unit families from theater-consumer relationships, regional formations from mercenary companies, Realm-roster responsibilities from shared families, and genuine gaps from brainstorm labels or roster symmetry.

The audit decides whether another mercenary unit profile is presently justified and identifies the next bounded development gate. It creates no companies, captains, populations, contracts, routes, prices, quantities, recruitment pools, opening forces, stable IDs, Registry records, payloads, runtime data, or Unreal assets.
