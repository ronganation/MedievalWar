## 1. Purpose

This review determines whether Italian Condottieri support a coherent later-era Medieval War unit-family profile, what chronology and source relationships govern that family, and whether Stainless Steel's mounted lance-and-plate card should be inherited directly, narrowed, broadened, or treated as one later expression.

The review does not create an opening company, universal Italian pool, exact price, exact unit size, guaranteed employer access, or runtime content.
