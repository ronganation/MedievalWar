## 1. Purpose

This review tests whether the proposed Romagnol Mace-Serjeants concept supports a distinct Italia unit card and, if so, whether that card belongs to mercenary, regional, municipal, lordly, papal, imperial, household, or garrison ownership.

The review does not presume that the submitted name, mace-centered equipment, urban service, political neutrality, contract status, or combat mechanics are historical facts.

## 6. Sergeant Terminology Firewall

```yaml
sergeant_label_does_not_prove:
  - Infantry
  - MercenaryStatus
  - MaceEquipment
  - UrbanGuardStatus
  - RomagnolIdentity
  - UniformOrganization
  - EleventhCenturyContinuity
```

The retrieved general source describes medieval sergeants as appearing in many forms and notes that chroniclers could use the term broadly for non-knightly soldiers without reliably distinguishing origin or even infantry from cavalry. A future Romagnol source must therefore identify the actual service and formation rather than rely on the English label “serjeant.”
