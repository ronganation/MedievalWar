## 1. Purpose

This review executes the Sicilia paid-or-contracted infantry gate selected by the Italia roster-gap audit. It decides whether current evidence supports a separate recruitable mercenary infantry card, a bounded historical role family, a regional or institutional formation with conditional paid service, a shared mercenary-family expression, or continued research quarantine.

This review does not create a unit, company, captain, contract, broker, market, route, opening offer, recruitment pool, price, quantity, employer, active service relationship, or implementation record.
