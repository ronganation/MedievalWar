## 1. Purpose and Decision

This register records a bounded source-selection review for the nine Civil Systems lanes. It is not a source-owner acceptance, a substantive Realm-authority approval, an accepted consumed-source manifest, or an implementation authorization.

Nine physically present authority candidates cover the requested subject areas. The current-authority index uses current-domain or current-successor language for several of them, but its source-specific routes also say that owner acceptance is not inferred or remains unverified. None can be bound as an accepted `Substantive` source for a Civil Systems definition under the stated source-selection rules.

**Decision:** assign `NoCurrentSubstantiveAuthority` to all nine Civil Systems lanes and `ExistingAuthorityRequiresCurrentnessDisposition` as the reason for each hold. No genuine content gap is established. Do not create the Civil Systems manifest, do not authorize the provider, and do not draft replacement Realm authority in this tranche.

## 3. Source-Selection Rule Applied

The source-role gate is conjunctive. A candidate must physically exist, control a defined Realm capability, have clear currentness and an internally consistent identity/version, not be superseded or provisional, have no unresolved family conflict for the selected scope, and have explicit owner and implementation boundaries. A filename, a broad family heading in the index, a static validation statement, or a plausible subject match is not owner acceptance.

The exact row-level statuses in the live index and each candidate's own authority-control section take precedence over a simplified family heading. In particular, `CurrentSubstantiveAuthority` is not read as owner acceptance where the same index provides `OwnerAcceptanceNotInferred` or `OwnerAcceptanceUnverified`.
