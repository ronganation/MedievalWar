## 1. Purpose and Decision Boundary

This portfolio routes the unresolved Southern Norman Human Network questions to their actual owner domains. It records no owner decision, does not create owner route IDs, and does not convert named references into people, offices, households, relationships, or campaign state. `BlockedByOwner` is the current disposition for every lane until an accepted, scope-specific source manifest and the relevant owner decisions exist.

The generic Human Network contract provides twelve lanes and read-only identity/evidence/definition/dependency/owner-route records. It separates evidence from canonical identity, owner review from activation, and opening eligibility from later history. This portfolio follows those boundaries; it does not copy values or owner receipts from the existing Venice, Genoa, or Pisa providers.

The preparation baseline and inspected source bindings are in [the SN-IMP-01N Character Completeness Preparation and Gap Register](./Southern_Norman_Human_Network_Character_Completeness_Preparation_and_Gap_Register_SN_IMP_01N_V1_FULL.md). Its hashes are observed custody values, not accepted Human Network pins.

## 6. Required Owner Package for a Future Gate

Before any Human Network source acceptance, the responsible project/source owner must provide a manifest and explicit disposition with, at minimum:

- exact package-relative path, document ID, stable document ID, authority ID, and version for every row;
- physical byte count and SHA-256 re-read at the acceptance boundary;
- source role (`Substantive`, `Evidence`, `Planning`, `Navigation`, `Preflight`, `Validation`, or `ReviewOnlyStateAuthority`);
- source currentness and declared owner-review standing, kept as separate fields;
- exact source locators and exact observed claims, including chronology and uncertainty;
- source-owner route and affected external-owner route for every cross-owner dependency;
- candidate identity/alias, duplicate/collision, admission class, and explicit non-admission treatment;
- explicit owner decision for the five opening candidates, seven review-only subjects, predecessor memory, and external references;
- explicit `LaterHistoryBackProjectionFirewall` entries for future titles/events and a statement that no opening state is created;
- invalidation/re-review rules if a path, version, hash, status, locator, owner, or scope changes.

This list does not itself constitute approval and does not supply missing decisions. Any later project owner acceptance must name the exact Human Network purpose and cannot be inferred from prior acceptance for another provider.
