## 4. Priority One Gap: Health, Disease, Medicine, Food Security, Mortality, Burial, Care, and Recovery

The population authority identifies disease, injury, food, water, shelter, seasonal stress, mortality, institutional care, and demographic recovery, but routes the disease model to Health and Population owners. It also routes health, birth, death, disease, and biological demography to Population, Health, and Character owners. That is an explicit unresolved specialist ownership lane rather than complete health coverage.

The supply authority uses disease, sanitation, medical consumption, camp health, animal health, wounded-person recovery, burial needs, and civilian care as operational inputs and consequences. Supply owns delivery, allocation, consumption, and military recovery interfaces, not the underlying civilian and institutional health model.

The economy authority owns food, water, stocks, production, delivery, protected civilian floors, relief material, and care-support resources, but explicitly does not own people, health, or labor truth. The ecclesiastical authority owns institutions and religious-political functions, not a complete realm health model.
