## 1. Purpose

This analysis converts the supplied web sources into a disciplined opening-era actor and relationship portfolio for Apulia and Calabria. It separates January 1, 1062 evidence from later history, identifies contradictions and weak points, assesses the package's player and AI potential, and defines the next evidence outputs required before the Realm Foundation authority.

```text
Historical Success != Guaranteed Campaign Outcome
Strong Realm Potential != Easy Opening
Papal Favor != Permanent Safety
Norman Kinship != Automatic Loyalty
Sicilian Rivalry != One Arab Faction
Later Lordship != Opening Title
```

Venice and Genoa should later become strong Italian Realm experiences through maritime, commercial, institutional, and strategic capabilities.
