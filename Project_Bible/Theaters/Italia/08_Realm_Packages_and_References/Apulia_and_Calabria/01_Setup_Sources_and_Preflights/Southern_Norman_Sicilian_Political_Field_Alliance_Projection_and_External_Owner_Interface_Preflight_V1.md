## 1. Purpose

This preflight defines the Southern Norman package boundary with the Sicilian political field. It separates Robert Guiscard's Papally supported Sicilian claim from territorial ownership, identifies candidate Muslim Sicilian political centers and alliances, protects their external ownership, defines the North African and Zirid interface, and establishes the finite transport, supply, landing, command, and administration requirements for Norman projection.

This is the final external-interface preflight before the Realm-specific historical reconciliation and source manifest.

```text
Sicilian Claim != Sicilian Ownership
Local Alliance != Submission
Invitation Or Cooperation != Transfer Of Realm
Landing != Conquest
Conquest != Administration
Port Access != Fleet
Norman Momentum != Guaranteed Victory
Muslim Sicily != One Generic Arab Faction
North African Support != Unlimited Reinforcement
```

## 2. Historical Orientation Boundary

The current orientation sources explicitly describe a fragmented Muslim Sicilian political field and Norman entry through cooperation with a local Sicilian actor. One source states that by 1060 <Person>Ibn al-Hawas</Person> had conquered several strongholds, that rival <Person>Ibn al-Thumna</Person> sought Norman help, and that <Person>Robert Guiscard</Person> and <Person>Roger de Hauteville</Person> captured <Place>Messina</Place> in 1061. citeturn36search152

Another source describes the island as divided among four commanders after the collapse of centralized Kalbid authority and says <Person>Ibn al-Thumna</Person> invited <Person>Robert Guiscard</Person> and <Person>Roger de Hauteville</Person> to assist against rivals. That source also says the Normans captured <Place>Messina</Place> and nearby territory before the alliance ended with <Person>Ibn al-Thumna</Person>'s death. citeturn36search155
