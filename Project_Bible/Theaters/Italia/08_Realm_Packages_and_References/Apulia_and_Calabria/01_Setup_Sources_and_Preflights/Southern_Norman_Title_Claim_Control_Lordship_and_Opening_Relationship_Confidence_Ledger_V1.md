## 1. Purpose

This ledger converts the prior source analysis into typed, nonactive confidence records. It separates titles from claims, claims from effective control, control from administration, administration from property, and kinship from service or loyalty. It also calibrates the proposed campaign challenge without treating informal discussion as a locked difficulty classification.

```text
Discussion Impression != Final Difficulty Rating
Title != Territory
Claim != Control
Control != Administration
Administration != Property
Kinship != Loyalty
Papal Recognition != Permanent Alliance
Historical Momentum != Guaranteed Victory
```
