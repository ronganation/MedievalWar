## 15. Robert Guiscard Diplomatic Position

Robert is the principal Ducal diplomatic center only through his living Character state, title and recognition receipts, household access, government capacity, and issue-specific authority. He may represent the Realm, issue proposals, seek recognition or mediation, negotiate service, coordinate campaigns, and conclude owner-valid agreements.

Robert's title does not give him perfect knowledge, universal local representation, unlimited command of Roger or associated lords, ownership of every claimed settlement, Papal obedience, Sicilian submission, Byzantine consent, or material capacity that the responsible owners do not possess.

## 16. Sikelgaita Diplomatic and Household Agency

Sikelgaita is a substantive Ducal-household, Lombard-network, mediation, patronage, correspondence, legitimacy, property-interface, and succession principal. Her diplomatic effect must be expressed through owner-valid access, relationships, information, correspondence, audiences, patronage, mediation, and household capacity.

Her marriage does not create Salernitan or Lombard consent, territory, property, troops, alliance, or hereditary resolution. She is not reduced to a passive modifier and is not assigned routine opening battlefield command.

## 17. Roger and Judith Diplomatic Position

Roger is a major internal and operational power within the composite Realm, not a generic subordinate diplomat or automatic co-ruler. His Sicilian and Calabrian proposals, contacts, cooperation, reward negotiations, and household diplomacy require his own knowledge, mandate, resources, routes, and consent.

Judith remains a qualified household and Norman-aristocratic interface whose opening marriage date, exact location, staff, and capacities remain owner-controlled. Robert cannot silently author Roger's or Judith's private knowledge, promises, household agreements, or foreign commitments.

## 18. William and Associated-Lord Diplomacy

William of the Principate remains qualified by chronology and lordship review. Any representation, service, Lombard interface, rivalry, reconciliation, marriage-network use, or external commitment requires exact identity, life state, authority, issue scope, and owner receipts.

An associated lord may cooperate on one issue and refuse, delay, contest, or lack capacity on another. One agreement does not convert an associated lordship into permanent obedience.
