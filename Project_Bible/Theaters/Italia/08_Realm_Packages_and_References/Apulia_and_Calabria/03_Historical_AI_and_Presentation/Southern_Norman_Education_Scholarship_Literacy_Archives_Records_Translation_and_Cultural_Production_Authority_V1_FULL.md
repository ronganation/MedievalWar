## 1. Strong Southern Norman Education and Knowledge Verdict

Implement Southern Norman education as an **institutionally distributed, relationship-based, multilingual, and materially constrained knowledge system**. Learning grows through people, households, mentors, institutions, offices, texts, records, languages, travel, practice, patronage, tools, safety, and time. Conquest may capture a building or record collection, but it does not create comprehension, lawful custody, competence, loyalty, or continuity.
