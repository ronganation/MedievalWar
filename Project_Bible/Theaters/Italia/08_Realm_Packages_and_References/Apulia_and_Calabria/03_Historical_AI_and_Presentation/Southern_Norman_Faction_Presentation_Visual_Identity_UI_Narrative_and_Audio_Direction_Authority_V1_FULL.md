## 1. Strong Presentation Verdict

Present the Southern Norman polity as **a hard-driving conquest society becoming a government**: a layered composite of Norman households, Lombard and Greek-speaking communities, ports and inland strongpoints, Latin and Greek ecclesiastical institutions, Papal recognition, Byzantine resistance, and a still-conditional Sicilian horizon.

```yaml
PresentationArchetype: ConquestPowerBecomingACompositeRealm
FirstImpression: MartialMobileNegotiatedAndAdministrativelyUnfinished
VisualPromise: ShowMilitaryInitiativeAndHouseholdPowerConfrontingTheWorkOfHoldingRoutesPortsStrongpointsCommunitiesAndInstitutions
NarrativePromise: MakeEveryGainFeelLikeAQuestionOfAuthoritySupplyRewardRecognitionAndRetention
AudioPromise: BuildPlaceWorkCommandDistanceShippingSiegeAndRecoveryThroughLayeredStateDrivenSound
CorePlayerQuestion: CanConquestBeMadeDurableWithoutBreakingTheHouseholdsCommunitiesAndInstitutionsThatMakeRulePossible
OpeningStateReadiness: Hold
```

The presentation must communicate exceptional strategic potential without showing the later Norman Kingdom of Sicily as already inevitable or visually complete.
