## 1. Strong Southern Norman Intelligence Verdict

Implement Southern Norman intelligence as a **bounded collection, assessment, warning, counterintelligence, and agent-operations system** serving a composite Realm with dispersed authority, uneven access, multilingual communication, military pressure, maritime exposure, and actor-specific external relations. Intelligence reduces uncertainty only when actual sources, observers, routes, language competence, authentication, access, time, protection, and review exist. It never exposes hidden truth simply because the player owns a spy, port, watchtower, court, or settlement.
