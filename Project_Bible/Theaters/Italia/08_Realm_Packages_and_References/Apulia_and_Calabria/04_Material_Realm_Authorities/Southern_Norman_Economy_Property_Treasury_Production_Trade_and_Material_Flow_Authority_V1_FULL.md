## 1. Strong Economy Verdict

Implement the Southern Norman economy as a **distributed conquest-and-consolidation economy**. Its strength comes from coordinating separately owned household, lordly, local, urban, rural, ecclesiastical, commercial, transport, and military-facing capacities across imperfectly governed territory. Military success may open access or seize custody, but it does not create lawful ownership, productive cooperation, revenue, liquid Treasury, market activity, transport, or durable recovery.
