## 1. Strong Population and Community Verdict

Implement Southern Norman society as a **place-specific composite population field** whose people participate through households, settlements, lordships, communities, languages, rites, occupations, institutions, property relations, patronage, migration, military obligations, and local political arrangements. No community is a static percentage, loyalty modifier, ethnic unit pool, or uniform political bloc.
