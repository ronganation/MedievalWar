## 1. Strong Public Order Verdict

Implement Southern Norman public order as a place-specific, audience-specific, and issue-specific interpretation of how communities, institutions, lords, households, officials, soldiers, property holders, workers, merchants, clergy, towns, and rural settlements receive government, law, obligation, occupation, protection, damage, remedy, and recovery.

Southern Norman strength may compel obedience, secure a road, hold a strongpoint, or win a battle without producing accepted government. Durable order develops only when protection, lawful process, credible commitments, local participation, service delivery, restraint, restitution, institutional continuity, and material recovery reinforce one another.

```yaml

  strategic_form: NegotiatedConquestToGovernmentPublicResponseField

  external_interfaces: [ByzantineSouthernItalia, PapalAndLombardFields, CapuanAndOtherNormanPowers, SicilianPoliticalCentersAndCommunities]
```

The Realm must earn administration after conquest. Victory can create a new political problem faster than it creates a stable settlement.
