## 1. Strong Southern Norman Settlement Development Verdict

Implement the Southern Norman built environment as a **distributed conquest-and-consolidation settlement system**. The Realm may develop many useful building families, but every project remains constrained by site, rights, people, expertise, material, treasury, transport, security, season, maintenance, and competing obligations. The system preserves Medieval II readability and broad construction freedom without tiny universal slot limits or buildings that create their own inputs.
