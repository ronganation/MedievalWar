## 1. Strong Geography Verdict

Implement the Southern Norman geographic field as a **network of place-specific regions, settlements, strongpoints, ports, roads, mountain and coastal corridors, Strait interfaces, institutions, and external-owner boundaries**. Geography must make military opportunity, local rule, communication, economy, population, recruitment, supply, shipping, and recovery legible without turning broad historical orientation into an invented January 1062 political map.
