## 1. Strong Contract Force and External Service Verdict

Implement Southern Norman contract forces, allied contingents, and external military service as **finite, person-bound, force-bound, agreement-bound, and owner-routed military relationships**, not as a generic mercenary shop or endlessly refreshing hire pool. A contract can define terms, responsibilities, payment, command, access, withdrawal, and remedy. A contract cannot create people, captains, retainers, units, animals, equipment, treasury funds, supplies, transport, consent, readiness, or loyalty.
