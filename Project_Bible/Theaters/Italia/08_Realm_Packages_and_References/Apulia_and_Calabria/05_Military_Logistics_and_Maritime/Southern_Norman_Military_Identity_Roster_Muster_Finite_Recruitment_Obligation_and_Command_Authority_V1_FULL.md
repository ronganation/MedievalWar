## 1. Strong Southern Norman Military Verdict

Implement the Southern Norman military as a composite, contingent, household, lordship, regional, civic, specialist, contracted, and externally coordinated military system. Its identity is defined by forceful Norman military leadership and mobility operating inside a broader Southern Italian field of local communities, lordships, strongpoints, towns, ports, institutions, roads, obligations, and material constraints.

Every Norman is not a knight. Every lord does not answer every summons. Every controlled settlement does not provide a free garrison. Every port does not provide ships. Every victory does not create administration. The player must join political authority, valid service, willing or compelled contributors, finite eligible people, equipment, horses, officers, route access, supply, command clarity, and recovery capacity into forces that can actually operate.
