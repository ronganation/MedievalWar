## 1. Strong Southern Norman Siege Verdict

Implement Southern Norman siege warfare as a **material, operational, human, environmental, and political contest around fortified places**. Besiegers must establish access, command, camps, investment, supply, engineering, protection, observation, negotiation, and endurance. Defenders must maintain works, command, food, water, sanitation, communication, repair, restraint, civilian protection, sorties, relief coordination, and capitulation options. Neither side receives engines, breaches, isolation, starvation, surrender, occupation, repair, or victory merely because a siege posture or fortification icon exists.
