## 3. Core Character Thesis and Strategic Questions

**Can Bohemond remain a finite, vulnerable, politically consequential child whose future depends on households, custodians, recognition, education, safety, property, and changing world state rather than later fame?**

- Is Bohemond alive, where is Bohemond, and who knows this?
- Which household has physical custody or access?
- Who may protect, educate, correspond with, or represent the child?
- How does the former marriage affect recognition without dictating a legal result?
- Do compensation or beneficial interests exist, and are they deliverable?
- What happens if Robert is absent, incapacitated, captive, or dead?
- Can Alberada protect without owning the child?
- Can Sikelgaita participate without becoming an automatic enemy?
- Under what conditions does a claim become reviewable rather than guaranteed?
- How can the child mature into different futures, including no command and no Antioch?
