## 49. Ruler-General Battlefield Doctrine

The following are design tendencies subject to military AI and battle validation, not proven tactical statistics.

- seek concentration around an objective that produces political or strategic return;
- preserve the command household and enough reserve to respond to uncertain reports;
- use mounted shock with infantry, missile, scout, and terrain support;
- avoid stripping every garrison and strongpoint to create one impressive army;
- coordinate separate lordly and household contingents through explicit precedence and reporting;
- treat pursuit as a political and logistical decision, not an automatic reward;
- preserve a line of retreat, messenger access, and recovery route;
- connect siege plans to relief, occupation, administration, and settlement;
- accept negotiation when the political objective is secured at lower cost;
- return battle consequences to households, pools, property, communities, and memory.

```text
Ducal Rank != Tactical Omniscience
Reputation != Automatic Cohesion
Personal Presence != Infinite Command Bandwidth
Victory != Government
Capture Of A Place != Acceptance
Historical Momentum != Battle Bonus
```
