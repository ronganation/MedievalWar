## 49. Commander-Specific Battlefield Doctrine

The following are design tendencies subject to military AI and battle validation, not proven tactical statistics.

- seek actionable reconnaissance before committing scarce mounted cadres;
- concentrate against separated forces rather than disperse for display;
- use mounted shock as a timed decision, not a universal opening move;
- keep infantry, missiles, scouts, and reserves connected to the objective;
- protect officers, messengers, standards, and the line of retreat;
- use terrain, camps, crossings, walls, and coastal constraints deliberately;
- stop pursuit when cohesion, horses, relief, or the political objective no longer justify risk;
- prefer a bounded victory that can be supplied and governed over a spectacular advance that cannot be held;
- demand battle-to-campaign receipts for casualties, prisoners, horses, equipment, baggage, and position.

```text
Reputation != Tactical Omniscience
Personal Courage != Cohesion
Mounted Shock != Guaranteed Breakthrough
Victory != Free Recovery
Pursuit != Destruction
Battlefield Presence != Sovereignty
```
