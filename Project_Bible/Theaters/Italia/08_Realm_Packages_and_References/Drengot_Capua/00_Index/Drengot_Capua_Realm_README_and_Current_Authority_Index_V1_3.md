## 1. Start Here

This package is the bounded design home for Drengot Capua, a playable Italia Realm centered on the Drengot dynasty, the established Norman base at Aversa, the princely inheritance of Capua, and the political corridors joining Rome, Campania, and Southern Italia.

Drengot Capua is not a minor wrapper around the Southern Norman Realm, a generic Norman faction, a completed centralized state, or a static historical museum piece. The package supports a creative, fluid, replayable Modern Medieval II faction while preserving honest uncertainty and the distinction between static design authority and activated Opening State data.

### 3.1 Aversa

Aversa is the older Drengot power base and represents dynastic continuity, household confidence, Norman settlement and patronage networks, dependable military relationships, and political memory before the acquisition of princely dignity. Aversa remains a political anchor and potential fallback center if authority in Capua becomes contested.

### 3.2 Capua

Capua provides princely rank, Lombard political inheritance, regional prestige, institutional depth, and access to an older order that the Drengots can absorb, bargain with, reshape, or damage. Capua must not begin as a blank Norman city. Its value depends upon surviving local institutions, interests, relationships, property structures, clergy, elites, communities, and memories.

### 3.3 The Campanian Hinge

Drengot Capua belongs within Italia as a Realm whose strategic importance exceeds its compact scale. It interfaces with Rome and the Papacy, Montecassino and ecclesiastical property, Benevento, Naples, Gaeta, Salerno, the Hauteville sphere, inland corridors, and Tyrrhenian approaches. The package may define Drengot-facing relationships and consequences but may not author the internal state of separately owned Realms or institutions.
