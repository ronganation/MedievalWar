## 1. Strong Campaign Verdict

Drengot Capua's campaign experience is the struggle to convert Richard I's personal leverage, the older Aversan base, Capuan princely opportunity, Norman military credibility, Lombard institutional depth, Papal recognition, and dense Campanian relationships into a principality that can survive military loss, political compromise, failed expansion, and the eventual absence of its founder.

The faction's identity is **Consolidation, Concentration, and Continuity**. Expansion is a conditional expression of strength, not the mandatory definition of success.

## 41. Prohibited Shortcuts

Reject any proposal that:

- turns the campaign into a permanent mission tree or checklist;
- activates objectives because a date arrives;
- makes the Keys of Capua one mandatory completion button;
- treats military victory as political consolidation;
- makes Capua, Papal alignment, Hauteville cooperation, Jordan's succession, or expansion compulsory victory requirements;
- rewards objective completion with free people, troops, horses, money, property, buildings, relationships, titles, or external consent;
- collapses all audiences into one standing score;
- makes failure an automatic campaign loss;
- erases failure through recovery;
- exposes hidden information or future history;
- lets Realm AI bypass specialist owners;
- claims static design as Opening State, runtime, save, battle, or Unreal validation.
