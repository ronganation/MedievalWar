## 1. Purpose

This authority defines how Drengot Capua functions politically without reducing the Realm to Richard I, the princely title, one city, one loyalty score, or a centralized state. It establishes the constitutional relationship among personal lordship, title, Papal recognition, Aversan dynastic power, Capuan political inheritance, local participation, property, military presence, offices, justice, institutions, and compliance.

It creates design rules and owner interfaces. It does not create exact January 1062 officeholders, laws, properties, titles, garrisons, agreements, relationships, or Opening State records.

```yaml
AuthorityPurpose:
  - DefineLayeredPoliticalConstitution
  - SeparatePersonTitleRecognitionControlPropertyAndExecution
  - EstablishAversaCapuaDualCenterGovernment
  - PreserveLombardInstitutionalContinuity
  - DefineParticipationClassesWithoutInventingPeople
  - DefineRecognitionAndConsentAsDynamic
  - ConnectGovernmentToKeysOfCapua
  - PrepareLaterOfficeCourtHouseholdPropertyPublicOrderAndOpeningStateWork
```

## 2. Constitutional Verdict

Drengot Capua is a composite principality under active consolidation. Richard I is the opening prince and principal ruler, but the Realm's effective government depends on distinct people, offices, institutions, rights, properties, military commands, routes, records, and recognized relationships.

The political constitution should make the faction powerful because it can coordinate multiple authority types, not because the princely title grants universal control.

```yaml
ConstitutionalVerdict:
  realm_model: LayeredCompositePrincipality
  sovereign_center: RichardIOfCapuaAtDesignLevel
  dynastic_base: Aversa
  princely_center: Capua
  government_condition: UnevenAndConsolidating
  legal_and_political_character: PersonalInstitutionalPropertyBasedAndNegotiated
  uniform_administration: false
  universal_obedience: false
  permanent_constitutional_form: false
  historical_result_guaranteed: false
```

```text
Title != Government
Recognition != Control
Control != Property
Property != Jurisdiction
Military Presence != Administrative Capacity
Participation != Obedience
Compliance != Legitimacy
```

## 3. Evidence and Research Boundary

The located scholarly gateway confirms that surviving diplomas of the Norman princes of Capua and close family are important evidence for chronology, chancery practice, document preservation, and patronage. The source description also states that the chancery was not highly active and ordinarily did not require more than one notary. These points justify a documentary and patronage research lane, but they do not authorize an invented opening bureaucracy or named office roster.
