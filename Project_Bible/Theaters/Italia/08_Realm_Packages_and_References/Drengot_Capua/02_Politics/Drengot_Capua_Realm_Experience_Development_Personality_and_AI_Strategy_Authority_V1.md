## 1. Purpose

This authority defines how Drengot Capua should feel to play, how it may develop, what strategic personality distinguishes it from other Norman and Italian factions, and how its AI should evaluate opportunities, constraints, risks, and reasons to abstain.

It translates the Realm foundation, January 1062 opening boundary, Richard I character authority, and political constitution into an integrated campaign experience. It does not create exact AI weights, opening armies, treasury, settlements, diplomatic instances, scripted missions, or runtime behavior.

```yaml
AuthorityPurpose:
  - DefineDistinctPlayerExperience
  - DefineFactionStrategicPersonality
  - BuildNonbinaryDevelopmentPortfolio
  - ConnectAversaCapuaDualCenterToCampaignPlay
  - MakeRichardPowerfulWithoutMakingRealmAutomatic
  - BuildExplainableAIWithSharedPlayerRules
  - PreserveFinitePeopleMaterialAndInformation
  - SupportFailureRecoveryAndAlternateHistory
  - PreventGenericNormanOrCityStateDesign
```

## 2. Strong Realm Experience Verdict

Drengot Capua should play as a compact but high-agency principality whose opening strength comes from Richard I, an established Aversan base, military credibility, Papal recognition, and access to a prestigious Capuan political inheritance. Its weakness is that these advantages are not yet aligned into durable government.

The player should frequently possess enough leverage to act, but not enough capacity to pursue every opportunity simultaneously. The central experience is choosing where to convert limited personal, military, material, institutional, and diplomatic capacity into durable advantage.

```yaml
RealmExperienceVerdict:
  campaign_identity: CompactHighAgencyCompositePrincipality
  opening_strength: PersonalLeadershipMilitaryCredibilityAndPoliticalLeverage
  opening_weakness: IncompleteInstitutionalAlignmentAndSuccessionDependence
  primary_player_task: ConvertOpportunityIntoDurableCapacity
  primary_campaign_tension: ExpansionVersusConsolidation
  secondary_campaign_tension: PersonalRuleVersusInstitutionalContinuity
  geographic_character: PoliticallyDenseCampanianHinge
  historical_outcome_guaranteed: false
```

## 3. Player Fantasy

**Begin as a dangerous prince whose reputation and military leverage exceed the uniformity of his state. Secure Capua without exhausting Aversa, use Papal and Norman relationships without becoming subordinate to them, preserve or transform Lombard institutions, and build a principality capable of surviving Richard I.**

