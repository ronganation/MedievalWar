## 2. Strong Fiscal Verdict

Drengot Capua should possess fragmented and negotiated fiscal capacity rather than one perfectly liquid treasury. Richard's household resources, Aversan revenues, Capuan princely claims, tolls, rents, services, fines, negotiated contributions, institutional rights, military burdens, and external obligations remain distinct until assessed, collected, delivered, received, recorded, reconciled, and lawfully available.
