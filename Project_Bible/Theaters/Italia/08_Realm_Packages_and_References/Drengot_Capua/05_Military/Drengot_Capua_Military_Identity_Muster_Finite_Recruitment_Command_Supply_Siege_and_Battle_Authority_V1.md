## 2. Strong Military Verdict

Drengot Capua should fight as a compact, politically dense military power able to concentrate a dangerous mounted and household core around Richard I, reinforce it with broader Aversan service, and conditionally integrate Capuan Lombard and regional forces. Its strength is decisive concentration and political use of force. Its weakness is finite elite depth, narrow replacement capacity, dual-center exposure, and dependence on supply, officers, participation, and recovery.
