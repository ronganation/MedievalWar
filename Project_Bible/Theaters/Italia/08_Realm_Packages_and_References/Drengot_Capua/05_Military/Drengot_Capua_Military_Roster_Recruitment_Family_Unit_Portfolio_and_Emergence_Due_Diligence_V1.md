## 6. Roster Identity Thesis

**Drengot Capua should field a compact, source-visible roster built around a very finite princely and household nucleus, broader but still bounded Aversan service, conditionally accessible Capuan and Lombard regional forces, local settlement and fortress defense, and selectively negotiated external support. Its roster identity is not generic Norman cavalry plus generic Italian levies. Its distinctive campaign experience is the political and logistical conversion of Aversa, Capua, households, local institutions, horses, equipment, officers, and service rights into a force that can concentrate decisively without exhausting the dual-center Realm.**

RosterIdentity:
