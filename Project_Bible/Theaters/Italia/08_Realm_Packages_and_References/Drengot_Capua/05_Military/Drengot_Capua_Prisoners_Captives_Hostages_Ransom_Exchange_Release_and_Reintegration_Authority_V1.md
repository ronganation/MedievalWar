## 1. Purpose

This authority defines battlefield prisoners, political captives, detainees under valid authority, hostages or sureties where historically supported, custodianship, identity, records, health, food, shelter, communication, negotiation, ransom, exchange, release, return, reintegration, disappearance, death, and long-term consequences for Drengot Capua.

It establishes a humane, accountable simulation boundary. It does not authorize torture, abuse, collective punishment, disappearance as a shortcut, or operational guidance for mistreatment.

It does not publish exact January 1062 captives, prisoners, hostages, sureties, custodians, places of confinement, ransoms, exchanges, guarantees, release terms, or Opening State bindings.

## 2. Strong Custody-System Verdict

Drengot Capua should treat custody as a temporary, accountable relationship requiring authority, a real custodian, a known or investigated identity, food, water, shelter, health care, security, records, communication, review, and an exit path. Captured people remain people and retain independent identities, households, claims, offices, relationships, health, and Historical Memory.
