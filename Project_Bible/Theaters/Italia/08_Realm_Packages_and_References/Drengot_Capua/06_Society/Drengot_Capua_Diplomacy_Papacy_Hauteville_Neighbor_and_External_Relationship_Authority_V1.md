## 1. Purpose

This authority defines how Drengot Capua conducts diplomacy through recognition, service, claims, kinship, marriage, guarantees, gifts, military support, mediation, sanctions, hostages, access, information, and remembered conduct. It establishes the Papal, Hauteville, Lombard, urban, monastic, coastal, and neighboring interfaces without manufacturing exact January 1062 alliances, wars, treaties, attitudes, or relationship scores.

## 2. Strong Diplomatic Verdict

Drengot Capua should be diplomatically dangerous because it occupies a politically dense position among the Papacy, Lombard principalities, Norman powers, coastal polities, monastic institutions, and claimant networks. Its strength lies in flexible leverage and multiple channels. Its weakness is that every relationship is conditional, directional, and vulnerable to obligation, rivalry, sanction, succession, and military reversal.
