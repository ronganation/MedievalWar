## 2. Strong Legal-System Verdict

Drengot Capua should govern through layered, negotiated, and sometimes competing jurisdictions rather than one universal legal code. Aversa and Capua retain distinct histories, actors, customs, institutions, records, property relationships, and forums. Princely authority can recognize, reform, coordinate, hear, delegate, or enforce only where authority, access, people, information, and capacity exist.

```yaml

  model: LayeredJurisdictionEvidenceProcedureRemedyEnforcementAndMemoryCausality
  primary_strength: PrincelyHouseholdLocalReligiousAndDocumentaryInterfaces
  primary_weakness: CompetingJurisdictionsUnevenRecordsAndFiniteEnforcement
  one_realm_legal_code: rejected
  ruler_will_equals_law: false
  judgment_equals_enforcement: false
  custom_equals_unchangeable_rule: false
  exact_opening_forums: Deferred
```
