## 1. Purpose

This authority defines public order as the outcome of local participation, protection, justice, burden, memory, access, institutions, material conditions, coercion, and believable government. It establishes resistance, rebellion, dispute, enforcement, reconciliation, cohesion, fragmentation, and recovery without reducing society to one Realm-wide loyalty score.

It does not publish exact January 1062 public-order values, local attitudes, resistance groups, justice officers, disputes, grievances, rebel forces, support percentages, or Opening State bindings.

## 2. Strong Public-Order Verdict

Drengot Capua should not be stable merely because Richard holds titles or garrisons. Aversa begins with deeper Drengot familiarity but still contains households, property interests, institutions, and followers with separate expectations. Capua offers greater political value and deeper local capacity, but its participation remains conditional upon settlement, justice, rights, protection, religious relationships, burden, and remembered conduct.
