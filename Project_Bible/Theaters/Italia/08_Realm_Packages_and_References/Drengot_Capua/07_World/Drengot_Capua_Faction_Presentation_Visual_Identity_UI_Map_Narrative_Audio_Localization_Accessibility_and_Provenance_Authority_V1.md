## 1. Strong Presentation Verdict

Present Drengot Capua as **Two Centers Under One Dangerous but Unfinished Principality**.

The first impression should be of a compact Campanian power whose military confidence and princely ambition are clearer than its institutional unity. Aversa should read as the older Drengot household and military base. Capua should read as the prestigious, politically dense, Lombard-rooted princely center whose incorporation remains incomplete. The faction must communicate Norman leadership, Lombard continuity, ecclesiastical and Papal interfaces, route dependence, finite military depth, and the question of survival beyond Richard without collapsing these into one color, one symbol, or one cultural costume.

The faction introduction should establish:

1. Richard's leverage without presenting Richard as supernatural.
