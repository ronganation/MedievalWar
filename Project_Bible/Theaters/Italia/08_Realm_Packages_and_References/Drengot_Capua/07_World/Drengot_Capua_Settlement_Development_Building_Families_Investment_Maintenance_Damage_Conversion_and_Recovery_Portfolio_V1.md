## 1. Purpose and Scope

This portfolio establishes a general settlement-development and building-family architecture for Drengot Capua. It connects owner-controlled people, property, treasury, materials, labor, routes, water, institutions, military needs, and political participation to visible settlement choices.

The portfolio does not create an exact building list for 1 January 1062. It does not claim that a named site, wall, market, church, residence, storehouse, bridge, workshop, road, or port exists unless the relevant owner confirms it.

## 2. Strong Settlement Verdict

Drengot Capua should develop as a **dual-center Campanian principality whose settlements become stronger through selective coordination, maintained infrastructure, successful participation, and real material investment rather than through a universal building ladder**.

Aversa and Capua must possess different development pressures. Aversa should emphasize household continuity, military assembly, storage, routes, property, records, support, and fallback resilience. Capua should emphasize princely government, institutional coordination, urban participation, property and revenue administration, gates, garrisons, markets, religious interfaces, and contested public authority.

```yaml
SettlementDevelopmentVerdict:
  identity: DualCenterCampanianPrincipalityWithUnevenButComplementaryDevelopment
  primary_player_task: BuildDurableCapacityWithoutFlatteningAversaAndCapuaOrExhaustingPeopleMaterialsTreasuryAndPoliticalSupport
  primary_strength: CompactCentersCanConcentrateInvestmentAndMutuallySupportWhenRoutesAndGovernmentWork
  primary_weakness: MaintenanceLaborMaterialsPropertyAndParticipationCompeteAcrossTwoCenters
  development_model: IndependentBuildingFamiliesWithConditionalBranchesConversionsDamageAndRecovery
  universal_linear_building_tree: false
  one_building_creates_complete_capability: false
  exact_opening_buildings: Deferred
```
