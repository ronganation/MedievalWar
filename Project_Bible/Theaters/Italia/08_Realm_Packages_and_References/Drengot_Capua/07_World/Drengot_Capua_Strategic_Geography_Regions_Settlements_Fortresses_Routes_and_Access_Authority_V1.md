It establishes the strategic functions of Aversa and Capua, region and settlement categories, fortress and crossing logic, routes, access, movement, communications, supply, terrain, weather, and external interfaces.

## 2. Strong Strategic Geography Verdict

Drengot Capua should play as a compact Campanian connector Realm whose local distances are politically and operationally dense rather than automatically cheap. Aversa anchors the Drengot presence on the fertile plain north of Naples. Capua anchors princely authority at the northeastern edge of the Campanian plain and on the Volturno. The Realm's strategic power comes from coordinating those centers, routes, crossings, fortified places, communities, and external interfaces, not from owning a broad continuous map color.

```yaml
GeographyVerdict:
  realm_spatial_identity: CompactCampanianConnectorPrincipality
  primary_centers:
    - Aversa
    - Capua
  primary_strength: AccessBetweenPoliticalMilitaryEconomicAndEcclesiasticalNetworks
  primary_weakness: DenseExposureAndRouteDependence
  continuous_uniform_territory_assumed: false
  compact_distance_equals_easy_control: false
  exact_opening_polygon: Deferred
```

```text
Realm Association != Direct Control
Direct Control != Property
Property != Access
Access != Secure Route
Secure Route != Unlimited Movement
Fortress Presence != Regional Ownership
Settlement Capture != Durable Government
```

## 3. Evidence Boundary

External orientation places Aversa in the fertile Campanian plain north of Naples and identifies it as the first Norman county in Italy. It places Capua on the northeastern edge of the Campanian plain on the Volturno and identifies it as a historical political center. These facts support the dual-center connector model. They do not establish exact medieval roads, borders, cadastral rights, settlement ownership, or fortification status at the scenario opening.
