This assessment corrects the earlier conclusion that specialist authoring could close immediately. ## 14. Prohibited Shortcuts

Reject any proposal that:

- treats this parity assessment as authorization to create every comparator file;
- turns technology into a universal linear tree;
- lets research, reform, technology, buildings, or decisions create people, skills, equipment, horses, materials, money, institutions, units, or recruitment pools;
- creates Jordan, Fressenda, Ranulf, Jonathan, Bartholomew, or unnamed relatives from weak genealogy alone;
- creates unit cards without distinct people, obligation, equipment, training, role, replacement, and counterplay;
- creates buildings or projects as automatic assets;
- lets presentation create historical or gameplay state;
- creates campaign events without specialist-owner inputs;
- updates Opening State, Registry, payloads, runtime state, or Unreal assets during this sequence.
