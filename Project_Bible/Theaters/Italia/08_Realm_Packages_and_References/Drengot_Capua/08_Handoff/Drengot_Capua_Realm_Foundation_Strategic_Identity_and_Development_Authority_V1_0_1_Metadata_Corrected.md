## 2. Strong Foundational Verdict

Drengot Capua is accepted as a playable Realm whose compact geography is offset by high political agency, a powerful opening ruler, a dual-center structure, a prestigious but incompletely consolidated princely inheritance, and direct interfaces with the most consequential powers of central and southern Italia.

The Realm is not admitted because every opening fact is already settled. It is admitted because the known historical pressures support a distinctive campaign structure and because the remaining uncertainty can be represented honestly through layered authority, conditional emergence, competing interests, and later evidence-controlled calibration.

## 3. Evidence, Interpretation, and Design Discipline

### 3.1 Verified orientation basis

The current evidence basis supports the following orientation-level propositions:

- Richard Drengot held the county of Aversa before and during his princely rule.
- Richard assumed the title Prince of Capua before the January 1062 campaign opening.
- Papal recognition at Melfi belongs to the political background of the opening Realm.
- Sources distinguish the assumption of princely title from final possession or consolidation of Capua.
- Aversa predates Capua as the Drengot territorial and dynastic foundation.
- Drengot power interacted with Lombard, Papal, Hauteville, monastic, Gaetan, Neapolitan, Beneventan, and Salernitan contexts.

These propositions authorize a playable political transition. They do not settle every exact office, local faction, holding, garrison, allegiance, or treaty on January 1, 1062.

### 3.2 Evidence classes

```yaml
EvidenceClasses:
  VerifiedHistoricalClaim:
    use: MayGroundRealmAndCharacterFactsWhenSupportedByControllingSources
  SourceSupportedInference:
    use: MayFrameResearchAndConditionalDesignWhenLogicIsExplicit
  ProjectDesignInterpretation:
    use: MayTranslateHistoricalPressureIntoSystemsWithoutClaimingHistoricalFact
  GameplayAdaptation:
    use: MayImprovePlayabilityWithinBoundedHistoricalWindowAndProjectRules
  UnresolvedQuestion:
    use: MustRemainOpenOrBeRoutedToResearchAndOwnerDecision
  ProhibitedAssumption:
    use: MustNotEnterAuthorityPayloadOrPresentationAsFact
```

### 3.3 Bounded creativity rule

Creativity should emerge from documented historical pressure, institutional possibility, strategic geography, and plausible actor incentives. Where direct evidence is thin, the package should prefer configurable relationship classes, conditional outcomes, and unnamed institutional portfolios rather than invented named characters, offices, treaties, or military formations.

```text
Thin Evidence != Empty Design Space
Thin Evidence != Permission To Invent Named Facts
Historical Pressure + Owner Rules = Conditional Design Opportunity
```

## 4. Realm Identity

### 4.1 Core identity statement

Drengot Capua is a Norman-led Campanian principality built across two different foundations: the older dynastic and military base at Aversa and the newer, prestigious, politically layered inheritance at Capua. The Realm begins with unusual offensive and diplomatic potential, but its durability depends on whether personal conquest can become accepted government without destroying the local structures that make princely rule valuable.

## 5. Player Promise and Campaign Fantasy

### 5.1 Short-form promise

**Rule through conquest without being ruled by conquest. Turn Aversa's Drengot power and Capua's Lombard princely inheritance into a durable Campanian Realm capable of shaping Rome and Southern Italia.**

### 5.2 Opening fantasy

The player begins as a strong prince whose title and reputation exceed the uniformity of his administration. Armies and intimidation can force decisions, but lasting rule requires patronage, recognition, local participation, property control, institutional accommodation, and strategic restraint.

The player should feel powerful enough to alter the regional balance immediately, but not wealthy, populous, administratively integrated, or materially supplied enough to act without consequences.

### 5.3 Long campaign fantasies

```yaml
LongCampaignPossibilities:
  CampanianPrincipality:
    description: ConsolidateAStableRegionalPowerAcrossAversaCapuaAndConnectedTerritories
  PapalKingmaker:
    description: UseMilitaryServiceRecognitionAndRomanAccessToShapePapalPolitics
  MasterOfTheTyrrhenianApproaches:
    description: GainEvidenceSupportedAccessInfluenceAndCapacityAroundGaetaNaplesAndCoastalRoutes
  RivalNormanLeadership:
    description: ChallengeHautevillePrimacyWithoutRequiringPermanentNormanCivilWar
  LombardNormanSettlement:
    description: BuildADurableCompositeOrderRatherThanEraseRegionalInstitutions
  WiderSouthernItalianPower:
    description: ExpandBeyondCampaniaWhenWorldStateResourcesAndRelationshipsSupportIt
  SuccessorPrincipality:
    description: SurviveRichardAndConvertPersonalRuleIntoDynasticAndInstitutionalContinuity
```

None of these possibilities is a fixed victory script. Their availability depends on control, recognition, characters, property, economy, population, military capacity, diplomacy, information, public order, and the actions of external actors.

## 6. Aversa and Capua Dual-Center Model

### 6.1 Design rule

Aversa and Capua must not be represented as interchangeable settlement slots. Each center owns a different part of Realm identity and creates different opportunities and risks.

### 6.2 Aversa portfolio

```yaml
AversaPortfolio:
  foundational_role:
    - OlderDrengotTerritorialBase
    - DynasticContinuity
    - NormanFollowerAndPatronageNetworks
    - HouseholdConfidence
    - MilitaryAndPoliticalFallback
  design_strengths:
    - GreaterReliabilityForDrengotHouseholdAuthority
    - StrongerConnectionToDynasticMemoryAndService
```

Aversa should feel like the place where Drengot rule is older and more deeply rooted. That does not make every actor permanently loyal or every resource directly available.

## 7. Richard I Opening Leadership Model

### 7.1 Realm role

Richard I is not a generic general attached to a faction shell. He is the political and military center of the opening Realm and a major reason that a compact principality can operate as a high-agency player faction.

### 7.2 Leadership promise

```yaml
RichardIRealmRole:
  - OpeningPrince
  - CountOfAversaBackground
  - MilitaryCredibilitySource
  - PapalRelationshipActor
  - NormanDynasticCompetitorAndPartner
  - CapuanConsolidationDriver
  - HouseholdPatron
  - SuccessionRiskConcentrator
```

### 7.3 Strength without determinism

Richard should provide meaningful strategic advantages through character capability, reputation, relationships, and command. His existence must not automatically solve administration, local consent, treasury, supply, recruitment, succession, or Papal relations.

```text
Strong Prince != Stable Realm
Battlefield Success != Institutional Integration
Recognition != Obedience
Fear != Durable Compliance
```

### 7.4 Character authority dependency

The full Richard I authority must later determine verified biography, relationships, titles, household, command profile, strategic personality lanes, succession interfaces, life-cycle treatment, and Historical Emergence participation. This Realm authority controls only his structural importance to the faction.

## 9. Historical Emergence: The Keys of Capua

### 9.1 Purpose

The Keys of Capua translates the near-opening consolidation problem into a conditional Historical Emergence family. It is a systems bridge among character authority, political constitution, public order, diplomacy, property, ecclesiastical relations, military pressure, and Realm development.

### 9.2 Trigger philosophy

The emergence must respond to world state rather than a single date. Date windows may influence relevance, urgency, memory, or presentation, but they cannot guarantee an outcome regardless of events.

```yaml
KeysOfCapuaTriggerInputs:
  required_candidate_inputs:
    - RichardLifeStatusAndEffectiveAuthority
    - DrengotMilitaryCredibility
    - CapuanControlAndAccessState
    - LocalEliteParticipationOrOpposition
    - PublicOrderComplianceAndResistance
    - PapalRecognitionMediationOrSanction
    - EcclesiasticalAndMonasticRelations
    - RivalClaimsAndExternalPressure
    - PropertyPatronageAndFiscalCapacity
    - AversaCapuaRelationship
  optional_candidate_inputs:
    - RecentVictoriesOrDefeats
    - HostagesMarriageAndHouseholdConnections
    - ProtectionAgreements
    - CityPrivileges
    - GarrisonConduct
    - InformationConfidenceAndDeception
```

Exact data fields and numeric thresholds remain deferred to framework owners.

### 9.3 Resolution families

#### Integrated princely settlement

Richard confirms selected local rights and incorporates institutions into a durable princely order.

```yaml
IntegratedPrincelySettlement:
  design_direction:
    - HigherDurableLegitimacyPotential
    - BetterInstitutionalContinuity
    - SlowerOrMoreNegotiatedCentralization
    - ContinuedLocalParticipation
  required_cost_types_candidate:
    - Patronage
    - ConfirmedRights
    - SharedOfficeOrJurisdiction
    - LimitsOnImmediateExtraction
```

#### Norman household ascendancy

Richard relies more heavily on Drengot followers, household officers, and military clients.

```yaml
NormanHouseholdAscendancy:
  design_direction:
    - StrongerImmediateCoerciveCoordination
    - GreaterHouseholdDependence
    - HigherLocalAlienationRisk
    - GreaterSuccessionExposure
  required_cost_types_candidate:
    - LandAndRewardPressure
    - ElitePoolConsumption
    - LocalTrustLoss
    - HouseholdFactionCompetition
```

#### Negotiated divided authority

The prince accepts durable local privilege and layered jurisdiction in exchange for recognition, service, taxation, access, or stability.

```yaml
NegotiatedDividedAuthority:
  design_direction:
    - FlexibleCompositeGovernment
    - LowerDirectControl
    - StrongerLocalParticipation
    - PotentiallyComplexCrisisResponse
```

#### Coercive seizure

The prince compels submission through military occupation, confiscation, intimidation, or elite replacement where world-state capability permits.

```yaml
CoerciveSeizure:
  design_direction:
    - FasterDirectControlPotential
    - HigherResistanceAndLegitimacyRisk
    - GreaterPropertyRedistributionPressure
    - PapalEcclesiasticalAndExternalConsequences
  prohibited_shortcut: FreeControlWithoutMaterialPoliticalOrHumanCost
```

#### Failed or reversed consolidation

Drengot authority stalls, fractures, or loses ground because of defeat, death, rival intervention, rebellion, resource failure, or political isolation.

```yaml
FailedOrReversedConsolidation:
  design_direction:
    - RenewedClaimantPolitics
    - LocalAutonomyOrRevolt
    - ExternalGuaranteeOrIntervention
    - AversaFallbackPossibility
    - RealmTransformationRatherThanAutomaticGameOver
```

### 9.4 No false choice menu

The later implementation should avoid presenting all five lanes as simultaneous abstract buttons. They should be expressions of prior conduct and current capability. A player who has alienated every local institution should not receive the same integrated settlement as a player who cultivated participation.

## 11. Papal Interface Foundation

### 11.1 Core design

The Papacy is neither a permanent ally nor only an external threat. It is a source of recognition, legitimacy, mediation, service opportunities, claims, property disputes, sanctions, Roman access, and strategic competition.

```yaml
PapalInterface:
  opportunities:
    - RecognitionAndConfirmation
    - MilitaryService
    - MediationAndArbitration
    - ProtectionAndAlliance
    - InfluenceInPapalContests
    - AccessToWiderPoliticalNetworks
  obligations_and_risks:
    - ServiceExpectations
    - EcclesiasticalPropertyDisputes
    - LimitsOnExpansion
    - SanctionsAndExcommunication
    - RivalPapalClients
    - RomanAndTerritorialComplexity
  prohibited:
    - PermanentAllianceModifier
    - PapalRecognitionEqualsDirectCommand
    - ChurchJurisdictionEqualsSecularOwnership
    - ExcommunicationAsSimpleMoralePenaltyOnly
```

### 11.2 Rome connector

Rome should matter as a strategic and political connector without being absorbed into Drengot Capua's package. Drengot actions may influence access, protection, candidacies, alliances, military intervention, and reputation. Rome's internal political condition remains externally owned.

## 12. Hauteville Interface Foundation

Shared Norman origin and martial practice do not create one political faction. Drengot and Hauteville powers may cooperate, intermarry, exchange service, coordinate war, compete for recognition, contest territory, or attempt dominance.

```yaml
HautevilleInterface:
  relationship_modes:
    - DynasticConnection
    - MilitaryCooperation
    - StrategicRivalry
    - MutualRecognition
    - OpportunisticAlliance
    - ClaimConflict
    - RegionalSphereNegotiation
    - OpenWarWhenWorldStateSupportsIt
  design_rule: NeitherPermanentAllianceNorMandatoryCivilWar
  ownership_rule: SouthernNormanInternalStateRemainsExternal
```

The Drengot faction must remain capable of wider expansion, including maritime and southern ambitions, if it develops the necessary resources and relationships. Its starting identity emphasizes Campanian consolidation but does not imprison the player in Campania.

## 13. Lombard Continuity Foundation

### 13.1 Principle

Norman rulership does not erase Lombard people, institutions, martial traditions, property, language, law, memory, or political agency. Capua's distinctive value comes partly from the older order the Drengots seek to command.

### 13.2 Design applications

```yaml
LombardContinuityApplications:
  - LocalEliteParticipationAndOpposition
  - ExistingPropertyAndRevenueClaims

  - MilitaryServiceAndRecruitmentTraditions
```

### 13.3 Anti-tokenism rule

Lombard continuity must not be reduced to one loyalty meter, one unit, or one building chain. It belongs across political constitution, characters, recruitment, population, property, public order, religion, settlement development, and presentation.
