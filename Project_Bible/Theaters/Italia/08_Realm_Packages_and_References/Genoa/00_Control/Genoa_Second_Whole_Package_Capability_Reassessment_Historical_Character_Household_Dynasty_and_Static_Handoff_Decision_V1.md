## 5. Why History Must Come Before New Characters

The current authorities repeatedly identify unresolved evidence around the 958 diploma, the reported 1056 charter, the exact January 1062 government, <Person>Oberto Pevere</Person>, secular witnesses and officeholders, military-maritime commanders, household actors, Pisa relations, Ligurian intermediaries, and regional or imperial recognition. Those questions cannot be solved responsibly by starting with a character list.

```yaml
HistoryFirstRule:
  sequence:
    - EvidenceInventory
    - HistoricalFactsAndOpeningReconciliation
    - HistoricalMemory
    - HistoricalMomentum
    - HistoricalEmergenceAndTransformation
    - CharacterProsopographyReopen
    - CharacterHouseholdDynastyAdmissionDecision
    - NecessaryCharacterAndNetworkAuthoritiesOnly
  benefits:
    - PreventsLaterFamilyBackProjection
    - SeparatesDocumentedPeopleFromFunctionalRoles
```
