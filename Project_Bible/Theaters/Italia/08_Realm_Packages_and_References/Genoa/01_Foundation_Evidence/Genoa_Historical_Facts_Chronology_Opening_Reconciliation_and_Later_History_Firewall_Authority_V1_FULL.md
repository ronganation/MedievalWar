## 8. Consolidated Opening Historical Thesis

Genoa opens on January 1, 1062 as an urban harbor community with meaningful local agency, a bishopric and religious institutional continuity, maritime experience, merchant and craft activity, a constrained Ligurian landward base, and important coastal, valley, interior, imperial, Papal, Pisan, Sardinian, Corsican, and western Mediterranean interfaces. The opening polity is not a Byzantine province, not a mature commune, not an established later republic, not a bishopric-state, and not a hereditary principality. Its exact secular government, officeholders, household leadership, territory, forces, vessels, treasury, routes, agreements, and bilateral relationships remain unresolved.
