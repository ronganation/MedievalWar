## 2. Purpose

This authority decides whether Genoa's January 1062 human layer requires named households, lineages, families, associations, patronage networks, or a dynasty authority. It preserves these as distinct structures. It prevents later Genoese oligarchic and noble families from being projected backward merely because they became important after the opening.

The correct Genoese model is not an empty court and not a monarchy disguised as a republic. It is an emerging collective polity whose institutions, households, occupational relationships, church networks, voyage partnerships, neighborhood loyalties, patronage, and temporary coalitions may all matter before a ruling dynasty exists.

## 13. Later Prosopography and Genealogy Firewall

The located prosopography overview explicitly concentrates on twelfth- and thirteenth-century Genoa, states that the annals commence with the consular commune in 1099, and notes that their authors and continuators include officeholders and members of powerful later families. This is valuable for later Genoese political development, not proof of those families' January 1062 standing. citeturn102search25

Genealogical indexes and scanned noble-family compilations may generate research leads, but their presence does not qualify opening people or relationships. citeturn102search24turn102search27
