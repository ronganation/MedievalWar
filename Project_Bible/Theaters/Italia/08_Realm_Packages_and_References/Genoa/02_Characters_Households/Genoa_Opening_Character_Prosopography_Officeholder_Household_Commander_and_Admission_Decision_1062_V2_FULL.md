## 2. Purpose

This authority reopens Genoa's human layer after completion of the historical suite. It determines which named people, officeholders, commanders, household actors, clergy, merchants, notaries, judges, envoys, intermediaries, and women may proceed toward bounded character profiles or later Opening State review. It does not populate Genoa merely because gameplay benefits from visible leaders.

The absence of a king, doge, prince, or confirmed consular board is not a character-content defect. Genoa's human identity may be represented through several bounded actors attached to institutions, households, voyages, legal acts, communities, commands, and temporary mandates.
