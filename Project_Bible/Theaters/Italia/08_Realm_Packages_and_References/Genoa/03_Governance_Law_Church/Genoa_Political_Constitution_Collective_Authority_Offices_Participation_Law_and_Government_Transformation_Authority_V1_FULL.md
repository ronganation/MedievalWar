## 1. Purpose and Strong Verdict

This authority makes Genoa politically playable without inventing a prince, back-projecting the later commune, or reducing the Realm to a merchant oligarchy. It defines how an emerging self-governing urban and maritime polity may form decisions through overlapping households, commercial and maritime interests, neighborhoods, ecclesiastical institutions, offices, mandates, records, public recognition, local cooperation, and external relationships.
