## 1. Purpose and Strong Verdict

This authority defines Genoa as a living and finite social system rather than an abstract port, unlimited labor source, or recruitment button. Genoese political, economic, maritime, and military reach depends on households, communities, occupations, skills, residence, mobility, care, material support, and the opportunity costs created when people leave civilian work for service.

## 19. Finite Recruitment Doctrine

Genoa follows the finite recruitment-pool philosophy. Population and communities inform and constrain provider pools, but total population does not become spendable army manpower.
