## 1. Purpose and Strong Verdict

This authority defines Genoa through geographic compression. Urban life, harbor access, maritime movement, coastal communities, steep valleys, mountain approaches, and inland connections are interdependent strategic systems rather than decorative map labels. Genoa's geography creates defensibility and maritime opportunity, but also congestion, route dependence, limited expansion space, and vulnerability when harbor, pass, landing, or hinterland access fails.
