## 1. Strong Genoese Siege Verdict

Represent Genoese siege warfare as a material, operational, human, maritime, environmental, legal, and political contest around defended urban spaces, harbor approaches, coastal landings, routes, passes, strongpoints, camps, works, and institutions. Besiegers must establish authority, access, command, forces, camps, supply, engineering, observation, protection, alternatives, and an exit plan. Defenders need more than walls: command, people, watches, stores, water, repair capacity, communication, civilian protection, relief options, and lawful political continuity all matter.
