## 1. Purpose and Strong Verdict

This authority defines Genoa's diplomacy as a network of delayed communication, partial knowledge, negotiated recognition, access, safe conduct, privilege, agreement, conflict, settlement, and external-owner receipts. Genoa's merchant, maritime, clerical, household, coastal, and regional networks create opportunities to learn and negotiate, but never grant omniscience, automatic trade, guaranteed access, or control over another polity's private state.
