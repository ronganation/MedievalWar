## 1. Strong Genoese Public-Order Verdict

Genoese public order is the audience-specific, issue-specific, place-specific, and time-specific reception of collective authority, household and institutional participation, harbor and route burdens, maritime mobilization, commercial risk, property decisions, ecclesiastical conduct, security performance, external pressure, occupation, remedy, and recovery.

It is not one loyalty number. It is not a hidden timer that manufactures rebels. It does not treat elite agreement as universal consent, visible quiet as acceptance, military control as legitimacy, or suppression as recovery.

## 3. Authority Ownership and Nonownership

### This authority owns

- Genoa-specific public-response definitions and candidate portfolios.
- Audience-specific compliance, conditional cooperation, petition, evasion, resistance, disorder, escalation, stabilization, occupation reception, and recovery interpretation.
- Security-performance and garrison-conduct reception.
- Capitulation reception, civil-military handoff, and occupation-aftercare contracts.
- Revolt and rebellion candidate admission gates.
- Public-order AI observation, proposal, abstention, persistence, validation, and player explanation.

### This authority does not own

- People, households, institutions, communities, languages, health, identity, migration, or population facts.
- Political authority, office, participation, mandate, legitimacy, law, judgment, remedy execution, or appeal.
- Property, goods, money, debt, markets, buildings, projects, routes, vessels, or material delivery.
- Forces, garrisons, patrols, arrests, detention, suppression, capitulation execution, siege, battle, or occupation execution.
- Historical facts, memory, momentum, emergence, external private state, Opening State, Registry, payload, runtime, or Unreal content.
