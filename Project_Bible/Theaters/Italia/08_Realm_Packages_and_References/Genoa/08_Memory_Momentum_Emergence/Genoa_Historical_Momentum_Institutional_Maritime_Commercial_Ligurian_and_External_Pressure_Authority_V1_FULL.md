## 1. Purpose and Strong Momentum Verdict

Historical Momentum converts qualified current conditions, holder-specific memories, repeated practices, unresolved problems, demonstrated capacities, resistance, and world-state change into directional pressure. Momentum is not destiny. Momentum does not guarantee the historical Republic, the Compagna Communis, consular government, crusading participation, crossbow specialization, island influence, or overseas expansion.
