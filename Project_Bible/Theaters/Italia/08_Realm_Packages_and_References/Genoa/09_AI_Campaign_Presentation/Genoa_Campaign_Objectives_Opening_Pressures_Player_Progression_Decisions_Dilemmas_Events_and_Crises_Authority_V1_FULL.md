## 1. Booyah Campaign Verdict

Genoa's campaign experience must provide immediate direction and strong faction identity without pretending that the January 1062 government, ruling personnel, fleet, households, external relationships, or later communal institutions are already known and active. The player should build capacity and political coherence through visible tradeoffs among collective agency, harbor security, Ligurian access, household resilience, commercial credibility, ecclesiastical balance, temporary command, relations with Pisa, and bounded western opportunity.
