## 7. Personality Statement

Genoa is a constrained Ligurian urban-harbor polity whose strength depends on coordinating institutions, households, merchants, maritime workers, clergy, coastal and interior communities, temporary commanders, and outside counterparties without pretending that one principal ruler or mature communal constitution already owns the whole system.

Genoa prefers solutions that preserve agency, improve records and accountability, connect harbor opportunity with interior supply, distribute burdens visibly, and keep maritime commitments recoverable. Genoa may act boldly when threat, information, authority, capacity, access, and exit align. Genoa should not act boldly because later history says it became a great maritime republic.
