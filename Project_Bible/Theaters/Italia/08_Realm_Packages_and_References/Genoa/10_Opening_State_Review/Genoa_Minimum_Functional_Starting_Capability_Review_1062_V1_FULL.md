## 2. Readiness vocabulary and admission contract

- **Defined:** a reusable family or lifecycle exists; no instance follows.
- **Candidate:** historically and mechanically plausible for review; not selected.
- **Functionally required:** without a bounded version the player cannot make or resolve a necessary class of decision.
- **Selected for review:** a design choice proposed to the owner; no binding follows.
- **Owner-admitted:** the appropriate owner has accepted a specific instance, capacity, authority, or explicit null.
- **Held:** necessary exactness has not been resolved; never silently zero or available.
- **External lock:** another polity's rights, knowledge, agreement, or private state requires its owner.
- **Blocked for promotion:** a missing required provider or dependency makes a real opening unsafe.

A review record for each function must contain: function and player action; proposed representation (collective, institution, persistent character, anonymous role, aggregate pool, or explicit absence); responsible owner; people and capacity source; place and reach; mandate and rights; goods, equipment, finance and maintenance; information visibility; loss, vacancy and replacement; source evidence and uncertainty; current status; owner receipt; and failure or abstention behavior. Unknown values remain typed holds, not zeroes. Sources: Genoa population citeturn98search188; Pisa minimum-state brief citeturn98search218.

## 3. Minimum functional coverage matrix

### A. Player control and political decisions: REQUIRED; definition present; opening provider held

The player needs a Genoese decision point able to consider alternatives, authorize bounded mandates, request resources, receive reports, and review or revoke actions. A **functional urban collective** is the appropriate *candidate representation*, not a historical claim that a named council, Compagna Communis, consular board, or universal electorate existed. Specify who can submit a decision, the classes of affected participants, how an order is authenticated, its scope and expiry, and what happens when consultation fails. Do not turn collective authority into ownership of private vessels, ecclesiastical property, or regional communities. Owner gate: political constitution plus Opening State and any affected owners. Exact membership, votes, officeholders and legal procedure remain held. Source: citeturn98search185.

### B. Persistent character and command: REQUIRED command pathway; person selection OPEN

A campaign needs a way to appoint or recognize a finite, accountable land-force commander **if** an opening field force or battle-ready command is selected. That does not prove a named historical commander, require a monarch, or authorize a campaign-original person by default. Two review branches remain: (1) an owner-approved, openly campaign-original persistent commander with finite population source, mandate, location, pay and replacement; or (2) a bounded anonymous persistent command role if the character and battle owners explicitly confirm it can fulfill the playable command interface. If neither is acceptable, promotion is blocked. A commander cannot be an implicit free general or owner of government. A maritime master or pilot is not automatically an admiral. Owner gates: character, political, population, military-maritime, battle and Opening State. Sources: citeturn98search158turn98search183. Pisa's original commander is a comparator decision, not Genoa's selection: citeturn98search204.

### C. Population, households and finite work: REQUIRED; definition present; counts held

Select owner-controlled aggregate urban households and only those coastal, craft, clerical, merchant, maritime and interior interfaces that can be tied to actual eligible populations and places. Each functional provider needs a finite count or bounded capacity, skill, availability, commitments, losses and replenishment. Service must subtract from other work and care. No universal population-as-army-manpower conversion; no anonymous infinity. A missing count can remain a research hold in a review-only document but cannot finance, crew, staff or muster an active opening. Owner gate: population and affected service owners. Source: citeturn98search188.

### D. Core urban place, access and movement: REQUIRED; geography defined; exact map held

The minimum spatial profile needs an urban-harbor center and explicitly bounded shore and landward interfaces. The owner must select an actual campaign node or context, controlled scope, access, and at least one viable way to assess movement and supply without assuming every coastal or inland relationship is owned by Genoa. Separate city, harbor transfer, landing, anchorage, route, pass, presence, access, control and property. If map geometry or routing cannot resolve a required first-turn action, activation is blocked; a review-only map placeholder must not masquerade as a playable path. Owner gates: geography, registry/map, property and external owners. Source: citeturn98search187.

### E. Essential material continuity: REQUIRED; economic lifecycles present; opening stocks held

At minimum the campaign must be able to account for food, water, fuel, shelter and essential maintenance demands; owner-approved acquisition, storage, delivery, consumption, loss and recovery; and a finite means to pay or negotiate for actions. The review does **not** prescribe a treasury amount, commodity list, prices, producer, market route or trade income. A market function creates no goods; credit creates no cash; a harbor creates no revenue. A first-turn order requiring resources must either have a validated source and reservation or remain unavailable with a visible blocker. Owner gates: economy, property, population, health, geography and Opening State. Sources: citeturn98search189turn98search203.

### F. Buildings and operating space: REQUIRED functional capacity; inventory held

Consultation/records, market/handling, household shelter, storage, water and sanitation, care, watch/muster, ecclesiastical practice and harbor transfer are candidate *functions*, not a mandatory one-building-per-function roster. Shared, adapted or temporary spaces may be proposed only with valid site, rights, condition, staff, access and operating inputs. No building definition becomes an opening building; no church structure creates clergy; no wall creates a garrison; no warehouse creates grain. Owner gates: settlement, geography, property, ecclesiastical, health and Opening State. Source: citeturn98search202.

### G. Defense and land-force path: REQUIRED finite response; force selection held

A playable Genoa needs an owner-approved response to urban, harbor or route threats and a finite route from eligible providers through muster, equipment, command, supply, readiness, loss and demobilization. This does **not** require a standing army or a fixed number of starting units. The land roster supplies candidate families only. If the scenario requires a battle-capable opening force, its actual people, equipment, command and location must be selected; an empty or implicit roster is not an acceptable substitute. Owner gates: military-maritime, population, economy, political, geography, battle and Opening State. Source: citeturn98search183.

### H. Maritime identity and operations: REQUIRED bounded interface; fleet not required

Provide the ability to evaluate a voyage, shore transfer, warning, repair or transport proposal and explain why it is feasible or blocked. A *selected* maritime operation requires a vessel with owner and condition, actual master/crew, provisions, access, mission, route knowledge, return and loss handling. Genoa need not receive an opening state navy, admiral, free convoy or active overseas trade route. If no voyage is activated, the campaign must still present maritime opportunity and its current blockers honestly. Owner gates: military-maritime, property, population, geography, economy, diplomacy and Opening State. Source: citeturn98search183.

### I. Bishopric and institutional continuity: REQUIRED bounded interface; holder held

The historical reconciliation admits the bishopric as an opening fact *candidate*. A functional institutional interface may be reviewed without assigning a named bishop, building, treasury, jurisdiction, clergy roster or secular rulership. The later focused review explicitly did **not** admit Oberto Pevere as an active character or opening officeholder. The owner must distinguish an admitted institution from its holder, staff, property and operating capacity. Owner gates: ecclesiastical, historical evidence, character, settlement and Opening State. Sources: citeturn98search178turn98search156.

### J. Law, petition, records and public safety: REQUIRED bounded routes; exact actors held

The player needs an owner-routed way to receive a grievance, preserve a record, assess standing, request remedy, and respond to a threat or emergency without automatically inventing judges, notaries, guards or a later court. Anonymous functional coverage is a design candidate only if eligible people, mandate, forum, tools and finite capacity can be admitted. Public-order response must distinguish complaint, refusal, resistance and violence. Owner gates: law, political, population, public order, settlement and Opening State. Sources: Genoa political constitution citeturn98search185; Genoa prosopography citeturn98search158.

### K. Health, care, water and recovery: REQUIRED dependency model; exact conditions held

First-turn continuity requires food and water access, care and burial routes, hazard observation, and a way to register loss and recover capacity. It does not require an opening outbreak, hospital, physician, disaster or a universal health score. No function heals or supplies itself. Exact facilities, practitioners, stocks and conditions remain owner holds until admitted. Owner gates: health, environment, settlement, population, economy and Opening State. Sources: Genoa building portfolio citeturn98search202; population authority citeturn98search188.

### L. Communication, external relationships and information: REQUIRED bounded proposal path; bilateral state held

The player needs to send or assess a message through a selected provider, route, mandate and knowledge scope, while seeing what is known, stale or disputed. Genoa may propose contact but cannot create an envoy, delivery, Pisan consent, alliance, war, privilege, island control or external private knowledge. The opening Pisa relationship is UnknownMixed, not automatically neutral or hostile. Owner gates: diplomacy/information, political, geography, population, Pisa or other external owner, and Opening State. Sources: Genoa foundation citeturn98search191; Genoa historical facts citeturn98search178.

### M. Player and AI legibility: REQUIRED; definition present; exact seeds held

Show Genoa's harbor-to-hills identity, one or more actionable *owner-supported* first-turn questions, strongest blockers, affected people, opportunity costs, unknowns and alternatives including delay. AI uses the same available state and cannot fill gaps with hidden people, supplies or information. Historical Memory, Momentum and Emergence definitions create no opening seeds or events. Exact AI weights, opening objectives, active crises and presentation bindings remain Opening State and specialist-owner decisions. Sources: Genoa Realm Personality citeturn98search176; Genoa campaign objectives citeturn98search195.

## 4. Critical dependency paths

```text
Political decision -> mandate -> finite provider -> real inputs -> owner receipt -> action -> report/review.
Defense request -> population pool -> equipment/supply -> bounded command -> readiness -> battle handoff -> loss/recovery.
Voyage proposal -> property/vessel -> crew -> cargo/provisions -> access/route -> external receipt if needed -> return.
Food need -> lawful source/stock -> carrier/access -> delivery/acceptance -> household consumption.
Petition -> standing/record -> jurisdiction -> review/remedy -> lawful enforcement -> public response.
```

A failed link produces a visible hold, alternative, investigation or abstention. It never creates an implicit provider. A campaign with only attractive descriptions but no admitted political, material and movement dependencies is not minimum-playable. Source methodology: citeturn98search218.
