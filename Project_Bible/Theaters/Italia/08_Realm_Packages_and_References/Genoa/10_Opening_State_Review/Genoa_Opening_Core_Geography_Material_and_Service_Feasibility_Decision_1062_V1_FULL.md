**Purpose.** Complete the smallest geography, material, and service feasibility decision identified by the preceding political-controller/command/provider review. This file tests whether a *candidate* Genoese first-turn decision could be grounded in an accessible place, finite provider, lawful use of resources, and a verifiable return path. It does not invent a January 1062 map, opening stores, trade route, vessel, army, workforce, weather event, or government. It does not supersede the existing Genoa geography, economy, population, settlement, health, or military authorities. The immediately preceding decision recommended an issue-scoped collective interface, left the command branch conditional, and required finite providers; its source freeze and owner acceptance remain outstanding.

### Decision

**The minimum viable *design* bundle is a bounded urban-harbor context with an assessable shore interface, an assessable landward supply connection, household continuity, and an owner-routed means to observe, communicate, provision, and review defense.** This is a *functional bundle*, not a declaration that those exact places, rights, people, stocks, or routes are admitted on the opening date. A design proposal may show the player a blocked or investigable option; an active opening action cannot spend or traverse an unverified dependency. The maritime identity is legible without an opening state navy or active voyage. A battle-ready starting force is not required by this decision and may not be implied by a watch function.

## 3. Minimum geographic envelope

### G1. Urban decision and household context

Candidate function: an urban Genoese context from which the collective interface can request information, consider a mandate, and account for households and institutions. **Opening hold:** exact strategic node, extent, residence distribution, property, internal movement, political jurisdiction, buildings, safety and operating capacity. A node label does not create an assembly hall, records office, watch, market or water source. Owner receipts: geography/map, political, population, property, settlement and Opening State.

### G2. Shore observation and transfer context

Candidate function: a shore-facing interface where a warning or handling request can be evaluated. **Opening hold:** actual landing/anchorage/harbor works, condition, depth or transfer suitability, access, property, weather exposure, handling labor, storage and security. Observation does not imply a vessel; a landing does not imply a harbor; a harbor does not imply an all-weather berth or fleet. Owner receipts: geography, environment, property, settlement, population, military-maritime if defense is proposed, and Opening State.

### G3. Landward provision and communication connection

Candidate function: at least one way to *assess* whether food, water, fuel, people or messages can move between the urban context and a relevant landward source or intermediary. **Opening hold:** origin, destination, intervening community, route, access, season/condition, carrier, time, security, ownership, available goods and delivery. No named pass or inland supplier is selected. The connection may fail or require negotiation; an external community's cooperation cannot be authored by Genoa. Owner receipts: geography, local/external actors, diplomacy, property, population, economy, environment and Opening State.

### G4. First-turn reachability check

An *active* first-turn order needs a validated path from its origin to its recipient or destination, a viable mode and time window, a current access state, a carrier or messenger if needed, and a return/failure disposition. If any required link is unknown, the order is blocked or becomes a research/negotiation proposal. A UI may show an uncertain connection but must not portray it as open, safe or owned. A review-only Opening State may list held links, but cannot claim a playable movement path from them.

## 4. Minimum material continuity envelope

The following are **demands and accounting interfaces**, not an opening shopping list or invented reserves.

| Need | Owner-backed active requirement | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| Food | Source or accepted stock, rights, household/service demand, storage, carrier, delivery and consumption window | Required for continuity; all exact lots, quantities and sources held |
| Water | Source and quality claim, rights, collection, storage, delivery, competing demand and health review | Required for continuity; exact source, quality and capacity held |
| Shelter and care | Accessible place, household or institutional capacity, people, fuel, supplies, sanitation and review | Required for continuity; no building, healer or hospital selected |
| Fuel and essential upkeep | Owned or acquired material, labor, tools, transport, storage and maintenance priority | Candidate requirement; no exact lot or works selected |
| Money or negotiated service basis | Valid account, contribution, contract or obligation; authorization distinct from collection and payment | Required for any action with cost; no public balance or free credit selected |
| Equipment and transport | Actual owner, condition, availability, issue and return; vessel/animal if needed | Required only for an action that consumes them; no stock selected |

A known commodity *family* is not an available lot. A food stock is not delivered food; a water source is not safe distributed water; credit is not cash; a public request does not transfer private property. A temporary shared room may satisfy a function only when the owner verifies rights, site, condition, staff and operating inputs. Where exact quantities are missing, this file makes **no numeric estimate or Pisa-derived band**.

These are *functions*, not seven opening occupations or staffed departments. One person can be eligible for several roles but cannot be double-committed across overlapping windows. For any proposed service, the population owner must identify a finite eligible source, actual availability, skill, place, current commitments, care burden, obligation or consent, compensation/support, release and recovery. Maritime work cannot silently borrow the same mariner from trade, fishing and defense. A land-force readiness assessment cannot create an equipped unit or commander. If the provider is unknown, the action is **held**, not free and not historically absent.

## 6. Four bounded first-turn feasibility probes

These are test questions for the future Opening State owner, **not preloaded missions, events or actions**.

1. **Harbor warning.** Can a qualified observer report a concern to a recipient through a real message path? Required: place, observer, visibility and knowledge, messenger/channel, travel/access, authentication, political reception. If absent: `WarningPathUnverified`; offer observation preparation or information request, not a revealed threat or war.
2. **Household provision.** Can a demand for food or water be compared with an actual lawful source and delivered through a viable path? Required: demand, stock/source, rights, quality where relevant, carrier, route, storage, receipt. If absent: `SourceOrDeliveryHeld`; offer investigation, negotiation, reserve protection or abstention, not free imports.
3. **Maintenance and shore handling.** Can a limited repair or transfer request be costed without presuming a quay, yard, warehouse, skilled workforce or public vessel? Required: site and rights, damage/need, tools, materials, workers, access, funds, inspection. If absent: `SiteRightsOrInputsUnverified`; no construction or repair progress.
4. **Defensive readiness.** Can the collective request an assessment of a threat response? Required: political mandate, available provider, place, equipment, supply, command if a battle-ready force is claimed, loss and demobilization path. If absent: `ProviderCommandOrSupplyHeld`; an inquiry remains possible, but no army, garrison or battle unit is spawned.

**Probe result at present:** the domain *definitions* exist; exact dependencies have not been admitted. Therefore all four probes are **not executed and not passed as active first-turn actions**. This is a positive design decision with a negative promotion result, not an assertion that Genoa historically lacked food, water, harbor labor or defenders.
