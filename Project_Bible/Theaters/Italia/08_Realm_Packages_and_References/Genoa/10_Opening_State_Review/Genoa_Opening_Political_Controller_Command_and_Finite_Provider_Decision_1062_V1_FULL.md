## 1. Decision in plain language

**Recommend a collective political *interface*, not an invented historical council or individual ruler.** For civilian and political questions the player addresses the Genoese urban political community through a reviewable, issue-scoped decision case. A decision becomes an executable instruction only after the relevant political owner accepts its authority and procedure and the affected service owners confirm actual people, rights, location, time and inputs. The opening body name, members, vote, executive and constitutional form remain **held**, not selected. This follows the political authority's separation of Realm, government, office, mandate, delivery and performance. citeturn97search100

**Do not select an opening general now.** If an owner-approved starting force must enter a tactical battle on the first turn, require a qualified persistent command holder before promoting that force. Review two branches: an openly campaign-original person admitted by character and population owners, or a persistent bounded anonymous command holder *only if* the character, battle and military owners explicitly accept that representation. If neither is accepted, the force is blocked; the polity may still retain non-battle political and defensive *proposal* functions. The minimum-capability review explicitly leaves this branch unresolved, and the Genoa prosopography admits no named opening commander. citeturn97search97turn97search124

**Keep providers finite and owner-sourced.** Urban service, records, messengers, watch, port handling, mariners, pilots, shipwrights, care, transport and other functions are eligibility families, not staff lists. A first-turn action that consumes one must have a population/community source, available and uncommitted capacity, mandate or contract, place, support, and an opportunity-cost disposition. If that chain is absent, show the blocker and allow preparation, investigation, negotiation, delay or abstention instead of a free provider. citeturn97search101turn97search122

## 3. Controller profile: collective without a fabricated council

The player-facing controller is a **decision interface** through which Genoa's urban political community considers bounded issues. It is neither a new institution nor a person. Its allowed review-level functions are to frame a problem, request information, identify affected actors, consider alternatives, seek authorization, propose a mandate, receive a report, and initiate review. It cannot automatically tax, seize property, command a vessel, appoint a bishop, compel a regional community, declare a bilateral relationship or win a battle. citeturn97search100

For each *candidate* decision case record: issue and geographic scope; proposer class; claimed authority and evidence; affected households, institutions and external actors; participation or consultation needs; dissent and incapacity; authenticated instruction method; mandate scope, duration and review; requested people, goods, place and access; delivery and receipt; failure, appeal and handback. **Do not** populate these fields with fictional officeholders, membership, vote shares or a named assembly. A functional interface is acceptable for design review; active use requires the political and Opening State owners to admit an actual scenario representation and its executable limits. citeturn97search100turn97search97

## 4. Command decision tree

### A. No battle-ready opening force selected

No named general is required merely to display Genoa or offer a bounded defense proposal. A watch, muster or defensive *function* remains definition-only until its own personnel, equipment, place, command and support are admitted. A later mobilization may use an owner-approved command selection. This is **not** a finding that Genoa historically had no defenders or commanders. citeturn97search97turn97search122

### B. A battle-ready opening force is selected

The force must have an accepted commander representation and scope, along with real providers, equipment, supply, readiness, place and battle handoff. Two candidate routes exist:

1. **Openly campaign-original persistent person.** Admit through character and population owners, identify it as fictional scenario design to the player, and separately specify life state, finite household/support, place, appointment, mandate, supply, replacement, loss and succession. Do not borrow a historical identity, a famous family, or Pisa's original commander. No such Genoese person is selected here.
2. **Bounded anonymous persistent command holder.** Only if the character and battle owners establish that an anonymous but persistent, trackable holder can meet identity, casualty, capture, removal, replacement and tactical command contracts. An untracked generic officer icon or inexhaustible placeholder fails this branch.

A shipmaster, pilot, shipowner or merchant does not become an admiral or civic ruler by title. Land command does not grant maritime command. A temporary mission must include appointment or recognition, acceptance, scope, expiry, reporting, recall, handback and a vacancy response. citeturn97search122turn97search100turn97search101

## 5. Finite provider chain

Every proposed action follows the same chain:

```text
Decision need -> valid authority and mandate -> provider eligibility -> actual availability
-> household/civilian opportunity cost -> rights, consent or obligation -> place and access
-> equipment, money, goods and support -> assignment or muster -> delivery/performance
-> report, loss, release, handback and recovery.
```

The population owner already separates population from pools, eligibility from availability, reservation from recruitment, and muster from a ready unit. One person may qualify for multiple roles but cannot fulfill incompatible commitments simultaneously. Its provider families cover urban households, neighborhoods, coastal and valley communities, mariners, masters, pilots, shipwrights, port transport, specialists and bounded military service. These are definitions, not a Genoese opening headcount. citeturn97search101

**Candidate minimum continuity functions, not guaranteed opening jobs:**

- **Political records and communication:** an authenticated instruction and report route; literacy or messenger capacity requires actual providers and a valid place.
- **Household continuity:** food, water, shelter, care and work cannot be double-committed to defense, voyage or construction.
- **Urban/harbor warning and defense:** a finite observation and response path, not an automatically formed unit or garrison.
- **Harbor and inland handling:** people, property rights, shore or route access, transport and storage; a harbor label is insufficient.
- **Maritime evaluation:** possible vessel, master, pilot, crew, provisioning, access and return are separately checked; a state navy is not required for the design review.

For each candidate, the owner must provide a finite pool or persistent person reference, skill and location, effective window, already committed work, reserve/replacement and a recovery rule. A typed `HeldUnknown` is acceptable in this *review* but cannot be spent or assigned in an active start. citeturn97search97turn97search101turn97search122

## 6. Three first-turn probes, not preloaded missions

**Harbor warning:** Can the collective seek a report on an observed coastal concern? Require a valid information channel, observer, delivery route and political reception. Without them, show `ObserverOrDeliveryUnverified`, not a magically revealed threat. No incident or war is created.

**Household provision:** Can Genoa assess a food or water concern? Require a known source or a request for information, household demand, rights, carrier, route, storage and receipt. Without stocks or access, show `SupplySourceOrDeliveryHeld`; do not grant free imports or passive port income.

**Defense request:** Can the collective request readiness? Require eligible providers, opportunity-cost review, equipment, command, site and supply. Without a qualified command holder, a claimed battle-ready force is blocked; a request for information or future muster review may remain available.

These are **design validation probes** derived from the minimum-function matrix, not active opening events, crises, AI weights, objectives or orders. citeturn97search97

## 8. Verification cases and disposition

1. **No named ruler:** political decisions can be *proposed* through collective review; no fake historical council, bishop-ruler or prince appears.
2. **Missing command:** battle-capable force promotion fails when neither persistent holder branch is accepted; civilian political proposals do not thereby become an army.
3. **Double commitment:** a mariner already on a voyage cannot simultaneously fill a military crew slot without a valid transfer and civilian consequence.
4. **Unknown is not zero:** an uncalibrated pool, treasury or route stays held; it cannot be spent or described as historically absent.
5. **External lock:** a Genoese proposal cannot write Pisa's agreement, island access or another actor's private state.
6. **Save boundary:** a later owner-approved opening choice applies only to a new campaign; it cannot backfill an advanced save.

These are **static review criteria**, not executed tests. The three clocks remain separate: Historical +21 months, Biological +7 months, and one ordered Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn campaign-season step per strategic turn. No time step alone creates a commander, institution, ship, harvest or historical outcome. citeturn97search103turn97search97
