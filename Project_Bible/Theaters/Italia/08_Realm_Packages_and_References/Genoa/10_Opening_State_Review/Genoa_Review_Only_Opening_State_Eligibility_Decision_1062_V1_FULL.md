## 6. Minimum playable demonstration still required

Before active promotion, the future Opening State must demonstrate at least one owner-admitted path for each required class:

1. **Political action:** a reviewed mandate path with authority, delivery, report, expiry, and handback.
2. **Household continuity:** finite food, water, shelter, care, and work dependencies.
3. **Place and movement:** an admitted urban context and at least one viable message or supply path.
4. **Provider capacity:** finite people with skills, location, time, competing commitments, support, and recovery.
5. **Law and public safety:** bounded petition, record, remedy, warning, and emergency-response routes.
6. **Institutional continuity:** bishopric and other institutional functions without invented holders or assets.
7. **Defense:** either a real finite force-command-support chain or an explicit opening without a battle-ready force.
8. **Maritime legibility:** an honest opportunity and blocker model, with no active voyage unless vessel, crew, rights, provisions, route, and return are admitted.
9. **External restraint:** reciprocal receipts or explicit external locks.
10. **Player and AI explanation:** visible unknowns, strongest blockers, alternatives, delay, preparation, negotiation, and abstention.
