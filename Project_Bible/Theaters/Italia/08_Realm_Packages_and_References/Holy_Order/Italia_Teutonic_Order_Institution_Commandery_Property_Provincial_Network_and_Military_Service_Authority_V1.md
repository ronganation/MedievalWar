## 0. Authority Purpose

This authority defines the Teutonic Order as a later transregional religious-military institution with a materially documented Italian branch. It controls chronology, provincial and local command, dispersed commanderies, churches, monasteries, urban and rural properties, personnel, German-Italian contact networks, local adaptation, economic support, military-service gates, decline, loss, and legacy.

It does not create a January 1062 Order, a continuous Teutonic territorial state in Italia, a universal German-knight pool, or a generic Teutonic Knight battle card.

```yaml
primary_promise:
  RepresentTheTeutonicOrderInItaliaAsADispersedAdaptiveInstitutionalNetworkWhoseSitesPeoplePropertyServiceAndMilitaryCapacityRemainExactFiniteAndSourceOwned
```
