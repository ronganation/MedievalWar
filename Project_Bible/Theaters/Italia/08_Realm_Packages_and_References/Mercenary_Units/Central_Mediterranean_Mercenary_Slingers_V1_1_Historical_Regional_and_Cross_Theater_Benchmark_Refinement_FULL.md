Central Mediterranean Mercenary Slingers are practiced contract missile troops drawn from exact companies, communities, or released regional sources circulating within a Mediterranean environment of islands, coasts, ports, straits, changing rulers, and overlapping populations. Their military identity is the hand-sling skirmish cycle: establish casting space, select suitable projectiles, pressure exposed troops, and move before cavalry or close infantry traps the formation.

The profile is a sibling to West Francia Mercenary Slingers, not a global replacement. It owns separate source, circulation, political-access, maritime-transport, equipment-expression, and recovery questions. A Sicilian Muslim company, an Ifriqiyan company, or an island company may use this card only when actual evidence and campaign state establish the source. None is created by identity or geography alone.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Mercenary Slingers
- **Scope:** Central Mediterranean theater sibling
- **Class:** Practiced light contract hand-sling infantry
- **Core verb:** Cast, Displace, Repeat
- **Source:** Exact validated company, community, pastoral, urban, rural, military, or other practiced slinger source released or organized for contract
- **Battle promise:** Pressure exposed troops from an open skirmish line and shift position before cavalry, infantry, counter-fire, or terrain closes the escape route
- **Best use:** Screens, ridges, uneven open ground, settlement approaches, camps, siege approaches, crossings, lightly protected targets, and mixed armies lacking mobile missile pressure
- **Worst use:** Cavalry exposure, confined streets, dense friendly formations, close infantry, heavy counter-fire, deep mud, unsuitable projectiles, and failed maritime or land return
- **Strategic value:** Provides portable missile capability through contracted skill without creating a domestic slinger population for the employer
- **Strategic cost:** Practiced people, leaders, slings, prepared projectile supply, containers, transport, maritime or land routes, pay, medicine, return, and source opportunity cost
- **Signature decision:** Continue pressure, change the casting arc, conserve projectiles, or displace before the line is caught

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Central Mediterranean Mercenary Slingers are hired light missile troops from exact regional or company sources around a politically fragmented and highly connected maritime theater. Their slings are simple weapons, but battlefield effectiveness depends on long practice, projectile judgment, casting rhythm, spacing, target selection, leadership, and disciplined withdrawal.

The company may be locally sourced in Sicily or arrive through a validated North African, Sardinian, Corsican, or other Mediterranean route. Such labels describe origin and service, not automatic quality. A company operating in Sicily does not become Sicilian by default, and a Muslim, Christian, Greek-speaking, Arabic-speaking, Berber-speaking, island, pastoral, or urban population does not become a slinger source without evidence of actual practice and organization.
