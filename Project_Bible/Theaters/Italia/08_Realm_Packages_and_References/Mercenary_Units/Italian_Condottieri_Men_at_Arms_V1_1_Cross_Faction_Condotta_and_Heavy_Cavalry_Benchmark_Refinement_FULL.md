## 3. Player-Facing Description

Italian Condottieri Men-at-Arms are experienced mounted troops furnished by a captain-led mercenary company under a fixed or renewable condotta. Their battlefield power comes from practiced mounted cohesion, capable leaders, warhorses, lances, protection, and accumulated service experience. Their strategic danger comes from the same source: the employer depends on an armed organization with its own captain, followers, interests, reputation, debts, ambitions, and alternatives.

The unit is strongest when committed once the battlefield has created a valuable opening. A badly timed frontal charge wastes expensive horses and experienced personnel, weakens the company’s bargaining position, and may turn a temporary contract into a long recovery crisis.
