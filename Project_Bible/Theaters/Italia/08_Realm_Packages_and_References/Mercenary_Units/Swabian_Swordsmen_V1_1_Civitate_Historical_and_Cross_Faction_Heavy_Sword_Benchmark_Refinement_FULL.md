Swabian Swordsmen are premium contract heavy infantry whose battlefield identity combines mailed protection, disciplined close formation, and powerful large-sword pressure. They advance deliberately, use reach and committed cuts or thrusts to contest enemy weapon lanes, fight hard in close contact, and preserve enough cohesion to continue holding when ordinary hired infantry might fail.

Their reputation is earned through actual company quality, weapons, armor, leaders, veteran files, contract treatment, and combat history. The name **Swabian** does not grant supernatural strength. The company can be worn down by missiles, cavalry, fatigue, flanking, broken terrain, failed command, and prolonged isolation.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Swabian Swordsmen
- **Class:** Premium contract heavy sword infantry
- **Core verb:** Advance, Cut, Stand
- **Era:** Strong eleventh-century research direction anchored by Civitate in 1053; exact January 1062 availability unresolved
- **Source:** Exact Swabian, southern German, imperial, captain-led, or related company source with qualified infantry, weapons, armor, leaders, route, contract, and replacement
- **Battle promise:** Advance into a decisive infantry fight, apply powerful sword pressure, and maintain formation under severe close-combat stress
- **Best use:** Heavy infantry fights, hard reserves, critical lanes, gates, breaches, hill or camp positions, and counterattacks against disordered enemies
- **Worst use:** Long missile exposure, cavalry charges without support, open flanks, deep mud, dense woodland, several-direction attack, exhausted pursuit, and service after pay or supply collapse
- **Strategic value:** Purchases a scarce mature heavy infantry company without creating Swabian people, training, armor, or replacement for the employer
- **Strategic cost:** High pay, strong bargaining power, cross-Alpine or long-distance routes, heavy equipment, veteran scarcity, medicine, ransom, and slow replacement
- **Signature decision:** Commit the company to break or stabilize a decisive infantry fight, or preserve it rather than pursue into exposure

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Swabian Swordsmen are heavily equipped contract infantry whose large swords and disciplined files make them dangerous in decisive close combat. Their best performance comes from controlled advance, mutual support, secure flanks, and a target already fixed or unable to avoid contact. They may pressure armored, shielded, or spear-armed infantry, but no weapon automatically defeats armor, shields, or formation.

The company’s willingness to stand under pressure depends on leaders, veteran cohesion, purpose, retreat conditions, contract treatment, and surviving formation integrity. Casualties do not make the unit stronger. A company reduced below effective leadership, spacing, and support becomes less capable even if surviving veterans continue to resist.

## 37. Counterplay

- Use archers, crossbowmen, slingers, javelins, or other missile pressure before contact.
- Use cavalry against open flanks, rear, withdrawal, or disordered files.
- Use pikes or longer reach from stable formation where appropriate.
- Break spacing with terrain, obstacles, several-direction pressure, or forced movement.
- Refuse the heavy close fight and exhaust the company.
- Counterattack after overextended pursuit or committed blows.
- Attack leadership, standards, pay, supply, routes, and replacement.
- Isolate the company from supporting infantry.
