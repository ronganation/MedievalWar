Swiss Halberdiers are much-later professional mercenary infantry built around disciplined halberd use in dense but maneuverable close formations. Their polearms combine thrusting, cutting, and hooking possibilities, but those functions depend upon spacing, weapon handling, target geometry, leadership, fatigue, and company cohesion. They are designed to meet a dangerous approach, disrupt mounted or armored opponents, and exploit broken formations in hard melee.

They should be among the strongest contract polearm infantry available once valid Swiss regional sources, companies, captains, halberds, drill, routes, employers, and chronology exist. They are not universally superior infantry. Long-range missiles, artillery where present, terrain that breaks spacing, flank attack, exhaustion, disrupted formation, and prolonged exposure can defeat them.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Swiss Halberdiers
- **Class:** Professional later Swiss close-polearm mercenary infantry
- **Core verb:** Brace, Hook, Break
- **Era:** Conditional Late High emergence and mature Late Era service; never an opening 1062 unit
- **Source:** Exact Swiss regional or company source with qualified personnel, halberds, captains, formation practice, contract access, and replacement route
- **Battle promise:** Meet a valuable close threat with disciplined polearm control, disrupt its formation, and convert loss of order into lethal melee pressure
- **Best use:** Mounted approaches, armored infantry, constrained lanes, urban fighting, reserve counterattack, protecting critical frontage, and exploiting disrupted enemies
- **Worst use:** Long missile exposure, artillery fire, deep mud, dense woodland, severe slopes, flank and rear attack, isolated pursuit, and combat after spacing or cohesion collapses
- **Strategic value:** Purchases a mature premier polearm company without giving the employer ownership of Swiss people, communities, traditions, or replacement sources
- **Strategic cost:** Exceptional pay and political demand, finite veteran cadre, specialized weapons, formation depth, route access, difficult replacement, and high consequences for wasteful use
- **Signature decision:** Hold formation against the approach, commit to disruption, or preserve the company rather than pursue into broken ground

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Swiss Halberdiers are professional contract infantry whose reputation rests on much more than the shape of the weapon. The company must preserve spacing, recognize the moment of contact, coordinate thrusts and cuts, avoid tangling its own polearms, and maintain enough cohesion to exploit the enemy’s loss of order. In the right lane they can threaten cavalry, armored infantry, and other valuable close-combat troops. Once flanked, scattered, exhausted, or pinned under missiles, the same long weapons become difficult to use.

The card represents a later Swiss-source mercenary family, not every Swiss infantryman and not every company of Swiss mercenaries. Exact cantonal, regional, confederate, civic, captain-led, foreign-service, or other source expressions require separate historical validation. A famous reputation does not create an available company.
