## 3. Player-Facing Description

Swiss Pikemen are later Swiss-source infantry organized to use long pikes as a collective formation weapon. The unit is not a passive wall. A capable body can advance, deny mounted approaches, force opposing infantry to respect its reach, and press a disrupted line while maintaining depth and pace.

The player must preserve officers, standards, spacing, flanks, and open ground. Once the formation loses depth or is entered from several directions, the pikes become harder to employ and the soldiers require halberdiers, sidearms, supporting formations, or room to reform.
