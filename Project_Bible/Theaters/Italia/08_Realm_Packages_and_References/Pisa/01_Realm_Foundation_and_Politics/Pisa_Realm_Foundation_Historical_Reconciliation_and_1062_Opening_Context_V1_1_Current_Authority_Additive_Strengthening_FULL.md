## 5. Key Reconciliation: The Reported 1060 Pisa-Genoa Battle

The user-provided reference and the Republic of Pisa orientation page report a first battle between Pisa and Genoa in 1060 and describe a Pisan victory that strengthened Pisa's Mediterranean position. That date lies before the January 1, 1062 scenario opening, so this trunk does not create an active opening battle, war instance, fleet loss, territorial transfer, or automatic diplomatic state from that report alone.

A Cambridge chapter summary for the later Pisa-Genoa war instead identifies 1119 as the definite beginning of the long struggle between the communes. This does not automatically disprove a clash in 1060. It shows that the project must distinguish an earlier encounter or remembered victory from the later sustained communal war. The exact character, scale, location, belligerent authority, forces, outcome, and diplomatic consequences of the reported 1060 event remain open research questions.
