## 1. Modern Medieval II Doctrine Verdict

Pisan doctrine must convert finite rosters, vessels, people, command, routes, access, supplies, and political authority into coherent operations. Pisa should excel when land and maritime forces cooperate around the Arno, crossings, landings, urban approaches, convoys, and limited expeditionary objectives. Pisa should struggle when forced into unsupported deep inland campaigns, prolonged attrition, cavalry-dominated open terrain, or maritime operations without repair and return capacity.
