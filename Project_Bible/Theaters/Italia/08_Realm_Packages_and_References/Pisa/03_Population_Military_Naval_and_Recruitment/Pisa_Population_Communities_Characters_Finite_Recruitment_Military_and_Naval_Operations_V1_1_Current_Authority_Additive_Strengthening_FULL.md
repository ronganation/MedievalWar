## 1. Purpose

This authority develops the people who make Pisan power possible. It ties communities, households, characters, offices, labor, finite recruitment, ships, crews, military forces, command, operations, loss, and recovery into one bounded Realm capability.

It also introduces Giovanni Orlandi as a serious dated character candidate rather than silently placing him in the January 1, 1062 opening. Available orientation sources associate Giovanni Orlandi with Pisan political and naval leadership in 1063, while the exact opening identity, office, age, relationships, authority, and location remain insufficiently verified. The character is therefore prepared for evidence-led inclusion and possible near-opening Historical Emergence, not fabricated as an active opening ruler.

## 5. Consiglio degli Anziani Boundary

Orientation material places the Consiglio degli Anziani much later than the opening, with the Republic of Pisa summary listing it from 1202 to 1312. It therefore cannot be treated as Pisa's January 1062 government. citeturn6search97

The institution remains useful as a later political-development reference, but only as a possible long-range successor form after evidence, participation, office, legal, and transformation requirements are defined. It is not an automatic endpoint and should not erase earlier consular, household, ecclesiastical, military, or communal arrangements.
