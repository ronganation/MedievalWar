## 1. Purpose

This authority makes diplomacy and information central to Pisa's maritime identity. Pisa must negotiate access, security, recognition, trade, restitution, cooperation, claims, and rivalry across a political field where ships move faster than reliable knowledge and where merchants, clergy, envoys, captains, households, travelers, and captives may all carry partial or interested information.

Diplomacy does not merely toggle relations. Information does not become universal when one actor knows it. Agents do not create outcomes by title. Deception does not overwrite reality. Every agreement, report, mission, claim, warning, and negotiation must have an actor, source, route, mandate, confidence, audience, cost, risk, and consequence.
