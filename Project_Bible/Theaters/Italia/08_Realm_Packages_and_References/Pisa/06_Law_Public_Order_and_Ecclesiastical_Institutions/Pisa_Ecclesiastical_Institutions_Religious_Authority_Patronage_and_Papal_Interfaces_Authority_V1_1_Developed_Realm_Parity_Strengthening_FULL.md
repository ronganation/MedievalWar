## 3. Source and Evidence Boundary

The present orientation evidence establishes that the Pisan see predates the scenario and was elevated to an archdiocese only on April 21, 1092. The same orientation source reports that Pope Gregory VII appointed Bishop-elect Landulfus of Pisa as Papal legate in Corsica in 1077 and confirmed related privileges in 1078. These are later developments and cannot define the January 1, 1062 opening. citeturn14search27

The official cathedral institution states that the current cathedral was founded in 1064. This places that construction after the January 1, 1062 opening and prevents the developed later cathedral from being treated as an opening building or completed institutional complex. citeturn14search31
