## 1. Pisan Law and Justice Strategic Verdict

Pisan law and justice must operate as a plural, office-bound, record-dependent system rather than as a single modern court or an abstract public-order modifier. Urban political authority, margravial and imperial claims, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, customary practice, property relations, commercial agreements, maritime activity, household rights, military command, and external privileges may overlap, compete, cooperate, or remain unresolved.
