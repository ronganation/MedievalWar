## 1. Strong Public Order Verdict

Pisan public order is the place-specific, audience-specific, issue-specific reception of government, maritime mobilization, taxation, credit, property, defense, command, expedition, religious authority, external claims, damage, remedy, and recovery. It is not one loyalty number and it is not a hidden timer that manufactures generic rebels.

Pisa's opening problem is to convert plural urban power and recent maritime prestige into durable collective action without treating elite agreement as universal consent, military success as legitimacy, commercial profit as shared benefit, or enforcement as recovery.
