## 1. Modern Medieval II Building-Roster Verdict

Pisa requires a real portfolio of urban, river, maritime, commercial, military, ecclesiastical, health, and recovery facilities. Buildings must create usable capacity only after land, authority, design, materials, labor, finance, transport, construction, inspection, and commissioning are complete. Pisa receives no abstract building chain that creates people, fleets, revenue, trade, public order, or military units by itself.
