## 1. Strong Pisan Settlement-Development Verdict

Pisa must be developed as a **river-maritime urban polity whose built environment converts access, labor, property, materials, institutions, markets, waterfront handling, defense, and civic coordination into maintained capacity**. The result must preserve Medieval II readability and broad development freedom while refusing later monumental identity, free ports, free trade, free fleets, free cathedral construction, or automatic urban growth.
