## 1. Strong Pisan Historical Emergence Verdict

Pisan historical emergence is a **conditional, owner-routed, evidence-bounded field of possible people, institutions, relationships, events, projects, and successor forms**. It permits history to become available without scheduling history by date. Every pathway must preserve delay, alteration, partial occurrence, replacement, failure, dormancy, impossibility, and permanent nonoccurrence.
