## 1. Strong Pisan Historical Memory Verdict

Pisan historical memory must be represented as **holder-specific, evidence-bounded, transmitted, contested, revisable, and politically usable interpretation of owned past events and conditions**. There is no single Pisan memory, no automatic civic consensus, and no universal recollection of the reported 1060 Genoese conflict, Mediterranean expeditions, island relationships, urban precedent, religious patronage, household loss, maritime success, environmental crises, or broken agreements.
