## 9. Conversion, Redirection, Reversal, Exhaustion, and Termination

```yaml
Conversion: A pressure changes form after owned decisions or outcomes, such as rivalry pressure converting into fortification,
  diplomacy, commerce, or war preparation.
Redirection: The target, method, audience, or domain changes while some carried pressure persists.
Reversal: Evidence and owned outcomes establish movement against the prior direction.
Exhaustion: Carriers, inputs, legitimacy, attention, or willingness are spent or unavailable.
Termination: The pressure no longer has sufficient carriers, relevance, owner support, or viable pathway for its declared
  scope.
Rules:
- ConversionCreatesNoResources
- RedirectionRequiresTraceableCause
- ReversalDoesNotErasePriorCostsOrMemory
- ExhaustionDoesNotKillActorsOrAssets
- TerminationDoesNotDeleteHistoricalRecord
```
