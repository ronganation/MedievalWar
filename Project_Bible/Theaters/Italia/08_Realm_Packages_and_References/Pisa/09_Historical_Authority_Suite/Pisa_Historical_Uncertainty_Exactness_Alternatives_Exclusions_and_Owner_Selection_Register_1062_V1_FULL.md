## 1. Strong Uncertainty and Owner-Selection Verdict

This register controls what the Pisa historical package may do when the evidence is incomplete, imprecise, internally conflicting, dependent, later, or owned by another authority. It preserves meaningful alternatives without treating all alternatives as equally supported, identifies the owner who may resolve each dimension, and prevents an attractive interpretation from becoming January 1062 truth merely because the project needs a ruler, office, port, fleet, relationship, event, or landmark.
