## 1. Modern Medieval II Campaign Verdict

Pisa's campaign structure must provide immediate direction, strong faction identity, emergent midgame pressures, and memorable crises without converting later history into a fixed mission tree. Objectives should reward building a viable river-sea power, while decisions and dilemmas expose conflicts among command, commerce, law, households, ecclesiastical authority, external relationships, land security, and maritime ambition.

```yaml
CampaignVerdict:
  model: ConditionalObjectivePortfolioWithStateDrivenDecisionsDilemmasEventsAndCrises

    - DefendAndDevelopPisaArnoAndSeawardAccess
    - BuildFiniteMaritimeCommercialAndCommandCapacity
    - NavigateUrbanInstitutionalAndExternalAuthority
    - PursueOpportunityWithoutGuaranteedHistoricalOutcome
    - SurviveAndRecoverFromOverextensionConflictAndEnvironmentalShock
  fixed_historical_mission_tree: false
```
