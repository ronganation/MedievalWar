## 1. Modern Medieval II Presentation Verdict

Pisa must be legible at a glance as an eleventh-century river-sea power without being reduced to the later Leaning Tower, a generic Italian republic, or a naval stereotype. Presentation should communicate urban collective authority, the Arno, sea access, merchant shipping, finite command, ecclesiastical patronage, Tuscan land exposure, and the tension between ambition and overextension.
