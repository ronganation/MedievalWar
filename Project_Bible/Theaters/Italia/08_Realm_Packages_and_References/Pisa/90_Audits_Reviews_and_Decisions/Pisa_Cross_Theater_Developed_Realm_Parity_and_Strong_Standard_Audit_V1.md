## 1. Audit Verdict

The Pisa files possess a strong conceptual core and preserve the most important historical, ownership, anti-fabrication, finite-capacity, nonmutation, and Realm-differentiation rules. They are not weak drafts.

They do not yet meet the strongest project-wide developed-Realm standard represented by the current Burgundy, Flanders, Norway, Valencia, Hammadid, Southern Norman, and Venice authorities. The gap is not mainly historical content. The gap is authority engineering: owner and consumer contracts, state-layer separation, complete lifecycle contracts, reciprocal external-owner receipts, transaction and rollback architecture, persistence and migration, validation fixtures, source-preservation receipts, and implementation evidence gates.
