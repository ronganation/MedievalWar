## 1. Executive Finding

The opening relationship among Pisa, the March of Tuscany, Lucca, Florence, and the surrounding Arno and hinterland communities cannot be reduced to a single sovereignty or rivalry state. General reference material describes the March as a collection of counties largely in the Arno valley, originally centered on Lucca, with Florence listed as the center after 1057. It also describes Canossan control beginning in 1027. These statements provide a research frame, not sufficient proof of the exact January 1, 1062 authority, access, taxation, military obligation, urban autonomy, or relationships of Pisa with Lucca, Florence, and individual communities.
