## 1. Executive Design Verdict

Pisa requires an opening political decision layer capable of authorizing defense, appointing commanders, receiving reports, managing bounded public resources, and representing urban interests. The evidence currently does not justify a named consul, duce, council of elders, formal commune, or later republican constitution in January 1062. The correct opening solution is a historically bounded urban collective authority represented through functions, constituencies, procedures, and delegated mandates without inventing named members or later institutions.
