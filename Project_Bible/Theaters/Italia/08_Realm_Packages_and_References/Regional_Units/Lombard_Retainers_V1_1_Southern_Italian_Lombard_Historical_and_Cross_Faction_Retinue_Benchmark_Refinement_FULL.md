Lombard Retainers are regional medium infantry sustained by exact princely, lordly, district, household, garrison, or institutional sources in southern Italy. They hold a stable shield-and-spear frontage, anchor less reliable troops, defend important terrain, and move into controlled assault or counterattack when local command and formation remain intact.

Some expressions may carry a limited pre-contact throwing weapon, but that capability must be sourced separately. The formation does not automatically disable enemy brace, lower morale, or become an axe unit. Its strength comes from leaders, retinue cohesion, equipment, familiarity with its service system, and the ability to hold and press without becoming a premium shock formation.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Lombard Retainers
- **Class:** Southern Italian medium shield-and-spear retinue infantry
- **Core verb:** Anchor, Cast, Press
- **Source:** Exact Lombard prince, lord, district, household, garrison, institution, gastald-linked authority, captain, or existing retinue where historically supported
- **Battle promise:** Anchor a dependable frontage, use a limited pre-contact cast where qualified, and press or counterattack without abandoning formation
- **Best use:** Main battle line, fortified camps, hill positions, gates, streets, crossings, garrisons, mixed armies, and controlled assault support
- **Worst use:** Long missile exposure, heavy shock without support, deep pikes, open flanks, prolonged pursuit, dense mud, and service after political or command fragmentation
- **Strategic value:** Provides reliable regional infantry tied to real southern Italian political and military sources rather than a generic population levy
- **Strategic cost:** Draws from finite retainers, households, district defense, leaders, shields, spears, armor, pay or obligation, equipment maintenance, and political cooperation
- **Signature decision:** Hold as an anchor, spend the limited pre-contact missile capability, or press a weakening enemy without exposing the line

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Lombard Retainers are trained regional infantry attached to exact southern Italian rulers, lords, districts, garrisons, households, or institutions. Their identity is steadier and better organized than ordinary levy spearmen, but they remain less specialized and less expensive than premium foreign heavy infantry.

The formation uses shields and spears to hold a dependable line, supports attacks in constrained ground, and may counterattack when an enemy becomes disordered. Some historically validated companies may carry throwing axes or heavy javelins for a short pre-contact cast. That variant consumes real ammunition and does not automatically break braces or morale.
