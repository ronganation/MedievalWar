## 5. Sicily's maritime theaters and center-specific choices

**Strait and northeast.** The Strait of Messina is a crossing, observation, supply, landing, and communication interface. A Sicilian center might seek to watch a departure, protect a landing, interfere with a supply run, negotiate access, or recover a lost coastal position. Its ability depends on actual coastal sites, crews, vessels, warning routes, political permissions, and opposing operations. A narrow strait is not a free interception or a free Norman crossing.

**Palermo and northern coast.** Palermo is a major political and commercial port research priority. The opening holder, exact harbor service, defensive craft, vessels, command, merchant commitments, and Norman relationship remain held. Protecting commercial activity may compete with military conversion and port defense.

**Western Sicily.** Mazara and Trapani are priority interfaces for commercial shipping, potential Ifriqiyan contact, reception, alternative landings, and coastal defense. Their port functions and political positions must be admitted separately; a western port does not guarantee Zirid access or a ready fleet.

**East and southeast.** Syracuse and Catania are candidate ports in the Ibn al-Thumna sphere; Noto is a regional research priority. A local agreement with the Normans does not open all eastern harbors, commit all crews, or settle the political stance of Palermo or Ibn al-Hawwas.

**Southern coast and interior.** Agrigento is a maritime-hinterland research interface for the Ibn al-Hawwas sphere. Enna and Castronuovo are inland centers whose access to the sea requires political cooperation, routes, transport, ports, and delivery. Inland resistance is not automatically resupplied from a coastal faction-colored tile.

## 9. Port defense, warning, and counter-crossing

A defensible coast requires observers, reporting routes, identified approaches, actual response assets, command, safe staging, and a feasible window. Watch posts can warn without being able to intercept; a garrison can protect a port without being able to patrol offshore. The UI should distinguish **observed**, **suspected**, **contact lost**, and **confirmed** crossings.
