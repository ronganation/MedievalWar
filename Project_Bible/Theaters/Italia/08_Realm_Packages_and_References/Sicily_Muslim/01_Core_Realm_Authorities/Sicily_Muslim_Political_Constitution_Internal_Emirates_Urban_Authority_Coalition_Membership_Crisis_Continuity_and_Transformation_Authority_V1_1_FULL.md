The Sicilian Emirates are not represented as one centralized island government. The playable container coordinates a political field composed of city-centered and territorial authorities that may possess different rulers, institutions, military capacity, external relationships, strategic interests, and willingness to cooperate. The Realm container provides a coherent player and AI work surface without erasing those differences.
