## 2. Realm identity

The Sicilian Emirates represent the surviving Muslim-ruled political field of Sicily at the opening of 1062: urban governments, emir and qa'id spheres, households, military followings, juristic and religious institutions, merchants, ports, agricultural districts, fortified centers, local communities, and directed external relationships operating amid internal fragmentation and an invasion already underway.
