## 28. Player-facing strategic briefing

The player should receive a concise historical briefing, not a hidden spreadsheet. The recommended surface explains:

- What each center believes is happening
- Which interests and resources are at stake
- Which valid actions are available
- Why a coalition, truce, aid request, defense, withdrawal, or counterattack is favored
- What remains unknown
- Which center may refuse
- What the strongest blocker is
- What would cause the plan to change

The player may override a valid recommendation where the player possesses authority. The game must not silently manufacture consent, resources, or knowledge to obey that choice.
