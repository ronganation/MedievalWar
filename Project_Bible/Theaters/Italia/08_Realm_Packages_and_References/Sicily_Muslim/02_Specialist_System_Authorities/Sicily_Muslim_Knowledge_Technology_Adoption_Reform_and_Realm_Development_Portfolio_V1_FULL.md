The Sicily Muslim package was produced later than several earlier Realm packages and already contains unusually broad static authority coverage. That maturity does not make admitted military or vessel profiles appear automatically. It does, however, provide enough political, institutional, economic, geographic, maritime, military-family, siege, information, religious, campaign, and presentation evidence to define a Realm-development portfolio without inventing unit cards or opening assets.

The correct boundary is therefore not “no military profiles, no technology portfolio.” The correct boundary is: define the military, maritime, institutional, economic, and political development programs at capability-family level, preserve current candidate and provider relationships, and defer profile-specific lineages until the admission owners approve actual profiles.
