This authority controls the research, calibration, admission, progression, and production sequence for Sicilian military roster families. It does not replace the controlling Military authority. It sits beneath that authority and above any future individual research packet or master unit profile. Its purpose is to determine which military concepts deserve a separate battle card, which remain service assignments or attachments, which belong to external providers, which must merge, and which must remain held or rejected.
