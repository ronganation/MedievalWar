### Defining cycle

```text
Prepare and inspect the personnel, equipment, officers, frontage, reserve, relief routes, and fallback
-> establish maintained defensive files
-> hold while pressure remains manageable
-> rotate or relieve tired files
-> commit a local reserve to a threatened point
-> seal a limited penetration or countermove locally
-> fall back in order when the outer position fails
-> recover personnel, officers, equipment, records, and formation memory
```
