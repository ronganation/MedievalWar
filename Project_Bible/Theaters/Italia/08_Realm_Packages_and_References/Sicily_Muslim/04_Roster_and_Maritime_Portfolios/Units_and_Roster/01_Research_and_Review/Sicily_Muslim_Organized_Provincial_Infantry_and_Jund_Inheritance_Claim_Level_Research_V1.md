### Candidate battle cycle

```text
Prepare and inspect personnel, equipment, leaders, frontage, reserve, and fallback
-> form a stable defensive line
-> hold while ordinary pressure remains manageable
-> rotate or relieve tired files
-> commit a local reserve to a threatened point
-> seal a limited penetration or countermove locally
-> fall back in order if the outer position cannot be held
-> recover people, officers, equipment, records, and formation memory
```
