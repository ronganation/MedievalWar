## 6. Adopted object architecture

The urban-defense ecosystem should not be forced into one object. The benchmark separates the source capability from the duties and from any possible field formation.

```yaml
UrbanCivicService:
  object_type: ParentServiceCapability
CivicWatch:
  object_type: AssignmentProfile
WallAndGateDuty:
  object_type: AssignmentProfile
MarketWarehouseWaterAndArchiveSecurity:
  object_type: AssignmentProfile
HarborAndPortSecurity:
  object_type: AssignmentProfile
FireWatchIncidentResponseAndPublicSecurity:
  object_type: AssignmentProfile
EmergencyUrbanMobilization:
  object_type: RuntimeStateOrTemporaryAssignment
EmergencyFieldMilitia:
  object_type: ConditionalCardCandidate
SustainedUrbanFieldInfantry:
  object_type: ResearchGated
```

A single person may move among compatible duties only through explicit release, reassignment, equipment issue, command, and readiness checks. The same person cannot simultaneously guard a gate, police a market, operate a port-security watch, serve on a wall, and deploy in a field formation.
