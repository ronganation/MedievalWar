## A3. Player-facing description

Fursan al-Amir are selected heavy cavalry bound to an accountable ruler, household, center, client, contract, or other approved provider. They guard command and standards, hold as a scarce mounted reserve, countercharge cavalry under favorable alignment, strike exposed or already engaged flanks, and protect an orderly withdrawal.

Their strength comes from trusted riders, suitable warhorses, rider-horse familiarity, officers, formation practice, lances or mounted spears, shields, armor, tack, remounts, handlers, and a protected route back to reserve. Wealth, title, landholding, a palace, a stable, or the word ghilman creates none of those inputs.

The unit is not a Sicilian version of Norman knights and receives no automatic couched-lance doctrine, anti-cavalry bonus, breakthrough, armor penetration, morale immunity, or safe disengagement. The player succeeds by choosing the right target and preserving the formation after a bounded intervention.
