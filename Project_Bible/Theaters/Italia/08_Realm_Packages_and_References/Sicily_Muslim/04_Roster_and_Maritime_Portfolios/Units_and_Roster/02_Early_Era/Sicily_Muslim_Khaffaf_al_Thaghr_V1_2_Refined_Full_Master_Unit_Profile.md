## 3. Player-facing description

Khaffaf al-Thaghr are light bowmen assembled from Sicily's divided centers, frontier districts, regional communities, civic defenses, strongpoints, contracts, and coalition contributions where real bow skill and equipment exist. The formation provides the faction's broadest foundational bow presence, but broader access does not mean unlimited recruitment or uniform quality.

On the battlefield, Khaffaf al-Thaghr support spearmen, civic defenders, camps, routes, and prepared approaches with sustained ordinary bow fire. The player chooses the unit when flexible missile presence and numbers matter more than the stronger target judgment, fire control, equipment consistency, and cadre depth associated with Rammāt Siqilliyya.

The unit is not disposable. Losses remove real bow-capable people, local defenders, leaders, bows, arrows, and formation memory from finite providers. Poorly assembled companies may field mixed bows, limited protection, weak fire discipline, or shallow replacement depth.
