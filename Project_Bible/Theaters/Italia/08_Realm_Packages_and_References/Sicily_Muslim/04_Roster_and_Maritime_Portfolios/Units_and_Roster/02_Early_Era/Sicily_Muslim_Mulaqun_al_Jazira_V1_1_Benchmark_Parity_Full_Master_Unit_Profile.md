## 3. Player-facing description

Mulaqun al-Jazira are mobile hand slingers drawn from providers that can demonstrate real sling practice rather than from a generic coastal or rural population. They operate ahead of or beside formed troops, pressure exposed and dense targets with selected stone, clay, or specialized lead projectiles where actually available, and relocate before stronger enemies close.

Sling shot creates a different battlefield pressure from arrows and javelins, but the card does not receive automatic armor penetration, knockdown, shield breaking, or horse collapse. Projectile effect depends on ammunition, mass, release quality, range, angle, target movement, shields, helmets, armor, formation density, and battle calibration.

The player must preserve casting space, ammunition carriage, leaders, withdrawal routes, and supporting frontage. Once fixed in melee, the formation’s battlefield value collapses rapidly.
