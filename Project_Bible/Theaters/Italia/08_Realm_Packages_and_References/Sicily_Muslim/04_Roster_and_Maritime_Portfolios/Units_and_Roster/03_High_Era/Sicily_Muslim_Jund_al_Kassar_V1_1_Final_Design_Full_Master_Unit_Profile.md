## 3. Player-facing description

Jund al-Kassār are selected crossbow crews assigned to the most demanding guard and strongpoint duties. Their weapons, bolts, protection, officers, maintainers, and station discipline may mature beyond ordinary Qawwasun service, but every advantage remains material and conditional. The formation is strongest when a real position, frontage, reload depth, stores, relief, and withdrawal plan exist.

The card is not ordinary Qawwasun with inflated statistics. It concentrates scarce veteran crews, heavy-compatible systems, guard obligations, specialist ammunition possibilities, and recovery capacity. Losing one formation can damage Sicily's entire late crossbow institution.
