## 3. Player-facing description

Sicilian Jund Infantry are organized provincial field troops shaped by the recovery and reform of Sicily’s earlier Islamic military inheritance. They serve through recurring and accountable field arrangements supported by officers, subleaders, muster and inspection, maintained equipment stores, relief files, local reserves, supply, replacement, and return. Earlier Kalbid and wider Islamic Sicilian military inheritance informs what Sicily may recover, but this unit does not claim that one unchanged jund survived into 1062.

In battle, Sicilian Jund Infantry are designed to manage a defensive engagement over time. They establish a controlled frontage, relieve tired files, commit a reserve against a local threat, seal a limited penetration, protect missile troops, and fall back in order when the outer defense becomes untenable. They do not replace elite guards, shock infantry, skirmishers, urban garrison troops, or ordinary Sicilian Spearmen.

Campaign access is deliberately difficult. The formation requires more than eligible people and spears. Sicily must sustain an institution capable of identifying personnel, maintaining officers and records, issuing and recovering equipment, supporting repeat service, replacing losses, and protecting the source communities and providers that make the unit possible.
