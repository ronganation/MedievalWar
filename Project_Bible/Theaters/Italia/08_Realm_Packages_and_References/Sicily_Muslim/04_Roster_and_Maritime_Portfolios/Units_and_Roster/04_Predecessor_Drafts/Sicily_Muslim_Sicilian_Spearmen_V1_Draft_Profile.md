This draft creates a strong, playable defensive infantry parent for the Sicily Muslim package without inventing a standardized historical regiment. The unit is intentionally built as a transparent gameplay composite. Broad Muslim Sicilian infantry capability, center-based military organization, local and coalition contributions, and the need for ordinary defensive frontage provide the historical and factional foundation. The simulation underneath remains finite, provider-bound, politically fragmented, and population-informed.

# PART I. CURRENT HUMAN-READABLE PROFILE

## 1. Unit identity strip

- **Unit:** Sicilian Spearmen
- **Realm:** Sicilian Emirates working package
- **Class:** ordinary center-authorized spear-and-shield field infantry
- **Quality:** ordinary to basic-trained, provider-dependent, and non-elite
- **Source:** finite eligible people released through a valid center, urban, regional, household, coalition, or contracted contribution
- **Availability:** population-informed, source-bound, politically uneven, equipment-limited, and never automatically island-wide
- **Primary role:** defensive line, missile protection, approach control, and combined-arms stability
- **Best use:** hold controlled frontage, protect Sicilian Archers, secure narrow approaches, stabilize threatened positions, and cover organized withdrawal
- **Major weakness:** missile exposure, vulnerable flanks, formation dependence, uneven protection, fatigue, and limited offensive power
- **Development:** organization, equipment, command, and provider depth may improve within the parent family; later selected or maintained infantry requires a separate gate
- **Opening State:** unknown and not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-screen player card

### Battlefield

- Forms the dependable ordinary frontage of a Sicilian field force.
- Protects archers and lighter troops from direct pressure and careless mounted contact.
- Holds roads, crossings, gateways, camp approaches, and constrained ground when adequately supported.
- Rewards facing, cohesion, depth, leadership, manageable fatigue, and protected flanks.
- Loses value rapidly when outflanked, repeatedly turned, missile-suppressed, exhausted, or pulled into unsupported pursuit.

### Campaign

- Draws from the general eligible population only through real providers, obligations, agreements, or paid service.
- Does not require Sicily to possess a unique universal levy institution before the family can exist.
- Uses the project-wide population-informed finite recruitment model when no stronger faction-specific institution is proven.
- Recruitment can combine compatible contributors under one field command without erasing original ownership or protected duties.
- Heavy use weakens local defense, labor, household protection, routes, ports, markets, strongpoints, and future replacement.

### Access

- A population total, barracks, center label, fortress, culture, or research decision cannot create spearmen.
- A center may coordinate a formation only when eligible people, service authority, equipment, leaders, supply, and return arrangements exist.
- Urban, rural, household, coalition, contracted, and strongpoint contributors retain their own access and opportunity costs.
- Strongpoint and civic personnel remain assignments before becoming independent field pools.

### Development

- Better inspection, shields, helmets, limited mail, records, officers, training, and combined-arms practice may strengthen the same card.
- A better-equipped expression does not automatically become a new unit.
- A selected household, maintained guard, heavy spear, or pike family must prove an independent source and battlefield decision.
- The family does not automatically become elite, professional, urban militia, fortress garrison, or Norman-style heavy infantry.

## 3. Player-facing description

Sicilian Spearmen are ordinary field infantry assembled through the island's divided political centers and their associated communities, households, regions, and service relationships. Armed around a practical spear-and-shield role, these formations give a Sicilian army the stable frontage needed to protect archers, control an approach, anchor a camp, and resist careless mounted pressure.

The unit is not a single island-wide regiment and is not a free conversion of population into soldiers. Every formation requires eligible and available people, valid authority or consent, serviceable weapons and shields, recognized leaders, formation practice, food, water, routes, supply, and a plan for return and recovery. Raising field spearmen can weaken the same communities, city duties, strongpoints, households, and transport systems that sustain the realm.

In battle, Sicilian Spearmen work best as the steady center of a combined-arms force. The player should keep the formation faced toward the threat, protect the flanks, avoid prolonged missile exposure, and use archers, light troops, reserves, terrain, and mounted support to prevent encirclement. They can hold and stabilize a fight, but they should not defeat elite assault troops or heavy cavalry through a hidden faction bonus.

## 30. Battlefield rhythm

1. Reach and occupy a controlled frontage.
2. Dress files, establish depth, and check flank support.
3. Protect archers, lighter troops, baggage, or a threatened approach.
4. Face the principal threat and conserve cohesion.
5. Receive ordinary pressure without chasing minor opportunities.
6. Allow Sicilian Archers and lighter troops to disrupt the enemy.
7. Stabilize a threatened section or cover a controlled withdrawal.
8. Rotate, reform, or withdraw before complete cohesion collapse.
9. Pursue only when another formation can preserve the line.

- Exhaust the formation through prolonged contact and movement.
- Separate the spearmen from archers, reserves, and command.
- Attack during relief, passage, reform, or withdrawal.
- Deny stable ground or a clean retreat route.
- Use superior assault infantry after cohesion has fallen.
- Threaten the contributing center or provider so recall and local-defense pressure matter.
