## 2. Player-facing strategic promise

A Sicilian center may seek Zirid support, but aid is never a free faction ability. The player must create or preserve a directed relationship, define an objective, negotiate command and cost, secure shipping and reception, supply the arrival, manage coalition friction, and accept that a provider-owned force may be delayed, reduced, redirected, recalled, defeated, or withdrawn.

The Zirid player or AI retains independent interests. Intervention may protect central Mediterranean influence, assist a specific Sicilian partner, oppose Norman expansion, pursue compensation, or be refused because of home defense, transport, factional, financial, or political constraints. These are design directions for owner review, not activated motives or records.
