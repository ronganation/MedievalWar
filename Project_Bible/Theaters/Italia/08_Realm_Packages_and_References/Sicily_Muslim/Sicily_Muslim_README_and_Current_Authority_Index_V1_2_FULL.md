### 12. Package-use rules

- Preserve predecessor files outside the active work folder when provenance is useful.
- Prefer the newest explicit successor when one exists.
- Consult the current authority manifest and this README before relying on an older filename list alone.
- Keep the controlling Military and Maritime system authorities at V1 until their separate successor triggers are met.
- Treat the Military and Maritime V1.1 portfolios as subordinate research and admission contracts, not system-authority replacements.
- Treat the Knowledge, Technology, Adoption, Reform, and Realm Development Portfolio as a center-scoped static development portfolio, not an active technology tree or implementation payload.
- Treat the adopted military design brief and five family benchmarks as research and admission artifacts, not admitted profiles.
- Preserve bounded unknowns as qualified unknowns. Do not default them to zero, neutral, peaceful, empty, safe, or available.
- Do not fabricate named people, households, offices, relationships, control, administration, forces, vessels, providers, capacities, or external-owner state.
- Do not allocate canonical stable IDs from static authorities, research benchmarks, or synthetic fixtures.
- Do not treat successful benchmark completion or synthetic validation as historical admission, Opening Readiness, or implementation authorization.
- Do not create a Sicily Muslim Opening State unless a later authority explicitly passes the closure gates and authorizes a review-only artifact.
- Do not mutate Registry, payload, importer, runtime, save, battle map, or Unreal project state without separate explicit authorization.
