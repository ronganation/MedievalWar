## 5. Historical Source Reconciliation

<External>Treccani's Domenico Contarini biography</External> places the succession around spring or summer 1041, while noting that later tradition uses 1043. It states that no direct or indirect testimony describes the transfer procedure and presents a peaceful popular election as an interpretation of the surviving silence rather than a directly documented event. citeturn80view154

<External>Britannica's Domenico Contarini entry</External> gives a reign of 1043 to 1070 and describes Domenico as an active defender of the duchy's religious independence who began rebuilding St. Mark's Church. The entry is explicitly a quick summary and advises verification against Britannica's underlying coverage. citeturn80view157

<External>Domenico I Contarini</External> gives the reign as 1041 to 1071, identifies Marco Contarini as father, leaves the spouse unknown, and names Giovanni, Marino, and Enrico as sons. The same reference places Enrico's episcopate from 1074 to 1108. citeturn80view155
