## 2. Authority Purpose

This authority defines the bounded opening Character portfolio, the Contarini house and ducal household shell, the distinction between person and office, election and succession surfaces, relationship candidates, ecclesiastical and civic interfaces, character knowledge, lifecycle boundaries, AI use, player presentation, and the future profile sequence.

This authority does not create active person records, exact ages, unsupported spouses, traits, skills, armies, Property, offices, relationships, heirs, succession events, Registry IDs, Opening State bindings, payloads, runtime state, or Unreal assets.
