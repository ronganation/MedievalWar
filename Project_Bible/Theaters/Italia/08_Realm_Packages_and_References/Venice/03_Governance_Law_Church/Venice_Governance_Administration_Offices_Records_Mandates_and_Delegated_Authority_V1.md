## 4. Authority Scope

### 4.1 This authority owns

- Venice-specific administrative interpretation;
- governance definitions for public offices, mandates, delegations, records, seals, archives, reporting, audits, review, continuity, revocation, emergency authority, and handback;
- governance profiles for lagoon communities, strategic sites, government functions, and externally dependent operations;
- administration states and action-specific administrative reach summaries;
- governance-facing proposal, command, event, AI, player-presentation, persistence, migration, determinism, and handoff contracts;
- boundaries between public government, ducal household, leading families, communities, ecclesiastical institutions, private commerce, Military owners, and external actors.

- people, lives, ages, personalities, private thoughts, households, kinship, or dynasties;
- constitutional election outcomes, title ownership, succession outcomes, or historical fact;
- canon law, doctrine, church office validity, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, or religious personnel;
