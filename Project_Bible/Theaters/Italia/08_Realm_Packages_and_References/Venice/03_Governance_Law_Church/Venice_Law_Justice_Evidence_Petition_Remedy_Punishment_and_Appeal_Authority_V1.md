Venetian justice is a plural and practical legal order. Ducal coordination, lagoon custom, public procedure, household and property rights, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, merchant and maritime agreements, privileges, witnessed records, community participation, emergency measures, and external-owner law can overlap without collapsing into one modern code or one unlimited court.

The doge may hear, mediate, protect, confirm, commission, review, or command within valid authority. Ducal preference alone is not law. Commercial importance alone is not privilege. Military control alone is not jurisdiction. A judgment alone is not enforcement, restitution, or reconciliation.

## 3. Authority Scope

### 3.1 This authority owns

- Venice-specific law, custom, justice, petition, evidence, jurisdiction, case, remedy, punishment, appeal, enforcement, and restoration definitions.
- Legal-source classes and conflict review.
- Standing, representation, notice, summons, hearing, mediation, judgment, appeal, reopening, clemency, and closure semantics.

- Legal AI observations, proposals, abstention, and player explanation.

- People, households, kinship, life state, offices, officeholders, mandates, or seal custody.
