## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how person and household status, presence, residence, local attachment, civic belonging, mobility, migration, admission, temporary stay, integration, departure, return, removal, foreign-community relationships, external protection, obligations, participation, and remedies operate for Venice.

It closes the gap between the existing population authority, which owns people, households, communities, labor, mobility, and recruitment inputs, and the law, governance, economy, diplomacy, ecclesiastical, domestic-order, education, health, prisoner, court, information, and campaign authorities that consume specific status and relationship state.

It does not create a modern citizenship system. It does not create exact opening population groups, ethnic or religious percentages, foreign quarters, named communities, migration movements, admissions, expulsions, collective loyalties, recruitment rights, property holdings, privileges, treaties, or January 1062 status records.
