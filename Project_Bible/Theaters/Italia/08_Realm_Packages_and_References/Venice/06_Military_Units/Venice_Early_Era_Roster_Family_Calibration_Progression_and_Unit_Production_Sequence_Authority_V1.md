## 3. Italian City-State Comparison Rule

Italian city-states may share broad military problems, including urban and territorial defense, civic or community obligations, household and retainer service, regional access, hired capacity, equipment constraints, and limited professional cores. Shared structural problems do not justify identical rosters.

```yaml
SharedComparisonDimensions:
  - UrbanAndCivicDefense
  - HouseholdAndRetainerService
  - LocalAndRegionalRecruitment
  - EquipmentAndTreasuryConstraints
  - HiredAlliedAndExternalCapacity
  - FortificationAndSiegeNeeds
  - MountedCapabilityAccess
  - MaritimeOrRiverineInterfacesWhereRelevant
VeniceSpecificDeterminants:
  - LagoonChannelsInletsBarriersAndLandings
  - InternalWaterMovement
  - MainlandMaterialDependence
  - CommercialVesselAndMilitaryLiftConflict
  - SeparateMarinersPilotsCombatantsAndShipwrights
  - LimitedLandAndMountedDepth
  - GradoAquileiaCastelloAndAdriaticInterfaces
  - AccessInfluenceAndProtectionBeforeTerritorialControl
Rule: CompareStructuresThenDeriveRealmSpecificFamiliesFromPeoplePlaceInstitutionsAndAccess
```

Future work on Genoa, Pisa, Amalfi, or other city-states should compare the same dimensions while preserving each polity's geography, institutions, people sources, regional dependencies, military obligations, and historical trajectory.

## 4. Calibration Principles

```text
Roster Family != Required Unit Card

Functional Capability != Standalone Unit

Settlement Flavor != Separate Unit

Equipment Upgrade != New People

Training Improvement != New Population Pool

Regional Access != Permanent Recruitment

Hired Capacity != Realm-Owned Pool

Maritime Labor != Maritime Combatant

Maritime Combatant != Standing Marine Corps

Ducal Security Need != Permanent Guard Regiment

Later Progression != Replacement Of All Early Units

Production Priority != Opening Availability
```

The calibrated roster should be compact enough for readable battlefield identity and production quality, but broad enough to preserve local defense, maritime operations, missile support, prepared infantry, engineering, conditional mainland activity, and external capacity.

## 8. Progression Philosophy

Progression strengthens sources, training, equipment, organization, command, combined-arms use, availability, and recovery. It does not automatically replace all Early Era units with later variants.

```yaml
ProgressionMethods:
  - ImprovedTrainingAndFormation
  - BetterEquipmentAccessAndIssue
  - StrongerPublicOrInstitutionalServiceStructures
  - ImprovedCommandAndSubordinateLeadership
  - ExpandedButFiniteRecruitmentSources
  - BetterVesselCrewPilotAndTransportCoordination
  - ImprovedRepairStorageSupplyAndRecovery
  - BoundedRegionalOrExternalAccess
  - NewSpecialistRolesWhenHistoryAndWorldStateSupportThem
CoexistenceRule: EarlyFamiliesMayRemainUsefulAlongsideLaterPreparedOrSpecialistFamilies
ReplacementRule: NoAutomaticLinearUpgradeTree
ResearchAndDecisionRule: UnlockCapabilityButNeverCreatePeopleEquipmentHorsesVesselsOfficersOrPools
```
