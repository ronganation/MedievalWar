## 1. Purpose and Boundary

This authority reconciles Venetian roster-family direction against historical naming, recruitment source, service basis, equipment plausibility, visual evidence, campaign role, battlefield role, overlap risk, progression value, and promotion readiness.

It strengthens the existing Venetian military authority without replacing it. The military authority owns the overall military identity, doctrine, campaign operations, fleet, transport, siege, and battle interfaces. This authority owns the evidence and adjudication layer required before any roster family becomes a promoted unit definition or master unit profile.

```text
Broad Military Role != Final Unit
Roster Family != Unit Definition
Unit Definition != Active Unit
Equipment Envelope != Equipment Inventory
Visual Direction != Historical Proof
Historical Plausibility != Opening Availability
Readable Name != Attested Formation Title
Port Community != Marine Unit
Mariner != Combatant
Ducal Office != Guard Regiment
Later Venetian Navy != January 1062 Roster
```

This authority creates no people, pools, units, vessels, fleets, officers, equipment stock, tactical statistics, assets, Registry records, Opening State records, runtime state, or Unreal content.

## 39. Historical Research Work Packages

### Package A: terminology and service

Search Latin, early Venetian, imperial, Byzantine, ecclesiastical, legal, narrative, and specialist sources for military service, militia, retainer, mariner, pilot, escort, guard, contingent, commander, and fleet terminology. Deliver a term ledger with date, place, meaning, ambiguity, speaker, and safe player-facing use.

### Package B: arms, armor, shields, and textiles

Inventory securely dated northern Italian, Venetian-lagoon, Byzantine-Adriatic, and relevant regional objects, catalogues, iconography, archaeology, and texts. Separate existence from distribution and elite material from common issue.

### Package C: lagoon and maritime combat

Resolve boarding, landing, quay, barrier, convoy, escort, patrol, crew, combatant, pilot, vessel, command, and damage-control evidence without importing later naval institutions.

### Package D: urban, community, household, and public service

Resolve muster, local defense, civic participation, ducal or public security, household service, duration, equipment provision, command, compensation, and replacement.

### Package E: mainland, mounted, regional, hired, and allied access

Identify real providers, populations, agreements, routes, mounts, service bases, equipment, command relationships, and limits.

### Package F: engineering, siege, landing, and repair

Resolve personnel, tools, facilities, fieldworks, assault preparation, salvage, ship repair, landing construction, and whether these remain campaign capabilities or warrant distinct unit profiles.

### Package G: visual rights and provenance

Record source rights, provenance, date, subject, permitted use, derivative restrictions, attribution, and production suitability for every visual reference.
