## 0.1 Strong Ownership Verdict

The V1 Campaign Experience authority remains the owner of player-facing pressure, objective, decision, dilemma, crisis, progression, recovery, recommendation, and campaign-explanation definitions. The Historical Event Catalogue remains the source catalogue for historically grounded situation and interaction families. The two are adjacent but not interchangeable.

V1.1 preserves all V1 portfolios and adds a nonduplicating crosswalk. The Campaign Experience authority may package a catalogue family into a player-facing objective, decision, dilemma, event presentation, or crisis only after a future general framework creates an eligible runtime instance from actual owner state. The Catalogue does not own objective lifecycle, campaign hierarchy, reward semantics, or player progression. Campaign Experience does not rewrite historical event definitions or create specialist state.

## 6. Campaign Experience Thesis

The Venice campaign should teach the player to:

- distinguish lagoon protection from material self-sufficiency;
- understand what the doge can propose and what institutions, owners, communities, and external actors must accept or perform;
- inspect food, water, timber, fuel, metal, shipping, labor, repair, and mainland dependencies;
- separate ports, vessels, crews, pilots, combatants, military lift, and fleet readiness;
- treat Grado, Aquileia, Castello, the Papacy, Byzantium, imperial authorities, Istria, Dalmatia, and Adriatic cities as directed relationships rather than faction-color extensions;
- manage House Contarini without turning elective office into hereditary state ownership;

## 40. Player-Facing Opening Brief

### The Lagoon Holds, but Nothing Beyond It Is Free

Venice begins with a recognized doge, a defensible water-connected political core, important institutions, and long experience of maritime exchange. Venice does not begin with secure mainland lifelines, a finished navy, a mature public Arsenal, universal political agreement, permanent commercial privileges, or an Adriatic empire.

The first task is not to paint the map. The first task is to understand what keeps the lagoon alive. Food, water, fuel, timber, metal, rope, sailcloth, vessels, crews, pilots, records, credit, repair, public recognition, ecclesiastical settlement, and external access all belong to different people and systems. Every ambitious action competes with maintenance, households, trade, defense, and recovery.

The player should decide what Venice will become by proving capacity and building relationships, not by receiving the later Republic for free.

## 41. Player Presentation and Accessibility

The player must see why a pressure, objective, event, dilemma, or crisis is visible; what is known, unknown, stale, contradicted, or externally locked; which owners and audiences are affected; what contributes, blocks, reverses, or expires; meaningful alternatives including delay, withdrawal, and abstention; costs, risks, opportunity costs, reversibility, and recovery; partial success, failure, replacement, and nonoccurrence; and accessible text that does not rely on color alone.

The UI must not reveal hidden foreign intent, private human thoughts, guaranteed future history, or unsupported precision.
