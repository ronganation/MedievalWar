## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the lived public, diplomatic, household-adjacent, institutional, and material practice of the early Venetian dogeship through access, audiences, petition presentation, precedence, protocol, ceremony, hospitality, envoy reception, gift exchange, patronage, commemoration, prestige, public display, court expenditure, and recovery.

It creates a dedicated court-practice owner between Character, Household, Political Constitution, Governance, Law, Diplomacy, Ecclesiastical, Economy, Education, Historical Memory, Presentation, Domestic Order, Health, Environment, and Campaign Event authorities.

It does not create January 1062 court ranks, ceremonial offices, audience lists, ceremonies, regalia, gifts, wardrobe inventories, named courtiers, musicians, heralds, artists, envoys, household members, prestige values, or palace layouts.
