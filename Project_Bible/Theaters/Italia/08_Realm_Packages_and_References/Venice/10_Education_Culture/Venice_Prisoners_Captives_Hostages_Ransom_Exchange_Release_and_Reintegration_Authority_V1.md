## 1. Purpose

This authority defines the Venetian custody chain from capture, surrender, rescue, political detention, hostage arrangements, maritime seizure, shipwreck recovery, movement, guarded residence, holding conditions, records, health, property custody, ransom, exchange, release, return, and reintegration.

It creates no prisoners, captives, hostages, detainees, guards, custodians, holding sites, ransoms, exchanges, safe conducts, releases, escapes, deaths, missing persons, or Opening State records.

## 22. Food, Water, Shelter, Clothing, and Sanitation

## 46. Household and Population Consequences

## 47. Office, Military, and Commercial Status

Governance, military, and economy owners decide suspension, substitution, delegation, return, investigation, command eligibility, demobilization, employment, contract continuity, or discharge.

Capture does not automatically erase office or service. Release does not automatically restore office, command, employment, vessel control, credit, or commercial trust.

## 48. Property and Fiscal Consequences

Property owners control seized, inventoried, abandoned, damaged, entrusted, inherited, returned, salvaged, and disputed property. Release does not restore possession automatically.

Fiscal owners control guard, food, water, transport, care, ransom, exchange, compensation, debt, public-private allocation, and audit. Prisoners create no automatic income.
