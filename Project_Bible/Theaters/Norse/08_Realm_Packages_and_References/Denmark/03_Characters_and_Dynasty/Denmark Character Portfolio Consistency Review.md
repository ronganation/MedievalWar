## Coverage Interpretation

The portfolio covers all constitutional character functions needed to begin Dynasty Authority. It does not yet cover every operational and regional role needed for a complete Opening State.

This is acceptable because:

- character content must follow evidence;
- armies, fleets, levies, assemblies, dioceses, and regional power are not character-owned systems;
- an unknown holder or unassigned command is safer than an invented person;
- later evidence can add bounded support profiles without changing the portfolio architecture.

### Royal Partner/Consort

# 5. Opening Participation Review

## Participation Vocabulary

### Active Participant

May evaluate and propose age- and authority-valid commands in the opening turn.

### Restricted Active Participant

Active character with limited command scope due to office, location, law, evidence, or unresolved readiness.

### Underage Participant

Active person with child-safe behavior, no adult agency, and typed guardian/household/protection/formation dependencies.

### Historical Legacy Only

Historical identity may be referenced by genealogy, memory, claim, or institution but does not instantiate as a living actor.

### Lifecycle Review

Identity is canonical, but Opening State cannot instantiate until alive/dead status or effective range resolves.

### External Reference Only

Character is owned elsewhere and appears through a cross-realm lock.

### Review/Quarantine

No runtime activation.
