## 1. Concise Verdict

Estrid Svendsdatter is indispensable to Denmark's dynastic design as the maternal royal-line bridge through whom House Estridsen takes its matronymic identity and continuity. Estrid's parentage, historical marriage to Ulf Jarl, motherhood of Sweyn, possible household authority, property, patronage, church, and Memory significance deserve full treatment. Estrid's January 1062 lifecycle remains unresolved and no convenient default is authorized.

## 2. Core Character Thesis

**A maternal royal-line founder whose historical identity explains House Estridsen's name and legitimacy, but whose possible living role on 1 January 1062 remains lifecycle-gated.**

If living, Estrid may become a bounded dynastic elder, independent household principal, patron, mediator, property participant, and memory carrier. If deceased, Estrid remains a historical, Dynasty, Memory, property-provenance, and institutional legacy. Neither path gives Estrid ownership of the Crown, Dynasty, Sweyn, succession, Church, property, or household state.
