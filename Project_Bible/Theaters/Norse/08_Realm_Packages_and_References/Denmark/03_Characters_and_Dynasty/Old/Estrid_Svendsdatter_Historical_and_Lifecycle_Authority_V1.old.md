## 1. Concise Verdict

Estrid Svendsdatter is indispensable to Denmark's historical and dynastic design as the maternal royal-line bridge through whom Sweyn Estridsen's house takes its identity and continuity. Estrid's parentage, historical marriage to Ulf Jarl, motherhood of Sweyn, connection to the earlier Danish royal line, and possible property, patronage, household, and ecclesiastical legacy deserve full authority treatment.

Estrid's January 1062 lifecycle is not sufficiently settled by the controlling Denmark package or the reviewed external material. The attached family-tree image labels Estrid as deceased in 1057, while a retrieved tertiary biography gives death after 1057 and possibly around 1073. That conflict crosses the campaign opening and must remain unresolved. Estrid cannot be activated as a living opening character or forced into deceased-only status by this file.
