## 1. Concise Verdict

Denmark has enough evidence to prepare a bounded opening Character Network, but not enough evidence to assign detailed numerical relationships across the royal house, church, Norway, and the southern Baltic sphere.

The correct V1 approach is:

- implement kinship and office/institution bases as typed references;
- authorize a small set of directed relationship candidates;
- preserve uncertainty in trust, loyalty, rivalry, knowledge, and affection;
- require separate source-to-target and target-to-source decisions;
- treat future conflicts and marriages as possible emergence rather than opening truth;
- keep external people under their owning registries;
- defer unsupported regional, episcopal, military, and household edges.

- character personality;

### 2.3 Evidence rule

A kinship, marriage, office, or war connection authorizes a relationship basis, not a complete relationship value.

Examples:

- parentage does not prove loyalty;
- siblinghood does not prove rivalry;
- marriage does not prove alliance or affection;
- patronage does not prove obedience;
- war does not prove personal hatred;
- metropolitan authority does not prove personal trust;
- shared dynasty does not create shared knowledge.

## 3. Relationship Evidence Classes

- **Direct contemporary evidence:** explicit communication, action, grant, service, conflict, or documented association near the opening.
- **Institutional basis:** offices or institutions require interaction but do not determine personal sentiment.
- **Kinship basis:** genealogical relation only.
- **Marriage/household basis:** legal or household relation, subject to effective date.
- **Conflict basis:** opposing claims, war, raid, imprisonment, or command interaction.
- **Later narrative:** later account that may preserve or reshape a relationship tradition.
- **Inferred strategic interface:** actors must evaluate one another because of roles, but private attitude is unknown.
- **Unsupported:** no safe opening edge beyond optional unknown-reference metadata.

### Confidence

- high
- moderate
- low
- disputed
- unknown

### Opening dispositions

- **APPROVE-BASIS:** relationship basis may load, with bounded dimensions.
- **DECISION-CANDIDATE:** evidence review supports a later decision pass.
- **CONDITIONAL:** blocked by chronology, identity, activation, or external lock.
- **METADATA-ONLY:** retain fact of connection without active relationship processing.
- **DEFER:** insufficient strategic need or evidence.
- **QUARANTINE:** identity or interpretation conflict.
- **REJECT:** anachronistic, duplicate, symmetric shortcut, or later outcome.

# 22. Save, Migration, and Determinism Review

Persist canonical direction IDs, source and target IDs, evidence references, confidence, visibility, access state, effective dates, owner, and versions. Do not serialize display names, aliases, unsupported dimensions, or review placeholders as active people.
