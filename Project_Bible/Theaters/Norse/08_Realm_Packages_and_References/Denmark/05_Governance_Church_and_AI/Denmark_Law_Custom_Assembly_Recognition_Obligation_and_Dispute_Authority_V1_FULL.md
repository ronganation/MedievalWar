## 3. Purpose and Exact Scope

This authority defines Denmark-specific law, custom, policy, jurisdiction, assembly competence, recognition, obligation, exemption, dispute, remedy, promulgation, enforcement-routing, reform, suspension, repeal, and legal-memory contracts for the January 1062 campaign context. It allows Denmark to be governed through recognizable rules and procedures without inventing one codified national law, one permanently sovereign assembly, or one all-powerful Crown.

It owns definitions and owner-routing. It does not create a law in Opening State, convene an assembly, appoint an officeholder, transfer property, collect a contribution, muster a force, recognize a successor, enforce a judgment, or mutate runtime state.
