Denmark is admitted as a full major-faction military roster whose player identity combines broad distributed spear and missile capacity, selected aggressive sword and axe infantry, a scarce hird and retainer core, limited opening mounted depth, strong crossing and maritime reach, and substantial later development into more protected infantry, crossbows, mounted retinues, knights, fiscal service, and professional or contracted forces.

## 2. Playable Denmark Proposition

The Denmark player assembles military strength from regional and leidang-related contribution, royal and magnate households, qualified missile personnel, scarce mounted retainers, towns, ports, church and property institutions, and Theater mercenaries. Denmark can concentrate a capable army across a strategically connected but politically negotiated kingdom. The price is friction: obligation is not delivery, delivery is not readiness, ships compete for crews and fighters, household commitments are finite, and every expedition exposes some portion of the Realm.

The battlefield fantasy is a broad spear-and-bow foundation supported by hard-hitting selected infantry and a small dependable household core. The strategic fantasy is turning Denmark's straits, islands, routes, ports, institutions, and political relationships into faster and more reliable military concentration without pretending that centralization creates people or equipment.
