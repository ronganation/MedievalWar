## 2. Decisive Unit Verdict

**Danish Vaebnere are admitted as a conditional High Era sword-and-shield household infantry card only where Denmark develops a recurring, accountable service layer of armed attendants, retainers, and compatible household fighters capable of sustained foot service beyond the narrow Royal Hird.**

Their battlefield identity is dependable protected melee, line relief, confined-ground fighting, controlled pressure, and important-person or important-position service over a longer commitment than the Hird should normally accept.

They are narrower and more expensive than Landsmaend, more defensively balanced than Herremaend Axemen, less politically restricted than Danish Hirdmen, less decisive than Riddere or true foot-knightly concentrations, and finite through actual providers, people, equipment, officers, and support.

## 4. One-Screen Player Contract

### Battlefield

- Accompany an army, commander, magnate, court, or important train when actual service supports the assignment.
- Hold a compact sector longer and more coherently than broad regional infantry.
- Relieve damaged spears, axemen, or household troops through a real passage and handoff.
- Press an enemy already fixed, tired, compressed, or disordered.
- Stop before controlled pressure becomes unsupported shock assault or pursuit.

### Campaign

- Develop the card through people, recurring service, officers, equipment, maintenance, and institutions.
- Choose between fielding Vaebnere and retaining the same people for household, castle, escort, court, or mounted-support duties.
- Keep provider ownership, service terms, pay or maintenance, and return obligations visible.
- Recover headcount, officers, equipment, cohesion, and provider confidence separately.

A mailed retainer is not automatically a Vaebner, and a Vaebner is not automatically a knight, Hirdman, or Riddere rider.

## 5. Player-Facing Card

**Short description:** Retained Danish sword-and-shield infantry who sustain close combat, relieve the line, and defend important ground under accountable household service.

Danish Vaebnere represent an achieved High Era layer of armed attendants and retainers whose service, equipment, leadership, and repeated practice permit coherent foot action. They may accompany royal, magnate, castle, court, or knightly households, but the card does not assert one uniform legal rank or standardized national corps.

On the battlefield, Vaebnere carry shields and practical one-handed weapons, with swords providing the clearest roster identity. They are suited to sustained close fighting, reinforcement, relief, command-adjacent defense, and controlled retention of important ground. They are not the sharper axe pressure of Herremaend Axemen, the broad regional flexibility of Landsmaend, the closest royal reserve of Danish Hirdmen, or mounted Riddere ordered to appear twice.

**Flavor line:** **Armed for service, kept for the fight that must continue.**

## 24. Core Battle Rhythm: Accompany, Hold, Relieve, Press

**Accompany:** Remain near the army, provider, commander, train, or important position while preserving a deployment lane.

**Hold:** Take a compact threatened sector and sustain protected close combat.

**Relieve:** Replace tired or damaged infantry through an actual handoff, not by passing freely through engaged bodies.

**Press:** Apply controlled pressure once the target is fixed, tired, compressed, or disordered. Halt before losing support, frontage, or withdrawal.

## 25. Approach and Contact

The preferred approach uses friendly spear frontage, missile suppression, terrain cover, a short route, shielded files, and identified entry and exit lanes. The unit should avoid long exposed advances under bow or crossbow fire. Contact is strongest when files remain compact and the target cannot exploit superior reach or flank space.

## 26. Sustained Melee Contract

Vaebnere remain useful after initial contact through maintained shields, compatible one-handed weapons, leader depth, file cohesion, relief practice, and more support than temporary contributors. Sustained does not mean indefinite. Fatigue, casualties, shield damage, officer loss, missiles, and encirclement still degrade the company.

## 27. Relief and Passage Geometry

Relief requires a receiving formation able to shorten or stabilize frontage, a real lane, aligned facing, signals and leaders, rear space, manageable enemy pressure, and a rally point. Failed relief can create congestion, mixed files, exposed flanks, friendly missile masking, panic, and blocked withdrawal.


### Relief Outcomes and Failure Costs

Relief is the card's signature behavior and must be physically readable.

- **Clean relief:** the outgoing unit yields space, Vaebnere enter with usable facing, and both formations retain control.
- **Pressured relief:** mixed files and temporary gaps require recovery before either formation can act freely.
- **Failed relief:** congestion, enemy pressure, masked missiles, or blocked lanes pin both formations.
- **Aborted relief:** Vaebnere remain in reserve because the passage is no longer viable.
- **Emergency extraction:** Vaebnere accept high loss risk to recover command, a standard, wounded personnel, or a strategically essential formation.

The unit should not receive an abstract stamina-restoration aura. Its value comes from replacing exhausted frontage with a maintained company and creating the space in which the outgoing formation can actually withdraw.

## 28. Controlled Pressure and Assault Ceiling

Vaebnere may push ordinary or disordered infantry, fight through a gate or street, and secure captured ground. They should not lead every breach assault, charge prepared spears frontally, attack heavy axes unsupported, pursue cavalry, continue beyond friendly frontage, or substitute for Hird or Riddere in the decisive elite role.

The unit wins through continuing pressure and cohesion, not explosive shock.

### Cavalry, Reach, and Heavy-Weapon Boundaries

Vaebnere may finish cavalry already stopped and entangled by friendly spears or obstacles, but they do not receive charge reception or hidden anti-cavalry power. Fresh cavalry striking an exposed side remains a severe threat.

Deep spear or pike frontage can keep swords outside useful reach. Heavy two-handed axe or polearm specialists can punish prolonged frontal contact when they retain weapon space. Vaebnere answer through shields, support, relief, terrain, and timing, not universal sword superiority.

## 30. Spear and Cavalry Relationship

Vaebnere do not replace spear infantry. Against cavalry they depend upon friendly spears, obstacles, prepared ground, early warning, and protected flanks. Once impact has been stopped or horses are trapped in congestion, Vaebnere may reinforce the fight. They receive no hidden anti-cavalry tag.

## 31. Missile Interaction

Shields provide frontal mitigation, but crossbows remain dangerous, side and rear fire are serious, dense order creates a better target, loose order weakens melee capability, and long exposure wastes expensive personnel. Shield and armor damage persist into recovery. The answer is cover, suppression, timing, terrain, and shorter exposure, not immunity.

## 33. Terrain and Ground

Firm ground permits formation but exposes the card to cavalry and missiles. Farmland and lanes provide bounded frontage while fences and ditches obstruct relief. Hills improve some defense but tire armored assault. Sparse woods offer cover while disrupting files. Dense woods reduce visibility and order. Mud and marsh punish armor, withdrawal, and casualty evacuation. Crossings favor receiving-bank reserve work, not the crossing itself. Urban ground favors compact fighting if routes remain open. Walls and gates favor relief and inner defense, not disposable escalade.

No flat Danish, coastal, urban, or household bonus is authorized.

## 34. Siege and Settlement Roles

**Defense:** Gate reserve, breach containment, wall-access relief, court and yard defense, street fighting, and withdrawal-route preservation.

**Offense:** Assault support after suppression, secured breach exploitation, captured-gate defense, camp and engineer protection, and reserve behind specialists.

**Poor use:** Unsupported ladder assault, repeated frontal breach attack, expendable policing, or engineering labor merely because the unit is present.

## 35. Maritime and Landing Boundary

Vaebnere are fighters, not ships or crews. Embarkation requires vessels, crews, lift, stores, and a return plan. Landing proceeds through embarked, disembarking, shore assembly, equipment inspection, formation recovery, and foot readiness. The card receives no instant amphibious readiness or attack bonus.

## 37. Morale, Cohesion, and Command

Vaebnere should be steadier than militia and temporary musters but less personally cohesive than the closest Royal Hird. Positive inputs include surviving leaders, clear provider and command relationships, maintained support, intact standards, protected flanks, successful relief, and familiar files. Negative inputs include officer loss, isolation, arrears, provider dispute, encirclement, failed relief, heavy casualties, loss of escape route, and collapsed spear support.

No global morale aura or automatic last stand is created.

## 38. Campaign Endurance and Supply

The unit requires food, water, shelter, footwear, weapon and shield repairs, armor maintenance, care, transport, pay or valid maintenance, and viable routes. Vaebnere may campaign longer than temporary community service where institutions support them. Arrears, route failure, damaged equipment, and provider withdrawal still erode readiness.

## 39. Replacement and Replenishment

Replacement requires eligible people, valid current service, compatible equipment, officers, integration time, provider support, and supply. Raw headcount does not restore a veteran company. New soldiers dilute formation memory until practice rebuilds collective timing.


### Replacement Quality and Provider Continuity

Equal headcount loss can have unequal consequences. Captains, file leaders, trusted household veterans, experienced shield bearers, and personnel familiar with relief work are disproportionately important. A rebuilt company may return at nominal strength while remaining weak in command depth, equipment compatibility, provider confidence, or formation memory.

Provider withdrawal can close future recruitment without deleting survivors already in service. Restoration of the provider does not instantly restore people, trust, equipment, records, or leaders.

## 49. AI Recruitment Contract

## 50. AI Battlefield Contract

The AI should keep Vaebnere near the main line or important position, use protected approaches, relieve tired infantry, reinforce compact sectors, press fixed targets, avoid prepared cavalry and deep spears, stop pursuit early, preserve reform space, and avoid using the company as first-wave breach fodder. Difficulty improves decisions, not hidden statistics.

## 52. Visual Identity
