## 3. Player-Facing Description

Herremaend Axemen are qualified nonroyal Danish military servants and compatible household or regional fighters maintained for recurring aggressive close combat. Their proposed field expression combines military axes with shields, useful protection, and leaders able to turn a local opportunity into controlled pressure. They are sustained by an achieved service structure, not made elite by a name.

Let the ordinary line establish the battle, then bring the axemen into a useful sector. They can counterattack an overextended enemy, fight beside the spear frontage, or exploit a flank shaped by missile pressure. Once they reach suitable contact, they should be capable of continuing the necessary local melee rather than being useful only for a single charge animation.

Their shields help the approach and close fight, but do not make an exposed advance safe. Fresh spears, cavalry, missiles, fatigue, and superior infantry can defeat them. A successful company secures ground, halts before overextension, and returns with enough leaders and practiced fighters to answer the next summons.

The intended High Era protection is retained medium-to-heavy foot: practical clothing, stronger textile protection where supplied, more consistent helmet access, and mail among supportable contributors without universal full mail. ## 22. Signature Battle Cycle: Press, Hew, Exploit

1. Deploy behind or beside the spear frontage with a viable approach and rally point.
2. Preserve shields, stamina, and leaders during the opening missile exchange.
3. Identify a useful ordinary-infantry target, engaged flank, overextended attack, or threatened local sector.
4. Check cavalry, missile angles, fresh reserves, and the route back.
5. Approach in coherent files; coordinate friendly firing lanes and passage.
6. **Press:** establish purposeful local contact rather than a scattered rush.
7. **Hew:** apply axe-and-shield close pressure while preserving weapon space and mutual support.
8. **Exploit:** recover ground, widen a local opening, or finish the bounded objective while conditions remain favorable.
9. Halt when the target recovers, reserves arrive, fatigue rises, or flanks and withdrawal become unsafe.
10. Handoff, break contact where feasible, and reform for another considered decision.

The mnemonic is a proposed gameplay rhythm, not a reconstructed Danish drill. Exploit means make a local success useful, not pursue indefinitely. The company can remain in necessary supported melee; it is not limited to one impact animation.

## 24. Shield Pressure and Sustained Local Melee

## 25. Counterattack, Relief, and Passage Geometry

### 25A. Frontage, Relief, and Understrength Geometry
