## 1. Concise Verdict

Denmark's economy in January 1062 should be represented as a distributed network of properties, rights, obligations, production, storage, markets, ports, routes, households, churches, contributors, and negotiated access rather than as one universal wealth score.

The Crown may hold, request, bargain for, receive, store, assign, and spend resources only through valid owners and typed records. A property is not revenue. A right is not possession. An obligation is not delivery. A market is not Crown income. A port is not supply. A levy summons is not food, ships, crews, equipment, or transport.

Opening values that are not supported by authoritative evidence remain unknown. This authority does not invent treasury totals, estate inventories, tax rates, harvest quantities, ship counts, or army provisioning figures.

## 4. Economic Doctrine

### 4.1 Economy is distributed

Denmark's economic capacity emerges from current properties, use rights, obligations, production, storage, transport, access, contributors, institutions, routes, and political compliance.

### 4.2 Ownership and benefit are separate

A person or institution may own land, possess a use right, receive a revenue right, collect an obligation, manage a store, or benefit from patronage. These are separate authorities and may belong to different owners.

### 4.3 Assessment is not receipt

An assessed obligation may be disputed, delayed, partially delivered, commuted, lost in transit, redirected, or waived. Expected resources cannot be spent before validated receipt or credit authorization.

### 4.4 Money and material remain separate

Coin, bullion, food, fodder, timber, iron, cloth, animals, vessels, equipment, labor, transport, housing, and service are distinct resource families. Conversion requires an authorized market, contract, exchange, or owner-approved action.

### 4.5 Supply is a chain

Military supply requires a source, legal or negotiated access, stock, allocation, handling, transport, route, delivery, custody, consumption, loss, and recovery record. A nearby property does not automatically supply a force.

### 4.6 Unknown is not zero

Unknown treasury, yield, obligation, storage, route capacity, delivery, or consumption remains unknown. Unknown values cannot be used as zero, average, sufficient, or available.

### 4.7 Strategic consequence over routine clicking

The player should decide priorities, requests, assignments, safeguards, and responses to shortages. Routine collection, local accounting, and repeated transport execution should be automated by valid owners and policies, with exceptions surfaced when strategic consequences arise.

### 8.1 Contribution Lifecycle

Contribution processing keeps each stage separate:

2. assessment or request;
3. contributor response;

5. assembly or authority confirmation where required;
6. collection or service assembly;
7. storage or direct issue;
8. transport;
9. receipt;

- Underage beneficiaries require separate owner, custodian, administrator, permitted-use, reporting, and majority-transition records.

## 32. Validation Catalogue

### Fatal

- property, holder, right, obligation, account, stock, route, force, fleet, and supply are merged;
- an unknown value becomes zero, average, available, or sufficient;
- a request or promise becomes a delivered contribution;
- a market or port automatically creates Crown revenue;
- a royal command creates property, money, stock, transport, ships, crews, supply, or compliance;
- an unresolved or quarantined ID enters runtime content or a save;
- Opening State overwrites an advanced save;
- external property or economic state is duplicated as Denmark-owned;
- later historical outcomes are inserted into Opening State;
- AI directly mutates owner state.

### Review

- property holder, basis, or chronology is incomplete;
- market, mint, port, storage, or production function lacks owner validation;
- account exists but its opening balance is unknown;
- obligation exists but amount, form, trigger, or debtor is uncertain;
- supply source exists but transport or route access is unresolved;
- church property or immunity lacks final ecclesiastical evidence;
- Børglum/Vestervig chronology, jurisdiction, or officeholder evidence affects a proposed property record.

### Warning

- a strategic summary obscures owner or uncertainty;
