## 2. Concise Authority Verdict

This authority establishes Denmark's missing owner for diplomacy and external relations while preserving all external actors, realms, institutions, routes, forces, property, agreements, and private state under their proper owners.

Denmark may contact, represent, propose, negotiate, recognize, request access, exchange guarantees, seek safe conduct, mediate, conclude agreements, perform or breach obligations, wage or settle war, and pursue political settlement only through valid authority, communication, knowledge, counterpart participation, and owner receipts. Diplomatic language does not manufacture consent, access, delivery, alliance, tribute, peace, specialists, ships, military support, or foreign state.
