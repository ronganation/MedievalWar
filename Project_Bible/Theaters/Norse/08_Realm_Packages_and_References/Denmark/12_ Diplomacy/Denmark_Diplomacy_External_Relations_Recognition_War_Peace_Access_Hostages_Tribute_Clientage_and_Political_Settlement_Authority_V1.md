## 1. Concise Verdict

Denmark in January 1062 is a negotiated Christian maritime kingship whose external power depends on recognized rulership, regional and assembly support, household continuity, church cooperation, ports, crossings, routes, ships, crews, finance, Supply, military contribution, communication, and credible enforcement. The Realm cannot be reduced to one diplomatic score, one permanent foreign-policy voice, or one automatic Crown command.

The principal verified pressure is the unresolved Denmark-Norway sovereignty conflict. The Battle of Nisa remains future-facing and conditional, and the later Denmark-Norway settlement is not an opening result. Southern Baltic, Hamburg-Bremen, Sweden-facing, North Sea, England-facing, imperial, and island interfaces require explicit owner validation rather than inferred relations.

```text
Known Situation + Authorized Principal + Valid Mandate + Known Counterparty + Viable Terms + Owner Capacity + External Receipt + Ratification + Performance = Bounded Diplomatic Effect
```
