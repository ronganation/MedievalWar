## 2. Concise Authority Verdict

This authority establishes Denmark's missing integrated owner for population constituencies, household and labor capacity, finite recruitment and crew pools, training and institutional continuity, lawful or customary military obligation, contribution reservation, replacement, demobilization, and recovery.

The authority does not turn Denmark into one manpower pool. It does not create exact population totals, troops, crews, vessels, equipment, trainers, officers, units, fleets, or Opening State records. Denmark's military capacity remains assembled from separately owned people, obligations, consent, equipment, training, command, settlements, routes, supply, finance, vessels, and recovery capacity.
