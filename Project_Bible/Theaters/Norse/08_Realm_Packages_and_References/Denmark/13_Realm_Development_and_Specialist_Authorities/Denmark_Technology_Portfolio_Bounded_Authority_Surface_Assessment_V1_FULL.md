### 6. Required Authority 1: Law, Custom, and Assembly
```yaml
RequiredAuthority:
  candidate_filename: Denmark_Law_Custom_Assembly_Recognition_Obligation_and_Dispute_Authority_V1_FULL.md
  necessity: Proven
  reason:
    - DenmarkRealmWorkPlanExplicitlyListsLawCustomAssemblyAsPlannedAndGated
    - GovernanceRequiresLawAndPolicyAsUpstreamOwner
    - GovernanceDefersLawCustomPolicyRecordsAndEffectRouting
    - GovernanceRequiresValidationOfLawAndCustomForAppointmentsRecognitionSuccessionContributionsAndAssemblies
    - EconomyRequiresValidLegalOrCustomaryBasesForRightsObligationsAssessmentsContributionsAndDisputes
    - MilitaryRequiresAuthorityBasesForMusterFleetContributionCommandAndDemobilization
  must_own:
    - DenmarkSpecificLawAndCustomDefinitions
    - AssemblyCompetenceParticipationWitnessingQuorumAcceptanceAndDecisionEffectRules
    - KingshipRecognitionAndSuccessionRecognitionInterfaces
    - AppointmentDelegationContributionMusterFleetAndFiscalObligationBases
    - DisputeAppealRemedyWaiverCommutationAndEnforcementRouting
    - RegionalVariationAndUnknownOrContestedCustom
  must_not_own:
    - AssemblySessionRuntimeState
    - OfficeOrMandateExecution
    - PropertyMoneyStockForceFleetOrCompliance
    - ChurchCanonLawOutsideEcclesiasticalOwner
    - OpeningOutcomes
  authoring_method: NewBoundedAuthorityBuiltFromCurrentDenmarkGovernanceEconomyMilitaryHistoricalAndSharedLawOwners
```

This file is not symmetry paperwork. The existing Governance authority defines assembly identity, sessions, decisions, offices, and mandates but explicitly requires Law and Policy upstream and leaves the governing law and custom for appointments and recognition to validation. The Economy and Military authorities likewise require valid bases for rights, obligations, contributions, and musters. Technology programs cannot responsibly promise reform of assemblies, obligations, office, or regional integration while that legal basis remains ownerless at Realm level.
