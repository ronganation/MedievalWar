## 1. Concise Verdict

Norway in January 1062 is a unified Christian maritime kingdom under Harald Hardrada. It begins powerful, outward-looking, and founder-dependent, with an active Danish claim and conflict, negotiated Orcadian cooperation, a locked English route, an inactive Håkon-to-Sweden route, developing heirs, and institutions that cannot safely be collapsed into the king's person.

Norwegian diplomacy must convert reach into durable political result. Prestige, military victory, kinship, ships, or a royal claim may create leverage, but none creates recognition, agreement, possession, government, settlement, or continuity by itself.

```text
Maritime Reach + Recognized Kingship + Valid Objective + Authorized Principal + Negotiated Partners + Fleet and Return Capacity + Government During Absence + Protected Succession + External Receipt + Performance = Sustainable Norwegian Diplomatic Action
```

This authority owns Norway-specific diplomatic definitions and lifecycle contracts. It creates no new opening principal, relationship, agreement, claim, war, route, fleet, contribution, peace, settlement, or reciprocal foreign state.

## 31. Alliance, Guarantee, Neutrality, Nonaggression, Claimant Support, and External Service
