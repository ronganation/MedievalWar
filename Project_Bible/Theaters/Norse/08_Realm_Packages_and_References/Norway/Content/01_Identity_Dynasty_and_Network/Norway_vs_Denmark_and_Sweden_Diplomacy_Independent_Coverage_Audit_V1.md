## 5. Norway Differentiation Audit

### 5.1 Reach versus Continuity

Norway's central diplomatic problem is not Denmark's negotiated maritime kingship or Sweden's regionally uneven recognition. Norway begins unified and powerful, but its royal, military, diplomatic, and dynastic coordination is concentrated around Harald Hardrada. The authority correctly makes government during absence, command substitution, protected heirs, partner negotiation, fleet recovery, and return capacity part of diplomatic feasibility.

### 5.2 Active Denmark conflict rather than inferred hostility

The Norway authority consumes the opening Danish claim and conflict by reference. It does not create them. It separates claim, recognition, possession, victory, government, peace, and settlement. The Battle of Nisa remains pressure rather than a completed or guaranteed result, and later settlement is not preloaded.

### 5.3 Conditional English route

The authority gives Norway a distinct locked-route contract requiring English destabilization, changed Tostig status and authenticated contact, Danish-objective review, fleet and Supply readiness, government during absence, a protected successor, Orkney cooperation or an alternative, and no incompatible emergency. It explicitly prevents an opening invasion, invitation, commitment, or scheduled Stamford Bridge result.

### 5.4 Orkney as negotiated cooperation

Paul and Erlend remain separate people and joint rulers associated with a separate power center. Norwegian sphere or overlordship does not create unlimited ships, mariners, levies, intelligence, staging access, command, or obedience. Contributions, access, withdrawal, and settlement remain separately negotiated and externally receipted.

### 5.5 Håkon crisis from Norway's side

Håkon remains Norway-owned and does not begin as traitor, exile, rebel, Swedish client, or protected person. Norway cannot author Sweden's reverse state. Contact, evidence, mediation, route, flight, refuge, service, office, command, alliance, rebellion, return, and interstate escalation remain separate. Ragnhild, followers, household members, and resources remain independently owned.

### 5.6 Elisiv and eastern access

Elisiv's Rurik identity and queenly position support potential correspondence, hospitality, marriage negotiation, court access, peace consultation, and delegated representation. They do not create Rus government authority, alliances, funds, troops, sanctuary, obedience, complete knowledge, or power to bind Norway or foreign relatives.

### 5.7 Wider North Sea interfaces

The authority distinguishes Scotland, Caithness, the Isles, Ireland, Dublin, Iceland, Nidaros, and wider Christian courts. It protects Ingibiorg Finnsdottir's external ownership and treats Iceland as an independent commonwealth rather than a Norwegian province. Maritime relevance creates options, not possession or access.
