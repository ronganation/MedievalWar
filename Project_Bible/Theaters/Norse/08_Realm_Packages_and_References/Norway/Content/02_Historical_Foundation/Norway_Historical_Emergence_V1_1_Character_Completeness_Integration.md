## 1. Concise Verdict

Historical Emergence is the missing third authority in Norway's historical-system triad.

Historical Memory explains what people and institutions preserve, believe, contest, commemorate, or suppress. Historical Momentum evaluates current directional pressure. Historical Emergence owns conditional pathways through which current world state may produce recognizable but non-scripted political, military, dynastic, ecclesiastical, economic, cultural, and character developments.

Norway's Historical Emergence must never operate as a dated event list. January 1062 does not guarantee Niså, peace with Denmark, an English invasion, Harald's death, Håkon's exile, Magnus's early death, Olaf's kingship, Bergen's foundation, an Archbishopric at Nidaros, Orkney rivalry, or any later saga outcome. The Campaign may approach, transform, replace, delay, or permanently close those historical possibilities.

The Norway emergence problem is:

```text
RecognizableHistoricalOpportunity
without
ScriptedHistoricalOutcome
```

## 35. Validation Portfolio

Fail when:

- pressure, pathway, candidate, character, decision, fact, or outcome collapse;
- a date spawns a person or event;
- a pathway directly mutates another owner's state;
- a named historical person appears without valid identity conditions;
- a dynamic equivalent inherits a famous biography or guaranteed outcome;
- an existing qualified character is ignored without explanation;
- Niså, Danish peace, English invasion, Håkon exile, Bergen foundation, Archbishopric, or Orkney rivalry is scheduled;
- external consent is written by Norway;
- hidden pathway information is exposed;
- Opening State overwrites advanced state;
- equal inputs and versions produce unequal results.

Warn when:

- pathway conditions lack current owner summaries;
- candidate generation lacks an evidenced role gap;
- a pathway has no closure conditions;
- several pathways compete for the same person, resource, or principal objective;
- a historical identity remains chronologically possible but socially unsupported;
- a pathway is recognizable only because of its label rather than its simulation causes.
