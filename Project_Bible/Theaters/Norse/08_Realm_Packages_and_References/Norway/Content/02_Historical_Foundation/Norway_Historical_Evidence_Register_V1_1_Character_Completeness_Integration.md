## 1. Concise Verdict

Implement a Norway Historical Evidence Register as the evidence-governance layer between source material and Norway's Historical Reconciliation, Opening State, Historical Memory, Historical Momentum, Historical Emergence, Timeline and Chronology, and specialist authorities.

The register does not declare one historical narrative universally true. It identifies evidence families, records provenance and dependency, separates production date from asserted event date, assigns confidence by claim rather than by source, preserves contradictions, and defines which downstream uses are supported or prohibited.

Norway's current historical package is strategically mature but relies on evidence of uneven date, purpose, custody, survival, and independence. Near-contemporary narrative material, later saga synthesis, genealogical traditions, ecclesiastical records, legal traditions, numismatics, archaeology, settlement evidence, sacred-site evidence, institutional memory, and modern scholarship must not collapse into one certainty level.

Norway follows that architecture while defining Norway-specific portfolios.

## 3. Purpose

This register exists to answer:

- What evidence or source family supports a claim?
- When was the evidence produced relative to the claimed event?
- Is the surviving item an original, copy, compilation, translation, later narrative, material object, or modern interpretation?
- Does the source depend on another source?
- Who produced, preserved, copied, transmitted, or interpreted it?
- Which claims does it support strongly, weakly, indirectly, or not at all?
- Which contradictions remain unresolved?
- Can the evidence support an Opening State fact, a bounded Reconciliation statement, a holder-specific Memory, a Momentum input, or an Emergence eligibility review?
- Which inferences are prohibited?
- What remains Unknown?

This register does not reproduce copyrighted editions or substitute for a verified bibliography. Exact editions, manuscript identifiers, archival references, excavation reports, object catalogue numbers, translations, and modern citations remain research requirements before owner-controlled promotion.

```text
Source != EvidenceRecord
EvidenceRecord != Claim

AcceptedFact != OpeningStateFact
OpeningStateFact != UniversalHistoricalTruth
EvidenceExists != EvidenceAccessible
EvidenceAccessible != EvidenceUnderstood
EvidenceUnderstood != EvidenceAccepted
RepeatedNarrative != IndependentCorroboration
LaterCopy != ContemporaryOriginal
LaterSaga != OpeningKnowledge
InstitutionalCustody != PersonalMemory
GenealogicalTradition != ProvenDescent
PoliticalUse != HistoricalConfirmation
MaterialEvidence != NamedHistoricalActor
AbsenceOfEvidence != EvidenceOfAbsence
ConfidenceForOneClaim != ConfidenceForEveryClaim
Evidence != Memory
Memory != Momentum
```

Evidence supports owner decisions. Evidence never activates a person, office, title, claim, war, route, settlement, fleet, relationship, Memory, Momentum, Emergence pathway, command, or outcome by itself.

## 10. Source Hierarchy and Independence

No universal hierarchy settles every claim. As a default research preference, evaluation should consider:

1. contemporary or near-contemporary documents and material evidence;
2. independently preserved institutional, numismatic, archaeological, runic, burial, and settlement evidence;
3. distinct near-contemporary narrative witnesses;
4. later medieval narrative with explicit source and dependency review;
5. modern critical editions and scholarship that disclose method and evidence;
6. unsourced modern retellings only as discovery leads, never as promotion evidence.

```yaml
source_independence_rules:
  repeated_report_counts_as_independent_confirmation: false
  shared_exemplar_requires_dependency_record: true
  translation_counts_as_new_independent_source: false
  abridgment_counts_as_new_independent_source: false
  modern_summary_counts_as_primary_evidence: false
```

## 12. Authenticity and Integrity

```yaml
authenticity_states:
  - Unassessed
  - Contested
  - Plausible
  - Probable
  - HighConfidence
  - OwnerVerified
  - Rejected

integrity_states:
  - Unknown
  - Fragmentary
  - LaterCopy
  - Composite
  - InterpolatedOrSuspected
  - Translated
  - Reconstructed
  - MaterialContextIntact
  - OwnerVerified
```

Authenticity and integrity are distinct. A genuine later manuscript can preserve a much earlier narrative while remaining weak evidence for exact detail. A genuine material object can be poorly provenanced. A modern reconstruction can be useful for explanation without becoming historical evidence.

Royal households, episcopal institutions, churches, assemblies, claimants, regional households, envoys, foreign courts, chroniclers, and later archives may possess different evidence or copies. Exact opening custody and access remain owner-controlled.

## 50. Validation Rules

Fail when:

- a source is treated as proof of every claim it contains;
- repeated dependent narratives are counted as independent corroboration;
- a later saga becomes universal opening Knowledge;
- evidence creates a Memory, Momentum state, Emergence pathway, event, office, title, route, settlement, force, property, relationship, or outcome;
- exact Niså numbers or tactics become opening inventory;
- Håkon begins as traitor, rebel, exile, or Swedish client from later narrative;
- Magnus II merges with Magnus the Good;
- Olaf III merges with Saint Olaf or begins with a later epithet;
- Thorfinn remains living at opening;
- Paul and Erlend merge into one person;
- Ingegerd resolves to Ingibiorg Finnsdottir;
- Ingegerd and Maria merge or receive universal shared evidence conclusions;
- daughter maternity, exact age, age order, marriage, betrothal, adult authority, or later fate is promoted without claim-specific support and owner review;
- Ragnhild is placed in House Hardrada, absorbed into Håkon, treated as an automatic mediator, or used as an automatic claim source;
- the unidentified Bishop of Nidaros receives an invented name;
- Archbishopric, later cathedral, palace, fortress, or castle forms enter 1062;
- Bergen begins as an established city without owner-approved evidence;
- Fairhair descent becomes universally proven;
- source silence is treated automatically as disproof;
- candidate evidence IDs are treated as canonical;
- implementation validation is claimed without evidence.

Warn when:

- production date and asserted event date are not separated;
- source dependency is unclear;
- translation or compilation history is missing;
- custody and Knowledge are merged;
- confidence is assigned to a source rather than a claim;
- a site claim lacks archaeological or chronology review;
- evidence supports existence but not exact scale, motive, ownership, or current state;
- a modern reconstruction is used without classification.
