## 1. Concise Verdict

Norway has enough source and authority support to retain a strong, bounded January 1062 identity foundation. The Realm, Harald Hardrada, House Hardrada, the protected successors, royal household actors, Håkon Ivarsson, the See and Bishop's office of Nidaros, the unidentified bishop holder, and the separate Orkney actors already have current owner authorities. This review does not reopen those sound foundations merely because some secondary details remain uncertain.

The principal remaining risks are not absence of all identity. They are unsafe overprecision, identity conflation, later-history projection, title and office merging, household-property assumptions, unsupported named officeholders, candidate-site promotion, automatic external consent, and treating later saga narrative as opening truth.

Unknown values remain Unknown. Empty roles do not create placeholder people. A current identity may be approved or retained while parentage, exact date, office, location, property, relationship, route, or later outcome remains separately gated.

### 3.1 This review owns

- source-authority sufficiency recommendations;
- identity and duplicate-risk dispositions;
- lifecycle and chronology gates;

- alias migration;
- opening activation;
- person or institution creation;
- lifecycle execution;
- title, office, Dynasty, household, relationship, succession, government, property, route, military, economy, Memory, Momentum, or Emergence mutation;
- external Realm decisions;
- future Campaign outcomes;
- production implementation evidence.

## 4. Disposition Vocabulary

```yaml
dispositions:
  RetainCanonical:
    meaning: current stable typed identity is sufficiently supported and remains controlled by its owner
  ApproveConditional:
    meaning: safe only after explicit identity lifecycle chronology owner and opening gates pass
  ApprovedHistoricalOnly:
    meaning: durable past identity never living or active at opening
  ReviewOnly:
    meaning: relevant but non-instantiating and not opening-active
  ExternalCanonical:
    meaning: resolved typed identity owned by another Realm or package
  ReviewExternalRegistry:
    meaning: external identity or association requires external-owner and Registry decision
  Unknown:
    meaning: no safe value exists
  ExcludedLaterOutcome:
    meaning: later historical condition prohibited from opening state
  QuarantinedContradiction:
    meaning: conflated invented contradictory or unsafe identity or claim
  Reject:
    meaning: incompatible with evidence or controlling authority
```
