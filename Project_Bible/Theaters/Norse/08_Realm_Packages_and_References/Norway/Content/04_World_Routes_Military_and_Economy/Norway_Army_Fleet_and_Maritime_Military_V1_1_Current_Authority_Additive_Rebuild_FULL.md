## 5. Military Thesis

Norway's military strength comes from combining:

- Harald's strategic and battlefield leadership;
- capable senior commanders;
- royal household and elite fighting followings;
- regional noble followings;
- owner-defined service, levy, obligation, and contribution systems;
- seafaring communities and ship access;
- assembly, household, settlement, port, landing, and route networks;
- supply, repair, replacement, and return capacity;
- negotiated cooperation with Orkney and other external actors where valid.

The system should allow Norway to fight effectively at sea, move forces along coasts and fjords, raid or threaten across maritime approaches, defend regional centers, and undertake a major foreign objective when capacity is concentrated. It should also make clear that ships and warriors withdrawn for foreign war are unavailable for local defense, government, transport, fishing, trade, household protection, and recovery.
