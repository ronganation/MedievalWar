## 7. Why Embedded Ownership Is Insufficient

The located files provide essential inputs and handoffs, but no located file explicitly owns all or most of the following mutable Realm portfolios:

- Property and economic rights;
- productive potential and Production instances;
- goods and civilian stock custody;
- Markets and exchange transactions;
- Treasury funds and accounts;
- revenue assessment, collection, remittance, receipt, arrears, debt, and audit;
- civilian and commercial cargo shipment and delivery;
- investment, maintenance, construction, repair, commissioning, and economic recovery.

```text
Several Files Mention Economy != Complete Distributed Economy Owner
Route And Military Logistics != Civilian Commercial Economy
Governance Authority != Treasury And Property Runtime State
Opening State != Definition And Runtime Authority
AI Proposal != Owner
```

Leaving the state distributed without an explicit owner would preserve unresolved overlaps and gaps rather than intentionally bounded embedded ownership.
