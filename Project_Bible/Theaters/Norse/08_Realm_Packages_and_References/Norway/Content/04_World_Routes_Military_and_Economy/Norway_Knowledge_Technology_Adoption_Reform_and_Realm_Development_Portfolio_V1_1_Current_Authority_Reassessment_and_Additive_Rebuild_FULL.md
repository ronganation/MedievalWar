## 2. Playable development thesis

Norway begins with meaningful inherited strengths: district and household military service, maritime experience, regional assemblies and centers, a powerful royal command, bow continuity, seafaring communities, ports and landings, and a realm capable of concentrating force. Those strengths are not one national system and do not guarantee ships, crews, equipment, supply, consent, or readiness.

The player should decide which constraints to relieve and which capacities to deepen. Development should make Norway more coordinated, sustainable, specialized, or institutionally resilient while preserving the value of lower-cost district infantry, bows, local defense, household service, and maritime mobility.
