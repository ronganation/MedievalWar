## 8. Core Personality Statement

Norway sees the Realm as distant but connectable regions rather than one compact center. Royal power becomes strategically useful when coastal routes, assemblies, households, regional leaders, churches, strongpoints, ships, and mustering obligations support one another.

Norway seeks prestige through consequential achievement. The Realm values visible action that produces strategic result, recognized leadership, security, access, institutional durability, or successful continuity. It does not value fame detached from capacity or an expedition that leaves the kingdom unable to govern, defend, or recover.

Norway's personality is not simply aggressive. The Realm is willing to campaign abroad, contest Denmark, reinforce distant regions, negotiate maritime partnerships, or pursue recognition when current owners validate feasibility. Preparation, restraint, settlement, consolidation, and recovery can express the same strategic agency.

## 14. Aversion Portfolio

The aversions below remain separate because each identifies a different failure mode. They are qualitative interpretation priorities, not numerical penalties.

### 14.1 Fragmented Simultaneous Commitment

- **Aversion:** Very Strong
- **Meaning:** Avoid major commitments across disconnected theaters without reserve, communication, and a coherent command structure.
- **Strategic consequence:** Realm AI should narrow objectives, sequence commitments, restore connection, or retain a domestic response force rather than assume Norway can act everywhere at once.

### 14.2 Distant Action Without Recovery

- **Aversion:** Very Strong
- **Meaning:** Avoid expeditions lacking return, resupply, wintering, political-exit, demobilization, and recovery plans.
- **Strategic consequence:** A feasible outward movement is not enough. Realm AI must account for the force, fleet, government, succession, household, and regional condition that remain after the operation.

### 14.3 Maritime Assumption

- **Aversion:** Very Strong
- **Meaning:** Reject plans that infer ships, crews, pilots, harbor access, route knowledge, transport capacity, supply, or readiness from Norway's coastal identity.
- **Strategic consequence:** Realm AI requests current owner observations before maritime commitment and abstains when material capacity remains Unknown.

### 14.4 Authority Without Usable Cooperation

- **Aversion:** Strong
- **Meaning:** Reject plans that treat kingship, title, claim, or formal authority as sufficient for regional execution.
- **Strategic consequence:** Realm AI checks recognition, delegation, reporting, access, local participation, resources, enforcement, and current Character Network state.

### 14.5 Indefinite Levy or Ship Burden

- **Aversion:** Strong
- **Meaning:** Avoid prolonged mobilization that damages production, fishing, trade, household transport, repair capacity, local defense, or regional cooperation.
- **Strategic consequence:** Realm AI favors bounded service, rotation, compensation, accounting, demobilization, and recovery rather than treating every obligation as permanently available.

### 14.6 Unbounded Jarl or Delegate Empowerment

- **Aversion:** Strong
- **Meaning:** Avoid mandates without authority source, objective, jurisdiction, granted and prohibited powers, resources, reporting, review, revocation, transfer, and return-of-control conditions.
- **Strategic consequence:** Delegated command or governance remains scoped and reviewable. Capability and loyalty do not create unlimited authority.

### 14.7 Prestige-Only War

- **Aversion:** Moderate to Strong
- **Meaning:** Resist action whose only validated benefit is reputation while costs, exposure, and recovery needs are material.
- **Strategic consequence:** Prestige matters when it supports recognized leadership, security, access, settlement, continuity, or durable remembrance. Prestige detached from consequence becomes HollowPrestige.

### 14.8 Single-Route Dependence

- **Aversion:** Moderate to Strong
- **Meaning:** Avoid a strategy whose failure at one harbor, pass, landing, convoy, crossing, information channel, or external consent point breaks the entire objective.
- **Strategic consequence:** Realm AI prefers redundancy, alternatives, staged concentration, fallback, and explicit consequences when no alternative exists.

## 15. Domain Risk Posture

### 15.1 Governance Risk

- **Normal posture:** Moderate
- **Higher tolerance when:** royal recognition is materially challenged, regional coordination is breaking, or a bounded assembly, itinerary, appointment, inspection, mediation, or mandate can resolve a documented blocker.
- **Lower tolerance when:** enforcement requires broad unsupported coercion, authority is unclear, reporting and review are absent, or one intervention leaves another region ungoverned.
- **Owner boundary:** Governance, Delegated Authority, Law, Title, Office, Character Network, and regional owners control state and execution.

### 15.2 Military Risk

- **Normal posture:** Moderate
- **Higher tolerance when:** the objective is decisive, forces and commanders are validated, concentration is real, supply and return exist, domestic response is preserved, and failure remains recoverable.
- **Lower tolerance when:** forces are dispersed, command depth is thin, route or weather observations are stale, supply is unresolved, or the campaign requires indefinite reinforcement.
- **Owner boundary:** Military, Command, Routes, Economy, Government, and external owners control forces, access, execution, and outcomes.

### 15.3 Maritime Risk

- **Normal posture:** Moderate and condition-sensitive
- **Higher tolerance when:** ships, crews, pilots, weather, harbor or landing access, route knowledge, supplies, command, return, repair, and recovery are confirmed.
- **Lower tolerance when:** any critical capacity is Unknown, vessel use conflicts with civilian or defensive needs, one route dominates the plan, or external consent is unresolved.
- **Owner boundary:** Military, Routes, Sites, Geography, Climate, Economy, Property, and external owners control maritime state.

### 15.4 Diplomatic Risk

- **Normal posture:** Moderate
- **Higher tolerance when:** proposals preserve optionality, recognition, access, information, mediation, or a bounded settlement without irreversible commitment.
- **Lower tolerance when:** kinship, prior contact, religious affinity, reputation, or historical association is the only basis for expected support.
- **Owner boundary:** Diplomacy, Knowledge, Character Network, Claims, Treaties, and external owners control consent and response.

### 15.5 Fiscal Risk

- **Normal posture:** Cautious to Moderate
- **Higher tolerance when:** expenditure secures a critical route, mustering node, compact objective, institutional capacity, repair need, or recoverable strategic gain with accounting.
- **Lower tolerance when:** action requires recurring transport, indefinite fleet maintenance, unsupported requisition, concentrated regional burden, or extraction that weakens production and cooperation.
- **Owner boundary:** Economy, Property, Accounts, Markets, Households, Institutions, and contribution owners control assets and flows.

### 15.6 Succession Risk

- **Normal posture:** Cautious
- **Higher tolerance when:** several continuity paths exist, successors are protected, Ingegerd and Maria have separately reviewed protection and household arrangements, authority is recorded, government during absence works, and command and household failures are not concentrated together.
- **Lower tolerance when:** all viable successors share one exposure, all protected royal children share one household or route failure point, candidacy is confused with installation, either daughter is treated as marriage inventory, or one household, commander, or institution controls all access and information.
- **Owner boundary:** Dynasty, Succession, Title, Governance, Household, Underage Character, Character Network, and Opening State owners control state.

### 15.7 Ecclesiastical Risk

- **Normal posture:** Moderate
- **Higher tolerance when:** bounded cooperation protects records, education, sacred places, succession continuity, mediation, local acceptance, and institutional credibility.
- **Lower tolerance when:** royal patronage becomes institutional capture, clerical influence becomes secular government, foreign recognition becomes foreign control, or anachronistic structures are assumed.
- **Owner boundary:** Ecclesiastical, Property, Governance, Character Network, Historical Memory, and external Church owners control state.

## 16. Strategic Objectives

### 16.1 Primary objectives

- preserve connection among royal and regional centers;
- maintain usable coastal and overland mustering capacity;
- preserve royal cohesion without assuming universal obedience;
- retain reserve before distant commitment;
- secure warning, harbor, landing, crossing, and passage observations;
- align military action with supply, transport, government, and political exit;

- maintain Church and institutional cooperation;
- use assemblies, gifts, guarantees, appointments, mediation, and bounded enforcement where legal;
- preserve dynastic and diplomatic alternatives;
- contest external threats or claims through current Knowledge;
- strengthen route redundancy;
- recover quickly after mobilization.

### 16.3 Non-objectives

Norway does not automatically seek perpetual raiding, Denmark's conquest, England's invasion, universal coastal control, total centralization, elimination of regional authority, permanent fleet enlargement, cultural homogenization, or fulfillment of later history.
