## 8. Compact Icon Retained Elements

The first corrected icon should retain:

- one dominant structure;
- one covered frontage or gathering entrance;
- one coherent doorway or open bay;
- inspectable roof, walls, supports, and foundation;
- minimal immediate ground context;
- restrained timber, earth, turf, stone, woven, and simple iron cues as provisional material language;
- a dark, tactile medieval atmosphere;
- strong silhouette and value separation at small size.

## 9. Compact Icon Rejected Elements

Do not inherit:

- the rejected fjord panorama;
- a whole village, town, harbor, or settlement scene;
- mountains, water, ships, or coastline as important identity cues;
- several major buildings;
- crowds, active merchants, formal assemblies, or stocked markets;
- multiple stalls, carts, large fires, or dense object storytelling;
- modern Gulating monuments or named-site layouts;
- church-dominant, royal, military, or fortified compositions;
- later urban streets, guild architecture, or permanent shop fronts;
- photorealistic rendering;
- flags, heraldry, runes, signs, numerals, labels, or embedded UI;
- fantasy Viking ornament;
- copied Medieval II, Shogun 2, Attila, or mod art.
