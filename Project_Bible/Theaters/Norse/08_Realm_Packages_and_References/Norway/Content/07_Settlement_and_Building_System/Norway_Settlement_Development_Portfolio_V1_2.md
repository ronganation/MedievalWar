This portfolio closes the Norway expression, family-mapping, branch-candidate, and owner-interface gap identified by the controlling assessment. It does not close the later exact-building-definition or visual-asset gap. It translates the shared settlement-development system into a Norway-specific portfolio without replacing the mature owners for physical sites, routes, military capability, economy and property, governance, ecclesiastical content, population, Realm development, Theater-owned units, or Opening State.

Norway's settlement game is not a renamed England model and not a generic Viking building tree. It is a distributed maritime and regional system in which settlement cores, ports, landings, anchorages, assemblies, royal and household centers, ecclesiastical sites, protected stores, workshops, warning points, inland approaches, and seasonal support networks combine differently by place and circumstance.

### 10.1 Norway unit interfaces

- Local Drengjar interface: settlement-community defense, basic assembly, alarm, stores, maintenance, and short-service support.
- Leidang Spearmen interface: district contribution, inspection, assembly, verified equipment access or support, reporting, transport, and return.
- Leidang Bowmen interface: bow continuity, ammunition supply, practice, inspection, combined-arms assembly, and replenishment.
- Upland Spearmen interface: inland-community access, regional assembly, warning, stores, and local defense.
- Royal Hirdmen interface: household concentration, command protection, secure hospitality, equipment custody, and crisis reserve.
- Mounted Hirdmen interface: shared household pool, valid riders, horses, equipment, escort, command, and transport support.
- Haalogaland Bowmen interface: northern regional source, knowledge, seasonal access, warning, storage, and route constraints.
- Norwegian Crossbowmen interface: High Era specialist, equipment, ammunition, training, and protected-fire support without replacing bows automatically.

A settlement branch may satisfy one requirement while other requirements remain absent. No building constitutes a complete unit-admission rule.
