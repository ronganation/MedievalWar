## 1. Purpose

This portfolio defines how Norway's settlement regions may specialize without becoming identical templates, self-sufficient islands, or fragile one-center systems. It governs specialization depth, concentration, distribution, regional balance, fallback capacity, substitution limits, resilience investment, disruption absorption, and recovery reorganization.

Specialization is not self-sufficiency. Regional balance is not identical development. Concentration is not automatically efficient. Distribution is not automatically resilient. Redundancy is not free duplication. A substitute is not always equivalent. A damaged network may recover in a different form. A major center does not invalidate smaller specialized places. Seasonal and distributed forms may be mature outcomes rather than incomplete stages.
