This authority supplies the substantive Norway progression layer while preserving the shared Town and Civic versus Castle and Fortified Center spine and avoiding England's specific town, castle, and burh expression mix. Norway progresses through changing combinations of distributed community capacity, markets and assemblies, maritime hosts, inland crossings, royal and household presence, ecclesiastical depth, protected sites, attached productive functions, strategic nodes, regional centers, networks, and temporary or seasonal support.

Progression is not a universal ladder. A distributed fjord network may be more mature and strategically useful than a concentrated port in one context. A seasonal site may remain the correct answer rather than becoming permanent. A settlement may specialize, plateau, lose functions, recover differently, or shift its regional role without advancing toward one universal final form.

## 7. E01-E15 Progression Profiles

### E01 Distributed Rural and Maritime Community

- **Strengthening:** better local provision, warning, participation, repair, landing access, and network relationships.
- **Specialization:** fisheries, pastoral support, maritime participation, local defense, communication, or seasonal production.
- **Plateau:** stable dispersed capacity remains appropriate where concentration is unsupported.
- **Regression pressures:** household loss, route isolation, labor depletion, local-defense hollowing, property disruption, or repeated seasonal failure.
- **Recovery:** household return, rights restoration, local repair, route reopening, and network reconnection.
- **Possible transitions:** E02, E12, or participation in E14. No automatic town progression.

### E02 Village and Local Service Center

- **Strengthening:** recurring service, worship, craft, water, care, warning, and local exchange.
- **Specialization:** local market, craft, ecclesiastical support, defense/refuge, or route service.
- **Plateau:** viable local center need not become a town.
- **Regression pressures:** fire, water/service failure, property conflict, population loss, institutional withdrawal, or maintenance debt.
- **Recovery:** service restoration, habitation return, rights confirmation, and community repair.
- **Possible transitions:** conditional E03 or E04; seasonal concentration alone is insufficient.

### E03 Market and Assembly Center

- **Strengthening:** recurring participation, exchange, hospitality, records, judgment, warning, and secure access.
- **Specialization:** assembly, exchange, regional coordination, muster support, or information custody.
- **Plateau:** periodic importance can remain successful without becoming a permanent urban center.
- **Regression pressures:** route loss, insecurity, missing participants, absent rights, record loss, goods scarcity, or political rejection.
- **Recovery:** restore venue access, participation, rights, records, security, and supporting routes.
- **Possible transitions:** conditional E05 where durable multi-owner regional capacity develops. This does not define one market-and-assembly building or one visual asset.

### E04 Emerging Town and Port Settlement

- **Strengthening:** durable habitation, craft, market, storage, government, care, and maritime endpoint functions.
- **Specialization:** commercial exchange, craft production, port support, records, institutions, or regional service.
- **Plateau:** emerging status may persist when population, rights, maritime operation, maintenance, or specialist capacity remain limited.
- **Regression pressures:** fire, blockade, route loss, craft collapse, population displacement, market failure, or high maintenance.
- **Recovery:** staged habitation, route, market, craft, storage, and maritime reopening.
- **Possible transitions:** conditional E05 based on role breadth and owner-backed capacity, not size alone. E04 remains a civic settlement-core expression separate from its E08 maritime host relationships.

### E05 Regional Multi-Function Center

- **Strengthening:** deeper coordination among political, ecclesiastical, commercial, military, maritime, and information functions.
- **Specialization:** one or more functions may deepen, while overconcentration raises capture, maintenance, and regional-imbalance risk.
- **Plateau:** durable regional importance without permanent-capital status.
- **Regression pressures:** multi-system disruption, institutional withdrawal, route fracture, capture, fiscal strain, or local hollowing.
- **Recovery:** staged owner-specific reopening rather than one global repair action.
- **Transition rule:** E05 remains a composition of separate owners and does not absorb E06, E07, E08, or E09 automatically. E05 is not one building, icon, or map asset.

### E06 Royal Itinerant Household Center

- **Strengthening:** recurring royal or household presence, hospitality, custody, records, command, escort, and continuity support.
- **Specialization:** court hospitality, secure custody, household service, command protection, or travel support.
- **Plateau:** recurring presence may remain episodic and successful.
- **Regression pressures:** ruler absence, household displacement, route failure, supply strain, property conflict, regional rejection, or record loss.
- **Recovery:** restore household presence, property, access, supply, records, and regional acceptance.
- **Transition rule:** does not automatically become a permanent capital or E05.

### E07 Ecclesiastical and Sacred Center

- **Strengthening:** worship, records, education, mediation, pilgrimage, care, patronage, and sacred-memory support.
- **Specialization:** records/education, pilgrimage/hospitality, mediation, care, or ceremony.
- **Plateau:** institutional depth can remain regionally important without later archiepiscopal form.
- **Regression pressures:** property loss, clergy absence, record destruction, route insecurity, patronage withdrawal, physical damage, or disputed recognition.
- **Recovery:** institutional recommissioning requires clergy, property, records, access, maintenance, and patronage owners.
- **Historical firewall:** no automatic later cathedral, Archbishopric, palace, or universal legitimacy.

### E08 Port, Harbor, Landing, or Anchorage Host

- **Strengthening:** improved shelter, approach, transfer, storage, repair, embarkation, warning, and return support.
- **Specialization:** local landing, protected anchorage, transfer point, repair host, stores, embarkation, or coastal information.
- **Plateau:** a landing can remain a landing; not every host becomes a port.
- **Regression pressures:** storm, silting or physical damage, blockade, labor loss, vessel/crew absence, route failure, insecurity, or storage loss.
- **Recovery:** phased approach, landing, labor, storage, transfer, security, and route reopening.
- **Transition rule:** maritime host remains distinct from E04 settlement core and E14 network. Maritime improvement does not automatically change the primary settlement class.

### E09 Fortified Site and Protected Center

- **Strengthening:** refuge, warning, custody, stores, defense, command, and repair support.
- **Specialization:** local refuge, protected store, crossing defense, household protection, or regional fortified support.
- **Plateau:** modest protection may be appropriate and should not be forced toward later castle scale.
- **Regression pressures:** siege, occupation, structural damage, absent garrison/command, labor shortage, or maintenance failure.
- **Recovery:** lawful rights, control, labor, materials, command, and recommissioning are separately required.
- **Historical firewall:** no automatic later castle or fortress projection. E09 becomes a Castle and Fortified Center settlement expression only when a durable settlement core is separately admitted.

### E10 Inland Valley and Crossing Center

- **Strengthening:** route access, crossings, exchange, assembly, communication, warning, and regional defense support.
- **Specialization:** crossing service, inland market, assembly, relay, defense, or recovery coordination.
- **Plateau:** inland importance need not become coastal-style urban concentration.
- **Regression pressures:** crossing loss, route interruption, isolation, population decline, insecurity, or exchange failure.
- **Recovery:** route-led restoration followed by service, market, assembly, and defense functions.
- **Transition rule:** no universal national road-network assumption.

### E11 Northern Regional Service Center

- **Strengthening:** regional warning, storage, access, service, defense, specialist throughput, and recovery support.
- **Specialization:** northern warning, seasonal stores, bow-service support, landing support, or remote recovery.
- **Plateau:** limited seasonal throughput may be the correct mature condition.
- **Regression pressures:** seasonal closure, long-distance route failure, store loss, local population pressure, information delay, or supply interruption.
- **Recovery:** regional source, routes, season, storage, information, and supporting population must return.
- **Boundary:** does not create Realm-wide northern unit availability.

### E12 Attached Productive or Transport Site

- **Strengthening:** clearer attachment, operation, workforce, route, security, and host support.
- **Specialization:** fishery, craft, store, estate, repair, transport, or other owner-approved function.
- **Plateau:** attached status may remain preferable to independent-node admission.
- **Regression pressures:** operator loss, route isolation, property conflict, resource/input failure, raid, damage, or host decline.
- **Recovery:** owner-routed repair and reconnection.
- **Transition rule:** becomes E13 only when separate control, access, defense, occupation, and recovery materially matter. Neither E12 nor E13 is a primary settlement class by default.

### E13 Independent Strategic Node

- **Strengthening:** distinct access, defense, control, interdiction, warning, and recovery relevance.
- **Specialization:** crossing, maritime node, protected store, observation, or route-interdiction role.
- **Plateau:** strategic relevance need not produce a settlement.
- **Regression pressures:** bypass, isolation, occupation, loss of route relevance, absent operators, or maintenance failure.
- **Recovery:** independent access, control, defense, route, and operator conditions must be restored.
- **Boundary:** map-node admission remains externally owned. Strategic-node progression does not become settlement progression automatically.

### E14 Distributed Coastal or Fjord Network

- **Strengthening:** more reliable relationships among landings, communities, routes, stores, warnings, and fallback nodes.
- **Specialization:** transport, warning, district service, repair, exchange, or recovery network.
- **Plateau:** network breadth may remain strategically superior to port concentration.
- **Regression pressures:** endpoint loss, route break, coordination failure, community decline, vessel/crew shortage, information failure, or repeated weather disruption.
- **Recovery:** rerouting and node-by-node restoration; the network can function partially.
- **Boundary:** coastline does not create the network.

### E15 Temporary or Seasonal Operational Site

- **Strengthening:** improved permission, setup, supply, protection, communication, return, and repeatability.
- **Specialization:** assembly session, seasonal landing, campaign concentration, emergency refuge, repair, or recovery support.
- **Plateau:** recurring temporary use may be the correct stable form.
- **Regression pressures:** lost permission, season closure, supply failure, insecurity, mandate withdrawal, or failed return path.
- **Recovery:** renewed permission, mandate, access, supply, protection, and timing.
- **Boundary:** no automatic permanent settlement, port, or fortification.

## 9. Plateau and Stagnation

Plateau and stagnation are not synonyms.

```yaml
Plateau:
  condition: CurrentRoleIsSustainableAndFurtherExpansionIsNotRequiredOrSupported
  player_meaning: MaintainReorientOrAcceptStableRole
Stagnation:
  condition: CurrentRolePressuresExceedMaintenanceAdaptationOrOwnerBackedInputs
  player_meaning: AddressBottleneckReduceLoadRepairReorientOrAcceptDeclineRisk
```

Common stagnation pressures include:

- Maintenance debt.
- Population or specialist shortage.
- Property or rights conflict.
- Route, vessel, carrier, or seasonal unreliability.
- Weak market participation or goods access.
- Institutional vacancy or record failure.
- Local-defense hollowing from concentration.
- Repeated damage without completed recovery.
- Over-specialization without fallback.
- Excessive dependence on one center or endpoint.

## 10. Regression Grammar

Regression is role-specific. It may reduce depth, reliability, geographic reach, operating season, institutional continuity, protection, or network connectivity without erasing every prior function.

```yaml
RegressionChannels:
  Demographic: PopulationWorkforceSpecialistOrHouseholdLoss
  Physical: FireStormSiegeDamageDecayOrFacilityLoss
  RouteAndAccess: BlockadeCrossingLossSeasonalClosureIsolationOrUnsafeApproach
  Economic: FundingMaterialStockMarketCarrierOrMaintenanceFailure
  PropertyAndRights: DisputeConfiscationAccessLossOrInstitutionalRightFailure
  GovernmentAndInstitutional: OfficeHouseholdClergyMandateRecordOrRecognitionFailure
  MilitaryAndSecurity: LocalDefenseHollowingOccupationRaidWarningOrCommandFailure
  Information: DelayedStaleInterceptedMissingOrUntrustedCommunication
  Network: EndpointLossRelationshipFailureOverconcentrationOrFallbackCollapse
```

Regression does not automatically reclassify an expression. Reclassification requires owner review where the role itself has materially changed.

## 13. Regional Progression Lenses

### Nidaros and Trøndelag

Progression emphasizes multi-function coordination, assembly, ecclesiastical depth, records, sacred memory, river-mouth access, market/hospitality, and regional networks. The center must not become one merged universal object or an automatic permanent capital.

### Southern Norway

Progression emphasizes emerging settlements, maritime approaches, markets, royal movement, external connections, Oslofjord distribution, inland transfer, and defense. Later urban importance and later fortresses remain prohibited as opening proof.

### Western Coast and Fjords

Progression emphasizes distributed landings, anchorages, communities, repair, storage, exchange, attached sites, and fjord networks. Port concentration competes with distributed resilience.

### Inland Valleys and Uplands

Progression emphasizes crossings, local centers, markets, assembly, warning, regional defense, route restoration, and attached productive or transport sites. Inland development must not be treated as a lesser coastal model.

### Northern and Hålogaland Contexts

Progression emphasizes seasonal access, warning, storage, regional service, landing support, specialist throughput, temporary capacity, and distributed recovery. Plateau at limited seasonal capacity may be successful.
