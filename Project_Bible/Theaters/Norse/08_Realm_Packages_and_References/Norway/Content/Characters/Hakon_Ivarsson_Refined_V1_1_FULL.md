## Core Character Thesis

Håkon Ivarsson is Norway's indispensable but conditional noble commander. The same military competence, regional following, political experience, and royal-line connection that make him one of Harald Hardrada's most valuable servants also make him a credible focus of anxiety, negotiation, and opposition.

Håkon begins neither as an unquestioning loyalist nor as a predetermined traitor. His central strategic tension is whether the Norwegian Crown can use, reward, and constrain a powerful magnate without turning uncertainty into persecution or independent strength into rebellion. The campaign may make him a hero of Niså, reconciled magnate, heir mentor, regional defender, exile, Swedish client, kingmaker, rebel, or long-term Scandinavian intermediary, but none of those outcomes is opening truth.

## Historical Identity

Håkon Ivarsson, Old Norse Hákon Ívarsson, is a Norwegian Catholic magnate and the son of Ivar the White. Saga tradition associates him with the Lade jarl lineage. He is married under the project's opening interpretation to Ragnhild Magnusdatter, daughter of Magnus the Good.

At the campaign opening Håkon is alive, active in Norway, and associated with:

- high noble rank and a jarlship context;
- senior military command;
- regional influence in Romerike, the Uplands, or a related eastern Norwegian sphere under bounded source uncertainty;
- prior service under Sweyn II of Denmark;
- renewed service under Harald Hardrada;
- marriage into the preceding royal line through Ragnhild;
- the approaching Norwegian and Danish confrontation at Niså.

His durable identity is that of a powerful Norwegian noble and experienced Scandinavian political survivor. His current title, office, command, regional mandate, loyalty, trust, and alignment remain externally owned.

## Historical Uncertainty

Håkon's career is known primarily through later saga traditions, including material associated with Morkinskinna, Heimskringla, Fagrskinna, and the fragmentary Håkon Ivarsson saga. Those traditions preserve a broadly coherent career while differing on chronology, location, motive, and interpretation.

## Historically attested or strongly supported

## Restraints

Håkon's AI should not:

- begin as a traitor;
- defect because a historical date arrives;
- treat prior Danish service as permanent allegiance;
- assume Swedish protection before contact and agreement;
- convert Ragnhild's lineage into an automatic royal claim;
- mobilize a full faction from one grievance;
- know Harald's private intentions without evidence;
- interpret every slight as mortal danger;
- remain passively loyal under a credible threat of arbitrary death;
- bypass command, office, law, claim, or relationship owners;
- directly mutate authoritative state.

## Information boundary

Håkon may reason from direct observation, orders, messengers, retainers, Ragnhild, regional contacts, councils, prior Danish familiarity, and valid foreign communication. Evidence may be incomplete, delayed, contradictory, or manipulated. He should not know hidden royal plans, every witness account, foreign willingness, or future outcomes.
