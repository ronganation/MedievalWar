## Core Legacy Thesis

Thorfinn the Mighty is the absent founder whose political world remains strategically active after his death. His long rule joined personal authority, maritime force, regional lordship, Christian patronage, and negotiation between Norwegian and Scottish power into an Orcadian system whose scale exceeded its institutional security.

The campaign does not ask whether Thorfinn can act again. It asks whether Paul and Erlend can govern what he left behind, whether Ingibiorg can carry parts of his dynastic and political inheritance into a new Scottish setting, and whether Norway, Scotland, Orcadian communities, retainers, and Church institutions can agree on what his rule meant. His legacy provides pressure, evidence, expectations, claims, remembered obligations, and competing narratives. It does not issue commands or create automatic outcomes.

## Historical Identity

Thorfinn Sigurdsson, commonly called Thorfinn the Mighty and known in Old Norse as Þorfinnr inn ríki, was a powerful eleventh-century Earl of Orkney. He was the son of Sigurd Hlodvirsson, husband of Ingibiorg Finnsdottir, and father of Paul and Erlend Thorfinnsson.

His durable historical identity includes:

- association with the Norse Earls of Orkney;
- rulership centered on Orkney;
- political and military activity across the northern maritime world;
- interests or influence involving Shetland, Caithness, northern Scotland, the Hebrides, Ireland, and Norway;
- marriage to Ingibiorg Finnsdottir;
- fatherhood of Paul and Erlend;
- pilgrimage to Rome;
- support for Christian organization and patronage in Orkney;
- an enduring reputation as the most formidable Orcadian ruler of his period.

At campaign opening, Thorfinn is not a living character. His historical identity remains referenceable, while active agency, lifecycle processing, command, government, relationship mutation, and AI decision-making are prohibited.

## Final Design Summary

Thorfinn the Mighty begins as a deceased historical figure and maximum-relevance Orcadian legacy, never as an active character. His legacy creates pressure without scripts and meaning without agency. The refined profile preserves historical uncertainty, removes universal legacy attributes, separates memory from fact and reputation, and routes all mutable state to the proper specialist authorities.
