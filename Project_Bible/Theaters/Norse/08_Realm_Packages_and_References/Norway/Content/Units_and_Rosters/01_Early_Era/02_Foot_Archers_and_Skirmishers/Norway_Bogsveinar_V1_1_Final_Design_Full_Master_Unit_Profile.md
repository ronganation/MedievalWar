## 4. Player-facing identity strip

- **Unit:** Bogsveinar
- **Gloss:** Trained Bowmen
- **Class:** trained intermediate archers
- **Source:** selected bow-qualified people from validated households and recurring regional or retainer service
- **Primary use:** protected sustained fire, target disruption, and preparation of local opportunities
- **Secondary use:** counter-missile pressure and controlled withdrawal support
- **Major weaknesses:** cavalry, melee, concentrated counterfire, ammunition loss, poor sight lines, and specialist archers
- **Availability:** narrower than Leidang Bowmen, broader than Royal Hird or exceptional regional missile sources
- **Opening State:** not authorized for January 1062
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 5. One-screen Modern Medieval II contract

### Battlefield
- Deploy behind Landsmenn, Spears, terrain, or another protective frontage.
- Apply disciplined pressure to approaching, exposed, fatigued, or engaged targets.
- Concentrate fire only when the target and tactical purpose justify ammunition expenditure.
- Prepare openings for Drengja and mounted exploitation rather than expecting arrows to win every engagement alone.
- Withdraw behind friendly frontage before cavalry or serious melee contact.

### Campaign
- Develop from actual bow-qualified people, serviceable bows, strings, arrows, officers, instructors, provision, and recurring practice.
- Compete with district service, household defense, Landsmenn, local hunting and labor needs, and other military assignments.
- Remain regionally uneven and dependent on real training and supply.
- Preserve casualties, lost equipment, depleted ammunition, weakened cadres, and household costs.

### Player experience
- Provide Norway a dependable archer above broad Leidang service without creating longbowmen, Hird archers, or generic elite marksmen.
- Reward protection, fire discipline, target choice, ammunition management, and combined arms.
- Keep Leidang Bowmen relevant through broader availability and lower institutional demands.

## 25. Counterplay

- Break or bypass the protective frontage.
- Use cavalry, rapid protected advance, or concentrated counterfire.
- Apply shields, armor, obstruction, dispersal, and terrain.
- Force repeated movement and poor firing angles.
- Block withdrawal or isolate the formation.
- Exhaust arrows and damage bows, strings, officers, or supply.
- Attack the households, instructors, crafts, and service relationships that sustain replacement.
