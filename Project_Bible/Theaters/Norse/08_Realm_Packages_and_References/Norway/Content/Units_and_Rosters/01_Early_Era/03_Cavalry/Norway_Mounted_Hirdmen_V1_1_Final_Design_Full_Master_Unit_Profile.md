# PART I. PLAYER-FACING CURRENT PROFILE

## 4. Unit identity strip

- **Unit:** Mounted Hirdmen
- **Class:** selected royal-household mounted escort and crisis cavalry
- **Source:** qualified Royal Hirdmen exclusively reassigned from the finite royal household source, paired with separately owned and validated horses
- **Availability:** very scarce, royal-security-dependent, horse-dependent, politically costly, and slower to replace than Hestlid
- **Primary role:** protect royal command, escort a mounted ruler or commander, answer a critical flank threat, and preserve withdrawal
- **Best use:** mobile royal reserve, command protection, short controlled counterattack, interception of exposed attackers, and bounded pursuit
- **Major weakness:** tiny source, severe foot-guard opportunity cost, horse and tack burden, prepared Spears, counterfire, blocked withdrawal, and catastrophic political loss
- **Development:** may become more coherent through actual Hird, horse, equipment, officer, escort, and formation development without becoming Norwegian Knights by calendar
- **Opening State:** not authorized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 5. One-screen design card

### Battlefield
- Remains close enough to protect the royal commander or designated royal objective.
- Holds as a scarce mounted reserve rather than screening routinely like Hestlid.
- Counterattacks exposed threats, reinforces a collapsing royal flank, or opens a withdrawal route.
- Can pursue a broken high-value threat only while command protection remains secure.
- Does not receive automatic heavy charge, Spear immunity, morale immunity, or character invulnerability.

### Campaign
- Debits the same people used by general Royal Hirdmen, Axeguard, Bowmen, Royal Shipguard, bodyguards, instructors, court security, and recovery.
- Requires separate horses, tack, handlers, feed, route, training, remounts, and recovery.
- Weakens foot protection and household security while active.
- May be unavailable even when Hird people exist because suitable horses or protection coverage fail.

### Access
- Requires qualified unreserved Hirdmen, acceptable royal-security coverage, suitable horses, rider-horse familiarity, mounted combat practice, equipment, officers, signals, supply, route, rally, return, and recovery.
- Palace, stable, Hird title, horse resource, research, or ruler presence does not create the card.

### Development
- Begins, if admitted, as a narrow royal mounted assignment.
- May gain better coherence, horse quality, remount depth, equipment, and escort doctrine through actual development.
- Does not automatically become Royal Lancers, Skutilsveinar, Svenner, or Norwegian Knights.

## 6. Player-facing description

Mounted Hirdmen are trusted members of the Norwegian royal following assigned to mounted protection and crisis response. They escort a ruler or commander, guard movement between threatened positions, counterattack exposed enemies near the royal objective, and preserve an escape or withdrawal when the battle turns against the household.

Every mounted assignment removes Hirdmen from foot protection, Axeguard, Bowmen, Royal Shipguard, court security, instruction, or recovery. The riders also need real horses, tack, handlers, feed, routes, and mounted practice. Their strength comes from trusted command, selected personnel, and a clear protective mission, not from continental knightly institutions or automatic shock-cavalry power.

Hestlid are Norway's practical regional light horse. Mounted Hirdmen are the much scarcer royal layer. A player should recruit Hestlid for routine anti-skirmisher, flank, pursuit, and mobile-reserve work, while reserving Mounted Hirdmen for royal protection and decisive emergencies.
