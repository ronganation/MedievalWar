## 1. What the army needs, and what it does not

A field army with district spears, district bows, an axe assault choice and a scarce royal reserve may still benefit from **support infantry that can relieve a strained line, cover bowmen during a withdrawal, and handle a change of facing or frontage**. This is a gameplay hypothesis, not evidence for a particular social class. A useful middle card should be fieldable often enough to matter in more than a single scripted battle, but should not make spears, axemen, or household troops obsolete. The player should have a reason to take *some* middle foot rather than only stack spears and elites. The AI must be able to do the same.

**Crucial correction to an overly narrow admission rule:** a separate **unit card** does not require a separate **population pool**. Spear and axe cards already illustrate distinct assignments from one finite district source. The middle-field role can likewise be an accountable *selected district assignment* if its people, equipment, practice, availability and battlefield job are genuinely differentiated. It cannot also be a freely recruitable third district population. The existing Landsmenn definition's independent-source gate remains appropriate **for an independently sourced Landsmenn corps**; it should not veto the broader middle-field card experiment from existing district people. This comparison does not amend or silently override that definition: it distinguishes two design routes for later owner review.

## 2. Four routes to a middle-field experience

| Route | People and access | Distinct battle promise | What survives / what fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Experienced Leidang state** | Survivors of existing spear/axe formations; retain their original district identity | Improved order response, cohesion retention or reform through actual service, where shared battle rules support it | Best low-overhead baseline. Experience alone does not create a fresh recruitment card or an additional pool; loses the ability to recruit a *new* middle unit without first building veteran history. |
| **B. Selected district supporting foot** | Qualified, unassigned contributors drawn from the same finite district source, with equipment, officers and training actually available | Flexible relief and guarded movement, rather than spear brace or a short axe breakthrough | **Preferred provisional card experiment for playability**, subject to source debit and distinct battle behavior. Can coexist with spear and axe cards, not with simultaneous copies of the same people. Name remains descriptive pending review. |
| **C. Distinct regional service** | Independently identified community or regional obligation, not a place-name applied to the same district people | A supporting line with a service window, equipment, deployment and replacement different enough to matter | A separate Landsmenn-like card may be admitted if dated source, terminology and tactical case survive. No independent 1062 source has been established by the reviewed files. |
| **D. Noble household flexible foot** | Existing followers of a specific magnate; consent, duration and command remain with that source | Guard/line-support assignment instead of select close assault | Can be a *second tactical presentation* only by debiting the same household and distinguishing roles. If it simply duplicates Noble Drengja, use one household card with an equipment/deployment profile. |

**Not a fifth route:** taking royal hirdmen, lowering their equipment rating and calling them Landsmenn. The hird's protective obligations and political cost remain its own. Likewise, a Barracks, a new name, or a research decision cannot generate qualified people or swords.

## 3. Battle-function comparison under equal conditions

The following are **directional tests**, not awarded abilities or numeric balance. The formation standard separates eligibility, proficiency, entry, maintenance, turning and recovery; shield-wall access does not imply expertise. Compare equal equipment condition, terrain, formation size where meaningful, command, fatigue and experience first, then test actual source-specific kit and practice.

| Candidate / state | Best reason to field | Formation and weapon distinction to test | Clear counter and opportunity cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary Leidang Spearmen** | Affordable, repeatable defensive frontage and missile protection | Prepared spear line; conditional brace; shielded order only with actual kit/practice; reform may vary with cadre | Flank, missile pressure and disorder; district extraction competes with home and ship duties. |
| **Experienced Leidang** | Preserve hard-won line quality without creating new people | Faster or more dependable *specific* transitions only if actual survivor history supports them | Attrition destroys the cadre; cannot be purchased as a fresh pool. |
| **Leidang Axemen** | Non-elite supported assault after enemy contact | Axe-and-shield close order if eligible; no spear brace; test short commitment and disengagement | Fresh spear reach, mounted threat and missile exposure; competes for district contributors. |
| **Selected district middle foot, provisional** | Relieve line, plug a threatened gap, protect missile withdrawal and retain some close-fighting utility | Test passage/relief, controlled withdrawal, facing change and reform; no automatic advanced shield wall, sword issue, armor or universal morale advantage | Should lose to prepared spears in frontal reach and to dedicated assault in a favorable assault window; equipment and practice cost reduce ordinary district options. |
| **Landsmenn as independently sourced card** | Same flexible job only if a *different* source and service can be verified | Must distinguish itself from selected district foot under matched kit and experience, not simply have a higher attack value | If source/name are unverified or its role duplicates route B, keep legacy name in research and do not admit this separate card. |
| **Noble Drengja** | Politically sourced select assault reserve | Household close-order commitment after frontage is fixed; any relief duty depends on real kit and command | Negotiated access, household losses, dual-command risk, fresh spears and cavalry; not a mass middle-line substitute. |
| **Royal Hirdmen** | Concentrated royal protection and crisis reserve | Potentially better *specific* formation execution where trained/equipped; no blanket shield-wall mastery | Very scarce; commitment exposes royal protection; not the default way to plug every line gap. |

### A tangible battle choice

**Design scenario, not an historical or opening army:** a district spear line is pressured while its bowmen must reposition. Additional spears can extend or reinforce frontage but may have difficulty executing a quick passage under disorder. Axemen can attack an exposed enemy flank but are poor substitutes for a prepared anti-mounted front. A properly equipped and trained middle-foot assignment could cover the bowmen and relieve the threatened section without consuming the king's guard. Noble Drengja can instead deliver a stronger local assault if a magnate's contingent is actually present and its commander agrees. If all four solutions behave identically, the middle card has failed its test. If the middle assignment always wins, its cost or battle limits have failed.

## 4. Recruitment breadth and no-friction stress tests

**One district source, multiple valid outputs:** if the district has available eligible people, the player may choose spear, bow, provisional axe and possibly selected supporting foot, each with distinct equipment and service eligibility. Each formation's members are reserved once. Selection is not a new population, but neither does it require inventing a rare institution every time a player wants a useful mixed army. The design should show *what is currently available and why*, without a cascade of individual approvals.

**Three army contexts for owner testing:**

1. **Source-rich coastal campaign:** Can several valid districts furnish multiple spear formations, bow support, a modest axe component and, if admitted, a selected middle detachment while preserving credible home defense, crews and a limited household reserve? No exact counts are proposed.
2. **Damaged or politically divided realm:** Can the player still field a defensive army from remaining valid sources? A blocked selected card should explain whether people, kit, officers, obligation or access are missing; the AI should not be left with an empty roster because of stacked speculative gates.
3. **Noble-supported expedition:** Does a delivered household contingent create a genuine *alternative* to selected district foot, not a mandatory upgrade? Its losses and return remain with that household; the king cannot refill it through the district pool.

**Replacement:** casualties, surviving experience, returned equipment and obligations all affect recovery. Reorganizing veterans may create a different presentation from the same surviving people but does not instantly refill a formation. A selected district card should have a visible *relative* availability cost versus ordinary spears, yet remain attainable in a reasonably supported campaign; exact pool breadth, training time, upkeep and sizes require owner evidence and playtests. Population constrains service sources, not Rome-style direct manpower subtraction.

## 5. Landsmenn name and historical decision tree

- **Documented context:** `leidang` and `hird` describe broad institutions; `lendmann` is a regional aristocratic office with military support duties, not a name for a medium infantry regiment. Do not conflate `Landsmenn` with `lendmenn` because the words look similar.
- **Legacy only:** the old `Landsmenn.md` calls a generic Norse unit professional swordsmen with a sword/axe/shield kit and medium-line role. Its exact numbers, building/loyalty gates, traits and terrain bonuses are evidence of earlier game design, not present Norwegian balance or historical proof.
- **Working middle-card label:** `Selected District Foot` is a **design placeholder**, not a proposed attested medieval Norwegian title. `Landsmenn` can remain the research name while historical and language review determines whether it can be used in-game at all.
- **If an independent service source is evidenced:** compare it with selected district and household service; a distinct card can survive if it offers a meaningful additional army choice without multiplying the same people.
- **If no independent source is evidenced but route B plays well:** preserve the middle-field card under a supported, player-readable name as an explicit game adaptation drawing on accounted-for district personnel. The existing Landsmenn research file remains as provenance and is not silently reclassified as approved.
- **If route B fails but veterans play well:** keep flexible handling as an experience/reorganization profile, not a fresh recruitable card.
- **If the source is noble:** keep the magnate's people and command terms; decide whether one household card with multiple duties is clearer than two household cards.
- **If none produces a distinct player decision:** leave the separate middle-card slot empty. Do not insert an inflated sword unit to complete a visual tier ladder.

## 6. Test gates, provisional outcome and handoff

**Recommended next static disposition:** protect the *role* and authorize only a **design comparison** of selected district supporting foot, not a production card. Do not promote Landsmenn as a historical title, a third pool, or an opening regiment. Preserve the Noble Drengja candidate independently because it represents a different owner and political route. This recommendation is not owner adoption.

**Battle/AI gate:** paired tests of spear-line relief, passage through friendly lines, withdrawal under missile pressure, formation entry/maintenance/recovery, fatigue, command disruption, and counterplay against fresh spears and cavalry. Ask whether the middle card is selected for a recognizable purpose rather than a hidden +1. Test AI army composition with and without it; if the AI cannot field a mixed force because of needless gates, adjust the gates rather than fabricate people.

**Source/owner gate:** recruitment and population owners identify eligible district or independent regional people; obligation owners validate service; equipment and historical-art owners verify kit and name; household owners retain noble personnel; battle owners own formation and numeric tuning; Opening State owner alone can decide exact 1062 instances; Registry and implementation owners handle IDs and assets only after approval. No owner review, battle result, local repository inventory or Unreal test is claimed here.

**Next Early infantry comparison:** Noble Drengja versus Royal Hirdmen and Hird Axeguard, with explicit royal/noble ownership, assault/reserve roles, political access and guard-coverage tradeoffs. Thereafter compare the Early bow, scout and mounted lanes on their own terms. This is a bounded handoff, not a request to generate more paperwork for its own sake.
