## 4. Player-facing identity strip

- **Unit:** Household Axeriders
- **Class:** selected medium household melee cavalry
- **Era:** High Era I emergence candidate with High Era II persistence where institutions survive
- **Source:** accountable household warriors and lesser retainers with real horses, equipment, patronage, service, and mounted-melee training
- **Primary weapon:** one-handed axe
- **Shield:** chronology-compatible mounted shield where approved
- **Best use:** counter light cavalry, exploit disordered infantry, attack exposed missiles, and hold a mobile household flank reserve
- **Main weakness:** prepared Spears, mature heavy cavalry, concentrated missiles, bad ground, exhausted horses, and prolonged combat against heavy foot
- **Availability:** narrow and household-dependent
- **Opening State:** unavailable in January 1062
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 5. One-screen Modern Medieval II contract

### Battlefield
- Hold near household command or an army flank behind lighter screens.
- Engage light cavalry, exposed missiles, or troops already disrupted by infantry and missile pressure.
- Use axe, shield, and controlled mounted melee rather than relying on a single shock charge.
- Press briefly, break contact, reform, and pursue only after the enemy breaks.
- Refuse prepared Spears, superior heavy horse, and prolonged unsupported melee.

### Campaign
- Develop only from actual intermediate household people, horses, equipment, patronage, instructors, and service.
- Compete with Landsmenn-like foot service, Svenner, household defense, messenger duty, horse care, and future Riddarar candidacy.
- Remain unevenly distributed between households and regions.
- Preserve casualties, lost horses, broken tack, diminished cadres, and household-defense costs.

### Player experience
- Provide Norway a recognizably Norse-rooted mounted-melee choice without turning the faction into a cavalry power.
- Reward combined arms, target judgment, and finite recruitment-pool planning.
- Present one clear card rather than several thin ownership or armor variants.

## 32. Signature battle cycle

```text
Receive household flank-reserve or mounted-melee mission
-> identify target support prohibited frontage rally and breakaway route
-> use lighter mounted screens or infantry contact to expose the target
-> approach exposed missiles light cavalry or already-disordered infantry
-> make a limited mounted commitment
-> use axe and shield in brief mounted melee
-> press only while local advantage and exit remain credible
-> break contact before Spears heavy cavalry or fresh reserves fix the formation
-> reform behind supporting infantry or household command
-> pursue only after the enemy breaks and recall remains reliable
```

## 36. Counterplay

- Protect missile troops with Spears, terrain, or obstacles.
- Use disciplined medium or heavy cavalry.
- Disrupt the approach with missiles and force poor alignment.
- Deny safe breakaway, rally, and pursuit-recall routes.
- Force contact in narrow, muddy, marshy, wooded, steep, or urban ground.
- Threaten the owning household, horse, feed, tack, instructor, and replacement sources.
- Bait an overlong pursuit or prolonged melee.

## 22. Signature battle cycle

```text
Hold near the household or army flank
-> allow screens and command to identify a suitable target
-> approach exposed missiles light cavalry or already-disordered infantry
-> make a limited mounted commitment
-> use axe and shield in brief mounted melee
-> press the local advantage without entering a prolonged grind
-> disengage before Spears heavy cavalry or fresh reserves arrive
-> reform behind supporting infantry or household command
-> pursue only after the enemy breaks and recall remains credible
```

## 24. Counterplay

- protect missile troops with Spears, terrain, or obstacles;
- use disciplined medium or heavy cavalry;
- force contact in narrow, muddy, marshy, wooded, steep, or urban ground;
- disrupt the approach with missiles;
- deny safe breakaway and rally routes;
- threaten the riders' household, horse, feed, tack, and replacement sources;
- bait an overlong pursuit.
