## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Springald Longship is Norway's fourth Early naval unit: a standard longship-derived specialist carrying one compact mounted mechanical bolt weapon. It delivers occasional high-value anti-personnel shots against exposed crews, boarding concentrations, and light shipboard fittings while relying on friendly longships for protection.**

Its signature is **Angle, Brace, Loose, Screen, Break**.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Springald Longship
- **Support name:** Norwegian Light Mechanical-Missile Longship
- **Era:** Later Early, persistent into High
- **Class:** Light mechanical-missile warship
- **Signature:** Angle, Brace, Loose, Screen, Break
- **Best use:** Deliver occasional heavy bolts against exposed crews or dense boarding preparations, then relocate behind standard melee ships
- **Battle strengths:** Higher local impact than ordinary arrows, useful anti-personnel disruption, longship maneuver, and strong combined-arms value
- **Battle weaknesses:** Very low rate of fire, narrow arc, low ammunition, reduced boarding crew, vulnerable operators, and poor performance if pinned alongside
- **Campaign strengths:** Gives Early Norway a practical pre-gunpowder specialist and creates a clear progression into the Springald Great Longship
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Competes for longship hulls, craftsmen, operators, bolts, port work, and experienced crews
- **Player instruction:** Find a firing angle, protect the operators, spend bolts on worthwhile targets, and break contact before melee ships close.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Angle, Brace, Loose, Screen, Break

### 8.1 Angle

Use the longship's oars, sail, and formation to create a clear line past friendly hulls and rigging.

### 8.2 Brace

Steady the vessel, protect the operator team, finish spanning and loading, and hold the weapon on a feasible target.

### 8.3 Loose

Discharge one heavy bolt at an exposed concentration, boarding preparation, or visible light fitting.

### 8.4 Screen

Return behind friendly Leidang or Drengir Longships while the weapon crew reloads and the standard crew restores full maneuver.

### 8.5 Break

Disengage when enemy melee ships close, ammunition becomes too valuable to waste, or the mount, crew, steering, or escape route becomes unsafe.
