## 1. Direct Unit Verdict

**The Armed Knarr is Norway's long-endurance armed sailing auxiliary: a merchant-derived vessel configured to guard convoys, support major expeditions, sustain fleets, apply defensive missile pressure, and survive dangerous passages without pretending to be a longship.**

Its signature is **Carry, Watch, Screen, Endure, Withdraw**.

The unit is not routine aggregate army transport. The player recruits an Armed Knarr only when a merchant-derived hull is deliberately committed, armed, and crewed for fleet service.

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Armed Knarr
- **Support name:** Norwegian Armed Merchant Ship
- **Era:** Early conditional, maturing through High
- **Class:** Armed sailing auxiliary
- **Signature:** Carry, Watch, Screen, Endure, Withdraw
- **Best use:** Convoy guard, fleet sustainment, expedition support, defensive missile pressure, dangerous trade-route service, and protected long-distance operations
- **Battle strengths:** Better endurance and useful capacity, steadier sail-driven passage, defensive missile complement, stronger staying power than unarmed shipping, and real strategic value to the fleet
- **Battle weaknesses:** Poor oared maneuver, weak interception, vulnerable to purposeful boarding warships, limited close-action reserve, and high loss consequences when carrying stores
- **Campaign strengths:** Extends fleet endurance and supports operations beyond nearby ports
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Merchant and trade opportunity cost, slower tactical reaction, dependence on escort under major threat, and limited availability from suitable hulls and contracted crews
- **Player instruction:** Keep Armed Knarrs protected inside the formation. Use longships to intercept and board; use Armed Knarrs to sustain and preserve the expedition.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Hold, Pressure, Screen, Endure, Escape

### 8.1 Hold

Maintain a protected position within the fleet rather than seeking isolated contact.

### 8.2 Pressure

Use a modest defensive missile complement to discourage light attackers and support nearby warships.

### 8.3 Screen

Work with escorting longships, maintain signals, and keep the fleet's support element from being isolated.

### 8.4 Endure

Absorb ordinary passage, missile pressure, and limited damage while preserving stores, steering, sail, and crew order.

### 8.5 Escape

Withdraw behind fighting ships, seek refuge, jettison or abandon noncritical stores if authorized, and preserve the operation.
