**The Crossbowmen's Longship is Norway's High-era penetrating-missile warship: a longship configured with trained crossbowmen and reload support to pressure protected personnel, contest enemy missile vessels, cover boarding actions, and hold deliberate firing positions within a mixed fleet.**

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Crossbowmen's Longship
- **Support name:** Norwegian High-Era Missile Longship
- **Era:** High, capability-conditioned
- **Class:** Penetrating missile and support longship
- **Signature:** Position, Brace, Pierce, Cover, Withdraw
- **Best use:** Counter enemy missile crews, disrupt boarding preparations, pressure protected personnel, and cover friendly Drengir or Great Longships during approach
- **Battle strengths:** Better penetration and deliberate target pressure than bows, stronger counter-missile presence, and valuable combined-arms support
- **Battle weaknesses:** Slower cadence, greater reload and space burden, reduced performance during hard maneuver, finite bolts, weak sustained boarding, and high equipment cost
- **Campaign strengths:** Adds a High-era missile progression without introducing gunpowder
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Competes with land crossbow units, skilled craftsmen, weapon stocks, trained crews, and developed ports
- **Player instruction:** Establish a supported firing position, choose exposed high-value targets, and withdraw before dedicated boarding vessels close.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Position, Brace, Pierce, Cover, Withdraw

### 8.1 Position

Take a protected flank, center-rear, or crossing-fire position with a clear arc and friendly melee protection.

### 8.2 Brace

Stabilize crew order, manage ship motion, prepare crossbows, and preserve reload space.

### 8.3 Pierce

Apply deliberate fire against exposed protected personnel, missile crews, boarding preparations, and high-value targets where angle and visibility permit.

### 8.4 Cover

Support friendly boarding, counter enemy missile pressure, and protect withdrawal or landing approaches.

### 8.5 Withdraw

Break formation before enemy boarding contact, ammunition exhaustion, or maneuver loss turns the specialist advantage into a liability.

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - EstablishSupportedFiringPosition
```
