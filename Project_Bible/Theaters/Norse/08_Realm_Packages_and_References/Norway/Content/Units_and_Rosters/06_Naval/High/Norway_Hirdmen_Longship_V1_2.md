## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Hirdmen's Longship
- **Support name:** Norwegian Household Guard Longship
- **Era:** High, capability-conditioned
- **Class:** Elite escort and counterboarding longship
- **Signature:** Guard, Intercept, Hold, Counterboard, Extract
- **Best use:** Protect commanders, Great Longships, embarked rulers or high-value characters, vulnerable missile ships, and critical expeditionary objectives
- **Battle strengths:** Elite counterboarding, disciplined escort behavior, strong local command continuity, rapid threat response, reliable fighting withdrawal, and high morale under a clear protective mission
- **Battle weaknesses:** Extremely scarce personnel, high political and household opportunity cost, limited patrol efficiency, no heavy-hull advantage, and reduced value when no meaningful objective requires protection
- **Campaign strengths:** Gives important fleets a defensive elite rather than another generic attack ship
- **Campaign weaknesses:** Competes directly with household troops, guards, retainers, and trusted land commanders
- **Player instruction:** Keep the unit attached to something worth protecting. Intercept threats, counterboard attackers, and extract the protected objective rather than chasing prizes.

## 9. Core Battle Loop: Guard, Intercept, Hold, Counterboard, Extract

### 9.1 Guard

Maintain a connected position near the protected ship, commander, transport group, or fleet center.

### 9.2 Intercept

Move into the path of a boarding threat, pursuit, or local breakthrough without abandoning the wider withdrawal route.

### 9.3 Hold

Absorb the initial close-action pressure long enough for the protected objective to maneuver, regroup, or escape.

### 9.4 Counterboard

Commit the household fighting complement against attackers already fixed in contact or attempting to cross the gunwale.

### 9.5 Extract

Break contact, recover the protected objective, cover withdrawal, or remain behind as a disciplined rearguard when necessary.

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - MaintainProtectedObjectiveConnection
  - InterceptLocalBoardingThreat
  - HoldContactWithoutOverpursuit
  - CounterboardAndRestoreSeparation
  - ExtractCoverWithdrawalOrRegroup
success_condition: PreserveHighValueObjectiveAndFleetContinuityWithoutWastingScarceGuardPersonnel
failure_condition: ChasingIsolationSeveralDirectionPressureCommandLossMissileAttritionOrProtectedObjectiveSeparation
```
