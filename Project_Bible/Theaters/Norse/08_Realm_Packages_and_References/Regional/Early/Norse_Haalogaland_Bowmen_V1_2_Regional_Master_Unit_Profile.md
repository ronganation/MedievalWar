# PART I. CURRENT HUMAN-READABLE PROFILE

## 3. Unit identity strip

- **Unit:** Haalogaland Bowmen
- **Realm:** Norway
- **Class:** Northern regional bow infantry
- **Quality:** Regionally variable; not automatically elite or inferior
- **Source:** Finite, owner-validated northern community bow contribution
- **Availability:** Geographically restricted, source-bound, seasonal, equipment-dependent, ammunition-dependent, route-sensitive, and affected by local defense and other northern duties
- **Primary role:** Protected regional missile support
- **Best use:** Defend northern approaches, settlements, landings, roads, and withdrawal routes; support regional spear frontage; contribute to distant armies when routes, lift, supply, and home defense permit
- **Major weakness:** Light and variable protection, cavalry and melee exposure, ammunition dependence, long-distance replacement burden, and loss of northern local defense when committed elsewhere
- **Development:** May exist narrowly in the Early Era where an actual source is validated, or emerge more fully in a High Era through real regional organization, equipment, command, and supply development
- **Opening State:** Candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** None promoted

## 4. One-screen player card

- **Class:** Northern Norwegian regional bowmen
- **Source:** Bow-qualified, unreserved personnel from validated Haalogaland or other specifically approved northern communities
- **Availability:** Regional and finite; depends on people, service, bows, strings, arrows, leaders, assembly, route, supply, and retained home defense
- **Battle promise:** Useful protected missile support that gives northern Norway a distinct military contribution without creating a northern super-archer stereotype
- **Best use:** Defended northern approaches, protected firing behind Spearmen, local coastal or settlement defense, pressure against exposed troops, and carefully prepared distant service
- **Worst use:** Unsupported forward deployment, cavalry contact, prolonged melee, superior missile duels, exhausted ammunition, or distant use without return and replacement support
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Sending the formation away reduces northern missile defense, skilled people, equipment, local leadership, crew or transport options, and regional recovery
- **Counterplay:** Cavalry, counterfire, shields, armor, forced movement, obstruction, ammunition pressure, route disruption, blocked withdrawal, and threats against the source community
- **Special boundary:** Haalogaland identity changes source, access, geography, recovery, and campaign obligations, not range, accuracy, winter endurance, forest stealth, or armor penetration by itself

## 5. Player-facing description

Haalogaland Bowmen are a regional northern contribution raised from communities that can actually provide qualified people, maintained bows, strings, arrows, leaders, and accepted service. They defend local approaches and settlements or join a wider Norwegian host when routes, transport, supply, command, and the security of their home communities permit.

Their value comes from regional access and strategic choice rather than a hidden northern combat advantage. Well-supported Haalogaland Bowmen can perform the same core protected missile work as other regional Bowmen, but long-distance service is more difficult to assemble, replace, and recover. The unit may appear narrowly in an Early Era campaign or emerge more fully during a later era if the necessary source and institutions develop.

## 25. Battlefield rhythm

1. Deploy behind or beside a protected regional spear or defensive frontage.
2. Preserve clear fire lanes and a withdrawal route.
3. Hold fire when target value, range, obstruction, or ammunition is poor.
4. Pressure exposed light troops, advancing enemies, or disrupted formations.
5. Reposition before cavalry or heavy infantry closes.
6. Withdraw behind Spearmen or toward a validated settlement, landing, camp, or rally point.
7. Preserve remaining arrows and surviving qualified people.
