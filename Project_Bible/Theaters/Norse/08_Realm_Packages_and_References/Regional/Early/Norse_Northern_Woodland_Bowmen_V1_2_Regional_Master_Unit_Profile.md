## 2. Why This Is Not Generic “Norse Hunters”

A hunter label alone is insufficient. Hunting skill does not automatically create formation discipline, military target selection, volley control, friendly-fire judgment, ammunition organization, command, or willingness to serve abroad.

```text
Hunter != military archer automatically
Woodland resident != bow-qualified fighter
Bow owner != serviceable military bow formation
Local knowledge != invisibility
Regional familiarity != global terrain bonus
Mercenary payment != people, bows, arrows, leaders, or consent
```

“Mercenary Hunters” remains a readable player-support name, but the controlling design name is Northern Woodland Bowmen because the card requires an organized regional bow source rather than a profession stereotype.

## 4. Player-Facing Card

### Short description

Light woodland bowmen who screen flanks, pressure exposed troops, and relocate through suitable ground before heavier enemies can close.

### Encyclopedia description

Northern Woodland Bowmen are lightly equipped archers drawn from exact regional sources that can provide qualified people, maintained bows, strings, arrows, leaders, and disciplined external service. They operate in dispersed or extended files, using available cover and broken ground to screen an army, contest enemy skirmishers, pressure exposed troops, and protect a withdrawal.

They are not elite forest archers. Their arrows remain finite, concealment depends on actual terrain and detection, and their light protection leaves them vulnerable to cavalry, superior missile troops, and sustained melee. When hired outside their home source, distance, route, ammunition, return, and replacement become serious constraints.

### Strengths

- flexible dispersed bow screening;
- selective pressure against exposed light troops and skirmishers;
- useful at woodland margins, hedges, broken approaches, and settlement edges;
- credible withdrawal and flank-screen support;
- lower equipment burden than crossbow specialists;
- regional and mercenary access can create campaign variety without copying Realm pools.

### Weaknesses

- light protection and poor sustained melee;
- finite arrows and maintenance-dependent bows;
- vulnerable to cavalry in open ground;
- vulnerable to superior ranged formations;
- concealment and movement depend on real terrain and command;
- difficult long-distance replacement;
- local service, hunting, warning, guiding, labor, and defense compete for the same people.

### Player guidance

Place them where cover and a retreat lane exist. Use selective fire against exposed or advancing light troops, screen a flank, and relocate before cavalry or heavier missiles establish the engagement. Do not trade volleys indefinitely or treat concealment as invulnerability.

## 18. Target Contract, Counterplay, and Failure Modes

**Preferred targets:** exposed skirmishers, lightly protected infantry, advancing troops without strong missile cover, disrupted formations, vulnerable flanks, crews and support personnel where identified, and enemies pursuing through poor order.

**Poor targets:** heavily protected frontage, protected crossbowmen, cavalry already closing, concealed targets without identification, and enemies behind dense friendly obstruction.
