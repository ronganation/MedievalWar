- **Major weakness:** cavalry in open ground, longer-ranged missiles, dense shielded infantry, ammunition exhaustion, poor passage lanes, and sustained melee

## 5. One-screen design card

### Battlefield
- Deploys ahead of or beside the main body when cover, information, and withdrawal permit.
- Uses a limited javelin supply against exposed, advancing, disordered, or poorly protected targets.
- Screens Norwegian Spearmen, Bowmen, Fjordmen, and selected infantry during approach and contact.
- Contests woodland edges, hedges, settlement approaches, riverbanks, hills, rough ground, and ambush positions.
- Withdraws through friendly gaps, terrain, or prepared lanes before cavalry or heavy infantry establishes contact.
- Can enter emergency melee against broken or isolated light troops but should not hold conventional frontage.

### Campaign
- Draws from specific source owners rather than a kingdom-wide forest pool.
- Competes with guides, warning, hunting and food support, route work, local defense, household labor, transport, and other military obligations.
- Requires javelin production, inspection, issue, carriage, recovery, repair, and replacement.
- Gains value from valid local guidance and source familiarity without receiving perfect intelligence or free movement.
- Casualties damage the same communities, knowledge holders, leaders, and equipment sources supporting later recruitment.

### Access
- Requires a verified owner or community, valid service relationship, qualified light-foot personnel, javelins, shields or approved protection, leaders, signals, training, supply, route, rally, return, and recovery.
- A forest tile, hunting lodge, building, research decision, outlaw label, or population total cannot create the formation.
- Exact January 1062 availability belongs to Opening State and source authorities.

### Development
- Improves through veteran instruction, javelin supply, shield consistency, footwear, signal discipline, withdrawal practice, combined-arms familiarity, and regional organization.
- May produce veteran Skogmenn without becoming elite universal skirmishers.
- Does not automatically convert into Birkebeinar or later professional light infantry.

## 6. Player-facing description

Skogmenn are lightly equipped regional Norse foot trained or experienced in javelin use, loose movement, screening, and short violent engagements across woodland and broken ground. They move ahead of or beside heavier formations, contest covered approaches, harass exposed troops, pressure flanks, and withdraw before stronger enemies can fix them in sustained combat.

The name is a readable Norwegian-flavored gameplay label, not a claim that a standardized regiment called Skogmenn existed in 1062. The related Old Norse term `skogarmadr` refers to an outlaw, but outlawry does not define the card. Eligible woodland households, hunters, guides, local defenders, retainers, and regional light-service contributors may provide ordinary personnel. Pardoned or contracted outlaws remain a conditional political source with legal, reliability, compensation, and demobilization consequences.

Javelins are scarce compared with arrows. Once the missiles are expended, lost, or damaged, Skogmenn remain a living light formation capable of screening, warning, local pursuit, withdrawal support, or emergency contact, but their primary harassment role collapses. Their success depends on timing, distance, cover, target exposure, cohesion, command, friendly passage, and a real escape route.

## 5. Player-facing description

Skogmenn are light fighting men assembled from woodland and broken-ground communities or other validated local sources. Carrying limited javelins, small shields where available, and light sidearms, they screen heavier infantry, disrupt exposed troops, pressure flanks, and support ambushes or withdrawals.

The name is a readable Norwegian-flavored gameplay label rather than a claim that a standardized regiment existed in 1062. The related Old Norse term `skogarmadr` refers to an outlaw, but outlawry does not define the whole unit. Pardoned or contracted outlaws may form one conditional source alongside eligible hunters, guides, woodland households, and regional light-service contributors.

Skogmenn are not forest super-soldiers. Cavalry, counterfire, heavy infantry, open terrain, fatigue, command failure, and blocked retreat can destroy them quickly.
