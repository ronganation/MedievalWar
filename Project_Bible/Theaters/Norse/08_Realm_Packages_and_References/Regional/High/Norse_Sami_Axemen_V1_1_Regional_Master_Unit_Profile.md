The V1.0 draft established the correct source ownership, finite recruitment, relationship access, and cultural safeguards. The double check found one substantive design risk: the battlefield loop leaned too far toward approach, strike, and immediate withdrawal. That risked making Sámi Axemen feel like axe-armed Skogmenn rather than credible regional melee infantry.

V1.1 corrects this without turning the unit into generic heavy axemen. The refined formation is **durable medium axe infantry in favorable terrain**, capable of winning a real local melee after a controlled approach. Withdrawal remains an important survival option, but it is no longer the only successful outcome.

**Sámi Axemen are practiced regional medium infantry who use broken ground, controlled approach, mixed shields, and locally concentrated axes to enter close combat on favorable terms. Once committed, they can defeat ordinary infantry, punish armored or shielded opponents through repeated hewing pressure, and secure a limited objective. They must disengage when fresh heavy infantry, cavalry, missiles, or deep reserves turn the fight against them.**

This is a proper melee unit. The formation does not depend on magical concealment, ethnic bonuses, or constant charge cycling.

## PART I. REFINED PLAYER-FACING DESIGN

### 3. Player Identity Strip

- **Name:** Sámi Axemen
- **Class:** Practiced regional medium axe infantry
- **Source:** A finite Sámi regional source represented through relationship access at the northern edge of the playable Norse theater
- **Primary access:** Norway
- **Secondary access:** Denmark under a valid Norway-mediated, union, alliance, joint-expedition, or separately established relationship
- **Primary role:** Win bounded infantry fights in broken ground
- **Battlefield method:** Approach under screening, close with a shield-capable body, concentrate axe pressure, secure a small objective, then reform or withdraw
- **Best targets:** Ordinary infantry, disordered shielded foot, exposed flanks, troops compressed by routes or terrain, and missile infantry reached under cover
- **Bad targets:** Fresh pikes, deep elite heavy infantry, cavalry in open ground, prepared crossbows, and any fight with no exit or supporting formation
- **Campaign price:** Slow finite replenishment, political relationship dependence, and real opportunity cost to the source community

### 5. Refined Battlefield Role

V1.1 deliberately moves the unit away from a fragile hit-and-run reading. Sámi Axemen can remain in melee long enough to defeat ordinary regional infantry or create a local breakthrough. Their success comes from favorable approach, axe concentration, file leadership, and maintaining a shield-capable body around the assault files.

They are still not heavy line infantry. Against fresh household troops, dense pikes, or a broad open frontage, their mixed protection and finite stamina become decisive weaknesses. The player should seek a bounded fight, not avoid fighting altogether.

## PART IV. REFINED BATTLE SYSTEM

### 17. Core Battle Cycle

```text
Scout the ground and identify a bounded target
-> approach through terrain, screening, or friendly pressure
-> close with shield-capable files and preserve local cohesion
-> commit axe files into one reachable sector
-> sustain the melee long enough to break, displace, or materially damage that sector
-> secure the immediate ground or cover the friendly purpose
-> assess enemy reserves, cavalry, missiles, fatigue, leaders, and withdrawal
-> reform in place if the fight remains favorable
-> withdraw by files if fresh depth or open-ground danger makes continued contact unsound
```

The Medieval II: Kingdoms reference supplies a recognizable legacy concept: axe-focused Sámi infantry associated with service in Norwegian armies and presented as mobile, hardy woodland-capable troops. Medieval War retains the useful strategic and battlefield idea while rejecting copied statistics, automatic ethnic combat qualities, universal Norwegian equipment, and unrestricted Denmark or Norway ownership.

This profile treats the unit as a source-owned regional formation. Norway and, under narrower conditions, Denmark may gain access through valid relationships. Neither realm creates, owns, or duplicates the underlying Sámi population, equipment, leaders, or replacement pool.

**Sámi Axemen are mobile regional axe infantry drawn from independently owned northern communities and service networks. They approach through broken or screened ground, attack a flank or already-disordered infantry body, use axes to create severe local pressure, and withdraw before heavier formations fix them in prolonged contact.**

They are not berserkers, household guards, generic Viking axemen, wilderness super-soldiers, or a disguised Norwegian faction unit.

### 5. Player-Facing Description

Sámi Axemen are selected fighters serving through northern regional relationships rather than ordinary Norwegian or Danish recruitment. Axes give the formation strong close-range pressure, especially when striking a flank, a compressed body, or infantry already disrupted by terrain, missiles, or another friendly unit. Mixed shields, practical sidearms, and varied protection allow useful battlefield flexibility without turning the formation into heavy line infantry.

The unit performs best when an employer respects its mission. Sámi Axemen should approach through covered or broken ground, commit briefly, and preserve a withdrawal route. Sending them directly across open ground into fresh heavy infantry wastes the unit and damages the relationship and finite source that produced it.

- scout and deny covered approaches;

- force the formation into a broad open advance;
- extend the melee until axe stamina and local cohesion decline;
- target leaders and rally points;
- block routes and compel surrender or dispersal.
