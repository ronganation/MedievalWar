# PART I: OPENING VERDICT AND CHRONOLOGY

## 1. Concise Verdict

Use Stenkil as the reigning King of Sweden on 1 January 1062, but do not represent Sweden as a mature centralized territorial state or Stenkil as the direct owner of every region, assembly, household, church, route, property, settlement, army, or ship later associated with Sweden.

The opening Realm is a negotiated and uneven royal sphere connecting western and eastern regional networks through personal recognition, assemblies, households, local law and custom, Christian institutions, inherited royal memory, inland waterways, Baltic routes, contribution, hospitality, communication, and selective enforcement.

### Production-safe opening conclusions

- Stenkil is the living adult sovereign.
- Emund the Old is the previous ruler and a Historical Legacy authority.
- House Stenkil is the new ruling dynasty.
- The Royal Household of Stenkil is a separate, potentially mobile organization.
- A politically relevant connection exists between Stenkil and Emund's royal line, but the exact blood, step, or marital basis remains unresolved.
- Halsten and Inge are probable sons and conditional dynasts with independent age, lifecycle, household, residence, formation, readiness, and agency gates.
- Christianization is uneven and regionally varied.
- Skara is an active-bounded western ecclesiastical surface.
- Sigtuna is a royal, settlement, exchange, literacy, minting, and developing episcopal surface whose functions remain separately owned.
- Uppsala is a major religious, assembly, elite, and memory research surface, not a complete map specification or national religious government.
- Sweden includes distinct regional, assembly, church, Baltic, Norwegian, Danish, Gotlandic, island, Finnic, Sámi, and eastern-facing interfaces.
- No opening Sweden-Norway War is approved.

### Unsafe opening conclusions

Do not establish:

- a spouse canonically named “Ingamoder”;
- exact Stenkil parentage beyond bounded review;
- a Ragnvald the Old and Ragnvald Ulfsson identity merger;
- legendary ancestry;
- exact birthdays for Halsten or Inge;
- a fixed heir or primogeniture rule;
- a fixed national capital;
- one national assembly, law, church, culture, faith state, levy, army, fleet, economy, or route network;
- direct royal ownership of Gotland, Åland, Finland-facing spaces, or eastern routes;
- the post-Stenkil Eric conflict;
- Håkan the Red, Blot-Sweyn, or Anund Gårdske as opening rulers;
- Inge's later policy, exile, return, relationships, or victories;
- Stenkil's later death as a scheduled certainty;
- a fixed later succession or civil-war sequence.

## 2. Historical Opening Contract

### 2.1 Effective date

The authoritative scenario date is `1062-01-01`.

### 2.2 Temporal tracks

- Historical progression advances by 21 months per strategic turn through the Temporal owner.
- Biological progression advances by 7 months per strategic turn through the Biological owner.
- Campaign Season advances independently through one ordered Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn step per strategic turn.

Historical uncertainty is not resolved by choosing whichever track produces a convenient date. Exact, bounded, relative, seasonal, approximate, scenario-selected, emergent, and quarantined chronology remain separate classes.

### 2.3 Apply-once rule

Opening State may apply approved January 1062 facts once. It cannot:

- reapply after campaign progression;
- overwrite deaths, births, marriages, succession, titles, offices, wars, routes, properties, relationships, institutions, religious outcomes, or regional state in an advanced save;
- create a ReviewOnly or quarantined identity;
- restore Stenkil after death;
- remove a runtime ruler;
- schedule later history merely because a historical date approaches.

## 3. Chronology Doctrine

- **Exact:** supported exact date.
- **Bounded:** earliest and latest acceptable range.
- **Relative:** before or after another event.
- **Seasonal:** linked to sailing, assembly, harvest, campaign, climate, or liturgical conditions.
- **Approximate:** year or multi-year range.
- **Scenario-selected:** a value chosen from an approved range and serialized explicitly.
- **Emergent:** caused only by runtime conditions and owner-approved action.
- **ExcludedLaterOutcome:** historically later and prohibited from opening initialization.
- **Quarantined:** too weak, contradictory, conflated, or unsafe for authoritative scheduling.

An uncertain date must never become a fabricated birthday, marriage day, accession day, battle day, death day, church foundation day, or route opening.
