## 1. Concise Verdict

Adalvard the Elder should be represented as a major ecclesiastical historical figure whose strongest safe associations are Bremen clerical formation, Skara-facing episcopal service, missionary activity toward Värmland, and a material legacy connected with a grave chalice. His exact opening lifecycle and office state remain unresolved because the discovery sources conflict on his death chronology.

His profile must distinguish practical ministry, formally claimed office, local reception, Bremen authorization, Skara institutional continuity, physical buildings, and later material memory.

## 2. Core Character Thesis

**A Bremen-formed cleric seeking to turn contested external accreditation into durable local ecclesiastical presence through pastoral work, institutional organization, mission travel, and material continuity, while operating in a field already shaped by Osmund and uncertain Swedish recognition.**

His central tension is formal appointment versus practical possession.
