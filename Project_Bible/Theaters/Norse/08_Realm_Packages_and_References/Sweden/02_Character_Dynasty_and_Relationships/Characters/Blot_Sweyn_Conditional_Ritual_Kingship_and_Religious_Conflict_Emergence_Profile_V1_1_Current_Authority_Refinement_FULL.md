## 1. Concise Verdict

Blot-Sweyn should exist as a high-uncertainty later emergence candidate, not as a predetermined non-Christian reaction king. The strongest safe baseline is that later traditions describe a Sweyn who replaced Inge after a dispute concerning ruler participation in sacrifice. His historicity, name, kinship, chronology, territorial reach, ritual acts, and death remain source-dependent.

The design must allow religious practice to become politically consequential without scripting a binary Christian-versus-pagan civil war.

## 2. Core Character Thesis

**A disputed ruler candidate whose possible authority depends on whether an assembly, communities, and regional power-holders make ritual participation a condition of kingship, while his personal identity and the later narrative of religious reaction remain unresolved.**

His distinct strategic problem is whether public office can survive when the ruler's ritual obligations, personal commitments, institutional alliances, and regional recognition diverge.
