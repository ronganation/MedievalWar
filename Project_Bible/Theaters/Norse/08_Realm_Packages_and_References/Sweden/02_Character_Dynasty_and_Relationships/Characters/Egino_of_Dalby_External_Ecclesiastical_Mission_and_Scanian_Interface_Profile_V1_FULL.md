## 1. Concise Verdict

Egino should remain an externally owned Scanian and Danish ecclesiastical actor with bounded Sweden-facing relevance through Blekinge, missionary networks, Bremen authority, and possible interaction with other mission figures. His profile must distinguish person, Dalby office, Lund transition, church construction, missionary travel, conversion claims, Rome journey, and Sweden-facing knowledge.
