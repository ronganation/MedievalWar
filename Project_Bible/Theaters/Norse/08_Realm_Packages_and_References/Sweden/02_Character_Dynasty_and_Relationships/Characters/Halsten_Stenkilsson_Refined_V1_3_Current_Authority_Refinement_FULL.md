## 1. Concise Verdict

Halsten is a real dynastic possibility whose opening value lies in development rather than possession of power. He is not safely reducible to a child, youth, adult prince, older son, warrior, co-ruler, or later king because the evidence and current owner state do not establish those things for 1 January 1062.

His profile therefore turns uncertainty into gameplay. The strategic problem is how House Stenkil protects, forms, introduces, and eventually tests a possible continuity figure without spending his safety, forcing a premature factional identity, copying Stenkil's path, or treating later kingship as destiny.
