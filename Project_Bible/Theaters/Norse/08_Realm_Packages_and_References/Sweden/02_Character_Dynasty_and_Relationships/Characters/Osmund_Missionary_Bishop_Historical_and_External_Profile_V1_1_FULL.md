## 50. Expanded Player-Facing Decision Portfolio

### Defend practical mission continuity

Preserve worship, clergy, access, and communities even when formal status is disputed. Benefit: functioning local church life. Risk: deeper conflict with external authority.

### Seek formal recognition

Pursue accreditation, reconciliation, or accepted oversight. Benefit: canonical credibility and wider communication. Risk: loss of autonomy, delay, or rejection.

### Accept bounded royal protection

Use secular support for safety and resources. Benefit: capacity and public standing. Risk: political capture and vulnerability after regime change.

### Present the claim before an assembly

Seek public recognition or settlement. Benefit: visibility and local legitimacy. Risk: defeat, polarization, or only temporary acceptance.

### Withdraw to preserve the mission

Reduce confrontation and protect people, records, and continuity. Benefit: survival. Risk: loss of office, reputation, or reach.

### Preserve competing evidence

Retain documents and testimony rather than forcing one narrative. Benefit: future review and credibility. Risk: immediate political dissatisfaction.

### Seek external refuge or service

Only when lifecycle, route, reception, and external owners permit. Benefit: safety and continuity. Risk: permanent loss of Sweden-facing influence.
