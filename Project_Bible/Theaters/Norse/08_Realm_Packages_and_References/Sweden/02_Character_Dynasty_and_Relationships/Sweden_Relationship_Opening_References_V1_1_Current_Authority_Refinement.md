# PART I: VERDICT, PURPOSE, AND OWNERSHIP

## 1. Concise Verdict

Approve a deliberately bounded January 1062 relationship-reference set centered on the independently directed family and dynasty interfaces among Stenkil, Halsten, and Inge, together with Historical Legacy references to Emund the Old and carefully gated institutional and external interfaces.

# PART II: OPENING CLASSIFICATION CONTRACT

## 6. Classification Vocabulary

- **OpeningEligible:** may be instantiated only after all owner and participant gates pass.
- **ConditionalOpening:** may be instantiated only after unresolved lifecycle, parentage, household, location, knowledge, or contact gates pass.
- **HistoricalOnly:** preserved through Historical Fact, Memory, genealogy, or transition context, never as a current living relationship.
- **ReviewOnly:** plausible but non-instantiating.
- **ExternalOwnerRequired:** Sweden may hold only its permitted direction after external identity and contact resolution.
- **IntentionalEmpty:** the relationship category is deliberately absent at opening.
- **Unknown:** the value or existence is not safely determined.
- **ExcludedLaterOutcome:** belongs to later history and cannot enter opening state.
- **QuarantinedContradiction:** unsafe, conflated, invented, or prohibited.

## 7. Required Opening Reference Fields

The controlling shared Relationship owner remains authoritative. Sweden defines eligible directed content and opening references only. No new Relationship owner is created locally.

Every reference requires:

- reference ID;
- source participant;
- target participant or subject;
- direction;
- relationship basis;
- classification;
- evidence disposition;
- lifecycle requirements;
- effective chronology;
- contact or knowledge requirements where applicable;
- owner;
- opening activation rule;
- visibility rule;
- save and migration behavior;
- validation state.

No omitted field receives a synthetic default.
