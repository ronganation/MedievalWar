## 2. Concise Verdict

Sweden is substantially more developed at the general Realm-authority level than Denmark was at the beginning of Denmark's Technology entrance review. Sweden's foundation review explicitly closes the current foundation-authoring sequence and reports substantive authority coverage across governance and law, regional communities and assemblies, institutions and church, geography, routes, settlements, military, economy and supply, historical systems, Realm AI, presentation, Opening State planning, Registry-owner preflight, audit, file resolution, and promotion planning.

The current package therefore does not require a broad Realm reconstruction, a new governance wrapper, a new law wrapper, a new diplomacy wrapper, a new historical wrapper, a new economy wrapper, or a new presentation wrapper before Technology work.

One complete pre-portfolio owner gap remains:

> Sweden lacks a verified integrated Realm authority that owns population constituencies, household and seasonal labor capacity, finite recruitment and maritime crew pools, training organization, military-service reservation, replacement, demobilization, return, and layered recovery as one conservation-safe lifecycle.

The current military authority references recruitment, contribution, training inputs, personnel, ships, crews, demobilization, and recovery boundaries, but explicitly does not own active recruitment, exact training, personnel availability, or population capacity. The economy authority explicitly separates population from available labor. The shared future-work handoff identifies Population, Recruitment, Training, and Technology as separate future workstreams. A Sweden-specific integrated authority is therefore required before the Technology Portfolio can safely define development programs that depend on people and training.

### 6.2 Missing lifecycle ownership

The new integrated authority must own Sweden-specific definitions and contracts for:

- population constituencies and separately counted capacity sources;
- household, community, regional, town, institutional, coastal, island, and mobile population interfaces;
- agricultural, fishing, craft, transport, church, household, and military competing duties;
- seasonal and local-condition effects on availability;
- finite recruitment, crew, replacement, and recovery pools;
- eligibility, screening, reservation, release, and double-counting prevention;
- contribution and service interfaces without replacing law or assemblies;
- training dimensions, organization, competence, experience, cohesion, readiness contribution, and decay;
- equipment, vessel, horse, route, facility, command, supply, and instructor dependencies;
- muster, service, casualty, captivity, release, demobilization, return, household reintegration, replacement absorption, and recovery;
- migration, rollback, determinism, explanation, and owner handoff;
- noncreation of people, households, units, ships, crews, equipment, institutions, law, consent, or active state.

### 6.3 Why the file must be integrated

A narrow recruitment appendix would be insufficient. Recruitment without population conservation creates duplication. Training without people, institutions, equipment, instructors, time, and service history becomes an abstract upgrade currency. Recovery without casualties, households, health, property, routes, demobilization, and replacement absorption becomes instant pool refill.

The required authority must therefore integrate the full lifecycle while preserving execution ownership elsewhere.

### 6.4 Sweden-specific requirements

The authority must not copy Denmark's people-capacity model word for word. It must account for Sweden's existing authority shape:

- regional communities and assemblies with uneven recognition and contribution;
- western, eastern, Mälaren, island, inland, Baltic, and external interfaces;
- separate Crown, royal household, regional, community, church, settlement, route, and external subjects;
- no universal national levy, army, fleet, ship pool, crew pool, or tax base;
- no automatic Crown control over Gotland, Öland, Åland-facing, Finland-facing, Baltic, or eastern spaces;
- household and seasonal opportunity costs;
- distinct land-service, escort, garrison, ship, crew, transport, messenger, and support demands;
- independently validated equipment, ships, crews, command, route, access, transport, supply, and institutional hosts;
- finite replacement and long recovery effects;
- no later Swedish military or administrative institutions projected into the opening.
