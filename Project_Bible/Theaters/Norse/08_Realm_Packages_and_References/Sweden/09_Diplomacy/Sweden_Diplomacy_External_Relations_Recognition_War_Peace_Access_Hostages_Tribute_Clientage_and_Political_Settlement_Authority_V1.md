## 1. Concise Verdict

Sweden in January 1062 is a negotiated, regionally uneven Christian kingship under Stenkil. External action depends on recognized authority, regional and assembly competence, household and dynastic continuity, church cooperation, authenticated knowledge, viable communications, routes, access, transport, property, finance, forces, ships, crews, Supply, and reciprocal foreign acceptance. Sweden cannot be reduced to one diplomatic score, one national foreign-policy voice, or one automatic royal command.

This authority closes the substantive ownership gap between Sweden's specialist authorities. It does not create any exact opening diplomatic relationship, proposal, agreement, war, peace, passage grant, refuge arrangement, tribute obligation, coalition, clientage record, reciprocal foreign state, or runtime instance.

```text
Known Situation + Competent Principal + Valid Mandate + Authenticated Channel + Known Counterparty + Viable Terms + Owner Capacity + External Receipt + Ratification + Performance = Bounded Diplomatic Effect
```

## 48. Player Decision Portfolio

The Sweden diplomacy layer should support consequential decisions with explicit costs, dependencies, uncertainties, and alternatives.

- Authenticate or reject a foreign message.
- Offer an audience, refuse entry, or use an intermediary.
- Seek regional or assembly authorization before committing Sweden.
- Request church mediation, witnessing, or communication support.
- Offer bounded passage without broader access.
- Separate refuge, protection, service, office, and military support.
- Trade recognition, guarantees, money, access, information, or political concessions through typed terms.
- Enter, condition, delay, limit, or refuse coalition participation.
- Define war objectives and preserve a settlement path.
- Demand cure, restitution, security, mediation, or withdrawal after alleged breach.
- Negotiate ceasefire, truce, peace, reconciliation, or political settlement as distinct outcomes.
- Abstain when authority, information, route, capacity, or external receipt is insufficient.

The interface must explain why an option is unavailable without exposing hidden foreign facts.
