## 4. Sweden Differentiation Audit

### 4.1 Negotiated and regionally uneven kingship

Sweden correctly treats Stenkil's kingship as real but non-universal. Regional authorities, assemblies, church institutions, households, military contributors, route owners, and external counterparties retain separate competence. This is materially different from Denmark's negotiated maritime kingship and its principal Denmark-Norway sovereignty pressure.

### 4.2 Recognition and ratification

Sweden adds an audience-specific recognition matrix and a domestic ratification assessment. These correctly prevent recognition by one region, assembly, institution, ruler, or foreign principal from becoming universal Swedish legitimacy or universal compliance.

### 4.3 Geographic and external portfolio

Sweden's portfolio is not copied from Denmark. It distinguishes Norway-facing western security, Denmark-facing interaction, Baltic coast and archipelagos, Gotland, Oland, Aland-facing waters, Gulf of Finland approaches, southern Baltic connections, and Rus-facing links. Geography remains an interface and never becomes ownership, contact, passage, trade, or hostility automatically.

### 4.4 Ecclesiastical position

Sweden correctly consumes uneven Christian institutional development, Skara, conditional Sigtuna, Uppsala-facing religious and assembly surfaces, and external metropolitan or missionary relations without inventing one national church, universal conversion, bishop obedience, office occupancy, property, or mission state.

### 4.5 Håkon Ivarsson firewall

The candidate correctly treats the Håkon profile as a specialist worked interface rather than a replacement for general diplomacy. One Norway-owned person is preserved. Contact, movement, arrival, refuge, protection, patronage, service, office, command, alliance, sovereignty, rebellion, and return remain separate. Sweden may not create Norway's reverse relationship or absorb Ragnhild, followers, household members, and resources into Håkon's agreement.

## 5. Independent Defect Register

### SWE-DIP-AUD-001: status conflict

The control block declares `status: StaticReviewCandidate`. The closing validation and footer use `Static-Checked Owner-Review Candidate`. Static-checked status is not independently supported by a companion check for the current hash.

Required correction: use one honest status across control, validation, manifest, static check, and footer. Until a companion check passes, the defensible status is `StaticAuthoredOwnerReviewCandidate` or equivalent.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-002: split validation authority

Cases SWE-DIP-001 through SWE-DIP-028 appear in the first matrix. Cases SWE-DIP-029 through SWE-DIP-045 appear later under Expanded Validation Cases.

Required correction: consolidate all 45 cases into one authoritative matrix or explicitly state that the second table is a continuation of the first and bind the total count in control, handoff, validation status, manifest, and static check.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-003: unsupported closure boolean

The authority states `StaticAuthored: true`, which is supported by file existence, but its footer states `Static-Checked`. No independent companion currently binds and proves the current 51,586-byte artifact.

Required correction: generate a mechanical static-check companion only after the status and validation matrix are reconciled.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-004: missing manifest

Denmark has a manifest recording hash, bytes, physical lines, section count, validation count, differentiation, mutation ledger, next gate, exclusions, and authorizations. Sweden currently does not.

Required correction: create the Sweden manifest after the authority bytes are final. Do not create it against a file that is still being edited.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-005: unresolved exact source targets

The Sweden authority honestly retains package README, source inventory, historical refinement, Network, and relationship target verification as blockers. The audit does not convert those unresolved paths into claimed absence.

Required correction: preserve the blockers unless exact current package sources are retrieved and reconciled. Discovery of a substantive general diplomacy source triggers copy-first rebuild, not silent supersession.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-006: duplicate structural concepts require cross-reference discipline

The later expanded sections deepen missions, recognition, coalitions, protected parties, AI, migration, and validation. They do not contradict the earlier sections, but some subjects are defined twice at different depths.

Required correction: add explicit cross-references from the earlier general sections to the later detailed contracts, or integrate each detailed contract into its owning earlier section. Do not delete substantive content merely to reduce length.

### SWE-DIP-AUD-007: handoff count not bound

The first batch requires a complete validation matrix but does not bind the exact total of 45 cases.

Required correction: change acceptance to require `SWE-DIP-001 through SWE-DIP-045 present exactly once` after consolidation.

## 8. Final Audit Decision

The correct next action is not another broad rewrite and not more diplomacy scope expansion. It is a copy-preserving reconciliation of the current Sweden authority against the seven defects in this audit.
