## 2. Strong Authority Verdict

Sweden's Education system is the capacity to preserve, test, transmit, adapt, and apply knowledge across a negotiated and regionally uneven Realm. It does not reduce learning to a universal research currency, a literacy percentage, a school-building bonus, or a linear technology ladder.

Education exists through separately validated people, households, communities, assemblies, institutions, courts, offices, crafts, routes, languages, texts, records, memories, practical demonstrations, permissions, materials, and available time. Stenkil may patronize learning, records, communication, translation, training, or cultural work through proper owners. Kingship cannot create teachers, literate administrators, schools, archives, texts, translators, specialists, competence, consensus, or technological adoption.

The playable question is: what knowledge can Sweden preserve and use without pretending that every region, household, institution, or actor shares the same language, literacy, access, authority, interpretation, or practical capability?

## 3. Player Promise and Total War Scale

The player builds durable knowledge relationships and chooses which limited learning channels to support.

The player may:

- patronize a bounded teaching or record-keeping arrangement;
- support clerical, household, court, assembly, craft, diplomatic, or military-facing instruction;
- preserve or copy an admitted text or record;
- commission translation, interpretation, authentication, or documentary work;
- protect a teacher, learner, practitioner, archive, memory-holder, or route;
- choose between immediate service and longer learning;
- recover disrupted records or knowledge continuity;
- support practical training without creating competence instantly;
- decide whether regional learning remains local, is shared conditionally, or is allowed to diverge;
- accept uncertainty where evidence and custody do not support a clear answer.

The player does not personally set lessons, write texts, grade every learner, or manage a modern education ministry. Ordinary learning remains summarized. Named arrangements become visible when they affect governance, diplomacy, church, military, succession, household continuity, technology adoption, settlement function, cultural legitimacy, or a major decision.

## 4. Sweden Education Identity

Sweden's Education authority is shaped by:

- negotiated kingship rather than centralized administration;
- uneven regional recognition and distinct community and assembly traditions;
- uneven Christian institutional development;
- coexistence of oral, customary, runic, written, translated, practical, and commemorative transmission;
- Sigtuna, Skara, Uppsala, Mälaren, western, eastern, island, inland, Baltic, and external-facing knowledge surfaces;
- mobile royal presence and no required fixed capital;
- finite household, institutional, craft, transport, clerical, and military-service capacity;
- multilingual and cross-cultural contact requiring actual interpretation and access;
- separation of knowledge from adoption, and adoption from material capability;
- no automatic royal ownership of institutions, texts, archives, teachers, or cultural production.

Education differences emerge from actual people, access, custody, practice, evidence, routes, permissions, and continuity. They do not emerge from stereotypes about region, religion, social status, age, sex, language, or identity.

## 13. Teaching, Practice, and Assessment

A valid arrangement requires:

- admitted learner and provider scope;
- lawful or accepted purpose;
- a method compatible with the capability;
- provider availability and competence evidence;
- language or communication compatibility;
- materials, tools, place, route, and time where required;
- household, institutional, military, or office permission where required;
- practice opportunities;
- assessment method and limits;
- interruption and handback rules.

Assessment must record what was demonstrated, under what conditions, before whom, with which assistance, and for what scope. One successful performance does not create universal competence. Failure does not prove permanent incapacity.

## 13A. Apprenticeship, Mentorship, and Qualification Boundary

An apprenticeship or mentorship is a bounded relationship among an admitted learner, provider, purpose, practice context, terms, time window, supervision, resource support, review, and exit conditions. Household, craft, institutional, court, diplomatic, or military-facing settings may host arrangements only through their proper owners.

Education may recommend that observed performance supports a bounded qualification. The controlling office, institution, craft, military, governance, or Character owner determines whether that qualification exists, is recognized, expires, is contested, or permits a role.

## 13B. Retention, Decay Risk, and Reacquisition

Supported competence may weaken in practical availability when practice stops, tools or texts disappear, language contact ends, injury or health limits participation, or the surrounding institution collapses. The authority does not apply one automatic global decay rate.

A retention review considers:

- time since supported practice;
- continuity and frequency of use;
- task complexity and context change;
- access to people, tools, texts, and examples;
- health and functional capacity;
- contradictory or superseding evidence;
- whether the intended new task exceeds the earlier assessed scope.

Reacquisition may be shorter than first learning where valid prior evidence survives, but it still needs opportunity, practice, assessment, and owner acceptance. Absence of recent evidence does not automatically erase competence; old evidence does not guarantee current performance.

## 14. Oral, Customary, and Assembly Transmission

Oral and customary knowledge can carry law, genealogies, boundaries, obligations, routes, memory, practical methods, stories, precedents, and identity. It is not treated as automatically inferior to writing.

Assemblies may convey, witness, contest, repeat, or interpret knowledge through their proper owners. Attendance does not guarantee comprehension, agreement, representation, or retention. A later written account does not replace the earlier oral context without source review.

## 16. Ecclesiastical Learning and Literacy

Sweden's church authority supports bounded school, literacy, record, communication, mission, translation, and institutional-development functions without creating active institutions or holders. Education consumes only owner-approved institutional state.

Skara may support a bounded western ecclesiastical learning surface. Conditional Sigtuna may support missionary, literacy, communication, record, or translation development. Uppsala remains a distinct religious, assembly, elite, ceremonial, and memory surface. ## 17. Household, Court, and Mobile Kingship

Households may transmit practical, genealogical, political, linguistic, ceremonial, administrative, military-support, and property knowledge through admitted people and arrangements. Household membership does not mean shared knowledge.

Stenkil's mobile kingship may create demand for:

- record continuity across locations;
- messenger and route knowledge;
- witnesses and authentication;
- interpretation and diplomatic competence;
- household administration;
- regional custom and assembly understanding;

## 19. Archives, Custody, Access, and Loss

An archive is a custody and access relationship, not merely a building or collection label.

It requires:

- admitted objects or record references;
- provenance and chronology;
- custodian and authority;
- physical location and condition;
- access and disclosure rules;
- inventory confidence;
- copying and loan rules;
- return, loss, damage, dispute, and succession handling;
- persistence and migration behavior.

A collection may be partial, dispersed, private, inaccessible, damaged, contested, or unknown. Loss does not erase every memory or copy. Recovery does not prove authenticity or completeness.

## 22. Practical, Craft, Agricultural, and Maritime Learning

Practical knowledge may be learned through observation, participation, repetition, correction, demonstration, and supported responsibility. Text is not always required.

Craft, agricultural, livestock, forestry, iron, construction, transport, fishing, sailing, storage, and maintenance competence require actual practitioners, tools, materials, time, place, and opportunity. Knowledge of a method does not create production. Contact with a region or trader does not create adoption.

Education records arrangements and evidence. Economy, Population, Settlement, Route, Maritime, and Development owners control real capacity and outcomes.

## 23. Population, Labor, and Competing Duties

Learners and providers remain finite people. Education competes with:

- household support;
- agricultural and seasonal labor;
- fishing, transport, and craft work;
- church and institutional duty;
- messenger and diplomatic duty;
- military service and recovery;
- travel and route constraints;
- health and caregiving needs.

The same person cannot teach, travel, serve militarily, perform full productive work, and provide household care at incompatible times. A learning program must state its opportunity cost and substitution plan.

## 24. Military Training Boundary

Military owns formation training, drill, readiness, command, experience, cohesion, equipment use, and return to service. Education may provide general learning contracts, instructor evidence, language, records, practical demonstrations, or specialist competence recommendations only when Military accepts the interface.

No unit, recruitment pool, officer, instructor, readiness state, veteran status, or upgrade is created here. The active Sweden military-design workstream remains separate.

## 25. Regional Knowledge Surfaces

Regional surfaces are learning and transmission envelopes, not opening competence assignments.

- **Västergötland and Skara-facing surface:** ecclesiastical, household, assembly, route, legal-custom, and western-contact knowledge may intersect. Skara does not automatically create a school, archive, or literate administration.
- **Östergötland:** regional, elite, household, church, lake, settlement, and route knowledge remain separately owned. No western pattern is copied automatically.
- **Uppland and Mälaren sphere:** Sigtuna, Uppsala, waterways, islands, assemblies, exchange, missions, memory, and royal movement create competing knowledge channels. Strategic importance does not create one information center.
- **Södermanland and Västmanland:** Mälaren adjacency does not produce identical access, literacy, custom, or institutional capacity.
- **Närke and Värmland:** inland and western-facing routes may carry practical and political knowledge, but route relevance does not create teachers or shared interpretation.
- **Småland landscape groups:** no single curriculum, custom, literacy state, or knowledge owner applies across the grouping.
- **Gotland and Öland:** island identity may support distinctive community, assembly, maritime, exchange, religious, and linguistic interfaces. It does not create Swedish Crown control, universal merchant literacy, interpreters, schools, or open routes.
- **Dalarna and northern interiors:** research and interface surfaces only. Sparse documentation does not prove absence of knowledge, people, or sophisticated practice.
- **Åland-facing, Finland-facing, Baltic, and eastern interfaces:** contact plausibility does not create language competence, translation, trust, knowledge transfer, adoption, or Swedish authority.

## 26. Cultural Production

Cultural production includes source-approved forms of narrative, commemoration, genealogical memory, inscription, ritual expression, visual work, objects, performance, public witness, and commissioned documentary work.

Culture is not one score and not a passive legitimacy bonus. A work requires creators or performers, patronage or purpose, people, materials, venue or route, audience, custody, interpretation, and memory handoff.

```text
Commission != Work Completed
Performance != Audience Acceptance
Commemoration != Historical Truth
Genealogy != Uncontested Claim
Cultural Contact != Cultural Adoption
Shared Form != Shared Meaning
```

Presentation owners determine player-facing representation. Historical owners determine evidence and memory treatment. Governance, Church, Household, Community, and Diplomacy owners retain their own consequences.

**Options:** support the full arrangement; narrow the scope; delay; seek another provider; use household or community instruction; fund materials or travel; decline the current arrangement.

## 28. Decision Package: Preserve or Copy a Record

**Eligibility:** an admitted record or work exists and preservation, access, or duplication has a valid purpose.

**Review:** custody, authority, authenticity confidence, materials, labor, location, route, recipient, copying limits, and return.

**Options:** preserve locally; make a bounded copy; commission an authenticated version; relocate with permission; restrict access; prioritize another record; accept loss risk.

## 29. Decision Package: Translation and Diplomatic Preparation

**Eligibility:** an admitted contact, message, negotiation, mission, or external work requires interpretation.

**Review:** languages, competence, availability, access, secrecy, context, ambiguity, route, counterpart, review, and record custody.

## 30. Decision Package: Assembly and Custom Knowledge

**Review:** competent speakers, witnesses, jurisdiction, access, chronology, variants, dissent, later texts, and affected owners.

**Options:** convene or consult through proper owners; collect multiple accounts; preserve competing interpretations; request a bounded written record; delay action; proceed under declared uncertainty.

## 31. Decision Package: Practical Training Competes With Immediate Service

**Eligibility:** an admitted learner or provider is reserved for work, household, church, transport, diplomatic, or military duty.

**Review:** urgency, duration, substitutes, season, materials, future value, current need, and recovery.

**Options:** shorten training; rotate participants; defer service if lawful; defer training; use another source; preserve practice through a smaller arrangement.

The same person and tools cannot be double-booked. Training does not create production or readiness before performance and owner acceptance.

## 32. Decision Package: Archive Loss or Disputed Custody

**Eligibility:** a record, collection, inscription, or cultural work is missing, damaged, inaccessible, duplicated, or contested.

**Review:** provenance, custody chain, copies, witnesses, property, route, condition, legal and institutional claims, and public knowledge.

**Options:** investigate; preserve remaining evidence; negotiate access; copy what is lawfully available; record the dispute; seek another source; accept unresolved custody.

Administrative convenience cannot manufacture ownership, authenticity, completeness, or truth.

## 33. Decision Package: Support a Regional Knowledge Network

**Eligibility:** multiple communities, institutions, households, settlements, or routes have a concrete shared learning or record need.

**Review:** distinct owners, languages, access, providers, materials, transport, local authority, benefits, burdens, and exit conditions.

**Options:** support a limited exchange; establish periodic contact; circulate copies where permitted; sponsor travel; maintain separate local arrangements; decline centralization.

A network does not become a national school system, central archive, universal curriculum, or automatic Crown administration.
