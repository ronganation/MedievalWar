## 2. Concise Authority Verdict

This authority closes Sweden's single proven pre-Technology owner gap by defining the conservation-safe lifecycle that connects population constituencies, households, communities, seasonal labor, eligibility, lawful or customary service, finite recruitment and crew pools, training, military contribution, demobilization, return, replacement, and recovery.

Sweden does not receive one national population pool, levy pool, crew pool, army, fleet, or recruitment currency. People remain separately owned through communities, households, institutions, settlements, regions, and external owners. The authority creates no person, household, population total, pool instance, unit, ship, crew, equipment, officer, institution, force, Opening State record, or runtime state.
