## 2. Sweden High Era Infantry Thesis

Sweden develops from a campaign foundation of regional spears, selected trained spear infantry, shielded close-combat troops, household formations, and finite local sources into a broader High Era portfolio through independent channels:

- regional organization and inspection;
- stronger spear, shield, helmet, textile, and supported mail distribution;
- recurring field service beyond one short muster;
- maintained nonroyal or household foot service;
- selective shielded melee specialization;
- offensive axe, sword, or mixed-weapon service where a true role survives;
- town, castle, crossing, mine, route, or site defense where a distinct source exists;
- royal, magnate, ecclesiastical, or other household assignments;
- conditional named political or historical emergence;
- actual survivor experience and formation memory.

No channel creates people, armor, weapons, officers, institutions, compliance, experience, recruitment pools, or readiness by itself. Development may occur earlier, later, unevenly, temporarily, regionally, or never.

## 4. Development Is Not a Linear Ladder

The following ladder is rejected:

```text
Hundare or Härad Spearmen
→ Svenska Spjutmän
→ Armored Spearmen
→ Svenska Sköldmän
→ Household Infantry
→ Hirdmen
→ Huskarla
→ Men-at-Arms or Foot Knights
```

These families come from different people, providers, duties, equipment, and military needs. A regional spear contributor does not become a Sköldman because protection improves. A Sköldman does not become a Hirdman through experience. A Hirdman does not become a Huscarl, foot knight, or cavalryman on an era tick.

## 18. High Era I Direction

High Era I should emphasize uneven emergence:

- broad Hundare and Härad parents remain common where sources survive;
- Svenska Spjutmän and Sköldmän persist and mature without automatic replacement;
- the first protected regional spear companies may emerge where full support exists;
- maintained shielded service may exist under a few providers;
- an offensive company may emerge only if its role and source survive review;
- equipment, leaders, and service remain mixed;
- recovery and provider confidence remain fragile;
- urban and site-defense expressions remain conditional rather than kingdom-wide.

High Era I is a development window, not a roster replacement date.

## 28. Campaign Choice Contract

The player should understand real choices between:

- broad regional spear frontage and a narrow protected company;
- trained Spjutmän and more equipment-intensive protected spears;
- Sköldmän and a possible longer-service maintained company;
- household security and field commitment;
- Hird or Huskarla preservation and broader nonroyal service;
- infantry equipment and Crossbow or Bågsvener investment;
- field concentration and town, route, crossing, castle, or regional defense;
- infantry service and mounted Hastkarlar or Hirdryttare development;
- fresh formations and recovery of veteran survivors.

The UI should summarize these choices without individual-household micromanagement.

## 29. Campaign AI Contract

Campaign AI develops a branch only when actual people, service, provider capacity, equipment, leadership, mission, supply, replacement, and opportunity cost support it.

AI should:

- retain ordinary regional spears when width matters;
- use Svenska Spjutmän when trained spear control fits the mission;
- use Sköldmän when selected shielded close combat and frontage takeover matter;
- develop protected spears only for selected threatened-point needs;
- preserve Hirdmen and Huskarla for their actual household roles;
- choose maintained or offensive companies only when their distinct role is needed;
- avoid hollowing local defense or duplicating people;
- receive no free equipment, compliance, horses, ships, organization, or replacement.

## 32. Recruitment Closure and Survivor Continuity

Recruitment may close because:

- providers collapse or withdraw;
- obligations change;
- regions or sites change control;
- equipment or repair fails;
- political support ends;
- households recall personnel;
- another institution absorbs the same people and role;
- source population or leadership cannot recover.

Existing formations persist until destroyed, demobilized, dispersed, recalled, reassigned, absorbed through explicit transition, or rendered nonviable. Calendar progression alone does not delete them.
