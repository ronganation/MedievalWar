## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Svenska Borgarmän are Sweden's High Era urban militia: eligible residents of an actual developed town organized through real local duty, warning, muster, command, equipment, stores, and sector assignments to hold approaches, lanes, markets, stores, waterfront access, administrative places, and other defensible urban positions until reinforcement, counterattack, withdrawal, or local recovery becomes possible.**

The card is admitted to Sweden’s High Era roster. Recruitment is town-based: a town must supply the residents, duty, command, equipment, stores, and defensible sectors required to raise it. Settlements that cannot support those inputs simply cannot recruit the card; that availability rule does not reopen the unit’s roster survival.

## 2. One-Screen Medieval II Unit Card

- **Unit:** Svenska Borgarmän
- **English gloss:** Swedish Townsmen
- **Class:** Urban spear-and-shield militia
- **Era:** High Era development
- **Core rhythm:** Warn, Muster, Hold, Reinforce, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Respond from actual residents, occupy useful urban sectors, delay penetration, protect missile troops and stores, and reinforce threatened lanes without pretending to be field infantry
- **Best use:** Town approaches, broad streets, lanes, gates where structures exist, markets, stores, waterfront access, courtyards, administrative sites, and local withdrawal coverage
- **Strong against:** Raiders, light infantry, disorganized attackers, unsupported penetration, and enemies forced into narrow local approaches
- **Weak against:** Elite assault troops, heavy axes, sustained missile pressure, cavalry in open approaches, siege breach shock, encirclement, fire and panic where modeled, and prolonged field service
- **Strengths:** Local knowledge, warning and muster, sector assignment, short reinforcement routes, useful spears and shields where supported, and finite resident defense
- **Weaknesses:** Mixed equipment, ordinary training, town-bound source, labor and service loss, weak expeditionary endurance, and severe casualty consequences for the settlement
- **Campaign bargain:** Convert eligible urban residents, duty, equipment, leaders, stores, and working time into temporary defense, reducing labor, trade, services, crews, missile pools, and post-battle recovery
- **Player instruction:** **Warn the town. Muster only eligible residents. Hold the strongest useful lane. Reinforce the threatened sector. Recover the people and working life afterward.**
- **Flavor line:** **The walls do not defend the town. The people assigned to its streets do.**

## 14. Core Rhythm: Hold

Borgarmän hold the strongest useful local position rather than forming an open-field battle line.

Strong positions include:

- town approaches;
- broad streets and lanes;
- real gates and entries;
- market or store approaches;
- waterfront access;
- courtyards;
- administrative sites;
- defensible intersections;
- temporary barriers where physically created.

Hold depends upon facing, space, leaders, equipment, flanks, civilian movement, and an escape or reinforcement route.

## 16. Core Rhythm: Recover

Recovery includes more than unit replenishment:

- account for residents;
- treat wounded;
- recover missing and displaced people;
- return tools, weapons, shields, and stores;
- repair damaged equipment and defended sites;
- restore labor, trade, crews, and services;
- rebuild warning, muster, and command confidence;
- reconcile duties and households.

A defended town can win militarily while suffering severe population, labor, property, and service damage.

## 20. Short Local Counterattack

A short counterattack is permitted when:

- attackers are disordered;
- a local position must be restored;
- another section holds the flanks;
- the objective is near;
- a withdrawal route remains.

It is not the Yxmän offensive cycle and should not become prolonged pursuit.

## 29. Campaign AI Contract

Campaign AI develops Borgarmän when:

- the settlement is an actual qualifying town;
- a distinct eligible urban source exists;
- duty, warning, command, equipment, stores, sectors, and recovery are supportable;
- urban defense needs cannot be met better by assignments of existing units;
- labor and competing duties can absorb the debit.

AI avoids or limits Borgarmän when:

- only a settlement label or building exists;
- regional or field troops are merely present;
- the source overlaps other needed formations;
- labor, crews, repair, trade, missiles, or local defense would fall below required floors;
- the unit is being used primarily for remote campaigns.
