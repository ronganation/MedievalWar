## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Svenska Väpnare are Sweden's maintained armed-retainer foot company: accountable recurring-service fighters equipped for sustained shielded melee, repeated physical line relief, important-position retention, command-adjacent defense, and controlled pressure without consuming Sweden's narrower Hird or Huskarla formations.**

The card survives only where Sweden develops a recurring and supportable foot-service layer drawn from compatible magnate, royal, castle, court, institutional, or other validated providers. It is not every armed household member, every shielded veteran, every person called a väpnare, or mounted personnel counted twice.

Väpnare sit:

- above Sköldmän in recurring service, officer depth, repeated relief, and campaign continuity;
- below Hirdmen in command-protection reliability, political concentration, and crisis intervention;
- below Huskarla in assault, hard contact, and rupture;
- beside Pansrade Häradsspjutsmän as a close-melee relief partner rather than a spear-reception replacement.

## 2. One-Screen Medieval II Unit Card

- **Unit:** Svenska Väpnare
- **English gloss:** Swedish Armed Retainers
- **Class:** Maintained sword-or-axe-and-shield field infantry
- **Era:** Conditional High Era development
- **Core rhythm:** Accompany, Hold, Relieve, Press
- **Battle promise:** Remain available for repeated shielded service, take over a damaged sector, hold important ground, and apply controlled pressure after the enemy is fixed or disordered
- **Best use:** Line relief, compact reserve, gate or street defense, command approaches, camps, courtyards, captured-ground retention, and sustained ordinary melee
- **Strong against:** Tired or disordered ordinary infantry, light attackers, damaged spear or shield formations, and enemies already fixed by Swedish frontage
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry impact, long spears in prepared frontage, concentrated missiles, heavy two-handed axes, elite household assault, encirclement, prolonged unsupported pursuit, and provider collapse

- **Weaknesses:** Scarce providers, political and household competition, expensive equipment and upkeep, limited frontage, slow replacement, and lower decisiveness than household elites
- **Campaign bargain:** Maintain a recurring armed foot body without consuming the entire Hird, paid for through people, service terms, equipment, leaders, quarters, maintenance, provider cooperation, and return obligations
- **Player instruction:** **Accompany the important force. Hold the compact sector. Relieve the failing line. Press only while order and support remain.**
- **Flavor line:** **Armed for service, maintained for the fight that continues.**

## 14. Core Rhythm: Accompany

Accompany means the company exists as a maintained field body attached to an actual provider, army, command, court, train, castle, or important mission.

- valid service terms;
- real people and equipment;
- provider accountability;
- officers;
- quarters and supply;
- a mission;
- limits on duration and competing duty;

## 16. Core Rhythm: Relieve

Väpnare should enter a threatened fight through a real passage, accept contact, allow the outgoing formation to withdraw, and preserve enough order to continue the mission.

Possible outgoing formations include:

- regional spears;
- Svenska Spjutmän;
- Pansrade Häradsspjutsmän;
- Svenska Sköldmän;
- Bågsvener forced into defensive posture;
- Hirdmen or Huskarla whose exceptional role should be preserved.

Relief is not instant replacement or fatigue transfer.

## 26. Combined Arms

### Regional and protected spears

Spears receive frontal pressure. Väpnare relieve them when spear geometry breaks or fatigue becomes dangerous.

### Svenska Sköldmän

Sköldmän provide broader selected shielded close combat. Väpnare provide maintained service and repeated relief where providers sustain it.

### Hirdmen

Hirdmen protect command and resolve crises. Väpnare reduce the need to spend them on every prolonged ordinary fight.

### Huskarla

Huskarla rupture. Väpnare hold the ground, protect the assault's flanks, or retain the captured position.

### Missile units

Väpnare protect withdrawal passages, hold threatened approaches, or clear ordinary pressure. They do not provide spear-style cavalry reception.

### Cavalry

Mounted troops protect flanks, intercept cavalry, and exploit fixed enemies. Väpnare must preserve mounted passage.

No passive proximity bonus exists.

**Maintained Swedish armed retainers trained for sustained shielded fighting, repeated line relief, and the defense of important ground. Their service is expensive and politically finite, and they remain vulnerable to heavy cavalry, concentrated missiles, long spears, heavy axes, and elite assault troops.**
