## 1. Strong Unit Verdict

**Svenska Yxmän are Sweden's selected non-household axe-and-shield offensive infantry: a narrower company trained and equipped to close under shield, bind suitable ordinary infantry, apply purposeful axe pressure, exploit a local weakness, and recover before aggression turns into disordered pursuit or household-grade assault.**

The card owns a serious ordinary infantry attack without committing Huskarla. It is more offensively purposeful than Sköldmän, less enduring and repeatable than Väpnare, less reliable in crisis than Hirdmen, and substantially below Huskarla in hard assault and rupture.

## 2. One-Screen Medieval II Unit Card

- **Unit:** Svenska Yxmän
- **English gloss:** Swedish Axemen
- **Class:** Selected one-handed axe-and-shield offensive infantry
- **Era:** Conditional High Era development
- **Core rhythm:** Close, Bind, Hew, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Approach under shield, enter a chosen ordinary infantry fight, use axes to create local pressure, take limited ground, and reform before the company overextends
- **Best use:** Supported counterattack, engaged flank, disrupted shield line, gate or camp counterpressure, short assault into fixed ordinary infantry, and local exploitation after missile or spear preparation
- **Strong against:** Tired or disordered ordinary infantry, exposed shielded foot, overextended attackers, compromised spear formations, and light troops trapped in close combat
- **Weak against:** Prepared long spears, heavy cavalry, concentrated missiles, deep reserves, elite household infantry, two-handed axe specialists, encirclement, and prolonged unsupported pursuit
- **Strengths:** Purposeful axe pressure, active shields, concentrated commitment, local exploitation, and a recoverable offensive cycle
- **Weaknesses:** Short reach, fatigue, narrow frontage, limited replacements, need for support, vulnerability while closing, and lower staying power than maintained or household infantry
- **Campaign bargain:** Concentrate qualified fighters, axes, shields, leaders, protection, practice, and recovery into one offensive company at the cost of regional defense, Sköldmän, Väpnare, household support, and equipment elsewhere
- **Player instruction:** **Close behind support. Bind the selected line. Hew while the local bargain holds. Recover before the next enemy reserve arrives.**
- **Flavor line:** **The spears fix the fight. The axes turn the hold into ground gained.**

## 15. Core Rhythm: Bind

Bind means commit the target to a local melee where the axes can work without the company losing its own alignment.

The company may bind through:

- supported frontal contact against suitable ordinary infantry;
- counterattack against overextended attackers;
- flank contact against an already engaged enemy;
- takeover of a short sector after friendly spears or shields fix the threat;
- confined-ground counterpressure.

Bind is not an immobilization ability. The target can withdraw, rotate, reinforce, or counterattack according to physical conditions.

## 17. Core Rhythm: Recover

Recover distinguishes the card from expendable shock infantry.

The company must:

- halt after enough ground is gained;
- restore shield coverage;
- shorten or realign files;
- recover officers and rally reference;
- clear casualties;
- identify fresh threats;
- refuse deep pursuit;
- withdraw or prepare another bounded commitment.

A company that cannot recover may win the local melee and still cease to function as a useful formation.

## 19. Target Contract

- tired ordinary infantry;

## 25. Relationship to Spears and Protected Spears

Spjutmän and Pansrade Häradsspjutsmän establish reach, reception, and frontage. Yxmän exploit enemies fixed, compressed, or fatigued by that frontage.

They do not replace spears against cavalry.

## 26. Combined Arms

- **Spears:** Fix, receive, and define the reference line.
- **Sköldmän:** Stabilize and contain.
- **Väpnare:** Relieve and retain.
- **Yxmän:** Counterattack and gain limited ground.
- **Hirdmen:** Protect command and solve crises.
- **Huskarla:** Deliver household-grade rupture.
- **Missiles:** Disorder, expose, or fix suitable targets before commitment.
- **Cavalry:** Protect flanks, counter enemy mounted threats, or exploit the outcome.

No passive composition bonus is created.

## 27. Campaign Availability and Opportunity Cost

The card should be:

- scarcer than local Byavärn;
- narrower than broad regional infantry;
- comparable to other selected non-household offensive formations;
- less politically restricted than Hirdmen or Huskarla where several sources develop;
- less maintenance-heavy than Väpnare when service is shorter, but still equipment and leader intensive;
- finite through actual people, providers, axes, shields, armor, leaders, supply, and recovery.

Recruitment may reduce:

- regional and local defense;
- Byavärn and Sköldmän availability;
- Väpnare or household support where providers overlap;
- axes, shields, helmets, mail, repair, and transport;
- officers and experienced fighters;
- future replacements.

## 34. Morale, Cohesion, and Fatigue

Morale benefits from:

- selected fighters;
- complete shields and weapons;
- known leaders;
- a clear bounded objective;
- support on the approach;
- successful local pressure;
- a visible halt and recovery plan.

Morale falls through:

- open missile exposure;
- heavy cavalry;
- prepared spears;
- fresh elite reserves;
- flank loss;
- leader loss;
- failed bind;
- overextension;
- blocked recovery.

Axe pressure is fatigue-intensive. Continued attack should become less attractive as formation control and shield coverage decline.

## 35. Terrain and Context

Strong contexts:

- supported field counterattack;
- engaged flank;
- gates, broad streets, camps, and courtyards;
- fieldworks;
- short firm approaches;
- recently disrupted enemy frontage.

Weak contexts:

- open ground under missiles;
- cavalry-dominated terrain;
- prepared spear fronts;
- deep mud or steep broken ground;
- dense congestion that prevents controlled axe use;
- blocked withdrawal;
- long pursuit.

## 36. Campaign AI Contract

Campaign AI recruits the card when:

- Sweden needs a serious ordinary infantry attack below household commitment;
- valid non-household sources exist;
- axes, shields, officers, support, and recovery can be sustained;
- Sköldmän would be too defensive;
- Väpnare are needed for longer service or relief elsewhere;
- Hirdmen and Huskarla should be preserved.

AI avoids the card when:

- the actual need is spear reception, broad frontage, relief endurance, command protection, or household rupture;
- approach support is inadequate;
- equipment or leaders are insufficient;
- the card would hollow local defense or duplicate people.
