## 2. Swedish Four-Family Missile Thesis

Sweden's domestic missile identity uses four complementary decisions:

- **Swedish District Bowmen** provide broad ordinary domestic bow coverage, target-sector control, and Section Displacement.
- **Svenska Bågskyttar** provide selected trained bow pressure, Prepared Concentration, cease-fire discipline, target control, and Shift.
- **Svenska Bågsvener** provide durable armed field archery, counterfire endurance, Ready the Line, and a timed defensive transition.
- **Swedish Crossbowmen** provide Loaded Threat, protected deliberate fire, reload exposure, and defended-lane control.

Theater-owned Regional and Mercenary missile families remain external capabilities. The four domestic families coexist; none is an automatic upgrade from another.

## 5. District Bowmen Persistence

District Bowmen remain Sweden's broadest organized native bow family. Their High Era development may improve:

- source integration;

- broad District Bowmen coverage;

## 24. Campaign and Battle AI

Campaign AI develops Swedish Crossbowmen only when qualified people, equipment, bolts, strings, spanning, training, repair, command, protection, and recovery exist. It retains bows where availability, tempo, mobility, concentration, or cost make bows better.

Battle AI:

- occupies protected firing lanes;
- uses hold-loaded selectively;
- chooses targets worth the bolt cycle;
- protects spanning states;
- avoids friendly masking;
- displaces before closure;
- preserves ammunition and functioning weapons;
- withdraws behind actual frontage or cover;
- enters melee only when trapped.

AI receives no hidden shooters, crossbows, bolts, penetration, repair, pavises, or site bonuses.
