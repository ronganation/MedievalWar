## 3. Why the Five Cards All Stay

Each card answers a different Total War cavalry question:

- **Hastkarlar:** How does Sweden screen, counter-skirmish, counterraid, pursue, and solve repeated practical mounted tasks economically?
- **Hirdryttare:** Where does scarce household cavalry answer a crisis through countercharge, purposeful melee, and rally?
- **Frälsemän:** How does Sweden maintain a formed cavalry wing through supported commitments and repeated service?
- **Svenner av Vapen:** How does a close companion body cover, exploit, and return with a senior mounted action?
- **Riddare:** When should Sweden preserve and spend its decisive knightly heavy-shock formation?

These are different army-composition and battle-handling decisions, not statistical rungs.

## 4. Prohibited Linear Ladder

## 15. Combined Arms

- Regional spears and trained spear infantry provide cavalry-safe frontage and rally references.
- Swedish missiles pressure enemy light troops and disrupt valuable targets.
- Svea Hastkarlar clear skirmishers, preserve flank freedom, and control pursuit.
- Svenska Hirdryttare answer serious mounted crises.
- Frälsemän, if admitted, stabilize a developed cavalry wing and support sustained field campaigning.
- Riddare, if later realized, deliver the rare decisive commitment.

These are physical missions rather than passive composition bonuses.

## 16. Terrain, Weather, and Maritime Boundaries

Firm open or rolling ground supports mounted formation when approach and exit lanes exist. Woods, marsh, mud, steep slopes, walls, bridges, gates, narrow streets, snow, ice, fog, heat, rain, and thaw alter performance through actual ground, horse, route, visibility, and support states.

Sweden receives no national plains, forest, winter, lake, or cavalry bonus. Horse transport across water requires suitable vessels, crews, loading, handlers, feed, water, landing, shore assembly, route access, recovery, and return. Warrior lift does not prove horse lift.
