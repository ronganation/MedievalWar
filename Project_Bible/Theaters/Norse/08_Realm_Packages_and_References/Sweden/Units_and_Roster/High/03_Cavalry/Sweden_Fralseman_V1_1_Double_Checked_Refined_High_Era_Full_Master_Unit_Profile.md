## 1. Strong First-Trial Verdict

**Frälsemän are proposed as Sweden's maintained privileged mounted-service company: qualified riders and suitable horses provided through actual landed, privileged, or equivalent recognized service relationships to attend a developed field army, stabilize its cavalry wing, reinforce mounted action, deliver supported charges and countercharges, complete bounded mounted contact, and reform for repeat service without consuming the narrower household Hirdryttare or pretending to be later knightly Riddare.**

The proposal survives as a strong trial because it combines:

- a candidate source outside the narrowest household reserve;
- longer and more regular field obligation;
- provider-backed horse, equipment, and administrative support;
- common wing signals and formation practice;
- better repeat-service endurance than crisis-centered Hirdryttare;
- stronger formed support than practical Hastkarlar;
- explicit landed, household, administrative, local-defense, and political costs;
- a mandatory merge or rehome path if battle testing cannot show the difference.

## 2. One-Screen Medieval II Unit Card

- **Unit:** Frälsemän
- **English gloss:** Swedish Privileged Horsemen
- **Class:** Maintained privileged medium-to-heavy cavalry below the knightly ceiling
- **Era:** Conditional High Era development
- **Core rhythm:** Attend, Support, Commit, Reform
- **Battle promise:** Strengthen Sweden's formed cavalry wing, support a charge or countercharge, remain through a bounded mounted fight, protect reform, and return for another considered field mission
- **Best use:** Cavalry-wing support, reinforcement of a mounted flank, medium-cavalry countercharge, supported flank charge, reform-lane protection, withdrawal security, and controlled exploitation
- **Strong against:** Practical cavalry in committed contact, exposed missiles, disordered infantry flanks, tired mounted troops, and enemies fixed by another Swedish formation
- **Competitive against:** Peer privileged or retained cavalry, household cavalry when service and support favor Sweden, and medium-heavy mounted formations below the knightly ceiling
- **Weak against:** Prepared spears, knightly heavy cavalry, concentrated crossfire, deep mud or broken terrain, prolonged unsupported melee, blocked reform, horse depletion, recall disputes, and provider withdrawal
- **Strengths:** Formed support, credible mounted weight, recurring service, officer depth, wing coordination, supported charge, and controlled reform
- **Weaknesses:** Political and landed-service cost, scarce suitable horses, expensive maintenance, narrower pursuit, recall obligations, and slower replacement
- **Campaign bargain:** Gain a maintained mounted field contribution by accepting provider rights, horse and equipment costs, local-defense and administrative debits, defined service terms, recovery responsibilities, and future competition with Hirdryttare and Riddare
- **Player instruction:** **Attend the cavalry wing. Support the action that matters. Commit only when alignment and exit are sound. Reform before mounted advantage becomes trapped melee.**
- **Flavor line:** **The household reserve answers the crisis. The privileged service keeps the cavalry wing in the campaign.**
