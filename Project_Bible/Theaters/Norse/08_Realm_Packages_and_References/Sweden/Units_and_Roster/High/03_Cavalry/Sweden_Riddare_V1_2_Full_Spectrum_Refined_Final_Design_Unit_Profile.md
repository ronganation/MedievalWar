## 1. Full-Spectrum Refined Final-Design Verdict

**Riddare are Sweden's true High Era knightly cavalry ceiling: a scarce, institutionally supported formation of qualified riders, suitable heavy-contact horses, strong protection, disciplined mounted weapons, leaders, attendants, and recovery capacity, held for the moment when Sweden must break a decisive mounted or infantry position rather than merely support the cavalry wing.**

They are not armored Hirdryttare, promoted Frälsemän, older Svenner av Vapen, or a universal class of every titled or wealthy rider. The card exists only where Sweden has developed the people, service relationships, horses, equipment, formation practice, patronage, and recovery system required to place true heavy shock cavalry on the field.

## 1A. Decisive Power Contract

When fresh, formed, properly supported, and committed through suitable ground, Riddare should:

- deliver Sweden's strongest mounted first impact;
- defeat ordinary light, medium, and household cavalry in purposeful frontal contact more often than not;
- contest peer knightly cavalry through alignment, horse condition, formation depth, equipment, leadership, and support;
- countercharge hostile heavy cavalry with real stopping and reversal power;
- break or grievously disorder infantry already fixed, exposed, turning, compressed, or depleted;
- survive enough contact to master the immediate collision and withdraw as a reduced elite formation;
- create a visible battlefield crisis that the enemy must anticipate with spears, reserves, terrain, crossfire, or its own heavy cavalry.

They should not:

- defeat prepared deep spears by status or armor;
- preserve full shock after poor alignment, premature impact, horse loss, broken weapons, or leader failure;
- remain in dense melee indefinitely;
- screen, scout, counterraid, or chase light troops economically;
- regenerate charge weapons, horses, protection, or formation between attacks;
- receive invisible knightly morale, armor-piercing, or home-ground bonuses.

## 9. Core Rhythm: Hold and Prepare

Hold is the disciplined preservation of the formation; Prepare is the construction of a viable decisive action. Together they prevent Sweden’s rarest cavalry from being consumed by low-value movement, missile exposure, or premature contact.

A valid preparation requires:

- a high-value target or battle problem;
- a reserve position protected from wasteful fire;
- adequate warning and observation;
- firm enough ground;
- formation and acceleration space;
- friendly lanes kept clear;
- infantry, missile, or cavalry support where needed;
- a credible extraction and rally route;
- preserved rider and horse condition.


## 11. Post-Impact Mastery

Master is the short post-impact phase in which Riddare exploit the heavy collision without dissolving into indefinite melee.

They may remain long enough to:

- finish a disrupted mounted formation;
- widen a genuine rupture;
- drive cavalry from the decision sector;
- crush exposed support troops;
- prevent immediate enemy reform;
- secure space for Swedish infantry or lighter cavalry;
- protect their own extraction.

They should withdraw when:

- prepared spears or fresh reserves close;
- horse movement collapses;
- front files overextend;
- the target has already broken;
- leaders or standards lose control;
- the formation's decisive purpose has been achieved;
- continued melee risks converting victory into elite attrition.

## 15. Direct Swedish Cavalry Firewalls

### Svea Hastkarlar

Use Hastkarlar for breadth, screening, pursuit, counter-skirmishing, route security, and repeated smaller missions. Riddare must never become the economical answer to routine cavalry work.

### Svenska Hirdryttare

Use Hirdryttare for scarce household countercharge, crisis response, purposeful melee, and rally. Riddare exceed them in formed heavy shock and heavy countercharge, but cost more and are less flexible as a recurring household reserve.

### Frälsemän

Use Frälsemän to maintain the cavalry wing, support mounted action, protect reform, and contribute repeatedly across a campaign. Riddare decide the battle at a concentrated point rather than sustaining the wing broadly.

### Svenner av Vapen

Use Svenner av Vapen to remain close to a senior mounted action, cover exposure, exploit a protected opening, and return. They may accompany or support Riddare, but they neither reproduce Riddare impact nor become free replacement knights.

## 16. Combined-Arms Doctrine

### With infantry

Swedish spears establish safe reference frontage, fix enemy cavalry, guard the Riddare reserve, and channel threats. Shield infantry or household foot may hold the target in place. Riddare strike only through a physically open lane.

### With missiles

Archers and crossbows may disorder horses, force shields, expose movement, or fix attention. Fire must cease or redirect before Swedish heavy cavalry enters the lane.

### With other cavalry

Hastkarlar shape the mounted picture. Hirdryttare answer secondary crises. Frälsemän stabilize and support the wing. Svenner av Vapen cover and follow a major action. Riddare deliver the decisive heavy collision.

No passive combined-arms bonus exists. Every benefit comes from physical timing, position, support, and surviving capability.

## 18C. Alignment, Wheels, and Lane Grammar

A decisive mounted action requires five connected spaces:

1. **Holding area:** protected reserve position with information and command connection.
2. **Dressing area:** room to set intervals, frontage, depth, weapons, and leader alignment.
3. **Acceleration lane:** firm ground long and broad enough to build useful pace.
4. **Contact frontage:** the portion of the target actually reached in order.
5. **Pass-through, breakaway, and rally space:** enough ground to prevent local success becoming entrapment.

## 18E. Failed Charge Story

A failed heavy charge must create a meaningful military state rather than a weak impact followed by ordinary melee.

Possible consequences include:

- broken or lost lances;
- injured, panicked, or riderless horses;
- separated rider-horse pairs;
- front files passing beyond support;
- leaders or standards lost inside contact;
- sideways compression and blocked turns;
- isolated riders forced into sidearm survival;
- fragmented withdrawal;
- capture, ransom, surrender, or destruction;
- survival without another formed charge during the battle.

Friendly infantry or supporting cavalry may create extraction space. They cannot teleport Riddare through enemy bodies or restore their heavy-shock state.

One Riddare formation may debit:

- future leaders and instructors;
- suitable heavy-contact horses and remounts;
- mail, helmets, shields, mounted weapons, sidearms, tack, and repair capacity;
- attendants, handlers, farriers, armorers, transport, feed, water, and shelter;
- household, escort, local-defense, administrative, and diplomatic duties;
- Frälsemän, Hirdryttare, and Svenner av Vapen capacity where actual people or horses overlap;
- patronage, exemptions, reward, ransom, compensation, and political cooperation;
- long recovery time after casualties.

The first formation consumes the easiest compatible concentration. Later formations face steep marginal cost rather than an arbitrary global cap.

## 21. Campaign AI Contract

Campaign AI should recruit or maintain Riddare only when:

- a valid knightly or equivalent heavy mounted institution exists;
- the army needs decisive shock or heavy countercharge rather than practical cavalry breadth;
- qualified riders, horses, equipment, leaders, attendants, training, feed, routes, and recovery all pass;
- the faction can bear the displaced household, regional, political, and economic duties;
- earlier cavalry families remain viable enough to perform their own roles.

AI should avoid Riddare when the likely campaign is dominated by forests, marshes, sieges, poor horse supply, prepared spear armies, or duties better handled by cheaper cavalry and more infantry.

## 24. Player-Facing Card Text

**Sweden's most powerful mounted formation, Riddare combine qualified riders, selected combat horses, strong protection, disciplined shock weapons, and costly institutional support. Preserve them in reserve, create one clean lane, and commit them where a heavy charge or countercharge can decide the battle. They can dominate ordinary cavalry and devastate exposed formations, but prepared spears, crossfire, poor ground, trapped melee, and reckless repetition can destroy an irreplaceable elite.**

Suggested attributes:

- Elite Swedish Knights
- Decisive Heavy Shock
- Heavy Countercharge
- Elite Mounted Reserve
- Strong Short Melee After Impact

- Severe Recovery Burden

## 25. Visual, Animation, Audio, and Card-Art Direction

### Visual

The formation should read as Sweden's knightly cavalry ceiling through achieved concentration, not fantasy uniformity. Show stronger horses, substantial mounted weapons, shields, helmets, high mail concentration, selective additional protection, clearer leaders and standards, attendants, and disciplined formation depth. Preserve variation among providers.

Avoid universal plate, barding, identical heraldry, tournament spectacle, oversized horses, copied Danish or Norwegian colors, and later-medieval equipment without chronology support.

### Animation

Required families include reserve movement, alignment, lance or spear presentation, acceleration, heavy impact, meeting collision, weapon loss, sidearm transition, compressed mounted melee, leader signaling, extraction, guarded withdrawal, fragmented return, horse injury, rider separation, dismount, rally, rout, capture, and casualty recovery.

### Audio

Audio should clearly communicate prepare, lane clear, hold, release, countercharge, impact, press briefly, disengage, guard withdrawal, reform, horse distress, leader loss, and abort. Final Swedish-language production audio remains pending review.

### Card art

Show Riddare at the instant before or during a decisive formed charge, with a real target, visible lane, Swedish support, substantial but varied equipment, and enough background context to distinguish them from screening Hastkarlar, reserved Hirdryttare, supporting Frälsemän, and following Svenner av Vapen.
