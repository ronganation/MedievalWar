## 10. Prohibited Shortcuts

Reject any proposal that:

- restarts completed Realm or Theater research without reading current sources;
- reopens the closed comparator queue without new evidence or an in-place Realm need;
- converts retained directions into opening formations or automatic High Era cards;
- creates mounted archers, mounted crossbowmen, or localized missile cards through equipment swaps;
- treats Awdaghust conquest, market significance, or route access as military ownership;
- flattens distributed actors into universal factions;
- turns regional access into owned population or unlimited recruitment;
- moves or deletes packages before copy-first preservation, hashes, rollback, and owner approval;
- promotes Registry or Opening State candidates without owner and validation evidence;
- claims Unreal validation from static Markdown.
