## 15. Acceptance Fixtures

- **Opening 1062:** no Taifa or North African Realm receives mounted crossbowmen.
- **Foot crossbow development:** foot crossbowmen do not unlock this family.
- **Horse availability:** horses do not convert crossbowmen into a mounted formation.
- **Jinete equipment swap:** issuing crossbows to jinetes creates no competence or card.
- **Mounted infantry:** riders dismount, fire, protect horses, and remount only when source, space, time, and condition permit.
- **Mounted halt fire:** requires weapon, rider, horse, and animation proof.
- **Reload vulnerability:** pressure during spanning or transition can force withdrawal or defeat.
- **Horse loss:** survivors remain foot-mobile and do not summon replacement horses.
- **Later successor:** a source-valid Andalusi successor can reopen the family without guaranteed historical Granada.
- **Almoravid Iberian control:** control or alliance alone does not transfer the formation.
- **Comparator discipline:** a mod or miniature label creates no card, chronology, equipment, or formation.
- **Clean hold:** the current Theater queue advances.
