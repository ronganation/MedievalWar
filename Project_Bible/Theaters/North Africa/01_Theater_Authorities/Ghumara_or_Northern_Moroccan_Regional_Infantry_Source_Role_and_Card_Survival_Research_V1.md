## 3. Why the Regional Source Matters

A northern Moroccan regional source can materially strengthen the Theater because it may affect:

- routes between western Maghrebi centers and northern ports or crossings;
- hill, pass, coastal, and frontier security;
- relations among Almoravid, future Almohad, local, Iberian, and other North African powers;
- local defense, escorts, guides, levies, garrisons, allied detachments, or resistance where independently supported;
- access to Iberia without making the population an Andalusi source;
- faction-specific military expressions under different controllers;
- campaign variability when no major power fully dominates the region.

These are design applications of the Theater framework and require geography, population, diplomacy, and Realm owners. They are not claims that all listed military functions were performed by one historical Ghumara unit.

## 10. Population, Economy, and Opportunity Cost

Any future military contribution competes with local households, agriculture, pastoral activity where applicable, crafts, trade, route service, port or coastal work, local security, leadership, and institutional functions. The exact local economy and social structure require separate research.

```text
Regional population
!= spare manpower

Military tradition
!= current equipment

Local defense willingness
!= distant expedition willingness

Controller demand
!= community consent or obligation
```

Losses can damage later availability, leadership, political relations, and recovery. A controller cannot replenish a regional card outside the valid source without a separate transition or migration route.

## 11. AI and Player Contract

AI must evaluate whether the regional relationship, mission, route, population, equipment, supply, and political cost justify seeking or preserving access. AI cannot recruit the family from a generic northern Moroccan province.

The player-facing explanation should show:

- current regional relationship;
- whether access is local defense, allied detachment, limited recruitment, or mature service;
- current mission and command limits;
- strongest blocker;
- competing local claims;
- known recovery or reopening conditions;
- uncertainty where the player's faction lacks information.

## 12. Acceptance Fixtures

1. **Almoravid conquest without cooperation:** no regional card becomes available merely from map control.
2. **Negotiated local defense:** local forces defend their region but refuse distant Iberian campaigning.
3. **Allied contingent:** a bounded formation serves under its own leadership without becoming a permanent pool.
4. **Deep integration:** recruitment may deepen only when population, authority, equipment, training, supply, and recovery pass.
5. **Almohad emergence:** support is divided or absent unless the movement earns relationships; Masmuda association alone does not decide it.
6. **Rival North African dominance:** another controller may gain a distinct expression without inheriting an Almoravid profile.
7. **Source loss:** active veterans may persist while local replacement closes.
8. **Role collision:** if Atlas, Gudala, Lamtuna, levy, frontier, or garrison gameplay already covers the proposed role, no new card is admitted.
