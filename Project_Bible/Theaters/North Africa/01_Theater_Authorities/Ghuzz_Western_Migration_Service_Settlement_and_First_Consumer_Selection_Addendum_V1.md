Western Islamic factions continue to derive their ordinary light-cavalry identity chiefly from mounted javelin, regional horse, household, lancer, allied, and other source-valid families. No faction gains it from era progression, shared religion, conquest, research, or a building.

## 17. Acceptance Fixtures

- **Opening 1062:** no Realm begins with Ghuzz horse archers.
- **High Era without source:** era progression creates no formation, route, pool, or contact.
- **Almohad nonemergence:** without an established Almohad Realm, this consumer path does not fire.
- **Almohad emergence without contact:** access remains blocked until a source is discovered and reached.
- **Failed transit:** a formation may be delayed, diverted, depleted, stranded, or lost.
- **Service without settlement:** the formation can complete service and leave.
- **Settlement without spam:** a settled community remains finite and capacity-bound.
- **Mounted-javelin integrity:** Lamtuna Cavalry and Andalusi Jinetes do not upgrade into horse archers.
- **Foot-archer integrity:** available horses do not convert foot archers.
- **Finite source:** one formation cannot generate unlimited copies.
- **Ammunition and fatigue:** prolonged harassment loses effectiveness.
- **Interception:** failed withdrawal can be punished by enemy cavalry.
- **Controller transition:** a new ruler must renegotiate or lose service.
- **Alternative history:** the source may serve elsewhere, fragment, settle differently, or never appear.
