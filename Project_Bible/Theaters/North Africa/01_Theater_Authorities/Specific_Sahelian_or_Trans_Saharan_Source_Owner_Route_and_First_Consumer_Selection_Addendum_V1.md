The strongest specific interface located for this bounded pass is **Awdaghust**, a southern Saharan market and caravan terminus associated with the trans-Saharan trade system and captured by the Almoravid movement around 1054–55. That establishes a strategically important place and a conflict relationship. It does not establish a January 1062 Almoravid-owned population pool, a cooperative local military institution, a named contingent, a service agreement, or a direct unit card.

The recorded capture includes violence, seizure, and persecution directed at inhabitants associated with Ghanaian authority. That history makes an automatic post-conquest recruitment decision especially indefensible. Conquest, survival, submission, captivity, displacement, accommodation, and voluntary service must remain distinct states.

## 14. Acceptance Fixtures

- **Captured market:** Almoravid capture of Awdaghust creates strategic pressure but no recruitable local pool.
- **Local survival:** surviving inhabitants remain people with owners, status, relationships, and needs, not reserve manpower.
- **Route recovery:** restored commerce creates no formation unless a specific military source and agreement pass.
- **Existing escort:** Lamtuna or another eligible formation may protect a caravan without becoming an Awdaghust unit.
- **Resistance:** local resistance can coexist with commercial importance and block military cooperation.
- **Accommodation without service:** political accommodation may support market activity while field recruitment remains closed.
- **Protection-only service:** local protection can remain mission-bound and never become a general field card.
- **Coercion firewall:** captivity, confiscation, displacement, or conversion does not automatically produce soldiers.
- **External Realm:** a Hammadid, Zirid, Taifa, Ghanaian, or successor relationship requires its own reachable access and owner receipts.
- **No visual invention:** no generic southern costume or racialized silhouette is created without a source.
- **Clean hold:** the Theater advances even though no direct profile survives.
