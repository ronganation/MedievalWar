## 0.1 Purpose

This is a bounded review of the North Africa Theater's likely opening political structure and later conditional emergence possibilities. It records the strongest current direction without creating Zirid, Hammadid, Almohad, Barghawata, Saharan, or other Realm packages.

The review exists because the Theater cannot be reduced to one Almoravid faction surrounded by generic rebels. It must support living political actors, regional recruitment, shifting alignments, cross-Theater interfaces, and conditional historical developments while preserving separate owners and avoiding a forced historical script.

The strongest current direction is a Theater with at least three major political centers rather than one western Realm and a field of rebels. The Almoravid movement represents the western Maghreb and Saharan expansion system. A Hammadid Realm candidate represents the central Maghreb. A Zirid Realm candidate represents Ifriqiya and the central Mediterranean-facing coast. A later Almohad movement can arise conditionally from its own regional, religious, political, and character foundations.

## 2. Why the Three-Realm Candidate Model Is Strong

```yaml
DesignBenefits:
  - ReplacesGenericRebelsWithLivingPoliticalActors

  - CreatesSeveralPossiblePathsToLaterMaghrebiUnification
```
