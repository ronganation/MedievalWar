## 3. Authority and Ownership

### 3.1 Historical Memory owns

- realm-bounded memory-definition content;
- holder, audience, proposition, source, transmission, reception, confidence, salience, interpretation, contradiction, expiry, and revision;
- competing memory versions and memory collisions;
- memory consequences requested from specialist owners;
- memory save, migration, determinism, and presentation.

### 3.2 Historical Momentum owns

- directional pressure and carried-capacity definitions;
- drivers, resistances, conversion conditions, thresholds, decay, reversal, and termination;
- current momentum observations after authorized activation;
- momentum explanation and owner-facing requests.

### 3.3 Historical Emergence owns

- conditional pathway and candidate definitions;
- date windows and world-state gates;
- participant, identity, consent, capacity, location, authority, and owner requirements;
- pathway lifecycle, branching, dormancy, reactivation, failure, reversal, and alternate outcomes;
- admission requests for historical people, institutions, relationships, conflicts, political forms, and strategic developments.

### 3.4 These systems do not own

- underlying historical facts or evidence adjudication;
- people, lifecycle, relationships, consent, dynasty, household, marriage, or succession;
- confederational participation and commitments;
- governance, office, title, law, jurisdiction, or enforcement;
- religious doctrine, juristic opinion, or institution;
- geography, route, climate, access, control, or settlement identity;
- property, treasury, production, market, mint, stock, transport, or supply;
- military forces, pools, commands, movement, battle, siege, or occupation;
- capital project execution;
- external consent or counterpart private state;
- Realm AI command execution;
- temporal advancement.
