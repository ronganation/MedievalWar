## 2. Core Continuity Thesis

The Almoravid movement is not identical to a dynasty. Leadership may draw on personal standing, lineage, confederational recognition, religious legitimacy, military performance, delegated authority, urban support, household capacity, and political settlement. No one source automatically absorbs the others.

The opening campaign must support several credible continuity pathways:

- continued dual or layered authority between Abu Bakr and Yusuf;
- renewed or narrowed delegation;
- negotiated regional responsibility;
- stronger northern consolidation;
- stronger southern concentration;
- confederational mediation or fracture;
- household and marriage-based political change;
- later dynastic consolidation;
- non-dynastic or mixed institutional continuity;
- leadership crisis, dispute, restoration, or alternative settlement.

History creates pressure and eligible pathways. History does not guarantee a transfer date, marriage, heir, title, dynasty, capital, or empire.

Political authority is not presumed inheritable merely because property or family status passes.

## 42. Household Political Influence

Households may coordinate residence, protection, information, patronage, property, dependents, education, travel, and continuity. Household influence enters Governance and Realm AI through explicit observations and advice, not hidden universal modifiers.

## 43. Movement and Confederation Interface

Movement and Confederation owns participation, recognition, obligations, representation, breach, renewal, and fracture. Dynasty and Succession may consume those records as recognition or continuity inputs but cannot create membership, loyalty, service, or group consent.

## 44. Governance Interface

Governance owns mandates, offices, titles, jurisdiction, claims, laws, procedures, political recognition, and enforcement routing. Dynasty and Succession supplies relationship, household, eligibility, designation, continuity, regency, and inheritance observations. Family status never bypasses Governance authority.

## 45. Religious and Juristic Interface

Religious and Juristic owners provide marriage validity, consent and capacity interpretation, inheritance law, guardianship, legitimacy observation, dispute procedure, and institutional review where applicable. No juristic opinion executes a marriage, divorce, inheritance transfer, regency, or succession.

## 46. Character and Character Network Interface

Character Foundation owns person existence, age, health, incapacity, death, captivity, and location. Character Network owns directed communication, knowledge, trust, support, affection, grievance, and rivalry.

Dynasty and Succession must not infer personal feeling, loyalty, communication, or consent from kinship, marriage, household, or succession relevance.

## 47. Economy Property and Supply Interface

Economy and Property own assets, custody, accounts, costs, gifts, maintenance, dowry or dower-like arrangements where supported, estates, debts, and transfers. Supply and transport remain separate. A dynasty or household definition creates no economic capacity.

## 48. Military Interface

Military command and service do not pass automatically through kinship, marriage, succession designation, regency, or title. Every command requires Military and Governance validation. Military support is an audience-specific observation, not universal accession.

## 49. Capital and Residence Interface

Household residence, court presence, administrative concentration, and capital status remain distinct. A household move to the Marrakesh site does not create a settlement, capital phase, property transfer, government relocation, or universal political recognition.

## 50. Realm Personality and AI Interface

Realm AI may request succession readiness, household continuity, marriage feasibility, regency, guardianship, inheritance, and dispute observations. It may compare pathways and propose review. It cannot create a spouse, child, heir, marriage, dynasty, household, succession, regency, inheritance, or leadership transfer.

## 51. Historical Memory Momentum and Emergence Interface

Historical systems may supply founder memory, leadership precedent, accepted narratives, pressure, pathway eligibility, and later reference windows. They do not create current kinship, marriage, dynasty, succession, or outcome. Later Yusufid consolidation must emerge through people, relationships, institutions, recognition, and current state.

## 52. External Dynasty and Household Locks

External spouses, dynasties, households, rulers, and polities require external owner receipts for reciprocal state. The Almoravid package may author only its local claim, knowledge, proposal, or directed relationship. It may not author external consent, membership, intention, property, succession, or response.
