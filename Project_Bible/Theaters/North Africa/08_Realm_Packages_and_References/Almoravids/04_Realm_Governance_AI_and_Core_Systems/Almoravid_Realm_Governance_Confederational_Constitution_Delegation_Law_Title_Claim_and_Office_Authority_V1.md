## 5. Almoravid Constitutional Thesis

The opening political order is layered:

1. reform purpose and founder legacy provide a legitimacy and conduct vocabulary;
2. confederational participants retain distinct identities, interests, representatives, and commitments;
3. Abu Bakr may hold senior movement and military authority in bounded domains;
4. Yusuf may hold northern operational responsibility in bounded domains;
5. Aghmat presents urban, commercial, household, juristic, and local political interests that are not reducible to conquest;
6. military success may create pressure for governance but does not create administration;
7. Marrakesh may become a governance project only through phased political and material development;
8. regional authority depends on communication, presence, mandate, cooperation, records, and capacity.

The opening constitution is therefore not a single written instrument. It is the current, versioned arrangement among recognized authorities, commitments, mandates, jurisdictions, procedures, records, audiences, and remedies.

## 15. Abu Bakr Governance Interface

Abu Bakr ibn Umar is eligible to be represented as a senior movement, confederational, and military authority candidate. Governance may receive owner-validated evidence of senior mandate, delegation power, strategic coordination, dispute mediation, campaign authorization, or continuity responsibility.

Governance must not infer that Abu Bakr owns:

- every confederational participant;
- all religious authority;
- Aghmat government and property;
- all routes, markets, mints, treasuries, armies, or offices;
- Yusuf's private relationships or every operational decision;
- a hereditary monarchy;
- permanent northern renunciation or universal later title.

## 16. Yusuf Governance Interface

Yusuf ibn Tashfin is eligible to be represented as a northern operational authority candidate. A valid delegation may cover military coordination, reporting, bounded negotiation, logistics requests, Aghmat-facing administration requests, or capital-project proposals.

Yusuf's performance may support recognition, mandate renewal, expanded responsibility, or later political emergence. Performance alone does not create sole sovereignty, permanent authority, title, ownership, or succession.

## 17. Zaynab Governance Interface

Zaynab al-Nafzawiyya may participate through property, household, commercial, patronage, urban, diplomatic, informational, institutional, and political-advice channels. Governance must preserve capacity for independent support, opposition, neutrality, conditions, and changed judgment.

No marriage, office, wealth amount, property transfer, public authority, or guaranteed support is initialized from later tradition. Zaynab's influence does not require routine battlefield command or a fabricated formal office.

## 18. Confederational Constitution Interface

## 35. Juristic Opinion and Government Action Boundary

A legal opinion or fatwa may inform lawfulness, legitimacy, conduct, property, tax, spoils, market, campaign, or institutional questions. It does not itself enact policy, decide a case, impose punishment, appoint an officer, move an army, transfer property, or collect revenue.
