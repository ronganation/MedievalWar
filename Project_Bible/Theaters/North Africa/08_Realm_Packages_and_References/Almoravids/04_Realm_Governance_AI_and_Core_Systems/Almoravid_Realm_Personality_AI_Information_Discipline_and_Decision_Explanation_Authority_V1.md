## 5. Core Realm Personality Thesis

The Almoravid movement-state is strongest when it turns several different sources of power into a coherent but reviewable strategic settlement:

- reform legitimacy without a fabricated universal religious office;
- senior leadership without universal personal ownership;
- delegated operational capacity without automatic sole sovereignty;
- confederational participation without one loyalty pool;
- military effectiveness without free armies or replenishment;
- access to routes, water, pasture, markets, and oasis nodes without assuming ownership;
- urban cooperation and household influence without erasing independent interests;
- capital development without treating Marrakesh as inevitable or Aghmat as expendable.

Its principal strategic danger is **premature centralization through unsupported assumptions**. The AI must not confuse later success with present capacity.

## 6. Central Strategic Question

**Can the Almoravid movement-state build durable political, military, economic, and institutional capacity while preserving enough confederational consent, route support, urban cooperation, and leadership flexibility to survive its own expansion?**
