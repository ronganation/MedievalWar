Movement and Confederation owns political participation and commitments. Communities defines the populations and social groupings from which participants may arise. One political participant may not equal one demographic community, and one community may contain plural political positions.

## 51. Economy Property and Supply Interface

Economy and Property own production, property, accounts, markets, stocks, wages, prices, transport, and supply. Population and labor observations constrain feasible output and commitments without generating commodities or revenue.

## 52. Geography and Climate Interface

Geography owns locations, routes, terrain, water, pasture, climate, access, and containment. Communities owns presence, use, mobility, attachment, knowledge, and social consequences. A map label never creates a population record.

## 53. Military and Recruitment Interface

Military owns forces, command, readiness, casualties, recruitment pools, mobilization, equipment, horses, officers, and recovery. Communities supplies bounded demographic and social-capacity observations. Recruitment remains finite, regional, obligation-sensitive, and non-fungible.

## 54. Diplomacy and External Community Interface

Diplomacy owns external relationships and reciprocal receipts. This authority cannot author external population, institutions, loyalties, intentions, migration, or acceptance. Cross-realm community links require local records on each side.

## 55. Historical Systems and Realm AI Interface

Historical Memory may preserve plural community narratives. Momentum may create pressure toward integration, fracture, migration, or institution building. Emergence may make communities or institutions eligible. Realm AI may request observations and propose action. None creates people, communities, institutions, labor, or recruitment pools.
