## 3. Authority and Ownership

### 3.1 This authority owns

- local diplomatic knowledge and external-relation definitions;
- contact, envoy, representation, audience, negotiation, proposal, offer, response, and withdrawal contracts;
- agreements, performance, breach, suspension, termination, renewal, supersession, and restoration;
- guarantee, protection, tribute, safe conduct, access, mediation, hostage, prisoner, and conflict-termination interfaces;
- external pressure and diplomatic leverage observations;
- reciprocal cross-realm request and receipt contracts;
- diplomatic AI observation and proposal interfaces;
- diplomacy save, migration, rollback, determinism, presentation, and validation.

### 3.2 This authority does not own

- external realm, person, dynasty, household, office, title, law, military, economy, property, geography, or private state;
- person lifecycle, intention, trust, affection, fear, or private knowledge;
- internal confederational participation or commitments;
- route, port, settlement, water, pasture, maritime capacity, or geographic access;

## 24. Conflict War Ceasefire and Peace Interface

Military and shared war-state owners create and execute war state. Diplomacy owns negotiations, terms, recognition, ceasefire, peace, surrender, withdrawal, guarantee, prisoner, and settlement interfaces.

A hostile memory, claim, raid report, religious difference, failed negotiation, or breach does not automatically create war.

## 25. Mediation Arbitration and Third Parties

Mediation requires mediator identity, authority or acceptance, subject, parties, information access, process, confidentiality, proposal scope, costs, and review. Mediators cannot impose terms unless every required authority explicitly grants that power.

## 26. Hostages Prisoners and Security

Hostage or prisoner terms require person identity, lifecycle, custody, treatment, release, exchange, security purpose, jurisdiction, consent where applicable, property or payment terms, failure, and restoration. Diplomacy does not create or move the person.

## 27. Marriage and Dynastic Diplomacy

Diplomacy may create contact, proposal, counterpart negotiation, political terms, recognition, and external receipts. Dynasty, Character, Juristic, Governance, and Property owners retain marriage identity, consent, validity, household, succession, and asset consequences.

## 28. Religious and Juristic Diplomacy

Shared religious language, reform appeal, juristic correspondence, legitimacy requests, and institutional connections may support contact or negotiation. They do not create alliance, obedience, legal jurisdiction, political recognition, war, or persecution.

## 29. Confederational and Internal-External Boundary

Internal agreements among Almoravid movement participants remain with Movement and Confederation unless a distinct external polity or owner is involved. A confederational participant is not automatically a foreign state. A region, group, market, caravan, and political entity must not be conflated.

## 30. Aghmat Diplomatic Surface

Aghmat may host audiences, merchants, messengers, jurists, negotiations, safe-conduct requests, property discussions, and external reports. This does not create a formal chancery, permanent embassy district, court protocol, standardized tribute office, or universal diplomatic capital.

## 31. Sijilmasa Diplomatic Surface

Sijilmasa may involve control disputes, local elite negotiation, market and mint questions, caravan access, route security, recognition, and external owners. Coin or conquest evidence does not create current control, mint authority, tribute, treasury, or diplomatic acceptance.

## 32. Awdaghust and Saharan External Surface

Awdaghust and Saharan interfaces require external locks for local polity, settlement, market, property, tribute, route, and consent state. Almoravid files may author local claims, knowledge, contact attempts, and proposals only.

## 33. Barghawata and Tamesna External Surface

Current resistance, disagreement, religious difference, campaign history, and territorial pressure may support contact, threat assessment, mediation, ceasefire, surrender proposal, local settlement, or conflict negotiation. They do not authorize extermination, uniform hostility, automatic conquest, or ownerless opposition.

## 34. Maghrebi External Relations

Relations with regional polities, settlements, confederations, dynasties, and powers must use canonical or approved references, current knowledge, reachable contact, valid authority, and reciprocal receipts. Later Almoravid expansion cannot preload claims, hostility, friendship, or submission.

## 35. Iberian and Taifa Relations

January 1062 must not preload later taifa appeals, protector relationships, tribute terms, alliances, rivalries, military access, cross-strait capability, or intervention. Historical Emergence may make contact pathways eligible after current world-state conditions arise.
