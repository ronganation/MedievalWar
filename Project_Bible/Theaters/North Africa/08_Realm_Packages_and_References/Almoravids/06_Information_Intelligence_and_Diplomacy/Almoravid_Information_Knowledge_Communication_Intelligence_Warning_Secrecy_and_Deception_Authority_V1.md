## 3. Authority and Ownership

### 3.1 This authority owns

- realm information, observation, report, knowledge, intelligence, warning, secrecy, deception, and record definitions;
- source, provenance, verification, confidence, freshness, contradiction, interpretation, and visibility;
- communication channels, messages, dispatch, delivery, acknowledgment, translation, distortion, interception, and loss;
- reconnaissance, scouting, guide, messenger, watch, informant, analyst, and record-service interfaces;

- decision-support products and owner-facing requests;

## 21. Economic Market and Supply Intelligence

Economy owns prices, stocks, production, treasury, property, markets, transport, and supply state. Information owns observed availability, reported scarcity, delivery evidence, market talk, price reports, caravan reports, contradictions, and confidence.

A high price report does not prove universal shortage. Coin evidence does not prove spendable treasury or active mint production.

## 22. Community and Social Intelligence

Community observations may concern representation, grievances, burdens, migration, institutional capacity, assistance, neutrality, or resistance. They must not be converted into a single loyalty meter or claim to know every community member's view.

## 23. Governance Administrative and Legal Information

Governance owns mandates, offices, laws, jurisdiction, records, procedure, and authority. Information owns availability, transmission, authenticity, access, awareness, and interpretation of governance records. A valid law unknown to an actor may affect conduct differently without ceasing to exist.

## 24. Religious and Juristic Information

Religious and Juristic owners control doctrine, opinions, institutions, and judgments. Information may record who received a teaching, report, opinion, or public statement, through which channel, with what confidence and interpretation. One reported juristic view is not universal consensus.

## 25. Diplomatic Intelligence Boundary

Diplomacy owns external contact, representation, negotiation, agreements, performance, and reciprocal receipts. This authority owns non-diplomatic collection, provenance, transmission, intelligence assessment, warning, and secrecy. External private state remains locked.

## 26. Character and Network Boundary

Character owns personal capability, agency, life state, and immediate personal knowledge. Character Network owns directed relationship and communication state among people. This authority may reference holder-specific knowledge and messages but cannot create trust, loyalty, hostility, support, influence, or private intent.
