# 66. Specialist Decision Catalogue

## 66.1 Campaign legitimacy question

A campaign-legitimacy question begins only when Military, Governance, Movement, or an Agreement owner identifies a real strategic proposal. The Religious/Juristic owner then checks jurisdiction, available respondents, evidence, and whether the question is doctrinal, legal, political, or mixed.

Possible results:

```text
NoQualifiedRespondent
NoJurisdiction
ConsultationUnavailable
PermittedWithConditions
LawfulAccordingToOneOpinion
DisputedAcrossRespondents
PubliclyDefensibleButLegallyUnresolved
ProhibitedByAcceptedOpinion
RequiresAgreementReview
RequiresTreatmentOrSpoilsConditions
```

The result never creates a campaign. Military and Governance owners decide whether a validated command follows.

## 66.2 Tax and levy question

A tax question must identify the proposed levy, payer class, collector, jurisdiction, purpose, duration, exemptions, and enforcement owner. A generic complaint that a levy is unlawful is insufficient to set the value to zero.

Strategic consequences may include:

- public criticism;
- refusal or reduced compliance through Economy and Governance;
- confederational grievance;
- juristic disagreement;
- reputational pressure;
- a proposal to abolish, replace, reduce, or regularize the levy.

## 66.3 Spoils and captured property question

The opinion should identify whether the disputed object is battlefield spoil, surrendered property, private property, public revenue, ransom, tribute, or an unresolved claim. Each category routes to distinct owners. One remembered founder-era allocation formula cannot govern every later event.

## 66.4 Market and contract question

A market question may require evidence from merchants, witnesses, records, inspectors, property holders, and route owners. The absence of a named opening qadi means the issue may remain unresolved, be handled by local institutions, or motivate an institution-building proposal.

## 66.5 Religious discipline question

Discipline matters require a subject, alleged conduct, evidence, applicable authority, and proportionate owner response. The system must distinguish private conduct, public scandal, movement-member breach, military indiscipline, contract violation, and criminal accusation.

## 66.6 Capital foundation question

Religious or juristic participation in Marrakesh may concern public use, worship space, property, water, market order, charitable support, or political symbolism. No approval can substitute for Geography, Property, Economy, Military, Governance, and Capital Foundation validation.
