## 29. Religious Discipline and Selected Service

Religious reform identity may inform discipline expectations, selected-service narratives, training, legitimacy, and political response. It does not automatically make troops elite, fearless, loyal, professional, or uniformly equipped.

## 30. Allied Contingents and External Service

Allied contingents remain externally owned. Diplomacy defines agreements. Military owners define capabilities and command interfaces. Economy owns payment and supply. Communities retain personnel sources.

## 31. Contract and Mercenary Service

Contract service requires market access, available providers, terms, pay, duration, equipment, command, conduct, withdrawal, casualties, replacement, and external or regional ownership. It is never an unlimited emergency pool.

## 32. Garrisons and Site Defense

Garrison definitions are distinct from garrison instances. Aghmat, Sijilmasa, Marrakesh, route anchors, camps, and crossings receive no garrison because a strategic value or building exists.

## 33. Siege Labor Engineering and Support

Siege capability may require labor, specialists, timber, tools, metalwork, draft animals, transport, stores, reconnaissance, officers, security, and time. Laborers do not become combat units automatically.

## 34. Recruitment Burden

Recruitment can reduce agriculture, herding, transport, craft production, caravan security, household support, local defense, and institutional continuity. Repeated mobilization may increase grievance, avoidance, displacement, contract cost, and recovery time.

## 35. Casualty Replacement and Recovery

Replacement is not replenishment magic. Recovery must track surviving personnel, wounded and disabled people, returned service, captured and missing personnel, equipment loss, animal loss, officer loss, household consequences, training time, and political willingness.

## 36. Capture Spoils and Recovered Materiel

Captured equipment, animals, and supplies remain owner-validated resources. They may be unsuitable, damaged, contested, unfamiliar, inaccessible, or politically restricted. Victory does not instantly replenish a unit.

## 54. Migration and Rollback

Migration preserves unknown, ReviewOnly, Quarantined, regional, external, depleted, recovering, reserved, conditional, later, and rejected states. Legacy unlimited recruitment, building-only unlocks, faction-wide unit access, and generic manpower values must be decomposed.

Rollback releases only reservations created by the failed transaction and restores pool availability, equipment, animals, officers, training slots, supply, burdens, and owner receipts. It never resurrects casualties or erases valid prior commitments.

## 63. Final Military Roster Summary

The Almoravid roster is built from finite access to people and institutions, not a menu that manufactures soldiers.

- Communities define people and burdens.
- Confederational and political owners define obligations and consent.
- Regions define access rather than ownership.
- Economy and Property provide equipment and animals.
- Officers, instructors, time, infrastructure, routes, and supply determine whether recruits become formations.
- Casualties and repeated service damage future availability.
- Local, regional, allied, contract, and later-development forces remain distinct.
- Research and buildings organize capability but never create people, equipment, horses, camels, officers, or pools.
- Later imperial and Iberian military structures remain outside the 1062 opening.

**A roster becomes playable when historically defensible military families connect to finite communities, real obligations, available equipment and animals, trained officers, sustainable access, and recoverable human capacity.**
