### 2. Controlling Verdict

Selected Almoravid Foot and Dismounted Almoravid Retainers remain separate cards. Selected Foot are a flexible, assignment-bounded, cross-source veteran reserve. Dismounted Retainers are a continuing, owner-bound High Era II sword-and-shield formation for sustained close contact and strongpoint retention.

A qualified Selected Foot formation or some of its surviving personnel may contribute to Dismounted Retainers only through owner-approved reclassification or reorganization. No calendar date, experience level, research, weapon issue, household label, or completed battle automatically performs the transition.
