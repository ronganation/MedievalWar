**Decision:** retain trade and caravan protection as an important **owner-bound campaign service and battle-context assignment**. Do **not** admit a separate, generally recruitable Early Era “Caravan Infantry” card now. Keep open a **specific commercial-source infantry candidate** only if an actual trade center or caravan network supports identifiable owner-released people, a defensible field-formation employment, and a battlefield choice not already supplied by Lamtuna Spearmen, Aghmat militia/axemen, or existing escort and security assignments. This is a substantive admission result, not a rejection of caravan gameplay. It follows the existing calibration's ALM-EE-011 disposition: primary attachment or campaign service, with a conditional unit expression; no general fourth spear card. The later full unit profile is *not* the deliverable at this gate.

## 1. Evidence and authority precedence

**Controlling internal sources:** `Almoravid_Early_Era_Roster_Family_Calibration_Progression_and_Unit_Production_Sequence_Authority_V1.md` identifies ALM-EE-011 Caravan Escort Spearmen as route/caravan/agreement-bound service, explicitly not yet a card; `Almoravid_Early_Era_Infantry_Family_Source_Role_and_Progression_Authority_V1.md` treats caravan spear escorts as assignments unless independent source and battle role survive; `Almoravid_Recruitment_Matrix_V1_2.md` lists thirteen direct candidates, treats caravan security as a protected claim and assignment, and excludes the older `Camp_and_Route_Guards_V1.md` direct-card proposal. The latter profile remains a valuable **comparison and retained research artifact**, not permission to reinstate its rejected direct-card status. `Almoravid_Military_Roster_Recruitment_Pools_Obligations_Strategic_Access_and_Development_Authority_V1.md` identifies caravan/route escort as a capability portfolio and `ALM-SF-06_CaravanRouteEscortAndGuideService` as an authoring container, not an active source ID or ready force. Its labels must not be silently equated to the later matrix's S06 Selected Foot or S09 external/contract labels: these are distinct naming layers, and the older matrix has internal historical S-number collisions. No source ID is assigned here. `Almoravid_Movement_Confederation_and_Trade_Authority_V1.md` owns the separation of commitment, caravan, route, water, market and military delivery; `Almoravid_Economy_Property_and_Military_Supply_Authority_V1.md` owns caravan/market/stock/transport interfaces. The shared recruitment, assignment and regional-controller frameworks own conservation and access, not a free commercial troop pool.

**Historical evidence, bounded:** Sijilmasa's long-distance caravan role is documented by Dale R. Lightfoot and James A. Miller, “Sijilmassa: The Rise and Fall of a Walled Oasis in Medieval Morocco,” *Annals of the Association of American Geographers* 86.1 (1996), 78–101. Northwestern University's Block Museum, *Caravans of Gold: Sijilmasa*, presents the oasis as a Saharan trade interface. Jeannette Plummer Sires and Gonzalo J. Linares Matás, “Those who were bound together,” *Azania* 60.3 (2025), 422–442, DOI 10.1080/0067270X.2025.2513184, discusses the strategic importance of trans-Saharan trade networks for Almoravid expansion. These establish commercial context, **not** an attested named infantry corps, uniform weapons, an Almoravid monopoly on caravans, specific 1062 protection contracts, or a field-recruitment pool. Later imperial economic and coinage evidence must not be back-projected to January 1062.

## 2. What item 3 must add to the faction

The player should care whether a real commercial route, market access agreement or caravan organizer can obtain protection. Choosing to release armed people for a caravan should make those same people unavailable for local security or field recruitment until returned; withdrawing protection can leave a shipment, merchant relationship or water stop exposed. A successful escort protects an actual journey and may yield owner-routed delivery, trust or information; it does not generate a flat faction income bonus. The commercial path should create decisions about **which journey to protect, who supplies protection, which route and water rights are valid, whether the contract permits diversion to war, and what is lost if the escort is taken away**. This is the Almoravid-specific value of the service, distinct from a renamed generic guard card.

Three layers must stay distinct:

1. **Commercial protection agreement:** organizer, contributor, beneficiary, mission, geography, term, payment/reciprocity, conduct, recall and return. Agreement is not personnel.
2. **Escort assignment:** actual owner-released people, leaders, equipment and support attached to a specific caravan, route segment or market movement. Assignment is not a separate recruitable unit.
3. **Conditional field formation:** a sufficiently coherent, separately identifiable body that an owner explicitly permits to muster, fight and persist away from a caravan. It is a candidate for a card **only** after the admission test below. A single escort splitting off in an ambush does not itself meet the test.

### 2.1 Working names, not institutional assertions

“Caravan Escort Spearmen” is the existing functional service name. “Trade and Caravan Infantry” names this research question, not an approved unit. A future source-specific card could use a historically and linguistically reviewed role name linked to its actual owner and place. Avoid “Merchant Guild Guard,” “Christian Guild Infantry,” a universal Sijilmasa Guard, or a generic “Almoravid Caravan Infantry” if the institution, religion, ownership or universal access is unproven. Commercial networks can contain different owners and participants; no one ethnic or religious label generates a military institution.

## 3. Admission test: one defensible new card or no new card

| Gate | Required proof for separate card | Present decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Specific trade-center, organizer, route-network, community, or contracted provider; current owner, actual eligible people and recurring service | **Not established for a separate 1062 field pool.** Existing caravan/route service is an authority interface. |
| Access | Agreement or owner-valid service right, actual market/route access, command, recall and field-release terms | **Conditional.** Market importance or conquest is insufficient. |
| Distinct campaign use | Organized commercial protection with visible displacement of escort, guide, transport and merchant-security obligations | **Pass as service/assignment design.** |
| Distinct battle use | A coherent fieldable formation with training, leadership, equipment and tactical decision not reproduced by existing spear, civic or close-support sources | **Unproven.** Convoy defense alone does not make a new card. |
| Scale and persistence | People can muster together and persist/recover as a military body when the caravan ends; field use is actually permitted | **Unproven.** A temporary escort remains an assignment. |
| Conservation | No duplicate people, spear/shield issue, guide capacity, animals, water, transport, officers or local defense | **Mandatory; not yet tested on source records.** |
| Historical naming and chronology | No unsupported corps, guild, fixed uniform, trade monopoly or opening contract | **Only functional working labels pass.** |

**Result:** `ServiceAndAssignmentRetained; SeparateCardHeld`. The decision does not automatically merge all commercial security into a single existing unit either. If later evidence supports a genuinely distinct **commercially organized, source-bound formation**, reopen the card gate with its own source and battlefield decision; otherwise retain the full commercial-security gameplay through assignments, regional service and contract interfaces.

### 3.1 Explicit separate-card survival conditions

A candidate must pass **all** of these, not merely sound attractive: (a) independently identified organizer/source owner and release authority; (b) enough real qualified, unreserved people, leaders and compatible kit for a recognizable formation; (c) service terms permit field deployment beyond the current caravan and establish command, duration, pay or reciprocity, recall, casualty responsibility and return; (d) a distinct battle rhythm, such as organized convoy defense across a moving column, successive halts and a bounded fighting withdrawal, with actual formation training and clear counterplay, **rather than merely an existing spear unit assigned to a convoy**; (e) campaign access remains source/contract/geography-bound; (f) a full collision test against every current consumer and a player/AI choice that justifies the card. This rhythm is a **design hypothesis**, not a claim that a historical corps used it or that the engine supports it now. If any decisive gate fails, use the assignment model without losing the gameplay.

## 4. Source and recruitment pathways to investigate

**A. Aghmat commercial and civic interface:** Aghmat is a meaningful northern commercial interface, but the civic S03 source already has militia, bowmen and axemen competing with market, store, gate and public-order duties. A market contact may negotiate security, yet cannot create a fourth Aghmat pool or field-service right. Test whether an actual merchant-organized source exists outside, or in a distinct owner-controlled portion of, civic defense. If not, use Aghmat militia or other eligible defenders on a bounded caravan assignment. Do not infer opening market ownership, minting, inventory or trade income.

**B. Sijilmasa oasis and long-distance network:** historical commercial importance makes this the strongest **research interface**, not automatic Almoravid recruitment. Validate settlement and market authority, merchant/organizer consent, route and water access, equipment, qualified people and field-release terms with the respective owners. Sijilmasa's market, mint, caravan staging, settlement control and military source are separate records. A later valid contract may produce an external or regional contingent; it does not transfer its people to a realm-wide pool.

**C. Draa, Sus, Atlas and Saharan corridors:** investigate named communities, route-protection commitments, guide and water service, and actual local source/season. Massufa, Lamtuna and other groups are not interchangeable “desert escort” people. Their existing military, pastoral, transport and household duties remain protected. An existing Lamtuna spear detachment or Massufa-linked guide party may escort a caravan under owner permission without becoming a new permanent infantry family.

**D. Awdaghust and other external southern interfaces:** current control, compliance, market access and route rights are unresolved or externally owned. No opening troop pool, tribute, market ownership, or caravan protection is authored from a conquest narrative. A later external provider needs an actual agreement and a named owner.

**E. Contracted commercial provider:** a real paid or reciprocal provider could create a distinct service route if contract, personnel, equipment, command and replacement are separately evidenced. “Mercenary” or “market” is not an infinite emergency recruitment button; the provider retains its source identity. This route may support a limited regional or external contingent, but only a distinct field formation warrants a new unit card.

## 5. Side-by-side collision decision

| Existing family or capability | It already owns | What a trade/caravan candidate would need to add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lamtuna Spearmen | Organized spear-and-shield field frontage, mounted-threat deterrence and some route/camp duties | A separate commercial owner/service basis and distinct convoy-specific field formation; not another long-spear line. |
| Aghmat Militia, Bowmen and Axemen | Locally owned civic defense, market/store/watch claims, ranged defense and axe close support | Verified commercial organization beyond the same civic people and equipment, or stay an assignment. |
| Selected Almoravid Foot and Ribat Guardians candidate | Scarce mobile crisis reserve and proposed committed objective-defense choice | Neither becomes routine paid caravan security; an escort request competes for their actual people and kit if owners permit. |
| Camp and Route Guards V1 | Earlier ordinary close-support proposal includes caravan, water, baggage and route defense | **Current matrix excludes this direct card.** Its role is a collision comparator, not a source to resurrect or rename as caravan infantry. |
| ALM-EE-011 Caravan Escort Spearmen | Existing calibrated caravan protection service with conditional formation possibility | This decision specifies its commercial-source admission test; it does not add another candidate number or silently promote ALM-EE-011. |
| Guides, messengers, mounted scouts, camel riders and pack/baggage service | Information, route knowledge, transport, animal handling and mounted security | Infantry cannot gain free intelligence, water, camels, mounts, carrying capacity or escort speed by name. |

**No forced symmetry:** a trade center may have a strong security service without a dedicated infantry card. Conversely, a later owner-validated commercial formation can coexist with ordinary escorts if its personnel, obligations, field organization and battle choice are genuinely different. Same people may move between assignments only through authorized release and reassignment; they never occupy two formations simultaneously.

## 6. Player-facing campaign contract now; hypothetical card only after gate

**Current service choice:** “Protect this caravan or route segment.” Show organizer, source owner, available escorts and guides, protected local duties, service scope and return, route/water/seasonal uncertainty, cost or obligation, arrival and strongest blocker. Possible actions: negotiate protection; assign eligible existing infantry or a bounded escort; request guides or mounted screen separately; delay departure; select another permitted route; accept a clearly described risk. A decision is a request, not a free unit or guaranteed safe arrival. In a battle involving a real caravan, its assigned guards can appear through existing formation or attachment rules; the caravan's cargo and animals remain separate from a field infantry roster entry.

**Conditional future unit-card preview, not a live recruit button:**

> **Working label:** Commercial Caravan Foot, subject to historical/linguistic review.  
> **Class:** owner-bound convoy-defense infantry, only if separately admitted.  
> **Battle promise:** organize a defensive screen around an actual moving convoy, hold a threatened halt long enough for a controlled withdrawal, and preserve the route of escape.  
> **Best use:** defend a short, constrained convoy contact with verified guides, water, supporting spears or missiles, and a retreat route.  
> **Weakness:** dispersed frontage, cargo/animal congestion, flanking and missiles, water loss, separated wagons or pack strings, prolonged heavy melee.  
> **Campaign cost:** taking the field formation away can strip a commercial network of its real protection and jeopardize contracts or journeys.  
> **Status:** hypothetical card concept, **not admitted**; do not publish in normal settlement recruitment.

This proposed battle behavior must be compared in a sandbox with ordinary Lamtuna spear escort, Aghmat civic defense, the rejected Camp and Route Guards concept, and the existing ALM-EE-011 assignment. A caravan can be defended effectively without a bespoke troop. If the player cannot tell why the new card exists when the same qualified spear formation receives a convoy mission, reject the separate card.

## 7. Reservation, logistics and recovery contract

Potential contributors remain owned by their actual communities, merchants, organizers, civic institutions, confederational groups or contract providers. A protection commitment identifies the party making it, its scope and the people actually delivered; it does not reserve every member of a group. Before departure or military detachment, validate personnel, leaders, weapons, shields, guides, messengers, animals and handlers where required, cargo security, water/pasture rights, route knowledge and permission, food, compensation, medical/recovery, replacement and return. Protect existing caravan traffic, household and pastoral labor, civic watch, local defense, route/water security and other military assignments. Reserve atomically; failed requests release only their own provisional claims.

**Service states:** proposed → negotiated → accepted with limits → source responds → personnel/equipment assembled → caravan or detachment departs → in transit → contact/delay/diversion/arrival or loss → receipt and return/recovery. These are proposed interface stages, not claims that a runtime pipeline exists. A caravan may depart without a new infantry card if other lawful protection is adequate. A contract may expire while surviving guards remain in service only under valid renewed terms; the end of a contract does not teleport or delete people. Losses distinguish killed, wounded, missing, captured, dispersed, returned and recovering; lost cargo, animals, shields, guides and route information belong to their respective owners. Neither a successful delivery nor battle victory automatically refills personnel or treasury.

**Player and AI parity:** both must see the same owner-controlled capacity and information uncertainty, not a hidden AI caravan troop dispenser. A visible strongest blocker should say whether source permission, available qualified people, local protection floor, equipment, contract scope, water, guide, transport, route or arrival prevents the requested service. `Unknown` must not be displayed as `zero` or `available`.

## 8. Evidence questions and acceptance fixtures

**Historical and owner questions:** identify a particular 1062-compatible commercial organizer or protective community; determine whether any documented military service was routinely caravan-bound or could be field-deployed; establish whether any specific place's merchant/civic organization was distinct from existing militia; check spear/shield and other equipment against date and source; determine service command and payment/reciprocity without assuming a guild; verify Sijilmasa/Aghmat/Awdaghust opening owner and market/route access separately. Scholarship on trade significance does not answer these formation-level questions.

**Positive fixtures:** (1) owner-valid existing spearmen escort a caravan without creating a new card; (2) an escort request visibly reduces the same source's available field or civic protection; (3) a verified contract can be limited to a route and recalled under terms; (4) an actual independently organized field formation, if ever proved, can be evaluated as a separate conditional card; (5) a blocked route or water right prevents departure despite available infantry; (6) veterans persist when new service closes; (7) a caravan's outcome is routed to its organizer and cargo owners, not credited as automatic realm income.

**Negative fixtures:** (1) a market or route generates troops; (2) Sijilmasa historical importance equals opening Almoravid military ownership; (3) the rejected Camp and Route Guards card is reinstated by changing its name; (4) ALM-EE-011 is silently converted into a fourth general spear card; (5) a Christian guild or a religiously uniform merchant corps is fabricated; (6) the same people serve as Lamtuna Spearmen, escort, guides and new infantry simultaneously; (7) pack camels become combat mounts or free transport; (8) a trade agreement creates cargo, revenue, water, security or safe arrival; (9) exact 1062 pool quantities or opening units are invented.

## 9. Phased handoff, rollback and final disposition

**Phase A, current static decision:** preserve commercial protection as a service; hold the separate card. Acceptance: specific collisions and historical uncertainty recorded; no new source ID, matrix count, opening formation or live card. Evidence: direct comparison with the existing calibration, matrix, realm trade/economy authorities and historical commercial research. Rollback: withdraw only this decision candidate; leave every predecessor unchanged. Owner gate: Almoravid roster, history, trade/market, source/community and recruitment owners.

**Phase B, conditional source and card re-review:** if a named commercial source or contract is established, test owner, permission, service scope, people, equipment, formation persistence and unique battle decision. Acceptance: a distinct source *and* role, or a recorded assignment-only result; no symmetry card. Evidence: owner receipts, dated historical basis, source collision and player-choice comparison. Rollback: retain service path, withdraw only unsupported card candidate. Owner gate: history, roster, regional/external, battle and linguistic owners.

**Phase C, read-only service evaluation:** after approved owner schemas, show route, water, contract, protection and strongest-blocker information without reserving people. Evidence: owner query and AI/player parity fixtures. Rollback: remove read-only candidate bindings. Owner gate: trade, geography, logistics, recruitment, AI and UI owners.

**Phase D, later transaction and battle sandbox:** only after authorized service and assignment transactions, test atomic reservation, escort withdrawal, caravan contact, return and recovery; if a card survived Phase B, compare its battlefield behavior against existing units. Evidence: recruitment, caravan, battle, save/load, replay and rollback receipts. Rollback: restore only sandbox changes and prior valid assignments. Owner gate: transaction, campaign, battle, save and project owners.

**Phase E, production/opening gate:** separate stable IDs, registry, opening-source records and Unreal 5.8 import/compile/runtime/save/replay/performance evidence are required; this file grants none. No automatic January 1062 caravan, unit, market control or route access.
