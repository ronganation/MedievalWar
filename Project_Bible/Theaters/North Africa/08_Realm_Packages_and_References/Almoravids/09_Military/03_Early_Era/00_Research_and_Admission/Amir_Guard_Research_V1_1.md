The previous research was correct that ordinary personal protection should remain a readable assignment portfolio. The stronger conclusion is that this does not exhaust the roster question. An Amir-centered household and retainer layer can support a distinct mounted guard family if a continuing ruler-household source, horses, equipment, officers, pay or maintenance, quarters, and service continuity are established. This family would represent concentrated household and noble mounted capability rather than generic faction cavalry or a duplicate of Selected Almoravid Foot.

## 2. Nobility and Retainer Gap

The current eight-unit roster covers confederational field infantry, civic militia, trained archers, selected reserve infantry, foot skirmishers, mounted javelin cavalry, camel route troops, and allied regional medium cavalry. What remains comparatively underdeveloped is the ruler-household and upper-retainer military layer. That layer should not be copied from Norman feudal knighthood, English household institutions, or later palace-guard models. It needs an Almoravid-specific source grounded in the Amir's household, trusted retainers, mounted command needs, gifts, support, and political relationships.

```yaml
PotentialDistinctCapabilities:
  - ContinuingPersonalService
  - HighTrustCommandProximity
```
