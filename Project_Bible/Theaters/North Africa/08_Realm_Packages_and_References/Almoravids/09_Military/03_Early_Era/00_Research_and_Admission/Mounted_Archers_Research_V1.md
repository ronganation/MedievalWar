The mounted-archer concept survives as a serious future capability, but it does not pass admission as the ninth current Almoravid unit. Mounted archery requires more than riders, horses, and bows existing separately. The project needs a verified source capable of shooting effectively from the saddle, a defensible chronology, actual horse and equipment access, a training and command tradition, and a battlefield role that does not merely combine Lamtuna Cavalry mobility with Almoravid Archer missiles.
