The current name should remain. **Selected Almoravid Foot** is not perfect as a historical formation title, but it is more accurate than the proposed sword-based alternatives because selection, command reliability, assignment, and reserve function define the family. The present profile deliberately permits spear, sword, axe, or another owner-approved melee weapon. Renaming the unit “Selected Almoravid Swordsmen” would falsely turn one possible equipment state into the unit’s defining institution.

The required improvement is therefore not cosmetic renaming. The next profile must make the quality difference legible through source selection, veteran and officer scarcity, reserve discipline, morale behavior, equipment consistency, deployment access, source depletion, and recruitment and upkeep direction.
