## 0.1 Research Verdict

A distinct selected-foot layer is conditionally admissible for master-profile planning as a **small, finite, assignment-driven reserve assembled from proven infantry, veteran cadres, and owner-released higher-quality contributors**. The layer adds real capability when it protects command, archers, standards, crossings, camps, and a threatened frontage or conducts a bounded counterattack. It must not become a generic elite infantry pool, a disguised household guard, or an automatic upgrade from Lamtuna Spearmen.

The located internal comparator stack supports the method of separating ordinary regional service, selected service, household service, castle or custodial professionals, and later royal guard development. The current external search located broad descriptions of disciplined Almoravid infantry but did not provide sufficiently reliable, source-specific evidence for an official formation called Selected Almoravid Foot. The name is therefore a readable gameplay composite pending historical and linguistic review.
