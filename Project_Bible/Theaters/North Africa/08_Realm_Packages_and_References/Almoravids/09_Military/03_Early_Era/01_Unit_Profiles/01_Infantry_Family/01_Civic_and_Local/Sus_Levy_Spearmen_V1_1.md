Create **Sus Levy Spearmen** as the preferred Sus-localized expression of the persistent Almoravid Levy Spearmen family. ### 5. Player-Facing Description

Sus Levy Spearmen are regionally organized spear-and-shield troops drawn from valid communities and service relationships in the Sus. They provide a dependable field layer for armies that can no longer rely only on confederational contingents, civic militia, and scarce selected veterans. The formation can reinforce a battle line, relieve a threatened position, defend a road or crossing, secure a camp approach, and support a regional campaign without pretending to be elite infantry.

Their usefulness comes from availability, organization, and regional support rather than exceptional individual quality. Recruitment removes people, leaders, weapons, stores, transport, and labor from towns, districts, farms, workshops, markets, routes, and local defense. A provincial levy ordered too far from its support base or retained beyond its service terms may lose readiness, cooperation, and replacement depth even before battle losses are counted.
