# PART I. HUMAN-READABLE CURRENT PROFILE

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Aghmat Axemen
- **Class:** trained axe-and-shield field and close-support infantry
- **Source:** finite Aghmat civic contributors qualified, released, trained, and integrated for confederational field service
- **Quality:** ordinary-to-trained field close-combat infantry, above basic civic militia but below selected veterans
- **Primary role:** aggressive field close support against light, exposed, disordered, or locally fixed infantry
- **Best terrain:** broken frontage, camps, fieldworks, streets, gates, narrow approaches, and obstacle-rich ground
- **Best use:** close after spears, archers, javelinmen, or terrain have disrupted the enemy
- **Major weakness:** cavalry, missiles, long spears, heavy infantry, and prolonged open-field combat
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Uses one-handed axes and shields for direct, readable close combat.
- Strikes harder in the close press than Aghmat spear militia but has less reach and weaker mounted-threat resistance.
- Counters enemy skirmishers only after a protected approach closes the distance.
- Performs well in streets, gates, narrow approaches, camp entrances, and broken formations.
- Exploits enemies already disrupted by missiles, fatigue, terrain, or another friendly formation.
- Makes short counterattacks rather than prolonged unsupported assaults.
- Remains below Selected Almoravid Foot in leadership, equipment consistency, reserve discipline, and reliability.

### Campaign

- Draws from the same finite Aghmat civic ecosystem as Aghmat Militia and Aghmat Bowmen.
- Requires actual qualified people, battle-ready axes, shields, leaders, training, civic release, food, water, transport, and recovery.
- Competes with gate, market, store, water, fire-response, public-order, workshop, transport, and household duties.
- Does not become a faction-wide Almoravid unit simply because Aghmat is controlled.
- May persist as veterans after recruitment closes.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Aghmat Axemen are civic axe-and-shield infantry raised from qualified defenders and close-combat contributors in Aghmat. They provide an aggressive option beside the city's defensive spear militia, closing on enemy skirmishers, fighting through streets and gates, and counterattacking troops already disrupted by missiles, terrain, or another formation.

The axe gives the unit a strong close-combat identity but does not make the formation elite or grant automatic superiority against shields and armor. Aghmat Axemen have less reach than spearmen, limited protection, and poor security against cavalry. The unit performs best when protected during the approach and committed for a short, favorable fight.

Every formation draws from Aghmat's finite civic population, leaders, shields, axes, workshops, stores, transport, and support. Raising axemen can reduce the city's spear militia, bowmen, watch, craft labor, repair capacity, and public-order depth. The unit is therefore accessible and enjoyable without becoming disposable spam.
