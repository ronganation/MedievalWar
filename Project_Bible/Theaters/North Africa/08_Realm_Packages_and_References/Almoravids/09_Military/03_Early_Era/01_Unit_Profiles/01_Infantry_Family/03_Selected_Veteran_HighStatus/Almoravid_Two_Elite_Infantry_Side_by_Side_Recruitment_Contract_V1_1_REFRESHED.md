**Verdict.** Design two appealing, simultaneously visible *candidate choices* wherever both can be evaluated: **Selected Almoravid Foot** is a high-trust veteran field reserve selected from existing owner-held sources; **Ribat Guardians** is the proposed scarce shielded infantry for a committed objective-defense mission. The player can choose either, or both if genuinely separate unreserved contributors and all other capacities permit. There is no global either/or fork, no free second elite pool, and no presumption that the second card is already approved or present in January 1062. The current recruitment matrix recognizes Selected Foot only; this contract is a non-mutating proposal for later additive reconciliation, not a stealth update to the matrix or roster count.

**Source and comparator ledger.** The immediate unit sources are `Selected_Almoravid_Foot_V1_2.md` and `Ribat_Guardians_V1_1.md`. Current control references are `Almoravid_Recruitment_Matrix_V1_2.md`, `Military_Recruitment_Pool_Constitution_V1_1_Authority_Reference_Correction.md`, `Military_Muster_Concentration_and_Mobilization_Framework_V1.md`, and `Almoravid_Early_Era_Roster_Family_Calibration_Progression_and_Unit_Production_Sequence_Authority_V1.md`. `Housecarls_V1_1_Master_Unit_Profile_FULL.md` is used only for the general benchmark that scarce elite service can carry owner-specific displacement and protection costs; its household institution, response pattern, equipment, and source family are not transferred. Historical background is limited to Humphrey J. ## 1. The player choice, not an arbitrary scarcity trap

| Question | Selected Almoravid Foot | Ribat Guardians (working name) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why recruit? | Keep proven infantry and trusted leaders fresh to answer the crisis that has not yet appeared. | Commit a scarce shielded body to the position that must resist a foreseeable assault. |
| Battlefield promise | Stabilize and counter: reinforce spear frontage, protect archers/command, rally and cover withdrawal. | Commit and hold: establish a short defensible frontage, endure contact, rotate or locally counter-move, withdraw if position is untenable. |
| Personnel pathway | Existing S06 selected-service **assignment layer** across validated, owner-released sources; not a new population pool. | Proposed owner-released defenders with demonstrable position-defense competence, from a specific confederational, movement, route or site source **only if its owner and duty are verified**; no S06 extension or new source ID assumed. |
| Qualification bottleneck | Proven service/training, trust, experienced leaders, reserve discipline and valid release. | Actual source and defense-service terms, shielded close-defense competence, leaders and mission suitability; a ribat label or belief is not a qualification. |
| Equipment bottleneck | Source/mission-compatible shields, weapons, protection and signals; spear/sword/axe/mixed expressions do not create separate pools. | Serviceable shield and one-handed close weapon for principal concept; sword-and-shield preferred only if the source really supplies it. |
| Who pays? | Donor formations, veteran training, archer/command security, households and communities; scarce officers and equipment. | The actual contributor's local watch, camp, route, community or site protection and the shared officers, shields, weapons, muster and transport. |
| Recruitment geography | An accessible muster or settlement with real source-owner contribution and assembly support; not every faction settlement. | A verified source within a real service and field-release radius; not every frontier, mosque, ribat label or occupied town. |
| Deployment | Muster, inspect, train/integrate, travel, concentrate, then join the army; no instant fresh reserve. | Muster, equip and train for position defense, travel, concentrate, then reach and defend the assigned objective; not a site garrison teleported into an army. |
| Strategic regret | Spending the only fresh reserve early can leave archers or command exposed to a second crisis. | Spending scarce defenders on one objective can expose the home source and leave the army short of mobile response. |
| Status now | Existing review-only profile and S06 matrix expression; not an opening formation. | Review-only full profile; separate card/source/name/1062 availability held for owner review; not included in matrix V1.2. |

**Selection principle:** either card should be worth recruiting in a different situation. A player with a mobile army expecting uncertain threats may favor Selected Foot. A player protecting a known crossing, camp entrance or defensive objective may favor Guardians. This is design intent, not a promise of specific historical formations or tuned superiority.

## 2. Local source accounting: two card views, one world

The settlement or muster panel is a **window into actual contributing sources**, not the owner of all troops in the province. Before Ribat admission, the ordinary live recruitment panel continues to expose only currently admitted definitions. The two-card layout in this document is therefore a **design-review comparison preview**, not a normal settlement screen. It must label Ribat Guardians `Research candidate: cannot recruit`, provide no live recruit button, create no queue entry, and remain outside the current roster count. After and only after separate-card admission, source binding, owner authorization, and matrix reconciliation, a live side-by-side settlement or muster view may distinguish *owner access verified*, *capacity currently available*, *recruitment authorized*, *assembling*, and *ready to join*. Approval of the display concept alone never unlocks the unit or a faction-wide source.

**Source graph:** source owner → eligible people and existing assignments → owner release/service basis → available qualified candidates → leaders, weapons, shields and stores → protected civil/military minimums → muster site, training and route → formation and arrival. The S06 label describes the Selected Foot assignment layer; it is **not** a container of duplicate veterans. A future Ribat source expression may overlap actual people with S06's underlying donors, or may draw a distinct owner-held body. Both cases are legal only with explicit source records and one-person-one-current-primary-use. A Ribat card must not be inserted into S06 simply because both are high quality, nor be granted a fabricated S13 or other unused source ID.

**Protected claims before either request:** ordinary Lamtuna and other field frontage; veteran file leaders and trainers; archer/command cover; camp, water, crossing and route watch; caravan/transport and pastoral or household labor; civic/site defense and local relief; wounded care and reconstitution. Reserve actual officers, equipment, assembly space, food, water, transport and return capacity; the weakest decisive input can block a card. Shared shields or swords can constrain both even when personnel are distinct. Population informs source capacity; it is not a direct army manpower account.

**Eligibility is not exclusivity.** If an owner has enough *distinct* qualified, unreserved people, compatible equipment and command to support both without breaching protected duties, both orders may proceed, subject to real queues and travel. If one contributor qualifies for both, a committed order makes that contributor unavailable to the other. A player may inspect both before committing either. Cancellation or owner-valid reassignment releases only the canceled request's outstanding reservations; it does not conjure back a deployed unit, heal wounded, erase prior costs or silently transfer ownership.

## 3. The two recruitment contracts

### 3.1 Selected Almoravid Foot: select and keep fresh

**Request:** named requesting command, mission (reserve/crisis protection), source owners, scope, duration, destination, return, water and support. **Owner response:** specific eligible veterans, trusted leaders and high-trust contributors, including high-status/household-connected foot only if actually present and released. **Qualification:** service or training, health, unreserved status, command response, ability to work with spears, archers and signals. **Issue:** compatible shields, actual source-approved primary weapons, variable protection, campaign kit; no sword-only gate. **Formation:** select and assemble from validated donors, establish reserve cohesion, inspect and concentrate. **Cost:** selected source loses ordinary leaders, readiness and security; repeated selection can erode the broader army. **Recovery:** surviving contributors and equipment return to their actual owners or continue under renewed assignment; replacement does not instantly recreate veteran quality. The source family S06 remains an assignment layer, not a people generator.

### 3.2 Ribat Guardians: commit and hold, conditional admission

**Request:** actual contributor owner, defensible mission/objective, geography, duration, field-release or site-duty permission, home-defense floor, command, relief, return. **Owner response:** identifiable qualified defenders and leaders with relevant shielded close-defense service or training, *if such a source is established*. **Issue:** serviceable shields, one-handed close weapons and protection actually available; sword-and-shield is a preferred silhouette, not a guaranteed historical or stockroom fact. **Formation:** assemble, inspect and train for short-frontage hold, relief, controlled countermove and withdrawal, then travel to the assigned army/objective. **Cost:** weakened source defense and shared elite-quality officers, shields, weapons, training and logistics. **Recovery:** survivors and equipment remain source-owned; reassigning Selected Foot veterans is an explicit transfer, not simultaneous staffing. **Gate:** a valid independent card needs a defensible source/employment distinction; if evidence only supports a defensive assignment of Selected Foot or an existing garrison, preserve the tactical behavior and do not create a duplicate recruitable unit. Ribat does not itself establish a building, religious office, named corps or dedicated opening pool.

## 4. Player-facing side-by-side recruitment panel design

**Design-review default view:** two comparison cards with a single common “Elite infantry from local and contributing sources” context strip. Until the Ribat gate passes, this layout is not the production recruitment screen. The strip is a readable summary of overlapping claims, **not** a hidden aggregate pool. Each card shows: role and best use; current state; actual owner and source; available formation capacity or `Unknown`; decisive personnel/leader/equipment requirement; principal protected duty at risk; queue and muster location; earliest *owner-derived* readiness and army arrival condition; strongest blocker; and one-line consequence for the other card. A comparison toggle reveals the deeper source ledger without forcing a confirmation for every individual.

Example **illustrative UI copy, not actual 1062 inventory**:

When future owner data exist, display statuses such as `Available`, `Available with burden`, `Conditional`, `Reserved`, `Recovering`, `Blocked`, `Closed` and `Unknown` according to the existing matrix. **Never display Unknown as zero.** Show a specific blocker (“route watch minimum would fail,” “qualified officers already reserved,” “shield issue incomplete,” “owner permits local duty only,” “will arrive after the army departs”) only when backed by that owner's record. Where both could be raised eventually but not before departure, let the player choose to wait, take a ready alternative, depart with a partial force under the muster authority, or plan a later join-up. None of these actions teleports the unit or silently changes an obligation.

## 5. Shared queue, reservation and response sequence

1. **Preview both:** query actual owner records, current source and assignment state, protected minimums, equipment, muster and route. No preview holds capacity.
2. **Choose one or both:** identify intended mission, service scope, duration and destination; compare forecast displacement of the other card. An interface selection is a request, not yet a formation.
3. **Ask owners and validate:** a contribution can be accepted, limited, delayed, refused or unable. Refusal is not automatically treason. Re-evaluate the second card after each valid response or state change.
4. **Reserve atomically:** people, officers, weapons, shields, stores, assembly, transport, water, route and recovery/return as applicable; block conflicting claims at their actual owners. Commit or roll back as one transaction. No preemption without the owning authority and an explicit consequence.
5. **Queue and muster:** actual local assembly/inspection/training capacity controls whether requests can overlap, stage or wait. If a request cannot fit, expose the constraint; do not impose a universal “one elite slot per settlement.” A qualified source may be elsewhere, and a camp or regional muster may be more appropriate than a town barracks.
6. **Concentrate and arrive:** call, response, transit, muster, inspection, organization, concentration, field readiness, departure and deployment remain separate. An accepted request can arrive late, partially equipped or understrength; it cannot become a full army stack by clicking Recruit.
7. **After service:** account for casualties, missing/captured people, equipment damage, officer loss, service renewal or return, source defense, political trust and recovery. New formation, replacement of losses and healing are separate decisions.

**Priority and tie-breaking:** no permanent priority for either elite card and no global “first clicked always wins” policy. Already valid protected reservations remain protected; the actual request/owner priority and transaction ordering govern conflicts. If both requests race for the same unreserved contributor or shield, show the conflict and allow a player choice **before** final commitment, then evaluate the second request on remaining capacity. Exact priority algorithm, queue length and travel duration remain with the shared transaction and movement owners; this document does not allocate numbers or claim implementation.

## 6. Focused scenarios to prove this is fun and fair

**A. One local window, one departure:** both previews appear where definitions can be inspected. Source data support one scarce veteran/officer/equipment combination before the army leaves. The player chooses the flexible reserve or the committed defender; the other card updates to a specific blocker. The restriction comes from actual people, gear, assembly or time, not an arbitrary either/or switch.

**B. Both are genuinely possible:** different qualified owner-released contributors and sufficient separate leaders/equipment/support exist, with valid service and home-defense floors. Both can be queued and eventually fielded; if assembly or roads cannot carry both at once, stage their arrivals. The first formation does not spawn the second or eliminate its identity.

**C. Guardians can defend locally but not depart:** a verified source has a valid site or route-defense service but not field release. Show local-only or mission-limited access; do not recruit it into a distant army. Selected Foot may still be available from another owner if its own conditions pass.

**D. Swords unavailable:** Selected Foot can be evaluated with an approved non-sword equipment configuration. Guardians remain blocked for the intended sword-and-shield expression or require explicit owner-approved alternative and renewed distinct-role review. Do not issue the same swords or shields twice.

**E. Late response:** one owner accepts conditionally and another delays. The army may depart with the ready force or wait; a late contingent joins only through real travel and concentration, not a free recruitment queue completion.

**F. Losses and closure:** after battle, Selected Foot veteran losses degrade donor cadre; Guardian losses reduce their real source's defense and equipment. New recruitment can close while existing veterans persist; disbanding does not fully restore trust, training, people or damaged shields.

**G. No Ribat admission:** the planning preview remains research-only. The position-defense concept can become an assignment for existing qualified foot without a second live card or hidden duplicate pool. This is a fallback, not the preferred design outcome.

## 7. Cross-faction discipline and historical firewall

England Housecarls show that elite recruitment can be owner-specific, scarce, politically costly and visible in household-security consequences; the shared muster framework also compares their fast response *when already near their owner and equipped*. This is **a systems benchmark**, not a claim that Almoravid Selected Foot or Guardians are English household troops, mobilize at the same speed, or share an S05 source. The Almoravid roster calibration requires source/role separation and persistence rather than copied foreign unit labels. For Almoravid service, confederational participation is not a blank check: each actual owner, contribution response, route, water, command, displaced duty and return condition must be evaluated. Historical writing on *ribat* does not justify a standing, uniformly armed Ribat Guard corps in 1062. The name remains a replaceable gameplay label; the defensive choice is the design proposition.

## 8. Ownership, validation and stop point

**Owned here:** a realm-specific, side-by-side **definition and player-experience contract** for two candidate elite foot choices. **Owned elsewhere:** historical evidence and name; actual people and population; source permission and political relationship; equipment/property; assignment and atomic reservation; settlement and muster capacity; route and movement; battle behavior; roster admission; opening unit/pool; Registry; Unreal. The current 13-profile matrix is a prior snapshot, not proof that this new candidate is already integrated. Its S06 Selected Foot record remains unchanged. A later matrix successor must be copy-first and preserve its existing rows while adding only an owner-approved Ribat source/relationship if the separate-card gate passes.

**Static acceptance checks:** (1) both choices have distinct battle decisions and no universal either/or; (2) S06 is not a new people pool; (3) Ribat has no invented source ID or building; (4) overlap is recorded at contributor/equipment/owner level; (5) actual protected claims and strongest blocker are visible; (6) a local request does not create a ready army unit; (7) both may coexist when real capacity allows; (8) failure rolls back only its own reservation; (9) player and AI share constraints; (10) historical uncertainty and 1062 non-activation are explicit. These are **document checks**, not a tested simulation.

**Gate sequence:** owner review of Ribat source, name, equipment and distinct-card survival → if the card survives, approve the static two-card recruitment definition → only after the remaining infantry decisions, reconcile the existing recruitment matrix and progression copy-first → authorize a live side-by-side UI only after matrix/source binding passes → read-only eligibility tests → atomic transaction sandbox → battle and UI tests → separate Opening State and Unreal promotion gates. Evidence at each later gate: source-owner records, overlap/rollback receipts, queue/arrival captures, AI parity and owner sign-off. Rollback at this gate: withdraw this standalone contract and preserve both attached unit profiles and the existing matrix unchanged. No extra index, README or process artifact is requested or created.
