# PART I. HUMAN-READABLE CURRENT PROFILE

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Selected Almoravid Foot
- **Realm:** Almoravid movement-state
- **Class:** selected veteran and high-status infantry reserve
- **Quality:** highest-reliability Early Era Almoravid foot reserve, above ordinary and trained field infantry but below permanent household, guard, or later professional heavy infantry
- **Source:** proven veterans, trusted leaders, distinguished confederational fighters, eligible household-connected retainers, and other high-trust or high-status foot contributors selected through owner-valid release or assignment
- **Availability:** narrow, finite, source-depleting, equipment-contested, officer-dependent, and not automatically opening-active
- **Primary role:** crisis reserve, archer and command protection, threatened-frontage reinforcement, rally cover, and bounded counterattack
- **Best use:** behind Lamtuna or other valid spear frontage, near Almoravid Archers, command posts, standards, camps, water points, routes, crossings, and withdrawal lines
- **Major weakness:** small pool, slow recovery, high veteran and equipment cost, vulnerability to premature commitment, and inability to hold an entire battle line cheaply
- **Development:** may stabilize as a recurring selected-service practice without becoming a palace guard, household corps, or professional standing regiment
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Waits in compact reserve rather than extending routine frontage.
- Reinforces a threatened section of the spear line before a local failure becomes an army-wide collapse.
- Screens Almoravid Archers when enemy skirmishers, infantry, or mounted troops compress the missile line.
- Protects commanders, standards, messengers, rally points, and the communications space needed by a mobile confederational army.
- Conducts short, controlled counterattacks against disordered or overextended enemies.
- Covers withdrawal, reorganization, water-point defense, camp alarms, route emergencies, and convoy crises.
- Loses value when committed too early, kept in prolonged melee, scattered among routine guard tasks, or deprived of officers and source cohesion.

### Campaign

- Is assembled by selecting actual personnel, leaders, and equipment from existing sources.
- Weakens the ordinary formations, communities, households, escorts, camps, and training cadres that contribute.
- Requires contribution terms, owner release, mission scope, duration, command relationship, water, transport, camp support, equipment, recovery, and return.
- Cannot be recruited indefinitely from a building or a faction-wide elite pool.
- Repeated losses can hollow out both the selected formation and the ordinary Almoravid infantry system beneath it.

### Access

- Requires demonstrated service, current readiness, discipline, valid assignment, and unreserved status.
- Requires a selected commander and experienced subordinate leaders.
- Requires compatible shields, weapons, protection, campaign kit, and support.
- Requires the contributing source to remain viable after selection.
- A decision may authorize selection but cannot create contributors, veterans, weapons, officers, or source depth.

### Development

- Can improve reserve discipline, signals, rally practice, source records, equipment consistency, combined-arms coordination, and reconstitution.
- Can retain veterans even when new selection closes.
- Does not automatically become Amir's Guard, palace infantry, household troops, professional urban infantry, or dismounted cavalry.
- Can coexist with later specialized families because its purpose remains assignment-driven field continuity.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Selected Almoravid Foot are proven infantry and veteran leaders drawn from the movement's validated contributing sources for duties too important to leave to ordinary frontage alone. They reinforce threatened spear lines, protect archers and commanders, secure standards and rally points, answer camp and water-point alarms, and deliver controlled counterattacks when an enemy formation has become exposed.

Their quality is paid for elsewhere. Every selected fighter or leader removed from a Lamtuna-associated formation, community contribution, escort, household, site, or veteran cadre leaves that source less able to defend itself, train replacements, protect routes, or recover from losses. A player who builds too many selected formations weakens the broader army that makes those formations useful.

Selected Almoravid Foot are most effective when held back until the army needs them. They are not a cheap main line, an armored shock regiment, or a royal guard. Their battlefield value lies in disciplined commitment at the correct moment and in preserving the cohesion of a mobile spear, missile, mounted, camp, route, and command ecosystem.
