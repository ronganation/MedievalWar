# PART I. PLAYER-READABLE UNIT

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Maghrebi Slingers
- **Class:** light sling skirmishers
- **Quality:** affordable local or regional missile troops whose usefulness depends on practiced slinging, loose-order movement, ammunition access, and protection from stronger formations
- **Source:** named Maghrebi communities, settlements, regional owners, or military contributors with actual slinging practice and valid service access
- **Availability:** broader than trained field archers but still finite, source-specific, duty-contested, and not automatically opening-active
- **Primary role:** sustained harassment, disruption of light troops, screening, and pressure from rough or broken ground
- **Best use:** operate ahead of or beside the main line, throw repeated stones at exposed light infantry and missile troops, exploit slopes and broken ground, then withdraw through friendly protection
- **Major weakness:** cavalry, close combat, concentrated archery, disciplined heavy infantry, poor withdrawal space, and premature fixation
- **Era behavior:** persists from Early Era into both High Era development bands with evolving equipment, appearance, organization, and regional localization
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Delivers repeated low-cost missile harassment without duplicating the bow, javelin, or crossbow battle cycle.
- Uses longer harassment endurance than foot javelinmen, but lower precision, direct stopping power, and formation reliability than trained archers.
- Pressures exposed skirmishers, lightly protected infantry, animals, crews, and troops already disordered by terrain or another formation.
- Benefits from slopes, rocky ground, field obstacles, settlement approaches, walls, and positions that protect the slinging cycle.
- Can screen the main line, contest enemy skirmishers, and make an enemy advance less comfortable.
- Should withdraw before cavalry or formed infantry fixes the unit in melee.
- Does not receive hidden anti-armor power merely because sling projectiles can strike with force.

### Campaign

- Gives the Almoravid roster an unusual but readable Maghrebi missile identity.
- Can draw from more than one validated source without becoming one universal faction pool.
- Competes with local defense, herding, transport, labor, watch duties, other missile service, and the ordinary needs of the contributing community.
- Requires actual slings, compatible ammunition, basic leaders, food, water, route access, and recovery.
- Does not require a complicated player-facing transition system. If a valid settlement or regional source supports the unit, the recruitment pool appears and recovers under the ordinary finite-pool rules.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Maghrebi Slingers are light regional skirmishers who use simple slings and readily collected or prepared ammunition to trouble exposed troops before the main lines meet. Their stones lack the clean battlefield certainty of trained bow fire or the close-range violence of heavy javelins, but the slingers can maintain pressure, disturb enemy movement, and punish lightly protected targets without consuming expensive arrows or bolts. They are most useful on slopes, broken ground, settlement approaches, and the flanks of a protected line. Maghrebi Slingers are vulnerable if cavalry reaches them or if formed infantry traps them in close combat. Used with room to withdraw, they give the Almoravid army a distinctive and historically grounded western Mediterranean skirmishing family that remains useful even after stronger archers and crossbowmen appear.

Maghrebi Slingers should be enjoyable because the unit changes how the player shapes an approach. The formation is not merely a weaker archer.
