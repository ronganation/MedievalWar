# PART I. HUMAN-READABLE CURRENT PROFILE

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Massufa Javelinmen
- **Realm:** Almoravid movement-state
- **Class:** light foot javelin skirmishers
- **Quality:** useful early confederational skirmish infantry, not elite melee troops
- **Source:** qualified Massufa-associated contributors with valid owner release, javelins, light shields or approved protection, leaders, water, route, camp, and recovery support
- **Availability:** finite, source-bound, ammunition-dependent, politically conditioned, and not automatically opening-active
- **Primary role:** forward screening, short-range javelin harassment, counter-skirmishing, rough-ground work, ambush support, and approach disruption
- **Best use:** ahead of or beside the main body, in broken or rough ground, against exposed light troops, and where a protected withdrawal path exists
- **Major weakness:** fast cavalry in open ground, longer-ranged missiles, dense shielded infantry, ammunition exhaustion, and sustained melee
- **Development:** persists as a Massufa-associated light infantry family and may improve without becoming mounted guides, archers, spearmen, or elite shock troops
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Deploys ahead of or beside the spear and archer body.
- Throws a limited javelin supply at exposed, advancing, disordered, or poorly protected targets.
- Screens Lamtuna Spearmen and Almoravid Archers during deployment and contact.
- Contests rough ground, narrow approaches, camp edges, route approaches, and ambush positions.
- Withdraws through friendly gaps, terrain, or prepared lanes before cavalry or heavier infantry closes.
- Can enter emergency melee against broken or isolated light troops but should not hold a conventional line.
- Becomes far less valuable after ammunition exhaustion but remains a living formation capable of screening, warning, or withdrawal.

### Campaign

- Draws from a specific Massufa-associated source rather than a faction-wide light-infantry pool.
- Competes with guide service, route and water security, caravan escort, pastoral work, herd protection, local defense, household survival, and community leadership.
- Requires javelin manufacture, acquisition, repair, issue, carriage, recovery, and replacement.
- Gains value from valid local knowledge and route support without receiving perfect intelligence or free movement.
- Casualties damage the same communities, guide networks, caravan functions, and leadership sources that support later recruitment.

### Access

- Requires a verified Massufa-associated owner or community and a valid military contribution.
- Requires qualified foot skirmishers rather than every Massufa person.
- Requires serviceable javelins, compatible shields and sidearms, internal leaders, signals, training, water, camp, transport, medical, and return support.
- A building, research decision, faction label, population total, or route name cannot create the formation.
- Exact January 1062 availability remains owned by Opening State and supporting source authorities.

### Development

- Can improve through veteran instruction, javelin supply, shield consistency, withdrawal discipline, signals, ambush practice, and regional experience.
- Can produce veteran Massufa Javelinmen without becoming an elite universal skirmisher.
- May coexist with Massufa guide attachments, Lamtuna mounted javelin cavalry, Saharan camel spearmen, and Almoravid Archers.
- Does not automatically convert into mounted guides, archers, spearmen, swordsmen, or later professional infantry.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Massufa Javelinmen are lightly equipped foot skirmishers contributed from eligible Massufa-associated sources. They move ahead of the main Almoravid body, screen vulnerable formations, contest rough ground, and throw short-ranged javelins into exposed or advancing enemies before withdrawing behind spears, archers, terrain, or prepared gaps.

The unit is not an elite assault force. Its power comes from timing, short-range missile pressure, local movement, terrain, ambush positioning, and the ability to disrupt an approach before the main line receives contact. Fast cavalry, longer-ranged archers, dense shields, and sustained melee can destroy the formation quickly if withdrawal fails.

Every card represents real people, weapons, leaders, and service obligations. Recruiting too deeply can weaken guides, caravan escorts, pastoral security, water access, local defense, households, and later Massufa contributions. The underlying accounting remains mostly behind the scenes so the player still experiences a clear Medieval II-style skirmisher card.
