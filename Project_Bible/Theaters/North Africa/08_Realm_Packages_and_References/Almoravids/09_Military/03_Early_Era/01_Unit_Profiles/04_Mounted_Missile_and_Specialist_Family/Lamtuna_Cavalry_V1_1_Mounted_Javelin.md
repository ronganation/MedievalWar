# PART I. HUMAN-READABLE CURRENT PROFILE

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Lamtuna Cavalry
- **Realm:** Almoravid movement-state
- **Class:** light horse-mounted javelin skirmishers
- **Quality:** mobile Early mounted skirmishers with limited melee and charge capability
- **Source:** qualified Lamtuna-associated riders, suitable horses, remounts, tack, handlers, officers, and owner-valid mounted service
- **Availability:** finite, horse-limited, rider-limited, support-dependent, and not automatically opening-active
- **Primary role:** mounted javelin harassment, screening, anti-skirmisher pressure, flank security, controlled withdrawal, and attacks on exposed missiles
- **Best use:** harass exposed flanks, drive off foot skirmishers, pressure unprotected archers, screen the army, and withdraw before heavier troops fix the formation
- **Major weakness:** missile exhaustion, prepared spears, dense terrain, concentrated archery, stronger cavalry, prolonged melee, water and fodder failure, and failed withdrawal
- **Development:** persists as the opening mounted-javelin family while later Jinetes-style regional light cavalry, allied combat cavalry, lancers, mounted archers, and heavy cavalry remain separate developments
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Screens the army during deployment and movement.
- Protects the flanks of Lamtuna Spearmen and Almoravid Archers.
- Intercepts enemy skirmishers, light cavalry, raiders, and exposed missile troops.
- Harasses flanks and exposed light troops with a finite javelin supply without behaving like armored shock cavalry.
- Makes limited contact against isolated skirmishers, exposed archers, disordered light troops, or retreating enemies after harassment creates an opening.
- Pursues routing formations when the route is clear and command permits.
- Covers withdrawal and responds quickly to threats against camps, routes, crossings, baggage, and water points.
- Performs poorly in frontal attacks against prepared spears and in prolonged close combat.

### Campaign

- Draws from actual rider-horse pairs rather than a generic cavalry pool.
- Requires primary mounts, remount shares, tack, handlers, officers, weapons, shields, fodder, water, camp space, route access, and animal recovery.
- Competes directly with scouts, messengers, command mounts, escorts, caravans, breeding, pack functions, and future mounted families.
- Gains campaign value through screening, interception, counter-raiding, pursuit, and rapid local reinforcement.
- Does not provide perfect intelligence, automatic interception, unlimited movement, or free pursuit.

### Access

- Requires a valid Lamtuna-associated mounted source or another explicitly approved contribution.
- Requires actual riding competence and mounted formation competence.
- Requires a suitable, available, unreserved horse paired with the rider.
- Requires serviceable tack, weapon, shield, officer, handler, fodder, water, and recovery support.
- A stable, purchased horse, captured horse, research decision, or faction label cannot create a trained cavalry formation.

### Development

- Can improve rider cadre, screening, charge discipline, pursuit control, remount management, equipment consistency, and combined-arms coordination.
- May produce veteran Lamtuna cavalry without automatically becoming heavy cavalry.
- May coexist with allied northern Saharan cavalry, camel troops, mounted archers, regional Maghrebi horse, and later Andalusian cavalry.
- Does not automatically absorb allied, mercenary, regional, or household horsemen.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Lamtuna Cavalry are mobile horsemen contributed through Lamtuna-associated mounted service. They screen the Almoravid army, protect exposed flanks, intercept raiders and skirmishers, threaten vulnerable enemies, and pursue troops already broken by spear pressure, missile fire, or battlefield collapse.

This is not a force of armored knights. Lamtuna Cavalry depend on speed, timing, room to maneuver, and cooperation with the rest of the army. Their charges are dangerous against exposed or disordered enemies, but prepared spears, close terrain, sustained missile fire, heavy cavalry, and prolonged melee can stop them quickly.

Their campaign availability depends on real riders and horses. Every mounted formation consumes tack, handlers, remounts, fodder, water, route capacity, and animals that might otherwise serve command, reconnaissance, caravan, escort, breeding, or transport needs. Used carefully, the unit gives the Almoravids the mobile battlefield arm needed to turn pressure into pursuit and local success into wider victory.

## 20. Equipment Thesis

The family should present as practical mobile cavalry suited to screening, interception, and pursuit. Its identity is not built around universal mail, couched-lance shock, horse armor, or mounted archery. Equipment should remain visibly variable while presenting a clear horse-mounted javelin-skirmisher silhouette. Light spear and sidearm support self-protection, limited contact, and withdrawal rather than conventional cavalry shock.
