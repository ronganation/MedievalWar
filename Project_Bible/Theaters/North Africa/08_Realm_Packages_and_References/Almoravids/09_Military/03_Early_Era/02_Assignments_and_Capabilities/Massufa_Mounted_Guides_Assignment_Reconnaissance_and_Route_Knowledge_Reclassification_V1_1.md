## 0.1 Research Verdict

A Massufa mounted-guide family is conditionally admissible for master-profile planning, but the profile should be narrower and more distinctive than a generic light-cavalry unit. The strongest viable card is **Massufa Mounted Guides**, a small, lightly equipped reconnaissance, route-guidance, contact, warning, screening, and ambush-support unit whose combat value comes from positioning and information rather than conventional cavalry power.

The current evidence explicitly supports Massufa membership in the wider Almoravid coalition and the movement's Saharan geographic background. The retrieved sources do not explicitly establish a formal Massufa cavalry corps, a universal Massufa mount type, a named 1062 unit, or particular weapons. Accordingly, Massufa ownership, mounted-guide identity, horse-versus-camel composition, and weapon configuration remain gameplay-composite proposals grounded in the roster's need for a distinct reconnaissance card and in the project's own held Massufa light-service research.

The unit may use horse, camel, or source-specific mixed mounted contributions at the recruitment-source level, but a deployed unit must use one coherent mount class at a time unless a later mixed-mount formation test proves readable and technically viable. The preferred profile candidate is lightly armed with javelins or light spear and sidearm, avoids heavy combat, and does not create perfect intelligence or magical route access.
