## 1. Concise Verdict

The Early Era roster should express a mobile reforming confederational war system with a dependable infantry center, strong skirmishing and screening, useful but finite mounted capacity, route-aware reconnaissance, selected leader-associated forces, and limited siege depth. The roster must not become a later imperial army, an exoticized desert roster, or a complete unit card set assembled from assumptions.

## 5. Roster Shape and Battlefield Identity

```yaml
battlefield_identity:
  infantry_center: spear shield and selected foot hold the line without late heavy-infantry inflation
  ranged_layer: javelins and bows screen disrupt and punish exposed movement
  mounted_layer: light reconnaissance screening pursuit and bounded medium shock or countercharge
  command_layer: leader-associated followings provide concentration but remain scarce and costly
  mobility_layer: route knowledge guides messengers animal handlers and escorts improve campaign reach without becoming free combat strength
  siege_layer: labor and specialist support exist conditionally but mature siege corps do not
principal_strengths:
  - adaptable screening
  - movement and route awareness
  - combined foot and mounted pressure
  - defensive cohesion when properly organized
  - pursuit of disrupted enemies
principal_constraints:
  - finite mounted animals
  - limited armor and specialist equipment
  - officer and formation bottlenecks
  - supply water pasture and route dependence
  - obligations and household burden
  - shallow early siege capability
```

## 15. AI Employment Doctrine

```yaml
AI_rules:
  - preserve scouts and mounted skirmishers for information screening and pursuit
  - avoid frontal misuse of light mounted units
  - anchor lines with spear families when coherent
  - use javelins to disrupt exposed or constrained targets
  - use bows for sustained support rather than unsupported assault
  - protect scarce selected foot and leader followings
  - respect regional and external access limits
  - abstain when equipment animals officers or supply are missing
  - account for labor household and political burden
```

## 16. Counterplay and Weakness

The roster must expose counterplay through limited armor, finite mounted stock, vulnerability during concentration, dependence on routes and water, obligation refusal, shallow siege specialization, officer bottlenecks, and the risk of losing scarce selected or leader-associated formations.
