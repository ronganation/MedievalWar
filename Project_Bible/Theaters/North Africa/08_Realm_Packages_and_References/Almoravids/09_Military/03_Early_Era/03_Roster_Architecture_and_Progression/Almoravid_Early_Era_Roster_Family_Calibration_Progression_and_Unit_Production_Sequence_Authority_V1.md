## 1. Concise Verdict

The Almoravid Early Era roster should proceed as a set of connected military ecosystems rather than a linear unit ladder or a renamed version of another faction. The player-facing promise is a major western Muslim power in formation: confederational foot and mounted service, Aghmat-centered urban capability, Saharan route and escort functions, selected movement service, regional auxiliaries, and later institutional, Maghrebi, and Iberian development.

The existing Early Era candidates provide a strong initial basis, but individual master profiles must wait until each candidate has a settled source ecosystem, recruitment identity, battlefield role, campaign role, ownership class, era disposition, persistence relationship, nearest-neighbor calibration, and attachment-versus-unit result.

## 5. Benchmark Doctrine

### 5.1 England benchmark lesson

England demonstrates that a roster can contain connected service ecosystems without becoming a linear seven-tier ladder. Community mass, civic defense, trained regional service, noble household allocation, elite household service, and mounted support retain distinct sources and player value.

Applied to the Almoravids:

- lower-cost confederational foot is not automatically Aghmat militia;
- Aghmat defenders are not automatically field spearmen;
- selected movement foot is not automatically an upgrade from every levy source;
- leader-associated service is not automatically a bodyguard card;
- mounted service must distinguish scouting, skirmishing, battle cavalry, escort, and command support;
- regional expressions remain regionally owned.

### 5.2 Normandy benchmark lesson

Normandy demonstrates the need to differentiate emergency levy, organized settlement militia, regional field service, selected service, armored non-knightly service, knightly development, and multiple crossbow branches. It also demonstrates the need to reconcile survival, family collisions, adoption, and institutional development before multiplying cards.

Applied to the Almoravids:

- Spear Levy, Shield Spearmen, Aghmat Town Defenders, Selected Almoravid Foot, and Caravan Escort Spearmen require separate recruitment identities;
- similar weapons do not prove duplication, but different names do not prove distinct roles;
- the roster must resolve whether source, training, obligation, locality, equipment, or mission creates the distinction;
- later institutional forces should not delete useful Early Era services automatically.

### 5.3 External game and mod benchmark rule

Medieval II's Moors, Medieval II mods, Medieval Kingdoms 1212 AD, Rome II's Medieval 1100 AD mod, and other Total War sources may provide:

- discoverable unit-name leads;
- battlefield-role ideas;
- roster breadth comparisons;
- regional flavor prompts;
- progression and equipment presentation ideas;
- examples of population-class or regional recruitment design;
- examples of UI clarity and campaign readability.

They do not provide automatic admission, exact 1062 applicability, current Almoravid ownership, pool quantities, or historical proof. Every useful candidate must be reconciled against current historical, regional, community, equipment, animal, officer, recruitment, and campaign authorities.

## 10. Cross-Family Calibration Findings

### 10.1 Spear-family finding

The roster may support three distinct spear-bearing expressions if evidence confirms their recruitment identities:

1. broader lower-cost confederational or regional defensive service;
2. more organized field spear-and-shield service;
3. Aghmat-bound settlement defense.

Caravan escort spearmen are principally a mission-specific service and should not automatically become a fourth general spear card.

### 10.2 Missile-family finding

Saharan Javelinmen and Almoravid Bowmen are provisionally distinct by range, source, ammunition, terrain, formation, and campaign role. Neither should be admitted until the source community and opening applicability are better defined.

### 10.3 Mounted-family finding

The mounted ecosystem requires four distinctions:

- scouts and route-information service;
- battlefield mounted skirmishers;
- medium battle cavalry;
- scarce leader-associated mounted service.

Mounted Messengers and Guides remain attachments. The scout card must not absorb messenger, guide, and command-escort functions merely to appear more valuable.

### 10.4 Selected-foot finding

Selected Almoravid Foot is potentially roster-defining because it represents scarcity, repeated service, movement discipline, and higher cohesion. It must not become a religious-superiority card, guaranteed bodyguard, or universal upgrade destination.

### 10.5 Support-family finding

Baggage guards, camp and water-point security, engineering, messenger, guide, and many escort functions are important precisely because the project can model them without forcing each into a full recruitable card.

### 10.6 Major-faction completeness finding

The opening roster may remain intentionally compact if the ecosystems are deep. Major-faction status comes from development potential, regional access, institutional growth, confederational breadth, command, logistics, and future Maghrebi and Iberian interactions, not from inflating the opening unit count.
