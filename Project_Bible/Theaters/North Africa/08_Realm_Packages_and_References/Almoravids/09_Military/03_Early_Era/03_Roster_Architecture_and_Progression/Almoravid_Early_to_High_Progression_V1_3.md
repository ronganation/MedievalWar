# PART I. PROGRESSION CONSTITUTION

## 1. Whole-Roster Verdict

Almoravid progression is not a tier ladder. Early formations persist where their source relationships and material capacity survive. High Era growth broadens the roster through institutional development, regional access, external service, professionalization, and selective later adaptation without manufacturing people, equipment, animals, officers, pools, or formations.

## 2. Constitutional Separations

```text
Progression != upgrade ladder
Chronology != recruitment
Era change != unit creation or deletion
Research != people equipment animals officers or pools
Building != formation
Development access != current capacity
Later family != universally better family
Regional access != faction ownership
Specialization != replacement
Veteran persistence != open recruitment
Recruitment closure != formation deletion
```

The term Berber or Amazigh is useful at the broad cultural level, but it is too broad to function as one recruitment tribe or one universal military pool.
