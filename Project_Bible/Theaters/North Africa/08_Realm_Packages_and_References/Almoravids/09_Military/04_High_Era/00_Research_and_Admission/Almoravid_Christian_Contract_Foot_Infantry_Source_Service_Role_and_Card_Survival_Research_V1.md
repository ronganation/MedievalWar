Christian foot service is retained as a legitimate later Almoravid research direction because Christian soldiers could serve Muslim rulers and forces through alliance, exile, contract, captivity, release, resettlement, or negotiated military service. The research does not assume that cavalry evidence proves an infantry corps, that later `farfan` terminology applies automatically, or that Christian identity creates one standardized mercenary unit.

## 2. Why This Candidate Follows the Andalusi Regional Spearmen

The Andalusi Regional Spearmen close the ordinary source-owned Andalusi field-infantry gap. The next unresolved expansion-family question is whether external Christian foot service creates a genuinely different card rather than a contract skin over existing regional spears, garrison infantry, retainers, or guards.

## 19. Admission Decision

The candidate is retained because external Christian foot service can create a distinctive campaign relationship and battlefield support option. The candidate is not admitted directly because evidence of Christian contract cavalry or later farfan units does not by itself establish the required earlier infantry source, name, equipment, and mission.
