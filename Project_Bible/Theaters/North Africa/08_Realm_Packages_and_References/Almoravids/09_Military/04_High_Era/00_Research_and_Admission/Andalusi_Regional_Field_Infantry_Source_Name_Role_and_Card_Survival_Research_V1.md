This research tests whether the Andalusi expansion portfolio still needs an ordinary regional field-infantry card after frontier infantry, crossbowmen, garrisons, cavalry, and the Almoravid core are counted. It does not convert a comparator label into a historical corps.

A separate card remains useful only as the **ordinary organized Andalusi field contribution** between local militia or garrison service and specialized frontier, assault, guard, or elite families. It should form dependable regional frontage, support crossbows and cavalry, reinforce or relieve another formation, and defend a camp, crossing, settlement approach, or captured position without claiming elite quality.

The card fails if future work reduces the unit to “Andalusi Lamtuna Spearmen,” “fielded garrison infantry,” or a generic medium spear unit with different clothing.

## 2. The Roster Question

The expansion portfolio already contains or routes:

```yaml
AndalusiFrontierInfantry: WatchDelayAndWithdraw
AndalusiCrossbowmen: PrepareAndFire
AndalusiJinetes: HarassCounterAndPursue
AndalusiLancers: ChargeAndBreak
AndalusiCampaignVolunteerExpression: RoutesThroughGhaziUnlessDistinctProofPasses
```

What remains unresolved is ordinary field service that can:

- form a stable but non-elite regional line;
- protect missile troops;
- reinforce a threatened position;
- relieve another formation;
- accompany an Andalusi field army or contributed contingent;
- continue battlefield service outside routine city or frontier custody.

This is a valid gameplay question. It is not yet proof of a valid card.

## 16. Eligibility Chain

```text
Andalusi population != military source
Military source != regional field institution
Institution != owner-authorized contribution
Contribution right != unreserved people
People != trained formed infantry
Infantry != equipped supplied and field-ready formation
Field-ready formation != permanent Almoravid pool
```

## 17. Opportunity Cost

A contribution can reduce:

- local settlement and route defense;
- militia or garrison depth;
- officers and veteran cadres;
- crossbow protection;
- agricultural, craft, transport, and household capacity;
- the provider’s independent field army;
- political trust if service is extended or losses are excessive.

The same people cannot serve simultaneously as garrison, frontier infantry, field infantry, Ghazi volunteers, or another formation.

## 19. Campaign AI

The AI should request this family only when:

- a valid regional source exists;
- ordinary field infantry is genuinely needed;
- local-defense costs remain acceptable;
- officers, equipment, route, supply, and return pass;
- a specialized frontier, garrison, guard, Ghazi, or core family is not the better answer.

The AI must not maximize regional card count or treat this unit as a free upgrade to Lamtuna Spearmen.
