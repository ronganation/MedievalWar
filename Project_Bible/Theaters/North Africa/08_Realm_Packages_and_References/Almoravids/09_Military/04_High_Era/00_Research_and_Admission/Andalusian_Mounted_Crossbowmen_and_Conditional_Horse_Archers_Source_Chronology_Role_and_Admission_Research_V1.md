The stronger current admission is Andalusian mounted crossbowmen, not a generic Almoravid horse-archer family. The image reference itself states that mounted crossbowmen are recorded in Andalusian sources and not in relation to Maghrebi Berbers. External comparator material describes 14th-century Alhambra imagery in which many mounted troops carry crossbows, while one bow-armed mounted figure is treated as possibly foreign or mercenary. This supports a regional High Era II mounted-crossbow card and keeps true horse archers conditional.

The defining player decision is positional. Mobility delivers the crossbowmen to a firing point and extracts them before enemy cavalry, missiles, or infantry can fix the formation. The profile should not assume unrealistically effortless high-tempo reloading while galloping.
