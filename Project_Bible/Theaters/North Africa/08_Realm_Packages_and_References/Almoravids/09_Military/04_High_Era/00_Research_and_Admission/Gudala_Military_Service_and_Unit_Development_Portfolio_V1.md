### 0.2 High-Impact Verdict

The Gudala should no longer remain only a name in the confederational background. They provide a strong opportunity for a conditional, source-owned military expression that can appear through reconciliation, renewed participation, negotiated contribution, regional survival, or a later-start roster state. The portfolio must not assume that every Gudala-associated community shares one political position, that Gudala service exists at the January 1062 opening, or that a historical weapon specialization has already been proved.

The recommended first direct card is **Gudala Light Infantry**, a High Era I conditional regional-confederational formation using light spears, a limited javelin cast where equipment permits, shields, and sidearms. Its battlefield identity is mobile rough-ground flank infantry that can screen, contest broken terrain, support raids and counter-raids, and reinforce a threatened line without replacing Lamtuna Spearmen, Massufa Javelinmen, Aghmat Axemen, or Selected Almoravid Foot.
