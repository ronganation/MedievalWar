No reviewed source establishes a standardized corps formally called Lamtuna Heavy Camel Guard, a uniform noble camel guard, or fully mail-barded Almoravid camels. The card is admitted as a bounded High Era II gameplay composite because it extends the faction’s African camel identity, gives the camel line a strong Total War progression payoff, and remains distinct from existing camel and horse families.

The unit is not a fully armored camel cataphract, a slower Granadine Lancer, a ruler’s personal bodyguard, or an automatic replacement for Saharan Camel Riders.
