### 2. Profile Verdict

**Aghmat Garrison Infantry remain the approved conditional High Era institutional development of eligible Aghmat civic-defense capacity.** The profile is a real recruitable or validly transitioned maintained formation, not an invisible automatic garrison, not a building effect, and not simply Aghmat Militia with higher statistics.

The unit represents maintained spear-and-shield defenders supported by actual personnel, officers, stores, quarters, watch routines, relief schedules, prepared positions, repair, provision, sanitation, medical support, and replacement capacity. Its defining Total War battle cycle is to prepare, watch, occupy, hold, relieve, countermove, seal or fall back, then recover and reconstitute.

The reciprocal relationship with Aghmat Militia is controlling. Aghmat Militia may persist, narrow, seed cadre, transition through qualified lineage, split under owner authority, coexist where separate capacity exists, close to new recruitment while veterans persist, or demobilize without transition. Aghmat Garrison Infantry may also be raised from eligible unreserved capacity where all institutional requirements pass. ### 5. Player-Facing Description

Aghmat Garrison Infantry are maintained defenders raised through Aghmat's developed urban military institutions. Spear-and-shield files hold gates, streets, breaches, wall exits, camps, bridges, stores, and strongpoints while officers rotate worn defenders and commit local reserves to threatened points. They are more dependable in sustained prepared defense than civic militia, but are not elite assault troops and do not replace ordinary field spearmen.

Taking the formation into a field army creates a visible strategic tradeoff. The army gains trained defensive infantry, while Aghmat loses watch coverage, relief depth, replacement capacity, and protection for residents, gates, stores, water, and infrastructure. The unit remains finite, locally grounded, and dependent on continued service and support.
