# PART I. PLAYER IDENTITY AND HISTORICAL HONESTY

## 1. Final Unit Identity Strip

Christian Contract Foot are a later conditional external-service spear-and-shield formation. Their source is one named Christian exile, ally, contracted leader, household, warband, or separately validated settled-service community with a cohesive foot following. Their quality follows the actual provider and may range from trained to selected, but Christian identity, foreign origin, mail, or payment never creates elite status.

Their core verb is **Guard, Support, and Fulfill Contract**. They protect a defined person, camp, route, position, asset, or frontage while the agreed mission remains valid. They are strongest when the mission is narrow, the command interface works, their flanks and withdrawal route remain protected, and the receiving army respects the contract. They weaken when ordered beyond scope, stretched into general frontage, isolated, surrounded, exposed to concentrated missiles, or forced into prolonged close combat against troops that own that role.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Christian Contract Foot are cohesive external infantry serving the Almoravid movement under a defined agreement. They arrive with their own provider, officers, service history, equipment, internal relationships, and mission limits. Armed primarily with spear and shield, they guard a commander, camp, route, withdrawal lane, contracted frontage, or other agreed objective. They can reinforce an ordinary line or cover an extraction, but they are not a domestic main-line replacement and not a permanent elite guard.

Their reliability depends on more than pay. Clear orders, a valid target, functioning liaison, respected protections, adequate supplies, treatment of wounded and dependents, and confidence in departure or renewal all matter. A formation may refuse or delay an invalid order without being coded as universally disloyal. A receiving ruler who protects the contract can gain disciplined mission-bound support; one who breaches it may lose the formation, damage future access, or drive the provider toward another employer.

The profile may never emerge in a campaign. If it does, the source, leader, equipment, visual identity, exact restrictions, and quality are determined by the actual provider. The card never proves one continuous corps from 1062 and never guarantees the later name Farfanes.

## 25. Mounted Threat Boundary

Prepared spear-and-shield frontage can deter or blunt poorly supported mounted contact if facing, depth, cohesion, ground, and leadership pass. The formation has no cavalry immunity and no automatic brace. Heavy shock supported by missiles, a flank charge, shallow depth, fatigue, broken ground, or incomplete preparation may break it. The unit must not replace Lamtuna Spearmen or Maghrebi Foot Guards as the roster's dedicated broad or critical spear responses.

## 26. Missile, Fire, Reach, and Close-Melee Boundary

Shields mitigate some frontal missiles but do not erase repeated arrows, crossbows, elevated fire, flank fire, damaged equipment, or officer losses. Spear reach is useful only while files retain spacing and facing. Once compressed into sustained sword-and-shield contact, Dismounted Almoravid Retainers and other close-combat specialists should remain better choices. Fire, smoke, rubble, panic among animals or camp followers, and blocked routes can make an otherwise valid guard mission untenable.

## 30. Card-Failure Triggers

The separate card fails if any of the following becomes true:

- its only difference from Andalusi Regional Spearmen is payer, clothing, or slightly higher values;
- it anchors critical domestic frontage as well as Maghrebi Foot Guards without their maintained-source cost;
- it sustains close melee like Dismounted Almoravid Retainers;
- it becomes a universal Christian mercenary pool;
- Christian Contract Cavalry automatically supplies or replenishes it;
- battle AI cannot distinguish guarding a defined mission from ordinary line infantry behavior;
- the provider, restriction, renewal, replacement, and departure cycle has no meaningful campaign consequence.

If the card fails, preserve this authority and the attached research. Reassign only validated behavior to a source-specific allied contingent, provider attachment, regional spear assignment, camp or escort function, or another approved family. Do not retain a duplicate card for roster symmetry.

## 40. Siege, Urban, Camp, and Escort Boundaries

The formation may guard a siege camp approach, store, commander, engine park, route, withdrawal lane, or outer work when the contract permits. It does not become an engineer or machine crew. On offense, it may protect specialists or secure an agreed access point after another force creates entry, but it is not the preferred expendable breach assault. In towns it is useful around gates, courtyards, markets, wider streets, camp lanes, and protected approaches where spear files can function. Retainers or garrison troops remain better for routine custody and sustained compressed melee.
