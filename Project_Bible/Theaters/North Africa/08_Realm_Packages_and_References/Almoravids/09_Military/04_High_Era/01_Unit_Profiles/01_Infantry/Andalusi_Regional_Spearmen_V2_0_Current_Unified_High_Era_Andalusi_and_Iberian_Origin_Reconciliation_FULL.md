## 18. Battle Cycle

```yaml
states:
  - AssembleAndForm
  - SupportPosition
  - OccupyFrontage
  - ProtectMissilesOrReformSpace
  - ReinforceOrRelieve
  - HoldForHandoff
  - CountermoveIfOpportunityPasses
  - WithdrawAndReform
  - RecoverAndReturn
```

### Assemble and Form

The formation establishes files, leaders, frontage, supporting missile or cavalry relationships, and a withdrawal route. Poor assembly can produce gaps, shallow depth, conflicting commands, or delayed relief.

### Support Position

The unit forms near crossbowmen, archers, a main line, camp, crossing, or reserve lane. The unit should not drift away from the combined-arms purpose merely because an isolated target appears.

### Occupy Frontage

The formation accepts ordinary line contact and deters light or medium mounted pressure. The formation is not intended to absorb the strongest assault indefinitely.

### Protect Missiles or Reform Space

The formation protects the reload and withdrawal space of Andalusi Crossbowmen and preserves room for Jinetes or Lancers to withdraw and reform.

### Reinforce or Relieve

The formation moves through a viable route to replace or support a pressured formation. Relief consumes time, frontage, command attention, and cohesion. Relief is not instantaneous.

### Hold for Handoff

The formation retains a captured or threatened position until a garrison, guard, frontier formation, or another owner-valid force assumes the appropriate longer-term duty.

### Countermove

A limited counterattack is valid against disordered, fatigued, exposed, or light-to-medium opposition. The formation should not become a dedicated assault family.

### Withdraw and Reform

The formation leaves before encirclement or collapse, reforms with surviving officers and files, and may return to regional service after mission closure.

## 18.3 Relief Doctrine and Handoff Test

A valid relief requires a defined receiving formation, approach lane, exchange point, command signal, frontage capacity, and fallback plan. The incoming formation does not teleport through friendly troops. A rushed relief can disorder both formations, expose crossbow reloads, block cavalry reform space, or create the breach the enemy exploits.

Prepared spear frontage can deter or blunt careless light-to-medium mounted contact. The unit has no cavalry immunity, no automatic stop rule, and no guaranteed brace.
