### 5. Player-Facing Description

Atlas Pass Infantry are regionally raised foot trained to seize and control restricted mountain approaches. Spears and shields provide a credible short defensive frontage, while limited heavy javelins may punish troops compressed by a road cut, defile, slope, or broken approach. The unit can reinforce a pass, hold briefly, counterattack an exposed enemy, and disengage along a prepared exit before mobile or heavier forces close the position.

The card is strongest when the player chooses the ground, preserves the exit route, and leaves before the bounded hold becomes a trapped melee. Every formation depends on actual regional owners, qualified people, leaders, guides, current route knowledge, equipment, water, stores, pack support, service terms, and recovery.
