## 2. Controlling Verdict

The Almoravid infantry system uses one Early Era and one High Era. The High Era is not divided into numbered sub-bands and does not unlock a fixed list by date. Infantry persists, matures, localizes, branches, transitions, narrows, closes, or emerges through source survival, political relationships, technology and research, settlement and institutional development, equipment, officers, campaign experience, geography, mission need, contracts, regional access, and world state.

The current reviewed infantry roster contains persistent Early families and the completed reviewed High Era successor set listed in this authority. Exact physical file counts remain outside current control unless separately inventoried. Physical routing follows each profile's defining origin, not every acquisition method. Andalusi Frontier Infantry is removed as a redundant Total War card. Sus Provincial Infantry remains a distinct developed regional field-service card. Lamtuna Spearmen and Lamtuna Frontier Spearmen remain as a persistent parent and a conditional specialization. Aghmat Militia and Aghmat Garrison Infantry form an institutional development relationship rather than mandatory permanent parallel recruitment.

## 8. Persistent Early Families

### 8.1 Almoravid Spear Levy

The basic spear levy remains affordable, finite, locally variable secondary frontage and emergency defense where the source survives. It may mature, localize, narrow, or close at particular sources while veterans persist. Appearance alone never turns the family into professional, selected, guard, garrison, or frontier infantry.

### 8.2 Aghmat Militia

Aghmat Militia remains civic and local defense. It may persist, narrow, contribute veterans, transition through qualified lineage, coexist with maintained garrison service where separate capacity exists, or close at particular highly developed sources while veterans remain.

### 8.3 Lamtuna Spearmen

Lamtuna Spearmen remain the confederational spear spine for field frontage, bracing, mounted-threat deterrence, missile and flank protection, and rally anchoring. High Era maturation may improve equipment consistency, officers, signals, march, camp, and combined-arms practice without requiring a new card.

### 8.4 Aghmat Axemen

Aghmat Axemen persist as axe-and-shield close support, counter-skirmisher melee, disorder exploitation, and support in streets, camps, gates, and broken frontage. They do not automatically become Ghazi Infantry or merge with militia or garrison service.

### 8.5 Selected Almoravid Foot

Selected Almoravid Foot persist as the scarce veteran reserve for stabilization, command and missile protection, and controlled counterattack. Qualified lineages may contribute to Dismounted Retainers, but automatic conversion is prohibited.
