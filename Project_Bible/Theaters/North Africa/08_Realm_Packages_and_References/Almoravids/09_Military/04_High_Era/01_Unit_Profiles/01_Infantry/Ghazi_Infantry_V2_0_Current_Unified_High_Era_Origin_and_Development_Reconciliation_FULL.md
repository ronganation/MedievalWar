# PART I. HUMAN-READABLE CURRENT PROFILE

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- Unit: Ghazi Infantry
- Class: medium spear-and-shield assault infantry
- Era: High Era
- Source: bounded campaign volunteers, returning veterans, community contributors, and mission-bound followers
- Quality: variable but organized assault infantry supported by accepted leaders and veteran cadres
- Primary role: seize one important breach, camp, fieldwork, gate, street, or strongpoint
- Best use: after missiles, engineers, cavalry, or regular infantry create an approach and disorder the defenders
- Major weakness: vulnerable approach, uneven protection, command friction, cavalry, concentrated missiles, and prolonged heavy melee
- Recruitment: conditional emergence through an approved campaign call, actual people, leaders, equipment, supply, and service terms
- Persistence: objective-bound service, renewal, demobilization, veteran persistence, or verified extinction
- Opening State: not authorized
- Exact values: none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Modern Medieval II Design Card

### Battlefield

- Carries spears, shields, and sidearms in a readable medium assault-infantry formation.
- Advances through a prepared lane rather than charging blindly across open ground.
- Performs best against disordered infantry, damaged defenders, camps, breaches, streets, gates, and fieldworks.
- Receives strong morale value from a clear objective, accepted leadership, visible progress, and nearby regular support.
- Can seize and briefly secure a position after the assault.
- Loses efficiency when the objective is vague, leadership is lost, the approach is exposed, or the formation is left in prolonged melee.
- Does not receive fear immunity, fatigue immunity, automatic obedience, or suicide behavior.

### Campaign

- Does not exist as a permanent faction-wide pool.
- Emerges from a valid campaign call and actual contributor networks.
- Requires volunteers, veterans, leaders, weapons, shields, protection, food, water, medicine, transport, and return terms.
- Can serve for one siege, expedition, campaign season, or bounded objective.
- Heavy losses can reduce later volunteer response and damage political, community, and military confidence.
- Surviving veterans can form a cadre for later calls without becoming free permanent manpower.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Ghazi Infantry are motivated campaign volunteers assembled for a recognized expedition, siege, relief effort, frontier emergency, or charged objective. Armed primarily with spears and shields, the formation is intended to cross a prepared approach, break into a breach or strongpoint, press disordered defenders, and secure the position before enemy reserves can recover.

The unit is strongest when the purpose is clear, the commander is accepted, regular forces have already shaped the fight, and engineers or missile troops protect the approach. Ghazi Infantry can display formidable commitment under those conditions, but commitment is not immunity. Missiles, cavalry, obstacles, heavy infantry, leader loss, failed promises, supply failure, and a collapsing plan can break the formation.

The card represents a recognizable Total War assault unit. Beneath the card, the campaign tracks where the volunteers came from, why they answered, how long they agreed to serve, what equipment and support they possess, what communities were weakened by their absence, and what happens when the campaign ends.
