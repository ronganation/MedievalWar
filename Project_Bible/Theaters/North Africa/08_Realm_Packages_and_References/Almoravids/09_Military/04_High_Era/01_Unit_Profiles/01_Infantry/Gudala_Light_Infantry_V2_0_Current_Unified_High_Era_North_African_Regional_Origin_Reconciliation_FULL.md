### 2. Profile Verdict

**Gudala Light Infantry remain the user-approved conditional High Era North African regional profile for rough-ground movement, limited javelin opening, counter-skirmishing, flank support, gap reinforcement, raid and counter-raid support, and bounded light contact.** The light spear and shield allow useful contact after the javelin opening, but the formation is neither a dedicated javelin screen nor ordinary main-line spearmen.

Access requires a specific Gudala-associated source, a current political and military-service relationship, valid owners, eligible unreserved people, leaders, equipment, water, routes, transport, mission terms, recall, return, and recovery. Historical association, the Gudala name, faction control, identity, research, a building, or the High Era label does not create service or readiness.

The design direction is settled. Exact localization, source owners, political bindings, values, and implementation remain pending. Gudala Light Infantry remain distinct from Lamtuna Spearmen, Lamtuna Frontier Spearmen, Massufa Javelinmen, Atlas Pass Infantry, Aghmat Axemen, Selected Almoravid Foot, Aghmat Garrison Infantry, Ghazi Infantry, and Dismounted Almoravid Retainers.

### 5. Player-Facing Description

Gudala Light Infantry are mobile regional foot raised only where Gudala-associated owners and communities maintain a valid relationship with the recruiting power. Light spears and shields let the formation remain useful after a limited javelin cast, while loose order and terrain practice support skirmishing, flank protection, broken-ground fighting, and rapid reinforcement of a threatened gap.

The formation is most effective when working around a steadier spear line, missile troops, and mounted screens. Gudala Light Infantry can drive off enemy skirmishers, pressure exposed missile troops, support a raid, or contest rough ground. The unit should disengage before cavalry, heavy infantry, or a deep fresh spear formation fixes it in prolonged combat. Political access, service terms, owners, equipment, leaders, routes, water, and recovery all matter, so the card is never a free faction-wide pool.

### 32. Contact and Disengagement Boundary

The light spear permits planned, bounded contact against skirmishers, exposed missiles, broken light infantry, and compromised flanks. It does not make the formation a front-rank anti-cavalry wall or a substitute for heavier close infantry. The unit should press only while terrain, support, cohesion, fatigue, and withdrawal remain favorable. Loss of a retreat lane or fixation by a heavier formation is a major failure state.
