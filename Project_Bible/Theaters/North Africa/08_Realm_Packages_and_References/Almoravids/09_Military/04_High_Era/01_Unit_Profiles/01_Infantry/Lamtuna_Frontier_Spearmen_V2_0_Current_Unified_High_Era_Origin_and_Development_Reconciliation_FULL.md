### 2. Profile Verdict

**Lamtuna Frontier Spearmen remain the user-approved conditional High Era branch of Lamtuna Spearmen for route, crossing, water, camp, and frontier watch; warning; prepared delay; counter-raid response; rear guard; fighting withdrawal; rally; and reoccupation or rejoining.** The branch develops only where an actual Lamtuna-associated source acquires sustained frontier responsibilities and the required people, officers, guides, signals, equipment, water, routes, stores, service terms, and protected-source capacity pass.

The branch is not a universal upgrade, does not replace ordinary Lamtuna Spearmen, and does not create separate people automatically. Parent formations retain main frontage, bracing, mounted-threat deterrence, missile and flank protection, and rally anchoring. The branch trades some parent availability and ordinary frontage depth for information, route protection, delay, counter-raid response, rear guard, and controlled withdrawal.

The design direction is settled. Exact localization, source records, parent-branch bindings, values, and implementation remain pending. A frontier label, need, date, research, building, territorial control, identity, or faction status cannot create the branch or its required inputs.

### 5. Player-Facing Description

Lamtuna Frontier Spearmen are Lamtuna-associated spear-and-shield troops adapted to threatened routes, crossings, water approaches, camps, and frontier lines. They watch exposed approaches, warn nearby forces, occupy a prepared delaying position, disrupt an enemy column, and withdraw before heavier troops or cavalry can pin the formation.

Their value is measured in time, information, protected movement, and preserved routes rather than enemy casualties alone. Recruiting the branch removes people, leaders, equipment, guides, water capacity, transport, and service time from ordinary Lamtuna spearmen, mounted service, caravans, pastoral security, camp defense, and households.
