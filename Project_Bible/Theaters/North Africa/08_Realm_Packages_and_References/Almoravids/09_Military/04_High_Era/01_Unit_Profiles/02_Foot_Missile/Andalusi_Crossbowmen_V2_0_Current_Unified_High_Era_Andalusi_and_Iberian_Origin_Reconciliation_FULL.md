## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Andalusi Crossbowmen
- **Class:** trained regional crossbow infantry
- **Quality:** specialized regional missile infantry with deliberate fire and demanding support
- **Source:** finite Andalusi users, urban or fortified service institutions, crossbows, bolts, workshops, stores, officers, and regional owners
- **Availability:** later regional, conditional, finite, owner-bound, and never granted merely by conquest or research
- **Primary role:** protected direct fire, fortified defense, siege support, and pressure against selected better-protected targets
- **Best use:** walls, gates, streets, breaches, strongpoints, protected field lines, and clear firing lanes
- **Major weakness:** slow reload, weak melee, low maneuver tolerance during the fire cycle, bolt and mechanical burden, and local recall risk

- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Delivers slower, more deliberate direct fire than either Aghmat Bowmen or Almoravid Archers.
- Threatens selected exposed or better-protected targets without guaranteeing penetration or kills.
- Performs strongly from walls, gates, streets, obstacles, strongpoints, and protected field positions.
- Needs clear firing and reload lanes, subordinate control, flank protection, and a planned withdrawal route.
- Can coordinate alternating file fire only when training, spacing, leadership, and communication pass.
- Becomes highly vulnerable when forced to move, compressed, flanked, suppressed, or caught while reloading.
- Should conserve bolts against poor, distant, obscured, or low-value targets.

### Campaign

- Draws from specific Andalusi regional owners rather than a generic faction pool.
- Requires qualified users, crossbows, spanning equipment, bolts, parts, workshops, stores, instructors, officers, protection, transport, and return terms.
- Competes with city walls, gates, fortresses, siege readiness, local public order, workshops, bolt production, other regional missile troops, and civilian recovery.
- May become available through a regional contribution, city or fortified service agreement, durable integration, or a separate contract.
- Can close or become restricted after local threat, resistance, workshop damage, political recall, access loss, or severe casualties.
- Existing veterans may persist even when new regional recruitment closes.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Andalusi Crossbowmen are regional foot soldiers trained to deliver deliberate direct fire from walls, gates, streets, strongpoints, siege positions, and protected field lines. Their crossbows can pressure exposed and better-protected enemies, but each shot is followed by a vulnerable spanning and loading cycle that demands protection, space, discipline, and leadership.

The formation is not part of the Almoravid opening army. Access develops through actual relationships with Andalusi cities, communities, military owners, workshops, users, or contract providers. Conquest alone does not create cooperation, serviceable weapons, compatible bolts, experienced instructors, or a recruitable pool.

Once secured, the unit becomes a significant long-term reward. Andalusi Crossbowmen add a direct-fire capability that complements rather than replaces basic Aghmat Bowmen, trained Almoravid Archers, and Massufa Javelinmen. Their losses are difficult to replace because the people, equipment, workshops, and regional trust supporting the formation remain finite.
