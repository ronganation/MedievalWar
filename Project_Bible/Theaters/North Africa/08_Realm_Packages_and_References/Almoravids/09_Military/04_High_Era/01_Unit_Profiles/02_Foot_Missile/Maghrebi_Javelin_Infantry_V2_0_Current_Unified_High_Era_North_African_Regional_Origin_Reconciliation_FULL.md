## 3. Player-Facing Description

Maghrebi Javelin Infantry are organized regional foot who fight closer to the formed army than early light skirmishers. Protected by friendly frontage, terrain, or a prepared lane, the formation advances into effective throwing distance, releases disciplined javelin volleys, and pressures skirmishers, exposed infantry, engaged flanks, and broken local frontage.

The unit's decisive Total War choice occurs after the cast. If the target remains cohesive or mounted danger closes, the formation breaks contact and reforms behind friendly troops. If the target is disordered, unsupported, or already engaged, the formation may press a short shielded fight to secure a local advantage. That contact remains bounded. The unit is not a substitute for axemen, retainers, guards, or spear frontage.
