# PART I. PLAYER-READABLE UNIT

## 1. Unit Identity Strip

- **Unit:** Amir's Horse Guard
- **Class:** High Era household heavy cavalry
- **Quality:** scarce, trained, well-maintained mounted retainers built for decisive charges and command protection
- **Source:** the serving Amir's recognized household, trusted mounted retainers, viable horses, officers, equipment, and continuing support
- **Availability:** earned through ruler-household consolidation and mounted infrastructure, never automatically active in January 1062
- **Primary role:** shock reserve, bodyguard, controlled countercharge, command protection, and emergency extraction
- **Best use:** hold near the Amir or army reserve, wait for disruption or a dangerous breakthrough, then deliver a decisive charge and reform
- **Major weakness:** extreme scarcity, high maintenance, remount difficulty, prepared spears, concentrated missiles, poor ground, fatigue, and succession disruption
- **Player reward:** a powerful and visually distinctive cavalry unit whose arrival marks real campaign development
- **Opening State:** candidate not initialized
- **Exact values:** none promoted

## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Delivers the strongest controlled charge currently planned for the Almoravid High Era roster.
- Uses better-maintained horses, equipment consistency, command cohesion, and reserve discipline rather than universal knight superiority.
- Protects the Amir, standards, messengers, and command area.
- Countercharges enemy cavalry or infantry breaking toward the command element.
- Can attack an exposed flank once the immediate protection mission is secure.
- Can extract the Amir or cover withdrawal when the battle turns.
- Should not spend the battle chasing skirmishers, screening distant flanks, or grinding frontally against prepared spears.

### Campaign

- Requires a real Amir household and a stable continuing mounted-retainer source.
- Requires the Amir's Stables or an equivalent approved household mounted-service facility, plus horses, remounts, equipment, officers, grooms, quarters, fodder, water, and maintenance.
- Competes with command mounts, Lamtuna Cavalry, Saharan Horsemen, messengers, escorts, breeding, recovery, and household spending.
- May weaken, split, withdraw, or require reconstitution after succession.
- Surviving veterans may persist if recruitment closes, but the player cannot refill the unit from ordinary cavalry pools.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

The Amir's Horse Guard are the ruler's trusted mounted retainers, equipped and maintained for the protection of the command and for decisive battlefield action. Held near the Amir or in reserve, the guard can smash a disordered attack, countercharge cavalry threatening the command, strike an exposed flank, or force a path for withdrawal.

This is not an opening formation and not a mass cavalry arm. The guard becomes possible only after the Almoravid ruler develops a continuing household, reliable mounted retainers, suitable horses and remounts, stronger equipment maintenance, trusted officers, and the stable infrastructure needed to keep an elite mounted formation ready. Those foundations take time, investment, and political stability.

Once raised, the guard should feel like a campaign achievement. Used at the right moment, the unit can decide a battle. Used carelessly against prepared spears, concentrated arrows, bad ground, or repeated unsupported charges, the loss is difficult to replace and may weaken the ruler's household for years.
