## 2. One-Screen Design Card

### Battlefield

- Delivers a powerful formed lance charge when alignment, frontage, momentum, horse condition, and target state are favorable.
- Provides a regionally sourced heavy shock option distinct from household protection cavalry and earlier mounted-javelin families.
- Performs best against engaged, disordered, exposed, wavering, or retreating units.
- Can countercharge comparable cavalry only with sufficient formation, timing, condition, and support.
- Must disengage and reform after a committed charge rather than cycle full impact immediately.
- Loses much of its value in rough ground, narrow streets, woods, steep terrain, obstacles, or prolonged static melee.

### Campaign

- Requires a valid Andalusi regional source and owner-approved political, military, contractual, or integrated service relationship.
- Draws from qualified riders, suitable combat horses, remounts, regional officers, lances, swords, shields, protection, tack, handlers, workshops, pasture, fodder, water, transport, and recovery.
- Competes with regional light cavalry, household cavalry, escorts, messengers, local defense, breeding, training, and economic horse use.
- Can remain regionally owned while serving an Almoravid continuity realm, an authorized successor polity, or another valid controller.
- Recruitment may close while surviving formations and veterans persist.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Granadine Lancers are well-equipped regional cavalry trained to deliver formed lance charges against engaged or disordered enemies. Their more substantial protection, powerful mounts, disciplined approach, and commitment to shock combat distinguish them from the Almoravids' earlier mounted-javelin and allied medium-cavalry families.

The formation is most effective when another part of the army fixes or disrupts the target. Once a charge lane opens, the lancers form, approach in cohesion, commit at the proper distance, strike, and then either exploit a break or withdraw to reform. Prepared spears, obstacles, rough ground, concentrated missiles, tired horses, and deep reserves can defeat the charge before or after impact.

Granadine Lancers are not a faction-wide building unlock. Every formation depends on actual southern-Iberian riders, horses, remounts, officers, equipment, workshops, service relationships, and regional consent or obligation. Conquest alone does not transfer those people or resources to the realm.
