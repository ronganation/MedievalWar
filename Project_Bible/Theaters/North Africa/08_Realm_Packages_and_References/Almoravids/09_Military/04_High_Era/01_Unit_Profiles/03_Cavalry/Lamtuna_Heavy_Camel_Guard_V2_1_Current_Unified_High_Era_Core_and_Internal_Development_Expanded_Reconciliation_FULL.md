## 3. Player-Facing Description

Lamtuna Heavy Camel Guards are a later concentration of proven combat-camel riders, selected mounts, officers, armor, shields, spears, handlers, and recovery support. The formation advances more deliberately than ordinary camel riders. Its purpose is not to race horse cavalry or imitate a lancer charge. Its purpose is to move a protected camel body into one decisive local problem, disrupt a mounted threat, force open or deny an approach, and remain just long enough for the wider army to exploit the result.

The unit rewards preparation. Spearmen or infantry must fix the enemy, archers and mounted skirmishers must shape the approach, and command must preserve a clear disengagement lane. Once committed, the Guard can pressure exposed cavalry and vulnerable flanks, but the same armor and denser formation increase fatigue, congestion, water demand, and recovery cost. If the Guard remains in melee after the local mission is complete, prepared infantry, missiles, terrain, and faster cavalry can destroy an irreplaceable formation.
