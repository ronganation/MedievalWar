## 3. Player-Facing Description

Andalusian Mounted Crossbowmen are not horse archers and not ordinary foot crossbowmen given free mounts. Riders, horses, crossbows, bolts, spanning methods, tack, officers, horse holders, and posture drills must form one coherent regional service. Their battlefield value comes from moving deliberate missile power to a place where the enemy did not expect it, not from maintaining constant fire while galloping.

A successful formation reaches a screened firing position, selects a posture, establishes a fire line, delivers controlled bolts, and moves before the enemy closes. A failed formation dismounts without custody, wastes ammunition, reloads under mounted pressure, loses horses, or becomes trapped as underprotected foot troops. The unit therefore produces a strong Total War rhythm of approach, fire, danger, transition, and escape.
