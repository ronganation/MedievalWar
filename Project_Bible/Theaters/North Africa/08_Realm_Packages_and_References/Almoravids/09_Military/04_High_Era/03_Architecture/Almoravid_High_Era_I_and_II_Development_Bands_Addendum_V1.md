# PART I. PURPOSE AND CORRECTION

## 1. Purpose

This addendum divides the Almoravid High Era into two internal development bands so the roster can mature fluidly without forcing every later unit into the beginning of High Era or denying enjoyable later development because the historical polity did not survive indefinitely. It also restores unit-card readability as the governing presentation rule.

## 2. Correction Of Prior Drift

```yaml
PriorDrift:
  - ResearchAndAdmissionProcessBecameTooDominant
  - NamesBecameAdministrativeInsteadOfEvocative
  - UnitCardsWereExpectedToCarryCampaignSourceLanguage
  - HistoricallyPlausibleGameplayCompositesFacedExcessiveProofBurden
  - HighEraTreatedAsOneUndifferentiatedBucket
CorrectedDirection:
  - MakeTheBattlefieldUnitReadableFirst
  - KeepDeepMechanicsBehindTheCard
  - UseResearchToStrengthenNotSmotherTheUnitFantasy
  - PermitGroundedCompositeNamesAndRoles
  - PlaceLaterConceptsInHighEraII
```

## 3. Modern Medieval II Promise

```yaml
PlayerPromises:
  - RecognizableUnitNames
  - ClearBattlefieldRoles
  - StrongFactionFlavor
  - BroadButDisciplinedRoster
  - MeaningfulEarlyMidAndLateCampaignPayoff
  - HistoricallyGroundedEquipmentAndDevelopment
  - FamiliarTotalWarReadabilityWithDeeperCampaignSystems
  - PersistentEarlyFamiliesRatherThanWholesaleReplacement
  - PlausibleContinuationForSuccessfulFactions
```

## 4. Research Serves Play

```yaml
ResearchShouldAnswer:
  - WhatDoesTheUnitDo
  - WhatMakesItFactionSpecific
  - WhatWeaponsArmorAndFormationArePlausible
  - WhenAndHowDoesItBecomeAvailable
  - WhatNeighboringCardDoesItNotReplace
  - WhatCampaignMechanicsSupportIt
ResearchShouldNotAutomaticallyForce:
  - AdministrativeDisplayNames
  - SeparateAuthorityForEveryMinorVariation
  - NewPopulationFamilyForEveryCard
  - BespokeEmergenceSystemForEveryInterestingUnit
  - RejectionBecauseExactFormalCorpsNameIsUnattested
```
