## 4. Faction-Selection Presentation

### 4.1 Naming hierarchy

- **Almoravids** is the primary faction-selection, campaign-map, diplomacy-list, save-list, and general navigation name.
- **Almoravid movement-state** is the formal opening political descriptor.
- **Almoravid movement and confederation** is the compact explanatory form when the unfinished political structure matters.
- **Later Almoravid Empire** is a historical or emergence descriptor only and must not label the January 1062 opening realm.
- **Moors** may guide the broad Total War faction fantasy and player expectation, but it is not the canonical political name of this specific opening faction.

### 4.2 Short selection description

**A disciplined reform movement and military confederation rising from Saharan networks into the cities and plains of the western Maghreb. The Almoravids possess formidable strategic potential, experienced leadership, and access to long-distance routes, but their authority remains divided, their forces depend on finite commitments and supply, and their later empire has not yet been built.**

### 4.3 Compact interface description

**A mobile western Maghrebi movement-state balancing confederational cohesion, religious legitimacy, Aghmat consolidation, Saharan depth, and conditional expansion.**

### 4.4 One-line identity

**Bind the movement, secure the routes, and decide what kind of realm will emerge.**

### 4.5 Selection-screen strengths

- **Expansion Potential:** Strong military and political momentum supports several strategic directions without guaranteeing conquest.
- **Dual Leadership Capacity:** <Person>Abu Bakr ibn Umar</Person> and <Person>Yusuf ibn Tashfin</Person> offer distinct senior and northern operational perspectives.
- **Saharan Strategic Depth:** Route knowledge, confederational connections, mobility experience, and commercial interfaces create opportunities when access and supply validate.
- **Religious Reform Identity:** Discipline, legitimacy claims, teaching, and juristic consultation can support cohesion while creating meaningful standards and disputes.
- **Flexible Military Development:** Finite regional and group-based recruitment can support mobile, defensive, urban, mountain, and desert capabilities when people and material inputs exist.
- **Transformative Capital Choice:** Aghmat may remain central while Marrakesh develops conditionally through actual phases and resources.

### 4.6 Selection-screen risks

- **Distributed Authority:** Senior, northern, religious, confederational, urban, household, and military authority do not collapse into one ruler statistic.
- **Commitment Dependence:** Movement association does not guarantee troops, loyalty, access, or supplies.
- **Distance and Communication:** Northern and southern priorities may diverge before reports and commands arrive.
- **Finite Recovery:** Losses consume people, equipment, animals, officers, political commitments, and time.
- **Incomplete Urban State:** Aghmat is the strongest opening base candidate, but the movement is not yet a finished centralized kingdom.
- **External Boundaries:** Northern Maghrebi and Iberian expansion require specific targets, routes, political purposes, and external-owner compatibility.

- tents, camps, pack animals, water containers, route markers, guarded baggage, and mobile assemblies;
- Atlas foothills, passes, stony approaches, cultivated pockets, plains, wadis, wells, pasture, and desert-edge routes;
- Aghmat's urban, market, household, juristic, and agricultural interfaces;

- capital transformation expressed as survey, encampment, water planning, labor, material staging, and institutional concentration rather than an instant city;

## 9. Settlement and Capital Presentation
