The Almoravid portfolio must not depict a ladder from tribal backwardness to urban civilization, from Africa to al-Andalus, or from movement to inevitable empire. Its identity is the difficult conversion of several independent sources of strength into coherent but reviewable capacity while preserving Africa-only viability, finite participation, regional ownership, and alternate historical outcomes.
