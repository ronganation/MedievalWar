# PART IV. OPENING SUCCESSION AND CHARACTER FOUNDATION

## 7. Buluggin ibn Muhammad

The opened biography gives Buluggin ibn Muhammad a reign from June 1055 to 11 July 1062. It states that Buluggin led an expedition into Morocco, briefly entered Fez, and was assassinated near Tessala on the return journey by an agent of Nasir ibn Alnas.

The biography also describes a chain of earlier killings: Buluggin had succeeded after killing Muhsin ibn Qaid, later killed a vizier and the governor of Biskra, and killed Namirt bint Alnas after believing that Namirt had killed Buluggin's brother. The biography presents Nasir's action as revenge for the killing of Nasir's sister.
