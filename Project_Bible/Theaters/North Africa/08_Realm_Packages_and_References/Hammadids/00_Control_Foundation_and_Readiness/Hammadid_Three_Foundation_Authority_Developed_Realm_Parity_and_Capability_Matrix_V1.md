```yaml
    question: DoTheThreeFilesShareCompatibleReferencesStatesAndOwnerBoundaries

    question: CanFutureOwnersBuildValidatedDefinitionsWithoutGuessing
```

Every candidate addition receives one disposition:

```yaml
dispositions:
  Present:
    meaning: CapabilityIsSubstantivelyPresent
  Partial:
    meaning: CapabilityExistsButRequiresMaterialExpansion
  Missing:
    meaning: CapabilityIsSubstantivelyAbsent
  Routed:
    meaning: AnotherOwnerControlsTheStateButThisFileNeedsAnInterface
  Rejected:
    meaning: CapabilityWouldCreateDuplicationSymmetryOrForeignTransfer
  Deferred:
    meaning: CapabilityIsValidButPrematureForTheCurrentGate
  Quarantined:
    meaning: IdentityChronologyOwnershipOrEvidenceConflictPreventsUse
```
