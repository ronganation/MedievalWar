This authority organizes evidence, chronology, contested interpretations, memory eligibility, momentum, and conditional emergence before developed character profiles are authored. The purpose is not to produce a deterministic history script. The purpose is to preserve the pressures, precedents, memories, opportunities, and nonoccurrence branches from which historically grounded alternate outcomes can emerge.


## 1. Why history precedes the next character batch

- Character profiles need a dated evidence boundary for life state, kinship, office, location, and political relevance.

- Memory profiles need fact propositions, contested interpretations, transmission routes, holder eligibility, confidence, and political use.

- Historical momentum needs supported direction without importing later outcomes as opening inventory.

- Conditional emergence needs date windows and world-state thresholds rather than guaranteed scripts.

- The profile queue should follow evidence closure, not precede it.



## 2. Authority ownership

### 2.1 This authority owns

- Hammadid historical proposition classification.

- Chronology windows and contradiction tracking.

- Evidence-source and confidence requirements.

- Historical memory-family definitions and holder-eligibility rules.

- Historical momentum directions and decay, fulfillment, diversion, and failure conditions.

- Conditional historical-emergence gates.

- Historical nonoccurrence and alternate-outcome branches.

- Character and memory-profile research priorities.

- Historical-system presentation, save, migration, rollback, and validation contracts.



### 2.2 This authority does not own

- Opening State activation.

- Character identity or biological lifecycle execution.

- Exact kinship, marriage, household, court, office, or title bindings.

- Government, diplomatic, military, economic, property, population, religious, or geographic runtime state.

- Foreign private knowledge or intentions.

- Registry publication, executable payloads, importer work, or Unreal implementation.
