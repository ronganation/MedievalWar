## 2. Strong Foundational Verdict

Create the Hammadids as a distinct central-Maghrebi Realm candidate whose opening identity is not a generic eastern extension of the Almoravids and not a reduced copy of the Zirids. The Realm begins from an established dynastic and fortified political order centered on Qal'a Bani Hammad, but the preferred playable leadership boundary follows the recent assassination of Buluggin ibn Muhammad and accession of An-Nasir ibn Alnas. The opening political order is therefore established but unsettled: the capital, dynasty, court, regional authorities, returning campaign networks, western ambitions, eastern rivalry, mobile Banu Hilal actors, and future coastal development all exist as interacting sources of power rather than one uniform state apparatus.

```yaml
primary_player_promise: TurnAContestedMountainCapitalDynastyIntoTheLeadingPowerOfTheCentralMaghreb
primary_strategic_theme: AuthorityAcrossDistanceAfterViolentSuccession
secondary_themes:
  - FortifiedCapitalAndRegionalGovernors

  - MountainInteriorCoastAndSaharanConnections
  - QalatToBejaiaDevelopmentChoice
  - RecognitionLegitimacyAndCourtMemory
  - CitiesFortressesRoutesAndRegionalMilitarySources
```

The Hammadid campaign should feel like governing a broad central-Maghrebi Realm from a formidable interior capital while deciding whether to consolidate, renew western expansion, intervene eastward, negotiate with mobile regional actors, and build a Mediterranean future. The Realm is already significant. The campaign challenge is not to invent the state from nothing, but to make a recently transferred and geographically varied political order obey, contribute, communicate, and survive.

The Realm must remain distinct from the Almoravid player promise. The Almoravids build and hold together a movement-confederation-state. The Hammadids inherit an established dynastic order with monumental and fortified infrastructure, but that order is not synonymous with complete administrative penetration or uniform loyalty.

## 7. Typed Authority Model

```yaml
authority_dimensions:
  dynastic_authority:
    meaning: recognition of ruler and succession within the ruling house
  court_authority:
    meaning: command of officers households records access and central decisions
  military_authority:
    meaning: recognition by commanders garrisons veterans and contributing forces
  urban_authority:
    meaning: compliance and participation of cities markets notables and garrisons
  fortress_authority:
    meaning: control of fortified sites commanders stores and routes
  regional_authority:
    meaning: recognition by governors communities and local political networks
  religious_juristic_authority:
    meaning: public and institutional interpretation of rule law and legitimacy
  route_authority:
    meaning: actual capacity to secure communicate supply and move across corridors
  foreign_recognition:
    meaning: treatment by neighboring realms and external actors
```

One authority dimension cannot silently stand in for all others. A ruler may hold the capital and court while facing conditional military recognition. A governor may recognize the ruler but delay contribution. A city may pay or supply without accepting direct appointment. A fortress commander may remain loyal while a surrounding regional network does not.
