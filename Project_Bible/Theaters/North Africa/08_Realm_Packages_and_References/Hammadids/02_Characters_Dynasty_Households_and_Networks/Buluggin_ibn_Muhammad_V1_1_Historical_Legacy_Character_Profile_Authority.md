## 1. Historical-character promise

Buluggin ibn Muhammad does not survive as a living ruler under the preferred post-assassination Hammadid opening. Buluggin ibn Muhammad survives as recent political causality. The western expedition, reported entry into Fez, return route, assassination, patronage, command relationships, household, veterans, obligations, and accusations remain available to living actors only through evidence, memory, records, institutions, and current relationships.

The profile makes the predecessor consequential without granting posthumous command, private knowledge, automatic loyalty, automatic hostility, or permanent faction modifiers.
