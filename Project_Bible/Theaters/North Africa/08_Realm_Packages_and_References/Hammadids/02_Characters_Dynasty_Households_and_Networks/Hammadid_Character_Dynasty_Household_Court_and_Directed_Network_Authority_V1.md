The Hammadid opening already contains a distinctive political network. The design does not need a fabricated crowd of portraits. It needs a focused network in which every admitted person, collective constituency, office, household, and relationship materially affects succession, access, military recognition, regional government, capital politics, information, memory, or foreign relations.


## 1. Purpose

This authority converts the source-grounded character inventory into the current Hammadid character, dynasty, household, court, succession, and directed-network design. It preserves uncertainty and opening-state ownership while defining the full substantive behavior expected from a developed Realm package. It does not activate people, households, offices, relationships, memories, or succession outcomes.

## 3. Authority ownership

### 3.1 This authority owns

- Hammadid character-network topology and directed relationship intent.

- Realm-local dynasty and branch candidate structure.

- Household definition, membership classes, residence, dependency, access, protection, and continuity contracts.

- Court presence, access, representation, patronage, petition, and political-alignment surfaces.

- Succession eligibility, designation, preparation, recognition, accession, consolidation, dispute, and settlement design.

- Historical-memory holder eligibility and person-relative interpretation interfaces.

- Character information visibility and relationship evidence requirements.

- Character, household, court, dynasty, and network presentation contracts.

- AI query and proposal boundaries for Hammadid character politics.

- Save, migration, rollback, replay, and determinism guidance for these domains.



### 3.2 This authority does not own

- Exact Opening State activation or location.

- Person biological state, health, age, fertility, or death execution.

- Public office definition and governmental mandate.

- Title hierarchy, law, judgment, or institutional enforcement.

- Property ownership, treasury, inheritance execution, or household assets.

- Military force, recruitment, army command, or battle state.

- Settlement, route, region, fortress, or capital operational state.

- Diplomatic agreements or external private state.

- Historical fact, Historical Memory, Momentum, or Emergence execution.

- Registry publication, payload import, runtime mutation, or Unreal activation.
