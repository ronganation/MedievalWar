## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how Hammadid dynastic continuity may operate when rulership, household leadership, property, marriage, minority, incapacity, widowhood, guardianship, regency, competing claims, or political transition place the Realm under pressure.

It creates no heir, spouse, marriage, pregnancy, child, widow, guardian, regent, claimant, estate, inheritance share, minority, incapacity, deposition, or future succession result. All people must first exist through approved Character and Opening State owners.

## 66. Fertility and Pregnancy Boundary

## 67. Adoption, Fosterage, and Affiliation Candidates

## 68. Household Formation and Division

Marriage, death, inheritance, widowhood, guardianship, office, migration, or dispute may reorganize households. Population and Character authorities own actual people and household membership.

## 69. Residence

Residence after marriage, widowhood, guardianship, or regency requires place, property, consent or authority, support, security, household capacity, and travel. Status does not create a residence.

## 70. Property Interface

Property authority owns land, buildings, movable assets, ownership, use, damage, transfer, custody, and maintenance. Succession authority owns the continuity process and claim relationships.

## 71. Fiscal Interface

Fiscal authority owns treasury, public revenue, expenditure, debt, obligations, audit, and public-private separation. Dynastic transition cannot transfer the treasury into an estate.

## 72. Court Interface

Court culture owns recognition practices, audiences, access, ceremonies, gifts, marriage display, mourning, commemoration, and precedence. Court practice cannot settle legal succession or inheritance alone.

## 73. Governance Interface

Governance owns offices, appointments, mandates, delegation, administration, emergency authority, regional control, and continuity of government. Succession authority owns dynastic transition and claimant coordination.

## 74. Law Interface

Law owns standing, evidence, marriage validity, property, inheritance procedure, guardianship authority, custody, disputes, remedies, enforcement, and appeal. This authority cannot invent a legal code.

## 75. Religion and Juristic Interface

Religious and juristic authority owns doctrine, interpretation, marriage and inheritance opinions, institutions, legitimacy, and religious procedure. A generic Islamic rule does not become specific Hammadid practice without evidence.

## 76. Diplomacy Interface

Diplomacy owns foreign marriages, envoys, guarantees, safe conduct, recognition, asylum, prisoner or claimant negotiations, and foreign private state. Foreign recognition does not settle domestic succession.

## 77. Military Interface

Military authority owns commanders, units, garrisons, readiness, force, protection, conflict, and security. Military backing is a material political fact but does not automatically create legal priority.

## 78. Information Interface

Information owns death reports, genealogy documents, messages, seals, rumors, forgery, secrecy, succession propaganda, provenance, and confidence. Succession uses only lawfully available information.

## 79. Education Interface

Education owns upbringing, instruction, scribes, records, legal and dynastic knowledge, and preparation. Education does not create maturity, competence, recognition, or a claim.

## 80. Public Order Interface

Contested succession, estate disputes, guardian abuse claims, marriage conflict, regency restrictions, or claimant detention may create bounded incidents and grievances. None causes automatic rebellion.

## 81. Event Interface

Events may surface death reports, claim presentations, marriage proposals, guardian disputes, regency reviews, estate petitions, access requests, recognition, renunciation, or restoration. Event definitions cannot create people, claims, marriages, heirs, or outcomes.
