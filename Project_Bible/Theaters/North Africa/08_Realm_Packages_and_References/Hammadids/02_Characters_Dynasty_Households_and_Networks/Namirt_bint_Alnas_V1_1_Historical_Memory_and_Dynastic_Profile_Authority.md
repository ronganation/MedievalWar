## 7. Core character thesis

Namirt bint Alnas matters because a dynastic person can shape political order through kinship, marriage, household continuity, memory, property, patronage, and the interpretation of violence even after death. The profile represents her as an independent historical subject. Living actors may invoke her memory, inherit obligations associated with her household, contest the meaning of her death, or suppress the narrative. None of those outcomes is automatic.
