## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the procedural system through which claims, petitions, disputes, accusations, evidence, testimony, hearings, judgments, administrative dispositions, remedies, restitution, punishment, enforcement, review, appeal, reopening, and clemency may operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It unifies legal procedure across governance, juristic institutions, property, households, markets, public order, offices, military service, migration, succession, and regional authority while preserving the subject-matter ownership of those systems.

It does not fabricate a complete eleventh-century Hammadid code, opening judges, named legal officials, active courts, opening crimes, guilt, punishments, cases, prisons, enforcement forces, or appeal institutions.

## 36. Restitution

Restitution seeks to restore property, access, status, or material position where feasible. It requires an identified harm, responsible party or fund where applicable, owned property or entitlement, available resources, delivery, and verification.

property declaration != physical return
return != repaired condition
