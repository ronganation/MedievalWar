## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the complete Hammadid custody chain from battlefield capture, surrender, political detention, hostage arrangements, transport, holding, records, health, household consequences, ransom, exchange, release, return, and reintegration.

It creates no prisoner, captive, hostage, detainee, prison, guard, custodian, ransom, exchange, safe conduct, release, escape, death in custody, or opening detention state. Every affected person must already exist through Character and Opening State owners.

## 71. Reintegration

Reintegration may involve health recovery, household return, residence, property, work or office review, military status, reputation, debt, trauma-aware support if later owned, information security, and legal closure. Release is only the first step.

## 72. Household Consequences

Captivity may alter income, labor, care, guardianship, residence, protection, inheritance fears, patronage, claims, marriage negotiations, and household authority. Character and population owners determine actual household effects.

## 73. Dynastic Consequences

A captive ruler, claimant, spouse, heir candidate, guardian, or regent may affect succession, recognition, regency, diplomacy, and household security. Captivity does not automatically remove claims or office.

## 74. Office and Military Status

Governance and military owners decide suspension, replacement, delegation, return, investigation, reintegration, or discharge. Capture does not automatically erase office or restore it upon release.

## 75. Property Consequences

Property authority owns seized, abandoned, damaged, entrusted, inherited, returned, and disputed property. Release does not automatically restore possession.

## 76. Fiscal Consequences

Fiscal authority owns custody costs, guards, food, transport, ransom finance, exchange expenditure, compensation, debt, public-private allocation, and audit. Prisoners do not generate automatic income.

## 77. Diplomatic Consequences

Diplomacy owns foreign captives, envoys, negotiations, guarantees, exchanges, safe conduct, protest, mediation, and foreign private state. Hammadid custody cannot author foreign response.

## 78. Legal Consequences

Law owns authority, classification, petitions, conditions, questioning limits, property, abuse claims, punishment, remedies, release, restitution, enforcement, and appeal. Custody does not prove guilt.

## 79. Religious and Juristic Interface

Religious and juristic authority owns doctrine, ethical and legal interpretation, institutions, mediation, charity, and religious practice. This authority does not invent religious prisoner law.

## 80. Health Interface

Health owns injury, illness, diagnosis, treatment, mortality, food and water safety, recovery, and disability where separately admitted. Custody state creates no diagnosis.

## 81. Information Interface

Information owns capture reports, identity claims, interrogations, statements, letters, rumors, propaganda, secrecy, missing-person reports, provenance, and confidence. Custody owners preserve access and chain of record.

## 82. Public Order Interface

Abuse allegations, missing prisoners, delayed release, ransom burdens, hostage disputes, escape, guard failure, or unequal treatment may create bounded incidents and grievances. None causes automatic unrest.

## 83. Court-Culture Interface

Court culture owns reception, audience, visible treatment, gift or ransom presentation, commemorative response, and reputation. Public display cannot create custody, guilt, humiliation, honor, or loyalty automatically.

## 84. Event Interface

Events may surface capture reports, identity disputes, ransom offers, exchange negotiations, hostage requests, petitions, illness reports, escape attempts, releases, or reintegration choices. Definitions cannot spawn people or custody states.

## 85. Treatment and Abuse Claims

Any mistreatment, neglect, violence, deprivation, extortion, theft, or unlawful detention claim requires an incident record, evidence, health and property review, responsible actors, legal routing, and confidence. Custody does not hide or prove abuse.
