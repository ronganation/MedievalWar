## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how religious affiliation, juristic interpretation, Friday recognition, mosque-centered public communication, institutional patronage, mediation, dynastic legitimacy, religious knowledge, and bounded communal authority operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It converts the religious and juristic needs exposed by the completed Hammadid governance, diplomacy, public-order, information, economy, property, geography, and character-network authorities into one coherent realm-specific system. It does not create universal consensus, automatic obedience, a single clerical hierarchy, free legitimacy, guaranteed public belief, invented named jurists, fabricated opening offices, or direct control over foreign religious institutions.
