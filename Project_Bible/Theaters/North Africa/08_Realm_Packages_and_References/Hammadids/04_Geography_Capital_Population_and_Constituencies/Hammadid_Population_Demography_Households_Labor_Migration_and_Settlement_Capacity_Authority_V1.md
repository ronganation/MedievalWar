## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how population claims, demographic cohorts, households, dependents, labor, migration, displacement, settlement capacity, and finite recruitment inputs operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It supplies the missing population owner needed by the completed economy, construction, public-order, military, regional, household, health, taxation, route, and faction-AI systems. It establishes structures, records, causal relationships, uncertainty, and owner routing without inventing exact January 1062 population totals or identity percentages.
