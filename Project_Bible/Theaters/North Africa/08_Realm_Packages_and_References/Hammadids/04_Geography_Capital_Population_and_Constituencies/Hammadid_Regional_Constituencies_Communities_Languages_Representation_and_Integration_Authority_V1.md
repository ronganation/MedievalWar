## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how bounded regional constituencies and communities may be researched, admitted, represented, negotiated with, integrated, divided internally, affected by movement, and connected to Hammadid governance, law, religion, diplomacy, economy, military service, public order, and presentation.

It provides a common owner for community identity and representation without converting language, religion, ancestry, locality, livelihood, or mobility into fixed traits, population percentages, universal leaders, permanent loyalties, automatic hostilities, or unit pools.

## 42. Collective Action

Collective petitions, work, refusal, migration, defense, protest, or violence require identified participants, organizers or representatives where known, objective, place, timing, capacity, and evidence. A community label alone cannot perform an action.

## 43. Governance Interface

Governance owns offices, delegated authority, jurisdiction, compliance, taxation, and administrative relations. Constituency authority supplies bounded groups, representatives, access needs, and integration state.

## 44. Law and Justice Interface

Law owns standing, jurisdiction, evidence, procedure, disposition, remedy, enforcement, and appeal. Constituency authority owns group boundaries and representation claims. Identity never determines guilt or credibility.

## 45. Population Interface

Population owns households, magnitude claims, labor, migration, displacement, and settlement capacity. Constituency authority owns bounded identity and representation. Neither owner may infer percentages from the other.

## 46. Character and Network Interface
