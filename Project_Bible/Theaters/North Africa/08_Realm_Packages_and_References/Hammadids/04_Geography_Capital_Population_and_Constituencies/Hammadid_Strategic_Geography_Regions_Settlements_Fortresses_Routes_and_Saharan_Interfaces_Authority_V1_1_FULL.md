## 1. Purpose
This authority defines the Hammadid Realm candidate as a central-Maghrebi political order shaped by a fortified mountain capital, interior plateaus and valleys, coastal approaches, city and fortress networks, western and eastern frontiers, oasis corridors, and Saharan interfaces. It creates a strategically useful geography model without converting a circa-1065 orientation map into exact January 1062 borders or settlement ownership.
The file is intentionally substantial. It provides the geographic foundation required by later governance, diplomacy, military, economy, population, property, construction, route, information, public-order, and Opening State authorities.
```text
map color != direct government
settlement label != opening ownership
route line != secure movement
fortress symbol != known garrison
regional influence != tax collection
vassal label != population ownership
coastal access != port capacity
oasis access != Saharan control
capital reach != uniform obedience
```

```yaml
  - Fortified mountain capital
  - Interior central position
  - Multiple western eastern coastal and southern directions
  - City fortress and caravan interfaces
  - Access to plateau oasis and Mediterranean systems
geographic_vulnerabilities:
  - Long communication distances
  - Uneven direct authority
  - Route insecurity
  - Mobile political military actors
  - Difficult coast interior coordination
  - Regional governors and fortress commanders with local leverage
player_fantasy:
  HoldTheCentralMaghrebTogetherAcrossMountainsCitiesRoutesAndOases
```
The Hammadid campaign should not feel like governing one continuous green polygon. The Realm should feel like commanding a network of stronger and weaker political relationships across a difficult central position. Qal'a Bani Hammad gives the ruler defense, prestige, and an interior command center, but every outward direction creates a different strategic problem.
