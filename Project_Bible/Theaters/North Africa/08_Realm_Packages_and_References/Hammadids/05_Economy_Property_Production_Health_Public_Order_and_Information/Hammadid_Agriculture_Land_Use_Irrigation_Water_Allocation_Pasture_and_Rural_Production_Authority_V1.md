## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how agricultural land, cultivation, planting material, labor, tools, animals, irrigation candidates, water allocation, pasture, rural households, harvest, storage, subsistence, surplus, market delivery, fiscal claims, military requisition, environmental pressure, and recovery may operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It establishes a rural and agrarian owner beneath the existing population, production, health, fiscal, property, military, and market systems. It does not fabricate January 1062 crops, yields, fields, estates, irrigation works, canal grids, landowners, tenants, water officials, grazing boundaries, harvest stocks, or agricultural output values.

## 53. Population Interface

## 54. Production Interface

Production owns tools, animals as material capacity, processing, workshops, inventories, quality, storage, and transport. Agriculture owns cultivation, grazing, rural allocation, and harvest state.

## 55. Economy Interface

Economy owns markets, prices, trade, contracts, currency, and transactions. Agriculture owns production feasibility and rural availability. Price incentives do not create land, water, seed, labor, or harvest.

## 56. Constituency Interface

Constituency authority owns rural, pastoral, oasis, water-user, market, and regional groups and representatives. Identity never creates land access, water rights, output, or compliance.

## 57. Law and Governance Interface

Law owns disputes, evidence, procedure, remedies, enforcement, and appeal. Governance owns authority, offices, water or land administration where supported, taxation, requisition, and emergency measures. Agriculture supplies land-use, water, production, damage, and capacity records.

## 58. Information Interface

Information owns reports, messenger chains, secrecy, rumor, provenance, and confidence. Agriculture classifies claims concerning weather, water, planting, livestock, damage, harvest, and stocks.

## 59. Education Interface

Education owns agrarian knowledge, apprenticeship, records, literacy, and technical transmission. Agriculture owns application, practice, production state, and outcomes.

## 60. Public Order Interface

Land, water, pasture, requisition, taxation, damage, missing receipts, market access, or unequal maintenance burdens may generate bounded grievances. No rural identity creates automatic unrest.

## 61. Religious and Institutional Interface

Religious or juristic authorities own doctrine, endowments, institutions, charity, and mediation. Agriculture owns land and production use. Institutional affiliation does not prove landholding, exemption, or agricultural practice.

## 62. Environmental Interface

A future environmental authority should own climate patterns, drought, flood, erosion, woodland pressure, long-term degradation, and adaptation. Agriculture owns farm and pasture responses and production consequences.
