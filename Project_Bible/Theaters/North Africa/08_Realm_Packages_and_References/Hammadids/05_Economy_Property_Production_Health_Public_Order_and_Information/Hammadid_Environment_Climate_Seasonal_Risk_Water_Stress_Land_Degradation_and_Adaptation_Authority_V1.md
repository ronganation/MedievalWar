## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how environmental observations, climate context, seasonal conditions, water stress, drought, flood, heat, cold, wind, fire, erosion, soil and pasture degradation, woodland and fuel pressure, settlement exposure, environmental health, adaptation, and long-term recovery may operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It adds the environmental owner consumed by agriculture, health, property, production, population, routes, military operations, markets, fiscal government, and events. It does not invent January 1062 weather, climate measurements, drought, flood, fire, land degradation, woodland extent, carrying capacity, disaster cycles, or environmental infrastructure.

## 47. Urban Environment

## 48. Rural Environment

## 49. Pastoral Environment

Pastoral use depends on bounded water, forage, routes, season, animal condition, agreements, and recovery. Mobile use can reduce or increase pressure depending on actual timing and capacity; no cultural stereotype determines the outcome.

## 50. Oasis and Saharan Interface

Oasis and Saharan interfaces require specific sources, routes, settlements, households, markets, and diplomatic or constituency relationships. The label does not create water, agriculture, trade, or territorial control.

## 51. Agriculture Interface

Agriculture owns land use, cultivation, irrigation operation, pasture use, rural households, output, and agrarian recovery. Environment owns weather, hydrological stress, soil degradation, fire, erosion, and long-term environmental condition.

## 52. Health Interface

Health owns symptoms, disease, food and water security, mortality, care, disaster classification, relief, and recovery. Environment owns hazards, exposure, and environmental conditions. Hazard exposure never creates illness automatically.

## 53. Geography Interface

Geography owns relatively stable terrain, locations, water sources, routes, regions, settlements, and distances. Environment owns changing condition, weather, stress, hazard, degradation, and environmental recovery.

## 54. Property Interface

Property owns buildings, waterworks, drainage, roads, walls, storage, maintenance, damage, construction, and repair. Environment supplies hazard, exposure, condition, and adaptation requirements. A protective structure creates no guarantee.

## 55. Production Interface

Production owns fuel, materials, animals, tools, workshops, storage, transport, quality, and delivery. Environment owns resource pressure and conditions. Demand does not create environmental damage or supply automatically.

## 56. Population Interface

Population owns people, households, dependents, labor, migration, displacement, and settlement capacity. Environment owns environmental exposure and pressure. Population count alone does not create carrying-capacity failure.

## 57. Economy Interface

Economy owns markets, prices, trade, contracts, currency, and material exchange. Environment may alter access, production, risk, loss, and route conditions. Price change does not prove environmental cause.

## 58. Fiscal Interface

Fiscal authority owns taxation, treasury, expenditure, debt, remission, and emergency finance. Environment supplies hazard, damage, pressure, and adaptation evidence. Environmental response funding does not create labor, goods, or completed works.

## 59. Military Interface

Military owners control forces, camps, campaigns, routes, logistics, fortifications, and operational choices. Environment owns weather and exposure inputs. Neither side receives deterministic environmental victory or immunity.

## 60. Public Order and Law Interfaces

Environmental pressure may produce disputes, grievances, movement, damage claims, or emergency measures. Law and public order own procedure, protection, enforcement, and recovery coordination. Drought or disaster does not suspend rights automatically.

## 61. Constituency Interface

Constituency authority owns bounded users, communities, languages, representation, and integration. Environment owns conditions affecting them. No constituency is inherently destructive, resilient, adapted, or vulnerable.

## 62. Information Interface

Information owns reports, provenance, transmission, secrecy, rumor, contradiction, and confidence. Environment classifies conditions and hazards using available reports without reading hidden truth.

## 63. Education Interface

Education owns environmental knowledge, observation practices, records, technical learning, and transmission. Environment owns state and risk. Knowledge does not create water, shelter, repair, or adaptation.

## 64. Event Interface

Event definitions may surface environmental warnings, choices, and crises when owned thresholds and evidence are met. Date windows and random selection cannot manufacture droughts, floods, fires, or degradation.

## 71. Adaptation Tradeoffs
