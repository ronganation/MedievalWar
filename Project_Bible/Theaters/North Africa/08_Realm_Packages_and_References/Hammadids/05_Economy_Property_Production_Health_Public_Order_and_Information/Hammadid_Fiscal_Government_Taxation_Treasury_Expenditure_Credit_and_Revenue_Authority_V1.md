## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how public revenue, taxation, assessment, liability, exemption, collection, remittance, treasury custody, expenditure, credit, debt, arrears, audit, diversion, fiscal negotiation, and fiscal legitimacy may operate for the Hammadid Realm.

It closes the strategic gap between general economic activity and the government's actual ability to acquire, custody, commit, spend, verify, and recover public resources. It does not invent January 1062 tax schedules, tax rates, treasury balances, fiscal offices, officials, taxpayers, exemptions, debts, revenue totals, expenditure totals, or coin stocks.

## 72. Governance Interface

## 73. Law Interface

Law owns disputes, evidence, procedure, judgments, restitution, enforcement, appeal, and protections. Fiscal authority owns fiscal claims, records, calculations, custody, and requested corrections.

## 74. Religion and Juristic Interface

Religion owns juristic interpretation, institutions, endowments, charity, and recognition. Fiscal authority may consume owner-approved interpretations but cannot infer tax categories or rules from religious labels alone.

## 75. Property and Construction Interface

Property owns land, buildings, waterworks, stores, construction, maintenance, and damage. Fiscal authority owns public financing and claims connected to those assets. Ownership does not create taxable value automatically.

## 76. Production Interface

Production owns goods, materials, animals, workshops, tools, inventories, quality, storage, transport, and delivery. Fiscal authority cannot collect, spend, or promise goods that production does not verify.

## 77. Health and Disaster Interface

Health owns illness, food and water security, care, mortality, disaster, relief needs, and recovery. Fiscal authority owns funding, allocation, credit, arrears adjustment, and expenditure records. Declaring relief does not create supply.

## 78. Military Interface

Military owns command, recruitment, units, readiness, logistics, campaigns, and defense. Fiscal authority owns public commitments, pay or support candidates, records, and opportunity costs. Military necessity does not create unlimited fiscal authority.

## 79. Diplomacy Interface

Diplomacy owns foreign proposals, gifts, subsidies, indemnities, tribute relationships, ransoms, safe conduct, and consent. Fiscal authority owns domestic custody, valuation, commitment, and receipt after foreign action occurs.

## 80. Information Interface

Information owns reports, provenance, secrecy, messenger state, forgery risk, and confidence. Fiscal authority owns material classification, accounting meaning, and fiscal response.

## 81. Public Order Interface

Unclear claims, abusive collectors, unequal treatment, inability to pay, missing receipts, diversion, requisition, or failed public performance may create bounded grievances. None generates automatic unrest.

## 82. Court Culture Interface

A future court-culture authority should own ceremony, precedence, hospitality, gifts, display, and political meaning. Fiscal authority owns public cost, custody, valuation, and expenditure consequences.
