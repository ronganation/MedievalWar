## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how health claims, symptoms, illness, disease, injury, disability, nutrition, food and water security, sanitation, care, diagnosis, treatment, medicines, mortality, burial interfaces, disaster response, relief, and recovery may operate in the Hammadid Realm.

It closes the missing survival and recovery loop connecting population, households, markets, water, settlements, property, public order, military campaigns, law, education, information, and AI. It does not fabricate an opening epidemic, physician, hospital, pharmacy, medical institution, disease prevalence, mortality rate, treatment inventory, burial ground, or relief stock.

## 5. Historical Evidence Boundary

The external sources reviewed identify Qal'a as a fortified mountain capital. One secondary orientation says the site receives water from surrounding mountains, while UNESCO confirms only the mountainous setting, first-capital status, fortified city, and major mosque. They do not establish water quality, sanitation systems, hospitals, physicians, epidemics, medical practice, food stocks, or mortality.

## 42. Cause of Death Boundary

Cause of death may remain unknown, disputed, or broadly classified. Modern certainty must not be imposed where evidence is insufficient. Death following illness or injury does not prove the exact causal mechanism.

## 43. Missing Persons

Missing, captured, delayed, separated, displaced, and deceased states remain distinct. Property, marriage, inheritance, office, and household consequences require law and character ownership.

## 44. Burial and Body Disposition Interface

Religious and constituency authorities own ritual, affiliation, institution, and community relations. Law owns investigation, custody disputes, and records. Property and geography own places. Health owns public-health and handling concerns. No opening burial ground or practice is created here.

## 45. Household Mortality Consequences

Death can affect care, income, labor, property, debt, inheritance, leadership, memory, grief-related functioning where person-owned, military capacity, and household continuity. It does not automatically dissolve the household.

## 46. Maternal, Infant, and Childhood Health Boundary

Pregnancy, childbirth, postpartum state, infant health, and childhood illness require person- and household-specific evidence or approved aggregate systems. No universal fertility, maternal mortality, or infant mortality rate is created.

## 47. Disaster Definition

A hazard becomes a disaster only when an event causes harm exceeding affected households', institutions', or settlements' immediate capacity to respond without extraordinary support.
