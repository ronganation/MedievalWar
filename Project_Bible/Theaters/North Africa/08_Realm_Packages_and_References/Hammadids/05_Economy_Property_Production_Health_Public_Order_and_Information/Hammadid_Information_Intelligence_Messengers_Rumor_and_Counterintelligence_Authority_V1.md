# 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how information becomes knowable, transmissible, disputable, actionable, secret, distorted, delayed, or lost in the Hammadid Realm. It supports the violent-accession opening, the mountain capital at Qal'a Bani Hammad, regional government across distance, diplomacy with neighboring powers, public order, military warning, caravan and Saharan information, and later coastal development.

The authority does not create omniscience, free spies, automatic messenger networks, perfect maps, guaranteed assassination plots, or a universal intelligence score. Every information claim is routed through provenance, access, transmission, confidence, interpretation, and owner-controlled action.
