## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the Hammadid Realm's material production logic from source claim through extraction or acquisition, processing, craft work, assembly, finishing, storage, allocation, transport, delivery, use, maintenance, repair, recovery, reuse, and loss.

It provides the detailed capability layer required by economy, construction, health, education, military, settlement, caravan, household, and faction-AI systems. It does not fabricate mines, fields, herds, workshops, craftspeople, animals, tools, weapons, armor, building materials, medicines, writing supplies, stocks, or opening production volumes.

## 5. External Evidence Boundary

The study identifies varied glazing and decoration techniques, including lustre, and reports tin oxide in glazes. ## 17. Household Production

## 18. Urban Production
