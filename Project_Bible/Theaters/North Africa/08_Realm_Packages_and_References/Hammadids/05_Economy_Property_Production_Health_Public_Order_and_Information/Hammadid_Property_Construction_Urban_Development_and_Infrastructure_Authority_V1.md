## 1. Purpose and Realm-Specific Verdict

This authority defines how land, residences, palaces, mosques, markets, workshops, stores, roads, water systems, fortifications, public spaces, productive sites, and future urban-development projects exist, change hands, operate, fail, and recover within the Hammadid Realm. It translates the political importance of Qal'a Bani Hammad and the future coastal opportunity at Bejaia into a property and construction system without turning historical monuments into free opening buildings or reducing urban development to a linear settlement upgrade.

## 4. Historical and Archaeological Evidence Boundary

The authority admits only the broad historical propositions already established by the Hammadid capital and geography authorities: Qal'a Bani Hammad was the first Hammadid capital, founded in 1007, developed as a fortified Muslim city in a mountainous setting, and contained a monumental Great Mosque. Archaeological and secondary references provide research leads for palatial complexes, residences, gardens, ceramics, water-related features, markets, and administrative spaces. These leads justify capability categories and research questions. These leads do not prove exact 1062 ownership, condition, operation, capacity, floor plan, or quantity.
