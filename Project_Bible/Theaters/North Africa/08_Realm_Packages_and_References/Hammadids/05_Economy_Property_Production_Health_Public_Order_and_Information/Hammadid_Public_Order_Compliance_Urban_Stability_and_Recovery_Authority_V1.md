## 1. Purpose and Strong Realm Verdict

This authority defines Hammadid public order as the changing ability of households, neighborhoods, markets, courts, garrisons, institutions, regional authorities, mobile constituencies, and the ruler's government to coexist, comply, bargain, petition, resist, recover, or fracture under real political and material pressures. It rejects a single happiness number and refuses to turn a violent accession, Biskra memory, market stress, religious recognition, or Banu Hilal contact into automatic rebellion.
