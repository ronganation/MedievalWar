## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the Hammadid Realm's army-composition logic, recruitment-source architecture, finite capacity, roster families, unit identity, campaign roles, battlefield roles, intentional weaknesses, development paths, lineage, AI composition guidance, and player-facing military promise.

It transforms the completed command, mobilization, logistics, regional-defense, information, governance, economy, property, geography, public-order, religious, and character foundations into a developed faction military identity. It does not create opening armies, approved stable unit IDs, exact unit names, tactical statistics, recruitment pools, equipment inventories, commanders, garrisons, mercenary providers, siege assets, fleets, or Unreal assets.
