## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the political operation of Hammadid court culture through access, audience, precedence, ceremony, hospitality, diplomatic reception, gift exchange, patronage, commemoration, dynastic display, ruler visibility, material production, expenditure, recognition, and reputation.

It creates a court-practice owner between household relationships, government, diplomacy, religious legitimacy, cultural production, fiscal government, presentation, and events. It does not invent January 1062 court ranks, ceremonial offices, protocols, titles, gifts, regalia, clothing inventories, named courtiers, artists, poets, envoys, performances, ceremonies, or audience lists.

Court expenditure may compete with defense, water, agriculture, relief, roads, institutions, salaries or support, and reserves. Modesty may preserve resources but reduce some forms of display, hospitality, patronage, or access.

## 70. Gift and Patronage Audit

Publicly sourced gifts and commissions require authorization, valuation, custody, delivery, recipient, purpose, and audit. Missing records create review, not automatic corruption.

## 71. Governance Interface

Governance owns offices, mandates, appointments, delegation, regional authority, proclamations' legal effect, and administrative capacity. Court culture owns access, reception, visible recognition, audience, and ceremonial treatment.

## 72. Character and Household Interface

Character authority owns identity, kinship, health, age, household, directed relationships, personality evidence, and availability. Court culture consumes actual people and cannot infer private feelings or loyalty.

## 73. Diplomacy Interface

Diplomacy owns envoys, credentials, foreign messages, agreements, safe conduct, gifts in foreign relations, marriage proposals, and foreign consent. Court culture owns Hammadid reception, presentation, hospitality, and visible response.

## 74. Law and Justice Interface

Law owns standing, property, contracts, disputes, gifts' legal treatment, fraud, bribery classification, enforcement, remedies, and appeal. Court culture cannot declare an accepted gift lawful or corrupt by symbolism alone.

## 75. Religion and Juristic Interface

Religious authority owns doctrine, juristic opinion, religious institutions, endowments, piety claims, and ritual classification. Court culture owns political display and reception around approved practices.

## 76. Education and Cultural Interface

Education owns literacy, scribes, scholarship, texts, archives, poetry, knowledge, teachers, artists or cultural producers where admitted, and transmission. Court culture owns patronage relation, audience, commissioning context, and political reception.

## 77. Economy and Market Interface

Economy owns prices, exchange, contracts, merchants, currency, market availability, and trade. Court culture owns symbolic selection and relational use. Market availability does not create acquisition or authenticity.

## 78. Information Interface

Information owns reports, rumor, secrecy, messenger state, public knowledge, provenance, forgery, and confidence. Court culture owns staged visibility and court interpretation. Public display may spread unevenly and be misunderstood.

## 79. Public Order Interface

Access disputes, humiliations, exclusion claims, gift disputes, excessive expenditure, broken protocol, crowding, or security actions may create bounded grievances. None produces automatic unrest.

## 80. Military Interface

Military authority owns command, forces, ceremonies tied to actual service, victory claims, guards, security, and military capability. Court culture owns recognition, reception, reward presentation, and display. Ceremonial weapons create no combat capacity.

## 81. Event Interface

Events may present audiences, petitions, receptions, protocol disputes, patronage requests, gifts, ceremonies, commemorations, funerals, public appearances, or envoy incidents. Event definitions cannot create people, objects, recognition, or completed ceremonies.

## 82. Presentation Interface

Presentation authority owns UI, visual identity, narration, audio, cinematic composition, and player-facing style. Court culture supplies approved state, participants, objects, sequence, space, and meaning. Presentation cannot invent state.

## 83. Prestige Definition

Prestige is a descriptive, actor- and audience-specific reputation concerning standing, magnificence, generosity, dignity, success, learning, piety, martial capacity, hospitality, lineage, or reliability. It is not one universal spendable resource.

## 87. Reputation Change

## 88. Reputation Decay and Memory

Reputation may persist through records, monuments, objects, poetry, household memory, institutions, political repetition, or continuing relationships. It does not decay through one universal numeric rate.

## 89. Court Crisis and Continuity

During succession, siege, disaster, fiscal stress, illness, or relocation, court practice may contract, move, become restricted, emphasize continuity, or change audiences. Crisis does not automatically end court culture.

## 90. Capital Reorientation Interface
