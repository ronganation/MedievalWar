## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how Hammadid decisions, dilemmas, petitions, events, crises, opportunities, and conditionally emerging historical developments should be authored, evaluated, presented, resolved, persisted, and validated.

It turns the mature Hammadid authority package into player-facing causality without scripting a predetermined campaign. It does not create opening incidents, active event instances, guaranteed wars, automatic historical outcomes, hidden culprits, foreign consent, characters, armies, buildings, goods, knowledge, or Registry identifiers.

## 2. Strong Event-System Verdict

core_identity: EvidenceTriggeredOwnerRoutedStatefulAndNonDeterministicFactionEventSystem

## 22. Crisis Is Not Predetermined Collapse

A crisis may be mitigated, redirected, exploited, negotiated, mishandled, prolonged, or transformed. No crisis automatically generates rebellion, war, famine, epidemic, or regime change.

## 23. Petition Integration

Petitions use standing, representation, jurisdiction, evidence, access, and remedy from the Law and Constituency authorities. An event may present a petition but cannot establish its truth or outcome.
