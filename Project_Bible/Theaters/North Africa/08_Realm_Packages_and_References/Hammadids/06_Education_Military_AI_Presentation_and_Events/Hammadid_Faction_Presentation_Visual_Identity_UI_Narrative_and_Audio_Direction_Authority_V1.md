## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how the completed Hammadid faction framework should be presented coherently to the player across campaign selection, strategy map, settlements, court and institution views, diplomacy, economy, military interfaces, information systems, narrative text, music, ambience, and sound.

It converts faction causality into a legible experience without flattening the Hammadids into generic North Africa, generic Islam, the Almoravids with another color, or a later coastal dynasty retrofitted into January 1062.

This authority controls direction, vocabulary, hierarchy, evidence boundaries, and handoff requirements. It does not create final art, models, textures, icons, fonts, recordings, voice performances, localization strings, UI widgets, cinematics, opening-state instances, Registry entries, runtime bindings, or Unreal assets.

## 5. Verified External Presentation Foundation

UNESCO describes Al Qal'a of Beni Hammad as the first capital of the Hammadid emirs, founded in 1007, located in a mountainous setting, and providing an authentic picture of a fortified Muslim city. UNESCO also identifies a major mosque with a prayer hall of thirteen aisles and eight bays. These facts support a mountain-capital, fortified-city, monumental-institution presentation direction. They do not authorize reconstruction of undocumented surfaces, colors, furnishings, people, ceremonies, or opening-state conditions.
