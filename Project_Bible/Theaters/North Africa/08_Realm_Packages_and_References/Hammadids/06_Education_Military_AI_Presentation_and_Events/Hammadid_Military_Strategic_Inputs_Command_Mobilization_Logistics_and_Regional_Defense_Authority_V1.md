## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines the strategic inputs required before the Hammadid Realm can command, mobilize, move, supply, employ, recover, or demobilize military power. It covers command legitimacy, service obligation, readiness, force information, regional defense, fortress responsibility, routes, water, food, fodder, transport, campaign burden, veteran networks, western-campaign consequences, and the separation between military potential and active forces.

It does not create armies, units, commanders, garrisons, recruitment pools, mercenaries, tribal contingents, siege assets, fleets, tactical statistics, opening deployments, or battlefield outcomes. Those require separate owned evidence and later authority.
