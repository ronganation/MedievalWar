## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority defines how the Hammadid Realm should think, prioritize, explain decisions, respond to uncertainty, manage violent succession, protect the mountain capital, integrate regions, negotiate with constituencies, allocate scarce resources, compose forces, and present a distinct player experience.

It coordinates existing Hammadid authorities. It does not replace their ownership, invent opening facts, assign hidden bonuses, read private foreign state, create armies or economic resources, or activate runtime AI.
