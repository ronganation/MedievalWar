## 1. Purpose and Authority

This authority reconciles the nineteen Hammadid candidate roster families against available historical terminology, arms and armor evidence, recruitment logic, equipment plausibility, visual-source quality, gameplay role, overlap risk, and promotion requirements.

It protects the developing faction from two opposite failures: reducing the Hammadids to generic North African labels and invented exotic units, or withholding every useful roster family merely because surviving evidence is incomplete. It allows broad historically supported ranges and clearly marked design interpretation while prohibiting fabricated names, exact loadouts, universal ethnic units, opening-force claims, and visual invention presented as history.

## 5. Current Source Position

A 2024 article abstract titled `The Hammadi army: reading the structure and organization` states that it examines the Hammadid army through organizational categories represented by infantry, cavalry, and sailors. This confirms broad categories worth researching but does not expose final Hammadid unit names, weapons, armor, recruitment systems, or January 1062 force composition in the accessible abstract.

The current external search did not produce a reliable Hammadid-specific catalogue of unit terminology or securely dated complete soldier loadouts. Search noise included unrelated ancient and modern military material. Such results are excluded rather than stretched into evidence.
