## 0.2. Strong Emergence Verdict

The Zirid historical suite supports a broad family of conditional pathways, not a queue of scripted historical outcomes. Emergence is the owner-gated process through which current actors, knowledge, relationships, resources, access, decisions, momentum, counterforces, and date or world-state windows may produce a new state.

Every pathway must allow delay, partial resolution, transformation, failure, alternative successors, recovery, expiry, and nonoccurrence. Historical resemblance is valid when produced by the campaign state, but the system may not create missing people, offices, armies, fleets, money, goods, agreements, institutions, territory, knowledge, or consent to force that resemblance.
