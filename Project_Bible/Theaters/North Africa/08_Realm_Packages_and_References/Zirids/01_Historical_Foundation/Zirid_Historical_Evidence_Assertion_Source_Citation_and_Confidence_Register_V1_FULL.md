## 0.2. Strong Register Verdict

This register is the claim-level evidence control surface for the Zirids at the January 1, 1062 boundary. It is stronger than a bibliography and more cautious than an Opening State. It identifies each proposition, its supporting and complicating source records, citation and locator status, confidence, exactness, chronology compatibility, opening admissibility, allowed and prohibited uses, dependent authorities, promotion blockers, and reopening triggers.

The current internal authority family supports a coherent historical foundation, but it does not constitute independent historical corroboration. External scholarly sources identified by the source inventory remain claim-specific, and several are bibliographic leads rather than fully extracted passages. Accordingly, the register preserves strong project treatment while refusing unsupported exact dates, quantities, territorial bindings, offices, forces, institutions, agreements, or event activation.
