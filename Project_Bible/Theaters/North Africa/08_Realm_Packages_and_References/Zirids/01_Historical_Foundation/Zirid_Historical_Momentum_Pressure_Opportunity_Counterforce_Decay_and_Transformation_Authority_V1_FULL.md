## 0.2. Strong Momentum Verdict

The Zirid opening supports a strong portfolio of historical pressures and opportunities, but none is an inevitability, probability score, hidden countdown, or command. Momentum exists only where current world-state drivers remain active, counterforces are assessed, relevant actors possess usable knowledge, owner-supported capacity exists, and the pressure has not decayed, resolved, or transformed.

Momentum may shape proposals, priorities, explanations, risks, and conditional-emergence eligibility. It cannot create people, offices, forces, resources, agreements, relationships, territorial control, public response, memory, or events. Its proper role is causal continuity: explaining why an option, danger, opportunity, or unresolved problem remains live in the present world state.
