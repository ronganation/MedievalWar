## 0.2. Strong Chronology Verdict

The Zirid chronology is strong enough to distinguish pre-opening foundations, the exact technical opening boundary, year-level later-1062 succession, broader historical pressure windows, and later-history exclusions. It is not strong enough to promote every reported date, event sequence, regional transition, relationship, campaign, or consequence to exact historical or Opening State fact.

The chronology therefore uses exact dates only where supported at that precision, bounded windows where evidence is broader, relative sequences where only order is defensible, and protected unknowns where neither date nor sequence is closed. The technical scenario date of January 1, 1062 does not grant day-level precision to historical claims that are supported only at the year, range, or relative-sequence level.
