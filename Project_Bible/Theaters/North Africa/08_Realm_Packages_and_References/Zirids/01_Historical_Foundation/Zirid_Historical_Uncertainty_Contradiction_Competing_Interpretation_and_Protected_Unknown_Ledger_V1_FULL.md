## 0.2. Strong Ledger Verdict

The Zirid historical suite contains enough evidence to support a coherent January 1062 Realm foundation, but not enough to collapse every disputed date, political relationship, urban status, actor identity, institution, population, resource, or force into one false certainty. This ledger makes those limits productive and reviewable.

Uncertainty is represented as owned state with evidence, alternatives, blockers, safe fallbacks, downstream consequences, reopening triggers, and retirement requirements. Unknown does not mean absent, neutral, zero, safe, disloyal, or unusable. A fallback may preserve playability or presentation, but it may never masquerade as historical proof or create active Opening State.

Later outcome != opening explanation
Silence != absence
