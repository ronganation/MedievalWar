## 1. Principal Character Verdict

Al-Mu'izz ibn Badis is the supported January 1062 ruler candidate, the principal playable leader for the Zirids of Ifriqiya, and the strongest human perspective through which the opening Realm should be experienced. The profile does not reduce Al-Mu'izz to a generic emir, a doomed decline ruler, or merely Tamim's predecessor. The central problem is whether a long-ruling sovereign can turn concentrated authority at Mahdia into durable government, defend finite coastal capacity, manage fragmented urban and regional relationships, and prepare continuity without making the historical death or succession inevitable.

**Player fantasy:** Govern a famous but pressured Ifriqiyan dynasty from Mahdia after the loss of Kairouan, preserve surviving political and maritime capacity, decide how far to negotiate or contest the interior, manage the consequences of the Fatimid rupture, and create a succession settlement that can survive personal unavailability.

**Central campaign question:** Can Al-Mu'izz convert dynastic legitimacy, Mahdia's capital functions, and surviving Zirid networks into a resilient government before fragmentation, maritime pressure, and succession risk expose every remaining dependency?
