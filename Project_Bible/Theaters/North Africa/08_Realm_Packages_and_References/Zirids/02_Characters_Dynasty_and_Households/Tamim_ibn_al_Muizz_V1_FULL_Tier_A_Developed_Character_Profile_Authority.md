## 1. Principal Character Verdict

Tamim ibn al-Mu'izz is the principal historical successor candidate for the Zirids of Ifriqiya and the required second Tier-A character in the Realm package. At the 1 January 1062 global anchor, Tamim is not assumed to be ruler, officeholder, commander, resident at Mahdia, or designated heir. Tamim's opening importance comes from supported kinship and historical succession direction, while every practical role remains owner-gated. In an approved post-transition variant or a valid runtime succession, Tamim becomes the principal Realm-anchor candidate whose central problem is turning inherited dynastic claim into recognized, supplied, informed, and durable government.

**Player fantasy:** Begin as an uncommitted but politically consequential dynastic successor, survive the final months of a long reign, prepare without prematurely dividing the court, and if succession occurs, take control of a contracted maritime Realm whose coastal survival, urban relationships, Sicilian opportunities, and interior pressures must be actively rebuilt rather than inherited for free.

**Central campaign question:** Can Tamim convert kinship and historical succession potential into legitimate and effective Zirid rulership without relying on a forced death date, automatic recognition, invented opening command, or later achievements loaded as starting assets?

## 15. Conditional Blind Spots and Failure Tendencies

These are conditional risks, not opening penalties.
