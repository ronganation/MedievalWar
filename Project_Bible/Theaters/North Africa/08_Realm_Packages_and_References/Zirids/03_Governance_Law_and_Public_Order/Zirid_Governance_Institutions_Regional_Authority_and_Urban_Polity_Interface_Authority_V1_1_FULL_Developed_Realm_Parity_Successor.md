## 1. Strong Governance Verdict

The January 1062 Zirid government is a functioning but materially constrained Mahdia-centered dynastic emirate. It is neither a fully centralized territorial state nor a powerless remnant. Government depends on the ruler, court access, records, seals, treasury custody, military mandates, juristic and public recognition, port and provisioning capacity, urban negotiation, route security, and directed relationships with regional and external actors.

The authority integrates two Tier-A character states. Al-Mu'izz ibn Badis is the January ruler candidate and principal government actor. Tamim ibn al-Mu'izz is the principal succession candidate but holds no automatic January office. Only an approved transition may make Tamim the ruler candidate, and even then recognition, installation, command, records, treasury custody, and regional compliance remain separate steps.
