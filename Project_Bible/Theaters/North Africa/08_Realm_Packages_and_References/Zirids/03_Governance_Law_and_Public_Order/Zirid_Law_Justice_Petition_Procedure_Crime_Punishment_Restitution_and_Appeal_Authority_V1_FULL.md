## 1. Strong Law and Justice Verdict

The current Zirid package already contains governance petition loops, juristic consultation and mediation, public-order petition and grievance channels, information provenance and authentication, and property, tax, debt, market, and confiscation boundaries. None of those files owns the full lifecycle from standing and jurisdiction through evidence, hearing, legal disposition, remedy, enforcement authorization, appeal, reopening, and clemency.

This authority fills that real procedural gap without becoming a duplicate government, juristic, public-order, information, economic, military, custody, or character owner. It defines how claims are heard and resolved while all people, offices, institutions, property, money, forces, detention sites, records, and consequences remain with their existing owners.
