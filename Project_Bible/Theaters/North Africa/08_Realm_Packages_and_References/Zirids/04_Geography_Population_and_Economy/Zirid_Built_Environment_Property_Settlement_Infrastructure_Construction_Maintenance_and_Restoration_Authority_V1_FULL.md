## 1. Strong Zirid Built-Environment Verdict

The Zirid built-environment problem is to preserve and adapt a contracted, Mahdia-centered coastal capital system while every building, parcel, public space, service connection, and project remains dependent on lawful or contested rights, occupants, operators, labor, knowledge, materials, water, funding, routes, security, maintenance, and inspection. Capital status does not create adequate housing, safe water, maintained streets, defended walls, operating markets, staffed workshops, usable stores, functioning harbor facilities, or unlimited refuge capacity.

The player must choose what to inspect, maintain, repair, adapt, convert, protect, expand, suspend, salvage, abandon, or restore. Construction cannot be a linear building tree, and recovery cannot be an automatic reset.
