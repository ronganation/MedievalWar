## 1. Strong Diplomacy Verdict

The January 1062 Zirid diplomatic position is a dense but incompletely resolved network centered on a contracted Mahdia government. The Realm possesses historical diplomatic standing and possible contact channels, but no active treaty, alliance, tribute, protection, truce, safe conduct, access right, military intervention, or commercial agreement is inferred merely from historical connection.

The diplomatic campaign problem is to preserve sovereign room for action while negotiating with stronger, neighboring, fragmented, maritime, urban, caliphal, and distributed actors. Every relationship is directed. The Zirids may know, claim, propose, promise, perform, breach, protest, recognize, pressure, or settle only their own side. Every counterparty retains its own intentions, consent, resources, obligations, knowledge, and implementation state.

## 44. Validation Catalogue

**Fatal:** one-sided draft becomes treaty; historical relationship becomes opening alliance, war, tribute, or protection; Zirid authority writes external private state; need creates envoy, army, fleet, money, goods, route, or consent; intervention creates annexation; succession resets every agreement; Opening State overwrites an advanced save.

**Blocking:** relationship lacks parties, direction, date, domain, owner, evidence, and confidence; negotiation lacks representative and mandate; agreement lacks reciprocal receipts, terms, duration, obligations, resources, performance, remedy, and exit; external action lacks information, access, consent, and specialist-owner capacity.

**Advisory:** diplomacy becomes one attitude score; caliphal recognition becomes universal legitimacy; shared religion or dynasty predetermines alignment; breach always causes war; AI lacks abstention, exit, or recovery.
