## 1. Strong Current Authority Verdict

The Zirid knowledge system is a current, usable Realm capability, not a future placeholder. It provides the people-and-institution layer that allows government orders to be drafted and authenticated, petitions and judgments to be recorded, treasury and market accounts to be maintained, diplomatic correspondence to cross language boundaries, military and maritime knowledge to persist, religious and juristic teaching to continue, and dynastic and regional memory to survive political contraction.

Mahdia and Kairouan create a deliberate two-center design. Mahdia concentrates the current court, government, chancery, port, diplomatic reception, military administration, and capital patronage interfaces. Kairouan supplies a distinct juristic, scholarly, Qur'anic, manuscript, teaching, witnessing, and historical-memory relationship. The player can preserve, reconnect, compete over, neglect, or redirect these capabilities without being handed invented universities, libraries, literacy rates, named scholars, or free technology.
