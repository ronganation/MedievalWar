## 1. Strong Information Verdict

The January 1062 Zirid information system is a finite, delayed, actor-visible network centered on Mahdia but dependent on courts, households, chancery records, messengers, ports, ships, markets, merchants, jurists, scholars, urban representatives, military reports, travelers, and verified external communications. The capital may concentrate reports, but capital concentration does not create complete knowledge.

Information is a strategic resource with provenance, age, confidence, access, cost, route, recipient, and distortion. The player and AI must distinguish observation from report, report from assessment, warning from certainty, secrecy from invisibility, and deception from guaranteed belief. No actor may read hidden state merely because a related city, dynasty, army, community, or route exists.

## 47. Validation Catalogue

**Fatal:** ruler or faction receives omniscience; historical outcome becomes opening knowledge; need creates messenger, agent, scout, source, horse, ship, route, office, or funds; external or urban private state is read locally; a seal proves truth; a warning creates an event; deception overwrites target belief without evidence; Opening State overwrites an advanced save.

**Blocking:** record lacks subject, source, holder or recipient, date, route or channel, confidence, access, and owner; message jumps from draft to receipt; character acts on knowledge not held; counterintelligence case lacks authority and evidence; deception lacks initiator, target, means, intended belief, and outcome.

**Advisory:** all reports become shared faction knowledge; uncertainty is hidden; rumor is always false; counterintelligence becomes automatic purge; warning has no time horizon or indicators; bad outcomes create permanent incompetence traits.
