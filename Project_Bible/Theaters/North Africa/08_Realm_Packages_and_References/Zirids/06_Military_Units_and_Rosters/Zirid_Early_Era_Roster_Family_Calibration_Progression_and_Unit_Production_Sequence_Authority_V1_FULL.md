## 1. Purpose and Control Scope

This authority controls:

- cross-family calibration of all twenty inherited Zirid Early Era candidates;
- disposition into unit-card, attachment, function, external-provider, merge, hold, or quarantine lanes;
- nearest-neighbor collision review;
- recruitment-source, ownership, battlefield-role, campaign-role, burden, and counterplay differentiation;
- progression, persistence, coexistence, specialization, and closure relationships;
- evidence work needed before any master unit profile;
- research-packet order and conditional profile-production order;
- rollback and owner gates for every transition.

This authority does not create historical facts, final names, exact equipment, exact statistics, people, pools, units, officers, mounts, vessels, crews, inventories, formations, armies, garrisons, registry rows, opening-state records, runtime state, importers, or Unreal assets.
