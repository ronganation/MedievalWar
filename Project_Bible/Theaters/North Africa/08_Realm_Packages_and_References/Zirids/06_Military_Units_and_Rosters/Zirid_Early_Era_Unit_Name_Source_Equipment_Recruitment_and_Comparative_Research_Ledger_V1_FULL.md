The current source base supports a disciplined research program, not a finalized Zirid roster. Broad historical orientation confirms the relevance of the Zirid dynasty in Ifriqiya, its retreat to Mahdia, and its wider Mediterranean and Sicilian interfaces. Those facts do not establish January 1062 unit names, formations, weapons, armor distributions, providers, pool sizes, garrisons, ships, crews, or opening availability. - Create one authoritative research ledger for Early Era Zirid military terminology, source identity, equipment, recruitment, and comparator use.
