## 0.1 Bounded Purpose

This packet closes the first research wave at a practical planning level without opening a deeper historical-research branch. It evaluates the five highest-priority Zirid roster families against the existing authority set, preserves useful candidate identity, states the strongest unresolved evidence requirement, and returns each family to a clear disposition.

It does not claim that broad historical context proves exact January 1062 units, names, equipment distributions, recruitment pools, formations, or opening availability.
