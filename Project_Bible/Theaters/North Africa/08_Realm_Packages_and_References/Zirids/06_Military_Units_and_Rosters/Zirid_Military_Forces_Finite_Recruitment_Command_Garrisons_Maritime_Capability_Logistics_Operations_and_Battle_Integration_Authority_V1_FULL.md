## 1. Strong Military Verdict

The January 1062 Zirid military position is a capital-centered, coastal-defense and survival problem with uncertain standing-force depth, uncertain inland access, and potentially valuable but unproven maritime capability. The authority does not impose a large army, a collapsed army, a navy, or a preset roster. It defines the evidence and system contracts needed to determine what force can exist, mobilize, move, fight, recover, and remain politically and economically sustainable.

The intended gameplay identity is disciplined finite force generation under pressure. The Realm must protect Mahdia, preserve command and provisioning, negotiate access, assess whether urban or distributed actors will cooperate, and choose between defense, relief, limited campaigning, maritime support, intervention, or abstention. Every option consumes real people, time, equipment, transport, food, water, fodder, money, information, and political capacity.
