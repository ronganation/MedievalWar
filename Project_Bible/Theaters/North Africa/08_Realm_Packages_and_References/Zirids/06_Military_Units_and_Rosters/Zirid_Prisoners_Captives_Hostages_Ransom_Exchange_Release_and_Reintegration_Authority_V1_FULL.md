## 1. Strong Zirid Custody Verdict

The Zirid custody system treats every captured, detained, protected, transferred, held, exchanged, released, or returning person as an existing person whose identity, status, custody basis, custodian, support, property, communication, health access, review, and eventual disposition must remain traceable. Capture creates responsibility, not ownership, guilt, intelligence, labor, recruitment, or income.

Mahdia may become a capital, military, maritime, diplomatic, household, and legal custody interface without possessing a prison system, unlimited guards, adequate space, safe water, food, care, transport, or ransom capacity. Regional and foreign custody requires directed owner records and reciprocal receipts. Release is only the beginning of return and reintegration.
