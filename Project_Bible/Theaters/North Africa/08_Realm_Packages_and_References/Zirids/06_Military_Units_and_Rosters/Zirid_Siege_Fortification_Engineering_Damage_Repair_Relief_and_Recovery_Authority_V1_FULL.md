## 1. Strong Zirid Siege Verdict

The Zirid siege problem is a contest around defended places, coastal access, routes, water, food, people, command, information, labor, materials, engineering, health, negotiated authority, and recovery. Mahdia may be strategically central and defensible without being invulnerable, fully supplied, fully garrisoned, or fully maintained. A besieger may appear outside a place without completing investment, isolation, blockade, breach, assault, or capture. A defender may possess walls without possessing adequate command, stores, repair capacity, or relief.

The player-facing promise is to prepare, observe, supply, engineer, protect, negotiate, endure, break off, relieve, repair, and recover through finite capability. Neither side receives a free siege train, automatic starvation, scripted surrender, instant occupation, or restored settlement because an icon or timer exists.
