The current evidence and package authorities support preserving a differentiated Zirid roster research architecture, but they do not support immediate promotion of any candidate into a final named historical unit. The correct result is neither an empty roster nor a fabricated finalized roster. It is a controlled candidate set with explicit survival, merge, quarantine, equipment, visual, and research dispositions.

The Zirid military identity remains Mahdia-centered, coastal Ifriqiyan, finite, combined-arms, and materially constrained. Historical context supports the relevance of Sanhaja dynastic military memory, coastal concentration, land campaigning, and Sicilian intervention, but broad context does not establish exact January 1062 unit names, equipment distributions, formations, pool sizes, or opening availability.
