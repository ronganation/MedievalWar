## 1. Strong Faction-Event Verdict

The Zirid event system should turn the realm's mature authority portfolio into readable causality without replacing simulation with scripted history. Events should arise because Mahdia-centered government, coastal access, fragmented inland relationships, urban-polity interfaces, dynastic transition, provisioning, material limits, military risk, juristic institutions, historical memory, foreign pressure, and information uncertainty reach owner-verified conditions.

The player should face meaningful choices about preservation, negotiation, investigation, investment, relief, defense, withdrawal, succession preparation, and conditional transformation. Every option must use actual authority, evidence, people, time, goods, money, routes, relationships, and institutional capacity. Delay, partial performance, refusal, nonoccurrence, and recovery remain valid outcomes.
