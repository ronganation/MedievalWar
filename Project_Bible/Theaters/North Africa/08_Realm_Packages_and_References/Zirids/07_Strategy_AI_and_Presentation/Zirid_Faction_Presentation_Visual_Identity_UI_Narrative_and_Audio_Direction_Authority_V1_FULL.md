## 1. Strong Presentation Verdict

Present the Zirids as **a resilient Mahdia-centered Mediterranean dynasty preserving court, port, urban life, religious and juristic authority, coastal mobility, and historical dignity after territorial contraction**.

The faction must read as capable but compressed, maritime-aware but not automatically naval, courtly but not fantastically rich, religiously and institutionally layered but not visually homogenized, and politically threatened without being presented as already doomed. Mahdia is the core visual and narrative anchor, while Kairouan, Tunis, Sfax, Gabes, Gafsa, Tripoli, rural communities, maritime actors, and mobile constituencies remain distinct relationship surfaces rather than decorative copies of the capital.
