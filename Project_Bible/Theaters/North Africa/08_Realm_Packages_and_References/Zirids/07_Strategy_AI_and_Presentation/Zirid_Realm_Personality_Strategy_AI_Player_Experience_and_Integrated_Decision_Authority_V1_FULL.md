## 1. Developed Realm Personality Verdict

The Zirids are a **contracted Mediterranean dynastic power defending and rebuilding from Mahdia while negotiating the gap between historic Ifriqiyan claim, practical coastal capacity, urban relationships, institutional memory, mobile constituencies, and external pressure**.

Their governing personality is **resilient, status-conscious, maritime-aware, institutionally adaptive, selectively assertive, and deeply capacity-sensitive**. Their strategic identity is not passive survival and not inevitable reconquest. It is **hold Mahdia, preserve dynastic and governmental continuity, sustain the capital through land and sea, maintain credible relationships beyond direct control, recover useful regional links, and exploit opportunity only when information, supply, force, aftermath, and political cost align**.
