## 1. Strong Zirid Environmental Verdict

The Zirid environment is a dynamic, place-specific, observed, uncertain, seasonal, cumulative, and adaptable system organized around Mahdia’s coastal capital exposure and a wider network of coastal cities, ports, inland routes, oasis interfaces, agricultural and pastoral spaces, distributed constituencies, and central Mediterranean movement. Environmental pressure must arise from actual place, season, weather, water, soil, vegetation, coastal and maritime conditions, land use, maintenance, prior damage, and owner-provided activity rather than static climate bonuses.

Mahdia’s coastal and promontory setting creates strategic opportunity and exposure: sea access, communication, defense, and maritime provisioning may coexist with storm, wind, wave, landing, erosion, salt, fire, water-storage, blockade, and transport risks. Kairouan, Tunis, Sfax, Gabes, Gafsa, countryside communities, routes, pasture, oases, and external interfaces require distinct environmental records. No exact January 1062 weather, climate normal, storm, drought, flood, fire, coastline, erosion, land degradation, pasture condition, woodland extent, or adaptation program is authored here.
