## 1. Strong Zirid Health Verdict

Zirid health is a household, capital, coastal, port, market, military, displacement, and recovery system. Mahdia concentrates population, government, garrison, maritime traffic, storage, markets, visitors, labor, dependants, and political pressure, but capital status creates no safe water, food access, sanitation, practitioners, medicines, care capacity, or healthy housing. Kairouan, Tunis, Sfax, Gabes, Gafsa, Tripoli, countryside communities, maritime actors, travelers, and distributed constituencies retain distinct health and access conditions.

The player-facing problem is survival under uncertainty: protect food and water access, preserve household care, maintain work and service capacity, respond proportionately to illness and injury, support military and maritime recovery, manage displacement and urban pressure, and rebuild household and institutional function after damage. No opening epidemic, mortality rate, hospital, physician, pharmacy, burial ground, practitioner roster, or medical inventory is fabricated.
