## 1. Strong Production Verdict

The Zirid production problem is not a list of faction bonuses. It is the problem of converting uncertain and fragmented access to land, water, animals, labor, knowledge, tools, fuels, raw materials, workshops, transport, and external exchange into received, inspected, owned, usable, and maintainable goods.

Mahdia may possess demand, institutional concentration, port access, and craft potential without possessing every input or every output. Inland or external sources may possess materials without granting access. A building may support a process without operating. An operator may possess skill without tools. A treasury may authorize expenditure without obtaining goods. A shipment may arrive without acceptance. Military priority may redirect material without eliminating civilian cost.
