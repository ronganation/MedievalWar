## 1. Strong Verdict

The existing Zirid portfolio contains education and knowledge interfaces, but no current file owns the complete knowledge lifecycle from teaching and learning through literacy, scribal competence, texts, collections, archives, scholarship networks, translation training, patronage, cultural production, preservation, loss, and recovery. This authority fills that nonduplicative gap.

Mahdia is the capital-centered court, chancery, port, government, and institutional interface. Kairouan remains a major juristic, scholarly, Qur'anic, manuscript, and historical-memory interface. Neither place label creates a school, madrasa, university, library, archive, named scholar, teacher, scribe, manuscript inventory, or literacy rate.
