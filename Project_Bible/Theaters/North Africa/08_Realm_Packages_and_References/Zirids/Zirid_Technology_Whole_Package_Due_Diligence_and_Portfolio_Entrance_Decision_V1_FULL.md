## 10. Acceptance Criteria for the Zirid Portfolio

- Preserve all current owner boundaries and external locks.
- Use the four-layer shared model: Shared Knowledge, Realm Program, Regional Realization, Fielded Capability.
- Define faction-specific development programs rather than a universal linear tree.
- Retain lower-cost and inherited capabilities where their owners support them.
- Include opportunity cost, maintenance, failure, reversal, loss, recovery, and nonoccurrence.
- Preserve regional and local realization rather than Realm-wide automatic unlocks.
- Keep exact opening selections, numbers, costs, durations, and modifiers deferred.
- Create no people, buildings, goods, equipment, ships, units, recruitment pools, agreements, events, Registry records, Opening State bindings, payloads, runtime state, or Unreal assets.
- Preserve the three independent temporal tracks.
- Include AI and player explanations without allowing AI to bypass owner hard gates.
