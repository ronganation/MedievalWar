## 4. Player Contract

- **Class:** Trained contract crossbow missile infantry basis
- **Best use:** Prepared lines, valuable targets, siege approaches, walls, gates, crossings, and chokepoints
- **Worst use:** Unsupported open flanks, cavalry attack, rapid movement, prolonged melee, obstructed fire, and ammunition exhaustion
- **Campaign value:** Temporary deliberate crossbow capability without ownership of the source
- **Replacement:** Original or separately approved compatible source only
- **Opening state:** No company, pool, route, equipment pattern, quantity, or availability authorized

### Short Description

Hired crossbow infantry delivering powerful, deliberate missile fire but requiring protection during their reload cycle.


### Encyclopedia Description

Mercenary Crossbowmen are contract missile troops organized around deliberate crossbow fire rather than rapid skirmishing. Their exact origin may differ by theater, but a valid company must bring practiced users, working crossbows, compatible bolts and loading equipment, leadership, fire discipline, and a recoverable source relationship. The shared card does not make every regional expression visually identical and does not create a global hiring pool.

On the battlefield, the company is most effective when protected by terrain, fortifications, or steady infantry. Its crossbows can threaten valuable exposed targets, reinforce a siege line, and punish movement through controlled approaches. The same firing cycle leaves the formation vulnerable while spanning and reloading. Fast infantry, cavalry, flank pressure, hostile missiles, ammunition loss, and a blocked withdrawal lane can turn a strong firing position into a trap.

The player should treat the company as a positional contract asset. Preserve clear lanes, choose targets carefully, protect the reload cycle, conserve ammunition, and displace before the enemy reaches contact. Regional equipment and presentation may evolve, but pavise formations, mounted crossbows, militia crossbows, and named city companies require their own evidence and, where their gameplay changes materially, their own profile.

One company may be experienced and well equipped; another may be reduced, mixed, or recently reconstituted. The card owns the shared contract-crossbow decision while the company record owns actual quality.

## 4B. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Mercenary Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Contract foot crossbow infantry
- **Tier:** Source- and company-dependent baseline to established
- **Era:** Conditional wherever exact chronology and service networks permit
- **Source:** An actual independent, displaced, regional, captain-led, or externally released crossbow company under valid contract
- **Battle promise:** Immediate deliberate crossbow pressure from a coherent hired formation without giving the employer permanent ownership of its source
- **Best use:** Protected lines, walls, gates, crossings, siege positions, valuable targets, and temporary missile-role gaps
- **Worst use:** Unsupported flanks, cavalry contact, repeated relocation, poor contract support, depleted bolts, prolonged melee, and operations far beyond the company route
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Faster access than developing a domestic institution, but expensive, finite, externally replaceable, politically conditional, and capable of leaving
- **Distinctive risk:** The employer can lose the company, its future willingness, or the whole source relationship even after a battlefield victory
