Mercenary Pavise Crossbowmen are contract missile specialists whose defining capability is not greater universal damage. The company transports, places, orients, and maintains large protective shields so the crossbow crews can survive more of the vulnerable reload cycle from a chosen direction. The system trades mobility, setup time, transport capacity, formation space, and flank security for sustained deliberate fire from prepared positions.

The unit is distinct from ordinary Mercenary Crossbowmen because the pavise is an equipment, crew, deployment, geometry, recovery, and campaign-supply system. It is distinct from Heavy Crossbowmen because its defining advantage is protected reload and directional cover rather than heavier mechanisms or reinforced ammunition.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Mercenary Pavise Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Later protected-reload contract crossbow specialists
- **Core verb:** Plant, Shoot, Shelter
- **Source:** Exact company with crossbow users, serviceable pavises, shield handling, transport, ammunition, leadership, contract, and protected-reload practice
- **Battle promise:** Establish a directional protected firing station and sustain deliberate crossbow pressure while the pavise system remains deployed, oriented, supplied, and defended
- **Best use:** Siege lines, walls, gates, defended crossings, prepared field lanes, missile exchanges, urban approaches, and positions with protected flanks
- **Worst use:** Rapid maneuver, pursuit, several-direction attacks, cavalry penetration, setup under pressure, broken withdrawal, abandoned pavises, and melee
- **Strategic value:** Buys a mature protected crossbow package where the employer lacks time or institutions to create one
- **Strategic cost:** Professional crews, pavise teams, shields, carts, crossbows, bolts, tools, storage, roads, quarters, repair, pay, and very slow replacement
- **Signature decision:** Hold the prepared line, reorient it, or abandon equipment to save people before the position collapses

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Mercenary Pavise Crossbowmen fight from prepared directional cover. Their large shields protect the vulnerable reload cycle only when the pavises are present, serviceable, transported, deployed, oriented, and supported by terrain and friendly troops. The front may be difficult to suppress while the sides, rear, setup process, withdrawal, transport, and gaps remain exploitable.

Some source expressions may carry pavises on the back and turn the shield toward the threat while reloading. Others may plant individual pavises, use supports, work in shooter-bearer teams, or employ another historically supported arrangement. The shared card does not force one universal posture. The actual company expression must state how the shields are carried, emplaced, held, moved, recovered, and paired with shooters.

Use the company to control a lane, win a missile exchange, or anchor a siege position. Do not mistake the shield line for a melee wall. When the enemy bypasses the protected face or forces movement, the company must choose between preserving shields, weapons, ammunition, and people.

# PART VII. BATTLEFIELD SYSTEM

## 27. Signature Battle Cycle

```text
Select a tactically valuable lane with defensible flanks and a rear route
-> transport and unload pavises, crossbows, bolts, tools, and stores
-> assign stations, spacing, orientation, shooters, and handlers
-> deploy the shield line and verify gaps
-> hold fire until a valuable target enters the approved lane
-> shoot, shelter, span, reload, and repeat under directional cover
-> reassess ammunition, orientation, flank pressure, fire, cavalry, and withdrawal
-> hold, reorient, contract the frontage, or withdraw
-> recover pavises, weapons, wounded, bolts, and property where possible
```
