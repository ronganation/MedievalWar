## 2. Concise Verdict

France is a verified Realm package or reference folder under the West Francia Theater, and it is a plausible but still provisional anchor for the Theater modernization sequence. Current evidence establishes a substantial France Character Authority and identifies upstream France Historical Reconciliation and ID Registry authorities by name. It does not establish a complete recursive package inventory, complete companion-manifest closure, a verified France Opening State file, importer execution, canonical Registry adoption, Unreal compilation, runtime execution, save-load behavior, replay, performance, battle integration, or physical promotion.

France therefore remains correctly classified as Partial. The immediate modernization lane is readiness and source discovery, Registry-owner verification, cross-Realm lock review, quarantine resolution, and importer evidence. This packet does not promote France as the permanent anchor and does not infer missing files.

## 8. France Strategic Thesis Retained for Discovery

France should preserve the central tension already visible in the discovered authority: a consecrated child king and recognized Crown exist alongside limited direct capacity, contested access, powerful princes, a minority government, household and ecclesiastical mediation, and unevenly enforceable service. The modernization must not flatten France into West Francia, the Regnum Francorum, royal demesne, all Capetian-recognizing princes, or a later centralized kingdom.

```yaml
must_preserve:
  - crowned minority kingship
  - Anne of Kiev as a distinct political and household actor
  - Baldwin V as externally owned and mandate-bounded
  - underage-role separation
  - princely counterweights
  - directed and asymmetric relationships
  - Crown recognition without automatic obedience
  - later-history exclusion
  - conditional personal-rule development
must_not_infer:
  - universal regency
  - unified French army or treasury
  - direct Crown administration of all Theater Realms
  - automatic knowledge sharing
  - automatic Flemish, Norman, Blois, Valois, Aquitanian, or Breton compliance
  - scheduled later deaths, marriages, wars, accessions, or consolidations
```
