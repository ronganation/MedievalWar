## 1. Concise Verdict

Aquitaine begins with broad economic potential but no verified unified treasury, property inventory, tax system, production ledger, or guaranteed access to the resources associated with the duchies of Aquitaine and Gascony or the county of Poitiers.

The economic campaign should center on converting **scattered rights, negotiated cooperation, property access, production, markets, routes, and receipts into usable capacity** without exhausting the households, communities, institutions, and magnates that make the composite principality function.

```text
Broad title geography
+ distinct Poitevin, Aquitanian, and Gascon property worlds
+ Atlantic, Gironde, Garonne, Charente, and Pyrenean opportunity
+ negotiated service and local rights
- leakage, delay, dispute, damage, local consumption, and competing commitments
= conditional Aquitanian economic capacity
```

No exact opening treasury balance, income, tax rate, property list, market price, yield, stock, debt, arrears, toll, rent, or trade flow is invented.

## 41. Risks and Mitigations

- **Aquitaine receives fantasy wealth from broad titles.** Restrict capacity to verified rights and completed pipelines.
- **The economy becomes one opaque score.** Preserve sources, accounts, commitments, and explanations beneath a readable summary.
- **Gascony and Poitou collapse into one fiscal block.** Preserve regional title, property, access, and collection differences.
- **Bordeaux becomes free money.** Require property, operators, goods, vessels, routes, security, agreements, and collection.
- **Property becomes ownership-only.** Track possession, custody, administration, use, operation, beneficiaries, and disputes.
- **Taxation ignores communities.** Consume labor, seed, animals, recruitment, transport, local consumption, and recovery.
- **Church and magnate wealth is absorbed.** Enforce owner locks and negotiated interfaces.
- **War generates free income.** Require custody, distribution, transport, legal and diplomatic consequences, and occupation costs.
- **Barbastro becomes a guaranteed payout.** Keep every cost and reward conditional.
- **Finance becomes paperwork.** Automate routine processes and surface only consequential choices.
