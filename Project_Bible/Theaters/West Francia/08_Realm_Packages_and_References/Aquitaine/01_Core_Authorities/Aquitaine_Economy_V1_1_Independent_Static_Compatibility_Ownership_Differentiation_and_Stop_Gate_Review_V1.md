## 17. Final Position

Aquitaine Economy is closed at static-authority scope. The V1 authority remains the controlling substantive owner, and the V1.1 addendum supplies the accepted bounded shared-family compatibility layer. The closure preserves the composite title economy, nine regional portfolios, conditional Atlantic and southern opportunities, owner-routed Population and Supply boundaries, Barbastro nonfabrication, Regional Power ownership, honest unknowns, and the unsorted package-wide discovery requirement.

The static closure does not claim that every Economy-relevant file in the Aquitaine core authority collection has been physically inventoried or opened. That remains a required read-only intake gate before implementation-facing work. No broad rewrite, second general Economy authority, immediate V1.2, folder restructuring, Registry commitment, Opening-State activation, payload authoring, importer execution, runtime mutation, or Unreal implementation is justified.
