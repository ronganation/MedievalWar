## 1. Authority Verdict

Aquitaine is a composite southern principality whose title scale exceeds routinely usable authority. Its diplomacy must convert recognized rank, negotiated government, regional relationships, routes, ecclesiastical channels, military capability, property, and information into bounded agreements without treating William VIII as the Realm, local powers as generic rebels, claims as war permissions, or future opportunities as active events.

Aquitaine diplomacy is therefore **Negotiated Ducal Coalition and Regional-Power Diplomacy**.

```text
Broad Title Reach + Valid Principal + Regional Recognition + Bounded Mandate + Reliable Information + Negotiated Local Cooperation + External Receipt + Reserved Capacity + Performance + Credible Exit = Sustainable Aquitanian Diplomatic Action
```

This authority owns Aquitaine-specific diplomatic definitions and coordination lifecycles. It creates no opening message, envoy, recognition record, proposal, agreement, alliance, war, coalition, access grant, financial obligation, person arrangement, political settlement, opportunity instance, or reciprocal external state.
