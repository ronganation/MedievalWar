# 1. Verdict

Aquitaine's opening personality should be proud, expansive, negotiated, Atlantic-facing, and southern-aware, but constrained by succession uncertainty, uneven authority, long internal distances, and dependence on local cooperation.

The AI should act like a ruler of a great principality whose potential exceeds its immediately usable capacity.

> Preserve the union, convert prestige into cooperation, and project power selectively without abandoning the Poitevin center that makes the principality governable.

The personality explains preferences. It does not determine destiny.

## 5.1 Preserve the current union

The AI values keeping Aquitaine, Gascony, and Poitiers under workable common leadership.

Reasons may include:

- no approved male heir;
- no surviving brother fallback;
- unresolved collateral candidates;
- distinct regional recognition;
- dependence on William VIII's personal authority.

The AI may propose:

- succession consultation;
- collateral research;
- marriage diplomacy;
- household protection;
- regional recognition work;
- bounded delegation.

The AI cannot select an heir or create a child.

## 5.2 Protect Poitiers

Poitiers is the primary political center candidate.

The AI values:

- ruler access;
- court continuity;
- northern security;
- communication with major Poitevin lords;
- succession and household stability.

A distant expedition becomes less attractive when Poitiers is exposed.

## 5.3 Negotiate rather than flatten

The AI prefers negotiation with major lords when negotiated cooperation can secure:

- military service;
- route access;
- attendance;
- dispute resolution;
- local recognition;
- temporary support.

The AI does not treat every independent lord as an enemy or every refusal as rebellion.

## 5.4 Improve internal connectivity

The Charente-Garonne corridor is strategically important.

The AI may value:

- route security;
- court access;
- reporting;
- toll negotiation;
- assembly placement;
- Bordeaux connectivity;
- movement between Poitou and Gascony.

## 5.5 Use Atlantic potential

The AI recognizes possible value from:

- wine;
- salt;
- markets;
- ports and river trade;
- tolls;
- pilgrimage traffic.

The AI must distinguish local prosperity from ducal revenue.

## 5.6 Maintain southern awareness

The AI considers Pyrenean and Iberian opportunities when:

- succession exposure is manageable;
- court continuity exists;
- assembly and service are credible;
- routes and supplies are available;
- war aims and coalition terms are acceptable;
- external threats do not require William VIII at home.
