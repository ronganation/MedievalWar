## 1. Concise Verdict

Aquitaine is a large route-dependent composite principality. Its strategic power does not come from owning one uniform road system. It comes from converting distinct Poitevin, Charentais, Bordelais, Gascon, Atlantic, Garonne, Pyrenean, and external connections into usable movement, communication, supply, military concentration, and return paths.

The campaign problem is:

```text
Wide title geography
+ uneven local authority
+ negotiated passage
+ limited transport capacity
+ delayed information
= opportunity that must be made operational
```

Poitiers is the strongest opening government and itinerary anchor. Bordeaux is a major Atlantic, Gironde, Garonne, commercial, ecclesiastical, and Gascon-facing interface. Neither center creates automatic control over the route between them. No road, river lane, crossing, port, landing, ship, messenger, escort, convoy, or travel time exists merely because two places appear close on the campaign map.

## 3. Authority and Ownership

### 3.1 This authority owns

- Aquitaine-specific route-family, crossing, communication, messenger, itinerary, convoy, river-access, Atlantic-access, Pyrenean-access, and maritime-access definitions.
- Candidate corridor portfolios and their strategic functions.
- Route, route-segment, crossing, access, permission, transport, message-transit, warning, itinerary, diplomatic-mission, and convoy schemas.
- Aquitaine-facing route interfaces with France, Anjou, Toulouse, Burgundy, Brittany, Iberian powers, the Papacy, and other external owners.
- Route disruption, diversion, interception, repair, reopening, observation, AI, player presentation, save, migration, determinism, and validation rules.

### 3.2 This authority does not own

- place, settlement, river, estuary, coast, mountain, pass, port, landing, road, bridge, ford, or ferry identity;
- route ownership, passage rights, treaties, safe conduct, toll rights, access permissions, or active political control;
- ships, boats, crews, pilots, handlers, horses, draft animals, carts, wagons, garrisons, escorts, armies, fleets, or commanders;
- supply stocks, throughput, consumption, markets, population, labor, recruitment pools, or treasury balances;
- a message as knowledge, agreement, relationship, command, legal judgment, Papal order, or military result;
- exact January 1062 route segments, travel times, capacities, security, weather, or opening state;
- external reverse state or direct owner mutation.

## 48. Fatal and Warning Conditions

Fatal when:

- an unknown or unapproved route ID is activated;
- external owner state is written locally;
- a route family generates an operational segment;
- access is treated as ownership or capacity;
- a port generates a fleet;
- a delivered message becomes universal knowledge;
- a title or map color generates passage rights;
- Opening State is applied to an advanced campaign;
- equal inputs and versions produce unequal results.

Warn when:

- endpoint or crossing evidence is unresolved;
- access exists but capacity is unknown;
- a route lacks a return path;
- expected time depends on unverified weather or local state;
- a message lacks acknowledgment;
- a convoy lacks explicit custody or destination handling;
- an external mission lacks safe conduct;
- a strategic corridor has only one known route.

## 52. Player-Facing Summary

Aquitaine's power moves through a chain of negotiated connections. Poitiers can direct policy, Bordeaux can open Atlantic and Garonne opportunities, Gascony can extend southern reach, and the Pyrenees can connect Aquitaine to Iberian diplomacy and war. None of those advantages functions automatically.

The player must protect communication, maintain several route options, secure guides and passage, prepare household and government continuity, distinguish messenger access from army access, and avoid campaigns whose outward route is better understood than the return.

## 53. Final Design Summary

Aquitaine's route system should make a large realm feel large. Distance, local authority, transfer points, rivers, estuaries, Atlantic access, passes, capacity, security, and delayed information turn broad title geography into a real campaign challenge.

The design preserves Total War freedom. Players and AI may attack without a claim or Papal permission. Routes determine whether the action can be supported, reinforced, supplied, communicated, and survived. Diplomacy, Papal standing, reliability, recognition, occupation, and expansion systems determine the consequences.
