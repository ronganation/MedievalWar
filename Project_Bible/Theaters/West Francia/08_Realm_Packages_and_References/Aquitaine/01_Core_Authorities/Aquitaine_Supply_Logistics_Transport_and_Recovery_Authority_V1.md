## 1. Concise Verdict

Aquitaine should treat supply as an owned and observable chain from production or acquisition through custody, storage, allocation, loading, transport, transfer, delivery, distribution, consumption, loss, recovery, and replenishment support.

Aquitaine's military and political scale does not create one Realm supply pool. Poitiers, Thouars, Lusignan, Saintonge, the Charente corridor, Bordeaux, the Gironde and Garonne, Gascony, Armagnac-facing regions, Bayonne, and the Pyrenean approaches expose different sources, custodians, routes, transport assets, seasonal risks, political permissions, and local burdens.

```text
Authorized Source
+ Collected or Acquired Stock
+ Storage and Custody
+ Valid Route and Access
+ Transport and Handlers
+ Security and Local Cooperation
+ Delivery and Receipt
- Consumption, Loss, Delay, Spoilage, Diversion, and Competing Demand
= Strategic Endurance
```

A market, treasury, road, river, estuary, port, allied territory, or friendly settlement does not by itself supply an army, garrison, household, institution, refugee population, or siege. No exact opening stock, depot, wagon, cart, animal, vessel, crew, handler, consumption rate, convoy, endurance limit, attrition value, repair capacity, or recovery rate is created by this authority.

- direct and readable Total War army movement;

The military authority owns forces, command, garrisons, readiness, operations, casualties, and demobilization. The recruitment authority owns pools, availability, unit access, replenishment, and reconstitution. This authority owns the support chain those systems consume.

```text
Recruitment Pool != Supply
Available Recruits != Fed Recruits
Equipment Access != Delivered Equipment
Garrison Store != Field Army Store
Field Army Store != Siege Store
Treasury Funds != Delivered Provisions
```

Recruitment, musters, garrison release, reinforcement, and demobilization must expose supply effects without giving Supply ownership of people, units, or command.

## 36. Player-Facing Presentation

The player should receive clear Total War summaries such as:

- **Well Supplied**: current sources and delivery support the planned operation;
- **Limited Endurance**: the operation is possible but duration or force size is constrained;
- **Route Dependent**: one or more critical corridors or transfer points dominate the plan;
- **Local Burden Rising**: requisition, billeting, or transport demands threaten cooperation;
- **Garrison Exposed**: field support competes with retained local defense;
- **Fodder Constraint**: animals or mounted forces limit reach;
- **Maritime Lift Unconfirmed**: coast or port access exists but vessels, crews, or loading remain unresolved;
- **Return At Risk**: the outbound plan is stronger than the return plan;
- **Shortage Imminent**: known stock and delivery cannot sustain the expected demand.

The campaign UI should show broad endurance bands, leading causes, major dependencies, and recommended alternatives. It should not require daily ration management.

## 39. Save, Migration, and Replay

Saved supply state must preserve:

- definition and state versions;
- owners, custodians, operators, and beneficiaries;
- source, stock, storage, allocation, reservation, and commitment state;
- transport assets and assignments;
- convoys, routes, transfers, deliveries, and receipts;
- consumer demand, endurance, shortage, rationing, and relief;
- observations, confidence, and staleness;
- loss, capture, repair, demobilization, and recovery;
- external-owner locks;
- explicit unknown, intentional-empty, review, deferred, and quarantined states.

Migration must not:

- create stock from unknown fields;
- convert money into delivered goods;
- create transport from route access;
- merge title-specific or owner-specific stocks;
- duplicate external supply;
- refill stores because time advanced;
- transform old universal supply values into exact new records without provenance;
- replay already-applied orders or events.

Replay must produce deterministic decisions from identical authoritative inputs.

## 42. Performance and Workload Contract

Implementation must avoid:

- scanning every source, stock, consumer, and route every strategic tick;
- continuously simulating every sack, animal, cart, crew member, or meal;
- making every local delivery a player decision;
- representing every village as a depot;
- convoy spam;
- silent attrition with no leading-cause explanation.

Prefer:

- event-driven reassessment;
- aggregation by source, stock, consumer, and route class;
- dirty-record processing;
- cached endurance and leading-cause summaries;
- detailed simulation only for major armies, sieges, garrisons, expeditions, relief operations, and crises;
- direct player attention only for strategically meaningful allocations, requisitions, convoys, shortages, withdrawals, and recovery choices.
