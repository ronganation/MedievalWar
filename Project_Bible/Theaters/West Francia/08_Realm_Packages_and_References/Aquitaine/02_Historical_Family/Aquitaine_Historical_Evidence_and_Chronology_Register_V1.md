## 1. Creation and Developed-Realm Benchmark Verdict

The Aquitaine historical coverage decision found a genuine ownership gap for stable proposition identity, claim-level evidence, source classification, provenance, custody, authenticity, corroboration, contradiction, date precision, opening-date compatibility, and reusable evidence receipts. This authority fills that gap without re-owning broad historical interpretation, uncertainty resolution, Memory, Momentum, Emergence, claims, characters, titles, offices, institutions, property, geography, military state, or exact Opening State.

Developed England and Normandy authorities provide the benchmark architecture: evidence must be separated from claims, access, understanding, chronology, memory, and activation; confidence belongs to a particular claim and scope; provenance and source dependency must remain visible; contradiction is preserved rather than erased; and a candidate evidence portfolio does not pretend that exact archival records have already been populated.

## 3. Ownership Model

### 3.1 This authority owns

- proposition, claim, source, assertion, chronology, provenance, contradiction, and evidence-receipt identity;
- evidence classification and source dependence;
- custody, access, integrity, authenticity, and legibility review;
- claim-level support, contradiction, confidence, and qualification;
- exact, approximate, relative, and bounded chronology records;
- compatibility with the 1 January 1062 opening;
- later-history exclusion and chronology warnings;
- downstream receipts to Uncertainty, Memory, Momentum, Emergence, characters, Dynasty, Governance, Ecclesiastical, titles, specialists, Realm AI, and Opening State;
- source-action requests and review state;
- deterministic ordering, validation, save, migration, and serialization contracts for evidence records.

### 3.2 This authority does not own

- which broad historical interpretation is adopted by Historical Reconciliation;
- selection among unresolved alternatives;
- holder belief, memory, salience, or reception;
- pressure, tendency, or readiness;
- pathway eligibility, activation, resolution, or retirement;
- canonical person, title, office, institution, dynasty, household, relationship, claim, army, property, route, settlement, or Realm state;
- exact January 1062 initialization;
- source retrieval rights outside authorized systems;
- event scheduling, historical outcome, gameplay effect, or Unreal mutation.
