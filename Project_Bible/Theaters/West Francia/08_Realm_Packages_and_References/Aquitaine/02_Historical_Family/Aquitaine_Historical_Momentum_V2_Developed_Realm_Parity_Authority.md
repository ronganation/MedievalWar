# 1. Concise Verdict

Implement Aquitaine Historical Momentum as eight bounded, dated pressure families inherited from Historical Reconciliation and evaluated from current owner state. The 1062 opening contains a recently assembled composite principality, unresolved continuity, a remote French Crown, contested corridors, maritime opportunity, southern access, and powerful neighbors. Core rule:

**Facts establish conditions. Memory explains perceived precedent. Momentum evaluates present pressure. AI proposes action. Owners execute commands. Emergence resolves eligible pathways. No layer guarantees history.**

# 9. Unfinished Union Momentum

## 9.1 Canonical ID

`pressure.aquitaine.unfinished_union`

## 9.2 Strategic thesis

William VIII holds Aquitaine, Gascony, and Poitiers through distinct title histories. Recent accessions create reach but not automatic administrative integration, shared obedience, or one uniform political community.

## 9.3 Support inputs

- valid title possession and recognition;
- effective itinerant presence and communications;
- accepted mandates and assemblies;
- Poitevin, Gascon, and corridor cooperation;
- adjudicated disputes;
- lawful ecclesiastical mediation;
- reliable routes, officers, and contributions;
- owner-approved memories of negotiated lordship and Gascon inheritance.

## 9.4 Resistance and blockers

- title-specific customs and succession rules;
- unresolved lordship disputes;
- weak route or household data;
- office vacancies or unclear mandates;
- regional noncompliance;
- external war or overextension;
- attempts to collapse Gascony or Poitiers into generic Aquitaine state.

## 9.5 Eligible proposals

Convene targeted assemblies, confirm bounded mandates, travel to exposed jurisdictions, mediate disputes, protect routes, publish validated judgments, or defer integration where capacity is absent.

## 9.6 Prohibited outcomes

No automatic centralization, title merger, cultural homogenization, permanent Gascon obedience, or universal direct domain.

# 10. Succession Uncertainty Momentum

## 10.1 Canonical ID

`pressure.aquitaine.succession_uncertainty`

## 10.2 Strategic thesis

Recent brother-to-brother accessions and unresolved direct continuity create pressure to preserve the composite inheritance without preselecting a later spouse, child, collateral, title partition, or historical successor.

## 10.3 Inputs

Support may come from ruler health, lawful marriage continuity, validated offspring, prepared collateral lines, title-specific recognition channels, household stability, church process, and accepted succession arrangements. Risk rises through incapacity, unresolved marriage status, absent heirs, disputed collateral standing, divided title customs, weak household records, and external intervention.

`pressure.aquitaine.marriage_continuity` evaluates the current lawful continuity surface only. `pressure.aquitaine.collateral_uncertainty` evaluates eligible collateral depth only. Neither creates a spouse, child, claimant, or title right.

## 10.4 Eligible proposals

Review title-specific succession, protect the ruler and household, validate marriage and legitimacy jurisdiction, identify but do not activate lawful candidates, improve recognition channels, and avoid exposing all continuity options to one military risk.

## 10.5 Prohibited outcomes

No fixed heir, invented child, later marriage, Eleanor or William IX leakage, automatic partition, or one succession rule across all titles.

# 11. Distant Crown Momentum

## 11.1 Canonical ID

`pressure.aquitaine.distant_crown`

## 11.2 Strategic thesis

Aquitaine exists within a French royal hierarchy whose ceremonial and legal relevance exceeds the Crown's routine capacity to administer the southern principality. This creates recurring negotiation over recognition, service, summons, mediation, and practical autonomy.

## 11.3 Support inputs

Royal recognition, valid summons, negotiated service, shared church or diplomatic interests, lawful judgments, safe routes, reciprocal concessions, and successful mediation may support cooperation.

## 11.4 Resistance inputs

Distance, limited Crown capacity, disputed service scope, southern priorities, route weakness, princely noncompliance, conflicting legal interpretation, and coercive overreach may support autonomy or refusal.

## 11.5 Cross-realm lock

France owns Philip I, Crown commands, royal claims, French memories, and reverse relationships. Aquitaine may publish only Aquitaine-facing signals and compatibility events.

## 11.6 Prohibited outcomes

No automatic rebellion, submission, war, independence, troop obligation, or later dynastic union.

# 25. Migration Contract

Legacy pressure-like data decomposes rather than importing as one modifier:

- union score to titles, governance, routes, relationships, memories, and union signals;
- succession meter to lifecycle, marriage, household, eligibility, recognition, and continuity signals;
- autonomy score to Crown relations, summons, service, capacity, and distant-Crown signals;
- trade score to property, routes, ports, economy, security, and Atlantic signals;
- expedition countdown to routes, diplomacy, forces, supply, authority, signals, and Emergence prerequisites;
- border hostility score to claims, diplomacy, routes, Network state, and neighbor-specific signals.

Migration preserves canonical ID, direction, stage, active signals, blockers, transition history, and eligibility. Deprecated IDs resolve through one direct alias only. Alias chains and unknown IDs fail safely. A countdown never migrates into a guaranteed event.

# 26. Determinism Contract

Randomness cannot decide historical fact, identity, ownership, signal validity, or opening state. Tie-breaking among equally valid outcomes belongs to the owning runtime system and must use stable sorting plus a saved deterministic seed where randomness is permitted.

## 56. Runtime, Save, Migration, and Atomic Import Contract

Runtime records remain owner-routed, versioned, deterministic, and inactive until approved. Save preserves definitions, instances, scopes, audiences, contributions, stages, directions, blockers, relief, collision and redirection history, owner versions, and traces. Loading never resets Momentum to January 1062.

Migration may map deprecated definitions, rebuild derived summaries, preserve divergent pressure state, and quarantine unresolved ownerless inputs. It may not activate candidates, fabricate sources, erase collision history, create events, select Emergence, merge scopes, or execute owner commands.

```text
validate versions and ids
then resolve definitions and direct aliases
then validate sources scopes audiences visibility owners blockers and collisions
then reject later-history and cross-owner violations
then build dry-run transaction
then emit accepted deferred rejected and quarantined report
then commit only after explicit activation authority
then write receipt rollback reference completion marker and deterministic trace
```
