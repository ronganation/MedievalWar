## 5. Brittany Diplomatic Identity

The new authority should define Brittany as:

> **Restored Ducal Authority, Regional Bargaining, Ecclesiastical Contest, and Cross-Channel Frontier Diplomacy**

This identity must preserve:

- Conan II as restored ducal principal without universal obedience;
- Odo of Penthièvre as a distinct regional and dynastic actor;
- Hawise as an independent dynastic and political actor;
- Hoël of Cornouaille as a regional principal and conditional succession, continuity, and military actor;
- Rivallon I of Dol as a frontier, ecclesiastical, and conditional external-aid actor;
- Dol and Tours ecclesiastical interfaces without local authorship of external Church state;
- Normandy, France, Anjou, Maine, England, Tours, and the external Church as separate external owners;
- maritime and port-facing opportunities without invented ships, routes, access, forces, or alliances;
- directed relationships rather than one mutual opinion score;
- later conflict as conditional rather than scripted.
