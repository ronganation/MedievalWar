## 1. Concise Verdict

Alan III enters the January 1062 campaign only as a deceased predecessor whose reign, death, family, foreign relationships, and disputed poisoning narrative still shape Brittany's living political order. His legacy helps explain Conan II's legitimacy, Hawise's dynastic standing, Odo of Penthievre's prior regency, the House of Rennes, Breton caution toward Norman involvement, and the memory of ducal authority before the prolonged minority and restoration struggle.

Alan III is not a ghost ruler. He cannot act, command, advise, own the duchy, generate hostility, prove Norman guilt, or predetermine Brittany's future.

## 6. Historical Identity and Project Baseline

Alan III is the canonical deceased Breton predecessor represented by `person.alan_iii_brittany`. The current Brittany Character authority assigns him Tier H, historical only. The project baseline preserves him as a former Duke of Brittany, a House of Rennes member, father of Conan II and Hawise, a predecessor whose death enabled the minority and regency sequence, and a subject of a disputed poisoning narrative.

The profile does not independently activate exact birth, marriage, office, campaign, property, or title records. Those require the Historical Evidence, Chronology, Dynasty, Title, Governance, and Opening State owners.
