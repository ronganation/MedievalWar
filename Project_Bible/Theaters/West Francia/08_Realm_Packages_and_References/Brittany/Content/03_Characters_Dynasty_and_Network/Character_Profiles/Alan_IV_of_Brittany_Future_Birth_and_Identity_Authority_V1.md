## 1. Concise Verdict

Add Alan IV to Brittany as a near-future, historically expected person candidate whose identity may become a living child only after a validated live-birth transition. Alan IV does not exist as an opening character on 1 January 1062, does not occupy an opening succession slot, and does not receive later ducal titles, adult traits, or historical outcomes in advance.

The authority preserves a strong historical direction without turning chronology into destiny.

```text
Historical expectation != Guaranteed birth
Parent candidates != Guaranteed conception
Pregnancy candidate != Guaranteed live birth
Live birth != Guaranteed name
Name != Guaranteed historical identity
Identity != Title
Dynastic descent != Succession
Later ducal reign != Scripted destiny
```
