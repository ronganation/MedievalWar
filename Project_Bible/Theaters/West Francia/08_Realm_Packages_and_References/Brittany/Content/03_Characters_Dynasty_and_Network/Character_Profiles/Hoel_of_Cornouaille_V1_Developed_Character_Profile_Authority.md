## 3. Concise Verdict

Hoël should enter the January 1062 Brittany design as a major regional principal whose Cornouaille authority, Nantes-facing review, marriage to Hawise, household capacity, routes, military sources, and succession relevance make Hoël indispensable to Breton politics without making Hoël a co-ruler, automatic Duke, or owner of Hawise's agency.

Hoël's strategic value lies in converting a strong southwestern base into negotiated participation in wider Breton government. Hoël can cooperate with Conan, bargain over Nantes, coordinate with Hawise, defend Cornouaille, support a bounded campaign, mediate a succession, or build an alternative regional coalition. Every result requires separate authority, access, resources, consent, and owner receipts.

## 4. Core Character Thesis

Hoël's central tension is:

**regional power versus ducal integration**

Hoël is strongest when Cornouaille and Nantes-facing interests give Hoël real bargaining power but do not predetermine hostility or succession. Marriage to Hawise connects Hoël to the Rennes line while preserving two persons, two birth dynasties, two knowledge states, two consent states, and potentially different political goals.

```text
Regional authority != Ducal authority
Marriage != Shared agency
Nantes relevance != Nantes ownership
Later ducal succession != Opening title
Military capacity != Automatic command
```
