## 1. Authority Verdict

Brittany diplomacy is **Restored Ducal Authority, Regional Bargaining, Ecclesiastical Contest, and Cross-Channel Frontier Diplomacy**. The system converts recognized rank, negotiated cooperation, directed relationships, household and ecclesiastical channels, ports, military obligations, and uncertain information into bounded diplomatic action without treating restoration as universal obedience or later conflict as inevitable.

```text
Restored Rank + Valid Principal + Directional Recognition + Limited Mandate + Regional Cooperation + Authenticated Communication + Owner-Reserved Capacity + Reciprocal Receipt + Performance + Credible Exit = Sustainable Breton Diplomatic Action
```

This authority closes the general-diplomacy coordination gap. It creates no opening message, envoy, recognition, proposal, agreement, alliance, war, coalition, access grant, payment, person arrangement, settlement, or reciprocal external state.

## 12. Restored Ducal Recognition and Consolidation

Restoration supplies standing, audiences, petitions, precedence, and claims to cooperation. It does not prove direct administration, unrestricted service, automatic attendance, taxation, access, command, or regional consent. Consolidation may use confirmation, mediation, bounded service, guarantee, office recognition, settlement, and negotiated autonomy.

## 13. Penthièvre and Odo

Odo of Penthièvre remains a distinct dynastic and regional actor. Kinship, grievance, title interest, military capacity, and access may inform proposals but cannot create loyalty, hostility, rebellion, claimant support, or contribution. Every direction requires separate knowledge, recognition, mandate, and performance state.

## 14. Hawise, Hoël, Cornouaille, and Nantes Interfaces

Hawise and Hoël retain separate agency. Marriage does not merge persons, titles, claims, households, regional interests, succession, command, or diplomatic consent. Cornouaille and Nantes-facing questions require their title, governance, law, property, military, and external receipts.

## 15. Dol, Combourg, Rivallon, Tours, and Church Interfaces

Rivallon, Dol, Combourg, Tours, and Church institutions remain distinct actors and owner surfaces. Ecclesiastical recognition, jurisdiction, appeal, mediation, sanctuary, protection, and secular support must not collapse. The authority does not preload a Dol rebellion or external-aid request.
