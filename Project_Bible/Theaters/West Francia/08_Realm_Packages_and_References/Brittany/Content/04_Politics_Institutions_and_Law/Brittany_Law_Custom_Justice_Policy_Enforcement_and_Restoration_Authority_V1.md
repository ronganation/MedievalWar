## 1. Breton Legal Verdict

Brittany should operate through a layered legal order combining recognized ducal peace, ducal and regional justice, lordship and castellany custom, household authority, military and fiscal obligation, witnessed acts, church jurisdiction, privilege, negotiated settlement, and practical enforcement capacity. Brittany does not begin with one codified national law book, uniform bureaucracy, modern police force, automatic ducal jurisdiction, or frictionless enforcement.

```text
Valid authority and jurisdiction
+
applicable law, custom, charter, oath, privilege, or policy
+
notice, evidence, procedure, and affected participants
+
judgment, settlement, or administrative decision
+
enforcement capacity, compliance, review, and restoration
=
lawful and durable outcome for a defined scope
```

```text
Ducal will != Law
Law != Policy
Policy != Judgment
Judgment != Enforcement
Enforcement != Compliance
Compliance != Legitimacy
Custom != Immutable Rule
Privilege != Universal Immunity
Emergency != Permanent Constitution
Punishment != Restoration
Refusal != Rebellion
```

## 18. Household and Court Contract

## 19. Titles, Claims, and Succession Contract

## 20. Governance and Office Contract

Governance supplies office, mandate, delegation, consultation, promulgation, administrative process, and continuity. A valid office still requires legal scope; a valid legal rule still requires an authorized executor.

## 21. Ecclesiastical Contract

Ecclesiastical owners retain church identity, office, jurisdiction, property, discipline, sanctuary acceptance, marriage and burial interfaces, and external recognition. Brittany law may record secular reception and owner receipts but cannot author church state.
