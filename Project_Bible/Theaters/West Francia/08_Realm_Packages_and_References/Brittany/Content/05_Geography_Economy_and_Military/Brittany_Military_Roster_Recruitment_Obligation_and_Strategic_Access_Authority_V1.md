## 1. Military Verdict

Brittany is a compact, regionally mediated military polity. Armed power comes from ducal and regional households, title and lordship obligations, castles and castellanies, settlement communities, market and maritime labor, paid specialists, and negotiated external contributions. Brittany does not possess a standing national army, an unlimited levy, free heavy cavalry, a fleet generated by coastline, or automatic regional obedience.

Brittany's distinguishing military problem is not lack of martial capacity. It is the political and material work required to assemble compatible sources without stripping households, castles, regions, ports, farms, and routes of their own protection and labor. Its battlefield identity should combine durable spear infantry, useful archery and light troops, scarce mounted retainers, strong local defense, rough-ground competence, route and crossing control, coastal flexibility, and bounded siege support.

## 13. Recruit, Replenish, Reconstitute, and Demobilize

Recruitment creates a new authorized formation from valid sources. Replenishment restores an existing unit from compatible pools and material. Reconstitution rebuilds a damaged identity around surviving cadre or an approved lineage. Demobilization returns people, animals, equipment, obligations, arrears, rewards, and consequences to their owners. These processes never collapse into free healing.

## 14. Obligation and Muster

Ordinary defense, extraordinary campaign service, garrison duty, household retainer service, paid service, mercenary contract, affinity contribution, allied contribution, transport, and military labor remain separate. Each has a geography, duration, notice, command, equipment, compensation, exemption, substitution, casualty, release, and dispute profile.

## 15. Command and Force Composition

Command appointment requires an objective, appointing authority, force components, hierarchy, geography, duration, tactical discretion, strategic limits, supply authority, reinforcement authority, siege and occupation scope, reporting, relief, and succession on loss. Packaging forces never transfers title, property, household control, or permanent troop ownership.

## 16. Personal and Political Boundaries

<Person>Conan II of Brittany</Person> is the recognized ruler and principal ducal command candidate, not the physical commander of every force. <Person>Odo of Penthièvre</Person> remains politically active but opening military access is constrained and review-bound. <Person>Hawise of Brittany</Person> retains independent rights and consent. <Person>Hoël of Cornouaille</Person> does not gain universal Nantes or Breton command through marriage. <Person>Rivallon I of Dol</Person> does not gain church ownership, metropolitan authority, or rebellion status through frontier responsibility.

## 17. Readiness and Tactical Identity

## 68. Battlefield Casualty Resolution and Campaign Persistence Boundary

Battle simulation must return component-specific results to the campaign owners. Unit strength alone is insufficient. Results must preserve dead, wounded, missing, captured, desertion, returned personnel, surviving cadre, officers, horses, equipment, ammunition, standards, cohesion, fatigue, prisoners, spoils, transport, and location.

The campaign layer then updates recruitment pools, households, population and labor, property, treasury, Network, obligations, garrisons, command succession, treatment, recovery, rewards, and future willingness. The battle layer may not directly create replacement people, legal title, property ownership, government, or an enduring political settlement.
