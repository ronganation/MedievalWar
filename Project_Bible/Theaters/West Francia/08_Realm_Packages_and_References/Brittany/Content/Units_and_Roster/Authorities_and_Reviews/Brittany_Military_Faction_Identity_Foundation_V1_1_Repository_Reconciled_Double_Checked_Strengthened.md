## 1. Executive Identity Verdict

> **Brittany is the Armed Duchy Between Kingdoms: a proud, regionally divided principality with royal memory, dangerous cavalry, powerful lordships, fortified approaches, Atlantic and Channel connections, and several plausible routes toward consolidation, confederation, overseas influence, or restored kingship.**

```yaml
faction_fantasy: ArmedDuchyBetweenKingdoms
political_engine: DucalAuthorityRegionalLordshipsAndExternalPatrons
military_engine: MusterScreenFixChargeRecover
iconic_strength: LayeredFastAndMediumBretonCavalry
essential_support:
  - RegionalSpears
  - JavelinAndLightFoot
  - BowAndCrossbowSupport
  - CastleAndTownTroops
  - SelectedLordshipRetainers
primary_weakness: CoordinationAndPoliticalAccessNotPrimitiveCapability
strategic_ceiling:
  - ConsolidatedDucalPrincipality
  - NegotiatedLordshipConfederation
  - ChannelAndAtlanticPower
  - AngloBretonDynasticNetwork
  - RestoredBretonKingdom
```

Brittany should never play as a small copy of France, an inferior Normandy, or a transplanted Celtic faction. Breton identity comes from the interaction between regional military cultures, ducal ambition, mounted warfare, strong local lordships, frontier castles, overseas circulation, and the problem of making the duchy act together.

## 2. Opening Political Character

Around the opening era, ducal prestige did not equal direct rule over every Breton territory. The duchy contained major political regions and lordships with their own dynasties, military resources, castles, clients, and interests. The ducal house could claim Brittany as a principality, but effective military coordination remained a campaign achievement rather than an opening fact.

```yaml
opening_status: DuchyWithRoyalMemory
opening_government_character: FirstAmongPowerfulRegionalLords
opening_military_character: StrongRegionalCapacityUnevenCentralCoordination
opening_player_problem:
  - SecureSuccessionAndLegitimacy
  - ObtainOrCompelRegionalService
  - DefendEasternAndSouthernFrontiers
  - PreventForeignPowersFromWeaponizingInternalDisputes
  - BuildACrossRegionalArmyWithoutErasingRegionalProviders
```

The faction should begin neither helpless nor fully unified. Brittany possesses soldiers, horses, castles, ports, and military traditions, but the duke cannot automatically convert all of them into one central recruitment pool.

## 5. Core Military Doctrine

## 5.1 Muster

Secure actual regional contingents, ducal troops, retained companies, town forces, and mounted followings. Mobilization depends upon political relationships, distance, season, service obligation, local-defense floors, and available officers.

## 5.2 Screen

Use bows, javelins, light foot, riders, scouts, and terrain to shape the approach, protect cavalry deployment, and reveal the enemy's intended attack.

## 5.3 Fix

Regional spears, shielded foot, castle sergeants, and selected retainers hold or occupy the enemy. Fixing remains physical and positional, never a hidden movement-lock effect.

## 5.4 Charge

Breton light, medium, lordship, and later elite cavalry attack exposed sections, flanks, missiles, or enemies already committed to the infantry battle.

## 5.5 Recover

Mounted forces separate and reform; infantry retains the ground gained; missile troops relocate; regional contingents preserve enough integrity to serve again.

```yaml
battle_doctrine:
  core_verbs:
    - Muster
    - Screen
    - Fix
    - Charge
    - Recover
  ideal_result: CoordinatedLocalDecisionWithoutWholeArmyAttritionalCollapse
  not_a_doctrine:
    - FrontalCavalrySpam
    - PrimitiveWarbandHorde
    - StaticSpearOnlyDefense
    - UniversalFeudalHeavyArmy
```

## 37. V1.1 Acceptance Fixtures

All fixtures are proposed and unexecuted.

- A powerful lordship has excellent cavalry but remains in `LocalDutyOnly`; the duke cannot recruit it for an unrelated expedition.
- Breton Riders screen and pursue while Breton Lancers own the decisive available lance mission.
- Lordship Horsemen outperform ordinary Lancers in short selected contact but remain politically conditional and less available.
- Ducal Muster Spears coordinate a mixed host better than ordinary levy frontage without becoming elite armored infantry.
- Castle Sergeants retain a fortified-node maintenance and reliability purchase distinct from Ducal Muster Spears.
- Cornouaille Warband and Breton Household Retainers remain separate only if regional ground-taking and selected household counterpress are both readable.
- General Breton Bowmen and a western specialty survive only if provider, battlefield loop, and availability differ materially.
- Maritime investment without ships, crews, ports, and supply creates no naval unit.
- Royal restoration changes rank and political opportunity but creates no free elite formations.
- AI chooses infantry cohesion or provider recovery rather than another cavalry family when the army cannot support mounted combined arms.
