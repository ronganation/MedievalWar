This profile develops **Ducal Muster Spears** as Brittany's seventh unit profile and the faction's first explicit purchase of cross-regional ducal coordination. The company is not a standing national regiment and not simply Breton Levy Spearmen with improved statistics. It exists only when the ducal authority can assemble separate contingents, place accepted officers and standards among them, establish signals and support expectations, and keep the mixed formation functioning after movement, contact, and local disorder.

## 2. Why This Unit Comes After Breton Riders

The first five profiles established the fundamental combat system. Breton Riders then added observation, relay, screening, pursuit, and withdrawal protection. Ducal Muster Spears now test whether Brittany can convert political coordination into a real battlefield capability.

```yaml
sequence_logic:
  BretonLevySpearmen: EstablishLocalRegionalFrontage
  BretonRiders: ConnectAndInformTheDividedHost
  DucalMusterSpears: AssembleSeveralRealPacketsIntoOneSupportedMainBattleFormation
```

The unit demonstrates what the duke purchases through successful consolidation: not better human bodies created from nothing, but better assembly, accepted command, signals, standards, relief arrangements, and mixed-host coordination.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ducal Muster Spears
- **Class:** Coordinated cross-regional spear infantry
- **Signature:** Assemble, Brace, Support, Reform
- **Battle promise:** Hold the center of a mixed Breton host more reliably than local levy companies, receive mounted pressure with better preparation, transfer support into threatened sections, and recover formation order after bounded disruption.
- **Best use:** Main battle line, protection of Bowmen, cavalry recovery lanes, mixed-contingent junctions, and positions requiring reliable facing changes or controlled relief.
- **Strong against:** Light cavalry frontally, ordinary infantry, disordered mounted troops, and local attacks that cannot overwhelm the coordinated line quickly.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry when braced, selected ordinary infantry with support, and peer coordinated spears.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry on a clean approach, elite shock infantry, strong crossbows, encirclement, command collapse, and prolonged fighting after ducal coordination breaks down.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better battlefield coordination and service duration than local Levy Spearmen, but requiring several real regional packets, accepted ducal authority, officers, messengers, standards, supply, political concessions, and higher upkeep.
- **Player instruction:** Use Ducal Muster Spears where several Breton contingents must act as one line. Preserve the command packet, because without it the formation falls toward ordinary regional capability.
- **Flavor:** **The duke does not create the spearmen. The duke makes separate spearmen arrive, understand the same battle, and remain long enough to support one another.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Convene, Align, Support, Reform

### 8.1 Convene

Bring several eligible regional sections into one formation under accepted ducal officers, standards, messengers, route plans, and support agreements. Assembly consumes campaign time and political capacity.

### 8.2 Align

Receive a visible frontal mounted threat with better timing and file coordination than a purely local levy formation. Brace still requires ground, facing, cohesion, and preparation.

### 8.3 Support

Transfer frontage, close a junction, protect a withdrawing Bowmen or Javelinmen section, receive returning cavalry, or relieve a threatened neighboring contingent. Support is physical movement and file replacement, not an aura.

### 8.4 Reform

Reconnect regional sections, reestablish standards and command paths, restore facing, and rebuild useful spear order after bounded disruption. Reform requires space and time.

## 10. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 10.1 Ducal Muster Line

The default cross-regional formation organizes several sections around accepted standards and command paths.

### 10.2 Coordinated Brace

A prepared frontal spear reception with improved timing and section support. It does not defeat heavy cavalry automatically.

### 10.3 Support the Junction

Move selected files or a complete section into a physical gap between contingents. This temporarily weakens the donating frontage.

### 10.4 Receive Through the Line

Open a controlled passage for Javelinmen, Bowmen, Riders, or Lancers, then close the frontage after they pass. Passage requires timing, spacing, and command.

### 10.5 Section Reform

A regional section halts behind protected frontage, reconnects leaders, and returns to the main line when viable.

### Preferred physical sequence

2. Regional spear sections assemble under the ducal command packet.
3. Muster Spears establish the main support line and passage lanes.
4. Bowmen harrow from protected positions.
5. Javelinmen pass through or around the line and return physically.

7. Muster sections support a threatened junction and reform after contact.
8. Household Retainers remain available for the most critical local failure.

## 22. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- form Muster Spears only when several real packets and a command packet exist;
- preserve Levy Spearmen where cheap local frontage is sufficient;
- account for political and local-defense costs;
- avoid using every regional spear provider in one ducal formation;
- release or preserve surviving sections when political access changes.

### Battle AI

- anchor the mixed host's central support line;
- preserve controlled lanes for missiles and cavalry;
- brace against real frontal mounted threats;
- support junctions physically;
- reform before renewed pressure;
- avoid pursuing like a Warband or selected Retainer formation.
