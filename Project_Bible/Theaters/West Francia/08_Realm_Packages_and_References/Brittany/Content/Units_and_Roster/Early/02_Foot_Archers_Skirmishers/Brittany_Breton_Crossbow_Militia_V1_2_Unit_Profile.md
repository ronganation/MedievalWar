## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Breton Crossbow Militia
- **Class:** Early local crossbow infantry
- **Signature:** Muster, Load, Loose, Withdraw
- **Battle promise:** Field a modest crossbow threat from settlements, castles, ports, camps, and local militia providers without paying for a mature specialist company.
- **Best use:** Gates, passages, defended slopes, infantry shoulders, compact ordinary targets, exposed mounted approaches, and local settlement defense.
- **Strong against:** Exposed ordinary infantry, compact light or medium troops, slow shielded targets at useful angles, and cavalry approaching through a visible lane.
- **Conditional against:** Better-protected infantry, enemy missile troops, and ordinary cavalry before contact.
- **Weak against:** Fast cavalry, aggressive infantry, sustained counterfire, poor firing lanes, depleted bolts, dispersed targets, and any prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Wider local access and lower institutional burden than Castle Crossbowmen, but uneven weapons, weaker crews, shorter endurance, and poorer field recovery.
- **Player instruction:** Establish a simple lane, hold the loaded threat, spend bolts on worthwhile targets, and leave before the militia packet is trapped.
- **Flavor:** **The weapon is dangerous. The militia is not yet an institution.**

## 6. Core Battle Loop: Muster, Load, Loose, Withdraw

### Muster

Bring together local users, weapons, bolts, strings, leaders, and one defensible firing position. The militia may arrive unevenly equipped and must not be assumed ready merely because a settlement or castle exists.

### Load

Span and load crossbows using the actual methods, tools, space, and cover available to the packet. Loading creates vulnerability and fixes the unit temporarily to its position.

### Loose

Release measured bolts against targets worth the ammunition and exposure. The unit does not sustain the tempo or disciplined targeting of mature Castle Crossbowmen.

### Withdraw

Cease fire and leave before cavalry, counterfire, or assault infantry reaches the position. Withdrawal may preserve users while weapons, bolts, tools, or order are lost.

```yaml

  - AssembleLocalPacket
  - EstablishSimpleFiringPosition
```

## 25. AI Doctrine

### Battle AI

- Establish one simple protected lane.
- Prefer ordinary protected or compact targets worth the bolt.
- Use measured fire unless a concentration is clearly valuable.
- Hold loaded against visible cavalry or a better target.
- Pause for friendly passage.
- Withdraw before cavalry or assault infantry reaches the position.
- Preserve users and weapons when the position becomes untenable.

### Campaign AI

- Recruit only where users, weapons, bolts, strings, tools, service, and local-defense capacity exist.
- Prefer Bowmen for broad mobile pressure.
- Prefer Javelinmen for close disruption.
- Prefer Castle Crossbowmen only when mature fortified packets exist.
- Accept zero field availability.
