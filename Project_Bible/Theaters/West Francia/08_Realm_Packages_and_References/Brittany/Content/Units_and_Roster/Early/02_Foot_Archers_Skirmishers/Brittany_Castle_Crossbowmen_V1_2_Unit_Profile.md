This profile develops Castle Crossbowmen as Brittany's narrow fortified-node missile specialist. The family represents crossbow users sustained by a qualified castle, fortified town, household, craft, ammunition, officer, and maintenance packet. The unit is not automatically present in every Breton castle, is not assumed to be universally recruitable on 1 January 1062, and does not replace Breton Bowmen as Brittany's broad missile foundation.

**Castle Crossbowmen establish one prepared firing position, apply slow and consequential pressure against protected or compact targets, loosen the position before enemy contact or encirclement destroys the exchange, and recover weapons, bolts, crews, and order behind a defensible node.**

## 2. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Castle Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Fortified-node crossbow specialists
- **Signature:** Establish, Loose, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Hold a prepared lane and make protected, compact, or movement-constrained targets respect each deliberate volley.
- **Best use:** Castle approaches, gates, walls, narrow streets, defended slopes, passages, infantry shoulders, and selected protected targets.
- **Strong against:** Armored ordinary infantry, compact troops, exposed cavalry approaches, shielded formations from useful angles, and troops moving through narrow lanes.
- **Conditional against:** Medium cavalry before contact, selected infantry, enemy missile troops behind partial protection, and open-field targets when the Crossbowmen possess a secure line.
- **Weak against:** Fast cavalry, aggressive skirmishers, broad open flanks, sustained archery, disrupted reload order, ammunition exhaustion, and close combat.
- **Campaign bargain:** Greater protected-target threat than Breton Bowmen, but narrower providers, slower firing tempo, heavier equipment burden, and weaker relocation.
- **Player instruction:** Prepare the lane before the enemy arrives. Spend bolts on targets that justify the reload cycle, then recover before the position becomes a trap.
- **Flavor:** **A bow company shapes the battle. A crossbow position makes one defended lane expensive.**

## 7. Core Battle Loop

### Establish

Choose a useful firing node, clear or reserve the lane, connect the crews to protection and ammunition, and establish enough order that deliberate volleys do not waste the specialist packet.

### Loose

Fire controlled bolts at targets whose protection, compactness, movement, or local importance justifies the reload cycle.

### Recover

Cease fire, preserve spanning and repair equipment, withdraw or resettle behind protection, reconnect sections and stocks, and avoid being fixed by cavalry or assault infantry.

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - SelectPreparedNodeAndFiringLane
  - EstablishSectionsProtectionAmmunitionAndReloadOrder
  - ObserveHighValueProtectedOrConstrainedTarget
  - LooseControlledBoltCycle
  - AssessTargetReturnFireAndApproachThreat
  - ContinueShiftOrCease
  - RecoverWeaponsStocksCrewsAndOrder
success_condition: UsefulProtectedTargetPressureDeliveredWithoutLosingSpecialistPacket
failure_condition: PoorTargetChoiceBlockedLaneCounterfireCavalryClosureAmmunitionWasteOrPositionBecomingATrap
```
