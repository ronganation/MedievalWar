## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Breton Light Cavalry
- **Class:** Early mounted-javelin light cavalry
- **Signature:** Screen, Cast, Displace, Return
- **Battle promise:** Use speed and a finite javelin load to pressure exposed or movement-sensitive targets, force physical responses, and leave before heavier troops control the contact.
- **Best use:** Enemy skirmishers, exposed archers, slow shielded troops from an angle, moving cavalry, formation edges, withdrawal cover, and pursuit after genuine collapse.
- **Strong against:** Unsupported skirmishers, fragmented light infantry, exposed missiles, slow formations without immediate mounted support, and routers.
- **Conditional against:** Peer light cavalry, medium infantry from useful angles, tired medium cavalry, and protected infantry already occupied elsewhere.
- **Weak against:** Fast aggressive cavalry, Breton Lancers or peer charge cavalry in formed contact, Lordship Horsemen, prepared spears, concentrated archery or crossbows, depleted ammunition, closed routes, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** A useful light mounted combat family with lower horse and armor burden than lance cavalry, but real dependence on practiced throwing, ammunition, practical horses, section leaders, tack, and recovery space.
- **Player instruction:** Cast to change the enemy's choices. Displace before the enemy's answer becomes contact.
- **Flavor:** **Breton Riders carry the warning. Breton Light Cavalry carry the javelins.**

## 7. Core Battle Loop: Screen, Cast, Displace, Return

### Screen

Occupy one useful flank, approach, or counter-skirmish sector. Screening here is narrower and more combat-oriented than Breton Riders' operational screen.

### Cast

Approach at a controlled pace and deliver a limited mounted-javelin release against a target worth the ammunition and exposure.

### Displace

Leave before prepared spears, missiles, fast cavalry, or deeper reserves close the route.

### Return

Reconnect sections, leaders, horses, remaining ammunition, and a useful mission. Return may lead to another cast, withdrawal cover, counter-screening, or pursuit.

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - SelectOneUsefulScreenSector
  - ApproachThroughARealCastingLane
  - CastFiniteJavelinLoad
  - ReadEnemyPhysicalResponse
  - DisplaceBeforeFixation
  - ReconnectAtReceivingGround
  - ReturnOnlyIfAnotherMissionIsWorthTheCost
```

## 15. After Ammunition Is Spent

Spent Breton Light Cavalry may:

- Screen one narrow sector
- Escort withdrawing troops
- Maintain distant contact
- Pursue genuine routers
- Threaten exposed skirmishers
- Carry reports locally where represented

Spent Breton Light Cavalry may not:

- Become Breton Riders at full operational value
- Become Breton Lancers
- Fight selected cavalry
- Replenish javelins automatically
- Receive projectile and melee value simultaneously in autoresolve

## 23. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- Recruit only when qualified mounted throwers, horses, javelins, leaders, and service exist.
- Preserve Breton Riders when operational coverage is the real need.
- Preserve Lancers when charge impact is the real need.
- Avoid recruiting Light Cavalry merely because Brittany is associated with javelin horse.
- Track ammunition and horse recovery independently.

### Battle AI

- Select one useful screen or harassment sector.
- Prefer exposed skirmishers, missiles, slow formation edges, and occupied targets.
- Cast from controlled pace and real angle.
- Displace before cavalry, spears, or concentrated missiles close.
- Stop repeated missions when horses or ammunition no longer support return.
- Pursue only genuine collapse.
