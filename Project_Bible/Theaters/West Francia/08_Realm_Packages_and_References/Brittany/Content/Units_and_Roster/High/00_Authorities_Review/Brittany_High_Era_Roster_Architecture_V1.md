This authority defines the intended structure of Brittany's High Era land roster before broad High profile production begins. It preserves the complete Early portfolio, establishes the first High families, prevents symmetry-only expansion, and keeps every unit dependent on real people, providers, equipment, horses, leaders, service, routes, and recovery.

## 1. Direct Architecture Verdict

Brittany's High Era roster should express a duchy becoming more capable without becoming miniature France or western Normandy.

```yaml
faction_fantasy: ArmedDuchyBetweenKingdoms
HighEraMilitaryQuestion: CanBrittanyCoordinateRegionalPowerWithoutDestroyingItsProviders
battlefield_engine: MusterScreenFixChargeRecover
HighEraTransformation:
  - BetterDucalCoordination
  - MoreMaintainedFieldForces
  - StrongerRegionalSelectedCompanies
  - DeeperCastleTownAndLoireMilitaryInstitutions
  - LayeredCavalryWithoutUniversalHeavyKnightDominance
  - MaritimePotentialWithoutPrematureNavalActivation
primary_weakness_preserved:
  - FragmentedProviders
  - PoliticalConditionality
  - ExpensiveHorsesAndSelectedEquipment
  - UnevenRecruitmentDepth
  - DangerousAttritionalLosses
  - DependenceOnCombinedArmsAndRecovery
```

High Brittany should feel stronger because its institutions, relationships, and military packets are deeper, not because every Early card receives higher statistics.

## 2. High Era Development Channels

### 2.1 Ducal Consolidation

```yaml
channel_id: Brittany.High.DucalConsolidation
military_purpose: BuildMaintainedCoordinatedDucalAndCastleMilitaryInstitutions
improves:
  - MusterReliability
  - OfficerAndMessengerDensity
  - CrossRegionalCommand
  - CastleAndTownMilitarySupport
  - EquipmentInspectionMaintenanceAndReplacement
  - FieldServiceDuration
  - CommandReserveDepth
likely_families:
  - DucalSergeants
  - BretonMenAtArms
  - DucalHouseholdHorse
possible_later_families:
  - DucalCrossbowmen
  - BretonArmoredSpearmen
```

Ducal Consolidation cannot manufacture soldiers, horses, crossbows, armor, officers, loyalty, or service obligations. It can create organizational relationships and investments through which complete physical packets become possible.

### 2.2 Lordship Confederation

```yaml
channel_id: Brittany.High.LordshipConfederation
military_purpose: PreserveRegionalPowerAndCoordinateIndependentProvidersThroughNegotiatedService
improves:
  - RegionalSelectedPools
  - BannerAndLordshipCommand
  - LocalRecovery
  - IndependentProviderDepth
  - PoliticalFlexibility
  - RegionalEquipmentExpression
likely_families:
  - BretonBannerCompanions
  - LeonWarSpears
  - BretonLordshipHorsemen
possible_later_families:
  - PenthievreRetainers
  - RegionalBannerHorse
```

Lordship Confederation does not absorb every regional pool into one ducal pool. Independent lordships retain their people, officers, equipment, duties, and replacement consequences.

### 2.3 Loire and Eastern Institutional Development

```yaml
channel_id: Brittany.High.LoireAndEasternInstitutionalDevelopment
military_purpose: DevelopNantesLoireMarcherTownCastleAndRiverMilitaryCapacity
improves:
  - CivicAndCastleCrossbowSupport
  - RiverAndTownDefense
  - MaintainedMissileStocks
  - LoireFrontierService
  - FieldRelocationAndSupply
likely_families:
  - NantesCrossbowmen
possible_later_families:
  - LoireMarchSergeants
  - NantaisLancers
  - RiverAndPortGuards
```

This channel overlaps politically with Ducal Consolidation but remains provider-specific. Nantes and Loire institutions should not become generic realm-wide access.

### 2.4 Channel and Atlantic Expansion

```yaml
channel_id: Brittany.High.ChannelAtlanticExpansion
military_purpose: ConvertPortsCoastalProvidersAndOverseasRelationshipsIntoExpeditionaryCapacity
current_status: DeferredPendingMaritimeSystemReopening

```

The maritime channel remains reserved until the naval, amphibious, port, vessel, crew, cargo, landing, and reembarkation systems are reviewed comparatively.

## 10. Player-Facing Channel Bargains

### Ducal Consolidation

> Build maintained field forces, better command, stronger castle support, and a central reserve.

```yaml
strengths:
  - ReliableCoordination
  - MaintainedInfantry
  - StrongerFieldDuration
  - CentralCommandReserve
costs:
  - GreaterDucalPoliticalBurden
  - HighEquipmentOfficerAndMaintenanceDemand
  - CompetitionForSelectedPeopleAndHorses
```

### Lordship Confederation

> Preserve powerful regional providers and coordinate them without dissolving their identities.

```yaml
strengths:
  - RegionalSelectedCompanies
  - MultipleIndependentPackets
  - PoliticalFlexibility
  - LocalRecovery
costs:
  - ConditionalService
  - CommandPrecedenceDisputes
  - FragmentedReplacementAndAvailability
```

### Loire Development

> Turn Nantes, river wealth, civic providers, and fortified support into a deeper missile and frontier institution.

```yaml
strengths:
  - DevelopedCrossbowSupport
  - TownCastleAndRiverOperations
  - BetterPreparedPositionMobility
costs:
  - CivicCraftStockAndSupplyBurden
  - GeographicAndPoliticalDependency
```

### Channel and Atlantic Expansion

> Build maritime operations only when vessels, crews, ports, stores, landing systems, and overseas relationships support them.

```yaml
strengths:
  - LandingAndExpeditionaryPotential
  - PortAndOverseasOperations
costs:
  - HighestSystemDependency
  - VesselsCrewsPortsStoresAndWeather
```

## 12. Availability States

```yaml
HighAvailabilityStates:
  KnownButUnavailable:
    meaning: FamilyOrInstitutionIsRecognizedButPacketIsIncomplete
  EligibleNotReady:
    meaning: PoliticalAndDevelopmentAccessExistButPhysicalInputsAreMissing
  LimitedRecruitment:
    meaning: OneOrFewCompleteProviderPacketsExist
  NormalProviderRecruitment:
    meaning: ValidPacketsRecoverWithinTheirOwnLimits
  VeteranPersistence:
    meaning: ExistingCompaniesRemainWhileNewRecruitmentCloses
  ReplacementRestricted:
    meaning: LossesCannotBeFullyRestored
  Suspended:
    meaning: PoliticsDutyOrMissingInputsTemporarilyCloseAccess
  Closed:
    meaning: NoCurrentCompletePacketOrAuthority
```

Choosing a High development channel does not unlock every family immediately.
