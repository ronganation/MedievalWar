## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ducal Sergeants
- **Class:** Maintained High-Era field infantry
- **Signature:** Attend, Advance, Relieve, Reform
- **Battle promise:** Provide a dependable infantry company that reaches the host in useful order, advances without excessive fragmentation, manages passages and relief, and restores pressured ordinary formations over a longer campaign.
- **Best use:** Main-line support, controlled advance, reinforcement of Levy or Muster Spears, protection of missile positions and passages, relief of tired sections, and field armies requiring longer maintained service.
- **Strong against:** Ordinary infantry, tired levy or militia, fragmented attacks, light troops caught near the main line, and local pressure that depends on exhausting one unsupported section.
- **Conditional against:** Peer maintained infantry, selected ordinary foot when supported, and cavalry in connected spear-and-shield order.
- **Weak against:** Men-at-Arms, elite shock infantry, prepared heavy cavalry, concentrated crossbows, encirclement, extended offensive melee, officer collapse, and simultaneous relief demands across excessive frontage.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better availability, duration, equipment consistency, officers, and recovery than temporary muster infantry, purchased through expensive ducal consolidation, castle relationships, pay or duty, and sustained maintenance.
- **Player instruction:** Use Ducal Sergeants to keep the army functioning. Attend on time, preserve the advance, relieve the section that matters, and reform before continuity becomes attrition.
- **Flavor:** **The levy fills the line. The sergeant makes certain the next company reaches the line, knows the order, and remains useful after the first exchange.**

## 4. Historical and Terminological Discipline

Medieval *servientes* or sergeants were defined broadly by service rather than by a single standardized rank. The term could cover non-knightly military followers, mounted or foot soldiers, garrison personnel, household servants, and people owing particular services; it should not be mistaken for the modern noncommissioned-officer rank.

### Recruit when

- the army already has enough cheap frontage;
- relief, passage, and longer maintained service matter;
- a complete provider packet exists;
- officers, messengers, stores, equipment, and recovery can be sustained;
- the player needs continuity rather than elite melee power.

### Do not recruit when

- Levy Spearmen provide sufficient frontage;
- Muster Spears are needed for cross-regional integration;
- Household Retainers are needed for selected crisis response;
- Warband pressure or Men-at-Arms contact is the actual mission;
- the provider cannot spare maintained people or officers.

### Ducal Sergeants

*Maintained High-Era field infantry*

Ducal Sergeants are non-knightly service infantry maintained through developing ducal, castle, and field institutions.
