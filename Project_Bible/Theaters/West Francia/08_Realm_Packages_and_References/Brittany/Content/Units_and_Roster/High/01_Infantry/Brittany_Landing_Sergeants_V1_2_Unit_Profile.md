### Breton Shipmen retain

- Cheaper and more practical sea-road service
- Wider use across boats, ferries, ports, river crossings, and ordinary coastal movement
- Faster transition from vessel to a usable first line
- Lower equipment, officer, and maintenance burden
- Better affordability and reduced-company persistence
- Ordinary field value without requiring a maintained High institution

### Landing Sergeants add

- Maintained sergeant leadership
- Better protection and equipment consistency
- Stronger landing-wave command
- A dedicated shorehead relief reserve
- Better contested passage management
- More reliable stores, tools, and repair support
- Better reception of follow-on infantry and missile troops
- Stronger reembarkation rear-guard procedure
- Longer expeditionary service
- Higher cost, narrower providers, and greater loss severity

## 3. Player-Facing Total War Card

- **Unit:** Landing Sergeants
- **Class:** High Era maintained maritime infantry
- **Signature:** Embark, Establish, Relieve, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Establish one contested shorehead, relieve the first landed files, preserve the route for follow-on troops, and recover before the expeditionary packet is cut off.
- **Best use:** Beaches, quays, port gates, riverbanks, ferries, estuaries, landing camps, stores, and constrained passages immediately connected to maritime movement.
- **Strong against:** Light or ordinary troops contesting a landing, exposed missiles near the shore, fragmented defenders, and local counterattacks against an established shorehead.
- **Conditional against:** Peer maintained infantry in constrained ground, ordinary spears without complete preparation, and cavalry denied room by terrain and Breton frontage.
- **Weak against:** Prepared heavy infantry at the only exit, concentrated crossbows, firearms where present, heavy cavalry after open ground is reached, deep pikes or spears, artillery, severe weather, split waves, and prolonged inland battle.
- **Campaign bargain:** More reliable contested landings and expeditionary shoreheads purchased through scarce sergeants, ships, crews, weapons, stores, transport, and port capacity.
- **Player instruction:** Do not use Landing Sergeants as generic Men-at-Arms. Establish the landing, rotate the first files, receive the follow-on formation, then preserve the sea road.

## 45. Revised Player-Facing Card

### Landing Sergeants

*Maintained shorehead infantry*

Landing Sergeants are High Era Breton expeditionary infantry organized to turn a hazardous arrival into one functioning shorehead. Better leaders, protection, wave planning, and dedicated relief files allow the company to establish a front, preserve a passage, rotate the first landed troops, receive the next formation, and protect the route back to the boats. Landing Sergeants remain ordinary maintained infantry once the battle moves inland and can be defeated by prepared heavy infantry, concentrated crossbows, split landing waves, or a closed recovery route.
