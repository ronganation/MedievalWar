## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Léon War Spears
- **Class:** Western regional spear-pressure infantry
- **Signature:** Advance, Receive, Press, Secure
- **Battle promise:** Move a coherent spear frontage through constrained or uneven ground, receive one enemy response without losing the mission, counteradvance through ordinary resistance, and secure the resulting local ground.
- **Best use:** Broken but passable ground, lanes between obstacles, slopes, settlement approaches, coastal and river routes, flank frontage, and battles where the army needs a spear formation that can move forward rather than remain a receiving wall.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, ordinary foot, fragmented attacks, unsupported light cavalry, and troops disordered by terrain or Breton missile pressure.
- **Conditional against:** Peer spear infantry, maintained ordinary foot, medium cavalry when connected and facing correctly, and selected infantry already fixed or tired.
- **Weak against:** Heavy cavalry on a clean approach, concentrated crossbows, elite shock infantry, deep prepared spears, encirclement, severe fragmentation, and unsupported pressure beyond the secure line.
- **Campaign bargain:** A limited Léon regional spear family with stronger forward and constrained-ground handling than levy or cross-regional Spears, purchased through local people, leadership, routes, equipment, service, and recovery.
- **Player instruction:** Choose a bounded approach, keep the spear frontage connected, receive the enemy response, press only while support and route integrity remain, then secure the ground before pursuing farther.
- **Flavor:** **The War Spears do not ignore difficult ground. The War Spears keep enough frontage through difficult ground to make the next step possible.**
