## 3. Portfolio Principle: Development Is Not Replacement

```yaml
ProgressionPrinciples:
  - EarlyFamiliesPersistWhenTheirMissionRemainsEconomicallyOrOperationallyBest
  - HighFamiliesRequireNewCompletePhysicalAndPoliticalPackets
  - IndividualsMayTransferOnlyAfterReleaseQualificationEquipmentServiceTimeAndReceivingCapacity
  - WholeCompaniesDoNotTransformByTechnologyButton
  - BuildingsResearchAndDecisionsMayUnlockCapabilityButNeverCreateInputs
  - RegionalAndInstitutionalDevelopmentChannelsRemainIndependent
  - SimilarWeaponsDoNotRequireUnitMerger
```

The player should continue recruiting Early infantry in the High Era whenever broader access, lower cost, simpler service, more frontage, lighter institutional burden, local defense, or practical maritime movement remains the actual requirement.
