## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Breton War Archers
- **Class:** Maintained High Era bow company
- **Signature:** Establish, Range, Concentrate, Displace
- **Battle promise:** Control a wider local missile front through connected sections, briefly mass arrows on one consequential exposure, then move sections sequentially so the whole company does not lose its fire mission at once.
- **Best use:** Enemy missiles, exposed formation edges, cavalry approaches, troops fixed by Breton infantry, passages the enemy must cross, and local concentration before a Breton infantry or cavalry commitment.
- **Strong against:** Light infantry, exposed bows and skirmishers, ordinary troops under lateral fire, disordered cavalry approaches, and formations already fixed or changing facing.
- **Conditional against:** Shielded ordinary infantry from useful angles, medium cavalry with warning, and protected troops during concentrated side or rear exposure.
- **Weak against:** Prepared crossbows, heavy frontal armor, fast cavalry at close range, deep obstruction, rain and strong wind, simultaneous threats across separated sections, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better leaders, campaign service, arrow distribution, position control, and recoverable concentration than Breton Bowmen, purchased through narrower people, equipment, transport, maintenance, and replacement pools.
- **Player instruction:** Establish connected arcs, verify the target, concentrate only for a real decision window, and displace before the enemy closes the angle.
- **Flavor:** **Breton Bowmen harrow the field. Breton War Archers decide which part of the field must answer their arrows now.**

## 4. Historical and Claim Discipline

Archery remained a normal part of medieval warfare, while the High Medieval period saw continued importance and organization of infantry alongside mounted forces. Continental armies also placed substantial emphasis on crossbows, so a Breton High Era Archer must remain distinct from crossbow practice rather than becoming an invented Breton longbow analogue.

Brittany's divided political structure and limited ducal reach outside direct lands support a provider model assembled from several regional, lordship, castle, town, household, or paid packets rather than one uniform national corps.
