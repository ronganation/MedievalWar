## 4. Player-Facing Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ducal Crossbow Sergeants
- **Class:** Maintained High Era field crossbow infantry
- **Signature:** Assemble, Stagger, Support, Relieve
- **Battle promise:** Maintain a dependable crossbow threat beside the main Breton host by staggering loaded, firing, reloading, and relief sections instead of committing the entire company to one exposed cycle.
- **Best use:** Protected targets facing the Breton center, infantry shoulders, reserve lanes, defended approaches, counter-missile exchanges, and battles demanding repeated field support rather than one fixed node.
- **Strong against:** Protected ordinary infantry, compact targets, exposed mounted approaches, enemy missile specialists, and troops fixed by Breton Men-at-Arms or War Spears.
- **Conditional against:** Selected infantry, peer crossbow companies, medium cavalry before contact, and open-field targets when reliable receiving infantry and bolt transport exist.
- **Weak against:** Fast flanking cavalry, dispersed skirmishers, broad-front archery, artillery, collapsed transport, disrupted officers, unsupported displacement, and melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better field continuity and section relief than Castle or Nantes Crossbowmen, purchased through direct ducal officers, pay, transport, equipment, political burden, and competition with other maintained ducal institutions.
- **Player instruction:** Preserve at least one loaded section, one reloading section, and one relief option. Support the main host; do not chase the battle across unsupported ground.

## 72. Final Player-Facing Card

### Ducal Crossbow Sergeants

_Maintained main-host crossbowmen_

Ducal Crossbow Sergeants keep a dependable bolt threat working beside Brittany's principal field army. Officers stagger loaded, firing, reloading, covering, and relief sections around a finite ammunition train, allowing the company to support several bounded battlefield windows without exposing every crossbow at once. The company is expensive, slow to replace, dependent on receiving infantry, and highly vulnerable if cavalry, assault troops, or counterfire reach its sections and train.
