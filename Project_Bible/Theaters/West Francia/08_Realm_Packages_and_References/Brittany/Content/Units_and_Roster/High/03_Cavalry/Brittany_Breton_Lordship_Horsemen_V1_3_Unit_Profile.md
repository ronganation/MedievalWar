This profile develops **Breton Lordship Horsemen** as Brittany's selected regional mounted following. The family represents horsemen gathered through a particular lordship, banner, household network, and political relationship. It is neither a broader Breton Lancer company with extra armor nor a weaker version of Ducal Household Horse.

Its distinct purchase is **selected short-contact authority**. Lordship Horsemen gather around an accepted regional banner, commit with stronger protection and cohesion than Breton Lancers, remain useful through a bounded melee or countercharge, and recover as a politically identifiable following whose losses weaken a specific lordship.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Breton Lordship Horsemen
- **Class:** Selected regional medium cavalry
- **Signature:** Gather, Commit, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Deliver a politically meaningful regional mounted following that charges well, survives short contact better than Breton Lancers, and stabilizes a local mounted contest without becoming heavy knight cavalry.
- **Best use:** Local countercharges, exposed flanks, important medium cavalry fights, protecting a lordship contingent, and committing after Riders and Lancers reveal the enemy response.
- **Strong against:** Light cavalry, exposed missiles, disordered ordinary infantry, tired medium cavalry, and local targets already fixed by Breton infantry.
- **Conditional against:** Peer medium cavalry, ordinary spears from flank or rear, and stronger cavalry when supported and committed on favorable terms.
- **Weak against:** Heavy knights, prepared spears, dense supported infantry, concentrated crossbows, deep mud, encirclement, and prolonged cavalry melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better horses, equipment, leaders, morale, and short-contact ability than Lancers, but scarce, politically controlled, costly, slow to replace, and tied to specific lordship interests.
- **Player instruction:** Commit the banner where the result matters. Win the local mounted contest, secure the lordship contingent, and recover before a regional elite is wasted.
- **Flavor:** **A Lancer company belongs to the Breton army. A Lordship following arrives with a name, a banner, obligations, and consequences.**

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Gather, Commit, Recover

### 8.1 Gather

Assemble the selected mounted following around its lordship banner, recognized leaders, compatible horses, equipment, and accepted campaign purpose. Gathering preserves political identity and clarifies command.

### 8.2 Commit

Charge or countercharge one important target. The commitment may accept short contact longer than Breton Lancers, but must remain bounded by the local purpose.

### 8.3 Recover

Separate, reconnect around the banner, assess leaders and horse condition, and return to protect the regional contingent or prepare another limited mission.

## 10. Formation and Order Portfolio

### 10.1 Banner Formation

The default formation balances mounted cohesion, leader visibility, and capacity to threaten or countercharge.

### 10.2 Regional Commitment

A denser attack against one important local target. Stronger than a Rider charge and more durable in contact than Breton Lancers, but slower to reform.

### 10.3 Banner Countercharge

A controlled mounted response near the lordship contingent, standards, or threatened flank.

### 10.4 Protect the Following

Remain near the regional infantry or commander, intercepting light cavalry and discouraging exposed attacks without becoming an immobile aura unit.

- an important regional contingent needs selected mounted support;

### Preferred physical sequence

1. Riders reveal threats and preserve routes.
2. Lancers threaten several angles.
3. Spears and missiles fix or expose a target.
4. Lancers commit first where speed and charge-for-weight are enough.
5. Lordship Horsemen remain near the important regional banner or reserve position.
6. Enemy medium cavalry or a serious local counterattack appears.
7. Lordship Horsemen commit, sustain short contact, and stabilize the local decision.
8. Riders cover recovery while the following reconnects around its banner.

## 21. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- recruit only from complete lordship packets;
- preserve Riders and Lancers for their cheaper and broader missions;
- avoid stripping every strong lordship of home protection;
- calculate political cost and replacement time;
- recognize that two lordship companies are separate providers, not one generic pool.

### Battle AI

- keep near the relevant regional contingent or selected reserve position;
- countercharge or commit against important local targets;
- accept short contact but exit before attrition;
- refuse healthy heavy knights and prepared spears;
- recover around banner or deputy marker;
- pursue only when recall and political mission permit.
