This profile develops **Ducal Household Horse** as Brittany's High Era selected ducal command-reserve family. The unit is the scarce mounted reserve directly connected to the ducal household, command continuity, and one crisis commitment. It is not a generic heavy-knight tier, not Breton Lordship Horsemen with ducal colors, and not a permanent aura-bearing bodyguard.

The family completes the roster architecture by giving the duke one concentrated mounted packet whose battlefield purchase is **preserved decision authority**: remain available while the wider host reveals the real crisis, protect the command system physically, commit where losing the sector would compromise the army, then restore command space, recovery lanes, or local order before the reserve is exhausted.

## 3. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Ducal Household Horse
- **Class:** Scarce ducal selected reserve cavalry
- **Signature:** Hold, Commit, Restore
- **Battle promise:** Preserve one exceptional mounted intervention near the command reserve, defeat or contain a critical local threat, and restore the army's decision space without wasting the ducal household in routine combat.
- **Best use:** Command-sector protection, crisis countercharge, breaking an enemy mounted breakthrough, clearing a recovery lane, rescuing a compromised central junction, and committing after other Breton mounted families have exposed the real decision.
- **Strong against:** Light and medium cavalry, exposed selected troops, disordered ordinary infantry, local attackers already fixed or tired, and mounted threats entering a constrained command sector.
- **Conditional against:** Peer selected cavalry, heavier cavalry when supported and committed on favorable terms, and prepared ordinary infantry from flank or rear.
- **Weak against:** Fully prepared heavy knights, deep braced spears, concentrated crossbows, encirclement, repeated crisis commitments, prolonged melee, and any battle plan that spends the reserve before the true crisis appears.
- **Campaign bargain:** Brittany's strongest Early selected mounted contact and best command protection, purchased through the realm's narrowest rider, horse, leader, equipment, maintenance, and political packet.
- **Player instruction:** Keep the reserve unspent until the army faces a real loss of command space, critical route, or decisive sector. Commit once, restore the battle, then reassess whether the surviving household can still serve as a reserve.
- **Flavor:** **The ducal household does not ride first. It rides when the duchy can least afford the wrong result.**

## 4. Historical and Claim Discipline

In 1062, Duke Conan II's rule was defined by contested ducal authority, rebellion, and the need to assert control against powerful nobles. Conan had only recently overcome his uncle Odo's refusal to surrender authority and still faced noble resistance, showing why a direct ducal household and command-protection packet must be scarce and politically consequential rather than a national cavalry pool.

Brittany's principal regions and counts could conflict with the Breton ruler, and ducal authority did not erase local power. This supports a narrow household reserve attached to the duke while rejecting automatic access to all selected Breton horsemen.

## 8. Core Battle Loop: Hold, Commit, Restore

### Hold

Remain physically positioned to protect the ducal standard, commander, messenger routes, reserve lane, or critical central sector. Holding preserves choice and is not inactivity.

### Commit

Enter one genuine crisis where failure would compromise command continuity, a critical route, or the army's central battle plan. Commitment spends reserve integrity.

### Restore

Clear the immediate threat, reconnect command space, reopen routes, recover surviving files, and determine whether the household remains a functional reserve or has become a spent selected company.

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - AssignConcreteDucalResponsibilityAndReservePosition
  - HoldWhileRidersLancersInfantryAndMissilesRevealTheRealCrisis
  - SelectOneCommandCriticalOrBattleDecisiveThreat
  - CommitThroughARealApproachLane
  - SustainPremierHighEraSelectedShortContact
  - BreakContainOrDisplaceTheImmediateThreat
  - RestoreCommandSpaceRoutesAndHouseholdOrder
  - ReturnToReducedReserveOrEndReserveMission
success_condition: CriticalThreatResolvedAndDucalDecisionSpaceRestoredWithoutDestroyingTheHouseholdPacket
```

## 29. AI Doctrine

### Campaign AI

- accept zero availability if the complete ducal packet does not exist;
- field the family only after broad frontage, general missiles, Riders, and ordinary cavalry needs are met;
- preserve ducal-seat and succession security;
- track selected riders, horses, leaders, equipment, command duty, and maintenance separately;
- do not mass recruit through castle or palace construction;
- protect depleted survivors even when replacement closes.

### Battle AI

- assign one concrete ducal responsibility;
- hold the reserve through routine threats;
- track enemy selected cavalry and command-sector danger;
- commit only when the crisis gate passes;
- prefer exposed or constrained critical targets;
- restore command space and routes rather than pursue;
- reassess reserve state after every commitment;
