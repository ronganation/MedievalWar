## 1. Direct Verdict

The current four cavalry profiles are not four same-era opening cards. They form a development ladder and institutional spread across Early and High Brittany.

```yaml
EarlyCore:
  - BretonRiders
  - BretonLancers
HighCore:
  - BretonLordshipHorsemen
  - DucalHouseholdHorse
NewHighCandidate:
  - BretonLightCavalry
```

The fifth cavalry family should be a javelin-armed High Era light cavalry development. It should not be retrofitted into Breton Riders, because Breton Riders already own screening, relay, escort, route security, controlled pursuit, and withdrawal protection without requiring javelins.

## 2. Era Placement

### 2.1 Breton Riders

```yaml
primary_era: Early
persistence: EarlyToHigh
role: OperationalLightCavalry
signature: WatchThreatenReturn
HighEraDisposition: PersistsAsCheaperOperationalScreenAndProviderFoundation
```

Breton Riders remain the common practical mounted foundation. In High play they continue to provide broad screening, communications, escort, pursuit, and withdrawal protection. They should not be upgraded automatically into missile cavalry.

### 2.2 Breton Lancers

```yaml
primary_era: Early
persistence: EarlyToHighConditional
role: AvailableLightToMediumLanceCavalry
signature: ThreatenChargeSeparateReturn
HighEraDisposition: PersistsWhereQualifiedRegionalLancePacketsRemainUseful
```

Breton Lancers remain Brittany's accessible lance-cavalry family below selected regional and ducal tiers. High-era progression may improve maintenance, equipment distribution, leadership, and provider depth without changing the family into heavy knights.

### 2.3 Breton Lordship Horsemen

```yaml
primary_era: High
EarlyEraStatus: KnownProviderDirectionOrRareConditionalPrecursor
role: SelectedRegionalMediumCavalry
signature: GatherCommitRecover
HighEraDisposition: FullSelectedRegionalMountedFamily
```

The mature Lordship Horsemen profile is institutionally stronger than an ordinary Early card. Its selected regional following, banner cohesion, political-provider identity, better protection, and stronger bounded contact fit the High roster more naturally.

Early Brittany may possess individual lordship mounted followings or a narrow veteran precursor, but the complete repeatable family belongs in High development.

### 2.4 Ducal Household Horse

```yaml
primary_era: High
EarlyEraStatus: RestrictedHouseholdEscortOrKnownCapabilityOnly
role: ScarceDucalSelectedReserveCavalry
signature: HoldCommitRestore
HighEraDisposition: FullCentralCommandReserveFamily
```

The complete field-company expression belongs in High Brittany, when ducal consolidation, household depth, command continuity, selected horses, maintenance, and replacement can support the institution.

An Early opening may contain a personal escort, named household survivors, or known capability without authorizing the full repeatable unit card.

## 3. New High Family: Breton Light Cavalry

```yaml
working_name: BretonLightCavalry

  - BretonJavelinHorse
  - BretonSkirmishHorse
primary_era: High

```

### 3.1 Battlefield Purchase

Breton Light Cavalry should own:

- Mounted javelin screening
- Repeated finite cast-and-displace pressure
- Harassment of exposed heavy or medium troops
- Counter-skirmishing against light cavalry
- Pursuit and flank pressure after ammunition use
- Better direct skirmish combat than Breton Riders
- Less operational communication and route-control value than Breton Riders
- Less impact and commitment than Breton Lancers

### 3.2 Battle Loop

```yaml
battle_loop:
  - Screen
  - ApproachAtUsefulAngle
  - CastFiniteJavelinLoad
  - DisplaceBeforeContact
  - RepositionOrCounterScreen
  - ReturnForLaterCastOrPursuit
```

The unit should not receive an automatic Cantabrian-circle clone, infinite ammunition, perfect skirmish behavior, or a generic Celtic cavalry bonus.

## 5. Player-Facing Development Identity

### Early Brittany

- Learns the field through Riders.
- Creates mounted decisions through Lancers.
- Depends heavily on infantry-created openings.
- Has useful cavalry without complete selected institutions.

### High Brittany

- Retains Riders and Lancers as practical foundations.
- Adds mounted-javelin pressure through Breton Light Cavalry.
- Adds politically selected regional contact through Lordship Horsemen.
- Adds scarce central crisis response through Ducal Household Horse.

The result is not a generic progression from light cavalry to heavy cavalry. It is a widening Breton mounted institution with distinct operational, missile, lance, regional, and ducal purchases.
