This profile develops a missing High Era mounted-skirmisher family from Brittany's earlier practical mounted-javelin tradition. It is not a replacement for Breton Watch Riders, not Breton Lancers with javelins, and not a selected lordship or ducal cavalry card.

Its distinct purchase is a more developed mounted-skirmish company able to **screen a combat sector, apply organized finite missile pressure, displace under section control, reform behind a protected route, and return for another bounded mission when ammunition, horses, leaders, and spacing still permit.**

### High family adds

- Denser section leadership

- More reliable coordination with High infantry and missile formations

## 5. One-Screen Total War Card

- **Unit:** Breton Skirmish Horsemen
- **Class:** Developed High Era mounted-javelin skirmish cavalry
- **Signature:** Screen, Pressure, Displace, Reform
- **Battle promise:** Apply organized finite mounted-javelin pressure from changing angles, contest enemy skirmish cavalry, and reform through prepared lanes before heavier forces close.
- **Best use:** Enemy skirmishers, exposed archers, slow protected troops already occupied, formation edges, tired light cavalry, withdrawal cover, and opening lanes for Breton infantry or crossbows.
- **Strong against:** Unsupported skirmishers, exposed missile troops, fragmented light infantry, tired light cavalry, slow occupied formation edges, and routers.
- **Conditional against:** Peer mounted skirmishers, medium infantry from an open angle, ordinary cavalry already fixed, and protected infantry under combined-arms pressure.
- **Weak against:** Fast lance cavalry, selected medium cavalry, prepared spears, concentrated archery or crossbows, blocked displacement lanes, exhausted horses, and prolonged melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Better organized and more sustainable in bounded harassment than the Early mounted-javelin foundation, but materially more expensive in leaders, ammunition, horses, tack, transport, practice, and recovery.
- **Player instruction:** Pressure one sector at a time. Preserve the displacement lane, stagger sections when counterpressure rises, and reform before another mission.
- **Flavor:** **Early riders cast and escape. Breton Skirmish Horsemen make mounted pressure part of the developed Breton battle plan.**

### Preferred physical sequence

1. Watch Riders identify the threat, routes, and enemy counterforce.
2. Skirmish Horsemen occupy one combat sector.
3. Lead sections probe while pressure sections cast.
4. Enemy troops turn, compress, seek protection, commit missiles, or release cavalry.
5. Skirmish Horsemen displace through a protected route.
6. Lancers, War Spears, Sergeants, Lordship Horsemen, or Crossbowmen exploit the physical response when appropriate.
7. Skirmish Horsemen reform for another bounded mission or become spent mounted support.

No automatic combined-arms bonus exists.
