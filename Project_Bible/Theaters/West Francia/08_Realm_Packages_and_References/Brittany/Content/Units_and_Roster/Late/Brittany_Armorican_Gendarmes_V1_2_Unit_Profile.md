**Armorican Gendarmes are Brittany's rare Late Era elite heavy cavalry: fully developed noble men-at-arms and selected retainers who assemble one complete armored charge packet, deliver a powerful lance commitment, survive a short protected mounted contact, and recover only if formation order, horses, leaders, and an exit route remain.**

## 3. Player-Facing Total War Card

- **Unit:** Armorican Gendarmes
- **Class:** Elite Late heavy lance cavalry
- **Signature:** Assemble, Shock, Break, Recover
- **Battle promise:** Deliver Brittany's strongest mounted charge against a high-value target and preserve enough order to leave before pikes, firearms, deep spears, or prolonged melee destroy the company.
- **Best use:** Enemy heavy cavalry already committed, exposed flanks, wavering selected infantry, vulnerable command sectors, and decisive countercharge opportunities.
- **Strong against:** Exposed cavalry, disordered infantry, missile troops caught without protection, and formations fixed by Breton infantry.
- **Conditional against:** Peer heavy cavalry, armored men-at-arms, and compact infantry from a favorable angle.
- **Weak against:** Prepared pikes, deep spear formations, concentrated crossbows or firearms, artillery, bad ground, closed exits, and attritional melee.
- **Campaign bargain:** Exceptional battlefield impact purchased through the realm's rarest rider, horse, armor, command, maintenance, and political packet.
- **Player instruction:** Assemble deliberately. Spend the charge only on a decisive target. Break contact while the exit remains open. Recover before considering another commitment.
