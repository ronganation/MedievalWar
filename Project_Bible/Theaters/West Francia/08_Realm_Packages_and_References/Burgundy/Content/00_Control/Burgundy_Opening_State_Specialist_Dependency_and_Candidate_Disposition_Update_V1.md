## 8. Character and Household Dispositions

Robert remains the candidate duke and historical military principal, but health, exact residence, household presence, staff, property, force, and command are not created here. Henry remains a principal adult dynast under review without installed heirship, opening command, or co-rule. Protected dynasts retain age-appropriate candidate presence only where lifecycle and Opening State owners approve it.

Ermengarde and other women retain independent household, property, patronage, diplomatic, correspondence, protection, provision, and continuity interfaces. Marriage does not merge households, property, authority, knowledge, or military command. Routine battlefield command is not created.

## 9. Geography, Economy, and Property Dispositions

Dijon, Semur, Vergy, Chalon, Autun, Langres, Auxerre, and Nevers remain separately dispositioned. The Opening State may not infer settlement kind, property, residence, control, route, crossing, capacity, garrison, market, population, or revenue from a name or map label.

Semur does not become generic rebels. Vergy does not generate a castle garrison. Chalon and the Saône do not generate navigation, crossing capacity, boats, or trade volume. Auxerre and Nevers remain external-owner interfaces where applicable.
