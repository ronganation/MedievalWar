## 18. Positive System Fixtures

- A title-holder lacks direct property until property receipts exist.
- Dijon remains important without becoming the sole capital, treasury, recruitment center, or archive.
- Semur can negotiate service without becoming generic rebels.
- Vergy can retain local defense and block full field recruitment.
- Chalon can support a crossing economy without producing free navigation or a fleet.
- A population band constrains a finite pool without direct manpower spending.
- A recruitment reservation removes capacity from competing orders.
- Household retainers cannot occupy mounted and dismounted profiles simultaneously.

- A victory creates occupation and recovery burdens rather than automatic annexation.
- An advanced save retains developed property, route, pool, force, and damage state.
