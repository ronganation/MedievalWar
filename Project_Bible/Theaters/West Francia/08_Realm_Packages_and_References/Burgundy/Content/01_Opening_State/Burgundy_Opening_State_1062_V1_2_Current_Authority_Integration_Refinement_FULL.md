## 1. Redo Verdict

The first Burgundy V1 opening authority was structurally safe but below developed-faction standard. It described gates more thoroughly than it described Burgundy. This V1.1 is a complete integrated redo. It preserves every substantive V1 boundary, owner separation, atomicity rule, character disposition, portfolio subject, save safeguard, and UE 5.8 limitation while replacing the generic portfolio treatment with a concrete Burgundy opening snapshot, record catalogue, first-turn strategic dilemmas, regional and institutional map, player-facing campaign identity, and binding-ready operating records.

**Opening campaign truth:** Burgundy begins as **The Inherited Founder-State**, a cadet duchy with a living founder, a usable adult generation, protected grandchildren, powerful local intermediaries, uncertain exact assets, and a political order that can become durable only if Robert's personal authority is converted into institutions, service, access, and recoverable military capacity.

**Opening promise:** The player does not begin with a blank review packet. The player begins with a real political machine whose exact bindings remain honest: Robert can act, Henry can be prepared, collateral dynasts can be used, institutions can be courted, regional lords can be negotiated with, and military power can be built from finite pools, but none of those outcomes is free or automatic.

## 5. Opening Identity and Playable Shape

Burgundy is a distinct West Francia Realm candidate, neither French royal demesne nor the County of Burgundy. Its opening shape joins five tensions:

1. **Founder strength versus founder dependency.** Robert's long tenure gives Burgundy an experienced center, but every function concentrated in one person is a succession and campaign-absence vulnerability.
2. **Adult capacity versus uninstalled succession.** Henry and adult collateral dynasts provide usable political capacity, but no later accession, office, command, or bloc is preloaded.
3. **Recognized dignity versus negotiated execution.** The ducal title matters, but service, castles, justice, routes, and revenue require specific authority and local performance.
4. **Institutional depth versus institution-specific conflict.** Burgundy has powerful church surfaces, archives, patronage, mediation, and jurisdiction, but no universal Church score.
5. **Military opportunity versus finite recovery.** Burgundy may fight and expand, but recruitment pools, population, equipment, service, supply, garrisons, casualties, and recovery define what can be sustained.
