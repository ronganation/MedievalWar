## 1. Developed-Realm Verdict

Burgundy's justice is **The Duke on the Road**. Law is not a modern code stored at Dijon and projected evenly across colored territory. It is produced through recognized ducal dignity, local custom, remembered settlements, charters, witnesses, households, lordships, castles, institutions, markets, itinerant hearings, negotiated remedies, and the practical ability to make a judgment hold.

**Justice-system fantasy:** Turn judgment into durable order without pretending every village, lordship, castle, household, and church answers the same way.

**Core political question:** Can Robert and Burgundy's emerging institutions make fair, credible, and enforceable decisions whose authority survives distance, dispute, succession, and Robert's absence?

The system favors pressure and consequence over permission gates. Burgundy may command, negotiate, punish, compensate, restore, or use force when valid owners permit, but ineffective, disproportionate, procedurally weak, or unsupported choices create political and practical consequences.

## 13. Burgundy Custom as a Living Interface

Burgundy's custom must be plural, place-specific, audience-specific, and historically bounded. The authority may record a custom of service, succession, dower, market peace, castle custody, witness, possession, sanctuary, or local judgment only when an owner-approved basis identifies where, between whom, during what period, and with what exceptions.

A local custom can constrain ducal action without denying ducal dignity. Robert may confirm, negotiate, reinterpret, challenge, or override custom only through valid authority and consequences. Repeated ducal nonperformance can undermine credibility; repeated local resistance can escalate obligations or enforcement without creating a generic rebel category.

## 14. The Duke on the Road

The itinerant court turns geography into justice. A hearing at Dijon, Semur, Vergy, Chalon, Autun, Langres, or another validated venue changes which witnesses, archives, institutions, guards, supplies, and political audiences are available. Travel creates delay, exposure, hospitality burdens, and communication gaps.

Dijon is an important justice constellation, not the sole court. Robert's personal presence can increase attention and dignity while also concentrating risk. Delegation can extend reach but requires mandate, competence, access, records, resources, review, revocation, and handback.

## 15. Robert, Henry, and Justice Continuity

Robert is historically the opening duke, not an unlimited judge. Robert must operate through jurisdiction, information, venue, household access, records, witnesses, officers, military capacity, and enforceable remedies.

Henry may observe, represent, mediate, witness, or execute a bounded mandate where owners approve. Successful justice can improve recognition and readiness; failure can expose inexperience or weak acceptance. No case, appointment, or later outcome installs Henry as heir, co-ruler, or successor.

## 16. Semur, Helie, and the Dalmace Narrative

Semur-facing justice must separate at least five possible issue families: kinship, marriage, lordship, property, service, and historical narrative. The reported Dalmace death narrative remains evidence-bounded and contested. It does not initialize Robert's guilt, Semur hostility, Church sanction, blood feud, or a mandatory revenge arc.

Helie of Semur remains an independent uncertainty-bound person. Marriage history, repudiation, institutional reception, property, household, burial, or later religious association cannot be adjudicated by this authority without specialist evidence and owner state.

## 17. Ecclesiastical and Secular Jurisdiction

Autun, Langres, Saint-Benigne, Cluniac interfaces, Saint-Germain, and other validated institutions remain separate. Ecclesiastical venues may address marriage, oath, sanctuary, discipline, property, patronage, burial, clerical matters, or institutional privilege according to their owners.

The duke may protect an institution, request evidence, mediate, enforce a secular obligation, negotiate restitution, or contest a jurisdictional claim. No single Church score decides every case. Papal standing never becomes a general war-permission gate.

## 18. Property, Possession, and Economic Justice

Property justice separates title, ownership, possession, use, custody, revenue right, collection, damage, and available treasury. A plaintiff may win recognition but lack possession; recover possession but lack revenue; receive compensation without restored access; or secure future service rather than an immediate transfer.

Market, road, toll, bridge, cartage, hospitality, and supply disputes should affect campaign movement and sustainment. They cannot generate revenue or access from map labels alone.

## 19. Household Justice and Protected People

Household cases can concern membership, access, residence, officer misconduct, custody, provision, correspondence, seals, gifts, widowhood, guardianship, protected children, guests, hostages, prisoners, or succession handover.

Ermengarde may seek property, residence, household, Hildegarde-protection, correspondence, or widowhood remedies without becoming automatic regent. Protected people require treatment, contact, health, religion, education, security, review, and release conditions. No person becomes property.

## 23. Appeal, Reopening, and Competing Venues

Appeal may seek factual review, legal review, custom interpretation, procedural correction, remedy adjustment, owner referral, institutional mediation, or external recognition. A case may reopen when new evidence, a returned witness, restored archive, changed possession, new claimant capacity, or failed settlement materially changes the record.

External appeals require external-owner receipts. Burgundy cannot author France, the County of Burgundy, Cluny, the papacy, Anjou, Aquitaine, Auxerre, or Nevers private judgments.

## 24. Memory, Momentum, Emergence, and Realm AI

Memory can shape how an audience understands a dispute. Momentum can increase pressure for settlement or enforcement. Emergence can evaluate conditional pathways. Realm Personality can prefer executable dignity, institution-specific settlement, regional negotiation, information confidence, and recovery.

None can decide guilt, validity, jurisdiction, claim, remedy, enforcement, or restoration. AI explanations must name the known facts, uncertainty, venue, standing, evidence, procedure, remedy, capacity, proportionality, and rejected alternatives.

## 25. Player Experience and Anti-Micromanagement

Routine uncontested service, ordinary market enforcement, minor household discipline, and low-value petitions may follow owner-approved policy. Player attention belongs to ducal justice, disputed lordship, castle possession, succession, institutional conflict, protected people, significant property, military enforcement, and restoration after coercion.

The UI should show what is alleged, what is known, who has standing, what venue can hear each issue, which evidence is accessible, what remedy is requested, what enforcement would cost, what restoration remains, and what uncertainty prevents a stronger conclusion.
