## 15. Final Position

Burgundy Economy is closed at static-authority scope. The V1 authority remains the controlling substantive owner, and the V1.1 addendum supplies the accepted bounded shared-family compatibility layer. The closure preserves The Working Hinge, the integrated Population-Economy model, all 32 portfolios, Burgundy-specific regional and institutional differentiation, finite recruitment constraints, Duchy-County separation, honest unknowns, package-wide owner routing, and strict nonactivation boundaries.

No broad rewrite, split authority family, immediate V1.2, folder move, Registry commitment, Opening-State promotion, importer execution, runtime payload, or Unreal implementation is justified.
