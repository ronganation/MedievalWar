## 2. Dynasty Thesis

Burgundy begins as a Capetian cadet dynasty with a living senior founder-ruler candidate, multiple adult or review-only collateral dynasts, a developing line of protected descendants, uncertain marriage and household interfaces, and unresolved succession recognition. The dynasty is strategically deep enough to support continuity, delegated service, branch competition, marriage diplomacy, protected minority development, institutional patronage, and alternate futures. It is not identical to the Duchy, government, title, household, army, property portfolio, or France.
