## 3. Concise Verdict

Constance is a probable living adult daughter of Robert I whose opening importance lies in her present Burgundian identity, not in marriages and titles associated with later chronology. Sshe can act as a household principal, patronage participant, dynastic communicator, mediator, marriage-negotiation principal, and external diplomatic connector only through valid owner records and her own directed agency. Her design challenge is to preserve meaningful adult action while keeping exact marital status, residence, property, relationships, and future Iberian significance unresolved at the 1062 opening.

## 4. Core Character Thesis

**Constance is an adult Burgundian dynast whose diplomatic potential must expand her agency rather than replace it with a predetermined marriage itinerary.**

Her central campaign question is whether sshe can build a durable household, patronage, institutional, mediating, or cross-Realm role on terms shaped by current evidence, consent, resources, and political conditions. The profile must remain valid if Constance is unmarried, if an early Chalon connection is validated, if another marriage path emerges, if no Iberian marriage occurs, if she becomes a Burgundian patron, if she mediates family conflict, if she maintains an independent household, or if later history diverges completely.

## 10. Opening Strategic Function

Constance widens Burgundy's adult political portfolio through forms of agency that are often lost when a dynastic woman is written only as a future spouse.

1. **Independent household agency.** Constance may manage or participate in a household through owner-valid residence, staff, income, property access, and authority records.
2. **Marriage negotiation with consent.** She may receive, evaluate, resist, redirect, or accept proposals. A negotiation does not create a marriage, and marriage does not create alliance or shared knowledge.
3. **Patronage and institutional connection.** Constance may support, petition, mediate with, or patronize institutions only through valid property, treasury, legal, and ecclesiastical owners.
4. **Dynastic communication.** She may connect Burgundian, Chalon-facing, Capetian, or later external interests through actual messages, audiences, movement, and directed relationships.
5. **Household and succession continuity.** Her location, marriage, widowhood, children, and household can affect continuity only if separately established. None is assumed here.
6. **Player choice without scripted export.** The player may cultivate her local role, pursue or decline marriage diplomacy, support an independent household, or allow emergent cross-Realm paths without requiring historical marriages.

## 30. Final Design Summary

Constance should remain in the Burgundy portfolio as an independently modeled adult dynastic woman under strict review controls. Her profile does more than preserve a genealogical name, but it does not fill evidentiary gaps with a predetermined marriage narrative. If activated after Registry, chronology, lifecycle, marriage-state, and Opening State adjudication, Constance can become a household principal, patron, institutional intermediary, dynastic mediator, marriage-negotiation principal, Chalon-facing connector if validated, or an emergent cross-Realm actor through actual causes and owner-valid state.

Her distinctive value is agency without preloaded destiny. The player receives an adult member of the founder's generation whose Burgundian role can develop locally or across Realms, and whose consent, household, property access, relationships, movement, and political standing remain separate owner-controlled questions. The simulation gains diplomatic and household depth without requiring a Chalon marriage, an Iberian marriage, queenly office, motherhood, alliance, or departure from Burgundy. This preserves historical honesty, directed relationships, meaningful player choice, and the project's three-track temporal model.
