**Helie is a protected young Burgundian dynast whose later religious association must remain one possible adult outcome rather than an opening destiny.**

## 16. Decision Portfolio

During infancy or childhood, Helie's agency is expressed through age-appropriate attachment, trust, distress, preferences, requests, refusals, learning, ceremony, play, travel response, and relationships. Guardians and household owners decide broader protections but must not erase her personhood.

## 17. Failure, Recovery, and Divergence

Failure may result from poor care, illness, disrupted education, household displacement, coercion, isolation, inadequate resources, damaged relationships, ignored consent, or institutional misuse.

Recovery can include safer residence, improved care, new attendants or educators, medical attention where available, household reconstruction, mediation, delayed decisions, revised education, restored family contact, or withdrawal from an unsuitable placement or proposal.

Divergent futures include household principal, patron, institutional intermediary, religious life after valid development, marriage-diplomacy principal, independent unmarried adult, private household member, politically marginal dynast, displaced dependent, early death, or survival without a major public role.
