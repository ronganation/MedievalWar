## 3. Concise Verdict

Helie matters because repudiated spouses and women who entered or may have entered religious life did not simply vanish from medieval politics. If living in 1062, she could remain relevant through Semur kinship, property or support questions, institutional standing, correspondence, patronage, memory, family relationships, or disputes arising from the marriage rupture. If deceased, those same matters may persist through historical facts, claims, commemoration, property arrangements, and directed memories. Which branch applies must be resolved by evidence and the proper owners.

## 4. Core Character Thesis

**Helie of Semur is an unresolved-lifecycle former spouse whose political significance may survive repudiation without granting her invented opening agency.**

Her design question is not whether she remains Robert I's wife. She does not. The question is what lawful, household, dynastic, institutional, property, kinship, and memory consequences remain after the rupture, and whether Helie herself is alive to exercise bounded agency within them.

## 8. Repudiation and Marriage-Rupture Boundary

Repudiation is a historical and legal relationship event, not a personality trait. It may create owner-valid consequences involving marital status, legitimacy disputes, support, property, residence, kinship reactions, institutional judgment, reputation, memory, or later diplomacy. None is created here.

```text
Former marriage != Current spouse state
Repudiation != Universal guilt
Repudiation != Automatic hostility
Marriage rupture != Property settlement
Kinship connection != Alliance
Narrative allegation != Historical fact
Fact != Knowledge
Knowledge != Directed memory
```

The game should preserve uncertainty and disagreement where sources or actors differ. Robert I, Helie, Semur kin, children, institutions, and later storytellers need not share one interpretation.

## 9. Religious and Institutional Boundary

Possible religious life must remain specific. Residence at or association with a religious house does not equal profession, office, authority, or permanent withdrawal from politics. Patronage does not equal membership. Profession does not equal office. Office does not create universal Church authority.

```text
Religious association != Residence
Residence != Profession
Profession != Office
Office != Institution
Institutional protection != Political obedience
Religious life != Absence of agency
```

If Helie is validated as living within a religious institution, her agency may include correspondence, patronage, petitions, kinship communication, property claims, mediation, commemoration, or institutional participation within owner-valid limits. This does not create military command or secular jurisdiction.

## 10. Household, Property, and Support Interfaces

Potential questions include former marital property, support, residence, attendants, gifts, dower-like expectations, patronage access, burial provision, and obligations toward dependents. These are research and owner interfaces only.

A validated property interest must identify the asset, legal basis, holder, income, control, restrictions, duration, inheritance, enforcement, and competing claims. Household membership must identify residence, staff, access, authority, reporting, and separation from Robert I's current household.

## 12. Directed Memory, Reputation, and Legacy

If the marriage rupture was known to particular people, it may produce separate memories and public narratives. Possible themes include loss of status, legal grievance, household displacement, institutional intervention, family embarrassment, political necessity, reconciliation, continuing obligation, or indifference. These are possibilities, not opening facts.

If Helie died before opening, she may remain relevant through facts, memories, property arrangements, commemoration, and contested narrative without posthumous agency. If alive, her own knowledge and decisions remain distinct from memories held by others.

## 13. Political and Grand-Campaign Agency

A validated living Helie may influence the campaign through historically grounded channels:

- correspondence and kinship communication;
- household management or protected residence;
- patronage and petitions;
- property and support negotiations;
- mediation between kin or institutions;
- testimony or claims concerning past marriage and family history;
- commemoration and religious relationships;
- directed support for dependents or institutions.

She is not authored as a routine general. Exceptional military emergence principles do not convert ordinary household or institutional influence into command eligibility.

## 15. Failure, Recovery, and Divergence

If living, setbacks may include insecure residence, insufficient support, property denial, institutional conflict, damaged kinship, isolation, misinformation, coercion, or being politically erased. Recovery may involve mediation, legal review, restored support, new residence, patronage, correspondence, kin intervention, reconciliation, or independent institutional standing.

Possible living outcomes include Semur-connected household principal, religious resident, professed religious after valid evidence and process, patron, mediator, property claimant, reconciled former spouse, politically marginal survivor, or private individual. A deceased branch supports historical memory and legacy only. None is scheduled.

## 16. Player-Facing Experience

The player should see lifecycle uncertainty explicitly rather than a false living or dead claim. Presentation should separate verified identity, former marriage, Semur connection, disputed location, possible religious state, known property or institutional claims, directed memories, and blocked actions.

The player should not see a generic discarded-wife trait, automatic revenge agenda, universal victim status, invented convent, current spouse label, or hidden certainty disguised as history.

## 23. Final Design Summary

Helie of Semur is now preserved as a strategically meaningful but unresolved-lifecycle character. Her former marriage, repudiation, Semur connection, possible religious life, property and support questions, institutional relationships, and directed memories can matter without treating her as Robert I's current spouse or inventing present agency.

The profile supports both possible states honestly. A validated living Helie may exercise bounded household, patronage, kinship, property, correspondence, or institutional influence. A validated deceased Helie may remain important through facts, memory, commemoration, and legacy. Until owners adjudicate survival, location, and religious status, she remains ReviewOnly and non-instantiating.
