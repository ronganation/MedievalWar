## 3. Concise Verdict

Hugh belongs in the grand campaign because a dead character can alter the political world without acting in it. If his identity and pre-opening death are confirmed, his absence changes the living family structure and may shape how particular people understand succession, danger, duty, grief, military service, commemoration, and Robert I's household. Those effects must be recorded as separate facts, memories, reputations, claims, and institutional practices rather than as a supernatural or universal legacy modifier.

## 4. Core Character Thesis

**Hugh is a deceased elder son whose historical importance lies in the political and personal consequences of his absence, not in posthumous agency.**

A death near a scenario opening can be strategically stronger than an arbitrary opening bonus. It can explain a vacant succession position, a changed household, a surviving relative's caution, a retaliatory grievance, a disputed narrative, a memorial obligation, or distrust surrounding an alleged killing. Each consequence requires its own evidence, affected subject, knowledge path, confidence, persistence, and owner.

A legacy effect must identify:

Commemoration can be sincere, obligatory, selective, contested, neglected, revived, or politically used. It neither restores agency nor proves universal affection.

## 22. Final Design Summary

Hugh is now represented as a meaningful Historical Tier character. His confirmed absence can reshape succession context, household expectations, military-risk arguments, commemoration, and directed memories without granting him posthumous agency. The profile preserves uncertainty over his exact dates, role, death circumstances, battle participation, and legacy.

The broader deceased-character principle is also captured. Death near the campaign opening, including an evidence-backed or disputed assassination, can remain politically active through living people and institutions. Facts, claims, knowledge, memories, reputations, legal effects, retaliation, security responses, and memorial practices stay separate. That gives the grand campaign legacy and consequence without ghosts, universal modifiers, or invented certainty.
