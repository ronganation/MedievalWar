## 3. Concise Verdict

Simon is a probable living adult son of Robert I whose strategic value lies in widening Burgundy beyond the founder, the transition principal, and a single collateral brother. He can supply household representation, dynastic diplomacy, regional service, and conditional military utility without being prewritten as a duplicate of Henry or Robert the Younger, or as an automatic supporter, rival, commander, titleholder, or claimant. His design problem is how to turn dynastic proximity into useful and independently earned standing when the evidence for his exact opening role is sparse and every consequential assignment remains owner-bound.

## 4. Core Character Thesis

**A third adult dynastic path gives Burgundy resilience and political texture, but Simon must earn every role through evidence, mandate, resources, relationships, and events rather than receiving a generic spare-prince biography.**

Simon's central campaign question is whether a poorly documented adult cadet can become a distinct household, diplomatic, regional, or military actor without merely copying the functions of Henry or Robert the Younger. The design must preserve the possibility that Simon remains marginal, becomes a useful mediator, receives a bounded assignment, develops an independent network, or becomes succession-relevant only through actual crisis. The profile must remain valid if Simon serves effectively, receives a bounded mandate, remains household-centered, develops regional ties, competes with siblings through real causes, reconciles after conflict, leaves Burgundy, survives into an alternate succession, or dies without achieving major office.

## 10. Opening Strategic Function

Simon's opening value is to widen Burgundy's adult decision and continuity portfolio without erasing the primacy of Robert I or the transition importance of Henry. If activated by the Opening State owner, his function may include:

1. **Collateral continuity.** Simon provides an adult dynastic alternative when illness, captivity, absence, or death reduces available principals, but no succession right is created here.
2. **Scoped ducal service.** Simon may undertake inspection, escort, representation, negotiation, ceremonial attendance, household supervision, or military support under a valid mandate.
3. **Regional embedding.** A documented or runtime-developed association with a region, castle, abbey, bishopric, or magnate can give Burgundy another channel of communication and implementation.
4. **Household differentiation.** Simon can build a distinct retinue, patronage pattern, reputation, and network only through relevant owners and actual events.
5. **Succession pressure without scripting.** His existence can affect how the founder, transition principal, spouse, siblings, magnates, and institutions evaluate continuity, but it does not create loyalty or rivalry.
6. **Player choice.** The player can invest in Simon, leave him unassigned, bind him through service, permit a separate household, or manage emerging conflicts based on causes rather than a preset event chain.

Simon may reasonably weigh:

## 30. Final Design Summary

Simon should remain in the Burgundy portfolio as an independently modeled adult cadet dynast under strict review controls. His profile deliberately does more than preserve a genealogical name, but it does not fill evidentiary gaps with a fictional biography. If activated after Registry, chronology, lifecycle, and Opening State adjudication, Simon can become a collateral continuity actor, household principal, regional representative, diplomatic instrument, military contributor, reconciled rival, or alternate successor candidate through actual causes and owner-valid state.

His distinctive value is uncertainty with agency. The player receives another adult member of the founder's generation whose place can be built, contested, narrowed, redirected, or lost. The simulation receives a flexible person-specific actor who can matter without requiring a predetermined rebellion, title, marriage, command, or death. Burgundy gains depth while preserving historical honesty, owner separation, open outcomes, finite military access, and the project's three-track temporal model.
