## 2. Authority Thesis

Historical Emergence converts current world conditions and owner-accepted Momentum handoffs into conditional pathways. It owns pathway eligibility, lifecycle, participants, consent, competition, branching, redirection, reversal, failure, closure, and owner-routed outcome handoff. It does not schedule history, spawn capability, fabricate characters, create titles, start wars, or guarantee that any named figure or recorded event appears.

Burgundy's emergence fantasy is not a march toward later ducal greatness. Burgundy begins with founder-age authority, multiple dynastic generations, regional pluralism, institution-specific tensions, Crown kinship, finite military and economic capacity, and unresolved narratives. The campaign may produce durable government, negotiated plurarchy, principled restraint, regional conflict, institutional reform, military adaptation, dynastic fracture, alternate succession, new specialists, or outcomes unlike recorded history.
