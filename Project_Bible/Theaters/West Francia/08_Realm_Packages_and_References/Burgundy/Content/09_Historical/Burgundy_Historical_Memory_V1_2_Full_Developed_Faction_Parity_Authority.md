## 1. Redo Verdict

The earlier Burgundy V1 and V1.1 review artifacts were not sufficient. They contained the correct core subjects but compressed the operating records, candidate portfolio, audience distinctions, cross-system interfaces, tests, and implementation handoff below the level demonstrated by the strongest developed factions. This authority is a full integrated redo. It preserves every useful Burgundy subject and boundary while rebuilding the authority at record level.

Burgundy begins with several usable and conflicting pasts: Capetian cadet settlement, Robert I's long founder rule, family transition, the Semur rupture, disputed responsibility narratives, institution-specific patronage and conflict, the loss of an elder son, Castle and regional service precedents, Crown kinship and service, frontier settlements, and the distinction between the Duchy and County of Burgundy. None is universal, identical, complete, or automatically active.

## 3. Modern Medieval II Design Doctrine

Memory exists to make the campaign map more intelligible and causally rich. It should alter how actors interpret summons, service, succession preparation, castles, institution claims, regional settlement, military risk, diplomacy, routes, patronage, burial, and public ceremony. It must not become a detached lore collection, a universal opinion bar, or constant court-event administration.
