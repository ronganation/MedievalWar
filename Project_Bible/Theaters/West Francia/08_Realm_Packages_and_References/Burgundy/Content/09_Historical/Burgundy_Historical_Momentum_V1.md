## 2. Authority Thesis

Burgundy Historical Momentum translates owner-approved facts, evidence, chronology, selected or unresolved uncertainty, active holder-specific memory, directed relationships, dynasty and household state, governance, institutions, territory, routes, economy, property, military readiness, finite recruitment, supply, recovery, diplomacy, and current campaign outcomes into directional pressure.

Momentum makes questions harder to ignore. It can make preparation, consultation, restraint, investigation, negotiation, concentration, recovery, or qualified escalation strategically intelligible. It cannot choose policy, create capability, schedule an event, manufacture a claimant, force war, install an heir, create a relationship, or guarantee later Burgundian history.

**Campaign fantasy:** Convert founder-age ducal dignity into durable regional, dynastic, institutional, and military capacity without losing Burgundy to overreach, succession failure, or outside absorption.
