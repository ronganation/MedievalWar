## 1. Developed-Faction Verdict

Implement Burgundy as **The Cadet Consolidator**, a legitimate but unevenly executable duchy that values durable Capetian-cadet autonomy, founder continuity, tested succession preparation, negotiated regional service, institution-specific settlement, finite military credibility, recoverable campaigns, and the gradual conversion of ducal dignity into repeatable capacity.

Burgundy is not passive. Burgundy may defend, punish, arbitrate, negotiate, expand, acquire rights, call service, support allies, exploit opportunity, and wage ambitious war. The AI must first ask whether the proposed act has a valid actor, authority, information, route, service response, recruitment pool, command structure, treasury, supply, home-defense reserve, institutional reception, and retention plan.

**Core question:** How does Burgundy survive the founder generation and become a durable duchy without becoming either a French province or an overextended personal war machine?

**Campaign fantasy:** Turn a hard-won cadet duchy into a government that can survive its founder.

## 26. Character, Dynasty, and Network AI

Robert I is the principal ducal anchor candidate, not a source of unlimited capacity. The AI may value personal arbitration, command, patronage, negotiation, and founder credibility while actively reducing single-point dependence. It cannot infer health, private intent, omniscience, direct ownership, or universal obedience.

Henry may be evaluated for bounded responsibility, household continuity, military service, representation, and regional consultation. No preference installs Henry as heir, co-ruler, commander, or future duke. Robert the Younger, Simon, Ermengarde, Constance, Hildegarde, and protected descendants remain independent owner-controlled actors rather than one dynastic bloc.

Every relationship direction and information path is separate. Kinship, proximity, marriage, office, service, patronage, or memory does not prove trust, willingness, access, loyalty, hostility, consent, or copied knowledge.

## 27. Governance, Offices, and Ducal Reach AI

The AI consumes typed mandates, offices, vacancies, seals, procedures, castellanies, itineraries, judgments, summonses, and delegated authority. It prefers a real actor with defined scope, information, route, resources, reporting, review, and termination.

Ducal Reach is explanatory context for one action and target. It is not a universal score. When execution is weak, the AI may narrow jurisdiction, secure an agent, seek witnesses, negotiate service, improve communication, change venue, prepare enforcement, or abstain.

## 28. Ecclesiastical AI

The AI treats Autun, Langres, Saint-Benigne of Dijon, Cluniac interfaces, Saint-Germain of Auxerre, and later validated institutions separately. It may seek counsel, mediation, witnessing, patronage, safe conduct, protection, documentary review, restitution, appointment influence where lawful, or reconciliation.

It cannot invent officeholders, merge institution with office or property, create universal Church approval, settle Robert's marriage controversy, resolve Dalmace responsibility, author papal policy, or turn sacred standing into troops or war permission.

## 29. Diplomacy and External-Owner AI

France is a related Crown and separate owner. The preferred posture is useful kinship, reciprocal recognition, bounded service, mediation, information exchange, and selective cooperation without absorption. The County of Burgundy remains a separate polity. Anjou, Aquitaine, Nevers, Auxerre, Champagne, imperial spaces, Cluny, and the papacy own their private state.

Diplomacy may seek access, assurances, time, recognition, settlement, coalition terms, marriage inquiry, frontier restraint, or joint action. No external hostility, alliance, claim, knowledge, or commitment is initialized here.

## 30. Military, Recruitment, Supply, Battle, and Recovery AI

Military evaluation requires a valid objective, actor, command, finite recruitment pools, responder commitments, equipment, assembly time, route capacity, stores, supply, season, home-defense reserve, cohesion, casualty tolerance, recovery, occupation, administration, and exit terms.

Preferred wars include defense of the ducal household, validated holdings, essential routes, castles, or institutions; enforcement of a high-value executable settlement; containment of a current threat; bounded frontier action; rescue of a critical partner; and strategically retainable acquisition. Guarded wars include prestige-only expeditions, multi-front conflict, long sieges without resources, campaigns dependent on expiring service, and conquest without garrison or recovery.

Battle opportunities exist to serve campaign objectives, not to farm experience. Participation or victory does not guarantee character development. Recruitment uses finite regional pools informed and constrained by population and specialist owner state.

## 31. Economy, Property, Population, and Development AI

The AI distinguishes regional prosperity, property ownership, revenue right, collectable revenue, available treasury, committed funds, labor, stores, recruitment population, supply stockpiles, and delivery capacity. Title extent does not create cash.

Priority investments include routes, bridges, storage, market protection, castle and settlement defenses, messenger capacity, archives, offices, recruitment recovery, equipment production, and projects that reduce brittle dependence. Every proposal requires owner-published resources, labor, duration, exposure, and effect.

## 32. Information, Uncertainty, Memory, Momentum, and Emergence AI

The AI may read only accessible, owner-published information. Memory supplies holder-specific interpretation, Momentum supplies directional pressure, and Emergence supplies conditional pathway status. None becomes fact, objective, command, probability, or destiny.

When sources conflict, the AI may request corroboration, inspect, send an envoy, consult an institution, prepare reversible options, delay irreversible action, or act under clearly stated uncertainty. Unknown is neither favorable nor neutral.

## 33. Proposal, Abstention, and Escalation Policy

A complete proposal explains actor, authority, known need, eligible alternatives, gates, benefits, risks, uncertainty, resources, home exposure, retention, recovery, rejected alternatives, owner route, and what would change the recommendation.

Abstention is valid when no eligible proposal exists, information cost is lower than action risk, current recovery deserves priority, or simultaneous commitments would create unacceptable exposure. Escalation requires a material owner-published state change, not impatience, calendar progression, or a narrative resemblance to later history.

## 34. Anti-Micromanagement and Player Presentation

Routine valid service, safe travel, recurring reporting, stable garrison support, and uncontroversial administration should follow standing policy. Player attention belongs to succession preparation, major delegation, contested service, institutional settlement, frontier choices, war planning, recovery, strategic investment, and decisions with incompatible valid alternatives.

The UI should display strategic identity, current posture candidate, primary reasons, hard blockers, uncertainty, resource and retention requirements, owner route, rejected shortcuts, and expected review trigger. It must not expose one aggression, loyalty, centralization, or historical-destiny score.
