Hommes d'Armes de Bourgogne are the concentrated foot expression of a validated Burgundian ducal or magnate household. Their identity is not simply better infantry. They surrender broad availability, cheap recovery, and potentially mounted capacity to produce a compact formation that restores control where the command structure, standard, gate, street, camp entrance, breach interior, or strongpoint is in danger.

The card survives only if three costs remain real: an exact household source, strict mounted-versus-foot reservation, and visible weakening of household security or another protected duty. If implementation turns the unit into Regional Spearmen with higher statistics, Castle Sergeants without custody, or Norman Foot Knights with a Burgundy label, the card fails.

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Hommes d'Armes de Bourgogne are selected household fighters committed on foot when a Burgundian owner needs a dependable body at one critical point. They remain behind ordinary infantry or near the command group until a sector begins to fail, a gate or street must be held, a strongpoint requires relief, or a fixed enemy becomes vulnerable to a compact counterstroke.

Their strength comes from familiar leaders, household cohesion, better access to protection, a clear mission, and scarce people concentrated in limited frontage. They do not possess automatic elite morale, universal mail, uniform weapons, cavalry immunity, or the breadth of a full professional line. If isolated, exhausted, surrounded, exposed to missiles, or committed without withdrawal, their quality becomes an expensive casualty list.

Recruiting or releasing them is a campaign decision. The formation can remove experienced people from residence defense, escort, command protection, custody, or mounted service. Victory does not erase those costs, and money cannot instantly replace dead retainers, captured personnel, officers, standards, damaged armor, broken shields, or household trust.

## 20. Battlefield Rhythm

- Begin in the second line, near command, or behind the position most likely to require relief.
- Avoid unnecessary missiles while the main line identifies the true threat.
- Confirm one crisis, objective, route, and commander.
- Move in compact order toward the threatened sector, gate, street, camp entrance, standard, commander, or strongpoint.
- Establish a bounded anchor and stop local collapse.
- Relieve friendly troops or counterattack an enemy already fixed, tired, compressed, or disordered.
- Refuse pursuit and broadening frontage.
- Consolidate briefly or break contact before fatigue, flank exposure, and encirclement destroy the body.
- Recover wounded, standards, officers, equipment, and sensitive property.
- Return survivors to owner-controlled recovery and reconcile mounted service.
