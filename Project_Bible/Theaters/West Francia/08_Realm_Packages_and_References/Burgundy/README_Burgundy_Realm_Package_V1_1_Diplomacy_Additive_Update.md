## 9. Military Design Rule

Burgundy uses finite recruitment pools rather than recruitment spam. Population informs and constrains those pools but is not directly deducted as army manpower.

The military design identity is **The Assembled Duchy**. Burgundy's future force generation depends on valid obligations, responders, command, equipment, horses where applicable, routes, supply, home-defense floors, and recovery.

The package currently preserves 23 candidate unit families. The literal family-name and ID merge into the designer-completion successor remains a downstream blocker. No exact opening armies, unit instances, garrisons, pool values, or commanders are activated by the handoff documents.

## 10. Realm Personality and AI Rule

Burgundy's Realm Personality is **The Cadet Consolidator**.

Future Realm AI may consume owner-published observations, compare eligible alternatives, reject invalid options, abstain, explain its reasoning, and propose a typed command to the appropriate owner. Realm AI may not manufacture missing state, infer foreign private state, grant authority, execute commands, or force later history.
