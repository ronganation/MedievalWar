## 3. Developed Profile Verdict

Adela of France begins as Countess of Flanders, wife and political partner of Baldwin V, a Capetian princess, mother of the principal Flemish dynasts, an ecclesiastical patron, and a principal continuity actor for the comital household. She is not a decorative spouse and not a universal substitute ruler.

**Her central campaign tension is continuity through influence versus authority through explicit mandate.**

Adela can make Baldwin's divided responsibilities sustainable by preserving household function, correspondence, patronage, ceremony, documentary continuity, family mediation, and French-facing access. Her influence becomes most durable when officers, resources, jurisdiction, public recognition, and reporting are clear. It becomes vulnerable when rank is mistaken for unrestricted command, informal influence substitutes for accountable government, patronage appears to capture institutions, or Capetian identity is treated as foreign dominance.

## 5. Core Character Thesis

**Can Adela convert rank, experience, family access, patronage, and household trust into resilient government without becoming an implausible co-count, an invisible spouse modifier, or an informal power center whose authority no one can verify?**

Her strength is connective capacity. She links Baldwin's household to Capetian kinship, adult children, religious communities, clerical literacy, witnessed acts, relief, ceremony, and the domestic consequences of the count's absence. Her weakness is that most of this power is relational, customary, resource-dependent, and mandate-sensitive.

The profile remains valid if Baldwin reduces his French role, if Adela never receives a broad mandate, if family harmony fails, if institutional patronage becomes contentious, if later retirement never occurs, or if she becomes the decisive continuity actor after an unexpected crisis.

## 34. Conditional Development Paths

### Household Guardian

Builds reliable officers, correspondence, access, hospitality, and continuity. Risk: household concentration and gatekeeping.

### Religious Founder and Patron

Builds institutional, educational, relief, and memorial capacity. Risk: obligations, autonomy, property conflict, and resource strain.

### Capetian Intermediary

Strengthens French access and mediation. Risk: suspicion that French interests outweigh Flemish needs.

### Dynastic Mediator

Preserves family dignity and succession options. Risk: appearing partisan or delaying necessary decisions.

### Scoped Domestic Governor

Exercises explicit, reviewable authority during Baldwin's absence. Risk: overlap with castellans, heir, and returning ruler.

### Emergency Continuity Principal

Acts during capture, incapacity, or sudden crisis. Risk: mandate ambiguity and extraordinary measures becoming precedent.

### Partisan Household Center

Failure transformation in which access and information become aligned to one family interest.

### Overextended Patron

Failure transformation in which promises exceed treasury, staff, and institutional capacity.

## 36. Absence, Capture, Incapacity, Widowhood, and Death

If Adela is absent, captive, incapacitated, disgraced, widowed, or dead, the system must review household access, correspondence, patronage, officers, resources, family mediation, French channels, institutional relationships, and continuity duties.

Widowhood does not automatically create retirement, monastic profession, universal regency, property transfer, or political withdrawal. Any later religious life requires current choice, access, institution acceptance, property and household arrangements, and owner validation.

No successor inherits her personal trust, correspondence knowledge, institutional credibility, or family relationships automatically.

## 37. Historical Memory, Momentum, and Emergence
