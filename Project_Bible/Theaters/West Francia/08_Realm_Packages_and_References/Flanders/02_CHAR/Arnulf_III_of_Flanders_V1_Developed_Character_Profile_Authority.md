### 3. Developed Profile Verdict

Arnulf is treated as the elder identified son of Baldwin of Mons and Richilde and as an underage next-generation candidate whose exact January 1062 life state, residence, and household binding remain subject to owner review. He stands at the center of a highly consequential Flanders-Hainaut succession line without receiving an opening title or active claim from this profile. He is neither an empty family-tree token nor a miniature adult ruler.

**His central campaign tension is protected formation versus premature dynastic instrumentalization.**

Arnulf matters because his health, recognition, residence, education, protection, household access, and relationships can affect long-term continuity. Yet every political use of his name risks confusing expectation with a claim, a claim with recognition, recognition with title, or future history with present authority. His strongest design is therefore not early command or artificial precocity. It is a contested but protected childhood whose formation can produce several lawful futures.

### 27. Conditional Development Paths

#### Hainaut-Raised Dynast

Strong local familiarity, household continuity, and Hainaut recognition. Risk: weaker Flemish relationships and greater perception that his future belongs exclusively to Hainaut.

#### Cross-Realm Heir-in-Formation

Carefully negotiated exposure to Hainaut and Flanders builds plural familiarity. Risk: travel, duplicated expectations, contested access, and politicized education.

#### Clerical-Documentary Formation

Literacy, records, institutions, and lawful process become central. Risk: later commentators mistake education for religious vocation or guaranteed administrative competence.

#### Martial-Household Formation

Supervised riding, discipline, household defense awareness, and noble physical training receive emphasis. Risk: adults project command readiness before age or evidence supports it.

#### Protected but Isolated Child

Security is prioritized over broad access. Risk: weak networks, dependence on a narrow household circle, and poor recognition outside it.

#### Dynastic Symbol Under Pressure

Adults increasingly invoke Arnulf in disputes. This is a danger path, not a power-up. His personal safety and future options narrow while factions claim authority through him.

#### Campaign-Divergent Adult Principal

If Arnulf survives, reaches valid majority, receives suitable formation, and is recognized through owner processes, he may become an adult political principal. No title or personality is predetermined.
