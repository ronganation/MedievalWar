### 3. Developed Profile Verdict

Baldwin is treated as the younger identified son of Baldwin of Mons and Richilde and as an underage next-generation candidate whose exact January 1062 life state, residence, custody, and household binding remain owner-controlled. He must not exist only as Arnulf's substitute.

**His central campaign tension is independent formation versus reserve-heir instrumentalization.**

Baldwin matters as a separate child with his own protection, education, relationships, and possible adult future. His younger position can provide flexibility and alternate continuity, but adults may neglect him, overcompare him with Arnulf, or preserve him only as dynastic insurance. Strong design gives him a meaningful childhood without inventing adult powers or guaranteeing later rule.

**Character fantasy:** Protect and develop the younger son as a real person whose value is not limited to replacing his elder brother.

**Strategic question:** Can Baldwin receive secure care, fair resources, independent relationships, and a coherent formation path without being neglected beside Arnulf, weaponized as a rival, or scripted into later Hainaut rule and Flemish claims?

### 25. Conditional Development Paths

#### Hainaut-Centered Younger Dynast

Local familiarity and household continuity dominate. Risk: narrow external recognition.

#### Distinctly Educated Brother

Baldwin develops different institutional, linguistic, courtly, or practical strengths without becoming an artificial opposite to Arnulf.

#### Shared Fraternal Formation

The brothers gain common experience and potential cooperation. Risk: comparison and loss of individual treatment.

#### Protected Alternate Continuity

Records and formation preserve lawful future options without active heirship. Risk: adults treat him as insurance rather than a child.

#### Politicized Junior Candidate

Adults rally around his possible future. This danger path increases exposure, factional labeling, and sibling strain.

#### Campaign-Divergent Adult Hainaut Principal

If valid world state produces survival, majority, recognition, and title process, Baldwin may become an adult Hainaut principal. No historical policy or outcome is guaranteed.

#### Adult Cross-Realm Claimant or Settler

A future adult may pursue, negotiate, renounce, settle, or ignore title interests through current law, evidence, resources, and relationships. Childhood does not choose this path.
