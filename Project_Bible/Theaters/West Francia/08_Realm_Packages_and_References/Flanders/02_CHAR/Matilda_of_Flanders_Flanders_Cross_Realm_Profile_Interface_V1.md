## 2. Purpose and Strong Interface Verdict

This is deliberately not a second Matilda character profile. Normandy already owns the complete person authority for Matilda as Duchess of Normandy, principal consort, ducal household anchor, dynastic connector, and conditional regent. citeturn18search208turn18search223

Flanders needs a strong interface because Matilda is Baldwin V and Adela's daughter, sibling of Baldwin of Mons and Robert, and a high-value connection to Normandy. The Flanders character baseline explicitly says that she belongs to the Norman court and connects Flanders to Normandy without becoming a Flemish-controlled agent. citeturn18search206turn18search207

**Interface fantasy: Maintain a powerful daughter and dynastic channel abroad while accepting that access is not control, kinship is not obedience, and every useful exchange must survive Normandy's interests and Matilda's own agency.**

## 7. Flanders Strategic Functions

Matilda's Flanders-facing functions are conditional and request-based:

- maintain family correspondence when delivered and answered;
- provide a Normandy-facing introduction or audience request;
- mediate a family or diplomatic dispute when she chooses and has access;
- receive or transmit news about Baldwin V, Adela, Baldwin of Mons, Richilde, or Robert;
- support hospitality, safe conduct, refuge, or travel proposals through Normandy owners;
- participate in marriage, succession, ecclesiastical, memorial, or patronage discussion;
- affect specific audience memories of Flanders in Normandy;
- communicate Normandy's own limits, requests, or concerns back to Flanders.

None creates a standing alliance, intelligence feed, command, force delivery, money transfer, or automatic agreement.

Father-daughter communication may involve health, household news, diplomacy, Normandy, succession, Robert's future, or ceremony. Baldwin cannot command Matilda through paternity.

Mother-daughter correspondence may carry family, institutional, patronage, household, or mediation concerns. Similar rank and experience do not create shared private knowledge.

Matilda may receive news, congratulations, warnings, succession questions, or mediation requests. She cannot transfer Hainaut support or settle Richilde's rights.

She may help explore an introduction, external opportunity, safe conduct, mediation, or household future. Her involvement does not create Robert's later marriage or Norman sponsorship.

## 11. Diplomacy and Mediation

## 13. Succession, Claims, and Family Crisis

She may propose mediation, recognition, neutrality, hospitality, an envoy, or conditions. She cannot create a Flemish claim, vote, title transfer, Norman intervention, guardianship, or army.

The Flanders titles and succession interface remains the local authority for title-specific processes and does not transfer ownership to this cross-Realm document. citeturn18search190
