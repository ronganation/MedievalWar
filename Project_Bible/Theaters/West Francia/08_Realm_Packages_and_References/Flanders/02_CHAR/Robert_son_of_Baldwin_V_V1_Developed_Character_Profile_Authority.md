## 3. Developed Profile Verdict

Robert begins as Baldwin V's capable adult younger son: useful enough to command, negotiate, travel, or establish an external future, but without the Flemish inheritance and without a defined opening office, title, marriage, or army.

**His central tension is earned independence versus destabilizing concentration.**

Robert must not be written as a resentful rebel waiting for history to activate. He is an adult dynast whose unsettled position is a strategic resource and a strategic risk. Flanders can give him responsibility, dignity, compensation, marriage support, external opportunity, or a bounded household. Each solution builds capacity and relationships while changing the future balance around Baldwin of Mons, Richilde, and succession.
