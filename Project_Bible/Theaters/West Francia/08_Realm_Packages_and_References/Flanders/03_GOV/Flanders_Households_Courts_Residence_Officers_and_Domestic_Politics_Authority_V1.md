## 1. Strong Flanders Household Verdict

Flanders' political households are operating institutions, not family-tree containers. The senior comital household coordinates residence, access, records, counsel, petitions, officers, guests, travel, provisioning, protection, worship, diplomacy, and continuity. It is not the county, dynasty, comital title, treasury, government, army, or every Flemish household.

The opening domestic game is **The Connected Moving Court**. Baldwin V and Adela can govern through a mobile household across several Flemish centers while engaging France, Hainaut, Normandy, castellanies, markets, and religious institutions. That strength produces dependence on routes, hosts, officers, clerks, guards, stores, messengers, and local cooperation. Concentrating access or records may improve speed while creating gatekeeper risk. Distributing them may improve resilience while slowing coordination.

Baldwin of Mons and Richilde head a separately owned Hainaut household. Matilda belongs to the separately owned Norman ducal household. Robert's exact establishment remains unresolved. Kinship creates interfaces, not membership, residence, access, command, shared information, or household merger. Adela remains an active senior household principal without becoming a spouse modifier, universal regent, title owner, or battlefield commander. Arnulf III and Baldwin II are separate underage Hainaut-owned protected-person candidates. Household review must never merge their residence, custody, guardianship, education, maintenance, access, or future title interfaces.
