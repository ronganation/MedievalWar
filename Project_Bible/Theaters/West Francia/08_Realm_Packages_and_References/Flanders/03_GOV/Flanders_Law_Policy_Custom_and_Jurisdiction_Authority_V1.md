## 1. Strong Flanders Legal Verdict

Flanders should operate through overlapping comital peace, local custom, title and lordship right, castellany jurisdiction, household mandate, town and market practice, ecclesiastical privilege, written and witnessed act, property possession, French-facing obligation, imperial-facing obligation, negotiated settlement, and practical enforcement capacity. Flanders does not begin with one codified law, one permanent capital court, one appeal ladder, one uniform officer corps, or unlimited comital jurisdiction.

The central legal campaign is to convert strong comital authority and dense institutional connection into credible, scoped, and performable order. Baldwin V may hear, summon, mediate, confirm, protect, judge, delegate, negotiate, and enforce where authority, subject matter, place, parties, evidence, notice, officers, communication, resources, acceptance, and restoration are sufficient. Personal power cannot silently replace process.

Flanders is especially shaped by legal plurality. An order valid in Crown-facing Flanders may need different reasoning for an imperial-facing holding. An abbey privilege may qualify castellan action. A market custom may regulate toll or passage without creating communal sovereignty. A military emergency may justify temporary action without creating permanent law. Hainaut, France, Normandy, and external church authorities retain their own legal state.

No exact opening custom, law, court roster, case, judgment, fine, forfeiture, immunity, officer, appeal path, enforcement action, or restoration is created here.
