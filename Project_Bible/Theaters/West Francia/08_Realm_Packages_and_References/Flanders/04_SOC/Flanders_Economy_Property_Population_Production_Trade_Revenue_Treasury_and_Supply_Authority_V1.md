## 1. Strong Flanders Economic Verdict

Flanders opens with exceptional economic potential derived from dense settlements, market connections, rural production, waterways, coastal access, institutions, itinerant government, and negotiated rights. Potential is not spendable wealth. The comital title does not grant ownership of every estate, mill, market, toll, bridge, fishery, woodland, water right, church property, household asset, communal resource, or merchant stock.

The economic campaign is **Connected Prosperity Under Negotiated Extraction**. The player converts verified rights, productive access, records, collection, transport, storage, market participation, and receipts into usable capacity while protecting the households, communities, routes, water systems, institutions, and labor on which future receipts depend.

Flanders must remain Total War-readable at the surface while preserving causal depth beneath the summary. Income, expenses, balance, trade, upkeep, construction, and damage remain clear. Inspection reveals ownership, possession, custody, operation, assessment, collection, receipt, commitment, delivery, uncertainty, and recovery. No exact January 1062 balance, income, rate, price, yield, stock, debt, arrears, property inventory, or trade flow is invented.
