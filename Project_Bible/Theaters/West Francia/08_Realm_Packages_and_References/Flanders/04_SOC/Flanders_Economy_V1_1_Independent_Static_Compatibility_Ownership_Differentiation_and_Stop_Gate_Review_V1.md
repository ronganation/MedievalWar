## 14. Final Position

Flanders Economy is closed at static-authority scope. The V1 authority remains the controlling substantive owner, and the V1.1 addendum supplies the accepted shared-family compatibility layer. The closure preserves The Connected March, 36 Flanders-specific operating portfolios, negotiated extraction, finite Population and labor constraints, external owner locks, later-history firewalls, honest unknowns, and strict nonactivation boundaries.

No broad rewrite, split authority family, immediate V1.2, folder move, Registry commitment, Opening-State promotion, importer execution, runtime payload, or Unreal implementation is justified.
