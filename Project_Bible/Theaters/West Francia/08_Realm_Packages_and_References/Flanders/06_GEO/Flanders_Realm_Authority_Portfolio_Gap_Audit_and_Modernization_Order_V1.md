## 1. Executive Verdict

Flanders has a strong and unusually coherent older design package. The package already possesses a broad campaign spine, principal character and household coverage, governance and community systems, ecclesiastical and geographic foundations, a twelve-family Historical Memory suite, a twelve-family Historical Momentum suite, twelve Historical Emergence pathways, a January 1062 Opening State, The Connected March Realm Personality and proposal-only AI, visual presentation guidance, and a Registry candidate-preflight and owner-routing chain.

The correct action is not a wholesale reset. The correct action is **copy-first additive modernization plus specialist owner completion**.

Four owner domains are confirmed missing or deferred by the Flanders sources themselves:

```yaml
confirmed_missing_specialist_owners:
  - Knowledge_Communication_Diplomacy_and_External_Interfaces
  - Routes_Logistics_Transport_Infrastructure_and_Supply_Movement
  - Economy_Property_Population_Production_Trade_Revenue_Treasury_and_Supply
  - Military_Roster_Recruitment_Obligation_Command_Force_and_Strategic_Access
```

Additional modern parity gaps concern historical evidence and uncertainty, Character Network, title and succession interfaces, household and domestic-politics specialization, law and restoration specialization, exact roster and finite recruitment pools, owner-reconciled Opening State bindings, cross-owner integration, package indexing, candidate serialization, root navigation, and future implementation batching.

Flanders is entwined with Capetian France through Adela of France, Baldwin V's role in the minority government of Philip I, and French-facing title and Crown relationships. That entwinement is a first-class dependency of the modernization, but it does not transfer France-owned people, government, commands, resources, reverse relationships, or private state into Flanders.
