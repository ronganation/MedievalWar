- every runtime pathway remains `RuntimePathwayStatus = Absent` and has no runtime stage;

### 5.1 Legal status and stage combinations

- `Absent` has no runtime stage.
- `Open` may use `Observed`, `Eligible`, `Proposed`, `Validated`, `AwaitingConsent`, `Preparing`, `Executing`, `Resolving`, or `Aftermath`.
- `Suspended` preserves the last valid stage and records blockers.
- `Completed` normally closes through `Aftermath` after owner results satisfy completion conditions.
- `Failed`, `Cancelled`, and `Expired` preserve the last valid stage and archival trace.
- No transition may bypass required owner validation, independent consent, authority, capacity, cost, risk, or access checks.

## 6. Common Runtime Stage Pattern

A pathway may use the following sequence where relevant:

1. **Observed:** committed owner observations or compatible handoffs make the definition relevant.
2. **Eligible:** hard conditions pass.
3. **Proposed:** a typed player or AI proposal exists.
4. **Validated:** required owners accept the proposal for consideration.
5. **AwaitingConsent:** required participants decide independently.
6. **Preparing:** resources, access, information, reservations, and command requests are assembled.
7. **Executing:** owning systems execute owner-accepted commands or events.
8. **Resolving:** owner results are compared with completion, failure, suspension, and continuation rules.
9. **Aftermath:** owners publish consequences to Memory, Momentum, Network, AI, and save systems.

A pathway may omit irrelevant stages but cannot bypass a stage containing a required owner, consent, authority, capacity, cost, risk, access, or command gate.

**Consent gate.** Silence, kinship, prior cooperation, shared religion, political pressure, and historical outcome never imply consent. Conditional acceptance is rechecked before each governed command. Withdrawal blocks unexecuted command requests without erasing recorded consequences.

**Authority and capacity gate.** The relevant owners must publish title, office, property, force, route, stock, treasury, labor, institution, diplomatic, knowledge, climate, and access state needed by the proposal. Momentum intensity does not manufacture capacity.

**Preparation gate.** Estimates, reservations, commitments, expenditures, route windows, instructions, messages, and command requests remain separate records. Preparation can fail, expire, suspend, or be released without creating the selected outcome.

**Execution and resolution gate.** Only receiving owners execute commands. Emergence compares owner results with completion, continuation, partial success, failure, suspension, cancellation, and aftermath conditions. The historically recognizable result is never privileged merely because it occurred.
