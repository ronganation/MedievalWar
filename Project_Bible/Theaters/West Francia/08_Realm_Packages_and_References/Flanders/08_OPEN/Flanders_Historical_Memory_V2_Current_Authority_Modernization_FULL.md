## 6. Non-Negotiable Memory Rules

- Memory is not fact, evidence, law, property, authority, pressure, personality, or outcome.
- A holder requires a valid acquisition path.
- Kinship, Realm membership, language, religion, proximity, and generic intelligence do not automatically transmit memory.
- A person, household, institution, office, and community audience remain distinct holder types.
- Institutional memory is archive, ritual, custom, commemoration, or office precedent, not a human emotional state.
- Contradictory interpretations may coexist.
- Salience may change independently from factual accuracy.
- Memory may explain a proposal but cannot issue a command.
- Later events, epithets, victories, offices, deaths, and historiography cannot be loaded backward into January 1062.
