## 3. Momentum Doctrine

### 3.1 Momentum is derived pressure

Momentum consumes dated owner state and approved memory projections. It does not become the owner of those inputs.

### 3.2 Momentum must be directional

A pressure record must identify what is changing or being pressed toward. A high pressure without direction is invalid.

### 3.3 Momentum is not inevitability

Strong pressure may increase the relevance or eligibility of proposals and Emergence pathways. It never guarantees execution or result.

### 3.4 Momentum can be resisted

Authority, capacity, resources, relationships, institutions, routes, player policy, external conditions, and successful counteractions may slow, redirect, divide, or reverse pressure.

### 3.5 Momentum is owner-bounded

A Flanders record may observe France, Normandy, Hainaut, or imperial actors through published signals. It cannot write their claims, attitudes, forces, memories, decisions, or reverse relationships.

### 3.6 Unknown is not zero

Missing force, route, economic, office, relationship, diplomatic, or institutional data reduces confidence or blocks calculation. Missing support is not zero support. Missing resistance is not zero resistance.

### 3.7 Momentum is date-valid

No signal contributes before its effective date or before a valid observer, owner, report, event, or system makes it available.

### 3.8 Momentum may be plural

One subject may support simultaneous opportunity and risk. The Capetian regency may increase prestige while increasing domestic overextension. These pressures must not be collapsed into one unexplained score.

### 3.9 Stage is not outcome

Escalating does not mean war, accession, reform, foundation, marriage, privilege, or rebellion has occurred.

## 6. Runtime Momentum Stages

- absent;
- active;
- escalating;
- stabilizing;
- redirected;
- suspended;
- resolved;
- expired.

### 6.1 Runtime stage rules

- `absent` means no runtime Momentum state exists; it is not zero pressure.
- `active` means a validated runtime state is currently evaluated and visible within its scope.
- `escalating` means support is outpacing resistance or an owner-approved threshold was crossed.
- `stabilizing` means resistance or a resolution input is containing direction.
- `redirected` means the same underlying pressure now points toward another valid direction or outcome family.
- `suspended` means required data, access, owner signals, or compatible interfaces are unavailable.
- `resolved` requires an authoritative owner outcome or an accepted Emergence result; the archival record remains.
- `expired` requires the pressure subject or valid conditions to cease being relevant. Historical Date alone does not force expiry unless the definition includes an approved dated rule.

Definition lifecycle and opening eligibility are controlled separately in Section 6A. Quarantine is a definition, record, or interface handling state, not a live pressure stage.

## 23. Escalation, Stabilization, Redirection, and Closure

### 23.1 Escalation

A pressure may escalate when:

- support signals strengthen;
- resistance signals weaken;
- multiple independent owners publish compatible observations;
- a threshold is crossed;
- a related pressure reinforces the same direction;
- player policy deliberately accepts the risk;
- an external event increases urgency.

### 23.2 Stabilization

A pressure may stabilize when:

- authority and capacity align;
- a bounded settlement reduces uncertainty;
- routes, stores, or communication improve;
- external owners publish de-escalation;
- a rival pressure limits available action;
- current policy contains rather than resolves the issue.

### 23.3 Redirection

Examples:

- Robert pressure may redirect from household service to external marriage;
- Ename pressure may redirect from demilitarization to continued custody;
- Regency pressure may redirect from broad protection to bounded mediation;
- urban growth may redirect toward defensive provisioning during war.

### 23.4 Suspension

Suspend when:

- required owner state is unavailable;
- the holder is inactive;
- route or communication is severed;
- contradictory facts quarantine the definition;
- pressure cannot be observed within visibility rules.

### 23.5 Closure

A momentum closes only when:

- an owner publishes a resolving outcome;
- the subject ceases to exist;
- the conditions expire;
- an Emergence outcome replaces the pressure with a new valid state;
- the pressure becomes historically and strategically irrelevant.

Historical dates do not close momentum automatically.
