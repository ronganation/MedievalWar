## 2. Authority Verdict

Flanders is **The Connected Diplomatic Principality**. Its external power comes from Baldwin V's prestige and French governmental role, Adela's Capetian connection, Matilda's Norman position, the Hainaut marriage of Baldwin of Mons and Richilde, Robert's open political future, ecclesiastical and commercial networks, and unusually dense internal routes. None of those connections equals control.

The player should manage reach without omniscience, influence without ownership, kinship without automatic consent, and agreements without guaranteed performance. Information must travel through people, offices, households, institutions, markets, waterways and roads. External actions remain requests to outside owners until valid responses return.
