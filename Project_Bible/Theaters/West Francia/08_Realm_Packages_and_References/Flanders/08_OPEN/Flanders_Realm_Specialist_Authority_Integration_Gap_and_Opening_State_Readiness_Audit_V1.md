## 1. Audit Verdict

Flanders is no longer missing the broad military, exact-roster, population, economy, supply, route-evidence, law, titles, household, or principal evidence authorities reported as absent by the older readiness and handoff records. Those older records remain valid evidence of an earlier package phase, but they no longer describe the current authority portfolio.

The package should not move directly into an Opening State successor yet. The strongest next substantive modernization is the inherited ecclesiastical baseline because it still names the V1 Character, Dynasty, Governance, and Communities authorities in its control block, while a newer ecclesiastical evidence specialist already consumes its full source. The correct action is a copy-first integrated ecclesiastical successor that preserves the original strategic and playable church content while reconciling current specialist ownership.

After ecclesiastical modernization, the package requires one more integration check across external knowledge, diplomacy, communication, AI, institutions, economy, supply, routes, military, law, titles, households, population, territory, and exact opening bindings. Only then should the Opening State be rebuilt as the downstream January 1062 integration authority.

## 4. Why Ecclesiastical Modernization Is Next

The inherited ecclesiastical authority remains strategically substantive. It owns the integrated playable relationship among diocesan structures, monasteries, collegiate churches, parish life, patronage, advocacy, reform, jurisdiction, legitimacy, relief, information, property pressure, and campaign choices. The newer historical ecclesiastical file is an evidence specialist. It does not justify deleting or compressing the playable realm authority.

The successor must:

- copy the full immediate source first;
- preserve every institution, role, decision, pathway, fact, memory, pressure and uncertainty treatment;
- reconcile the current Character, Dynasty, Governance, Communities, Population, Territory, Economy, Supply, Law, Titles, Household, Historical Evidence, Emergence, Military and Opening State chain;
- preserve Saint-Bertin, Saint Peter's at Ghent, Saint Bavo's at Ghent, Messines, Lille's collegiate foundation and every other named or bounded institution as distinct records;
- separate institution, site, building, community, office, holder, property, privilege, patronage, advocacy, jurisdiction and military access;
- preserve historically grounded political, household, educational, patronage, diplomatic, refuge, relief and institutional agency for women without routine battlefield-general treatment;
- retain Ename Abbey as unavailable at the January 1062 opening;
- add dependencies, acceptance criteria, evidence requirements, rollback plans and owner gates;
- create no active institution, office, property, store, levy, garrison, troop, route, Registry, Opening State or Unreal record.

## 8. Negative Fixtures

Reject any next-step plan that:

- creates another economy, population, route, supply, law, titles, household, military-capability or exact-roster authority without first showing that the existing file is unusable or substantively distinct;
- treats the historical ecclesiastical evidence specialist as a replacement for the integrated playable ecclesiastical authority;
- moves directly to Opening State while an inherited controlling authority still points to superseded V1 dependencies;
- invents exact units, pools, garrisons, offices, institutions, properties, routes, stores, commanders or external support;
- treats Hainaut, France, Normandy or imperial-facing state as Flanders-owned;
- activates Ename Abbey in January 1062;
- overwrites advanced saves with opening defaults;
