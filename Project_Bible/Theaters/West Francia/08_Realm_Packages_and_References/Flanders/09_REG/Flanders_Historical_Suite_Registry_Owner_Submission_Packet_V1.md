## 16. Risks if Promoted Prematurely

- a V1.1 source could map to the wrong kind or owner;
- the chronology discrepancy could be hidden through destructive normalization;
- external state could be registered as Flanders-owned;
- unknown could be converted into zero;
- an alias chain could make migration and rollback nondeterministic;
- a definition import could accidentally create runtime state;
- opening defaults could overwrite advanced saves;
- later history could leak into January 1062.

The batch and quarantine controls are designed to prevent these failures.
