- January 1, 1062 remains the opening boundary.
- Later history remains a horizon or conditional possibility, not opening destiny.
- Unknown remains distinct from zero.
- Project authority remains distinct from primary historical corroboration.
- Memory relevance remains distinct from current truth.
- Momentum pressure remains distinct from command.
- Emergence eligibility remains distinct from activation.
- Realm AI remains proposal-only.
- External Realm references remain externally owned.
- Women characters primarily retain historically grounded political, household, dynastic, patronage, diplomatic, and institutional roles unless an exceptional conditional emergence is separately supported.
- No exact force, treasury, route, population, stock, ship, office, property, or border value is invented.
