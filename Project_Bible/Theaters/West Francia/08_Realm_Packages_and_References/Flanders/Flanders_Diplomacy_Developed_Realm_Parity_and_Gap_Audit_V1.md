## 1. Independent Verdict

The existing Flanders combined authority is substantively strong and should remain the single general owner for Flanders-facing Knowledge, Communication, Diplomacy, and External Interfaces. It is not a weak file and should not be replaced by a separate general diplomacy authority.

It already exceeds several developed-Realm benchmarks in information provenance, messenger lifecycle, authentication, rumor, commercial and ecclesiastical information channels, cross-Realm interface breadth, and player explanation of source, age, confidence, mandate, and blockers.

It does not yet reach full Denmark-Sweden-Norway lifecycle parity in several narrowly identifiable areas. These are additive gaps inside the existing combined authority, not grounds for a second authority:

1. principal, representative, mandate, audience, and ratification architecture is present but not consolidated as a full contract;
2. ceasefire, truce, peace, reconciliation, and political settlement are not separated into a complete lifecycle;
3. war entry, objectives, escalation, bounded action, and settlement paths are underdeveloped;
4. coalition-member entry, contribution, shared command, withdrawal, and separate peace are not fully typed;
5. tribute, clientage, submission, protection, autonomy, and retained government are not comprehensively modeled;
6. obligation feasibility lacks the complete capacity, reservation, mobilization, execution, delivery, inspection, and acceptance chain;
7. there is no consolidated developed-Realm-style Flanders validation matrix covering all general diplomacy failures;
8. cross-owner atomic commit is stated in prose but lacks the full expected-version, reservation, receipt, commit-state, and rollback schema;
9. no explicit anti-churn rule declares the combined authority the single general diplomacy owner;
10. the authority's broad Static-Checked status is not paired in the reviewed material with a subject-specific manifest and static-check companion.

## 5. Detailed Gap Register

### FLA-DIP-GAP-001: Principal and ratification contract

The authority includes actor, representative, mandate, signatory, witness, and owner fields, but it does not provide a single contract that distinguishes principal, representative, audience, mandate scope, negotiation authority, counteroffer authority, ratification authority, and performance authority.

Required additive correction: insert a consolidated principal-representation-ratification contract without stripping existing record fields.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-002: War lifecycle

War appears through external military requests, coalition activity, truce and peace action families, AI constraints, and negative fixtures. The authority does not provide a full war-objective record separating grievance, claim, recognition demand, declaration, objective, geographic scope, permitted action, prohibited action, duration, review, domestic sustainability, and settlement path.

Required additive correction: add a Flanders war-entry and bounded-objective contract. Do not create an opening war.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-003: Coalition and separate peace

The subject covers military contribution, coalition, command, and withdrawal as an action family, but lacks a member-specific commitment contract covering entry, objectives, promised contribution, access, command relationship, Supply, finance, information sharing, breach, withdrawal, and separate peace.

Required additive correction: add one typed coalition-member contract.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-004: Peace and settlement

Truce, peace, nonaggression, dispute management, agreement, withdrawal, and termination are present, but ceasefire, truce, peace, reconciliation, political settlement, integration, and retained government are not fully separated.

Required additive correction: add a staged de-escalation and political-settlement architecture.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-005: Subordination and protection arrangements

Homage, service, holding-specific obligation, protection, and Hainaut/French/imperial relations are present. Tribute, clientage, submission, autonomy, retained government, and integration safeguards are not comprehensively typed.

Required additive correction: add a bounded arrangement contract that preserves French-facing, imperial-facing, Hainaut, local-law, office, property, and jurisdiction ownership.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-006: Obligation execution depth

The current authority correctly distinguishes agreement from performance and promise from delivery. It needs the complete chain:

```text
Promise != Capacity
Capacity != Reservation
Reservation != Mobilization
Mobilization != Execution
Execution != Delivery
Delivery != Receipt
Receipt != Inspection
Inspection != Acceptance
Partial Performance != Full Performance
```

Required additive correction: add owner receipts at each material stage.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-007: Atomic transaction schema

The authority states that failed multi-owner transactions roll back reservations and proposed commitments together. It does not expose one formal record with expected versions, owner-change requests, reservation receipts, external receipts, validation receipts, commit state, and rollback plan.

Required additive correction: add the schema while preserving the existing rule.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-008: Consolidated domestic sustainability

The authority routes many constraints but does not provide one Flanders assessment covering comital government, households, castellanies, towns, markets, abbeys, routes, crossings, treasury, obligations, military contributions, external owners, and the three-decision player budget.

Required additive correction: add a consolidated sustainability assessment.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-009: Validation matrix

The current static and negative fixtures are useful but not as auditable as the consolidated case matrices used by the developed Denmark, Sweden, and Norway files.

Required additive correction: create one numbered Flanders matrix covering information, messages, kinship, regency, Hainaut, Normandy, tribute, subordination, war, coalition, peace, person arrangements, external owners, AI, Save, Migration, opening replay, and anti-churn.

### FLA-DIP-GAP-010: Anti-churn and closure controls

The package freezes broad redesign and the authority reports no predecessor, but the file itself does not state that it remains the single general owner for Flanders knowledge, communication, diplomacy, and external interfaces.

Required additive correction: add one anti-churn block prohibiting a second general Flanders diplomacy authority and allowing reopening only for demonstrated omission, contradiction, failed validation, or newly authorized material need.
