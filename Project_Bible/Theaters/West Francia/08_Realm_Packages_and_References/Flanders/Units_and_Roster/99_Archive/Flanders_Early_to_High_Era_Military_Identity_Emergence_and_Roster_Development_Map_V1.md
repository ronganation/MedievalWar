## 1. Strong Whole-Roster Verdict

Flanders does not enter the High Era by replacing the Connected March or deleting the Count's Web. It enters the High Era when separately owned military threads become more capable of organizing, equipping, recording, financing, defending, negotiating, and mustering themselves while remaining connected through the county's routes, strongpoints, markets, political agreements, and command relationships.

**The Mustered Web** is the leading High Era expression of that change.

Early Flanders asks:

> Which threads may the count pull into war without breaking the web at home?

High Era Flanders asks:

> How can the count, towns, castellanies, landed households, regional communities, and military institutions concentrate a stronger host when each thread increasingly possesses its own leadership, obligations, privileges, costs, and political voice?

## 3. The Mustered Web Naming and Meaning Contract

The phrase is retained as the leading working expression because it develops the existing metaphor without abandoning it. The web remains the network. Mustered describes what has changed: more threads can produce recognized companies, cadres, records, stores, equipment systems, service expectations, and collective action.

```yaml
Mustered_means:
  - MoreSourcesCanAssembleRecognizedMilitaryBodies
  - LeadershipRecordsEquipmentAndServiceBecomeMoreExplicit
  - TownAndCastellanyInstitutionsCanActCollectively
  - FieldConcentrationCanBecomeMoreReliableWhenPoliticsAndRoutesPass
  - MilitaryCapacityHasStrongerLocalOwnersAndNegotiatingVoices
Mustered_does_not_mean:
  - PermanentStandingArmy
  - UniversalCentralControl
  - EveryTownBecomesCommuneOrGuildArmy
  - EveryCommunityProducesSameUnit
  - FreeRealmWideRecruitment
  - AutomaticProfessionalization
  - AutomaticMassInfantrySuperiority
  - LaterHistoricalOutcomeGuaranteed
identity_tension: StrongerWebCreatesMorePowerAndMorePoliticalFriction
```

## 5. Refined High Era Core Pillars

### 5.1 Pillar One: Muster Without Ownership Collapse

The High Era improves the reliability and depth of collective service without changing every source into comital property.

```yaml
benefits:
  - MoreReliableAssembly
  - MoreRecognizableCompaniesAndCadres
  - BetterRecordsSignalsEquipmentInspectionAndIssue
  - BetterCombinedContingentPlanning
costs:
  - StrongerSourceClaims
  - NegotiationAndPrivilegePressure
  - ServiceScopeDisputes
  - LocalDefenseReservations
  - RefusalOrDelayWhenPoliticalRelationshipFails
```

### 5.2 Pillar Two: Stronger Threads, Stronger Seams

Every military institution that becomes stronger also becomes harder to treat as an anonymous pool.

```yaml

  - TownCompanies
  - CastellanyProfessionals
  - RegionalSpearAndShieldCompanies

```

### 5.3 Pillar Three: The Infantry Ecosystem Matures

Flanders becomes stronger through coordination among several useful infantry families, not one universally superior pike, spear, shield, or urban card.

```yaml
maturing_ecosystem:
  - PersistentRuralEmergencyDepth
  - PersistentRegionalSpearBackbone
  - PersistentShieldAndCloseSupport
  - StrongerTownAndGateService
  - StrongerCastellanyReliefAndProfessionalDepth
  - ConditionalCivicFieldInstitutions
  - ConditionalCrossbowAndTechnicalSupport
universal_best_infantry_card: false
```

### 5.4 Pillar Four: Wealth Equips Institutions but Does Not Manufacture Them

Markets, finance, craft, storage, transport, and repair may improve military readiness. They do not create trained people, lawful service, civic cooperation, military horses, cadres, or recovered pools.
