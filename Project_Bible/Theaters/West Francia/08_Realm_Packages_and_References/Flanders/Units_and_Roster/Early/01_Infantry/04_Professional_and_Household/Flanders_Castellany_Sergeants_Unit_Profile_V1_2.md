**Castellany Sergeants are Flanders' trained professional spear-and-shield infantry drawn from the military households, custodial staffs, retained fighters, and validated service structures attached to functioning castles and castellanies.** They are the first unit in the Flanders sequence that can be trusted to own difficult frontage for more than a short emergency. Their readiness comes from recurring duty, stronger leadership, better equipment completion, familiarity with alarms and strongpoints, and practical experience maintaining order around gates, walls, stores, courtyards, roads, and crossings.

The unit is not free field infantry. Every company released from a castellany reduces protection, response, custody, training, watch, escort, and relief capacity at the source strongpoint. The player's defining choice is not whether Castellany Sergeants are better than ordinary spearmen. They are. The choice is whether the field mission justifies opening a **custody gap** in the defensive web.


## 1A. V1.2 Decisive Strengthening

The V1.1 family was structurally correct but too defensive in presentation. V1.2 retains the professional spear-and-shield identity and raises the battlefield promise to the standard expected of a mature Total War professional infantry card.

**Castellany Sergeants are Flanders' premier professional spear-and-shield line infantry.** They are not merely the unit that arrives after another formation fails. They can begin on the main line, own difficult frontage from the opening contact, receive cavalry on prepared ground, defeat ordinary infantry through disciplined spear-and-shield work, execute one controlled advance, and still conduct the roster's most reliable physical relief or withdrawal-cover operation.

Their defining Total War fantasy is **Hold, Relieve, Restore**:

1. Hold the professional frontage without wasting formation order.
2. Relieve a failing allied unit through a real lane.
3. Restore a coherent Flemish line and remain useful after the transfer.

## 2. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Castellany Sergeants
- **Class:** Trained professional spear-and-shield strongpoint infantry
- **Source:** Validated sergeants, retained fighters, experienced guards, and military personnel maintained through functioning castles and castellanies
- **Availability:** Narrow, finite, strongpoint-specific, equipment- and leadership-dependent, and limited by nonreleasable custody floors
- **Battle promise:** A dependable trained spear anchor that can take over a failing position, restore ordered frontage through bounded relief, and hold a critical route or strongpoint while its source castellany accepts a real custody loss
- **Best use:** Gates, roads, crossings, fortified relief, disciplined center or flank anchor, camp security, siege defense, controlled field detachment, and receiving mounted pressure on prepared ground
- **Worst use:** Cheap mass, reckless assault, deep pursuit, expendable siege waves, broad occupation duty, unsupported missile exposure, and distant campaigning that leaves the source castellany uncovered
- **Strategic cost:** Every field detachment removes exact people, leaders, equipment, response depth, custody capacity, and technical familiarity from a castle or castellany
- **Recovery:** Slow because replacements require suitable people, recurring duty, equipment, leadership, strongpoint integration, trust, and time rather than money alone
- **Counters:** Concentrated missiles, elite armored infantry, encirclement, fatigue, sustained assault without relief, severe muddy movement, and simultaneous threats to the field company and source strongpoint
- **Notable distinction:** More professional and reliable than Flemish Spearmen, but divided between nonreleasable custody personnel, releasable field detachments, and recalled or recovering professionals rather than existing as one free standing pool

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Castellany Sergeants are the trained professionals of Flanders' fortified network. Some guard gates, stores, courtyards, prisoners, records, roads, and approaches. Others carry messages, escort officers, respond to alarms, reinforce threatened posts, or form the disciplined core around which less experienced defenders can gather. The profile represents a field-capable selection from that wider professional world, not every servant or guard attached to a castle.

On the battlefield, the unit provides what ordinary Flemish Spearmen cannot: reliable order under pressure. Castellany Sergeants can maintain a prepared spear line, receive mounted contact, relieve a failing frontage, restore cohesion around a gate or crossing, and continue fighting after lower-tier troops begin to fragment. Better equipment and training improve survival, but the unit remains a spear anchor rather than an assault elite.

Their opportunity cost defines the card. Flanders' connected defensive network depends on staffed strongpoints. Releasing professionals may strengthen one field army while exposing a gate, route, store, command post, crossing, or neighboring community. A good player concentrates Castellany Sergeants for a reason, uses them where their steadiness matters, and returns survivors before the defensive web becomes hollow.

## 12. Muster, Detachment, and Return Cycle

1. A field, relief, escort, or strongpoint mission is identified.
2. The exact castellany source evaluates custody obligations and threats.
3. Eligible professionals, leaders, equipment, and supplies are checked.
4. A releasable detachment is selected without double counting.
5. Remaining personnel receive redistributed posts and alarm duties.
6. The detachment assembles as a professional spear-and-shield body.
7. The company serves within a defined objective, area, and duration.
8. The source strongpoint tracks the custody gap and recall pressure.
9. The company returns, transfers by owner authority, or enters recovery.
10. People, equipment, posts, training capacity, and source readiness recover separately.

## 24. Battlefield Rhythm

1. Enter battle as a compact professional body with clear leaders and signals.
2. Establish or reinforce a prepared line connected to a road, gate, camp, crossing, settlement approach, or main field frontage.
3. Receive ordinary pressure without wasting the formation in premature advance.
4. Use internal files and cadres to preserve spacing and replace exhausted or damaged elements.
5. Relieve Flemish Spearmen, Town and Gate Companies, or another threatened friendly section.
6. Face mounted pressure with prepared spears and shielded order.
7. Advance a short distance when the objective requires pressure or recovery of ground.
8. Stop before professional order becomes an unsupported assault.
9. Withdraw by files or toward a known route if the mission fails.
10. Return surviving people, leaders, equipment, and institutional knowledge to the castellany network.

## 25. Strengths

- Clearly trained and more reliable than Flemish Spearmen.
- Strong professional spear frontage.
- Good prepared response to mounted pressure.
- Strong gate, road, crossing, camp, and fortified-relief utility.
- Better equipment completion and maintenance.
- Better leaders, signals, and disorder recovery.
- Can relieve weaker friendly frontage without immediate collapse.
- Moderate campaign endurance with proper support.
- Valuable bridge between broad ordinary infantry and scarce household elites.
- Retains use throughout later development if the source institution survives.

## 26. Weaknesses

- Narrow and strongpoint-specific source.
- Field use creates a custody gap.
- Slow replacement and recovery.
- More expensive to maintain than ordinary spearmen.
- Vulnerable to concentrated missiles and crossbows.
- Limited offensive shock.
- Limited pursuit.
- Fatigue accumulates during prolonged static defense or repeated relief.
- Open flanks remain dangerous.
- Long-distance campaigns weaken both the company and source castellany.
- Simultaneous threat to source and field company can force recall or strategic failure.

## 34. Demobilization and Return

6. Leaders and surviving professionals rebuild duty rosters and training.
7. Custody capacity, alarm response, confidence, and field availability recover separately.

## 35. Garrison, Settlement, Road, and Siege Use

### Castle and castellany custody

Core institutional role. The unit family exists because recurring professional strongpoint duty exists.

### Settlement and route relief

May reinforce towns, roads, crossings, markets, stores, camps, and neighboring posts where owner responsibilities permit.

### Siege defense

Strong at gate relief, wall-access control, courtyard defense, breach containment, inner reserve, and sortie response. Not automatically the entire garrison.

### Siege offense

Useful for siege-work security, camp defense, gate or breach consolidation after access is won, and resisting sorties. Not default ladder, ram, mining, or first-wave breach infantry.

### Field army

Best used as a professional anchor or relief body within a mixed host. Prolonged field use must expose and track the source cost.

## 39B. Stronger Total War Combat Contract

### Professional Spear Work

The unit wins through a repeatable battlefield method:

- **Set the professional front:** establish useful spear reach, shields, depth, leaders, and protected flanks.
- **Receive and fix:** absorb ordinary infantry or frontal mounted pressure without surrendering formation control.
- **Work the frontage:** use repeated spear thrusts, shield protection, local file replacement, and controlled yielding rather than static bracing.
- **Relieve or advance:** either replace a failing neighbor or take one bounded piece of ground.
- **Restore the line:** shorten, redress, hand off, withdraw by files, or remain as the new anchor.

```yaml
professional_spear_method:
  SetTheFront: EstablishReachFacingDepthAndFlanks
  ReceiveAndFix: PreserveOrderThroughContact
  WorkTheFrontage: SustainedSpearShieldAndFileCombat
  RelieveOrAdvance: ExecuteOneBoundedProfessionalTransaction
  RestoreTheLine: EndOnUsableFrontageOrWithdrawInOrder
```

### Power Contract

When serviceable and correctly supported, Castellany Sergeants should:

- decisively defeat emergency and low-tier spear infantry;
- hold a clear advantage over ordinary organized regional spears;
- defeat civic spear companies in an equal professional contest;
- fight selected sword-and-shield infantry as a conditions-driven reach-versus-close-combat matchup;
- make frontal cavalry commitment dangerous on prepared ground;
- stabilize a sector already under material pressure;
- remain useful after one major intervention if cadres and equipment survive.

They should not:

- defeat Count's Household Foot in an equal prolonged sword melee;
- beat intact pikes at clean reach;
- out-perform Goedendag Companies at their exact prepared impact;
- ignore concentrated bolts, flanking, deep mud, exhaustion, or encirclement;
- cycle repeated full-strength relief actions without degradation.
