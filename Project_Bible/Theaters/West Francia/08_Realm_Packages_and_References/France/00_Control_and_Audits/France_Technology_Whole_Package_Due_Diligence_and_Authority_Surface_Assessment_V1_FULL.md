## 4. France Realm Development Thesis

France opens with a crowned protected minor, a real consecrated Crown, narrow direct military and material capacity, wide recognition, a limited Royal Demesne, distributed minority government, and powerful princely interfaces. Development is therefore not one linear national modernization meter.

France's Realm-development game should ask whether royal recognition can become durable practical capacity through:

- supervised formation and conditional personal rule;
- reliable offices, records, mandates, seal custody, communication, and delegated execution;
- protection and recovery of direct communities and households;
- sustainable taxation, revenue, property, markets, transport, and supply;
- enforceable but bounded law, custom, jurisdiction, and remedies;
- negotiated service, accepted contingents, finite recruitment, and command capacity;
- settlement, route, crossing, river, castle, ecclesiastical, and institutional networks;
- learning, literacy, record continuity, technical competence, and transmission;
- diplomacy, recognition, mediation, external locks, and princely cooperation;
- campaign experience that may strengthen, redirect, stall, or fragment royal development.

## 9. Candidate France Development Portfolios for Later Authoring

The following are bounded design directions for the eventual portfolio, not approved unlocks or opening facts:

- **Minority Government and Personal-Rule Capacity:** supervised participation, information access, office coordination, mandate review, lawful command eligibility, and conditional personal government.
- **Royal Demesne Reliability:** direct communities, castles, garrisons, routes, officers, records, revenue, storage, and local cooperation without pretending the demesne is the kingdom.
- **Princely Coordination and Negotiated Service:** summons, contributions, mediation, bounded contingents, joint action, refusal, substitution, and withdrawal terms.
- **Administrative and Documentary Capacity:** literacy, clerks, records, messages, accounting, seals, custody, archives, and durable institutional memory where owner support exists.
- **Legal and Ecclesiastical Consolidation:** jurisdiction, custom, witnessed acts, remedies, recognition, Church interfaces, and regional reception without one uniform national code.
- **Settlement, Route, River, and Strategic Infrastructure:** crossings, communications, storage, repair, markets, castles, river transfer, and route security through correct owners.
- **Economic and Material Coordination:** treasury reliability, property, revenue, markets, credit, transport, stock visibility, allocation, and recovery without free resources.
- **Military Access, Recruitment, and Endurance:** technology-facing requirements only, routed to the separate military workstream for units, pools, equipment, training, command, and supply.
- **Knowledge Adoption and Regional Realization:** competence transmission, apprenticeship, ecclesiastical and household learning, external contact, local adoption, maintenance, loss, and recovery.
- **Conditional Capetian Development:** multiple viable paths toward stronger royal capacity, continued negotiated weakness, shared rule, stalled development, or alternative institutional forms.
