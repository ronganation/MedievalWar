## 2. Refined Verdict

France begins with a crowned child king, a distributed minority government, a narrow and disconnected Royal Demesne, operationally independent princes, and cross-Realm dynastic, ecclesiastical, military, and commercial interfaces. These are opening conditions, not an event queue.

Historical Emergence converts owner-approved state into conditional strategic pathways. It does not reproduce a fixed chronology. A pathway may become eligible, activate, compete, redirect, reverse, suspend, fragment, resolve differently from recorded history, or retire without producing its familiar historical analogue.

**History provides conditions, opportunities, constraints, and remembered precedents. Runtime state determines what happens.**

The V1 portfolio remains substantive. V2 strengthens it with developed-Realm parity: explicit candidate classes, participant roles and consent, competition groups, positive and negative eligibility fixtures, owner-command outcome packets, military and settlement consequence handoffs, failure and closure states, opening activation prohibitions, and a complete validation catalogue.
