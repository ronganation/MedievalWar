## 1. Refined Verdict and France Character Fantasy

Implement the French opening cast as a small set of strategically meaningful characters, not an exhaustive court directory. The active France-facing core remains Philip I, Anne of Kiev, Hugh of France, Baldwin V of Flanders, Ralph IV of Valois, and Theobald III of Blois. Henry I remains Historical Tier only. Other rulers are consumed through cross-Realm references and retain the authority of their owning packages.

**Wear the Crown before possessing the kingdom.**

France is distinctive because a recognized, consecrated Capetian kingship already exists, while direct royal machinery, military execution, household control, regional obedience, and territorial administration remain distributed. The character game is therefore not about inventing France. It is about protecting and developing a real Crown whose dignity exceeds its immediate operational reach.

The player must decide how to:

- preserve Philip and Hugh without turning them into passive tokens;
- use Anne's maternal, household, ceremonial, diplomatic, and political capacity without granting universal regency;
- use Baldwin's experience without allowing Flanders to become France's hidden government;
- use Ralph's proximity and military weight without confusing access with authority;
- negotiate with Theobald and other great princes whose recognition does not equal automatic service;
- convert ceremony, relationships, offices, routes, forces, revenues, and institutions into executable royal action;
- permit personal rule, negotiated consolidation, princely partnership, continued plural authority, dynastic crisis, or alternate political settlement to emerge from current conditions.
