## 1. Refined Verdict

Implement the January 1062 French political network as a sparse, directed, asymmetric graph of relationships, access, communication, expectations, promises, grievances, dependencies, recognition, service interpretation, public posture, private posture, and holder-specific knowledge.

The controlling political core remains:

- Philip I as crowned child king;
- Anne of Kiev as queen mother and household anchor;
- Baldwin V of Flanders as senior protector and minority-government actor;
- Ralph IV of Valois as a powerful local adviser and alternative access route;
- Hugh of France as underage succession depth;
- Theobald III of Blois as a major independently capable prince;
- William of Normandy, the Flemish-Hainaut family, Burgundy, Anjou, Aquitaine, and Brittany as owner-locked external interfaces.

**Recognition of the Crown does not imply trust in a government actor, agreement with a summons, shared knowledge, safe access, available service, or willingness to perform.**

## 2. Preserved V1 Substance

This V2 successor preserves and develops the complete substantive V1 portfolio:

- sparse directed records rather than an all-to-all opinion matrix;
- separate forward and reverse relationships;
- qualitative relationship dimensions;
- Philip-Anne, Philip-Hugh, Philip-Baldwin, Anne-Baldwin, Anne-Ralph, Philip-Ralph, Ralph-Baldwin, Philip-Theobald, Theobald-Ralph, Crown-Normandy, Flanders-Hainaut, Burgundy, Anjou, Aquitaine, and Brittany interfaces;
- institution-mediated access;
- knowledge and communication channels;
- promise, obligation, grievance, dependency, public posture, and private posture separation;
- Network commands, events, AI, save, migration, determinism, performance, player presentation, Unreal handoff, validation, classification, and acceptance requirements;
- prohibitions against inferred symmetry, adult child politics, cross-Realm duplication, universal knowledge, direct authority mutation, and later-history preload.

No substantive V1 relationship surface is intentionally discarded. V2 supersedes obsolete metadata, universal seven-month cadence, Unreal Engine 5 targeting, ambiguous `active` labels, and the obsolete V1 next action.
