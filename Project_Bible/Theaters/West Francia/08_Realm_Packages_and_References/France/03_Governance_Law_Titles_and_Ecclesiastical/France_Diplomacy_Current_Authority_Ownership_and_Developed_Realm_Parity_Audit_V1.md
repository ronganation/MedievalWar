**Capetian Royal Recognition, Minority Government, Narrow Direct Capacity, Sacral and Dynastic Standing, and Negotiated Princely Cooperation**.

## 12. Recognition, Homage, Service, and Jurisdiction Gap

France particularly requires a disciplined distinction among:

- recognition of king, Crown, title, office, jurisdiction, claimant, government, and settlement;
- homage or other political acknowledgment;
- service expectation;
- negotiated or lawfully required obligation;
- capacity reservation;
- mobilized contribution;
- arrived and ready contribution;
- command relationship;
- completed performance;
- remedy for nonperformance.

None may be collapsed into one loyalty score or one automatic hierarchy edge.

## 13. Ecclesiastical and Sacral Interface Gap

The recovered France law authority references a dedicated ecclesiastical content owner. A diplomacy authority must coordinate, not absorb, that owner.

```text
Sacral Kingship != Papal Obedience

Episcopal Recognition != Secular Administrative Control
Papal Interest != Papal Action
Church Mediation != Diplomatic Result
Sanction Threat != Sanction
Excommunication Or Interdict Question != Automatic War Permission
```

Petition, mediation, patronage, canonical review, appointment, sanctuary, oath, sanction, dispensation, and ecclesiastical recognition require Church-owner receipts.
