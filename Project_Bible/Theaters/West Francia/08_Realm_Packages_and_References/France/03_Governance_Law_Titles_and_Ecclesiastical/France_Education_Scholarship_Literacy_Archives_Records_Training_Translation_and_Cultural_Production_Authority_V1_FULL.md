## 1. Purpose and Strong France Verdict

This authority defines how education, learning, literacy, numeracy, scribal and documentary practice, training, apprenticeship, texts, records, archives, interpretation, translation, scholarship, patronage, historical transmission, and cultural production may operate in France. It fills the meaning-layer gap between France's current Character, Dynasty, Governance, Law, Ecclesiastical, Community, Economic, Military, Historical, Diplomatic, AI, and Opening State authorities without absorbing their private state.

```yaml
FranceEducationVerdict:
  model: DistributedRoyalHouseholdEcclesiasticalLocalPracticalAndExperientialKnowledgeNetwork
  opening_problem: >-
    Represent the competence needed to protect and form a crowned minor, govern through delegated authority, witness and preserve acts, correspond, account, interpret, negotiate, administer direct holdings, sustain castles and routes, coordinate military and logistical work, preserve memory, and transmit regional practice without inventing universal literacy, one mature royal chancery, fixed schools, libraries, archives, famous scholars, complete institutional rosters, or automatic technology progress.
  player_promise: >-
    Build durable but uneven competence through identified people, relationships, practice, texts, records, institutions, patronage, travel, access, protection, and repeated use while accepting scarcity, distortion, loss, regional difference, interrupted transmission, competing custody, and incomplete recovery.
  opening_state_readiness: HOLD
  exact_opening_education_instances_created: 0
```

France's educational system is not one court, one building, one church, one literacy percentage, one school network, or one technology meter. The Royal Demesne, princely territories, ecclesiastical institutions, towns, castles, households, and regional communities may possess different people, practices, languages, texts, records, materials, access conditions, and transmission risks. Crown recognition creates no automatic access to the competence or records of the wider kingdom.
