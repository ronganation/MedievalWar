## 1. Concise Verdict

Implement France diplomacy as **Capetian Royal Recognition, Minority Government, Narrow Direct Capacity, Sacral and Dynastic Standing, and Negotiated Princely Cooperation**.

France is not a centralized command hierarchy projected backward from later royal power. The Crown possesses recognition, continuity, precedence, sacral standing, household and ecclesiastical channels, and the ability to convene, mediate, petition, bargain, and seek service. Those advantages do not create direct control of every prince, lordship, castle, bishopric, military pool, treasury, route, or foreign counterparty.

```text
Recognized Crown
+ Valid Minority-Government Authority
+ Actor-Visible Knowledge
+ Authenticated Communication
+ Directional Recognition
+ Negotiated Princely And External Consent
+ Owner-Reserved Capacity
+ Recorded Performance And Remedy
+ Credible Peace, Settlement, And Continuity
= Sustainable French Diplomatic Action
```

This authority creates no opening envoy, message, treaty, homage record, alliance, war, service obligation, contribution, access grant, subsidy, wardship, papal action, princely submission, external response, or later Capetian outcome.

## 15. Capetian Recognition and Royal Precedence

France's Crown can possess wide symbolic and political importance while direct material capacity remains narrower. Diplomatic strength may derive from continuity, recognition, sacral presentation, convening power, mediation, dynastic connections, church interfaces, and the ability to make disputes legible across princely boundaries.

The system must not convert those strengths into universal control. Royal recognition can improve access, credibility, precedence, and settlement leverage. It cannot manufacture administrative reach, troops, taxes, obedience, property, jurisdiction, or foreign acceptance.
