## 1. Inspection Verdict

The internal France board succeeds as a dense first-pass visual culmination and may be retained as a ReviewOnly internal reference. The board communicates the intended high-level identity, **The Consecrated Crown**, and presents a recognizable hierarchy of faction identity, opening ruler, royal household, minority government, Royal Demesne, great principalities, ecclesiastical legitimacy, political network, economy, military, routes, historical context, emergence, Realm AI, player presentation, state legend, and technical handoff.

The board is not production-ready. Several labels, role descriptions, map details, heraldic treatments, and historical-looking claims require later correction or owner verification. Those findings do not require immediate redesign because the current decision is to continue France development rather than enter a visual-refinement loop.

## 3. Objective Visual Inventory

### 3.1 Overall composition

The board uses a wide landscape layout with a dark blue and gold header, parchment-colored content panels, thin dark borders, and a dark technical footer. The title area spans most of the upper center and reads **FRANCE**, **THE CONSECRATED CROWN**, and **Opening State • January 1, 1062 (Winter)**.

The content is divided into labeled modules from A through S. Large portrait art occupies the opening-ruler and royal-household areas. A small parchment map appears in the Royal Demesne section. Heraldic shield graphics appear in the faction identity, great principalities, Realm AI, and technical-handoff sections. Small symbolic icons represent recognition, royal reach, possibility, governmental roles, economy, military, routes, historical memories, emergence pathways, and state classifications.

### 3.2 Header and hero region

The upper-left faction-identity panel contains Realm, Faction, Dynasty, Theme, Reality, and Promise labels. A crown and blue shield with gold floral devices appear beside a large architectural landscape.

The upper-center title uses oversized gold serif lettering over a dark background. A settlement or cathedral skyline appears behind the title.

The upper-right promise-and-tension panel shows three gold icons with the labels **Sacred Recognition**, **Narrow Royal Reach**, and **Strategic Possibility**, followed by the line **A king is crowned, a kingdom is not yet possessed.**

### 3.3 Character and government region

The opening-ruler panel contains a large crowned youthful portrait and text identifying Philip I of France as crowned, underage, dynastically and ecclesiastically recognized, without adult governance authority, and dependent on distributed minority government.

The royal-household panel contains four smaller portrait cards labeled Anne of Kiev, Hugh of France, Henry I of France, and Baldwin V of Flanders. Each card includes a short role description.

The minority-government panel uses five icons for Legal Guardian, Political Protector, Household Keeper, Military Protector, and Seal and Diplomatic Representation. Supporting text states that authority is decomposed and bounded and that no single regent controls all functions.

### 3.4 Political, ecclesiastical, and geographic region

The Royal Demesne panel lists Paris, Orleans, Senlis, Etampes, and Melun and includes a stylized parchment map with several royal nodes.

The Great Principalities panel highlights Valois and Blois-Champagne and displays additional shield symbols for other princely houses.

The Ecclesiastical Legitimacy panel lists Reims, Laon, Beauvais, and Saint-Denis and includes a cathedral illustration and short statements about consecration, church partnership, bishops, and abbeys.

The Political Network panel uses six round portrait nodes connected by solid and dashed directional or relational lines. A small legend distinguishes alliance, influence or contact, and stake or interest.

### 3.5 Systems and player-facing region

The Economy and Treasury panel uses coin, scales, and chest icons. The Military and Recruitment panel uses shield, troop, and cavalry symbols. The Routes and Communication panel uses horse, bridge, ship, and letter icons. The Historical Context panel uses short bullet points.

The Emergence and Memory panel separates Memories from Emergence Pathways. The Faction AI panel identifies a Consecrated Arbiter concept. The Player Presentation panel contains a landscape image and the lines **High legitimacy. Narrow reach. The Crown is real. The realm is becoming.**

The First-Turn Priorities panel shows four numbered priorities. The State Legend panel uses colored circular symbols and labels for approved, review-only, external reference, deferred exact record, intentional empty, historical only, quarantined, legacy reader only, and runtime validated states.

### 3.6 Technical footer

The production-and-technical-handoff area includes palette samples, an heraldry example, typography notes, map-style direction, portrait-style direction, iconography examples, and Unreal Engine 5.8 handoff candidates. A narrow status strip at the bottom states that the board is static-checked and review-only and disclaims Registry mutation, Opening State activation, import, and runtime mutation.

## 6. Specific Correction Ledger for Later Revision

### VIS-FRA-001: Exact age displayed for Philip

```yaml
severity: High
observed_text: Underage (16)
issue: The controlling presentation requires an underage ruler but does not approve this exact age display.
risk: A precise unsupported or incorrect age may be treated as historical authority.
later_correction: Replace with Underage or insert only an owner-verified date-derived age.
immediate_redesign_required: false
```

### VIS-FRA-002: Henry I role label

```yaml
severity: High
observed_text: Elder Prince
issue: The board's own description presents Henry as deceased historical legacy, while the role label says Elder Prince.
risk: The label can obscure Henry's status as predecessor and historical-only subject.
later_correction: Use Deceased Predecessor or Historical Predecessor.
immediate_redesign_required: false
```

### VIS-FRA-003: Baldwin V relationship wording

```yaml
severity: Medium
observed_text: Neighbor and Ally
issue: The visual authority requires an external senior protector and princely actor with bounded, owner-controlled relations. Ally is broader than the documented bounded relationship.
risk: The board may imply a permanent alliance or automatic French access to Flemish resources.
later_correction: Use External Protector and Princely Actor or Bounded Protector.
immediate_redesign_required: false
```
