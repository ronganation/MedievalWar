## 1. Concise Verdict

France in January 1062 is not one homogeneous population, one language block, one national identity, or one body of subjects directly governed by the Crown. It is a recognized kingdom containing the narrow direct Royal Demesne, royal and ecclesiastical centers, rural communities, settlement populations, local lordships, princely territories, institutions, households, clerks, cultivators, craftspeople, merchants, transport workers, castle dependents, and mobile people whose cooperation is mediated by local authority, custom, property, routes, markets, and protection.

The Crown-facing community game should center on **reliable cooperation under minority government**. The player must protect and connect direct communities, preserve labor and household resilience, obtain clerical and local administrative capacity, maintain market and route access, negotiate institutional assistance, and avoid exhausting the small directly accessible base through simultaneous taxation, requisition, construction, recruitment, billeting, and transport demands.

Exact population totals, urban shares, language proportions, literacy rates, institutional rosters, and community obligations remain unknown unless specialist owners validate them. Unknown does not mean empty. Qualitative bands and typed capacities support play without fabricated precision.

## 49. Risks and Mitigations

- **France becomes culturally homogeneous.** Preserve local, regional, princely, settlement, institutional, and household plurality.
- **Population becomes a fake precise number.** Use qualitative capacities until evidence owners validate quantities.
- **Population becomes direct manpower.** Maintain typed finite recruitment inputs and opportunity costs.
- **Community play becomes micromanagement.** Summarize conditions and surface only consequential tradeoffs.
- **Institutions duplicate Ecclesiastical authority.** Use institution interfaces and retain owner locks.
- **The Crown accesses the entire kingdom.** Separate direct, negotiated, external, and unavailable capacity.
- **Modern French identity is backdated.** Enforce chronology and later-history exclusions.
- **Burden becomes automatic rebellion.** Require actors, organization, opportunity, leadership, and owner-routed outcomes.
- **Relief becomes free resources.** Require stores, finance, transport, authority, and distribution.
