France should retain three distinct lowest-service expressions:

1. assignment-only civilian emergency defense;
2. a recruitable Armed Peasant Levy family where a repeatable safe source and movable campaign decision exist;
3. Rural Levy Spearmen where enough compatible spear equipment, leadership, and formation practice support a spear-centered levy card.

The Armed Peasant Levy survives because its strategic purpose is not simply to be a weaker Rural Levy Spearmen formation. It owns cheap movable garrison presence, camp and baggage work, task absorption, emergency bodies, and mixed-weapon obstruction where a spear-standardized frontage cannot be assembled or should not consume the better rural levy pool.

## 2. Historical and Claim Boundary

This authority does not claim one uniform French peasant military institution, a kingdom-wide conscription system, a universal serf army, or one legally identical class of combatants. “Armed Peasant Levy” is a working gameplay family name for validated lowest-pool service drawn from eligible and released people in peasant, rural household, village, parish, estate-dependent, or related local branches where France-specific owners establish the source and obligation.
