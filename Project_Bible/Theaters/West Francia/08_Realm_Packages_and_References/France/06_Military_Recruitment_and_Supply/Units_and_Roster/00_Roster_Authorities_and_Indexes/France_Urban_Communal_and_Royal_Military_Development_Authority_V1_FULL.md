This authority defines how French urban defense, communal organization, royal service, crossbow capability, professional sergeant service, and later household equipment may develop from existing people, obligations, settlements, institutions, workshops, records, officers, and material capacity. It does not create a universal technology tree, a national standing army, or a parade of fixed-date unit upgrades.
