## 2. Decision Standard

A category may become a gameplay formation without claiming a permanent historical corps only when:

- the category exists in a validated institution;
- personnel can be selected and grouped repeatedly;
- compatible weapons and protection can be inspected;
- command and assembly exist;
- the battlefield cycle is distinct;
- campaign costs and recovery are distinct;
- the card communicates rather than obscures the institution;
- the historical classification remains honest.

## 5. Why the Category Alone Is Insufficient

Voulge equipment alone does not determine:

- formation size;
- command;
- spacing;
- mission;
- service duration;
- regional access;
- tactical behavior;
- replacement capacity;
- relationship to other weapon categories.

## 6. Why One Gameplay Formation Is Justified

The broader system supplies:

- identifiable local sources;
- a selection and registration process;
- equipment qualification and custody;
- recurring practice and inspection;
- captaincy assembly;
- mobilized royal field service;
- demobilization and replacement;
- explicit weapon-category differentiation.

The game can therefore represent assembled Voulge-qualified contributors as one bounded card without inventing a national standing corps.

## 16. Role Guardrails

Voulgiers are not:

- better-stat Guisarmiers;
- spear-wall infantry;
- pike infantry;
- halberdiers;
- shock-receiving heavy infantry;
- household guards;
- standing professionals;
- universal anti-cavalry infantry.

## 17. Combined-Arms Difference

Guisarmiers rely strongly on an already engaged target and brief disruptive entry. Voulgiers still require support but can maintain a more deliberate polearm presence once committed.

## 27. Selected Voulgiers Ruling

Selection is currently better represented as veteran or quality state. A separate card requires a persistent separate selection process, formation role, recovery burden, and player decision.

## 28. Royal Voulgiers Ruling

Royal command of the base institution does not create a Crown-owned royal card. A separate royal expression requires a direct Crown source and distinct battlefield and campaign cycle.

## 29. Professional Voulgiers Ruling

The base formation is organized recurring service, not standing professionalism. A professional branch requires sustained pay, continuous or recurring institutional training, inspection, supply, and separate recovery.

## 30. Urban and Regional Ruling

Urban and regional differences should first appear through availability, source, visuals, obligations, equipment details, and administration. Separate cards remain blocked unless battle cycles diverge.

## 32. Regional Availability Boundary

No France-wide uniform availability is authorized. Each region requires functioning local sources, administration, equipment, inspection, routes, and captaincy access.

## 33. Category Composition Boundary

The profile may represent a Voulge-dominant assembled formation for gameplay clarity. It must not claim exact historical proportions or prohibit all supporting personnel unless later evidence resolves composition.

## 34. Adjacent Weapon Categories

Bow, crossbow, lance, and other permitted categories remain separate research tracks. Their existence does not force symmetrical cards.

## 35. Francs-Archers Name Boundary

The broader institution remains `FrancsArchersInfantrySystem`. The Voulgier card must not imply that every franc-archer carried a bow or that all francs-archers were Voulgiers.

## 36. Mobilization State Interface

The profile should inherit:

- registered but not ready;
- equipped but not practiced;
- category qualified;
- assembled;
- active service;
- reduced;
- returning;
- recovering;
- replacement pending;
- recruitment suspended.

## 37. Equipment Custody Interface

The profile should inherit contributor-held, parish-supported, parish-stored, muster-issued, campaign-carried, damaged, certified-lost, negligently-lost, repair, and replacement states.

## 38. Abuse and Reform Interface

Administrative abuse, unfair burdens, equipment compulsion, false rolls, or captaincy failure may reduce or suspend access. Reform can improve operation without creating capacity.

## 39. Later Start-Date Interface

A later start may include the institution and Voulgier card where a separate Opening State authority validates rolls, sources, equipment, captaincies, and formations.

## 40. Master Profile Requirements

The first France Voulgier master profile must include:

- honest gameplay-composite classification;
- royal-parochial source identity;
- complete recruitment ladder;
- local and royal ownership matrix;
- category-versus-formation explanation;
- equipment custody and replacement;
- captaincy assembly and active service;
- battlefield differentiation from Guisarmiers;
- finite pools and no double counting;
- mobilization, casualties, return, and recovery;
- AI, UI, art direction, fixtures, and stopping conditions;
- historical source ledger and unresolved questions.

## 45. Rejection Conditions

Reject the profile if it:

- claims a permanent universal corps;
- loses the royal-parochial institutional source;
- duplicates Guisarmier disruption behavior;
- turns equipment coherence into elite status;
- treats the formation as professional;
- invents category ratios;
- creates national manpower;
- removes regional and administrative gates.

## 46. Acceptance Criteria

The decision passes when:

- category and formation are distinguished;
- one gameplay card is justified transparently;
- Guisarmier collision is resolved;
- institutional and battlefield differences are both meaningful;
- all additional cards remain blocked;
- profile authoring remains review-only and non-activating.
