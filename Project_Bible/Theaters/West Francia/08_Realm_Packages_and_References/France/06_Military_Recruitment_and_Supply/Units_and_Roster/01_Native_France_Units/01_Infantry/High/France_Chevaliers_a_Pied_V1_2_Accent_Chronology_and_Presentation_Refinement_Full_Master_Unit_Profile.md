## 6. Player-Facing Description

Chevaliers a Pied are French knights and high-status fighting personnel deliberately committed to combat on foot. Their armor, training, standards, leaders, and household support allow them to press into hard local fighting and remain on an objective where lighter infantry may fail. Dismounting makes them useful in breaches, gates, streets, courtyards, broken approaches, and infantry centers that deny cavalry room to charge.

They remain scarce people drawn from real knightly households. Every dismounted formation reduces mounted options, consumes foot-compatible weapons and armor, requires squires and retainers, and still needs the horses, remounts, tack, grooms, and security that permit movement before and after battle. The player gains a powerful foot commitment, not free elite infantry.
