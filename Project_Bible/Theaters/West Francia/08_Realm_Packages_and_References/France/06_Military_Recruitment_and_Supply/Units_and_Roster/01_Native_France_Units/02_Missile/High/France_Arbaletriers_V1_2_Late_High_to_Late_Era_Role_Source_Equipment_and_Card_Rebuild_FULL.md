## 4. Player-Facing Description

Arbalétriers are mature French crossbow formations maintained by institutions capable of sustaining experienced shooters, officers, file leaders, instructors, maintainers, weapons, bolts, records, pay or duty, transport, relief, and replacement over demanding campaigns. The unit concentrates valuable fire, coordinates files or sections where training permits, reforms under command, and preserves an organized crossbow service through repeated operations.

Ordinary Crossbowmen remain broader, more flexible, and less damaging to replace. Arbalétriers are narrower and more institutionally demanding. Their name does not grant superior damage, penetration, morale, armor, or equipment.
