The Garde Écossaise is a rare royal-service card. The formation protects the sovereign, royal person, household, command group, standard, or an explicitly assigned high-value royal mission while contributing disciplined missile fire and credible emergency close defense.

The unit is not France's best generic longbow infantry. It should not be recruited merely because the player wants superior archers. Its value is the integration of trusted royal guard duty, scarce Scottish-origin or Franco-Scottish service continuity, mature missile competence, disciplined withdrawal, and extraction of the protected royal object.

## 4. Player-Facing Description

The Garde Écossaise represents a later Franco-Scottish royal guard formation adapted for Medieval War as a scarce missile-capable household unit. Its members serve a protected royal person, command group, standard, palace, camp, or mission. Missile power is part of the card, but guard duty controls deployment.

When committed to battle, the formation holds disciplined fire, covers the royal position, maintains an extraction route, and uses its close-defense weapons if the command perimeter is breached. The unit can support a field line, but dispersing it for ordinary missile work weakens the royal-security mission that justifies its existence.

## 20. Signature Battle Cycle

```text
Receive a royal protection, command, standard, escort, palace, camp, or exceptional field mission
-> inspect guard personnel, missile equipment, close-defense equipment, ammunition, route, and extraction plan
-> establish a protective perimeter and clear firing lanes
-> hold fire until the target threatens the royal mission or passes target policy
-> deliver controlled missile pressure without blocking command or friendly shock movement
-> contract, shift, or cover movement as the protected object relocates
-> use close defense only when the perimeter is breached or extraction requires it
-> cover withdrawal and extract the protected object
-> rally, account for people and property, restore household security, and adjudicate losses
```

## 23. Strengths

- Scarce trusted royal guard identity.
- Disciplined missile pressure from the command perimeter.
- Strong protection, covered withdrawal, and extraction mission.
- Credible emergency close defense.
- Better cohesion, command continuity, and equipment support where the mature institution passes.
- High historical Total War readability without generic super-archer status.

## 24. Weaknesses

- Very limited and politically sensitive source.
- Guard deployment debits palace, household, escort, or command security.
- Difficult replacement after captain, veteran, household, or diplomatic losses.
- Less efficient than ordinary archers for broad screening or attrition.
- Vulnerable to isolation, cavalry penetration, concentrated counterfire, and prolonged melee.
- Royal-protection mission can restrict target choice and movement.
- Losses may damage royal trust and Franco-Scottish service continuity.
