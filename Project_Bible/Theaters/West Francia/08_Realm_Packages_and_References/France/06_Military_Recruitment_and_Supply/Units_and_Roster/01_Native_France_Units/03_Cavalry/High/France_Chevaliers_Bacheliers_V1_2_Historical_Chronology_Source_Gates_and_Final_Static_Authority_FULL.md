Chevaliers Bacheliers are recognized knights who serve beneath another authorized banner, household, or senior knightly command where the relevant historical relationship is supported. Their strength is disciplined supporting shock within a larger bannered force. Their weakness is reduced independent command, narrower initiative, and dependence on the banner commander, standard, household network, and assigned battle.

They are not “junior Milites” with cheaper statistics. They own a different organizational contract: attach to a senior banner, reinforce the decisive mounted action, exploit the opening created by the principal charge, secure its flank, and rally back to the commanding standard.

## 3. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Chevaliers Bacheliers
- **Gloss:** Bachelor Knights
- **Class:** Restricted banner-dependent supporting knightly shock cavalry
- **Era:** Conditional High Era, persistent where viable
- **Source:** Recognized knights and their real horses, equipment, squires, retainers, and service relationships accepted beneath a senior banner command
- **Battle promise:** Align beneath the senior banner, support the principal charge, strike exposed seams, reinforce the mounted melee, and rally without claiming independent command
- **Best use:** Second-wave shock, flank support to Milites, reinforcement of a decisive cavalry melee, exploitation after the main charge, counterattack under banner direction, and recovery of a compromised knightly line
- **Worst use:** Independent detached command, routine scouting, castle escort duty, prepared spear fronts, unsupported frontal charges, prolonged attrition, and missions without a viable banner commander or rally standard
- **Strategic cost:** Recognized knights, households, warhorses, remounts, armor, standards, squires, retainers, pay or duty, banner capacity, local defense, succession, and slow recovery
- **Progression:** Independent High-era organizational family, not an automatic upgrade from Vavassors or Milites

## 4. Player-Facing Description

Chevaliers Bacheliers are recognized knights serving beneath a senior banner rather than commanding an independent bannered force of their own. The formation gathers compatible knights and household contingents under a validated banner commander, standard, service relationship, and campaign mission.

In battle, the unit is strongest as the supporting knightly blow. Chevaliers Bacheliers align with the senior banner, reinforce or follow the principal charge, strike exposed seams, protect a knightly flank, counterattack under direction, and rally around the commanding standard. They possess real knightly combat capability, but the card pays for its concentration through banner dependency, limited independent command, household opportunity cost, and severe losses to knights, horses, standards, and supporting personnel.

## 22. Strengths

- Recognized knightly combat capability without duplicating direct royal-household service.
- Strong second-wave shock and flank reinforcement.
- Effective support to Milites du Domaine and other senior knightly forces.
- Strong rally around a validated senior standard.
- Useful mounted-melee reinforcement and counterattack under command.
- High French High-era roster flavor grounded in organization rather than equipment inflation.

## 23. Weaknesses

- Requires valid senior banner command and accepted subordinate service.
- Weaker independent command and detached-operation value than broader Milites formations.
- Narrow knightly, horse, retainer, and household sources.
- Banner commander or standard loss can fragment coordination.
- Vulnerable to prepared spears, missiles, bad terrain, superior fresh cavalry, and repeated unsupported charges.
- Extremely slow recovery after household, knight, horse, or banner-network losses.
