## 6. Player-Facing Description

Gendarmes are France’s selected royal-company heavy cavalry: carefully reviewed mounted combatants sustained by commissioned captains, regular-pay capacity, garrisons, horses, harness, retainers, and a broader ordonnance institution. They are committed to break a decisive target, answer a heavy enemy charge, or secure a brief mounted advantage that the rest of the army can exploit.

They are not expendable super-knights. Poor terrain, prepared anti-cavalry formations, missile concentration, horse losses, captain disruption, arrears, and repeated charges can waste an exceptionally expensive royal investment.

-> select a decisive target and preserve an approach and rally lane
-> form under captain, standard, and lance relationships
-> advance without exhausting horses

## 27. Strengths

- Elite selected heavy cavalry.
- Powerful charge and countercharge.
- Strong brief mounted melee.
- Better recurring institutional support than variable pre-ordonnance service.
- Royal commissioned command and review.
- Strong rally and reform potential when captain, standards, horses, and support survive.
- Iconic late French battlefield identity.

## 28. Weaknesses

- Extremely expensive people, horses, harness, pay, garrisons, servants, and maintenance.
- Vulnerable to prepared anti-cavalry formations, obstacles, missiles, and poor terrain.
