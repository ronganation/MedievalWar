```text

!= hold the formal infantry line
Wait for stronger troops missiles terrain or confusion to create disorder
-> move through broken or contested space
-> attack isolated disordered or exposed targets
-> recover useful property only where command and battle state permit
-> withdraw before fresh cavalry or disciplined heavy infantry counterattack
-> rally around recognized Ribaud leadership or the royal army body
```
