## 1. Purpose

This review determines whether the West Francia Holy Order admission authority, Templar authority, and Hospitaller authority form one coherent institutional layer.

It checks:

- January 1062 chronology and opening exclusion;
- canonical and transregional ownership;
- Realm relationship routing;
- houses, hospitals, commanderies, preceptories, estates, and property terminology;
- Templar and Hospitaller institutional distinction;
- Papal, ecclesiastical, royal, ducal, comital, urban, and local relationships;
- Templar suppression and later property transfer;
- personnel and capacity conservation;
- military mission escalation and unit-profile firewalls;
- opening and implementation quarantine;
- whether exact site, Realm-relationship, or unit files should begin now.

This review does not create an Order, site, property, formation, unit, pool, price, stable ID, opening binding, payload, runtime state, or Unreal asset.
