## 1. Purpose

This dossier assembles the source classes, supported claims, limits, contradictions, and promotion status for the medieval Paris Temple complex and its later suppression and disposition lifecycle.

It distinguishes:

- evidence supporting a Paris Templar institutional complex;
- evidence supporting a fortified monastic complex and broad component types;
- evidence supporting royal treasure custody by the thirteenth century;
- archival evidence for commanderies and royal trial administration;
- the Papal commission proceedings conducted at Paris between 1309 and 1311;
- suppression and post-suppression property administration;
- later Hospitaller institutional succession;
- evidence that is insufficient to establish exact geometry, values, residents, inventories, or military formations.
