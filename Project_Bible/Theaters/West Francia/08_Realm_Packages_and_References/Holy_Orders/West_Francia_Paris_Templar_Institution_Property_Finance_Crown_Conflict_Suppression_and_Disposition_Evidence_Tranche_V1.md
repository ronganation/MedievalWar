## 1. Purpose

This tranche establishes the evidence architecture required before the Paris Temple may become an exact institutional site authority.

It focuses on:

- the Paris Temple as a major Templar institutional and administrative subject;
- the relationship between the Paris complex and the wider Province of France;
- property, revenue, custody, transfer, and financial functions where exactly supported;
- royal, Papal, ecclesiastical, urban, and local jurisdiction;
- the French Crown action beginning in 1307;
- arrest, custody, inquiry, trial, and suppression as separate states;
- administration, seizure, misuse, transfer, recovery, and long-tail property disposition;
- the distinction between the institution, site, residents, visitors, prisoners, accounts, properties, and military formations;
- the exact evidence still required before site promotion.

This tranche does not create the Paris Temple in the campaign, reconstruct its building plan, create a treasury value, assign exact residents, create a Templar formation, or bind later events into Opening State.
