## 1. Purpose

This audit determines which Templar and Hospitaller site, network, property, route, and Realm-relationship candidates possess enough evidence to justify the next bounded research authority.

It does not convert heritage references, archaeological summaries, modern site names, Order traditions, tourist descriptions, or broad regional scholarship directly into campaign entities.

It answers:

- which evidence lanes are strong enough for exact-site research;
- which candidate sites remain held pending primary or critical scholarly verification;
- which relationships belong to France, Normandy, Flanders, Brittany, Burgundy and Champagne, or Aquitaine and southern West Francia;
- which Templar properties may later require Hospitaller transfer and disposition records;
- which route, hospital, charity, estate, and military claims remain separate;
- whether any exact Order formation or unit profile is currently justified;
- where the Holy Order workstream should stop to avoid symmetry-driven paperwork.
