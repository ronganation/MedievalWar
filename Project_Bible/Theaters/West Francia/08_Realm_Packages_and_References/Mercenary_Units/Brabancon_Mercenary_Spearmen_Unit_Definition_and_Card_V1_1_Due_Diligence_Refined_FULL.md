Brabançon Mercenary Spearmen are a proposed hired long-spear infantry family associated with exact validated Brabançon, Flemish, or compatible Low Countries sources. Their battlefield promise is **Brace and Press**. They establish a deep, ordered spear frontage, deter mounted approaches and conditionally receive a charge when properly prepared, protect contracted missile troops and vulnerable flanks, and advance deliberately when cohesion, ground, leadership, and support permit.

They are not merely generic spearmen with better statistics. Their value comes from experienced company service, long-spear discipline, source-bound equipment and leadership, operational mobility between employers through valid contracts, and a campaign burden that includes payment, supply, political risk, return, replacement, and possible disorder when service or demobilization fails.

## 4. Player-Facing Card

### Short Description

Experienced Low Countries mercenary spearmen whose long weapons and ordered frontage can deter mounted approaches and anchor a hired battle line.

### Encyclopedia Description

Brabançon Mercenary Spearmen are contracted infantry drawn from exact companies or bands whose personnel, long spears, protection, leaders, service history, and terms permit outside employment. They provide employers with disciplined frontage that may be unavailable through ordinary recruitment, but hiring them does not transfer ownership of their source or create an unlimited pool.

On the battlefield, they are strongest when positioned deliberately. Their long spears create a dangerous approach for cavalry and help secure crossbowmen, archers, camps, crossings, and coalition infantry. They may press forward in order, but they are not shock troops. Missiles, flank attack, obstacles, fatigue, rapid turning, leadership loss, and close infantry combat after the spear formation breaks can dismantle their advantage.

### Strengths

- strong prepared long-spear frontage
- conditional mounted-approach deterrence
- dependable line anchoring for a mercenary or coalition army
- protects crossbowmen, archers, camps, crossings, and withdrawal routes
- useful deliberate advance on suitable ground
- experienced company cohesion pending exact-source validation
- clear Low Countries mercenary identity

### Weaknesses

- vulnerable to missiles
- vulnerable to flank and rear attack
- formation-sensitive during turns and obstacle contact
- poor pursuit
- slow withdrawal under pressure
- limited shock value
- loses advantage if the enemy enters inside the spear frontage
- external replacement and long-spear equipment remain source-bound
- contract, payment, recall, return, and demobilization can affect future availability

### Player Guidance

Use Brabançon Mercenary Spearmen to hold one valuable frontage, shelter missile troops, or make a deliberate supported push. Keep their flanks protected and do not chase broken enemies. If the formation becomes fragmented, withdraw behind fresher troops before close combat consumes the company.
