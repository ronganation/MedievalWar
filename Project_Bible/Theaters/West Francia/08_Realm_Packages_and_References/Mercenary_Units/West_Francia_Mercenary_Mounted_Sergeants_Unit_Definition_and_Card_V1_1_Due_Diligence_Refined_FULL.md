Mercenary Mounted Sergeants provide a West Francia employer with mounted troops capable of escort, screening, counter-cavalry work, local reinforcement, controlled charges, pursuit, and withdrawal protection. Their battlefield promise is **Escort, Engage, Withdraw**. They preserve formation and mission discipline, commit against a suitable light or exposed target, maintain only bounded contact, and disengage before knights, prepared spears, or a prolonged melee destroys their advantage.

They are not weaker knights. Their value lies in dependable medium cavalry utility, lower shock commitment, broader escort work, and a different campaign source and replacement burden.

## 4. Player-Facing Card

### Short Description

Hired medium cavalry who escort vulnerable troops, counter lighter horsemen, and deliver controlled charges without the weight or staying power of knights.

### Encyclopedia Description

Mercenary Mounted Sergeants are contracted cavalry drawn from exact companies possessing trained riders, suitable horses, weapons, protection, leaders, and a sustainable service route. They give an employer useful mounted capability without granting ownership of their people, animals, equipment, or home sources.

Their strength is flexibility. They can guard crossbowmen and slingers, patrol a flank, reinforce a threatened sector, pursue a broken enemy, or charge exposed troops. They should not fight as improvised knights. Prepared spears, heavier cavalry, poor ground, horse fatigue, deep pursuit, and prolonged melee can reduce or destroy the company quickly.

### Strengths

- dependable medium cavalry mobility
- escort and flank security
- conditional interception and pressure against light cavalry when speed, formation, terrain, and support permit
- mobile reserve and crisis reinforcement
- controlled charge against exposed or disordered targets
- pursuit and withdrawal protection
- more durable in contact than light skirmish cavalry

### Weaknesses

- inferior shock and protection to heavy knights
- vulnerable to prepared spears and dense infantry
- vulnerable to heavier cavalry in prolonged melee
- finite horses, remounts, tack, riders, and leaders
- poor in severe congestion or broken ground
- repeated charges exhaust horses and cohesion
- expensive to sustain compared with light cavalry
- replacement remains external and source-bound

### Player Guidance

Keep Mercenary Mounted Sergeants near vulnerable troops or behind a threatened flank until a useful target appears. Charge exposed light troops or disordered infantry, maintain brief contact, then withdraw and reform. Preserve them for escort, reserve, and pursuit instead of wasting them in a frontal contest with knights.
