West Francia Mercenary Slingers are a proposed theater-level light missile family drawn from exact companies, communities, or practiced sources willing and able to contract for service. Their battlefield promise is **Cast, Displace, Repeat**. They move ahead of or beside the main line, use clear arcs and suitable ground to deliver sling-stone pressure, then shift before cavalry, close infantry, or concentrated missile fire can trap them.

They survive as a separate card because sling handling, projectile selection, firing arc, ammunition weight, field collection, formation spacing, and skirmish rhythm differ materially from bows, crossbows, and javelins. They do not survive merely because slings are inexpensive.

## 4. Player-Facing Card

### Short Description

Practiced hired slingers who pressure exposed troops with arcing missiles, then shift before heavier enemies can close.

### Encyclopedia Description

Mercenary Slingers provide West Francia armies with temporary access to practiced light missile troops outside ordinary domestic recruitment. Their slings are simple, but effective military use depends on repeated familiarity with casting rhythm, projectile selection, distance judgment, formation spacing, and safe displacement. A treasury can pay a company; it cannot instantly teach a population to sling accurately under battle pressure.

On the battlefield, slingers work best where they can exploit open arcs, uneven ground, screening space, and a protected withdrawal route. Their missiles can disrupt exposed or lightly protected troops and contribute to sieges or camp fighting, but they do not guarantee armor penetration. Cavalry, close infantry, counter-fire, obstacles, confined space, and poor ammunition can dismantle the formation quickly.

### Strengths

- practiced light missile harassment
- distinctive sling trajectory where line of sight, release clearance, range, and battlefield geometry permit
- mobile skirmish formation
- counter-skirmisher pressure
- relatively simple personal weapon
- flexible natural or prepared projectile sourcing only after selection, sorting, carriage, and owner-controlled access
- useful camp, siege-approach, and screen support

### Weaknesses

- highly skill-dependent
- vulnerable to cavalry
- vulnerable to close infantry
- poor melee performance
- projectile weight and transport burden can accumulate
- unsuitable stones or projectiles reduce effectiveness and readiness
- casting needs spacing and safe arcs
- counter-fire and formation congestion are dangerous
- external replacement remains source-bound

### Player Guidance

Use Mercenary Slingers ahead of a protected line or from ground that gives them room to cast and withdraw. Pressure exposed troops, skirmishers, and movement corridors. Do not leave them in front once enemy cavalry or close infantry begins a committed approach.
