## 5. Core Character Thesis

William Rufus begins as a living younger child inside a powerful but succession-sensitive ducal household. William Rufus matters because the direct line contains more than one future possibility, not because later English kingship is already latent as mechanical destiny.

The defining opening tension is **distinct formation without back-projected kingship**. William Rufus needs protection, education, recognition, and room to form a personal relationship with service, competition, religion, household discipline, and family expectation. Yet no later reputation can be used to make the child prematurely ruthless, militarized, irreligious, politically calculating, royal, or destined for England.

William Rufus should develop differently from Robert Curthose and Richard because lived experience, treatment, education, opportunity, and later campaign conditions diverge, not because the game secretly knows the recorded future.
