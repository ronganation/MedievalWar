## 1. Authority Thesis

Normandy Historical Emergence owns conditional pathways through which accumulated owner-approved state, accessible Memory, active Momentum, decisions, institutions, relationships, claims, military conditions, geography, resources, and player or AI action can produce recognizable political transformations without replaying recorded history by script.

Emergence owns pathway eligibility, participant selection, consent, lifecycle stage, competition, redirection, reversal, failure, dissolution, and owner-routed outcome requests. Emergence does not own the facts, memories, pressures, people, titles, offices, armies, resources, relationships, claims, government, or resulting owner state.

## 2. Ownership Model

### Emergence owns

- pathway definitions and instances;
- prerequisite and blocker evaluation;
- candidate participants and required consent classes;
- lifecycle stage and transition rules;
- competition, compatibility, counter-Emergence, and arbitration;
- redirection, fusion, succession, reversal, failure, dissolution, and closure;
- AI and player explanation requirements;
- proposed owner handoffs and receipts;
- deterministic update, save, migration, validation, and test contracts.

### Emergence does not own

- source facts, Memory, Momentum, claims, relationships, authority, offices, titles, institutions, people, households, forces, logistics, economy, property, or recognition;
- opening activation;
- AI decisions or owner commands;
- event probability or scheduling;
- automatic historical outcomes;
- owner-state mutation.

## 21. Cross-Realm Boundary

Pathways involving England, France, Maine, Brittany, Flanders, Boulogne, or external church audiences require their owners for participants, consent, titles, institutions, forces, access, recognition, public memory, and outcomes. Normandy may define Normandy-facing eligibility and proposed terms. Normandy may not create another Realm's internal pathway or consent.

## 22. Later-History Firewall

The following may not activate a pathway at opening:

- guaranteed English invasion or victory;
- proven universal designation or oath breach;
- predetermined papal endorsement;
- future English crown, earldoms, honors, grants, garrisons, or settlements;
- later births, deaths, rebellions, imprisonments, or partitions;
- predetermined <Person>Robert Curthose</Person> conflict;
- predetermined <Person>William FitzOsbern</Person> death;
- completed or universally accepted `Gesta Guillelmi`;
- a predetermined Norman Empire or permanent cross-Channel monarchy.
