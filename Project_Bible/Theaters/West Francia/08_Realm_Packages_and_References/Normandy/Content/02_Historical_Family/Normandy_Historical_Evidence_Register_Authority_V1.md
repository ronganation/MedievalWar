## 1. Creation and Benchmark Verdict

The Historical Suite audit found a genuine Normandy ownership gap for stable proposition identity, source classification, provenance, custody, authenticity, corroboration, contradiction, evidence confidence, opening eligibility, and reusable evidence receipts. This authority fills that gap without re-owning historical interpretation, chronology, uncertainty, Memory, Momentum, Emergence, claims, people, institutions, or exact opening state.

The developed benchmark pattern is explicit. Wales states that its Evidence Register owns historical proposition identity, evidence classification, source provenance, contradiction references, confidence, opening eligibility, downstream routing, and later-history exclusion. England identifies Historical Evidence as a Realm authority consuming Reconciliation, Memory, Momentum, and Emergence. Scotland defines its register as Historical Evidence, proposition provenance, contradiction, and source-action authority. Normandy adopts these functions without copying their Realm propositions.

## 3. Ownership Model

### Historical Evidence owns

- proposition and evidence-record identity;
- source records and source classes;
- provenance and transmission chains;
- custody, integrity, authenticity, access, and legibility state;
- support, contradiction, dependency, exclusion, and supersession links;
- evidence-level confidence and confidence rationale;
- opening-cutoff eligibility and later-history exclusion;
- downstream receipts to Chronology, Uncertainty, Memory, Momentum, Emergence, claims, AI, and Opening State;
- source-action requests, review state, save, migration, determinism, and validation contracts.

### Historical Evidence does not own

- which historical interpretation is adopted;
- dates, ranges, ordering, simultaneity, or chronology resolution;
- uncertainty-alternative selection;
- holder belief, memory, salience, or reception;
- pressure or pathway state;
- person, title, office, institution, household, relationship, claim, army, property, or geography state;
- exact January 1062 initialization;
- source retrieval rights outside authorized systems;
- historical event scheduling or outcomes.

## 20. Proposition Decomposition Rules

A composite portfolio must be decomposed before binding. One proposition should assert one addressable historical claim at one defined scope. Person identity, kinship, office, title, location, action, date, motive, legal effect, audience reception, and later interpretation should be separate propositions when each can vary independently.

```text
OneParagraph != OneProposition
SharedSource != SharedConfidence
SamePerson != SameOffice
SameEvent != SameAudienceReception
SupportedIdentity != SupportedLocation
SupportedAction != SupportedMotive
SupportedClaimAssertion != ValidClaim
```

## 21. AI and Player Presentation

AI receives only evidence summaries permitted by access and owner routing. The player-facing view may distinguish known project proposition, source assertion, corroborated evidence, contested evidence, inaccessible evidence, later-history exclusion, and unresolved question.

The interface must not display a single omniscient certainty score where sources disagree on different dimensions.

## 24. Save and Load

Save preserves proposition, source, assertion, provenance, custody, access, relation, confidence, eligibility, exclusion, receipt, supersession, quarantine, and version state. Loading does not turn proposed or unavailable evidence into confirmed evidence.

## 25. Migration

Migration may map deprecated identifiers, preserve provenance and contradictions, split composite propositions where lossless, merge true duplicates with retained aliases, rebuild derived summaries, or quarantine unresolved records. Migration may not invent a source, author, witness, custodian, date, assertion, confidence, permission, or historical conclusion.

## 27. Validation Matrix

### Fatal

- a portfolio is represented as a populated primary-source record;
- a source assertion becomes verified proposition without review;
- evidence confidence creates historical interpretation or opening state;
- custody is treated as authenticity or public access;
- contradiction is resolved randomly or through later-history hindsight;
- a composite proposition binds without decomposition;
- unsupported foreign state is authored by Normandy;
- later history becomes opening evidence;
- source retrieval, permission, translation, authentication, compile, runtime, or import validation is claimed without receipts;
- this authority mutates Opening State, Registry, runtime, payload, or Unreal.

### Warning

- a proposition mixes identity, date, action, motive, office, and legal effect;
- evidence lacks locator, provenance, custody, confidence rationale, or prohibited inference;
- negative evidence is treated as proof of absence without scope;
- modern synthesis is presented as a contemporary source;
- internal authority language is presented as independent historical evidence;
- audience-specific reception is collapsed into one Realm conclusion.
