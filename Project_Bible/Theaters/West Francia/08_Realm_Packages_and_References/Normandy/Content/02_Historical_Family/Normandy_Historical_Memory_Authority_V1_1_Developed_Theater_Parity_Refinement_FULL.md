## 1. Authority Thesis

Normandy Historical Memory owns holder-specific and audience-specific interpretations of already owned facts, experiences, claims, ceremonies, conflicts, service, promises, institutions, places, victories, defeats, and precedents. Memory changes how actors interpret current options. Memory does not create the underlying fact, claim, title, office, relationship, army, resource, authority, or outcome.

The opening Norman world remembers strength and danger at the same time. The duchy may remember the survival of a vulnerable ruler, violence among magnates, consolidation under ducal authority, rewarded and unrewarded service, household protection, church patronage, military and administrative achievement, underage succession risk, continental pressure, and disputed traditions concerning England. No memory is universal, identical, complete, or automatically active.

## 2. Ownership Model

### Historical Memory owns

- memory-family definitions;
- memory-instance structure;
- holder and audience scope;
- remembered proposition references;
- source and acquisition path;
- interpretation and confidence;
- salience and accessibility;
- public, private, institutional, household, regional, and external visibility;
- reinforcement, contradiction, contest, reinterpretation, suppression, commemoration, transmission, and erosion;
- memory-to-Momentum and memory-to-AI handoffs;
- save, migration, deterministic update, and explanation requirements.

### Historical Memory does not own

- whether the remembered event happened;
- historical chronology or evidence truth;
- opening person, title, office, household, dynasty, property, location, army, or government assignments;
- directed relationship truth;
- current claims or legal validity;
- current reputation;
- Momentum pressure stages;
- Emergence pathway activation;
- AI decisions or owner commands;
- future historical outcomes.

## 9. Memory Lifecycle

```text
Eligible proposition or experience exists
then
valid source and acquisition path reaches a holder
then
holder-specific reception and interpretation
then
confidence, salience, accessibility, and visibility recorded
then
reinforcement, contradiction, contest, commemoration, suppression, or erosion
then
possible transmission to new holders
then
owner-routed consequences for interpretation, relationships, Momentum, AI, narrative, or institutions
then
review, transformation, dormancy, archival persistence, or loss of access
```

Memory age alone does not determine decay. Repetition, ritual, records, institutions, political usefulness, trauma, reward, contradiction, and generational distance may affect persistence through owner-defined rules.
