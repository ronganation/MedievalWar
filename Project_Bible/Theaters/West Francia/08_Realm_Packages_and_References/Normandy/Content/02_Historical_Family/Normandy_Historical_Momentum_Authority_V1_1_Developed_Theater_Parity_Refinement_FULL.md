## 1. Authority Thesis

Normandy Historical Momentum translates owner-approved facts, memories, claims, relationships, institutions, military conditions, routes, economic and supply conditions, households, offices, regional reception, promises, evidence, and current events into directional pressure. Momentum makes some questions harder to ignore and some alternatives more or less urgent. Momentum does not choose policy, create events, manufacture capabilities, or guarantee recorded history.

Normandy's opening condition supports several candidate pressures: prepared ambition, founder dependency, service and reward, generational transition, English opportunity, continental balancing, regional consultation, church reception, military readiness, government capacity, narrative credibility, external commitment, and consolidation. None is active merely because the definition exists.

## 2. Ownership Model

### Momentum owns

- Momentum-family definitions and instances;
- source-input references and scope;
- direction, stage, salience, urgency, and visibility;
- escalation, stabilization, relief, redirection, transformation, dormancy, and closure rules;
- supporting, opposing, and contradictory pressures;
- collision groups and portfolio interaction;
- AI, Personality, player, and Emergence handoffs;
- deterministic update, save, migration, validation, and explanation contracts.

### Momentum does not own

- historical facts, memories, claims, evidence, relationships, offices, titles, households, armies, routes, money, supply, recognition, or consent;
- exact opening activation;
- AI selection or owner commands;
- Emergence eligibility, stage, selection, resolution, or outcome;
- event scheduling or probability;
- future historical results.

## 21. Later-History Firewall

Momentum may not initialize guaranteed pressure from:

- a known 1066 invasion or victory;
- a proven oath breach;
- predetermined papal endorsement;
- future English grants, offices, garrisons, or settlement;
- later births, deaths, rebellions, imprisonments, or partitions;
- a completed `Gesta Guillelmi`;
- predetermined <Person>Robert Curthose</Person> conflict;
