## 12. Final Review Position

Normandy already has a strong integrated Economy authority with broader Population and Settlement capacity than France's narrower Economy file. That integration is intentional and useful. The correct next step is not to split Population, Settlement, and Economy merely for symmetry. The bounded addendum should close transaction, receipt, commercial-delivery, fiscal-remittance, commissioning, rollback, and implementation-handoff currentness while preserving the existing 33 Normandy-specific portfolios and all external-owner boundaries.
