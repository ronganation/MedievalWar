## 1. Normandy Legal Verdict

Normandy should operate through a layered legal order combining ducal peace, ducal judgment and policy, regional lordship custom, castellany practice, household authority, military obligation, written and witnessed acts, church jurisdiction, negotiated privilege, and practical enforcement capacity. Normandy does not begin with one codified law book, uniform bureaucracy, modern police force, or unlimited ducal jurisdiction.

```text
ValidAuthorityAndJurisdiction
+
ApplicableLawCustomCharterOrPolicy
+
NoticeEvidenceProcedureAndAffectedParticipants
+
JudgmentSettlementOrAdministrativeDecision
+
EnforcementCapacityComplianceReviewAndRestoration
=
LawfulAndDurableOutcomeForADefinedScope
```

```text
DucalWill != Law
Law != Policy
Policy != Judgment
Judgment != Enforcement
Enforcement != Compliance
Compliance != Legitimacy
Custom != ImmutableRule
Privilege != UniversalImmunity
Emergency != PermanentConstitution
Punishment != Restoration
```

## 3. Legal Constitution and Ownership

This authority owns Normandy's law, custom, policy, jurisdiction, case, dispute, judgment, enforcement, compliance, appeal, exemption, emergency, reform, repeal, and restoration definitions and owner-handoff contracts.

This authority does not own people, titles, offices, dynasties, households, property, treasury, military forces, places, churches, external Realm decisions, historical evidence truth, Character Network state, AI commands, Opening State bindings, Registry publication, or runtime execution.

## 15. Ducal Peace and Norman Political Play

Possible player approaches include:

- direct judgment;
- mediation between lordships;
- branch compact;
- sureties and hostages with safeguards;
- limited castle restraint;
- service substitution;
- fines or compensation;
- charter confirmation;
- amnesty with guarantees;
- temporary emergency measure;
- deliberate nonintervention when authority or evidence is insufficient.
