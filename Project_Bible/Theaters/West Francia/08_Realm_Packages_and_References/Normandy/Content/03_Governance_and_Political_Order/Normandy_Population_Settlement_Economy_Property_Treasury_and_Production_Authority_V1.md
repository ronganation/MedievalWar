## 1. Concise Verdict

Normandy begins as a powerful duchy with substantial but incompletely inventoried economic capacity distributed across ducal and regional households, rural communities, settlements, castles, ecclesiastical institutions, estates, workforces, markets, routes, ports, rights, obligations, accounts, and stores. Ducal power improves coordination, but it does not make the Duke the owner of all Norman property, labor, revenue, production, or money.

The campaign economy should reward the player for converting **rights, people, property, production, routes, and records into dependable capacity** without exhausting the households and communities that sustain Norman government and war. Exact opening headcounts, property inventories, treasury balances, yields, prices, tax rates, stocks, debts, and recruitment values remain unknown until their owners validate them.

```text
Verified Population and Property Context
+ Valid Rights, Possession, Access, and Administration
+ Labor, Skills, Tools, Animals, Facilities, Security, and Season
+ Production, Storage, Routes, Markets, Accounts, and Collection
- Local Consumption, Existing Commitments, Damage, Loss, and Dispute
= Conditional Norman Economic Capacity for a Defined Place and Purpose
```

