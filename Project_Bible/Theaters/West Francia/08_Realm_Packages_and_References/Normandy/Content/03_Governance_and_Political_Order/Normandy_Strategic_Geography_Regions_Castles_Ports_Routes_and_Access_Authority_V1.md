## 1. Concise Verdict

Normandy must read as a compact but internally differentiated maritime duchy whose strength comes from connecting the ducal household circuit, regional lordships, castles, ecclesiastical centers, river valleys, roads, markets, ports, anchorages, and Channel-facing approaches. Geography should make Normandy unusually capable of concentrating force and administration while still exposing the duchy to route interruption, castle autonomy, coastal weather, regional gatekeeping, succession disruption, and the cost of projecting power beyond the Realm.

```text
Verified Physical Place or Area
+
Approved Representation Tier
+
Terrain, Hydrology, Adjacency, Route, and Seasonal Access
+
Distinct Norman Strategic Function
+
Owner-Controlled Control, Operation, Damage, and Recovery
=
Readable Modern Medieval II Campaign Geography
```

Normandy is not one settlement, one capital, one ducal demesne polygon, or a preassembled English invasion platform. Major sites remain anchors. Separate castles, ports, crossings, ecclesiastical sites, markets, estates, and route nodes require independent strategic justification and owner receipts.

## 2. Normandy-Specific Design Promise

Normandy's geographic identity rests on five connected ideas:

1. A compact territory can still contain meaningful distance, regional interests, and interrupted communication.
2. Castles and fortified residences shape authority, movement, warning, refuge, coercion, and siege warfare without becoming free garrisons or automatic property transfers.
3. Ports and coastal access create maritime opportunity, not ships, crews, lift, safe passage, or English conquest.
4. The ducal court is itinerant. Residence, records, officers, hospitality, and government reach depend on routes and actual presence.
5. A successful expedition beyond Normandy can weaken home defense, regional access, household continuity, and recovery unless Normandy preserves a functioning geographic network.

## 7. Representation Tiers and Anti-Clutter Gate

```yaml
representation_tiers:
  Tier1_PrincipalSettlement: primary_regional_anchor
  Tier2_IndependentStrategicSite: separate_object_passing_admission_gate
  Tier3_EmbeddedRegionalFeature: simulated_through_region_settlement_or_route
  Tier4_LightweightReference: required_by_another_owner_without_independent_interaction
  Tier5_HistoricalOrLaterReference: contextual_disputed_dormant_or_later_place
  ReviewOnly: unsafe_for_activation
  Quarantined: identity_location_chronology_type_ownership_or_Registry_conflict
node_admission_gate:
  minimum: TwoMaterialSystemEffectsOrOneExceptionalFunction
  qualifying_effects:
    - changes_movement_or_access
    - changes_supply_transport_trade_or_recruitment
    - changes_government_communication_or_public_order
    - creates_distinct_battle_context
    - affects_diplomacy_maritime_access_or_Historical_systems
    - can_be_raided_blockaded_occupied_or_recovered_separately
  additional_checks:
    - geographically_distinct
    - separable_owner_controller_operator_or_damage_state
    - AI_usable
    - player_readable
    - visual_cost_justified
```

Ordinary villages, farms, parish churches, mills, woods, fisheries, tracks, and minor crossings remain embedded unless this gate is satisfied.
