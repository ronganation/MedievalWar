## 10. Strategy Modes

- **Opportunity Posture:** survey theaters, gather information, preserve readiness and flexibility.
- **Prepared Expansion:** valid objective, feasible operation, secure home government, consultation, reward and settlement capacity.
- **Consolidation:** acquisitions, resistance, arrears, office overload, communication failure, or succession complexity.
- **Founder Protection:** ruler exposure or replacement capacity below minimum.
- **Succession Preparation:** heir-development gap, ruler health concern, regency weakness, family division, or title complexity.
- **Continental Balancing:** pressure or opportunity involving France, Flanders, Brittany, Boulogne, Maine, or other continental actors.
- **Strategic Restraint:** weak evidence, inadequate capacity, low consent, exposed Normandy, costly coalition, or unmanageable follow-up.
- **Emergency Government:** valid absence, incapacity, invasion, breakdown, or minority succession with owner-created authority.

A mode is mutable runtime state. No mode forces a historical outcome.
