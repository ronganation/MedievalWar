## 1. Due Diligence and Authority Verdict

The Normandy package was assessed as an integrated developed-Realm portfolio rather than as a blank faction shell. Current package control identifies a mature suite covering Opening State, historical causality, titles and succession, households, governance, law, ecclesiastical institutions, geography, economy, military capability, exact roster, Character Network, Realm Personality, and proposal-only AI. No current standalone Normandy general diplomacy authority was found in the reviewed authority surface. This file therefore adds a substantive missing capability rather than a symmetry wrapper.

Normandy diplomacy is **Prepared Ducal Ambition, Reciprocal Service, Continental Brokerage, and Conditional Cross-Channel Transformation**.

```text
Recognized Ducal Standing
+ Valid Principal and Bounded Mandate
+ Actor-Visible Knowledge
+ Authenticated Communication
+ Directional Recognition
+ Negotiated Consent and External Receipt
+ Owner-Reserved Capacity
+ Recorded Performance and Reward
+ Credible Withdrawal, Settlement, and Continuity
= Sustainable Norman Diplomatic Action
```

Normandy is unusually capable of turning diplomacy into coordinated military and political action, but capability is not ownership. William's authority, Matilda's Flemish connection, Norman ecclesiastical channels, Breton affinities, Boulogne contact, regional magnates, ports, military readiness, and disputed English arguments create options. They do not create automatic alliance, recognition, transport, war entry, troops, papal action, conquest, government, or reward settlement.

This authority creates no opening message, envoy, recognition record, proposal, agreement, alliance, war, coalition, access grant, financial obligation, protected-person arrangement, English claim activation, invasion plan, fleet, settlement, or reciprocal external state.

## 3. Developed-Realm Benchmark Result

The benchmark set included the mature general-diplomacy authorities for Flanders, Aquitaine, Burgundy, Brittany, England, Scotland, Wales, Ireland, Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Zaragoza, and the Almoravid movement-state. Normandy inherits their authority discipline, not their faction content.

Required parity areas carried into Normandy are:

- principal, representative, bearer, witness, audience, interpreter, and ratifier separation;
- directional recognition rather than one mutual-opinion scalar;
- knowledge provenance, confidence, visibility, staleness, and contradiction;
- full communication lifecycle including delay, interception, loss, forgery, misunderstanding, and response;
- proposal, counterproposal, reservation, agreement, performance, breach, remedy, and termination separation;
- alliances, guarantees, neutrality, claimant support, external service, and information-sharing clauses;
- war authorization, contribution, coalition, command, withdrawal, separate peace, and settlement;
- access, safe conduct, ports, transport, supply, landing, and return capacity;
- gifts, tribute, subsidy, ransom, compensation, restitution, property, and treasury owner locks;
- marriage negotiation, wards, hostages, prisoners, refuge, welfare, release, and safe return;
- definition, opening, runtime, save, migration, and implementation separation;
- atomic cross-owner transactions, idempotence, rollback, deterministic replay, and evidence receipts.

Normandy-specific differentiation is not generic diplomatic competence. It is the need to transform high personal authority and operational credibility into agreements that survive absence, expedition, reward pressure, succession risk, and possible multi-Realm government.

## 13. Ducal Recognition and Internal Diplomatic Constitution

Normandy's strong ducal position improves standing, agenda control, access to counsel, credibility, and the capacity to coordinate. It does not erase magnate agency, ecclesiastical jurisdiction, household authority, local rights, office limits, extraordinary-service negotiation, or reward expectations.

Internal Realm diplomacy is required when policy imposes extraordinary burdens, prolonged absence, overseas service, new taxation or payment, unusual military contribution, succession exposure, office concentration, jurisdictional overlap, or disputed reward. Consultation may yield support, conditional support, substitute contribution, negotiated exemption, delay, refusal with alternative, or opposition. Disagreement does not equal rebellion.

## 14. France and Capetian Crown Interface

France remains an external owner. Normandy may negotiate recognition, precedence, service, mediation, nonaggression, frontier arrangements, hostages, guarantees, access, arbitration, or conflict settlement. Crown dignity does not create direct administration of Normandy. Norman service expectation does not create current performance.

## 15. Flanders, Matilda, and Dynastic Brokerage

Matilda's connection supplies access, credibility, information channels, household and dynastic interests, and potential mediation. It does not merge Normandy and Flanders, transfer Flemish troops, bind Baldwin V, settle Boulogne rivalry, create alliance, or authorize war.

Any Flemish arrangement must preserve:

- Matilda's person, household, and agency owner boundaries;
- the distinction between dynastic contact and Realm commitment;
- exact principals, terms, contribution, command, reward, withdrawal, and external receipts;
- French, Boulogne, Hainaut, ecclesiastical, and commercial interests where relevant;
- the possibility of support, neutrality, limited cooperation, competing priorities, or refusal.

## 16. Brittany and Breton Affinity

Breton affinity, including Alan Rufus where valid, may support contact, intelligence, brokerage, retinue or contingent negotiation, frontier mediation, and cross-cultural command. Affinity does not create Breton manpower, recruitment rights, obedience, universal cultural confidence, or permanent allegiance.

Brittany retains control of its rulers, lordships, forces, routes, ports, church, claims, and decisions. Normandy may request and record. It may not author Brittany's reverse state.

## 17. Boulogne and Eustace II

Eustace II is treated as a sovereign external principal, not as a Norman vassal. Potential cooperation requires explicit objective, contribution, precedence, command relationship, access, compensation, guarantees, withdrawal, separate-peace, and settlement terms.
