## 1. Authority Purpose

This authority defines one finite mature Norman household knightly service family with mounted and foot assignments. It prevents the active Norman Household Foot Knights card from creating a second population of knights, duplicate horses, duplicate equipment, or an unrestricted infantry upgrade.

The family is part of Internal Norman Military Development. It does not require a Pure Norman culture fork, does not block selective continental adoption, and does not convert Normandy into a second Capetian or Anglo-Norman roster.

## 3. Strong Player-Facing Family Summary

- **Family:** Mature High Era Norman household knightly service.
- **People source:** Eligible household knights, knightly retainers, officers, standards, attendants, and owner-approved associated service personnel.
- **Mounted promise:** Preserve an elite mounted reserve, protect household command, and deliver Prepared Decisive Impact against a critical target.
- **Foot promise:** Commit the same scarce knightly personnel to difficult ground, breach, gate, urban, siege, or command-area action where mounted shock is unsuitable or horses must be preserved.
- **Central decision:** Whether the household’s rare knightly people and equipment are more valuable mounted or fighting on foot for the current operation.
- **Strategic cost:** Whichever assignment is chosen removes those people, officers, armor, weapons, household duties, and associated horse capacity from every competing use.
- **Recovery:** Slow, layered, household-sensitive, politically visible, and never completed by headcount alone.

- Assigning a person to foot service removes that person from the mounted card.
- Horses remain accounted for and consume custody support.
- Foot assignment is preferred for a valid breach or confined-ground mission.
- Mounted assignment is preferred for open maneuver and countercharge.
- AI refuses instant switching during contact.
- Wounds, experience, ownership, and reputation persist across reassignment.
- Norman Milites veterans can transfer only through explicit development, destination qualification, and accounting.
- Norman Men-at-Arms remain more suitable for sustained heavy-infantry field service.
- Norman Castle Sergeants remain custody-and-fortification centered.
- Recruitment closure preserves active veteran formations.

## 41. Negative Validation Fixtures

- One person appears simultaneously in mounted and foot formations.
- Every Household Knight creates a spare warhorse for another unit.
- Clicking research instantly creates Foot Knights.
- A castle automatically produces household knights.
- Foot Knights replace Norman Men-at-Arms because of better values.
- Knightly status automatically creates castle custody or protected-access trust.
- Foot assignment grants universal anti-cavalry capability.
- AI dismounts for a hidden numerical bonus.
- High-era chronology automatically converts Norman Milites.
- England or France shares Normandy's active people pool.
