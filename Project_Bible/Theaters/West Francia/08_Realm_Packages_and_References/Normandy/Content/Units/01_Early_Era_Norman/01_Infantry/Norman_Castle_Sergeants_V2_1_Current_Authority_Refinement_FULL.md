## 6. Player-Facing Description

Norman Castle Sergeants are dependable nonknightly professionals attached to a castle or castellany service structure. They guard gates, support watches, control access, protect stores and prisoners, respond to alarms, and reinforce threatened approaches with greater order than ordinary local infantry.

Their value is inseparable from the site that sustains them. A ruler may detach Castle Sergeants to escort a convoy, reinforce a relief column, secure a captured gate, or protect engineers, but the same personnel are then unavailable for routine custody. The player gains reliable infantry by accepting a visible reduction in castle security.

Castle Sergeants are not a universal professional field army. They are most valuable when their knowledge, officers, equipment, signals, reserves, and fallback positions remain connected to fortified service.

## 9. Distinction from Norman Spearmen

Norman Spearmen are Normandy's organized town and developed-settlement spear card. They own ordinary settlement defense, road and gate approaches, crossing protection, missile support, and broader local frontage. Castle Sergeants instead belong to a specific castle or castellany service structure and carry protected custody duties.

## 26. Battlefield Rhythm

1. Receive a clear site or detachment duty.
2. Occupy controlled frontage with known fallback positions.
3. Maintain access, custody, or protected movement.
4. Brace or block only with adequate warning and depth.
5. Support missiles, engineers, or reserves.
6. Shift to sidearms when space defeats the spear.
7. Rotate exhausted files when custody continuity permits.
8. Withdraw toward a strongpoint instead of pursuing.
9. Restore duty rosters after action.

## 27. Strengths

- Reliable prepared defensive order.
- Familiarity with gates, approaches, signals, and fallback positions.
- Strong custody and escort discipline.
- Better equipment maintenance than ordinary local infantry.
- Useful breach reserve and captured-gate security.
- Dependable support for missiles, engineers, commanders, and relief forces.
- Visible campaign tradeoff when detached.

## 28. Weaknesses

- Narrow site-bound source.
- Slow professional recovery.
- Field detachment reduces castle readiness.
- Limited pursuit and shock.
- Spears can become awkward in tight interiors.
- Vulnerable to missiles, fire, fatigue, officer loss, flanking, and internal disorder.
- Distant service strips away site familiarity and strains terms.
- Multiple simultaneous threats can overload a small professional core.

## 29. Counterplay

Opponents may:

- threaten several access points;
- use missiles and fire pressure;
- attack command, gates, water, stores, or signals;
- create internal disorder or betrayal where shared systems permit;
- force the unit away from fallback positions;
- exhaust the watch through repeated alarms;
- isolate a field detachment from its site and relief route;
- commit assault infantry after disruption.

## 32. Siege and Garrison Use

### Defense

- gates, approaches, courtyards, wider streets, walls where space permits, stores, water, prisoners, commanders, and breach reserves;
- strongest with intact signals, officers, reserves, missiles, fire response, and fallback routes;
- sidearms become more important in compressed interiors.

### Offense

- protect specialists and engines;
- resist sorties;
- secure captured gates and approaches;
- guard camps, prisoners, stores, and routes;
- avoid first-wave ladder, bridge, or breach assault.

### Garrison

This family is a professional garrison component, but it does not equal the entire nominal garrison. Militia, regional troops, household personnel, missiles, servants, labor, and other sources remain distinct.

## 33. Escort, Patrol, and Detachment

Escort and patrol duties consume actual capacity. Prisoners, messages, pay, stores, commanders, and convoys do not receive free background protection.

A controlled field detachment requires:

- explicit objective;
- custody reservation check;
- command assignment;
- service terms;
- route and supply;
- duration;
- replacement duty roster;
- return plan.
