### 6. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Norman Militia Crossbowmen
- **Class:** Organized local crossbow militia
- **Source:** Qualifying settlements, castles, towns, ports, markets, households, or local institutions possessing real defenders, operators, weapons, bolts, tools, officers, storage, repair, and service authority
- **Availability:** Narrow, finite, uneven, equipment-limited, and workshop-dependent
- **Battle promise:** Affordable local deliberate fire against protected or compressed targets from prepared positions
- **Best use:** Walls, towers, gates, bridgeheads, fords, settlement lanes, palisades, chokepoints, camp defenses, and static support behind infantry
- **Worst use:** Mobile screening, exposed relocation, melee, cavalry contact, pursuit, unsupported assault, deep expeditionary service, and ammunition exhaustion
- **Strategic tradeoff:** Consumes local defenders, crews, workshops, crossbows, strings, spanning tools, bolts, stores, carts, officers, and repair capacity
- **Counterplay:** Rapid closing, cavalry, counterfire, smoke, cover, dispersal, multiple approaches, reload pressure, forced relocation, and attacks on equipment or supply

### 7. Player-Facing Description

Norman Militia Crossbowmen are organized local defenders trained or repeatedly practiced in the handling of early crossbows. The formation is strongest when familiar officers, prepared positions, dry storage, compatible bolts, repair support, and infantry protection preserve its slow firing cycle.

The militia delivers fewer shots than bow units, but each successful shot may place stronger pressure on protected or compressed targets under favorable conditions. This does not create universal armor penetration. Weapon power, bolt construction, range, angle, target protection, crew skill, weather, and current equipment condition shape every result.

The formation is neither a broad peasant levy nor a trained field specialist corps. Emergency personnel can reinforce or degrade the unit, but recurring local organization is the core. Trained Norman Crossbowmen remain superior in crew discipline, equipment consistency, target control, campaign endurance, withdrawal, and recovery.
