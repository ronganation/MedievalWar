### 4. One-Screen Player Card

- **Unit:** Norman Town Militia Swordsmen
- **Class:** Organized local sword-and-shield militia
- **Source:** Eligible Normandy settlement communities with recurring local-defense organization
- **Availability:** Broad to moderate locally, finite, seasonal, equipment-dependent, and cooperation-dependent
- **Battle promise:** Affordable local infantry that defends streets, gates, roads, stores, village approaches, and compressed frontage better than an emergency levy
- **Best use:** Villages, wider streets, gates, courtyards, roads, markets, stores, hedges, settlement approaches, missile protection, and fighting disordered light infantry
- **Worst use:** Receiving cavalry, attacking intact spear lines, distant expeditions, unsupported open-field advance, concentrated missiles, elite assault infantry, and prolonged pursuit

- **Counterplay:** Spears, cavalry, missiles, armor, flanking, fatigue, forced movement, officer loss, and isolation from supporting formations

### 5. Player-Facing Description

Norman Town Militia Swordsmen are organized local defenders drawn from eligible settlements and maintained through recurring musters, familiar leaders, community equipment, and short-service obligations. Swords and shields make the formation useful in villages, streets, gates, courtyards, roads, markets, and other places where long spear frontage is difficult to preserve.

The militia is more orderly than an emergency rural levy but remains neither professional nor household infantry. The formation can protect local missile troops, reinforce a threatened entrance, fight through compressed terrain, or counterattack an exposed light formation. It should not receive cavalry in open ground or attack intact professional spear lines without combined-arms support.

A militia muster is not free manpower. Every formation removes actual people, tools, carts, shields, blades, leaders, and time from the settlement. Heavy casualties weaken local watches, workshops, transport, fire response, household stability, and the next muster even when the treasury remains healthy.

- Withdraw toward a known local rally point rather than pursuing.
