Summary Description:
Norman Militia Spearmen are local levy infantry drawn from villages and small holdings, armed with spears and shields to hold ground against mounted enemies. They are less disciplined than household retainers but still valuable as a cheap defensive backbone for early Norman settlements and campaigning forces.
