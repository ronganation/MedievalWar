Summary Description:
Norman Sergeants are the dependable infantry backbone of early Norman armies. They stand between simple levy spearmen and elite household retainers, offering better discipline, sturdier equipment, and stronger cohesion in close-order fighting. Their role is to anchor lines, protect flanks, and counter mounted threats when the battle demands stability rather than shock.
