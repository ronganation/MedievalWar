Summary Description:
Norman Levy Crossbowmen are militia crossbow users equipped with simple early crossbows. They are less disciplined and slower than trained Norman Crossbowmen, but they provide an affordable early armor-piercing option that fits Norman military adoption of crossbow warfare.

Equipment & Appearance:

Traits & Abilities (explicit game rules):
- Early Crossbow Adoption - Gives Norman levies early access to armor-piercing missile fire
- Armor-Piercing Bolts - Better against armor than simple bows
- Slow Reload - Lower sustained fire than bow levies
- Militia Training - Inconsistent cohesion and targeting under pressure

Strengths:
- Affordable early armor-piercing ranged option
- Useful for defending chokepoints and settlements
- Performs well when protected by spear screens

Weaknesses:
- Slow reload and low total volume of fire
- Very weak in melee and vulnerable to fast flankers
- Low morale and prone to collapse when pressured

Tactics (player):
- Position behind spear levies or militia infantry
- Target armored infantry, shield walls, and dense formations
- Use on walls, bridges, and defensive lines rather than open pursuit

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Holds prepared positions and fires on armored targets
- Avoids forward melee contact
- Retreats once flanked or when morale breaks
