Summary Description:
French Men-at-Arms are the High Era expression of the Norman faction's continental turn. They adopt French-style feudal drill, heavier line fighting, and a more formal battlefield presence while still retaining Norman discipline and a preference for orderly combat.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
French Men-at-Arms represent the Norman world's continental pull toward French feudal style. They are still Norman in command, loyalty, and battlefield discipline, but their equipment and battlefield role show how Norman rulers absorbed French military habits without losing their own identity.
