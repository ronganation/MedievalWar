Summary Description:
French Town Militia Spearmen are the Norman faction's urban spear levy for the hybrid French path. They are cheaper and less prestigious than household or guard infantry, but they are vital for defending towns, stabilizing roads, and giving the faction a reliable civic spear line.

- Good for holding roads, gates, and settlement approaches
- Useful in anti-cavalry screens and defensive formations

Tactics (player):
- Hold choke points, roads, and gate approaches
- Support stronger infantry and cavalry by screening flanks
- Use them to stabilize urban fronts rather than win elite clashes

Tactics (AI behavior):
- Form defensive lines and hold urban positions
- Counter mounted threats with spear discipline
- Fall back toward stronger infantry if morale begins to fail
