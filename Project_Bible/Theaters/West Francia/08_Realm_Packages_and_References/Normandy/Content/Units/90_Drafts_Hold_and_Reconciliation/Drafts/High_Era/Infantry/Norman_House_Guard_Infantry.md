Summary Description:
Norman House Guard Infantry are the most prestigious infantry of the Norman martial household. They are the protectors of a lord’s banner, the hard center of a household field force, and the chosen men for defense in crisis. Their armor, discipline, and cohesion make them a fearsome presence when standing firm.

Historical Flavor / Lore:
Norman house guard infantry represent the most personal and prestigious infantry of the Norman lordly system. These were the men who stood closest to the lord, protected the banner, and embodied the household’s authority. Their role was not simply to fight, but to embody the power and discipline of the Norman household itself.
