## 1. Purpose

This reconciliation preserves the strong French Pavise Crossbowmen specialist profile while removing its obsolete dependence on a mutually exclusive Hybrid French cultural branch. The unit becomes a selective continental adoption available to Normandy only when professional crews, pavises, handlers, transport, workshops, institutional access, training, and battlefield doctrine genuinely exist.

The correction preserves Normandy's crossbow advantage without making the Pavise formation automatically Norman, automatically French, or automatically available through research.

## 23. Positive Validation Fixtures

- Normandy can adopt pavise practice without culture conversion.
- Professional crews remain finite and source-owned.
- Pavises require manufacture, transport, handlers, setup, orientation, repair, and recovery.
- Normandy's crossbow advantage helps integrate the practice but creates no resources.
- Heavy and Pavise specializations may coexist when actual capacity exists.
- A foreign company can remain foreign while serving Normandy.
- A localized company requires its own people, institutions, equipment, and service history.

## 24. Negative Validation Fixtures

- Selecting a French cultural path instantly creates Pavise Crossbowmen.
- Research creates pavises or trained handlers.
- One crew appears in general, Heavy, and Pavise units simultaneously.
- Pavises grant protection in every direction.
- The unit relocates without carts, handlers, time, or disorder.
- Normandy's crossbow advantage erases continental provenance.
- The word French automatically means all soldiers are foreign.
- Culture adoption blocks Heavy Crossbowmen.
