## 1. Purpose

This reconciliation preserves the strong Norman Heavy Crossbowmen profile while removing its obsolete dependence on a mutually exclusive Pure Norman branch. It also integrates the owner-approved direction that Normandy should possess a historically rooted crossbow advantage expressed through faction capability, research options, institutional readiness, and unit-family depth rather than a flat universal damage bonus.

## 2. Source-Grounded Historical Direction

The current project sources already establish crossbow use as part of Normandy's military identity across Early trained Crossbowmen, High Era professional Crossbowmen, Heavy Crossbowmen, and continental pavise development. The existing Heavy profile defines selected professional crews, heavier compatible weapons, reinforced bolts, fortified warfare, and deep institutional support.

External comparison material describes Norman forces as integrating crossbowmen and archers alongside cavalry and infantry, and broader crossbow history emphasizes that effective use depended on training, protection, ammunition, disciplined tactics, and suitable positions. These sources support an institutional and combined-arms advantage rather than guaranteed superior damage.

## 4. Normandy Crossbow Advantage Constitution

Normandy's advantage should mean that the Realm has stronger potential to organize, sustain, develop, and combine crossbow service when the underlying sources exist.

```yaml
advantage_may_improve:
  - access to crossbow-focused research options
  - crew training and replacement integration
  - officer instructor and maintainer development
  - workshop inspection repair and throughput organization
  - bolt compatibility stock management and resupply
  - siege and fortified-position doctrine
  - combined-arms coordination with infantry and cavalry
  - recovery of weapons crews and specialist knowledge
  - eligibility for Heavy and Pavise specializations
advantage_does_not_grant:
  - free crossbowmen
  - free weapons bolts pavises or armor
  - perfect accuracy
  - universal armor penetration
  - instant reload
  - immunity to cavalry or melee
  - automatic superiority over every faction's crossbowmen
```

## 5. Faction Strength With Tradeoffs

The advantage should create recognizable strengths and corresponding burdens.

```yaml
strengths:
  - earlier or broader opportunity to pursue crossbow institutions where chronology and sources permit
  - deeper crossbow family development choices
  - stronger potential crew maintenance and fire-control organization
  - better siege and fortified integration
  - more resilient specialist knowledge when institutions are protected
tradeoffs:
  - greater demand for workshops skilled labor and dry storage
  - competition for trained crews officers instructors and maintainers
  - ammunition and transport burden
  - field deployment may weaken towns castles and garrisons
  - heavy specialization reduces mobility and increases recovery cost
  - faction investment in crossbows delays competing military investments
```

A faction advantage is therefore a strategic character, not a free stat package.

## 9. Cross-Faction Consistency Rule

```yaml
faction_bonus_standard:
  required_for_every_realm:
    - historically or institutionally grounded strength
    - visible enabling sources
    - meaningful tradeoff or limitation
    - AI comprehension
    - player-facing explanation
    - counterplay
  prohibited:
    - unexplained percentage superiority
    - universal immunity
    - free population equipment or formations
    - cloning Normandy's advantage across every Realm
```

Other factions may equal or surpass Normandy in a particular crossbow expression through their own regional, civic, mercenary, technological, naval, or siege institutions. The advantage should preserve faction identity without making all alternatives inferior.

## 19. Positive Validation Fixtures

- Normandy gains distinctive crossbow development options when sources exist.
- Research improves capability but creates no crews or weapons.
- Heavy Crossbowmen require selected crews and heavy-compatible equipment.
- Internal heavy-crossbow and continental pavise investments may coexist.
- Crossbow investment delays competing military work when resources overlap.
- AI can choose not to exploit the advantage when circumstances make another investment better.
- Another faction can achieve superior results through its own stronger institutions.

## 20. Negative Validation Fixtures

- Every Norman crossbow unit receives free damage.
- Normandy begins with unlimited trained crossbowmen.
- A research button produces weapons, ammunition, crews, or officers.
- Heavy Crossbowmen appear because the calendar reaches the High Era.
- Pavise research blocks internal Norman development by culture.
- Faction advantage removes reload, supply, melee, cavalry, or recovery weaknesses.
- Other factions are prohibited from developing strong crossbow traditions.
