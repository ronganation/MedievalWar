This portfolio applies France's regional-access constitution to Brittany. It respects Brittany's established identity as a compact, high-agency duchy with restored ducal authority but incomplete territorial consolidation. Breton military capacity remains distributed among the ducal household, regional powers, castles, local musters, coastal communities, and negotiated coalitions. France may obtain access only through a valid political, territorial, contractual, or military relationship tied to actual owners and finite capacity.
