This portfolio applies the general regional-access authority to the specific France-Flanders relationship. It defines what kinds of military access may become possible, who retains ownership, what must be verified, how access changes with world state, and which candidate capabilities should advance to later research. It does not create any Flemish unit, starting contingent, recruitment pool, or French roster entry.

France and Flanders begin with a meaningful political connection, but that connection does not make Flemish people, towns, castellanies, household forces, horses, equipment, money, ships, officers, or regional pools French property. The military value of the relationship must emerge through a valid request, an accepted obligation or agreement, an available real source, an approved mission, a reachable route, a command arrangement, and a return or recovery path.

The relationship supplies court access, information, mediation, prestige, and political leverage.
