Gascon Bidowers should remain a held later research candidate. The available material supports investigating fourteenth-century Gascon, Bayonne-associated, or Anglo-Gascon service terminology, but it does not yet prove that Bidowers were an unchanged continuation of the earlier Bidet or Bidaux family. It also does not securely establish one universal spear-and-buckler package, one missile-skirmishing cycle, one institution, or one recruitment pool. A separate card would currently convert disputed continuity and secondary reconstruction into unsupported certainty.

The Catalan follow-on remains justified because the Bidet authorities already separate Almughavars as a related family, while the retrieved synthesis emphasizes similar equipment. That unresolved similarity requires a bounded collision authority so equipment resemblance does not become automatic source, institutional, or gameplay identity.
