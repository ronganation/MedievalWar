## 3. Historical Boundary

The relationship authority identifies a **Flemish Heavy Defensive Spear Capability** as a strong research candidate with possible comital, castellany, town, regional, noble, or landed sources. The authority gives the candidate a proposed role in crossing defense, camp and standard defense, heavy-spear anvil work, anti-mounted frontage, and local strongpoint defense. Exact 1062 formation depth, equipment, and ownership remain unresolved. citeturn28search108

- one standardized Flemish national regiment;
- universal communal government in 1062;
- later guild armies;
- goedendags;
- pikes;
- universal long mail;
- a permanent Flemish regiment at Paris;
- automatic French command;

## 17. Battlefield Rhythm

1. Select the critical ground before combat begins.
2. March into position under missile and cavalry cover.
3. Establish depth, facing, standards, and protected flanks.
4. Brace before mounted impact.
5. Absorb and fix frontal pressure.
6. Let the authorized host’s missiles, mounted forces, or other exploitation formation use the stalled enemy.
7. Refuse pursuit and restore formation.
8. Withdraw under agreement or recall while preserving officers and standards.

## 18. Strengths

- Strong frontal staying power when properly formed.
- Excellent defensive value at bridges, fords, roads, camps, and settlement approaches.
- Credible mounted denial without becoming an absolute cavalry counter.
- Strong protection for missile units and command assets.
- Creates a stable anvil that an authorized host can exploit.
- Better cohesion and equipment ceiling than emergency levy infantry.
- Distinct political and recruitment identity as a Flemish-owned contingent.

## 19. Weaknesses

- Narrow foreign source and uncertain access.
- Slow redeployment and poor pursuit.
- Vulnerable to flank and rear attacks.
- Vulnerable to sustained missiles when unsupported.
- Formation quality falls sharply on broken or constricted terrain.
- Home threats, recall, political disputes, or route closure can remove recruitment and replenishment.
- Heavy casualties damage the real Flemish source and recover slowly.
