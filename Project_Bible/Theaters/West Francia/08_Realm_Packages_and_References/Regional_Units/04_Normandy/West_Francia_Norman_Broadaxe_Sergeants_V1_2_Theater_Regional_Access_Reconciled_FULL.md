Piétons Hacheurs Normands are a Normandy-sourced heavy assault contingent that an authorized Realm may request or receive through a valid Realm-to-Norman-source access instrument. They give an authorized army a capability its native early infantry spine does not currently own: a compact axe-led assault body for a fixed shielded frontage, breach, gate interior, or short counterstroke against committed heavy infantry.

The card survives because it changes battlefield method and campaign politics. The requesting Realm gains a dangerous assault tool but cannot recruit, replace, or command it like native infantry. Normandy loses scarce qualified people, weapons, officers, and home-defense depth. The unit may be reduced, separately commanded, geographically restricted, recalled, or unavailable.

- **Class:** Normandy-owned regional heavy assault infantry
- **Realm-access status:** Conditional regional contingent, never native capital recruitment by default

- **Core verb:** Break and Open
- **Battle promise:** Apply concentrated axe-led pressure to a fixed, compressed, shielded, or engaged enemy frontage
- **Best use:** Supported shield-line disruption, gate fighting, breach consolidation, armored-infantry pressure, and short counterstrokes
- **Worst use:** Unscreened approach, concentrated missiles, fresh spear depth, cavalry contact, broad frontage, prolonged melee, encirclement, and pursuit
- **Requesting-Realm cost:** Diplomacy, reward, command coordination, supply, route access, recall exposure, and dependence on the Norman source
- **Norman cost:** Reduced household, castle, regional, or reserve capacity and slow replacement of axe-qualified cadres
- **Recovery:** Very slow through the original Norman source only
- **Opening state:** No starting contingent, access right, quantity, or location authorized

## 3. Player-Facing Description

Piétons Hacheurs Normands are Norman heavy foot selected for aggressive axe-led service and released to an authorized host only through a valid political and military arrangement. They retain Norman ownership, leaders, source identity, and replacement limits even when a host commander directs their battlefield objective.

They remain behind spears, shields, missiles, terrain, or another engaged formation until one hard local target is fixed. They then use a short protected approach, create weapon space, and deliver concentrated blows intended to disorder shields, pressure protected opponents, and open a local gap. They are not guaranteed to break a shield line, ignore armor, or defeat fresh spears.

Two-handed commitment creates visible danger. Active shield coverage falls when the axes come into use. Missiles, cavalry, reach weapons, fatigue, compression, and failed withdrawal can destroy the unit after its first impact. The authorized host must coordinate it rather than treat it as an automatic elite upgrade.

## 17. Battlefield Rhythm

- Hold behind Demesne Spear Service, Castle Sergeants, Sergents des Bourgs, terrain, or another protected frontage.
- Let authorized host missiles, spears, cavalry, or allied pressure fix the target.
- Confirm one objective and one approach.
- Advance through the shortest protected route.
- Open spacing immediately before commitment.
- Strike a compressed, engaged, tired, protected, or shielded target for a short interval.
- Let authorized host sword-and-shield or spear formations secure the opened position.
- Refuse pursuit, broad frontage, and repeated low-value attacks.
- Withdraw while command, standard, cadre, and route remain intact.
- Return casualties and equipment losses to the Norman source record.
