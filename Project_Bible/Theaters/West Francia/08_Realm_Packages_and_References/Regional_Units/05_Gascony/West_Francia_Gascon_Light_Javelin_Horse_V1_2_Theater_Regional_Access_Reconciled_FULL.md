## 3. Player-Facing Description

Cavalerie Gasconne are light mounted fighters drawn from validated southern Aquitanian and Gascon sources and released to an authorized host under bounded terms. They carry a limited javelin supply and light close-combat weapons while favoring agile mounts, open spacing, repeated approach and withdrawal, and pursuit of enemies who have already lost cohesion.

They are most useful before or after the decisive collision. Before contact, they screen, provoke, and threaten exposed flanks. During the battle, they draw heavy cavalry away from ideal charge lanes, pressure tired or isolated troops, and protect the outer movement of the authorized host. After collapse, they pursue broken infantry and prevent easy regrouping.

They are not miniature knights and should not win a prolonged cavalry melee. Missile concentration, disciplined light troops, fresh cavalry, spear depth, bad terrain, lost routes, exhausted horses, and depleted javelins can rapidly remove their advantage.
