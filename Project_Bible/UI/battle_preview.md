# Pre-Battle Preview System

## Overview
The pre-battle preview provides players with essential tactical information before entering the battlefield. This system allows players to evaluate terrain, weather, deployment zones, and approach options to make informed strategic decisions.

---

# Preview Elements

## Terrain Overview
Displays:
- Terrain type (plains, hills, forest, marsh, river, coastal)
- Elevation map
- Chokepoints (bridges, passes)
- Resource nodes (mines, timber camps, grain fields)

Purpose:
- Helps players choose favorable terrain.
- Reflects Medieval II’s terrain preview system.

---

## Weather Forecast
Displays:
- Current weather (clear, rain, fog, snow)
- Seasonal modifiers
- Expected changes during battle

Effects:
- Archers reduced in rain
- Visibility reduced in fog
- Movement reduced in snow

---

## Deployment Zones
Shows:
- Attacker deployment zone
- Defender deployment zone
- Flanking deployment options (if available)
- High-ground positions

Purpose:
- Allows players to plan formations before entering battle.

---

## Attacker Approach Options
Players may choose:
- Frontal assault
- Flanking approach
- High-ground approach
- Forest approach
- River crossing
- Night attack

Each option changes:
- Deployment zones
- Terrain modifiers
- Morale effects
- Visibility

Inspired by:
- Medieval II’s “attack from multiple directions”
- CK3’s terrain advantage system

---

## Defender Advantage Indicators
Displays:
- High-ground advantage
- Chokepoint control
- Fortification level
- Wall strength (in sieges)
- Archer elevation bonuses

Purpose:
- Helps attackers understand defender strength before committing.

---

## Supply & Logistics Preview
Shows:
- Army supply level
- Attrition risk
- Reinforcement availability
- Nearby supply sources (towns, castles, ports)

Purpose:
- Integrates supply system into battle planning.

---

## Loyalty & Rebellion Indicators
Displays:
- Region loyalty
- Rebellion risk
- Local militia presence

Purpose:
- Helps players anticipate morale swings and local support.

---

# Notes
The pre-battle preview system provides tactical clarity and strategic depth. It integrates terrain, weather, supply, loyalty, and approach options to create meaningful pre-battle decisions.
