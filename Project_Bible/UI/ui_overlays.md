# UI Overlays System

## Overview
UI overlays provide visual information on the campaign map. These overlays help players understand terrain, supply, loyalty, resources, and strategic features at a glance.

---

# Overlay Types

## Terrain Overlay
Shows:
- Plains
- Hills
- Forests
- Marshes
- Rivers
- Passes
- Coastal zones

Purpose:
- Helps players plan movement and anticipate battle terrain.

---

## Resource Overlay
Shows:
- Mines (iron)
- Timber camps
- Grain fields
- Horse pastures

Purpose:
- Highlights economic and recruitment-critical locations.

---

## Trade Route Overlay
Shows:
- Land routes
- Coastal routes
- River routes
- Blockaded ports
- Rebel disruptions

Purpose:
- Makes trade and supply lines visible and interactive.

---

## Supply Overlay
Shows:
- Supply radius from towns, castles, ports
- Out-of-supply zones
- Attrition risk areas
- Winter supply penalties

Purpose:
- Helps players avoid attrition and maintain army effectiveness.

---

## Loyalty Overlay
Shows:
- High-loyalty regions
- Low-loyalty regions
- Rebellion-prone areas
- Recent unrest

Purpose:
- Helps players anticipate rebellions and morale penalties.

---

## Weather Overlay
Shows:
- Rain zones
- Fog zones
- Snow zones
- Seasonal weather patterns

Purpose:
- Helps players plan battles and movement.

---

## Strategic Feature Overlay
Shows:
- Bridges
- Passes
- Ports
- Chokepoints
- Fortifications

Purpose:
- Highlights key tactical locations on the map.

---

# Notes
UI overlays make the campaign map readable and strategic. They integrate terrain, economy, supply, loyalty, and weather into a single visual system that enhances player decision-making.
