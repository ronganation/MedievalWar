Faction_Roster_Procedure:



####

faction_roster_workflow:
  Step_1:
    - identify existing units and concepts

  Step_2:
    - manually keep, refine, merge, regionalize, rehome, or cut

  Step_3:
    - classify intentional strengths and weaknesses

  Step_4:
    - map each retained unit to a real recruitment source and finite pool

  Step_5:
    - define settlement, household, institutional, cultural, regional, and mercenary access

  Step_6:
    - establish era persistence, visual modernization, cultural branches, and new parallel additions

  Step_7:
    - inspect genuine gaps

  Step_8:
    - use legacy concepts, the 31-card idea bank, historical research, and new ideas selectively

  Step_9:
    - create the actual roster and unit cards


####

  Phase_1_Source_Recovery:
    - identify existing roster indexes, legacy cards, progression charts, mods, references, and authorities
    - preserve useful concepts without assuming every concept is a unit
    - distinguish verified sources from suggestions

  Phase_2_Manual_Roster_Decisions:
    - Keep
    - KeepAndRefine
    - KeepAndRename
    - Merge
    - RehomeAsVariant
    - RehomeAsFormationOrCapability
    - Regionalize
    - Cut
    - Undecided

  Phase_3_Faction_Identity:
    - define military strengths
    - define intentional weaknesses
    - identify cultural, regional, institutional, and political distinctions
    - reject the assumption that every faction needs every battlefield capability

  Phase_4_Recruitment_Sources:
    - population and community
    - household
    - castle professional
    - civic institution
    - guild or association
    - military order
    - regional ecosystem
    - mercenary company
    - contingent or allied contribution
    - maritime source

  Phase_5_Finite_Pools:
    - capacity
    - recovery
    - recruitment duration
    - equipment availability
    - recruitment channel
    - settlement access
    - political or cultural access
    - opportunity cost

  Phase_6_Formation_and_Battle_Role:
    - battlefield role
    - permitted formations
    - formation proficiency
    - equipment profiles
    - terrain behavior
    - combined-arms dependencies
    - AI use and counterplay

  Phase_7_Era_Transition:
    - persistence
    - visual modernization
    - equipment modernization
    - cultural successors
    - optional retraining
    - parallel additions
    - gradual pool shifts
    - regional survival
    - genuine decline or retirement

  Phase_8_Gap_Review:
    - intentional weakness
    - temporary weakness
    - regional solution
    - mercenary solution
    - allied or contingent solution
    - formation or capability solution
    - genuine new unit requirement

  Phase_9_Actual_Content:
    - approved roster
    - unit definitions
    - unit cards
    - recruitment profiles
    - progression relationships
    - later Opening State and implementation work
